Bazhong weather in July

Average July weather in Bazhong, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Bazhong averages 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 23.2 °C (74 °F) nights, with 289 mm (11.4 in) of rain over the month and about 190 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Bazhong

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bazhong. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 100 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Bazhong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bazhong in July?
In July, Bazhong averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bazhong?
July is not one of Bazhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
