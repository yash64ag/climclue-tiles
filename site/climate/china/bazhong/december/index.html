How December compares in Bazhong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bazhong. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Bazhong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bazhong in December?
In December, Bazhong averages a day high of 10.6 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 75 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bazhong?
December is not one of Bazhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bazhong in December?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (289 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Bazhong in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bazhong get in December?
About 75 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bazhong in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
