Bazhong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bazhong in April?
In April, Bazhong averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bazhong?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Bazhong’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bazhong in April?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (289 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Bazhong in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bazhong get in April?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bazhong in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
