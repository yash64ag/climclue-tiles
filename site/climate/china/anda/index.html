Anda at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Anda is 654th warmest (4.5 °C year average), 99th sunniest (about 2,635 hours of sunshine a year) and 615th wettest (452 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Longfeng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Anda is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 42.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 618 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Anda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 618 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 42.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
