How September compares in Yishui

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yishui. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 107 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. October is 6.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Yishui in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yishui in September?
In September, Yishui averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yishui?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Yishui’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yishui in September?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Yishui in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yishui get in September?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yishui in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
