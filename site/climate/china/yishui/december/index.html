How December compares in Yishui

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Yishui. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Yishui in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yishui in December?
In December, Yishui averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.9 °C (25 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Yishui?
December is not one of Yishui’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yishui in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Yishui in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yishui get in December?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yishui in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
