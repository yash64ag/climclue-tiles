Yishui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yishui is 491st warmest (12.9 °C year average), 223rd sunniest (about 2,437 hours of sunshine a year) and 443rd wettest (688 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xinzhai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Yishui is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 107 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.7 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Why Yishui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yishui itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.785°N, 118.628°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-482-2986), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
