Yishui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yishui is 484th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 223rd sunniest (about 2,437 hours of sunshine a year) and 435th wettest (718 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xinzhai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Yishui is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 27.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Why Yishui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
