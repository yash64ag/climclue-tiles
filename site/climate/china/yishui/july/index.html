How July compares in Yishui

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Yishui. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Yishui in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yishui in July?
In July, Yishui averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 184 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Yishui?
July is not one of Yishui’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yishui in July?
184 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (184 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Yishui in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yishui get in July?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yishui in July?
