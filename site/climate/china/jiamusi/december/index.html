How December compares in Jiamusi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jiamusi. Compared with November: 10.0 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 2.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Jiamusi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiamusi in December?
In December, Jiamusi averages a day high of -10.9 °C (12 °F) and a night low of -21.7 °C (-7 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jiamusi?
December is not one of Jiamusi’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiamusi in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (141 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Jiamusi in December?
Yes, on average: about 10 cm (4.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Jiamusi get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiamusi in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
