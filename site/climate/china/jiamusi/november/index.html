How November compares in Jiamusi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jiamusi. Compared with October: 11.8 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. December is 9.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Jiamusi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiamusi in November?
In November, Jiamusi averages a day high of -1.7 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -10.6 °C (13 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jiamusi?
No month in Jiamusi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiamusi in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (148 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jiamusi in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Jiamusi get in November?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiamusi in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
