How July compares in Jiamusi

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Jiamusi. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. August is 1.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Jiamusi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiamusi in July?
In July, Jiamusi averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jiamusi?
No month in Jiamusi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiamusi in July?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (148 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jiamusi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiamusi get in July?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
