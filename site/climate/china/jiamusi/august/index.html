How August compares in Jiamusi

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jiamusi. Compared with July: 1.7 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. September is 6.6 °C cooler than August on average.
