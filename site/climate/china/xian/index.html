Xi’an at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xi’an is 1119th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 1261st sunniest (about 1,909 hours of sunshine a year) and 1459th wettest (664 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xianyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Xi’an is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 86 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.7 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 915 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 118% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xi’an has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 915 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xi’an itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.258°N, 108.929°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-497-2889), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
