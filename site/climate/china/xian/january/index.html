How January compares in Xi’an

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xi’an. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 3.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Xi’an in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xi’an in January?
In January, Xi’an averages a day high of 6.2 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -3.1 °C (26 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Xi’an?
January is not one of Xi’an’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xi’an in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Xi’an in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.4 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xi’an get in January?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xi’an in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
