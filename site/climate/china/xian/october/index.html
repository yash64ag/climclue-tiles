How October compares in Xi’an

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Xi’an. Compared with September: 6.0 °C cooler, 50 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. November is 6.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Xi’an in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xi’an in October?
In October, Xi’an averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xi’an?
October is not one of Xi’an’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xi’an in October?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Xi’an in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xi’an get in October?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xi’an in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
