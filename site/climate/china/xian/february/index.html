How February compares in Xi’an

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Xi’an. Compared with January: 3.7 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 5.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Xi’an in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xi’an in February?
In February, Xi’an averages a day high of 10.2 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -1.0 °C (30 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Xi’an?
February is not one of Xi’an’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xi’an in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (118 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Xi’an in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.5 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xi’an get in February?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xi’an in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
