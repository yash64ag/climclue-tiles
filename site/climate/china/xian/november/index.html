How November compares in Xi’an

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Xi’an. Compared with October: 6.8 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Xi’an in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xi’an in November?
In November, Xi’an averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Xi’an?
November is not one of Xi’an’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xi’an in November?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (118 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Xi’an in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xi’an get in November?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xi’an in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
