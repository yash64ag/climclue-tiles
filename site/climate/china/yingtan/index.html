Yingtan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yingtan is 116th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 507th sunniest (about 1,854 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (2,057 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinfeng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Yingtan is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 102 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.4 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 343 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yingtan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yingtan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 343 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
