Zhaodong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhaodong is 649th warmest (4.7 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 2,598 hours of sunshine a year) and 590th wettest (503 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hulan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Zhaodong is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 41.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 554 km from the sea.

Why Zhaodong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 554 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 41.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
