Dianga at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Dianga is 1948th warmest (6.0 °C year average), 835th sunniest (about 2,208 hours of sunshine a year) and 1724th wettest (536 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liulin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Dianga is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.7 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,385 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dianga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,385 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C. The 10 km area around Dianga, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 3.4 °C a year, 2.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
