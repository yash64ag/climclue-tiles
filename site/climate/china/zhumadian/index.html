Zhumadian at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhumadian is 359th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 332nd sunniest (about 2,089 hours of sunshine a year) and 353rd wettest (1,024 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luohe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Zhumadian is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 125 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 524 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zhumadian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 524 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
