Yufengshan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Yufengshan is 804th warmest (17.3 °C year average), 2074th sunniest (about 1,213 hours of sunshine a year) and 555th wettest (1,345 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wangjia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Yufengshan is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.2 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 921 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Yufengshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 921 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
