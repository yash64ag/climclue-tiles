Why Hechi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 339 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hechi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hechi?
August, with an average of 27.8 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.2 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 23.5 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hechi?
January, with an average of 10.3 °C (50 °F), day highs around 12.8 °C (55 °F) and night lows around 6.5 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hechi?
June, with 356 mm (14.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,737 mm (68.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hechi?
December, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hechi?
July, with about 205 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hechi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Hechi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, October and November.
What type of climate does Hechi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Hechi’s GeoNames location (24.693°N, 108.084°E; ClimaClue area sq-593-2880), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 330,131 (GeoNames).
