Hechi at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Hechi is 202nd warmest (20.6 °C year average), 1605th sunniest (about 1,490 hours of sunshine a year) and 245th wettest (1,624 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yizhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Hechi is 0.4 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.3 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, January (10.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 339 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hechi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 339 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hechi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.693°N, 108.084°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-593-2880), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
