Tongliao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tongliao is 602nd warmest (7.8 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,683 hours of sunshine a year) and 636th wettest (386 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Siping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (178 km away), Tongliao is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 244 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 37.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 320 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tongliao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 320 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
