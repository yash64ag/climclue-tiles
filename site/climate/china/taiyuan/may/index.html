How May compares in Taiyuan

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Taiyuan. Compared with April: 6.0 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. June is 4.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Taiyuan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiyuan in May?
In May, Taiyuan averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taiyuan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Taiyuan’s mild months (May, June, July and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiyuan in May?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (103 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Taiyuan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiyuan get in May?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiyuan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
