How November compares in Taiyuan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Taiyuan. Compared with October: 8.0 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. December is 6.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Taiyuan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiyuan in November?
In November, Taiyuan averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -1.8 °C (29 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Taiyuan?
November is not one of Taiyuan’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiyuan in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (103 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Taiyuan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Taiyuan get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiyuan in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
