How September compares in Taiyuan

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Taiyuan. Compared with August: 5.1 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Taiyuan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiyuan in September?
In September, Taiyuan averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Taiyuan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Taiyuan’s mild months (May, June, July and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiyuan in September?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (103 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Taiyuan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiyuan get in September?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiyuan in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
