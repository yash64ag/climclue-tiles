How October compares in Taiyuan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Taiyuan. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. November is 7.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Taiyuan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiyuan in October?
In October, Taiyuan averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Taiyuan?
October is not one of Taiyuan’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiyuan in October?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (101 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Taiyuan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiyuan get in October?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiyuan in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
