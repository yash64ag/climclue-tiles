How February compares in Taiyuan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Taiyuan. Compared with January: 4.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 5.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Taiyuan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiyuan in February?
In February, Taiyuan averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -6.0 °C (21 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Taiyuan?
February is not one of Taiyuan’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiyuan in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (103 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Taiyuan in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.0 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiyuan get in February?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiyuan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
