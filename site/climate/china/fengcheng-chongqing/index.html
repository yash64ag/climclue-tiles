Fengcheng, Chongqing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fengcheng, Chongqing is 138th warmest (18.8 °C year average), 676th sunniest (about 1,242 hours of sunshine a year) and 279th wettest (1,240 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yanjia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Fengcheng, Chongqing is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 106 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 926 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Fengcheng, Chongqing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 926 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
