Huaiyang weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Huaiyang, China (Hebei), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Huaiyang is warmest in July, with 32.5 °C (91 °F) days and 22.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 4.0 °C (39 °F) days and -7.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (133 mm); January is driest (4 mm).

Huaiyang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Huaiyang is 1339th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 389th sunniest (about 2,541 hours of sunshine a year) and 1795th wettest (501 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luancheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Huaiyang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 29.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 289 km from the sea.

Why Huaiyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 289 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huaiyang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.761°N, 114.517°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-462-2945), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
