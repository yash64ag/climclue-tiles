Kunyang weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kunyang, China (Zhejiang), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kunyang is warmest in July, with 31.5 °C (89 °F) days and 25.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 11.9 °C (53 °F) days and 5.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around June (258 mm); December is driest (49 mm).

Kunyang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Kunyang is 449th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 1422nd sunniest (about 1,789 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (1,852 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rui’an, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Kunyang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 212 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kunyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kunyang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.666°N, 120.566°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-563-3005), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
