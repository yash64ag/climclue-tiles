How March compares in Huainan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Huainan. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. April is 6.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Huainan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huainan in March?
In March, Huainan averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Huainan?
March is not one of Huainan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huainan in March?
67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Huainan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huainan get in March?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huainan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
