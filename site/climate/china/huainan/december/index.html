How December compares in Huainan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Huainan. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Huainan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huainan in December?
In December, Huainan averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -0.2 °C (32 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Huainan?
December is not one of Huainan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huainan in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Huainan in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Huainan get in December?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huainan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
