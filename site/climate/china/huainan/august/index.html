How August compares in Huainan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Huainan. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 99 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Huainan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huainan in August?
In August, Huainan averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Huainan?
August is not one of Huainan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huainan in August?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Huainan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huainan get in August?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huainan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
