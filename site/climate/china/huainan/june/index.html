How June compares in Huainan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Huainan. Compared with May: 3.9 °C warmer, 80 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Huainan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huainan in June?
In June, Huainan averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Huainan?
June is not one of Huainan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huainan in June?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Huainan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huainan get in June?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huainan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
