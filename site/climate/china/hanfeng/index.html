Hanfeng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hanfeng is 146th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 630th sunniest (about 1,442 hours of sunshine a year) and 268th wettest (1,262 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taibai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Hanfeng is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,057 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hanfeng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,057 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
