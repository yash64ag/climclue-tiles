Qingnian at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Qingnian is 423rd warmest (14.4 °C year average), 187th sunniest (about 2,512 hours of sunshine a year) and 568th wettest (542 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liaocheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Qingnian is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 28.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 249 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qingnian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qingnian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
