Why Tianfu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tianfu climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tianfu?
July, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 29.3 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 21.8 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tianfu?
January, with an average of -1.9 °C (29 °F), day highs around 1.9 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -5.6 °C (22 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tianfu?
August, with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 680 mm (26.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tianfu?
January, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tianfu?
May, with about 265 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tianfu?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 37 cm (14.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Tianfu?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Tianfu have?
Continental, hot summer, dry winter (Köppen Dwa). Hot, wet summers and cold, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Tianfu’s GeoNames location (37.264°N, 122.052°E; ClimaClue area sq-467-3020), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 115,370 (GeoNames).
