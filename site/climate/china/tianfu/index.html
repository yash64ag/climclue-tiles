Tianfu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tianfu is 504th warmest (12.3 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,544 hours of sunshine a year) and 452nd wettest (680 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Weihai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Tianfu is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tianfu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tianfu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
