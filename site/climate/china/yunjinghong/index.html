Yunjinghong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yunjinghong is 19th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 309th sunniest (about 2,143 hours of sunshine a year) and 237th wettest (1,350 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pu'er, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Yunjinghong is 3.5 °C warmer in June and 3.4 °C warmer in December, with 308 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (26.9 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, December (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 602 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yunjinghong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yunjinghong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 602 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
