How August compares in Guilin

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Guilin. Compared with July: about as warm, 86 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. September is 2.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Guilin in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guilin in August?
In August, Guilin averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Guilin?
August is not one of Guilin’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guilin in August?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (444 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Guilin in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guilin get in August?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guilin in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
