How September compares in Guilin

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Guilin. Compared with August: 2.2 °C cooler, 66 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. October is 4.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Guilin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guilin in September?
In September, Guilin averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guilin?
September is not one of Guilin’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guilin in September?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (455 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Guilin in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guilin get in September?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guilin in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
