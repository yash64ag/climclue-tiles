How March compares in Guilin

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guilin. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 5.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Guilin in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guilin in March?
In March, Guilin averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Guilin?
March is not one of Guilin’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guilin in March?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (444 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Guilin in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guilin get in March?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guilin in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
