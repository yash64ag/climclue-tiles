How July compares in Guilin

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Guilin. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 209 mm less precipitation and 81 hours more sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Guilin in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guilin in July?
In July, Guilin averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 246 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guilin?
July is not one of Guilin’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guilin in July?
246 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (455 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Guilin in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guilin get in July?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guilin in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
