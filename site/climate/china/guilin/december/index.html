Guilin weather in December

Average December weather in Guilin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Guilin averages 15.3 °C (60 °F) days and 7.4 °C (45 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 109 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Guilin

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guilin. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Guilin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guilin in December?
In December, Guilin averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guilin?
December is not one of Guilin’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guilin in December?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (444 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Guilin in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guilin get in December?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guilin in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
