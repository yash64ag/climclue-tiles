How May compares in Guilin

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guilin. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 122 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 2.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Guilin in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guilin in May?
In May, Guilin averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 380 mm (15.0 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guilin?
May is not one of Guilin’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guilin in May?
380 mm (15.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (455 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Guilin in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guilin get in May?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guilin in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
