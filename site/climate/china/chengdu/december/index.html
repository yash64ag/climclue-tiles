How December compares in Chengdu

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Chengdu. Compared with November: 5.3 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Chengdu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengdu in December?
In December, Chengdu averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 66 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chengdu?
December is not one of Chengdu’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengdu in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (249 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Chengdu in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengdu get in December?
About 66 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengdu in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
