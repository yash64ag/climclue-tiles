How May compares in Chengdu

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chengdu. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 2.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Chengdu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengdu in May?
In May, Chengdu averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Chengdu?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Chengdu’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengdu in May?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (249 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Chengdu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengdu get in May?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengdu in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
