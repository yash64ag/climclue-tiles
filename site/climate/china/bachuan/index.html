Why Bachuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 948 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bachuan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bachuan?
July, with an average of 28.1 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.1 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.0 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bachuan?
January, with an average of 8.1 °C (46 °F), day highs around 10.7 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 4.4 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bachuan?
July, with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,296 mm (51.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bachuan?
January, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bachuan?
August, with about 202 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bachuan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Bachuan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.
What type of climate does Bachuan have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Bachuan’s GeoNames location (29.849°N, 106.050°E; ClimaClue area sq-541-2860), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 208,520 (GeoNames).
