How August compares in Huizhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Huizhou. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 1.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Huizhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huizhou in August?
In August, Huizhou averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Huizhou?
August is not one of Huizhou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huizhou in August?
289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (376 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Huizhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huizhou get in August?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huizhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
