How February compares in Huizhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Huizhou. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Huizhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huizhou in February?
In February, Huizhou averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 89 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Huizhou?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Huizhou’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huizhou in February?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (376 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Huizhou in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huizhou get in February?
About 89 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huizhou in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
