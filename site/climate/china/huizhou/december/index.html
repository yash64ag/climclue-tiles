How December compares in Huizhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Huizhou. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Huizhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huizhou in December?
In December, Huizhou averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Huizhou?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Huizhou’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huizhou in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (376 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Huizhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huizhou get in December?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huizhou in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
