Huizhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Huizhou is 57th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 478th sunniest (about 1,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (1,884 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luoyang, Guangdong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Huizhou is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 261 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 14.7 °C more than its coolest, January (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huizhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Huizhou

For mild weather, the best months to visit Huizhou are February, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, October, November and December.

Why Huizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
