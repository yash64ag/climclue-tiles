Huizhou weather in July

Average July weather in Huizhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Huizhou averages 32.5 °C (91 °F) days and 25.3 °C (78 °F) nights, with 286 mm (11.3 in) of rain over the month and about 232 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Huizhou

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Huizhou. Compared with June: 1.1 °C warmer, 102 mm less precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Huizhou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huizhou in July?
In July, Huizhou averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 286 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Huizhou?
July is not one of Huizhou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huizhou in July?
286 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (388 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Huizhou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huizhou get in July?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huizhou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
