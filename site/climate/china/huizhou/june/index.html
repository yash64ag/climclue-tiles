How June compares in Huizhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Huizhou. Compared with May: 1.9 °C warmer, 128 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 1.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Huizhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huizhou in June?
In June, Huizhou averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 388 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Huizhou?
June is not one of Huizhou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huizhou in June?
388 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (388 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Huizhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huizhou get in June?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huizhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
