How November compares in Laiyang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Laiyang. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. December is 6.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Laiyang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Laiyang in November?
In November, Laiyang averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Laiyang?
November is not one of Laiyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Laiyang in November?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Laiyang in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Laiyang get in November?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Laiyang in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
