How April compares in Laiyang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Laiyang. Compared with March: 7.1 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 5.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Laiyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Laiyang in April?
In April, Laiyang averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Laiyang?
April is not one of Laiyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Laiyang in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Laiyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Laiyang get in April?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Laiyang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
