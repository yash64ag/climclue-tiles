How May compares in Laiyang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Laiyang. Compared with April: 5.8 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Laiyang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Laiyang in May?
In May, Laiyang averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Laiyang?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Laiyang’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Laiyang in May?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Laiyang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Laiyang get in May?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Laiyang in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
