Laiyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Laiyang is 500th warmest (12.5 °C year average), 175th sunniest (about 2,533 hours of sunshine a year) and 491st wettest (621 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Laixi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Laiyang is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Why Laiyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
