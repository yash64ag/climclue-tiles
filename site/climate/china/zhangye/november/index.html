How November compares in Zhangye

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhangye. Compared with October: 7.7 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. December is 6.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhangye in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangye in November?
In November, Zhangye averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhangye?
November is not one of Zhangye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangye in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (34 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangye in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangye get in November?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangye in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
