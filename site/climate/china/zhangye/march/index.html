How March compares in Zhangye

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zhangye. Compared with February: 6.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 7.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Zhangye in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangye in March?
In March, Zhangye averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -4.1 °C (25 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zhangye?
March is not one of Zhangye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangye in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (34 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangye in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangye get in March?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangye in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
