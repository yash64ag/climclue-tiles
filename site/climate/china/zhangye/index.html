Zhangye at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhangye is 593rd warmest (8.2 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,992 hours of sunshine a year) and 676th wettest (151 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinchang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (159 km away), Zhangye is 2.0 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 28.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,487 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zhangye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,487 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
