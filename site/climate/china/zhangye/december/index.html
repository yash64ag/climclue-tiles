How December compares in Zhangye

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zhangye. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Zhangye in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangye in December?
In December, Zhangye averages a day high of 1.8 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -11.0 °C (12 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zhangye?
December is not one of Zhangye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangye in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (34 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangye in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.6 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangye get in December?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangye in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
