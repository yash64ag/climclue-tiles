How August compares in Nantong

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Nantong. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. September is 4.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Nantong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nantong in August?
In August, Nantong averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nantong?
August is not one of Nantong’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nantong in August?
169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (217 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Nantong in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nantong get in August?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nantong in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
