Nantong, Jiangsu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nantong, Jiangsu in November?
In November, Nantong, Jiangsu averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nantong, Jiangsu?
November is not one of Nantong, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
