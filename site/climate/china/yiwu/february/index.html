How February compares in Yiwu

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Yiwu. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. March is 3.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Yiwu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yiwu in February?
In February, Yiwu averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 99 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yiwu?
February is not one of Yiwu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yiwu in February?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (239 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Yiwu in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yiwu get in February?
About 99 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yiwu in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
