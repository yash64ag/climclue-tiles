Yiwu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yiwu is 197th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 499th sunniest (about 1,866 hours of sunshine a year) and 165th wettest (1,517 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dongyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Yiwu is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yiwu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yiwu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
