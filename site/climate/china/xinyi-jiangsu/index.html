Xinyi, Jiangsu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xinyi, Jiangsu is 414th warmest (14.5 °C year average), 273rd sunniest (about 2,275 hours of sunshine a year) and 378th wettest (906 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pizhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Xinyi, Jiangsu is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Why Xinyi, Jiangsu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
