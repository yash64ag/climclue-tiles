Heze weather in June

Average June weather in Heze, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Heze averages 31.9 °C (89 °F) days and 20.2 °C (68 °F) nights, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of rain over the month and about 245 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Heze

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Heze. Compared with May: 4.8 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 1.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Heze in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heze in June?
In June, Heze averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Heze?
June is not one of Heze’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heze in June?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (164 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Heze in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Heze get in June?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
