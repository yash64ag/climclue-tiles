Heze in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heze in March?
In March, Heze averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Heze?
March is not one of Heze’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heze in March?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (164 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Heze in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Heze get in March?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Heze in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
