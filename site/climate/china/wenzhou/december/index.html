How December compares in Wenzhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with November: 5.4 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Wenzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in December?
In December, Wenzhou averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wenzhou?
December is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in December?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (252 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in December?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
