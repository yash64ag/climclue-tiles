How May compares in Wenzhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with April: 4.6 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Wenzhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in May?
In May, Wenzhou averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Wenzhou?
May is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in May?
203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (252 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in May?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
