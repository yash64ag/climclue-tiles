How February compares in Wenzhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wenzhou. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Wenzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in February?
In February, Wenzhou averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wenzhou?
February is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in February?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in February?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
