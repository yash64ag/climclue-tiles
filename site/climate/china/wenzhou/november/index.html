How November compares in Wenzhou

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 5.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Wenzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in November?
In November, Wenzhou averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Wenzhou?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Wenzhou’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in November?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (252 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in November?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
