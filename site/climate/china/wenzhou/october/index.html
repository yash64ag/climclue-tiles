How October compares in Wenzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with September: 4.5 °C cooler, 130 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. November is 4.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Wenzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in October?
In October, Wenzhou averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Wenzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Wenzhou’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in October?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in October?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenzhou in October?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
