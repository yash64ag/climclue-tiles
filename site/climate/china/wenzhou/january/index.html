How January compares in Wenzhou

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Wenzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in January?
In January, Wenzhou averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Wenzhou?
January is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in January?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in January?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenzhou in January?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
