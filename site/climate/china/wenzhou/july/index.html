How July compares in Wenzhou

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with June: 3.4 °C warmer, 54 mm less precipitation and 99 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Wenzhou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in July?
In July, Wenzhou averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Wenzhou?
July is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in July?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in July?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenzhou in July?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
