Wenzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wenzhou is 162nd warmest (18.4 °C year average), 544th sunniest (about 1,794 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (1,809 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rui’an, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Wenzhou is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 203 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Wenzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
