Wenzhou weather in June

Average June weather in Wenzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Wenzhou averages 27.8 °C (82 °F) days and 22.2 °C (72 °F) nights, with 225 mm (8.9 in) of rain over the month and about 136 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Wenzhou

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 43 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. July is 3.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Wenzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in June?
In June, Wenzhou averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Wenzhou?
June is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in June?
225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in June?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenzhou in June?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
