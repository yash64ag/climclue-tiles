How September compares in Wenzhou

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Wenzhou. Compared with August: 3.0 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. October is 4.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Wenzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenzhou in September?
In September, Wenzhou averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Wenzhou?
September is not one of Wenzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenzhou in September?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Wenzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenzhou get in September?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenzhou in September?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
