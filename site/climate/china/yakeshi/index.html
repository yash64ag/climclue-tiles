Why Yakeshi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 935 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 44.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Yakeshi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Yakeshi?
July, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 24.0 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 14.3 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Yakeshi?
January, with an average of -24.8 °C (-13 °F), day highs around -18.8 °C (-2 °F) and night lows around -29.7 °C (-21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Yakeshi?
July, with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 415 mm (16.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Yakeshi?
February, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Yakeshi?
May, with about 279 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Yakeshi?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 100 cm (39.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in April; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 7 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Yakeshi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June.
What type of climate does Yakeshi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.3 × 11 km, that contains Yakeshi’s GeoNames location (49.283°N, 120.733°E; ClimaClue area sq-347-3007), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 116,284 (GeoNames).
