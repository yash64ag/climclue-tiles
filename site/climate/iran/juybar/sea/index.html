Jūybār sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Māzandarān · Iran

The sea off Jūybār averages 27.8 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.1 °C (52 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to September.

Jūybār sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jūybār?
In August, at about 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jūybār?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 11.1 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jūybār in February?
About 11.1 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jūybār?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Jūybār nearby?
In August: Bābolsar (28.5 °C, 24 km), Fereydūn Kenār (28.5 °C, 35 km) and Maḩmūdābād (28.5 °C, 58 km).
