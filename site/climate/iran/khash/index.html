Khāsh at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Khāsh is 99th warmest (20.5 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 3,179 hours of sunshine a year) and 398th wettest (134 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Iranshahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (125 km away), Khāsh is 5.5 °C cooler in July and 6.3 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.7 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 330 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khāsh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khāsh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 330 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khāsh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.221°N, 61.216°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-557-2412), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
