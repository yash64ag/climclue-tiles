Kabūdarāhang at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Kabūdarāhang is 362nd warmest (12.7 °C year average), 344th sunniest (about 2,718 hours of sunshine a year) and 225th wettest (318 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bahār, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Kabūdarāhang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 29.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 261 km from the sea.

Why Kabūdarāhang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 261 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-487-2287), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
