Piranshahr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Piranshahr is 99th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,927 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (629 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mahābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Piranshahr is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 222 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 27.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 366 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Piranshahr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Piranshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 366 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
