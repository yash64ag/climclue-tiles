How September compares in Tehran

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tehran. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Tehran in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tehran in September?
In September, Tehran averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tehran?
September is not one of Tehran’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tehran in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tehran in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tehran get in September?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tehran in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
