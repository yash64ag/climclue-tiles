Tehran weather in June

Average June weather in Tehran, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Tehran averages 34.6 °C (94 °F) days and 21.0 °C (70 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 332 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Tehran

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tehran. Compared with May: 6.4 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. July is 3.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Tehran in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tehran in June?
In June, Tehran averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 332 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tehran?
June is not one of Tehran’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tehran in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (72 mm), the driest September (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tehran in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tehran get in June?
About 332 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tehran in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
