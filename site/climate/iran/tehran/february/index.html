How February compares in Tehran

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tehran. Compared with January: 1.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 5.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Tehran in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tehran in February?
In February, Tehran averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -3.1 °C (26 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tehran?
February is not one of Tehran’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tehran in February?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tehran in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tehran get in February?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tehran in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
