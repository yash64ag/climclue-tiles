How March compares in Tehran

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tehran. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 7.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Tehran in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tehran in March?
In March, Tehran averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tehran?
March is not one of Tehran’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tehran in March?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tehran in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tehran get in March?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tehran in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
