Why Sāveh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 209 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the sunniest month in Sāveh?

Does it snow in Sāveh?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 45 cm (17.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sāveh?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Sāveh’s GeoNames location (35.021°N, 50.357°E; ClimaClue area sq-489-2303), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 220,762 (GeoNames).
