Marīvān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Marīvān is 77th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,941 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (945 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bāneh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (60 km away), Marīvān is 1.7 °C warmer in July and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 362 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Marīvān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Marīvān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 362 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
