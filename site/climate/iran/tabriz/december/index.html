How December compares in Tabriz

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tabriz. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Tabriz in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in December?
In December, Tabriz averages a day high of 4.7 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -4.7 °C (24 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tabriz?
December is not one of Tabriz’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in December?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
