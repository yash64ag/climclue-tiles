How May compares in Tabriz

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tabriz. Compared with April: 4.9 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. June is 5.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Tabriz in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in May?
In May, Tabriz averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tabriz?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Tabriz’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in May?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
