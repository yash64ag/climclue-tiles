How June compares in Tabriz

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tabriz. Compared with May: 5.2 °C warmer, 33 mm less precipitation and 70 hours more sunshine. July is 3.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Tabriz in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in June?
In June, Tabriz averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 333 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tabriz?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tabriz’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in June?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in June?
About 333 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
