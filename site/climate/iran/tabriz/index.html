Tabriz at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Tabriz is 96th warmest (11.3 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 2,707 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (269 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Marand, the nearest covered town with different numbers (60 km away), Tabriz is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 104 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 237 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tabriz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tabriz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 237 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
