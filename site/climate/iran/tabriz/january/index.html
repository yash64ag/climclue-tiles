How January compares in Tabriz

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tabriz. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Tabriz in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in January?
In January, Tabriz averages a day high of 2.7 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -6.7 °C (20 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tabriz?
January is not one of Tabriz’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in January?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
