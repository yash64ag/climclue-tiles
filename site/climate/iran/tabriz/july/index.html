How July compares in Tabriz

July is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tabriz. Compared with June: 3.3 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Tabriz in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in July?
In July, Tabriz averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 350 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tabriz?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Tabriz’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in July?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in July?
About 350 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
