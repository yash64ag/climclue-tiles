How April compares in Tabriz

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tabriz. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Tabriz in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in April?
In April, Tabriz averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tabriz?
April is not one of Tabriz’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in April?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in April?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
