How March compares in Tabriz

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tabriz. Compared with February: 4.9 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Tabriz in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in March?
In March, Tabriz averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tabriz?
March is not one of Tabriz’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in March?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.5 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in March?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
