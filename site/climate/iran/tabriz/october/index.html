How October compares in Tabriz

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tabriz. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. November is 7.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Tabriz in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabriz in October?
In October, Tabriz averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tabriz?
October is not one of Tabriz’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabriz in October?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (49 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tabriz in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabriz get in October?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabriz in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
