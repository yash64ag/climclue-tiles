Shiraz weather in December

Average December weather in Shiraz, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Shiraz averages 14.4 °C (58 °F) days and 0.7 °C (33 °F) nights, with 91 mm (3.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 213 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Shiraz

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shiraz. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 63 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 2.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Shiraz in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiraz in December?
In December, Shiraz averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shiraz?
December is not one of Shiraz’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiraz in December?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (104 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Shiraz in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.3 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiraz get in December?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiraz in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
