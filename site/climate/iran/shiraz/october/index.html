How October compares in Shiraz

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Shiraz. Compared with September: 5.9 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Shiraz in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiraz in October?
In October, Shiraz averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Shiraz?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Shiraz’s mild months (May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiraz in October?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (119 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Shiraz in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiraz get in October?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiraz in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
