How February compares in Shiraz

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shiraz. Compared with January: 2.2 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Shiraz in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiraz in February?
In February, Shiraz averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Shiraz?
February is not one of Shiraz’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiraz in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (104 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Shiraz in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiraz get in February?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiraz in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
