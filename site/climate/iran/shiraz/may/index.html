How May compares in Shiraz

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Shiraz. Compared with April: 6.4 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. June is 4.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Shiraz in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiraz in May?
In May, Shiraz averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 315 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Shiraz?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Shiraz’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiraz in May?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (104 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Shiraz in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiraz get in May?
About 315 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiraz in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
