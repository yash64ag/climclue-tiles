How March compares in Shiraz

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Shiraz. Compared with February: 3.6 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Shiraz in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiraz in March?
In March, Shiraz averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Shiraz?
March is not one of Shiraz’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiraz in March?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (119 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Shiraz in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiraz get in March?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiraz in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
