Shiraz at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shiraz is the sunniest (about 3,282 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shahr-e Ṣadrā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Shiraz is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 24.8 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Why Shiraz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
