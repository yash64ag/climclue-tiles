Gorgān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Gorgān is 31st warmest (17.7 °C year average), 82nd sunniest (about 2,738 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (560 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shahrud, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Gorgān is 3.4 °C warmer in August and 6.9 °C warmer in January, with 278 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gorgān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gorgān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
