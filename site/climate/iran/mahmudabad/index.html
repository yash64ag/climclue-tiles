Maḩmūdābād at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Maḩmūdābād is 161st warmest (17.6 °C year average), 402nd sunniest (about 2,059 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (931 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Āmol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Maḩmūdābād is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 1.3 °C warmer in February, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.5 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, February (8.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Maḩmūdābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
