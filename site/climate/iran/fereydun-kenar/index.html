Fereydūn Kenār at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Fereydūn Kenār is 156th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 401st sunniest (about 2,075 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (791 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bābolsar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Fereydūn Kenār is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.2 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Fereydūn Kenār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-473-2325), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
