Kīsh sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hormozgan · Iran

The sea off Kīsh averages 33.1 °C (92 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 21.7 °C (71 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Kīsh sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kīsh?
In August, at about 33.1 °C (92 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kīsh?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 21.7 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kīsh in February?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Kīsh?
About 27.5 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kīsh nearby?
In August: Pārsiān (33.7 °C, 121 km) and Jebel Ali (33.7 °C, 204 km).
