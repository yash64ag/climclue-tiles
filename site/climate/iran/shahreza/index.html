Shahreẕā at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shahreẕā is the 5th sunniest (about 3,210 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bahārestān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Shahreẕā is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 278 km from the sea.

Why Shahreẕā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 278 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
