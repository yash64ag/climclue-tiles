Rūdān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Rūdān is 10th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 3,088 hours of sunshine a year) and 334th wettest (209 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manūjān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Rūdān is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.1 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rūdān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rūdān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rūdān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.442°N, 57.192°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-565-2371), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
