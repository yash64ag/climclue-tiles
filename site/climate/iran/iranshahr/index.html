Iranshahr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Iranshahr is 6th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 3,168 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (146 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zahedan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (256 km away), Iranshahr is 5.9 °C warmer in July and 6.8 °C warmer in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.9 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 212 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iranshahr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iranshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 212 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
