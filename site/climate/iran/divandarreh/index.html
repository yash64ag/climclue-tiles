Dīvāndarreh weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dīvāndarreh, Iran (Kurdistan Province), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dīvāndarreh at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Dīvāndarreh is 407th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 259th sunniest (about 2,834 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (419 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bījār, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Dīvāndarreh is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.7 °C), averages 29.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 272 km from the sea.

Why Dīvāndarreh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dīvāndarreh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.914°N, 47.024°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-480-2270), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
