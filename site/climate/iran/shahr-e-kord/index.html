Shahr-e Kord at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shahr-e Kord is the 5th coolest (10.8 °C year average). Compared with Najafābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Shahr-e Kord is 4.7 °C cooler in July and 5.3 °C cooler in January, with 262 mm more precipitation a year. Why Shahr-e Kord has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 259 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
