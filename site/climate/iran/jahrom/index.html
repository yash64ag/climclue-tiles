Jahrom at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Jahrom is the 4th sunniest (about 3,212 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shiraz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (159 km away), Jahrom is 4.3 °C warmer in July and 5.0 °C warmer in January, with 86 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.1 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 155 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jahrom, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jahrom has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 155 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jahrom itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.500°N, 53.560°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-554-2335), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
