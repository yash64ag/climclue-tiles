Najafābād at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Najafābād is 62nd warmest (15.6 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,174 hours of sunshine a year) and 84th wettest (200 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khomeynī Shahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Najafābād is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 29.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 308 km from the sea.

Why Najafābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
