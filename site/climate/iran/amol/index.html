Āmol at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Āmol is 48th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,189 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (827 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bābol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Āmol is 1.1 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Āmol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Āmol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
