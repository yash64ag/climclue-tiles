Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shahrak-e Pardīsān is 25th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (161 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Shahrak-e Pardīsān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
