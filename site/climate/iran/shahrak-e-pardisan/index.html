Shahrak-e Pardīsān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shahrak-e Pardīsān is 25th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (163 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qom, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Shahrak-e Pardīsān is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.5 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 249 km from the sea.

Why Shahrak-e Pardīsān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
