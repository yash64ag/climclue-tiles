Farīmān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Farīmān is 370th warmest (12.4 °C year average), 187th sunniest (about 2,935 hours of sunshine a year) and 305th wettest (239 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Farīmān is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 554 km from the sea.

Why Farīmān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 554 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Farīmān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.707°N, 59.850°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-482-2398), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
