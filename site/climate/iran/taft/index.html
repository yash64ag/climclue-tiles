Taft at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Taft is 154th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 3,130 hours of sunshine a year) and 382nd wettest (150 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yazd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Taft is 2.8 °C cooler in July and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (4.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 437 km from the sea.

Why Taft has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 437 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taft itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.744°N, 54.203°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-522-2342), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
