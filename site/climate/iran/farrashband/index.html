Farrāshband at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Farrāshband is 76th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 3,223 hours of sunshine a year) and 164th wettest (389 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fīrūzābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Farrāshband is 2.6 °C warmer in July and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 99 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.7 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (10.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 107 km from the sea.

Why Farrāshband has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 107 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-551-2320), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
