Hendījān sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Khuzestan · Iran

The sea off Hendījān averages 32.3 °C (90 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.2 °C (61 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Hendījān sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hendījān?
In August, at about 32.3 °C (90 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hendījān?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 16.2 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hendījān in February?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hendījān?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hendījān nearby?
In August: Bushehr (32.9 °C, 178 km) and ‘Ālī Shahr (33.0 °C, 195 km).
