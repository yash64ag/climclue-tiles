Gonbad-e Kāvūs at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Gonbad-e Kāvūs is 29th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,754 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (498 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gorgān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Gonbad-e Kāvūs is 1.7 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 62 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gonbad-e Kāvūs, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gonbad-e Kāvūs has this climate
