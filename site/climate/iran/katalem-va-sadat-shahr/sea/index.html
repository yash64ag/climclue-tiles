Katālem va Sādāt Shahr sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Māzandarān · Iran

The sea off Katālem va Sādāt Shahr averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.4 °C (51 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Katālem va Sādāt Shahr sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Katālem va Sādāt Shahr?
In August, at about 27.2 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Katālem va Sādāt Shahr?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 10.4 °C (51 °F).
How warm is the sea in Katālem va Sādāt Shahr in February?
About 10.4 °C (51 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Katālem va Sādāt Shahr?
About 18.6 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Katālem va Sādāt Shahr nearby?
In August: Chālūs (28.3 °C, 70 km), Khomām (28.0 °C, 108 km) and Nūr (28.6 °C, 123 km).
