Khvāf at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Khvāf is 176th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 149th sunniest (about 3,009 hours of sunshine a year) and 338th wettest (201 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tāybād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Khvāf is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.0 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 628 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Khvāf has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 628 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khvāf itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.572°N, 60.144°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-494-2401), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
