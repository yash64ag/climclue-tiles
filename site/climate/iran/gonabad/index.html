Gonābād at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Gonābād is 144th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 155th sunniest (about 3,000 hours of sunshine a year) and 347th wettest (188 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Gonābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 521 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-496-2386), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
