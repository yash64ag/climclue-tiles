Kāzerūn at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Kāzerūn is 17th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 3,191 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (683 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Borāzjān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Kāzerūn is 4.1 °C cooler in July and 4.7 °C cooler in January, with 280 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.4 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 102 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kāzerūn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kāzerūn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 102 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
