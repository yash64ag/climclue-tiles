Ahar at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Ahar is the coolest (9.6 °C year average). Compared with Tabriz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Ahar is 3.2 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 165 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ahar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ahar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 165 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
