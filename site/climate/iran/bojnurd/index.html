Bojnūrd at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bojnūrd is 84th warmest (12.6 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 2,833 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (286 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qūchān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (112 km away), Bojnūrd is 2.8 °C warmer in July and 2.7 °C warmer in January, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 309 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bojnūrd, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bojnūrd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 309 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
