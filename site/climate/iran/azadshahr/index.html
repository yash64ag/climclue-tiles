Āzādshahr at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Āzādshahr is 162nd warmest (17.6 °C year average), 311th sunniest (about 2,774 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (700 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gonbad-e Kāvūs, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Āzādshahr is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 196 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.0 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (6.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Why Āzādshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
