Bandar-e Māhshahr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bandar-e Māhshahr is the 4th warmest (26.4 °C year average). Compared with Abadan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Bandar-e Māhshahr is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 40 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.6 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bandar-e Māhshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
