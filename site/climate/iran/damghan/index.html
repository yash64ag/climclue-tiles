Dāmghān weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dāmghān, Iran (Semnan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dāmghān is warmest in July, with 34.9 °C (95 °F) days and 21.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 7.1 °C (45 °F) days and -2.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around March (23 mm); September is driest (5 mm).

Dāmghān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Dāmghān is 258th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 202nd sunniest (about 2,918 hours of sunshine a year) and 379th wettest (154 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shahrud, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Dāmghān is 2.7 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Why Dāmghān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dāmghān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.168°N, 54.343°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-478-2343), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
