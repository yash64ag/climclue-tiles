Golbahār at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Golbahār is 347th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 229th sunniest (about 2,869 hours of sunshine a year) and 248th wettest (299 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chenārān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Golbahār is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. Why Golbahār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 474 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Golbahār itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.570°N, 59.190°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-474-2391), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
