Shūshtar at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shūshtar is 10th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 2,757 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (404 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Masjed Soleymān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Shūshtar is 2.2 °C warmer in July and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 179 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.5 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.1 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 180 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shūshtar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shūshtar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 180 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
