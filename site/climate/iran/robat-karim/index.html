Robāţ Karīm at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Robāţ Karīm is 34th warmest (17.3 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 2,779 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (281 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Golestān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Robāţ Karīm is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.6 °C), averages 29.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Robāţ Karīm, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Robāţ Karīm has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
