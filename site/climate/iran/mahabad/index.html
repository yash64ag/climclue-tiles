Why Mahābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 316 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mahābād climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mahābād?
July, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 32.4 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 17.7 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mahābād?
January, with an average of -2.4 °C (28 °F), day highs around 2.3 °C (36 °F) and night lows around -7.4 °C (19 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mahābād?
April, with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 406 mm (16.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mahābād?
August, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mahābād?
July, with about 369 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mahābād?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 130 cm (51.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Mahābād?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Mahābād have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Mahābād’s GeoNames location (36.763°N, 45.722°E; ClimaClue area sq-472-2257), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest).
