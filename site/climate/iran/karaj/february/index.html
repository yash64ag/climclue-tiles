How February compares in Karaj

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Karaj. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. March is 5.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Karaj in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karaj in February?
In February, Karaj averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.4 °C (24 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Karaj?
February is not one of Karaj’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karaj in February?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (85 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Karaj in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 10 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Karaj get in February?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karaj in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
