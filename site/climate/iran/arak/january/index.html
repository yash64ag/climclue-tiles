How January compares in Arāk

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Arāk. Compared with December: 3.5 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Arāk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arāk in January?
In January, Arāk averages a day high of 2.3 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -8.9 °C (16 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Arāk?
January is not one of Arāk’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arāk in January?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Arāk in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Arāk get in January?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arāk in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
