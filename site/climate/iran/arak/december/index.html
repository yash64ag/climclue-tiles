How December compares in Arāk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Arāk. Compared with November: 5.9 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. January is 3.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Arāk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arāk in December?
In December, Arāk averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -3.0 °C (27 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Arāk?
December is not one of Arāk’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arāk in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (60 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Arāk in December?
Yes, on average: about 28 cm (11.1 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Arāk get in December?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arāk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
