How March compares in Arāk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Arāk. Compared with February: 5.6 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 5.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Arāk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arāk in March?
In March, Arāk averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Arāk?
March is not one of Arāk’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arāk in March?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (60 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Arāk in March?
Yes, on average: about 22 cm (8.5 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Arāk get in March?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arāk in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
