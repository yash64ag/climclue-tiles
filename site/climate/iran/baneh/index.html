Bāneh at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bāneh is the 5th wettest (1,013 mm a year). Compared with Saqqez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Bāneh is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 478 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Why Bāneh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
