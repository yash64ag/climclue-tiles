Bāneh at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bāneh is the 5th wettest (907 mm a year). Compared with Saqqez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Bāneh is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 427 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Why Bāneh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bāneh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.998°N, 45.885°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-480-2258), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
