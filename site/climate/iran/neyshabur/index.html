Neyshābūr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Neyshābūr is 72nd warmest (14.3 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,874 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (390 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mashhad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Neyshābūr is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 125 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 444 km from the sea.

Why Neyshābūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 444 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
