Semnan at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Semnan is 41st warmest (16.9 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,937 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (209 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qā’em Shahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Semnan is 3.9 °C warmer in July and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 679 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 149 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Semnan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Semnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 149 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
