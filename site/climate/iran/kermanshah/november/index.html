How November compares in Kermanshah

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kermanshah. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Kermanshah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kermanshah in November?
In November, Kermanshah averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kermanshah?
November is not one of Kermanshah’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kermanshah in November?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kermanshah in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kermanshah get in November?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kermanshah in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
