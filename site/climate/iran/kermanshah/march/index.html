March · Kermanshah Province · Iran

How March compares in Kermanshah

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kermanshah. Compared with February: 4.2 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Kermanshah in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kermanshah in March?
In March, Kermanshah averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 1.2 °C (34 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kermanshah?
March is not one of Kermanshah’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kermanshah in March?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kermanshah in March?
Yes, on average: about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Kermanshah get in March?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kermanshah in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
