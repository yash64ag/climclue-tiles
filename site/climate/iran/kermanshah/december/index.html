How December compares in Kermanshah

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kermanshah. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Kermanshah in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kermanshah in December?
In December, Kermanshah averages a day high of 8.7 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -1.7 °C (29 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kermanshah?
December is not one of Kermanshah’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kermanshah in December?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kermanshah in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kermanshah get in December?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kermanshah in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
