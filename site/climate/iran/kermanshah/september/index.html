Kermanshah weather in September

Average September weather in Kermanshah, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Kermanshah averages 34.5 °C (94 °F) days and 12.8 °C (55 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 304 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Kermanshah

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kermanshah. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. October is 6.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Kermanshah in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kermanshah in September?
In September, Kermanshah averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 304 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kermanshah?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Kermanshah’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kermanshah in September?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kermanshah in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kermanshah get in September?
About 304 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kermanshah in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
