How January compares in Kermanshah

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kermanshah. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Kermanshah in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kermanshah in January?
In January, Kermanshah averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -4.7 °C (24 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kermanshah?
January is not one of Kermanshah’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kermanshah in January?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kermanshah in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 38 cm (14.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kermanshah get in January?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kermanshah in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
