Chālūs at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Chālūs is the 4th wettest (1,078 mm a year). Compared with Āmol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (86 km away), Chālūs is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C warmer in February, with 250 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, February (8.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Chālūs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
