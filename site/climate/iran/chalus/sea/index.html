Chālūs sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Māzandarān · Iran

The sea off Chālūs averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.5 °C (51 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to September.

Chālūs sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chālūs?
In August, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chālūs?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 10.5 °C (51 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chālūs in February?
About 10.5 °C (51 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Chālūs?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
