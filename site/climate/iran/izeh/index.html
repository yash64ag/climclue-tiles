Īẕeh at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Īẕeh is 18th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,948 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (813 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Masjed Soleymān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Īẕeh is 2.7 °C cooler in July and 3.8 °C cooler in January, with 229 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.6 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, January (6.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 169 km from the sea.

Why Īẕeh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 169 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
