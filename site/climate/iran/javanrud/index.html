Javānrūd at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Javānrūd is 307th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 173rd sunniest (about 2,971 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (712 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ravānsar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Javānrūd is 0.7 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 28.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 400 km from the sea.

Why Javānrūd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 400 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Javānrūd itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.807°N, 46.491°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-2264), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
