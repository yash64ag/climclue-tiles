Khosrowshahr at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Khosrowshahr is 341st warmest (13.1 °C year average), 339th sunniest (about 2,727 hours of sunshine a year) and 295th wettest (256 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sahand, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Khosrowshahr is 1.3 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 255 km from the sea.

Why Khosrowshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 255 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khosrowshahr itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.950°N, 46.053°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-460-2260), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
