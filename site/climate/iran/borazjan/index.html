Borāzjān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Borāzjān is 7th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 3,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (403 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bushehr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Borāzjān is 3.2 °C warmer in July and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 129 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.5 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Borāzjān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Borāzjān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
