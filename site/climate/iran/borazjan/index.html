Borāzjān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Borāzjān is 34th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 105th sunniest (about 3,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 173rd wettest (381 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Āb Pakhsh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Borāzjān is 0.4 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.7 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Borāzjān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Borāzjān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Borāzjān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.270°N, 51.219°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-547-2312), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
