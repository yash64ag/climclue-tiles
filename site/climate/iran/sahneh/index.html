Şaḩneh at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Şaḩneh is 270th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 273rd sunniest (about 2,822 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Harsīn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Şaḩneh is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 87 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.7 °C), averages 29.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 381 km from the sea.

Why Şaḩneh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 381 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Şaḩneh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.481°N, 47.691°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-495-2276), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
