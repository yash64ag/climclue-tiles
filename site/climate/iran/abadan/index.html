Abadan at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Abadan is the 2nd warmest (26.8 °C year average). Compared with Khorramshahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Abadan is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (39.3 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Abadan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Abadan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
