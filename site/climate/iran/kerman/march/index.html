Kerman weather in March

Average March weather in Kerman, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Kerman averages 19.0 °C (66 °F) days and 3.9 °C (39 °F) nights, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 210 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Kerman

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kerman. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. April is 5.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Kerman in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kerman in March?
In March, Kerman averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kerman?
March is not one of Kerman’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kerman in March?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (34 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kerman in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kerman get in March?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kerman in March?
