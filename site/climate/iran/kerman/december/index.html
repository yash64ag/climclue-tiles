How December compares in Kerman

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kerman. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Kerman in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kerman in December?
In December, Kerman averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kerman?
December is not one of Kerman’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kerman in December?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (34 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kerman in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Kerman get in December?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kerman in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
