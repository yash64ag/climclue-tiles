How November compares in Kerman

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kerman. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. December is 4.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Kerman in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kerman in November?
In November, Kerman averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kerman?
November is not one of Kerman’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kerman in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (32 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kerman in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kerman get in November?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kerman in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
