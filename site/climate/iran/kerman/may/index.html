How May compares in Kerman

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kerman. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Kerman in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kerman in May?
In May, Kerman averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kerman?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kerman’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kerman in May?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (32 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kerman in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kerman get in May?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kerman in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
