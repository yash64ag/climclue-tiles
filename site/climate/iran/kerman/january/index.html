How January compares in Kerman

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kerman. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Kerman in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kerman in January?
In January, Kerman averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kerman?
January is not one of Kerman’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kerman in January?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (34 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kerman in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kerman get in January?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kerman in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
