Torbat-e Jām at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Torbat-e Jām is 54th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,967 hours of sunshine a year) and 85th wettest (185 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (111 km away), Torbat-e Jām is 4.7 °C warmer in July and 3.9 °C warmer in January, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 639 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Torbat-e Jām, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.
