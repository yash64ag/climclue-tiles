Torbat-e Jām at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Torbat-e Jām is 236th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 174th sunniest (about 2,967 hours of sunshine a year) and 344th wettest (190 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tāybād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Torbat-e Jām is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 639 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Torbat-e Jām has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 639 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
