Torbat-e Jām at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Torbat-e Jām is 55th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,967 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (190 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (111 km away), Torbat-e Jām is 4.9 °C warmer in July and 4.1 °C warmer in January, with 90 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 639 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Torbat-e Jām has this climate
