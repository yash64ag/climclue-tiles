Masjed Soleymān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Masjed Soleymān is 14th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 2,826 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (583 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shūshtar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Masjed Soleymān is 2.2 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 179 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.4 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (9.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 168 km from the sea.

Why Masjed Soleymān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 168 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
