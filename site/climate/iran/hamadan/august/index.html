How August compares in Hamadān

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Hamadān. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 4.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Hamadān in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in August?
In August, Hamadān averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hamadān?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Hamadān’s mild months (June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (83 mm), the driest September (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in August?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
