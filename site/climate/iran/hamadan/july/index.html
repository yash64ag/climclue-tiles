Hamadān weather in July

Average July weather in Hamadān, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Hamadān averages 37.1 °C (99 °F) days and 16.5 °C (62 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 347 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Hamadān

July is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hamadān. Compared with June: 3.8 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Hamadān in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in July?
In July, Hamadān averages a day high of 37.1 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 347 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hamadān?
July is not one of Hamadān’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (67 mm), the driest September (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in July?
About 347 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
