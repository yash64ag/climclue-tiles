How January compares in Hamadān

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hamadān. Compared with December: 3.3 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Hamadān in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in January?
In January, Hamadān averages a day high of 1.6 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -7.3 °C (19 °F), with 44 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hamadān?
January is not one of Hamadān’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in January?
44 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (67 mm), the driest September (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 46 cm (18.3 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in January?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
