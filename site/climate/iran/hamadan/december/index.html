How December compares in Hamadān

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hamadān. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. January is 3.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Hamadān in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in December?
In December, Hamadān averages a day high of 3.5 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -5.6 °C (22 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hamadān?
December is not one of Hamadān’s mild months; those are June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in December?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (83 mm), the driest September (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 34 cm (13.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
