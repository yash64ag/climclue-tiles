How September compares in Hamadān

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hamadān. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. October is 6.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Hamadān in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in September?
In September, Hamadān averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hamadān?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Hamadān’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in September?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (67 mm), the driest September (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in September?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in September?
