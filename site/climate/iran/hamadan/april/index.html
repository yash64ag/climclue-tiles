How April compares in Hamadān

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hamadān. Compared with March: 6.5 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 5.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Hamadān in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in April?
In April, Hamadān averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hamadān?
April is not one of Hamadān’s mild months; those are June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in April?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (83 mm), the driest September (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in April?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
