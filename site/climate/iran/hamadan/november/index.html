How November compares in Hamadān

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hamadān. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Hamadān in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamadān in November?
In November, Hamadān averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hamadān?
November is not one of Hamadān’s mild months; those are June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamadān in November?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (83 mm), the driest September (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hamadān in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.5 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamadān get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamadān in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
