Khorramabad at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Khorramabad is 58th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,904 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (537 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Borūjerd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Khorramabad is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 2.8 °C warmer in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 342 km from the sea.

Why Khorramabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 342 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
