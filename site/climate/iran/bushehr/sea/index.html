Bushehr sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Iran

The sea off Bushehr averages 32.9 °C (91 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.3 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Bushehr sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bushehr?
In August, at about 32.9 °C (91 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bushehr?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.3 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bushehr in February?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bushehr?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
