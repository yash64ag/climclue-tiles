Bushehr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bushehr is 9th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 3,102 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (275 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Borāzjān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Bushehr is 2.0 °C cooler in August and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 129 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (33.8 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bushehr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bushehr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
