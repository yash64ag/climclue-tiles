Ferdowsīyeh at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Ferdowsīyeh is 212th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 285th sunniest (about 2,807 hours of sunshine a year) and 233rd wettest (311 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vaḩīdīyeh, Tehran (35.6°N 51.0°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Ferdowsīyeh is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.9 °C), averages 29.5 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ferdowsīyeh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ferdowsīyeh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ferdowsīyeh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.601°N, 51.064°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-483-2310), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
