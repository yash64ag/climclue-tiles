Pārsiān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Pārsiān is 16th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,255 hours of sunshine a year) and 322nd wettest (220 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lamerd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Pārsiān is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 3.2 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.4 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pārsiān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pārsiān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. The 10 km area around Pārsiān, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.5 °C a year, 2.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pārsiān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.208°N, 53.036°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-567-2330), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
