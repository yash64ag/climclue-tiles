Sirjan at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Sirjan is 150th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 3,115 hours of sunshine a year) and 346th wettest (189 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Najafshahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sirjan is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Why Sirjan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sirjan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.451°N, 55.681°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-545-2356), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
