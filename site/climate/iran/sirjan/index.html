Sirjan at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Sirjan is 30th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 3,115 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (180 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rafsanjān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Sirjan is 1.7 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 65 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sirjan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sirjan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
