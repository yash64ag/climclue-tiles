Why Bandar-e Anzalī has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bandar-e Anzalī climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bandar-e Anzalī?
August, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 27.6 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 24.0 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bandar-e Anzalī?
February, with an average of 7.8 °C (46 °F), day highs around 10.0 °C (50 °F) and night lows around 5.5 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bandar-e Anzalī?
October, with 220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,348 mm (53.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bandar-e Anzalī?
June, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bandar-e Anzalī?
July, with about 242 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bandar-e Anzalī?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Bandar-e Anzalī?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and June.
