Salmās weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Salmās, Iran (West Azerbaijan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Salmās at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Salmās is 391st warmest (11.5 °C year average), 263rd sunniest (about 2,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 188th wettest (355 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khūy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Salmās is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 1.9 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 367 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Salmās, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Salmās has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 367 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Salmās itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.197°N, 44.765°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-458-2247), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
