Eqlīd at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Eqlīd is 361st warmest (12.7 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,254 hours of sunshine a year) and 298th wettest (248 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Abadeh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Eqlīd is 2.2 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 83 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 255 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Eqlīd, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Eqlīd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 255 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C. The 10 km area around Eqlīd, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.9 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Eqlīd itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.899°N, 52.697°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-531-2326), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
