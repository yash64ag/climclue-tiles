Khalkhāl weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Khalkhāl, Iran (Ardabil Province), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Khalkhāl is warmest in July, with 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 10.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -0.8 °C (31 °F) days and -9.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around April (68 mm); July is driest (15 mm).

Khalkhāl at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Khalkhāl is the 2nd coolest (7.4 °C year average). Compared with Hashtpar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Khalkhāl is 7.3 °C cooler in July and 12.9 °C cooler in January, with 681 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khalkhāl, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khalkhāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C. The 10 km area around Khalkhāl, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 5.7 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khalkhāl itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.618°N, 48.529°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-463-2285), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
