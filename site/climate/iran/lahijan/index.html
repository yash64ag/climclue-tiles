Lāhījān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Lāhījān is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,587 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,234 mm a year). Compared with Rasht, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Lāhījān is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lāhījān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lāhījān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
