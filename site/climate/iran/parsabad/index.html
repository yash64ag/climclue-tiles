Pārsābād at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Pārsābād is the 5th least sunny (about 2,114 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Ahar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (149 km away), Pārsābād is 8.0 °C warmer in July and 7.0 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 124 km from the sea.

Why Pārsābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
