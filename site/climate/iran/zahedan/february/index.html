How February compares in Zahedan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zahedan. Compared with January: 2.6 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Zahedan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zahedan in February?
In February, Zahedan averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zahedan?
February is not one of Zahedan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zahedan in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zahedan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zahedan get in February?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zahedan in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
