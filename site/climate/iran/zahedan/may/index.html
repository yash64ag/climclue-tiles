How May compares in Zahedan

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Zahedan. Compared with April: 4.6 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Zahedan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zahedan in May?
In May, Zahedan averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 299 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zahedan?
May is not one of Zahedan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zahedan in May?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zahedan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zahedan get in May?
About 299 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zahedan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
