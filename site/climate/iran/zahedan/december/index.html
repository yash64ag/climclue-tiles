How December compares in Zahedan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Zahedan. Compared with November: 4.7 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Zahedan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zahedan in December?
In December, Zahedan averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zahedan?
December is not one of Zahedan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zahedan in December?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zahedan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zahedan get in December?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zahedan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
