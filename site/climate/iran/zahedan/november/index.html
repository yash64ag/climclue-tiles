How November compares in Zahedan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zahedan. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. December is 4.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Zahedan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zahedan in November?
In November, Zahedan averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zahedan?
November is not one of Zahedan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zahedan in November?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zahedan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zahedan get in November?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zahedan in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
