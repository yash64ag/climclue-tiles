Zahedan weather in October

Average October weather in Zahedan, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Zahedan averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 9.9 °C (50 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 284 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Zahedan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zahedan. Compared with September: 5.2 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Zahedan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zahedan in October?
In October, Zahedan averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zahedan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Zahedan’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zahedan in October?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zahedan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zahedan get in October?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zahedan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
