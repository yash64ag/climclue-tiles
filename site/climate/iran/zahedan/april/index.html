How April compares in Zahedan

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zahedan. Compared with March: 5.7 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Zahedan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zahedan in April?
In April, Zahedan averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zahedan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Zahedan’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zahedan in April?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zahedan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zahedan get in April?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zahedan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
