Shāhīn Shahr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Shāhīn Shahr is 52nd warmest (16.2 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,164 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (150 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khomeynī Shahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Shāhīn Shahr is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 336 km from the sea.

Why Shāhīn Shahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 336 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
