Why Sanandaj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 328 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sanandaj climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sanandaj?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 33.3 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sanandaj?
January, with an average of -4.3 °C (24 °F), day highs around -0.6 °C (31 °F) and night lows around -9.9 °C (14 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sanandaj?
March, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 515 mm (20.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sanandaj?
August, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sanandaj?
July, with about 359 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sanandaj?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 213 cm (83.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sanandaj?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Sanandaj have?
Continental, hot dry summer (Köppen Dsa). Hot, dry summers and cold, snowy winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Sanandaj’s GeoNames location (35.315°N, 46.999°E; ClimaClue area sq-486-2269), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 349,176 (GeoNames).
