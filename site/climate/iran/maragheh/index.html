Why Marāgheh, East Azerbaijan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 253 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
