Ahvaz at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Ahvaz is the 5th warmest (26.3 °C year average). Compared with Shūshtar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (83 km away), Ahvaz is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 172 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (39.3 °C), averages 27.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ahvaz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ahvaz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
