How April compares in Ahvaz

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ahvaz. Compared with March: 6.9 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 7.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Ahvaz in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ahvaz in April?
In April, Ahvaz averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ahvaz?
April is not one of Ahvaz’s mild months; those are March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ahvaz in April?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (53 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ahvaz in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ahvaz get in April?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ahvaz in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
