Ahvaz weather in May

Average May weather in Ahvaz, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Ahvaz averages 40.3 °C (105 °F) days and 25.2 °C (77 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 270 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Ahvaz

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ahvaz. Compared with April: 7.0 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. June is 4.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Ahvaz in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ahvaz in May?
In May, Ahvaz averages a day high of 40.3 °C (105 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ahvaz?
May is not one of Ahvaz’s mild months; those are March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ahvaz in May?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (54 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ahvaz in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ahvaz get in May?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ahvaz in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
