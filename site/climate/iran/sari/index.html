Why Sari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Sari climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sari?
August, with an average of 26.8 °C (80 °F), day highs around 31.2 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 21.7 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sari?
January, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 12.1 °C (54 °F) and night lows around 0.9 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sari?
November, with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 842 mm (33.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sari?
June, with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sari?
June, with about 272 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sari?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sari?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June and September.
What type of climate does Sari have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Sari’s GeoNames location (36.563°N, 53.060°E; ClimaClue area sq-474-2330), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 255,396 (GeoNames).
