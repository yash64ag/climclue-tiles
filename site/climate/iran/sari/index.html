Sari at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Sari is 43rd warmest (16.7 °C year average), 96th sunniest (about 2,310 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (842 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qā’em Shahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Sari is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
