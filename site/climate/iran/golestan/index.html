Golestān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Golestān is 174th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 291st sunniest (about 2,799 hours of sunshine a year) and 243rd wettest (305 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ṣāleḥīeh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Golestān is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.7 °C), averages 29.5 °C more than its coolest, January (2.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Golestān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Golestān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Golestān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.518°N, 51.182°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-484-2311), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
