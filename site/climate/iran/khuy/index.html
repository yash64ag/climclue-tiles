Khūy at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Khūy is 82nd warmest (12.8 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,761 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (353 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Marand, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Khūy is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 27.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 348 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khūy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khūy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 348 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
