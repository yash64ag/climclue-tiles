How April compares in Orūmīyeh

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Orūmīyeh. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Orūmīyeh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orūmīyeh in April?
In April, Orūmīyeh averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Orūmīyeh?
April is not one of Orūmīyeh’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
