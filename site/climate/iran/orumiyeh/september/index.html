Orūmīyeh in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orūmīyeh in September?
In September, Orūmīyeh averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Orūmīyeh?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Orūmīyeh’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Orūmīyeh in September?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (55 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Orūmīyeh in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Orūmīyeh get in September?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Orūmīyeh in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
