How March compares in Orūmīyeh

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Orūmīyeh. Compared with February: 4.9 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 6.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Orūmīyeh in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orūmīyeh in March?
In March, Orūmīyeh averages a day high of 8.7 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -1.5 °C (29 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Orūmīyeh?
March is not one of Orūmīyeh’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Orūmīyeh in March?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (55 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Orūmīyeh in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Orūmīyeh get in March?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Orūmīyeh in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
