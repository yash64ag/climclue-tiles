How January compares in Orūmīyeh

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Orūmīyeh. Compared with December: 2.9 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Orūmīyeh in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orūmīyeh in January?
In January, Orūmīyeh averages a day high of 2.7 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -7.1 °C (19 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Orūmīyeh?
January is not one of Orūmīyeh’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Orūmīyeh in January?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (55 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Orūmīyeh in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (10.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Orūmīyeh get in January?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Orūmīyeh in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
