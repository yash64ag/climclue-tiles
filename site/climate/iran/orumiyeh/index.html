Orūmīyeh at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Orūmīyeh is 373rd warmest (12.4 °C year average), 341st sunniest (about 2,723 hours of sunshine a year) and 244th wettest (302 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oshnavīyeh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Orūmīyeh is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 184 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 349 km from the sea.

Why Orūmīyeh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 349 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Orūmīyeh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.553°N, 45.076°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-464-2250), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
