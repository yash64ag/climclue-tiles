Alīgūdarz at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Alīgūdarz is 402nd warmest (10.7 °C year average), 151st sunniest (about 3,007 hours of sunshine a year) and 124th wettest (454 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aznā, Lorestan Province (33.5°N 49.5°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Alīgūdarz is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 337 km from the sea.

Why Alīgūdarz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 337 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
