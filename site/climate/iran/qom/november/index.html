How November compares in Qom

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Qom. Compared with October: 7.7 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Qom in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in November?
In November, Qom averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Qom?
November is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in November?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in November?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
