How September compares in Qom

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Qom. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. October is 6.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Qom in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in September?
In September, Qom averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Qom?
September is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in September?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in September?

How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in September?
