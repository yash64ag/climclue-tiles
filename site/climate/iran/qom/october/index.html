How October compares in Qom

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Qom. Compared with September: 6.7 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. November is 7.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Qom in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in October?
In October, Qom averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Qom?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Qom’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in October?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in October?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
