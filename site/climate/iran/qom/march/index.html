How March compares in Qom

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Qom. Compared with February: 5.5 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 6.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Qom in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in March?
In March, Qom averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Qom?
March is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in March?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in March?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
