How January compares in Qom

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Qom. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 2.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Qom in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in January?
In January, Qom averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Qom?
January is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in January?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in January?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
