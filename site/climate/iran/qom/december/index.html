How December compares in Qom

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Qom. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Qom in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in December?
In December, Qom averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Qom?
December is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in December?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
