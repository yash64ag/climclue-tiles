How July compares in Qom

July is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Qom. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Qom in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in July?
In July, Qom averages a day high of 38.6 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 320 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Qom?
July is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
