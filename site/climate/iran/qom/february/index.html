How February compares in Qom

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Qom. Compared with January: 2.8 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. March is 5.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Qom in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in February?
In February, Qom averages a day high of 12.1 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Qom?
February is not one of Qom’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in February?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in February?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
