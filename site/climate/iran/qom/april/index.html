How April compares in Qom

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Qom. Compared with March: 6.9 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 5.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Qom in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qom in April?
In April, Qom averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Qom?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Qom’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qom in April?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Qom in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qom get in April?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qom in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
