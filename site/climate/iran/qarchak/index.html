Qarchak at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Qarchak is 27th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,813 hours of sunshine a year) and 64th wettest (294 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pākdasht, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Qarchak is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.9 °C), averages 29.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 136 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qarchak, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qarchak has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 136 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
