Gomīshān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Gomīshān is 131st warmest (18.4 °C year average), 361st sunniest (about 2,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 137th wettest (436 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sīmīn Shahr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Gomīshān is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 19.5 °C more than its coolest, January (8.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gomīshān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gomīshān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gomīshān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.072°N, 54.077°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-469-2340), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
