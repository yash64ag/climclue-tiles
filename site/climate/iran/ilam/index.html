Why Īlām has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 437 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the warmest month in Īlām?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 32.5 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 17.2 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Īlām?
January, with an average of -0.5 °C (31 °F), day highs around 3.9 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -6.0 °C (21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Īlām?

What is the driest month in Īlām?

What is the sunniest month in Īlām?
July, with about 378 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Īlām?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 72 cm (28.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Īlām?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Īlām’s GeoNames location (33.637°N, 46.423°E; ClimaClue area sq-503-2264), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 140,940 (GeoNames).
