Īlām at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Īlām is 266th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 99th sunniest (about 3,115 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (699 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Eyvān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Īlām is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 437 km from the sea.

Why Īlām has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 437 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C. The 10 km area around Īlām, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 12.8 °C a year, 2.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Īlām itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.637°N, 46.423°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-503-2264), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
