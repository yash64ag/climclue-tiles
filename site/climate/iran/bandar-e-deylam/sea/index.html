Bandar-e Deylam sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bushehr · Iran

The sea off Bandar-e Deylam averages 32.5 °C (91 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.2 °C (61 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Bandar-e Deylam sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bandar-e Deylam?
In August, at about 32.5 °C (91 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bandar-e Deylam?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 16.2 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bandar-e Deylam in February?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bandar-e Deylam?
About 24.8 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
