Bandar-e Deylam at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Bandar-e Deylam is 43rd warmest (25.7 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,987 hours of sunshine a year) and 231st wettest (312 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hendījān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Bandar-e Deylam is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.9 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bandar-e Deylam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
