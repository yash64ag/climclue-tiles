Ardabīl at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Ardabīl is the 2nd coolest (10.1 °C year average). Compared with Mīāneh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Ardabīl is 5.5 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 61 km from the sea.

Why Ardabīl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 61 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
