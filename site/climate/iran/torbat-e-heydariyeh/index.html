Torbat-e Ḩeydarīyeh at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Torbat-e Ḩeydarīyeh is 71st warmest (14.9 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,953 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (274 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kāshmar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Torbat-e Ḩeydarīyeh is 3.5 °C cooler in July and 2.5 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (1.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 521 km from the sea.

Why Torbat-e Ḩeydarīyeh has this climate
