Bahārestān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bahārestān is the 5th driest (135 mm a year). Compared with Isfahan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Bahārestān is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.2 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 308 km from the sea.

Why Bahārestān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
