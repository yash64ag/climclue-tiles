Bahārestān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Bahārestān is 60th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,202 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (147 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Isfahan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Bahārestān is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 308 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bahārestān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bahārestān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
