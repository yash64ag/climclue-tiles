How May compares in Rasht

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Rasht. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 4.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Rasht in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in May?
In May, Rasht averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Rasht?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Rasht’s mild months (May, June and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in May?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (198 mm), the driest June (43 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in May?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
