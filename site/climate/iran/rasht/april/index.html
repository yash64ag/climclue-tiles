How April compares in Rasht

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Rasht. Compared with March: 4.6 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Rasht in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in April?
In April, Rasht averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Rasht?
April is not one of Rasht’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in April?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (198 mm), the driest June (43 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in April?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
