Rasht at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Rasht is the least sunny (about 1,575 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,339 mm a year). Compared with Bandar-e Anzalī, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Rasht is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rasht, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rasht has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
