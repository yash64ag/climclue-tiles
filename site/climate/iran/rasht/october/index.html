Rasht weather in October

Average October weather in Rasht, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Rasht averages 21.9 °C (71 °F) days and 13.5 °C (56 °F) nights, with 205 mm (8.1 in) of rain over the month and about 110 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Rasht

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Rasht. Compared with September: 4.1 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. November is 4.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Rasht in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in October?
In October, Rasht averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Rasht?
October is not one of Rasht’s mild months; those are May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in October?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest June (42 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in October?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
