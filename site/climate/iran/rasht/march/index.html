How March compares in Rasht

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Rasht. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Rasht in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in March?
In March, Rasht averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Rasht?
March is not one of Rasht’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in March?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (198 mm), the driest June (43 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in March?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
