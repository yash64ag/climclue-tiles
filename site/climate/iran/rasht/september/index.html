How September compares in Rasht

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Rasht. Compared with August: 3.4 °C cooler, 84 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. October is 4.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Rasht in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in September?
In September, Rasht averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Rasht?
September is not one of Rasht’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in September?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (198 mm), the driest June (43 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in September?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
