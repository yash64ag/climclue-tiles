Rasht weather in August

Average August weather in Rasht, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Rasht averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) days and 20.3 °C (69 °F) nights, with 83 mm (3.3 in) of rain over the month and about 175 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Rasht

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rasht. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. September is 3.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Rasht in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in August?
In August, Rasht averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Rasht?
August is not one of Rasht’s mild months; those are May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in August?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest June (42 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in August?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
