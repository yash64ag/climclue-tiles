How December compares in Rasht

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Rasht. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 2.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Rasht in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasht in December?
In December, Rasht averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Rasht?
December is not one of Rasht’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasht in December?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (198 mm), the driest June (43 mm).
Does it snow in Rasht in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.8 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasht get in December?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rasht in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
