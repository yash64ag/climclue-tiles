Qūchān at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Qūchān is 400th warmest (10.9 °C year average), 244th sunniest (about 2,856 hours of sunshine a year) and 274th wettest (280 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shīrvān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Qūchān is 2.2 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.5 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 416 km from the sea.

Why Qūchān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 416 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Qūchān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.106°N, 58.510°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-468-2385), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
