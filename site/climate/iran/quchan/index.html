Qūchān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Qūchān is the 4th coolest (10.2 °C year average). Compared with Neyshābūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (103 km away), Qūchān is 4.4 °C cooler in July and 4.5 °C cooler in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 416 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qūchān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qūchān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 416 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
