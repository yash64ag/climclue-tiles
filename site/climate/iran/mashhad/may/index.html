How May compares in Mashhad

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 82 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Mashhad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in May?
In May, Mashhad averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mashhad?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Mashhad’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in May?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in May?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
