How June compares in Mashhad

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with May: 5.3 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Mashhad in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in June?
In June, Mashhad averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 342 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Mashhad?
June is not one of Mashhad’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in June?
About 342 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
