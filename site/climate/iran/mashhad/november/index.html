How November compares in Mashhad

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Mashhad in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in November?
In November, Mashhad averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 17 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mashhad?
November is not one of Mashhad’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in November?
17 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in November?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
