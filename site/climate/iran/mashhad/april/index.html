How April compares in Mashhad

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Mashhad in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in April?
In April, Mashhad averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mashhad?
April is not one of Mashhad’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in April?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (58 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
