How September compares in Mashhad

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. October is 6.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Mashhad in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in September?
In September, Mashhad averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 299 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mashhad?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Mashhad’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in September?
About 299 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
