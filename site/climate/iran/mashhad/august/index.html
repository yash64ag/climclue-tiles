How August compares in Mashhad

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Mashhad in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in August?
In August, Mashhad averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 350 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mashhad?
August is not one of Mashhad’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in August?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in August?
About 350 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in August?
