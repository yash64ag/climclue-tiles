How July compares in Mashhad

July is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mashhad. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. August is 1.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Mashhad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in July?
In July, Mashhad averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 359 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mashhad?
July is not one of Mashhad’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in July?
About 359 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
