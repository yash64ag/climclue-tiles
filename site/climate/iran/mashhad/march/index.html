How March compares in Mashhad

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mashhad. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Mashhad in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mashhad in March?
In March, Mashhad averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mashhad?
March is not one of Mashhad’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mashhad in March?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (59 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mashhad in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Mashhad get in March?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mashhad in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
