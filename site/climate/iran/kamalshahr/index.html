Kamālshahr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Kamālshahr is 67th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 81st sunniest (about 2,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (448 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karaj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kamālshahr is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 29.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kamālshahr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kamālshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
