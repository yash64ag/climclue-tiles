Bābolsar at a glance

Among the 423 towns ClimaClue covers in Iran, Bābolsar is 158th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 400th sunniest (about 2,093 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (753 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amīr Kolā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Bābolsar is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 74 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bābolsar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
