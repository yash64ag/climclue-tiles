How August compares in Isfahan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Isfahan. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. September is 4.8 °C cooler than August on average.
