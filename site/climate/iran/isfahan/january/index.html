How January compares in Isfahan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Isfahan. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 2.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Isfahan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Isfahan in January?
In January, Isfahan averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -1.7 °C (29 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Isfahan?
January is not one of Isfahan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Isfahan in January?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Isfahan in January?
Yes, on average: about 10 cm (4.1 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Isfahan get in January?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Isfahan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
