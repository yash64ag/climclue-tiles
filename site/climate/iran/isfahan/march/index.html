How March compares in Isfahan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Isfahan. Compared with February: 4.9 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. April is 6.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Isfahan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Isfahan in March?
In March, Isfahan averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Isfahan?
March is not one of Isfahan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Isfahan in March?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (32 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Isfahan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.6 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Isfahan get in March?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Isfahan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
