Isfahan weather in May

Average May weather in Isfahan, Iran: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Isfahan averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) days and 14.9 °C (59 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 296 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Isfahan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Isfahan. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. June is 5.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Isfahan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Isfahan in May?
In May, Isfahan averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Isfahan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Isfahan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Isfahan in May?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Isfahan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Isfahan get in May?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Isfahan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
