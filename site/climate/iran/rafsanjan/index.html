Rafsanjān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Rafsanjān is the 4th driest (110 mm a year). Compared with Kerman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Rafsanjān is 3.1 °C warmer in July and 1.9 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.0 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 369 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rafsanjān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rafsanjān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 369 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rafsanjān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.407°N, 55.994°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-535-2359), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
