Rafsanjān at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Rafsanjān is the 4th driest (116 mm a year). Compared with Kerman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Rafsanjān is 3.3 °C warmer in July and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.3 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 369 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rafsanjān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rafsanjān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 369 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
