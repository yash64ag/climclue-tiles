Why Qazvin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Qazvin climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Qazvin?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 33.3 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 18.1 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Qazvin?
January, with an average of -0.6 °C (31 °F), day highs around 3.9 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -5.2 °C (23 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Qazvin?
April, with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 501 mm (19.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Qazvin?
September, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Qazvin?
July, with about 295 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Qazvin?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 122 cm (48.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Qazvin?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Qazvin have?
Continental, hot dry summer (Köppen Dsa). Hot, dry summers and cold, snowy winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Qazvin’s GeoNames location (36.269°N, 50.004°E; ClimaClue area sq-477-2300), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 333,635 (GeoNames).
