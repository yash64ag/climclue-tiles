Qazvin at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Qazvin is 78th warmest (13.4 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 2,442 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (501 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naz̧arābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Qazvin is 3.0 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qazvin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qazvin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
