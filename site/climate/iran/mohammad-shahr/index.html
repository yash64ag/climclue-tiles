Moḩammad Shahr at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Moḩammad Shahr is 63rd warmest (15.2 °C year average), 84th sunniest (about 2,738 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (403 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karaj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Moḩammad Shahr is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 81 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 29.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 117 km from the sea.

Why Moḩammad Shahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 117 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
