Mīāneh at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iran, Mīāneh is 81st warmest (13.4 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,510 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (282 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ardabīl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Mīāneh is 5.9 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.5 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mīāneh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mīāneh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mīāneh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.421°N, 47.715°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-465-2277), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
