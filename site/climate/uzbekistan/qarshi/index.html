Qarshi at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Qarshi is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,929 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shahrisabz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Qarshi is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 356 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 975 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Qarshi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 975 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
