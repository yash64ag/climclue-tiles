Zomin Shaharchasi weather by month and climate

Zomin Shaharchasi at a glance

Among the 109 towns ClimaClue covers in Uzbekistan, Zomin Shaharchasi is 98th warmest (12.7 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,802 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (431 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dashtobod, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Zomin Shaharchasi is 2.4 °C cooler in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 71 mm more precipitation a year. Why Zomin Shaharchasi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,163 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
