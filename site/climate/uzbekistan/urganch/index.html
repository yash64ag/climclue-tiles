Urganch at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Urganch is the 5th coolest (13.4 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (112 mm a year). Compared with Xiva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Urganch is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 506 km from the sea.

Why Urganch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 506 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
