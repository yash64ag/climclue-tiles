Namangan weather in July

Average July weather in Namangan, Uzbekistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Namangan averages 37.7 °C (100 °F) days and 22.6 °C (73 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 355 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Namangan

July is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Namangan. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. August is 1.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Namangan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Namangan in July?
In July, Namangan averages a day high of 37.7 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 355 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Namangan?
July is not one of Namangan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Namangan in July?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (35 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Namangan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Namangan get in July?
About 355 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Namangan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
