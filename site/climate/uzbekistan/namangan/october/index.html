How October compares in Namangan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Namangan. Compared with September: 7.4 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. November is 7.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Namangan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Namangan in October?
In October, Namangan averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Namangan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Namangan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Namangan in October?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (32 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Namangan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Namangan get in October?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Namangan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
