How December compares in Namangan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Namangan. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Namangan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Namangan in December?
In December, Namangan averages a day high of 6.3 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -1.7 °C (29 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Namangan?
December is not one of Namangan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Namangan in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (35 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Namangan in December?
Yes, on average: about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Namangan get in December?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Namangan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
