How March compares in Namangan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Namangan. Compared with February: 6.6 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 7.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Namangan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Namangan in March?
In March, Namangan averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Namangan?
March is not one of Namangan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Namangan in March?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (35 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Namangan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Namangan get in March?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Namangan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
