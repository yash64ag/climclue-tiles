How December compares in Samarkand

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Samarkand. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Samarkand in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in December?
In December, Samarkand averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.5 °C (28 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Samarkand?
December is not one of Samarkand’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in December?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in December?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
