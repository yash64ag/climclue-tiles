Samarkand weather in September

Average September weather in Samarkand, Uzbekistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Samarkand averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 10.8 °C (51 °F) nights, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 302 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Samarkand

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Samarkand. Compared with August: 5.0 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 6.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Samarkand in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in September?
In September, Samarkand averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 302 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Samarkand?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Samarkand’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in September?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (70 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in September?
About 302 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
