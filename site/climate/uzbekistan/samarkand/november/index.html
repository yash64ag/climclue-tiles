How November compares in Samarkand

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Samarkand. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Samarkand in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in November?
In November, Samarkand averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Samarkand?
November is not one of Samarkand’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in November?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.8 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in November?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
