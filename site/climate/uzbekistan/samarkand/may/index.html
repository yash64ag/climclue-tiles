How May compares in Samarkand

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Samarkand. Compared with April: 4.8 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 91 hours more sunshine. June is 5.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Samarkand in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in May?
In May, Samarkand averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Samarkand?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Samarkand’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in May?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in May?
About 309 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
