How April compares in Samarkand

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Samarkand. Compared with March: 6.1 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Samarkand in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in April?
In April, Samarkand averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Samarkand?
April is not one of Samarkand’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in April?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (70 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in April?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
