How January compares in Samarkand

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Samarkand. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Samarkand in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in January?
In January, Samarkand averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Samarkand?
January is not one of Samarkand’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in January?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (70 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in January?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
