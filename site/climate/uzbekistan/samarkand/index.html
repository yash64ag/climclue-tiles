Why Samarkand has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,052 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Samarkand climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Samarkand?
July, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 32.1 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Samarkand?
January, with an average of 0.6 °C (33 °F), day highs around 5.5 °C (42 °F) and night lows around -4.7 °C (24 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Samarkand?
March, with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 382 mm (15.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Samarkand?
August, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Samarkand?
July, with about 385 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Samarkand?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 104 cm (40.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Samarkand?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, August and September.
What type of climate does Samarkand have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.6 × 11 km, that contains Samarkand’s GeoNames location (39.655°N, 66.964°E; ClimaClue area sq-443-2469), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 595,200 (GeoNames).
