Samarkand at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Samarkand is the 4th coolest (13.3 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,901 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shahrisabz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Samarkand is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 239 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,052 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Samarkand has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,052 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
