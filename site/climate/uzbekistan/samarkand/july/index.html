How July compares in Samarkand

July is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Samarkand. Compared with June: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Samarkand in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarkand in July?
In July, Samarkand averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 385 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Samarkand?
July is not one of Samarkand’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarkand in July?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (71 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Samarkand in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarkand get in July?
About 385 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarkand in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
