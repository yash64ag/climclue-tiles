Yangiyŭl at a glance

Among the 109 towns ClimaClue covers in Uzbekistan, Yangiyŭl is 59th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,800 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (384 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zafar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Yangiyŭl is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.0 °C), averages 27.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,206 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Yangiyŭl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,206 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yangiyŭl itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.112°N, 69.047°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-428-2490), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
