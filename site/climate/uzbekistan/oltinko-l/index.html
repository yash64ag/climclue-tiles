Oltinko‘l weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Oltinko‘l, Uzbekistan (Karakalpakstan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Oltinko‘l at a glance

Among the 109 towns ClimaClue covers in Uzbekistan, Oltinko‘l is the 3rd coolest (11.8 °C year average). Compared with Qŭnghirot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Oltinko‘l is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Why Oltinko‘l has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 420 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Oltinko‘l itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.069°N, 58.904°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-409-2389), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
