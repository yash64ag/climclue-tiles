Xo‘jaobod weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Xo‘jaobod, Uzbekistan (Andijan Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Xo‘jaobod is warmest in July, with 35.7 °C (96 °F) days and 20.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.7 °C (39 °F) days and -5.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around March (43 mm); August is driest (5 mm).

Among the 109 towns ClimaClue covers in Uzbekistan, Xo‘jaobod is the 4th least sunny (about 2,542 hours of sunshine a year). Why Xo‘jaobod has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,504 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xo‘jaobod itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.669°N, 72.560°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-433-2525), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
