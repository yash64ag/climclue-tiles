Why Sultonobod has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,537 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sultonobod itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.765°N, 72.976°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-432-2529), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
