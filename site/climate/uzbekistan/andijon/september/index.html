How September compares in Andijon

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Andijon. Compared with August: 5.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. October is 7.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Andijon in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Andijon in September?
In September, Andijon averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Andijon?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Andijon’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Andijon in September?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Andijon in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Andijon get in September?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Andijon in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
