How January compares in Andijon

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Andijon. Compared with December: 1.9 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 3.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Andijon in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Andijon in January?
In January, Andijon averages a day high of 4.5 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -4.5 °C (24 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Andijon?
January is not one of Andijon’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Andijon in January?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Andijon in January?
Yes, on average: about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Andijon get in January?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Andijon in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
