Andijon at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Andijon is the least sunny (about 2,551 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Namangan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Andijon is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 29.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,486 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Andijon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,486 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
