How June compares in Tashkent

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with May: 5.2 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Tashkent in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in June?
In June, Tashkent averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 357 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tashkent?
June is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in June?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (72 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in June?
About 357 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
