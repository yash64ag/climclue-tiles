How July compares in Tashkent

July is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tashkent. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. August is 1.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Tashkent in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in July?
In July, Tashkent averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 377 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tashkent?
July is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in July?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in July?
About 377 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
