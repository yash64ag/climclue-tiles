How February compares in Tashkent

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. March is 6.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Tashkent in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in February?
In February, Tashkent averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -4.1 °C (25 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tashkent?
February is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in February?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in February?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
