How January compares in Tashkent

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Tashkent in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in January?
In January, Tashkent averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tashkent?
January is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in January?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in January?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
