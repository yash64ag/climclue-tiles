How October compares in Tashkent

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with September: 6.9 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 78 hours less sunshine. November is 6.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Tashkent in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in October?
In October, Tashkent averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tashkent?
October is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in October?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in October?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
