How August compares in Tashkent

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with July: 1.6 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 5.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Tashkent in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in August?
In August, Tashkent averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 360 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tashkent?
August is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in August?
About 360 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
