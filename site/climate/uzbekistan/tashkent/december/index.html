How December compares in Tashkent

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tashkent. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Tashkent in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in December?
In December, Tashkent averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tashkent?
December is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in December?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (72 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in December?
Yes, on average: about 15 cm (6.1 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in December?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
