How May compares in Tashkent

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 84 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Tashkent in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in May?
In May, Tashkent averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 300 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tashkent?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Tashkent’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in May?
About 300 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
