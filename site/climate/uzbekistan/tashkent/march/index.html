How March compares in Tashkent

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tashkent. Compared with February: 6.2 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Tashkent in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tashkent in March?
In March, Tashkent averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tashkent?
March is not one of Tashkent’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tashkent in March?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (74 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tashkent in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tashkent get in March?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tashkent in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
