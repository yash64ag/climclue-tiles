Tirmiz at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Tirmiz is the warmest (18.9 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 2,891 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shahrisabz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (208 km away), Tirmiz is 6.3 °C warmer in July and 3.2 °C warmer in January, with 447 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.8 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,161 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tirmiz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,161 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
