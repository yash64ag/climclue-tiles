Why Chirchiq has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,246 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Chirchiq climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chirchiq?
July, with an average of 25.8 °C (78 °F), day highs around 32.1 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chirchiq?
January, with an average of -1.3 °C (30 °F), day highs around 4.1 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -6.9 °C (20 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chirchiq?
March, with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 739 mm (29.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Chirchiq?
August, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Chirchiq?
July, with about 375 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chirchiq?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 147 cm (57.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Chirchiq?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, August and September.
What type of climate does Chirchiq have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.3 × 11 km, that contains Chirchiq’s GeoNames location (41.469°N, 69.582°E; ClimaClue area sq-425-2495), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 162,800 (GeoNames).
