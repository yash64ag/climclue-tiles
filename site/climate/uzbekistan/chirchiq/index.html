Chirchiq at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Chirchiq is the 3rd coolest (12.6 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (739 mm a year). Compared with Tashkent, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Chirchiq is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 283 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,246 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chirchiq, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chirchiq has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,246 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
