Olmaliq at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Olmaliq is the 4th wettest (550 mm a year). Compared with Angren, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Olmaliq is 4.5 °C warmer in July and 2.8 °C warmer in January, with 404 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,251 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Olmaliq has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,251 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
