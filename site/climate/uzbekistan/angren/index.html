Angren at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uzbekistan, Angren is the coolest (10.7 °C year average) and the wettest (954 mm a year). Compared with Olmaliq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Angren is 4.5 °C cooler in July and 2.8 °C cooler in January, with 404 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,299 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Angren, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Angren has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,299 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
