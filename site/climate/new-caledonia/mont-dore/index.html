Mont-Dore weather by month and climate

Mont-Dore at a glance

Among the 3 towns ClimaClue covers in New Caledonia, Mont-Dore is the 3rd warmest (22.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,398 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,412 mm a year). Compared with Nouméa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Mont-Dore is about as warm in February and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 204 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.5 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, August (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.4 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mont-Dore, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mont-Dore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mont-Dore itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.263°S, 166.566°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1062-3465), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
