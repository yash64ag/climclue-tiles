Kandy at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sri Lanka, Kandy is the coolest (23.1 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 2,267 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Negombo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Kandy is 3.4 °C cooler in May and 5.0 °C cooler in January, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in February. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kandy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kandy

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kandy are February and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (24.4 °C), April (24.4 °C) and March (24.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): August (98 mm), February (99 mm) and July (109 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): March (249 h), April (218 h) and January (208 h).

Why Kandy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
