Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sri Lanka, Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,535 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Maharagama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia is 0.1 °C warmer in March and 0.1 °C warmer in November, with 188 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.1 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, November (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.1 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Dehiwala-Mount Lavinia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
