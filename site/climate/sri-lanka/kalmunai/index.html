Kalmunai at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sri Lanka, Kalmunai is the warmest (27.7 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,389 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,517 mm a year). Compared with Kandy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (133 km away), Kalmunai is 6.0 °C warmer in June and 4.0 °C warmer in January, with 540 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalmunai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalmunai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
