Katunayaka sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Province · Sri Lanka

The sea off Katunayaka averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 27.2 °C (81 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Katunayaka sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Katunayaka?
In April, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Katunayaka?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 27.2 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Katunayaka in August?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Katunayaka?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
