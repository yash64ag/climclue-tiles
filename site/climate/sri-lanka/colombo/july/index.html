How July compares in Colombo

July is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Colombo. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 80 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Colombo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Colombo in July?
In July, Colombo averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Colombo?
No month in Colombo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Colombo in July?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (338 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Colombo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Colombo get in July?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Colombo in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
