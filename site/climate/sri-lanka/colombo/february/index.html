How February compares in Colombo

February is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Colombo. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 0.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Colombo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Colombo in February?
In February, Colombo averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Colombo?
No month in Colombo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Colombo in February?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (338 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Colombo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Colombo get in February?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Colombo in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
