How December compares in Colombo

December is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Colombo. Compared with November: about as warm, 211 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Colombo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Colombo in December?
In December, Colombo averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Colombo?
No month in Colombo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Colombo in December?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (338 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Colombo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Colombo get in December?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Colombo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
