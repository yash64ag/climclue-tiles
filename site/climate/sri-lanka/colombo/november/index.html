How November compares in Colombo

November is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Colombo. Compared with October: 0.2 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Colombo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Colombo in November?
In November, Colombo averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 338 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Colombo?
No month in Colombo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Colombo in November?
338 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (338 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Colombo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Colombo get in November?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Colombo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
