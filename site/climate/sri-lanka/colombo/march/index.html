How March compares in Colombo

March is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Colombo. Compared with February: 0.6 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 0.1 °C cooler than March on average.

Colombo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Colombo in March?
In March, Colombo averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Colombo?
No month in Colombo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Colombo in March?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (333 mm), the driest February (67 mm).
Does it snow in Colombo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Colombo get in March?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Colombo in March?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Colombo in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
