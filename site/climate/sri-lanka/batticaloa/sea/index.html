Batticaloa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Province · Sri Lanka

The sea off Batticaloa averages 30.3 °C (87 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 27.3 °C (81 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Batticaloa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Batticaloa?
In May, at about 30.3 °C (87 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Batticaloa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.3 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Batticaloa in January?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Batticaloa?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
