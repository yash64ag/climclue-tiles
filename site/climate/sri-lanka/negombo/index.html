Negombo at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sri Lanka, Negombo is 4th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,523 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (2,008 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Colombo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Negombo is 0.1 °C cooler in March and about as warm in December, with 199 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.1 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, December (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Negombo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Negombo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
