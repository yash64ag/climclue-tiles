Trincomalee at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sri Lanka, Trincomalee is the 3rd warmest (27.7 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (1,533 mm a year). Compared with Kalmunai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (146 km away), Trincomalee is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.3 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Trincomalee, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Trincomalee has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
