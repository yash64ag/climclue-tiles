How October compares in Taiz

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Taiz. Compared with September: 1.1 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. November is 1.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Taiz in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiz in October?
In October, Taiz averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Taiz?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Taiz’s mild months (January, February, March, April, August, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiz in October?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Taiz in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiz get in October?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiz in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
