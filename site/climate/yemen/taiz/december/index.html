Taiz weather in December

Average December weather in Taiz, Yemen: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Taiz averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 13.5 °C (56 °F) nights, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 264 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Taiz

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Taiz. Compared with November: 1.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Taiz in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiz in December?
In December, Taiz averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Taiz?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Taiz’s mild months (January, February, March, April, August, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiz in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Taiz in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiz get in December?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiz in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
