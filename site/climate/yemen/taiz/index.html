Why Taiz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 97 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 11.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Taiz climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Taiz?
June, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 27.2 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Taiz?
January, with an average of 15.8 °C (60 °F), day highs around 20.7 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 11.2 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Taiz?
May, with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 633 mm (24.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Taiz?
January, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Taiz?
May, with about 287 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Taiz?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Taiz?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, June, July, August, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Taiz’s GeoNames location (13.580°N, 44.021°E; ClimaClue area sq-704-2240), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 940,600 (GeoNames).
