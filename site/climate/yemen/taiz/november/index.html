How November compares in Taiz

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Taiz. Compared with October: 1.4 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. December is 1.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Taiz in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiz in November?
In November, Taiz averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Taiz?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Taiz’s mild months (January, February, March, April, August, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiz in November?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Taiz in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiz get in November?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiz in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
