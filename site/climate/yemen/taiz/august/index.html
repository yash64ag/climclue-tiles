How August compares in Taiz

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Taiz. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. September is 1.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Taiz in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiz in August?
In August, Taiz averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Taiz?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Taiz’s mild months (January, February, March, April, June, July, August, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiz in August?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Taiz in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiz get in August?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiz in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
