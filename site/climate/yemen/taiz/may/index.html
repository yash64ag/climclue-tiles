How May compares in Taiz

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Taiz. Compared with April: 1.3 °C warmer, 42 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. June is 0.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Taiz in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taiz in May?
In May, Taiz averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taiz?
May is not one of Taiz’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, June, July, August, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taiz in May?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Taiz in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taiz get in May?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taiz in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
