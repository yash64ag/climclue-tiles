Mukalla at a glance

Among the 25 towns ClimaClue covers in Yemen, Mukalla is 8th warmest (27.7 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 3,009 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (109 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ghayl Bā Wazīr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Mukalla is 0.5 °C warmer in June and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.2 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mukalla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mukalla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mukalla itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.542°N, 49.124°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-694-2291), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
