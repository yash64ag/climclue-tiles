How August compares in Mukalla

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mukalla. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 1.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Mukalla in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mukalla in August?
In August, Mukalla averages a day high of 36.6 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mukalla?
August is not one of Mukalla’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mukalla in August?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (19 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mukalla in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mukalla get in August?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mukalla in August?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mukalla in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
