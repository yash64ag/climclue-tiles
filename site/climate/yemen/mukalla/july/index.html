How July compares in Mukalla

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mukalla. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 85 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Mukalla in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mukalla in July?
In July, Mukalla averages a day high of 37.0 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 26.9 °C (80 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mukalla?
July is not one of Mukalla’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mukalla in July?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (19 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mukalla in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mukalla get in July?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mukalla in July?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mukalla in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
