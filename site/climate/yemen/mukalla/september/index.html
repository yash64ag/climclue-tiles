How September compares in Mukalla

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mukalla. Compared with August: 1.7 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. October is 2.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Mukalla in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mukalla in September?
In September, Mukalla averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mukalla?
September is not one of Mukalla’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mukalla in September?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (21 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mukalla in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mukalla get in September?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mukalla in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
