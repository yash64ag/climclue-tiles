How April compares in Mukalla

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mukalla. Compared with March: 2.2 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 2.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Mukalla in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mukalla in April?
In April, Mukalla averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mukalla?
April is not one of Mukalla’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mukalla in April?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (21 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mukalla in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mukalla get in April?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mukalla in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
