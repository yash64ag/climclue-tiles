How December compares in Mukalla

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mukalla. Compared with November: 1.8 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Mukalla in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mukalla in December?
In December, Mukalla averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mukalla?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Mukalla’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mukalla in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (19 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mukalla in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mukalla get in December?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mukalla in December?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mukalla in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
