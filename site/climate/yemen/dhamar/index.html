Dhamār at a glance

Among the 25 towns ClimaClue covers in Yemen, Dhamār is the 2nd coolest (16.8 °C year average), the 5th least sunny (about 2,913 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (644 mm a year). Compared with Yarīm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Dhamār is 1.3 °C warmer in June and 0.9 °C warmer in December, with 355 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (20.4 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, December (13.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in December. The area lies about 160 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dhamār, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dhamār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 160 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dhamār itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.543°N, 44.405°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-694-2244), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
