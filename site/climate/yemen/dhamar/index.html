Dhamār at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Yemen, Dhamār is the coolest (16.8 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,913 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (575 mm a year). Compared with Ibb, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Dhamār is 0.8 °C warmer in June and 1.0 °C cooler in December, with 345 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (20.6 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, December (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 160 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dhamār, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dhamār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 160 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
