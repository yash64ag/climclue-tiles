How March compares in Sanaa

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Sanaa. Compared with February: 1.2 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 0.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Sanaa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanaa in March?
In March, Sanaa averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sanaa?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Sanaa’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanaa in March?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (55 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sanaa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanaa get in March?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanaa in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
