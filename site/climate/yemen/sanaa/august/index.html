How August compares in Sanaa

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sanaa. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Sanaa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanaa in August?
In August, Sanaa averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sanaa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Sanaa’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanaa in August?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (50 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sanaa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanaa get in August?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanaa in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
