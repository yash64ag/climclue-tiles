How December compares in Sanaa

December is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Sanaa. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Sanaa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanaa in December?
In December, Sanaa averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sanaa?
December is not one of Sanaa’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanaa in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (55 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sanaa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanaa get in December?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanaa in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
