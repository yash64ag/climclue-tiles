Sanaa at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Yemen, Sanaa is the 3rd coolest (17.9 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 2,915 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Dhamār, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Sanaa is 1.0 °C warmer in June and 0.7 °C warmer in December, with 359 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.6 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, December (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in December. The area lies about 160 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sanaa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sanaa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 160 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
