How January compares in Al Ḩudaydah

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Al Ḩudaydah. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Al Ḩudaydah in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Ḩudaydah in January?
In January, Al Ḩudaydah averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Al Ḩudaydah?
No month in Al Ḩudaydah is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Ḩudaydah in January?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (18 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Al Ḩudaydah in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Ḩudaydah get in January?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Al Ḩudaydah in January?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (31.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Al Ḩudaydah in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
