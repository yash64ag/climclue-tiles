How July compares in Al Ḩudaydah

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Al Ḩudaydah. Compared with June: 0.5 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Al Ḩudaydah in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Ḩudaydah in July?
In July, Al Ḩudaydah averages a day high of 38.3 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 28.7 °C (84 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Al Ḩudaydah?
No month in Al Ḩudaydah is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Ḩudaydah in July?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (18 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Al Ḩudaydah in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Ḩudaydah get in July?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Al Ḩudaydah in July?
About 31.4 °C (89 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (31.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Al Ḩudaydah in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
