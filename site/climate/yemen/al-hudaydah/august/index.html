Al Ḩudaydah in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Ḩudaydah in August?
In August, Al Ḩudaydah averages a day high of 37.9 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 28.5 °C (83 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Al Ḩudaydah?
No month in Al Ḩudaydah is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Ḩudaydah in August?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (18 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Al Ḩudaydah in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Ḩudaydah get in August?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Al Ḩudaydah in August?
About 31.4 °C (89 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (31.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Al Ḩudaydah in August?
