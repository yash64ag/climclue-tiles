Al Ḩudaydah in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Ḩudaydah in May?
In May, Al Ḩudaydah averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 28.7 °C (84 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Al Ḩudaydah?
No month in Al Ḩudaydah is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Ḩudaydah in May?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (18 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Al Ḩudaydah in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Ḩudaydah get in May?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Ḩudaydah in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
