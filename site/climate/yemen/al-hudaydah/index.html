Al Ḩudaydah at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Yemen, Al Ḩudaydah is the warmest (30.2 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,082 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (116 mm a year). Compared with Sanaa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (148 km away), Al Ḩudaydah is 12.7 °C warmer in July and 11.5 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.6 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Ḩudaydah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Ḩudaydah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
