How January compares in Aden

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Aden. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Aden in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aden in January?
In January, Aden averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aden?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Aden’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aden in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (12 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aden in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aden get in January?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aden in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
