How June compares in Aden

June is the warmest month, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Aden. Compared with May: 1.3 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Aden in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aden in June?
In June, Aden averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 29.5 °C (85 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Aden?
June is not one of Aden’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aden in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (12 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aden in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aden get in June?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aden in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
