How May compares in Aden

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Aden. Compared with April: 2.0 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Aden in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aden in May?
In May, Aden averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 312 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aden?
May is not one of Aden’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aden in May?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (12 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aden in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aden get in May?
About 312 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Aden in May?
About 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (30.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Aden in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
