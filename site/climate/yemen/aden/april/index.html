How April compares in Aden

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Aden. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 2.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Aden in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aden in April?
In April, Aden averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Aden?
April is not one of Aden’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aden in April?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (12 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aden in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aden get in April?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aden in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
