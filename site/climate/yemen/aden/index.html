Aden at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Yemen, Aden is the 2nd warmest (27.4 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,132 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (84 mm a year). Compared with Taiz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (142 km away), Aden is 8.0 °C warmer in June and 8.4 °C warmer in January, with 548 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.4 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aden, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Aden

For mild weather, the best months to visit Aden are January, February and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and December.

Why Aden has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
