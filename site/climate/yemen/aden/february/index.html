How February compares in Aden

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Aden. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 1.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Aden in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aden in February?
In February, Aden averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aden?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Aden’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aden in February?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (12 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aden in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aden get in February?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aden in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
