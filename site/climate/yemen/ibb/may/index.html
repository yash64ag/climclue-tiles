Ibb weather in May

Average May weather in Ibb, Yemen: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Ibb averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) days and 14.6 °C (58 °F) nights, with 107 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 278 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Ibb

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ibb. Compared with April: 1.3 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Ibb in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibb in May?
In May, Ibb averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ibb?
May is not one of Ibb’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibb in May?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (211 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibb in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibb get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibb in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
