Ibb at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Yemen, Ibb is the 2nd coolest (17.2 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,944 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (920 mm a year). Compared with Taiz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Ibb is 2.7 °C cooler in June and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 288 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (19.8 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 109 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ibb, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ibb has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 109 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
