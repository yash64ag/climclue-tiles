How June compares in Ibb

June is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ibb. Compared with May: 0.5 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Ibb in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibb in June?
In June, Ibb averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ibb?
June is not one of Ibb’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibb in June?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (214 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibb in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibb get in June?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibb in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
