How January compares in Ibb

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ibb. Compared with December: about as warm, about the same precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Ibb in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibb in January?
In January, Ibb averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ibb?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Ibb’s mild months (January, February, March, April, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibb in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (211 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibb in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibb get in January?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibb in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
