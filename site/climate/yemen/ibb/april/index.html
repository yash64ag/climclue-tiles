How April compares in Ibb

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ibb. Compared with March: 1.0 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 1.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Ibb in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibb in April?
In April, Ibb averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ibb?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ibb’s mild months (January, February, March, April, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibb in April?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (211 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibb in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibb get in April?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibb in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
