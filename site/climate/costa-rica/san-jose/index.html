Why San José has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

San José climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in San José?
April, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 25.9 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 17.0 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in San José?
January, with an average of 19.1 °C (66 °F), day highs around 23.5 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 15.8 °C (60 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in San José?
October, with 415 mm (16.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,895 mm (114.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in San José?
March, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in San José?
March, with about 220 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in San José?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in San José?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February and March.
What type of climate does San José have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains San José’s GeoNames location (9.934°N, 84.085°W; ClimaClue area sq-740-959), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 335,007 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
