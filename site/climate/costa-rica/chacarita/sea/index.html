Chacarita sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Puntarenas Province · Costa Rica

The sea off Chacarita averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 27.5 °C (82 °F) in November. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Chacarita sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chacarita?
In April, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chacarita?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is November, at 27.5 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chacarita in November?
About 27.5 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Chacarita?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
