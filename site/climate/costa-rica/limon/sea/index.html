Limón sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Limón Province · Costa Rica

The sea off Limón averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.9 °C (80 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Limón sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Limón?
In September, at about 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Limón?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 26.9 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Limón in January?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Limón?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
