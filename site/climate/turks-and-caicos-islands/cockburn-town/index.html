Cockburn Town at a glance

Compared with Providenciales, the nearest covered town with different numbers (120 km away), Cockburn Town is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, February (24.1 °C). The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cockburn Town, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Cockburn Town

For mild weather, the best months to visit Cockburn Town are January, February, March and April.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March and April.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (27.6 °C), September (27.6 °C) and July (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (49 mm), March (61 mm) and April (74 mm).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Cockburn Town has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
