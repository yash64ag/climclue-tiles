Mary at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkmenistan, Mary is the 2nd warmest (16.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,902 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (149 mm a year). Compared with Türkmenabat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (224 km away), Mary is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 706 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Mary has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 706 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
