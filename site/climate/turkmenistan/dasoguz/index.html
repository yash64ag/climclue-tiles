Why Daşoguz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 453 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Daşoguz climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Daşoguz?
July, with an average of 28.6 °C (83 °F), day highs around 34.8 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 22.0 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Daşoguz?
January, with an average of -2.5 °C (27 °F), day highs around 2.6 °C (37 °F) and night lows around -6.0 °C (21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Daşoguz?
March, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 110 mm (4.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Daşoguz?
August, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Daşoguz?
July, with about 371 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Daşoguz?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Daşoguz?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.3 × 11 km, that contains Daşoguz’s GeoNames location (41.836°N, 59.967°E; ClimaClue area sq-421-2399), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 201,142 (GeoNames).
