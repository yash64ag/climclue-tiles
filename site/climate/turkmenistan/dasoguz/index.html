Daşoguz at a glance

Among the 37 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkmenistan, Daşoguz is the 2nd driest (110 mm a year). Compared with Oýrat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Daşoguz is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 31.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 453 km from the sea.

Why Daşoguz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 453 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Daşoguz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.836°N, 59.967°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-421-2399), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
