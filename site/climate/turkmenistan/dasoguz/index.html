Daşoguz at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkmenistan, Daşoguz is the coolest (13.2 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,851 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (110 mm a year). Compared with Türkmenabat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (433 km away), Daşoguz is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 4.8 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 31.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 453 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Daşoguz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Daşoguz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 453 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
