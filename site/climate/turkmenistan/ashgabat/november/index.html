How November compares in Ashgabat

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ashgabat. Compared with October: 6.4 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. December is 5.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Ashgabat in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ashgabat in November?
In November, Ashgabat averages a day high of 15.8 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ashgabat?
November is not one of Ashgabat’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ashgabat in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (54 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ashgabat in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ashgabat get in November?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ashgabat in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
