How February compares in Ashgabat

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ashgabat. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. March is 5.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Ashgabat in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ashgabat in February?
In February, Ashgabat averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ashgabat?
February is not one of Ashgabat’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ashgabat in February?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (54 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ashgabat in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ashgabat get in February?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ashgabat in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
