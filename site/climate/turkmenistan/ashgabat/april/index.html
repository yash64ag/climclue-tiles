Ashgabat in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ashgabat in April?
In April, Ashgabat averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ashgabat?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ashgabat’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ashgabat in April?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (54 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ashgabat in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ashgabat get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ashgabat in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
