Why Ashgabat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 400 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ashgabat climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ashgabat?
July, with an average of 31.4 °C (88 °F), day highs around 36.3 °C (97 °F) and night lows around 25.7 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ashgabat?
January, with an average of 3.4 °C (38 °F), day highs around 8.2 °C (47 °F) and night lows around -0.9 °C (30 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ashgabat?
March, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 278 mm (11.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ashgabat?
August, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ashgabat?
July, with about 352 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ashgabat?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ashgabat?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Ashgabat have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Ashgabat’s GeoNames location (37.950°N, 58.383°E; ClimaClue area sq-460-2383), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,030,063 (GeoNames).
