Ar Ramthā at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Jordan, Ar Ramthā is the 3rd least sunny (about 3,206 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (280 mm a year). Compared with Irbid, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Ar Ramthā is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 146 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 17.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 112 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ar Ramthā, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ar Ramthā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 112 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
