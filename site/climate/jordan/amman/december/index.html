How December compares in Amman

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Amman. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Amman in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amman in December?
In December, Amman averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Amman?
December is not one of Amman’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amman in December?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (82 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Amman in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amman get in December?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amman in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
