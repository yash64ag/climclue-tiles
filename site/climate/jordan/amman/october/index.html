How October compares in Amman

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Amman. Compared with September: 3.7 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. November is 6.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Amman in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amman in October?
In October, Amman averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Amman?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Amman’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amman in October?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (82 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Amman in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amman get in October?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amman in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
