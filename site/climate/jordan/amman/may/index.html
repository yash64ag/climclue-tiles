How May compares in Amman

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Amman. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 76 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Amman in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amman in May?
In May, Amman averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 337 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Amman?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Amman’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amman in May?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (83 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Amman in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amman get in May?
About 337 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amman in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
