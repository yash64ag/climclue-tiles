How April compares in Amman

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Amman. Compared with March: 4.8 °C warmer, 38 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Amman in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amman in April?
In April, Amman averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Amman?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Amman’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amman in April?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (83 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Amman in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amman get in April?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amman in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
