Amman weather in February

Average February weather in Amman, Jordan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Amman averages 12.9 °C (55 °F) days and 3.5 °C (38 °F) nights, with 82 mm (3.2 in) of rain over the month and about 179 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Amman

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Amman. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 3.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Amman in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amman in February?
In February, Amman averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.5 °C (38 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Amman?
February is not one of Amman’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amman in February?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (82 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Amman in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amman get in February?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amman in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
