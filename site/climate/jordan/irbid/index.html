Irbid at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Jordan, Irbid is the 3rd warmest (17.5 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 3,166 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Ar Ramthā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Irbid is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Irbid, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Irbid has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
