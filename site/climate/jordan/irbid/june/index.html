Irbid in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irbid in June?
In June, Irbid averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 365 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Irbid?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Irbid’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irbid in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (97 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Irbid in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Irbid get in June?
About 365 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
