Irbid in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irbid in September?
In September, Irbid averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Irbid?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Irbid’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irbid in September?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (97 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Irbid in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Irbid get in September?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irbid in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
