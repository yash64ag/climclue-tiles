How February compares in Irbid

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Irbid. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 2.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Irbid in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irbid in February?
In February, Irbid averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Irbid?
February is not one of Irbid’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irbid in February?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Irbid in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Irbid get in February?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irbid in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
