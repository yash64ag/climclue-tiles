How November compares in Irbid

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Irbid. Compared with October: 5.9 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Irbid in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irbid in November?
In November, Irbid averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Irbid?
November is not one of Irbid’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irbid in November?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Irbid in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Irbid get in November?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irbid in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
