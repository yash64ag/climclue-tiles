How May compares in Irbid

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Irbid. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 75 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Irbid in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irbid in May?
In May, Irbid averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 324 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Irbid?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Irbid’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irbid in May?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (97 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Irbid in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Irbid get in May?
About 324 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irbid in May?
