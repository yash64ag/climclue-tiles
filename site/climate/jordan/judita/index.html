Judita at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Jordan, Judita is the 2nd least sunny (about 3,172 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (677 mm a year). Compared with ‘Ajlūn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Judita is 1.5 °C warmer in August and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 17.1 °C more than its coolest, January (9.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 85 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Judita, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Judita has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 85 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.1 °C. The 10 km area around Judita, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 17.0 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Judita itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.408°N, 35.708°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-515-2157), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
