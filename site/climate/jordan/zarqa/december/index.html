How December compares in Zarqa

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zarqa. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Zarqa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zarqa in December?
In December, Zarqa averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zarqa?
December is not one of Zarqa’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zarqa in December?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (57 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zarqa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zarqa get in December?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zarqa in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
