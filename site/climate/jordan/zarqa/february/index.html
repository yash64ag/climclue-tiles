How February compares in Zarqa

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Zarqa. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. March is 2.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Zarqa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zarqa in February?
In February, Zarqa averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zarqa?
February is not one of Zarqa’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zarqa in February?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (49 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zarqa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zarqa get in February?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zarqa in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
