How March compares in Zarqa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zarqa. Compared with February: 3.2 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 4.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Zarqa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zarqa in March?
In March, Zarqa averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zarqa?
March is not one of Zarqa’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zarqa in March?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (57 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zarqa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zarqa get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zarqa in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
