How October compares in Zarqa

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zarqa. Compared with September: 3.6 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. November is 6.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Zarqa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zarqa in October?
In October, Zarqa averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zarqa?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Zarqa’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zarqa in October?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (57 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zarqa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zarqa get in October?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zarqa in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
