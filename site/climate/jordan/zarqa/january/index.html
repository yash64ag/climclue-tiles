How January compares in Zarqa

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zarqa. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Zarqa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zarqa in January?
In January, Zarqa averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zarqa?
January is not one of Zarqa’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zarqa in January?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (57 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zarqa in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zarqa get in January?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zarqa in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
