Zarqa at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Jordan, Zarqa is the warmest (17.8 °C year average) and the driest (232 mm a year). Compared with Amman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Zarqa is 1.2 °C warmer in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 124 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zarqa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zarqa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
