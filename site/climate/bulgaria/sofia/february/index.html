How February compares in Sofia

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sofia. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. March is 4.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Sofia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sofia in February?
In February, Sofia averages a day high of 5.2 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -2.3 °C (28 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Sofia?
February is not one of Sofia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sofia in February?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Sofia in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sofia get in February?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sofia in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
