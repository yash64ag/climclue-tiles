How December compares in Sofia

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sofia. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Sofia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sofia in December?
In December, Sofia averages a day high of 4.2 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -2.1 °C (28 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sofia?
December is not one of Sofia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sofia in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Sofia in December?
Yes, on average: about 23 cm (9.2 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sofia get in December?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sofia in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
