How November compares in Sofia

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Sofia. Compared with October: 6.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Sofia in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sofia in November?
In November, Sofia averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sofia?
November is not one of Sofia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sofia in November?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (86 mm), the driest February (47 mm).
Does it snow in Sofia in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.8 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Sofia get in November?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sofia in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
