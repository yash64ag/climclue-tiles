How September compares in Sofia

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Sofia. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Sofia in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sofia in September?
In September, Sofia averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Sofia?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Sofia’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sofia in September?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (86 mm), the driest February (47 mm).
Does it snow in Sofia in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sofia get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sofia in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
