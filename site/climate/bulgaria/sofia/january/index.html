How January compares in Sofia

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sofia. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Sofia in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sofia in January?
In January, Sofia averages a day high of 3.8 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 82 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sofia?
January is not one of Sofia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sofia in January?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (86 mm), the driest February (47 mm).
Does it snow in Sofia in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 32 cm (12.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 14 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sofia get in January?
About 82 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sofia in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
