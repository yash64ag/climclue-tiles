Stara Zagora at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bulgaria, Stara Zagora is the 3rd coolest (12.0 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,284 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (652 mm a year). Compared with Plovdiv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Stara Zagora is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 172 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Stara Zagora, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Stara Zagora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 172 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
