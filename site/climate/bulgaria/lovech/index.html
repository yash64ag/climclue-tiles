Lovech at a glance

Among the 55 towns ClimaClue covers in Bulgaria, Lovech is 30th warmest (12.3 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 2,108 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (636 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Troyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Lovech is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 157 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.2 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 253 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lovech, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lovech has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 253 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lovech itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.132°N, 24.718°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-408-2047), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
