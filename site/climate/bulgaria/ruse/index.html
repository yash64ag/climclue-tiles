Ruse at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bulgaria, Ruse is the 3rd warmest (12.5 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,112 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (595 mm a year). Compared with Stara Zagora, the nearest covered town with different numbers (160 km away), Ruse is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 186 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ruse, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ruse has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 186 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
