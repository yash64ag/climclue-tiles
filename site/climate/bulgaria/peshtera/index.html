Peshtera at a glance

Among the 55 towns ClimaClue covers in Bulgaria, Peshtera is 35th warmest (11.9 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,247 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (673 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pazardzhik, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Peshtera is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Why Peshtera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Peshtera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.034°N, 24.300°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-419-2043), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
