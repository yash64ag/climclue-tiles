Velingrad at a glance

Among the 55 towns ClimaClue covers in Bulgaria, Velingrad is the 4th coolest (10.3 °C year average). Compared with Peshtera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Velingrad is 2.1 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 140 km from the sea.

Why Velingrad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 140 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C. The 10 km area around Velingrad, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 7.8 °C a year, 2.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Velingrad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.028°N, 23.992°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-419-2039), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
