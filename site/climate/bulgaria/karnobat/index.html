Karnobat at a glance

Among the 55 towns ClimaClue covers in Bulgaria, Karnobat is 31st warmest (12.2 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,242 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (559 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aytos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Karnobat is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Karnobat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Karnobat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Karnobat itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.650°N, 26.983°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-413-2069), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
