Plovdiv at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bulgaria, Plovdiv is the 2nd warmest (13.0 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,231 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (582 mm a year). Compared with Stara Zagora, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Plovdiv is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 70 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 146 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Plovdiv, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Plovdiv has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 146 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
