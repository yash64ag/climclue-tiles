Varna sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bulgaria

The sea off Varna averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.0 °C (43 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Varna sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Varna?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Varna?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 6.0 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Varna in February?
About 6.0 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Varna?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
