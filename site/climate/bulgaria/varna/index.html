Varna at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bulgaria, Varna is the 2nd coolest (11.7 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,989 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (601 mm a year). Compared with Burgas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Varna is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 76 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Varna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Varna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
