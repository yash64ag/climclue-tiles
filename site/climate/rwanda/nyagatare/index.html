Nyagatare at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Rwanda, Nyagatare is the warmest (21.1 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,970 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (848 mm a year). Compared with Kigali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Nyagatare is 1.3 °C warmer in February and 0.8 °C warmer in November, with 196 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.8 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, November (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.4 °C (60 °F), in November. The area lies about 1,070 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nyagatare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nyagatare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,070 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
