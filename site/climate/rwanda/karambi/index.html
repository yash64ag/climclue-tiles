Karambi at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in Rwanda, Karambi is 38th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 1,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,955 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Macuba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Karambi is 2.1 °C cooler in April and 2.1 °C cooler in July, with 255 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (18.1 °C), averages 0.7 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,140 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Karambi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Karambi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,140 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. The 10 km area around Karambi, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.3 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Karambi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (2.317°S, 29.244°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-863-2092), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
