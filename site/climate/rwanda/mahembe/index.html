Mahembe at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in Rwanda, Mahembe is 33rd warmest (18.2 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 1,961 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,975 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gihombo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Mahembe is 0.9 °C cooler in March and 0.9 °C cooler in November, with 118 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (18.5 °C), averages 0.6 °C more than its coolest, November (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.5 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,144 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mahembe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mahembe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,144 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mahembe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (2.239°S, 29.293°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-862-2092), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
