Why Musanze has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,134 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Musanze climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Musanze?
August, with an average of 16.6 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.0 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 12.0 °C (54 °F).
What is the coldest month in Musanze?
November, with an average of 15.8 °C (61 °F), day highs around 20.0 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 11.9 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Musanze?
October, with 194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,561 mm (61.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Musanze?
July, with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Musanze?
July, with about 182 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Musanze?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Musanze?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Musanze have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Musanze’s GeoNames location (1.500°S, 29.635°E; ClimaClue area sq-854-2096), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 153,368 (GeoNames).
