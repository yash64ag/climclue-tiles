Gisenyi at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Rwanda, Gisenyi is the 2nd warmest (20.5 °C year average), the sunniest (about 1,995 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,647 mm a year). Compared with Musanze, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Gisenyi is 4.5 °C warmer in February and 4.0 °C warmer in July, with 87 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.9 °C), averages 0.7 °C more than its coolest, July (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.3 °C (65 °F), in December. The area lies about 1,163 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gisenyi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Gisenyi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Gisenyi are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

  • Driest (least precipitation): July (33 mm), June (56 mm) and August (68 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (215 h), June (199 h) and September (171 h).

Why Gisenyi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,163 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
