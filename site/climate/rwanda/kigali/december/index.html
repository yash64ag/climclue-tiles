How December compares in Kigali

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kigali. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 66 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Kigali in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kigali in December?
In December, Kigali averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kigali?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Kigali’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August, September and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kigali in December?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (11 mm).
Does it snow in Kigali in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kigali get in December?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kigali in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
