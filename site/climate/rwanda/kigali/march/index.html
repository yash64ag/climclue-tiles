Kigali weather in March

Average March weather in Kigali, Rwanda: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Kigali averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) days and 14.4 °C (58 °F) nights, with 121 mm (4.8 in) of rain over the month and about 155 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Kigali

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kigali. Compared with February: 0.3 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. April is 0.4 °C cooler than March on average.

Kigali in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kigali in March?
In March, Kigali averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kigali?
March is not one of Kigali’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July, August, September and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kigali in March?
121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (165 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Kigali in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kigali get in March?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kigali in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
