How September compares in Kigali

September is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kigali. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. October is 0.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Kigali in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kigali in September?
In September, Kigali averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kigali?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Kigali’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August, September and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kigali in September?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (165 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Kigali in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kigali get in September?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kigali in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
