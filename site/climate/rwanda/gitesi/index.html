Gitesi at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in Rwanda, Gitesi is 31st warmest (18.4 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 1,902 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,505 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kibuye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Gitesi is 2.0 °C cooler in August and 2.3 °C cooler in November, with 160 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.8 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, November (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,126 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gitesi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Gitesi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Gitesi are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (18.8 °C), September (18.7 °C) and March (18.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (22 mm), June (38 mm) and August (51 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (213 h), June (192 h) and August (174 h).

Why Gitesi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,126 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C. The 10 km area around Gitesi, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.8 °C a year, 1.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gitesi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (2.114°S, 29.402°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-861-2094), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 24,859, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
