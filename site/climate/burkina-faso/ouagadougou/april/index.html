How April compares in Ouagadougou

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ouagadougou. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Ouagadougou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ouagadougou in April?
In April, Ouagadougou averages a day high of 39.2 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ouagadougou?
April is not one of Ouagadougou’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ouagadougou in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ouagadougou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ouagadougou get in April?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ouagadougou in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
