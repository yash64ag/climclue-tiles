How March compares in Ouagadougou

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ouagadougou. Compared with February: 3.5 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. April is 1.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Ouagadougou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ouagadougou in March?
In March, Ouagadougou averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ouagadougou?
March is not one of Ouagadougou’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ouagadougou in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ouagadougou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ouagadougou get in March?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ouagadougou in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
