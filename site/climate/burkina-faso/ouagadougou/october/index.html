How October compares in Ouagadougou

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ouagadougou. Compared with September: 1.5 °C warmer, 103 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Ouagadougou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ouagadougou in October?
In October, Ouagadougou averages a day high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ouagadougou?
October is not one of Ouagadougou’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ouagadougou in October?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ouagadougou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ouagadougou get in October?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ouagadougou in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
