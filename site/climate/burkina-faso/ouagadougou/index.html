Ouagadougou at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Burkina Faso, Ouagadougou is the 3rd driest (728 mm a year). Compared with Saaba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Ouagadougou is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.9 °C), averages 8.5 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 765 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ouagadougou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ouagadougou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 765 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
