How September compares in Ouagadougou

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ouagadougou. Compared with August: 0.6 °C warmer, 77 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Ouagadougou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ouagadougou in September?
In September, Ouagadougou averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ouagadougou?
September is not one of Ouagadougou’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ouagadougou in September?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ouagadougou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ouagadougou get in September?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ouagadougou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
