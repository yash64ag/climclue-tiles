How May compares in Ouagadougou

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ouagadougou. Compared with April: 0.8 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Ouagadougou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ouagadougou in May?
In May, Ouagadougou averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 26.8 °C (80 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ouagadougou?
May is not one of Ouagadougou’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ouagadougou in May?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ouagadougou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ouagadougou get in May?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ouagadougou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
