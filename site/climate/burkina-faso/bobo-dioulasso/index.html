Bobo-Dioulasso at a glance

Among the 74 towns ClimaClue covers in Burkina Faso, Bobo-Dioulasso is the 2nd coolest (27.1 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (1,085 mm a year). Compared with Karankasso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Bobo-Dioulasso is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 86 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.8 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 667 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Bobo-Dioulasso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 667 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bobo-Dioulasso itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.181°N, 4.295°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-728-1757), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
