Bobo-Dioulasso at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Burkina Faso, Bobo-Dioulasso is the coolest (27.1 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,825 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,085 mm a year). Compared with Banfora, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Bobo-Dioulasso is about as warm in April and 0.3 °C cooler in August, with 117 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.8 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 667 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bobo-Dioulasso, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bobo-Dioulasso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 667 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
