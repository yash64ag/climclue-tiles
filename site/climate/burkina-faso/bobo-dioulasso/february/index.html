Bobo-Dioulasso in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bobo-Dioulasso in February?
In February, Bobo-Dioulasso averages a day high of 35.5 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bobo-Dioulasso?
No month in Bobo-Dioulasso is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
