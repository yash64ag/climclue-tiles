How November compares in Bobo-Dioulasso

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bobo-Dioulasso. Compared with October: 0.1 °C warmer, 58 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. December is 1.5 °C cooler than November on average.

What is the weather like in Bobo-Dioulasso in November?
In November, Bobo-Dioulasso averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bobo-Dioulasso?
No month in Bobo-Dioulasso is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
