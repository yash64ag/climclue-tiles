How October compares in Bobo-Dioulasso

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bobo-Dioulasso. Compared with September: 1.4 °C warmer, 131 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. November is 0.1 °C warmer than October on average.

Bobo-Dioulasso in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bobo-Dioulasso in October?
In October, Bobo-Dioulasso averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bobo-Dioulasso?
