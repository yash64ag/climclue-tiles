Bobo-Dioulasso weather in August

Average August weather in Bobo-Dioulasso, Burkina Faso: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Bobo-Dioulasso averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) days and 20.9 °C (70 °F) nights, with 286 mm (11.3 in) of rain over the month and about 184 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Bobo-Dioulasso

August is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Bobo-Dioulasso. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 70 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.
