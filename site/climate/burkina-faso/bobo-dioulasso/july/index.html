Bobo-Dioulasso in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bobo-Dioulasso in July?
In July, Bobo-Dioulasso averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bobo-Dioulasso?
No month in Bobo-Dioulasso is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bobo-Dioulasso in July?
216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (284 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bobo-Dioulasso in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bobo-Dioulasso get in July?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bobo-Dioulasso in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
