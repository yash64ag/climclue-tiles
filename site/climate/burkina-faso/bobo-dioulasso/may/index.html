Bobo-Dioulasso in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bobo-Dioulasso in May?
In May, Bobo-Dioulasso averages a day high of 34.9 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bobo-Dioulasso?
No month in Bobo-Dioulasso is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bobo-Dioulasso in May?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (284 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bobo-Dioulasso in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bobo-Dioulasso get in May?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bobo-Dioulasso in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
