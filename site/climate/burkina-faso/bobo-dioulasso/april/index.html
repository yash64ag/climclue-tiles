How April compares in Bobo-Dioulasso

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bobo-Dioulasso. Compared with March: 0.3 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. May is 1.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Bobo-Dioulasso in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bobo-Dioulasso in April?
In April, Bobo-Dioulasso averages a day high of 36.9 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bobo-Dioulasso?
No month in Bobo-Dioulasso is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bobo-Dioulasso in April?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (284 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bobo-Dioulasso in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bobo-Dioulasso get in April?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bobo-Dioulasso in April?
