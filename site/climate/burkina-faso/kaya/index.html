Why Kaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 818 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kaya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kaya?
April, with an average of 33.3 °C (92 °F), day highs around 39.9 °C (104 °F) and night lows around 25.5 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kaya?
January, with an average of 23.8 °C (75 °F), day highs around 31.5 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 16.4 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kaya?
August, with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 624 mm (24.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kaya?
January, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kaya?
November, with about 275 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kaya?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kaya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kaya’s GeoNames location (13.088°N, 1.084°W; ClimaClue area sq-709-1789), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 121,970 (GeoNames).
