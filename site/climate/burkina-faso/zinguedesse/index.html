Zinguédéssé at a glance

Among the 74 towns ClimaClue covers in Burkina Faso, Zinguédéssé is 53rd warmest (28.1 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,943 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (755 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saonré, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Zinguédéssé is about as warm in April and about as warm in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.8 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 755 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zinguédéssé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 755 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zinguédéssé itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.247°N, 1.584°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-717-1784), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
