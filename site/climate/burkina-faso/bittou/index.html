Bittou at a glance

Among the 74 towns ClimaClue covers in Burkina Faso, Bittou is 19th warmest (28.6 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 2,832 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (898 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zabré, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Bittou is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.9 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, August (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in December. The area lies about 604 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bittou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bittou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 604 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bittou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.258°N, 0.306°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-727-1796), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
