Ouahigouya at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Burkina Faso, Ouahigouya is the 2nd warmest (28.3 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,023 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (642 mm a year). Compared with Koudougou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Ouahigouya is 1.5 °C warmer in May and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 183 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.0 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 927 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ouahigouya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ouahigouya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 927 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
