Sembawang Estate at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Singapore, Sembawang Estate is the 2nd coolest (26.8 °C year average). Compared with Woodlands, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Sembawang Estate is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.4 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Why Sembawang Estate has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
