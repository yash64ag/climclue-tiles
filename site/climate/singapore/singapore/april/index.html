How April compares in Singapore

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Singapore. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Singapore in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Singapore in April?
In April, Singapore averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Singapore?
No month in Singapore is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Singapore in April?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (315 mm), the driest February (120 mm).
Does it snow in Singapore in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Singapore get in April?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Singapore in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
