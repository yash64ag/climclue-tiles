How October compares in Singapore

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Singapore. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. November is 0.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Singapore in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Singapore in October?
In October, Singapore averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 192 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Singapore?
No month in Singapore is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Singapore in October?
192 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (315 mm), the driest February (120 mm).
Does it snow in Singapore in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Singapore get in October?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Singapore in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
