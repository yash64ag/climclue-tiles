Kampong Pasir Ris at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Singapore, Kampong Pasir Ris is the 3rd warmest (26.9 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 2,051 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sengkang New Town, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Kampong Pasir Ris is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.6 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.5 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kampong Pasir Ris, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kampong Pasir Ris has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
