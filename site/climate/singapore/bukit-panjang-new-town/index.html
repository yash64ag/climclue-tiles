Bukit Panjang New Town at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Singapore, Bukit Panjang New Town is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,062 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (2,419 mm a year). Compared with Woodlands, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Bukit Panjang New Town is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.3 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bukit Panjang New Town has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
