Stanley sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Falkland Islands

The sea off Stanley averages 9.9 °C (50 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 5.0 °C (41 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Stanley sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Stanley?
In February, at about 9.9 °C (50 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Stanley?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 5.0 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Stanley in August?
About 5.0 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Stanley?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Stanley nearby?
In February: Puerto Deseado (13.3 °C, 725 km), Caleta Olivia (15.2 °C, 913 km) and Comodoro Rivadavia (14.7 °C, 957 km).
