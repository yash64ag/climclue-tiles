Adamstown sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pitcairn

The sea off Adamstown averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 22.2 °C (72 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to May.

Adamstown sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Adamstown?
In March, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Adamstown?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to May. The coolest month is August, at 22.2 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Adamstown in August?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Adamstown?
About 24.2 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
