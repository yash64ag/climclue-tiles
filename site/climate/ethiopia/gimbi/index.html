Gimbi at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Gimbi is 121st warmest (18.8 °C year average), 186th sunniest (about 2,846 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (2,176 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nejo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Gimbi is 0.3 °C cooler in March and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 175 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (21.9 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, July (16.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in October. The area lies about 814 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gimbi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gimbi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 814 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gimbi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.170°N, 35.835°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-748-2158), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
