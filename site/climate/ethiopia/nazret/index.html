Nazrēt at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Nazrēt is the 5th driest (860 mm a year). Compared with Bishoftu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Nazrēt is 2.0 °C warmer in May and 1.0 °C warmer in December, with 102 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.6 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, December (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in December. The area lies about 524 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nazrēt, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nazrēt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 524 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
