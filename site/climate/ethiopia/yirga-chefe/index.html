Yirga Ch’efē at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Yirga Ch’efē is the 3rd wettest (2,536 mm a year). Compared with Wenago, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Yirga Ch’efē is 1.0 °C cooler in March and 1.3 °C cooler in July, with 96 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.6 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in December. The area lies about 793 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yirga Ch’efē, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yirga Ch’efē has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 793 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C. The 10 km area around Yirga Ch’efē, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.8 °C a year, 2.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yirga Ch’efē itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.167°N, 38.200°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-778-2182), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
