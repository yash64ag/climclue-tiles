Warder at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Warder is the warmest (26.6 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,082 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (251 mm a year). Compared with Jijiga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (385 km away), Warder is 6.9 °C warmer in March and 6.9 °C warmer in November, with 380 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.8 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, November (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 397 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Warder, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Warder has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 397 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
