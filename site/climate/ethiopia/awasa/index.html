Awasa at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Awasa is 8th warmest (19.6 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,885 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (1,038 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shashamane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Awasa is 2.4 °C warmer in March and 1.6 °C warmer in July, with 836 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (21.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, July (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in December. The area lies about 703 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Awasa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Awasa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 703 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
