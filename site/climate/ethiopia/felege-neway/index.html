Felege Neway at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Felege Neway is 54th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 140th sunniest (about 2,883 hours of sunshine a year) and 125th wettest (1,172 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bako, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Felege Neway is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 254 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.0 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, July (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in December. The area lies about 884 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Felege Neway, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Felege Neway has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 884 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C. The 10 km area around Felege Neway, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.4 °C a year, 2.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Felege Neway itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.300°N, 36.883°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-776-2168), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
