Bale Hawassa at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Bale Hawassa is 38th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 2,895 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gununo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Bale Hawassa is 5.8 °C warmer in March and 5.5 °C warmer in July, with 410 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.6 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 782 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bale Hawassa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bale Hawassa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 782 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bale Hawassa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.919°N, 37.529°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-770-2175), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
