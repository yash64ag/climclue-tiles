Lumamē at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Lumamē is 191st warmest (16.0 °C year average), 159th sunniest (about 2,869 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (1,732 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Debre Mark’os, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Lumamē is 0.6 °C warmer in March and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 167 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (18.2 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (14.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in December. The area lies about 557 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lumamē, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lumamē has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 557 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lumamē itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.250°N, 37.933°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-737-2179), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
