Dembī Dolo at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Dembī Dolo is 124th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 225th sunniest (about 2,801 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (1,811 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gambēla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Dembī Dolo is 10.4 °C cooler in February and 9.1 °C cooler in July, with 745 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.1 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in November. The area lies about 933 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dembī Dolo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dembī Dolo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 933 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C. The 10 km area around Dembī Dolo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 20.0 °C a year, 1.4 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dembī Dolo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.533°N, 34.800°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-754-2148), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
