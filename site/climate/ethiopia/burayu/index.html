Burayu at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Burayu is the 2nd coolest (14.3 °C year average) and the 5th least sunny (about 2,830 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Addis Ababa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Burayu is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 166 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (15.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, December (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in November. The area lies about 543 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Burayu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Burayu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 543 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
