Bedēsa, South Ethiopia Regional State at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Bedēsa, South Ethiopia Regional State is 45th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 123rd sunniest (about 2,905 hours of sunshine a year) and 185th wettest (745 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dimtu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Bedēsa, South Ethiopia Regional State is 1.1 °C warmer in March and 1.1 °C warmer in July, with 162 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.5 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, July (20.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in December. The area lies about 750 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Bedēsa, South Ethiopia Regional State has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 750 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bedēsa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.833°N, 38.083°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-771-2180), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
