Digih Habar Es at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Digih Habar Es is 19th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 3,080 hours of sunshine a year) and 227th wettest (231 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Raqo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Digih Habar Es is 0.1 °C warmer in September and about as warm in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Why Digih Habar Es has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
