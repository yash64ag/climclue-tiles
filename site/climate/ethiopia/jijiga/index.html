Jijiga at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Jijiga is the 3rd driest (633 mm a year). Compared with Harar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (75 km away), Jijiga is 1.7 °C warmer in March and 1.1 °C warmer in December, with 325 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (21.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, December (18.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in December. The area lies about 203 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jijiga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jijiga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 203 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jijiga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.350°N, 42.800°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-746-2228), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
