Bishoftu at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Bishoftu is 12th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,887 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (962 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nazrēt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Bishoftu is 2.0 °C cooler in May and 1.0 °C cooler in December, with 102 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.6 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, December (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in November. The area lies about 535 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bishoftu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bishoftu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 535 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
