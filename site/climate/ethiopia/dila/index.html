Why Dīla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 760 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Dīla climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dīla?
March, with an average of 19.8 °C (68 °F), day highs around 25.3 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 14.0 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dīla?
July, with an average of 16.9 °C (63 °F), day highs around 20.1 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 13.6 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dīla?
May, with 318 mm (12.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,289 mm (90.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dīla?
January, with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Dīla?
December, with about 292 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dīla?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Dīla?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February and December.
What type of climate does Dīla have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Dīla’s GeoNames location (6.417°N, 38.317°E; ClimaClue area sq-775-2183), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 158,800 (GeoNames).
