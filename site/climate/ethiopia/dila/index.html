Dīla at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Dīla is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,815 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (2,289 mm a year). Compared with Awasa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Dīla is 1.7 °C cooler in March and 1.3 °C cooler in July, with 1,251 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.8 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in December. The area lies about 760 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dīla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dīla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 760 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
