Gīnch’ī at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Gīnch’ī is 183rd warmest (16.4 °C year average), 214th sunniest (about 2,817 hours of sunshine a year) and 85th wettest (1,387 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ādīs ‘Alem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Gīnch’ī is 0.1 °C cooler in March and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (17.6 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, July (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in December. The area lies about 590 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gīnch’ī, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gīnch’ī has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 590 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gīnch’ī itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.033°N, 38.150°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-749-2181), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
