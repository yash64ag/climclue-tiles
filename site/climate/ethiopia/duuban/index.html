Duuban at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Duuban is 18th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 3,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 226th wettest (232 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yamarugley, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Duuban is 0.1 °C warmer in September and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (28.4 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.2 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 315 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Duuban, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Duuban has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 315 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Duuban itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.680°N, 46.066°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-773-2260), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
