Bedelē at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Bedelē is 141st warmest (18.1 °C year average), 198th sunniest (about 2,835 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (1,913 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nek’emtē, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Bedelē is about as warm in March and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 476 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (20.2 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in December. The area lies about 796 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bedelē, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bedelē has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 796 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bedelē itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.456°N, 36.353°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-755-2163), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
