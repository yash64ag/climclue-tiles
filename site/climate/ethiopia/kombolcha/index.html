Kombolcha at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Kombolcha is 17th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,949 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (1,061 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dessie, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Kombolcha is 2.7 °C warmer in June and 2.8 °C warmer in December, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.4 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, December (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in December. The area lies about 344 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kombolcha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kombolcha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 344 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
