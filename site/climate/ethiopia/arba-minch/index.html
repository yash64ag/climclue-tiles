Arba Minch at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Arba Minch is the 3rd warmest (22.1 °C year average). Compared with Sodo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Arba Minch is 3.4 °C warmer in February and 4.2 °C warmer in July, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.6 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, July (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 852 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Arba Minch, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Arba Minch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 852 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
