Mek'ele at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Mek'ele is the 4th driest (738 mm a year). Compared with Ādīgrat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Mek'ele is 2.4 °C warmer in May and 2.2 °C warmer in December, with 269 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.6 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, December (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 204 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mek'ele, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mek'ele has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 204 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
