How December compares in Addis Ababa

December is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Addis Ababa. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. January is 0.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Addis Ababa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Addis Ababa in December?
In December, Addis Ababa averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Addis Ababa?
December is not one of Addis Ababa’s mild months; those are February, March, April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Addis Ababa in December?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (283 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Addis Ababa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Addis Ababa get in December?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Addis Ababa in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
