How April compares in Addis Ababa

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Addis Ababa. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Addis Ababa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Addis Ababa in April?
In April, Addis Ababa averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Addis Ababa?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Addis Ababa’s mild months (February, March, April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Addis Ababa in April?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (283 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Addis Ababa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Addis Ababa get in April?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Addis Ababa in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
