How February compares in Addis Ababa

February is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Addis Ababa. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. March is 0.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Addis Ababa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Addis Ababa in February?
In February, Addis Ababa averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Addis Ababa?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Addis Ababa’s mild months (February, March, April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Addis Ababa in February?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (273 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Addis Ababa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Addis Ababa get in February?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Addis Ababa in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
