Addis Ababa at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Addis Ababa is 211th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 209th sunniest (about 2,828 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,142 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Burayu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Addis Ababa is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.7 °C warmer in December, with 160 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.8 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, December (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in December. The area lies about 534 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Addis Ababa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Addis Ababa

For mild weather, the best months to visit Addis Ababa are February, March, April and May.

Why Addis Ababa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Addis Ababa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.025°N, 38.747°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-749-2187), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
