Addis Ababa at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Addis Ababa is the 5th coolest (15.2 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,828 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Burayu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Addis Ababa is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.7 °C warmer in December, with 160 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.8 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, December (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in November. The area lies about 534 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Addis Ababa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Addis Ababa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Addis Ababa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.025°N, 38.747°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-749-2187), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
