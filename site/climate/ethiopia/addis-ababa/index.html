Addis Ababa at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Addis Ababa is the 4th coolest (14.5 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,828 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Burayu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Addis Ababa is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 166 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.1 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, December (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in November. The area lies about 534 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Addis Ababa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Addis Ababa are February, March, April and May.

Why Addis Ababa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
