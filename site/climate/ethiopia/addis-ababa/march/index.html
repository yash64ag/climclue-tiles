How March compares in Addis Ababa

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Addis Ababa. Compared with February: 0.9 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. April is 0.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Addis Ababa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Addis Ababa in March?
In March, Addis Ababa averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Addis Ababa?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Addis Ababa’s mild months (February, March, April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Addis Ababa in March?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (283 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Addis Ababa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Addis Ababa get in March?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Addis Ababa in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
