How May compares in Addis Ababa

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Addis Ababa. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Addis Ababa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Addis Ababa in May?
In May, Addis Ababa averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Addis Ababa?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Addis Ababa’s mild months (February, March, April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Addis Ababa in May?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (273 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Addis Ababa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Addis Ababa get in May?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Addis Ababa in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
