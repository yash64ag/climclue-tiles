Dire Dawa at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Dire Dawa is 40th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 3,042 hours of sunshine a year) and 170th wettest (851 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with ‘Alemaya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Dire Dawa is 7.1 °C warmer in June and 4.8 °C warmer in December, with 243 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.0 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, December (20.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in December. The area lies about 258 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dire Dawa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dire Dawa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 258 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C. The 10 km area around Dire Dawa, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.8 °C a year, 1.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dire Dawa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.593°N, 41.866°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-744-2218), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
