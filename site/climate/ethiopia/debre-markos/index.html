Debre Mark’os at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Debre Mark’os is the 5th wettest (1,939 mm a year). Compared with Bahir Dar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (143 km away), Debre Mark’os is 4.9 °C cooler in March and 3.8 °C cooler in July, with 637 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (17.6 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, July (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in November. The area lies about 575 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Debre Mark’os, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Debre Mark’os has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 575 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
