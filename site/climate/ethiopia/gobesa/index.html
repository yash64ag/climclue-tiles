Gobēsa at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Gobēsa is 211th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 165th sunniest (about 2,866 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (1,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bek’ojī, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Gobēsa is 2.6 °C warmer in March and 2.5 °C warmer in November, with 96 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (16.2 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, November (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 573 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gobēsa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gobēsa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 573 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C. The 10 km area around Gobēsa, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.3 °C a year, 1.1 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gobēsa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.617°N, 39.500°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-763-2195), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
