Tarch’a Sodo at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Tarch’a Sodo is 42nd warmest (22.7 °C year average), 160th sunniest (about 2,869 hours of sunshine a year) and 153rd wettest (948 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Waka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Tarch’a Sodo is 4.7 °C warmer in February and 4.6 °C warmer in July, with 303 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.8 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in December. The area lies about 801 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tarch’a Sodo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tarch’a Sodo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 801 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tarch’a Sodo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.151°N, 37.172°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-768-2171), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
