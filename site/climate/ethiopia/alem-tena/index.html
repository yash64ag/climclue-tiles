‘Alem T’ēna at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, ‘Alem T’ēna is 60th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,927 hours of sunshine a year) and 202nd wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dēra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), ‘Alem T’ēna is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.9 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, December (19.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in December. The area lies about 563 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of ‘Alem T’ēna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why ‘Alem T’ēna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 563 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
