Nek’emtē at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Nek’emtē is the 2nd wettest (2,389 mm a year). Compared with Jimma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (160 km away), Nek’emtē is 0.4 °C cooler in March and 2.1 °C cooler in July, with 817 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (20.2 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, July (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in October. The area lies about 747 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nek’emtē, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nek’emtē has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 747 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
