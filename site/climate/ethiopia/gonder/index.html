Gonder at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Gonder is the 5th sunniest (about 3,001 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Debre Tabor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (103 km away), Gonder is 2.2 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 882 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (19.9 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 400 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gonder, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gonder has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 400 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
