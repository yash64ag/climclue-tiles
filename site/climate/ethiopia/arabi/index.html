Ārabī at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Ārabī is the 2nd warmest (25.2 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,075 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (354 mm a year). Compared with Jijiga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Ārabī is 8.6 °C warmer in June and 3.7 °C warmer in January, with 277 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.9 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.5 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 187 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ārabī, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ārabī has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 187 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
