Sodo at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Sodo is 14th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,842 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,299 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hosa’ina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Sodo is 2.9 °C warmer in March and 1.9 °C warmer in July, with 75 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (20.4 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, July (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in November. The area lies about 773 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sodo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sodo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 773 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
