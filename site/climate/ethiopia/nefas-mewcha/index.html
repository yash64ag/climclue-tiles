Nefas Mewch’a at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Nefas Mewch’a is 226th warmest (13.9 °C year average), 152nd sunniest (about 2,875 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (1,587 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Filakit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Nefas Mewch’a is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 577 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.1 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, August (12.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 420 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nefas Mewch’a, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nefas Mewch’a has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 420 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
