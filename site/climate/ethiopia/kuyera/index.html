Kuyera at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Kuyera is 143rd warmest (18.0 °C year average), 143rd sunniest (about 2,880 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (1,932 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arsi Negele, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kuyera is 0.1 °C warmer in March and about as warm in July, with 187 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.5 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 664 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kuyera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kuyera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 664 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kuyera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.300°N, 38.650°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-766-2186), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
