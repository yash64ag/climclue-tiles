Ādīgrat at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Ādīgrat is the 5th coolest (14.8 °C year average). Compared with Mek'ele, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Ādīgrat is 2.4 °C cooler in May and 2.2 °C cooler in December, with 269 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (17.1 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, December (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ādīgrat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ādīgrat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
