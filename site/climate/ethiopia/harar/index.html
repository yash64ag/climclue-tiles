Harar at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Harar is 13th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,966 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (918 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dire Dawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Harar is 2.3 °C cooler in March and 2.2 °C cooler in December, with 63 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, December (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in December. The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Harar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Harar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
