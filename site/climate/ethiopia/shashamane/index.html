Shashamane at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Shashamane is 16th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,729 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Awasa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Shashamane is 1.9 °C cooler in March and 1.4 °C cooler in July, with 645 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.5 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, July (17.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in December. The area lies about 672 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shashamane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shashamane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 672 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shashamane itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.200°N, 38.600°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-767-2186), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
