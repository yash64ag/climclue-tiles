Shashamane at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Shashamane is 18th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,874 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Awasa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Shashamane is 2.4 °C cooler in March and 1.6 °C cooler in July, with 836 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.1 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.7 °C (51 °F), in December. The area lies about 672 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shashamane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shashamane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 672 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
