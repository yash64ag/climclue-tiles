Āsbe Teferī at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Āsbe Teferī is 105th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (1,017 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mī’ēso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Āsbe Teferī is 2.8 °C cooler in March and 3.0 °C cooler in December, with 175 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (20.5 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, December (17.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in December. The area lies about 363 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Āsbe Teferī, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Āsbe Teferī has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 363 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. The 10 km area around Āsbe Teferī, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 17.4 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Āsbe Teferī itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.086°N, 40.867°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-749-2208), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
