Metahāra at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Metahāra is 27th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 3,029 hours of sunshine a year) and 208th wettest (542 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Āwash, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Metahāra is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 136 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, December (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in December. The area lies about 437 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Metahāra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Metahāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 437 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C. The 10 km area around Metahāra, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 25.3 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Metahāra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.900°N, 39.917°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-750-2199), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
