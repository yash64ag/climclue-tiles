Bambesi at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Bambesi is 67th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 207th sunniest (about 2,829 hours of sunshine a year) and 87th wettest (1,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mendī, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Bambesi is 0.8 °C warmer in March and 1.0 °C warmer in July, with 430 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.1 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in November. The area lies about 836 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bambesi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bambesi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 836 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bambesi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.750°N, 34.733°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-742-2147), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
