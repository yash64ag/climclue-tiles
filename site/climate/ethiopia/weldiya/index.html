Weldiya at a glance

Among the 233 towns ClimaClue covers in Ethiopia, Weldiya is 115th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 2,958 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (989 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mersa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Weldiya is 2.5 °C cooler in June and 2.0 °C cooler in December, with 169 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.8 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, December (16.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 342 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Weldiya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Weldiya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 342 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Weldiya itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.828°N, 39.592°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-721-2195), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
