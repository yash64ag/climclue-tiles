Sebeta at a glance

Among the 30 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ethiopia, Sebeta is 25th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,854 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (1,385 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Burayu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Sebeta is 0.9 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in December, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (16.9 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, December (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.9 °C (44 °F), in November. The area lies about 549 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sebeta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sebeta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 549 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
