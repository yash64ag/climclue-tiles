Dili at a glance

Its warmest month, November (27.4 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, July (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dili, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Dili

No month in Dili is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are June (25.8 °C, 30 mm), July (25.4 °C, 19 mm) and August (25.6 °C, 9 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): November (27.4 °C), October (27.3 °C) and December (26.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): August (9 mm), September (18 mm) and July (19 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): October (279 h), September (261 h) and August (259 h).

Why Dili has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
