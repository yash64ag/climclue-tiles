Maubara sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liquiçá · Timor Leste

The sea off Maubara averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 27.0 °C (81 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Maubara sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Maubara?
In November, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Maubara?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 27.0 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Maubara in August?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Maubara?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Maubara nearby?
In November: Suai (30.2 °C, 78 km).
