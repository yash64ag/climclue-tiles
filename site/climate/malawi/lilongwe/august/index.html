How August compares in Lilongwe

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Lilongwe. Compared with July: 1.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. September is 2.9 °C warmer than August on average.

Is August a good time to visit Lilongwe?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Lilongwe’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
