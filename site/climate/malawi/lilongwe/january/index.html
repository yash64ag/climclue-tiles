Lilongwe weather in January

Average January weather in Lilongwe, Malawi: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Lilongwe averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) days and 17.7 °C (64 °F) nights, with 220 mm (8.7 in) of rain over the month and about 160 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Lilongwe

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Lilongwe. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 57 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Lilongwe in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lilongwe in January?
In January, Lilongwe averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lilongwe?
January is not one of Lilongwe’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lilongwe in January?
220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (220 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lilongwe in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lilongwe get in January?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lilongwe in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
