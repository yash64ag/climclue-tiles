How October compares in Lilongwe

October is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Lilongwe. Compared with September: 2.2 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. November is 0.6 °C warmer than October on average.

Lilongwe in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lilongwe in October?
In October, Lilongwe averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lilongwe?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Lilongwe’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lilongwe in October?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (224 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lilongwe in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lilongwe get in October?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lilongwe in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
