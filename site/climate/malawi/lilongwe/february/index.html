How February compares in Lilongwe

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lilongwe. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is about as warm as February on average.

Lilongwe in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lilongwe in February?
In February, Lilongwe averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lilongwe?
February is not one of Lilongwe’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lilongwe in February?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (220 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lilongwe in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lilongwe get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lilongwe in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
