Ntcheu at a glance

Among the 23 towns ClimaClue covers in Malawi, Ntcheu is the 5th coolest (19.7 °C year average). Compared with Balaka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Ntcheu is 3.7 °C cooler in November and 3.2 °C cooler in July, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.6 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, July (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 432 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ntcheu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ntcheu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 432 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ntcheu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.820°S, 34.636°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-988-2146), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
