Mzuzu at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malawi, Mzuzu is the coolest (18.3 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,587 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,206 mm a year). Compared with Lilongwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (279 km away), Mzuzu is 3.3 °C cooler in November and 2.4 °C cooler in July, with 397 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (20.8 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, July (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 658 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mzuzu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mzuzu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 658 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
