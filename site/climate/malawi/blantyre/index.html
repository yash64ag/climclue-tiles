Blantyre at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malawi, Blantyre is the 2nd warmest (19.5 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,603 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,207 mm a year). Compared with Zomba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Blantyre is 0.3 °C warmer in November and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.6 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 331 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Blantyre, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Blantyre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 331 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
