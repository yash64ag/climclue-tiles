Why Blantyre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 331 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Blantyre climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Blantyre?
November, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 27.5 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 17.5 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Blantyre?
July, with an average of 15.2 °C (59 °F), day highs around 20.0 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 10.0 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Blantyre?
January, with 286 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,207 mm (47.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Blantyre?
June, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Blantyre?
September, with about 268 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Blantyre?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Blantyre?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Blantyre have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Blantyre’s GeoNames location (15.785°S, 35.009°E; ClimaClue area sq-997-2150), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 902,588 (GeoNames).
