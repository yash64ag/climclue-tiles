How November compares in Blantyre

November is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Blantyre. Compared with October: 0.9 °C warmer, 68 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. December is 0.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Blantyre in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Blantyre in November?
In November, Blantyre averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Blantyre?
November is not one of Blantyre’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Blantyre in November?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (286 mm), the driest June (12 mm).
Does it snow in Blantyre in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Blantyre get in November?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Blantyre in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
