How May compares in Blantyre

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Blantyre. Compared with April: 2.0 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Blantyre in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Blantyre in May?
In May, Blantyre averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Blantyre?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Blantyre’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Blantyre in May?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (286 mm), the driest June (12 mm).
Does it snow in Blantyre in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Blantyre get in May?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Blantyre in May?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
