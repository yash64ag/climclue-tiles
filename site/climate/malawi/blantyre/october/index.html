Blantyre weather in October

Average October weather in Blantyre, Malawi: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Blantyre averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 15.9 °C (61 °F) nights, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 264 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Blantyre

October is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Blantyre. Compared with September: 1.9 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Blantyre in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Blantyre in October?
In October, Blantyre averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Blantyre?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Blantyre’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Blantyre in October?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (298 mm), the driest June (13 mm).
Does it snow in Blantyre in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Blantyre get in October?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Blantyre in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
