Zomba at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malawi, Zomba is the 3rd warmest (19.4 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,642 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,216 mm a year). Compared with Blantyre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Zomba is 0.3 °C cooler in November and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.4 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, July (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 342 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zomba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zomba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 342 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
