Saint John’s at a glance

Its warmest month, September (27.2 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, February (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saint John’s, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Saint John’s

For mild weather, the best months to visit Saint John’s are January, February and March.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and March.
  • Warmest (average temperature): September (27.2 °C), August (27.2 °C) and July (26.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (53 mm), March (54 mm) and January (70 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (252 h), August (247 h) and March (243 h).

Why Saint John’s has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saint John’s itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.121°N, 61.843°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-668-1181), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
