Bern at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Switzerland, Bern is the coolest (9.6 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,655 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,183 mm a year). Compared with Basel, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Bern is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 171 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 18.3 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 306 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bern, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bern has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 306 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
