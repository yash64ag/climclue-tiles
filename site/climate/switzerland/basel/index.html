Basel at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Switzerland, Basel is the 2nd warmest (10.6 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 1,615 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,012 mm a year). Compared with Bern, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Basel is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 171 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 368 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 120% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Basel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 368 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
