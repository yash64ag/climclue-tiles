Littau at a glance

Among the 73 towns ClimaClue covers in Switzerland, Littau is 38th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 1,581 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,550 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kriens, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Littau is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.2 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 303 km from the sea.

Why Littau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 303 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Littau itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.050°N, 8.263°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-369-1882), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
