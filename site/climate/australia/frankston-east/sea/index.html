Frankston East sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Victoria · Australia

The sea off Frankston East averages 19.7 °C (67 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 11.5 °C (53 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Frankston East sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Frankston East?
In February, at about 19.7 °C (67 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Frankston East?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 11.5 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Frankston East in August?
About 11.5 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Frankston East?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Frankston East nearby?
In February: Batemans Bay (22.2 °C, 523 km), Wollongong (22.6 °C, 661 km) and Adelaide (21.5 °C, 684 km).
