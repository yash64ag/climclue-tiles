How February compares in Adelaide

February is the warmest month, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. March is 1.8 °C cooler than February on average.

Adelaide in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in February?
In February, Adelaide averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Adelaide?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Adelaide’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in February?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (97 mm), the driest February (19 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in February?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adelaide in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
