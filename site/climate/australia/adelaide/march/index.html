How March compares in Adelaide

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with February: 1.9 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. April is 2.7 °C cooler than March on average.

Adelaide in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in March?
In March, Adelaide averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Adelaide?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Adelaide’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in March?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in March?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Adelaide in March?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (21.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Adelaide in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
