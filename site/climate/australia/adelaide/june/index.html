How June compares in Adelaide

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Adelaide. Compared with May: 2.5 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Adelaide in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in June?
In June, Adelaide averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Adelaide?
June is not one of Adelaide’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in June?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in June?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Adelaide in June?
About 15.0 °C (59 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (21.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Adelaide in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
