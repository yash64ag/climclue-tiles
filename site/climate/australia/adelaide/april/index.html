How April compares in Adelaide

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with March: 2.8 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. May is 2.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Adelaide in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in April?
In April, Adelaide averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Adelaide?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Adelaide’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in April?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Adelaide in April?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (21.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Adelaide in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
