How August compares in Adelaide

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with July: 0.8 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. September is 1.8 °C warmer than August on average.

Adelaide in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in August?
In August, Adelaide averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Adelaide?
August is not one of Adelaide’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in August?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in August?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Adelaide in August?
About 12.9 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (21.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Adelaide in August?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
