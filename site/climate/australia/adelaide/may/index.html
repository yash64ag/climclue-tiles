How May compares in Adelaide

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with April: 2.6 °C cooler, 35 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. June is 2.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Adelaide in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in May?
In May, Adelaide averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Adelaide?
May is not one of Adelaide’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in May?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in May?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adelaide in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
