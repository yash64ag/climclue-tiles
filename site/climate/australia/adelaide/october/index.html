How October compares in Adelaide

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with September: 1.7 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 2.6 °C warmer than October on average.

Adelaide in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in October?
In October, Adelaide averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Adelaide?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Adelaide’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in October?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (97 mm), the driest February (19 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in October?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adelaide in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
