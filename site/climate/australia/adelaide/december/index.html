Adelaide weather in December

Average December weather in Adelaide, Australia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Adelaide averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 13.6 °C (56 °F) nights, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of rain over the month and about 282 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Adelaide

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Adelaide. Compared with November: 1.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. January is 1.7 °C warmer than December on average.

Adelaide in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adelaide in December?
In December, Adelaide averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Adelaide?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Adelaide’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adelaide in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Adelaide in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adelaide get in December?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Adelaide in December?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (21.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Adelaide in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
