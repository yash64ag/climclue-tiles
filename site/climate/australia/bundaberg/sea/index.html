Bundaberg sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Queensland · Australia

The sea off Bundaberg averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 19.6 °C (67 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to April.

Bundaberg sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bundaberg?
In February, at about 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bundaberg?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to April. The coolest month is August, at 19.6 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bundaberg in August?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bundaberg?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Bundaberg nearby?
In February: Mackay (27.8 °C, 526 km).
