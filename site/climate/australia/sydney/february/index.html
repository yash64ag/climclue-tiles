How February compares in Sydney

February is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sydney. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 43 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. March is 1.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Sydney in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sydney in February?
In February, Sydney averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 136 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Sydney?
February is not one of Sydney’s mild months; those are January, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sydney in February?
136 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (136 mm), the driest September (62 mm).
Does it snow in Sydney in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sydney get in February?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sydney in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
