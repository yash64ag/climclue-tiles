How August compares in Sydney

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Sydney. Compared with July: 1.0 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. September is 2.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Sydney in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sydney in August?
In August, Sydney averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sydney?
August is not one of Sydney’s mild months; those are January, March, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sydney in August?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (129 mm), the driest September (60 mm).
Does it snow in Sydney in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sydney get in August?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sydney in August?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (22.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Sydney in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
