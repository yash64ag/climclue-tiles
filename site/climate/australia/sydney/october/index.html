How October compares in Sydney

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sydney. Compared with September: 1.6 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. November is 1.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Sydney in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sydney in October?
In October, Sydney averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sydney?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Sydney’s mild months (January, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sydney in October?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (136 mm), the driest September (62 mm).
Does it snow in Sydney in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sydney get in October?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sydney in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
