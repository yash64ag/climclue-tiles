How May compares in Sydney

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sydney. Compared with April: 2.4 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. June is 2.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Sydney in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sydney in May?
In May, Sydney averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sydney?
May is not one of Sydney’s mild months; those are January, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sydney in May?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (136 mm), the driest September (62 mm).
Does it snow in Sydney in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sydney get in May?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sydney in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
