Sunshine Coast sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Queensland · Australia

The sea off Sunshine Coast averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 20.5 °C (69 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April.

Sunshine Coast sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sunshine Coast?
In February, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sunshine Coast?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 20.5 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sunshine Coast in August?
About 20.5 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Sunshine Coast?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sunshine Coast nearby?
In February: Hervey Bay (27.2 °C, 155 km) and Bundaberg (27.1 °C, 212 km).
