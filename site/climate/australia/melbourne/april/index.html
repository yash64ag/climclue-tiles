How April compares in Melbourne

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with March: 2.8 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. May is 2.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Melbourne in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in April?
In April, Melbourne averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Melbourne?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Melbourne’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in April?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (66 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in April?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
