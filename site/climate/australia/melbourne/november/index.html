How November compares in Melbourne

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with October: 2.1 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. December is 1.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Melbourne in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in November?
In November, Melbourne averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Melbourne?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Melbourne’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in November?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (65 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in November?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
