How February compares in Melbourne

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. March is 1.5 °C cooler than February on average.

Melbourne in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in February?
In February, Melbourne averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Melbourne?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Melbourne’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in February?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (65 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in February?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in February?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
