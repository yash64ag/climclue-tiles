Melbourne sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Victoria · Australia

The sea off Melbourne averages 20.1 °C (68 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 11.1 °C (52 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from February.

Melbourne sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Melbourne?
In February, at about 20.1 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Melbourne?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from February. The coolest month is August, at 11.1 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Melbourne in August?
About 11.1 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Melbourne?
About 15.7 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Melbourne nearby?
In February: Batemans Bay (22.2 °C, 520 km), Wollongong (22.6 °C, 652 km) and Adelaide (21.5 °C, 654 km).
