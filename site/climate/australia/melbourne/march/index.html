How March compares in Melbourne

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with February: 1.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. April is 3.0 °C cooler than March on average.

Melbourne in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in March?
In March, Melbourne averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Melbourne?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Melbourne’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in March?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (66 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in March?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Melbourne in March?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (20.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Melbourne in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
