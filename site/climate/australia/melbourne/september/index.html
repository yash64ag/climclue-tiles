How September compares in Melbourne

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with August: 1.6 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. October is 1.7 °C warmer than September on average.

Melbourne in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in September?
In September, Melbourne averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Melbourne?
September is not one of Melbourne’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in September?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (65 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in September?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
