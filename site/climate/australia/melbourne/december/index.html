How December compares in Melbourne

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with November: 1.6 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. January is 1.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Melbourne in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in December?
In December, Melbourne averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Melbourne?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Melbourne’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in December?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (65 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in December?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
