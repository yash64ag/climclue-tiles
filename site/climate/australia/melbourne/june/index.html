How June compares in Melbourne

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Melbourne. Compared with May: 2.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Melbourne in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in June?
In June, Melbourne averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Melbourne?
June is not one of Melbourne’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in June?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (66 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in June?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Melbourne in June?
About 13.2 °C (56 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (20.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Melbourne in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
