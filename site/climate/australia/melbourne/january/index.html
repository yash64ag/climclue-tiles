How January compares in Melbourne

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Melbourne. Compared with December: 1.6 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Melbourne in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in January?
In January, Melbourne averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Melbourne?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Melbourne’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in January?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (65 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in January?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
