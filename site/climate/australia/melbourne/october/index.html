How October compares in Melbourne

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Melbourne. Compared with September: 1.7 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. November is 2.1 °C warmer than October on average.

Melbourne in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Melbourne in October?
In October, Melbourne averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Melbourne?
October is not one of Melbourne’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Melbourne in October?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (65 mm), the driest January (41 mm).
Does it snow in Melbourne in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Melbourne get in October?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Melbourne in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
