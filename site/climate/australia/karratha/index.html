Karratha at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Australia, Karratha is the 4th warmest (25.9 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,628 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (285 mm a year). Compared with Port Hedland, the nearest covered town with different numbers (190 km away), Karratha is 0.5 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.6 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 8 km from Karratha measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Karratha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Karratha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.738°S, 116.846°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1047-2968), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
