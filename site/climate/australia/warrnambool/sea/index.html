Warrnambool sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Victoria · Australia

The sea off Warrnambool averages 17.7 °C (64 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 13.5 °C (56 °F) in September. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Warrnambool sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Warrnambool?
In February, at about 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Warrnambool?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is September, at 13.5 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Warrnambool in September?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Warrnambool?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Warrnambool nearby?
In February: Torquay (19.1 °C, 160 km), Geelong (19.1 °C, 166 km) and Melbourne (20.1 °C, 226 km).
