Warrnambool at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Australia, Warrnambool is 73rd warmest (13.9 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,228 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (798 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ballarat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (150 km away), Warrnambool is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 3.6 °C warmer in July, with 89 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (18.1 °C), averages 7.9 °C more than its coolest, July (10.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 88% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Warrnambool has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Warrnambool itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.382°S, 142.488°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1223-3224), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
