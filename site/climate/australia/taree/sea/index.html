Taree sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New South Wales · Australia

The sea off Taree averages 24.5 °C (76 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 19.1 °C (66 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March.

Taree sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Taree?
In February, at about 24.5 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Taree?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March. The coolest month is August, at 19.1 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Taree in August?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Taree?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Taree nearby?
In February: Coffs Harbour (25.5 °C, 190 km), Gold Coast (26.2 °C, 445 km) and Capalaba (26.6 °C, 491 km).
