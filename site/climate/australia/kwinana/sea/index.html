Kwinana sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Australia · Australia

The sea off Kwinana averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 18.6 °C (65 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to June.

Kwinana sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kwinana?
In March, at about 22.2 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kwinana?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to June. The coolest month is September, at 18.6 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kwinana in September?
About 18.6 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kwinana?
About 20.5 °C (69 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kwinana nearby?
In March: Geraldton (23.4 °C, 400 km).
