Cairns at a glance

Among the 21 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Australia, Cairns is the 3rd warmest (23.5 °C year average) and the wettest (2,083 mm a year). Compared with Townsville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (283 km away), Cairns is 1.5 °C cooler in January and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 989 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.3 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 6 km from Cairns measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Best time to visit Cairns

For mild weather, the best months to visit Cairns are June, July, August, September and October.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August, September and October.

Why Cairns has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
