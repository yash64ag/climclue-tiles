Cairns sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Queensland · Australia

The sea off Cairns averages 28.0 °C (82 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 22.2 °C (72 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to May.

Cairns sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cairns?
In February, at about 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cairns?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to May. The coolest month is July, at 22.2 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cairns in July?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Cairns?
About 25.3 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Cairns nearby?
In February: Port Moresby (28.9 °C, 842 km), Daru (29.3 °C, 915 km) and Popondetta (29.3 °C, 946 km).
