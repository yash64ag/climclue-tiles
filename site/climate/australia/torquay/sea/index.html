Torquay sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Victoria · Australia

The sea off Torquay averages 19.1 °C (66 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 12.4 °C (54 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Torquay sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Torquay?
In February, at about 19.1 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Torquay?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 12.4 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Torquay in August?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Torquay?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Torquay nearby?
In February: Frankston East (19.7 °C, 74 km), Melbourne (20.1 °C, 80 km) and Batemans Bay (22.2 °C, 595 km).
