Wollongong at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Australia, Wollongong is 44th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,436 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,165 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liverpool, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Wollongong is 1.0 °C cooler in February and 1.7 °C warmer in July, with 324 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, July (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 91% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Wollongong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Wollongong itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.424°S, 150.893°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1184-3308), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
