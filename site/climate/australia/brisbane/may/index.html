How May compares in Brisbane

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Brisbane. Compared with April: 2.8 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. June is 2.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Brisbane in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in May?
In May, Brisbane averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Brisbane?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Brisbane’s mild months (May, June, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in May?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (151 mm), the driest September (38 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in May?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brisbane in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
