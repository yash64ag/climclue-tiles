How October compares in Brisbane

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Brisbane. Compared with September: 2.1 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. November is 1.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Brisbane in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in October?
In October, Brisbane averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Brisbane?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Brisbane’s mild months (May, June, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in October?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (151 mm), the driest September (38 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in October?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brisbane in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
