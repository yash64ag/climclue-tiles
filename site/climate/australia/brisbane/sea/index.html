Brisbane sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Queensland · Australia

The sea off Brisbane averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 20.0 °C (68 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April.

Brisbane sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Brisbane?
In February, at about 26.6 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Brisbane?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 20.0 °C (68 °F).
How warm is the sea in Brisbane in August?
About 20.0 °C (68 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Brisbane?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Brisbane nearby?
In February: Hervey Bay (27.2 °C, 244 km).
