How September compares in Brisbane

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Brisbane. Compared with August: 2.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. October is 2.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Brisbane in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in September?
In September, Brisbane averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Brisbane?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Brisbane’s mild months (May, June, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in September?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (151 mm), the driest September (38 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in September?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brisbane in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
