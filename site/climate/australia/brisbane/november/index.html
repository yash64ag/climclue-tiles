How November compares in Brisbane

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Brisbane. Compared with October: 1.6 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. December is 1.8 °C warmer than November on average.

Brisbane in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in November?
In November, Brisbane averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Brisbane?
November is not one of Brisbane’s mild months; those are April, May, June, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in November?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (147 mm), the driest September (37 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in November?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Brisbane in November?
About 23.6 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (26.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Brisbane in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
