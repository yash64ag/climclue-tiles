How March compares in Brisbane

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Brisbane. Compared with February: 1.4 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. April is 2.2 °C cooler than March on average.

Brisbane in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in March?
In March, Brisbane averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Brisbane?
March is not one of Brisbane’s mild months; those are May, June, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in March?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (151 mm), the driest September (38 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brisbane in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
