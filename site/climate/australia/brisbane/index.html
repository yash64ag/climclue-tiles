Why Brisbane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Brisbane climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Brisbane?
January, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 21.5 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Brisbane?
July, with an average of 14.7 °C (58 °F), day highs around 19.4 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 10.6 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Brisbane?
February, with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,070 mm (42.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Brisbane?
September, with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Brisbane?
September, with about 266 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Brisbane?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Brisbane?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Brisbane have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Brisbane’s GeoNames location (27.468°S, 153.028°E; ClimaClue area sq-1114-3330), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,780,063 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
