Brisbane at a glance

Among the 21 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Australia, Brisbane is the 4th sunniest (about 2,833 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Logan City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Brisbane is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.6 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, July (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Best time to visit Brisbane

For mild weather, the best months to visit Brisbane are May, June, August, September and October.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, August, September and October.
  • Warmest (average temperature): January (24.6 °C), February (24.4 °C) and December (23.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): September (38 mm), August (40 mm) and July (41 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): September (266 h), December (262 h) and October (261 h).

Why Brisbane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
