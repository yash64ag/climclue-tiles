How December compares in Brisbane

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Brisbane. Compared with November: 1.8 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Brisbane in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in December?
In December, Brisbane averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Brisbane?
December is not one of Brisbane’s mild months; those are May, June, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in December?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (151 mm), the driest September (38 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in December?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brisbane in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
