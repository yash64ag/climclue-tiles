How July compares in Brisbane

July is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Brisbane. Compared with June: 0.7 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. August is 1.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Brisbane in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brisbane in July?
In July, Brisbane averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Brisbane?
July is not one of Brisbane’s mild months; those are May, June, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brisbane in July?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (151 mm), the driest September (38 mm).
Does it snow in Brisbane in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brisbane get in July?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brisbane in July?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
