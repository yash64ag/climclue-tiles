Toowoomba at a glance

Among the 21 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Australia, Toowoomba is 13th warmest (17.0 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,732 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (869 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Brisbane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Toowoomba is 2.2 °C cooler in January and 4.2 °C cooler in July, with 201 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.4 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, July (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 127 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Toowoomba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 127 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
