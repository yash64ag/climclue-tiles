Why Toowoomba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 127 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Toowoomba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Toowoomba?
January, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.0 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Toowoomba?
July, with an average of 10.4 °C (51 °F), day highs around 16.1 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 5.8 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Toowoomba?
December, with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 869 mm (34.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Toowoomba?
August, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Toowoomba?
December, with about 262 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Toowoomba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Toowoomba?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, March, April, September and October.
What type of climate does Toowoomba have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Toowoomba’s GeoNames location (27.561°S, 151.954°E; ClimaClue area sq-1115-3319), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 142,163 (GeoNames).
