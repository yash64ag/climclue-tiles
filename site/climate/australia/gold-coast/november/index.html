How November compares in Gold Coast

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Gold Coast. Compared with October: 1.7 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. December is 1.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Gold Coast in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gold Coast in November?
In November, Gold Coast averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Gold Coast?
November is not one of Gold Coast’s mild months; those are July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gold Coast in November?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (209 mm), the driest September (52 mm).
Does it snow in Gold Coast in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gold Coast get in November?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Gold Coast in November?
About 23.3 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (26.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Gold Coast in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
