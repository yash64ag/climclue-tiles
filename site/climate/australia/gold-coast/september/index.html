How September compares in Gold Coast

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Gold Coast. Compared with August: 1.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. October is 1.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Gold Coast in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gold Coast in September?
In September, Gold Coast averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Gold Coast?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Gold Coast’s mild months (July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gold Coast in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (205 mm), the driest September (50 mm).
Does it snow in Gold Coast in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gold Coast get in September?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gold Coast in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
