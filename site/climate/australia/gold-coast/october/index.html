How October compares in Gold Coast

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Gold Coast. Compared with September: 1.6 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is 1.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Gold Coast in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gold Coast in October?
In October, Gold Coast averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Gold Coast?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Gold Coast’s mild months (July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gold Coast in October?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (205 mm), the driest September (50 mm).
Does it snow in Gold Coast in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gold Coast get in October?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gold Coast in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
