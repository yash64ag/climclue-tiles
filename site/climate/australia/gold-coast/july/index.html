How July compares in Gold Coast

July is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Gold Coast. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C warmer than July on average.

Gold Coast in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gold Coast in July?
In July, Gold Coast averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Gold Coast?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Gold Coast’s mild months (July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gold Coast in July?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (205 mm), the driest September (50 mm).
Does it snow in Gold Coast in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gold Coast get in July?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gold Coast in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
