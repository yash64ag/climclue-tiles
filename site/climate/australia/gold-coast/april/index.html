How April compares in Gold Coast

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gold Coast. Compared with March: 1.8 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. May is 2.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Gold Coast in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gold Coast in April?
In April, Gold Coast averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gold Coast?
April is not one of Gold Coast’s mild months; those are July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gold Coast in April?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (205 mm), the driest September (50 mm).
Does it snow in Gold Coast in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gold Coast get in April?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gold Coast in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
