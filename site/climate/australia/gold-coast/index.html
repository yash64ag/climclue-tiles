Gold Coast at a glance

Among the 21 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Australia, Gold Coast is the 4th warmest (20.4 °C year average) and the 4th wettest (1,487 mm a year). Compared with Logan City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Gold Coast is 0.4 °C cooler in January and 1.6 °C warmer in July, with 438 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.1 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Gold Coast

For mild weather, the best months to visit Gold Coast are July, August, September and October.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August, September and October.
  • Warmest (average temperature): January (24.1 °C), February (24.1 °C) and December (23.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): September (50 mm), August (59 mm) and July (64 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): September (261 h), December (259 h) and October (257 h).

Why Gold Coast has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
