How December compares in Gold Coast

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Gold Coast. Compared with November: 1.6 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 0.9 °C warmer than December on average.

Gold Coast in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gold Coast in December?
In December, Gold Coast averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Gold Coast?
December is not one of Gold Coast’s mild months; those are July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gold Coast in December?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (205 mm), the driest September (50 mm).
Does it snow in Gold Coast in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gold Coast get in December?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gold Coast in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
