Why Hobart has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Hobart climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hobart?
February, with an average of 16.4 °C (61 °F), day highs around 21.1 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 12.0 °C (54 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hobart?
July, with an average of 7.8 °C (46 °F), day highs around 10.9 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 4.2 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hobart?
December, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 560 mm (22.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hobart?
May, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hobart?
January, with about 247 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hobart?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Hobart?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January and February.
What type of climate does Hobart have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.2 × 11 km, that contains Hobart’s GeoNames location (42.879°S, 147.329°E; ClimaClue area sq-1268-3273), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 254,930 (GeoNames).
