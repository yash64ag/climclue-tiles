Coffs Harbour sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New South Wales · Australia

The sea off Coffs Harbour averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 19.9 °C (68 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to April.

Coffs Harbour sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Coffs Harbour?
In February, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Coffs Harbour?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is August, at 19.9 °C (68 °F).
How warm is the sea in Coffs Harbour in August?
About 19.9 °C (68 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Coffs Harbour?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Coffs Harbour nearby?
In February: Gold Coast (26.2 °C, 257 km), Capalaba (26.6 °C, 306 km) and Brisbane (26.6 °C, 315 km).
