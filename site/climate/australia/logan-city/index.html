Logan City at a glance

Among the 21 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Australia, Logan City is 7th warmest (20.0 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,812 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (1,049 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Brisbane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Logan City is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.6 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, July (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Logan City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
