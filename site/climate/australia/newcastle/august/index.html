How August compares in Newcastle

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Newcastle. Compared with July: 0.8 °C warmer, 26 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. September is 2.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Newcastle in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Newcastle in August?
In August, Newcastle averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Newcastle?
August is not one of Newcastle’s mild months; those are January, September, October and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Newcastle in August?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (155 mm), the driest August (61 mm).
Does it snow in Newcastle in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Newcastle get in August?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Newcastle in August?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (22.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Newcastle in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
