Newcastle sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New South Wales · Australia

The sea off Newcastle averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.6 °C (64 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to May.

Newcastle sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Newcastle?
In February, at about 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Newcastle?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to May. The coolest month is August, at 17.6 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Newcastle in August?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Newcastle?
About 20.2 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Newcastle nearby?
In February: Taree (24.5 °C, 130 km), Port Macquarie (24.9 °C, 198 km) and Coffs Harbour (25.5 °C, 319 km).
