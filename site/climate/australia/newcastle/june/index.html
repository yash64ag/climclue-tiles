How June compares in Newcastle

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Newcastle. Compared with May: 2.3 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. July is 0.9 °C cooler than June on average.

Newcastle in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Newcastle in June?
In June, Newcastle averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Newcastle?
June is not one of Newcastle’s mild months; those are January, September, October and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Newcastle in June?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (160 mm), the driest August (65 mm).
Does it snow in Newcastle in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Newcastle get in June?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Newcastle in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
