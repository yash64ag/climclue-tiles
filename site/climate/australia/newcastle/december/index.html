How December compares in Newcastle

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Newcastle. Compared with November: 1.9 °C warmer, 24 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Newcastle in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Newcastle in December?
In December, Newcastle averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Newcastle?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Newcastle’s mild months (January, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Newcastle in December?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (155 mm), the driest August (61 mm).
Does it snow in Newcastle in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Newcastle get in December?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Newcastle in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
