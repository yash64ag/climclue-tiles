How February compares in Newcastle

February is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Newcastle. Compared with January: about as warm, 79 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. March is 1.0 °C cooler than February on average.

Newcastle in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Newcastle in February?
In February, Newcastle averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Newcastle?
February is not one of Newcastle’s mild months; those are January, September, October and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Newcastle in February?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (160 mm), the driest August (65 mm).
Does it snow in Newcastle in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Newcastle get in February?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Newcastle in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
