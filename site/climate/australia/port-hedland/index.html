Port Hedland at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Australia, Port Hedland is the 3rd warmest (26.4 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 3,620 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Karratha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (190 km away), Port Hedland is 0.5 °C warmer in March and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (30.1 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, July (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.2 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Port Hedland has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Port Hedland itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.312°S, 118.611°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1043-2986), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
