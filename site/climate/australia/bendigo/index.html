Why Bendigo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 151 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Bendigo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bendigo?
February, with an average of 21.1 °C (70 °F), day highs around 27.9 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 15.0 °C (59 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bendigo?
July, with an average of 7.5 °C (46 °F), day highs around 11.6 °C (53 °F) and night lows around 4.3 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bendigo?
July, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 529 mm (20.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bendigo?
February, with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bendigo?
January, with about 275 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bendigo?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bendigo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.
What type of climate does Bendigo have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Bendigo’s GeoNames location (36.758°S, 144.280°E; ClimaClue area sq-1207-3242), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 103,034 (GeoNames).
