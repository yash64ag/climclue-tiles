How June compares in Perth

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Perth. Compared with May: 2.3 °C cooler, 59 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. July is 1.0 °C cooler than June on average.

Perth in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perth in June?
In June, Perth averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Perth?
June is not one of Perth’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perth in June?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (160 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Perth in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perth get in June?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perth in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
