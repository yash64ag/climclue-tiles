How September compares in Perth

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Perth. Compared with August: 1.3 °C warmer, 51 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. October is 1.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Perth in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perth in September?
In September, Perth averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Perth?
September is not one of Perth’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perth in September?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (162 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Perth in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perth get in September?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perth in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
