How December compares in Perth

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Perth. Compared with November: 2.1 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. January is 2.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Perth in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perth in December?
In December, Perth averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 336 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Perth?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Perth’s mild months (January, February, March, April, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perth in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (162 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Perth in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perth get in December?
About 336 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Perth in December?
About 20.6 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Perth in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
