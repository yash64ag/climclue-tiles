Why Perth has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Perth climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Perth?
February, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 30.4 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 18.8 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Perth?
July, with an average of 13.3 °C (56 °F), day highs around 16.6 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 9.8 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Perth?
June, with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 777 mm (30.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Perth?
December, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Perth?
January, with about 346 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Perth?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Perth?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Perth have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Perth’s GeoNames location (31.952°S, 115.861°E; ClimaClue area sq-1159-2958), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,384,371 (GeoNames).
