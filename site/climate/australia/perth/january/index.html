Perth weather in January

Average January weather in Perth, Australia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Perth averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 17.6 °C (64 °F) nights, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of rain over the month and about 346 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Perth

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Perth. Compared with December: 2.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Perth in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perth in January?
In January, Perth averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 346 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Perth?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Perth’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perth in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (162 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Perth in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perth get in January?
About 346 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Perth in January?
About 21.5 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Perth in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
