How March compares in Perth

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Perth. Compared with February: 1.6 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. April is 3.1 °C cooler than March on average.

Perth in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perth in March?
In March, Perth averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Perth?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Perth’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perth in March?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (160 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Perth in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perth get in March?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perth in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
