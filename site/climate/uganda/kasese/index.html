Kasese at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Kasese is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,044 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,811 mm a year). Compared with Ibanda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Kasese is 2.2 °C warmer in February and 2.5 °C warmer in November, with 803 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.3 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, November (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in November. The area lies about 1,168 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kasese, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kasese has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,168 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
