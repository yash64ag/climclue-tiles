Why Hoima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,099 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hoima climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hoima?
February, with an average of 23.3 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 18.4 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hoima?
July, with an average of 21.1 °C (70 °F), day highs around 25.4 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 16.9 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hoima?
October, with 239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,737 mm (68.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hoima?
January, with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hoima?
December, with about 214 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hoima?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Hoima?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, June and December.
What type of climate does Hoima have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Hoima’s GeoNames location (1.433°N, 31.352°E; ClimaClue area sq-825-2113), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 122,700 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
