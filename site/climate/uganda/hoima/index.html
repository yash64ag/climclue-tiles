Hoima at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Hoima is the 3rd wettest (1,737 mm a year). Compared with Masindi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Hoima is 1.4 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 417 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.3 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,099 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hoima, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hoima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,099 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
