Njeru at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Njeru is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,575 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Lugazi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Njeru is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.6 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 870 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Njeru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Njeru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 870 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
