Ishaka at a glance

Among the 143 towns ClimaClue covers in Uganda, Ishaka is 137th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 137th sunniest (about 1,853 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (1,292 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kyamahungu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Ishaka is 0.5 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in November, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (19.8 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, November (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in December. The area lies about 1,123 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ishaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ishaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,123 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ishaka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (0.543°S, 30.137°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-845-2101), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
