Gulu at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Gulu is the 2nd warmest (23.5 °C year average), the 4th sunniest (about 2,575 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (1,629 mm a year). Compared with Lira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Gulu is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 171 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.3 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, July (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,082 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gulu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,082 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
