Why Gulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,082 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Gulu climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Gulu?
February, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 32.0 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 20.6 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Gulu?
July, with an average of 21.8 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Gulu?
August, with 235 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,629 mm (64.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Gulu?
January, with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Gulu?
December, with about 246 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Gulu?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Gulu?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: December.
What type of climate does Gulu have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Gulu’s GeoNames location (2.775°N, 32.299°E; ClimaClue area sq-812-2122), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 177,400 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
