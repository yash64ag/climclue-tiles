Why Mityana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 983 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Mityana climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mityana?
February, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.7 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 16.9 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mityana?
July, with an average of 19.9 °C (68 °F), day highs around 24.3 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 15.7 °C (60 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mityana?
April, with 188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,430 mm (56.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mityana?
January, with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mityana?
December, with about 211 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mityana?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mityana?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, June and July.
What type of climate does Mityana have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Mityana’s GeoNames location (0.418°N, 32.023°E; ClimaClue area sq-835-2120), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 105,200 (GeoNames).
