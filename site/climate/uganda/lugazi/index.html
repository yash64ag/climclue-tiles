Lugazi at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Lugazi is the 5th sunniest (about 2,530 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mukono, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Lugazi is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 128 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.4 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, July (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 886 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lugazi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Lugazi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lugazi are January, February, June and July.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, June and July.

Why Lugazi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 886 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
