Kasangati at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Kasangati is the 3rd driest (1,279 mm a year). Compared with Nansana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Kasangati is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 116 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.1 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, July (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.4 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 921 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kasangati, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kasangati has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 921 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
