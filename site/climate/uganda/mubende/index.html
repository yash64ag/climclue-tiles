Why Mubende has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,059 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Mubende climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mubende?
February, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 27.2 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 16.5 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mubende?
July, with an average of 20.1 °C (68 °F), day highs around 24.9 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 15.4 °C (60 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mubende?
October, with 184 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,285 mm (50.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mubende?
January, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mubende?
June, with about 200 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mubende?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mubende?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, June, July and December.
What type of climate does Mubende have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Mubende’s GeoNames location (0.558°N, 31.395°E; ClimaClue area sq-834-2113), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 121,600 (GeoNames).
