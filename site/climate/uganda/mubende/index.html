Mubende at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Mubende is the 2nd coolest (20.5 °C year average), the 4th least sunny (about 2,161 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (1,285 mm a year). Compared with Mityana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Mubende is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 145 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.6 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.4 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,059 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mubende, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mubende has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,059 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
