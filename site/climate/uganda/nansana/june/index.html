How June compares in Nansana

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nansana. Compared with May: 0.4 °C cooler, 79 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Nansana in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nansana in June?
In June, Nansana averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nansana?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Nansana’s mild months (January, February, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nansana in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Nansana in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nansana get in June?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nansana in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
