How March compares in Nansana

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nansana. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 81 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. April is 0.5 °C cooler than March on average.

Nansana in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nansana in March?
In March, Nansana averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nansana?
March is not one of Nansana’s mild months; those are January, February, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nansana in March?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (183 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Nansana in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nansana get in March?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nansana in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
