How September compares in Nansana

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nansana. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Nansana in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nansana in September?
In September, Nansana averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nansana?
September is not one of Nansana’s mild months; those are January, February, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nansana in September?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (183 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Nansana in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nansana get in September?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nansana in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
