How December compares in Nansana

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Nansana. Compared with November: 0.3 °C warmer, 47 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Nansana in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nansana in December?
In December, Nansana averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nansana?
December is not one of Nansana’s mild months; those are January, February, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nansana in December?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (183 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Nansana in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nansana get in December?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nansana in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
