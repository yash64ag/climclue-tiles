Kebisoni at a glance

Among the 143 towns ClimaClue covers in Uganda, Kebisoni is 117th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 1,896 hours of sunshine a year) and 109th wettest (1,130 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nyamunuka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kebisoni is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in November, with 135 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.1 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, November (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in December. The area lies about 1,114 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kebisoni, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kebisoni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,114 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kebisoni itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (0.921°S, 30.037°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-849-2100), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
