How April compares in Kampala

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Kampala. Compared with March: 0.5 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 0.2 °C cooler than April on average.

Kampala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in April?
In April, Kampala averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kampala?
April is not one of Kampala’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in April?
180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in April?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
