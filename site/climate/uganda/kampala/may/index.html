Kampala weather in May

Average May weather in Kampala, Uganda: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Kampala averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) days and 16.4 °C (62 °F) nights, with 154 mm (6.1 in) of rain over the month and about 202 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Kampala

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kampala. Compared with April: 0.2 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. June is 0.3 °C cooler than May on average.

What to pack for Kampala in May

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Kampala in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in May?
In May, Kampala averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kampala?
May is not one of Kampala’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in May?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in May?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
