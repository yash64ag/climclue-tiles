How December compares in Kampala

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kampala. Compared with November: 0.3 °C warmer, 47 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Kampala in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in December?
In December, Kampala averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kampala?
December is not one of Kampala’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in December?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in December?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
