Kampala weather in September

Average September weather in Kampala, Uganda: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Kampala averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) days and 15.4 °C (60 °F) nights, with 120 mm (4.7 in) of rain over the month and about 207 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Kampala

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kampala. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Kampala in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in September?
In September, Kampala averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kampala?
September is not one of Kampala’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in September?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in September?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
