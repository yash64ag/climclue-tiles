How January compares in Kampala

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kampala. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 35 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Kampala in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in January?
In January, Kampala averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kampala?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kampala’s mild months (January, February, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in January?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in January?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
