How February compares in Kampala

February is the warmest month, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kampala. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Kampala in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in February?
In February, Kampala averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kampala?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Kampala’s mild months (January, February, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in February?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in February?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
