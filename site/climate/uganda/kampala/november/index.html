How November compares in Kampala

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kampala. Compared with October: about as warm, about the same precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. December is 0.3 °C warmer than November on average.

Kampala in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampala in November?
In November, Kampala averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kampala?
November is not one of Kampala’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampala in November?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (180 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Kampala in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampala get in November?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampala in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
