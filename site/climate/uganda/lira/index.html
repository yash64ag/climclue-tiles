Lira at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Lira is the warmest (23.5 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 2,661 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Gulu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Lira is 0.5 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 171 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.8 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 987 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lira, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 987 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
