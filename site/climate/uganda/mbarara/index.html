Mbarara at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uganda, Mbarara is the 4th coolest (20.6 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,854 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (994 mm a year). Compared with Ibanda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Mbarara is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.6 °C warmer in November, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.6 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, November (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,068 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mbarara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mbarara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,068 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
