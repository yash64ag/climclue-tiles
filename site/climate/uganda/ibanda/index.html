Ibanda at a glance

Among the 143 towns ClimaClue covers in Uganda, Ibanda is 125th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 131st sunniest (about 1,889 hours of sunshine a year) and 130th wettest (984 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kasozi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Ibanda is 1.4 °C cooler in February and 1.1 °C cooler in November, with 272 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.3 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, November (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in November. The area lies about 1,113 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ibanda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ibanda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,113 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ibanda itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (0.134°S, 30.496°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-841-2104), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
