Port Moresby at a glance

Its warmest month, January (26.9 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 124% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Port Moresby has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
