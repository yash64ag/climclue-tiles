Arawa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bougainville · Papua New Guinea

The sea off Arawa averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 28.0 °C (82 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Arawa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Arawa?
In January, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Arawa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 28.0 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Arawa in August?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Arawa?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Arawa nearby?
In January: Kokopo (30.0 °C, 421 km) and Lae (30.0 °C, 948 km).
