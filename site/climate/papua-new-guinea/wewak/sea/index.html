Wewak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · East Sepik Province · Papua New Guinea

The sea off Wewak averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 28.1 °C (83 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Wewak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Wewak?
In May, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Wewak?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 28.1 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Wewak in August?
About 28.1 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Wewak?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Wewak nearby?
In May: Madang (29.9 °C, 303 km).
