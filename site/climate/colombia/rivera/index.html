Rivera at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, Rivera is 158th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 253rd sunniest (about 1,607 hours of sunshine a year) and 169th wettest (1,868 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campoalegre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Rivera is 0.8 °C cooler in September and 1.1 °C cooler in November, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (25.9 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, November (24.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 248 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rivera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rivera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 248 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C. The 10 km area around Rivera, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.3 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rivera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (2.777°N, 75.256°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-812-1047), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
