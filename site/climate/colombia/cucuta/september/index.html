How September compares in Cúcuta

September is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 63 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. October is 1.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Cúcuta in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in September?
In September, Cúcuta averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in September?
173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in September?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
