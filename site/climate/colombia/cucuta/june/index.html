How June compares in Cúcuta

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with May: 0.8 °C warmer, 81 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Cúcuta in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in June?
In June, Cúcuta averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in June?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in June?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
