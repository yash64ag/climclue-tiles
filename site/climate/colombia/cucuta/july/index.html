How July compares in Cúcuta

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with June: 0.1 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Cúcuta in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in July?
In July, Cúcuta averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in July?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in July?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
