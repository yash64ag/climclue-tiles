How December compares in Cúcuta

December is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with November: 0.6 °C cooler, 89 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Cúcuta in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in December?
In December, Cúcuta averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in December?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in December?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
