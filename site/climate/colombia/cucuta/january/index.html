How January compares in Cúcuta

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 57 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Cúcuta in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in January?
In January, Cúcuta averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in January?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in January?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
