How March compares in Cúcuta

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with February: 0.3 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. April is 0.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Cúcuta in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in March?
In March, Cúcuta averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in March?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in March?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
