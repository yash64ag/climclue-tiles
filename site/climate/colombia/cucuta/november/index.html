How November compares in Cúcuta

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with October: 1.2 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. December is 0.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Cúcuta in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in November?
In November, Cúcuta averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in November?
242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (260 mm), the driest June (86 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in November?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
