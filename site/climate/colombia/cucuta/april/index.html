How April compares in Cúcuta

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cúcuta. Compared with March: 0.2 °C warmer, 71 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. May is 0.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Cúcuta in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in April?
In April, Cúcuta averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in April?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (232 mm), the driest June (81 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in April?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cúcuta in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
