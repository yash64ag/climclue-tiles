How May compares in Cúcuta

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cúcuta. Compared with April: 0.8 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 0.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Cúcuta in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cúcuta in May?
In May, Cúcuta averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cúcuta?
No month in Cúcuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cúcuta in May?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (232 mm), the driest June (81 mm).
Does it snow in Cúcuta in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cúcuta get in May?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
