How December compares in Cali

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cali. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 58 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Cali in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cali in December?
In December, Cali averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cali?
December is not one of Cali’s mild months; those are January, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cali in December?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (187 mm), the driest July (43 mm).
Does it snow in Cali in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cali get in December?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cali in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
