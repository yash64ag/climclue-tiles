How June compares in Cali

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cali. Compared with May: 0.1 °C cooler, 68 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Cali in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cali in June?
In June, Cali averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cali?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Cali’s mild months (January, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cali in June?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (187 mm), the driest July (43 mm).
Does it snow in Cali in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cali get in June?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cali in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
