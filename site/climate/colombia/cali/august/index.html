Cali weather in August

Average August weather in Cali, Colombia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Cali averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) days and 16.5 °C (62 °F) nights, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Cali

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Cali. Compared with July: about as warm, 4 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Cali in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cali in August?
In August, Cali averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cali?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Cali’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cali in August?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (177 mm), the driest July (36 mm).
Does it snow in Cali in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cali get in August?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cali in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
