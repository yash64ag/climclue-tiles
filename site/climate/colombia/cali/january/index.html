How January compares in Cali

January is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cali. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 37 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Cali in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cali in January?
In January, Cali averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cali?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Cali’s mild months (January, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cali in January?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (187 mm), the driest July (43 mm).
Does it snow in Cali in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cali get in January?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cali in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
