Cali at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Cali is 28th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 1,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,365 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Palmira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Cali is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 1.0 °C cooler in October, with 474 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.3 °C), averages 0.7 °C more than its coolest, October (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 85 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 20 km from Cali measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.7 °C (6.7 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Best time to visit Cali

For mild weather, the best months to visit Cali are January, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, June, July, August and September.

Why Cali has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 85 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
