Soledad at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Soledad is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (849 mm a year). Compared with Malambo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Soledad is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.6 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, January (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 25.2 °C (77 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Soledad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Soledad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
