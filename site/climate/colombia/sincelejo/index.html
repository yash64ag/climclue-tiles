Sincelejo at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Sincelejo is 14th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,037 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (1,375 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Magangué, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Sincelejo is 1.4 °C cooler in March and 1.3 °C cooler in October, with 259 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.6 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, October (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in December. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sincelejo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sincelejo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
