How August compares in Barranquilla

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Barranquilla. Compared with July: about as warm, 33 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Barranquilla in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barranquilla in August?
In August, Barranquilla averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Barranquilla?
No month in Barranquilla is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barranquilla in August?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (157 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Barranquilla in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barranquilla get in August?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barranquilla in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
