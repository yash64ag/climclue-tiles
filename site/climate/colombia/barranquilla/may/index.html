Barranquilla weather in May

Average May weather in Barranquilla, Colombia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Barranquilla averages 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 23.4 °C (74 °F) nights, with 103 mm (4.0 in) of rain over the month and about 187 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Barranquilla

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Barranquilla. Compared with April: 0.3 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. June is 0.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Barranquilla in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barranquilla in May?
In May, Barranquilla averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Barranquilla?
No month in Barranquilla is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barranquilla in May?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (158 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Barranquilla in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barranquilla get in May?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barranquilla in May?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (28.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
