Barranquilla sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Atlántico · Colombia

The sea off Barranquilla averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 25.8 °C (78 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Barranquilla sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Barranquilla?
In September, at about 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Barranquilla?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.8 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Barranquilla in February?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Barranquilla?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Barranquilla nearby?
In September: San Onofre (29.5 °C, 159 km), Santiago de Tolú (29.5 °C, 183 km) and Tolú Viejo (29.5 °C, 184 km).
