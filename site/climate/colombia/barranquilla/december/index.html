How December compares in Barranquilla

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Barranquilla. Compared with November: 0.2 °C cooler, 68 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Barranquilla in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barranquilla in December?
In December, Barranquilla averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Barranquilla?
No month in Barranquilla is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barranquilla in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (157 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Barranquilla in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barranquilla get in December?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barranquilla in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
