How February compares in Barranquilla

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Barranquilla. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Barranquilla in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barranquilla in February?
In February, Barranquilla averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Barranquilla?
No month in Barranquilla is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barranquilla in February?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (157 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Barranquilla in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barranquilla get in February?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barranquilla in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
