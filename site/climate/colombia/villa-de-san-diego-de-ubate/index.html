Villa de San Diego de Ubaté at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, Villa de San Diego de Ubaté is 311th warmest (13.4 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 1,903 hours of sunshine a year) and 280th wettest (1,187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Mesa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Villa de San Diego de Ubaté is 2.0 °C warmer in April and 1.6 °C warmer in July, with 789 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (13.9 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, July (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 390 km from the sea.

Why Villa de San Diego de Ubaté has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 390 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C. The 10 km area around Villa de San Diego de Ubaté, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 11.7 °C a year, 1.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
