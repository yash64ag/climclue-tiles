How June compares in Bucaramanga

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bucaramanga. Compared with May: 0.3 °C cooler, 90 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Bucaramanga in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucaramanga in June?
In June, Bucaramanga averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bucaramanga?
June is not one of Bucaramanga’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucaramanga in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (233 mm), the driest January (79 mm).
Does it snow in Bucaramanga in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucaramanga get in June?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucaramanga in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
