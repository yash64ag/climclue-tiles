How July compares in Bucaramanga

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bucaramanga. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Bucaramanga in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucaramanga in July?
In July, Bucaramanga averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bucaramanga?
July is not one of Bucaramanga’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucaramanga in July?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (255 mm), the driest January (87 mm).
Does it snow in Bucaramanga in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucaramanga get in July?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucaramanga in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
