How November compares in Bucaramanga

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bucaramanga. Compared with October: 0.1 °C warmer, 50 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Bucaramanga in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucaramanga in November?
In November, Bucaramanga averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bucaramanga?
November is not one of Bucaramanga’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucaramanga in November?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (255 mm), the driest January (87 mm).
Does it snow in Bucaramanga in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucaramanga get in November?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucaramanga in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
