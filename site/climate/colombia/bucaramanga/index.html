Why Bucaramanga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 277 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Bucaramanga climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bucaramanga?
April, with an average of 22.8 °C (73 °F), day highs around 25.7 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 19.5 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bucaramanga?
January, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 25.6 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 18.4 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bucaramanga?
October, with 255 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,922 mm (75.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bucaramanga?
January, with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bucaramanga?
January, with about 196 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bucaramanga?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bucaramanga?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January.
What type of climate does Bucaramanga have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Bucaramanga’s GeoNames location (7.125°N, 73.119°W; ClimaClue area sq-768-1068), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 581,130 (GeoNames).
