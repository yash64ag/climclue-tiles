How August compares in Bucaramanga

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bucaramanga. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. September is about as warm as August on average.

Bucaramanga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucaramanga in August?
In August, Bucaramanga averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bucaramanga?
August is not one of Bucaramanga’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucaramanga in August?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (255 mm), the driest January (87 mm).
Does it snow in Bucaramanga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucaramanga get in August?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucaramanga in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
