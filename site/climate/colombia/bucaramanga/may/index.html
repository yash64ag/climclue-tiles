How May compares in Bucaramanga

May is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bucaramanga. Compared with April: about as warm, 4 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. June is 0.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Bucaramanga in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucaramanga in May?
In May, Bucaramanga averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bucaramanga?
May is not one of Bucaramanga’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucaramanga in May?
215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (255 mm), the driest January (87 mm).
Does it snow in Bucaramanga in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucaramanga get in May?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucaramanga in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
