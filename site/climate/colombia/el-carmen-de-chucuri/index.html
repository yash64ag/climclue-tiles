El Carmen de Chucurí at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, El Carmen de Chucurí is 174th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,042 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (4,070 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Socorro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), El Carmen de Chucurí is 2.4 °C warmer in March and 2.1 °C warmer in October, with 2,058 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.4 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, October (23.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.3 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 348 km from the sea.

Why El Carmen de Chucurí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 348 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
