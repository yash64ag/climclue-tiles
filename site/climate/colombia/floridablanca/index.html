Floridablanca at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Floridablanca is 34th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 1,765 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (1,572 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bucaramanga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Floridablanca is 3.1 °C cooler in April and 3.1 °C cooler in January, with 351 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (19.6 °C), averages 0.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 280 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Floridablanca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Floridablanca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 280 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 11.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
