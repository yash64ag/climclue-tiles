Why Mosquera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 351 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Mosquera climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mosquera?
August, with an average of 15.6 °C (60 °F), day highs around 20.5 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 11.8 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mosquera?
January, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 17.9 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 10.3 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mosquera?
November, with 207 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,505 mm (59.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mosquera?
August, with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mosquera?
January, with about 175 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mosquera?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mosquera?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.
What type of climate does Mosquera have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Mosquera’s GeoNames location (4.706°N, 74.230°W; ClimaClue area sq-792-1057), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 128,012 (GeoNames).
