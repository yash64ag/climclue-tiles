Popayán at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Popayán is 37th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 1,639 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (2,476 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pitalito, the nearest covered town with different numbers (90 km away), Popayán is about as warm in August and 2.2 °C cooler in November, with 721 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.6 °C), averages 0.5 °C more than its coolest, November (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 136 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Popayán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Popayán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 136 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
