Why Palmira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 113 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Palmira climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Palmira?
April, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 24.6 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Palmira?
July, with an average of 21.4 °C (70 °F), day highs around 24.4 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 16.5 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Palmira?
April, with 251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,839 mm (72.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Palmira?
August, with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Palmira?
January, with about 176 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Palmira?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Palmira?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Palmira’s GeoNames location (3.535°N, 76.298°W; ClimaClue area sq-804-1037), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 312,519 (GeoNames).
