Ocaña at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Ocaña is 31st warmest (20.4 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,038 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (1,856 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cúcuta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Ocaña is 5.9 °C cooler in March and 7.9 °C cooler in October, with 91 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (21.1 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, October (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 212 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ocaña, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ocaña has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 212 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
