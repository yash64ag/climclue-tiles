How August compares in Cartagena

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cartagena. Compared with July: about as warm, 7 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Cartagena in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cartagena in August?
In August, Cartagena averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cartagena?
No month in Cartagena is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cartagena in August?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (214 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Cartagena in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cartagena get in August?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cartagena in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
