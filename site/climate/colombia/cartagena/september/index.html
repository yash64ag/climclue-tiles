How September compares in Cartagena

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Cartagena. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 0.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Cartagena in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cartagena in September?
In September, Cartagena averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cartagena?
No month in Cartagena is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cartagena in September?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (206 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Cartagena in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cartagena get in September?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cartagena in September?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cartagena in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
