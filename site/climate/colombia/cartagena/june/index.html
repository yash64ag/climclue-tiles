How June compares in Cartagena

June is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cartagena. Compared with May: 0.1 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Cartagena in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cartagena in June?
In June, Cartagena averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cartagena?
No month in Cartagena is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cartagena in June?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (206 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Cartagena in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cartagena get in June?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cartagena in June?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cartagena in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
