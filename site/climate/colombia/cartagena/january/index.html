Cartagena weather in January

Average January weather in Cartagena, Colombia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Cartagena averages 32.0 °C (90 °F) days and 22.5 °C (73 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 252 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Cartagena

January is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Cartagena. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 43 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Cartagena in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cartagena in January?
In January, Cartagena averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cartagena?
No month in Cartagena is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cartagena in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (206 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Cartagena in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cartagena get in January?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cartagena in January?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cartagena in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
