Riohacha sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · La Guajira Department · Colombia

The sea off Riohacha averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) in October, its warmest month, and 25.1 °C (77 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Riohacha sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Riohacha?
In October, at about 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Riohacha?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.1 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Riohacha in February?
About 25.1 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Riohacha?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Riohacha nearby?
In October: Concepción (30.2 °C, 183 km), Santa Rita (29.9 °C, 189 km) and Cabimas (29.9 °C, 204 km).
