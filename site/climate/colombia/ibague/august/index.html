Ibagué weather in August

Average August weather in Ibagué, Colombia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Ibagué averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) days and 17.4 °C (63 °F) nights, with 120 mm (4.7 in) of rain over the month and about 163 hours of sunshine.

Ibagué in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibagué in August?
In August, Ibagué averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ibagué?
August is not one of Ibagué’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibagué in August?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (257 mm), the driest January (94 mm).
Does it snow in Ibagué in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibagué get in August?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibagué in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
