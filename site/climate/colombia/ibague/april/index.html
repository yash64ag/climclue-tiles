How April compares in Ibagué

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Ibagué. Compared with March: 0.1 °C cooler, 88 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Ibagué in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibagué in April?
In April, Ibagué averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ibagué?
No month in Ibagué is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibagué in April?
270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (270 mm), the driest January (103 mm).
Does it snow in Ibagué in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibagué get in April?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
