Ibagué in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibagué in March?
In March, Ibagué averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ibagué?
No month in Ibagué is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibagué in March?
182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (270 mm), the driest January (103 mm).
Does it snow in Ibagué in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibagué get in March?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibagué in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
