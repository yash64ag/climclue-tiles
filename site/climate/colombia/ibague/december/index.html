How December compares in Ibagué

December is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ibagué. Compared with November: about as warm, 78 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Ibagué in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibagué in December?
In December, Ibagué averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ibagué?
No month in Ibagué is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibagué in December?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (270 mm), the driest January (103 mm).
Does it snow in Ibagué in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibagué get in December?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibagué in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
