Yopal at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Yopal is 17th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 1,863 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (3,202 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sogamoso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Yopal is 13.5 °C warmer in February and 10.7 °C warmer in July, with 2,165 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.8 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 430 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yopal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yopal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 430 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
