Yopal at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Yopal is 18th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 1,863 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (3,544 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sogamoso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Yopal is 13.1 °C warmer in February and 10.3 °C warmer in July, with 2,588 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.6 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 430 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yopal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yopal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 430 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yopal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (5.336°N, 72.394°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-786-1076), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
