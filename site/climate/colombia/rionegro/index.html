Rionegro at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Rionegro is 42nd warmest (16.2 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 1,878 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (2,652 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Medellín, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Rionegro is 1.3 °C cooler in August and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 636 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (16.6 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.5 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 224 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rionegro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rionegro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 224 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
