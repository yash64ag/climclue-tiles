Why Manizales has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 210 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

April, with an average of 18.9 °C (66 °F), day highs around 22.5 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 15.1 °C (59 °F).
What is the coldest month in Manizales?
July, with an average of 18.1 °C (65 °F), day highs around 22.3 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 13.8 °C (57 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Manizales?

What is the driest month in Manizales?
July, with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Manizales?
July, with about 184 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Manizales?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Manizales?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Manizales’s GeoNames location (5.067°N, 75.507°W; ClimaClue area sq-789-1044), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 434,403 (GeoNames).
