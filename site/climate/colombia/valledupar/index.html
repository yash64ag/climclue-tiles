Valledupar at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Valledupar is the sunniest (about 2,533 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Riohacha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (126 km away), Valledupar is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 682 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 101 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valledupar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valledupar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 101 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
