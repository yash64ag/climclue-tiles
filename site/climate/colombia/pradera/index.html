Pradera at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, Pradera is 207th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 199th sunniest (about 1,751 hours of sunshine a year) and 182nd wettest (1,785 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Florida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Pradera is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.4 °C), averages 0.7 °C more than its coolest, August (21.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in August. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 21 km from Pradera measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Pradera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pradera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.420°N, 76.242°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-805-1037), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
