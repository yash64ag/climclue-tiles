San Martín, Cesar Department at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, San Martín, Cesar Department is 120th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,089 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (2,567 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ocaña, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), San Martín, Cesar Department is 5.7 °C warmer in February and 5.4 °C warmer in September, with 552 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.1 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, September (26.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in August. The area lies about 242 km from the sea.

Why San Martín, Cesar Department has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 242 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Martín itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.002°N, 73.511°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-759-1064), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
