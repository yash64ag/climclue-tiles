Magangué at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Magangué is the warmest (27.9 °C year average). Compared with Sincelejo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Magangué is 1.4 °C warmer in March and 1.3 °C warmer in October, with 259 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.1 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, October (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in December. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Magangué, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Magangué has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
