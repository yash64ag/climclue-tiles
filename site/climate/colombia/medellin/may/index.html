How May compares in Medellín

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Medellín. Compared with April: about as warm, 44 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. June is 0.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Medellín in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medellín in May?
In May, Medellín averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 223 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Medellín?
May is not one of Medellín’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medellín in May?
223 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (223 mm), the driest January (56 mm).
Does it snow in Medellín in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medellín get in May?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medellín in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
