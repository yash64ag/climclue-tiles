How January compares in Medellín

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Medellín. Compared with December: 0.1 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Medellín in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medellín in January?
In January, Medellín averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Medellín?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Medellín’s mild months (January and February), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medellín in January?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (254 mm), the driest January (63 mm).
Does it snow in Medellín in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medellín get in January?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medellín in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
