How August compares in Medellín

August is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Medellín. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Medellín in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medellín in August?
In August, Medellín averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Medellín?
August is not one of Medellín’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medellín in August?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (223 mm), the driest January (56 mm).
Does it snow in Medellín in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medellín get in August?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medellín in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
