How February compares in Medellín

February is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Medellín. Compared with January: 0.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Medellín in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medellín in February?
In February, Medellín averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Medellín?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Medellín’s mild months (January and February), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medellín in February?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (254 mm), the driest January (63 mm).
Does it snow in Medellín in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medellín get in February?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medellín in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
