How December compares in Medellín

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Medellín. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 81 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Medellín in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medellín in December?
In December, Medellín averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Medellín?
December is not one of Medellín’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medellín in December?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (254 mm), the driest January (63 mm).
Does it snow in Medellín in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medellín get in December?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medellín in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
