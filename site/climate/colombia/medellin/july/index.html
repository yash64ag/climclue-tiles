How July compares in Medellín

July is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Medellín. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Medellín in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medellín in July?
In July, Medellín averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Medellín?
July is not one of Medellín’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medellín in July?
178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (254 mm), the driest January (63 mm).
Does it snow in Medellín in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medellín get in July?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medellín in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
