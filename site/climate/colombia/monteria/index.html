Montería at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Montería is 8th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 1,886 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (1,547 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sincelejo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Montería is 0.7 °C warmer in March and 0.7 °C warmer in October, with 172 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.4 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, October (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in October. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Montería, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Montería has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
