How April compares in Soacha

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Soacha. Compared with March: 0.4 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Soacha in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in April?
In April, Soacha averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Soacha?
No month in Soacha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in April?
136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (159 mm), the driest September (61 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in April?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
