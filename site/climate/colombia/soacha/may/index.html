How May compares in Soacha

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Soacha. Compared with April: 0.2 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. June is 0.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Soacha in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in May?
In May, Soacha averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Soacha?
May is not one of Soacha’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in May?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (182 mm), the driest September (74 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in May?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
