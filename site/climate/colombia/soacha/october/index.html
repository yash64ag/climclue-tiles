How October compares in Soacha

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Soacha. Compared with September: 0.3 °C cooler, 77 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. November is about as warm as October on average.

Soacha in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in October?
In October, Soacha averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Soacha?
No month in Soacha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in October?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (159 mm), the driest September (61 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in October?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
