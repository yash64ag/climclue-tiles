How December compares in Soacha

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Soacha. Compared with November: about as warm, 85 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Soacha in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in December?
In December, Soacha averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Soacha?
December is not one of Soacha’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in December?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (182 mm), the driest September (74 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in December?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
