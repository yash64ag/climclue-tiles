Soacha weather in November

Average November weather in Soacha, Colombia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Soacha averages 19.0 °C (66 °F) days and 8.9 °C (48 °F) nights, with 159 mm (6.3 in) of rain over the month and about 119 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Soacha

November is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Soacha. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Soacha in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in November?
In November, Soacha averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Soacha?
November is not one of Soacha’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in November?
159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (159 mm), the driest September (61 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in November?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
