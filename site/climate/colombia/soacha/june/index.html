How June compares in Soacha

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Soacha. Compared with May: 0.2 °C warmer, 30 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. July is 0.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Soacha in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in June?
In June, Soacha averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Soacha?
June is not one of Soacha’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in June?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (182 mm), the driest September (74 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in June?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
