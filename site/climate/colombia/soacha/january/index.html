How January compares in Soacha

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Soacha. Compared with December: 0.1 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Soacha in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soacha in January?
In January, Soacha averages a day high of 18.3 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Soacha?
January is not one of Soacha’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soacha in January?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (159 mm), the driest September (61 mm).
Does it snow in Soacha in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soacha get in January?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soacha in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
