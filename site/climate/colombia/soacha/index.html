Why Soacha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 344 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Soacha climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Soacha?
August, with an average of 15.4 °C (60 °F), day highs around 20.6 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 11.6 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Soacha?
January, with an average of 13.6 °C (56 °F), day highs around 17.9 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 10.2 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Soacha?
November, with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,442 mm (56.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Soacha?
September, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Soacha?
January, with about 172 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Soacha?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Soacha?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: August.
What type of climate does Soacha have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Soacha’s GeoNames location (4.579°N, 74.217°W; ClimaClue area sq-794-1057), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 655,025 (GeoNames).
