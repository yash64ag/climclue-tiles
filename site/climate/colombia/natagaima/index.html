Natagaima at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, Natagaima is the warmest (28.4 °C year average). Compared with Purificación, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Natagaima is 1.2 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in November, with 187 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (30.0 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, November (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in November. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Natagaima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Natagaima itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.621°N, 75.094°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-803-1049), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
