Zipaquirá at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Zipaquirá is the coolest (11.9 °C year average). Compared with Chía, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Zipaquirá is 2.2 °C cooler in March and 2.1 °C cooler in July, with 621 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (12.3 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, July (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 374 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zipaquirá, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zipaquirá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 374 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
