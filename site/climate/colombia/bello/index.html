Bello at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Bello is 41st warmest (17.5 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 1,742 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (2,285 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Medellín, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Bello is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 270 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (17.9 °C), averages 0.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 207 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bello, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bello has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 207 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
