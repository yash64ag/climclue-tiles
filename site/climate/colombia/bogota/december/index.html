How December compares in Bogotá

December is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bogotá. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 61 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Bogotá in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogotá in December?
In December, Bogotá averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bogotá?
No month in Bogotá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogotá in December?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (127 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Bogotá in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogotá get in December?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogotá in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
