How February compares in Bogotá

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bogotá. Compared with January: 0.2 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. March is about as warm as February on average.

Bogotá in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogotá in February?
In February, Bogotá averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bogotá?
No month in Bogotá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogotá in February?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (127 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Bogotá in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogotá get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogotá in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
