Bogotá at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Bogotá is the 5th coolest (13.1 °C year average). Compared with Soacha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Bogotá is 0.5 °C cooler in February and 2.3 °C cooler in July, with 396 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (13.4 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, July (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in December. The area lies about 369 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 129% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bogotá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 369 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
