How June compares in Bogotá

June is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bogotá. Compared with May: 0.6 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. July is 0.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Bogotá in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogotá in June?
In June, Bogotá averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 98 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bogotá?
No month in Bogotá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogotá in June?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (119 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bogotá in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogotá get in June?
About 98 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogotá in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
