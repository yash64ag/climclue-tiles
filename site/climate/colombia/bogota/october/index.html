How October compares in Bogotá

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bogotá. Compared with September: 0.1 °C warmer, 55 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. November is 0.1 °C warmer than October on average.

Bogotá in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogotá in October?
In October, Bogotá averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bogotá?
No month in Bogotá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogotá in October?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (119 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bogotá in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogotá get in October?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogotá in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
