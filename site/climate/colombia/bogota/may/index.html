How May compares in Bogotá

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bogotá. Compared with April: 0.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. June is 0.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Bogotá in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogotá in May?
In May, Bogotá averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bogotá?
No month in Bogotá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogotá in May?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (119 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bogotá in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogotá get in May?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogotá in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
