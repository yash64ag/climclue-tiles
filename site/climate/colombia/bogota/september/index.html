How September compares in Bogotá

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bogotá. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. October is 0.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Bogotá in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogotá in September?
In September, Bogotá averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bogotá?
No month in Bogotá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogotá in September?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (127 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Bogotá in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogotá get in September?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogotá in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
