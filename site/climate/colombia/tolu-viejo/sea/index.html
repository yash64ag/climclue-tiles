Tolú Viejo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sucre Department · Colombia

The sea off Tolú Viejo averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 27.8 °C (82 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Tolú Viejo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tolú Viejo?
In June, at about 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tolú Viejo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is March, at 27.8 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tolú Viejo in March?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Tolú Viejo?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
