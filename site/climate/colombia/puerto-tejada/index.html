Puerto Tejada at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, Puerto Tejada is 186th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 175th sunniest (about 1,806 hours of sunshine a year) and 186th wettest (1,765 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jamundí, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Puerto Tejada is 0.2 °C warmer in June and about as warm in November, with 227 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.9 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, November (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 105 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puerto Tejada, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puerto Tejada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 105 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Puerto Tejada itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.232°N, 76.418°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-807-1035), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
