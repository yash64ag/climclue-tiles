Sogamoso at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Sogamoso is 50th warmest (13.9 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 1,770 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (1,036 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tunja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Sogamoso is 2.0 °C warmer in March and 2.2 °C warmer in July, with 319 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (14.3 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, July (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in August. The area lies about 404 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sogamoso, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sogamoso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 404 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
