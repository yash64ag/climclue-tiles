Dosquebradas at a glance

Among the 317 towns ClimaClue covers in Colombia, Dosquebradas is 241st warmest (19.2 °C year average), 229th sunniest (about 1,660 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (3,515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pereira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Dosquebradas is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in September, with 205 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.6 °C), averages 0.6 °C more than its coolest, September (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 199 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dosquebradas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dosquebradas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 199 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.6 °C. The 10 km area around Dosquebradas, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 17.9 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
