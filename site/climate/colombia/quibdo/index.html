Quibdó at a glance

Among the 55 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Colombia, Quibdó is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,201 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (8,026 mm a year). Compared with Itagüí, the nearest covered town with different numbers (129 km away), Quibdó is 10.6 °C warmer in March and 10.1 °C warmer in October, with 5,759 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.3 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, October (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quibdó, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quibdó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
