Al Finţās sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Al Aḩmadī · Kuwait

The sea off Al Finţās averages 32.0 °C (90 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.6 °C (60 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Al Finţās sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Al Finţās?
In August, at about 32.0 °C (90 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Al Finţās?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 15.6 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Al Finţās in February?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Al Finţās?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
