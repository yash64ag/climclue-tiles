Ar Riqqah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Al Aḩmadī · Kuwait

The sea off Ar Riqqah averages 31.8 °C (89 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.6 °C (60 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Ar Riqqah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ar Riqqah?
In August, at about 31.8 °C (89 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ar Riqqah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 15.6 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ar Riqqah in February?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ar Riqqah?
About 24.2 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ar Riqqah nearby?
In August: Al Khafjī (32.5 °C, 88 km).
