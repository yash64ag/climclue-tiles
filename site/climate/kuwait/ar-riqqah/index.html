Ar Riqqah at a glance

Among the 13 towns ClimaClue covers in Kuwait, Ar Riqqah is the 2nd wettest (117 mm a year). Compared with Al Mahbūlah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Ar Riqqah is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.4 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ar Riqqah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ar Riqqah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-548-2280), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
