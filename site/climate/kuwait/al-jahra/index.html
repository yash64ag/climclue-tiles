Al Jahrā’ at a glance

Among the 13 towns ClimaClue covers in Kuwait, Al Jahrā’ is the warmest (26.6 °C year average) and the sunniest (about 3,193 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Al Farwānīyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Al Jahrā’ is 2.3 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.9 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Jahrā’, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Jahrā’ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Al Jahrā’ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.338°N, 47.658°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-546-2276), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
