Al Aḩmadī in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Aḩmadī in January?
In January, Al Aḩmadī averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Al Aḩmadī?
January is not one of Al Aḩmadī’s mild months; those are March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Aḩmadī in January?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Aḩmadī in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Aḩmadī get in January?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Aḩmadī in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
