Al Aḩmadī in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Aḩmadī in March?
In March, Al Aḩmadī averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Al Aḩmadī?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Al Aḩmadī’s mild months (March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Aḩmadī in March?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Aḩmadī in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Aḩmadī get in March?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Aḩmadī in March?
