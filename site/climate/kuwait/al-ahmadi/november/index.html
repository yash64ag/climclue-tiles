How November compares in Al Aḩmadī

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Al Aḩmadī. Compared with October: 7.3 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Al Aḩmadī in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Aḩmadī in November?
In November, Al Aḩmadī averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Al Aḩmadī?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Al Aḩmadī’s mild months (March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Aḩmadī in November?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (30 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Aḩmadī in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Aḩmadī get in November?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Al Aḩmadī in November?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (32.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Al Aḩmadī in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
