Al Aḩmadī in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Aḩmadī in December?
In December, Al Aḩmadī averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Al Aḩmadī?
December is not one of Al Aḩmadī’s mild months; those are March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Aḩmadī in December?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Aḩmadī in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Aḩmadī get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Aḩmadī in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
