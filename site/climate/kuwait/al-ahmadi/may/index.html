Al Aḩmadī in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Aḩmadī in May?
In May, Al Aḩmadī averages a day high of 37.8 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Al Aḩmadī?
May is not one of Al Aḩmadī’s mild months; those are March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Aḩmadī in May?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Aḩmadī in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Aḩmadī get in May?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Aḩmadī in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
