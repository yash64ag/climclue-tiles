Curralinho at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Curralinho is 170th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 1379th sunniest (about 2,172 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (2,567 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oeiras do Pará, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Curralinho is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 85 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.4 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, February (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in February. The area lies about 50 km from the sea.

Why Curralinho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-858-1302), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
