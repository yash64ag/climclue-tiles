How May compares in Niterói

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Niterói. Compared with April: 2.2 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Niterói in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niterói in May?
In May, Niterói averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Niterói?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Niterói’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niterói in May?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (194 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Niterói in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niterói get in May?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niterói in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
