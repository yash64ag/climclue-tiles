Niterói weather in November

Average November weather in Niterói, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Niterói averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) days and 20.8 °C (69 °F) nights, with 141 mm (5.5 in) of rain over the month and about 162 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Niterói

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Niterói. Compared with October: 1.3 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. December is 1.1 °C warmer than November on average.

Niterói in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niterói in November?
In November, Niterói averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Niterói?
November is not one of Niterói’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niterói in November?
141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (197 mm), the driest August (46 mm).
Does it snow in Niterói in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niterói get in November?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Niterói in November?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Niterói in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
