How March compares in Niterói

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Niterói. Compared with February: 0.9 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. April is 1.4 °C cooler than March on average.

Niterói in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niterói in March?
In March, Niterói averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Niterói?
March is not one of Niterói’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niterói in March?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (194 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Niterói in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niterói get in March?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niterói in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
