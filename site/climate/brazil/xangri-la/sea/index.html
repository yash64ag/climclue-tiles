Xangri-lá sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio Grande do Sul · Brazil

The sea off Xangri-lá averages 23.7 °C (75 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 15.3 °C (60 °F) in July. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April.

Xangri-lá sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xangri-lá?
In March, at about 23.7 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xangri-lá?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is July, at 15.3 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xangri-lá in July?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Xangri-lá?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Xangri-lá nearby?
In March: Balneário Gaivota (24.3 °C, 85 km), Araranguá (24.5 °C, 110 km) and Balneário Rincão (24.4 °C, 133 km).
