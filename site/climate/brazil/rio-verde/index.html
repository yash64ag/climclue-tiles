Rio Verde at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Rio Verde is 160th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,399 hours of sunshine a year) and 158th wettest (1,564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jataí, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Rio Verde is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C cooler in June, with 101 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.4 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, June (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 844 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rio Verde, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Rio Verde

For mild weather, the best months to visit Rio Verde are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): October (24.4 °C), September (24.1 °C) and November (23.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (6 mm), June (14 mm) and August (18 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (277 h), June (256 h) and August (242 h).

Why Rio Verde has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 844 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
