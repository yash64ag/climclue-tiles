Lago da Pedra at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Lago da Pedra is 216th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 1108th sunniest (about 2,254 hours of sunshine a year) and 977th wettest (1,530 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paulo Ramos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Lago da Pedra is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in March, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.7 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, March (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 225 km from the sea.

Why Lago da Pedra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 225 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lago da Pedra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.560°S, 45.132°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-885-1348), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
