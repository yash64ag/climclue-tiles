Chaves sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pará · Brazil

The sea off Chaves averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in October, its warmest month, and 27.9 °C (82 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Chaves sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chaves?
In October, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chaves?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 27.9 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chaves in February?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Chaves?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
