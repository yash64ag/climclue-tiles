Why Dourados has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 761 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Dourados climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dourados?
January, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.3 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 22.0 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dourados?
July, with an average of 18.6 °C (65 °F), day highs around 24.4 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 14.0 °C (57 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dourados?
January, with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,537 mm (60.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dourados?
July, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Dourados?
March, with about 218 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dourados?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Dourados?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Dourados’s GeoNames location (22.221°S, 54.806°W; ClimaClue area sq-1062-1251), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 264,017 (GeoNames).
