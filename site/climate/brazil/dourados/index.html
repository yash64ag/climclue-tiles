Dourados at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Dourados is 161st warmest (22.9 °C year average), 82nd sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (1,598 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campo Grande, the nearest covered town with different numbers (198 km away), Dourados is 1.0 °C warmer in January and 1.5 °C cooler in July, with 137 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.6 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, July (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 761 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dourados, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dourados has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 761 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
