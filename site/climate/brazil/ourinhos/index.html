Ourinhos at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Ourinhos is 167th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 145th sunniest (about 2,270 hours of sunshine a year) and 281st wettest (1,359 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Assis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Ourinhos is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 215 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.1 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, July (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 315 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ourinhos, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ourinhos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 315 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
