Guarapari at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Guarapari is 124th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 130th sunniest (about 2,316 hours of sunshine a year) and 231st wettest (1,452 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vitória, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Guarapari is about as warm in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 132 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.4 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, July (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guarapari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guarapari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
