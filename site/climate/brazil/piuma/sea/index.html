Piúma sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Espírito Santo · Brazil

The sea off Piúma averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 22.4 °C (72 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to May.

Piúma sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Piúma?
In March, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Piúma?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to May. The coolest month is September, at 22.4 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Piúma in September?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Piúma?
About 23.9 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Piúma nearby?
In March: Guarapari (26.2 °C, 29 km), Viana, Espírito Santo (26.5 °C, 55 km) and Vitória (26.5 °C, 70 km).
