Iguape sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · São Paulo · Brazil

The sea off Iguape averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 19.2 °C (67 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April.

Iguape sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Iguape?
In February, at about 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Iguape?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 19.2 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Iguape in August?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Iguape?
About 23.1 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
