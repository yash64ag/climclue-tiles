Contagem in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Contagem in August?
In August, Contagem averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Contagem?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Contagem’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Contagem in August?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (392 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Contagem in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Contagem get in August?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Contagem in August?
