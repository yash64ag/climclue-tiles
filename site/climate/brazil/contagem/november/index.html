Contagem in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Contagem in November?
In November, Contagem averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 254 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Contagem?
November is not one of Contagem’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Contagem in November?
254 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (384 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Contagem in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Contagem get in November?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Contagem in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
