Contagem in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Contagem in April?
In April, Contagem averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Contagem?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Contagem’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Contagem in April?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (384 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Contagem in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Contagem get in April?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Contagem in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
