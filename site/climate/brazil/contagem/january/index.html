How January compares in Contagem

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Contagem. Compared with December: 1.0 °C warmer, 47 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Contagem in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Contagem in January?
In January, Contagem averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 345 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Contagem?
January is not one of Contagem’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Contagem in January?
345 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (392 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Contagem in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Contagem get in January?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Contagem in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
