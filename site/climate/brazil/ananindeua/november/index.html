Ananindeua in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ananindeua in November?
In November, Ananindeua averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ananindeua?
No month in Ananindeua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ananindeua in November?
