Conceição do Coité at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Conceição do Coité is 964th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 1517th sunniest (about 2,137 hours of sunshine a year) and 2157th wettest (641 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Conceição do Coité has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 163 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
