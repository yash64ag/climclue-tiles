Elói Mendes at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Elói Mendes is 1847th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 1260th sunniest (about 2,206 hours of sunshine a year) and 1132nd wettest (1,467 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Varginha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Elói Mendes is 0.1 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 77 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.1 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 196 km from the sea.

Why Elói Mendes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 196 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Elói Mendes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.610°S, 45.565°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1056-1344), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
