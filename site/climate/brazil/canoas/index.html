Canoas at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Canoas is 299th warmest (19.6 °C year average), 162nd sunniest (about 2,204 hours of sunshine a year) and 236th wettest (1,440 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cachoeirinha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Canoas is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.6 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, July (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Canoas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Canoas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
