Garopaba sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Santa Catarina · Brazil

The sea off Garopaba averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.1 °C (63 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to March.

Garopaba sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Garopaba?
In February, at about 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Garopaba?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to March. The coolest month is August, at 17.1 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Garopaba in August?
About 17.1 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Garopaba?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Garopaba nearby?
In February: Ribeirão da Ilha (25.6 °C, 37 km), Florianópolis (25.7 °C, 48 km) and Biguaçu (25.7 °C, 59 km).
