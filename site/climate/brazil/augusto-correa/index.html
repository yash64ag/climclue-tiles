Augusto Corrêa at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Augusto Corrêa is 484th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 1403rd sunniest (about 2,164 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (2,638 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bragança, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Augusto Corrêa is 0.1 °C cooler in November and 0.1 °C warmer in March, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.1 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, March (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Why Augusto Corrêa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
