How April compares in São Luís

April is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in São Luís. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 0.4 °C warmer than April on average.

São Luís in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in April?
In April, São Luís averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 395 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in April?
395 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (397 mm), the driest October (9 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in April?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Luís in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
