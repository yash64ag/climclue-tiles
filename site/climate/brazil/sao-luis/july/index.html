How July compares in São Luís

July is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in São Luís. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

São Luís in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in July?
In July, São Luís averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in July?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (401 mm), the driest October (8 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in July?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in São Luís in July?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for São Luís in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
