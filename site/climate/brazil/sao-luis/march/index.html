How March compares in São Luís

March is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in São Luís. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 89 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. April is 0.1 °C warmer than March on average.

São Luís in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in March?
In March, São Luís averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 397 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in March?
397 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (397 mm), the driest October (9 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in March?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Luís in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
