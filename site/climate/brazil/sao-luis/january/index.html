São Luís weather in January

Average January weather in São Luís, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, São Luís averages 30.4 °C (87 °F) days and 22.4 °C (72 °F) nights, with 220 mm (8.7 in) of rain over the month and about 190 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in São Luís

January is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in São Luís. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 134 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

São Luís in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in January?
In January, São Luís averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in January?
220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (401 mm), the driest October (8 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in January?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in São Luís in January?
About 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for São Luís in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
