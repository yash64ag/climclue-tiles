How May compares in São Luís

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in São Luís. Compared with April: 0.4 °C warmer, 90 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 0.2 °C warmer than May on average.

São Luís in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in May?
In May, São Luís averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in May?
305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (397 mm), the driest October (9 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in May?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Luís in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
