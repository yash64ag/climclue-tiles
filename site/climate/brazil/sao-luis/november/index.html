How November compares in São Luís

November is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in São Luís. Compared with October: 0.2 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

São Luís in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in November?
In November, São Luís averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (397 mm), the driest October (9 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in November?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Luís in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
