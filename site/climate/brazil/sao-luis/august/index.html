How August compares in São Luís

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in São Luís. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 93 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. September is about as warm as August on average.

São Luís in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Luís in August?
In August, São Luís averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit São Luís?
No month in São Luís is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Luís in August?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (397 mm), the driest October (9 mm).
Does it snow in São Luís in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Luís get in August?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Luís in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
