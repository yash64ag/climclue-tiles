Barra do Bugres at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Barra do Bugres is 532nd warmest (26.1 °C year average), 1614th sunniest (about 2,101 hours of sunshine a year) and 956th wettest (1,540 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nova Olímpia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Barra do Bugres is about as warm in October and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 148 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.7 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,465 km from the sea (deep inland).

Best time to visit Barra do Bugres

Why Barra do Bugres has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,465 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Barra do Bugres itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.072°S, 57.181°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-990-1228), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
