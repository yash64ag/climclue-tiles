São José dos Quatro Marcos at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São José dos Quatro Marcos is 620th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 1909th sunniest (about 1,946 hours of sunshine a year) and 1290th wettest (1,411 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mirassol d'Oeste, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), São José dos Quatro Marcos is 0.1 °C warmer in October and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.5 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, July (23.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,344 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why São José dos Quatro Marcos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,344 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
