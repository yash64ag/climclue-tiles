Why Ponta Grossa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Ponta Grossa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ponta Grossa?
January, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 25.8 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 17.9 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ponta Grossa?
July, with an average of 13.9 °C (57 °F), day highs around 18.8 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 9.9 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ponta Grossa?
January, with 192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,585 mm (62.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ponta Grossa?
August, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ponta Grossa?
November, with about 171 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ponta Grossa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ponta Grossa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Ponta Grossa’s GeoNames location (25.095°S, 50.162°W; ClimaClue area sq-1090-1298), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 292,177 (GeoNames).
