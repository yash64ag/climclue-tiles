Ponta Grossa at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Ponta Grossa is 331st warmest (18.2 °C year average), 276th sunniest (about 1,874 hours of sunshine a year) and 149th wettest (1,585 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campo Largo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (75 km away), Ponta Grossa is 0.7 °C warmer in January and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.6 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, July (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 192 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ponta Grossa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ponta Grossa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
