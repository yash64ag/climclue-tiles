Pouso Alegre at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Pouso Alegre is 267th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 216th sunniest (about 2,094 hours of sunshine a year) and 178th wettest (1,527 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Poços de Caldas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Pouso Alegre is 2.4 °C warmer in February and 1.8 °C warmer in July, with 507 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.0 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 168 km from the sea.

Why Pouso Alegre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 168 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
