Pouso Alegre at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Pouso Alegre is 1899th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 1633rd sunniest (about 2,094 hours of sunshine a year) and 990th wettest (1,527 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Borda da Mata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Pouso Alegre is 0.1 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.0 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 168 km from the sea.

Why Pouso Alegre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 168 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pouso Alegre itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.230°S, 45.936°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1062-1340), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
