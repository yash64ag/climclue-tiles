São Francisco do Sul sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Santa Catarina · Brazil

The sea off São Francisco do Sul averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.8 °C (64 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April.

São Francisco do Sul sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São Francisco do Sul?
In February, at about 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São Francisco do Sul?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 17.8 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in São Francisco do Sul in August?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in São Francisco do Sul?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in São Francisco do Sul nearby?
In February: Paranaguá (27.0 °C, 82 km).
