São Bernardo do Campo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Bernardo do Campo in September?
In September, São Bernardo do Campo averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit São Bernardo do Campo?
September is not one of São Bernardo do Campo’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Bernardo do Campo in September?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (310 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in São Bernardo do Campo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Bernardo do Campo get in September?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Bernardo do Campo in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
