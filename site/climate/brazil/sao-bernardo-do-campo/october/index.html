São Bernardo do Campo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Bernardo do Campo in October?
In October, São Bernardo do Campo averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit São Bernardo do Campo?
October is not one of São Bernardo do Campo’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Bernardo do Campo in October?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (310 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in São Bernardo do Campo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Bernardo do Campo get in October?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Bernardo do Campo in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
