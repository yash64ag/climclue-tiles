São Bernardo do Campo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Bernardo do Campo in November?
In November, São Bernardo do Campo averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit São Bernardo do Campo?
November is not one of São Bernardo do Campo’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Bernardo do Campo in November?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (310 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in São Bernardo do Campo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Bernardo do Campo get in November?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Bernardo do Campo in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
