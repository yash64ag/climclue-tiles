São Bernardo do Campo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Bernardo do Campo in April?
In April, São Bernardo do Campo averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit São Bernardo do Campo?
April is not one of São Bernardo do Campo’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Bernardo do Campo in April?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (310 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in São Bernardo do Campo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Bernardo do Campo get in April?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Bernardo do Campo in April?
