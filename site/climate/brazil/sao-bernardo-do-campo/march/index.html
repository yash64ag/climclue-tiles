São Bernardo do Campo weather in March

Average March weather in São Bernardo do Campo, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

What is the weather like in São Bernardo do Campo in March?

Is March a good time to visit São Bernardo do Campo?
March is not one of São Bernardo do Campo’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
