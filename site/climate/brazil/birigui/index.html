Birigui at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Birigui is 115th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,453 hours of sunshine a year) and 288th wettest (1,332 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Araçatuba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Birigui is about as warm in February and about as warm in June, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.9 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, June (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 492 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Birigui, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Birigui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 492 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
