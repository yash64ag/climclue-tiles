Rio Grande sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio Grande do Sul · Brazil

The sea off Rio Grande averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 13.8 °C (57 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March.

Rio Grande sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rio Grande?
In February, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rio Grande?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March. The coolest month is July, at 13.8 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rio Grande in July?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rio Grande?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rio Grande nearby?
In February: São Lourenço do Sul (25.1 °C, 75 km).
