Rio Grande at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Rio Grande is 329th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,402 hours of sunshine a year) and 311th wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pelotas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Rio Grande is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 164 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.4 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, July (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rio Grande, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Rio Grande

For mild weather, the best months to visit Rio Grande are January, March, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, March, October, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): February (23.4 °C), January (22.9 °C) and March (22.8 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (72 mm), March (87 mm) and October (90 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): December (277 h), January (263 h) and November (247 h).

Why Rio Grande has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
