How March compares in Macapá

March is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Macapá. Compared with February: 0.1 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. April is 0.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Macapá in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macapá in March?
In March, Macapá averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 366 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Macapá?
No month in Macapá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macapá in March?
366 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (367 mm), the driest September (28 mm).
Does it snow in Macapá in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macapá get in March?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Macapá in March?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is November (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Macapá in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
