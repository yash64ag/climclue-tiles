How October compares in Macapá

October is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Macapá. Compared with September: 0.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. November is 0.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Macapá in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macapá in October?
In October, Macapá averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Macapá?
No month in Macapá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macapá in October?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (366 mm), the driest September (28 mm).
Does it snow in Macapá in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macapá get in October?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macapá in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
