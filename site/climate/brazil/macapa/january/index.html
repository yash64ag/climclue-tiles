How January compares in Macapá

January is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Macapá. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 134 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Macapá in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macapá in January?
In January, Macapá averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Macapá?
No month in Macapá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macapá in January?
284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (366 mm), the driest September (28 mm).
Does it snow in Macapá in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macapá get in January?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macapá in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
