How December compares in Macapá

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Macapá. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 99 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Macapá in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macapá in December?
In December, Macapá averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Macapá?
No month in Macapá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macapá in December?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (366 mm), the driest September (28 mm).
Does it snow in Macapá in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macapá get in December?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macapá in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
