How September compares in Macapá

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Macapá. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 56 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. October is 0.4 °C warmer than September on average.

Macapá in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macapá in September?
In September, Macapá averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Macapá?
No month in Macapá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macapá in September?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (366 mm), the driest September (28 mm).
Does it snow in Macapá in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macapá get in September?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macapá in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
