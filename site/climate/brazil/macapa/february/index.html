How February compares in Macapá

February is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Macapá. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 46 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Macapá in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macapá in February?
In February, Macapá averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Macapá?
No month in Macapá is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macapá in February?
330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (366 mm), the driest September (28 mm).
Does it snow in Macapá in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macapá get in February?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macapá in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
