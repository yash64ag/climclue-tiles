Porto Nacional at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Porto Nacional is 146th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 647th sunniest (about 2,425 hours of sunshine a year) and 847th wettest (1,584 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Palmas, Tocantins, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Porto Nacional is 0.8 °C warmer in September and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 198 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (29.8 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, January (26.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in July. The area lies about 972 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Porto Nacional has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 972 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Porto Nacional itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.708°S, 48.417°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-947-1315), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
