Umuarama at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Umuarama is 170th warmest (22.3 °C year average), 219th sunniest (about 2,083 hours of sunshine a year) and 132nd wettest (1,632 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campo Mourão, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Umuarama is 1.7 °C warmer in January and 1.8 °C warmer in July, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.6 °C), averages 7.8 °C more than its coolest, July (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 547 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Umuarama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Umuarama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 547 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
