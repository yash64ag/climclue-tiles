São Lourenço da Serra at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São Lourenço da Serra is 2076th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 2145th sunniest (about 1,714 hours of sunshine a year) and 264th wettest (2,037 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Embu-Guaçu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), São Lourenço da Serra is about as warm in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 141 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.6 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, July (16.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Why São Lourenço da Serra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for São Lourenço da Serra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.852°S, 46.942°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
