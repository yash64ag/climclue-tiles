Why Itabaiana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Itabaiana climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Itabaiana?
February, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 22.5 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Itabaiana?
July, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 25.4 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 19.5 °C (67 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Itabaiana?
June, with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,050 mm (41.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Itabaiana?
November, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Itabaiana?
November, with about 255 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Itabaiana?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Itabaiana?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Itabaiana’s GeoNames location (10.685°S, 37.425°W; ClimaClue area sq-946-1425), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 103,440 (GeoNames).
