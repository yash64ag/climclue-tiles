Itabaiana at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itabaiana is 86th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 2,571 hours of sunshine a year) and 327th wettest (1,050 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lagarto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Itabaiana is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.2 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itabaiana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itabaiana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
