Campo Belo at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Campo Belo is 1762nd warmest (21.0 °C year average), 911th sunniest (about 2,325 hours of sunshine a year) and 1268th wettest (1,418 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Perdões, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Campo Belo is 0.1 °C cooler in February and about as warm in July, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.3 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, July (17.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 266 km from the sea.

Why Campo Belo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 266 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Campo Belo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.897°S, 45.277°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1048-1347), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
