Why Atibaia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Atibaia climate: questions and answers

February, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.5 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 18.1 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Atibaia?
July, with an average of 15.9 °C (61 °F), day highs around 21.0 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 11.0 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Atibaia?

What is the driest month in Atibaia?
August, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Atibaia?
July, with about 187 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Atibaia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Atibaia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Atibaia’s GeoNames location (23.117°S, 46.550°W; ClimaClue area sq-1071-1334), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 158,647 (GeoNames).
