Vitória at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Vitória is 112th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 2,324 hours of sunshine a year) and 292nd wettest (1,320 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vila Velha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Vitória is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.4 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, July (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in July. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vitória, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Vitória

For mild weather, the best months to visit Vitória are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.

Why Vitória has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
