Why Sarandi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 430 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Sarandi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sarandi?
January, with an average of 24.3 °C (76 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 20.5 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sarandi?
July, with an average of 17.4 °C (63 °F), day highs around 22.4 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 13.1 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sarandi?
January, with 248 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,807 mm (71.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sarandi?
July, with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sarandi?
March, with about 143 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sarandi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sarandi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Sarandi’s GeoNames location (23.444°S, 51.874°W; ClimaClue area sq-1074-1281), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,455 (GeoNames).
