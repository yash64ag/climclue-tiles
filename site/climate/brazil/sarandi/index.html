Sarandi at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Sarandi is 221st warmest (21.4 °C year average), 340th sunniest (about 1,555 hours of sunshine a year) and 75th wettest (1,807 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maringá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Sarandi is 0.3 °C cooler in January and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 64 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.3 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 430 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sarandi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sarandi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 430 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
