São Félix do Xingu at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, São Félix do Xingu is the least sunny (about 1,495 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Parauapebas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (240 km away), São Félix do Xingu is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 229 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 566 km from the sea.

Why São Félix do Xingu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 566 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
