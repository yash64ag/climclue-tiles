Soledade, Paraíba at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Soledade, Paraíba is 969th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 529th sunniest (about 2,479 hours of sunshine a year) and 2259th wettest (507 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pocinhos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Soledade, Paraíba is 1.4 °C warmer in February and 1.4 °C warmer in July, with 175 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.8 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, July (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in August. The area lies about 167 km from the sea.

Why Soledade, Paraíba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 167 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-910-1436), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
