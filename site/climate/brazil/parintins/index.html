Parintins at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Parintins is 14th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 186th sunniest (about 2,150 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (2,307 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itacoatiara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (198 km away), Parintins is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.3 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, February (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in June. The area lies about 518 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Parintins, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Parintins has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 518 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Parintins itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (2.628°S, 56.736°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-866-1232), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
