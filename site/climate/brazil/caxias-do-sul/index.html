Caxias do Sul at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Caxias do Sul is the 2nd coolest (16.1 °C year average). Compared with Bento Gonçalves, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Caxias do Sul is 1.4 °C cooler in January and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.6 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, July (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 138 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Caxias do Sul, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Caxias do Sul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 138 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
