Belmonte sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bahia · Brazil

The sea off Belmonte averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 24.8 °C (77 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Belmonte sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Belmonte?
In March, at about 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Belmonte?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 24.8 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Belmonte in August?
About 24.8 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Belmonte?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Belmonte nearby?
In March: Ilhéus (28.3 °C, 119 km), Itacaré (28.3 °C, 176 km) and Maraú (28.3 °C, 196 km).
