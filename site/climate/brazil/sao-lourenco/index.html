São Lourenço at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São Lourenço is 1920th warmest (20.2 °C year average), 991st sunniest (about 2,299 hours of sunshine a year) and 1394th wettest (1,371 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caxambu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), São Lourenço is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.8 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (16.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in July. The area lies about 122 km from the sea.

Why São Lourenço has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 122 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for São Lourenço itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.116°S, 45.054°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1061-1349), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
