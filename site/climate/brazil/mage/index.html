Magé at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Magé is 106th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 241st sunniest (about 2,028 hours of sunshine a year) and 101st wettest (1,732 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Gonçalo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Magé is 0.3 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 212 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, July (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Magé, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Magé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
