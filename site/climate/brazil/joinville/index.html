Joinville at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Joinville is 232nd warmest (21.0 °C year average), 327th sunniest (about 1,653 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (1,990 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jaraguá do Sul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Joinville is 0.7 °C warmer in February and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 93 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.1 °C), averages 8.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Joinville, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Joinville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
