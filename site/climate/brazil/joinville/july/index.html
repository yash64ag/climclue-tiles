How July compares in Joinville

July is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Joinville. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C warmer than July on average.

Joinville in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Joinville in July?
In July, Joinville averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Joinville?
July is not one of Joinville’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Joinville in July?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest June (96 mm).
Does it snow in Joinville in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Joinville get in July?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Joinville in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
