How December compares in Joinville

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Joinville. Compared with November: 1.6 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. January is 1.0 °C warmer than December on average.

Joinville in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Joinville in December?
In December, Joinville averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Joinville?
December is not one of Joinville’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Joinville in December?
189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (289 mm), the driest June (96 mm).
Does it snow in Joinville in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Joinville get in December?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Joinville in December?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is February (26.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Joinville in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
