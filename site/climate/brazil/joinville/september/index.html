How September compares in Joinville

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Joinville. Compared with August: 0.9 °C warmer, 61 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. October is 1.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Joinville in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Joinville in September?
In September, Joinville averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Joinville?
September is not one of Joinville’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Joinville in September?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest June (96 mm).
Does it snow in Joinville in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Joinville get in September?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Joinville in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
