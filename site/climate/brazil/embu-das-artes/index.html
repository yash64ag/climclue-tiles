Embu das Artes at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Embu das Artes is 318th warmest (19.4 °C year average), 288th sunniest (about 1,813 hours of sunshine a year) and 103rd wettest (1,728 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taboão da Serra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Embu das Artes is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.4 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 63 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Embu das Artes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Embu das Artes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
