Três Lagoas at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Três Lagoas is 93rd warmest (24.6 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 2,449 hours of sunshine a year) and 279th wettest (1,365 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Araçatuba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (140 km away), Três Lagoas is 0.6 °C warmer in January and 0.6 °C warmer in June, with 56 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.4 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, June (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 627 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Três Lagoas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Três Lagoas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 627 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
