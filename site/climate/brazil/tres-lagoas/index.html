Três Lagoas at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Três Lagoas is 1025th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 599th sunniest (about 2,449 hours of sunshine a year) and 1421st wettest (1,360 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Castilho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Três Lagoas is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.0 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, July (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 627 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Três Lagoas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 627 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Três Lagoas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.788°S, 51.703°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1047-1282), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
