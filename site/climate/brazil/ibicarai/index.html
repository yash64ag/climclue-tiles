Ibicaraí at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ibicaraí is 1172nd warmest (23.8 °C year average), 1090th sunniest (about 2,261 hours of sunshine a year) and 1786th wettest (1,048 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Coaraci, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Ibicaraí is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 338 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.6 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Why Ibicaraí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ibicaraí itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.865°S, 39.588°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-988-1404), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
