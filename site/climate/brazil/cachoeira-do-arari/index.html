Cachoeira do Arari at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Cachoeira do Arari is 303rd warmest (26.8 °C year average), 1116th sunniest (about 2,251 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (2,825 mm a year), near the middle on each. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, February (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in February. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Why Cachoeira do Arari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
