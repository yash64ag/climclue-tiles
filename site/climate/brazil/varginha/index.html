Varginha at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Varginha is 258th warmest (20.5 °C year average), 143rd sunniest (about 2,271 hours of sunshine a year) and 272nd wettest (1,377 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lavras, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Varginha is 0.2 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.9 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 201 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Varginha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Varginha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 201 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
