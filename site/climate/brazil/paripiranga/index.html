Paripiranga at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Paripiranga is 1123rd warmest (24.0 °C year average), 498th sunniest (about 2,497 hours of sunshine a year) and 1838th wettest (986 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Simão Dias, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Paripiranga is 0.7 °C cooler in March and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.6 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, July (21.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Paripiranga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Paripiranga

For mild weather, the best months to visit Paripiranga are August, September, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): August, September, October and November.

Why Paripiranga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Paripiranga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.688°S, 37.862°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-946-1421), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
