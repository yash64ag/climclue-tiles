Piraquara at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Piraquara is 341st warmest (17.1 °C year average), 334th sunniest (about 1,613 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (1,889 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pinhais, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Piraquara is 0.4 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 266 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.6 °C), averages 7.3 °C more than its coolest, July (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 74 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Piraquara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Piraquara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 74 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
