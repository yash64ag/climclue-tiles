Piquet Carneiro at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Piquet Carneiro is 108th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 106th sunniest (about 2,759 hours of sunshine a year) and 1995th wettest (827 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mombaça, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Piquet Carneiro is 0.1 °C warmer in November and about as warm in June, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (29.1 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, June (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 251 km from the sea.

Why Piquet Carneiro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 251 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
