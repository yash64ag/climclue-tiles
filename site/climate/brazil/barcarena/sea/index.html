Barcarena sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pará · Brazil

The sea off Barcarena averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 28.2 °C (83 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Barcarena sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Barcarena?
In June, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Barcarena?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 28.2 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Barcarena in February?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Barcarena?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
