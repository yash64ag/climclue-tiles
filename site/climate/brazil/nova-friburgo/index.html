Nova Friburgo at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Nova Friburgo is 330th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 309th sunniest (about 1,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (1,824 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Teresópolis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Nova Friburgo is 1.3 °C cooler in February and 1.2 °C cooler in July, with 484 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 76 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nova Friburgo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nova Friburgo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
