Criciúma at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Criciúma is 292nd warmest (19.6 °C year average), 200th sunniest (about 2,116 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (1,731 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tubarão, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Criciúma is 0.6 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 183 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.9 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, July (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Criciúma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Criciúma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
