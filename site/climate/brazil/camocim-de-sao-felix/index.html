Camocim de São Félix at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Camocim de São Félix is 1559th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 744th sunniest (about 2,384 hours of sunshine a year) and 1837th wettest (987 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Joaquim do Monte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Camocim de São Félix is 1.1 °C cooler in March and 1.0 °C cooler in August, with 136 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (23.4 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, August (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in August. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Why Camocim de São Félix has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
