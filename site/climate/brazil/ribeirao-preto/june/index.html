What is the weather like in Ribeirão Preto in June?

Is June a good time to visit Ribeirão Preto?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Ribeirão Preto’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
