Ribeirão Preto in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ribeirão Preto in December?
In December, Ribeirão Preto averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 259 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ribeirão Preto?
December is not one of Ribeirão Preto’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ribeirão Preto in December?
259 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (306 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ribeirão Preto in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ribeirão Preto get in December?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ribeirão Preto in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
