Ribeirão Preto in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ribeirão Preto in July?
In July, Ribeirão Preto averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ribeirão Preto?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Ribeirão Preto’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ribeirão Preto in July?

Does it snow in Ribeirão Preto in July?
