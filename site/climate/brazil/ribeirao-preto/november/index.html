Ribeirão Preto in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ribeirão Preto in November?
In November, Ribeirão Preto averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ribeirão Preto?
November is not one of Ribeirão Preto’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ribeirão Preto in November?
159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (306 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ribeirão Preto in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ribeirão Preto get in November?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ribeirão Preto in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
