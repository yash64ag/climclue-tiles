Ribeirão Preto in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ribeirão Preto in January?
In January, Ribeirão Preto averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 282 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ribeirão Preto?
January is not one of Ribeirão Preto’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ribeirão Preto in January?
282 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (282 mm), the driest July (18 mm).
Does it snow in Ribeirão Preto in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ribeirão Preto get in January?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ribeirão Preto in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
