Ribeirão Preto at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Ribeirão Preto is 155th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 234th sunniest (about 2,045 hours of sunshine a year) and 183rd wettest (1,534 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sertãozinho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Ribeirão Preto is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in June, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.9 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, June (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in June. The area lies about 358 km from the sea.

Why Ribeirão Preto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 358 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
