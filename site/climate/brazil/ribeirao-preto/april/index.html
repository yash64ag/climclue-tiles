Ribeirão Preto in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ribeirão Preto in April?
In April, Ribeirão Preto averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ribeirão Preto?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ribeirão Preto’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ribeirão Preto in April?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (282 mm), the driest July (18 mm).
Does it snow in Ribeirão Preto in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ribeirão Preto get in April?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ribeirão Preto in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
