Jardim, Ceará at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Jardim, Ceará is 979th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 2,751 hours of sunshine a year) and 1901st wettest (914 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Porteiras, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Jardim, Ceará is 1.4 °C cooler in November and 1.3 °C cooler in July, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.9 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, July (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 379 km from the sea.

Why Jardim, Ceará has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 379 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C. The 10 km area around Jardim, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.3 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
