Cariacica at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Cariacica is 137th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,317 hours of sunshine a year) and 192nd wettest (1,520 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vitória, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Cariacica is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 201 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.1 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cariacica, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cariacica has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
