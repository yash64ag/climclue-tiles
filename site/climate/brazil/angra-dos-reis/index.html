Angra dos Reis at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Angra dos Reis is 171st warmest (22.3 °C year average), 290th sunniest (about 1,801 hours of sunshine a year) and 100th wettest (1,736 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barra Mansa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Angra dos Reis is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 1.1 °C warmer in July, with 276 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, July (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Angra dos Reis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Angra dos Reis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
