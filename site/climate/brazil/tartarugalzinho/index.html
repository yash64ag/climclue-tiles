Tartarugalzinho at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Tartarugalzinho is 529th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 1369th sunniest (about 2,174 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (2,892 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Porto Grande, the nearest covered town with different numbers (104 km away), Tartarugalzinho is 0.3 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 671 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.8 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, February (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 85 km from the sea.

Why Tartarugalzinho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 85 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
