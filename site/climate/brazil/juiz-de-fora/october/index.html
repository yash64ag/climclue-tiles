Juiz de Fora in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in October?
In October, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
October is not one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in October?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in October?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Juiz de Fora in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
