Juiz de Fora in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in November?
In November, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
November is not one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in November?
211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in November?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Juiz de Fora in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
