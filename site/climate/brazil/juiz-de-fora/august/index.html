Juiz de Fora in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in August?
In August, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in August?
22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in August?
