Juiz de Fora weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Juiz de Fora, Brazil (Minas Gerais), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Juiz de Fora at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Juiz de Fora is 1855th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 2048th sunniest (about 1,816 hours of sunshine a year) and 736th wettest (1,645 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santos Dumont, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Juiz de Fora is 1.0 °C warmer in February and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 99 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.6 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Why Juiz de Fora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Juiz de Fora itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.764°S, 43.350°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1057-1366), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
