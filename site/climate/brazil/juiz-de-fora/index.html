Juiz de Fora at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Juiz de Fora is 266th warmest (20.2 °C year average), 286th sunniest (about 1,816 hours of sunshine a year) and 124th wettest (1,659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barbacena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Juiz de Fora is 2.0 °C warmer in February and 1.6 °C warmer in July, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.1 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Juiz de Fora, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Juiz de Fora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
