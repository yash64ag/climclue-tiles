Juiz de Fora in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in December?
In December, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 309 mm (12.2 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
December is not one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in December?
309 mm (12.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in December?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Juiz de Fora in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
