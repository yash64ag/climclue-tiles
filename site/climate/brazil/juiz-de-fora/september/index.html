Juiz de Fora in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in September?
In September, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in September?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in September?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Juiz de Fora in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
