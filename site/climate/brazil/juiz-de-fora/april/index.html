Juiz de Fora in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in April?
In April, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in April?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in April?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Juiz de Fora in April?
