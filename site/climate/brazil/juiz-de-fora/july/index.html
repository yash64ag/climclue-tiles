Juiz de Fora in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in July?
In July, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in July?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (316 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Juiz de Fora in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Juiz de Fora get in July?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Juiz de Fora in July?
