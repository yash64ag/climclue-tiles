Juiz de Fora in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Juiz de Fora in May?
In May, Juiz de Fora averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Juiz de Fora?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Juiz de Fora’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Juiz de Fora in May?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (319 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
