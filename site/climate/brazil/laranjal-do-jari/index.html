Laranjal do Jari at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Laranjal do Jari is 257th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 1551st sunniest (about 2,124 hours of sunshine a year) and 292nd wettest (1,996 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Almeirim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Laranjal do Jari is 0.1 °C warmer in November and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.7 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, February (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 75 km from the sea.

Why Laranjal do Jari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 75 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
