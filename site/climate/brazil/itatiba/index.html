Itatiba at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itatiba is 268th warmest (20.1 °C year average), 192nd sunniest (about 2,130 hours of sunshine a year) and 147th wettest (1,592 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Valinhos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Itatiba is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.9 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itatiba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itatiba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
