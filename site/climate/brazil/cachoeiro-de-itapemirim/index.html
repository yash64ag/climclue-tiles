Cachoeiro de Itapemirim at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Cachoeiro de Itapemirim is 139th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 2,328 hours of sunshine a year) and 285th wettest (1,340 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guarapari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Cachoeiro de Itapemirim is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 112 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Why Cachoeiro de Itapemirim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
