São Gonçalo do Amarante at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, São Gonçalo do Amarante is the 5th sunniest (about 2,774 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Natal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), São Gonçalo do Amarante is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 161 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.8 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, July (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Why São Gonçalo do Amarante has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
