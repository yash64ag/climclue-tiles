Almirante Tamandaré at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Almirante Tamandaré is 341st warmest (17.1 °C year average), 330th sunniest (about 1,638 hours of sunshine a year) and 140th wettest (1,609 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Colombo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Almirante Tamandaré is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.7 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, July (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Why Almirante Tamandaré has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
