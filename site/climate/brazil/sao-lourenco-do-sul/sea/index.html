São Lourenço do Sul sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio Grande do Sul · Brazil

The sea off São Lourenço do Sul averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 14.1 °C (57 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to March.

São Lourenço do Sul sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São Lourenço do Sul?
In February, at about 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São Lourenço do Sul?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to March. The coolest month is July, at 14.1 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in São Lourenço do Sul in July?
About 14.1 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in São Lourenço do Sul?
About 19.7 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
