How December compares in Brasília

December is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Brasília. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Brasília in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brasília in December?
In December, Brasília averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 264 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Brasília?
December is not one of Brasília’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brasília in December?
264 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Brasília in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brasília get in December?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brasília in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
