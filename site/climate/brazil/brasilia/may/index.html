How May compares in Brasília

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Brasília. Compared with April: 1.1 °C cooler, 88 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Brasília in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brasília in May?
In May, Brasília averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Brasília?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Brasília’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brasília in May?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Brasília in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brasília get in May?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brasília in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
