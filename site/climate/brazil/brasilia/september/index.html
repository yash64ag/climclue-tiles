Brasília weather in September

Average September weather in Brasília, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Brasília averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) days and 16.4 °C (62 °F) nights, with 46 mm (1.8 in) of rain over the month and about 201 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Brasília

September is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Brasília. Compared with August: 1.7 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Brasília in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brasília in September?
In September, Brasília averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Brasília?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Brasília’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brasília in September?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Brasília in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brasília get in September?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brasília in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
