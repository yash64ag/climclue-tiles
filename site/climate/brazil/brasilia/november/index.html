How November compares in Brasília

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Brasília. Compared with October: 1.1 °C cooler, 79 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Brasília in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brasília in November?
In November, Brasília averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Brasília?
November is not one of Brasília’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brasília in November?
227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (256 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Brasília in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brasília get in November?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brasília in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
