How June compares in Brasília

June is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Brasília. Compared with May: 1.3 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is about as warm as June on average.

Brasília in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brasília in June?
In June, Brasília averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Brasília?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Brasília’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brasília in June?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (256 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Brasília in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brasília get in June?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brasília in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
