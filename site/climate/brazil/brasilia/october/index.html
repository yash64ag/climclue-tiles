Brasília weather in October

Average October weather in Brasília, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Brasília averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) days and 17.6 °C (64 °F) nights, with 150 mm (5.9 in) of rain over the month and about 170 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Brasília

October is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Brasília. Compared with September: 0.1 °C warmer, 105 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. November is 1.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Brasília in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brasília in October?
In October, Brasília averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Brasília?
October is not one of Brasília’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brasília in October?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Brasília in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brasília get in October?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brasília in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
