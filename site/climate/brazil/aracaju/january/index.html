How January compares in Aracaju

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Aracaju. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Aracaju in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aracaju in January?
In January, Aracaju averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aracaju?
January is not one of Aracaju’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aracaju in January?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Aracaju in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aracaju get in January?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aracaju in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
