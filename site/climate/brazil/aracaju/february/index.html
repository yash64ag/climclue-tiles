How February compares in Aracaju

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Aracaju. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Aracaju in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aracaju in February?
In February, Aracaju averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aracaju?
February is not one of Aracaju’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aracaju in February?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Aracaju in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aracaju get in February?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aracaju in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
