How October compares in Aracaju

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Aracaju. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. November is 0.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Aracaju in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aracaju in October?
In October, Aracaju averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aracaju?
October is not one of Aracaju’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aracaju in October?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Aracaju in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aracaju get in October?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aracaju in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
