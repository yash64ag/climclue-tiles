How September compares in Aracaju

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Aracaju. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 38 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. October is 0.7 °C warmer than September on average.

Aracaju in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aracaju in September?
In September, Aracaju averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aracaju?
No month in Aracaju is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aracaju in September?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (252 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Aracaju in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aracaju get in September?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Aracaju in September?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (28.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Aracaju in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
