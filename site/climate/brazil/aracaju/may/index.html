How May compares in Aracaju

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Aracaju. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 45 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Aracaju in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aracaju in May?
In May, Aracaju averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 262 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aracaju?
May is not one of Aracaju’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aracaju in May?
262 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Aracaju in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aracaju get in May?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aracaju in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
