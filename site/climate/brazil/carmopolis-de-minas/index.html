Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Carmópolis de Minas is 1753rd warmest (21.1 °C year average), 1072nd sunniest (about 2,267 hours of sunshine a year) and 1094th wettest (1,483 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Carmópolis de Minas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 285 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
