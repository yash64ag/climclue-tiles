Urucurituba at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Urucurituba is 222nd warmest (27.1 °C year average), 2180th sunniest (about 1,668 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (2,330 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itacoatiara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Urucurituba is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.3 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, February (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 682 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Urucurituba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 682 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Urucurituba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.129°S, 58.155°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-871-1218), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
