Botucatu at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Botucatu is 251st warmest (20.5 °C year average), 231st sunniest (about 2,048 hours of sunshine a year) and 186th wettest (1,532 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jaú, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Botucatu is 1.3 °C cooler in February and 1.7 °C cooler in July, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.4 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 233 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Botucatu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Botucatu

For mild weather, the best months to visit Botucatu are April, May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): April, May, June, July, August and September.

Why Botucatu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 233 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
