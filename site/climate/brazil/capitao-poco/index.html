Capitão Poço at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Capitão Poço is 460th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 1487th sunniest (about 2,144 hours of sunshine a year) and 141st wettest (2,279 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Garrafão do Norte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Capitão Poço is 0.3 °C cooler in November and 0.3 °C cooler in March, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.7 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, March (25.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in July. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Why Capitão Poço has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Capitão Poço itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (1.746°S, 47.059°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-857-1329), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
