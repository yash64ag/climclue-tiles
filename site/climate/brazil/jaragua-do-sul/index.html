Jaraguá do Sul at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Jaraguá do Sul is 260th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 336th sunniest (about 1,585 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (1,898 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Joinville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Jaraguá do Sul is 0.7 °C cooler in February and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 93 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.4 °C), averages 8.5 °C more than its coolest, July (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jaraguá do Sul, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jaraguá do Sul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
