Barreiras at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Barreiras is 75th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,704 hours of sunshine a year) and 333rd wettest (983 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luis Eduardo Magalhães, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Barreiras is 2.0 °C warmer in October and 1.7 °C warmer in July, with 305 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.6 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 673 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Barreiras, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Barreiras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 673 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
