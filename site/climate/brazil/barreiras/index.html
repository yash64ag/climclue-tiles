Barreiras at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Barreiras is 68th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,704 hours of sunshine a year) and 334th wettest (902 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luis Eduardo Magalhães, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Barreiras is 2.4 °C warmer in October and 2.1 °C warmer in July, with 378 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.9 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (24.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 673 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Barreiras, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Barreiras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 673 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Barreiras itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.153°S, 44.990°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-961-1350), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
