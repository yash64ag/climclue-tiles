Colatina at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Colatina is 102nd warmest (24.1 °C year average), 141st sunniest (about 2,280 hours of sunshine a year) and 325th wettest (1,058 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Linhares, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Colatina is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 191 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, July (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 81 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Colatina, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Colatina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 81 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
