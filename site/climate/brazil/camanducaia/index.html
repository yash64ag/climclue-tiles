Camanducaia at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Camanducaia is 2129th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 2053rd sunniest (about 1,813 hours of sunshine a year) and 771st wettest (1,624 mm a year), near the middle on each. Its warmest month, February (21.7 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 124 km from the sea.

Why Camanducaia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
