Why Bagé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 204 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Bagé climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bagé?
January, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.8 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 18.8 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bagé?
July, with an average of 11.9 °C (54 °F), day highs around 16.3 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 8.3 °C (47 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bagé?
April, with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,568 mm (61.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bagé?
August, with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bagé?
December, with about 258 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bagé?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bagé?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Bagé have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Bagé’s GeoNames location (31.331°S, 54.107°W; ClimaClue area sq-1153-1258), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 121,335 (GeoNames).
