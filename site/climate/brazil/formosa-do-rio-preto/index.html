Formosa do Rio Preto at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Formosa do Rio Preto is 617th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 187th sunniest (about 2,669 hours of sunshine a year) and 1921st wettest (893 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Corrente, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Formosa do Rio Preto is 0.6 °C cooler in October and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 727 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Formosa do Rio Preto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 727 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-950-1348), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
