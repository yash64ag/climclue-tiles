Nova Esperança do Piriá at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Nova Esperança do Piriá is 492nd warmest (26.3 °C year average), 1493rd sunniest (about 2,143 hours of sunshine a year) and 204th wettest (2,124 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Garrafão do Norte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Nova Esperança do Piriá is 0.3 °C cooler in November and 0.4 °C cooler in March, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Why Nova Esperança do Piriá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 159 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-862-1330), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
