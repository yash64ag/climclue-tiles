How June compares in Belo Horizonte

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Belo Horizonte. Compared with May: 1.6 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Belo Horizonte in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belo Horizonte in June?
In June, Belo Horizonte averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Belo Horizonte?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Belo Horizonte’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belo Horizonte in June?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (388 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Belo Horizonte in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belo Horizonte get in June?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belo Horizonte in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
