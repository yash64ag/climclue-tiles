How July compares in Belo Horizonte

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Belo Horizonte. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. August is 1.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Belo Horizonte in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belo Horizonte in July?
In July, Belo Horizonte averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Belo Horizonte?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Belo Horizonte’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belo Horizonte in July?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (388 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Belo Horizonte in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belo Horizonte get in July?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belo Horizonte in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
