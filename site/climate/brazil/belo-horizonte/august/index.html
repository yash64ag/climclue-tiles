How August compares in Belo Horizonte

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Belo Horizonte. Compared with July: 1.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. September is 1.9 °C warmer than August on average.

Belo Horizonte in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belo Horizonte in August?
In August, Belo Horizonte averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Belo Horizonte?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Belo Horizonte’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belo Horizonte in August?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (370 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Belo Horizonte in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belo Horizonte get in August?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belo Horizonte in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
