How April compares in Belo Horizonte

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Belo Horizonte. Compared with March: 1.1 °C cooler, 140 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 2.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Belo Horizonte in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belo Horizonte in April?
In April, Belo Horizonte averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Belo Horizonte?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Belo Horizonte’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belo Horizonte in April?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (388 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Belo Horizonte in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belo Horizonte get in April?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belo Horizonte in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
