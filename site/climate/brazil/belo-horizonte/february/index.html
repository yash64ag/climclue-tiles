How February compares in Belo Horizonte

February is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Belo Horizonte. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 157 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. March is 0.5 °C cooler than February on average.

Belo Horizonte in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belo Horizonte in February?
In February, Belo Horizonte averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Belo Horizonte?
February is not one of Belo Horizonte’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belo Horizonte in February?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (388 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Belo Horizonte in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belo Horizonte get in February?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belo Horizonte in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
