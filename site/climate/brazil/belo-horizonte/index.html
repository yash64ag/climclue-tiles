Belo Horizonte at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Belo Horizonte is 261st warmest (20.3 °C year average), 88th sunniest (about 2,383 hours of sunshine a year) and 111th wettest (1,697 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nova Lima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Belo Horizonte is 0.6 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 150 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.6 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Belo Horizonte, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Belo Horizonte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
