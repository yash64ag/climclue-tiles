Belo Horizonte weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Belo Horizonte, Brazil (Minas Gerais), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Belo Horizonte is warmest in February, with 28.4 °C (83 °F) days and 18.2 °C nights, and coolest in July, with 24.1 °C (75 °F) days and 11.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around December (370 mm); July is driest (7 mm).

Belo Horizonte at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Belo Horizonte is 1764th warmest (21.0 °C year average), 746th sunniest (about 2,383 hours of sunshine a year) and 791st wettest (1,615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nova Lima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Belo Horizonte is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 77 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.3 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Belo Horizonte, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Belo Horizonte

For mild weather, the best months to visit Belo Horizonte are April, May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): April, May, June, July, August and September.

Why Belo Horizonte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Belo Horizonte itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.921°S, 43.938°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1039-1360), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
