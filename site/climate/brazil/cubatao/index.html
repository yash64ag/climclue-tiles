Cubatão at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Cubatão is 239th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 317th sunniest (about 1,695 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (2,675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Vicente, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Cubatão is 1.8 °C cooler in February and 2.0 °C cooler in July, with 349 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.3 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, July (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cubatão, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cubatão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
