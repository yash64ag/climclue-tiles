Garanhuns at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Garanhuns is 194th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 137th sunniest (about 2,300 hours of sunshine a year) and 335th wettest (961 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caruaru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Garanhuns is 1.1 °C cooler in January and 1.5 °C cooler in July, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.3 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Garanhuns, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Garanhuns has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
