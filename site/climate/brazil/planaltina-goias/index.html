Planaltina, Goiás at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Planaltina, Goiás is 179th warmest (22.1 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,404 hours of sunshine a year) and 259th wettest (1,406 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Planaltina, Federal District, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Planaltina, Goiás is 0.1 °C cooler in October and about as warm in July, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.8 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 914 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Planaltina, Goiás has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 914 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
