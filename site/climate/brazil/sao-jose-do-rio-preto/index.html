São José do Rio Preto at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, São José do Rio Preto is 144th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,524 hours of sunshine a year) and 250th wettest (1,416 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Catanduva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), São José do Rio Preto is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in June, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.1 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, June (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in June. The area lies about 463 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of São José do Rio Preto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why São José do Rio Preto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 463 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
