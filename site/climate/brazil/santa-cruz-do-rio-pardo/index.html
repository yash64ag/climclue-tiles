Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo, Brazil (São Paulo), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo is warmest in February, with 30.6 °C (87 °F) days and 19.1 °C nights, and coolest in July, with 25.2 °C (77 °F) days and 11.5 °C nights. Most rain falls around January (239 mm); August is driest (45 mm).

Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo is 1512th warmest (22.2 °C year average), 1158th sunniest (about 2,240 hours of sunshine a year) and 1174th wettest (1,452 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ourinhos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo is 0.1 °C cooler in February and about as warm in July, with 95 mm more precipitation a year. Why Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Santa Cruz do Rio Pardo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.899°S, 49.632°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1068-1303), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
