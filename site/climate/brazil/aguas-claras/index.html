Águas Claras at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Águas Claras is 234th warmest (21.0 °C year average), 121st sunniest (about 2,332 hours of sunshine a year) and 104th wettest (1,724 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guará, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Águas Claras is 0.7 °C cooler in September and 0.6 °C cooler in June, with 218 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (22.4 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, June (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 891 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Águas Claras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 891 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
