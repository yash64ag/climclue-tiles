Cordeiro at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Cordeiro is 1757th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 1893rd sunniest (about 1,956 hours of sunshine a year) and 1522nd wettest (1,317 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cantagalo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Cordeiro is 0.4 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.1 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 76 km from the sea.

Why Cordeiro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cordeiro itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.029°S, 42.361°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1060-1376), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
