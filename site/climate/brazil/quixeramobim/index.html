Quixeramobim at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Quixeramobim is 97th warmest (27.6 °C year average), 270th sunniest (about 2,618 hours of sunshine a year) and 2112th wettest (690 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quixadá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Quixeramobim is 0.4 °C warmer in November and about as warm in June, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.9 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, June (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 181 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quixeramobim, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quixeramobim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 181 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
