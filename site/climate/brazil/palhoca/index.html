Why Palhoça has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Palhoça climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Palhoça?
February, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 27.6 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 22.2 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Palhoça?
July, with an average of 15.9 °C (61 °F), day highs around 19.5 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 13.1 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Palhoça?
January, with 260 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,778 mm (70.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Palhoça?
June, with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Palhoça?
January, with about 188 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Palhoça?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Palhoça?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June and August.
What type of climate does Palhoça have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Palhoça’s GeoNames location (27.645°S, 48.668°W; ClimaClue area sq-1116-1313), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 222,598 (GeoNames).
