Palhoça at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Palhoça is 253rd warmest (20.5 °C year average), 259th sunniest (about 1,973 hours of sunshine a year) and 84th wettest (1,778 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Florianópolis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Palhoça is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 103 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.9 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, July (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Palhoça, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Palhoça has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
