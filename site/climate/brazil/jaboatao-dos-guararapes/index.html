Jaboatão dos Guararapes at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Jaboatão dos Guararapes is 78th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,570 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (1,895 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Camaragibe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Jaboatão dos Guararapes is 0.6 °C cooler in March and 0.6 °C cooler in July, with 73 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.4 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, July (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Why Jaboatão dos Guararapes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
