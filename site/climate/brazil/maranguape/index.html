Maranguape at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Maranguape is 36th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,577 hours of sunshine a year) and 307th wettest (1,265 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caucaia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Maranguape is 0.1 °C cooler in November and 0.4 °C cooler in June, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.4 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, June (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in July. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maranguape, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maranguape has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
