Balneário Gaivota sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Santa Catarina · Brazil

The sea off Balneário Gaivota averages 24.3 °C (76 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 16.4 °C (62 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March.

Balneário Gaivota sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Balneário Gaivota?
In March, at about 24.3 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Balneário Gaivota?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March. The coolest month is July, at 16.4 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Balneário Gaivota in July?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Balneário Gaivota?
About 20.4 °C (69 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
