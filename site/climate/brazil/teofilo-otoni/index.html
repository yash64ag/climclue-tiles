Teófilo Otoni at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Teófilo Otoni is 153rd warmest (23.1 °C year average), 264th sunniest (about 1,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 322nd wettest (1,115 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Governador Valadares, the nearest covered town with different numbers (120 km away), Teófilo Otoni is 1.7 °C cooler in February and 1.7 °C cooler in July, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.6 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 212 km from the sea.

Why Teófilo Otoni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 212 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
