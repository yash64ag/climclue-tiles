Nova Xavantina at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Nova Xavantina is 470th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 1079th sunniest (about 2,266 hours of sunshine a year) and 1188th wettest (1,447 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campinápolis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Nova Xavantina is 1.9 °C warmer in September and 1.7 °C warmer in July, with 478 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (28.3 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, July (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,218 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nova Xavantina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,218 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nova Xavantina itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.664°S, 52.359°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-986-1276), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
