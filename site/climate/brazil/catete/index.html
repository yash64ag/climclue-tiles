Catete weather by month and climate

Catete at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Catete is 1170th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 1647th sunniest (about 2,091 hours of sunshine a year) and 1445th wettest (1,349 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Teresa, Rio de Janeiro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Catete is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.0 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Catete has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Catete itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.925°S, 43.181°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1069-1368), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
