How June compares in Rio de Janeiro

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Rio de Janeiro. Compared with May: 1.3 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Rio de Janeiro in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rio de Janeiro in June?
In June, Rio de Janeiro averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rio de Janeiro?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Rio de Janeiro’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rio de Janeiro in June?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (197 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Rio de Janeiro in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rio de Janeiro get in June?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rio de Janeiro in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
