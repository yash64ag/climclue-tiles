How October compares in Rio de Janeiro

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Rio de Janeiro. Compared with September: 1.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Rio de Janeiro in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rio de Janeiro in October?
In October, Rio de Janeiro averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Rio de Janeiro?
October is not one of Rio de Janeiro’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rio de Janeiro in October?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (197 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Rio de Janeiro in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rio de Janeiro get in October?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rio de Janeiro in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
