How April compares in Rio de Janeiro

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Rio de Janeiro. Compared with March: 1.4 °C cooler, 46 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. May is 2.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Rio de Janeiro in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rio de Janeiro in April?
In April, Rio de Janeiro averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Rio de Janeiro?
April is not one of Rio de Janeiro’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rio de Janeiro in April?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (197 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Rio de Janeiro in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rio de Janeiro get in April?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rio de Janeiro in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
