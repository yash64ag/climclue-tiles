How August compares in Rio de Janeiro

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rio de Janeiro. Compared with July: 0.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Rio de Janeiro in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rio de Janeiro in August?
In August, Rio de Janeiro averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Rio de Janeiro?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Rio de Janeiro’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rio de Janeiro in August?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (197 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Rio de Janeiro in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rio de Janeiro get in August?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rio de Janeiro in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
