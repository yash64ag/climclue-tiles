Serra do Ramalho at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Serra do Ramalho is 462nd warmest (26.4 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 2,782 hours of sunshine a year) and 1976th wettest (839 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bom Jesus da Lapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Serra do Ramalho is 0.4 °C cooler in October and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.4 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, July (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 506 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Serra do Ramalho, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Serra do Ramalho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 506 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-975-1364), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
