Paty do Alferes weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Paty do Alferes, Brazil (Rio de Janeiro), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Paty do Alferes at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Paty do Alferes is 1818th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 1905th sunniest (about 1,950 hours of sunshine a year) and 616th wettest (1,710 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Paty do Alferes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Paty do Alferes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.429°S, 43.419°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1064-1365), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
