Cruzeiro do Sul at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Cruzeiro do Sul is 721st warmest (25.5 °C year average), 2188th sunniest (about 1,656 hours of sunshine a year) and 181st wettest (2,165 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guajará, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Cruzeiro do Sul is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 114 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.0 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (24.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 669 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Cruzeiro do Sul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 669 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cruzeiro do Sul itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.628°S, 72.678°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-916-1073), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
