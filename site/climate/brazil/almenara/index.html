Almenara at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Almenara is 986th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 1652nd sunniest (about 2,090 hours of sunshine a year) and 1895th wettest (919 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jequitinhonha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Almenara is 0.7 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 220 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.7 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, July (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 183 km from the sea.

Why Almenara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1001-1393), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
