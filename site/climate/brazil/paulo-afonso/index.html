Paulo Afonso at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Paulo Afonso is the 3rd driest (532 mm a year). Compared with Itabaiana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (166 km away), Paulo Afonso is 1.6 °C warmer in January and 0.9 °C warmer in July, with 518 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (28.1 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, July (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.5 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 218 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Paulo Afonso, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Paulo Afonso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 218 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
