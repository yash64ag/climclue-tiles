São Francisco de Itabapoana sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio de Janeiro · Brazil

The sea off São Francisco de Itabapoana averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 22.3 °C (72 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to May.

São Francisco de Itabapoana sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São Francisco de Itabapoana?
In April, at about 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São Francisco de Itabapoana?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to May. The coolest month is August, at 22.3 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in São Francisco de Itabapoana in August?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in São Francisco de Itabapoana?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in São Francisco de Itabapoana nearby?
In April: Guarapari (26.1 °C, 85 km), Viana, Espírito Santo (26.4 °C, 112 km) and Vitória (26.4 °C, 127 km).
