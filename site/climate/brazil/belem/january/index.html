How January compares in Belém, Pará

January is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Belém, Pará. Compared with December: 0.6 °C warmer, 126 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. February is 1.7 °C cooler than January on average.

Belém, Pará in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belém, Pará in January?
In January, Belém, Pará averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 348 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Belém, Pará?
No month in Belém, Pará is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belém, Pará in January?
348 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (429 mm), the driest November (93 mm).
Does it snow in Belém, Pará in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belém, Pará get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Belém, Pará in January?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Belém, Pará in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
