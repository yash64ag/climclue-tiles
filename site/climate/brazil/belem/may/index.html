How May compares in Belém

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Belém. Compared with April: 0.4 °C warmer, 79 mm less precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. June is 0.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Belém in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belém in May?
In May, Belém averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Belém?
No month in Belém is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belém in May?
295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (421 mm), the driest October (82 mm).
Does it snow in Belém in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belém get in May?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belém in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
