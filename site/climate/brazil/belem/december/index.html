How December compares in Belém

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Belém. Compared with November: 0.5 °C cooler, 129 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. January is 0.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Belém in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belém in December?
In December, Belém averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Belém?
No month in Belém is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belém in December?
222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (429 mm), the driest November (93 mm).
Does it snow in Belém in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belém get in December?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belém in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
