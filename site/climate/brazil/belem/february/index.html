How February compares in Belém

February is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Belém. Compared with January: 0.3 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. March is about as warm as February on average.

Belém in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belém in February?
In February, Belém averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 354 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Belém?
No month in Belém is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belém in February?
354 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (421 mm), the driest October (82 mm).
Does it snow in Belém in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belém get in February?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belém in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
