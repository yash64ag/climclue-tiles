How August compares in Belém

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Belém. Compared with July: 0.5 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Belém in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belém in August?
In August, Belém averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Belém?
No month in Belém is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belém in August?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (421 mm), the driest October (82 mm).
Does it snow in Belém in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belém get in August?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belém in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
