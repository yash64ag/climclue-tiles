Poços de Caldas at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Poços de Caldas is 2176th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 1375th sunniest (about 2,172 hours of sunshine a year) and 269th wettest (2,034 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caconde, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Poços de Caldas is 2.9 °C cooler in February and 3.1 °C cooler in July, with 638 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.6 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, July (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 241 km from the sea.

Why Poços de Caldas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 241 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Poços de Caldas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.788°S, 46.561°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1057-1334), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
