Poços de Caldas at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Poços de Caldas is 325th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 176th sunniest (about 2,172 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (1,917 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mogi Guaçu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Poços de Caldas is 3.1 °C cooler in February and 2.7 °C cooler in June, with 364 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, June (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.7 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 241 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Poços de Caldas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Poços de Caldas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 241 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
