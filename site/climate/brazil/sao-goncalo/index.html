São Gonçalo at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, São Gonçalo is 111th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 229th sunniest (about 2,053 hours of sunshine a year) and 187th wettest (1,515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Niterói, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), São Gonçalo is 0.1 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.3 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of São Gonçalo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why São Gonçalo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
