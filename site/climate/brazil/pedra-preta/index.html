Pedra Preta at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Pedra Preta is 540th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 888th sunniest (about 2,332 hours of sunshine a year) and 1173rd wettest (1,452 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rondonópolis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Pedra Preta is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.6 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,160 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Pedra Preta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,160 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pedra Preta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.623°S, 54.474°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1006-1255), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
