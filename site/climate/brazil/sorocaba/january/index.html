How January compares in Sorocaba

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sorocaba. Compared with December: 0.6 °C warmer, 76 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Sorocaba in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sorocaba in January?
In January, Sorocaba averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sorocaba?
January is not one of Sorocaba’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sorocaba in January?
245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (245 mm), the driest August (32 mm).
Does it snow in Sorocaba in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sorocaba get in January?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sorocaba in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
