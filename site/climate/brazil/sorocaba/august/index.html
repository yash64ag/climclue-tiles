Sorocaba weather in August

Average August weather in Sorocaba, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Sorocaba averages 24.4 °C (76 °F) days and 12.9 °C (55 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 183 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Sorocaba

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Sorocaba. Compared with July: 1.3 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Sorocaba in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sorocaba in August?
In August, Sorocaba averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sorocaba?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Sorocaba’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sorocaba in August?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (245 mm), the driest August (32 mm).
Does it snow in Sorocaba in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sorocaba get in August?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sorocaba in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
