How October compares in Sorocaba

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sorocaba. Compared with September: 1.5 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Sorocaba in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sorocaba in October?
In October, Sorocaba averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sorocaba?
October is not one of Sorocaba’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sorocaba in October?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (247 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in Sorocaba in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sorocaba get in October?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sorocaba in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
