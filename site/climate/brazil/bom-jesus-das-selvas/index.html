Bom Jesus das Selvas at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Bom Jesus das Selvas is 418th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 1524th sunniest (about 2,135 hours of sunshine a year) and 1484th wettest (1,332 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buriticupu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Bom Jesus das Selvas is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C cooler in March, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.0 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, March (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 333 km from the sea.

Why Bom Jesus das Selvas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 333 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bom Jesus das Selvas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.484°S, 46.853°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-884-1331), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
