Why Jundiaí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jundiaí climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jundiaí?
February, with an average of 23.1 °C (73 °F), day highs around 27.1 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 18.8 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jundiaí?
July, with an average of 16.6 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.7 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 11.6 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jundiaí?
January, with 257 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,420 mm (55.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jundiaí?
August, with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jundiaí?
August, with about 196 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jundiaí?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Jundiaí?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Jundiaí’s GeoNames location (23.186°S, 46.884°W; ClimaClue area sq-1071-1331), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 443,221 (GeoNames).
