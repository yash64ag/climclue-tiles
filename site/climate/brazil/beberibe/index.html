Beberibe at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Beberibe is 291st warmest (26.8 °C year average), 207th sunniest (about 2,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 1667th wettest (1,190 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cascavel, Ceará, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Beberibe is 0.1 °C cooler in December and about as warm in July, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.3 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, July (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beberibe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beberibe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Beberibe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.180°S, 38.131°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-881-1418), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
