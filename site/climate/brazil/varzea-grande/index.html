Várzea Grande at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Várzea Grande is 47th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 2,335 hours of sunshine a year) and 215th wettest (1,474 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuiabá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Várzea Grande is 0.3 °C warmer in October and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 119 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.9 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, July (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,360 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Várzea Grande, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Várzea Grande has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,360 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
