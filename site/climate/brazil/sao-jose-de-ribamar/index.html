São José de Ribamar at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, São José de Ribamar is 34th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 83rd sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (2,029 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paço do Lumiar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), São José de Ribamar is about as warm in November and 0.1 °C warmer in March, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.3 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, March (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.5 °C (76 °F), in March. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of São José de Ribamar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why São José de Ribamar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
