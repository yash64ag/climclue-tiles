São José de Ribamar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maranhão · Brazil

The sea off São José de Ribamar averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 27.4 °C (81 °F) in October. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

São José de Ribamar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São José de Ribamar?
In May, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São José de Ribamar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is October, at 27.4 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in São José de Ribamar in October?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in São José de Ribamar?
About 28.1 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
