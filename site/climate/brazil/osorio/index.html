Osório at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Osório is 2100th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 1466th sunniest (about 2,149 hours of sunshine a year) and 665th wettest (1,683 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Imbé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Osório is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 260 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.8 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, July (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Osório, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Osório has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Osório itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.887°S, 50.270°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1138-1297), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
