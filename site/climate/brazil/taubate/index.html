Taubaté at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Taubaté is 226th warmest (21.2 °C year average), 294th sunniest (about 1,798 hours of sunshine a year) and 232nd wettest (1,450 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pindamonhangaba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Taubaté is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.1 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, July (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 73 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taubaté, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taubaté has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 73 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
