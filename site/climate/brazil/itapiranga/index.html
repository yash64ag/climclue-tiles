Itapiranga at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Itapiranga is 1808th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 1142nd sunniest (about 2,244 hours of sunshine a year) and 489th wettest (1,817 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Frederico Westphalen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Itapiranga is 1.8 °C warmer in January and 1.2 °C warmer in July, with 756 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.7 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, July (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 446 km from the sea.

Why Itapiranga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 446 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Itapiranga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.169°S, 53.712°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1111-1262), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
