Ibirité at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ibirité is 1827th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 1163rd sunniest (about 2,237 hours of sunshine a year) and 919th wettest (1,552 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sarzedo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ibirité is 0.9 °C cooler in February and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.0 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, July (17.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 334 km from the sea.

Why Ibirité has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 334 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ibirité itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.022°S, 44.059°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1040-1359), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
