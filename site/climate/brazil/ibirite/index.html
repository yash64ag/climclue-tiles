Why Ibirité has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 334 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ibirité climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ibirité?
February, with an average of 21.9 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 17.3 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ibirité?
July, with an average of 16.4 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.7 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 10.5 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ibirité?
December, with 402 mm (15.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,765 mm (69.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ibirité?
July, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ibirité?
July, with about 230 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ibirité?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ibirité?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Ibirité’s GeoNames location (20.022°S, 44.059°W; ClimaClue area sq-1040-1359), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 179,582 (GeoNames).
