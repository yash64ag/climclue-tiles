Ibirité at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Ibirité is 289th warmest (19.7 °C year average), 150th sunniest (about 2,237 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,765 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Contagem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Ibirité is 0.9 °C cooler in February and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 95 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.9 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 334 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ibirité, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ibirité has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 334 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
