Ituiutaba at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Ituiutaba is 99th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,629 hours of sunshine a year) and 268th wettest (1,387 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itumbiara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Ituiutaba is 0.8 °C cooler in October and 0.6 °C cooler in June, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.1 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, June (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in June. The area lies about 651 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ituiutaba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ituiutaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 651 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
