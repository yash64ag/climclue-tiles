Why Ituiutaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 651 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ituiutaba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ituiutaba?
October, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 31.3 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 21.3 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ituiutaba?
June, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 27.1 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 16.8 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ituiutaba?
January, with 263 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,387 mm (54.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ituiutaba?
July, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ituiutaba?
July, with about 271 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ituiutaba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ituiutaba?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Ituiutaba’s GeoNames location (18.974°S, 49.462°W; ClimaClue area sq-1029-1305), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 106,775 (GeoNames).
