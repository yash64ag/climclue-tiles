Lucas do Rio Verde at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Lucas do Rio Verde is 779th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 1627th sunniest (about 2,097 hours of sunshine a year) and 664th wettest (1,683 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sorriso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Lucas do Rio Verde is 0.1 °C cooler in September and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.5 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,345 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lucas do Rio Verde, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Lucas do Rio Verde

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lucas do Rio Verde are May, June and July.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June and July.
  • Warmest (average temperature): September (26.5 °C), October (26.2 °C) and August (25.5 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (3 mm), June (8 mm) and August (13 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (258 h), June (237 h) and August (219 h).

Why Lucas do Rio Verde has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,345 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lucas do Rio Verde itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.071°S, 55.915°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-970-1240), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
