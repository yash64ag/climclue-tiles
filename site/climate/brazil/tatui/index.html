Tatuí at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Tatuí is 243rd warmest (20.8 °C year average), 191st sunniest (about 2,133 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (1,417 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itapetininga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Tatuí is 0.8 °C warmer in February and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 93 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.9 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 150 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tatuí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tatuí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 150 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
