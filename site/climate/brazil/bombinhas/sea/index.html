Bombinhas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Santa Catarina · Brazil

The sea off Bombinhas averages 25.8 °C (78 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.6 °C (64 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to March.

Bombinhas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bombinhas?
In February, at about 25.8 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bombinhas?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to March. The coolest month is August, at 17.6 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bombinhas in August?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bombinhas?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
