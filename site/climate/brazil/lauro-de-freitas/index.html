Lauro de Freitas at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Lauro de Freitas is 69th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 139th sunniest (about 2,284 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (1,838 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Simões Filho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Lauro de Freitas is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 123 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.8 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, August (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lauro de Freitas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lauro de Freitas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
