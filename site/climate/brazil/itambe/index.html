Itambé, Pernambuco at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Itambé, Pernambuco is 794th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 307th sunniest (about 2,596 hours of sunshine a year) and 1513th wettest (1,321 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pedras de Fogo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Itambé, Pernambuco is about as warm in March and about as warm in July, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Why Itambé, Pernambuco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Itambé itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.410°S, 35.113°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-914-1448), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
