Japeri at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Japeri is 1122nd warmest (24.0 °C year average), 1833rd sunniest (about 2,004 hours of sunshine a year) and 1599th wettest (1,252 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paracambi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Japeri is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 128 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.3 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, July (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Why Japeri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Japeri itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.643°S, 43.653°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1066-1363), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
