Colombo at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Colombo is the 4th coolest (16.9 °C year average). Compared with Almirante Tamandaré, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Colombo is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 108 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.4 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, July (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 101 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Colombo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Colombo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 101 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
