Boa Vista do Tupim at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Boa Vista do Tupim is 745th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 2249th sunniest (about 1,526 hours of sunshine a year) and 2171st wettest (635 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itaberaba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Boa Vista do Tupim is 0.8 °C cooler in February and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.3 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, July (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 206 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Boa Vista do Tupim, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Boa Vista do Tupim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 206 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Boa Vista do Tupim itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.662°S, 40.607°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-966-1393), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
