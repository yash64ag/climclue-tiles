How November compares in Goiânia

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Goiânia. Compared with October: 1.1 °C cooler, 71 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. December is 0.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Goiânia in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Goiânia in November?
In November, Goiânia averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Goiânia?
November is not one of Goiânia’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Goiânia in November?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Goiânia in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Goiânia get in November?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Goiânia in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
