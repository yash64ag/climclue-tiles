How August compares in Goiânia

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Goiânia. Compared with July: 2.2 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 1.6 °C warmer than August on average.

Goiânia in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Goiânia in August?
In August, Goiânia averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Goiânia?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Goiânia’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Goiânia in August?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (266 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Goiânia in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Goiânia get in August?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Goiânia in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
