How March compares in Goiânia

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Goiânia. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 31 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is about as warm as March on average.

Goiânia in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Goiânia in March?
In March, Goiânia averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Goiânia?
March is not one of Goiânia’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Goiânia in March?
235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Goiânia in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Goiânia get in March?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Goiânia in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
