How June compares in Goiânia

June is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Goiânia. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 0.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Goiânia in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Goiânia in June?
In June, Goiânia averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Goiânia?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Goiânia’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Goiânia in June?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (264 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Goiânia in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Goiânia get in June?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Goiânia in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
