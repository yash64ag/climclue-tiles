How June compares in Osasco

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Osasco. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Osasco in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osasco in June?
In June, Osasco averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Osasco?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Osasco’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osasco in June?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (246 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Osasco in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osasco get in June?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osasco in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
