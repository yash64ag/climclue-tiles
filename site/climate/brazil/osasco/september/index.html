Osasco weather in September

Average September weather in Osasco, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Osasco averages 23.6 °C (74 °F) days and 13.6 °C (56 °F) nights, with 85 mm (3.3 in) of rain over the month and about 132 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Osasco

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Osasco. Compared with August: 0.9 °C warmer, 50 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. October is 1.4 °C warmer than September on average.

Osasco in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osasco in September?
In September, Osasco averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Osasco?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Osasco’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osasco in September?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (240 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Osasco in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osasco get in September?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osasco in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
