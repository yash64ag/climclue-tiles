How March compares in Osasco

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Osasco. Compared with February: 0.7 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C cooler than March on average.

Osasco in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osasco in March?
In March, Osasco averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Osasco?
March is not one of Osasco’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osasco in March?
164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (246 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Osasco in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osasco get in March?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osasco in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
