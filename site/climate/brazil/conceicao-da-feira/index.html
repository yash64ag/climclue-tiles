Conceição da Feira at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Conceição da Feira is 1035th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 1703rd sunniest (about 2,070 hours of sunshine a year) and 1758th wettest (1,082 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Gonçalo dos Campos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Conceição da Feira is 0.1 °C cooler in February and about as warm in July, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.1 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in August. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Conceição da Feira, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Conceição da Feira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Conceição da Feira itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.506°S, 38.999°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-965-1410), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
