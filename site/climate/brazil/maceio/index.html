Maceió at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Maceió is 72nd warmest (25.4 °C year average), 45th sunniest (about 2,547 hours of sunshine a year) and 77th wettest (1,798 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arapiraca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Maceió is 0.1 °C cooler in March and 1.4 °C warmer in August, with 747 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.4 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, August (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maceió, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maceió has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
