Maceió in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maceió in January?
In January, Maceió averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Maceió?
No month in Maceió is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maceió in January?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (295 mm), the driest November (43 mm).
Does it snow in Maceió in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maceió get in January?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maceió in January?
