Maceió in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maceió in October?
In October, Maceió averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Maceió?
No month in Maceió is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
