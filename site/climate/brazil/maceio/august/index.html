Maceió in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maceió in August?
In August, Maceió averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Maceió?
