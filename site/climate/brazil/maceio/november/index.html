Maceió in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maceió in November?
In November, Maceió averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Maceió?
No month in Maceió is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maceió in November?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (295 mm), the driest November (43 mm).
Does it snow in Maceió in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maceió get in November?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maceió in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
