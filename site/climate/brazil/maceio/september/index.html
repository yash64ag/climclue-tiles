Maceió in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maceió in September?
In September, Maceió averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Maceió?
No month in Maceió is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
