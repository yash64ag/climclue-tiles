América Dourada at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, América Dourada is 1275th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 993rd sunniest (about 2,299 hours of sunshine a year) and 2241st wettest (546 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cafarnaum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), América Dourada is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.9 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 346 km from the sea.

Why América Dourada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 346 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
