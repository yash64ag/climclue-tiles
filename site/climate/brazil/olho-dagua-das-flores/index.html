Olho d'Água das Flores at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Olho d'Água das Flores is 622nd warmest (25.8 °C year average), 357th sunniest (about 2,574 hours of sunshine a year) and 2134th wettest (668 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Olho d'Água das Flores has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
