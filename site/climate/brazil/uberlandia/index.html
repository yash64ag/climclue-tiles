Uberlândia at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Uberlândia is 180th warmest (22.1 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,620 hours of sunshine a year) and 190th wettest (1,521 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Araguari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Uberlândia is about as warm in October and 0.2 °C warmer in June, with 123 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.8 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, June (19.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 595 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Uberlândia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Uberlândia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 595 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
