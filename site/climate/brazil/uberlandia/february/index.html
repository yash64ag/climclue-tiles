How February compares in Uberlândia

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Uberlândia. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 90 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Uberlândia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Uberlândia in February?
In February, Uberlândia averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Uberlândia?
February is not one of Uberlândia’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Uberlândia in February?
200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (297 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Uberlândia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Uberlândia get in February?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Uberlândia in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
