How December compares in Uberlândia

December is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Uberlândia. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 108 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Uberlândia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Uberlândia in December?
In December, Uberlândia averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 297 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Uberlândia?
December is not one of Uberlândia’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Uberlândia in December?
297 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (297 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Uberlândia in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Uberlândia get in December?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Uberlândia in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
