How August compares in Uberlândia

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Uberlândia. Compared with July: 1.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. September is 1.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Uberlândia in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Uberlândia in August?
In August, Uberlândia averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Uberlândia?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Uberlândia’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Uberlândia in August?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (297 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Uberlândia in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Uberlândia get in August?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Uberlândia in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
