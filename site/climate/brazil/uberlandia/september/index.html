How September compares in Uberlândia

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Uberlândia. Compared with August: 1.7 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Uberlândia in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Uberlândia in September?
In September, Uberlândia averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Uberlândia?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Uberlândia’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Uberlândia in September?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (297 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Uberlândia in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Uberlândia get in September?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Uberlândia in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
