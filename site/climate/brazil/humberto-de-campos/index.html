Humberto de Campos at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Humberto de Campos is 280th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 438th sunniest (about 2,530 hours of sunshine a year) and 532nd wettest (1,775 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Primeira Cruz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Humberto de Campos is 0.3 °C warmer in November and about as warm in March, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.6 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, March (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Why Humberto de Campos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
