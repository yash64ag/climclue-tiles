How May compares in Londrina

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Londrina. Compared with April: 3.5 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Londrina in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Londrina in May?
In May, Londrina averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Londrina?
May is not one of Londrina’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Londrina in May?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (241 mm), the driest August (52 mm).
Does it snow in Londrina in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Londrina get in May?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Londrina in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
