How November compares in Londrina

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Londrina. Compared with October: 1.2 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. December is 0.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Londrina in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Londrina in November?
In November, Londrina averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Londrina?
November is not one of Londrina’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Londrina in November?
174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (252 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Londrina in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Londrina get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Londrina in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
