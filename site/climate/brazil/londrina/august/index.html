How August compares in Londrina

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Londrina. Compared with July: 1.9 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. September is 1.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Londrina in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Londrina in August?
In August, Londrina averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Londrina?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Londrina’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Londrina in August?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (241 mm), the driest August (52 mm).
Does it snow in Londrina in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Londrina get in August?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Londrina in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
