How January compares in Londrina

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Londrina. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Londrina in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Londrina in January?
In January, Londrina averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Londrina?
January is not one of Londrina’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Londrina in January?
241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (241 mm), the driest August (52 mm).
Does it snow in Londrina in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Londrina get in January?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Londrina in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
