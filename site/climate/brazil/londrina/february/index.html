How February compares in Londrina

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Londrina. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 49 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. March is 0.3 °C cooler than February on average.

Londrina in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Londrina in February?
In February, Londrina averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Londrina?
February is not one of Londrina’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Londrina in February?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (241 mm), the driest August (52 mm).
Does it snow in Londrina in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Londrina get in February?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Londrina in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
