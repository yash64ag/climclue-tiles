Ceilândia at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Ceilândia is 213th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 2,337 hours of sunshine a year) and 174th wettest (1,541 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Águas Claras, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Ceilândia is 0.6 °C warmer in September and 0.6 °C warmer in June, with 183 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, June (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 895 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ceilândia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ceilândia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 895 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
