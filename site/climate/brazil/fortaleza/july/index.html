How July compares in Fortaleza

July is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Fortaleza. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Fortaleza in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fortaleza in July?
In July, Fortaleza averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Fortaleza?
No month in Fortaleza is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fortaleza in July?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (308 mm), the driest October (10 mm).
Does it snow in Fortaleza in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fortaleza get in July?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fortaleza in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
