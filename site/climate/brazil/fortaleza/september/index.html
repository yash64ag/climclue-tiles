How September compares in Fortaleza

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Fortaleza. Compared with August: 0.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 100 hours more sunshine. October is 0.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Fortaleza in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fortaleza in September?
In September, Fortaleza averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Fortaleza?
No month in Fortaleza is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fortaleza in September?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (308 mm), the driest October (10 mm).
Does it snow in Fortaleza in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fortaleza get in September?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fortaleza in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
