Fortaleza in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fortaleza in August?
In August, Fortaleza averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Fortaleza?
No month in Fortaleza is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fortaleza in August?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (316 mm), the driest October (10 mm).
Does it snow in Fortaleza in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fortaleza get in August?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Fortaleza in August?
About 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (28.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Fortaleza in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
