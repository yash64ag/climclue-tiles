How December compares in Fortaleza

December is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Fortaleza. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Fortaleza in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fortaleza in December?
In December, Fortaleza averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fortaleza?
No month in Fortaleza is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fortaleza in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (308 mm), the driest October (10 mm).
Does it snow in Fortaleza in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fortaleza get in December?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fortaleza in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
