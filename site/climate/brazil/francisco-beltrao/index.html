Francisco Beltrão at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Francisco Beltrão is 317th warmest (19.4 °C year average), 167th sunniest (about 2,198 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (2,047 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chapecó, the nearest covered town with different numbers (121 km away), Francisco Beltrão is 0.6 °C warmer in January and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 258 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 9.0 °C more than its coolest, July (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 450 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Francisco Beltrão, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Francisco Beltrão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 450 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
