Piracuruca at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Piracuruca is the warmest (29.2 °C year average). Compared with Piripiri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Piracuruca is 0.4 °C warmer in November and 0.5 °C warmer in April, with 231 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (31.5 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, April (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in February. The area lies about 124 km from the sea.

Why Piracuruca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Piracuruca itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.928°S, 41.709°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-879-1382), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
