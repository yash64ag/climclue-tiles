How August compares in Florianópolis

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Florianópolis. Compared with July: 0.7 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C warmer than August on average.

Florianópolis in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Florianópolis in August?
In August, Florianópolis averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Florianópolis?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Florianópolis’s mild months (June and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Florianópolis in August?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (224 mm), the driest June (78 mm).
Does it snow in Florianópolis in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Florianópolis get in August?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Florianópolis in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
