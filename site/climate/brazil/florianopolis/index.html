Florianópolis at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Florianópolis is 250th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 253rd sunniest (about 2,002 hours of sunshine a year) and 117th wettest (1,675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São José, Santa Catarina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Florianópolis is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.8 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Florianópolis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Florianópolis

For mild weather, the best months to visit Florianópolis are June and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June and August.

Why Florianópolis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
