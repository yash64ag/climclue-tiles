How December compares in Florianópolis

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Florianópolis. Compared with November: 1.7 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Florianópolis in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Florianópolis in December?
In December, Florianópolis averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Florianópolis?
December is not one of Florianópolis’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Florianópolis in December?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (231 mm), the driest June (80 mm).
Does it snow in Florianópolis in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Florianópolis get in December?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Florianópolis in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
