How January compares in Florianópolis

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Florianópolis. Compared with December: 1.1 °C warmer, 68 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Florianópolis in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Florianópolis in January?
In January, Florianópolis averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Florianópolis?
January is not one of Florianópolis’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Florianópolis in January?
231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (231 mm), the driest June (80 mm).
Does it snow in Florianópolis in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Florianópolis get in January?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Florianópolis in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
