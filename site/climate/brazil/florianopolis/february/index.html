How February compares in Florianópolis

February is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Florianópolis. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 34 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 0.7 °C cooler than February on average.

Florianópolis in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Florianópolis in February?
In February, Florianópolis averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Florianópolis?
February is not one of Florianópolis’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Florianópolis in February?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (231 mm), the driest June (80 mm).
Does it snow in Florianópolis in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Florianópolis get in February?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Florianópolis in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
