How April compares in Florianópolis

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Florianópolis. Compared with March: 2.3 °C cooler, 54 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. May is 2.9 °C cooler than April on average.

Florianópolis in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Florianópolis in April?
In April, Florianópolis averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Florianópolis?
April is not one of Florianópolis’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Florianópolis in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (224 mm), the driest June (78 mm).
Does it snow in Florianópolis in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Florianópolis get in April?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Florianópolis in April?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Florianópolis in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
