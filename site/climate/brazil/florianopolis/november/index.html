How November compares in Florianópolis

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Florianópolis. Compared with October: 1.9 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. December is 1.7 °C warmer than November on average.

Florianópolis in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Florianópolis in November?
In November, Florianópolis averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Florianópolis?
November is not one of Florianópolis’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Florianópolis in November?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (224 mm), the driest June (78 mm).
Does it snow in Florianópolis in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Florianópolis get in November?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Florianópolis in November?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Florianópolis in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
