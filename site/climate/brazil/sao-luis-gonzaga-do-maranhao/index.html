Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São Luís Gonzaga do Maranhão is 114th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 857th sunniest (about 2,342 hours of sunshine a year) and 805th wettest (1,609 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why São Luís Gonzaga do Maranhão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 190 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
