Plano Piloto at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Plano Piloto is 201st warmest (21.7 °C year average), 97th sunniest (about 2,363 hours of sunshine a year) and 267th wettest (1,392 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Brasília, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Plano Piloto is 0.2 °C warmer in October and about as warm in July, with 115 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.3 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 892 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Plano Piloto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Plano Piloto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 892 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
