Castelo at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Castelo is 1252nd warmest (23.5 °C year average), 1049th sunniest (about 2,275 hours of sunshine a year) and 1221st wettest (1,431 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vargem Alta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Castelo is 3.1 °C warmer in February and 3.0 °C warmer in July, with 322 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.7 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 61 km from the sea.

Why Castelo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 61 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Castelo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.604°S, 41.185°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1046-1388), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
