Sombrio sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Santa Catarina · Brazil

The sea off Sombrio averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 16.0 °C (61 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March.

Sombrio sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sombrio?
In March, at about 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sombrio?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March. The coolest month is July, at 16.0 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sombrio in July?
About 16.0 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sombrio?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
