Brusque at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Brusque is 290th warmest (19.7 °C year average), 300th sunniest (about 1,761 hours of sunshine a year) and 80th wettest (1,793 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Blumenau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Brusque is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 40 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.9 °C), averages 8.8 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Brusque, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Brusque has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
