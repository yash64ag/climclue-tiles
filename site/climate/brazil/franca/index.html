Franca at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Franca is 231st warmest (21.1 °C year average), 333rd sunniest (about 1,624 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (1,859 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ribeirão Preto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (83 km away), Franca is 2.1 °C cooler in February and 1.7 °C cooler in June, with 325 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.8 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, June (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 398 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Franca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Franca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 398 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
