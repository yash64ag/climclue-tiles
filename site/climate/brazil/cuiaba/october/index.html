How October compares in Cuiabá

October is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cuiabá. Compared with September: 0.9 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. November is 0.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Cuiabá in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in October?
In October, Cuiabá averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Cuiabá?
October is not one of Cuiabá’s mild months; those are May, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in October?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in October?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
