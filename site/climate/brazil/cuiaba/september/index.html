How September compares in Cuiabá

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cuiabá. Compared with August: 1.4 °C warmer, 50 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. October is 0.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Cuiabá in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in September?
In September, Cuiabá averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cuiabá?
September is not one of Cuiabá’s mild months; those are May, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in September?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in September?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
