How November compares in Cuiabá

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cuiabá. Compared with October: 0.4 °C cooler, 66 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 0.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Cuiabá in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in November?
In November, Cuiabá averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cuiabá?
November is not one of Cuiabá’s mild months; those are May, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in November?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in November?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
