How July compares in Cuiabá

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Cuiabá. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. August is 1.9 °C warmer than July on average.

Cuiabá in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in July?
In July, Cuiabá averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cuiabá?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Cuiabá’s mild months (May, June and July), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in July?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in July?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
