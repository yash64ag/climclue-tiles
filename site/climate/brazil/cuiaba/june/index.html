How June compares in Cuiabá

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Cuiabá. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 0.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Cuiabá in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in June?
In June, Cuiabá averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cuiabá?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Cuiabá’s mild months (May, June and July), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in June?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in June?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
