How December compares in Cuiabá

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Cuiabá. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 40 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Cuiabá in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in December?
In December, Cuiabá averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cuiabá?
December is not one of Cuiabá’s mild months; those are May, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in December?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
