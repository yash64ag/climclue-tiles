How April compares in Cuiabá

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cuiabá. Compared with March: 0.2 °C cooler, 86 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. May is 1.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Cuiabá in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in April?
In April, Cuiabá averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cuiabá?
April is not one of Cuiabá’s mild months; those are May, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in April?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (253 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in April?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
