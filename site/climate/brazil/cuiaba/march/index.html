Cuiabá in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuiabá in March?
In March, Cuiabá averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 224 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cuiabá?
March is not one of Cuiabá’s mild months; those are May, June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuiabá in March?
224 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (273 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Cuiabá in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuiabá get in March?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuiabá in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
