Itapipoca at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itapipoca is 24th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,447 hours of sunshine a year) and 332nd wettest (1,001 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sobral, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Itapipoca is 1.1 °C cooler in November and 0.2 °C cooler in April, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.6 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, April (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.1 °C (74 °F), in June. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itapipoca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itapipoca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
