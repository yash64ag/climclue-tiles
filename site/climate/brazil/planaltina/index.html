Planaltina, Federal District at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Planaltina, Federal District is 173rd warmest (22.2 °C year average), 84th sunniest (about 2,392 hours of sunshine a year) and 286th wettest (1,337 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Planaltina, Goiás, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Planaltina, Federal District is 0.1 °C warmer in October and about as warm in July, with 69 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 895 km from the sea (deep inland).
