Itabuna at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itabuna is 103rd warmest (24.1 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 2,372 hours of sunshine a year) and 185th wettest (1,533 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ilhéus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Itabuna is 0.4 °C cooler in February and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 315 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.8 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in August. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itabuna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itabuna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
