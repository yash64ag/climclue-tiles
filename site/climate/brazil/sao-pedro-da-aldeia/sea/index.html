São Pedro da Aldeia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio de Janeiro · Brazil

The sea off São Pedro da Aldeia averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 21.5 °C (71 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April.

São Pedro da Aldeia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São Pedro da Aldeia?
In March, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São Pedro da Aldeia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is September, at 21.5 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in São Pedro da Aldeia in September?
About 21.5 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in São Pedro da Aldeia?
About 23.4 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
