Nova Soure at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Nova Soure is 573rd warmest (25.9 °C year average), 852nd sunniest (about 2,346 hours of sunshine a year) and 2210th wettest (581 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cipó, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Nova Soure is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.6 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 122 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nova Soure, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nova Soure has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 122 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-952-1415), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
