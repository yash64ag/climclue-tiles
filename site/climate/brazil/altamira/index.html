Altamira at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Altamira is 44th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 303rd sunniest (about 1,756 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (2,089 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Breves, the nearest covered town with different numbers (256 km away), Altamira is 0.1 °C cooler in September and 0.4 °C cooler in February, with 395 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (27.8 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, February (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 189 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Altamira, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Altamira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 189 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
