João Pessoa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in João Pessoa in December?
In December, João Pessoa averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit João Pessoa?
No month in João Pessoa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in João Pessoa in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (270 mm), the driest November (24 mm).
Does it snow in João Pessoa in December?
