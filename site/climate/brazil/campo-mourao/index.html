Campo Mourão at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Campo Mourão is 244th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 236th sunniest (about 2,035 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (1,737 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maringá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Campo Mourão is 0.6 °C cooler in January and 1.5 °C cooler in July, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.9 °C), averages 7.9 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 441 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Campo Mourão, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Campo Mourão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 441 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
