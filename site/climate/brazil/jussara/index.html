Jussara, Goiás at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Jussara, Goiás is 463rd warmest (26.4 °C year average), 997th sunniest (about 2,296 hours of sunshine a year) and 917th wettest (1,553 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Iporá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Jussara, Goiás is 1.9 °C warmer in September and 1.9 °C warmer in July, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (28.3 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, July (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,026 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jussara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Jussara, Goiás

For mild weather, the best months to visit Jussara, Goiás are June and July.

Why Jussara, Goiás has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,026 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jussara itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.865°S, 50.868°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-998-1291), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
