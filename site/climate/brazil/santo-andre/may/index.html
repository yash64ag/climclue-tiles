How May compares in Santo André

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Santo André. Compared with April: 2.6 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Santo André in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo André in May?
In May, Santo André averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Santo André?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Santo André’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo André in May?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (310 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Santo André in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo André get in May?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo André in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
