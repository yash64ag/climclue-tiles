How October compares in Santo André

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Santo André. Compared with September: 1.5 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Santo André in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo André in October?
In October, Santo André averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Santo André?
October is not one of Santo André’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo André in October?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (310 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Santo André in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo André get in October?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo André in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
