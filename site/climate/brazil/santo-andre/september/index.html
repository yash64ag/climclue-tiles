How September compares in Santo André

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santo André. Compared with August: 0.8 °C warmer, 63 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Santo André in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo André in September?
In September, Santo André averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Santo André?
September is not one of Santo André’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo André in September?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (311 mm), the driest August (46 mm).
Does it snow in Santo André in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo André get in September?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo André in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
