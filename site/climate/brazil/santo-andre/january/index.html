How January compares in Santo André

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Santo André. Compared with December: 0.9 °C warmer, 87 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Santo André in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo André in January?
In January, Santo André averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 311 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Santo André?
January is not one of Santo André’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo André in January?
311 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (311 mm), the driest August (46 mm).
Does it snow in Santo André in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo André get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo André in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
