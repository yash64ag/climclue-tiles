Ouro Branco at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ouro Branco is 2082nd warmest (19.3 °C year average), 1868th sunniest (about 1,976 hours of sunshine a year) and 519th wettest (1,787 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Congonhas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Ouro Branco is 0.8 °C cooler in February and 0.9 °C cooler in July, with 237 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.9 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (16.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 281 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ouro Branco, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ouro Branco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 281 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ouro Branco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.523°S, 43.695°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1045-1363), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
