Poconé at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Poconé is 491st warmest (26.3 °C year average), 1418th sunniest (about 2,160 hours of sunshine a year) and 1269th wettest (1,417 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santo Antônio do Leverger, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Poconé is about as warm in October and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, July (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,345 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Poconé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,345 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Poconé itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.257°S, 56.623°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1002-1233), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
