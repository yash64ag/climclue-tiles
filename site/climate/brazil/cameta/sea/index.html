Cametá sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pará · Brazil

The sea off Cametá averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 27.4 °C (81 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Cametá sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cametá?
In November, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cametá?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 27.4 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cametá in February?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Cametá?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Cametá nearby?
In November: Chaves (29.1 °C, 238 km).
