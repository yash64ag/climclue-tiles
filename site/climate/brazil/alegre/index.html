Alegre at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Alegre is 1401st warmest (22.9 °C year average), 1077th sunniest (about 2,266 hours of sunshine a year) and 1485th wettest (1,331 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guaçuí, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Alegre is 2.0 °C warmer in February and 1.9 °C warmer in July, with 187 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.9 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, July (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 90 km from the sea.

Why Alegre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 90 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Alegre itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.764°S, 41.533°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1047-1384), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
