Why Santarém has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 299 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Santarém climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Santarém?
October, with an average of 28.6 °C (84 °F), day highs around 31.3 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 26.1 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Santarém?
February, with an average of 26.4 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 24.6 °C (76 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Santarém?
March, with 408 mm (16.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,280 mm (89.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Santarém?
September, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Santarém?
August, with about 237 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Santarém?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Santarém?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Santarém have?
Tropical monsoon (Köppen Am). Hot all year, with a very wet monsoon season and a short drier spell. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Santarém’s GeoNames location (2.443°S, 54.708°W; ClimaClue area sq-864-1252), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 360,871 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
