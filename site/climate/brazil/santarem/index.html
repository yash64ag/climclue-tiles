Santarém at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Santarém is 13th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 240th sunniest (about 2,029 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (2,280 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Parintins, the nearest covered town with different numbers (226 km away), Santarém is 0.3 °C warmer in October and about as warm in February, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.6 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, February (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.5 °C (76 °F), in June. The area lies about 299 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santarém, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santarém has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 299 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
