Why Jequié has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Jequié climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jequié?
February, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.7 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 21.6 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jequié?
July, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 24.3 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 17.7 °C (64 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jequié?
December, with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 704 mm (27.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jequié?
September, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jequié?
March, with about 193 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jequié?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Jequié?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Jequié have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Jequié’s GeoNames location (13.859°S, 40.085°W; ClimaClue area sq-978-1399), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 127,475 (GeoNames).
