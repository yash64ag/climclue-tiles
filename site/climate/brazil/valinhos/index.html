Valinhos at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Valinhos is 238th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 2,335 hours of sunshine a year) and 168th wettest (1,537 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Valinhos is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 70 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.7 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 140 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valinhos, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valinhos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 140 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Valinhos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.971°S, 46.996°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1069-1330), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
