Otacílio Costa at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Otacílio Costa is 2266th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 2061st sunniest (about 1,808 hours of sunshine a year) and 503rd wettest (1,802 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Correia Pinto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Otacílio Costa is 0.4 °C cooler in January and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 186 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.4 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, July (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 167 km from the sea.

Why Otacílio Costa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 167 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Otacílio Costa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.483°S, 50.122°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1114-1298), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
