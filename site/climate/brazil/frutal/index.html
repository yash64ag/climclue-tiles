Frutal at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Frutal is 1093rd warmest (24.1 °C year average), 365th sunniest (about 2,571 hours of sunshine a year) and 1035th wettest (1,511 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Conceição das Alagoas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Frutal is 0.2 °C cooler in October and 0.2 °C cooler in June, with 37 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.7 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, June (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 519 km from the sea.

Why Frutal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 519 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Frutal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.025°S, 48.941°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1040-1310), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
