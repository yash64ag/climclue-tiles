Presidente Prudente at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Presidente Prudente is 151st warmest (23.1 °C year average), 174th sunniest (about 2,180 hours of sunshine a year) and 237th wettest (1,438 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Assis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (117 km away), Presidente Prudente is 1.0 °C warmer in January and 1.0 °C warmer in June, with 136 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.4 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, June (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 488 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Presidente Prudente, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Presidente Prudente has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 488 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
