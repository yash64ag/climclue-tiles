Colônia Leopoldina at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Colônia Leopoldina is 983rd warmest (24.5 °C year average), 761st sunniest (about 2,377 hours of sunshine a year) and 845th wettest (1,588 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Colônia Leopoldina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-929-1442), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
