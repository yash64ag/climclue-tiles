Arraial do Cabo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio de Janeiro · Brazil

The sea off Arraial do Cabo averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 21.3 °C (70 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to April.

Arraial do Cabo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Arraial do Cabo?
In March, at about 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Arraial do Cabo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is September, at 21.3 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in Arraial do Cabo in September?
About 21.3 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Arraial do Cabo?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
