How January compares in Recife

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Recife. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Recife in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Recife in January?
In January, Recife averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Recife?
No month in Recife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Recife in January?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (299 mm), the driest November (41 mm).
Does it snow in Recife in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Recife get in January?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Recife in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
