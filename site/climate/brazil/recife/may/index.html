How May compares in Recife

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Recife. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Recife in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Recife in May?
In May, Recife averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Recife?
No month in Recife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Recife in May?
258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (297 mm), the driest November (41 mm).
Does it snow in Recife in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Recife get in May?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Recife in May?
About 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Recife in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
