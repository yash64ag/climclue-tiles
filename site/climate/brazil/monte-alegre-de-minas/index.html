Monte Alegre de Minas at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Monte Alegre de Minas is 1365th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 273rd sunniest (about 2,615 hours of sunshine a year) and 1061st wettest (1,500 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tupaciguara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Monte Alegre de Minas is 0.7 °C warmer in October and 0.8 °C warmer in June, with 118 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 632 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Monte Alegre de Minas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 632 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Monte Alegre de Minas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.871°S, 48.881°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1028-1311), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
