Valparaíso de Goiás at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Valparaíso de Goiás is 223rd warmest (21.3 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,358 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (1,650 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novo Gama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Valparaíso de Goiás is 0.3 °C cooler in October and 0.4 °C cooler in June, with 149 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (22.9 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, June (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 866 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valparaíso de Goiás, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valparaíso de Goiás has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 866 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
