How October compares in Feira de Santana

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Feira de Santana. Compared with September: 1.6 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Feira de Santana in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in October?
In October, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
October is not one of Feira de Santana’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in October?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in October?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
