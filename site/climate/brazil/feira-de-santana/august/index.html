Feira de Santana weather in August

Average August weather in Feira de Santana, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Feira de Santana averages 27.4 °C (81 °F) days and 17.1 °C (63 °F) nights, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of rain over the month and about 171 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Feira de Santana

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Feira de Santana. Compared with July: 0.2 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 1.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Feira de Santana in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in August?
In August, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Feira de Santana’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in August?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (98 mm), the driest October (41 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in August?
