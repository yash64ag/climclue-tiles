How September compares in Feira de Santana

Feira de Santana in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in September?
In September, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Feira de Santana’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in September?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in September?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
