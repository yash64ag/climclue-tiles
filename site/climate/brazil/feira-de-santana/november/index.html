Feira de Santana in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in November?
In November, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
November is not one of Feira de Santana’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in November?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in November?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
