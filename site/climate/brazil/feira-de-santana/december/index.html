Feira de Santana in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in December?
In December, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
December is not one of Feira de Santana’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in December?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in December?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
