Why Feira de Santana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Feira de Santana climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Feira de Santana?
February, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 31.3 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 22.1 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Feira de Santana?
July, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 25.2 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 18.7 °C (66 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Feira de Santana?
April, with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 895 mm (35.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Feira de Santana?
October, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Feira de Santana?
January, with about 192 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Feira de Santana?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Feira de Santana’s GeoNames location (12.267°S, 38.967°W; ClimaClue area sq-962-1410), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 616,272 (GeoNames).
