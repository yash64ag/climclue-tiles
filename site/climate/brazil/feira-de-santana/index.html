Feira de Santana at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Feira de Santana is 91st warmest (24.7 °C year average), 235th sunniest (about 2,040 hours of sunshine a year) and 336th wettest (895 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alagoinhas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Feira de Santana is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 260 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, July (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 80 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Feira de Santana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Feira de Santana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
