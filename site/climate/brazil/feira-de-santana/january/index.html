Feira de Santana in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in January?
In January, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
January is not one of Feira de Santana’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in January?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in January?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
