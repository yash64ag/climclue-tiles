Feira de Santana in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in July?
In July, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Feira de Santana’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in July?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in July?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
