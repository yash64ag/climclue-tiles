How June compares in Feira de Santana

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Feira de Santana. Compared with May: 1.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Feira de Santana in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feira de Santana in June?
In June, Feira de Santana averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Feira de Santana?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Feira de Santana’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feira de Santana in June?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (104 mm), the driest October (43 mm).
Does it snow in Feira de Santana in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feira de Santana get in June?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feira de Santana in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
