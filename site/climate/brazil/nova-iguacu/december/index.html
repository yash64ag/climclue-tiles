Nova Iguaçu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nova Iguaçu in December?
In December, Nova Iguaçu averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nova Iguaçu?
December is not one of Nova Iguaçu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nova Iguaçu in December?
214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (234 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Nova Iguaçu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nova Iguaçu get in December?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nova Iguaçu in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
