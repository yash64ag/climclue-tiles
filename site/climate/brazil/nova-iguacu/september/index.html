How September compares in Nova Iguaçu

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nova Iguaçu. Compared with August: 0.7 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 1.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Nova Iguaçu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nova Iguaçu in September?
In September, Nova Iguaçu averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nova Iguaçu?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Nova Iguaçu’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nova Iguaçu in September?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (243 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Nova Iguaçu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nova Iguaçu get in September?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nova Iguaçu in September?
About 21.0 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nova Iguaçu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
