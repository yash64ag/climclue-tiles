Nova Iguaçu weather in October

Average October weather in Nova Iguaçu, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Nova Iguaçu averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) days and 18.8 °C (66 °F) nights, with 106 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Nova Iguaçu

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nova Iguaçu. Compared with September: 1.5 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Nova Iguaçu in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nova Iguaçu in October?
In October, Nova Iguaçu averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nova Iguaçu?
October is not one of Nova Iguaçu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nova Iguaçu in October?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (243 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Nova Iguaçu in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nova Iguaçu get in October?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nova Iguaçu in October?
About 21.4 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nova Iguaçu in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
