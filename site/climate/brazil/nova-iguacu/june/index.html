How June compares in Nova Iguaçu

Nova Iguaçu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nova Iguaçu in June?
In June, Nova Iguaçu averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nova Iguaçu?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Nova Iguaçu’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nova Iguaçu in June?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (234 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Nova Iguaçu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nova Iguaçu get in June?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nova Iguaçu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
