How February compares in Nova Iguaçu

February is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nova Iguaçu. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 77 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 0.9 °C cooler than February on average.

Nova Iguaçu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nova Iguaçu in February?
In February, Nova Iguaçu averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nova Iguaçu?
February is not one of Nova Iguaçu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nova Iguaçu in February?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (234 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Nova Iguaçu in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nova Iguaçu get in February?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nova Iguaçu in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
