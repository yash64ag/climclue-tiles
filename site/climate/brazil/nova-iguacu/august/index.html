How August compares in Nova Iguaçu

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nova Iguaçu. Compared with July: 0.8 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. September is 0.8 °C warmer than August on average.

Nova Iguaçu in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nova Iguaçu in August?
In August, Nova Iguaçu averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nova Iguaçu?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Nova Iguaçu’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nova Iguaçu in August?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (234 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Nova Iguaçu in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nova Iguaçu get in August?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nova Iguaçu in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
