Mossoró at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Mossoró is the 3rd warmest (27.9 °C year average). Compared with Maracanaú, the nearest covered town with different numbers (204 km away), Mossoró is 1.1 °C warmer in November and 1.1 °C warmer in April, with 405 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.4 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, April (27.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mossoró, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mossoró has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
