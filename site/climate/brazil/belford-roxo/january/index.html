Belford Roxo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belford Roxo in January?
In January, Belford Roxo averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Belford Roxo?
January is not one of Belford Roxo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belford Roxo in January?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (213 mm), the driest August (32 mm).
Does it snow in Belford Roxo in January?

How many hours of sunshine does Belford Roxo get in January?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
