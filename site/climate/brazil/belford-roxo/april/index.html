Belford Roxo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belford Roxo in April?
In April, Belford Roxo averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Belford Roxo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Belford Roxo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
