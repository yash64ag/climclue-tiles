Abaetetuba at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Abaetetuba is 33rd warmest (26.7 °C year average), 172nd sunniest (about 2,185 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (2,658 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barcarena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Abaetetuba is 0.1 °C cooler in November and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.8 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, February (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in February. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Abaetetuba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Abaetetuba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
