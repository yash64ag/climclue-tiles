Uberaba at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Uberaba is 163rd warmest (22.7 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,621 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (1,794 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uberlândia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Uberaba is 0.4 °C warmer in October and 0.6 °C warmer in June, with 274 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.1 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, June (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in June. The area lies about 500 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Uberaba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Uberaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 500 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
