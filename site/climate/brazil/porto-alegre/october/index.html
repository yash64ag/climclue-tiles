How October compares in Porto Alegre

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Porto Alegre. Compared with September: 2.6 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. November is 2.2 °C warmer than October on average.

Porto Alegre in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Alegre in October?
In October, Porto Alegre averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Porto Alegre?
October is not one of Porto Alegre’s mild months; those are March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Alegre in October?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (149 mm), the driest March (81 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Alegre in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Alegre get in October?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Porto Alegre in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
