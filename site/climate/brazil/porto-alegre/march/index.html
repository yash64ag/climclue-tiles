How March compares in Porto Alegre

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Porto Alegre. Compared with February: 1.0 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. April is 3.1 °C cooler than March on average.

Porto Alegre in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Alegre in March?
In March, Porto Alegre averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Porto Alegre?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Porto Alegre’s mild months (March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Alegre in March?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (149 mm), the driest March (81 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Alegre in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Alegre get in March?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Porto Alegre in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
