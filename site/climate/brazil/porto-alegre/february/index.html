How February compares in Porto Alegre

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Porto Alegre. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. March is 1.0 °C cooler than February on average.

Porto Alegre in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Alegre in February?
In February, Porto Alegre averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Porto Alegre?
February is not one of Porto Alegre’s mild months; those are March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Alegre in February?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (149 mm), the driest March (81 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Alegre in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Alegre get in February?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Porto Alegre in February?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is February (25.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Porto Alegre in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
