How January compares in Porto Alegre

January is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Porto Alegre. Compared with December: 1.1 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Porto Alegre in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Alegre in January?
In January, Porto Alegre averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Porto Alegre?
January is not one of Porto Alegre’s mild months; those are March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Alegre in January?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (149 mm), the driest March (81 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Alegre in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Alegre get in January?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Porto Alegre in January?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is February (25.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Porto Alegre in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
