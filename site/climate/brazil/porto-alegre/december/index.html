How December compares in Porto Alegre

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Porto Alegre. Compared with November: 2.1 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Porto Alegre in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Alegre in December?
In December, Porto Alegre averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Porto Alegre?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Porto Alegre’s mild months (March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Alegre in December?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (149 mm), the driest March (81 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Alegre in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Alegre get in December?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Porto Alegre in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
