How September compares in Porto Alegre

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Porto Alegre. Compared with August: 1.3 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. October is 2.7 °C warmer than September on average.

Porto Alegre in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Alegre in September?
In September, Porto Alegre averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Porto Alegre?
September is not one of Porto Alegre’s mild months; those are March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Alegre in September?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (149 mm), the driest March (81 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Alegre in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Alegre get in September?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Porto Alegre in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
