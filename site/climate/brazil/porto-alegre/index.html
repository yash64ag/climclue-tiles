Porto Alegre at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Porto Alegre is 294th warmest (19.6 °C year average), 157th sunniest (about 2,217 hours of sunshine a year) and 241st wettest (1,428 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Canoas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Porto Alegre is 0.1 °C warmer in January and about as warm in July, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.8 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, July (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Porto Alegre, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Porto Alegre

For mild weather, the best months to visit Porto Alegre are March and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): January (24.8 °C), February (24.6 °C) and March (23.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): March (81 mm), December (99 mm) and May (111 mm).

Why Porto Alegre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
