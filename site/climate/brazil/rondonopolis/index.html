Rondonópolis at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Rondonópolis is 59th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 126th sunniest (about 2,327 hours of sunshine a year) and 212th wettest (1,488 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuiabá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (184 km away), Rondonópolis is 0.3 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,190 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rondonópolis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rondonópolis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,190 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
