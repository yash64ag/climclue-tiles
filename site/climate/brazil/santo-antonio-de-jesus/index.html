Santo Antônio de Jesus at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Santo Antônio de Jesus is 104th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 246th sunniest (about 2,020 hours of sunshine a year) and 303rd wettest (1,290 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salvador, the nearest covered town with different numbers (83 km away), Santo Antônio de Jesus is 0.6 °C cooler in March and 2.0 °C cooler in July, with 471 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.9 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, July (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Why Santo Antônio de Jesus has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
