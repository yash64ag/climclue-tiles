Lambari at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Lambari is 1930th warmest (20.2 °C year average), 1181st sunniest (about 2,229 hours of sunshine a year) and 734th wettest (1,647 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Campanha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Lambari is about as warm in February and about as warm in July, with 144 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.8 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (16.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.7 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 157 km from the sea.

Why Lambari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 157 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lambari itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.976°S, 45.350°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1059-1346), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
