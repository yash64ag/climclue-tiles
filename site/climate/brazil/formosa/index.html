Formosa at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Formosa is 164th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,441 hours of sunshine a year) and 198th wettest (1,512 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Planaltina, Goiás, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Formosa is 0.5 °C warmer in October and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 106 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.3 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 880 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Formosa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Formosa

For mild weather, the best months to visit Formosa are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.

Why Formosa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 880 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
