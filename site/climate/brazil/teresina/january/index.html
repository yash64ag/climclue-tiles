Teresina in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Teresina in January?
In January, Teresina averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Teresina?
No month in Teresina is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Teresina in January?
219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (317 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Teresina in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Teresina get in January?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Teresina in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
