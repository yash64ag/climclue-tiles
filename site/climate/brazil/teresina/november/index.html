Teresina in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Teresina in November?
In November, Teresina averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Teresina?
