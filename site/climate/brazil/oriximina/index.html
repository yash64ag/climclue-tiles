Oriximiná at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Oriximiná is 147th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 1645th sunniest (about 2,092 hours of sunshine a year) and 223rd wettest (2,100 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Óbidos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Oriximiná is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.7 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, February (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 402 km from the sea.

Why Oriximiná has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 402 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-857-1241), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
