Why Gama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 870 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Gama climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Gama?
October, with an average of 23.1 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 18.4 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Gama?
June, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 25.0 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 14.2 °C (58 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Gama?
December, with 265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,501 mm (59.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Gama?
June, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Gama?
July, with about 261 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Gama?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Gama?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Gama have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Gama’s GeoNames location (16.025°S, 48.071°W; ClimaClue area sq-1000-1319), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 139,467 (GeoNames).
