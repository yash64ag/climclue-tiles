Jataí at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Jataí is 156th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 78th sunniest (about 2,401 hours of sunshine a year) and 222nd wettest (1,463 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rio Verde, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Jataí is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C warmer in June, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.4 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, June (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 880 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jataí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jataí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 880 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
