How January compares in Campo Grande

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Campo Grande. Compared with December: 0.1 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Campo Grande in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campo Grande in January?
In January, Campo Grande averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Campo Grande?
January is not one of Campo Grande’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campo Grande in January?
235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (235 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Campo Grande in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campo Grande get in January?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campo Grande in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
