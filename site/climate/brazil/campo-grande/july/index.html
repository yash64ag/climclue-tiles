How July compares in Campo Grande

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Campo Grande. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. August is 2.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Campo Grande in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campo Grande in July?
In July, Campo Grande averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Campo Grande?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Campo Grande’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campo Grande in July?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (235 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Campo Grande in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campo Grande get in July?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campo Grande in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
