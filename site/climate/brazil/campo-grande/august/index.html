How August compares in Campo Grande

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Campo Grande. Compared with July: 2.1 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 1.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Campo Grande in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campo Grande in August?
In August, Campo Grande averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Campo Grande?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Campo Grande’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campo Grande in August?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (236 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Campo Grande in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campo Grande get in August?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campo Grande in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
