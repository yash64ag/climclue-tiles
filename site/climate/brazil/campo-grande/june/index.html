How June compares in Campo Grande

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Campo Grande. Compared with May: 0.9 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Campo Grande in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campo Grande in June?
In June, Campo Grande averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Campo Grande?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Campo Grande’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campo Grande in June?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (235 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Campo Grande in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campo Grande get in June?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campo Grande in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
