How November compares in Campo Grande

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Campo Grande. Compared with October: 0.2 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. December is 0.1 °C warmer than November on average.

Campo Grande in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campo Grande in November?
In November, Campo Grande averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Campo Grande?
November is not one of Campo Grande’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campo Grande in November?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (235 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Campo Grande in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campo Grande get in November?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campo Grande in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
