Santa Maria das Barreiras at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Santa Maria das Barreiras is 261st warmest (26.9 °C year average), 1646th sunniest (about 2,091 hours of sunshine a year) and 263rd wettest (2,037 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Conceição do Araguaia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (83 km away), Santa Maria das Barreiras is 0.1 °C warmer in September and about as warm in January, with 157 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (28.4 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.2 °C (67 °F), in July. The area lies about 757 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Santa Maria das Barreiras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 757 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Santa Maria das Barreiras itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.872°S, 49.713°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-928-1302), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
