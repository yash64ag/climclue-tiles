São Joaquim da Barra at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São Joaquim da Barra is 1297th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 1683rd sunniest (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 904th wettest (1,560 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orlândia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), São Joaquim da Barra is 0.6 °C warmer in October and 0.7 °C warmer in June, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.9 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, June (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in June. The area lies about 416 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of São Joaquim da Barra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why São Joaquim da Barra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 416 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for São Joaquim da Barra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.581°S, 47.855°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1045-1321), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
