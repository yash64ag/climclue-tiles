How September compares in Serra

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Serra. Compared with August: 0.7 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. October is 1.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Serra in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Serra in September?
In September, Serra averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Serra?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Serra’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Serra in September?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (245 mm), the driest June (54 mm).
Does it snow in Serra in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Serra get in September?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Serra in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
