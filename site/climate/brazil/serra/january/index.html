How January compares in Serra

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Serra. Compared with December: 1.1 °C warmer, 53 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Serra in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Serra in January?
In January, Serra averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 166 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Serra?
January is not one of Serra’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Serra in January?
166 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (245 mm), the driest June (54 mm).
Does it snow in Serra in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Serra get in January?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Serra in January?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (26.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Serra in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
