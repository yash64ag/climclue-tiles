Serra weather in February

Average February weather in Serra, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Serra averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 22.1 °C (72 °F) nights, with 100 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 215 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Serra

February is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Serra. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 66 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 0.4 °C cooler than February on average.

Serra in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Serra in February?
In February, Serra averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Serra?
February is not one of Serra’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Serra in February?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (245 mm), the driest June (54 mm).
Does it snow in Serra in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Serra get in February?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Serra in February?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (26.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Serra in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
