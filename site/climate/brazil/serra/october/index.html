How October compares in Serra

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Serra. Compared with September: 1.1 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Serra in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Serra in October?
In October, Serra averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Serra?
October is not one of Serra’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Serra in October?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (245 mm), the driest June (54 mm).
Does it snow in Serra in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Serra get in October?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Serra in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
