Why Serra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Serra climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Serra?
February, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.2 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 23.3 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Serra?
July, with an average of 21.3 °C (70 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 18.4 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Serra?
November, with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,411 mm (55.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Serra?
June, with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Serra?
April, with about 227 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Serra?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Serra?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Serra’s GeoNames location (20.129°S, 40.308°W; ClimaClue area sq-1041-1396), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 520,653 (GeoNames).
