How December compares in Serra

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Serra. Compared with November: 1.0 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Serra in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Serra in December?
In December, Serra averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 212 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Serra?
December is not one of Serra’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Serra in December?
212 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (231 mm), the driest June (50 mm).
Does it snow in Serra in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Serra get in December?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Serra in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
