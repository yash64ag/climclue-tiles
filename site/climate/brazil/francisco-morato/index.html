Francisco Morato at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Francisco Morato is 298th warmest (19.6 °C year average), 265th sunniest (about 1,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 162nd wettest (1,553 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Franco da Rocha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Francisco Morato is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 90 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.6 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 102 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Francisco Morato, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Francisco Morato has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 102 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
