Blumenau at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Blumenau is 274th warmest (19.9 °C year average), 320th sunniest (about 1,683 hours of sunshine a year) and 93rd wettest (1,753 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Brusque, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Blumenau is 0.3 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.3 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, July (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 50 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Blumenau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Blumenau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
