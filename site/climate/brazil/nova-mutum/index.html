Nova Mutum at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Nova Mutum is 1003rd warmest (24.4 °C year average), 1357th sunniest (about 2,177 hours of sunshine a year) and 306th wettest (1,977 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Diamantino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (75 km away), Nova Mutum is 1.4 °C cooler in October and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 191 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.5 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, July (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,433 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nova Mutum has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,433 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nova Mutum itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.829°S, 56.082°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-978-1239), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
