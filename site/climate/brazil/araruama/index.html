Araruama at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Araruama is 143rd warmest (23.4 °C year average), 146th sunniest (about 2,261 hours of sunshine a year) and 316th wettest (1,205 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Pedro da Aldeia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Araruama is 0.6 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 122 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.1 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.8 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Araruama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Araruama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
