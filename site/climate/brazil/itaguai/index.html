Itaguaí at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itaguaí is 101st warmest (24.2 °C year average), 201st sunniest (about 2,114 hours of sunshine a year) and 266th wettest (1,392 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Japeri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Itaguaí is 0.3 °C warmer in February and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 116 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itaguaí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itaguaí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
