Canguaretama sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio Grande do Norte · Brazil

The sea off Canguaretama averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 26.1 °C (79 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Canguaretama sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Canguaretama?
In April, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Canguaretama?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.1 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Canguaretama in August?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Canguaretama?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
