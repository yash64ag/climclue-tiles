Chapecó at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Chapecó is 326th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 135th sunniest (about 2,308 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (2,305 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Francisco Beltrão, the nearest covered town with different numbers (121 km away), Chapecó is 0.6 °C cooler in January and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 258 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.9 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, July (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 390 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chapecó, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chapecó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 390 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
