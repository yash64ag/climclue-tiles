Sertãozinho at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Sertãozinho is 159th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 230th sunniest (about 2,050 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (1,515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ribeirão Preto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Sertãozinho is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in June, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.6 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, June (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in June. The area lies about 363 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sertãozinho, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sertãozinho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 363 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
