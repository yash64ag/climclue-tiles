Why Marabá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 368 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Marabá climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Marabá?
September, with an average of 28.1 °C (83 °F), day highs around 33.2 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 23.8 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Marabá?
February, with an average of 25.8 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.0 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 22.9 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Marabá?
March, with 381 mm (15.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,955 mm (76.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Marabá?
August, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Marabá?
July, with about 248 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Marabá?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Marabá?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Marabá have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Marabá’s GeoNames location (5.381°S, 49.132°W; ClimaClue area sq-893-1308), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 283,542 (GeoNames).
