Teresópolis at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Teresópolis is 325th warmest (18.8 °C year average), 269th sunniest (about 1,894 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (2,325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Petrópolis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Teresópolis is 0.4 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 273 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.9 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Teresópolis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Teresópolis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Teresópolis itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.417°S, 42.978°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1064-1370), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
