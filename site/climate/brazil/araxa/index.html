Araxá at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Araxá is 229th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 92nd sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 229th wettest (1,457 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uberaba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Araxá is 1.1 °C cooler in February and 1.8 °C cooler in June, with 338 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.8 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, June (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.7 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 471 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Araxá, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Araxá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 471 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
