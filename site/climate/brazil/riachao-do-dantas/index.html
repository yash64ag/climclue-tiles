Riachão do Dantas at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Riachão do Dantas is 875th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 523rd sunniest (about 2,483 hours of sunshine a year) and 1647th wettest (1,208 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Boquim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Riachão do Dantas is about as warm in March and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 161 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.6 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Why Riachão do Dantas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Riachão do Dantas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.069°S, 37.725°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-950-1422), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
