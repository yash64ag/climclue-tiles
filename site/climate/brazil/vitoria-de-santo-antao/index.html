Vitória de Santo Antão at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Vitória de Santo Antão is 100th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,550 hours of sunshine a year) and 233rd wettest (1,443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jaboatão dos Guararapes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Vitória de Santo Antão is 0.9 °C cooler in March and 1.1 °C cooler in July, with 452 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.6 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, July (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vitória de Santo Antão, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vitória de Santo Antão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
