Ipu at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ipu is 304th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 601st sunniest (about 2,448 hours of sunshine a year) and 1965th wettest (847 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guaraciaba do Norte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Ipu is 4.4 °C warmer in November and 3.7 °C warmer in April, with 76 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.2 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, April (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.6 °C (69 °F), in July. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ipu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ipu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C. The 10 km area around Ipu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.8 °C a year, 2.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ipu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.322°S, 40.711°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-883-1392), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
