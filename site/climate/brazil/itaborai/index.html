Why Itaboraí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Itaboraí climate: questions and answers

February, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 23.6 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Itaboraí?
July, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 24.6 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 17.1 °C (63 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Itaboraí?

What is the driest month in Itaboraí?

What is the sunniest month in Itaboraí?

Does it snow in Itaboraí?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Itaboraí?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Itaboraí’s GeoNames location (22.744°S, 42.859°W; ClimaClue area sq-1067-1371), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 240,040 (GeoNames).
