Itaboraí at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itaboraí is 113th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 221st sunniest (about 2,079 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,651 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maricá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Itaboraí is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 333 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.3 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itaboraí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itaboraí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
