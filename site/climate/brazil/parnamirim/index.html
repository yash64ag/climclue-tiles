Parnamirim at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Parnamirim is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,816 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Natal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Parnamirim is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, July (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Parnamirim, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Parnamirim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
