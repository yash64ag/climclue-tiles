Cumaru do Norte at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Cumaru do Norte is 685th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 2066th sunniest (about 1,804 hours of sunshine a year) and 303rd wettest (1,979 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Cumaru do Norte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 663 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cumaru do Norte itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.825°S, 50.773°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-918-1292), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
