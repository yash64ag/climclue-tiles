Poá at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Poá is 295th warmest (19.6 °C year average), 321st sunniest (about 1,681 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (1,773 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itaquaquecetuba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Poá is 0.1 °C cooler in February and about as warm in July, with 169 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.8 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Poá, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Poá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
