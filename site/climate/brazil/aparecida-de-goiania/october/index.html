Aparecida de Goiânia in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aparecida de Goiânia in October?
In October, Aparecida de Goiânia averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aparecida de Goiânia?
October is not one of Aparecida de Goiânia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aparecida de Goiânia in October?
146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (305 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Aparecida de Goiânia in October?

How many hours of sunshine does Aparecida de Goiânia get in October?
