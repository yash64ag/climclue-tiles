Aparecida de Goiânia in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aparecida de Goiânia in August?
In August, Aparecida de Goiânia averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Aparecida de Goiânia?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Aparecida de Goiânia’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

Does it snow in Aparecida de Goiânia in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aparecida de Goiânia get in August?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
