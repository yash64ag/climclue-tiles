Aparecida de Goiânia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aparecida de Goiânia in February?
In February, Aparecida de Goiânia averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 232 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aparecida de Goiânia?
February is not one of Aparecida de Goiânia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aparecida de Goiânia in February?
232 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (305 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Aparecida de Goiânia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aparecida de Goiânia get in February?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aparecida de Goiânia in February?
