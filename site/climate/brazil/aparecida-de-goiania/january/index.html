Aparecida de Goiânia in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aparecida de Goiânia in January?
In January, Aparecida de Goiânia averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aparecida de Goiânia?
January is not one of Aparecida de Goiânia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aparecida de Goiânia in January?
256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (305 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Aparecida de Goiânia in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aparecida de Goiânia get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aparecida de Goiânia in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
