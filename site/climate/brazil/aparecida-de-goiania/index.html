Aparecida de Goiânia at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Aparecida de Goiânia is 154th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,504 hours of sunshine a year) and 136th wettest (1,617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Goiânia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Aparecida de Goiânia is 0.4 °C cooler in September and 0.4 °C cooler in June, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (24.8 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, June (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in June. The area lies about 851 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Aparecida de Goiânia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 851 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
