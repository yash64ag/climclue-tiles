Aparecida de Goiânia in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aparecida de Goiânia in May?
In May, Aparecida de Goiânia averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
