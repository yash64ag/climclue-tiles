Aparecida de Goiânia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aparecida de Goiânia in December?
In December, Aparecida de Goiânia averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 296 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Aparecida de Goiânia?
December is not one of Aparecida de Goiânia’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aparecida de Goiânia in December?
296 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (296 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aparecida de Goiânia in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aparecida de Goiânia get in December?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aparecida de Goiânia in December?
