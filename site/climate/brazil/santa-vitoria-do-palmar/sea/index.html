Santa Vitória do Palmar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio Grande do Sul · Brazil

The sea off Santa Vitória do Palmar averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 12.3 °C (54 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April.

Santa Vitória do Palmar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santa Vitória do Palmar?
In February, at about 23.3 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santa Vitória do Palmar?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 12.3 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santa Vitória do Palmar in August?
About 12.3 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Santa Vitória do Palmar?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Santa Vitória do Palmar nearby?
In February: Rio Grande (24.2 °C, 203 km), São Lourenço do Sul (25.1 °C, 273 km) and Magdalena (23.9 °C, 418 km).
