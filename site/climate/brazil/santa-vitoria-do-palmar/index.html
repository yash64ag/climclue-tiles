Santa Vitória do Palmar at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Santa Vitória do Palmar is 2226th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 802nd sunniest (about 2,359 hours of sunshine a year) and 1465th wettest (1,340 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Santa Vitória do Palmar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
