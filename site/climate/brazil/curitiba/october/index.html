How October compares in Curitiba

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Curitiba. Compared with September: 1.8 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. November is 1.7 °C warmer than October on average.

Curitiba in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in October?
In October, Curitiba averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Curitiba?
October is not one of Curitiba’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in October?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest August (75 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in October?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
