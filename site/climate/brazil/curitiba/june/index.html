How June compares in Curitiba

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Curitiba. Compared with May: 1.4 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Curitiba in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in June?
In June, Curitiba averages a day high of 18.0 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Curitiba?
June is not one of Curitiba’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in June?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (206 mm), the driest August (76 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in June?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
