How December compares in Curitiba

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Curitiba. Compared with November: 1.3 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Curitiba in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in December?
In December, Curitiba averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Curitiba?
December is not one of Curitiba’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in December?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (206 mm), the driest August (76 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in December?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
