How May compares in Curitiba

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Curitiba. Compared with April: 3.1 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. June is 1.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Curitiba in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in May?
In May, Curitiba averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Curitiba?
May is not one of Curitiba’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in May?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (206 mm), the driest August (76 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in May?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
