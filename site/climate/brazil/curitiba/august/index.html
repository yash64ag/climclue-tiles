How August compares in Curitiba

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Curitiba. Compared with July: 1.1 °C warmer, 33 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. September is 0.8 °C warmer than August on average.

Curitiba in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in August?
In August, Curitiba averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Curitiba?
August is not one of Curitiba’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in August?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest August (75 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in August?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
