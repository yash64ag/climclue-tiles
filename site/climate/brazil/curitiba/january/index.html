How January compares in Curitiba

January is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Curitiba. Compared with December: 0.8 °C warmer, 50 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Curitiba in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in January?
In January, Curitiba averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Curitiba?
January is not one of Curitiba’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in January?
204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest August (75 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in January?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
