How April compares in Curitiba

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Curitiba. Compared with March: 1.8 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. May is 3.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Curitiba in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Curitiba in April?
In April, Curitiba averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Curitiba?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Curitiba’s mild months (April), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Curitiba in April?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (206 mm), the driest August (76 mm).
Does it snow in Curitiba in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Curitiba get in April?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Curitiba in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
