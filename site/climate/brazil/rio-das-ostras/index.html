Rio das Ostras at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Rio das Ostras is 119th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 132nd sunniest (about 2,313 hours of sunshine a year) and 196th wettest (1,514 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Macaé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Rio das Ostras is about as warm in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 172 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rio das Ostras, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rio das Ostras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
