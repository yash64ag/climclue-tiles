Rio das Ostras sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio de Janeiro · Brazil

The sea off Rio das Ostras averages 24.8 °C (77 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 21.2 °C (70 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to April.

Rio das Ostras sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rio das Ostras?
In March, at about 24.8 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rio das Ostras?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to April. The coolest month is September, at 21.2 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rio das Ostras in September?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Rio das Ostras?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rio das Ostras nearby?
In March: São Pedro da Aldeia (25.5 °C, 38 km), Iguaba Grande (25.5 °C, 45 km) and Araruama (25.5 °C, 56 km).
