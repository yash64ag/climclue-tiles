Campos dos Goytacazes at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Campos dos Goytacazes is 97th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 149th sunniest (about 2,240 hours of sunshine a year) and 329th wettest (1,044 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itaperuna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Campos dos Goytacazes is 0.8 °C warmer in February and 1.2 °C warmer in July, with 140 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, July (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Why Campos dos Goytacazes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
