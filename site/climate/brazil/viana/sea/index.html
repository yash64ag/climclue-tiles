Viana, Espírito Santo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Espírito Santo · Brazil

The sea off Viana, Espírito Santo averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to June.

Viana, Espírito Santo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Viana, Espírito Santo?
In March, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Viana, Espírito Santo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to June. The coolest month is August, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Viana, Espírito Santo in August?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Viana, Espírito Santo?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Viana, Espírito Santo nearby?
In March: São Mateus (27.3 °C, 197 km).
