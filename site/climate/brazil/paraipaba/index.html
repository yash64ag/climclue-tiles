Paraipaba at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Paraipaba is 299th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 468th sunniest (about 2,515 hours of sunshine a year) and 1736th wettest (1,104 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paracuru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Paraipaba is 0.4 °C warmer in December and 0.1 °C warmer in March, with 126 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.4 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, March (26.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Paraipaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Paraipaba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.439°S, 39.148°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-874-1408), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
