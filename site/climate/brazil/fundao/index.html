Fundão at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Fundão is 1137th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 1035th sunniest (about 2,279 hours of sunshine a year) and 1344th wettest (1,391 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aracruz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Fundão is about as warm in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.4 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fundão, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fundão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C. The 10 km area around Fundão, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.8 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fundão itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.934°S, 40.405°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1039-1395), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
