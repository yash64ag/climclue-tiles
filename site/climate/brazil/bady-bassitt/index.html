Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Bady Bassitt is 1306th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 456th sunniest (about 2,522 hours of sunshine a year) and 1368th wettest (1,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Bady Bassitt has this climate
