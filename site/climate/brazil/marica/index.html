Why Maricá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Maricá climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Maricá?
February, with an average of 26.9 °C (80 °F), day highs around 29.3 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 24.3 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Maricá?
July, with an average of 21.3 °C (70 °F), day highs around 23.8 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Maricá?
December, with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,319 mm (51.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Maricá?
August, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Maricá?
January, with about 202 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Maricá?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Maricá?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Maricá have?
Tropical monsoon (Köppen Am). Hot all year, with a very wet monsoon season and a short drier spell. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Maricá’s GeoNames location (22.919°S, 42.819°W; ClimaClue area sq-1069-1371), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 211,986 (GeoNames).
