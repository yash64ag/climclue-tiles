Vargem Alta at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Vargem Alta is 1866th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 1111th sunniest (about 2,252 hours of sunshine a year) and 551st wettest (1,753 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Vargem Alta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
