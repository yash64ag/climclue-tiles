São Sebastião, São Paulo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · São Paulo · Brazil

The sea off São Sebastião, São Paulo averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 20.1 °C (68 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to April.

São Sebastião, São Paulo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São Sebastião, São Paulo?
In February, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São Sebastião, São Paulo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is August, at 20.1 °C (68 °F).
How warm is the sea in São Sebastião, São Paulo in August?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in São Sebastião, São Paulo?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in São Sebastião, São Paulo nearby?
In February: Bertioga (26.2 °C, 75 km), Guarujá (26.2 °C, 90 km) and Paraty (26.1 °C, 93 km).
