Cambé at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Cambé is 233rd warmest (21.0 °C year average), 205th sunniest (about 2,106 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (1,684 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Londrina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Cambé is 0.3 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.9 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 397 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cambé, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cambé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 397 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
