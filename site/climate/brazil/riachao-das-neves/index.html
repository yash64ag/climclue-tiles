Riachão das Neves at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Riachão das Neves is 580th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 147th sunniest (about 2,707 hours of sunshine a year) and 1751st wettest (1,090 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barreiras, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Riachão das Neves is 0.3 °C warmer in October and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 188 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 683 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Riachão das Neves has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 683 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C. The 10 km area around Riachão das Neves, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.8 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
