Paço do Lumiar at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Paço do Lumiar is 38th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 2,383 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (2,071 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São José de Ribamar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Paço do Lumiar is about as warm in November and 0.1 °C cooler in March, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.3 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, March (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.6 °C (76 °F), in March. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Paço do Lumiar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Paço do Lumiar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
