Passos at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Passos is 220th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 243rd sunniest (about 2,027 hours of sunshine a year) and 167th wettest (1,549 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Franca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Passos is 0.9 °C warmer in February and about as warm in June, with 310 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.6 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, June (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 344 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Passos, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Passos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 344 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
