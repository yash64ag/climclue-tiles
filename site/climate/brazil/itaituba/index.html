Itaituba at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Itaituba is 40th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 252nd sunniest (about 2,003 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (1,993 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Parintins, the nearest covered town with different numbers (201 km away), Itaituba is 0.6 °C cooler in October and 0.5 °C cooler in February, with 317 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.8 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, February (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 516 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itaituba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itaituba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 516 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
