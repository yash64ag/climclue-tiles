Ouricuri at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ouricuri is 482nd warmest (26.3 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,764 hours of sunshine a year) and 2169th wettest (637 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bodocó, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Ouricuri is 0.1 °C cooler in November and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.9 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, July (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 459 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ouricuri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ouricuri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 459 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ouricuri itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.882°S, 40.082°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-918-1399), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
