Codó at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Codó is 8th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,490 hours of sunshine a year) and 230th wettest (1,455 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caxias, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Codó is 0.8 °C cooler in October and 0.2 °C cooler in March, with 75 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (29.1 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, March (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 211 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Codó, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Codó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 211 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
