Praia Grande at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Praia Grande is 157th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 318th sunniest (about 1,692 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (2,373 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Vicente, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Praia Grande is 0.4 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.4 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, July (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Praia Grande, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Praia Grande has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
