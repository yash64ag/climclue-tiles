Duque de Caxias in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duque de Caxias in July?
In July, Duque de Caxias averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Duque de Caxias?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Duque de Caxias’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
