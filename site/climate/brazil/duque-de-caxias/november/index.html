Duque de Caxias in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duque de Caxias in November?
In November, Duque de Caxias averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Duque de Caxias?
November is not one of Duque de Caxias’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duque de Caxias in November?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (215 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Duque de Caxias in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Duque de Caxias get in November?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duque de Caxias in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
