Duque de Caxias at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Duque de Caxias is 108th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 208th sunniest (about 2,103 hours of sunshine a year) and 301st wettest (1,295 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São João de Meriti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Duque de Caxias is 0.2 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, July (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Why Duque de Caxias has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
