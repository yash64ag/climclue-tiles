Duque de Caxias in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duque de Caxias in October?
In October, Duque de Caxias averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Duque de Caxias?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Duque de Caxias’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duque de Caxias in October?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (214 mm), the driest August (37 mm).
Does it snow in Duque de Caxias in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Duque de Caxias get in October?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Duque de Caxias in October?
About 21.4 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Duque de Caxias in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
