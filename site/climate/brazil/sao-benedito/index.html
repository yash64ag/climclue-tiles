São Benedito at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São Benedito is 1435th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 673rd sunniest (about 2,414 hours of sunshine a year) and 1830th wettest (992 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ibiapina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), São Benedito is 0.2 °C cooler in November and 0.2 °C cooler in June, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (23.9 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, June (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of São Benedito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why São Benedito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C. The 10 km area around São Benedito, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.0 °C a year, 1.4 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for São Benedito itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.049°S, 40.865°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-880-1391), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
