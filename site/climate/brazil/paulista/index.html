Paulista at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Paulista is 52nd warmest (26.0 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,667 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (1,867 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Abreu e Lima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Paulista is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in August, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.1 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Paulista, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Paulista has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
