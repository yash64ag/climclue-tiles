Anápolis at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Anápolis is 195th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 2,407 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (1,795 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Senador Canedo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Anápolis is 1.8 °C cooler in September and 1.6 °C cooler in June, with 282 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.4 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, June (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 885 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Anápolis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Anápolis

For mild weather, the best months to visit Anápolis are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): September (23.4 °C), October (23.1 °C) and February (22.3 °C).

Why Anápolis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 885 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
