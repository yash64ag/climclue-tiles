How May compares in São Paulo

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in São Paulo. Compared with April: 2.6 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

São Paulo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Paulo in May?
In May, São Paulo averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit São Paulo?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of São Paulo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Paulo in May?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (260 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in São Paulo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Paulo get in May?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Paulo in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
