How October compares in São Paulo

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in São Paulo. Compared with September: 1.5 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C warmer than October on average.

São Paulo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Paulo in October?
In October, São Paulo averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit São Paulo?
October is not one of São Paulo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Paulo in October?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (257 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in São Paulo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Paulo get in October?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Paulo in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
