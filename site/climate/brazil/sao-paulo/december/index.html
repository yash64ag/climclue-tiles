How December compares in São Paulo

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in São Paulo. Compared with November: 0.9 °C warmer, 58 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

São Paulo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Paulo in December?
In December, São Paulo averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit São Paulo?
December is not one of São Paulo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Paulo in December?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (257 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in São Paulo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Paulo get in December?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Paulo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
