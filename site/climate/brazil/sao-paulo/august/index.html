How August compares in São Paulo

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in São Paulo. Compared with July: 1.0 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. September is 0.9 °C warmer than August on average.

São Paulo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Paulo in August?
In August, São Paulo averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit São Paulo?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of São Paulo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Paulo in August?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (257 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in São Paulo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Paulo get in August?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Paulo in August?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
