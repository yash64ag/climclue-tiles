How July compares in São Paulo

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in São Paulo. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. August is 0.9 °C warmer than July on average.

São Paulo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Paulo in July?
In July, São Paulo averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit São Paulo?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of São Paulo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Paulo in July?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (260 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in São Paulo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Paulo get in July?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Paulo in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
