How June compares in São Paulo

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in São Paulo. Compared with May: 1.3 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

São Paulo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São Paulo in June?
In June, São Paulo averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit São Paulo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of São Paulo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São Paulo in June?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (260 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in São Paulo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São Paulo get in June?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São Paulo in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
