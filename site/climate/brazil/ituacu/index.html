Ituaçu at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ituaçu is 1274th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 1516th sunniest (about 2,137 hours of sunshine a year) and 2107th wettest (696 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barra da Estiva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Ituaçu is 3.4 °C warmer in February and 3.4 °C warmer in July, with 295 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.3 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, July (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ituaçu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ituaçu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C. The 10 km area around Ituaçu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.9 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ituaçu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.813°S, 41.297°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-978-1387), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
