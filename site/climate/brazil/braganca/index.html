Bragança at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Bragança is 46th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 182nd sunniest (about 2,159 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (2,573 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Castanhal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (132 km away), Bragança is 0.7 °C cooler in November and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 328 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.1 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, February (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bragança, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bragança has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
