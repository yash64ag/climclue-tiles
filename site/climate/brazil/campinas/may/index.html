How May compares in Campinas, São Paulo

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Campinas, São Paulo. Compared with April: 2.8 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. June is 1.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Campinas, São Paulo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas, São Paulo in May?
In May, Campinas, São Paulo averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Campinas, São Paulo?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Campinas, São Paulo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
