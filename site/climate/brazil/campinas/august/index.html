How August compares in Campinas

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Campinas. Compared with July: 1.5 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. September is 1.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Campinas in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas in August?
In August, Campinas averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Campinas?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Campinas’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campinas in August?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (289 mm), the driest August (37 mm).
Does it snow in Campinas in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campinas get in August?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campinas in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
