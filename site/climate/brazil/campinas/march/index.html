How March compares in Campinas

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Campinas. Compared with February: 0.6 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 1.3 °C cooler than March on average.

Campinas in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas in March?
In March, Campinas averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Campinas?
March is not one of Campinas’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campinas in March?
166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (289 mm), the driest August (37 mm).
Does it snow in Campinas in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campinas get in March?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campinas in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
