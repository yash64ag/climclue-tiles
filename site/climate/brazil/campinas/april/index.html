How April compares in Campinas

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Campinas. Compared with March: 1.3 °C cooler, 90 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. May is 2.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Campinas in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas in April?
In April, Campinas averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Campinas?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Campinas’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campinas in April?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (289 mm), the driest August (37 mm).
Does it snow in Campinas in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campinas get in April?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campinas in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
