Campinas, São Paulo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas, São Paulo in April?
In April, Campinas, São Paulo averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Campinas, São Paulo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Campinas, São Paulo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campinas, São Paulo in April?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (301 mm), the driest August (40 mm).
Does it snow in Campinas, São Paulo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
