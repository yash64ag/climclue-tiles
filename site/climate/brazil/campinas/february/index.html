How February compares in Campinas

February is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Campinas. Compared with January: 0.2 °C warmer, 104 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. March is 0.6 °C cooler than February on average.

Campinas in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas in February?
In February, Campinas averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Campinas?
February is not one of Campinas’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campinas in February?
185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (289 mm), the driest August (37 mm).
Does it snow in Campinas in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campinas get in February?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campinas in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
