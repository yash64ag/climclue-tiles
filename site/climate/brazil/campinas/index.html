Campinas at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Campinas is 236th warmest (21.0 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,472 hours of sunshine a year) and 181st wettest (1,536 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Valinhos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Campinas is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.6 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Campinas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Campinas

For mild weather, the best months to visit Campinas are April, May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): April, May, June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): February (23.6 °C), January (23.4 °C) and March (23.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): August (37 mm), July (45 mm) and June (54 mm).

Why Campinas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
