How October compares in Campinas

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Campinas. Compared with September: 1.4 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Campinas in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Campinas in October?
In October, Campinas averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Campinas?
October is not one of Campinas’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Campinas in October?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (289 mm), the driest August (37 mm).
Does it snow in Campinas in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Campinas get in October?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Campinas in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
