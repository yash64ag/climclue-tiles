Why Barra Mansa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Barra Mansa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Barra Mansa?
February, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 29.3 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 20.5 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Barra Mansa?
July, with an average of 18.4 °C (65 °F), day highs around 23.3 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 13.3 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Barra Mansa?
January, with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,460 mm (57.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Barra Mansa?
July, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Barra Mansa?
July, with about 177 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Barra Mansa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Barra Mansa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Barra Mansa’s GeoNames location (22.544°S, 44.171°W; ClimaClue area sq-1065-1358), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 181,679 (GeoNames).
