Barra Mansa at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Barra Mansa is 191st warmest (21.8 °C year average), 274th sunniest (about 1,881 hours of sunshine a year) and 224th wettest (1,460 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Resende, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Barra Mansa is 0.4 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 231 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.9 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Barra Mansa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Barra Mansa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
