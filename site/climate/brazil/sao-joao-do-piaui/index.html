São João do Piauí at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São João do Piauí is 28th warmest (28.2 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,841 hours of sunshine a year) and 2196th wettest (604 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Canto do Buriti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), São João do Piauí is 0.5 °C warmer in October and 0.7 °C warmer in March, with 115 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (30.6 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, March (26.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 616 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why São João do Piauí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 616 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for São João do Piauí itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.358°S, 42.247°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-923-1377), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
