Why Vila Velha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Vila Velha climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Vila Velha?
February, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 27.9 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 24.4 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Vila Velha?
July, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 23.6 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Vila Velha?
November, with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,332 mm (52.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Vila Velha?
June, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Vila Velha?
January, with about 229 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Vila Velha?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Vila Velha?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Vila Velha’s GeoNames location (20.330°S, 40.292°W; ClimaClue area sq-1043-1397), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 394,930 (GeoNames).
