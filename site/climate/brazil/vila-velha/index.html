Vila Velha at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Vila Velha is 114th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 2,308 hours of sunshine a year) and 289th wettest (1,332 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vitória, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Vila Velha is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.1 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, July (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vila Velha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vila Velha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
