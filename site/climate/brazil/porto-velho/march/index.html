Porto Velho in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Velho in March?
In March, Porto Velho averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 335 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Porto Velho?
