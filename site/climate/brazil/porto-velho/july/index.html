Porto Velho in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Velho in July?
In July, Porto Velho averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Porto Velho?
No month in Porto Velho is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
