How September compares in Porto Velho

September is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Porto Velho. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 61 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

Porto Velho in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Porto Velho in September?
In September, Porto Velho averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Porto Velho?
No month in Porto Velho is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Porto Velho in September?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (363 mm), the driest July (33 mm).
Does it snow in Porto Velho in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Porto Velho get in September?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Porto Velho in September?
