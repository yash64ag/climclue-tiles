Itapagé at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Itapagé is 547th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 958th sunniest (about 2,311 hours of sunshine a year) and 1876th wettest (932 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uruburetama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Itapagé is 0.5 °C cooler in November and 0.7 °C cooler in April, with 123 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.0 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, April (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in July. The area lies about 63 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itapagé, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itapagé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C. The 10 km area around Itapagé, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 25.0 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Itapagé itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.687°S, 39.586°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-876-1404), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
