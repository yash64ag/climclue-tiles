Bonito, Pernambuco at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Bonito, Pernambuco is 1370th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 754th sunniest (about 2,379 hours of sunshine a year) and 1823rd wettest (999 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Joaquim do Monte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Bonito, Pernambuco is 0.1 °C warmer in March and about as warm in August, with 148 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.6 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, August (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in August. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Why Bonito, Pernambuco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bonito itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.470°S, 35.729°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-924-1442), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
