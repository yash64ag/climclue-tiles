Igreja Nova at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Igreja Nova is 594th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 369th sunniest (about 2,570 hours of sunshine a year) and 1817th wettest (1,011 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Penedo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Igreja Nova is about as warm in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in August. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Why Igreja Nova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-941-1433), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
