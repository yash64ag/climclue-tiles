Tapejara, Paraná at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Tapejara, Paraná is 1604th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 1864th sunniest (about 1,979 hours of sunshine a year) and 623rd wettest (1,706 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Tapejara, Paraná has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 501 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
