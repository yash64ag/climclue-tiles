Silva Jardim at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Silva Jardim is 1228th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 1178th sunniest (about 2,230 hours of sunshine a year) and 1113th wettest (1,474 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rio Bonito, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Silva Jardim is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 317 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.7 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Why Silva Jardim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Silva Jardim itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.651°S, 42.392°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1066-1376), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
