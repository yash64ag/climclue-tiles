Conceição da Barra sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Espírito Santo · Brazil

The sea off Conceição da Barra averages 27.3 °C (81 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 23.9 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Conceição da Barra sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Conceição da Barra?
In March, at about 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Conceição da Barra?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.9 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Conceição da Barra in August?
About 23.9 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Conceição da Barra?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
