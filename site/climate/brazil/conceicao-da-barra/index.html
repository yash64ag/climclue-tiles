Conceição da Barra at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Conceição da Barra is 995th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 1801st sunniest (about 2,024 hours of sunshine a year) and 1483rd wettest (1,333 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Mateus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Conceição da Barra is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C warmer in July, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.3 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (22.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Conceição da Barra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Conceição da Barra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.593°S, 39.732°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1025-1402), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
