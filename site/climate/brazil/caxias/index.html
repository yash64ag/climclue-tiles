Caxias at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Caxias is the 4th warmest (27.8 °C year average). Compared with Timon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Caxias is 0.5 °C cooler in October and 0.2 °C cooler in March, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (29.9 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, March (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in February. The area lies about 269 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Caxias, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Caxias has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 269 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
