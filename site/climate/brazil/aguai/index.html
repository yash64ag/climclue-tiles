Aguaí at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Aguaí is 1556th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 1141st sunniest (about 2,245 hours of sunshine a year) and 849th wettest (1,584 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São João da Boa Vista, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Aguaí is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 553 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.3 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, July (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 225 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aguaí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aguaí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 225 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aguaí itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.059°S, 46.979°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1060-1330), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
