Cambuci at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Cambuci is 1121st warmest (24.0 °C year average), 1323rd sunniest (about 2,188 hours of sunshine a year) and 1797th wettest (1,034 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Fidélis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Cambuci is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Why Cambuci has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C. The 10 km area around Cambuci, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.6 °C a year, 1.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cambuci itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.575°S, 41.911°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1055-1380), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
