Capão do Leão at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Capão do Leão is 2169th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 830th sunniest (about 2,353 hours of sunshine a year) and 984th wettest (1,528 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Capão do Leão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Capão do Leão itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.763°S, 52.484°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1157-1275), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
