Correntes at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Correntes is 1303rd warmest (23.3 °C year average), 1228th sunniest (about 2,216 hours of sunshine a year) and 1710th wettest (1,143 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São João, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Correntes is 1.7 °C warmer in March and 1.5 °C warmer in July, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.9 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (21.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in August. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Correntes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Correntes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Correntes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.129°S, 36.330°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-931-1436), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
