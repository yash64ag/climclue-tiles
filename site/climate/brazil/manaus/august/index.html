How August compares in Manaus

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Manaus. Compared with July: 0.7 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Manaus in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manaus in August?
In August, Manaus averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Manaus?
No month in Manaus is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manaus in August?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (297 mm), the driest August (87 mm).
Does it snow in Manaus in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manaus get in August?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manaus in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
