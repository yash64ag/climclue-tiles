How April compares in Manaus

April is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Manaus. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. May is about as warm as April on average.

Manaus in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manaus in April?
In April, Manaus averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 290 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Manaus?
No month in Manaus is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manaus in April?
290 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (297 mm), the driest August (87 mm).
Does it snow in Manaus in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manaus get in April?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manaus in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
