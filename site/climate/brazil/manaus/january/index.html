How January compares in Manaus

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Manaus. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Manaus in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manaus in January?
In January, Manaus averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Manaus?
No month in Manaus is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manaus in January?
295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (298 mm), the driest August (89 mm).
Does it snow in Manaus in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manaus get in January?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manaus in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
