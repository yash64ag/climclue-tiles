How November compares in Manaus

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Manaus. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 66 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. December is 0.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Manaus in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manaus in November?
In November, Manaus averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Manaus?
No month in Manaus is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manaus in November?
185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (297 mm), the driest August (87 mm).
Does it snow in Manaus in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manaus get in November?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manaus in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
