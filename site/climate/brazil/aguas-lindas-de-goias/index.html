Águas Lindas de Goiás at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Águas Lindas de Goiás is 210th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 2,338 hours of sunshine a year) and 133rd wettest (1,631 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ceilândia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Águas Lindas de Goiás is 0.2 °C warmer in September and 0.1 °C warmer in June, with 90 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.3 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, June (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 910 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Águas Lindas de Goiás has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 910 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
