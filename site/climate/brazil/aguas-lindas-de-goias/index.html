Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Águas Lindas de Goiás is 1669th warmest (21.5 °C year average), 862nd sunniest (about 2,338 hours of sunshine a year) and 712th wettest (1,658 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Águas Lindas de Goiás has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 910 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Águas Lindas de Goiás itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.762°S, 48.282°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-997-1317), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
