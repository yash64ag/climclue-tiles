How August compares in Salvador

August is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Salvador. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 69 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Salvador in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salvador in August?
In August, Salvador averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Salvador?
August is not one of Salvador’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salvador in August?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (293 mm), the driest January (62 mm).
Does it snow in Salvador in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salvador get in August?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salvador in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
