Salvador sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bahia · Brazil

The sea off Salvador averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 25.3 °C (78 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Salvador sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Salvador?
In March, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Salvador?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.3 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Salvador in August?
About 25.3 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Salvador?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
