How December compares in Salvador

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Salvador. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 45 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Salvador in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salvador in December?
In December, Salvador averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Salvador?
December is not one of Salvador’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salvador in December?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (293 mm), the driest January (62 mm).
Does it snow in Salvador in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salvador get in December?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salvador in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
