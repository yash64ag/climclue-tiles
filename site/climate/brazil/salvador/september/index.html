How September compares in Salvador

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Salvador. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. October is 0.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Salvador in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salvador in September?
In September, Salvador averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Salvador?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Salvador’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salvador in September?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (293 mm), the driest January (62 mm).
Does it snow in Salvador in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salvador get in September?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salvador in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
