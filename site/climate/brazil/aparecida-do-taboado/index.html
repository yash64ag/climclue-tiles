Aparecida do Taboado weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Aparecida do Taboado, Brazil (Mato Grosso do Sul), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Aparecida do Taboado is warmest in November, with 32.4 °C (90 °F) days and 20.5 °C nights, and coolest in June, with 28.5 °C (83 °F) days and 14.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around January (286 mm); July is driest (17 mm).

Aparecida do Taboado at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Aparecida do Taboado is 940th warmest (24.7 °C year average), 309th sunniest (about 2,595 hours of sunshine a year) and 1177th wettest (1,451 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Fé do Sul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Aparecida do Taboado is 0.1 °C cooler in November and about as warm in June, with 125 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.4 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, June (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in June. The area lies about 643 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Aparecida do Taboado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 643 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aparecida do Taboado itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.087°S, 51.094°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1040-1289), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
