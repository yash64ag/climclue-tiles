Encantado at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Encantado is 2080th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 1304th sunniest (about 2,194 hours of sunshine a year) and 751st wettest (1,632 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arroio do Meio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Encantado is 0.2 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.3 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, July (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 150 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Encantado, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Encantado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 150 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1132-1281), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
