Governador Valadares at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Governador Valadares is 87th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 277th sunniest (about 1,868 hours of sunshine a year) and 321st wettest (1,116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ipatinga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (92 km away), Governador Valadares is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 312 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.3 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 237 km from the sea.

Why Governador Valadares has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 237 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
