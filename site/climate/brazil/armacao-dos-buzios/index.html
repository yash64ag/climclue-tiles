Armação dos Búzios at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Armação dos Búzios is 1336th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 750th sunniest (about 2,381 hours of sunshine a year) and 1735th wettest (1,105 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cabo Frio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Armação dos Búzios is 0.1 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.3 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, July (21.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Armação dos Búzios, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Armação dos Búzios has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Armação dos Búzios itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.747°S, 41.882°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1067-1381), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
