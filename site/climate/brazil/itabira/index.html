Why Itabira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 304 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Itabira climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Itabira?
February, with an average of 22.8 °C (73 °F), day highs around 26.8 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 18.3 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Itabira?
July, with an average of 17.3 °C (63 °F), day highs around 21.7 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 12.3 °C (54 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Itabira?
December, with 403 mm (15.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,752 mm (69.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Itabira?
July, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Itabira?
August, with about 214 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Itabira?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Itabira?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Itabira have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Itabira’s GeoNames location (19.619°S, 43.227°W; ClimaClue area sq-1036-1367), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,053 (GeoNames).
