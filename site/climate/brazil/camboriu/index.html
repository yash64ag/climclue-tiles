Why Camboriú has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Camboriú climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Camboriú?
February, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 26.8 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 22.3 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Camboriú?
July, with an average of 15.8 °C (61 °F), day highs around 18.7 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 13.6 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Camboriú?
January, with 262 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,911 mm (75.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Camboriú?
June, with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Camboriú?
January, with about 170 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Camboriú?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Camboriú?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Camboriú’s GeoNames location (27.025°S, 48.654°W; ClimaClue area sq-1110-1313), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,324 (GeoNames).
