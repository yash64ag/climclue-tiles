São José de Mipibu at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São José de Mipibu is 556th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 2,774 hours of sunshine a year) and 1646th wettest (1,209 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nísia Floresta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), São José de Mipibu is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, July (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why São José de Mipibu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for São José de Mipibu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.075°S, 35.238°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-900-1447), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
