Natal weather in October

Average October weather in Natal, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Natal averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) days and 21.8 °C (71 °F) nights, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of rain over the month and about 282 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Natal

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Natal. Compared with September: 0.5 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. November is 0.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Natal in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Natal in October?
In October, Natal averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Natal?
October is not one of Natal’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Natal in October?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest October (20 mm).
Does it snow in Natal in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Natal get in October?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Natal in October?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Natal in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
