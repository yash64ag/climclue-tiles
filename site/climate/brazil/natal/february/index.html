How February compares in Natal

February is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Natal. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. March is about as warm as February on average.

Natal among towns in Brazil in February

Of the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Natal ranks 45th warmest (average temperature), 5th sunniest and 293rd wettest in February.

Natal in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Natal in February?
In February, Natal averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Natal?
February is not one of Natal’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Natal in February?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (242 mm), the driest October (19 mm).
Does it snow in Natal in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Natal get in February?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Natal in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
