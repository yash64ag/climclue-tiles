How September compares in Natal

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Natal. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 55 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Natal in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Natal in September?
In September, Natal averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Natal?
September is not one of Natal’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Natal in September?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest October (20 mm).
Does it snow in Natal in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Natal get in September?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Natal in September?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Natal in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
