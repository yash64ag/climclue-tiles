How April compares in Natal

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Natal. Compared with March: 0.2 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. May is 0.3 °C cooler than April on average.

Of the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Natal ranks 21st warmest (average temperature), 100th sunniest and 55th wettest in April.

Natal in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Natal in April?
In April, Natal averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 220 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Natal?
April is not one of Natal’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Natal in April?
220 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (242 mm), the driest October (19 mm).
Does it snow in Natal in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Natal get in April?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Natal in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
