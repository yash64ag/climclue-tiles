How December compares in Natal

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Natal. Compared with November: 0.3 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Natal in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Natal in December?
In December, Natal averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Natal?
December is not one of Natal’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Natal in December?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest October (20 mm).
Does it snow in Natal in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Natal get in December?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Natal in December?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Natal in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
