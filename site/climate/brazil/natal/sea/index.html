Natal sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rio Grande do Norte · Brazil

The sea off Natal averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 26.1 °C (79 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Natal sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Natal?
In March, at about 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Natal?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.1 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Natal in August?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Natal?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
