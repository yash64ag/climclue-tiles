Why Guarapuava has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 301 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Guarapuava climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Guarapuava?
January, with an average of 20.4 °C (69 °F), day highs around 24.6 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 16.7 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Guarapuava?
July, with an average of 12.7 °C (55 °F), day highs around 17.3 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 8.9 °C (48 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Guarapuava?
October, with 276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,477 mm (97.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Guarapuava?
August, with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Guarapuava?
December, with about 212 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Guarapuava?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Guarapuava?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Guarapuava’s GeoNames location (25.390°S, 51.465°W; ClimaClue area sq-1093-1285), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 189,630 (GeoNames).
