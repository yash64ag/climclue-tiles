Santa Luzia at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Santa Luzia is 183rd warmest (22.0 °C year average), 80th sunniest (about 2,399 hours of sunshine a year) and 287th wettest (1,333 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vespasiano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Santa Luzia is 0.1 °C warmer in February and about as warm in July, with 96 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.3 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santa Luzia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santa Luzia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
