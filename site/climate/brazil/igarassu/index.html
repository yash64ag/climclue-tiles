Igarassu at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Igarassu is 64th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,669 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (1,737 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Abreu e Lima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Igarassu is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Igarassu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Igarassu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
