Why Araras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 216 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Araras climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Araras?
February, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 19.8 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Araras?
July, with an average of 17.9 °C (64 °F), day highs around 23.6 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 12.2 °C (54 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Araras?
January, with 305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,573 mm (61.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Araras?
August, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Araras?
August, with about 215 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Araras?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Araras?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Araras have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Araras’s GeoNames location (22.357°S, 47.384°W; ClimaClue area sq-1063-1326), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 135,744 (GeoNames).
