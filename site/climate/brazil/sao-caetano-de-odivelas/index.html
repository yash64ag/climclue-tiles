São Caetano de Odivelas at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, São Caetano de Odivelas is 420th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 1164th sunniest (about 2,237 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (2,902 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vigia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), São Caetano de Odivelas is 0.1 °C cooler in November and about as warm in February, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.4 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, February (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why São Caetano de Odivelas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
