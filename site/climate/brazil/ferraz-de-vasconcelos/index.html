Ferraz de Vasconcelos at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Ferraz de Vasconcelos is 2032nd warmest (19.6 °C year average), 2173rd sunniest (about 1,681 hours of sunshine a year) and 577th wettest (1,734 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Poá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Ferraz de Vasconcelos is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.7 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Why Ferraz de Vasconcelos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ferraz de Vasconcelos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.541°S, 46.369°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1075-1336), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
