Vitória do Xingu at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Vitória do Xingu is 255th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 2078th sunniest (about 1,791 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (2,306 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Altamira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Vitória do Xingu is 0.3 °C warmer in October and 0.4 °C warmer in February, with 273 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.3 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, February (26.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Why Vitória do Xingu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vitória do Xingu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (2.880°S, 52.010°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-868-1279), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
