Recanto das Emas at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Recanto das Emas is 240th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 2,334 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (1,685 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Riacho Fundo II, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Recanto das Emas is 0.1 °C cooler in September and 0.2 °C cooler in June, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (22.4 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, June (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 880 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Recanto das Emas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Recanto das Emas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 880 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
