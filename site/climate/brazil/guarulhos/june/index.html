How June compares in Guarulhos

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Guarulhos. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Guarulhos in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guarulhos in June?
In June, Guarulhos averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guarulhos?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Guarulhos’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guarulhos in June?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in Guarulhos in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guarulhos get in June?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guarulhos in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
