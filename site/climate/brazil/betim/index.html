Betim at a glance

Among the 346 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Brazil, Betim is 230th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 2,356 hours of sunshine a year) and 166th wettest (1,550 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Contagem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Betim is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 120 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.4 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, July (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Betim, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Betim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
