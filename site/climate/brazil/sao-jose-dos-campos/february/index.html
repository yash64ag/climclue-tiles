São José dos Campos in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São José dos Campos in February?
In February, São José dos Campos averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit São José dos Campos?
February is not one of São José dos Campos’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São José dos Campos in February?
204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (264 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in São José dos Campos in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São José dos Campos get in February?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for São José dos Campos in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
