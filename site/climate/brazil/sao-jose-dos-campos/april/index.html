São José dos Campos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São José dos Campos in April?
In April, São José dos Campos averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit São José dos Campos?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of São José dos Campos’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
