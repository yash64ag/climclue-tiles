São José dos Campos weather in December

Average December weather in São José dos Campos, Brazil: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, São José dos Campos averages 27.6 °C (82 °F) days and 19.0 °C (66 °F) nights, with 200 mm (7.9 in) of rain over the month and about 136 hours of sunshine.

São José dos Campos in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in São José dos Campos in December?
In December, São José dos Campos averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit São José dos Campos?
December is not one of São José dos Campos’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in São José dos Campos in December?
200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (262 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in São José dos Campos in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does São José dos Campos get in December?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
