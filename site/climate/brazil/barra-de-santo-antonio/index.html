Barra de Santo Antônio at a glance

Among the 2275 towns ClimaClue covers in Brazil, Barra de Santo Antônio is 719th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 443rd sunniest (about 2,530 hours of sunshine a year) and 435th wettest (1,855 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Luís do Quitunde, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Barra de Santo Antônio is 0.1 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 113 mm more precipitation a year. Why Barra de Santo Antônio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
