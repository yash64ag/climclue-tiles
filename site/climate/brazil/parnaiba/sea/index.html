Parnaíba sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Piauí · Brazil

The sea off Parnaíba averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 26.8 °C (80 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Parnaíba sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Parnaíba?
In April, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Parnaíba?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is September, at 26.8 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Parnaíba in September?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Parnaíba?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
