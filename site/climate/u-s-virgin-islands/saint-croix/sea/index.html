Saint Croix sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Saint Croix Island · U.S. Virgin Islands

The sea off Saint Croix averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Saint Croix sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint Croix?
In September, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint Croix?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint Croix in February?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint Croix?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
