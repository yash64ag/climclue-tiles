Tromsø at a glance

Among the 40 towns ClimaClue covers in Norway, Tromsø is the 3rd coolest (3.1 °C year average). Compared with Tromsdalen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Tromsø is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in February, with 92 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (12.6 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, February (-4.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 1 km from Tromsø by up to 4.3 °C (7.8 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Tromsø has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C. The 10 km area around Tromsø, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 0.5 °C a year, 2.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Tromsø climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tromsø?
July, with an average of 12.6 °C (55 °F), day highs around 16.0 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 9.2 °C (49 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tromsø?
February, with an average of -4.1 °C (25 °F), day highs around -1.4 °C (29 °F) and night lows around -6.8 °C (20 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tromsø?
October, with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,815 mm (71.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tromsø?
May, with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tromsø?
June, with about 227 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tromsø?
Yes. About 723 cm (284.6 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 9 months.
When is the best time to visit Tromsø?

How warm is the sea in Tromsø?
The nearby open sea averages 10.8 °C (51 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.7 °C (39 °F) in March. It stays below 20 °C (68 °F) all year. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tromsø itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (69.649°N, 18.955°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 3.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-143-1989), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 41,915, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). How ClimaClue makes these numbers.
