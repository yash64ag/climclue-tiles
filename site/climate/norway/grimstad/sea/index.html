Grimstad sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Agder · Norway

The sea off Grimstad averages 17.2 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.2 °C (38 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Grimstad sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Grimstad?
In August, at about 17.2 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Grimstad?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 3.2 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Grimstad in March?
About 3.2 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Grimstad?
About 9.7 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Grimstad nearby?
In August: Sandefjord (17.9 °C, 129 km), Tønsberg (17.9 °C, 147 km) and Horten (17.9 °C, 162 km).
