Why Drammen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Drammen climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Drammen?
July, with an average of 17.6 °C (64 °F), day highs around 21.1 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 13.2 °C (56 °F).
What is the coldest month in Drammen?
January, with an average of -4.7 °C (24 °F), day highs around -3.0 °C (27 °F) and night lows around -8.4 °C (17 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Drammen?
October, with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 938 mm (36.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Drammen?
February, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Drammen?
June, with about 240 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Drammen?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 280 cm (110.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 7 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Drammen?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 5.6 × 11 km, that contains Drammen’s GeoNames location (59.744°N, 10.204°E; ClimaClue area sq-242-1902), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 106,013 (GeoNames).
