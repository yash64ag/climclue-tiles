Drammen at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Norway, Drammen is the 3rd coolest (6.2 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,493 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (938 mm a year). Compared with Oslo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Drammen is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 22.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Why Drammen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
