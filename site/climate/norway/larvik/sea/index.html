Larvik sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Vestfold · Norway

The sea off Larvik averages 17.5 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.8 °C (37 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Larvik sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Larvik?
In August, at about 17.5 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Larvik?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 2.8 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Larvik in March?
About 2.8 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Larvik?
About 9.7 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
