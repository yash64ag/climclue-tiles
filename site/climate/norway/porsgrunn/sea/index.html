Porsgrunn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Telemark · Norway

The sea off Porsgrunn averages 17.2 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.7 °C (37 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Porsgrunn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Porsgrunn?
In August, at about 17.2 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Porsgrunn?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 2.7 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Porsgrunn in March?
About 2.7 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Porsgrunn?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Porsgrunn nearby?
In August: Sandefjord (17.9 °C, 32 km), Tønsberg (17.9 °C, 45 km) and Horten (17.9 °C, 56 km).
