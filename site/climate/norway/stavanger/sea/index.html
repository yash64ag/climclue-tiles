Stavanger sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rogaland · Norway

The sea off Stavanger averages 16.0 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.5 °C (40 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Stavanger sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Stavanger?
In August, at about 16.0 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Stavanger?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 4.5 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Stavanger in March?
About 4.5 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Stavanger?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Stavanger nearby?
In August: Kristiansand (16.8 °C, 160 km), Grimstad (17.2 °C, 180 km) and Arendal (17.2 °C, 184 km).
