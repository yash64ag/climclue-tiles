Trondheim sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Trøndelag · Norway

The sea off Trondheim averages 13.0 °C (55 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.4 °C (40 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Trondheim sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Trondheim?
In August, at about 13.0 °C (55 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Trondheim?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 4.4 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Trondheim in March?
About 4.4 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Trondheim?
About 8.0 °C (46 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Trondheim nearby?
In August: Sundsvall (15.7 °C, 368 km), Härnösand (15.5 °C, 390 km) and Hudiksvall (15.8 °C, 392 km).
