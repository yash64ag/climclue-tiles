Kongsberg at a glance

Among the 40 towns ClimaClue covers in Norway, Kongsberg is 31st warmest (5.7 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 1,468 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (926 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Drammen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Kongsberg is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Why Kongsberg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kongsberg itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (59.669°N, 9.650°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-243-1896), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
