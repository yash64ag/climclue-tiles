How June compares in Oslo

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Oslo. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Oslo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oslo in June?
In June, Oslo averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Oslo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Oslo’s mild months (June and July), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oslo in June?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (108 mm), the driest February (49 mm).
Does it snow in Oslo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oslo get in June?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oslo in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
