How February compares in Oslo

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Oslo. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Oslo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oslo in February?
In February, Oslo averages a day high of -1.5 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -7.7 °C (18 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 64 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Oslo?
No month in Oslo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oslo in February?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (119 mm), the driest February (54 mm).
Does it snow in Oslo in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 42 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Oslo get in February?
About 64 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oslo in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
