Oslo weather in March

Average March weather in Oslo, Norway: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Oslo averages 5.1 °C (41 °F) days and -3.7 °C (25 °F) nights, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 116 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Oslo

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Oslo. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. April is 5.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Oslo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oslo in March?
In March, Oslo averages a day high of 5.1 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -3.7 °C (25 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Oslo?
March is not one of Oslo’s mild months; those are June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oslo in March?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (108 mm), the driest February (49 mm).
Does it snow in Oslo in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 45 cm (17.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 23 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Oslo get in March?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oslo in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
