How January compares in Oslo

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Oslo. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Oslo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oslo in January?
In January, Oslo averages a day high of -0.1 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -5.3 °C (22 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 33 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Oslo?
January is not one of Oslo’s mild months; those are June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oslo in January?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (108 mm), the driest February (49 mm).
Does it snow in Oslo in January?
Yes, on average: about 58 cm (22.8 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 18 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Oslo get in January?
About 33 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oslo in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
