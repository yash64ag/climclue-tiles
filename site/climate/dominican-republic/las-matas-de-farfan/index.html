Las Matas de Farfán at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in the Dominican Republic, Las Matas de Farfán is 35th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,704 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (1,231 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Juan de la Maguana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Las Matas de Farfán is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 351 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Las Matas de Farfán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Las Matas de Farfán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
