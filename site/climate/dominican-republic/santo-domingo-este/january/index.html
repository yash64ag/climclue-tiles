How January compares in Santo Domingo Este

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Santo Domingo Este. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Santo Domingo Este in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo Este in January?
In January, Santo Domingo Este averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Santo Domingo Este?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Santo Domingo Este’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo Este in January?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (220 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo Este in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo Este get in January?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo Este in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
