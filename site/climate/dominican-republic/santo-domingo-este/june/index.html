Santo Domingo Este in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo Este in June?
In June, Santo Domingo Este averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Santo Domingo Este?
June is not one of Santo Domingo Este’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo Este in June?
174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (220 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo Este in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo Este get in June?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo Este in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
