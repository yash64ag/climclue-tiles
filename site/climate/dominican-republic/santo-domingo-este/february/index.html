Santo Domingo Este in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo Este in February?
In February, Santo Domingo Este averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Santo Domingo Este?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Santo Domingo Este’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo Este in February?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (220 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo Este in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo Este get in February?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo Este in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
