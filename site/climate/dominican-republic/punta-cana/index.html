Why Punta Cana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Punta Cana climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Punta Cana?
August, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Punta Cana?
February, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 26.0 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 22.9 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Punta Cana?
May, with 169 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,306 mm (51.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Punta Cana?
March, with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Punta Cana?
March, with about 273 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Punta Cana?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Punta Cana?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February and March.
What type of climate does Punta Cana have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Punta Cana’s GeoNames location (18.582°N, 68.404°W; ClimaClue area sq-654-1115), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 100,023 (GeoNames).
