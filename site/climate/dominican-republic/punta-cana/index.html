Punta Cana at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Dominican Republic, Punta Cana is the 3rd warmest (25.9 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,975 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (1,306 mm a year). Compared with Salvaleón de Higüey, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Punta Cana is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in February, with 118 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, February (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Punta Cana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Punta Cana

For mild weather, the best months to visit Punta Cana are January, February and March.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and March.

Why Punta Cana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
