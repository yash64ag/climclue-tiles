San Ignacio de Sabaneta at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in the Dominican Republic, San Ignacio de Sabaneta is 14th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,598 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (1,242 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), San Ignacio de Sabaneta is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 129 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Why San Ignacio de Sabaneta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Ignacio de Sabaneta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.478°N, 71.339°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-645-1086), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
