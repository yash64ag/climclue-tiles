San Pedro de Macorís at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Dominican Republic, San Pedro de Macorís is the 2nd warmest (26.0 °C year average). Compared with La Romana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), San Pedro de Macorís is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 192 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, February (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Pedro de Macorís, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit San Pedro de Macorís

For mild weather, the best months to visit San Pedro de Macorís are January, February and March.

Why San Pedro de Macorís has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
