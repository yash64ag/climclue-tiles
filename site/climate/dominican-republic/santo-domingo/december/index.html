How December compares in Santo Domingo

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santo Domingo. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 57 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Santo Domingo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo in December?
In December, Santo Domingo averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Santo Domingo?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Santo Domingo’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo in December?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (218 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo get in December?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Santo Domingo in December?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
