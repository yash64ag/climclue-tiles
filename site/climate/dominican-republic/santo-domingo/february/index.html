How February compares in Santo Domingo

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Santo Domingo. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Santo Domingo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo in February?
In February, Santo Domingo averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Santo Domingo?
No month in Santo Domingo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo in February?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (218 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo get in February?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Santo Domingo in February?
About 26.4 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
