How September compares in Santo Domingo

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Santo Domingo. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. October is 0.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Santo Domingo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo in September?
In September, Santo Domingo averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Santo Domingo?
September is not one of Santo Domingo’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo in September?
208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (218 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo get in September?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
