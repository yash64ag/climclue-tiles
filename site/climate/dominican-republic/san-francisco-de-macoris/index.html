San Francisco de Macorís at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Dominican Republic, San Francisco de Macorís is 9th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,533 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Vega, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), San Francisco de Macorís is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Francisco de Macorís, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Francisco de Macorís has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
