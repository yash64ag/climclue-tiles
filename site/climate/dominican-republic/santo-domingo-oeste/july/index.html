Santo Domingo Oeste in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo Oeste in July?
In July, Santo Domingo Oeste averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Santo Domingo Oeste?
July is not one of Santo Domingo Oeste’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo Oeste in July?
170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (218 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo Oeste in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo Oeste get in July?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo Oeste in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
