Santo Domingo Oeste in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo Oeste in April?
In April, Santo Domingo Oeste averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Santo Domingo Oeste?
April is not one of Santo Domingo Oeste’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo Oeste in April?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (218 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo Oeste in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo Oeste get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo Oeste in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
