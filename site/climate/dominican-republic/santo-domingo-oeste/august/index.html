Santo Domingo Oeste weather in August

Average August weather in Santo Domingo Oeste, Dominican Republic: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Santo Domingo Oeste averages 31.2 °C (88 °F) days and 21.7 °C (71 °F) nights, with 190 mm (7.5 in) of rain over the month and about 240 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Santo Domingo Oeste

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Santo Domingo Oeste. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. September is 0.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Santo Domingo Oeste in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santo Domingo Oeste in August?
In August, Santo Domingo Oeste averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Santo Domingo Oeste?
August is not one of Santo Domingo Oeste’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santo Domingo Oeste in August?
190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (215 mm), the driest February (63 mm).
Does it snow in Santo Domingo Oeste in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santo Domingo Oeste get in August?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Santo Domingo Oeste in August?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Santo Domingo Oeste in August?
