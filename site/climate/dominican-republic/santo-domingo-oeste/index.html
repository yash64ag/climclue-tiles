Santo Domingo Oeste at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Dominican Republic, Santo Domingo Oeste is 8th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,729 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (1,666 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santo Domingo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Santo Domingo Oeste is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santo Domingo Oeste, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santo Domingo Oeste has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
