Villa Bisonó at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in the Dominican Republic, Villa Bisonó is 16th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 2,508 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (1,159 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Esperanza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Villa Bisonó is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 75 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Why Villa Bisonó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-644-1091), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
