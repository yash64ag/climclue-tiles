San Fernando de Monte Cristi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Monte Cristi Province · Dominican Republic

The sea off San Fernando de Monte Cristi averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.3 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

San Fernando de Monte Cristi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Fernando de Monte Cristi?
In September, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Fernando de Monte Cristi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.3 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Fernando de Monte Cristi in February?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in San Fernando de Monte Cristi?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in San Fernando de Monte Cristi nearby?
In September: Saint-Marc (30.0 °C, 138 km).
