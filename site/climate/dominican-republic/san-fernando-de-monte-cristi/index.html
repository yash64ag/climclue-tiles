San Fernando de Monte Cristi at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in the Dominican Republic, San Fernando de Monte Cristi is the 2nd warmest (26.6 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (921 mm a year). Compared with Dajabón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), San Fernando de Monte Cristi is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 283 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (24.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.6 °C (67 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Fernando de Monte Cristi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit San Fernando de Monte Cristi

For mild weather, the best months to visit San Fernando de Monte Cristi are January and February.

Why San Fernando de Monte Cristi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Fernando de Monte Cristi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.848°N, 71.646°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-641-1083), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
