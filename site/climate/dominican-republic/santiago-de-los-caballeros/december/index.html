How December compares in Santiago de los Caballeros

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santiago de los Caballeros. Compared with November: 1.1 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Santiago de los Caballeros in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in December?
In December, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
December is not one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in December?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (174 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in December?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de los Caballeros in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
