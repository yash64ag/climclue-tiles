How February compares in Santiago de los Caballeros

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Santiago de los Caballeros. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. March is 0.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Santiago de los Caballeros in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in February?
In February, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months (January, February and March), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in February?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (174 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in February?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de los Caballeros in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
