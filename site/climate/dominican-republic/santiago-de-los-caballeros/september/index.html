Santiago de los Caballeros in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in September?
In September, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
September is not one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in September?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (171 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in September?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de los Caballeros in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
