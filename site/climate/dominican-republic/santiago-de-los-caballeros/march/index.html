How March compares in Santiago de los Caballeros

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Santiago de los Caballeros. Compared with February: 0.8 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Santiago de los Caballeros in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in March?
In March, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in March?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (171 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in March?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
