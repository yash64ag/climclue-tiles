Santiago de los Caballeros in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in August?
In August, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
August is not one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in August?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (171 mm), the driest February (65 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in August?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
