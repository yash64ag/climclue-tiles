Santiago de los Caballeros in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in June?
In June, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
June is not one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in June?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (174 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in June?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de los Caballeros in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
