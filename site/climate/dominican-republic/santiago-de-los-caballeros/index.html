Santiago de los Caballeros at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Dominican Republic, Santiago de los Caballeros is the 3rd coolest (24.9 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,489 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,304 mm a year). Compared with La Vega, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Santiago de los Caballeros is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 242 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in February. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santiago de los Caballeros, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros

For mild weather, the best months to visit Santiago de los Caballeros are January, February and March.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and March.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (26.9 °C), July (26.8 °C) and June (26.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (70 mm), July (76 mm) and August (82 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): August (247 h), July (244 h) and June (226 h).

Why Santiago de los Caballeros has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
