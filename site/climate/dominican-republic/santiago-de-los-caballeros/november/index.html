Santiago de los Caballeros in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de los Caballeros in November?
In November, Santiago de los Caballeros averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Santiago de los Caballeros?
November is not one of Santiago de los Caballeros’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de los Caballeros in November?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (174 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de los Caballeros in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de los Caballeros get in November?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de los Caballeros in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
