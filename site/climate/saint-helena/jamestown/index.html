Jamestown at a glance

Its warmest month, March (22.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (18.9 °C). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jamestown, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jamestown has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
