Maldonado at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Uruguay, Maldonado is the 2nd warmest (16.4 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,341 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,208 mm a year). Compared with Montevideo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Maldonado is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 129 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.9 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, July (10.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maldonado, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maldonado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
