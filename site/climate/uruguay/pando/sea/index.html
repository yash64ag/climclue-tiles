Pando sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canelones · Uruguay

The sea off Pando averages 22.9 °C (73 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 11.0 °C (52 °F) in July. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to March.

Pando sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pando?
In February, at about 22.9 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pando?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to March. The coolest month is July, at 11.0 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pando in July?
About 11.0 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pando?
About 17.1 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pando nearby?
In February: Magdalena (23.9 °C, 148 km), Colonia del Sacramento (24.0 °C, 175 km) and Berisso (24.1 °C, 177 km).
