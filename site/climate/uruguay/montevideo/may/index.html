How May compares in Montevideo

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Montevideo. Compared with April: 3.2 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. June is 2.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Montevideo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montevideo in May?
In May, Montevideo averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Montevideo?
May is not one of Montevideo’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montevideo in May?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (110 mm), the driest December (76 mm).
Does it snow in Montevideo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montevideo get in May?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montevideo in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
