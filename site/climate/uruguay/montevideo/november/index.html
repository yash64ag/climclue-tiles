How November compares in Montevideo

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Montevideo. Compared with October: 2.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. December is 2.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Montevideo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montevideo in November?
In November, Montevideo averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Montevideo?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Montevideo’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montevideo in November?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (110 mm), the driest December (76 mm).
Does it snow in Montevideo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montevideo get in November?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Montevideo in November?
About 18.5 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (22.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Montevideo in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
