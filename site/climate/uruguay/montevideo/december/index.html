How December compares in Montevideo

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Montevideo. Compared with November: 2.4 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. January is 1.7 °C warmer than December on average.

Montevideo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montevideo in December?
In December, Montevideo averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Montevideo?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Montevideo’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montevideo in December?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (110 mm), the driest December (76 mm).
Does it snow in Montevideo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montevideo get in December?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montevideo in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
