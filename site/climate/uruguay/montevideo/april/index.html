How April compares in Montevideo

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Montevideo. Compared with March: 3.2 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. May is 3.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Montevideo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montevideo in April?
In April, Montevideo averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Montevideo?
April is not one of Montevideo’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montevideo in April?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (110 mm), the driest December (76 mm).
Does it snow in Montevideo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montevideo get in April?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montevideo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
