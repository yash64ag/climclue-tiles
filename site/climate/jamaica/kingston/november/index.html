How November compares in Kingston

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kingston. Compared with October: 0.6 °C cooler, 110 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. December is 0.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Kingston in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kingston in November?
In November, Kingston averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kingston?
November is not one of Kingston’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kingston in November?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (265 mm), the driest February (53 mm).
Does it snow in Kingston in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kingston get in November?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kingston in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
