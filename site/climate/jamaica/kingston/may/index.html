How May compares in Kingston

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kingston. Compared with April: 0.7 °C warmer, 109 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. June is 0.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Kingston in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kingston in May?
In May, Kingston averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kingston?
May is not one of Kingston’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kingston in May?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (265 mm), the driest February (53 mm).
Does it snow in Kingston in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kingston get in May?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kingston in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
