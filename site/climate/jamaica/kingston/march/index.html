Kingston weather in March

Average March weather in Kingston, Jamaica: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Kingston averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) days and 20.2 °C (68 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 234 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Kingston

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kingston. Compared with February: 0.3 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Kingston in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kingston in March?
In March, Kingston averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kingston?
March is not one of Kingston’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kingston in March?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (247 mm), the driest February (48 mm).
Does it snow in Kingston in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kingston get in March?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kingston in March?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kingston in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
