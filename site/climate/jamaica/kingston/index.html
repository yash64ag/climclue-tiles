Why Kingston has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Kingston climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kingston?
August, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 29.4 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kingston?
January, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 22.0 °C (72 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kingston?
October, with 265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,500 mm (59.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kingston?
February, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kingston?
April, with about 238 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kingston?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kingston?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March and December.
What type of climate does Kingston have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kingston’s GeoNames location (17.997°N, 76.794°W; ClimaClue area sq-660-1032), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 937,700 (GeoNames).
