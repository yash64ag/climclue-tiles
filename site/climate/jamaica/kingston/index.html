Kingston at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Jamaica, Kingston is the 3rd warmest (25.6 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,540 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,500 mm a year). Compared with Portmore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Kingston is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 278 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kingston, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kingston has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
