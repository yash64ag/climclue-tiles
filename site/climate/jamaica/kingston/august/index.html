How August compares in Kingston

August is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kingston. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 60 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Kingston in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kingston in August?
In August, Kingston averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kingston?
August is not one of Kingston’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kingston in August?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (265 mm), the driest February (53 mm).
Does it snow in Kingston in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kingston get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kingston in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
