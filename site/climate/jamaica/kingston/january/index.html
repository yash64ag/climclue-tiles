How January compares in Kingston

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kingston. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Kingston in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kingston in January?
In January, Kingston averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kingston?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kingston’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kingston in January?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (265 mm), the driest February (53 mm).
Does it snow in Kingston in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kingston get in January?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kingston in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
