How December compares in Kingston

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kingston. Compared with November: 0.8 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Kingston in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kingston in December?
In December, Kingston averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kingston?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Kingston’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kingston in December?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (265 mm), the driest February (53 mm).
Does it snow in Kingston in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kingston get in December?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kingston in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
