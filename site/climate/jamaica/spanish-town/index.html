Spanish Town at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Jamaica, Spanish Town is the 2nd warmest (26.3 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,568 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,164 mm a year). Compared with Portmore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Spanish Town is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Spanish Town, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Spanish Town has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
