Spanish Town sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Saint Catherine Parish · Jamaica

The sea off Spanish Town averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Spanish Town sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Spanish Town?
In September, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Spanish Town?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Spanish Town in February?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Spanish Town?
About 27.5 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Spanish Town nearby?
In September: Ocho Rios (29.5 °C, 49 km), Barrett Town (29.6 °C, 107 km) and Montego Bay (29.5 °C, 115 km).
