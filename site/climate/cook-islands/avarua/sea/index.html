Avarua sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rarotonga · Cook Islands

The sea off Avarua averages 27.6 °C (82 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 23.9 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Avarua sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Avarua?
In March, at about 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Avarua?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.9 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Avarua in August?
About 23.9 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Avarua?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
