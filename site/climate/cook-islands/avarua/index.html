Best time to visit Avarua

No month in Avarua is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are June (23.2 °C, 150 mm), August (22.2 °C, 154 mm) and October (22.9 °C, 162 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (26.0 °C), February (26.0 °C) and January (25.5 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): June (150 mm), August (154 mm) and October (162 mm).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Avarua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Avarua itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.208°S, 159.775°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1052-202), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
