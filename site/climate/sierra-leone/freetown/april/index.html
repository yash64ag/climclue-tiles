How April compares in Freetown

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Freetown. Compared with March: 0.3 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Freetown in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Freetown in April?
In April, Freetown averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Freetown?
No month in Freetown is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Freetown in April?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (979 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Freetown in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Freetown get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Freetown in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
