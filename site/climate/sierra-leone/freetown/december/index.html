How December compares in Freetown

December is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Freetown. Compared with November: 0.5 °C warmer, 82 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Freetown in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Freetown in December?
In December, Freetown averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Freetown?
No month in Freetown is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Freetown in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (979 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Freetown in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Freetown get in December?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Freetown in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
