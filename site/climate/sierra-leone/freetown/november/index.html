Freetown weather in November

Average November weather in Freetown, Sierra Leone: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Freetown averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) days and 23.1 °C (74 °F) nights, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 196 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Freetown

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Freetown. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 206 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. December is 0.5 °C warmer than November on average.

Freetown in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Freetown in November?
In November, Freetown averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Freetown?
No month in Freetown is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Freetown in November?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (886 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Freetown in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Freetown get in November?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Freetown in November?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is November (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Freetown in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
