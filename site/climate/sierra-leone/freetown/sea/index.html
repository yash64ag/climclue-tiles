Freetown sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Area · Sierra Leone

The sea off Freetown averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Freetown sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Freetown?
In November, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Freetown?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.9 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Freetown in August?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Freetown?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Freetown nearby?
In November: Contumo (29.2 °C, 456 km), Bissau (29.2 °C, 456 km) and Reino de Antula (29.2 °C, 458 km).
