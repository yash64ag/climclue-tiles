Kenema at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sierra Leone, Kenema is the 2nd warmest (25.4 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,848 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (2,391 mm a year). Compared with Bo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Kenema is 0.3 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 226 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.4 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, August (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 118 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kenema, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kenema has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
