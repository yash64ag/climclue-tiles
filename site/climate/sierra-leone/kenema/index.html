Why Kenema has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kenema climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kenema?
March, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 33.5 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 23.3 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kenema?
August, with an average of 23.8 °C (75 °F), day highs around 27.4 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 21.7 °C (71 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kenema?
August, with 412 mm (16.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,391 mm (94.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kenema?
January, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kenema?
March, with about 195 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kenema?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kenema?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kenema’s GeoNames location (7.877°N, 11.190°W; ClimaClue area sq-761-1688), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 255,110 (GeoNames).
