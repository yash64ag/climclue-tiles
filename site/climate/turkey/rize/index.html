Rize at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Rize is the least sunny (about 2,430 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (2,375 mm a year). Compared with Trabzon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Rize is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in February, with 1,467 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.9 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, February (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rize, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rize has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
