Denizli at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Denizli is 40th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 3,051 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (707 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Merkezefendi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Denizli is 3.3 °C cooler in July and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 148 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 120 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Denizli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Denizli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 120 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 9.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
