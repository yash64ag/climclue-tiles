Why Amasya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 93 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Amasya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Amasya?
August, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.2 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 15.6 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Amasya?
January, with an average of 1.9 °C (35 °F), day highs around 5.4 °C (42 °F) and night lows around -2.9 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Amasya?
April, with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 618 mm (24.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Amasya?
August, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Amasya?
July, with about 368 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Amasya?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 125 cm (49.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Amasya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Amasya have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Amasya’s GeoNames location (40.653°N, 35.833°E; ClimaClue area sq-433-2158), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 114,921 (GeoNames).
