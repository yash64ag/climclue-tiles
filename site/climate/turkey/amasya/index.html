Amasya at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Amasya is 94th warmest (12.3 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 2,832 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Turhal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Amasya is 0.9 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 199 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.3 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 93 km from the sea.

Why Amasya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 93 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
