Gediz at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Gediz is 262nd warmest (13.7 °C year average), 222nd sunniest (about 2,858 hours of sunshine a year) and 295th wettest (555 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uşak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Gediz is about as warm in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 108 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 175 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gediz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gediz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 175 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gediz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.994°N, 29.391°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-450-2093), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
