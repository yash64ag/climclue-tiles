Why Osmaniye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Osmaniye climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Osmaniye?
August, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 32.5 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 21.5 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Osmaniye?
January, with an average of 7.0 °C (45 °F), day highs around 10.9 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 2.6 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Osmaniye?
December, with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,380 mm (54.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Osmaniye?
July, with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Osmaniye?
July, with about 376 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Osmaniye?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Osmaniye?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June and September.
What type of climate does Osmaniye have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Osmaniye’s GeoNames location (37.074°N, 36.248°E; ClimaClue area sq-469-2162), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 202,837 (GeoNames).
