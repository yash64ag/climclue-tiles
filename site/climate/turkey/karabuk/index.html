Why Karabük has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Karabük climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Karabük?
August, with an average of 21.4 °C (70 °F), day highs around 26.3 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 14.6 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Karabük?
January, with an average of 1.1 °C (34 °F), day highs around 5.2 °C (41 °F) and night lows around -4.0 °C (25 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Karabük?
December, with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 969 mm (38.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Karabük?
August, with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Karabük?
July, with about 363 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Karabük?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 192 cm (75.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Karabük?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Karabük have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Karabük’s GeoNames location (41.205°N, 32.628°E; ClimaClue area sq-427-2126), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 125,403 (GeoNames).
