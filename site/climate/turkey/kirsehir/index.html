Kırşehir at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Kırşehir is 93rd warmest (12.3 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 2,840 hours of sunshine a year) and 104th wettest (416 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aksaray, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Kırşehir is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 272 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kırşehir, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kırşehir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
