Arhavi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Artvin · Turkey

The sea off Arhavi averages 24.5 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.3 °C (45 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Arhavi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Arhavi?
In August, at about 24.5 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Arhavi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 7.3 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Arhavi in March?
About 7.3 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Arhavi?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Arhavi nearby?
In August: Ardeşen (25.6 °C, 33 km), Batumi (25.9 °C, 42 km) and Çayeli (25.6 °C, 57 km).
