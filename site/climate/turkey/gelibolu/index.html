Gelibolu at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Gelibolu is 125th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 3,024 hours of sunshine a year) and 255th wettest (603 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Çanakkale, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Gelibolu is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.7 °C), averages 17.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gelibolu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gelibolu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gelibolu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.408°N, 26.672°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-435-2066), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
