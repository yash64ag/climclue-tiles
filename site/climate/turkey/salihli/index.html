Salihli at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Salihli is 27th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 3,018 hours of sunshine a year) and 87th wettest (482 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Turgutlu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Salihli is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.0 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (7.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Salihli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Salihli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C. The 10 km area around Salihli, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.6 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
