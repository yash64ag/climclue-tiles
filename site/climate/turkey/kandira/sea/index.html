Kandıra sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kocaeli · Turkey

The sea off Kandıra averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.1 °C (45 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Kandıra sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kandıra?
In August, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kandıra?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 7.1 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kandıra in February?
About 7.1 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kandıra?
About 15.2 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
