Dörtyol at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Dörtyol is 36th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 112th sunniest (about 3,000 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,426 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Erzin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Dörtyol is about as warm in August and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dörtyol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dörtyol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C. The 10 km area around Dörtyol, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 17.7 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
