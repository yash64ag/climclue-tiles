Çerkeş at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Çerkeş is 391st warmest (9.3 °C year average), 202nd sunniest (about 2,883 hours of sunshine a year) and 309th wettest (534 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kızılcahamam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Çerkeş is 2.4 °C cooler in August and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 86 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.6 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Why Çerkeş has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. The 10 km area around Çerkeş, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 8.2 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Çerkeş itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.812°N, 32.894°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-431-2128), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
