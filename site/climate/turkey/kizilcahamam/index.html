Why Kızılcahamam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 141 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C. The 10 km area around Kızılcahamam, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.3 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
