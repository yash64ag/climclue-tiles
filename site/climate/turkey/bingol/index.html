Bingöl at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Bingöl is the 3rd coolest (9.0 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (1,326 mm a year). Compared with Diyarbakır, the nearest covered town with different numbers (111 km away), Bingöl is 7.0 °C cooler in August and 8.2 °C cooler in January, with 760 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 29.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 245 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bingöl, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bingöl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 245 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
