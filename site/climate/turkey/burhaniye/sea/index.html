Burhaniye sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Balıkesir · Turkey

The sea off Burhaniye averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.4 °C (56 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Burhaniye sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Burhaniye?
In August, at about 22.0 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Burhaniye?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.4 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Burhaniye in February?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Burhaniye?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Burhaniye nearby?
In August: Mytilene (23.0 °C, 57 km), Aliağa (23.4 °C, 78 km) and Çanakkale (24.3 °C, 87 km).
