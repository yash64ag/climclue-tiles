Başakşehir at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Başakşehir is 58th warmest (14.5 °C year average), 87th sunniest (about 2,757 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (797 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sultangazi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Başakşehir is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 65 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, February (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Başakşehir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
