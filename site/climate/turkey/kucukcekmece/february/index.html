How February compares in Küçükçekmece

February is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Küçükçekmece. Compared with January: 0.4 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. March is 1.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Küçükçekmece in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Küçükçekmece in February?
In February, Küçükçekmece averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Küçükçekmece?
February is not one of Küçükçekmece’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Küçükçekmece in February?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (116 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Küçükçekmece in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.5 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Küçükçekmece get in February?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Küçükçekmece in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
