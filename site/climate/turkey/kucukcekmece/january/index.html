How January compares in Küçükçekmece

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Küçükçekmece. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Küçükçekmece in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Küçükçekmece in January?
In January, Küçükçekmece averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Küçükçekmece?
January is not one of Küçükçekmece’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Küçükçekmece in January?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (116 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Küçükçekmece in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.2 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Küçükçekmece get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Küçükçekmece in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
