Küçükçekmece at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Küçükçekmece is 165th warmest (14.9 °C year average), 296th sunniest (about 2,783 hours of sunshine a year) and 179th wettest (704 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bahçelievler, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Küçükçekmece is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, February (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Küçükçekmece has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
