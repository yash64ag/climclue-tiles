Küçükçekmece weather in March

How March compares in Küçükçekmece

Küçükçekmece in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Küçükçekmece in March?
In March, Küçükçekmece averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Küçükçekmece?
March is not one of Küçükçekmece’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Küçükçekmece in March?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (112 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Küçükçekmece in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Küçükçekmece get in March?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Küçükçekmece in March?
About 8.3 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
