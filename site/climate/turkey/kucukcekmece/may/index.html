Küçükçekmece in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Küçükçekmece in May?
In May, Küçükçekmece averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 304 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Küçükçekmece?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Küçükçekmece’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Küçükçekmece in May?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (116 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Küçükçekmece in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Küçükçekmece get in May?
About 304 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Küçükçekmece in May?
