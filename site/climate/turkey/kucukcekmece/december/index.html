How December compares in Küçükçekmece

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Küçükçekmece. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Küçükçekmece in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Küçükçekmece in December?
In December, Küçükçekmece averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Küçükçekmece?
December is not one of Küçükçekmece’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Küçükçekmece in December?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (112 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Küçükçekmece in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Küçükçekmece get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Küçükçekmece in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
