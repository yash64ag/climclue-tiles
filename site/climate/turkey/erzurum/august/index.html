How August compares in Erzurum

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Erzurum. Compared with July: about as warm, 12 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Erzurum in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in August?
In August, Erzurum averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 336 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Erzurum?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Erzurum’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in August?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (83 mm), the driest August (21 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in August?
About 336 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erzurum in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
