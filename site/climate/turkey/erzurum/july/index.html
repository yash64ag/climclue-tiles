How July compares in Erzurum

July is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Erzurum. Compared with June: 3.3 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Erzurum in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in July?
In July, Erzurum averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 359 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Erzurum?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Erzurum’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in July?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (76 mm), the driest August (19 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in July?
About 359 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erzurum in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
