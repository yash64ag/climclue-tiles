How February compares in Erzurum

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Erzurum. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Erzurum in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in February?
In February, Erzurum averages a day high of 0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -10.2 °C (14 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Erzurum?
February is not one of Erzurum’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in February?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (76 mm), the driest August (19 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in February?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erzurum in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
