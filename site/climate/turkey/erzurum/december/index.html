How December compares in Erzurum

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Erzurum. Compared with November: 5.4 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Erzurum in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in December?
In December, Erzurum averages a day high of 0.9 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -10.0 °C (14 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Erzurum?
December is not one of Erzurum’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in December?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (76 mm), the driest August (19 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 24 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in December?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erzurum in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
