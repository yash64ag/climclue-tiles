How May compares in Erzurum

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Erzurum. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. June is 4.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Erzurum in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in May?
In May, Erzurum averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Erzurum?
May is not one of Erzurum’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in May?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (83 mm), the driest August (21 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in May?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erzurum in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
