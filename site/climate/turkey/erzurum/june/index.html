Erzurum weather in June

Average June weather in Erzurum, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Erzurum averages 21.1 °C (70 °F) days and 7.5 °C (46 °F) nights, with 52 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 306 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Erzurum

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Erzurum. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Erzurum in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in June?
In June, Erzurum averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Erzurum?
June is not one of Erzurum’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in June?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (83 mm), the driest August (21 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in June?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
