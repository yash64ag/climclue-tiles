How October compares in Erzurum

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Erzurum. Compared with September: 5.7 °C cooler, 29 mm more precipitation and 75 hours less sunshine. November is 7.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Erzurum in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erzurum in October?
In October, Erzurum averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Erzurum?
October is not one of Erzurum’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erzurum in October?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (76 mm), the driest August (19 mm).
Does it snow in Erzurum in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.8 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Erzurum get in October?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erzurum in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
