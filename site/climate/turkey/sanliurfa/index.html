Şanlıurfa at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Şanlıurfa is the sunniest (about 3,232 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Suruç, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Şanlıurfa is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 168 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.9 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 235 km from the sea.

Why Şanlıurfa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 235 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
