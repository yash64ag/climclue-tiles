Antalya at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Antalya is 8th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 3,103 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,012 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Muratpaşa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Antalya is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Antalya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Antalya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
