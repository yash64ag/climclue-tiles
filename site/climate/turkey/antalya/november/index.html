Antalya weather in November

Average November weather in Antalya, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Antalya averages 20.7 °C (69 °F) days and 9.9 °C (50 °F) nights, with 162 mm (6.4 in) of rain over the month and about 180 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Antalya

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Antalya. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 92 mm more precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. December is 3.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Antalya in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antalya in November?
In November, Antalya averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Antalya?
November is not one of Antalya’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antalya in November?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (231 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Antalya in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antalya get in November?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Antalya in November?
About 20.5 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Antalya in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
