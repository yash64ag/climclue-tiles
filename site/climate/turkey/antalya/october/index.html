How October compares in Antalya

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Antalya. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, 52 mm more precipitation and 79 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Antalya in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antalya in October?
In October, Antalya averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Antalya?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Antalya’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antalya in October?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Antalya in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antalya get in October?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antalya in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
