How April compares in Antalya

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Antalya. Compared with March: 3.6 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Antalya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antalya in April?
In April, Antalya averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Antalya?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Antalya’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antalya in April?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (231 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Antalya in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antalya get in April?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Antalya in April?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Antalya in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
