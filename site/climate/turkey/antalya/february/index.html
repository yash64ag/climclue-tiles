How February compares in Antalya

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Antalya. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 60 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 2.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Antalya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antalya in February?
In February, Antalya averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Antalya?
February is not one of Antalya’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antalya in February?
126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Antalya in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Antalya get in February?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antalya in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
