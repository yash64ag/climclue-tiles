How January compares in Antalya

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Antalya. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Antalya in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antalya in January?
In January, Antalya averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Antalya?
January is not one of Antalya’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antalya in January?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Antalya in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.4 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Antalya get in January?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antalya in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
