How March compares in Antalya

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Antalya. Compared with February: 2.8 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Antalya in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antalya in March?
In March, Antalya averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Antalya?
March is not one of Antalya’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antalya in March?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Antalya in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antalya get in March?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antalya in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
