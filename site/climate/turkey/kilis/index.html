Why Kilis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kilis climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kilis?
July, with an average of 27.8 °C (82 °F), day highs around 35.2 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 18.9 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kilis?
January, with an average of 4.3 °C (40 °F), day highs around 8.3 °C (47 °F) and night lows around -0.3 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kilis?
December, with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 655 mm (25.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kilis?
July, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kilis?
July, with about 419 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kilis?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kilis?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Kilis have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Kilis’s GeoNames location (36.716°N, 37.115°E; ClimaClue area sq-472-2171), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 111,648 (GeoNames).
