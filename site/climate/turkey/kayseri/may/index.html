How May compares in Kayseri

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kayseri. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. June is 4.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Kayseri in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kayseri in May?
In May, Kayseri averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kayseri?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kayseri’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kayseri in May?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (55 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kayseri in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kayseri get in May?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kayseri in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
