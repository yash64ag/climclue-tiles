Kayseri at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Kayseri is 95th warmest (12.2 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,867 hours of sunshine a year) and 107th wettest (373 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kırşehir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (123 km away), Kayseri is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 218 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kayseri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kayseri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 218 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
