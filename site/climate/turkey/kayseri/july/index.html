How July compares in Kayseri

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kayseri. Compared with June: 3.7 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 62 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Kayseri in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kayseri in July?
In July, Kayseri averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 391 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kayseri?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Kayseri’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kayseri in July?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (51 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kayseri in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kayseri get in July?
About 391 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kayseri in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
