How April compares in Kayseri

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kayseri. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 4.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Kayseri in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kayseri in April?
In April, Kayseri averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kayseri?
April is not one of Kayseri’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kayseri in April?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (55 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kayseri in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.2 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kayseri get in April?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kayseri in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
