How January compares in Kayseri

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kayseri. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Kayseri in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kayseri in January?
In January, Kayseri averages a day high of 4.3 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -5.6 °C (22 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kayseri?
January is not one of Kayseri’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kayseri in January?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (51 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kayseri in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kayseri get in January?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kayseri in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
