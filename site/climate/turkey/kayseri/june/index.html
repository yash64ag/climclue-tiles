How June compares in Kayseri

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kayseri. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Kayseri in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kayseri in June?
In June, Kayseri averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 329 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kayseri?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Kayseri’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kayseri in June?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (55 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kayseri in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kayseri get in June?
About 329 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kayseri in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
