How November compares in Kayseri

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kayseri. Compared with October: 7.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 66 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Kayseri in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kayseri in November?
In November, Kayseri averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.2 °C (34 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kayseri?
November is not one of Kayseri’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kayseri in November?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (55 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kayseri in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kayseri get in November?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kayseri in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
