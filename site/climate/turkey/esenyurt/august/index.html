Esenyurt in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenyurt in August?
In August, Esenyurt averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 328 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Esenyurt?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Esenyurt’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
