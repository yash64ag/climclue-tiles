Esenyurt at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Esenyurt is 199th warmest (14.5 °C year average), 292nd sunniest (about 2,789 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (803 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yakuplu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Esenyurt is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.0 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, February (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Esenyurt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
