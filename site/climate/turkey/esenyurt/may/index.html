Esenyurt in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenyurt in May?
In May, Esenyurt averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 304 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Esenyurt?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Esenyurt’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
