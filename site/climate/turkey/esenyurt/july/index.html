Esenyurt weather in July

Average July weather in Esenyurt, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How July compares in Esenyurt

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Esenyurt. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Esenyurt in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenyurt in July?
In July, Esenyurt averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 371 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Esenyurt?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Esenyurt’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenyurt in July?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (128 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Esenyurt in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenyurt get in July?
About 371 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Esenyurt in July?
