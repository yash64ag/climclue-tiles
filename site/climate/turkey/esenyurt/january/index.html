How January compares in Esenyurt

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Esenyurt. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C cooler than January on average.

Esenyurt in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenyurt in January?
In January, Esenyurt averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Esenyurt?
January is not one of Esenyurt’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenyurt in January?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (27 mm).
Does it snow in Esenyurt in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenyurt get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Esenyurt in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
