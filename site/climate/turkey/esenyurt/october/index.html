Esenyurt in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenyurt in October?
In October, Esenyurt averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Esenyurt?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Esenyurt’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenyurt in October?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (128 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Esenyurt in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenyurt get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Esenyurt in October?
About 18.7 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Esenyurt in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
