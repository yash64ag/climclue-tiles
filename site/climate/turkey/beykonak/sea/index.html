Beykonak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Antalya · Turkey

The sea off Beykonak averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.6 °C (62 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Beykonak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Beykonak?
In August, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Beykonak?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 16.6 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Beykonak in March?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Beykonak?
About 21.6 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Beykonak nearby?
In August: Belek (29.3 °C, 91 km) and Konaklı (28.8 °C, 145 km).
