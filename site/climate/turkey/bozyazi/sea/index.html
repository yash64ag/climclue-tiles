Bozyazı sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mersin · Turkey

The sea off Bozyazı averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.6 °C (60 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Bozyazı sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bozyazı?
In August, at about 26.7 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bozyazı?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 15.6 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bozyazı in March?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bozyazı?
About 20.7 °C (69 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Bozyazı nearby?
In August: Anamur (28.1 °C, 12 km), Gazipaşa (28.1 °C, 60 km) and Taşucu (28.3 °C, 86 km).
