Van at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Van is the 5th coolest (9.7 °C year average). Compared with Ağrı, the nearest covered town with different numbers (139 km away), Van is 2.1 °C warmer in July and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 177 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 373 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Van has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 373 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
