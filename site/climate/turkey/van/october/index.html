Van weather in October

Average October weather in Van, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Van averages 18.6 °C (65 °F) days and 6.7 °C (44 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 225 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Van

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Van. Compared with September: 6.3 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 89 hours less sunshine. November is 6.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Van in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Van in October?
In October, Van averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Van?
October is not one of Van’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Van in October?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (61 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Van in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Van get in October?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Van in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
