How August compares in Van

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Van. Compared with July: about as warm, 3 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. September is 4.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Van in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Van in August?
In August, Van averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 366 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Van?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Van’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Van in August?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (64 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Van in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Van get in August?
About 366 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Van in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
