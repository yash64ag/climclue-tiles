How March compares in Van

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Van. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Van in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Van in March?
In March, Van averages a day high of 6.0 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Van?
March is not one of Van’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Van in March?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (61 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Van in March?
Yes, on average: about 22 cm (8.8 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Van get in March?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Van in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
