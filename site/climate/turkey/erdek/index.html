Erdek at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Erdek is 129th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 170th sunniest (about 2,918 hours of sunshine a year) and 296th wettest (554 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bandırma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Erdek is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 112 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, February (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Erdek, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Erdek has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C. The 10 km area around Erdek, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.0 °C a year, 1.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Erdek itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.400°N, 27.793°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-435-2077), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
