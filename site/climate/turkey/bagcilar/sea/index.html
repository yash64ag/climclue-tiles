Bağcılar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Istanbul · Turkey

The sea off Bağcılar averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.6 °C (46 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Bağcılar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bağcılar?
In August, at about 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bağcılar?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 7.6 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bağcılar in February?
About 7.6 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bağcılar?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
