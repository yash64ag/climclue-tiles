Bağcılar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bağcılar in March?
In March, Bağcılar averages a day high of 11.4 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bağcılar?
March is not one of Bağcılar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

How many hours of sunshine does Bağcılar get in March?
