How December compares in Bağcılar

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Bağcılar. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Bağcılar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bağcılar in December?
In December, Bağcılar averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bağcılar?
December is not one of Bağcılar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bağcılar in December?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (125 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Bağcılar in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bağcılar get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bağcılar in December?
