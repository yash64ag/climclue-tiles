Bağcılar at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Bağcılar is 51st warmest (14.6 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,769 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (796 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zeytinburnu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Bağcılar is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, February (6.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bağcılar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bağcılar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
