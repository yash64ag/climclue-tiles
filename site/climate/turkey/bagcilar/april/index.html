Bağcılar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bağcılar in April?
In April, Bağcılar averages a day high of 15.9 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bağcılar?
April is not one of Bağcılar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
