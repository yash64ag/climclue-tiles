Bozüyük at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Bozüyük is 346th warmest (11.6 °C year average), 367th sunniest (about 2,657 hours of sunshine a year) and 374th wettest (427 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bilecik, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Bozüyük is 1.5 °C cooler in August and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.6 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (1.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 112 km from the sea.

Why Bozüyük has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 112 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bozüyük itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.908°N, 30.037°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-440-2100), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
