Göksun at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Göksun is 370th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 213th sunniest (about 2,867 hours of sunshine a year) and 277th wettest (577 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yeşilova, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Göksun is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 138 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Göksun, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Göksun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 138 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C. The 10 km area around Göksun, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 9.1 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Göksun itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.021°N, 36.497°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-459-2164), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
