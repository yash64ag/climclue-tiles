Viranşehir at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Viranşehir is the 5th sunniest (about 3,140 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Siverek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Viranşehir is 2.4 °C warmer in July and 2.5 °C warmer in January, with 225 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.9 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 324 km from the sea.

Why Viranşehir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 324 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
