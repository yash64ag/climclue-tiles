Why Turhal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Turhal climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Turhal?
August, with an average of 23.1 °C (74 °F), day highs around 29.2 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Turhal?
January, with an average of 1.6 °C (35 °F), day highs around 6.0 °C (43 °F) and night lows around -3.7 °C (25 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Turhal?
May, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 418 mm (16.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Turhal?
August, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Turhal?
July, with about 368 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Turhal?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 128 cm (50.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Turhal?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Turhal have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains Turhal’s GeoNames location (40.388°N, 36.081°E; ClimaClue area sq-436-2160), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 110,884 (GeoNames).
