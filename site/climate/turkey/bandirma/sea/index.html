Bandırma sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Balıkesir · Turkey

The sea off Bandırma averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.9 °C (46 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Bandırma sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bandırma?
In August, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bandırma?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 7.9 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bandırma in February?
About 7.9 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bandırma?
About 15.7 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
