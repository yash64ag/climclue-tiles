Bahçelievler in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bahçelievler in November?
In November, Bahçelievler averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bahçelievler?
November is not one of Bahçelievler’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
