Çorum at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Çorum is 96th warmest (12.0 °C year average), 45th sunniest (about 2,941 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (456 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amasya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (75 km away), Çorum is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 162 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.6 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 140 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Çorum, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Çorum has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 140 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
