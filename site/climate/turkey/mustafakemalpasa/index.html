Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Mustafakemalpaşa is 41st warmest (15.2 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 2,887 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (673 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nilüfer, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Mustafakemalpaşa is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 207 mm less precipitation a year. Why Mustafakemalpaşa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
