Why Muratpaşa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Muratpaşa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Muratpaşa?
July, with an average of 29.8 °C (86 °F), day highs around 34.3 °C (94 °F) and night lows around 24.7 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Muratpaşa?
January, with an average of 10.6 °C (51 °F), day highs around 13.5 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 7.3 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Muratpaşa?
December, with 253 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,096 mm (43.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Muratpaşa?
August, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Muratpaşa?
July, with about 402 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Muratpaşa?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Muratpaşa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May and October.
What type of climate does Muratpaşa have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Muratpaşa’s GeoNames location (36.892°N, 30.765°E; ClimaClue area sq-471-2107), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 450,000 (GeoNames).
