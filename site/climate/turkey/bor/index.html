Bor at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Bor is 318th warmest (12.3 °C year average), 161st sunniest (about 2,934 hours of sunshine a year) and 362nd wettest (444 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Niğde, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Bor is 0.7 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.3 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 124 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bor, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bor has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bor itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.891°N, 34.559°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-461-2145), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
