Espiye sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Giresun · Turkey

The sea off Espiye averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.8 °C (46 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Espiye sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Espiye?
In August, at about 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Espiye?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is March, at 7.8 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Espiye in March?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Espiye?
About 15.9 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
