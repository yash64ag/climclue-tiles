Kahramanmaraş at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Kahramanmaraş is 30th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 3,046 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (827 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gaziantep, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Kahramanmaraş is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 123 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kahramanmaraş, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kahramanmaraş has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
