Taşköprü at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Taşköprü is 306th warmest (12.5 °C year average), 364th sunniest (about 2,672 hours of sunshine a year) and 303rd wettest (547 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kastamonu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Taşköprü is 1.7 °C warmer in August and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.9 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Why Taşköprü has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taşköprü itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.510°N, 34.214°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-424-2142), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
