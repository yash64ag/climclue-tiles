How March compares in Bursa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bursa. Compared with February: 2.9 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 4.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Bursa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bursa in March?
In March, Bursa averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bursa?
March is not one of Bursa’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bursa in March?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (154 mm), the driest August (29 mm).
Does it snow in Bursa in March?
Yes, on average: about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Bursa get in March?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bursa in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
