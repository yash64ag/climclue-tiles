How May compares in Bursa

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bursa. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 33 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Bursa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bursa in May?
In May, Bursa averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bursa?
May is not one of Bursa’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bursa in May?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (197 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Bursa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bursa get in May?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bursa in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
