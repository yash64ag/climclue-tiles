How June compares in Bursa

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bursa. Compared with May: 4.0 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Bursa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bursa in June?
In June, Bursa averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 328 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bursa?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Bursa’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bursa in June?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (197 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Bursa in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bursa get in June?
About 328 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bursa in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
