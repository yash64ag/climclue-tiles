How January compares in Bursa

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bursa. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Bursa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bursa in January?
In January, Bursa averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -2.5 °C (28 °F), with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bursa?
January is not one of Bursa’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bursa in January?
166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (197 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Bursa in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bursa get in January?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bursa in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
