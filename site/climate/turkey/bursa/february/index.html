How February compares in Bursa

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bursa. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Bursa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bursa in February?
In February, Bursa averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bursa?
February is not one of Bursa’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bursa in February?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (197 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Bursa in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 43 cm (17.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bursa get in February?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bursa in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
