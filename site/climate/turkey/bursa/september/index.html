How September compares in Bursa

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bursa. Compared with August: 3.7 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 4.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Bursa in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bursa in September?
In September, Bursa averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bursa?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Bursa’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bursa in September?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (197 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Bursa in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bursa get in September?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bursa in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
