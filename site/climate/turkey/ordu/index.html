Ordu at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Ordu is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,531 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Giresun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Ordu is 0.9 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in February, with 181 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.3 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, February (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ordu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ordu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
