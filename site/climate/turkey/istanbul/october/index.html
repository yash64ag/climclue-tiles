How October compares in Istanbul

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Istanbul. Compared with September: 4.2 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. November is 4.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Istanbul in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Istanbul in October?
In October, Istanbul averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Istanbul?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Istanbul’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Istanbul in October?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (128 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Istanbul in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Istanbul get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Istanbul in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
