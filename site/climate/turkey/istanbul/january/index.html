How January compares in Istanbul

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Istanbul. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C cooler than January on average.

Of the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Istanbul ranks 34th warmest (average temperature), 63rd sunniest and 46th wettest in January.

Istanbul in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Istanbul in January?
In January, Istanbul averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Istanbul?
January is not one of Istanbul’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Istanbul in January?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Istanbul in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Istanbul get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Istanbul in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
