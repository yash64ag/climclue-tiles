How March compares in Istanbul

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Istanbul. Compared with February: 1.7 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. April is 3.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Istanbul in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Istanbul in March?
In March, Istanbul averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Istanbul?
March is not one of Istanbul’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Istanbul in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (128 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Istanbul in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Istanbul get in March?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Istanbul in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
