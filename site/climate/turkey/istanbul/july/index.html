How July compares in Istanbul

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Istanbul. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Istanbul in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Istanbul in July?
In July, Istanbul averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 364 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Istanbul?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Istanbul’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Istanbul in July?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Istanbul in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Istanbul get in July?
About 364 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Istanbul in July?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Istanbul in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
