Istanbul sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Turkey

The sea off Istanbul averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.2 °C (45 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Istanbul sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Istanbul?
In August, at about 23.0 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Istanbul?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 7.2 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Istanbul in February?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Istanbul?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Istanbul nearby?
In August: Bağcılar (23.8 °C, 8 km), Sultangazi (23.8 °C, 12 km) and Küçükçekmece (23.8 °C, 15 km).
