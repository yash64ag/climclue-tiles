How November compares in Istanbul

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Istanbul. Compared with October: 4.8 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Istanbul in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Istanbul in November?
In November, Istanbul averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Istanbul?
November is not one of Istanbul’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Istanbul in November?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Istanbul in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Istanbul get in November?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Istanbul in November?
About 13.9 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Istanbul in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
