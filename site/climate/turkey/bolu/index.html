Bolu at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Bolu is 104th warmest (10.7 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 2,776 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (719 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Düzce, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Bolu is 2.2 °C cooler in August and 3.4 °C cooler in January, with 147 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.4 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 61 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bolu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bolu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 61 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
