Why Merkezefendi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Merkezefendi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Merkezefendi?
July, with an average of 29.9 °C (86 °F), day highs around 35.7 °C (96 °F) and night lows around 21.3 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Merkezefendi?
January, with an average of 7.5 °C (45 °F), day highs around 11.3 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 1.8 °C (35 °F).

What is the driest month in Merkezefendi?

What is the sunniest month in Merkezefendi?
July, with about 409 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Merkezefendi?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Merkezefendi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May and October.
