Merkezefendi at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Merkezefendi is 9th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 3,058 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (559 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Denizli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Merkezefendi is 3.3 °C warmer in July and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 148 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 130 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Merkezefendi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Merkezefendi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
