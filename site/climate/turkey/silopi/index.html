Silopi at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Silopi is 21st warmest (17.6 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 3,114 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (775 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cizre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Silopi is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 47 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.0 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 461 km from the sea.

Why Silopi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 461 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
