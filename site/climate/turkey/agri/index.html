Why Ağrı has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 237 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ağrı climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ağrı?
July, with an average of 20.4 °C (69 °F), day highs around 26.9 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 12.7 °C (55 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ağrı?
January, with an average of -5.5 °C (22 °F), day highs around 0.3 °C (33 °F) and night lows around -11.8 °C (11 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ağrı?
April, with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 631 mm (24.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ağrı?
August, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ağrı?
July, with about 354 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ağrı?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 233 cm (91.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ağrı?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.6 × 11 km, that contains Ağrı’s GeoNames location (39.715°N, 43.040°E; ClimaClue area sq-442-2230), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 124,483 (GeoNames).
