How July compares in Üsküdar

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Üsküdar. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Üsküdar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in July?
In July, Üsküdar averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 359 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Üsküdar?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Üsküdar’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in July?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (124 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in July?
About 359 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
