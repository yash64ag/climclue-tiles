How October compares in Üsküdar

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Üsküdar. Compared with September: 4.4 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. November is 4.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Üsküdar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in October?
In October, Üsküdar averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Üsküdar?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Üsküdar’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in October?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in October?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Üsküdar in October?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (23.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
