How March compares in Üsküdar

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Üsküdar. Compared with February: 1.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Üsküdar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in March?
In March, Üsküdar averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Üsküdar?
March is not one of Üsküdar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in March?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (124 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in March?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
