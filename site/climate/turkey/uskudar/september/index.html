How September compares in Üsküdar

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Üsküdar. Compared with August: 3.4 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. October is 4.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Üsküdar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in September?
In September, Üsküdar averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Üsküdar?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Üsküdar’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in September?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in September?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Üsküdar in September?
About 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
