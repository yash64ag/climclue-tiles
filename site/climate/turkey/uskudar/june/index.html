How June compares in Üsküdar

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Üsküdar. Compared with May: 4.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Üsküdar in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in June?
In June, Üsküdar averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 333 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Üsküdar?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Üsküdar’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in June?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (124 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in June?
About 333 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
