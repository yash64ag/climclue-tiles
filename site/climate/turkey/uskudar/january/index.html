Üsküdar weather in January

Average January weather in Üsküdar, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Üsküdar averages 9.6 °C (49 °F) days and 3.8 °C (39 °F) nights, with 95 mm (3.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 142 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Üsküdar

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Üsküdar. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Üsküdar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in January?
In January, Üsküdar averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Üsküdar?
January is not one of Üsküdar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in January?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (28 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.0 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Üsküdar in January?
About 8.3 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
