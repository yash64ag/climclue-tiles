How August compares in Üsküdar

August is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Üsküdar. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. September is 3.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Üsküdar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Üsküdar in August?
In August, Üsküdar averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 316 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Üsküdar?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Üsküdar’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Üsküdar in August?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (124 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Üsküdar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Üsküdar get in August?
About 316 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Üsküdar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
