Nilüfer at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Nilüfer is 37th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,853 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (880 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bursa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Nilüfer is 4.0 °C warmer in August and 4.2 °C warmer in January, with 526 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 18.7 °C more than its coolest, January (6.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nilüfer, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nilüfer has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
