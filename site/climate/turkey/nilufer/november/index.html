How November compares in Nilüfer

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nilüfer. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 3.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Nilüfer in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nilüfer in November?
In November, Nilüfer averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nilüfer?
November is not one of Nilüfer’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nilüfer in November?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (127 mm), the driest August (24 mm).
Does it snow in Nilüfer in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nilüfer get in November?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nilüfer in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
