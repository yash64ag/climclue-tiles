How December compares in Nilüfer

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Nilüfer. Compared with November: 3.5 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Nilüfer in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nilüfer in December?
In December, Nilüfer averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nilüfer?
December is not one of Nilüfer’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nilüfer in December?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (127 mm), the driest August (24 mm).
Does it snow in Nilüfer in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.0 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nilüfer get in December?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nilüfer in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
