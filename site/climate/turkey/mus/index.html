Muş at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Muş is 381st warmest (10.0 °C year average), 282nd sunniest (about 2,792 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (1,248 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Solhan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Muş is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 406 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 275 km from the sea.

Why Muş has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 275 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Muş itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.732°N, 41.485°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-452-2214), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
