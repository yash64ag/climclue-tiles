Suruç at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Suruç is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,217 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (371 mm a year). Compared with Şanlıurfa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Suruç is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 168 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.5 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 206 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Suruç, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Suruç has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 206 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
