Nusaybin at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Nusaybin is 38th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 146th sunniest (about 2,959 hours of sunshine a year) and 350th wettest (458 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Midyat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Nusaybin is 2.6 °C warmer in July and 4.6 °C warmer in January, with 234 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.2 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 453 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Nusaybin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 453 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nusaybin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.070°N, 41.215°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-469-2212), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
