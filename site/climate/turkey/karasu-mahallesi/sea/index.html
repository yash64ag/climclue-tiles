Karasu Mahallesi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sakarya · Turkey

The sea off Karasu Mahallesi averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.0 °C (45 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Karasu Mahallesi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Karasu Mahallesi?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Karasu Mahallesi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 7.0 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Karasu Mahallesi in February?
About 7.0 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Karasu Mahallesi?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
