Derince at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Derince is 66th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 2,694 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (730 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with İzmit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Derince is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.8 °C), averages 18.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Derince, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Derince has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
