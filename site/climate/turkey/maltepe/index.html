Maltepe at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Maltepe is 69th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 2,799 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (819 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ümraniye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Maltepe is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in February, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 17.6 °C more than its coolest, February (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maltepe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maltepe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
