How January compares in Gaziantep

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Gaziantep. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Gaziantep in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in January?
In January, Gaziantep averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.9 °C (30 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Gaziantep?
January is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in January?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in January?
Yes, on average: about 32 cm (12.5 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
