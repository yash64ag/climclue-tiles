How November compares in Gaziantep

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gaziantep. Compared with October: 7.5 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 66 hours less sunshine. December is 4.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Gaziantep in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in November?
In November, Gaziantep averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Gaziantep?
November is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in November?
93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in November?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
