Gaziantep weather in April

Average April weather in Gaziantep, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Gaziantep averages 19.6 °C (67 °F) days and 7.2 °C (45 °F) nights, with 63 mm (2.5 in) of rain over the month and about 240 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Gaziantep

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Gaziantep. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Gaziantep in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in April?
In April, Gaziantep averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gaziantep?
April is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in April?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in April?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
