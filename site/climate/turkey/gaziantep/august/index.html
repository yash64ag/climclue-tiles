Gaziantep weather in August

Average August weather in Gaziantep, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Gaziantep averages 35.1 °C (95 °F) days and 20.0 °C (68 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 398 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Gaziantep

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Gaziantep. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Gaziantep in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in August?
In August, Gaziantep averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 398 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Gaziantep?
August is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in August?
About 398 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
