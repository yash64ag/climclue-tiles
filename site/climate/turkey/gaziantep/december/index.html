How December compares in Gaziantep

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Gaziantep. Compared with November: 4.7 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Gaziantep in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in December?
In December, Gaziantep averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Gaziantep?
December is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in December?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in December?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
