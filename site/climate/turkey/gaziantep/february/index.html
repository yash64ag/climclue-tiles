How February compares in Gaziantep

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Gaziantep. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 4.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Gaziantep in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in February?
In February, Gaziantep averages a day high of 8.9 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -0.1 °C (32 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Gaziantep?
February is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in February?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in February?
Yes, on average: about 33 cm (13.0 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in February?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
