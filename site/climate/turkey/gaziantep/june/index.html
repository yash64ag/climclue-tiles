How June compares in Gaziantep

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Gaziantep. Compared with May: 5.5 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 77 hours more sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Gaziantep in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in June?
In June, Gaziantep averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 387 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Gaziantep?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Gaziantep’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in June?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in June?
About 387 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
