Gaziantep weather in July

Average July weather in Gaziantep, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Gaziantep averages 35.3 °C (96 °F) days and 20.3 °C (69 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 429 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Gaziantep

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Gaziantep. Compared with June: 3.7 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Gaziantep in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gaziantep in July?
In July, Gaziantep averages a day high of 35.3 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 429 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Gaziantep?
July is not one of Gaziantep’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gaziantep in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (115 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Gaziantep in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gaziantep get in July?
About 429 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gaziantep in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
