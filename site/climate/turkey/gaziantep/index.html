Gaziantep at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Gaziantep is 42nd warmest (15.3 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,122 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (689 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kilis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Gaziantep is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 91 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 112 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gaziantep, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gaziantep has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 112 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gaziantep itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.059°N, 37.382°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-469-2173), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
