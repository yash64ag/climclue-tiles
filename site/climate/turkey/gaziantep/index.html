Gaziantep at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Gaziantep is 44th warmest (15.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,122 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (705 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kilis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Gaziantep is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 112 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gaziantep, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gaziantep has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 112 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
