Why İnegol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 81 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

İnegol climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in İnegol?
August, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 17.1 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in İnegol?
January, with an average of 4.0 °C (39 °F), day highs around 7.8 °C (46 °F) and night lows around -0.9 °C (30 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in İnegol?

What is the driest month in İnegol?
August, with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in İnegol?
July, with about 366 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in İnegol?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 52 cm (20.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in İnegol?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains İnegol’s GeoNames location (40.078°N, 29.513°E; ClimaClue area sq-439-2095), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 133,959 (GeoNames).
