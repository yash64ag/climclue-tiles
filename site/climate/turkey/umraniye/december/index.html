How December compares in Ümraniye

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Ümraniye. Compared with November: 3.4 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Ümraniye in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ümraniye in December?
In December, Ümraniye averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ümraniye?
December is not one of Ümraniye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ümraniye in December?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest July (31 mm).
Does it snow in Ümraniye in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.1 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ümraniye get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ümraniye in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
