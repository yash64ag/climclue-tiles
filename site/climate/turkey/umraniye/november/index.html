How November compares in Ümraniye

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ümraniye. Compared with October: 4.7 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. December is 3.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Ümraniye in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ümraniye in November?
In November, Ümraniye averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ümraniye?
November is not one of Ümraniye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ümraniye in November?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest July (31 mm).
Does it snow in Ümraniye in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ümraniye get in November?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ümraniye in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
