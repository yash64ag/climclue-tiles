How March compares in Ümraniye

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ümraniye. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Ümraniye in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ümraniye in March?
In March, Ümraniye averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ümraniye?
March is not one of Ümraniye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ümraniye in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (125 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Ümraniye in March?
Yes, on average: about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Ümraniye get in March?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Ümraniye in March?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Ümraniye in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
