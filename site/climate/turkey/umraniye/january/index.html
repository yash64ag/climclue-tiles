How January compares in Ümraniye

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ümraniye. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C cooler than January on average.

Ümraniye in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ümraniye in January?
In January, Ümraniye averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ümraniye?
January is not one of Ümraniye’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ümraniye in January?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest July (31 mm).
Does it snow in Ümraniye in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ümraniye get in January?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ümraniye in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
