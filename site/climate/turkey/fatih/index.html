Fatih at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Fatih is 56th warmest (14.7 °C year average), 82nd sunniest (about 2,763 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (803 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Istanbul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Fatih is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.0 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, February (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fatih, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fatih has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fatih itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.022°N, 28.941°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-429-2089), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
