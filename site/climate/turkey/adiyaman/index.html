Adıyaman at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Adıyaman is 32nd warmest (16.5 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,132 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (611 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Malatya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Adıyaman is 3.2 °C warmer in July and 2.5 °C warmer in January, with 163 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.9 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 217 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Adıyaman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Adıyaman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-462-2182), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
