Aydın at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Aydın is 15th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,134 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nazilli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Aydın is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 61 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aydın, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aydın has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 61 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
