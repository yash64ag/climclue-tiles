Zeytinburnu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Istanbul · Turkey

The sea off Zeytinburnu averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.2 °C (45 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Zeytinburnu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Zeytinburnu?
In August, at about 23.0 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Zeytinburnu?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 7.2 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Zeytinburnu in February?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Zeytinburnu?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Zeytinburnu nearby?
In August: Bağcılar (23.8 °C, 6 km), Küçükçekmece (23.8 °C, 11 km) and Sultangazi (23.8 °C, 13 km).
