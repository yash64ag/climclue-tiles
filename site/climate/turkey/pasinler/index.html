Pasinler at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Pasinler is 410th warmest (7.1 °C year average), 344th sunniest (about 2,709 hours of sunshine a year) and 395th wettest (389 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Erzurum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Pasinler is 1.5 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 127 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.2 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Why Pasinler has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pasinler itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.980°N, 41.670°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-440-2216), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
