Why Ataşehir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Ataşehir climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ataşehir?
August, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 25.8 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 21.0 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ataşehir?
February, with an average of 5.8 °C (43 °F), day highs around 7.5 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 3.8 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ataşehir?
December, with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 819 mm (32.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ataşehir?
July, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ataşehir?
July, with about 368 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ataşehir?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ataşehir?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Ataşehir’s GeoNames location (40.983°N, 29.117°E; ClimaClue area sq-430-2091), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 361,615 (GeoNames).
