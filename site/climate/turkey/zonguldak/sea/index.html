Zonguldak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zonguldak Province · Turkey

The sea off Zonguldak averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.6 °C (44 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Zonguldak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Zonguldak?
In August, at about 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Zonguldak?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 6.6 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Zonguldak in February?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Zonguldak?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Zonguldak nearby?
In August: Bartın (24.7 °C, 50 km), Kocaali (24.7 °C, 90 km) and Karasu Mahallesi (24.7 °C, 94 km).
