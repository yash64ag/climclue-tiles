Çat weather by month and climate

Çat at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Çat is the 5th coolest (6.3 °C year average). Compared with Erzurum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Çat is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 262 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 167 km from the sea.

Why Çat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 167 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C. The 10 km area around Çat, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 5.2 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Çat itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.614°N, 40.978°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-443-2209), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
