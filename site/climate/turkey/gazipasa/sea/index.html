Gazipaşa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Antalya · Turkey

The sea off Gazipaşa averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.5 °C (62 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Gazipaşa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gazipaşa?
In August, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gazipaşa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is March, at 16.5 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gazipaşa in March?
About 16.5 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gazipaşa?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Gazipaşa nearby?
In August: Konaklı (28.8 °C, 52 km), Manavgat (28.7 °C, 97 km) and Belek (29.3 °C, 130 km).
