Kaynaşlı at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Kaynaşlı is 303rd warmest (12.6 °C year average), 327th sunniest (about 2,736 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (873 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Düzce, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Kaynaşlı is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.9 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kaynaşlı, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kaynaşlı has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C. The 10 km area around Kaynaşlı, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.9 °C a year, 1.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kaynaşlı itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.769°N, 31.322°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-432-2113), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
