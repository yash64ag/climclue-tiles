Tekkeköy at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Tekkeköy is 245th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 393rd sunniest (about 2,554 hours of sunshine a year) and 101st wettest (864 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Samsun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Tekkeköy is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 17.4 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Tekkeköy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-427-2164), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
