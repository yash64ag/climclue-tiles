How June compares in İzmir

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in İzmir. Compared with May: 5.1 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. July is 2.8 °C warmer than June on average.

İzmir in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in İzmir in June?
In June, İzmir averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 374 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit İzmir?
June is not one of İzmir’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in İzmir in June?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (143 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in İzmir in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does İzmir get in June?
About 374 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for İzmir in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
