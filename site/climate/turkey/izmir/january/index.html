How January compares in İzmir

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in İzmir. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

İzmir in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in İzmir in January?
In January, İzmir averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit İzmir?
January is not one of İzmir’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in İzmir in January?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (143 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in İzmir in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does İzmir get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for İzmir in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
