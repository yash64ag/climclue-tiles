How April compares in İzmir

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in İzmir. Compared with March: 4.3 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

İzmir in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in İzmir in April?
In April, İzmir averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit İzmir?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of İzmir’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in İzmir in April?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (143 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in İzmir in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does İzmir get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for İzmir in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
