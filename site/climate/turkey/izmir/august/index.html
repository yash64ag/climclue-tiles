How August compares in İzmir

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in İzmir. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

İzmir in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in İzmir in August?
In August, İzmir averages a day high of 34.1 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 390 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit İzmir?
August is not one of İzmir’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in İzmir in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (143 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in İzmir in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does İzmir get in August?
About 390 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for İzmir in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
