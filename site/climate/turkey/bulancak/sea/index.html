Bulancak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Giresun · Turkey

The sea off Bulancak averages 24.9 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.6 °C (46 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Bulancak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bulancak?
In August, at about 24.9 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bulancak?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 7.6 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bulancak in March?
About 7.6 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bulancak?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
