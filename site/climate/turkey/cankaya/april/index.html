How April compares in Çankaya

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Çankaya. Compared with March: 5.3 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 4.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Çankaya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Çankaya in April?
In April, Çankaya averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Çankaya?
April is not one of Çankaya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Çankaya in April?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest August (12 mm).
