Çankaya at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Çankaya is 88th warmest (12.7 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,811 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (439 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gölbaşı, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Çankaya is 1.2 °C warmer in August and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 23.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 192 km from the sea.

Why Çankaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
