Why Çankaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Çankaya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Çankaya?
August, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 30.2 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 16.2 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Çankaya?
January, with an average of 1.1 °C (34 °F), day highs around 5.1 °C (41 °F) and night lows around -3.6 °C (26 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Çankaya?
April, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 439 mm (17.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Çankaya?
August, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Çankaya?
July, with about 374 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Çankaya?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 91 cm (35.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Çankaya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Çankaya have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains Çankaya’s GeoNames location (39.918°N, 32.863°E; ClimaClue area sq-440-2128), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 792,189 (GeoNames).
