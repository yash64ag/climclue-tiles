How September compares in Çankaya

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Çankaya. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Çankaya in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Çankaya in September?
In September, Çankaya averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Çankaya?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Çankaya’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Çankaya in September?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (54 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in Çankaya in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Çankaya get in September?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Çankaya in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
