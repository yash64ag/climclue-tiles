How December compares in Çankaya

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Çankaya. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Çankaya in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Çankaya in December?
In December, Çankaya averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -1.4 °C (29 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Çankaya?
December is not one of Çankaya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Çankaya in December?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (54 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in Çankaya in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Çankaya get in December?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Çankaya in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
