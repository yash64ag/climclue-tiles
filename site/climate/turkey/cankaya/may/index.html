How May compares in Çankaya

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Çankaya. Compared with April: 4.7 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. June is 4.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Çankaya in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Çankaya in May?
In May, Çankaya averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Çankaya?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Çankaya’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Çankaya in May?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest August (12 mm).
Does it snow in Çankaya in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Çankaya get in May?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Çankaya in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
