Trabzon sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Turkey

The sea off Trabzon averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.8 °C (46 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Trabzon sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Trabzon?
In August, at about 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Trabzon?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is March, at 7.8 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Trabzon in March?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Trabzon?
About 16.0 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Trabzon nearby?
In August: Arsin (25.7 °C, 18 km), Araklı (25.7 °C, 29 km) and Rize (25.8 °C, 67 km).
