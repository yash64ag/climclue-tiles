Ortaca sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Muğla · Turkey

The sea off Ortaca averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.2 °C (61 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Ortaca sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ortaca?
In August, at about 26.6 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ortaca?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 16.2 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ortaca in March?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ortaca?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ortaca nearby?
In August: Fethiye (27.2 °C, 39 km) and Beykonak (28.2 °C, 149 km).
