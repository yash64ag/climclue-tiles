Esenler in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenler in March?
In March, Esenler averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Esenler?
March is not one of Esenler’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenler in March?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (125 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Esenler in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenler get in March?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Esenler in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
