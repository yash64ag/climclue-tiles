Esenler in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenler in April?
In April, Esenler averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Esenler?
April is not one of Esenler’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenler in April?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Esenler in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenler get in April?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Esenler in April?
About 11.0 °C (52 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Esenler in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
