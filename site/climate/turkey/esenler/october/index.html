Esenler in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenler in October?
In October, Esenler averages a day high of 18.3 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Esenler?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Esenler’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenler in October?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (125 mm), the driest July (29 mm).
Does it snow in Esenler in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenler get in October?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Esenler in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
