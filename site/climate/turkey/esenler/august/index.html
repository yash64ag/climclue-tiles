How August compares in Esenler

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Esenler. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. September is 3.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Esenler in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenler in August?
In August, Esenler averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 322 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Esenler?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Esenler’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenler in August?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Esenler in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenler get in August?
About 322 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Esenler in August?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Esenler in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
