How November compares in Esenler

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Esenler. Compared with October: 4.7 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Esenler in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Esenler in November?
In November, Esenler averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Esenler?
November is not one of Esenler’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Esenler in November?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Esenler in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Esenler get in November?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Esenler in November?
About 14.4 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Esenler in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
