Yüksekova at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Yüksekova is 407th warmest (7.8 °C year average), 141st sunniest (about 2,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (871 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Yüksekova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 418 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-464-2242), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
