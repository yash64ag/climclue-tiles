How June compares in Mersin

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Mersin in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in June?
In June, Mersin averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 349 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Mersin?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Mersin’s mild months (April, May, June, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in June?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in June?
About 349 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
