How December compares in Mersin

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Mersin. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Mersin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in December?
In December, Mersin averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mersin?
December is not one of Mersin’s mild months; those are April, May, June, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in December?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (135 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in December?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mersin in December?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mersin in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
