How October compares in Mersin

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with September: 3.7 °C cooler, 29 mm more precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. November is 5.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Mersin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in October?
In October, Mersin averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mersin?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mersin’s mild months (April, May, June, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in October?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in October?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
