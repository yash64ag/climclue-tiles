How April compares in Mersin

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with March: 3.7 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. May is 3.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Mersin in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in April?
In April, Mersin averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mersin?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mersin’s mild months (April, May, June, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
