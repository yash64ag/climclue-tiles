How January compares in Mersin

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Mersin in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in January?
In January, Mersin averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mersin?
January is not one of Mersin’s mild months; those are April, May, June, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in January?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in January?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
