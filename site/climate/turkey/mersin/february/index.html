How February compares in Mersin

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 26 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 2.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Mersin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in February?
In February, Mersin averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mersin?
February is not one of Mersin’s mild months; those are April, May, June, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in February?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in February?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
