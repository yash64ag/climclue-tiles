Mersin weather in November

Average November weather in Mersin, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Mersin averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) days and 11.2 °C (52 °F) nights, with 80 mm (3.2 in) of rain over the month and about 182 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Mersin

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 43 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Mersin in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in November?
In November, Mersin averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mersin?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Mersin’s mild months (April, May, June, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in November?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (135 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in November?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mersin in November?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mersin in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
