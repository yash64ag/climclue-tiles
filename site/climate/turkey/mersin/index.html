Mersin at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Mersin is 6th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 3,059 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (623 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tarsus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Mersin is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 17.4 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.9 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mersin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mersin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
