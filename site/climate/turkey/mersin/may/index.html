How May compares in Mersin

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mersin. Compared with April: 3.8 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Mersin in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in May?
In May, Mersin averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mersin?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Mersin’s mild months (April, May, June, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in May?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in May?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
