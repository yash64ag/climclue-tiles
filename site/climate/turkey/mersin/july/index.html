How July compares in Mersin

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mersin. Compared with June: 2.6 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C warmer than July on average.

Mersin in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mersin in July?
In July, Mersin averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 379 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mersin?
July is not one of Mersin’s mild months; those are April, May, June, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mersin in July?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (137 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mersin in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mersin get in July?
About 379 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mersin in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
