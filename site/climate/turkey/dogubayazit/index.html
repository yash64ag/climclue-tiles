Doğubayazıt weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Doğubayazıt, Turkey (Ağrı), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Doğubayazıt is warmest in July, with 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 14.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -0.4 °C (31 °F) days and -9.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around May (51 mm); September is driest (11 mm).

Why Doğubayazıt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Doğubayazıt itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.547°N, 44.084°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-444-2240), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
