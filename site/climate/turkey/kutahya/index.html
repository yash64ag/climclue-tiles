Kütahya at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Kütahya is 103rd warmest (10.9 °C year average), 96th sunniest (about 2,725 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (480 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Eskişehir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Kütahya is 1.1 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 141 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.1 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 151 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kütahya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kütahya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 151 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
