Kütahya at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Kütahya is 359th warmest (11.3 °C year average), 334th sunniest (about 2,725 hours of sunshine a year) and 336th wettest (483 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tavşanlı, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Kütahya is 0.8 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.5 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 151 km from the sea.

Why Kütahya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 151 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kütahya itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.424°N, 29.983°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-445-2099), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
