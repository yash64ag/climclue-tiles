Batman at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Batman is 32nd warmest (16.0 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (614 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Siirt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Batman is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 335 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.4 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 364 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Batman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Batman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 364 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
