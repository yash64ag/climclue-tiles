Kızıltepe at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Kızıltepe is 19th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 3,024 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (459 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mardin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Kızıltepe is 3.1 °C warmer in July and 2.8 °C warmer in January, with 140 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.4 °C), averages 27.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 393 km from the sea.

Why Kızıltepe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 393 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
