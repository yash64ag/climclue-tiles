Genç at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Genç is 302nd warmest (12.6 °C year average), 218th sunniest (about 2,864 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (889 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bingöl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Genç is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 29.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Why Genç has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Genç itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.748°N, 40.553°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-452-2205), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
