Zekeriya sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Istanbul · Turkey

The sea off Zekeriya averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.2 °C (45 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Zekeriya sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Zekeriya?
In August, at about 23.0 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Zekeriya?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 7.2 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Zekeriya in February?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Zekeriya?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Zekeriya nearby?
In August: Sultangazi (23.8 °C, 17 km), Bağcılar (23.8 °C, 23 km) and Arnavutköy (23.8 °C, 24 km).
