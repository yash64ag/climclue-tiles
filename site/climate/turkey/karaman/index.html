Karaman at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Karaman is 285th warmest (13.1 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 2,992 hours of sunshine a year) and 412th wettest (319 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Çumra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Karaman is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 118 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Karaman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Karaman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Karaman itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.181°N, 33.215°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-468-2132), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
