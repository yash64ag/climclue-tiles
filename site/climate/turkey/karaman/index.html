Karaman at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Karaman is the 4th driest (350 mm a year). Compared with Ereğli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Karaman is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 118 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Karaman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Karaman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
