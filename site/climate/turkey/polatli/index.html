Polatlı at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Polatlı is 293rd warmest (12.9 °C year average), 263rd sunniest (about 2,816 hours of sunshine a year) and 355th wettest (451 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gölbaşı, Ankara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Polatlı is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.0 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 197 km from the sea.

Why Polatlı has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 197 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Polatlı itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.577°N, 32.141°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-444-2121), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
