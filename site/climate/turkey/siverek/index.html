Siverek at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Siverek is 114th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 3,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 188th wettest (691 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hilvan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Siverek is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.5 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 304 km from the sea.

Why Siverek has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 304 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Siverek itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.755°N, 39.317°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-462-2193), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
