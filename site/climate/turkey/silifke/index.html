Silifke at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Silifke is the warmest (19.6 °C year average). Compared with Mersin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Silifke is 1.1 °C warmer in August and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 92 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 17.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Silifke, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Silifke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
