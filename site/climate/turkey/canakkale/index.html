Why Çanakkale has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Çanakkale climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Çanakkale?
July, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 29.2 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Çanakkale?
January, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 9.0 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 3.3 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Çanakkale?
December, with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 690 mm (27.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Çanakkale?
August, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Çanakkale?
July, with about 416 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Çanakkale?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Çanakkale?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Çanakkale have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains Çanakkale’s GeoNames location (40.156°N, 26.413°E; ClimaClue area sq-438-2064), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 143,622 (GeoNames).
