Iğdır at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Iğdır is the driest (320 mm a year). Compared with Ağrı, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Iğdır is 5.5 °C warmer in July and 3.9 °C warmer in January, with 311 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 282 km from the sea.

Why Iğdır has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 282 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
