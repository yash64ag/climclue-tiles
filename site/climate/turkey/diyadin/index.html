Diyadin at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Diyadin is 412th warmest (6.4 °C year average), 316th sunniest (about 2,757 hours of sunshine a year) and 315th wettest (528 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Doğubayazıt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Diyadin is 3.5 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 235 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.5 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 287 km from the sea.

Why Diyadin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 287 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Diyadin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.541°N, 43.671°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-444-2236), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
