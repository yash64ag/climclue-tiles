Edirne at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Edirne is 63rd warmest (14.5 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 2,659 hours of sunshine a year) and 80th wettest (584 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tekirdağ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (111 km away), Edirne is 1.7 °C warmer in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 112 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Edirne, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Edirne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 112 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
