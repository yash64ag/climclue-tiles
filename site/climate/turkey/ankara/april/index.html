How April compares in Ankara

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ankara. Compared with March: 5.3 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Ankara in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankara in April?
In April, Ankara averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ankara?
April is not one of Ankara’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankara in April?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (54 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in Ankara in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankara get in April?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankara in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
