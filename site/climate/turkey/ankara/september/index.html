Ankara weather in September

Average September weather in Ankara, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Ankara averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 12.5 °C (55 °F) nights, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of rain over the month and about 294 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Ankara

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ankara. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. October is 5.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Ankara in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankara in September?
In September, Ankara averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ankara?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Ankara’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankara in September?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (51 mm), the driest August (12 mm).
Does it snow in Ankara in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankara get in September?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankara in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
