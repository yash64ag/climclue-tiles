How November compares in Ankara

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ankara. Compared with October: 6.7 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 66 hours less sunshine. December is 4.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Ankara in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankara in November?
In November, Ankara averages a day high of 11.8 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ankara?
November is not one of Ankara’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankara in November?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (54 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in Ankara in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.8 cm (2.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankara get in November?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankara in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
