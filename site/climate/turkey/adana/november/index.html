How November compares in Adana

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Adana. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Adana in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adana in November?
In November, Adana averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Adana?
November is not one of Adana’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adana in November?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (133 mm), the driest August (8 mm).
Does it snow in Adana in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adana get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adana in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
