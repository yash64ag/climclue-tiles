Adana at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Adana is the 4th warmest (18.9 °C year average). Compared with Tarsus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Adana is 1.3 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 59 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.9 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Adana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Adana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
