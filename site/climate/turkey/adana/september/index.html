How September compares in Adana

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Adana. Compared with August: 2.7 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Adana in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adana in September?
In September, Adana averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 302 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Adana?
September is not one of Adana’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adana in September?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (133 mm), the driest August (8 mm).
Does it snow in Adana in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adana get in September?
About 302 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adana in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
