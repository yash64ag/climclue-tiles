How May compares in Adana

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Adana. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Adana in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adana in May?
In May, Adana averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Adana?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Adana’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adana in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (133 mm), the driest August (8 mm).
Does it snow in Adana in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adana get in May?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adana in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
