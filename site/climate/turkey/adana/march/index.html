Adana weather in March

Average March weather in Adana, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Adana averages 18.7 °C (66 °F) days and 7.9 °C (46 °F) nights, with 65 mm (2.6 in) of rain over the month and about 214 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Adana

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Adana. Compared with February: 3.5 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Adana in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adana in March?
In March, Adana averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Adana?
March is not one of Adana’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adana in March?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (129 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Adana in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adana get in March?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adana in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
