How December compares in Adana

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Adana. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, 47 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Adana in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Adana in December?
In December, Adana averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Adana?
December is not one of Adana’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Adana in December?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (133 mm), the driest August (8 mm).
Does it snow in Adana in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Adana get in December?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Adana in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
