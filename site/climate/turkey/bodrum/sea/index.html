Bodrum sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Muğla · Turkey

The sea off Bodrum averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.6 °C (60 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Bodrum sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bodrum?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bodrum?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 15.6 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bodrum in March?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bodrum?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Bodrum nearby?
In August: Marmaris (26.3 °C, 78 km), Rhodes (26.5 °C, 97 km) and Sarigerme (26.6 °C, 119 km).
