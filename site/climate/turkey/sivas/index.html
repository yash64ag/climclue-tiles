Sivas at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Sivas is the 4th coolest (9.6 °C year average). Compared with Tokat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Sivas is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.9 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 159 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sivas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sivas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 159 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
