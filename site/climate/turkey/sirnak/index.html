Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Şırnak is 296th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 3,086 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (1,026 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Şırnak has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.9 °C. The 10 km area around Şırnak, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 11.6 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-464-2224), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
