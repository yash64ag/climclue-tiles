Why Nazilli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Nazilli climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Nazilli?
July, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 35.1 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 20.6 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Nazilli?
January, with an average of 7.7 °C (46 °F), day highs around 11.1 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 2.2 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Nazilli?
December, with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 625 mm (24.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Nazilli?
August, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Nazilli?
July, with about 418 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Nazilli?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Nazilli?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Nazilli have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Nazilli’s GeoNames location (37.916°N, 28.322°E; ClimaClue area sq-460-2083), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 119,370 (GeoNames).
