Nazilli at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Nazilli is 7th warmest (19.1 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 3,091 hours of sunshine a year) and 80th wettest (559 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aydın, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Nazilli is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.4 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nazilli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nazilli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C. The 10 km area around Nazilli, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.0 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nazilli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.916°N, 28.322°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-460-2083), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
