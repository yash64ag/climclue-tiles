Nazilli at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Nazilli is 17th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 3,091 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (625 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aydın, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Nazilli is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (7.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nazilli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nazilli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
