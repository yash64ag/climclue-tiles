Turgutlu at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Turgutlu is 35th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 3,061 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (671 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manisa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Turgutlu is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Turgutlu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Turgutlu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
