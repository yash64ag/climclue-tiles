Şarkışla weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Şarkışla, Turkey (Sivas), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Şarkışla at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Şarkışla is 371st warmest (10.4 °C year average), 281st sunniest (about 2,793 hours of sunshine a year) and 405th wettest (362 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gemerek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Şarkışla is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 123 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.9 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 217 km from the sea.

Why Şarkışla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Şarkışla itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.352°N, 36.410°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-446-2164), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
