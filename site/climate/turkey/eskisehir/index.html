Eskişehir at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Eskişehir is the 2nd driest (339 mm a year). Compared with Kütahya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Eskişehir is 1.1 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 141 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.1 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 157 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Eskişehir, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Eskişehir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 157 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
