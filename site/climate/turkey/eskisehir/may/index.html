How May compares in Eskişehir

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Eskişehir. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Eskişehir in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Eskişehir in May?
In May, Eskişehir averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Eskişehir?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Eskişehir’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Eskişehir in May?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (42 mm), the driest August (9 mm).
Does it snow in Eskişehir in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Eskişehir get in May?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Eskişehir in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
