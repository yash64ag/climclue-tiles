How August compares in Eskişehir

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Eskişehir. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 4.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Eskişehir in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Eskişehir in August?
In August, Eskişehir averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 345 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Eskişehir?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Eskişehir’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Eskişehir in August?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest August (9 mm).
Does it snow in Eskişehir in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Eskişehir get in August?
About 345 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Eskişehir in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
