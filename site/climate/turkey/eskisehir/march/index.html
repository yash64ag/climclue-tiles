How March compares in Eskişehir

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Eskişehir. Compared with February: 3.6 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. April is 4.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Eskişehir in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Eskişehir in March?
In March, Eskişehir averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Eskişehir?
March is not one of Eskişehir’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Eskişehir in March?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest August (9 mm).
Does it snow in Eskişehir in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Eskişehir get in March?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Eskişehir in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
