How October compares in Diyarbakır

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Diyarbakır. Compared with September: 7.5 °C cooler, 35 mm more precipitation and 86 hours less sunshine. November is 8.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Diyarbakır in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in October?
In October, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Diyarbakır’s mild months (May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Diyarbakır in October?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (85 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Diyarbakır get in October?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Diyarbakır in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
