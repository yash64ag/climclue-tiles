How September compares in Diyarbakır

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Diyarbakır. Compared with August: 5.4 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. October is 7.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Diyarbakır in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in September?
In September, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 318 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
September is not one of Diyarbakır’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Diyarbakır in September?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (85 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Diyarbakır get in September?
About 318 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Diyarbakır in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
