How February compares in Diyarbakır

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Diyarbakır. Compared with January: 1.7 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. March is 4.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Diyarbakır in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in February?
In February, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
February is not one of Diyarbakır’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Diyarbakır in February?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (83 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in February?
Yes, on average: about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Diyarbakır get in February?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Diyarbakır in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
