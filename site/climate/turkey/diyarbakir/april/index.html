How April compares in Diyarbakır

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Diyarbakır. Compared with March: 5.2 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Diyarbakır in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in April?
In April, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
April is not one of Diyarbakır’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Diyarbakır in April?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (83 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Diyarbakır get in April?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Diyarbakır in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
