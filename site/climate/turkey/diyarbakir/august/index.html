Diyarbakır in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in August?
In August, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 38.6 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 378 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
August is not one of Diyarbakır’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (83 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in August?
