Diyarbakır weather in December

Average December weather in Diyarbakır, Turkey: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Diyarbakır averages 8.4 °C (47 °F) days and 0.1 °C (32 °F) nights, with 80 mm (3.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 132 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Diyarbakır

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Diyarbakır. Compared with November: 5.4 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Diyarbakır in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in December?
In December, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
December is not one of Diyarbakır’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Diyarbakır in December?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (83 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.3 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Diyarbakır get in December?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Diyarbakır in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
