How November compares in Diyarbakır

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Diyarbakır. Compared with October: 8.1 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. December is 5.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Diyarbakır in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Diyarbakır in November?
In November, Diyarbakır averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Diyarbakır?
November is not one of Diyarbakır’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Diyarbakır in November?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (85 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Diyarbakır in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Diyarbakır get in November?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Diyarbakır in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
