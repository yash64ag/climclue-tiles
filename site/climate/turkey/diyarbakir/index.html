Diyarbakır at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Diyarbakır is 31st warmest (16.3 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 3,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (567 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Batman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Diyarbakır is 0.9 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.3 °C), averages 28.8 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Diyarbakır, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Diyarbakır has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
