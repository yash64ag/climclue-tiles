How June compares in Konya

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Konya. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Konya in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Konya in June?
In June, Konya averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 337 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Konya?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Konya’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Konya in June?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (56 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Konya in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Konya get in June?
About 337 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Konya in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
