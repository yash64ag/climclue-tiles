How October compares in Konya

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Konya. Compared with September: 5.9 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. November is 6.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Konya in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Konya in October?
In October, Konya averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Konya?
October is not one of Konya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Konya in October?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (56 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Konya in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Konya get in October?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Konya in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
