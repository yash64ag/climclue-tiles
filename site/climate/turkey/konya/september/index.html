How September compares in Konya

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Konya. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Konya in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Konya in September?
In September, Konya averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Konya?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Konya’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Konya in September?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (53 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Konya in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Konya get in September?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Konya in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
