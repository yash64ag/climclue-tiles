How March compares in Konya

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Konya. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 63 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Konya in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Konya in March?
In March, Konya averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Konya?
March is not one of Konya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Konya in March?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (56 mm), the driest August (7 mm).
Does it snow in Konya in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Konya get in March?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Konya in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
