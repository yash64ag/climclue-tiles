Why Tekirdağ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tekirdağ climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tekirdağ?
August, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 27.4 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 20.3 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tekirdağ?
January, with an average of 5.5 °C (42 °F), day highs around 7.8 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 2.7 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tekirdağ?
December, with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 556 mm (21.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tekirdağ?
August, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tekirdağ?
July, with about 381 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tekirdağ?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Tekirdağ?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Tekirdağ’s GeoNames location (40.978°N, 27.511°E; ClimaClue area sq-430-2075), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 122,287 (GeoNames).
