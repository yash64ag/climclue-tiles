Tekirdağ sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Turkey

The sea off Tekirdağ averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.8 °C (46 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Tekirdağ sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tekirdağ?
In August, at about 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tekirdağ?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 7.8 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tekirdağ in February?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tekirdağ?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
