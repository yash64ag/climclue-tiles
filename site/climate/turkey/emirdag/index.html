Emirdağ weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Emirdağ, Turkey (Afyonkarahisar Province), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Emirdağ is warmest in August, with 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 15.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 4.6 °C (40 °F) days and -2.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around April (48 mm); September is driest (14 mm).

Emirdağ at a glance

Among the 417 towns ClimaClue covers in Turkey, Emirdağ is 323rd warmest (12.2 °C year average), 299th sunniest (about 2,777 hours of sunshine a year) and 389th wettest (412 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bolvadin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Emirdağ is about as warm in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 234 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Emirdağ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Emirdağ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 234 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Emirdağ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.020°N, 31.150°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-449-2111), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
