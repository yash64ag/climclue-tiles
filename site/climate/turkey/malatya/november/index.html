How November compares in Malatya

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Malatya. Compared with October: 7.8 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Malatya in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malatya in November?
In November, Malatya averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Malatya?
November is not one of Malatya’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malatya in November?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (62 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Malatya in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Malatya get in November?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malatya in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
