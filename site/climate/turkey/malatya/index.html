Malatya at a glance

Among the 112 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Turkey, Malatya is 84th warmest (13.2 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 3,019 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (480 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Adıyaman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Malatya is 4.1 °C cooler in July and 3.4 °C cooler in January, with 125 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 262 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Malatya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Malatya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 262 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
