San Miguelito at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Panama, San Miguelito is the 2nd warmest (26.2 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,123 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (2,612 mm a year). Compared with Panama City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), San Miguelito is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C cooler in October, with 312 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.9 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, October (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Miguelito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Miguelito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
