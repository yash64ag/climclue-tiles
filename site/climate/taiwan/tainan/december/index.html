How December compares in Tainan

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tainan. Compared with November: 3.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Tainan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tainan in December?
In December, Tainan averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tainan?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Tainan’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tainan in December?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (401 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Tainan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tainan get in December?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tainan in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
