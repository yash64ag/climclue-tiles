How April compares in Tainan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tainan. Compared with March: 3.1 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 2.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Tainan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tainan in April?
In April, Tainan averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tainan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Tainan’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tainan in April?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (415 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Tainan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tainan get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tainan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
