Tainan at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Tainan is the 3rd warmest (24.1 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,992 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (1,855 mm a year). Compared with Yongkang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Tainan is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tainan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Tainan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Tainan are January, February, March, April, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, April, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.6 °C), August (28.1 °C) and June (27.8 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (18 mm), January (21 mm) and November (21 mm).

Why Tainan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
