How August compares in Tainan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tainan. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 45 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 0.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Tainan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tainan in August?
In August, Tainan averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 401 mm (15.8 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tainan?
August is not one of Tainan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tainan in August?
401 mm (15.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (401 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Tainan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tainan get in August?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tainan in August?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tainan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
