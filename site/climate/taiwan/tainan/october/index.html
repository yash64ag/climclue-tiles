How October compares in Tainan

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tainan. Compared with September: 1.7 °C cooler, 191 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. November is 2.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Tainan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tainan in October?
In October, Tainan averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tainan?
October is not one of Tainan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tainan in October?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (415 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Tainan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tainan get in October?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tainan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
