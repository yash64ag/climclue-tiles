How May compares in Tainan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tainan. Compared with April: 2.2 °C warmer, 109 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 1.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Tainan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tainan in May?
In May, Tainan averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tainan?
May is not one of Tainan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tainan in May?
196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (415 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Tainan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tainan get in May?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tainan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
