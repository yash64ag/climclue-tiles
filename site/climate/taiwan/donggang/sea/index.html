Donggang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Taiwan · Taiwan

The sea off Donggang averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 23.4 °C (74 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Donggang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Donggang?
In July, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Donggang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is January, at 23.4 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Donggang in January?
About 23.4 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Donggang?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Donggang nearby?
In July: Hengchun (28.8 °C, 60 km) and Taitung (28.9 °C, 79 km).
