Why Keelung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Keelung climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Keelung?
July, with an average of 28.3 °C (83 °F), day highs around 29.2 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 26.5 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Keelung?
January, with an average of 16.1 °C (61 °F), day highs around 17.1 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 14.3 °C (58 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Keelung?
September, with 521 mm (20.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 3,821 mm (150.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Keelung?
July, with 204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Keelung?
July, with about 247 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Keelung?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Keelung?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Keelung have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Keelung’s GeoNames location (25.131°N, 121.741°E; ClimaClue area sq-588-3017), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 362,487 (GeoNames).
