Keelung at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Keelung is the least sunny (about 1,686 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (3,821 mm a year). Compared with Xizhi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Keelung is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 81 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Keelung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Keelung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
