How September compares in Taipei

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with August: 1.8 °C cooler, 158 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. October is 2.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Taipei in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in September?
In September, Taipei averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 470 mm (18.5 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in September?
470 mm (18.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (470 mm), the driest January (155 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
