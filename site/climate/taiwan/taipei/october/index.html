How October compares in Taipei

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with September: 2.9 °C cooler, 116 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. November is 3.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Taipei in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in October?
In October, Taipei averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 354 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in October?
354 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (470 mm), the driest January (155 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in October?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
