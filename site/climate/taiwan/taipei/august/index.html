How August compares in Taipei

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 69 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. September is 2.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Taipei in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in August?
In August, Taipei averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in August?
305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (455 mm), the driest January (145 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in August?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
