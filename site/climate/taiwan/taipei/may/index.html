How May compares in Taipei

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with April: 3.3 °C warmer, 75 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Taipei in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in May?
In May, Taipei averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in May?
260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (470 mm), the driest January (155 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in May?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
