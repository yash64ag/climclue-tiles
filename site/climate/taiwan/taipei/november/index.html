How November compares in Taipei

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with October: 3.1 °C cooler, 88 mm less precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Taipei in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in November?
In November, Taipei averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 266 mm (10.5 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in November?
266 mm (10.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (470 mm), the driest January (155 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in November?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
