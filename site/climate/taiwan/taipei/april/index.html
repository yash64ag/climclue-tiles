How April compares in Taipei

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with March: 3.5 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. May is 3.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Taipei in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in April?
In April, Taipei averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in April?
185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (470 mm), the driest January (155 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in April?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
