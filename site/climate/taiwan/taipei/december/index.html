Taipei weather in December

Average December weather in Taipei, Taiwan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Taipei averages 20.5 °C (69 °F) days and 14.3 °C (58 °F) nights, with 163 mm (6.4 in) of rain over the month and about 100 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Taipei

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with November: 3.5 °C cooler, 87 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Of the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Taiwan, Taipei ranks 54th warmest (average temperature), 61st sunniest and 7th wettest in December.

Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Taipei in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in December?
In December, Taipei averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in December?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (455 mm), the driest January (145 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in December?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
