How March compares in Taipei

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Taipei. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 3.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Taipei in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taipei in March?
In March, Taipei averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Taipei?
No month in Taipei is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taipei in March?
197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (470 mm), the driest January (155 mm).
Does it snow in Taipei in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taipei get in March?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taipei in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
