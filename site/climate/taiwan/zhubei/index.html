Zhubei at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Zhubei is 15th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 1,753 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (2,110 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hsinchu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Zhubei is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 202 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhubei, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Zhubei

For mild weather, the best months to visit Zhubei are January, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.6 °C), August (28.1 °C) and June (27.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (63 mm), January (68 mm) and November (80 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (247 h), August (227 h) and September (185 h).

Why Zhubei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
