Neili at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Neili is the 3rd coolest (22.2 °C year average). Compared with Sanxia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Neili is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 130 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Neili, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Neili has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
