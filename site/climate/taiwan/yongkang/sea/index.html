Yongkang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Taiwan · Taiwan

The sea off Yongkang averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 22.3 °C (72 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to November.

Yongkang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yongkang?
In July, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yongkang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to November. The coolest month is February, at 22.3 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yongkang in February?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Yongkang?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
