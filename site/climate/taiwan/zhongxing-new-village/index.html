Zhongxing New Village at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Taiwan, Zhongxing New Village is 39th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 1,848 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (2,217 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caotun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Zhongxing New Village is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 79 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Why Zhongxing New Village has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-600-3006), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
