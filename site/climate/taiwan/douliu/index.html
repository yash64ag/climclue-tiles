Douliu at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Douliu is 11th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 1,877 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (2,500 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nantou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Douliu is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Douliu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Douliu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Douliu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.709°N, 120.543°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-602-3005), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
