Hsinchu at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Hsinchu is 13th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 1,784 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (1,908 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhubei, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Hsinchu is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 202 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hsinchu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Hsinchu

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hsinchu are January, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, October, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.8 °C), August (28.4 °C) and June (27.6 °C).

Why Hsinchu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
