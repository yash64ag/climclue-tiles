Shulin at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Shulin is the coolest (21.8 °C year average), the 5th least sunny (about 1,706 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (3,568 mm a year). Compared with Banqiao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Shulin is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 275 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shulin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shulin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
