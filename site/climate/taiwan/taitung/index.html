Why Taitung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Taitung climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Taitung?
July, with an average of 28.6 °C (83 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 26.3 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Taitung?
January, with an average of 18.9 °C (66 °F), day highs around 20.5 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 16.9 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Taitung?
September, with 381 mm (15.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,116 mm (83.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Taitung?
January, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Taitung?
July, with about 234 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Taitung?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Taitung?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Taitung’s GeoNames location (22.760°N, 121.145°E; ClimaClue area sq-612-3011), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 103,260 (GeoNames).
