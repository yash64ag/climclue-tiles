Taitung at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Taitung is the 4th warmest (24.0 °C year average). Compared with Fengshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Taitung is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 154 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taitung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taitung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
