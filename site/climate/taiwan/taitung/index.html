Taitung weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Taitung, Taiwan (Taiwan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Taitung is warmest in July, with 32.3 °C (90 °F) days and 25.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 23.4 °C (74 °F) days and 14.5 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (376 mm); January is driest (39 mm).

Taitung at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Taiwan, Taitung is 6th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 1,904 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (2,070 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chaozhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Taitung is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 218 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, January (19.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.5 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taitung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taitung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taitung itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.760°N, 121.145°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-612-3011), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
