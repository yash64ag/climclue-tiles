Taitung sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Taiwan · Taiwan

The sea off Taitung averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 24.3 °C (76 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Taitung sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Taitung?
In July, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Taitung?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 24.3 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Taitung in January?
About 24.3 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Taitung?
About 26.7 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
