How February compares in Kaohsiung

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kaohsiung. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. March is 2.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Kaohsiung in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in February?
In February, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Kaohsiung’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in February?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in February?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in February?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
