How January compares in Kaohsiung

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Kaohsiung in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in January?
In January, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kaohsiung’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
