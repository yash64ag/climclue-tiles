Kaohsiung weather in July

Average July weather in Kaohsiung, Taiwan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Kaohsiung averages 32.3 °C (90 °F) days and 24.9 °C (77 °F) nights, with 368 mm (14.5 in) of rain over the month and about 237 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Kaohsiung

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kaohsiung. Compared with June: 0.7 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Kaohsiung in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in July?
In July, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 368 mm (14.5 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
July is not one of Kaohsiung’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in July?
368 mm (14.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (413 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in July?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kaohsiung in July?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (28.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
