How September compares in Kaohsiung

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with August: 0.7 °C cooler, 181 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. October is 1.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Kaohsiung in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in September?
In September, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
September is not one of Kaohsiung’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in September?
265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in September?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
