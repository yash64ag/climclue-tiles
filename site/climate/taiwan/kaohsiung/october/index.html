Kaohsiung weather in October

Average October weather in Kaohsiung, Taiwan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Kaohsiung averages 30.5 °C (87 °F) days and 21.3 °C (70 °F) nights, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of rain over the month and about 181 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Kaohsiung

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with September: 1.6 °C cooler, 194 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 2.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Kaohsiung in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in October?
In October, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
October is not one of Kaohsiung’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in October?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (413 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in October?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kaohsiung in October?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (28.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
