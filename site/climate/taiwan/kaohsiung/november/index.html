How November compares in Kaohsiung

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with October: 2.6 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. December is 3.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Kaohsiung in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in November?
In November, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Kaohsiung’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in November?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
