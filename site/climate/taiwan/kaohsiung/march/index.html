How March compares in Kaohsiung

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with February: 2.4 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 2.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Kaohsiung in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in March?
In March, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Kaohsiung’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in March?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in March?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
