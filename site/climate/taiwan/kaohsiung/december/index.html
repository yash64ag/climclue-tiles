How December compares in Kaohsiung

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Kaohsiung in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in December?
In December, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Kaohsiung’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in December?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in December?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
