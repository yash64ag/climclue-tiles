How April compares in Kaohsiung

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kaohsiung. Compared with March: 2.8 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 1.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Kaohsiung in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaohsiung in April?
In April, Kaohsiung averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kaohsiung?
April is not one of Kaohsiung’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaohsiung in April?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (446 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kaohsiung in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaohsiung get in April?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaohsiung in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
