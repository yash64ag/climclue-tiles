Xizhi at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Xizhi is the 2nd coolest (22.0 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 1,697 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (3,740 mm a year). Compared with Neihu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Xizhi is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 585 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xizhi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xizhi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
