Chiayi City at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Chiayi City is the 5th sunniest (about 1,927 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Douliu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Chiayi City is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 134 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chiayi City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chiayi City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
