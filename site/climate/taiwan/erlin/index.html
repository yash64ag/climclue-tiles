Erlin at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Taiwan, Erlin is 26th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 1,891 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (1,820 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pitou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Erlin is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 82 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Erlin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Erlin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Erlin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.901°N, 120.369°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-600-3003), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
