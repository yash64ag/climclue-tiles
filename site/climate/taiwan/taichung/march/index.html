How March compares in Taichung

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Taichung. Compared with February: 2.1 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 3.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Taichung in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taichung in March?
In March, Taichung averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Taichung?
March is not one of Taichung’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taichung in March?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (352 mm), the driest November (26 mm).
Does it snow in Taichung in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taichung get in March?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taichung in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
