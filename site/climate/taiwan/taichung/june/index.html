How June compares in Taichung

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Taichung. Compared with May: 1.6 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. July is 0.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Taichung in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taichung in June?
In June, Taichung averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 339 mm (13.4 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Taichung?
June is not one of Taichung’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taichung in June?
339 mm (13.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (339 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Taichung in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taichung get in June?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taichung in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
