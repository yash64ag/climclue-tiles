How May compares in Taichung

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Taichung. Compared with April: 2.7 °C warmer, 115 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. June is 1.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Taichung in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taichung in May?
In May, Taichung averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taichung?
May is not one of Taichung’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taichung in May?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (339 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Taichung in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taichung get in May?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taichung in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
