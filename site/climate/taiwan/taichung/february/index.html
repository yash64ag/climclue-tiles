How February compares in Taichung

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Taichung. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 61 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. March is 2.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Taichung in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taichung in February?
In February, Taichung averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 89 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Taichung?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Taichung’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taichung in February?
95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (339 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Taichung in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taichung get in February?
About 89 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taichung in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
