Taichung at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Taichung is 8th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 1,837 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (1,949 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chang-hua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Taichung is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taichung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Taichung

For mild weather, the best months to visit Taichung are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.1 °C), August (27.9 °C) and June (27.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (25 mm), December (32 mm) and October (33 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (246 h), August (225 h) and September (187 h).

Why Taichung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
