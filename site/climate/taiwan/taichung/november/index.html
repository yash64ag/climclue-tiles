How November compares in Taichung

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Taichung. Compared with October: 2.9 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Taichung in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taichung in November?
In November, Taichung averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Taichung?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Taichung’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taichung in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (352 mm), the driest November (26 mm).
Does it snow in Taichung in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taichung get in November?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taichung in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
