How June compares in New Taipei City

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in New Taipei City. Compared with May: 2.6 °C warmer, 69 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. July is 1.7 °C warmer than June on average.

New Taipei City in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New Taipei City in June?
In June, New Taipei City averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 356 mm (14.0 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit New Taipei City?
No month in New Taipei City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New Taipei City in June?
356 mm (14.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in New Taipei City in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New Taipei City get in June?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New Taipei City in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
