How March compares in New Taipei City

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in New Taipei City. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 3.7 °C warmer than March on average.

New Taipei City in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New Taipei City in March?
In March, New Taipei City averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit New Taipei City?
No month in New Taipei City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New Taipei City in March?
217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (484 mm), the driest January (146 mm).
Does it snow in New Taipei City in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New Taipei City get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in New Taipei City in March?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for New Taipei City in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
