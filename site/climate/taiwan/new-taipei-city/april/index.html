How April compares in New Taipei City

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in New Taipei City. Compared with March: 3.6 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. May is 3.3 °C warmer than April on average.

New Taipei City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New Taipei City in April?
In April, New Taipei City averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit New Taipei City?
No month in New Taipei City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New Taipei City in April?
203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in New Taipei City in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New Taipei City get in April?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New Taipei City in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
