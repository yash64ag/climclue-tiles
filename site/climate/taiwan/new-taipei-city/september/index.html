How September compares in New Taipei City

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in New Taipei City. Compared with August: 1.8 °C cooler, 165 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. October is 2.9 °C cooler than September on average.

New Taipei City in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New Taipei City in September?
In September, New Taipei City averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 484 mm (19.0 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit New Taipei City?
No month in New Taipei City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New Taipei City in September?
484 mm (19.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (484 mm), the driest January (146 mm).
Does it snow in New Taipei City in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New Taipei City get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in New Taipei City in September?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for New Taipei City in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
