How November compares in New Taipei City

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in New Taipei City. Compared with October: 3.2 °C cooler, 88 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

New Taipei City in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New Taipei City in November?
In November, New Taipei City averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit New Taipei City?
No month in New Taipei City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New Taipei City in November?
251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (484 mm), the driest January (146 mm).
Does it snow in New Taipei City in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New Taipei City get in November?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New Taipei City in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
