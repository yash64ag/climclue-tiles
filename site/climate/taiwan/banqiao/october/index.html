Banqiao in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in October?
In October, Banqiao averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 346 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in October?
346 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in October?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banqiao in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
