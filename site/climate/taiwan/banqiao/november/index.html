How November compares in Banqiao

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Banqiao. Compared with October: 3.2 °C cooler, 99 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Banqiao in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in November?
In November, Banqiao averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 253 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in November?
253 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (486 mm), the driest January (140 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in November?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Banqiao in November?
About 23.1 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Banqiao in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
