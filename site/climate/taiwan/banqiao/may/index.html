Banqiao in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in May?
In May, Banqiao averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in May?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in May?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banqiao in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
