How July compares in Banqiao

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Banqiao. Compared with June: 1.7 °C warmer, 94 mm less precipitation and 85 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Banqiao in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in July?
In July, Banqiao averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 262 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in July?
262 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in July?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banqiao in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
