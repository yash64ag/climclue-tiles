Banqiao weather in July

Average July weather in Banqiao, Taiwan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How July compares in Banqiao

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Banqiao. Compared with June: 1.7 °C warmer, 87 mm less precipitation and 85 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.
