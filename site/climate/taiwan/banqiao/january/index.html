How January compares in Banqiao

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Banqiao. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Banqiao in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in January?
In January, Banqiao averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in January?
147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in January?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banqiao in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
