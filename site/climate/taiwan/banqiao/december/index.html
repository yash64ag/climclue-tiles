How December compares in Banqiao

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Banqiao. Compared with November: 3.6 °C cooler, 91 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Banqiao in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in December?
In December, Banqiao averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in December?
161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (486 mm), the driest January (140 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in December?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Banqiao in December?
About 20.2 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Banqiao in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
