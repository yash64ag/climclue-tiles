Banqiao in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in September?
In September, Banqiao averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 497 mm (19.6 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in September?
497 mm (19.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in September?
