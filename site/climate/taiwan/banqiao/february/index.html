How February compares in Banqiao

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Banqiao. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 78 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 2.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Banqiao in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banqiao in February?
In February, Banqiao averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Banqiao?
No month in Banqiao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banqiao in February?
225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (497 mm), the driest January (147 mm).
Does it snow in Banqiao in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banqiao get in February?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banqiao in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
