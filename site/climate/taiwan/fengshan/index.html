Fengshan at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Fengshan is the warmest (24.5 °C year average) and the sunniest (about 2,008 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Tainan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Fengshan is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 107 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fengshan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fengshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
