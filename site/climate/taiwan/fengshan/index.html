Fengshan weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Fengshan, Taiwan (Takao), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Fengshan is warmest in July, with 32.2 °C (90 °F) days and 24.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 23.8 °C (75 °F) days and 13.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (451 mm); December is driest (17 mm).

Fengshan at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Taiwan, Fengshan is the 4th warmest (24.5 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,008 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kaohsiung, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Fengshan is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 142 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fengshan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fengshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fengshan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.627°N, 120.361°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-613-3003), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
