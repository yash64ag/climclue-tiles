Danshui at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Danshui is 21st warmest (22.3 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 1,723 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (3,087 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with New Taipei City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Danshui is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 206 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.7 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Danshui, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Danshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
