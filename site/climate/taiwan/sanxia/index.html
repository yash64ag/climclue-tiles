Sanxia at a glance

Among the 27 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Taiwan, Sanxia is 22nd warmest (22.2 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 1,715 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (2,919 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shulin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sanxia is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 649 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sanxia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sanxia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
