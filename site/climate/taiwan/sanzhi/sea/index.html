Sanzhi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Taipei · Taiwan

The sea off Sanzhi averages 27.8 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 19.7 °C (67 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Sanzhi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sanzhi?
In August, at about 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sanzhi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 19.7 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sanzhi in February?
About 19.7 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sanzhi?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sanzhi nearby?
In August: Danshui (28.4 °C, 11 km), New Taipei City (28.4 °C, 22 km) and Taoyuan (28.5 °C, 36 km).
