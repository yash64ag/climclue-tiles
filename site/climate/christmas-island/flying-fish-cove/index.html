Flying Fish Cove at a glance

Its warmest month, April (26.4 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Best time to visit Flying Fish Cove

For mild weather, the best months to visit Flying Fish Cove are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): April (26.4 °C), March (26.3 °C) and May (26.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): September (21 mm), August (32 mm) and October (38 mm).

Why Flying Fish Cove has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
