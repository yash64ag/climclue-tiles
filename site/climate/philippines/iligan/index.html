Why Iligan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

May, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 24.4 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Iligan?
January, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 26.6 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 22.7 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Iligan?
June, with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,563 mm (61.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Iligan?
April, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Iligan?
April, with about 234 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Iligan?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Iligan?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Iligan’s GeoNames location (8.229°N, 124.243°E; ClimaClue area sq-757-3042), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 342,618 (GeoNames).
