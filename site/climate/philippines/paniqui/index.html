Paniqui at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Paniqui is 61st warmest (27.3 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,578 hours of sunshine a year) and 64th wettest (2,114 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bayambang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Paniqui is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 358 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.3 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Paniqui, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Paniqui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
