How December compares in General Santos

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in General Santos. Compared with November: 0.2 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

General Santos in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in December?
In December, General Santos averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in December?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (128 mm), the driest March (59 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in December?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for General Santos in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
