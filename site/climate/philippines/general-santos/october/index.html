General Santos in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in October?
In October, General Santos averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in October?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (128 mm), the driest March (59 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in October?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for General Santos in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
