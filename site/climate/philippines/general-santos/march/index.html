How March compares in General Santos

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in General Santos. Compared with February: 0.7 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. April is 0.3 °C warmer than March on average.

General Santos in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in March?
In March, General Santos averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in March?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (121 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in March?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in General Santos in March?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for General Santos in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
