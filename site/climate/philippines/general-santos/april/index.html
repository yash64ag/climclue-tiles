General Santos weather in April

Average April weather in General Santos, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, General Santos averages 33.9 °C (93 °F) days and 22.7 °C (73 °F) nights, with 65 mm (2.6 in) of rain over the month and about 219 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in General Santos

April is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in General Santos. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

General Santos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in April?
In April, General Santos averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in April?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (121 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in April?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in General Santos in April?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for General Santos in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
