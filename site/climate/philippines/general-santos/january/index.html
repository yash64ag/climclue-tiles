How January compares in General Santos

January is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in General Santos. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

General Santos in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in January?
In January, General Santos averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in January?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (121 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in January?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for General Santos in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
