How July compares in General Santos

July is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in General Santos. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

General Santos in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in July?
In July, General Santos averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in July?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (121 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in July?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in General Santos in July?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for General Santos in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
