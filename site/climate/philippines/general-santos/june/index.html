How June compares in General Santos

June is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in General Santos. Compared with May: 0.8 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

General Santos in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in June?
In June, General Santos averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in June?
121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (121 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in June?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in General Santos in June?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for General Santos in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
