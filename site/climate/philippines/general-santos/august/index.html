General Santos in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in General Santos in August?
In August, General Santos averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit General Santos?
No month in General Santos is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in General Santos in August?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (128 mm), the driest March (59 mm).
Does it snow in General Santos in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does General Santos get in August?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
