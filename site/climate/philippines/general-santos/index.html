General Santos at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, General Santos is the driest (1,082 mm a year). Compared with Koronadal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), General Santos is 1.2 °C cooler in April and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.9 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 117% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why General Santos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
