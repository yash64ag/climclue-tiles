Bagong Silangan at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Bagong Silangan is 106th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 2,325 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (2,886 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Mateo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Bagong Silangan is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 419 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.4 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bagong Silangan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
