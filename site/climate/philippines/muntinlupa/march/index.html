How March compares in Muntinlupa

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Muntinlupa. Compared with February: 1.3 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 1.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Muntinlupa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muntinlupa in March?
In March, Muntinlupa averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Muntinlupa?
No month in Muntinlupa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muntinlupa in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (335 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Muntinlupa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muntinlupa get in March?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Muntinlupa in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
