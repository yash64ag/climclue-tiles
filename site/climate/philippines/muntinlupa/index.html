Muntinlupa at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Muntinlupa is 75th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 2,368 hours of sunshine a year) and 109th wettest (1,826 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Las Piñas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Muntinlupa is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.8 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Muntinlupa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Muntinlupa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
