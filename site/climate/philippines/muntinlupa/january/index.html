How January compares in Muntinlupa

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Muntinlupa. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 63 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Muntinlupa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muntinlupa in January?
In January, Muntinlupa averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Muntinlupa?
No month in Muntinlupa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muntinlupa in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (335 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Muntinlupa in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muntinlupa get in January?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Muntinlupa in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
