Dasmariñas at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Dasmariñas is 84th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,388 hours of sunshine a year) and 105th wettest (1,882 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Poblacion, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Dasmariñas is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 57 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dasmariñas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dasmariñas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
