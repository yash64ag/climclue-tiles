Dasmariñas at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Dasmariñas is 382nd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 177th sunniest (about 2,388 hours of sunshine a year) and 365th wettest (1,909 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with General Trias, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Dasmariñas is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Dasmariñas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-696-3009), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
