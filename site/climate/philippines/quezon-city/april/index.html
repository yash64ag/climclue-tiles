How April compares in Quezon City

April is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Quezon City. Compared with March: 1.4 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is about as warm as April on average.

Quezon City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quezon City in April?
In April, Quezon City averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Quezon City?
No month in Quezon City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quezon City in April?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (468 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Quezon City in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quezon City get in April?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quezon City in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
