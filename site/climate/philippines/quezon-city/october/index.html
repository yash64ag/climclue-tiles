Quezon City weather in October

Average October weather in Quezon City, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Quezon City averages 31.4 °C (89 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 273 mm (10.8 in) of rain over the month and about 180 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Quezon City

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Quezon City. Compared with September: 0.3 °C warmer, 120 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. November is 0.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Quezon City in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quezon City in October?
In October, Quezon City averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 273 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Quezon City?
No month in Quezon City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quezon City in October?
273 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (474 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Quezon City in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quezon City get in October?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Quezon City in October?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Quezon City in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
