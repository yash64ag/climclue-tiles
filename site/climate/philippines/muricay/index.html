Muricay at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Muricay is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,184 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mantampay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (90 km away), Muricay is 1.3 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 383 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.6 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, July (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.9 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Muricay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Muricay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
