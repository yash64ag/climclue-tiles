San Antonio, Central Luzon (14.9°N 120.1°E) at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, San Antonio, Central Luzon (14.9°N 120.1°E) is 25th warmest (27.9 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,527 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (3,394 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Narciso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), San Antonio, Central Luzon (14.9°N 120.1°E) is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Why San Antonio, Central Luzon (14.9°N 120.1°E) has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
