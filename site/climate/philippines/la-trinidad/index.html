La Trinidad at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, La Trinidad is the coolest (21.5 °C year average) and the wettest (4,155 mm a year). Compared with Dagupan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), La Trinidad is 6.3 °C cooler in May and 6.1 °C cooler in January, with 1,446 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.6 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of La Trinidad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why La Trinidad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
