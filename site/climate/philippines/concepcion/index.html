Concepcion at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Concepcion is 80th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,531 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (1,863 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Capas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Concepcion is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 83 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 76 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Concepcion, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Concepcion has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
