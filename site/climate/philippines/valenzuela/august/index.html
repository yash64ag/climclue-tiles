Valenzuela weather in August

Average August weather in Valenzuela, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Valenzuela averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 24.1 °C (75 °F) nights, with 448 mm (17.6 in) of rain over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Valenzuela

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Valenzuela. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Valenzuela in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valenzuela in August?
In August, Valenzuela averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 448 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Valenzuela?
