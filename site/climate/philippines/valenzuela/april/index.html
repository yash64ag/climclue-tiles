How April compares in Valenzuela

April is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Valenzuela. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 0.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Valenzuela in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valenzuela in April?
In April, Valenzuela averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Valenzuela?
No month in Valenzuela is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valenzuela in April?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (439 mm), the driest January (15 mm).
Does it snow in Valenzuela in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valenzuela get in April?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valenzuela in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
