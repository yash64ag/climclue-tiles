How March compares in Valenzuela

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Valenzuela. Compared with February: 1.4 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 1.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Valenzuela in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valenzuela in March?
In March, Valenzuela averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Valenzuela?
No month in Valenzuela is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valenzuela in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (449 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Valenzuela in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valenzuela get in March?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Valenzuela in March?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Valenzuela in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
