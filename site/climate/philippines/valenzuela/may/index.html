Valenzuela in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valenzuela in May?
In May, Valenzuela averages a day high of 34.1 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Valenzuela?
No month in Valenzuela is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valenzuela in May?
147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (449 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Valenzuela in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valenzuela get in May?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Valenzuela in May?
About 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Valenzuela in May?
