Bulan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bicol Region · Philippines

The sea off Bulan averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 25.9 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Bulan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bulan?
In June, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bulan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.9 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bulan in February?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Bulan?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Bulan nearby?
In June: Bato (29.6 °C, 94 km) and Nabua (29.6 °C, 98 km).
