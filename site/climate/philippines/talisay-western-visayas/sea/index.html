Talisay, Western Visayas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Visayas · Philippines

The sea off Talisay, Western Visayas averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 26.7 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Talisay, Western Visayas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Talisay, Western Visayas?
In June, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Talisay, Western Visayas?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.7 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Talisay, Western Visayas in February?
About 26.7 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Talisay, Western Visayas?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
