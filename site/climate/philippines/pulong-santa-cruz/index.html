Pulong Santa Cruz at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Pulong Santa Cruz is 120th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 2,363 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,979 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Carmona, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Pulong Santa Cruz is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 154 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pulong Santa Cruz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pulong Santa Cruz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
