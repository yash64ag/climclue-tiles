How March compares in Caloocan

Caloocan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caloocan in March?
In March, Caloocan averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Caloocan?
No month in Caloocan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caloocan in March?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (434 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Caloocan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caloocan get in March?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Caloocan in March?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Caloocan in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
