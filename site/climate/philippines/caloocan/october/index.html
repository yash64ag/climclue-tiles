Caloocan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caloocan in October?
In October, Caloocan averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 250 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Caloocan?
No month in Caloocan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caloocan in October?
250 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (433 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Caloocan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caloocan get in October?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caloocan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
