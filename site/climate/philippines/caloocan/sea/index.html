Caloocan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · National Capital Region · Philippines

The sea off Caloocan averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 26.5 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Caloocan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Caloocan?
In June, at about 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Caloocan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.5 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Caloocan in February?
About 26.5 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Caloocan?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
