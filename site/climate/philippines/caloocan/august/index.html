How August compares in Caloocan

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Caloocan. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.
