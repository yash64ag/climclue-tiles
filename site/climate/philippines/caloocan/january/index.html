How January compares in Caloocan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Caloocan. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Caloocan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caloocan in January?
In January, Caloocan averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Caloocan?
No month in Caloocan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caloocan in January?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (433 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Caloocan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caloocan get in January?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caloocan in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
