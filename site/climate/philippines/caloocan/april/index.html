Caloocan weather in April

Average April weather in Caloocan, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Caloocan averages 34.6 °C (94 °F) days and 24.3 °C (76 °F) nights, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 261 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Caloocan

April is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Caloocan. Compared with March: 1.4 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is about as warm as April on average.

Caloocan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caloocan in April?
In April, Caloocan averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Caloocan?
No month in Caloocan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caloocan in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (434 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Caloocan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caloocan get in April?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Caloocan in April?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Caloocan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
