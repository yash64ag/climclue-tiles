How May compares in Cebu City

May is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cebu City. Compared with April: 0.4 °C warmer, 54 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. June is 0.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Cebu City in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cebu City in May?
In May, Cebu City averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cebu City?
No month in Cebu City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cebu City in May?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (233 mm), the driest March (72 mm).
Does it snow in Cebu City in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cebu City get in May?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cebu City in May?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cebu City in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
