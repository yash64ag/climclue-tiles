San Pablo at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, San Pablo is 122nd warmest (26.4 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 2,265 hours of sunshine a year) and 87th wettest (2,013 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Los Baños, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), San Pablo is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 98 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.9 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Pablo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Pablo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
