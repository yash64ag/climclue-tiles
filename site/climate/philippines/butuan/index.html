Butuan at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Butuan is 54th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 2,295 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (2,441 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bayugan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Butuan is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 509 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.3 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.5 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Butuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Butuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
