Cagayan de Oro in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cagayan de Oro in September?
In September, Cagayan de Oro averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 215 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cagayan de Oro?
No month in Cagayan de Oro is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cagayan de Oro in September?
215 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest March (70 mm).
Does it snow in Cagayan de Oro in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cagayan de Oro get in September?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cagayan de Oro in September?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cagayan de Oro in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
