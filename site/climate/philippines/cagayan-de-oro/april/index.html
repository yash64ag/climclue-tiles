What is the weather like in Cagayan de Oro in April?
In April, Cagayan de Oro averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cagayan de Oro?
