Rodriguez at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Rodriguez is 141st warmest (24.6 °C year average), 130th sunniest (about 2,262 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (3,285 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Mateo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Rodriguez is 2.0 °C cooler in May and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 818 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.1 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 24 km from Rodriguez measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.5 °C (6.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Rodriguez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
