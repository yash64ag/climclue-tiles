Marikina City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Marikina City is 114th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,333 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (2,467 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bagong Silangan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Marikina City is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 419 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Marikina City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
