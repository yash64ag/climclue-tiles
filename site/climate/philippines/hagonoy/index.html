Hagonoy at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Hagonoy is 52nd warmest (27.4 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,451 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (1,947 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Malolos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Hagonoy is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hagonoy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hagonoy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
