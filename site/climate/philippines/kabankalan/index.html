Kabankalan at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Kabankalan is 93rd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 131st sunniest (about 2,245 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (2,171 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Himamaylan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Kabankalan is 0.5 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.8 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kabankalan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kabankalan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
