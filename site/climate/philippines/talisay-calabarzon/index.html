Talisay, Calabarzon at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Talisay, Calabarzon is 313th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 223rd sunniest (about 2,352 hours of sunshine a year) and 357th wettest (1,919 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Patuto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Talisay, Calabarzon is 2.6 °C warmer in May and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 522 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.7 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in February. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Why Talisay, Calabarzon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Talisay itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.092°N, 121.022°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-699-3010), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
