Iloilo at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Iloilo is 73rd warmest (27.1 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,308 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (2,233 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bago City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Iloilo is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 176 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iloilo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iloilo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
