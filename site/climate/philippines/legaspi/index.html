Legaspi at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Legaspi is 86th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,264 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (3,103 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sorsogon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Legaspi is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 243 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.4 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 90% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Legaspi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
