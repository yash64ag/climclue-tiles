Why Legaspi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

What is the coldest month in Legaspi?
January, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 27.0 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 22.7 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Legaspi?

What is the driest month in Legaspi?

What is the sunniest month in Legaspi?

Does it snow in Legaspi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Legaspi?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Legaspi’s GeoNames location (13.141°N, 123.741°E; ClimaClue area sq-708-3037), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 179,481 (GeoNames).
