Why Tabuk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tabuk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tabuk?
June, with an average of 27.9 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 23.6 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tabuk?
January, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 24.3 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 18.5 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tabuk?
August, with 322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,845 mm (72.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tabuk?
February, with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tabuk?
April, with about 264 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tabuk?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tabuk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March and December.
What type of climate does Tabuk have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Tabuk’s GeoNames location (17.415°N, 121.442°E; ClimaClue area sq-665-3014), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 122,771 (GeoNames).
