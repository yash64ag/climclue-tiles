Zamboanga weather in September

Average September weather in Zamboanga, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Zamboanga averages 31.2 °C (88 °F) days and 22.6 °C (73 °F) nights, with 200 mm (7.9 in) of rain over the month and about 185 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Zamboanga

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zamboanga. Compared with August: about as warm, 4 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

What to pack for Zamboanga in September

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Zamboanga in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zamboanga in September?
In September, Zamboanga averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zamboanga?
No month in Zamboanga is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zamboanga in September?
200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (222 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Zamboanga in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zamboanga get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zamboanga in September?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zamboanga in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
