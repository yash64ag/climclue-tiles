Zamboanga weather in February

Average February weather in Zamboanga, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Zamboanga averages 31.6 °C (89 °F) days and 22.0 °C (72 °F) nights, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 163 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Zamboanga

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zamboanga. Compared with January: about as warm, 13 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. March is 0.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Zamboanga in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zamboanga in February?
In February, Zamboanga averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zamboanga?
No month in Zamboanga is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zamboanga in February?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (222 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Zamboanga in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zamboanga get in February?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zamboanga in February?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zamboanga in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
