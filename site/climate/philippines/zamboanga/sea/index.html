Zamboanga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zamboanga Peninsula · Philippines

The sea off Zamboanga averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 26.8 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Zamboanga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Zamboanga?
In May, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Zamboanga?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.8 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Zamboanga in February?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Zamboanga?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Zamboanga nearby?
In May: Siocon (29.5 °C, 89 km), Ipil (29.2 °C, 112 km) and Pagadian (29.4 °C, 182 km).
