How January compares in Zamboanga

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zamboanga. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Zamboanga in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zamboanga in January?
In January, Zamboanga averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zamboanga?
No month in Zamboanga is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zamboanga in January?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (266 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Zamboanga in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zamboanga get in January?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zamboanga in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
