How December compares in Zamboanga

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zamboanga. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 80 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Zamboanga in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zamboanga in December?
In December, Zamboanga averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zamboanga?
No month in Zamboanga is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zamboanga in December?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (266 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Zamboanga in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zamboanga get in December?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zamboanga in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
