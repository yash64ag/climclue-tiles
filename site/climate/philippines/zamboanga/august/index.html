How August compares in Zamboanga

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zamboanga. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is about as warm as August on average.

Zamboanga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zamboanga in August?
In August, Zamboanga averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zamboanga?
No month in Zamboanga is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zamboanga in August?
241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (266 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Zamboanga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zamboanga get in August?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zamboanga in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
