How March compares in Zamboanga

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zamboanga. Compared with February: 0.4 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Zamboanga in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zamboanga in March?
In March, Zamboanga averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zamboanga?
No month in Zamboanga is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zamboanga in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (222 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Zamboanga in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zamboanga get in March?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zamboanga in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
