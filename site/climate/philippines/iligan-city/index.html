Why Iligan City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Iligan City climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Iligan City?
May, with an average of 25.3 °C (77 °F), day highs around 27.0 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 21.5 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Iligan City?
January, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 25.1 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 20.1 °C (68 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Iligan City?
July, with 276 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,123 mm (83.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Iligan City?
March, with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Iligan City?
April, with about 231 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Iligan City?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Iligan City?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March and April.
What type of climate does Iligan City have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Iligan City’s GeoNames location (8.250°N, 124.400°E; ClimaClue area sq-757-3044), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 312,323 (GeoNames).
