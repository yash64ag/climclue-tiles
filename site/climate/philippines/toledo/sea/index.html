Toledo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Visayas · Philippines

The sea off Toledo averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 26.4 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Toledo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Toledo?
In June, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Toledo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.4 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Toledo in February?
About 26.4 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Toledo?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Toledo nearby?
In June: Balamban (29.7 °C, 16 km).
