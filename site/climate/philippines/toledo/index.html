Toledo at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Toledo is 44th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 106th sunniest (about 2,299 hours of sunshine a year) and 141st wettest (1,447 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naga, Central Visayas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Toledo is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 214 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.0 °C (75 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Toledo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Toledo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
