Santa Rosa at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Santa Rosa is 81st warmest (27.0 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 2,356 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (1,761 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cabuyao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Santa Rosa is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santa Rosa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santa Rosa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
