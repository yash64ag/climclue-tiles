Tanza at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Tanza is the 5th warmest (27.8 °C year average). Compared with Valenzuela, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Tanza is about as warm in April and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.4 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 84% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tanza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
