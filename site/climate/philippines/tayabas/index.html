Tayabas at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Tayabas is the 3rd coolest (24.0 °C year average). Compared with Lucena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Tayabas is 2.5 °C cooler in May and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 878 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.3 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tayabas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Tayabas

For mild weather, the best months to visit Tayabas are February and March.

Why Tayabas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
