Amadeo at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Amadeo is 496th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 228th sunniest (about 2,346 hours of sunshine a year) and 159th wettest (2,415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mendez-Nuñez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Amadeo is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.4 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Amadeo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Amadeo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Amadeo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.171°N, 120.924°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-698-3009), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
