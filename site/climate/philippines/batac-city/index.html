Batac City at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Batac City is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,729 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with San Nicolas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Batac City is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 133 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.3 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 15 km from Batac City measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (yearly rain and snow: these numbers have 150% of the station’s). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Batac City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Batac City itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.055°N, 120.565°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-659-3005), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
