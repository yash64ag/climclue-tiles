Lucena at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Lucena is 113th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 123rd sunniest (about 2,270 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (2,391 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tayabas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Lucena is 2.5 °C warmer in May and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 878 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.8 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lucena, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lucena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
