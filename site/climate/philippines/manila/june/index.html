How June compares in Manila

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Manila. Compared with May: 0.9 °C cooler, 133 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Manila in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manila in June?
In June, Manila averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 271 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Manila?
No month in Manila is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manila in June?
271 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (433 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Manila in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manila get in June?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manila in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
