How August compares in Manila

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Manila. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. September is about as warm as August on average.

Manila in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manila in August?
In August, Manila averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 418 mm (16.5 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Manila?
No month in Manila is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manila in August?
418 mm (16.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (418 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Manila in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manila get in August?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Manila in August?
About 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Manila in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
