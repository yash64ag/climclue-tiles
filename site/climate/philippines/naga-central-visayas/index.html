Naga, Central Visayas at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Naga, Central Visayas is 56th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 107th sunniest (about 2,298 hours of sunshine a year) and 130th wettest (1,660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Talisay, Central Visayas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Naga, Central Visayas is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.4 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Naga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Naga, Central Visayas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
