Bagong Silang at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Bagong Silang is 49th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,356 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (2,467 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Jose del Monte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Bagong Silang is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.1 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 16 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bagong Silang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
