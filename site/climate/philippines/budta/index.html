Budta at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Budta is the 3rd warmest (28.2 °C year average). Compared with Malingao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Budta is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.6 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, July (27.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.5 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Budta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Budta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
