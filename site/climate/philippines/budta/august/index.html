How August compares in Budta

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Budta. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Budta in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budta in August?
In August, Budta averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Budta?
No month in Budta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budta in August?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (190 mm), the driest February (62 mm).
Does it snow in Budta in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Budta get in August?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budta in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
