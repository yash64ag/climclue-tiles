How December compares in Budta

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Budta. Compared with November: about as warm, 25 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Budta in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budta in December?
In December, Budta averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Budta?
No month in Budta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budta in December?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (193 mm), the driest February (64 mm).
Does it snow in Budta in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Budta get in December?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budta in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
