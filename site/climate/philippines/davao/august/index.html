How August compares in Davao

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Davao. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Davao in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Davao in August?
In August, Davao averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Davao?
No month in Davao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Davao in August?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (189 mm), the driest March (112 mm).
Does it snow in Davao in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Davao get in August?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Davao in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
