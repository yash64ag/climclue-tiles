How March compares in Davao

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Davao. Compared with February: 0.5 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Davao in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Davao in March?
In March, Davao averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Davao?
No month in Davao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Davao in March?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (189 mm), the driest March (97 mm).
Does it snow in Davao in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Davao get in March?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Davao in March?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Davao in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
