How December compares in Davao

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Davao. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Davao in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Davao in December?
In December, Davao averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Davao?
No month in Davao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Davao in December?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (189 mm), the driest March (112 mm).
Does it snow in Davao in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Davao get in December?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Davao in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
