How April compares in Davao

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Davao. Compared with March: 0.7 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. May is about as warm as April on average.

Davao in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Davao in April?
In April, Davao averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Davao?
No month in Davao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Davao in April?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (189 mm), the driest March (97 mm).
Does it snow in Davao in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Davao get in April?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Davao in April?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Davao in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
