Jalajala at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Jalajala is 417th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 356th sunniest (about 2,292 hours of sunshine a year) and 251st wettest (2,115 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Cruz, Calabarzon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Jalajala is 0.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 74 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.2 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.6 °C (69 °F), in February. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jalajala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jalajala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jalajala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.353°N, 121.322°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-696-3013), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
