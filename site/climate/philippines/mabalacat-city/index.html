Mabalacat City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Mabalacat City is 119th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,518 hours of sunshine a year) and 80th wettest (2,014 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Mabalacat City is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 190 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.3 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mabalacat City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mabalacat City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
