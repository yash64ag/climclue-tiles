Bacolod City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Bacolod City is 62nd warmest (27.3 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 2,321 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (2,509 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Talisay, Western Visayas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Bacolod City is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 88 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.6 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.9 °C (75 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bacolod City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bacolod City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
