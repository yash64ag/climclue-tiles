How November compares in Calamba

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Calamba. Compared with October: 0.5 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. December is 1.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Calamba in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calamba in November?
In November, Calamba averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Calamba?
No month in Calamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calamba in November?
179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (307 mm), the driest February (25 mm).
Does it snow in Calamba in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calamba get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calamba in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
