How May compares in Calamba

May is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Calamba. Compared with April: 0.3 °C warmer, 88 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. June is 0.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Calamba in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calamba in May?
In May, Calamba averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Calamba?
No month in Calamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calamba in May?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (307 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Calamba in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calamba get in May?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calamba in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
