How April compares in Calamba

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Calamba. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 0.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Calamba in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calamba in April?
In April, Calamba averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Calamba?
No month in Calamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calamba in April?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (307 mm), the driest February (25 mm).
Does it snow in Calamba in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calamba get in April?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calamba in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
