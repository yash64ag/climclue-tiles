How December compares in Malingao

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Malingao. Compared with November: about as warm, 23 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Malingao in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malingao in December?
In December, Malingao averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Malingao?
No month in Malingao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malingao in December?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (191 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Malingao in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malingao get in December?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malingao in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
