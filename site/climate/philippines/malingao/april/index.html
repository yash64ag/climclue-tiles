Malingao weather in April

Average April weather in Malingao, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Malingao averages 35.0 °C (95 °F) days and 24.5 °C (76 °F) nights, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of rain over the month and about 229 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Malingao

April is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Malingao. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. May is 0.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Malingao in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malingao in April?
In April, Malingao averages a day high of 35.0 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Malingao?
No month in Malingao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malingao in April?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (186 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Malingao in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malingao get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
