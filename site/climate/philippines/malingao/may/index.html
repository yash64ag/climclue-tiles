How May compares in Malingao

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Malingao. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 74 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. June is 1.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Malingao in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malingao in May?
In May, Malingao averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Malingao?
No month in Malingao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malingao in May?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (191 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Malingao in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malingao get in May?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malingao in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
