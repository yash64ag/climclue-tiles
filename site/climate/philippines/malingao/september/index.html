How September compares in Malingao

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Malingao. Compared with August: 0.1 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

Malingao in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malingao in September?
In September, Malingao averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Malingao?
No month in Malingao is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malingao in September?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (191 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Malingao in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malingao get in September?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malingao in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
