Manolo Fortich at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Manolo Fortich is the 5th coolest (24.3 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,195 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Cagayan de Oro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Manolo Fortich is 1.7 °C cooler in April and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 769 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.1 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in February. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Manolo Fortich, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Manolo Fortich has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
