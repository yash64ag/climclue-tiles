Himamaylan at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Himamaylan is 46th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 2,275 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (2,130 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kabankalan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Himamaylan is 0.5 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.3 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Himamaylan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Himamaylan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
