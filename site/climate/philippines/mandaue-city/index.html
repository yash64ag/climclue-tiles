Mandaue City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Mandaue City is 67th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,319 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (1,608 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cebu City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Mandaue City is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 381 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.9 °C (77 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mandaue City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mandaue City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
