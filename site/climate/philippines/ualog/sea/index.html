Ualog sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Visayas · Philippines

The sea off Ualog averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 25.9 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Ualog sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ualog?
In June, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ualog?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.9 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ualog in February?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Ualog?
About 27.5 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ualog nearby?
In June: Escalante (29.9 °C, 32 km), Paraiso (29.6 °C, 35 km) and Balamban (29.7 °C, 36 km).
