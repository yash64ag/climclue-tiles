How December compares in Antipolo

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Antipolo. Compared with November: 1.0 °C cooler, 49 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Antipolo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antipolo in December?
In December, Antipolo averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Antipolo?
December is not one of Antipolo’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antipolo in December?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (498 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Antipolo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antipolo get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antipolo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
