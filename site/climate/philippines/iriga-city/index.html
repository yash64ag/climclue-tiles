Iriga City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Iriga City is the 5th wettest (3,325 mm a year). Compared with Naga, Bicol Region, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Iriga City is 1.2 °C cooler in May and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 528 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, January (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in February. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iriga City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iriga City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
