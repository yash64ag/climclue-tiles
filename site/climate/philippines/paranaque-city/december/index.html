Paranaque City in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Paranaque City in December?
In December, Paranaque City averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Paranaque City?
No month in Paranaque City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Paranaque City in December?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (354 mm), the driest February (13 mm).
Does it snow in Paranaque City in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Paranaque City get in December?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Paranaque City in December?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Paranaque City in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
