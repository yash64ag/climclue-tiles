Paranaque City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Paranaque City in April?
In April, Paranaque City averages a day high of 34.7 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Paranaque City?
