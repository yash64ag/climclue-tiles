Bago City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Bago City is 12th warmest (27.7 °C year average), 91st sunniest (about 2,319 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (2,408 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mansilingan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Bago City is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, January (26.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.0 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bago City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bago City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
