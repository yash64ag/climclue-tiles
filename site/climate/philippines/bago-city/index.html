Why Bago City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

April, with an average of 29.4 °C (85 °F), day highs around 31.8 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.5 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bago City?
January, with an average of 26.8 °C (80 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 24.0 °C (75 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bago City?

What is the driest month in Bago City?

What is the sunniest month in Bago City?
April, with about 249 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bago City?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bago City?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Bago City’s GeoNames location (10.533°N, 122.833°E; ClimaClue area sq-734-3028), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 192,993 (GeoNames).
