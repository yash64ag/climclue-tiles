Mapun sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mimaropa · Philippines

The sea off Mapun averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 27.2 °C (81 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Mapun sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mapun?
In May, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mapun?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 27.2 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mapun in February?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Mapun?
About 28.7 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
