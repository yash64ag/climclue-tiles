How May compares in Makati City

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Makati City. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 101 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. June is 0.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Makati City in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makati City in May?
In May, Makati City averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Makati City?
No month in Makati City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makati City in May?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (404 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Makati City in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makati City get in May?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makati City in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
