Cotabato at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Cotabato is 86th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 363rd sunniest (about 2,290 hours of sunshine a year) and 450th wettest (1,704 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sultan Kudarat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Cotabato is about as warm in April and about as warm in July, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.4 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, July (27.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cotabato, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Cotabato

No month in Cotabato is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are July (27.0 °C, 195 mm), August (27.1 °C, 175 mm) and September (27.1 °C, 170 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): April (28.4 °C), March (28.1 °C) and May (28.0 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (72 mm), March (78 mm) and January (83 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (230 h), May (215 h) and March (204 h).

Why Cotabato has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cotabato itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.224°N, 124.246°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-767-3042), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
