Why Cadiz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Cadiz climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cadiz?
May, with an average of 28.3 °C (83 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 26.1 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cadiz?
January, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 26.5 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 24.1 °C (75 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cadiz?
November, with 317 mm (12.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,174 mm (85.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cadiz?
April, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cadiz?
April, with about 248 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cadiz?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cadiz?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Cadiz have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Cadiz’s GeoNames location (10.946°N, 123.288°E; ClimaClue area sq-730-3032), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 129,053 (GeoNames).
