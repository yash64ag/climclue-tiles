Panabo at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Panabo is 39th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 2,286 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (2,107 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Magugpo Poblacion, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Panabo is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1,323 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.5 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Panabo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Panabo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
