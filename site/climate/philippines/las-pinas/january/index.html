Las Piñas weather in January

Average January weather in Las Piñas, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Las Piñas averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) days and 21.8 °C (71 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain over the month and about 208 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Las Piñas

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Las Piñas. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Las Piñas in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Piñas in January?
In January, Las Piñas averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Las Piñas?
No month in Las Piñas is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Piñas in January?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (347 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Las Piñas in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Piñas get in January?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Las Piñas in January?
About 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
