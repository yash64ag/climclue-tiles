Las Piñas in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Piñas in March?
In March, Las Piñas averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Las Piñas?
No month in Las Piñas is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Piñas in March?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (347 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Las Piñas in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Piñas get in March?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Las Piñas in March?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Las Piñas in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
