Santa Maria, Davao Region sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Davao Region · Philippines

The sea off Santa Maria, Davao Region averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 27.6 °C (82 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Santa Maria, Davao Region sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santa Maria, Davao Region?
In June, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santa Maria, Davao Region?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 27.6 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santa Maria, Davao Region in February?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Santa Maria, Davao Region?
About 28.7 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
