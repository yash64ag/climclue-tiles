San Fernando, Ilocos sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ilocos · Philippines

The sea off San Fernando, Ilocos averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 27.0 °C (81 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

San Fernando, Ilocos sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Fernando, Ilocos?
In June, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Fernando, Ilocos?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.0 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Fernando, Ilocos in January?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in San Fernando, Ilocos?
About 28.7 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
