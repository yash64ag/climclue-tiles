How June compares in Pasig City

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Pasig City. Compared with May: 0.8 °C cooler, 124 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Pasig City in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pasig City in June?
In June, Pasig City averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Pasig City?
No month in Pasig City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pasig City in June?
260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (442 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Pasig City in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pasig City get in June?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pasig City in June?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pasig City in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
