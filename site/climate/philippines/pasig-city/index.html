Pasig City at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Pasig City is 21st warmest (27.5 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 2,362 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (2,013 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Juan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Pasig City is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 278 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.3 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 79% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Pasig City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
