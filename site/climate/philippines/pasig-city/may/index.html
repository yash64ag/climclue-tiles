Pasig City weather in May

Average May weather in Pasig City, Philippines: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Pasig City averages 34.0 °C (93 °F) days and 24.2 °C (76 °F) nights, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of rain over the month and about 228 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Pasig City

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Pasig City. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 109 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. June is 0.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Pasig City in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pasig City in May?
In May, Pasig City averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pasig City?
No month in Pasig City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pasig City in May?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (442 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Pasig City in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pasig City get in May?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pasig City in May?
About 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pasig City in May?
