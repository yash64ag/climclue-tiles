Maramag at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Maramag is 128th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 2,238 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (1,925 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with NIA Valencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Maramag is 1.8 °C warmer in April and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 510 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.8 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 105 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maramag, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Same area as Quezon: Maramag lies in the same ~10 km ClimaClue area as Quezon, so its numbers are the same as there.

Why Maramag has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 105 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
