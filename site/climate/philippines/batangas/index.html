Why Batangas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Batangas climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Batangas?
May, with an average of 28.4 °C (83 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 25.8 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Batangas?
January, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 27.4 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 23.1 °C (74 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Batangas?
July, with 273 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,724 mm (67.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Batangas?
February, with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Batangas?
April, with about 256 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Batangas?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Batangas?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Batangas have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Batangas’s GeoNames location (13.757°N, 121.058°E; ClimaClue area sq-702-3010), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 237,370 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
