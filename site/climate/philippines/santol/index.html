Santol at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Santol is 123rd warmest (26.3 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,504 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (2,204 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mabalacat City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Santol is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 190 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
