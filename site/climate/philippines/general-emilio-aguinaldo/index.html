General Emilio Aguinaldo at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, General Emilio Aguinaldo is 465th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 174th sunniest (about 2,391 hours of sunshine a year) and 173rd wettest (2,371 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Indang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), General Emilio Aguinaldo is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why General Emilio Aguinaldo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
