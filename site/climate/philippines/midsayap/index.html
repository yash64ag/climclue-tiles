Midsayap at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Midsayap is the warmest (28.3 °C year average). Compared with Malingao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Midsayap is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 75 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.8 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, July (27.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Midsayap, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Midsayap has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
