Limay at a glance

Among the 517 towns ClimaClue covers in the Philippines, Limay is 313th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 137th sunniest (about 2,424 hours of sunshine a year) and 222nd wettest (2,197 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orion, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Limay is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 114 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Limay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Limay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. The 10 km area around Limay, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 25.4 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Limay itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.562°N, 120.598°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-694-3005), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
