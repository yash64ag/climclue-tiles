Naga, Bicol Region at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Naga, Bicol Region is 53rd warmest (27.4 °C year average), 119th sunniest (about 2,275 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (2,797 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Iriga City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Naga, Bicol Region is 1.2 °C warmer in May and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 528 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.9 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Naga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Naga, Bicol Region has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
