Magugpo Poblacion at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Magugpo Poblacion is the 3rd wettest (3,430 mm a year). Compared with Panabo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Magugpo Poblacion is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1,323 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in February. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Magugpo Poblacion, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Magugpo Poblacion has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
