Why Sorsogon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Sorsogon climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sorsogon?
May, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 25.7 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sorsogon?
January, with an average of 25.3 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 23.8 °C (75 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sorsogon?
December, with 532 mm (21.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 3,347 mm (131.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sorsogon?
May, with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sorsogon?
May, with about 240 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sorsogon?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sorsogon?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Sorsogon have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Sorsogon’s GeoNames location (12.974°N, 123.993°E; ClimaClue area sq-710-3039), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 187,670 (GeoNames).
