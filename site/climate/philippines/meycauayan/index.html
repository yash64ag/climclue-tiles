Why Meycauayan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Meycauayan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Meycauayan?
April, with an average of 29.4 °C (85 °F), day highs around 32.2 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 26.1 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Meycauayan?
January, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 23.3 °C (74 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Meycauayan?
July, with 439 mm (17.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,164 mm (85.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Meycauayan?
January, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Meycauayan?
April, with about 261 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Meycauayan?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Meycauayan?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Meycauayan’s GeoNames location (14.737°N, 120.961°E; ClimaClue area sq-692-3009), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 228,023 (GeoNames).
