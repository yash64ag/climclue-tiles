How January compares in Taguig

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Taguig. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 60 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Taguig in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taguig in January?
In January, Taguig averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Taguig?
No month in Taguig is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taguig in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (404 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Taguig in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taguig get in January?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taguig in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
