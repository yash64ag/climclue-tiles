How April compares in Taguig

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Taguig. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 0.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Taguig in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taguig in April?
In April, Taguig averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Taguig?
No month in Taguig is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taguig in April?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (404 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Taguig in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taguig get in April?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taguig in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
