How February compares in Taguig

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Taguig. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. March is 1.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Taguig in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taguig in February?
In February, Taguig averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Taguig?
No month in Taguig is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taguig in February?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (374 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Taguig in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taguig get in February?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Taguig in February?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Taguig in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
