How July compares in Taguig

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Taguig. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 149 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Taguig in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taguig in July?
In July, Taguig averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 389 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Taguig?
No month in Taguig is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taguig in July?
389 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (404 mm), the driest February (15 mm).
Does it snow in Taguig in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taguig get in July?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taguig in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
