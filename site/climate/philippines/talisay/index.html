Talisay, Central Visayas at a glance

Among the 147 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Philippines, Talisay, Central Visayas is 13th warmest (27.7 °C year average), 88th sunniest (about 2,321 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (1,673 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cebu City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Talisay, Central Visayas is 1.3 °C warmer in May and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 316 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.8 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, January (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.3 °C (76 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Talisay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Talisay, Central Visayas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
