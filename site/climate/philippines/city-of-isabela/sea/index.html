City of Isabela sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao · Philippines

The sea off City of Isabela averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 26.8 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

City of Isabela sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in City of Isabela?
In May, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in City of Isabela?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.8 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in City of Isabela in February?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in City of Isabela?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in City of Isabela nearby?
In May: Al-Barka (28.8 °C, 33 km), Siocon (29.5 °C, 113 km) and Ipil (29.2 °C, 138 km).
