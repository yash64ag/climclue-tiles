Nassau at a glance

Its warmest month, August (27.8 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nassau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Nassau

For mild weather, the best months to visit Nassau are January, February, March, April, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, April, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (27.8 °C), July (27.6 °C) and September (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (41 mm), March (42 mm) and February (44 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (272 h), July (270 h) and May (266 h).

Why Nassau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
