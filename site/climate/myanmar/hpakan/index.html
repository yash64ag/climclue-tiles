Hpākān at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Hpākān is 141st warmest (23.5 °C year average), 138th sunniest (about 2,284 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (2,876 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mogaung, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Hpākān is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 416 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 595 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hpākān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hpākān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 595 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hpākān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.614°N, 96.315°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-583-2763), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
