Yawnghwe at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Yawnghwe is 30th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,398 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (1,207 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taunggyi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Yawnghwe is 2.7 °C warmer in April and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 313 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.4 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 362 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yawnghwe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yawnghwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 362 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
