Kone Ta La Paung at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Kone Ta La Paung is 11th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,573 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (2,799 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shwenatha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Kone Ta La Paung is about as warm in April and about as warm in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.5 °C), averages 7.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 81 km from the sea.

Why Kone Ta La Paung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 81 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kone Ta La Paung itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.008°N, 96.150°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-669-2761), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
