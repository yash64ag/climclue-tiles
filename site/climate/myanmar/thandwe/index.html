Thandwe at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Thandwe is the wettest (5,961 mm a year). Compared with Pyay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Thandwe is 2.4 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 4,498 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.8 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thandwe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thandwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
