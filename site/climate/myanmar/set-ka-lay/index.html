Set Ka Lay at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Set Ka Lay is 10th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,574 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (2,761 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hlaingthaya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Set Ka Lay is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in January, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.3 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Set Ka Lay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Same area as Yangon: Set Ka Lay lies in the same ~10 km ClimaClue area as Yangon, so its numbers are the same as there.

Why Set Ka Lay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
