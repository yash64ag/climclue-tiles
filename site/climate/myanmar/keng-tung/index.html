Kēng Tung at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Kēng Tung is the 5th coolest (22.0 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 2,229 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Möng Yang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Kēng Tung is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.3 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, December (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 586 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kēng Tung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kēng Tung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 586 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
