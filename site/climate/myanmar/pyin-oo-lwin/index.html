Pyin Oo Lwin at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Pyin Oo Lwin is the 2nd coolest (20.7 °C year average). Compared with Mandalay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Pyin Oo Lwin is 7.6 °C cooler in April and 6.1 °C cooler in January, with 571 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.4 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 395 km from the sea.

Why Pyin Oo Lwin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 395 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
