Kalaw at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Kalaw is the 3rd coolest (19.1 °C year average). Compared with Aungban, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kalaw is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 349 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (22.2 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 322 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalaw, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalaw has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 322 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kalaw itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.635°N, 96.565°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-633-2765), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
