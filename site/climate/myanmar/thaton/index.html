Thaton at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Thaton is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,638 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (4,612 mm a year). Compared with Mawlamyine, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Thaton is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 249 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.1 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.9 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thaton, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thaton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
