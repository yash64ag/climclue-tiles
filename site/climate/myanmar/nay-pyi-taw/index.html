Nay Pyi Taw at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Nay Pyi Taw is 25th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,502 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (1,371 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taungoo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Nay Pyi Taw is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 708 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.1 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 243 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nay Pyi Taw, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nay Pyi Taw has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 243 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
