How November compares in Nay Pyi Taw

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nay Pyi Taw. Compared with October: 2.0 °C cooler, 110 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. December is 3.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Nay Pyi Taw in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nay Pyi Taw in November?
In November, Nay Pyi Taw averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nay Pyi Taw?
November is not one of Nay Pyi Taw’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nay Pyi Taw in November?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (267 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nay Pyi Taw in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nay Pyi Taw get in November?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nay Pyi Taw in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
