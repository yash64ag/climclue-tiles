How April compares in Nay Pyi Taw

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nay Pyi Taw. Compared with March: 3.4 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. May is 1.3 °C cooler than April on average.

Nay Pyi Taw in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nay Pyi Taw in April?
In April, Nay Pyi Taw averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nay Pyi Taw?
April is not one of Nay Pyi Taw’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nay Pyi Taw in April?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (272 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nay Pyi Taw in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nay Pyi Taw get in April?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nay Pyi Taw in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
