Nay Pyi Taw in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nay Pyi Taw in October?
In October, Nay Pyi Taw averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nay Pyi Taw?
October is not one of Nay Pyi Taw’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nay Pyi Taw in October?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (272 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nay Pyi Taw in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nay Pyi Taw get in October?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nay Pyi Taw in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
