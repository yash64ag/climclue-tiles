How May compares in Nay Pyi Taw

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nay Pyi Taw. Compared with April: 1.3 °C cooler, 143 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. June is 2.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Nay Pyi Taw in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nay Pyi Taw in May?
In May, Nay Pyi Taw averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nay Pyi Taw?
May is not one of Nay Pyi Taw’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nay Pyi Taw in May?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (272 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nay Pyi Taw in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nay Pyi Taw get in May?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nay Pyi Taw in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
