How February compares in Nay Pyi Taw

Nay Pyi Taw in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nay Pyi Taw in February?
In February, Nay Pyi Taw averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nay Pyi Taw?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Nay Pyi Taw’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nay Pyi Taw in February?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (272 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nay Pyi Taw in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nay Pyi Taw get in February?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nay Pyi Taw in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
