Mu-se at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Mu-se is the 5th least sunny (about 2,200 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Namhkam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Mu-se is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 206 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.3 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 650 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mu-se, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mu-se has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 650 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C. The 10 km area around Mu-se, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 20.2 °C a year, 1.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mu-se itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.997°N, 97.901°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-600-2779), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
