Mu-se at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Mu-se is the 2nd coolest (20.2 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 2,200 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Lashio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (119 km away), Mu-se is 1.2 °C cooler in June and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.6 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 650 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mu-se, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mu-se has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 650 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
