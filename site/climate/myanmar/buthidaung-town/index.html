Buthidaung Town weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Buthidaung Town, Myanmar (Rakhine), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Buthidaung Town is warmest in May, with 31.6 °C (89 °F) days and 24.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 26.5 °C (80 °F) days and 14.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (1,089 mm); January is driest (3 mm).

Buthidaung Town at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Buthidaung Town is 121st warmest (25.4 °C year average), 143rd sunniest (about 2,271 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (4,075 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kyauktaw, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Buthidaung Town is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 358 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Buthidaung Town, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Buthidaung Town has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Buthidaung Town itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.875°N, 92.530°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-631-2725), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
