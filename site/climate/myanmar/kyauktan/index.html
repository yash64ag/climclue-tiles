Kyauktan at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Kyauktan is the 4th sunniest (about 2,588 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mingala Tangnyunt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Kyauktan is 0.7 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 203 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.4 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kyauktan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kyauktan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
