Sittwe at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Sittwe is the 4th wettest (4,783 mm a year). Compared with Kyaukpyu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Sittwe is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 1.9 °C cooler in January, with 149 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.4 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.9 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sittwe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sittwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
