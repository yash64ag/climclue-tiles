Hinthada at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Hinthada is 16th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,525 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (2,184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mingaladon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Hinthada is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 471 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.1 °C), averages 7.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hinthada, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hinthada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
