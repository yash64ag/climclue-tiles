Meiktila at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Meiktila is the 5th driest (887 mm a year). Compared with Myingyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Meiktila is 1.6 °C cooler in April and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.3 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.5 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Meiktila, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Meiktila has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
