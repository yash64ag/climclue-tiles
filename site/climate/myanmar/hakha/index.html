Hakha at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Hakha is the coolest (15.3 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 2,175 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kalemyo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Hakha is 10.1 °C cooler in June and 9.6 °C cooler in January, with 281 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (18.6 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 203 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hakha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hakha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 203 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C. The 10 km area around Hakha, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.6 °C a year, 1.3 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hakha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.645°N, 93.611°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-613-2736), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
