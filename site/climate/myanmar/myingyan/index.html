Myingyan at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Myingyan is the warmest (28.1 °C year average) and the driest (707 mm a year). Compared with Pakokku, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Myingyan is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.9 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 267 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Myingyan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Myingyan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 267 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
