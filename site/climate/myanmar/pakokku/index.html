Pakokku at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Pakokku is the 2nd warmest (27.8 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (738 mm a year). Compared with Myingyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Pakokku is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.5 °C), averages 10.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 236 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pakokku, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pakokku has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 236 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
