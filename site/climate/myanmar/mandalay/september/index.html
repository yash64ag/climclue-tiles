How September compares in Mandalay

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mandalay. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. October is 0.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Mandalay in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mandalay in September?
In September, Mandalay averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mandalay?
September is not one of Mandalay’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mandalay in September?
179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (179 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mandalay in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mandalay get in September?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mandalay in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
