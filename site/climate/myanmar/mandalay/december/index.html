How December compares in Mandalay

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mandalay. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Mandalay in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mandalay in December?
In December, Mandalay averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mandalay?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Mandalay’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mandalay in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (190 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mandalay in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mandalay get in December?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mandalay in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
