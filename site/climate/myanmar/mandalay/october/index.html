Mandalay weather in October

Average October weather in Mandalay, Myanmar: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Mandalay averages 32.2 °C (90 °F) days and 23.3 °C (74 °F) nights, with 161 mm (6.3 in) of rain over the month and about 194 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Mandalay

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mandalay. Compared with September: 1.0 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. November is 3.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Mandalay in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mandalay in October?
In October, Mandalay averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mandalay?
October is not one of Mandalay’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mandalay in October?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (190 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mandalay in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mandalay get in October?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mandalay in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
