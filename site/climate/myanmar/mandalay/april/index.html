How April compares in Mandalay

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mandalay. Compared with March: 3.4 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Mandalay in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mandalay in April?
In April, Mandalay averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mandalay?
April is not one of Mandalay’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mandalay in April?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (179 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mandalay in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mandalay get in April?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mandalay in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
