Mandalay weather in March

Average March weather in Mandalay, Myanmar: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Mandalay averages 36.8 °C (98 °F) days and 20.2 °C (68 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 263 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Mandalay

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Mandalay. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 3.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Mandalay in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mandalay in March?
In March, Mandalay averages a day high of 36.8 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mandalay?
March is not one of Mandalay’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mandalay in March?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (190 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mandalay in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mandalay get in March?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mandalay in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
