How May compares in Mandalay

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mandalay. Compared with April: 0.7 °C cooler, 113 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Mandalay in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mandalay in May?
In May, Mandalay averages a day high of 35.5 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 26.7 °C (80 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mandalay?
May is not one of Mandalay’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mandalay in May?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (179 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mandalay in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mandalay get in May?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mandalay in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
