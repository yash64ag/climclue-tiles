Taungoo at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Taungoo is 95th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 2,562 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (2,010 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yedashe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Taungoo is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 101 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, January (22.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 207 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taungoo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taungoo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 207 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taungoo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.943°N, 96.434°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-650-2764), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
