Taungoo at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Taungoo is 20th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,562 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (2,080 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nay Pyi Taw, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Taungoo is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 708 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 207 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taungoo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taungoo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 207 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
