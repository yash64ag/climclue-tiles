Kyaikto sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mon · Myanmar

The sea off Kyaikto averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 26.0 °C (79 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Kyaikto sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kyaikto?
In April, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kyaikto?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 26.0 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kyaikto in January?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Kyaikto?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
