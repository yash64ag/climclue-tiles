Ann at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Ann is 29th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,382 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (3,692 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kyaukpyu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Ann is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 3.0 °C cooler in January, with 1,241 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.4 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ann, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ann has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
