Dawei at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Dawei is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,600 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (5,694 mm a year). Compared with Myeik, the nearest covered town with different numbers (188 km away), Dawei is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.8 °C cooler in December, with 1,702 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.3 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, December (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dawei, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dawei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
