Why Taunggyi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 375 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Taunggyi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Taunggyi?
April, with an average of 22.8 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 15.9 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Taunggyi?
January, with an average of 14.8 °C (59 °F), day highs around 21.2 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 7.8 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Taunggyi?
August, with 293 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,520 mm (59.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Taunggyi?
January, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Taunggyi?
March, with about 272 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Taunggyi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Taunggyi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, November and December.
What type of climate does Taunggyi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Taunggyi’s GeoNames location (20.789°N, 97.038°E; ClimaClue area sq-632-2770), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 160,115 (GeoNames).
