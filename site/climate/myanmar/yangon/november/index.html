How November compares in Yangon

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Yangon. Compared with October: 1.1 °C cooler, 133 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. December is 2.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Yangon in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangon in November?
In November, Yangon averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Yangon?
November is not one of Yangon’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangon in November?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (594 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yangon in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangon get in November?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangon in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
