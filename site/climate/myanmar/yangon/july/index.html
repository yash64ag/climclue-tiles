How July compares in Yangon

July is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yangon. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Yangon in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangon in July?
In July, Yangon averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 594 mm (23.4 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Yangon?
July is not one of Yangon’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangon in July?
594 mm (23.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (594 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yangon in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangon get in July?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangon in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
