How October compares in Yangon

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Yangon. Compared with September: 0.4 °C warmer, 195 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Yangon in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangon in October?
In October, Yangon averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yangon?
October is not one of Yangon’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangon in October?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (594 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yangon in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangon get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangon in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
