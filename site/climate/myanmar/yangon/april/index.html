How April compares in Yangon

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Yangon. Compared with March: 2.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. May is 1.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Yangon in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangon in April?
In April, Yangon averages a day high of 37.3 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yangon?
April is not one of Yangon’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangon in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (594 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yangon in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangon get in April?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangon in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
