How May compares in Hlaingthaya

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hlaingthaya. Compared with April: 1.7 °C cooler, 275 mm more precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. June is 2.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Hlaingthaya in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hlaingthaya in May?
In May, Hlaingthaya averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 302 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hlaingthaya?
May is not one of Hlaingthaya’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hlaingthaya in May?
302 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (576 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hlaingthaya in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hlaingthaya get in May?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hlaingthaya in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
