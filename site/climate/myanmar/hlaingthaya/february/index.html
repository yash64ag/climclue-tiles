How February compares in Hlaingthaya

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hlaingthaya. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. March is 2.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Hlaingthaya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hlaingthaya in February?
In February, Hlaingthaya averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hlaingthaya?
February is not one of Hlaingthaya’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hlaingthaya in February?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (576 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hlaingthaya in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hlaingthaya get in February?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hlaingthaya in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
