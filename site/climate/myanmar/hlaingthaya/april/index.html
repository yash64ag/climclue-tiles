How April compares in Hlaingthaya

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hlaingthaya. Compared with March: 2.2 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. May is 1.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Hlaingthaya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hlaingthaya in April?
In April, Hlaingthaya averages a day high of 37.8 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hlaingthaya?
April is not one of Hlaingthaya’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hlaingthaya in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (577 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hlaingthaya in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hlaingthaya get in April?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hlaingthaya in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
