Hlaingthaya at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Hlaingthaya is 7th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,571 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (2,694 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Insein, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Hlaingthaya is 0.1 °C warmer in April and about as warm in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.4 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hlaingthaya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hlaingthaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
