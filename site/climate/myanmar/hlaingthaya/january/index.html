January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Hlaingthaya. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Is January a good time to visit Hlaingthaya?
