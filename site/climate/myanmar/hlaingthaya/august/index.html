How August compares in Hlaingthaya

August is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hlaingthaya. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Hlaingthaya in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hlaingthaya in August?
In August, Hlaingthaya averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 564 mm (22.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hlaingthaya?
August is not one of Hlaingthaya’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hlaingthaya in August?
564 mm (22.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (577 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hlaingthaya in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hlaingthaya get in August?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hlaingthaya in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
