Monywa at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Monywa is the 3rd warmest (27.6 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (850 mm a year). Compared with Myingyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Monywa is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 144 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.5 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 308 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Monywa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Monywa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
