Pathein at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Pathein is 21st warmest (26.7 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,485 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (2,919 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hinthada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (124 km away), Pathein is 1.3 °C cooler in April and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 735 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.8 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pathein, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pathein has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
