Kalemyo weather by month and climate

Kalemyo at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Kalemyo is 119th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 142nd sunniest (about 2,274 hours of sunshine a year) and 107th wettest (1,803 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mawlaik, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Kalemyo is 1.0 °C warmer in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 209 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.8 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 258 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalemyo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalemyo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 258 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kalemyo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.190°N, 94.064°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-608-2740), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
