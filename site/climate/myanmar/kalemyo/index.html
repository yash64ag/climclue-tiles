Kalemyo at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Kalemyo is the 5th least sunny (about 2,274 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Monywa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (163 km away), Kalemyo is 2.0 °C cooler in May and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 947 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.8 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 258 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalemyo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalemyo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 258 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
