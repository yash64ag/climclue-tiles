Pyay at a glance

Among the 36 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Myanmar, Pyay is 19th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,486 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (1,463 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thandwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Pyay is 3.6 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 4,498 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.1 °C), averages 8.5 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 118 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pyay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pyay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
