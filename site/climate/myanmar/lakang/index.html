Lakang at a glance

Among the 163 towns ClimaClue covers in Myanmar, Lakang is 136th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 149th sunniest (about 2,261 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (2,482 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhamo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Lakang is 2.3 °C cooler in May and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 420 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.3 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 612 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lakang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lakang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 612 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lakang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.661°N, 97.189°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-593-2771), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
