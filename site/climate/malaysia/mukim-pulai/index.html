Mukim Pulai at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Mukim Pulai is 58th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 2,087 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (2,662 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Perling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Mukim Pulai is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 135 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.3 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mukim Pulai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mukim Pulai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
