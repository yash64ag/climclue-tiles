How December compares in Mukim Pulai

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Mukim Pulai. Compared with November: 0.5 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Mukim Pulai in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mukim Pulai in December?
In December, Mukim Pulai averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 303 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mukim Pulai?
No month in Mukim Pulai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mukim Pulai in December?
303 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (303 mm), the driest February (147 mm).
Does it snow in Mukim Pulai in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mukim Pulai get in December?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mukim Pulai in December?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mukim Pulai in December?
