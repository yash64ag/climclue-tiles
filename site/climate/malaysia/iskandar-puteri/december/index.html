Iskandar Puteri weather in December

Average December weather in Iskandar Puteri, Malaysia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Iskandar Puteri averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 22.6 °C (73 °F) nights, with 281 mm (11.0 in) of rain over the month and about 133 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Iskandar Puteri

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Iskandar Puteri. Compared with November: 0.5 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Iskandar Puteri in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iskandar Puteri in December?
In December, Iskandar Puteri averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 281 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Iskandar Puteri?

How much does it rain in Iskandar Puteri in December?
