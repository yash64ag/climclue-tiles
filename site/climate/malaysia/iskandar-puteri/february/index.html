How February compares in Iskandar Puteri

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Iskandar Puteri. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 79 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. March is 0.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Iskandar Puteri in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iskandar Puteri in February?
In February, Iskandar Puteri averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Iskandar Puteri?
No month in Iskandar Puteri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iskandar Puteri in February?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (281 mm), the driest February (124 mm).
Does it snow in Iskandar Puteri in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iskandar Puteri get in February?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Iskandar Puteri in February?
