Iskandar Puteri at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Iskandar Puteri is 37th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,068 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (2,426 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Perling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Iskandar Puteri is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 110 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.9 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iskandar Puteri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iskandar Puteri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
