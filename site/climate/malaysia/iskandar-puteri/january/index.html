Iskandar Puteri in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iskandar Puteri in January?
In January, Iskandar Puteri averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Iskandar Puteri?
No month in Iskandar Puteri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iskandar Puteri in January?
202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (282 mm), the driest February (119 mm).
Does it snow in Iskandar Puteri in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iskandar Puteri get in January?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
