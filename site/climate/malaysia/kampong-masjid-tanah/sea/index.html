Kampong Masjid Tanah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Melaka · Malaysia

The sea off Kampong Masjid Tanah averages 30.4 °C (87 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 28.2 °C (83 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Kampong Masjid Tanah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kampong Masjid Tanah?
In June, at about 30.4 °C (87 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kampong Masjid Tanah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 28.2 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kampong Masjid Tanah in January?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Kampong Masjid Tanah?
About 29.4 °C (85 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
