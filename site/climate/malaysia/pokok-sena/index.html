Pokok Sena at a glance

Among the 352 towns ClimaClue covers in Malaysia, Pokok Sena is 57th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,433 hours of sunshine a year) and 275th wettest (2,286 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jitra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Pokok Sena is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in November, with 198 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (28.6 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, November (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pokok Sena, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pokok Sena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pokok Sena itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.171°N, 100.519°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-778-2805), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
