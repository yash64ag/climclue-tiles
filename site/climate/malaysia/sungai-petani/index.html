Sungai Petani at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Sungai Petani is the warmest (27.7 °C year average). Compared with Kota Kuala Muda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Sungai Petani is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in November, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (28.1 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, November (26.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.4 °C (74 °F), in December. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sungai Petani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
