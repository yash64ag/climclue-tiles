How May compares in Sungai Petani

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Sungai Petani. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. June is about as warm as May on average.

Sungai Petani in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sungai Petani in May?
In May, Sungai Petani averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sungai Petani?
No month in Sungai Petani is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sungai Petani in May?
219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (357 mm), the driest January (65 mm).
Does it snow in Sungai Petani in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sungai Petani get in May?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sungai Petani in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
