How January compares in Sungai Petani

January is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Sungai Petani. Compared with December: 0.7 °C warmer, 63 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Sungai Petani in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sungai Petani in January?
In January, Sungai Petani averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sungai Petani?
No month in Sungai Petani is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sungai Petani in January?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (357 mm), the driest January (65 mm).
Does it snow in Sungai Petani in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sungai Petani get in January?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sungai Petani in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
