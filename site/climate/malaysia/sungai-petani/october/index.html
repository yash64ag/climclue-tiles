Sungai Petani in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sungai Petani in October?
In October, Sungai Petani averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 353 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sungai Petani?
No month in Sungai Petani is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sungai Petani in October?
353 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (353 mm), the driest January (67 mm).
Does it snow in Sungai Petani in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sungai Petani get in October?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sungai Petani in October?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Sungai Petani in October?
