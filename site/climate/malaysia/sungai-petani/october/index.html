Sungai Petani in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sungai Petani in October?
In October, Sungai Petani averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 357 mm (14.0 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sungai Petani?
No month in Sungai Petani is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sungai Petani in October?
