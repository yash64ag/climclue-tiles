How December compares in Sungai Petani

December is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sungai Petani. Compared with November: about as warm, 129 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. January is 0.7 °C warmer than December on average.

Sungai Petani in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sungai Petani in December?
In December, Sungai Petani averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 128 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sungai Petani?
No month in Sungai Petani is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sungai Petani in December?
128 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (357 mm), the driest January (65 mm).
Does it snow in Sungai Petani in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sungai Petani get in December?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sungai Petani in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
