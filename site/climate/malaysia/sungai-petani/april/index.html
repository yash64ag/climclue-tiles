Sungai Petani in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sungai Petani in April?
In April, Sungai Petani averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sungai Petani?
No month in Sungai Petani is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sungai Petani in April?
229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (353 mm), the driest January (67 mm).
Does it snow in Sungai Petani in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sungai Petani get in April?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sungai Petani in April?
About 30.2 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
