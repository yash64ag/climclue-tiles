Kuala Nerus sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Terengganu · Malaysia

The sea off Kuala Nerus averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 27.4 °C (81 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Kuala Nerus sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kuala Nerus?
In May, at about 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kuala Nerus?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.4 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kuala Nerus in January?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Kuala Nerus?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
