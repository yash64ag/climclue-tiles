How October compares in Malacca

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Malacca. Compared with September: about as warm, 43 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. November is 0.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Malacca in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malacca in October?
In October, Malacca averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Malacca?
No month in Malacca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malacca in October?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (238 mm), the driest February (90 mm).
Does it snow in Malacca in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malacca get in October?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malacca in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
