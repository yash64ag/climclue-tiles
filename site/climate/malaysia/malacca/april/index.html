How April compares in Malacca

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Malacca. Compared with March: 0.2 °C warmer, 62 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. May is 0.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Malacca in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malacca in April?
In April, Malacca averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Malacca?
No month in Malacca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malacca in April?
229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (238 mm), the driest February (90 mm).
Does it snow in Malacca in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malacca get in April?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malacca in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
