How December compares in Malacca

December is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Malacca. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 58 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Malacca in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malacca in December?
In December, Malacca averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Malacca?
No month in Malacca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malacca in December?
174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (238 mm), the driest February (90 mm).
Does it snow in Malacca in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malacca get in December?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Malacca in December?
About 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Malacca in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
