How November compares in Malacca

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Malacca. Compared with October: 0.2 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 0.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Malacca in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malacca in November?
In November, Malacca averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Malacca?
No month in Malacca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malacca in November?
230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (233 mm), the driest February (91 mm).
Does it snow in Malacca in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malacca get in November?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malacca in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
