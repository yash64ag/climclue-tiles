How March compares in Malacca

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Malacca. Compared with February: 0.3 °C warmer, 77 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. April is 0.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Malacca in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malacca in March?
In March, Malacca averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Malacca?
No month in Malacca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malacca in March?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (238 mm), the driest February (90 mm).
Does it snow in Malacca in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malacca get in March?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Malacca in March?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Malacca in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
