How September compares in Malacca

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Malacca. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

Malacca in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malacca in September?
In September, Malacca averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Malacca?
No month in Malacca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malacca in September?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (238 mm), the driest February (90 mm).
Does it snow in Malacca in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malacca get in September?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malacca in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
