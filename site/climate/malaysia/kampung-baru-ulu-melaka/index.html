Kampung Baru Ulu Melaka at a glance

Among the 352 towns ClimaClue covers in Malaysia, Kampung Baru Ulu Melaka is 47th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,428 hours of sunshine a year) and 229th wettest (2,498 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kuah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kampung Baru Ulu Melaka is about as warm in March and about as warm in October, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.0 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, October (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.0 °C (72 °F), in February. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kampung Baru Ulu Melaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
