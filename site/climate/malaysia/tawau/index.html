Tawau at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Tawau is the driest (2,008 mm a year). Compared with Lahad Datu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Tawau is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 379 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.3 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tawau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tawau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
