Kulim at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Kulim is 14th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,414 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (2,880 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bukit Mertajam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kulim is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in November, with 320 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, November (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kulim, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kulim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
