Kuantan at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Kuantan is the 4th wettest (3,406 mm a year). Compared with Maran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Kuantan is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 914 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.4 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 24 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kuantan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
