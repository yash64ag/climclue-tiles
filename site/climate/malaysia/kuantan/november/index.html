How November compares in Kuantan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kuantan. Compared with October: 0.6 °C cooler, 175 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. December is 0.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Kuantan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in November?
In November, Kuantan averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 436 mm (17.2 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in November?
436 mm (17.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (776 mm), the driest July (140 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in November?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kuantan in November?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kuantan in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
