How October compares in Kuantan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kuantan. Compared with September: 0.2 °C cooler, 82 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 0.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Kuantan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in October?
In October, Kuantan averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in October?
261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (776 mm), the driest July (140 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in October?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kuantan in October?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kuantan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
