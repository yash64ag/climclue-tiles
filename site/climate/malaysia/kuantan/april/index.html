How April compares in Kuantan

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kuantan. Compared with March: 0.7 °C warmer, 45 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Kuantan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in April?
In April, Kuantan averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in April?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (776 mm), the driest July (140 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in April?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kuantan in April?
About 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kuantan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
