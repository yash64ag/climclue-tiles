How December compares in Kuantan

December is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Kuantan. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 340 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Kuantan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in December?
In December, Kuantan averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 776 mm (30.6 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in December?
776 mm (30.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (776 mm), the driest July (140 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in December?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kuantan in December?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kuantan in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
