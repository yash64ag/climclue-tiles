How July compares in Kuantan

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kuantan. Compared with June: 0.5 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Kuantan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in July?
In July, Kuantan averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in July?
148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (847 mm), the driest July (148 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in July?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuantan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
