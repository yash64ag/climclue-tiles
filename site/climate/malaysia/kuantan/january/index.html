How January compares in Kuantan

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kuantan. Compared with December: 0.1 °C cooler, 456 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Kuantan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in January?
In January, Kuantan averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 391 mm (15.4 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in January?
391 mm (15.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (847 mm), the driest July (148 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in January?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuantan in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
