How August compares in Kuantan

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kuantan. Compared with July: about as warm, 42 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Kuantan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuantan in August?
In August, Kuantan averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kuantan?
No month in Kuantan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuantan in August?
190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (847 mm), the driest July (148 mm).
Does it snow in Kuantan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuantan get in August?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuantan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
