Kuala Kubu Baharu at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Kuala Kubu Baharu is 75th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,200 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (2,989 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rawang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Kuala Kubu Baharu is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 256 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, December (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 63 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kuala Kubu Baharu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kuala Kubu Baharu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
