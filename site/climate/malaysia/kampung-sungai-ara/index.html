Kampung Sungai Ara at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Kampung Sungai Ara is 32nd warmest (26.8 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,407 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (2,835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kampung Sungai Glugur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kampung Sungai Ara is 0.7 °C cooler in June and 0.7 °C cooler in November, with 87 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.4 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, November (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.0 °C (75 °F), in December. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kampung Sungai Ara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kampung Sungai Ara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
