Bandar Utama at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Bandar Utama is 50th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 2,214 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (2,938 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kota Damansara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Bandar Utama is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.1 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, December (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bandar Utama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bandar Utama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
