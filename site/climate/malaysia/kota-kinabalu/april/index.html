How April compares in Kota Kinabalu

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kota Kinabalu. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Kota Kinabalu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kinabalu in April?
In April, Kota Kinabalu averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kota Kinabalu?
No month in Kota Kinabalu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kinabalu in April?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (319 mm), the driest February (91 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kinabalu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kinabalu get in April?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Kinabalu in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
