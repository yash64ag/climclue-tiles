How March compares in Kota Kinabalu

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kota Kinabalu. Compared with February: 0.6 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 0.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Kota Kinabalu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kinabalu in March?
In March, Kota Kinabalu averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kota Kinabalu?
No month in Kota Kinabalu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kinabalu in March?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (319 mm), the driest February (91 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kinabalu in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kinabalu get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Kinabalu in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
