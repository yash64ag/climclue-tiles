Kota Kinabalu weather in January

Average January weather in Kota Kinabalu, Malaysia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Kota Kinabalu averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) days and 22.9 °C (73 °F) nights, with 139 mm (5.5 in) of rain over the month and about 185 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Kota Kinabalu

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kota Kinabalu. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Kota Kinabalu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kinabalu in January?
In January, Kota Kinabalu averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kota Kinabalu?
No month in Kota Kinabalu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kinabalu in January?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (309 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kinabalu in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kinabalu get in January?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kota Kinabalu in January?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (30.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kota Kinabalu in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
