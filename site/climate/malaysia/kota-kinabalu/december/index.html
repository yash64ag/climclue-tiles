How December compares in Kota Kinabalu

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kota Kinabalu. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 62 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Kota Kinabalu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kinabalu in December?
In December, Kota Kinabalu averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 194 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kota Kinabalu?
No month in Kota Kinabalu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kinabalu in December?
194 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (309 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kinabalu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kinabalu get in December?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Kinabalu in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
