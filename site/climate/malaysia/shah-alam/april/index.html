How April compares in Shah Alam

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shah Alam. Compared with March: 0.2 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. May is 0.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Shah Alam in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shah Alam in April?
In April, Shah Alam averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Shah Alam?
No month in Shah Alam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shah Alam in April?
301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (356 mm), the driest June (143 mm).
Does it snow in Shah Alam in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shah Alam get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shah Alam in April?
About 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shah Alam in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
