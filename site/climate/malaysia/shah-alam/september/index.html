How September compares in Shah Alam

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shah Alam. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. October is 0.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Shah Alam in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shah Alam in September?
In September, Shah Alam averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 223 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shah Alam?
No month in Shah Alam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shah Alam in September?
223 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (356 mm), the driest June (143 mm).
Does it snow in Shah Alam in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shah Alam get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shah Alam in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
