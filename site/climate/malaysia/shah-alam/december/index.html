How December compares in Shah Alam

December is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shah Alam. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 69 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Shah Alam in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shah Alam in December?
In December, Shah Alam averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shah Alam?
No month in Shah Alam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shah Alam in December?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (356 mm), the driest June (143 mm).
Does it snow in Shah Alam in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shah Alam get in December?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shah Alam in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
