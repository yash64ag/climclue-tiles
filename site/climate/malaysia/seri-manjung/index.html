Seri Manjung at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Seri Manjung is the 4th driest (2,104 mm a year). Compared with Sitiawan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Seri Manjung is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.7 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Seri Manjung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
