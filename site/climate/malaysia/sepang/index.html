Sepang at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Sepang is 20th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,253 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (2,545 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Port Dickson, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Sepang is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sepang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sepang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
