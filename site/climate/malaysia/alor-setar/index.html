Alor Setar at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Alor Setar is the sunniest (about 2,444 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sungai Petani, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Alor Setar is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 190 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (28.4 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, December (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Alor Setar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Alor Setar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
