Kampung Sungai Glugur at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Kampung Sungai Glugur is the 5th warmest (27.5 °C year average). Compared with Paya Terubong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Kampung Sungai Glugur is 0.7 °C warmer in June and 0.7 °C warmer in November, with 87 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.1 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, November (26.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kampung Sungai Glugur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
