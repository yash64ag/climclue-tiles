How January compares in Kuala Lumpur

January is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kuala Lumpur. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 75 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Kuala Lumpur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuala Lumpur in January?
In January, Kuala Lumpur averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 194 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kuala Lumpur?
No month in Kuala Lumpur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuala Lumpur in January?
194 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Kuala Lumpur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuala Lumpur get in January?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuala Lumpur in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
