How October compares in Kuala Lumpur

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kuala Lumpur. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 63 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 0.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Kuala Lumpur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuala Lumpur in October?
In October, Kuala Lumpur averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kuala Lumpur?
No month in Kuala Lumpur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuala Lumpur in October?
304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Kuala Lumpur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuala Lumpur get in October?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuala Lumpur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
