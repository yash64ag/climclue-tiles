How August compares in Kuala Lumpur

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kuala Lumpur. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Kuala Lumpur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuala Lumpur in August?
In August, Kuala Lumpur averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kuala Lumpur?
No month in Kuala Lumpur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuala Lumpur in August?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Kuala Lumpur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuala Lumpur get in August?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuala Lumpur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
