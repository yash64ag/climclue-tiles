How May compares in Kuala Lumpur

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kuala Lumpur. Compared with April: 0.2 °C warmer, 84 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. June is about as warm as May on average.

Kuala Lumpur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kuala Lumpur in May?
In May, Kuala Lumpur averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kuala Lumpur?
No month in Kuala Lumpur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kuala Lumpur in May?
244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Kuala Lumpur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kuala Lumpur get in May?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kuala Lumpur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
