Pelentong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pelentong in April?
In April, Pelentong averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 230 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pelentong?
No month in Pelentong is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pelentong in April?
230 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (345 mm), the driest February (152 mm).
Does it snow in Pelentong in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pelentong get in April?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pelentong in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
