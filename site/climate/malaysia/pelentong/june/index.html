Pelentong weather in June

Average June weather in Pelentong, Malaysia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Pelentong averages 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 161 mm (6.3 in) of rain over the month and about 185 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Pelentong

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Pelentong. Compared with May: 0.1 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Pelentong in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pelentong in June?
In June, Pelentong averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Pelentong?
No month in Pelentong is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pelentong in June?

Does it snow in Pelentong in June?
