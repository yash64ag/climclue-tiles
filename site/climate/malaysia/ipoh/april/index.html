How April compares in Ipoh

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ipoh. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Ipoh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ipoh in April?
In April, Ipoh averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 273 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ipoh?
No month in Ipoh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ipoh in April?
273 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (292 mm), the driest June (120 mm).
Does it snow in Ipoh in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ipoh get in April?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ipoh in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
