How December compares in Ipoh

December is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ipoh. Compared with November: about as warm, 57 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Ipoh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ipoh in December?
In December, Ipoh averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ipoh?
No month in Ipoh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ipoh in December?
235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (292 mm), the driest June (120 mm).
Does it snow in Ipoh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ipoh get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ipoh in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
