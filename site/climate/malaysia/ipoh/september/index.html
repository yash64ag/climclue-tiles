How September compares in Ipoh

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ipoh. Compared with August: 0.5 °C cooler, 61 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. October is 0.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Ipoh in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ipoh in September?
In September, Ipoh averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ipoh?
No month in Ipoh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ipoh in September?
215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (292 mm), the driest June (120 mm).
Does it snow in Ipoh in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ipoh get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ipoh in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
