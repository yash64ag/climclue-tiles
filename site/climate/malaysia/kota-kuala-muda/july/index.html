Kota Kuala Muda in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kuala Muda in July?
In July, Kota Kuala Muda averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kota Kuala Muda?
No month in Kota Kuala Muda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kuala Muda in July?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (345 mm), the driest January (70 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kuala Muda in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kuala Muda get in July?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Kuala Muda in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
