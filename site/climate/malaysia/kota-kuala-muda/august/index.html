How August compares in Kota Kuala Muda

Kota Kuala Muda in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kuala Muda in August?
In August, Kota Kuala Muda averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kota Kuala Muda?
No month in Kota Kuala Muda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kuala Muda in August?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (351 mm), the driest January (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kuala Muda in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kuala Muda get in August?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kota Kuala Muda in August?
About 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kota Kuala Muda in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
