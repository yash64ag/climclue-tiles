Kota Kuala Muda in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kuala Muda in March?
In March, Kota Kuala Muda averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kota Kuala Muda?
No month in Kota Kuala Muda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kuala Muda in March?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (345 mm), the driest January (70 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kuala Muda in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kuala Muda get in March?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Kuala Muda in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
