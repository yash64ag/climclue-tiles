How November compares in Kota Kuala Muda

November is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kota Kuala Muda. Compared with October: about as warm, 95 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. December is 0.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Kota Kuala Muda in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Kuala Muda in November?
In November, Kota Kuala Muda averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 250 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kota Kuala Muda?
No month in Kota Kuala Muda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Kuala Muda in November?
250 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (345 mm), the driest January (70 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Kuala Muda in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Kuala Muda get in November?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Kuala Muda in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
