Pasir Gudang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pasir Gudang in September?
In September, Pasir Gudang averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pasir Gudang?
No month in Pasir Gudang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pasir Gudang in September?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (327 mm), the driest February (137 mm).
Does it snow in Pasir Gudang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pasir Gudang get in September?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pasir Gudang in September?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pasir Gudang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
