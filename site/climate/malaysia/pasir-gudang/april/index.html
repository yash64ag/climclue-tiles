Pasir Gudang weather in April

Average April weather in Pasir Gudang, Malaysia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Pasir Gudang averages 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 23.5 °C (74 °F) nights, with 206 mm (8.1 in) of rain over the month and about 181 hours of sunshine.
