How November compares in Pasir Gudang

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Pasir Gudang. Compared with October: 0.4 °C cooler, 73 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. December is 0.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Pasir Gudang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pasir Gudang in November?
In November, Pasir Gudang averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Pasir Gudang?
No month in Pasir Gudang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pasir Gudang in November?
270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (327 mm), the driest February (137 mm).
Does it snow in Pasir Gudang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pasir Gudang get in November?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pasir Gudang in November?
About 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pasir Gudang in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
