Kota Bharu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Bharu in December?
In December, Kota Bharu averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 664 mm (26.1 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kota Bharu?
No month in Kota Bharu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Bharu in December?

Does it snow in Kota Bharu in December?
