How April compares in Kota Bharu

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kota Bharu. Compared with March: 1.0 °C warmer, 39 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Kota Bharu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Bharu in April?
In April, Kota Bharu averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kota Bharu?
No month in Kota Bharu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Bharu in April?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (664 mm), the driest February (80 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Bharu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Bharu get in April?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Bharu in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
