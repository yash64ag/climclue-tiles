How February compares in Kota Bharu

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kota Bharu. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 67 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. March is 0.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Kota Bharu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Bharu in February?
In February, Kota Bharu averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kota Bharu?
No month in Kota Bharu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Bharu in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (664 mm), the driest February (79 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Bharu in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Bharu get in February?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Bharu in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
