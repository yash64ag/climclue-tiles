How March compares in Kota Bharu

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kota Bharu. Compared with February: 0.8 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. April is 1.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Kota Bharu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Bharu in March?
In March, Kota Bharu averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kota Bharu?
No month in Kota Bharu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Bharu in March?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (664 mm), the driest February (79 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Bharu in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Bharu get in March?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Bharu in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
