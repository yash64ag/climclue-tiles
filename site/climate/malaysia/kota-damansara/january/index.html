How January compares in Kota Damansara

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kota Damansara. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 73 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Kota Damansara in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Damansara in January?
In January, Kota Damansara averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kota Damansara?
No month in Kota Damansara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Damansara in January?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (346 mm), the driest June (142 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Damansara in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Damansara get in January?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Damansara in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
