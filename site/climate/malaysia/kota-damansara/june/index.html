How June compares in Kota Damansara

June is the warmest month, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kota Damansara. Compared with May: 0.1 °C warmer, 86 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Kota Damansara in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Damansara in June?
In June, Kota Damansara averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kota Damansara?
No month in Kota Damansara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Damansara in June?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (354 mm), the driest June (143 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Damansara in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Damansara get in June?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Damansara in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
