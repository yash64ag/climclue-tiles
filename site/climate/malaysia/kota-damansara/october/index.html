Kota Damansara in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Damansara in October?
In October, Kota Damansara averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 290 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kota Damansara?
No month in Kota Damansara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Damansara in October?
290 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (354 mm), the driest June (143 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Damansara in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Damansara get in October?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Damansara in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
