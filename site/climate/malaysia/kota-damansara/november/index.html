How November compares in Kota Damansara

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Kota Damansara. Compared with October: 0.5 °C cooler, 59 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Kota Damansara in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Damansara in November?
In November, Kota Damansara averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 346 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kota Damansara?
No month in Kota Damansara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

Does it snow in Kota Damansara in November?

About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
