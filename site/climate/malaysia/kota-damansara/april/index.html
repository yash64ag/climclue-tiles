Kota Damansara in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota Damansara in April?
In April, Kota Damansara averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 312 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kota Damansara?
No month in Kota Damansara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota Damansara in April?
312 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (346 mm), the driest June (142 mm).
Does it snow in Kota Damansara in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota Damansara get in April?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota Damansara in April?
