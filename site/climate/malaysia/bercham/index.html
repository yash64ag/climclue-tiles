Bercham at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Bercham is 66th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,302 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (2,756 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ipoh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Bercham is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in November, with 198 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, November (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bercham, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bercham has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
