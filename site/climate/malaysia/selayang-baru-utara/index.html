Selayang Baru Utara at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Selayang Baru Utara is 76th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,206 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (2,951 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taman Petaling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Selayang Baru Utara is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.6 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, December (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Selayang Baru Utara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Selayang Baru Utara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
