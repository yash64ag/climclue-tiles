Selayang Baru Utara in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Selayang Baru Utara in December?
In December, Selayang Baru Utara averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 258 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Selayang Baru Utara?
No month in Selayang Baru Utara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
