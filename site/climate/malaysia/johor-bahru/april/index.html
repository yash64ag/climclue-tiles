Johor Bahru weather in April

Average April weather in Johor Bahru, Malaysia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Johor Bahru averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 23.4 °C (74 °F) nights, with 222 mm (8.7 in) of rain over the month and about 183 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Johor Bahru

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Johor Bahru. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 0.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Johor Bahru in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Johor Bahru in April?
In April, Johor Bahru averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Johor Bahru?
No month in Johor Bahru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Johor Bahru in April?
222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (307 mm), the driest February (141 mm).
Does it snow in Johor Bahru in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Johor Bahru get in April?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Johor Bahru in April?
About 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (30.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Johor Bahru in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
