How December compares in Johor Bahru

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Johor Bahru. Compared with November: 0.5 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Johor Bahru in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Johor Bahru in December?
In December, Johor Bahru averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 308 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Johor Bahru?
No month in Johor Bahru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Johor Bahru in December?
308 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (308 mm), the driest February (141 mm).
Does it snow in Johor Bahru in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Johor Bahru get in December?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Johor Bahru in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
