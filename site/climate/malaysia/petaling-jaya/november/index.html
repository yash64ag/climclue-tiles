How November compares in Petaling Jaya

November is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Petaling Jaya. Compared with October: 0.4 °C cooler, 50 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. December is 0.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Petaling Jaya in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Petaling Jaya in November?
In November, Petaling Jaya averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 353 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Petaling Jaya?
No month in Petaling Jaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Petaling Jaya in November?
353 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Petaling Jaya in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Petaling Jaya get in November?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Petaling Jaya in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
