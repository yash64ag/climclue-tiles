How December compares in Petaling Jaya

December is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Petaling Jaya. Compared with November: 0.2 °C cooler, 77 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Petaling Jaya in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Petaling Jaya in December?
In December, Petaling Jaya averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Petaling Jaya?
No month in Petaling Jaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Petaling Jaya in December?
284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (361 mm), the driest June (150 mm).
Does it snow in Petaling Jaya in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Petaling Jaya get in December?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Petaling Jaya in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
