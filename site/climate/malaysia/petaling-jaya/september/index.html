How September compares in Petaling Jaya

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Petaling Jaya. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 58 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Petaling Jaya in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Petaling Jaya in September?
In September, Petaling Jaya averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Petaling Jaya?
No month in Petaling Jaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Petaling Jaya in September?
241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Petaling Jaya in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Petaling Jaya get in September?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Petaling Jaya in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
