Petaling Jaya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Petaling Jaya in February?
In February, Petaling Jaya averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Petaling Jaya?
No month in Petaling Jaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Petaling Jaya in February?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (353 mm), the driest June (154 mm).
Does it snow in Petaling Jaya in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Petaling Jaya get in February?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Petaling Jaya in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
