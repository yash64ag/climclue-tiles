Kampung Baru Subang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kampung Baru Subang in December?
In December, Kampung Baru Subang averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 281 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kampung Baru Subang?
No month in Kampung Baru Subang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kampung Baru Subang in December?
281 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (354 mm), the driest June (143 mm).
Does it snow in Kampung Baru Subang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kampung Baru Subang get in December?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kampung Baru Subang in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
