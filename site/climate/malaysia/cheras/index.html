Cheras at a glance

Among the 82 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Malaysia, Cheras is 68th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 2,202 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (2,848 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kuala Lumpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Cheras is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 90 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.9 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, December (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cheras, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cheras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
