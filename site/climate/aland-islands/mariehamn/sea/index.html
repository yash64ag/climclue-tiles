Mariehamn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Aland Islands

The sea off Mariehamn averages 16.5 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.1 °C (32 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Mariehamn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mariehamn?
In August, at about 16.5 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mariehamn?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -0.1 °C (32 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mariehamn in March?
About -0.1 °C (32 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mariehamn?
About 7.1 °C (45 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mariehamn nearby?
In August: Naantali (17.2 °C, 122 km), Raisio (17.2 °C, 130 km) and Väståboland (17.2 °C, 133 km).
