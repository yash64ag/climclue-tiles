Tamuning-Tumon-Harmon Village at a glance

Among the 4 towns ClimaClue covers in Guam, Tamuning-Tumon-Harmon Village is the warmest (26.8 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,040 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (2,375 mm a year). Compared with Mangilao Village, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Tamuning-Tumon-Harmon Village is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.3 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, February (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in March. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tamuning-Tumon-Harmon Village has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
