Catacamas at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, Catacamas is the 2nd driest (1,284 mm a year). Compared with Tocoa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Catacamas is about as warm in May and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 804 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Catacamas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Catacamas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
