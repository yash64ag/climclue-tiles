Why Catacamas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Catacamas climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Catacamas?
May, with an average of 26.9 °C (80 °F), day highs around 32.2 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 21.6 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Catacamas?
January, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.0 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 18.5 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Catacamas?
June, with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,284 mm (50.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Catacamas?
March, with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Catacamas?
May, with about 264 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Catacamas?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Catacamas?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Catacamas have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Catacamas’s GeoNames location (14.851°N, 85.894°W; ClimaClue area sq-691-941), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 140,548 (GeoNames).
