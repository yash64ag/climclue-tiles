Yorito at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Honduras, Yorito is 50th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,740 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (1,654 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sulaco, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Yorito is 3.0 °C cooler in May and 3.0 °C cooler in January, with 508 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.1 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, January (20.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.4 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 92 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yorito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yorito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 92 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yorito itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.067°N, 87.283°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-689-927), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
