Cofradía at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Honduras, Cofradía is 24th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,433 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (1,714 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro Sula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Cofradía is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 426 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.5 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cofradía, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cofradía has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C. The 10 km area around Cofradía, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.2 °C a year, 2.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cofradía itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.400°N, 88.150°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-685-918), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
