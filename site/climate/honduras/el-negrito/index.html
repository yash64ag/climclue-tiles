El Negrito at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Honduras, El Negrito is 32nd warmest (24.6 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,595 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (1,785 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Progreso, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), El Negrito is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 47 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.8 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Negrito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Negrito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C. The 10 km area around El Negrito, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.5 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for El Negrito itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.317°N, 87.700°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-686-923), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
