San Pedro Sula at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, San Pedro Sula is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,347 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (2,531 mm a year). Compared with Choloma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), San Pedro Sula is 2.0 °C cooler in May and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 233 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.9 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Pedro Sula, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit San Pedro Sula

For mild weather, the best months to visit San Pedro Sula are March and April.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March and April.

Why San Pedro Sula has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
