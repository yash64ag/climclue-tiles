How March compares in San Pedro Sula

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in San Pedro Sula. Compared with February: 1.2 °C warmer, 61 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

San Pedro Sula in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Pedro Sula in March?
In March, San Pedro Sula averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit San Pedro Sula?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of San Pedro Sula’s mild months (March and April), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Pedro Sula in March?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (302 mm), the driest April (66 mm).
Does it snow in San Pedro Sula in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Pedro Sula get in March?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Pedro Sula in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
