How November compares in San Pedro Sula

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in San Pedro Sula. Compared with October: 1.4 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. December is 0.9 °C cooler than November on average.

San Pedro Sula in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Pedro Sula in November?
In November, San Pedro Sula averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 288 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit San Pedro Sula?
November is not one of San Pedro Sula’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Pedro Sula in November?
288 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (302 mm), the driest April (66 mm).
Does it snow in San Pedro Sula in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Pedro Sula get in November?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Pedro Sula in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
