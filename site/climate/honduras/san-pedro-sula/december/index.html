How December compares in San Pedro Sula

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in San Pedro Sula. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

San Pedro Sula in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Pedro Sula in December?
In December, San Pedro Sula averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Pedro Sula?
No month in San Pedro Sula is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Pedro Sula in December?
211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (246 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in San Pedro Sula in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Pedro Sula get in December?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Pedro Sula in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
