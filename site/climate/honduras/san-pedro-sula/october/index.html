How October compares in San Pedro Sula

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in San Pedro Sula. Compared with September: 1.1 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. November is 1.4 °C cooler than October on average.

San Pedro Sula in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Pedro Sula in October?
In October, San Pedro Sula averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 302 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit San Pedro Sula?
October is not one of San Pedro Sula’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Pedro Sula in October?
302 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (302 mm), the driest April (66 mm).
Does it snow in San Pedro Sula in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Pedro Sula get in October?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Pedro Sula in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
