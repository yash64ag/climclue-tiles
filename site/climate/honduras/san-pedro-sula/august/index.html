San Pedro Sula weather in August

Average August weather in San Pedro Sula, Honduras: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, San Pedro Sula averages 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 22.1 °C (72 °F) nights, with 228 mm (9.0 in) of rain over the month and about 202 hours of sunshine.

San Pedro Sula in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Pedro Sula in August?
In August, San Pedro Sula averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit San Pedro Sula?
August is not one of San Pedro Sula’s mild months; those are March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Pedro Sula in August?
228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (246 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in San Pedro Sula in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Pedro Sula get in August?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Pedro Sula in August?
