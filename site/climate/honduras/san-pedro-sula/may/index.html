How May compares in San Pedro Sula

May is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in San Pedro Sula. Compared with April: 0.9 °C warmer, 64 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is about as warm as May on average.

San Pedro Sula in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Pedro Sula in May?
In May, San Pedro Sula averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit San Pedro Sula?
May is not one of San Pedro Sula’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Pedro Sula in May?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (302 mm), the driest April (66 mm).
Does it snow in San Pedro Sula in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Pedro Sula get in May?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Pedro Sula in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
