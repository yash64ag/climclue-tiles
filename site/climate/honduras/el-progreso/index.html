El Progreso at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, El Progreso is 8th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 2,583 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (2,154 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro Sula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), El Progreso is 1.2 °C warmer in May and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 377 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.1 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, January (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Progreso, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Progreso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
