Puerto Cortez at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, Puerto Cortez is the warmest (26.0 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (2,377 mm a year). Compared with Choloma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Puerto Cortez is 0.7 °C warmer in June and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 79 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.6 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.0 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puerto Cortez, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puerto Cortez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
