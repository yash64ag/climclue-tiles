Dulce Nombre de Culmí at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Honduras, Dulce Nombre de Culmí is 45th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,501 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,776 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Catacamas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Dulce Nombre de Culmí is 1.4 °C cooler in May and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 419 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.3 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dulce Nombre de Culmí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dulce Nombre de Culmí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. The 10 km area around Dulce Nombre de Culmí, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.4 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
