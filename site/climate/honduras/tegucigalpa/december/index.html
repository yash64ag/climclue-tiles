How December compares in Tegucigalpa

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tegucigalpa. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Tegucigalpa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in December?
In December, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in December?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in December?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
