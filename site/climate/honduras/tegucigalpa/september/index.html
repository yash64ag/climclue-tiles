How September compares in Tegucigalpa

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tegucigalpa. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 42 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. October is 1.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Tegucigalpa in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in September?
In September, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
September is not one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in September?
178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (182 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in September?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
