How April compares in Tegucigalpa

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tegucigalpa. Compared with March: 1.3 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Tegucigalpa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in April?
In April, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (182 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
