How March compares in Tegucigalpa

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tegucigalpa. Compared with February: 1.3 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. April is 1.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Tegucigalpa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in March?
In March, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in March?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in March?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
