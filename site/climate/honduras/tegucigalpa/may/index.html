How May compares in Tegucigalpa

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tegucigalpa. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 149 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. June is 1.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Tegucigalpa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in May?
In May, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
May is not one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in May?
203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in May?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
