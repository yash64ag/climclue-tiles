Tegucigalpa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in June?
In June, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 236 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
June is not one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in June?
236 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in June?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
