How February compares in Tegucigalpa

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tegucigalpa. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 1.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Tegucigalpa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in February?
In February, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in February?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in February?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
