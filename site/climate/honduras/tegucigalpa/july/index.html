Tegucigalpa in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in July?
In July, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
July is not one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in July?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in July?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
