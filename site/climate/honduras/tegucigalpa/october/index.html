How October compares in Tegucigalpa

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tegucigalpa. Compared with September: 0.6 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. November is 0.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Tegucigalpa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tegucigalpa in October?
In October, Tegucigalpa averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 192 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tegucigalpa?
October is not one of Tegucigalpa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tegucigalpa in October?
192 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Tegucigalpa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tegucigalpa get in October?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tegucigalpa in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
