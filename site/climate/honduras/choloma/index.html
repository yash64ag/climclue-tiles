Choloma at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, Choloma is the 3rd warmest (25.1 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (2,298 mm a year). Compared with San Pedro Sula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Choloma is 2.1 °C warmer in June and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 233 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (26.9 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Choloma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Choloma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
