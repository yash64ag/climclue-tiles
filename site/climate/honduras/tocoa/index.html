Tocoa at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, Tocoa is 6th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,497 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (2,089 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Olanchito, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Tocoa is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 701 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.9 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tocoa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tocoa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
