Olanchito at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Honduras, Olanchito is the sunniest (about 2,863 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with La Ceiba, Atlántida Department, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Olanchito is 1.1 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 801 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.9 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Olanchito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Olanchito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
