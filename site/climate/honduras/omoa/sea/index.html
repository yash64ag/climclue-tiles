Omoa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Cortés Department · Honduras

The sea off Omoa averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Omoa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Omoa?
In September, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Omoa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Omoa in January?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Omoa?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
