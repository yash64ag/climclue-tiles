Rustavi at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Georgia, Rustavi is the 3rd warmest (13.2 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,040 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (506 mm a year). Compared with Tbilisi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Rustavi is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 76 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 271 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 13 km from Rustavi measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Rustavi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 271 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
