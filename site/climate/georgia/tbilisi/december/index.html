How December compares in Tbilisi

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tbilisi. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Tbilisi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in December?
In December, Tbilisi averages a day high of 6.2 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -3.3 °C (26 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tbilisi?
December is not one of Tbilisi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in December?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (87 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in December?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
