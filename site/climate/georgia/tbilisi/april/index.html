How April compares in Tbilisi

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tbilisi. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. May is 4.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Tbilisi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in April?
In April, Tbilisi averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tbilisi?
April is not one of Tbilisi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in April?
67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (87 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in April?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
