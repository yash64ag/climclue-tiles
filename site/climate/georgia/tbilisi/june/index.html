How June compares in Tbilisi

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tbilisi. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. July is 3.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Tbilisi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in June?
In June, Tbilisi averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tbilisi?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tbilisi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in June?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (87 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in June?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
