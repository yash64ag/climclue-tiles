How September compares in Tbilisi

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tbilisi. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. October is 6.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Tbilisi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in September?
In September, Tbilisi averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tbilisi?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Tbilisi’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in September?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (86 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in September?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
