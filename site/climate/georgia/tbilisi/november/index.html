Tbilisi weather in November

Average November weather in Tbilisi, Georgia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Tbilisi averages 11.6 °C (53 °F) days and 2.8 °C (37 °F) nights, with 37 mm (1.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 104 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Tbilisi

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tbilisi. Compared with October: 6.9 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. December is 5.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Tbilisi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in November?
In November, Tbilisi averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tbilisi?
November is not one of Tbilisi’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in November?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (86 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in November?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
