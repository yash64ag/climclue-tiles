Tbilisi at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Georgia, Tbilisi is the coolest (12.4 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,047 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (582 mm a year). Compared with Rustavi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Tbilisi is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 76 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 266 km from the sea.

Best time to visit Tbilisi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Tbilisi are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.

Why Tbilisi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 266 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
