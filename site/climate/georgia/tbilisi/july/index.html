How July compares in Tbilisi

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tbilisi. Compared with June: 3.5 °C warmer, 39 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Tbilisi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in July?
In July, Tbilisi averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tbilisi?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Tbilisi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in July?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (87 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in July?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
