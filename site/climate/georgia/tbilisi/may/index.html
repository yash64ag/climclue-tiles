How May compares in Tbilisi

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tbilisi. Compared with April: 4.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. June is 4.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Tbilisi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in May?
In May, Tbilisi averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tbilisi?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Tbilisi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in May?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (87 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in May?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
