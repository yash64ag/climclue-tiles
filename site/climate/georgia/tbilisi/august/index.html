How August compares in Tbilisi

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tbilisi. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Tbilisi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tbilisi in August?
In August, Tbilisi averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tbilisi?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Tbilisi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tbilisi in August?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (87 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tbilisi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tbilisi get in August?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tbilisi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
