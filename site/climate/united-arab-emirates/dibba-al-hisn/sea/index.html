Dibba Al-Hisn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fujairah · United Arab Emirates

The sea off Dibba Al-Hisn averages 32.2 °C (90 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 22.8 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Dibba Al-Hisn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dibba Al-Hisn?
In July, at about 32.2 °C (90 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dibba Al-Hisn?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 22.8 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dibba Al-Hisn in February?
About 22.8 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Dibba Al-Hisn?
