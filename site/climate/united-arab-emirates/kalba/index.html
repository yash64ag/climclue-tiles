Kalbā at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Arab Emirates, Kalbā is the warmest (28.3 °C year average). Compared with Ţarīf Kalbā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Kalbā is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.6 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalbā, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalbā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kalbā itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.051°N, 56.354°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-589-2363), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
