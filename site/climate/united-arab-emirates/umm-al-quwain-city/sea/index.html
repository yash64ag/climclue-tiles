Umm Al Quwain City sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Umm Al Quwain · United Arab Emirates

The sea off Umm Al Quwain City averages 33.3 °C (92 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 22.5 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Umm Al Quwain City sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Umm Al Quwain City?
In August, at about 33.3 °C (92 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Umm Al Quwain City?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 22.5 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Umm Al Quwain City in February?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Umm Al Quwain City?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
