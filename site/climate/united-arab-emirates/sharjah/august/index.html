How August compares in Sharjah

August is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Sharjah. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. September is 2.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Sharjah in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sharjah in August?
In August, Sharjah averages a day high of 41.1 °C (106 °F) and a night low of 29.8 °C (86 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 311 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sharjah?
August is not one of Sharjah’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sharjah in August?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (32 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sharjah in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sharjah get in August?
About 311 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sharjah in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
