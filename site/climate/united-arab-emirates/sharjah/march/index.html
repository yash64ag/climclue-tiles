How March compares in Sharjah

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sharjah. Compared with February: 2.3 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Sharjah in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sharjah in March?
In March, Sharjah averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sharjah?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Sharjah’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sharjah in March?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sharjah in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sharjah get in March?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sharjah in March?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (33.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Sharjah in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
