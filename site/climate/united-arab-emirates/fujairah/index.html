Fujairah at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Fujairah is the 2nd least sunny (about 3,368 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (131 mm a year). Compared with Ras Al Khaimah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Fujairah is 0.3 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.6 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fujairah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fujairah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
