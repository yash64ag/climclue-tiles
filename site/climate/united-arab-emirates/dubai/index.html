Dubai at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Dubai is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,449 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Warīsān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Dubai is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (35.5 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.2 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dubai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Dubai

For mild weather, the best months to visit Dubai are January, February, March, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, November and December.

Why Dubai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
