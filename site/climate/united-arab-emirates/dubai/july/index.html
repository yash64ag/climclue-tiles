How July compares in Dubai

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dubai. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Dubai in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dubai in July?
In July, Dubai averages a day high of 43.2 °C (110 °F) and a night low of 28.5 °C (83 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 319 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Dubai?
July is not one of Dubai’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dubai in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dubai in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dubai get in July?
About 319 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dubai in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
