How March compares in Dubai

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Dubai. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Dubai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dubai in March?
In March, Dubai averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dubai?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Dubai’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dubai in March?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dubai in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dubai get in March?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dubai in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
