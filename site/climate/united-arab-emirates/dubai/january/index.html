How January compares in Dubai

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dubai. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Dubai ranks 8th warmest (average temperature), 5th sunniest and 6th wettest in January.

Dubai in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dubai in January?
In January, Dubai averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dubai?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Dubai’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dubai in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dubai in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dubai get in January?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dubai in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
