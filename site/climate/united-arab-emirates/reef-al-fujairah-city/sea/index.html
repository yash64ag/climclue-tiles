Reef Al Fujairah City sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fujairah · United Arab Emirates

The sea off Reef Al Fujairah City averages 32.4 °C (90 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Reef Al Fujairah City sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Reef Al Fujairah City?
In July, at about 32.4 °C (90 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Reef Al Fujairah City?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Reef Al Fujairah City in February?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Reef Al Fujairah City?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
