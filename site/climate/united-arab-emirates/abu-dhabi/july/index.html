How July compares in Abu Dhabi

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Abu Dhabi. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C warmer than July on average.

Abu Dhabi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abu Dhabi in July?
In July, Abu Dhabi averages a day high of 40.1 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 28.2 °C (83 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 312 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Abu Dhabi?
July is not one of Abu Dhabi’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abu Dhabi in July?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Abu Dhabi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abu Dhabi get in July?
About 312 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abu Dhabi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
