How February compares in Abu Dhabi

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Abu Dhabi. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 2.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Abu Dhabi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abu Dhabi in February?
In February, Abu Dhabi averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Abu Dhabi?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Abu Dhabi’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abu Dhabi in February?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Abu Dhabi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abu Dhabi get in February?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Abu Dhabi in February?
About 20.7 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (34.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Abu Dhabi in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
