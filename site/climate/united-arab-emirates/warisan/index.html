Warīsān at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Warīsān is 7th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 3,445 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (120 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dubai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Warīsān is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.3 °C), averages 16.6 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Warīsān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Warīsān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
