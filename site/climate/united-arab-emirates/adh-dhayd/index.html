Adh Dhayd at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Arab Emirates, Adh Dhayd is the wettest (151 mm a year). Compared with Al Sajaah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Adh Dhayd is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.3 °C), averages 16.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 52 km from the sea.

Why Adh Dhayd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 52 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Adh Dhayd itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.288°N, 55.882°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-587-2358), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
