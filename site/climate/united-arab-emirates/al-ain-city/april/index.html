Al Ain City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Ain City in April?
In April, Al Ain City averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Al Ain City?
April is not one of Al Ain City’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Ain City in April?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (27 mm), the driest November (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Ain City in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Ain City get in April?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Ain City in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
