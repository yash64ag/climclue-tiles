Jebel Ali at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Jebel Ali is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,450 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Dubai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Jebel Ali is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (35.0 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (19.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jebel Ali, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jebel Ali has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
