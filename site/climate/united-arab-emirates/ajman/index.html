Ajman at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Ajman is the coolest (27.4 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 3,410 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (125 mm a year). Compared with Sharjah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Ajman is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (34.5 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ajman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ajman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
