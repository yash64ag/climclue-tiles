Ras Al Khaimah at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Arab Emirates, Ras Al Khaimah is the warmest (28.0 °C year average), the least sunny (about 3,310 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (161 mm a year). Compared with Ajman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Ras Al Khaimah is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.5 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ras Al Khaimah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ras Al Khaimah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
