Ras Al Khaimah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · United Arab Emirates

The sea off Ras Al Khaimah averages 32.6 °C (91 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 22.2 °C (72 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Ras Al Khaimah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ras Al Khaimah?
In August, at about 32.6 °C (91 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ras Al Khaimah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 22.2 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ras Al Khaimah in February?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Ras Al Khaimah?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ras Al Khaimah nearby?
In August: Umm Al Quwain City (33.3 °C, 46 km), Ajman (33.2 °C, 63 km) and Sharjah (33.2 °C, 74 km).
