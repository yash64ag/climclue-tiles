Ţarīf Kalbā weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Ţarīf Kalbā, United Arab Emirates (Fujairah), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Ţarīf Kalbā is warmest in June, with 41.9 °C (107 °F) days and 27.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 26.2 °C (79 °F) days and 14.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around February (33 mm); June is driest (1 mm).

Ţarīf Kalbā at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Arab Emirates, Ţarīf Kalbā is the 2nd warmest (28.2 °C year average). Compared with Kalbā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Ţarīf Kalbā is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Why Ţarīf Kalbā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-589-2363), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
