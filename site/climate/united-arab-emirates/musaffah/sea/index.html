Musaffah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Abu Dhabi · United Arab Emirates

The sea off Musaffah averages 34.0 °C (93 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 20.7 °C (69 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Musaffah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Musaffah?
In August, at about 34.0 °C (93 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Musaffah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 20.7 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in Musaffah in February?
About 20.7 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Musaffah?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
