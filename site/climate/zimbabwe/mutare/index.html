Mutare at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zimbabwe, Mutare is the coolest (18.1 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,592 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (994 mm a year). Compared with Chitungwiza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (199 km away), Mutare is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 235 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.9 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, July (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.9 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 256 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mutare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mutare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 256 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
