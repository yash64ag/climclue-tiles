Kwekwe at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zimbabwe, Kwekwe is the 2nd warmest (20.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,122 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (655 mm a year). Compared with Gweru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Kwekwe is 2.3 °C warmer in November and 2.3 °C warmer in July, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (23.8 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, July (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.7 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 536 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kwekwe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kwekwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 536 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
