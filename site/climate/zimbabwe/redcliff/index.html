Redcliff at a glance

Among the 29 towns ClimaClue covers in Zimbabwe, Redcliff is 13th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,102 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kwekwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Redcliff is 0.3 °C cooler in November and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (23.6 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 543 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Redcliff, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Redcliff has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 543 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Redcliff itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.033°S, 29.783°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1030-2097), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
