How June compares in Bulawayo

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bulawayo. Compared with May: 2.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Bulawayo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in June?
In June, Bulawayo averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bulawayo?
June is not one of Bulawayo’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in June?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in June?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
