How March compares in Bulawayo

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bulawayo. Compared with February: 0.5 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. April is 2.1 °C cooler than March on average.

Bulawayo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in March?
In March, Bulawayo averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bulawayo?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Bulawayo’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in March?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in March?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
