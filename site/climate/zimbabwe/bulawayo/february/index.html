How February compares in Bulawayo

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bulawayo. Compared with January: 0.6 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. March is 0.8 °C cooler than February on average.

Bulawayo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in February?
In February, Bulawayo averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bulawayo?
February is not one of Bulawayo’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in February?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in February?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
