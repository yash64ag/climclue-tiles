How November compares in Bulawayo

November is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bulawayo. Compared with October: 0.3 °C warmer, 58 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. December is 0.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Bulawayo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in November?
In November, Bulawayo averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bulawayo?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Bulawayo’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in November?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in November?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
