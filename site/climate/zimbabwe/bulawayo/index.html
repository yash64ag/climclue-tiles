Bulawayo at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zimbabwe, Bulawayo is the 3rd warmest (19.2 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 3,081 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (597 mm a year). Compared with Gweru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (151 km away), Bulawayo is 0.9 °C warmer in November and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.4 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, July (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 647 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bulawayo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bulawayo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 647 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
