How July compares in Bulawayo

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bulawayo. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. August is 2.9 °C warmer than July on average.

Bulawayo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in July?
In July, Bulawayo averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bulawayo?
July is not one of Bulawayo’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in July?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
