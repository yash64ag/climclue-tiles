How October compares in Bulawayo

October is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bulawayo. Compared with September: 1.8 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. November is 0.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Bulawayo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in October?
In October, Bulawayo averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bulawayo?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Bulawayo’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in October?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
