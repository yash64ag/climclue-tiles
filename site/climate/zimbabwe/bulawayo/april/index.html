How April compares in Bulawayo

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bulawayo. Compared with March: 2.0 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 2.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Bulawayo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bulawayo in April?
In April, Bulawayo averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bulawayo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Bulawayo’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bulawayo in April?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bulawayo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bulawayo get in April?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bulawayo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
