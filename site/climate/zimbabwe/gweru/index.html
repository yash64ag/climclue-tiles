Gweru at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zimbabwe, Gweru is the 2nd coolest (18.4 °C year average). Compared with Kwekwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Gweru is 2.3 °C cooler in November and 2.3 °C cooler in July, with 101 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (21.4 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, July (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 522 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gweru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gweru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 522 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
