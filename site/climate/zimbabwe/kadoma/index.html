Kadoma at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zimbabwe, Kadoma is the warmest (21.2 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,125 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (735 mm a year). Compared with Kwekwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Kadoma is 0.8 °C warmer in October and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 80 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.4 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 545 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kadoma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kadoma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 545 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
