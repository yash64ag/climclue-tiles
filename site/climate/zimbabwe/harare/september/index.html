How September compares in Harare

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Harare. Compared with August: 3.4 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. October is 1.7 °C warmer than September on average.

Harare in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harare in September?
In September, Harare averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Harare?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Harare’s mild months (April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harare in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (194 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Harare in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Harare get in September?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harare in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
