How March compares in Harare

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Harare. Compared with February: 0.3 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C cooler than March on average.

Harare in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harare in March?
In March, Harare averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Harare?
March is not one of Harare’s mild months; those are April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harare in March?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (192 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Harare in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Harare get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harare in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
