How August compares in Harare

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Harare. Compared with July: 2.3 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. September is 3.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Harare in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harare in August?
In August, Harare averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 299 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Harare?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Harare’s mild months (April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harare in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (192 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Harare in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Harare get in August?
About 299 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harare in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
