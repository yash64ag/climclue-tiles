Maldegem sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Flanders · Belgium

The sea off Maldegem averages 18.7 °C (66 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.5 °C (42 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Maldegem sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Maldegem?
In August, at about 18.7 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Maldegem?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 5.5 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Maldegem in February?
About 5.5 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Maldegem?
About 11.6 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
