How June compares in Antwerp

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Antwerp. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Antwerp in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in June?
In June, Antwerp averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Antwerp?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Antwerp’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in June?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in June?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
