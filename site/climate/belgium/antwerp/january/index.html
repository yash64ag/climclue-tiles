How January compares in Antwerp

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Antwerp. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Antwerp in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in January?
In January, Antwerp averages a day high of 6.0 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 46 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Antwerp?
January is not one of Antwerp’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in January?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in January?
Yes, on average: about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in January?
About 46 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
