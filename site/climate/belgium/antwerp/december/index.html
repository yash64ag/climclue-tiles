How December compares in Antwerp

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Antwerp. Compared with November: 3.0 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Antwerp in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in December?
In December, Antwerp averages a day high of 6.6 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 42 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Antwerp?
December is not one of Antwerp’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in December?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in December?
About 42 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
