How May compares in Antwerp

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Antwerp. Compared with April: 3.8 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Antwerp in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in May?
In May, Antwerp averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Antwerp?
May is not one of Antwerp’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in May?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in May?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
