How September compares in Antwerp

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Antwerp. Compared with August: 3.0 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. October is 3.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Antwerp in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in September?
In September, Antwerp averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Antwerp?
September is not one of Antwerp’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in September?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in September?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
