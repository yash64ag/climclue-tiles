Antwerp weather in April

Average April weather in Antwerp, Belgium: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Antwerp averages 14.0 °C (57 °F) days and 5.1 °C (41 °F) nights, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of rain over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Antwerp

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Antwerp. Compared with March: 2.9 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Antwerp in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in April?
In April, Antwerp averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Antwerp?
April is not one of Antwerp’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in April?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in April?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
