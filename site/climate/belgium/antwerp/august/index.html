How August compares in Antwerp

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Antwerp. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 3.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Antwerp in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antwerp in August?
In August, Antwerp averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Antwerp?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Antwerp’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antwerp in August?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest April (49 mm).
Does it snow in Antwerp in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antwerp get in August?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antwerp in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
