How December compares in Brussels

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Brussels. Compared with November: 2.4 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. January is 0.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Brussels in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in December?
In December, Brussels averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 45 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Brussels?
December is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in December?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in December?
Yes, on average: about 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in December?
About 45 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
