How August compares in Brussels

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Brussels. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. September is 3.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Brussels in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in August?
In August, Brussels averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Brussels?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Brussels’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in August?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (81 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in August?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
