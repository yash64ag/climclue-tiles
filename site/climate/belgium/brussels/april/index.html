Brussels weather in April

Average April weather in Brussels, Belgium: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Brussels averages 14.2 °C (58 °F) days and 5.0 °C (41 °F) nights, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of rain over the month and about 157 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Brussels

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with March: 2.8 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. May is 3.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Brussels in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in April?
In April, Brussels averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Brussels?
April is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in April?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
