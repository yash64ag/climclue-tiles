How June compares in Brussels

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with May: 2.6 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Brussels in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in June?
In June, Brussels averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Brussels?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Brussels’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in June?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (81 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in June?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
