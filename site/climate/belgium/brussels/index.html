Brussels at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belgium, Brussels is the 2nd sunniest (about 1,587 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Leuven, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Brussels is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 92 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Brussels

For mild weather, the best months to visit Brussels are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (18.1 °C), August (17.9 °C) and June (15.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): April (52 mm), February (62 mm) and May (69 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): August (204 h), June (200 h) and May (199 h).

Why Brussels has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 92 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Brussels climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Brussels?
July, with an average of 18.1 °C (65 °F), day highs around 22.3 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 13.7 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Brussels?
January, with an average of 3.3 °C (38 °F), day highs around 5.7 °C (42 °F) and night lows around 0.7 °C (33 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Brussels?
December, with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 863 mm (34.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Brussels?
April, with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Brussels?
August, with about 204 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Brussels?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
