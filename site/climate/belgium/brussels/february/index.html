How February compares in Brussels

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. March is 3.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Brussels in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in February?
In February, Brussels averages a day high of 6.6 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Brussels?
February is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in February?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in February?
Yes, on average: about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in February?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
