How March compares in Brussels

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. April is 2.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Brussels in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in March?
In March, Brussels averages a day high of 10.2 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Brussels?
March is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in March?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (81 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in March?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
