How January compares in Brussels

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Brussels in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in January?
In January, Brussels averages a day high of 5.9 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 48 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Brussels?
January is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in January?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.5 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in January?
About 48 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
