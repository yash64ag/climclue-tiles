How October compares in Brussels

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with September: 3.5 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. November is 4.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Brussels in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in October?
In October, Brussels averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Brussels?
October is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in October?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in October?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
