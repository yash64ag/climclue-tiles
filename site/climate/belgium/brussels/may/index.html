How May compares in Brussels

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Brussels. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Brussels in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brussels in May?
In May, Brussels averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Brussels?
May is not one of Brussels’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brussels in May?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (81 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Brussels in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brussels get in May?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brussels in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
