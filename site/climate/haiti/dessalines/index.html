Dessalines at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Haiti, Dessalines is the 3rd warmest (26.8 °C year average). Compared with Saint-Marc, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Dessalines is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 344 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dessalines, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dessalines has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
