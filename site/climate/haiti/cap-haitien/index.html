Why Cap-Haïtien has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Cap-Haïtien climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cap-Haïtien?
August, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 29.4 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 25.7 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cap-Haïtien?
January, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 22.2 °C (72 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cap-Haïtien?
May, with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,535 mm (60.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cap-Haïtien?
February, with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cap-Haïtien?
July, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cap-Haïtien?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cap-Haïtien?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Cap-Haïtien’s GeoNames location (19.759°N, 72.198°W; ClimaClue area sq-642-1078), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 134,815 (GeoNames).
