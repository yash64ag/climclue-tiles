Grand Gosier at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Haiti, Grand Gosier is the driest (725 mm a year). Compared with Fond Parisien, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Grand Gosier is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in February, with 155 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, February (25.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Grand Gosier has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Grand Gosier itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.185°N, 71.918°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-658-1080), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
