Léogâne sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ouest · Haiti

The sea off Léogâne averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.6 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Léogâne sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Léogâne?
In September, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Léogâne?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.6 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Léogâne in February?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Léogâne?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Léogâne nearby?
In September: Saint-Marc (30.0 °C, 67 km).
