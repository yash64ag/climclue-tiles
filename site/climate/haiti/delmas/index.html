Delmas at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Haiti, Delmas is the sunniest (about 2,809 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Port-au-Prince, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Delmas is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 118 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Delmas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Delmas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
