How November compares in Port-au-Prince

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Port-au-Prince. Compared with October: 0.8 °C cooler, 74 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. December is 0.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Port-au-Prince in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port-au-Prince in November?
In November, Port-au-Prince averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Port-au-Prince?
November is not one of Port-au-Prince’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port-au-Prince in November?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (187 mm), the driest January (28 mm).
Does it snow in Port-au-Prince in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port-au-Prince get in November?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Port-au-Prince in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
