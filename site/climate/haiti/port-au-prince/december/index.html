How December compares in Port-au-Prince

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Port-au-Prince. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 68 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Port-au-Prince in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port-au-Prince in December?
In December, Port-au-Prince averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Port-au-Prince?
No month in Port-au-Prince is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port-au-Prince in December?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (165 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Port-au-Prince in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port-au-Prince get in December?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Port-au-Prince in December?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Port-au-Prince in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
