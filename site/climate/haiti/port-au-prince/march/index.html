How March compares in Port-au-Prince

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Port-au-Prince. Compared with February: 0.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Port-au-Prince in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port-au-Prince in March?
In March, Port-au-Prince averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Port-au-Prince?
No month in Port-au-Prince is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port-au-Prince in March?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (165 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Port-au-Prince in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port-au-Prince get in March?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Port-au-Prince in March?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Port-au-Prince in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
