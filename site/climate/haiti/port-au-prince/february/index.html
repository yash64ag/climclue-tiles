How February compares in Port-au-Prince

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Port-au-Prince. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 0.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Port-au-Prince in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port-au-Prince in February?
In February, Port-au-Prince averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Port-au-Prince?
No month in Port-au-Prince is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port-au-Prince in February?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (165 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Port-au-Prince in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port-au-Prince get in February?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Port-au-Prince in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
