Saint-Raphaël at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Haiti, Saint-Raphaël is the 2nd coolest (23.9 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (1,855 mm a year). Compared with Saint-Michel de l'Atalaye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Saint-Raphaël is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 302 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.7 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saint-Raphaël, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Saint-Raphaël has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saint-Raphaël itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.439°N, 72.199°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-645-1078), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
