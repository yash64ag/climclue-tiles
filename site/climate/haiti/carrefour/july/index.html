How July compares in Carrefour

July is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Carrefour. Compared with June: 0.4 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Carrefour in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Carrefour in July?
In July, Carrefour averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Carrefour?
July is not one of Carrefour’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Carrefour in July?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (187 mm), the driest January (28 mm).
Does it snow in Carrefour in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Carrefour get in July?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Carrefour in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
