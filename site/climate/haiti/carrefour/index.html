Carrefour at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Haiti, Carrefour is 17th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,795 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,460 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Port-au-Prince, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Carrefour is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 218 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.0 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.5 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Carrefour, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Carrefour has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Carrefour itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.533°N, 72.400°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-654-1076), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
