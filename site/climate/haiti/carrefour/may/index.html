How May compares in Carrefour

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Carrefour. Compared with April: 0.5 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Carrefour in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Carrefour in May?
In May, Carrefour averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Carrefour?
May is not one of Carrefour’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Carrefour in May?
182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (187 mm), the driest January (28 mm).
Does it snow in Carrefour in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Carrefour get in May?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Carrefour in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
