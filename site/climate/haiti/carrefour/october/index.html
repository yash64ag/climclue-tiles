How October compares in Carrefour

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Carrefour. Compared with September: 0.6 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. November is 0.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Carrefour in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Carrefour in October?
In October, Carrefour averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Carrefour?
No month in Carrefour is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Carrefour in October?
196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (196 mm), the driest January (30 mm).
Does it snow in Carrefour in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Carrefour get in October?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Carrefour in October?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Carrefour in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
