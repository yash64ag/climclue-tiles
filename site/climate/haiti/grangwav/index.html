Grangwav at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Haiti, Grangwav is 7th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,718 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,209 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tigwav, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Grangwav is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Grangwav, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Grangwav has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Grangwav itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.426°N, 72.770°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-655-1072), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
