Les Cayes at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Haiti, Les Cayes is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,662 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (978 mm a year). Compared with Tigwav, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Les Cayes is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 335 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, February (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Les Cayes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Les Cayes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
