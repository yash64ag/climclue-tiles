Cité Soleil at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Haiti, Cité Soleil is the 3rd coolest (25.9 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (1,402 mm a year). Compared with Delmas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Cité Soleil is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 118 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cité Soleil, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cité Soleil has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
