Telfs at a glance

Among the 29 towns ClimaClue covers in Austria, Telfs is the 2nd coolest (8.7 °C year average). Compared with Innsbruck, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Telfs is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 78 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Telfs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C. The 10 km area around Telfs, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 3.3 °C a year, 5.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Telfs itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.307°N, 11.068°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-366-1910), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
