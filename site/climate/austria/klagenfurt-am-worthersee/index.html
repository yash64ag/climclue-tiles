Klagenfurt am Wörthersee at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Austria, Klagenfurt am Wörthersee is the 2nd warmest (9.9 °C year average), the sunniest (about 1,871 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,071 mm a year). Compared with Graz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Klagenfurt am Wörthersee is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 211 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.3 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 124 km from the sea.
