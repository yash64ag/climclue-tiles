Hohenems at a glance

Among the 29 towns ClimaClue covers in Austria, Hohenems is the 2nd wettest (1,656 mm a year). Compared with Dornbirn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Hohenems is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 304 km from the sea.

Why Hohenems has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 304 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-366-1896), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
