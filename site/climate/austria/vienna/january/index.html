How January compares in Vienna

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Vienna in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in January?
In January, Vienna averages a day high of 2.5 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -3.7 °C (25 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 55 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Vienna?
January is not one of Vienna’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in January?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in January?
About 55 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
