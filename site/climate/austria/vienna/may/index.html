How May compares in Vienna

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. June is 2.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Vienna in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in May?
In May, Vienna averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Vienna?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Vienna’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in May?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in May?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
