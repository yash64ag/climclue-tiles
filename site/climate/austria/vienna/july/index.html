How July compares in Vienna

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Vienna. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Vienna in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in July?
In July, Vienna averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Vienna?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Vienna’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in July?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in July?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
