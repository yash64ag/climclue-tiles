How April compares in Vienna

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with March: 5.1 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Vienna in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in April?
In April, Vienna averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Vienna?
April is not one of Vienna’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in April?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
