How February compares in Vienna

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with January: 1.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Vienna in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in February?
In February, Vienna averages a day high of 5.1 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -1.0 °C (30 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Vienna?
February is not one of Vienna’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in February?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in February?
Yes, on average: about 15 cm (5.8 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in February?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
