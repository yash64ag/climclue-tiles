How March compares in Vienna

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with February: 4.2 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 5.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Vienna in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in March?
In March, Vienna averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Vienna?
March is not one of Vienna’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in March?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in March?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
