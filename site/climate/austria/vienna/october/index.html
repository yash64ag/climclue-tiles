Vienna weather in October

Average October weather in Vienna, Austria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Vienna averages 14.5 °C (58 °F) days and 6.5 °C (44 °F) nights, with 38 mm (1.5 in) of rain over the month and about 131 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Vienna

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with September: 5.4 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Vienna in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in October?
In October, Vienna averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Vienna?
October is not one of Vienna’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in October?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
