How August compares in Vienna

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Vienna in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in August?
In August, Vienna averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Vienna?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Vienna’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in August?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in August?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
