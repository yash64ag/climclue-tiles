How September compares in Vienna

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Vienna in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in September?
In September, Vienna averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Vienna?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Vienna’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in September?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in September?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
