How November compares in Vienna

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Vienna. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. December is 4.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Vienna in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vienna in November?
In November, Vienna averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Vienna?
November is not one of Vienna’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vienna in November?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Vienna in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.8 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Vienna get in November?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vienna in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
