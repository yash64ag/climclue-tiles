Chunian in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chunian in November?
In November, Chunian averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chunian?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Chunian’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chunian in November?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Chunian in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chunian get in November?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chunian in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
