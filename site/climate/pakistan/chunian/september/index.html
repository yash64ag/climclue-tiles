Chunian in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chunian in September?
In September, Chunian averages a day high of 35.9 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chunian?
September is not one of Chunian’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chunian in September?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Chunian in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chunian get in September?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chunian in September?
