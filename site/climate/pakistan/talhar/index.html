Talhar at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Talhar is 25th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 3,444 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (225 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tando Bago, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Talhar is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.1 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 153 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Talhar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Talhar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 153 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
