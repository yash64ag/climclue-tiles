How October compares in Arifwala

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Arifwala. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Arifwala in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in October?
In October, Arifwala averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Arifwala?
October is not one of Arifwala’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in October?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in October?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
