How February compares in Arifwala

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Arifwala. Compared with January: 3.2 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. March is 5.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Arifwala in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in February?
In February, Arifwala averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Arifwala?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Arifwala’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in February?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in February?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
