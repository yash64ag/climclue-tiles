How April compares in Arifwala

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Arifwala. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Arifwala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in April?
In April, Arifwala averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Arifwala?
April is not one of Arifwala’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in April?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in April?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
