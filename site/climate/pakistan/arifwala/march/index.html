How March compares in Arifwala

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Arifwala. Compared with February: 5.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Arifwala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in March?
In March, Arifwala averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Arifwala?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Arifwala’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in March?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
