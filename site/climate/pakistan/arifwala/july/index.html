How July compares in Arifwala

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Arifwala. Compared with June: 1.0 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Arifwala in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in July?
In July, Arifwala averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 28.9 °C (84 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Arifwala?
July is not one of Arifwala’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in July?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in July?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
