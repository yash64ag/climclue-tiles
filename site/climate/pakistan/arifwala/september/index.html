How September compares in Arifwala

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Arifwala. Compared with August: 1.6 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Arifwala in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in September?
In September, Arifwala averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Arifwala?
September is not one of Arifwala’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in September?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (81 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in September?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
