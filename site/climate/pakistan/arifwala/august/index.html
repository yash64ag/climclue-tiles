How August compares in Arifwala

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Arifwala. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. September is 1.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Arifwala in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arifwala in August?
In August, Arifwala averages a day high of 37.1 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 28.5 °C (83 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Arifwala?
August is not one of Arifwala’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arifwala in August?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Arifwala in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arifwala get in August?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arifwala in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
