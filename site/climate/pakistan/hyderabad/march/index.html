How March compares in Hyderabad

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with February: 5.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 4.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Hyderabad in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in March?
In March, Hyderabad averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hyderabad?
March is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (74 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in March?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
