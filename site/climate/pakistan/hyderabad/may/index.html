How May compares in Hyderabad

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hyderabad. Compared with April: 2.5 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. June is 0.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Hyderabad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in May?
In May, Hyderabad averages a day high of 40.9 °C (106 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 323 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hyderabad?
May is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in May?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in May?
About 323 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
