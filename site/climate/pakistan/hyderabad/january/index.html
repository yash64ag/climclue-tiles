How January compares in Hyderabad

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with December: 1.1 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 3.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Hyderabad in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in January?
In January, Hyderabad averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hyderabad?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Hyderabad’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (74 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in January?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
