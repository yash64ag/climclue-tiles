Hyderabad weather in June

Average June weather in Hyderabad, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Hyderabad averages 39.4 °C (103 °F) days and 27.2 °C (81 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 281 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Hyderabad

June is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with May: 0.2 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. July is 1.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Hyderabad in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in June?
In June, Hyderabad averages a day high of 39.4 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 27.2 °C (81 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Hyderabad?
June is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in June?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
