How December compares in Hyderabad

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Hyderabad in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in December?
In December, Hyderabad averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hyderabad?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Hyderabad’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in December?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
