How October compares in Hyderabad

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Hyderabad in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in October?
In October, Hyderabad averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hyderabad?
October is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in October?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (74 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in October?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
