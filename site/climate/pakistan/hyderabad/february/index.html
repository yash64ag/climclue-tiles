Hyderabad weather in February

Average February weather in Hyderabad, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Hyderabad averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) days and 12.8 °C (55 °F) nights, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 253 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Hyderabad

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with January: 3.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 5.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Hyderabad in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in February?
In February, Hyderabad averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hyderabad?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Hyderabad’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in February?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in February?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
