Hujra Shah Muqim at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Hujra Shah Muqim is 178th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 276th sunniest (about 2,936 hours of sunshine a year) and 169th wettest (409 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dipalpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Hujra Shah Muqim is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.1 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 942 km from the sea (deep inland).
