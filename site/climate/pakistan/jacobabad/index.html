Jacobabad at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Jacobabad is 12th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 3,209 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (129 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dera Murad Jamali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Jacobabad is 0.7 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (37.1 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 379 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jacobabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jacobabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 379 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
