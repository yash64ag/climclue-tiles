Dhirkot at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Dhirkot is the 4th coolest (15.5 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (1,514 mm a year). Compared with Abbottabad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Dhirkot is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 525 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,185 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dhirkot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dhirkot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,185 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 10.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
