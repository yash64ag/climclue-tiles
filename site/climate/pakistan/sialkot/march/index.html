How March compares in Sialkot

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sialkot. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. April is 5.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Sialkot in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sialkot in March?
In March, Sialkot averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sialkot?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Sialkot’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sialkot in March?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (364 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Sialkot in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sialkot get in March?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sialkot in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
