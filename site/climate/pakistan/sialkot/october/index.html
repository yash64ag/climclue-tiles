October · Punjab · Pakistan

How October compares in Sialkot

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sialkot. Compared with September: 4.4 °C cooler, 89 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Sialkot in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sialkot in October?
In October, Sialkot averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sialkot?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Sialkot’s mild months (February, March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sialkot in October?
22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Sialkot in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sialkot get in October?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sialkot in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
