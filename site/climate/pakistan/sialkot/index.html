Sialkot at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Sialkot is 113th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,963 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (1,170 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sambrial, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Sialkot is 0.5 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.1 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,115 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sialkot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Sialkot

For mild weather, the best months to visit Sialkot are March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (32.1 °C), July (30.4 °C) and May (29.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (10 mm), October (22 mm) and December (28 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (306 h), June (285 h) and October (283 h).

Why Sialkot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,115 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
