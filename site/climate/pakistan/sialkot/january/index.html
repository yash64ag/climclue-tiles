How January compares in Sialkot

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sialkot. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 2.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Sialkot in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sialkot in January?
In January, Sialkot averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sialkot?
January is not one of Sialkot’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sialkot in January?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Sialkot in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sialkot get in January?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sialkot in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
