How November compares in Sialkot

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Sialkot. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Sialkot in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sialkot in November?
In November, Sialkot averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sialkot?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Sialkot’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sialkot in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Sialkot in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sialkot get in November?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sialkot in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
