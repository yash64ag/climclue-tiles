Burewala at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Burewala is 53rd warmest (25.7 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 3,005 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (257 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vihari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Burewala is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.8 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 810 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Burewala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Burewala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 810 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
