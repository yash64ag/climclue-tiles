Jalalpur Jattan at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Jalalpur Jattan is 111th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 2,970 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (1,177 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gujrat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Jalalpur Jattan is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.6 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,110 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jalalpur Jattan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jalalpur Jattan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,110 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
