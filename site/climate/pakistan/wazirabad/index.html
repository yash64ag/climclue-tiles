Wazirabad at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Wazirabad is 106th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 78th sunniest (about 2,975 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (1,067 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gujrat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Wazirabad is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.0 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,088 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wazirabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Wazirabad

For mild weather, the best months to visit Wazirabad are March, October and November.

Why Wazirabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,088 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
