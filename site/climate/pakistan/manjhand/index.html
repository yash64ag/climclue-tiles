Manjhand at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Manjhand is the 4th warmest (27.7 °C year average). Compared with Shahdadpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Manjhand is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.0 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 184 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Manjhand, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Manjhand has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 184 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
