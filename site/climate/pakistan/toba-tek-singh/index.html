Toba Tek Singh at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Toba Tek Singh is 65th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,999 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (322 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gojra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Toba Tek Singh is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.5 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 856 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Toba Tek Singh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 856 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
