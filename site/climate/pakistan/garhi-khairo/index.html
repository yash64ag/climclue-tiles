Garhi Khairo at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Garhi Khairo is 6th warmest (27.6 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 3,338 hours of sunshine a year) and 118th wettest (153 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shahdad Kot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Garhi Khairo is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (37.5 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 335 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Garhi Khairo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Garhi Khairo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 335 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
