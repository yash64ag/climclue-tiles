Kurkali Sindh Pakistan at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Kurkali Sindh Pakistan is 76th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 3,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 295th wettest (166 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jhol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kurkali Sindh Pakistan is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 232 km from the sea.

Why Kurkali Sindh Pakistan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 232 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
