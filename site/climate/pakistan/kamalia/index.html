Why Kamalia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 854 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kamalia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kamalia?
June, with an average of 34.5 °C (94 °F), day highs around 40.2 °C (104 °F) and night lows around 28.2 °C (83 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kamalia?
January, with an average of 12.9 °C (55 °F), day highs around 19.4 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 6.7 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kamalia?
July, with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 312 mm (12.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kamalia?
November, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kamalia?
May, with about 291 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kamalia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kamalia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.6 × 11 km, that contains Kamalia’s GeoNames location (30.727°N, 72.646°E; ClimaClue area sq-532-2526), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 166,617 (GeoNames).
