Saddiqabad at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Saddiqabad is 94th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 80th sunniest (about 3,311 hours of sunshine a year) and 326th wettest (130 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rahim Yar Khan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Saddiqabad is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (36.1 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 496 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saddiqabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Saddiqabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 496 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-556-2501), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
