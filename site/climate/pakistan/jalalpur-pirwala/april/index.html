Jalalpur Pirwala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in April?
In April, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
April is not one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in April?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in April?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
