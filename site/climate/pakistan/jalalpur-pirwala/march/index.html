Jalalpur Pirwala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in March?
In March, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in March?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in March?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in March?
