Jalalpur Pirwala in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in August?
In August, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 28.9 °C (84 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
August is not one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in August?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in August?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
