Jalalpur Pirwala weather in August

Average August weather in Jalalpur Pirwala, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Jalalpur Pirwala averages 38.7 °C (102 °F) days and 28.0 °C (82 °F) nights, with 42 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 276 hours of sunshine.
