How February compares in Jalalpur Pirwala

Jalalpur Pirwala in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in February?
In February, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (45 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in February?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in February?
