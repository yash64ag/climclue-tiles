How December compares in Jalalpur Pirwala

Jalalpur Pirwala in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in December?
In December, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in December?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
