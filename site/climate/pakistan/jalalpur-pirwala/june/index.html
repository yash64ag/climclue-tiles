How June compares in Jalalpur Pirwala

June is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jalalpur Pirwala. Compared with May: 2.2 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. July is 1.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Jalalpur Pirwala in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in June?
In June, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 42.7 °C (109 °F) and a night low of 28.6 °C (83 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
June is not one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in June?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (45 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in June?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
