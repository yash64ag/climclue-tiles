Jalalpur Pirwala in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalalpur Pirwala in October?
In October, Jalalpur Pirwala averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jalalpur Pirwala?
October is not one of Jalalpur Pirwala’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalalpur Pirwala in October?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Jalalpur Pirwala in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalalpur Pirwala get in October?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalalpur Pirwala in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
