Pattoki at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Pattoki is 193rd warmest (24.8 °C year average), 266th sunniest (about 2,948 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (464 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chunian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Pattoki is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 963 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pattoki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pattoki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 963 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pattoki itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.020°N, 73.853°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-529-2538), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
