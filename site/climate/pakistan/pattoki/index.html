Pattoki at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Pattoki is 77th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 105th sunniest (about 2,948 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (472 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chunian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Pattoki is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 963 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pattoki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pattoki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 963 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
