Pano Aqil at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Pano Aqil is the 3rd driest (116 mm a year). Compared with Ghotki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Pano Aqil is 0.2 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (36.5 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 386 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pano Aqil, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pano Aqil has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 386 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
