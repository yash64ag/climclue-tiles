How March compares in Bahawalpur

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bahawalpur. Compared with February: 5.5 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. April is 6.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Bahawalpur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bahawalpur in March?
In March, Bahawalpur averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bahawalpur?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Bahawalpur’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bahawalpur in March?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (45 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bahawalpur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bahawalpur get in March?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bahawalpur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
