How August compares in Bahawalpur

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bahawalpur. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. September is 2.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Bahawalpur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bahawalpur in August?
In August, Bahawalpur averages a day high of 37.5 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 28.9 °C (84 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bahawalpur?
August is not one of Bahawalpur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bahawalpur in August?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (44 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bahawalpur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bahawalpur get in August?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bahawalpur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
