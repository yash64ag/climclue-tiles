How February compares in Bahawalpur

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bahawalpur. Compared with January: 3.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. March is 5.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Bahawalpur in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bahawalpur in February?
In February, Bahawalpur averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bahawalpur?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Bahawalpur’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bahawalpur in February?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (45 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bahawalpur in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bahawalpur get in February?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bahawalpur in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
