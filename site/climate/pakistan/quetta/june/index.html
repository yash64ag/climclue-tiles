How June compares in Quetta

June is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Quetta. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. July is about as warm as June on average.

Quetta in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quetta in June?
In June, Quetta averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Quetta?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Quetta’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quetta in June?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (65 mm), the driest September (7 mm).
Does it snow in Quetta in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quetta get in June?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quetta in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
