How March compares in Quetta

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Quetta. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 6.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Quetta in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quetta in March?
In March, Quetta averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Quetta?
March is not one of Quetta’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quetta in March?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (65 mm), the driest September (7 mm).
Does it snow in Quetta in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Quetta get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quetta in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
