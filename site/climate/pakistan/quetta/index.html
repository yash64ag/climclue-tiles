Quetta at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Quetta is the 3rd coolest (14.7 °C year average). Compared with Chaman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Quetta is 5.6 °C cooler in June and 4.5 °C cooler in January, with 132 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.9 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 537 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quetta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quetta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 537 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
