April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Quetta. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Quetta in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quetta in April?
In April, Quetta averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Quetta?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Quetta’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quetta in April?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (65 mm), the driest September (7 mm).
Does it snow in Quetta in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quetta get in April?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quetta in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
