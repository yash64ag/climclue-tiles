How November compares in Quetta

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Quetta. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 4.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Quetta in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quetta in November?
In November, Quetta averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Quetta?
November is not one of Quetta’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quetta in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (65 mm), the driest September (7 mm).
Does it snow in Quetta in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quetta get in November?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quetta in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
