How January compares in Quetta

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Quetta. Compared with December: 2.5 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Quetta in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quetta in January?
In January, Quetta averages a day high of 6.2 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Quetta?
January is not one of Quetta’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quetta in January?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (65 mm), the driest September (7 mm).
Does it snow in Quetta in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 38 cm (15.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Quetta get in January?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quetta in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
