How August compares in Quetta

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Quetta. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. September is 3.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Quetta in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quetta in August?
In August, Quetta averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Quetta?
August is not one of Quetta’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quetta in August?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (52 mm), the driest September (5 mm).
Does it snow in Quetta in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quetta get in August?
About 309 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quetta in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
