Chishtian at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Chishtian is 52nd warmest (25.7 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 3,001 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hasilpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Chishtian is 0.3 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.9 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 797 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chishtian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chishtian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 797 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
