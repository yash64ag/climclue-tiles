How June compares in Shekhupura

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shekhupura. Compared with May: 1.8 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. July is 2.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Shekhupura in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shekhupura in June?
In June, Shekhupura averages a day high of 39.7 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 27.0 °C (81 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shekhupura?
June is not one of Shekhupura’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shekhupura in June?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Shekhupura in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shekhupura get in June?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shekhupura in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
