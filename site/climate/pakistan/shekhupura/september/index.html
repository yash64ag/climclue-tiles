How September compares in Shekhupura

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Shekhupura. Compared with August: 1.4 °C cooler, 120 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. October is 4.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Shekhupura in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shekhupura in September?
In September, Shekhupura averages a day high of 34.9 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shekhupura?
September is not one of Shekhupura’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shekhupura in September?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Shekhupura in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shekhupura get in September?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shekhupura in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
