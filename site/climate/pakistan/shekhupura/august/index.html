How August compares in Shekhupura

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shekhupura. Compared with July: 1.1 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. September is 1.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Shekhupura in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shekhupura in August?
In August, Shekhupura averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shekhupura?
August is not one of Shekhupura’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shekhupura in August?
179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Shekhupura in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shekhupura get in August?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shekhupura in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
