Why Pasrur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,107 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Pasrur climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Pasrur?
June, with an average of 32.5 °C (90 °F), day highs around 38.3 °C (101 °F) and night lows around 25.8 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Pasrur?
January, with an average of 11.9 °C (54 °F), day highs around 17.8 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 6.1 °C (43 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Pasrur?
July, with 349 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,121 mm (44.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Pasrur?
November, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Pasrur?
May, with about 304 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Pasrur?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Pasrur?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, October and November.
What type of climate does Pasrur have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Pasrur’s GeoNames location (32.263°N, 74.663°E; ClimaClue area sq-517-2546), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 102,717 (GeoNames).
