Attock City at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Attock City is 120th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 119th sunniest (about 2,865 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (983 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tordher, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Attock City is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 95 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.9 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,094 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Attock City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Attock City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,094 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
