Mananwala at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Mananwala is 213th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 217th sunniest (about 2,973 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (571 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chak Five Hundred Seventy-five, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Mananwala is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 985 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mananwala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mananwala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 985 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mananwala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.588°N, 73.689°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-524-2536), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
