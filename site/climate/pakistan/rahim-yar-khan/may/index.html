What is the weather like in Rahim Yar Khan in May?
In May, Rahim Yar Khan averages a day high of 41.5 °C (107 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Rahim Yar Khan?
May is not one of Rahim Yar Khan’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
