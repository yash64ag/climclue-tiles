How January compares in Multan

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Multan. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 3.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Multan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Multan in January?
In January, Multan averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Multan?
January is not one of Multan’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Multan in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (57 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Multan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Multan get in January?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Multan in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
