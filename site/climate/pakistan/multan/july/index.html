How July compares in Multan

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Multan. Compared with June: 1.1 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Multan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Multan in July?
In July, Multan averages a day high of 38.4 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 29.5 °C (85 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Multan?
July is not one of Multan’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Multan in July?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (57 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Multan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Multan get in July?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Multan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
