Muzaffarābād in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muzaffarābād in February?
In February, Muzaffarābād averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Muzaffarābād?
February is not one of Muzaffarābād’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

Does it snow in Muzaffarābād in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Muzaffarābād get in February?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
