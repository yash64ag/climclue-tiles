Muzaffarābād weather in January

Average January weather in Muzaffarābād, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Muzaffarābād averages 12.5 °C (55 °F) days and 1.7 °C (35 °F) nights, with 103 mm (4.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 178 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Muzaffarābād

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Muzaffarābād. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, 31 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

What is the weather like in Muzaffarābād in January?

Is January a good time to visit Muzaffarābād?
