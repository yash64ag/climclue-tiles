What is the weather like in Muzaffarābād in September?

Is September a good time to visit Muzaffarābād?
September is not one of Muzaffarābād’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
