Muzaffarābād at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Muzaffarābād is the 4th wettest (1,499 mm a year). Compared with Abbottabad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Muzaffarābād is 2.5 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 540 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,206 km from the sea (deep inland).
