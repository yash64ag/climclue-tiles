Muzaffarābād in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muzaffarābād in December?
In December, Muzaffarābād averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Muzaffarābād?
December is not one of Muzaffarābād’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muzaffarābād in December?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (284 mm), the driest November (35 mm).
Does it snow in Muzaffarābād in December?
