How February compares in Kasur

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kasur. Compared with January: 3.2 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 5.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Kasur in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kasur in February?
In February, Kasur averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kasur?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Kasur’s mild months (February, March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kasur in February?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (180 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kasur in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kasur get in February?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kasur in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
