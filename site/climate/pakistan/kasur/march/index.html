How March compares in Kasur

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kasur. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 6.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Kasur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kasur in March?
In March, Kasur averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kasur?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Kasur’s mild months (February, March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kasur in March?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (180 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kasur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kasur get in March?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kasur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
