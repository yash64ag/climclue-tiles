Gojra at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Gojra is 69th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,989 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (353 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Toba Tek Singh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Gojra is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.5 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 885 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gojra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gojra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 885 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
