Dadu at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Dadu is the 3rd warmest (27.7 °C year average). Compared with Moro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Dadu is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (37.0 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 203 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dadu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dadu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 203 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dadu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.730°N, 67.777°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-572-2477), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
