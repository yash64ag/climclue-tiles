Umarkot at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Umarkot is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,477 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kunri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Umarkot is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.8 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 254 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Umarkot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Umarkot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 254 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-586-2497), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
