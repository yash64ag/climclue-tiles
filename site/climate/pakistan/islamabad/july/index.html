How July compares in Islamabad

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Islamabad. Compared with June: 1.3 °C cooler, 248 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Islamabad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in July?
In July, Islamabad averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Islamabad?
July is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in July?
344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in July?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
