How January compares in Islamabad

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Islamabad. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 35 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Islamabad in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in January?
In January, Islamabad averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Islamabad?
January is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in January?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in January?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
