How March compares in Islamabad

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Islamabad. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 5.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Islamabad in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in March?
In March, Islamabad averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Islamabad?
March is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in March?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in March?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
