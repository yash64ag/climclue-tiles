How February compares in Islamabad

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Islamabad. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 4.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Islamabad in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in February?
In February, Islamabad averages a day high of 15.8 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Islamabad?
February is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in February?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in February?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
