How September compares in Islamabad

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Islamabad. Compared with August: 1.7 °C cooler, 194 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Islamabad in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in September?
In September, Islamabad averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Islamabad?
September is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in September?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in September?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
