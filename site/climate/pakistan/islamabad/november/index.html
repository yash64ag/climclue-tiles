How November compares in Islamabad

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Islamabad. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Islamabad in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in November?
In November, Islamabad averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Islamabad?
November is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in November?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in November?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
