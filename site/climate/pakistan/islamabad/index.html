Why Islamabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,131 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Islamabad climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Islamabad?
June, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 34.2 °C (94 °F) and night lows around 22.0 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Islamabad?
January, with an average of 8.9 °C (48 °F), day highs around 14.2 °C (58 °F) and night lows around 2.7 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Islamabad?
July, with 344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,505 mm (59.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Islamabad?
November, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Islamabad?
May, with about 298 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Islamabad?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Islamabad?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Islamabad have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Islamabad’s GeoNames location (33.721°N, 73.043°E; ClimaClue area sq-502-2530), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 601,600 (GeoNames).
