How December compares in Islamabad

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Islamabad. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Islamabad in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Islamabad in December?
In December, Islamabad averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Islamabad?
December is not one of Islamabad’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Islamabad in December?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (344 mm), the driest November (25 mm).
Does it snow in Islamabad in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Islamabad get in December?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Islamabad in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
