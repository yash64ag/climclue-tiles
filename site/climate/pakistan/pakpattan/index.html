Pakpattan at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Pakpattan is 60th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,958 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (323 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bahawalnagar, Punjab, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Pakpattan is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.5 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 877 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pakpattan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Pakpattan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Pakpattan are February, March and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March and November.

Why Pakpattan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 877 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
