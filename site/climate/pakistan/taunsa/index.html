Taunsa at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Taunsa is 37th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 3,143 hours of sunshine a year) and 76th wettest (279 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Layyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Taunsa is 0.9 °C warmer in June and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (36.4 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 728 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taunsa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taunsa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 728 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
