Why Kharian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,100 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kharian climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kharian?
June, with an average of 32.4 °C (90 °F), day highs around 38.3 °C (101 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kharian?
January, with an average of 11.7 °C (53 °F), day highs around 17.7 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 5.4 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kharian?
July, with 325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,145 mm (45.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kharian?
November, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kharian?
May, with about 306 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kharian?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kharian?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, October and November.
What type of climate does Kharian have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Kharian’s GeoNames location (32.816°N, 73.887°E; ClimaClue area sq-511-2538), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 103,036 (GeoNames).
