Kharian at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Kharian is 114th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,954 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,145 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jhelum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Kharian is 0.6 °C cooler in June and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.4 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,100 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kharian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kharian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,100 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
