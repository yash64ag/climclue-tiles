Renala Khurd at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Renala Khurd is 73rd warmest (25.1 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,955 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (394 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Okara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Renala Khurd is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 926 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Renala Khurd, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Renala Khurd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
