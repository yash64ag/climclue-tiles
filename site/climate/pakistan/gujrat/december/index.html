How December compares in Gujrat

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Gujrat. Compared with November: 4.7 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Gujrat in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gujrat in December?
In December, Gujrat averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Gujrat?
December is not one of Gujrat’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gujrat in December?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (324 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Gujrat in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gujrat get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gujrat in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
