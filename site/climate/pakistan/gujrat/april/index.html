How April compares in Gujrat

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Gujrat. Compared with March: 5.9 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Gujrat in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gujrat in April?
In April, Gujrat averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gujrat?
April is not one of Gujrat’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gujrat in April?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (332 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Gujrat in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gujrat get in April?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gujrat in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
