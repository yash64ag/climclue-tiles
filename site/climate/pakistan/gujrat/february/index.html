How February compares in Gujrat

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Gujrat. Compared with January: 2.9 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 4.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Gujrat in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gujrat in February?
In February, Gujrat averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Gujrat?
February is not one of Gujrat’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gujrat in February?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (332 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Gujrat in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gujrat get in February?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gujrat in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
