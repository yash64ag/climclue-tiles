Baffa at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Baffa is 342nd warmest (18.0 °C year average), 351st sunniest (about 2,786 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (1,362 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mansehra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Baffa is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 254 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.7 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,205 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Baffa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Baffa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,205 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C. The 10 km area around Baffa, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.9 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Baffa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.438°N, 73.224°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-495-2532), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
