How February compares in Karachi

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Karachi. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Karachi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karachi in February?
In February, Karachi averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Karachi?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Karachi’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karachi in February?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (68 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Karachi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karachi get in February?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Karachi in February?
About 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Karachi in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
