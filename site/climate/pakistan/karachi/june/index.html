How June compares in Karachi

June is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Karachi. Compared with May: 0.6 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. July is 1.0 °C cooler than June on average.

Karachi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karachi in June?
In June, Karachi averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 26.9 °C (80 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Karachi?
June is not one of Karachi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karachi in June?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (68 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Karachi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karachi get in June?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Karachi in June?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Karachi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
