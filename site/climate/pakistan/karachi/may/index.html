How May compares in Karachi

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Karachi. Compared with April: 1.9 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 0.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Karachi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karachi in May?
In May, Karachi averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 311 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Karachi?
May is not one of Karachi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karachi in May?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Karachi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karachi get in May?
About 311 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karachi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
