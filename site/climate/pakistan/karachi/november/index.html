How November compares in Karachi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Karachi. Compared with October: 3.0 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. December is 4.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Karachi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karachi in November?
In November, Karachi averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Karachi?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Karachi’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karachi in November?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Karachi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karachi get in November?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karachi in November?
