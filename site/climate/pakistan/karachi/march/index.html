How March compares in Karachi

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Karachi. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 2.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Karachi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karachi in March?
In March, Karachi averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Karachi?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Karachi’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karachi in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Karachi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karachi get in March?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karachi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
