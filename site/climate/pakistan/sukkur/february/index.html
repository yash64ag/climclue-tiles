How February compares in Sukkur

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sukkur. Compared with January: 2.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. March is 5.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Sukkur in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sukkur in February?
In February, Sukkur averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Sukkur?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Sukkur’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sukkur in February?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sukkur in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sukkur get in February?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sukkur in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
