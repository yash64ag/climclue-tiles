How November compares in Sukkur

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sukkur. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. December is 5.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Sukkur in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sukkur in November?
In November, Sukkur averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sukkur?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Sukkur’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sukkur in November?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sukkur in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sukkur get in November?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sukkur in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
