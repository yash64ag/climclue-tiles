How March compares in Sukkur

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Sukkur. Compared with February: 5.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. April is 6.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Sukkur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sukkur in March?
In March, Sukkur averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sukkur?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Sukkur’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sukkur in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sukkur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sukkur get in March?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sukkur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
