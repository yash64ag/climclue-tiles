How June compares in Sukkur

June is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sukkur. Compared with May: 1.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. July is 1.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Sukkur in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sukkur in June?
In June, Sukkur averages a day high of 42.8 °C (109 °F) and a night low of 29.3 °C (85 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sukkur?
June is not one of Sukkur’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sukkur in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sukkur in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sukkur get in June?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sukkur in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
