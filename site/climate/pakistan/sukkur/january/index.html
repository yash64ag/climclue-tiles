How January compares in Sukkur

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Sukkur. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 2.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Sukkur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sukkur in January?
In January, Sukkur averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sukkur?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Sukkur’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sukkur in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest November (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sukkur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sukkur get in January?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sukkur in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
