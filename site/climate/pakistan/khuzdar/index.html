Khuzdar at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Khuzdar is the sunniest (about 3,509 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shahdad Kot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (127 km away), Khuzdar is 7.3 °C cooler in June and 7.0 °C cooler in January, with 117 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.1 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 279 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khuzdar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khuzdar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 279 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
