How October compares in Faisalabad

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Faisalabad. Compared with September: 4.8 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. November is 6.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Faisalabad in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faisalabad in October?
In October, Faisalabad averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Faisalabad?
October is not one of Faisalabad’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faisalabad in October?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (122 mm), the driest November (3 mm).
Does it snow in Faisalabad in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faisalabad get in October?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faisalabad in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
