Faisalabad at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Faisalabad is 74th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 2,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (421 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chak Jhumra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Faisalabad is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.4 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 936 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Faisalabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Faisalabad

For mild weather, the best months to visit Faisalabad are February, March and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March and November.

Why Faisalabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 936 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
