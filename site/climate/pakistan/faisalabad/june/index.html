How June compares in Faisalabad

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Faisalabad. Compared with May: 1.7 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. July is 1.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Faisalabad in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faisalabad in June?
In June, Faisalabad averages a day high of 40.0 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 27.7 °C (82 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Faisalabad?
June is not one of Faisalabad’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faisalabad in June?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (121 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Faisalabad in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faisalabad get in June?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faisalabad in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
