How April compares in Faisalabad

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Faisalabad. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Faisalabad in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faisalabad in April?
In April, Faisalabad averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Faisalabad?
April is not one of Faisalabad’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faisalabad in April?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (121 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Faisalabad in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faisalabad get in April?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faisalabad in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
