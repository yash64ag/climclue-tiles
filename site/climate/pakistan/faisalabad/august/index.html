How August compares in Faisalabad

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Faisalabad. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 1.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Faisalabad in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faisalabad in August?
In August, Faisalabad averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 27.2 °C (81 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Faisalabad?
August is not one of Faisalabad’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faisalabad in August?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (121 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Faisalabad in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faisalabad get in August?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faisalabad in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
