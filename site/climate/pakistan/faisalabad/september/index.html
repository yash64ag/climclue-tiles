Faisalabad weather in September

Average September weather in Faisalabad, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Faisalabad averages 36.6 °C (98 °F) days and 24.8 °C (77 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 264 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Faisalabad

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Faisalabad. Compared with August: 1.3 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. October is 4.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Faisalabad in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faisalabad in September?
In September, Faisalabad averages a day high of 36.6 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Faisalabad?
September is not one of Faisalabad’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faisalabad in September?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (122 mm), the driest November (3 mm).
Does it snow in Faisalabad in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faisalabad get in September?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faisalabad in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
