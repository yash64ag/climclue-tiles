How July compares in Faisalabad

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Faisalabad. Compared with June: 1.6 °C cooler, 80 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Faisalabad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faisalabad in July?
In July, Faisalabad averages a day high of 36.6 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 27.7 °C (82 °F), with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Faisalabad?
July is not one of Faisalabad’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faisalabad in July?
121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (121 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Faisalabad in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faisalabad get in July?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faisalabad in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
