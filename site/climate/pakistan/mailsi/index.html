Mailsi at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Mailsi is 49th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 3,052 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (213 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vihari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Mailsi is 0.3 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (35.1 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 752 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mailsi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mailsi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 752 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
