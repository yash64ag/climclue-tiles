Mingora at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Mingora is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,659 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (1,369 mm a year). Compared with Kabal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Mingora is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 96 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,189 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Mingora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,189 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
