Shabqadar at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Shabqadar is 107th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 2,825 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (598 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Charsadda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Shabqadar is 0.3 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (10.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,105 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shabqadar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shabqadar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,105 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
