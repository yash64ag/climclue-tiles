How June compares in Sargodha

June is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sargodha. Compared with May: 2.1 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. July is 1.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Sargodha in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sargodha in June?
In June, Sargodha averages a day high of 40.9 °C (106 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sargodha?
June is not one of Sargodha’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sargodha in June?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Sargodha in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sargodha get in June?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sargodha in June?
