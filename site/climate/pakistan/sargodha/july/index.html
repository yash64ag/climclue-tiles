Sargodha in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sargodha in July?
In July, Sargodha averages a day high of 36.1 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sargodha?
July is not one of Sargodha’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sargodha in July?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Sargodha in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sargodha get in July?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sargodha in July?
