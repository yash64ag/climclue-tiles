How March compares in Sargodha

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sargodha. Compared with February: 5.2 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Sargodha in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sargodha in March?
In March, Sargodha averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sargodha?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Sargodha’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sargodha in March?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Sargodha in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sargodha get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sargodha in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
