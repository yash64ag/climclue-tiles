How May compares in Sargodha

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Sargodha. Compared with April: 5.3 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. June is 2.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Sargodha in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sargodha in May?
In May, Sargodha averages a day high of 38.6 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 299 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sargodha?
May is not one of Sargodha’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sargodha in May?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Sargodha in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sargodha get in May?
About 299 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sargodha in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
