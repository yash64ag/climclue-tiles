How January compares in Sargodha

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Sargodha. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 3.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Sargodha in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sargodha in January?
In January, Sargodha averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sargodha?
January is not one of Sargodha’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sargodha in January?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Sargodha in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sargodha get in January?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sargodha in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
