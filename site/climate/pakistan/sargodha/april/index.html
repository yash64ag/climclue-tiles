Sargodha in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sargodha in April?
In April, Sargodha averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sargodha?
April is not one of Sargodha’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sargodha in April?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Sargodha in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sargodha get in April?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sargodha in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
