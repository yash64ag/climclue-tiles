Why Sargodha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 959 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sargodha climate: questions and answers

June, with an average of 34.0 °C (93 °F), day highs around 39.9 °C (104 °F) and night lows around 27.2 °C (81 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sargodha?
January, with an average of 12.2 °C (54 °F), day highs around 18.4 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 5.8 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sargodha?

What is the driest month in Sargodha?
November, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sargodha?
May, with about 299 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sargodha?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sargodha?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Sargodha’s GeoNames location (32.086°N, 72.674°E; ClimaClue area sq-519-2526), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 975,886 (GeoNames).
