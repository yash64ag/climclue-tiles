Gujranwala at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Gujranwala is 101st warmest (23.6 °C year average), 87th sunniest (about 2,969 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (923 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Eminabad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Gujranwala is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.4 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,065 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gujranwala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Gujranwala

For mild weather, the best months to visit Gujranwala are March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (33.4 °C), May (31.4 °C) and July (31.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (9 mm), December (19 mm) and October (20 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (304 h), October (282 h) and June (277 h).

Why Gujranwala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,065 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
