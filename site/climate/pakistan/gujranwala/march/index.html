Gujranwala weather in March

Average March weather in Gujranwala, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Gujranwala averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) days and 13.0 °C (55 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 232 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Gujranwala

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gujranwala. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. April is 6.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Gujranwala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gujranwala in March?
In March, Gujranwala averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Gujranwala?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Gujranwala’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gujranwala in March?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (285 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Gujranwala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gujranwala get in March?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gujranwala in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
