How September compares in Gujranwala

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Gujranwala. Compared with August: 1.6 °C cooler, 173 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. October is 4.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Gujranwala in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gujranwala in September?
In September, Gujranwala averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Gujranwala?
September is not one of Gujranwala’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gujranwala in September?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (285 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Gujranwala in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gujranwala get in September?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gujranwala in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
