Why Lahore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,035 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Lahore climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lahore?
June, with an average of 33.4 °C (92 °F), day highs around 39.1 °C (102 °F) and night lows around 26.6 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lahore?
January, with an average of 12.1 °C (54 °F), day highs around 18.3 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 6.0 °C (43 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lahore?
July, with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 680 mm (26.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lahore?
November, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lahore?
May, with about 299 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lahore?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Lahore?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October and November.
What type of climate does Lahore have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Lahore’s GeoNames location (31.558°N, 74.351°E; ClimaClue area sq-524-2543), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 13,004,135 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
