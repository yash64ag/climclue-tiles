Lahore at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Lahore is 96th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 2,937 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (680 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Model Town, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Lahore is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.4 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,035 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lahore, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Lahore

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lahore are February, March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (33.4 °C), May (31.8 °C) and July (31.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (8 mm), December (11 mm) and October (14 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (299 h), October (278 h) and April (268 h).

Why Lahore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,035 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
