How June compares in Lahore

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Lahore. Compared with May: 1.6 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. July is 2.0 °C cooler than June on average.

Lahore in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lahore in June?
In June, Lahore averages a day high of 39.1 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Lahore?
June is not one of Lahore’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lahore in June?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Lahore in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lahore get in June?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lahore in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
