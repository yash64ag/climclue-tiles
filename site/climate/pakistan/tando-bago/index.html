Tando Bago at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Tando Bago is the 4th sunniest (about 3,468 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Talhar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Tando Bago is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.1 °C), averages 15.4 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 150 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tando Bago, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tando Bago has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 150 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
