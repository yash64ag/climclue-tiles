Kuri Dulal at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Kuri Dulal is 328th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 298th sunniest (about 2,908 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (1,274 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sharifabad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kuri Dulal is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 95 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.4 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,106 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kuri Dulal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,106 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-506-2532), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
