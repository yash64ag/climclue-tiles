Mianwali at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Mianwali is 91st warmest (24.2 °C year average), 96th sunniest (about 2,960 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (554 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jauharabad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Mianwali is about as warm in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.1 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.1 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 942 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mianwali, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mianwali has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 942 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
