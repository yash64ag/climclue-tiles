How August compares in Okara

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Okara. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. September is 1.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Okara in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okara in August?
In August, Okara averages a day high of 36.1 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 27.6 °C (82 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Okara?
August is not one of Okara’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okara in August?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (108 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Okara in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okara get in August?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okara in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
