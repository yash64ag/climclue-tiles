How October compares in Okara

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Okara. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Okara in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okara in October?
In October, Okara averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Okara?
October is not one of Okara’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okara in October?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (107 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Okara in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okara get in October?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okara in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
