How November compares in Okara

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Okara. Compared with October: 6.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. December is 5.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Okara in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okara in November?
In November, Okara averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Okara?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Okara’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okara in November?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (108 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Okara in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okara get in November?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okara in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
