Ghotki at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Ghotki is the 5th driest (122 mm a year). Compared with Pano Aqil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Ghotki is 0.2 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (36.3 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 416 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ghotki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ghotki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 416 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
