Naudero at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Naudero is 38th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 3,344 hours of sunshine a year) and 350th wettest (116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ratodero, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Naudero is 0.2 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (37.1 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 318 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Naudero, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Naudero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 318 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-563-2483), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
