How June compares in Bannu

June is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bannu. Compared with May: 3.0 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. July is 2.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Bannu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bannu in June?
In June, Bannu averages a day high of 39.6 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 27.2 °C (81 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bannu?
June is not one of Bannu’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bannu in June?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (143 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bannu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bannu get in June?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bannu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
