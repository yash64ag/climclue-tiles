How January compares in Bannu

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bannu. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Bannu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bannu in January?
In January, Bannu averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bannu?
January is not one of Bannu’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bannu in January?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (143 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bannu in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bannu get in January?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bannu in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
