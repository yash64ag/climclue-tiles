How May compares in Bannu

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bannu. Compared with April: 5.8 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Bannu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bannu in May?
In May, Bannu averages a day high of 37.0 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 300 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bannu?
May is not one of Bannu’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bannu in May?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (143 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bannu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bannu get in May?
About 300 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bannu in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
