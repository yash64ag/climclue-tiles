Bannu weather in April

Average April weather in Bannu, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Bannu averages 32.7 °C (91 °F) days and 18.1 °C (65 °F) nights, with 37 mm (1.5 in) of rain over the month and about 243 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Bannu

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bannu. Compared with March: 6.1 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. May is 5.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Bannu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bannu in April?
In April, Bannu averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bannu?
April is not one of Bannu’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bannu in April?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (146 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bannu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bannu get in April?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
