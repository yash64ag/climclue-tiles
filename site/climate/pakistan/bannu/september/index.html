How September compares in Bannu

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bannu. Compared with August: 1.8 °C cooler, 99 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. October is 4.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Bannu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bannu in September?
In September, Bannu averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bannu?
September is not one of Bannu’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bannu in September?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (146 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bannu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bannu get in September?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bannu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
