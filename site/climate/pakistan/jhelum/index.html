Jhelum at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Jhelum is 103rd warmest (23.3 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 2,952 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,086 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kharian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Jhelum is 0.6 °C warmer in June and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.0 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,102 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jhelum, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jhelum has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,102 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
