Sinjhoro at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Sinjhoro is 22nd warmest (27.0 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,372 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (161 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shahdadpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Sinjhoro is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.5 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 239 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sinjhoro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sinjhoro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 239 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
