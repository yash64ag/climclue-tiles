Eminabad at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Eminabad is 100th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 95th sunniest (about 2,962 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (912 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kamoke, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Eminabad is 0.1 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.4 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,064 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Eminabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Eminabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,064 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
