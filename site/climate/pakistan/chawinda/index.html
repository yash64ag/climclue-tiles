Chawinda at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Chawinda is 279th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 286th sunniest (about 2,930 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (1,219 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pasrur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Chawinda is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 83 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.1 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,121 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Chawinda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,121 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chawinda itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.344°N, 74.705°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-516-2547), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
