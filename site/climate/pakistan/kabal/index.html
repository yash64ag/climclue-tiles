Kabal at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Kabal is the 4th least sunny (about 2,667 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mingora, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kabal is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 96 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,184 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kabal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,184 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
