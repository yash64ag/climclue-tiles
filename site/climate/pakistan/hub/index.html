Hub at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Hub is 50th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 3,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 103rd wettest (194 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karachi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Hub is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.1 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hub, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hub has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
