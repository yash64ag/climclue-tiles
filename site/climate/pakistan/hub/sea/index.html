Hub sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Balochistan · Pakistan

The sea off Hub averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Hub sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hub?
In June, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hub?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hub in February?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Hub?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hub nearby?
In June: Okha (29.5 °C, 361 km), Mundra (29.7 °C, 377 km) and Gāndhīdhām (29.7 °C, 394 km).
