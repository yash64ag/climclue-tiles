How January compares in Battagram

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Battagram. Compared with December: 2.6 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Battagram in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Battagram in January?
In January, Battagram averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Battagram?
January is not one of Battagram’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Battagram in January?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (39 mm).
Does it snow in Battagram in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 40 cm (15.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Battagram get in January?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Battagram in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
