Battagram weather in February

Average February weather in Battagram, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Battagram averages 11.2 °C (52 °F) days and 1.9 °C (35 °F) nights, with 153 mm (6.0 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 159 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Battagram

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Battagram. Compared with January: 1.9 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 5.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Battagram in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Battagram in February?
In February, Battagram averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 1.9 °C (35 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Battagram?
February is not one of Battagram’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Battagram in February?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest November (39 mm).
Does it snow in Battagram in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Battagram get in February?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Battagram in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
