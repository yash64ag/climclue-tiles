Battagram weather in June

Average June weather in Battagram, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Battagram averages 32.2 °C (90 °F) days and 19.7 °C (67 °F) nights, with 65 mm (2.5 in) of rain over the month and about 292 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Battagram

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Battagram. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 0.3 °C warmer than June on average.
