Battagram in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Battagram in November?
In November, Battagram averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Battagram?
November is not one of Battagram’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Battagram in November?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest November (39 mm).
Does it snow in Battagram in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Battagram get in November?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
