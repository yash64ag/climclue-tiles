Battagram at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Battagram is the 5th coolest (15.6 °C year average) and the 5th least sunny (about 2,695 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Muzaffarābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Battagram is 1.6 °C cooler in July and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 148 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,213 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Battagram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Battagram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,213 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
