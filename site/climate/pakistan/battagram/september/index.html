Battagram weather in September

Average September weather in Battagram, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Battagram averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 17.2 °C (63 °F) nights, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of rain over the month and about 247 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Battagram

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Battagram. Compared with August: 2.1 °C cooler, 93 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. October is 5.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Is September a good time to visit Battagram?
