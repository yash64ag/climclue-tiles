Battagram in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Battagram in September?
In September, Battagram averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Battagram?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Battagram’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Battagram in September?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (39 mm).
Does it snow in Battagram in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Battagram get in September?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Battagram in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
