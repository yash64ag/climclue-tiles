How December compares in Battagram

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Battagram. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. January is 2.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Battagram in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Battagram in December?
In December, Battagram averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Battagram?
December is not one of Battagram’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Battagram in December?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (39 mm).
Does it snow in Battagram in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Battagram get in December?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Battagram in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
