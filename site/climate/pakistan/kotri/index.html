Kotri at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Kotri is 26th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 3,301 hours of sunshine a year) and 111th wettest (171 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tando Muhammad Khan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Kotri is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.1 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 152 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kotri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Same area as Hyderabad: Kotri lies in the same ~10 km ClimaClue area as Hyderabad, so its numbers are the same as there.

Why Kotri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 152 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
