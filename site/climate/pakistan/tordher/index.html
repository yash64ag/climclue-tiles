Tordher at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Tordher is 119th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 123rd sunniest (about 2,848 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (889 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Attock City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Tordher is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 95 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.9 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (10.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,108 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tordher, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tordher has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,108 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
