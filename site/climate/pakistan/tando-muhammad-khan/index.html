Tando Muhammad Khan at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Tando Muhammad Khan is 88th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 3,360 hours of sunshine a year) and 270th wettest (189 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Matli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Tando Muhammad Khan is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.0 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 161 km from the sea.

Why Tando Muhammad Khan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 161 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tando Muhammad Khan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.124°N, 68.537°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-588-2485), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
