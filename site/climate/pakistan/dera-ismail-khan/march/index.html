Dera Ismail Khan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dera Ismail Khan in March?
In March, Dera Ismail Khan averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dera Ismail Khan?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Dera Ismail Khan’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dera Ismail Khan in March?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dera Ismail Khan in March?

How many hours of sunshine does Dera Ismail Khan get in March?
