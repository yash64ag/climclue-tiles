Dera Ismail Khan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dera Ismail Khan in June?
In June, Dera Ismail Khan averages a day high of 41.1 °C (106 °F) and a night low of 28.5 °C (83 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dera Ismail Khan?
June is not one of Dera Ismail Khan’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dera Ismail Khan in June?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dera Ismail Khan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dera Ismail Khan get in June?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
