Dera Ismail Khan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dera Ismail Khan in November?
In November, Dera Ismail Khan averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Dera Ismail Khan?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Dera Ismail Khan’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dera Ismail Khan in November?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dera Ismail Khan in November?

How many hours of sunshine does Dera Ismail Khan get in November?
