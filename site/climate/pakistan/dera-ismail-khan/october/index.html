Dera Ismail Khan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dera Ismail Khan in October?
In October, Dera Ismail Khan averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dera Ismail Khan?
October is not one of Dera Ismail Khan’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dera Ismail Khan in October?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dera Ismail Khan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dera Ismail Khan get in October?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dera Ismail Khan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
