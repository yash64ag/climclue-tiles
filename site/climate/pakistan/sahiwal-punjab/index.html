Sahiwal, Punjab at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Sahiwal, Punjab is 66th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 83rd sunniest (about 2,972 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (339 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bahawalnagar, Punjab, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Sahiwal, Punjab is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.4 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 883 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sahiwal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sahiwal, Punjab has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 883 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
