How January compares in Sahiwal, Punjab

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sahiwal, Punjab. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 3.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Sahiwal, Punjab in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sahiwal, Punjab in January?
In January, Sahiwal, Punjab averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sahiwal, Punjab?
January is not one of Sahiwal, Punjab’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sahiwal, Punjab in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Sahiwal, Punjab in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sahiwal, Punjab get in January?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sahiwal, Punjab in January?
