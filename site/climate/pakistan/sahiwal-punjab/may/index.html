Sahiwal, Punjab in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sahiwal, Punjab in May?
In May, Sahiwal, Punjab averages a day high of 39.5 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sahiwal, Punjab?
May is not one of Sahiwal, Punjab’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sahiwal, Punjab in May?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Sahiwal, Punjab in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sahiwal, Punjab get in May?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sahiwal, Punjab in May?
