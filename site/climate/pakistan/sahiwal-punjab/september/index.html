How September compares in Sahiwal, Punjab

Sahiwal, Punjab in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sahiwal, Punjab in September?
In September, Sahiwal, Punjab averages a day high of 36.4 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Sahiwal, Punjab?
September is not one of Sahiwal, Punjab’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sahiwal, Punjab in September?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Sahiwal, Punjab in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sahiwal, Punjab get in September?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
