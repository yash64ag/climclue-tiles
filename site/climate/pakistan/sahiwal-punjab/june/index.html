How June compares in Sahiwal, Punjab

June is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sahiwal, Punjab. Compared with May: 1.5 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. July is 1.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Sahiwal, Punjab in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sahiwal, Punjab in June?
In June, Sahiwal, Punjab averages a day high of 40.1 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 28.1 °C (83 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sahiwal, Punjab?
June is not one of Sahiwal, Punjab’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sahiwal, Punjab in June?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Sahiwal, Punjab in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sahiwal, Punjab get in June?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
