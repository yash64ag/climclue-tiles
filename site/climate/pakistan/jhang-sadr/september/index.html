Jhang Sadr in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jhang Sadr in September?
In September, Jhang Sadr averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jhang Sadr?
September is not one of Jhang Sadr’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jhang Sadr in September?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jhang Sadr in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jhang Sadr get in September?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jhang Sadr in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
