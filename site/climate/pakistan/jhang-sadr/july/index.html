Jhang Sadr in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jhang Sadr in July?
In July, Jhang Sadr averages a day high of 37.5 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 28.4 °C (83 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jhang Sadr?
July is not one of Jhang Sadr’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
