Jhang Sadr in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jhang Sadr in May?
In May, Jhang Sadr averages a day high of 39.6 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jhang Sadr?
May is not one of Jhang Sadr’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jhang Sadr in May?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jhang Sadr in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jhang Sadr get in May?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jhang Sadr in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
