Jhang Sadr in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jhang Sadr in February?
In February, Jhang Sadr averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jhang Sadr?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Jhang Sadr’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jhang Sadr in February?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jhang Sadr in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jhang Sadr get in February?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
