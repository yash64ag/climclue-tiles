Jhang Sadr in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jhang Sadr in August?
In August, Jhang Sadr averages a day high of 36.6 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 27.9 °C (82 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jhang Sadr?
August is not one of Jhang Sadr’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jhang Sadr in August?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jhang Sadr in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jhang Sadr get in August?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jhang Sadr in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
