Jhang Sadr at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Jhang Sadr is 63rd warmest (25.4 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 2,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 64th wettest (359 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Toba Tek Singh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Jhang Sadr is 0.3 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.9 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 874 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jhang Sadr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jhang Sadr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 874 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
