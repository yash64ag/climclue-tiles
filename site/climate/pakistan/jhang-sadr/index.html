Jhang Sadr at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Jhang Sadr is 154th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 172nd sunniest (about 2,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 184th wettest (362 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bagh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Jhang Sadr is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.9 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 874 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jhang Sadr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jhang Sadr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 874 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jhang Sadr itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.270°N, 72.317°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-527-2523), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
