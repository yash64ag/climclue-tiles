How January compares in Jhang Sadr

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jhang Sadr. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 3.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Jhang Sadr in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jhang Sadr in January?
In January, Jhang Sadr averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jhang Sadr?
January is not one of Jhang Sadr’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jhang Sadr in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jhang Sadr in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jhang Sadr get in January?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jhang Sadr in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
