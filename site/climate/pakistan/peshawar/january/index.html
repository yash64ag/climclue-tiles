How January compares in Peshawar

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Peshawar. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Peshawar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Peshawar in January?
In January, Peshawar averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Peshawar?
January is not one of Peshawar’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Peshawar in January?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (98 mm), the driest November (14 mm).
Does it snow in Peshawar in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Peshawar get in January?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Peshawar in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
