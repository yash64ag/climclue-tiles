How December compares in Peshawar

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Peshawar. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Peshawar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Peshawar in December?
In December, Peshawar averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Peshawar?
December is not one of Peshawar’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Peshawar in December?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (94 mm), the driest November (15 mm).
Does it snow in Peshawar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Peshawar get in December?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Peshawar in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
