How May compares in Peshawar

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Peshawar. Compared with April: 6.0 °C warmer, 39 mm less precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Peshawar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Peshawar in May?
In May, Peshawar averages a day high of 36.0 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 293 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Peshawar?
May is not one of Peshawar’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Peshawar in May?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (94 mm), the driest November (15 mm).
Does it snow in Peshawar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Peshawar get in May?
About 293 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Peshawar in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
