How August compares in Peshawar

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Peshawar. Compared with July: 1.7 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. September is 1.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Peshawar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Peshawar in August?
In August, Peshawar averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Peshawar?
August is not one of Peshawar’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Peshawar in August?
98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (98 mm), the driest November (14 mm).
Does it snow in Peshawar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Peshawar get in August?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Peshawar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
