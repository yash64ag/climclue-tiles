Peshawar at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Peshawar is 105th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 122nd sunniest (about 2,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (590 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Charsadda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Peshawar is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.1 °C), averages 22.8 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,085 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Peshawar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Peshawar

For mild weather, the best months to visit Peshawar are March, April, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March, April, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (34.1 °C), July (32.5 °C) and August (30.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (15 mm), October (24 mm) and December (26 mm).

Why Peshawar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,085 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
