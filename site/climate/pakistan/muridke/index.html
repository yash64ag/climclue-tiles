Why Muridke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,049 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Muridke climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Muridke?
June, with an average of 33.5 °C (92 °F), day highs around 39.3 °C (103 °F) and night lows around 26.7 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Muridke?
January, with an average of 12.1 °C (54 °F), day highs around 18.2 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 5.9 °C (43 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Muridke?
July, with 250 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 803 mm (31.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Muridke?
November, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Muridke?
May, with about 302 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Muridke?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Muridke?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October and November.
What type of climate does Muridke have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Muridke’s GeoNames location (31.803°N, 74.258°E; ClimaClue area sq-521-2542), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 254,291 (GeoNames).
