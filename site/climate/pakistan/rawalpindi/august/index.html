Rawalpindi weather in August

Average August weather in Rawalpindi, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Rawalpindi averages 33.5 °C (92 °F) days and 23.7 °C (75 °F) nights, with 316 mm (12.4 in) of rain over the month and about 248 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Rawalpindi

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Rawalpindi. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 1.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Rawalpindi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rawalpindi in August?
In August, Rawalpindi averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 316 mm (12.4 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Rawalpindi?
August is not one of Rawalpindi’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rawalpindi in August?
316 mm (12.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (323 mm), the driest November (20 mm).
Does it snow in Rawalpindi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rawalpindi get in August?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rawalpindi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
