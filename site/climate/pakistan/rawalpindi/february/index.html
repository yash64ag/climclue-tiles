How February compares in Rawalpindi

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Rawalpindi. Compared with January: 2.7 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 4.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Rawalpindi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rawalpindi in February?
In February, Rawalpindi averages a day high of 18.0 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Rawalpindi?
February is not one of Rawalpindi’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rawalpindi in February?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (323 mm), the driest November (20 mm).
Does it snow in Rawalpindi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rawalpindi get in February?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rawalpindi in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
