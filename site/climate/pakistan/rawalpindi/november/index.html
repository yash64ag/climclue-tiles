How November compares in Rawalpindi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Rawalpindi. Compared with October: 6.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Rawalpindi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rawalpindi in November?
In November, Rawalpindi averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rawalpindi?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Rawalpindi’s mild months (April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rawalpindi in November?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (323 mm), the driest November (20 mm).
Does it snow in Rawalpindi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rawalpindi get in November?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rawalpindi in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
