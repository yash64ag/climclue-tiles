Rawalpindi weather in June

Average June weather in Rawalpindi, Pakistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Rawalpindi averages 38.5 °C (101 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 80 mm (3.2 in) of rain over the month and about 292 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Rawalpindi

June is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Rawalpindi. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. July is 1.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Rawalpindi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rawalpindi in June?
In June, Rawalpindi averages a day high of 38.5 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rawalpindi?
June is not one of Rawalpindi’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rawalpindi in June?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (323 mm), the driest November (20 mm).
Does it snow in Rawalpindi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rawalpindi get in June?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rawalpindi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
