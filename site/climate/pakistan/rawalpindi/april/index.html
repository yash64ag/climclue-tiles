How April compares in Rawalpindi

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Rawalpindi. Compared with March: 5.4 °C warmer, 40 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Rawalpindi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rawalpindi in April?
In April, Rawalpindi averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Rawalpindi?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Rawalpindi’s mild months (April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rawalpindi in April?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (323 mm), the driest November (20 mm).
Does it snow in Rawalpindi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rawalpindi get in April?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rawalpindi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
