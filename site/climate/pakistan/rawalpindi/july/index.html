How July compares in Rawalpindi

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Rawalpindi. Compared with June: 1.6 °C cooler, 243 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Rawalpindi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rawalpindi in July?
In July, Rawalpindi averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 323 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Rawalpindi?
July is not one of Rawalpindi’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rawalpindi in July?
323 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (323 mm), the driest November (20 mm).
Does it snow in Rawalpindi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rawalpindi get in July?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rawalpindi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
