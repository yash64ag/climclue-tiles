Why Larkana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 303 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Larkana climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Larkana?
June, with an average of 37.3 °C (99 °F), day highs around 43.6 °C (110 °F) and night lows around 29.7 °C (85 °F).
What is the coldest month in Larkana?
January, with an average of 15.9 °C (61 °F), day highs around 22.9 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 9.4 °C (49 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Larkana?
July, with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 118 mm (4.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Larkana?
November, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Larkana?
May, with about 317 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Larkana?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Larkana?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Larkana’s GeoNames location (27.559°N, 68.212°E; ClimaClue area sq-564-2482), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 364,033 (GeoNames).
