Larkana at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Larkana is the 4th driest (118 mm a year). Compared with Dokri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Larkana is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (37.3 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 303 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Larkana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Larkana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 303 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
