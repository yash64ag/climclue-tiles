Tando Allahyar at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Tando Allahyar is 33rd warmest (26.7 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 3,374 hours of sunshine a year) and 107th wettest (184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mirpur Khas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Tando Allahyar is 0.2 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.5 °C), averages 16.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tando Allahyar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tando Allahyar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
