Mirpur Khas at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Mirpur Khas is 29th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,416 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (197 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tando Allahyar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Mirpur Khas is 0.2 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.8 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 225 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mirpur Khas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mirpur Khas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 225 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
