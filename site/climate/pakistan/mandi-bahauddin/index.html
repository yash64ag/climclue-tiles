Mandi Bahauddin at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Mandi Bahauddin is 258th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 237th sunniest (about 2,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (870 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phalia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Mandi Bahauddin is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.0 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,051 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Mandi Bahauddin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,051 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mandi Bahauddin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.587°N, 73.491°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-514-2534), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
