Lachi at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Lachi is 292nd warmest (22.6 °C year average), 295th sunniest (about 2,915 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (646 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kohat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Lachi is 0.6 °C warmer in June and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 106 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.9 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,008 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lachi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Lachi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lachi are March, April, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March, April, October and November.

Why Lachi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,008 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lachi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.383°N, 71.337°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-506-2513), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
