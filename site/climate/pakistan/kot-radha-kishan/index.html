Kot Radha Kishan at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Kot Radha Kishan is 83rd warmest (24.5 °C year average), 111th sunniest (about 2,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (542 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phool Nagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Kot Radha Kishan is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.8 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 992 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kot Radha Kishan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kot Radha Kishan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.
