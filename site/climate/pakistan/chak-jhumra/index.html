Chak Jhumra at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Chak Jhumra is 78th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 2,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (448 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Faisalabad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Chak Jhumra is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 951 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chak Jhumra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.
