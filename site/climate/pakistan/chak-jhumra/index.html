Chak Jhumra at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Chak Jhumra is 200th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 191st sunniest (about 2,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 161st wettest (454 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khurarianwala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Chak Jhumra is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.2 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 951 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Chak Jhumra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-524-2531), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
