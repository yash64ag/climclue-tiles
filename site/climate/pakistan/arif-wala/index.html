Arif Wala at a glance

Among the 134 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Pakistan, Arif Wala is 10th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,418 hours of sunshine a year) and 101st wettest (200 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dadu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (154 km away), Arif Wala is 2.0 °C cooler in May and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 15.3 °C more than its coolest, January (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Arif Wala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Arif Wala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
