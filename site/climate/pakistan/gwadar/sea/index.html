Gwadar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Balochistan · Pakistan

The sea off Gwadar averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Gwadar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gwadar?
In June, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gwadar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gwadar in February?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Gwadar?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Gwadar nearby?
In June: Jiwani (29.8 °C, 59 km), Chabahar (30.3 °C, 170 km) and Konārak (30.6 °C, 195 km).
