Dhanot at a glance

Among the 367 towns ClimaClue covers in Pakistan, Dhanot is 119th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 3,105 hours of sunshine a year) and 271st wettest (188 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lodhran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Dhanot is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (35.5 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 701 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dhanot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dhanot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 701 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dhanot itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.580°N, 71.752°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-544-2517), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
