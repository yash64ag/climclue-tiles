Nuuk at a glance

Its warmest month, July (7.5 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 2 km from Nuuk by up to 4.3 °C (7.8 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Best time to visit Nuuk

No month in Nuuk is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are June (5.1 °C, 78 mm), July (7.5 °C, 106 mm) and August (7.0 °C, 113 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (7.5 °C), August (7.0 °C) and June (5.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): April (56 mm), March (64 mm) and May (68 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): June (200 h), July (184 h) and May (177 h).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Nuuk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Nuuk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Nuuk?
July, with an average of 7.5 °C (45 °F), day highs around 10.8 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 4.1 °C (39 °F).
What is the coldest month in Nuuk?
February, with an average of -9.8 °C (14 °F), day highs around -7.1 °C (19 °F) and night lows around -12.4 °C (10 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Nuuk?
September, with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 984 mm (38.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Nuuk?
April, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Nuuk?
June, with about 200 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Nuuk?
Yes. About 434 cm (170.7 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 10 months.

How warm is the sea in Nuuk?

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nuuk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (64.183°N, 51.722°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 4.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-198-1282), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 14,798, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
