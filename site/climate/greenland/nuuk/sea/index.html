Nuuk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sermersooq · Greenland

The sea off Nuuk averages 4.1 °C (39 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.9 °C (30 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Nuuk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nuuk?
In August, at about 4.1 °C (39 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nuuk?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -0.9 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nuuk in March?
About -0.9 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nuuk?
About 1.1 °C (34 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
