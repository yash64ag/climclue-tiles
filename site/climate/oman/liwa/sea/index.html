Liwá sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Al Batinah North · Oman

The sea off Liwá averages 32.8 °C (91 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 23.5 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Liwá sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Liwá?
In July, at about 32.8 °C (91 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Liwá?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.5 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Liwá in February?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Liwá?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
