As Suwayq at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Oman, As Suwayq is the 2nd warmest (28.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,435 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (77 mm a year). Compared with Rustaq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), As Suwayq is 0.5 °C warmer in June and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 96 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (35.0 °C), averages 14.1 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of As Suwayq, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why As Suwayq has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
