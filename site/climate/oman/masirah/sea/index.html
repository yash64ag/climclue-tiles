Maşīrah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Southeastern Governorate · Oman

The sea off Maşīrah averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 23.9 °C (75 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to July and September to December.

Maşīrah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Maşīrah?
In May, at about 27.9 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Maşīrah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to July and September to December. The coolest month is January, at 23.9 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Maşīrah in January?
About 23.9 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Maşīrah?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Maşīrah nearby?
In May: Sur (28.7 °C, 224 km), Qurayyāt (29.2 °C, 290 km) and Muscat (29.8 °C, 330 km).
