Qurayyāt sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Muscat · Oman

The sea off Qurayyāt averages 30.5 °C (87 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 23.3 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Qurayyāt sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Qurayyāt?
In June, at about 30.5 °C (87 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Qurayyāt?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.3 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Qurayyāt in February?
About 23.3 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Qurayyāt?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Qurayyāt nearby?
In June: Muscat (31.4 °C, 63 km), Seeb (31.3 °C, 87 km) and As Suwayq (31.6 °C, 164 km).
