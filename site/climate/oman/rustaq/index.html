Rustaq at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Oman, Rustaq is the 2nd coolest (27.0 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 3,406 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (173 mm a year). Compared with As Suwayq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Rustaq is 0.5 °C cooler in June and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 96 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.5 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rustaq, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rustaq has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 9.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
