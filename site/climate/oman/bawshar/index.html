Bawshar at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Oman, Bawshar is the 3rd coolest (27.9 °C year average). Compared with Seeb, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Bawshar is 0.8 °C warmer in June and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bawshar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
