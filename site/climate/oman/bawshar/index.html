Bawshar at a glance

Among the 27 towns ClimaClue covers in Oman, Bawshar is 10th warmest (28.6 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,428 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (90 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Muscat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Bawshar is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.7 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bawshar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
