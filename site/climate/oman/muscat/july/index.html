How July compares in Muscat

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Muscat. Compared with June: 0.5 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Muscat in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muscat in July?
In July, Muscat averages a day high of 38.4 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 29.8 °C (86 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Muscat?
July is not one of Muscat’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muscat in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Muscat in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muscat get in July?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Muscat in July?
About 31.1 °C (88 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Muscat in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
