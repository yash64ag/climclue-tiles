How November compares in Muscat

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Muscat. Compared with October: 3.9 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 3.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Muscat in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muscat in November?
In November, Muscat averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Muscat?
November is not one of Muscat’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muscat in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Muscat in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muscat get in November?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Muscat in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
