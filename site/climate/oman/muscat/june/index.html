How June compares in Muscat

June is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Muscat. Compared with May: 1.4 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Muscat in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muscat in June?
In June, Muscat averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 29.6 °C (85 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 311 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Muscat?
June is not one of Muscat’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muscat in June?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Muscat in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muscat get in June?
About 311 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Muscat in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
