How October compares in Muscat

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Muscat. Compared with September: 1.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. November is 3.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Muscat in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muscat in October?
In October, Muscat averages a day high of 34.9 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Muscat?
October is not one of Muscat’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muscat in October?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Muscat in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muscat get in October?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Muscat in October?
About 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Muscat in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
