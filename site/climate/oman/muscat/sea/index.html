Muscat sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Oman

The sea off Muscat averages 31.4 °C (89 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 23.6 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to January.

Muscat sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Muscat?
In June, at about 31.4 °C (89 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Muscat?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to January. The coolest month is February, at 23.6 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Muscat in February?
About 23.6 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Muscat?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Muscat nearby?
In June: Şaḩam (32.4 °C, 168 km), Sohar (32.3 °C, 192 km) and Liwá (32.3 °C, 215 km).
