How May compares in Muscat

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Muscat. Compared with April: 4.3 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 1.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Muscat in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Muscat in May?
In May, Muscat averages a day high of 37.0 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 27.9 °C (82 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 339 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Muscat?
May is not one of Muscat’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Muscat in May?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (22 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Muscat in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Muscat get in May?
About 339 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Muscat in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
