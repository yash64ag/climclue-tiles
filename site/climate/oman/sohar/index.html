Sohar at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Oman, Sohar is the 3rd least sunny (about 3,419 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Şaḩam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Sohar is 0.7 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.1 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.4 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sohar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sohar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
