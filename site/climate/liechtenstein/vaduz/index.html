Vaduz at a glance

Its warmest month, July (14.6 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 294 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 18 km from Vaduz by up to 10.1 °C (18.1 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Best time to visit Vaduz

No month in Vaduz is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are June (12.3 °C, 155 mm), July (14.6 °C, 185 mm) and August (14.3 °C, 183 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (14.6 °C), August (14.3 °C) and June (12.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (88 mm), January (92 mm) and October (94 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (215 h), August (197 h) and June (187 h).

Why Vaduz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 294 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 10.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
