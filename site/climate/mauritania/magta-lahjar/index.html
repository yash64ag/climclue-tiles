Magṭa‘ Laḥjar at a glance

Among the 26 towns ClimaClue covers in Mauritania, Magṭa‘ Laḥjar is 7th warmest (30.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,145 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (171 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aleg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Magṭa‘ Laḥjar is 0.9 °C warmer in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (35.3 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 329 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Magṭa‘ Laḥjar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Magṭa‘ Laḥjar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 329 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
