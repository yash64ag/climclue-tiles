Dar Naim sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nouakchott North · Mauritania

The sea off Dar Naim averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 18.9 °C (66 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Dar Naim sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dar Naim?
In September, at about 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dar Naim?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 18.9 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dar Naim in February?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dar Naim?
About 21.9 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dar Naim nearby?
In September: Saint-Louis (28.2 °C, 240 km), Mboro (28.4 °C, 345 km) and Kayar (28.4 °C, 377 km).
