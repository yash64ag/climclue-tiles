Nouadhibou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Dakhlet Nouadhibou · Mauritania

The sea off Nouadhibou averages 22.4 °C (72 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 17.7 °C (64 °F) in January. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October.

Nouadhibou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nouadhibou?
In September, at about 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nouadhibou?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is January, at 17.7 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nouadhibou in January?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nouadhibou?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Nouadhibou nearby?
In September: Dar Naim (26.0 °C, 336 km), Nouakchott (26.0 °C, 336 km) and Saint-Louis (28.2 °C, 551 km).
