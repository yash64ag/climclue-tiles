How August compares in Nouakchott

Nouakchott in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nouakchott in August?
In August, Nouakchott averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nouakchott?
August is not one of Nouakchott’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nouakchott in August?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (27 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nouakchott in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nouakchott get in August?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nouakchott in August?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (26.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nouakchott in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
