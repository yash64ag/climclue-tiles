How January compares in Nouakchott

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nouakchott. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.
