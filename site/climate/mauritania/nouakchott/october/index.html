How October compares in Nouakchott

October is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nouakchott. Compared with September: 0.4 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. November is 2.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Nouakchott in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nouakchott in October?
In October, Nouakchott averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nouakchott?
October is not one of Nouakchott’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nouakchott in October?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (29 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nouakchott in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nouakchott get in October?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nouakchott in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
