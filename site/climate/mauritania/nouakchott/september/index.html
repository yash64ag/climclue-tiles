Nouakchott weather in September

Average September weather in Nouakchott, Mauritania: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Nouakchott averages 33.6 °C (92 °F) days and 23.9 °C (75 °F) nights, with 27 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 228 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Nouakchott

September is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nouakchott. Compared with August: 1.2 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. October is 0.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Nouakchott in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nouakchott in September?
In September, Nouakchott averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nouakchott?
September is not one of Nouakchott’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nouakchott in September?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (27 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nouakchott in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nouakchott get in September?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nouakchott in September?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (26.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nouakchott in September?
