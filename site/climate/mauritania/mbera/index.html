Mbera at a glance

Among the 26 towns ClimaClue covers in Mauritania, Mbera is 17th warmest (29.0 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 3,074 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (266 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bougadoum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (169 km away), Mbera is 0.9 °C cooler in May and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.3 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (21.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,108 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mbera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mbera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,108 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mbera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.856°N, 5.791°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-681-1742), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
