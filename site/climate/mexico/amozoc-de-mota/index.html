Amozoc de Mota weather by month and climate

Amozoc de Mota at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Amozoc de Mota is 521st warmest (16.0 °C year average), 292nd sunniest (about 2,301 hours of sunshine a year) and 308th wettest (782 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Casa Blanca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Amozoc de Mota is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.1 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 194 km from the sea.

Why Amozoc de Mota has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 194 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Amozoc de Mota itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.046°N, 98.045°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-649-819), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
