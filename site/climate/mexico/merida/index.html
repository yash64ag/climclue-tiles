Why Mérida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mérida climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mérida?
May, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 35.9 °C (97 °F) and night lows around 24.1 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mérida?
January, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.7 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 18.9 °C (66 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mérida?
June, with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,021 mm (40.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mérida?
March, with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mérida?
May, with about 258 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mérida?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mérida?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Mérida have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Mérida’s GeoNames location (20.967°N, 89.623°W; ClimaClue area sq-630-903), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,201,000 (GeoNames).
