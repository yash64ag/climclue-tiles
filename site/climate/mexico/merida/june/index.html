How June compares in Mérida

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with May: 0.5 °C cooler, 106 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Mérida in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in June?
In June, Mérida averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Mérida?
June is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in June?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (172 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in June?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
