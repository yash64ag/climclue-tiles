How July compares in Mérida

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with June: 0.5 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Mérida in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in July?
In July, Mérida averages a day high of 34.1 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mérida?
July is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in July?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (171 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in July?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
