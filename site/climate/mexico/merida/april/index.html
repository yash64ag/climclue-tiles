How April compares in Mérida

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with March: 1.7 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 1.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Mérida in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in April?
In April, Mérida averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mérida?
April is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (172 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in April?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
