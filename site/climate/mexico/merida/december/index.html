How December compares in Mérida

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mérida. Compared with November: 1.4 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Mérida in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in December?
In December, Mérida averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mérida?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Mérida’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in December?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (171 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in December?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
