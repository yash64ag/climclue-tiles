How March compares in Mérida

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with February: 1.6 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 2.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Mérida in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in March?
In March, Mérida averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mérida?
March is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in March?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (171 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in March?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
