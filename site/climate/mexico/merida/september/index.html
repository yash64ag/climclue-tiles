How September compares in Mérida

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with August: 0.5 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Mérida in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in September?
In September, Mérida averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mérida?
September is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in September?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (171 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in September?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
