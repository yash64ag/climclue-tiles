How October compares in Mérida

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with September: 1.6 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. November is 1.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Mérida in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in October?
In October, Mérida averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mérida?
October is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in October?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (172 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in October?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
