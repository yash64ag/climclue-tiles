How January compares in Mérida

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mérida. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Mérida in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in January?
In January, Mérida averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mérida?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Mérida’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in January?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (171 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in January?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
