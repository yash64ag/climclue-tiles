How May compares in Mérida

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mérida. Compared with April: 1.4 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 0.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Mérida in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mérida in May?
In May, Mérida averages a day high of 36.3 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mérida?
May is not one of Mérida’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mérida in May?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (172 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Mérida in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mérida get in May?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mérida in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
