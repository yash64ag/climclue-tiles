Abasolo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Abasolo is 331st warmest (19.9 °C year average), 278th sunniest (about 2,326 hours of sunshine a year) and 414th wettest (651 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pénjamo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Abasolo is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.0 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 292 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Abasolo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Abasolo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.450°N, 101.530°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-635-784), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
