Ciudad Guzmán at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ciudad Guzmán is 365th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 200th sunniest (about 2,476 hours of sunshine a year) and 324th wettest (764 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zapotiltic, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Ciudad Guzmán is 1.2 °C cooler in May and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 131 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.9 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad Guzmán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C. The 10 km area around Ciudad Guzmán, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.1 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ciudad Guzmán itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.704°N, 103.464°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-642-765), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
