Ciudad Guzmán at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Guzmán is 83rd warmest (18.0 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,476 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (788 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciudad de Villa de Álvarez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Ciudad Guzmán is 5.6 °C cooler in May and 7.4 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.9 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad Guzmán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
