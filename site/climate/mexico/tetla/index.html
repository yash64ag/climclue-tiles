Tetla at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Tetla is 580th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 399th sunniest (about 2,151 hours of sunshine a year) and 433rd wettest (627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Apizaco, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Tetla is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.5 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 175 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 79% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Tetla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 175 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tetla itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.442°N, 98.103°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-645-818), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
