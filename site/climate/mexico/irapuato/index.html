Irapuato at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Irapuato is 66th warmest (20.2 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (653 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salamanca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Irapuato is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.1 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 318 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Irapuato has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 318 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
