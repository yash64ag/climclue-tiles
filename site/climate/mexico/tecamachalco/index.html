Tecamachalco at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Tecamachalco is 412th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 244th sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 355th wettest (736 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Palmarito Tochapan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Tecamachalco is 0.9 °C warmer in May and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.3 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 171 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 2 km from Tecamachalco by up to 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Tecamachalco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 171 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tecamachalco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.884°N, 97.733°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-651-822), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
