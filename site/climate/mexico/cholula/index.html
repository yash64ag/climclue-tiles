Why Cholula has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 221 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cholula climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cholula?
May, with an average of 19.1 °C (66 °F), day highs around 24.5 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 12.3 °C (54 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cholula?
January, with an average of 14.3 °C (58 °F), day highs around 20.8 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 7.5 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cholula?
June, with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 806 mm (31.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cholula?
December, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cholula?
March, with about 225 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cholula?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cholula?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Cholula’s GeoNames location (19.064°N, 98.304°W; ClimaClue area sq-649-816), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 292,881 (GeoNames).
