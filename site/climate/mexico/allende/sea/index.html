Allende, Veracruz sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Veracruz · Mexico

The sea off Allende, Veracruz averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 23.6 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Allende, Veracruz sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Allende, Veracruz?
In September, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Allende, Veracruz?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.6 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Allende, Veracruz in February?
About 23.6 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Allende, Veracruz?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
