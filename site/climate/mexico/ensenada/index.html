Ensenada at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ensenada is the 5th sunniest (about 3,205 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Rosarito, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Ensenada is 1.8 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.9 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, December (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ensenada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
