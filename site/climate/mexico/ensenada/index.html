Why Ensenada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ensenada climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ensenada?
August, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 25.7 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 19.0 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ensenada?
December, with an average of 13.6 °C (56 °F), day highs around 17.8 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 9.8 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ensenada?
February, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 305 mm (12.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ensenada?
June, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ensenada?
July, with about 311 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ensenada?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ensenada?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Ensenada’s GeoNames location (31.871°N, 116.601°W; ClimaClue area sq-521-633), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 443,807 (GeoNames).
