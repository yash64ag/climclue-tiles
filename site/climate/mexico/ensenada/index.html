Ensenada at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ensenada is 429th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 3,205 hours of sunshine a year) and 599th wettest (305 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Ensenada is 0.4 °C warmer in August and about as warm in December, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.3 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, December (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ensenada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ensenada itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.871°N, 116.601°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-521-633), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
