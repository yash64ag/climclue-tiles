Tláhuac at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tláhuac is 119th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 126th sunniest (about 2,005 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (786 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xico, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Tláhuac is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.1 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 261 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 4 km from Tláhuac measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Best time to visit Tláhuac

Why Tláhuac has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 261 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tláhuac itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.287°N, 99.005°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-647-809), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
