Tláhuac at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tláhuac is 127th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 126th sunniest (about 2,005 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (893 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xico, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Tláhuac is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (17.4 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 261 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 4 km from Tláhuac measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Best time to visit Tláhuac

For mild weather, the best months to visit Tláhuac are March, April and May.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March, April and May.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (17.4 °C), June (17.3 °C) and April (16.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (6 mm), February (10 mm) and March (11 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): March (216 h), February (194 h) and January (187 h).

Why Tláhuac has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 261 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
