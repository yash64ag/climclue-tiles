Magdalena Contreras at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Magdalena Contreras is the 4th coolest (13.7 °C year average). Compared with Coyoacán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Magdalena Contreras is 1.6 °C cooler in June and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 201 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (15.9 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 272 km from the sea.

Why Magdalena Contreras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.
