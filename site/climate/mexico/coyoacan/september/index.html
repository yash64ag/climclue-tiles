How September compares in Coyoacán

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Coyoacán. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 1.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Coyoacán in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coyoacán in September?
In September, Coyoacán averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Coyoacán?
September is not one of Coyoacán’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coyoacán in September?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Coyoacán in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coyoacán get in September?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coyoacán in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
