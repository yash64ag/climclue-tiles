How March compares in Coyoacán

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Coyoacán. Compared with February: 1.6 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Coyoacán in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coyoacán in March?
In March, Coyoacán averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Coyoacán?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Coyoacán’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coyoacán in March?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Coyoacán in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coyoacán get in March?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coyoacán in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
