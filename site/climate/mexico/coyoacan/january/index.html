How January compares in Coyoacán

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Coyoacán. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Coyoacán in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coyoacán in January?
In January, Coyoacán averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Coyoacán?
January is not one of Coyoacán’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coyoacán in January?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Coyoacán in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coyoacán get in January?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coyoacán in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
