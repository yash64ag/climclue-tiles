La Cruz sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sinaloa · Mexico

The sea off La Cruz averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 20.7 °C (69 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

La Cruz sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in La Cruz?
In August, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in La Cruz?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 20.7 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in La Cruz in February?
About 20.7 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in La Cruz?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
