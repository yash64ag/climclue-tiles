Why Santa Catarina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Santa Catarina climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Santa Catarina?
June, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 30.0 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Santa Catarina?
January, with an average of 13.6 °C (56 °F), day highs around 19.9 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 9.0 °C (48 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Santa Catarina?
September, with 164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 621 mm (24.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Santa Catarina?
November, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Santa Catarina?
July, with about 201 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Santa Catarina?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Santa Catarina?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, October and November.
What type of climate does Santa Catarina have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Santa Catarina’s GeoNames location (25.673°N, 100.458°W; ClimaClue area sq-583-795), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 304,052 (GeoNames).
