Jesús María at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Jesús María is 411th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,734 hours of sunshine a year) and 500th wettest (525 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aguascalientes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Jesús María is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.8 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 315 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 1 km from Jesús María by up to 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Jesús María has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 315 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jesús María itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.961°N, 102.343°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-620-776), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
