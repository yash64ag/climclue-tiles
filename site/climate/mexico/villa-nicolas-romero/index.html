Villa Nicolás Romero at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Villa Nicolás Romero is 131st warmest (14.6 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 2,171 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (886 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuautitlán Izcalli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Villa Nicolás Romero is 1.2 °C cooler in May and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 148 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (17.1 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 128% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Villa Nicolás Romero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
