How September compares in Zapopan

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zapopan. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. October is 0.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Zapopan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zapopan in September?
In September, Zapopan averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zapopan?
September is not one of Zapopan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zapopan in September?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (229 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Zapopan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zapopan get in September?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zapopan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
