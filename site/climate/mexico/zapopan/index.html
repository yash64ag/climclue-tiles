Why Zapopan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 208 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Zapopan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zapopan?
May, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 32.7 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 17.0 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zapopan?
January, with an average of 17.3 °C (63 °F), day highs around 24.3 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 10.1 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zapopan?
July, with 229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 864 mm (34.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zapopan?
March, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zapopan?
May, with about 277 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zapopan?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Zapopan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Zapopan’s GeoNames location (20.721°N, 103.387°W; ClimaClue area sq-632-766), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,476,491 (GeoNames).
