How October compares in Zapopan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zapopan. Compared with September: 0.9 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. November is 1.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Zapopan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zapopan in October?
In October, Zapopan averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zapopan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Zapopan’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zapopan in October?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (229 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Zapopan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zapopan get in October?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zapopan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
