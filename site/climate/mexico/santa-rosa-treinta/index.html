Santa Rosa Treinta at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Santa Rosa Treinta is 107th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 381st sunniest (about 2,165 hours of sunshine a year) and 247th wettest (896 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zacatepec de Hidalgo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Santa Rosa Treinta is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.8 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 232 km from the sea.

Why Santa Rosa Treinta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 232 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
