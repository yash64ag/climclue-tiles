Ciudad General Escobedo at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad General Escobedo is 54th warmest (21.5 °C year average), 135th sunniest (about 1,816 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (572 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Monterrey, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Ciudad General Escobedo is 1.4 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 116 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 305 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ciudad General Escobedo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 305 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
