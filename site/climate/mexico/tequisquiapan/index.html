Tequisquiapan weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Tequisquiapan, Mexico (Querétaro), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Tequisquiapan at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Tequisquiapan is 391st warmest (18.6 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 2,615 hours of sunshine a year) and 563rd wettest (415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Juan del Río, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Tequisquiapan is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 112 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.9 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 271 km from the sea.

Why Tequisquiapan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 271 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tequisquiapan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.522°N, 99.892°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-634-801), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
