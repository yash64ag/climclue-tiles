Chilpancingo at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Chilpancingo is 76th warmest (18.7 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,152 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (1,283 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Iguala de la Independencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Chilpancingo is 9.2 °C cooler in May and 7.4 °C cooler in January, with 375 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.6 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Why Chilpancingo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 10.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
