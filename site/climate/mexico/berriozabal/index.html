Berriozábal at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Berriozábal is 199th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 584th sunniest (about 1,943 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (1,306 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ocozocoautla de Espinosa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Berriozábal is about as warm in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.1 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Why Berriozábal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
