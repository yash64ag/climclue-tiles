Salina Cruz at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Salina Cruz is 6th warmest (27.9 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,530 hours of sunshine a year) and 200th wettest (1,038 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santo Domingo Tehuantepec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Salina Cruz is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 107 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (26.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 16 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Salina Cruz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Salina Cruz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.175°N, 95.194°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-678-848), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
