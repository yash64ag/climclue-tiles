Yautepec at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Yautepec is 42nd warmest (23.2 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,153 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (1,114 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiutepec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Yautepec is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.1 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, January (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 246 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 6 km from Yautepec measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Yautepec has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 246 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
