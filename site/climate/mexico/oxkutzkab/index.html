Oxkutzkab at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Oxkutzkab is 86th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 159th sunniest (about 2,537 hours of sunshine a year) and 138th wettest (1,201 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ticul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Oxkutzkab is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.5 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 113 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station under 1 km from Oxkutzkab by up to 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Oxkutzkab has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 113 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Oxkutzkab itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.307°N, 89.418°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-636-905), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
