How September compares in Victoria de Durango

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Victoria de Durango. Compared with August: 1.0 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. October is 1.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Victoria de Durango in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in September?
In September, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in September?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in September?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
