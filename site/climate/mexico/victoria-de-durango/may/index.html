How May compares in Victoria de Durango

Victoria de Durango in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in May?
In May, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months (March, April, May, June, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in May?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (107 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in May?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
