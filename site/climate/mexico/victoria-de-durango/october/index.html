How October compares in Victoria de Durango

Victoria de Durango in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in October?
In October, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 10.7 °C (51 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in October?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in October?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
