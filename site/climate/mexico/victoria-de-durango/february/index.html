How February compares in Victoria de Durango

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Victoria de Durango. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 2.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Victoria de Durango in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in February?
In February, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
February is not one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in February?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
