How June compares in Victoria de Durango

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Victoria de Durango. Compared with May: 1.1 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. July is 1.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Victoria de Durango in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in June?
In June, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in June?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in June?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
