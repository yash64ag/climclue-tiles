How March compares in Victoria de Durango

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Victoria de Durango. Compared with February: 2.5 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 2.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Victoria de Durango in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in March?
In March, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months (March, April, May, June, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in March?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (107 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in March?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
