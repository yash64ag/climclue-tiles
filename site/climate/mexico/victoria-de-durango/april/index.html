How April compares in Victoria de Durango

Victoria de Durango in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in April?
In April, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in April?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
