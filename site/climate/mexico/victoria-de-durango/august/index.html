Victoria de Durango in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria de Durango in August?
In August, Victoria de Durango averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Victoria de Durango?
August is not one of Victoria de Durango’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria de Durango in August?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria de Durango in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria de Durango get in August?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria de Durango in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
