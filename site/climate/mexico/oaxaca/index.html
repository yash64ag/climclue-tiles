Why Oaxaca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Oaxaca climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Oaxaca?
April, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 29.6 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 13.8 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Oaxaca?
January, with an average of 17.9 °C (64 °F), day highs around 25.4 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 11.0 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Oaxaca?
June, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 604 mm (23.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Oaxaca?
January, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Oaxaca?
February, with about 210 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Oaxaca?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Oaxaca?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, July, August, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Oaxaca have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Oaxaca’s GeoNames location (17.060°N, 96.725°W; ClimaClue area sq-669-832), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 255,029 (GeoNames).
