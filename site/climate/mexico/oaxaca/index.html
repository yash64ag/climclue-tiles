Oaxaca at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Oaxaca is 70th warmest (19.9 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 1,991 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (604 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tuxtepec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (131 km away), Oaxaca is 6.2 °C cooler in April and 4.2 °C cooler in January, with 2,021 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (21.6 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 144 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 88% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Oaxaca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
