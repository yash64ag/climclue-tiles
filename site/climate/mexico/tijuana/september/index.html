Tijuana weather in September

Average September weather in Tijuana, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Tijuana averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) days and 15.4 °C (60 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 253 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Tijuana

September is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tijuana. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. October is 1.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Tijuana in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tijuana in September?
In September, Tijuana averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tijuana?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Tijuana’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tijuana in September?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (62 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tijuana in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tijuana get in September?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tijuana in September?
About 20.0 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (20.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tijuana in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
