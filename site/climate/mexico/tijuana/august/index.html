How August compares in Tijuana

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tijuana. Compared with July: 0.8 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 0.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Tijuana in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tijuana in August?
In August, Tijuana averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tijuana?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Tijuana’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tijuana in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (62 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tijuana in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tijuana get in August?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tijuana in August?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (20.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tijuana in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
