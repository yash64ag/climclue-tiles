How October compares in Tijuana

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tijuana. Compared with September: 1.7 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. November is 2.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Tijuana in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tijuana in October?
In October, Tijuana averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tijuana?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Tijuana’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tijuana in October?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (58 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tijuana in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tijuana get in October?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tijuana in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
