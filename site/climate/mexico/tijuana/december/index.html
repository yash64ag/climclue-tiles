Tijuana weather in December

Average December weather in Tijuana, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Tijuana averages 19.8 °C (68 °F) days and 7.3 °C (45 °F) nights, with 44 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 230 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Tijuana

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tijuana. Compared with November: 2.4 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Tijuana in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tijuana in December?
In December, Tijuana averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tijuana?
December is not one of Tijuana’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tijuana in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (62 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tijuana in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tijuana get in December?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tijuana in December?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (20.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tijuana in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
