How June compares in Tijuana

June is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tijuana. Compared with May: 1.4 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Tijuana in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tijuana in June?
In June, Tijuana averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tijuana?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tijuana’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tijuana in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (58 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tijuana in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tijuana get in June?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tijuana in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
