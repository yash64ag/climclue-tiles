Lerdo de Tejada at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Lerdo de Tejada is 108th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 441st sunniest (about 2,123 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (2,021 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santiago Tuxtla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Lerdo de Tejada is 1.1 °C warmer in May and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 887 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.5 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (21.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Lerdo de Tejada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lerdo de Tejada itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.629°N, 95.520°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-653-844), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
