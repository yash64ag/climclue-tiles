Tlalnepantla weather in September

Average September weather in Tlalnepantla, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Tlalnepantla averages 22.9 °C (73 °F) days and 10.6 °C (51 °F) nights, with 116 mm (4.6 in) of rain over the month and about 136 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Tlalnepantla

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tlalnepantla. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. October is 1.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Tlalnepantla in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalnepantla in September?
In September, Tlalnepantla averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tlalnepantla?
September is not one of Tlalnepantla’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalnepantla in September?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalnepantla in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalnepantla get in September?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
