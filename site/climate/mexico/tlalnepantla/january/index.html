How January compares in Tlalnepantla

Tlalnepantla in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalnepantla in January?
In January, Tlalnepantla averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tlalnepantla?
January is not one of Tlalnepantla’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalnepantla in January?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalnepantla in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalnepantla get in January?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalnepantla in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
