Acatlán de Osorio at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Acatlán de Osorio is 179th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 389th sunniest (about 2,158 hours of sunshine a year) and 471st wettest (573 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciudad de Huajuapan de León, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Acatlán de Osorio is 4.0 °C warmer in May and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.8 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 217 km from the sea.

Why Acatlán de Osorio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Acatlán de Osorio itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.202°N, 98.049°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-657-819), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
