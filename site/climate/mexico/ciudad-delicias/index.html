Ciudad Delicias at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ciudad Delicias is 279th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,904 hours of sunshine a year) and 598th wettest (310 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pedro Meoqui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ciudad Delicias is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 429 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad Delicias has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ciudad Delicias itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.190°N, 105.470°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-558-745), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
