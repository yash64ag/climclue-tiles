Why Ciudad Delicias has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

When is the best time for mild weather in Ciudad Delicias?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Ciudad Delicias’s GeoNames location (28.190°N, 105.470°W; ClimaClue area sq-558-745), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 148,045 (GeoNames).
