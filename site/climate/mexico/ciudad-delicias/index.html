Ciudad Delicias at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Delicias is 58th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,904 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (319 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chihuahua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Ciudad Delicias is 2.3 °C warmer in June and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (11.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 429 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad Delicias has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
