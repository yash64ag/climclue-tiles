San Francisco Coacalco at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, San Francisco Coacalco is 104th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 82nd sunniest (about 2,143 hours of sunshine a year) and 84th wettest (650 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pablo de las Salinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), San Francisco Coacalco is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.1 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 246 km from the sea.

Why San Francisco Coacalco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 246 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
