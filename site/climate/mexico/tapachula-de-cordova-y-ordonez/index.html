Tapachula de Córdova y Ordóñez at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tapachula de Córdova y Ordóñez is the wettest (3,661 mm a year). Compared with Comitán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (148 km away), Tapachula de Córdova y Ordóñez is 6.7 °C warmer in April and 7.7 °C warmer in December, with 2,183 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.4 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, December (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Why Tapachula de Córdova y Ordóñez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
