Técpan de Galeana at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Técpan de Galeana is 11th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 235th sunniest (about 2,415 hours of sunshine a year) and 167th wettest (1,116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Atoyac de Álvarez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Técpan de Galeana is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 200 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.8 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.5 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 97% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Técpan de Galeana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Técpan de Galeana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.223°N, 100.630°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-667-793), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
