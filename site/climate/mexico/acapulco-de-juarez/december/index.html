How December compares in Acapulco de Juárez

Acapulco de Juárez in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Acapulco de Juárez in December?
In December, Acapulco de Juárez averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Acapulco de Juárez?
No month in Acapulco de Juárez is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Acapulco de Juárez in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (291 mm), the driest April (1 mm).
Does it snow in Acapulco de Juárez in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Acapulco de Juárez get in December?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Acapulco de Juárez in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
