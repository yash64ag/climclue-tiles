How September compares in Acapulco de Juárez

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Acapulco de Juárez. Compared with August: 0.5 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Acapulco de Juárez in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Acapulco de Juárez in September?
In September, Acapulco de Juárez averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 291 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Acapulco de Juárez?
No month in Acapulco de Juárez is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Acapulco de Juárez in September?
291 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (291 mm), the driest April (1 mm).
Does it snow in Acapulco de Juárez in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Acapulco de Juárez get in September?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Acapulco de Juárez in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
