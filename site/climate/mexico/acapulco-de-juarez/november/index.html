Acapulco de Juárez in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Acapulco de Juárez in November?
In November, Acapulco de Juárez averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Acapulco de Juárez?
No month in Acapulco de Juárez is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Acapulco de Juárez in November?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (291 mm), the driest April (1 mm).
Does it snow in Acapulco de Juárez in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Acapulco de Juárez get in November?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Acapulco de Juárez in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
