Ario de Rosales at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ario de Rosales is 477th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 324th sunniest (about 2,245 hours of sunshine a year) and 90th wettest (1,460 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tacámbaro de Codallos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Ario de Rosales is 2.1 °C cooler in May and 2.5 °C cooler in January, with 354 mm more precipitation a year. Why Ario de Rosales has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 157 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ario de Rosales itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.208°N, 101.709°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-647-782), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
