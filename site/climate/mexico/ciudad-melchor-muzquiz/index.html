Ciudad Melchor Múzquiz at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ciudad Melchor Múzquiz is 262nd warmest (21.3 °C year average), 263rd sunniest (about 2,353 hours of sunshine a year) and 461st wettest (588 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Palaú, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ciudad Melchor Múzquiz is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 417 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad Melchor Múzquiz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 417 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ciudad Melchor Múzquiz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.879°N, 101.516°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
