Monclova at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Monclova is 259th warmest (21.3 °C year average), 355th sunniest (about 2,178 hours of sunshine a year) and 571st wettest (378 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Frontera, Coahuila, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Monclova is 1.1 °C cooler in June and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.3 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 396 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 16 km from Monclova by up to 4.6 °C (8.3 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Monclova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 396 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C. The 10 km area around Monclova, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.6 °C a year, 1.4 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Monclova itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.907°N, 101.421°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-570-785), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
