Ciudad Apodaca at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Apodaca is the 5th least sunny (about 1,792 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with San Nicolás de los Garza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Ciudad Apodaca is 1.4 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 45 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.9 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 286 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, under 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ciudad Apodaca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 286 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
