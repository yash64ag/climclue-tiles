Gómez Palacio at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Gómez Palacio is the 5th driest (271 mm a year). Compared with Victoria de Durango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (208 km away), Gómez Palacio is 6.7 °C warmer in June and 3.6 °C warmer in January, with 211 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (14.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 405 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 4 km from Gómez Palacio measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Gómez Palacio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 405 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
