Torreón in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Torreón in June?
In June, Torreón averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Torreón?
June is not one of Torreón’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Torreón in June?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (51 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Torreón in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Torreón get in June?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Torreón in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
