Torreón in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Torreón in July?
In July, Torreón averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Torreón?
July is not one of Torreón’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Torreón in July?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (51 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Torreón in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Torreón get in July?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Torreón in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
