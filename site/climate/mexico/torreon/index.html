Torreón at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Torreón is 204th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 196th sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 607th wettest (262 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gómez Palacio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Torreón is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.3 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 405 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Torreón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 405 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Torreón itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.544°N, 103.419°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-584-765), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
