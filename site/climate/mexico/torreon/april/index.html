Torreón in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Torreón in April?
In April, Torreón averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Torreón?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Torreón’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Torreón in April?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (51 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Torreón in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Torreón get in April?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Torreón in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
