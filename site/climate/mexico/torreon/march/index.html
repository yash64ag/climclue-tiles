Torreón in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Torreón in March?
In March, Torreón averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Torreón?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Torreón’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Torreón in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (51 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Torreón in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Torreón get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Torreón in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
