How May compares in Torreón

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Torreón. Compared with April: 3.1 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. June is 1.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Torreón in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Torreón in May?
In May, Torreón averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Torreón?
May is not one of Torreón’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Torreón in May?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (51 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Torreón in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Torreón get in May?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Torreón in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
