Martínez de la Torre at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Martínez de la Torre is 173rd warmest (24.0 °C year average), 585th sunniest (about 1,941 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,914 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Villa Independencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Martínez de la Torre is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.4 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, January (19.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Martínez de la Torre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Martínez de la Torre itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.071°N, 97.061°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-639-829), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
