Santiago de Querétaro in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Querétaro in July?
In July, Santiago de Querétaro averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Santiago de Querétaro?
July is not one of Santiago de Querétaro’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Querétaro in July?
131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Querétaro in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Querétaro get in July?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Querétaro in July?
