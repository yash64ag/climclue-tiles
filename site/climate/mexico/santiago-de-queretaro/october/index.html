How October compares in Santiago de Querétaro

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Santiago de Querétaro. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. November is 1.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Santiago de Querétaro in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Querétaro in October?
In October, Santiago de Querétaro averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Santiago de Querétaro?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Santiago de Querétaro’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Querétaro in October?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Querétaro in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Querétaro get in October?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Querétaro in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
