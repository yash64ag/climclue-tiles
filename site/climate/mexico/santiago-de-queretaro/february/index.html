How February compares in Santiago de Querétaro

Santiago de Querétaro in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Querétaro in February?
In February, Santiago de Querétaro averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Santiago de Querétaro?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Santiago de Querétaro’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Querétaro in February?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Querétaro in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Querétaro get in February?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Querétaro in February?
