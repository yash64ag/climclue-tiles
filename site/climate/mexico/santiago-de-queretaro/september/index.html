Santiago de Querétaro weather in September

Average September weather in Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Santiago de Querétaro averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) days and 13.5 °C (56 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain over the month and about 179 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Santiago de Querétaro

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santiago de Querétaro. Compared with August: 0.7 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Santiago de Querétaro in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Querétaro in September?
In September, Santiago de Querétaro averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Santiago de Querétaro?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Santiago de Querétaro’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Querétaro in September?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (108 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Querétaro in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Querétaro get in September?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Querétaro in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
