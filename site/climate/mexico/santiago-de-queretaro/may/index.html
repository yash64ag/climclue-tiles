How May compares in Santiago de Querétaro

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Santiago de Querétaro. Compared with April: 1.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. June is 0.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Santiago de Querétaro in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Querétaro in May?
In May, Santiago de Querétaro averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Santiago de Querétaro?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Santiago de Querétaro’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Querétaro in May?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (108 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Querétaro in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Querétaro get in May?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Querétaro in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
