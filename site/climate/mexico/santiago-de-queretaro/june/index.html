How June compares in Santiago de Querétaro

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Santiago de Querétaro. Compared with May: 0.7 °C cooler, 57 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. July is 1.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Santiago de Querétaro in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Querétaro in June?
In June, Santiago de Querétaro averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Santiago de Querétaro?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Santiago de Querétaro’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Querétaro in June?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Querétaro in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Querétaro get in June?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Querétaro in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
