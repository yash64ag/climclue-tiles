How January compares in Cancún

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cancún. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Cancún in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cancún in January?
In January, Cancún averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cancún?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Cancún’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cancún in January?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (214 mm), the driest April (48 mm).
Does it snow in Cancún in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cancún get in January?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cancún in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
