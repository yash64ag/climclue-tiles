Cancún sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Quintana Roo · Mexico

The sea off Cancún averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 26.0 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Cancún sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cancún?
In August, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cancún?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.0 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cancún in February?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Cancún?
About 27.5 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Cancún nearby?
In August: Viñales (29.6 °C, 362 km), Nueva Gerona (30.4 °C, 425 km) and Bahía Honda (29.6 °C, 426 km).
