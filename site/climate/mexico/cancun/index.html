Cancún at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Cancún is 12th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,508 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (1,405 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Playa del Carmen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Cancún is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 45 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Cancún

For mild weather, the best months to visit Cancún are January, February, March and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (27.6 °C), July (27.6 °C) and June (27.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): April (48 mm), February (58 mm) and March (64 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (246 h), April (238 h) and March (229 h).

Why Cancún has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
