How December compares in Cancún

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Cancún. Compared with November: 1.3 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Cancún in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cancún in December?
In December, Cancún averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cancún?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Cancún’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cancún in December?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (214 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Cancún in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cancún get in December?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cancún in December?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cancún in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
