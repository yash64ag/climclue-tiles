How November compares in Cancún

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Cancún. Compared with October: 1.1 °C cooler, 96 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. December is 1.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Cancún in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cancún in November?
In November, Cancún averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cancún?
November is not one of Cancún’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cancún in November?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (214 mm), the driest April (48 mm).
Does it snow in Cancún in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cancún get in November?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cancún in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
