How May compares in Cancún

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Cancún. Compared with April: 1.1 °C warmer, 56 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. June is 0.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Cancún in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cancún in May?
In May, Cancún averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cancún?
May is not one of Cancún’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cancún in May?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (214 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Cancún in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cancún get in May?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cancún in May?
About 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cancún in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
