Zacapu at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Zacapu is 444th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 288th sunniest (about 2,306 hours of sunshine a year) and 232nd wettest (950 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Purépero de Echáiz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Zacapu is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.1 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (14.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 223 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zacapu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 223 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zacapu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.815°N, 101.792°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-641-782), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
