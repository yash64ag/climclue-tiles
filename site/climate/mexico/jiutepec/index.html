Jiutepec at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Jiutepec is 50th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 81st sunniest (about 2,145 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (1,163 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuernavaca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Jiutepec is 3.6 °C warmer in May and 3.1 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.4 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (19.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.5 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 242 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Jiutepec has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 242 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
