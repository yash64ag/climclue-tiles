Toluca at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Toluca is the 2nd coolest (12.8 °C year average). Compared with Huixquilucan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Toluca is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 121 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (14.8 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 266 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, under 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 100% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Toluca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 266 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
