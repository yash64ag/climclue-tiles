Iztacalco at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Iztacalco is 115th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 124th sunniest (about 2,013 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (777 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mexico City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Iztacalco is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.3 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 254 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 2 km from Iztacalco measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Iztacalco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 254 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
