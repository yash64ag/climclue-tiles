Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Heróica Zitácuaro is 432nd warmest (17.4 °C year average), 473rd sunniest (about 2,098 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (1,299 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Heróica Zitácuaro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 245 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
