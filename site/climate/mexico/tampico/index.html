Why Tampico has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tampico climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tampico?
August, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tampico?
January, with an average of 19.9 °C (68 °F), day highs around 22.4 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tampico?
September, with 296 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,165 mm (45.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tampico?
March, with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tampico?
July, with about 220 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tampico?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tampico?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.
What type of climate does Tampico have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Tampico’s GeoNames location (22.285°N, 97.878°W; ClimaClue area sq-617-821), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 309,003 (GeoNames).
