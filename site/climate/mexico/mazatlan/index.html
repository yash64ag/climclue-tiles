Mazatlán at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Mazatlán is 21st warmest (24.7 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,738 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (779 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Victoria de Durango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (200 km away), Mazatlán is 7.5 °C warmer in July and 9.4 °C warmer in January, with 297 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mazatlán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mazatlán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
