San Pablo Autopan at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, San Pablo Autopan is 605th warmest (13.3 °C year average), 512th sunniest (about 2,077 hours of sunshine a year) and 205th wettest (1,028 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Andrés Cuexcontitlán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), San Pablo Autopan is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (15.3 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 277 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 131% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why San Pablo Autopan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 277 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Pablo Autopan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.357°N, 99.657°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-646-803), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
