Cerro Azul at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Cerro Azul is 189th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 542nd sunniest (about 2,040 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (1,390 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naranjos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Cerro Azul is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 73 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.3 °C), averages 8.8 °C more than its coolest, January (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 83% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Cerro Azul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cerro Azul itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.192°N, 97.741°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-822), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
