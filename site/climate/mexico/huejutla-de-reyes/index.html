Huejutla de Reyes at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Huejutla de Reyes is 164th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 440th sunniest (about 2,124 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (1,538 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tantoyuca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Huejutla de Reyes is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.3 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Why Huejutla de Reyes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huejutla de Reyes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.140°N, 98.419°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
