San Luis Río Colorado at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, San Luis Río Colorado is the sunniest (about 3,832 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (71 mm a year). Compared with Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), San Luis Río Colorado is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in December, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.1 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, December (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Why San Luis Río Colorado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Luis Río Colorado itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.456°N, 114.770°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-515-652), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
