Felipe Carrillo Puerto at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Felipe Carrillo Puerto is 64th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 195th sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 107th wettest (1,330 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tulum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Felipe Carrillo Puerto is 1.3 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.2 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Why Felipe Carrillo Puerto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Felipe Carrillo Puerto itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.578°N, 88.045°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-644-919), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
