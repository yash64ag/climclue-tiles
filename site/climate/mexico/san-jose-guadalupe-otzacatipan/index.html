San José Guadalupe Otzacatipan at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, San José Guadalupe Otzacatipan is 602nd warmest (13.2 °C year average), 517th sunniest (about 2,070 hours of sunshine a year) and 204th wettest (1,030 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Mateo Otzacatipan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), San José Guadalupe Otzacatipan is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (15.3 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 280 km from the sea.

Why San José Guadalupe Otzacatipan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 280 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San José Guadalupe Otzacatipan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.345°N, 99.592°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-646-804), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
