How October compares in Reynosa

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Reynosa. Compared with September: 3.1 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. November is 4.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Reynosa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Reynosa in October?
In October, Reynosa averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Reynosa?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Reynosa’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Reynosa in October?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (105 mm), the driest March (23 mm).
Does it snow in Reynosa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Reynosa get in October?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Reynosa in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
