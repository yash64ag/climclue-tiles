How January compares in Reynosa

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Reynosa. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Reynosa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Reynosa in January?
In January, Reynosa averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Reynosa?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Reynosa’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Reynosa in January?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (105 mm), the driest March (23 mm).
Does it snow in Reynosa in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Reynosa get in January?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Reynosa in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
