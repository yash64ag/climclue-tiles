How September compares in Reynosa

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Reynosa. Compared with August: 2.3 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. October is 3.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Reynosa in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Reynosa in September?
In September, Reynosa averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Reynosa?
September is not one of Reynosa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Reynosa in September?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (103 mm), the driest March (23 mm).
Does it snow in Reynosa in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Reynosa get in September?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Reynosa in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
