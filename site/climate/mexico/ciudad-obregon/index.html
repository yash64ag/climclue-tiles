Ciudad Obregón at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Obregón is 24th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,829 hours of sunshine a year) and 125th wettest (396 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Navojoa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Ciudad Obregón is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 72 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.6 °C), averages 14.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ciudad Obregón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
