Poza Rica de Hidalgo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Poza Rica de Hidalgo is 125th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 578th sunniest (about 1,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 101st wettest (1,371 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Coatzintla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Poza Rica de Hidalgo is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Why Poza Rica de Hidalgo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Poza Rica de Hidalgo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.533°N, 97.459°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-634-825), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
