Why San Juan del Río has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 288 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

San Juan del Río climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in San Juan del Río?
May, with an average of 20.3 °C (68 °F), day highs around 26.8 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 12.7 °C (55 °F).
What is the coldest month in San Juan del Río?
January, with an average of 13.6 °C (56 °F), day highs around 21.0 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 6.8 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in San Juan del Río?
July, with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 570 mm (22.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in San Juan del Río?
December, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in San Juan del Río?
March, with about 247 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in San Juan del Río?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in San Juan del Río?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains San Juan del Río’s GeoNames location (20.389°N, 99.996°W; ClimaClue area sq-636-800), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 138,878 (GeoNames).
