Ciudad Juárez at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ciudad Juárez is the 4th sunniest (about 3,671 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nuevo Casas Grandes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (200 km away), Ciudad Juárez is 3.2 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C cooler in December, with 83 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, December (7.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 600 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Ciudad Juárez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 600 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ciudad Juárez itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.720°N, 106.461°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-522-735), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
