Ciudad Juárez at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Juárez is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,671 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (239 mm a year). Compared with Chihuahua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (345 km away), Ciudad Juárez is 3.0 °C warmer in July and 3.1 °C cooler in December, with 138 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, December (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 600 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 97% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ciudad Juárez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 600 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
