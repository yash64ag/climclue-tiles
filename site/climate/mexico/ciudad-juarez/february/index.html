How February compares in Ciudad Juárez

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Juárez. Compared with January: 3.0 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 3.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Ciudad Juárez in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Juárez in February?
In February, Ciudad Juárez averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ciudad Juárez?
February is not one of Ciudad Juárez’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Juárez in February?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Juárez in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Juárez get in February?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Juárez in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
