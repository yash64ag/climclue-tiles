How November compares in Ciudad Juárez

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Juárez. Compared with October: 6.8 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Ciudad Juárez in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Juárez in November?
In November, Ciudad Juárez averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ciudad Juárez?
November is not one of Ciudad Juárez’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Juárez in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Juárez in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Juárez get in November?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Juárez in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
