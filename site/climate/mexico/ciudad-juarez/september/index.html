How September compares in Ciudad Juárez

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Juárez. Compared with August: 2.9 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. October is 5.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Ciudad Juárez in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Juárez in September?
In September, Ciudad Juárez averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ciudad Juárez?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Ciudad Juárez’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Juárez in September?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Juárez in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Juárez get in September?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Juárez in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
