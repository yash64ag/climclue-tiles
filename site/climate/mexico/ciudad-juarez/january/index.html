How January compares in Ciudad Juárez

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Juárez. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 3.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Ciudad Juárez in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Juárez in January?
In January, Ciudad Juárez averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ciudad Juárez?
January is not one of Ciudad Juárez’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Juárez in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Juárez in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Juárez get in January?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Juárez in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
