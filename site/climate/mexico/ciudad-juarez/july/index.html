How July compares in Ciudad Juárez

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ciudad Juárez. Compared with June: 0.4 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Ciudad Juárez in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Juárez in July?
In July, Ciudad Juárez averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 352 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ciudad Juárez?
July is not one of Ciudad Juárez’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Juárez in July?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (49 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Juárez in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Juárez get in July?
About 352 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Juárez in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
