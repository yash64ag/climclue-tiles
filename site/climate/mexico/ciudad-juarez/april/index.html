Ciudad Juárez in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Juárez in April?
In April, Ciudad Juárez averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 340 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ciudad Juárez?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ciudad Juárez’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Juárez in April?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (49 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Juárez in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Juárez get in April?
About 340 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Juárez in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
