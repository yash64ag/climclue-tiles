How September compares in Monterrey

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Monterrey. Compared with August: 2.8 °C cooler, 97 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. October is 3.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Monterrey in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monterrey in September?
In September, Monterrey averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Monterrey?
September is not one of Monterrey’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monterrey in September?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest November (18 mm).
Does it snow in Monterrey in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monterrey get in September?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Monterrey in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
