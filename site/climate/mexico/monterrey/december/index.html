How December compares in Monterrey

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Monterrey. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Monterrey in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monterrey in December?
In December, Monterrey averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Monterrey?
December is not one of Monterrey’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monterrey in December?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (186 mm), the driest November (18 mm).
Does it snow in Monterrey in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monterrey get in December?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Monterrey in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
