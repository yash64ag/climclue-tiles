Why Monterrey has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 302 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Monterrey climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Monterrey?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 20.6 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Monterrey?
January, with an average of 13.3 °C (56 °F), day highs around 19.7 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 8.7 °C (48 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Monterrey?
September, with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 688 mm (27.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Monterrey?
November, with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Monterrey?
July, with about 202 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Monterrey?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Monterrey?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, October and November.
What type of climate does Monterrey have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Monterrey’s GeoNames location (25.684°N, 100.317°W; ClimaClue area sq-583-796), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,135,512 (GeoNames).
