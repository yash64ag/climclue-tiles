Monterrey weather in May

Average May weather in Monterrey, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Monterrey averages 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 19.8 °C (68 °F) nights, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 171 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Monterrey

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Monterrey. Compared with April: 2.8 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. June is 1.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Monterrey in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monterrey in May?
In May, Monterrey averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Monterrey?
May is not one of Monterrey’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monterrey in May?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest November (18 mm).
Does it snow in Monterrey in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monterrey get in May?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Monterrey in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
