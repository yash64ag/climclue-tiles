How March compares in Hermosillo

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hermosillo. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 3.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Hermosillo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hermosillo in March?
In March, Hermosillo averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hermosillo?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Hermosillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hermosillo in March?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (87 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hermosillo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hermosillo get in March?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hermosillo in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
