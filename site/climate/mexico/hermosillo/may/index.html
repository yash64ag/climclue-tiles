How May compares in Hermosillo

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hermosillo. Compared with April: 3.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Hermosillo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hermosillo in May?
In May, Hermosillo averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 318 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hermosillo?
May is not one of Hermosillo’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hermosillo in May?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (91 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hermosillo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hermosillo get in May?
About 318 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hermosillo in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
