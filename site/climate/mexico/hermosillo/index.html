Hermosillo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Hermosillo is 166th warmest (24.2 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 3,083 hours of sunshine a year) and 585th wettest (342 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miguel Alemán (La Doce), the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Hermosillo is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 131 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.0 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, December (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 90% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hermosillo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hermosillo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.089°N, 110.967°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-549-690), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
