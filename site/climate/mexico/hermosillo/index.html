Hermosillo at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Hermosillo is 35th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,083 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (354 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Heroica Guaymas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (130 km away), Hermosillo is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 2.0 °C cooler in December, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.9 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, December (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 93% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hermosillo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
