How November compares in Hermosillo

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hermosillo. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Hermosillo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hermosillo in November?
In November, Hermosillo averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hermosillo?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Hermosillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hermosillo in November?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (91 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hermosillo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hermosillo get in November?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hermosillo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
