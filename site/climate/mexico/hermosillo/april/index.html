Hermosillo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hermosillo in April?
In April, Hermosillo averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hermosillo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Hermosillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hermosillo in April?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (91 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hermosillo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hermosillo get in April?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hermosillo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
