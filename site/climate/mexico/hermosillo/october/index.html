How October compares in Hermosillo

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Hermosillo. Compared with September: 4.4 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. November is 5.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Hermosillo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hermosillo in October?
In October, Hermosillo averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hermosillo?
October is not one of Hermosillo’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hermosillo in October?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (91 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hermosillo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hermosillo get in October?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hermosillo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
