How March compares in Tlalpan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tlalpan. Compared with February: 1.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. April is 1.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Tlalpan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in March?
In March, Tlalpan averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tlalpan?
March is not one of Tlalpan’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in March?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in March?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
