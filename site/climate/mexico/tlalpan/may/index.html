How May compares in Tlalpan

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tlalpan. Compared with April: 0.6 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. June is about as warm as May on average.

Tlalpan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in May?
In May, Tlalpan averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tlalpan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Tlalpan’s mild months (April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in May?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in May?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
