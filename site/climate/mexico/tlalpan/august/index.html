Tlalpan weather in August

Average August weather in Tlalpan, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Tlalpan averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) days and 9.5 °C (49 °F) nights, with 186 mm (7.3 in) of rain over the month and about 155 hours of sunshine.

Tlalpan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in August?
In August, Tlalpan averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tlalpan?
August is not one of Tlalpan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in August?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (188 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in August?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
