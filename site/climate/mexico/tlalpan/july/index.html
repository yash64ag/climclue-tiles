Tlalpan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in July?
In July, Tlalpan averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tlalpan?
July is not one of Tlalpan’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in July?
216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in July?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
