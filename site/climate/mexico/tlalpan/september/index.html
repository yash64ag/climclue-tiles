How September compares in Tlalpan

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tlalpan. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 1.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Tlalpan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in September?
In September, Tlalpan averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tlalpan?
September is not one of Tlalpan’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in September?
178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in September?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
