How April compares in Tlalpan

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tlalpan. Compared with March: 1.6 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Tlalpan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in April?
In April, Tlalpan averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tlalpan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Tlalpan’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in April?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (188 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in April?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
