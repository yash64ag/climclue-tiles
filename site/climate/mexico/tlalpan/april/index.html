Tlalpan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in April?
In April, Tlalpan averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tlalpan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Tlalpan’s mild months (April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in April?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
