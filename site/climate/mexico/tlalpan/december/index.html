How December compares in Tlalpan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tlalpan. Compared with November: 1.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Tlalpan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlalpan in December?
In December, Tlalpan averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tlalpan?
December is not one of Tlalpan’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlalpan in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (216 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tlalpan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlalpan get in December?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlalpan in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
