Nueva Rosita at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Nueva Rosita is 209th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 279th sunniest (about 2,322 hours of sunshine a year) and 526th wettest (481 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sabinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Nueva Rosita is 0.4 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (30.0 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 391 km from the sea.

Why Nueva Rosita has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 391 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nueva Rosita itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.937°N, 101.217°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-560-787), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
