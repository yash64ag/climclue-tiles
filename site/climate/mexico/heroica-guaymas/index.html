Heroica Guaymas at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Heroica Guaymas is 30th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 134th wettest (284 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciudad Obregón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Heroica Guaymas is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 112 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (31.4 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Heroica Guaymas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Heroica Guaymas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
