Champotón sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Campeche · Mexico

The sea off Champotón averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 24.0 °C (75 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Champotón sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Champotón?
In June, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Champotón?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 24.0 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Champotón in January?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Champotón?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
