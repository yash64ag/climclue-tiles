How April compares in Mexico City

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mexico City. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. May is 0.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Mexico City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexico City in April?
In April, Mexico City averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mexico City?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mexico City’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexico City in April?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (149 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mexico City in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexico City get in April?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexico City in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
