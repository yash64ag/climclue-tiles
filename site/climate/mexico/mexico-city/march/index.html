How March compares in Mexico City

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mexico City. Compared with February: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Mexico City in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexico City in March?
In March, Mexico City averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mexico City?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Mexico City’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexico City in March?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (149 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mexico City in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexico City get in March?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexico City in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
