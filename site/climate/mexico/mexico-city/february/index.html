How February compares in Mexico City

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Mexico City. Compared with January: 1.7 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. March is 2.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Mexico City in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexico City in February?
In February, Mexico City averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mexico City?
February is not one of Mexico City’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexico City in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (149 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mexico City in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexico City get in February?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexico City in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
