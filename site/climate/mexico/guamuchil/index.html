Guamúchil at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Guamúchil is 113th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 2,609 hours of sunshine a year) and 441st wettest (619 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guasave, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Guamúchil is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.0 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 52 km from the sea.

Why Guamúchil has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 52 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Guamúchil itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.459°N, 108.077°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-585-719), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
