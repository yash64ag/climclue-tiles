Tepexpan at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tepexpan is 100th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,196 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (521 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ojo de Agua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Tepexpan is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 227 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 3 km from Tepexpan measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Tepexpan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 227 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
