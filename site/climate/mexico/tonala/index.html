Tonalá at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tonalá is 60th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,640 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (883 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guadalajara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Tonalá is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.4 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 219 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 90% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tonalá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 219 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
