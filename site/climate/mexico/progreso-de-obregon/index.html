Progreso de Obregón at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Progreso de Obregón is 430th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 168th sunniest (about 2,524 hours of sunshine a year) and 562nd wettest (417 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mixquiahuala de Juárez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Progreso de Obregón is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.4 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Why Progreso de Obregón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
