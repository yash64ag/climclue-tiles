Chilapa de Álvarez at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Chilapa de Álvarez is 274th warmest (21.0 °C year average), 367th sunniest (about 2,174 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (1,056 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tixtla de Guerrero, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Chilapa de Álvarez is 1.3 °C cooler in May and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Why Chilapa de Álvarez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
