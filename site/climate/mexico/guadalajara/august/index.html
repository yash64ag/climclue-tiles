Guadalajara in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalajara in August?
In August, Guadalajara averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Guadalajara?
August is not one of Guadalajara’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalajara in August?
190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalajara in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalajara get in August?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalajara in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
