How May compares in Guadalajara

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Guadalajara. Compared with April: 1.6 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Guadalajara in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalajara in May?
In May, Guadalajara averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guadalajara?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Guadalajara’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalajara in May?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalajara in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalajara get in May?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalajara in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
