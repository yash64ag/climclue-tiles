How December compares in Guadalajara

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guadalajara. Compared with November: 1.6 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Guadalajara in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalajara in December?
In December, Guadalajara averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guadalajara?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Guadalajara’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalajara in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalajara in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalajara get in December?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalajara in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
