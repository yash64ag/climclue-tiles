How September compares in Guadalajara

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guadalajara. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. October is 0.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Guadalajara in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalajara in September?
In September, Guadalajara averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guadalajara?
September is not one of Guadalajara’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalajara in September?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalajara in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalajara get in September?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalajara in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
