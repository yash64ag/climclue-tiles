How January compares in Guadalajara

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guadalajara. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Guadalajara in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalajara in January?
In January, Guadalajara averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guadalajara?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Guadalajara’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalajara in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalajara in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalajara get in January?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalajara in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
