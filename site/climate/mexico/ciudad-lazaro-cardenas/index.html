Ciudad Lázaro Cárdenas at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ciudad Lázaro Cárdenas is 24th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 222nd sunniest (about 2,428 hours of sunshine a year) and 212th wettest (1,016 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Orilla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Ciudad Lázaro Cárdenas is about as warm in July and about as warm in March, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.0 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, March (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in March. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Ciudad Lázaro Cárdenas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
