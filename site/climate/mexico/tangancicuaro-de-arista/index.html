Tangancícuaro de Arista at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Tangancícuaro de Arista is 384th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 214th sunniest (about 2,447 hours of sunshine a year) and 261st wettest (861 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jacona de Plancarte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Tangancícuaro de Arista is 1.3 °C cooler in May and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.5 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 210 km from the sea.

Why Tangancícuaro de Arista has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 210 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C. The 10 km area around Tangancícuaro de Arista, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 17.2 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
