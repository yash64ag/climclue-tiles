Puente de Ixtla at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Puente de Ixtla is 60th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 386th sunniest (about 2,161 hours of sunshine a year) and 257th wettest (871 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xoxocotla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Puente de Ixtla is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 213 km from the sea.

Why Puente de Ixtla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 213 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Puente de Ixtla itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.615°N, 99.318°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-653-806), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
