Zamora de Hidalgo at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Zamora de Hidalgo is 74th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,468 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (826 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uruapan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Zamora de Hidalgo is 2.7 °C warmer in May and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 447 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.6 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 220 km from the sea.

Why Zamora de Hidalgo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 220 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
