Zamora de Hidalgo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Zamora de Hidalgo is 320th warmest (20.1 °C year average), 204th sunniest (about 2,468 hours of sunshine a year) and 340th wettest (748 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jacona de Plancarte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Zamora de Hidalgo is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Why Zamora de Hidalgo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 220 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C. The 10 km area around Zamora de Hidalgo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.0 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
