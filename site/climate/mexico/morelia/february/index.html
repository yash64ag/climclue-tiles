How February compares in Morelia

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with January: 1.5 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. March is 2.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Morelia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in February?
In February, Morelia averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Morelia?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Morelia’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in February?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
