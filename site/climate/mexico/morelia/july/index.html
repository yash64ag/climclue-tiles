How July compares in Morelia

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with June: 1.4 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Morelia in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in July?
In July, Morelia averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Morelia?
July is not one of Morelia’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in July?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in July?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
