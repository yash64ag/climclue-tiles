How November compares in Morelia

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with October: 1.5 °C cooler, 42 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. December is 1.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Morelia in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in November?
In November, Morelia averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Morelia?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Morelia’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in November?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
