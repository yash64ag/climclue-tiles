How August compares in Morelia

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with July: about as warm, 14 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Morelia in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in August?
In August, Morelia averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Morelia?
August is not one of Morelia’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in August?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in August?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
