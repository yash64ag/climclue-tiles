How December compares in Morelia

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with November: 1.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Morelia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in December?
In December, Morelia averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Morelia?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Morelia’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in December?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in December?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
