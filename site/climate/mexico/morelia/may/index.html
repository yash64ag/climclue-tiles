How May compares in Morelia

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with April: 1.0 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Morelia in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in May?
In May, Morelia averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Morelia?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Morelia’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in May?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in May?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
