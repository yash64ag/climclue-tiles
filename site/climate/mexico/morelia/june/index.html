How June compares in Morelia

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Morelia. Compared with May: 1.0 °C cooler, 98 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. July is 1.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Morelia in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Morelia in June?
In June, Morelia averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Morelia?
June is not one of Morelia’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Morelia in June?
136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Morelia in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Morelia get in June?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Morelia in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
