Coatzacoalcos weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Coatzacoalcos, Mexico (Veracruz), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Coatzacoalcos is warmest in May, with 33.4 °C (92 °F) days and 22.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 26.5 °C (80 °F) days and 18.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (524 mm); March is driest (27 mm).

Coatzacoalcos at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Coatzacoalcos is 87th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 465th sunniest (about 2,104 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (2,566 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Allende, Veracruz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Coatzacoalcos is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.2 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Coatzacoalcos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Coatzacoalcos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.149°N, 94.445°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-658-855), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
