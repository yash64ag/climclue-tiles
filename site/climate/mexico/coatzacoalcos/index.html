Coatzacoalcos at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Coatzacoalcos is the 3rd wettest (2,544 mm a year). Compared with Minatitlán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Coatzacoalcos is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 83 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.3 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.7 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 22 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Coatzacoalcos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
