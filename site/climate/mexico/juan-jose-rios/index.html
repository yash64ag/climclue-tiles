Juan José Ríos weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Juan José Ríos, Mexico (Sinaloa), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Juan José Ríos at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Juan José Ríos is 119th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,683 hours of sunshine a year) and 568th wettest (399 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Los Mochis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Juan José Ríos is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.8 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Why Juan José Ríos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Juan José Ríos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.757°N, 108.824°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-582-711), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
