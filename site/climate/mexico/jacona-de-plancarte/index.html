Jacona de Plancarte at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Jacona de Plancarte is 325th warmest (19.5 °C year average), 185th sunniest (about 2,493 hours of sunshine a year) and 282nd wettest (832 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zamora de Hidalgo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Jacona de Plancarte is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 84 mm more precipitation a year. Why Jacona de Plancarte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 214 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
