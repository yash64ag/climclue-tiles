Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in April?
In April, Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in April?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (146 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City get in April?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
