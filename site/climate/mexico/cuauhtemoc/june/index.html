Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in June?
In June, Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City?
June is not one of Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
