Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City is 109th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 2,016 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (719 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Benito Juarez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 102 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.4 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Why Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
