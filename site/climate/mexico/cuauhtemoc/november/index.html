Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City in November?
In November, Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City?
November is not one of Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (147 mm), the driest December (6 mm).

Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
