Zacatepec de Hidalgo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Zacatepec de Hidalgo is 163rd warmest (24.1 °C year average), 359th sunniest (about 2,176 hours of sunshine a year) and 263rd wettest (858 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tlaquiltenango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Zacatepec de Hidalgo is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.2 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, January (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 222 km from the sea.

Why Zacatepec de Hidalgo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 222 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C. The 10 km area around Zacatepec de Hidalgo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 25.4 °C a year, 1.3 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zacatepec de Hidalgo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.653°N, 99.191°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-653-808), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
