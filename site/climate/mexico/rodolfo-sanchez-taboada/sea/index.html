Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Baja California · Mexico

The sea off Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada averages 19.3 °C (67 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 14.6 °C (58 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada?
In September, at about 19.3 °C (67 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 14.6 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada in February?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada nearby?
In September: Rosarito (20.0 °C, 84 km), Villa del Prado 2da Sección (20.0 °C, 87 km) and Pórticos de San Antonio (20.0 °C, 91 km).
