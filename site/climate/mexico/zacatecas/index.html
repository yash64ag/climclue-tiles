Why Zacatecas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 323 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Zacatecas climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zacatecas?
May, with an average of 19.3 °C (67 °F), day highs around 25.9 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 11.3 °C (52 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zacatecas?
January, with an average of 10.2 °C (50 °F), day highs around 17.2 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 3.9 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zacatecas?
July, with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 518 mm (20.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zacatecas?
March, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zacatecas?
May, with about 275 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zacatecas?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Zacatecas?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Zacatecas have?
Cold semi-arid (Köppen BSk). Dry grassland climate with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Zacatecas’s GeoNames location (22.768°N, 102.581°W; ClimaClue area sq-612-774), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 129,011 (GeoNames).
