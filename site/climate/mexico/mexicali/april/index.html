Mexicali in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexicali in April?
In April, Mexicali averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 333 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mexicali?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mexicali’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexicali in April?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (12 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mexicali in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexicali get in April?
About 333 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexicali in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
