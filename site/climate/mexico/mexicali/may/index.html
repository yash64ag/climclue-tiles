Is May a good time to visit Mexicali?
May is not one of Mexicali’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
