How December compares in Mexicali

December is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Mexicali. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C warmer than December on average.

Mexicali in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexicali in December?
In December, Mexicali averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mexicali?
December is not one of Mexicali’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexicali in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (12 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mexicali in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexicali get in December?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexicali in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
