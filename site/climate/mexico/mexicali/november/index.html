How November compares in Mexicali

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mexicali. Compared with October: 6.2 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Mexicali in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexicali in November?
In November, Mexicali averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mexicali?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Mexicali’s mild months (January, February, March, April and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexicali in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (12 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mexicali in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexicali get in November?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexicali in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
