Mexicali at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Mexicali is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,652 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (77 mm a year). Compared with San Luis Río Colorado, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Mexicali is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.8 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, December (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 139 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Mexicali has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 139 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
