How October compares in Mexicali

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mexicali. Compared with September: 6.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. November is 6.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Mexicali in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mexicali in October?
In October, Mexicali averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mexicali?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mexicali’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mexicali in October?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (12 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mexicali in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mexicali get in October?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mexicali in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
