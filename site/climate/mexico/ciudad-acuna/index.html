Why Ciudad Acuña has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 422 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

August, with an average of 30.6 °C (87 °F), day highs around 35.7 °C (96 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ciudad Acuña?
January, with an average of 11.4 °C (53 °F), day highs around 18.9 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 7.0 °C (45 °F).

What is the sunniest month in Ciudad Acuña?

Does it snow in Ciudad Acuña?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ciudad Acuña?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Ciudad Acuña’s GeoNames location (29.323°N, 100.952°W; ClimaClue area sq-546-790), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 216,099 (GeoNames).
