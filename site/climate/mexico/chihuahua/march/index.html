How March compares in Chihuahua

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Chihuahua. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 4.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Chihuahua in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in March?
In March, Chihuahua averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chihuahua?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Chihuahua’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in March?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (88 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in March?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
