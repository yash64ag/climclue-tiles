How February compares in Chihuahua

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chihuahua. Compared with January: 2.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. March is 3.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Chihuahua in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in February?
In February, Chihuahua averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Chihuahua?
February is not one of Chihuahua’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in February?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (88 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in February?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
