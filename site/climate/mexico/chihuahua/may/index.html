How May compares in Chihuahua

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Chihuahua. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. June is 2.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Chihuahua in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in May?
In May, Chihuahua averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 300 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Chihuahua?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Chihuahua’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in May?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (88 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in May?
About 300 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
