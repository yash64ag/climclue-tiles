Chihuahua weather in June

Average June weather in Chihuahua, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Chihuahua averages 34.8 °C (95 °F) days and 18.6 °C (65 °F) nights, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 292 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Chihuahua

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Chihuahua. Compared with May: 2.8 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 1.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Chihuahua in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in June?
In June, Chihuahua averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chihuahua?
June is not one of Chihuahua’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in June?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (89 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in June?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
