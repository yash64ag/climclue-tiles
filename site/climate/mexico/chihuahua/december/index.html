How December compares in Chihuahua

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Chihuahua. Compared with November: 3.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Chihuahua in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in December?
In December, Chihuahua averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chihuahua?
December is not one of Chihuahua’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (88 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in December?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
