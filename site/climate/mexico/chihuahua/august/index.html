How August compares in Chihuahua

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Chihuahua. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. September is 1.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Chihuahua in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in August?
In August, Chihuahua averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chihuahua?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Chihuahua’s mild months (March, April, May, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in August?
88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (88 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in August?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
