How January compares in Chihuahua

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Chihuahua. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 2.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Chihuahua in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chihuahua in January?
In January, Chihuahua averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chihuahua?
January is not one of Chihuahua’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chihuahua in January?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (88 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Chihuahua in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chihuahua get in January?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chihuahua in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
