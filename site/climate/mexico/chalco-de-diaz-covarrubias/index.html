Chalco de Díaz Covarrubias at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Chalco de Díaz Covarrubias is 128th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 106th sunniest (about 2,079 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (865 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xico, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Chalco de Díaz Covarrubias is 1.0 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.9 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Chalco de Díaz Covarrubias has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
