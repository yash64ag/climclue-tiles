Alto Lucero at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Alto Lucero is 144th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 574th sunniest (about 1,986 hours of sunshine a year) and 121st wettest (1,272 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Túxpam de Rodríguez Cano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Alto Lucero is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.1 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, January (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Alto Lucero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Alto Lucero itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.954°N, 97.442°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-630-825), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
