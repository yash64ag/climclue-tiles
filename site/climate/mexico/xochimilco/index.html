Xochimilco at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Xochimilco is 133rd warmest (14.2 °C year average), 112th sunniest (about 2,037 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (1,007 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tláhuac, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Xochimilco is 1.1 °C cooler in May and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 115 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.4 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 269 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 6 km from Xochimilco measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.8 °C (6.8 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Xochimilco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 269 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
