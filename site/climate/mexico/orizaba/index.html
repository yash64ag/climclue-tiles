Why Orizaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 107 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Orizaba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Orizaba?
May, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.4 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 16.6 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Orizaba?
January, with an average of 16.6 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.5 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 11.8 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Orizaba?
June, with 325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,897 mm (74.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Orizaba?
February, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Orizaba?
March, with about 163 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Orizaba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Orizaba?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.
What type of climate does Orizaba have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Orizaba’s GeoNames location (18.852°N, 97.100°W; ClimaClue area sq-651-829), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 123,182 (GeoNames).
