Orizaba at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Orizaba is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,747 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Córdoba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Orizaba is 1.8 °C cooler in May and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.1 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 107 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 17 km from Orizaba measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Orizaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 107 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
