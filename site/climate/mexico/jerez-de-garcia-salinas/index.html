Jerez de García Salinas at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Jerez de García Salinas is 439th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,761 hours of sunshine a year) and 553rd wettest (434 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zacatecas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Jerez de García Salinas is 2.1 °C warmer in June and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Why Jerez de García Salinas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 281 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jerez de García Salinas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.650°N, 102.990°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-613-770), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
