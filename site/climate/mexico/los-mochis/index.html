Los Mochis at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Los Mochis is 19th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,686 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (380 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Navojoa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (149 km away), Los Mochis is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 88 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.9 °C), averages 12.3 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Los Mochis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
