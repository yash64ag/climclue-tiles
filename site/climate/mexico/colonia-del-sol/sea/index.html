Colonia del Sol sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Baja California Sur · Mexico

The sea off Colonia del Sol averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 20.6 °C (69 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to December.

Colonia del Sol sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Colonia del Sol?
In September, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Colonia del Sol?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to December. The coolest month is March, at 20.6 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in Colonia del Sol in March?
About 20.6 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Colonia del Sol?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Colonia del Sol nearby?
In September: La Cruz (29.9 °C, 329 km), Mazatlán (29.8 °C, 361 km) and Bucerías (29.8 °C, 531 km).
