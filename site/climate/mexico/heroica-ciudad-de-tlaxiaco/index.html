Heroica Ciudad de Tlaxiaco at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Heroica Ciudad de Tlaxiaco is 480th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 451st sunniest (about 2,112 hours of sunshine a year) and 237th wettest (936 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciudad de Huajuapan de León, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Heroica Ciudad de Tlaxiaco is 5.7 °C cooler in May and 4.5 °C cooler in January, with 325 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.0 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 138 km from the sea.

Why Heroica Ciudad de Tlaxiaco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 138 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Heroica Ciudad de Tlaxiaco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.269°N, 97.680°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-667-823), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
