How November compares in Tuxtla

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tuxtla. Compared with October: 0.9 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. December is 1.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Tuxtla in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuxtla in November?
In November, Tuxtla averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tuxtla?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Tuxtla’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuxtla in November?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (200 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tuxtla in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuxtla get in November?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuxtla in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
