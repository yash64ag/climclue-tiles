How April compares in Tuxtla

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tuxtla. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. May is 0.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Tuxtla in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuxtla in April?
In April, Tuxtla averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tuxtla?
April is not one of Tuxtla’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuxtla in April?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest March (8 mm).
Does it snow in Tuxtla in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuxtla get in April?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuxtla in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
