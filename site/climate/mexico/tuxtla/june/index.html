How June compares in Tuxtla

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tuxtla. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 110 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Tuxtla in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuxtla in June?
In June, Tuxtla averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 202 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tuxtla?
June is not one of Tuxtla’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuxtla in June?
202 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest March (8 mm).
Does it snow in Tuxtla in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuxtla get in June?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuxtla in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
