How October compares in Tuxtla

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tuxtla. Compared with September: 0.6 °C cooler, 108 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Tuxtla in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuxtla in October?
In October, Tuxtla averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tuxtla?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Tuxtla’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuxtla in October?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest March (8 mm).
Does it snow in Tuxtla in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuxtla get in October?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuxtla in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
