How December compares in Tuxtla

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tuxtla. Compared with November: 1.0 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Tuxtla in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuxtla in December?
In December, Tuxtla averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tuxtla?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Tuxtla’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuxtla in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (200 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tuxtla in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuxtla get in December?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuxtla in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
