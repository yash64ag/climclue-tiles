Tuxtla at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tuxtla is 28th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 130th sunniest (about 1,938 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (986 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Cristóbal de las Casas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Tuxtla is 10.0 °C warmer in May and 8.5 °C warmer in January, with 616 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.8 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, under 1 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 100% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tuxtla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C. The 10 km area around Tuxtla, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.4 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tuxtla itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.754°N, 93.116°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-672-868), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
