Tuxtla weather in January

Average January weather in Tuxtla, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Tuxtla averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) days and 14.6 °C (58 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 170 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Tuxtla

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tuxtla. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Tuxtla in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuxtla in January?
In January, Tuxtla averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tuxtla?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Tuxtla’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuxtla in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (200 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tuxtla in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuxtla get in January?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuxtla in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
