Miahuatlán de Porfirio Díaz at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Miahuatlán de Porfirio Díaz is 311th warmest (20.0 °C year average), 425th sunniest (about 2,139 hours of sunshine a year) and 201st wettest (1,037 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ocotlán de Morelos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Miahuatlán de Porfirio Díaz is 1.1 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 494 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.8 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 81 km from the sea.

Why Miahuatlán de Porfirio Díaz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 81 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Miahuatlán de Porfirio Díaz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.329°N, 96.596°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-676-834), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
