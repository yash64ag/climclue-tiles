Autlán de Navarro at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Autlán de Navarro is 233rd warmest (22.1 °C year average), 253rd sunniest (about 2,378 hours of sunshine a year) and 211th wettest (1,017 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Grullo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Autlán de Navarro is 1.0 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 257 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.7 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (19.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 18 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 117% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Autlán de Navarro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-642-756), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
