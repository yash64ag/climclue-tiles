Nogales at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Nogales is the 4th sunniest (about 3,494 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Hermosillo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (247 km away), Nogales is 4.7 °C cooler in June and 6.5 °C cooler in December, with 142 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (26.4 °C), averages 17.6 °C more than its coolest, December (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 210 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nogales has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 210 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
