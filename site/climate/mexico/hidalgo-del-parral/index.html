Hidalgo del Parral at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Hidalgo del Parral is 89th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,745 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (497 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciudad Delicias, the nearest covered town with different numbers (141 km away), Hidalgo del Parral is 4.6 °C cooler in June and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 178 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.4 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (9.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 344 km from the sea.

Why Hidalgo del Parral has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 344 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
