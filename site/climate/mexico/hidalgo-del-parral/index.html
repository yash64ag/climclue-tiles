Hidalgo del Parral at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Hidalgo del Parral is 420th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 2,745 hours of sunshine a year) and 517th wettest (498 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Rosalía de Camargo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Hidalgo del Parral is 4.5 °C cooler in June and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 142 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.5 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (10.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 344 km from the sea.

Why Hidalgo del Parral has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 344 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hidalgo del Parral itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.930°N, 105.666°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-570-743), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
