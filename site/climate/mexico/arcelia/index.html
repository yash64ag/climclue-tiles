Arcelia at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Arcelia is the 3rd warmest (29.2 °C year average). Compared with Ciudad Altamirano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Arcelia is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, December (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 151 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 2 km from Arcelia measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Arcelia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 151 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Arcelia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.318°N, 100.282°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-656-797), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
