Navojoa at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Navojoa is 22nd warmest (24.6 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,808 hours of sunshine a year) and 119th wettest (468 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciudad Obregón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Navojoa is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.1 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Navojoa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
