San Juan de los Lagos weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in San Juan de los Lagos, Mexico (Jalisco), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

San Juan de los Lagos is warmest in May, with 34.4 °C (94 °F) days and 14.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 26.1 °C (79 °F) days and 5.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (179 mm); March is driest (3 mm).

San Juan de los Lagos at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, San Juan de los Lagos is 313th warmest (20.2 °C year average), 162nd sunniest (about 2,531 hours of sunshine a year) and 421st wettest (642 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jalostotitlán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), San Juan de los Lagos is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.5 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 311 km from the sea.

Why San Juan de los Lagos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Juan de los Lagos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.249°N, 102.330°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-627-776), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
