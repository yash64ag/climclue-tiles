Tepic at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Tepic is 56th warmest (21.2 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 2,626 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (1,265 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Puerto Vallarta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Tepic is 3.1 °C cooler in June and 4.6 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.9 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tepic has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
