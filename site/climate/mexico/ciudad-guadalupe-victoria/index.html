Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,692 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (65 mm a year). Compared with San Luis Río Colorado, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in December, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.6 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, December (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in December. The area lies about 87 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 87 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.289°N, 115.103°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-517-648), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
