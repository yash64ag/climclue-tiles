Playa del Carmen at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Playa del Carmen is 14th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,491 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,361 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cancún, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Playa del Carmen is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 45 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Playa del Carmen, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Playa del Carmen

For mild weather, the best months to visit Playa del Carmen are January, February, March and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March and December.

Why Playa del Carmen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
