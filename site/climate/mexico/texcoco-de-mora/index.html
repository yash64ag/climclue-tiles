Texcoco de Mora at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Texcoco de Mora is 114th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 2,171 hours of sunshine a year) and 103rd wettest (562 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chicoloapan de Juárez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Texcoco de Mora is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.4 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 224 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Texcoco de Mora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 224 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
