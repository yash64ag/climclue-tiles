Atlixco at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Atlixco is 353rd warmest (19.5 °C year average), 272nd sunniest (about 2,342 hours of sunshine a year) and 333rd wettest (754 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Bernardino Tlaxcalancingo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Atlixco is 1.8 °C warmer in May and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.4 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 236 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 89% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Atlixco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 236 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C. The 10 km area around Atlixco, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.2 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Atlixco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.908°N, 98.436°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-650-815), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
