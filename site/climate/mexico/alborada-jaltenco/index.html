Alborada Jaltenco weather by month and climate

Alborada Jaltenco at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Alborada Jaltenco is 452nd warmest (17.0 °C year average), 405th sunniest (about 2,148 hours of sunshine a year) and 472nd wettest (571 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Alborada Jaltenco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Alborada Jaltenco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.662°N, 99.069°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-643-809), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
