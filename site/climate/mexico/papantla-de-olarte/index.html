Papantla de Olarte at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Papantla de Olarte is 193rd warmest (23.5 °C year average), 580th sunniest (about 1,968 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (1,673 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Coatzintla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Papantla de Olarte is 0.7 °C cooler in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 234 mm more precipitation a year. Why Papantla de Olarte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
