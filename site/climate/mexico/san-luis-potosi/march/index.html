How March compares in San Luis Potosí

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in San Luis Potosí. Compared with February: 2.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 2.3 °C warmer than March on average.

San Luis Potosí in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Luis Potosí in March?
In March, San Luis Potosí averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit San Luis Potosí?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of San Luis Potosí’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Luis Potosí in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (8 mm).
Does it snow in San Luis Potosí in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Luis Potosí get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Luis Potosí in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
