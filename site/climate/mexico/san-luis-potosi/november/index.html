How November compares in San Luis Potosí

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in San Luis Potosí. Compared with October: 2.0 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. December is 1.6 °C cooler than November on average.

San Luis Potosí in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Luis Potosí in November?
In November, San Luis Potosí averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit San Luis Potosí?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of San Luis Potosí’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Luis Potosí in November?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (8 mm).
Does it snow in San Luis Potosí in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Luis Potosí get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Luis Potosí in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
