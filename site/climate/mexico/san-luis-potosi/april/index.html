How April compares in San Luis Potosí

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in San Luis Potosí. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. May is 1.5 °C warmer than April on average.

San Luis Potosí in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Luis Potosí in April?
In April, San Luis Potosí averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit San Luis Potosí?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of San Luis Potosí’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Luis Potosí in April?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in San Luis Potosí in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Luis Potosí get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Luis Potosí in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
