San Luis Potosí in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Luis Potosí in July?
In July, San Luis Potosí averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit San Luis Potosí?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of San Luis Potosí’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Luis Potosí in July?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in San Luis Potosí in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Luis Potosí get in July?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Luis Potosí in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
