How February compares in San Luis Potosí

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in San Luis Potosí. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. March is 2.5 °C warmer than February on average.

San Luis Potosí in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Luis Potosí in February?
In February, San Luis Potosí averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit San Luis Potosí?
February is not one of San Luis Potosí’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Luis Potosí in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in San Luis Potosí in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Luis Potosí get in February?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Luis Potosí in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
