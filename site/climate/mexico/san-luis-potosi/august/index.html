San Luis Potosí in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Luis Potosí in August?
In August, San Luis Potosí averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit San Luis Potosí?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of San Luis Potosí’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Luis Potosí in August?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in San Luis Potosí in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Luis Potosí get in August?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Luis Potosí in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
