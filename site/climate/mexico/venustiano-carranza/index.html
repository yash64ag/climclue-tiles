Venustiano Carranza at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Venustiano Carranza is 160th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 499th sunniest (about 2,084 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (1,622 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Las Rosas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Venustiano Carranza is 4.0 °C warmer in May and 3.1 °C warmer in December, with 186 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.2 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, December (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Why Venustiano Carranza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
