Ciudad López Mateos at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad López Mateos is 124th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 99th sunniest (about 2,093 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (815 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tlalnepantla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Ciudad López Mateos is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 109 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (17.6 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 260 km from the sea.

Why Ciudad López Mateos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 260 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
