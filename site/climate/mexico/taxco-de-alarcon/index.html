Taxco de Alarcón at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Taxco de Alarcón is 279th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 519th sunniest (about 2,067 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (1,185 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Iguala de la Independencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Taxco de Alarcón is 5.2 °C cooler in April and 4.7 °C cooler in January, with 342 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (23.7 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Why Taxco de Alarcón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C. The 10 km area around Taxco de Alarcón, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.8 °C a year, 2.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taxco de Alarcón itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.557°N, 99.605°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-654-803), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
