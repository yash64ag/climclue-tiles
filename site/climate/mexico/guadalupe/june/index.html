Guadalupe, Nuevo León in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in June?
In June, Guadalupe, Nuevo León averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guadalupe, Nuevo León?
June is not one of Guadalupe, Nuevo León’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
