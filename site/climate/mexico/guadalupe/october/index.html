How October compares in Guadalupe, Nuevo León

Guadalupe, Nuevo León in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in October?
In October, Guadalupe, Nuevo León averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Guadalupe, Nuevo León?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Guadalupe, Nuevo León’s mild months (February, March, April, May, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in October?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (187 mm), the driest November (22 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalupe, Nuevo León get in October?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalupe, Nuevo León in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
