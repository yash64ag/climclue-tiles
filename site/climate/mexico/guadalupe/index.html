Guadalupe, Nuevo León at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Guadalupe, Nuevo León is 59th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 1,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 76th wettest (706 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Monterrey, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Guadalupe, Nuevo León is 0.6 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.7 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 292 km from the sea.

Why Guadalupe, Nuevo León has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
