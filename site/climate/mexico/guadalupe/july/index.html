Guadalupe, Nuevo León in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in July?
In July, Guadalupe, Nuevo León averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guadalupe, Nuevo León?
July is not one of Guadalupe, Nuevo León’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in July?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (172 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).

About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
