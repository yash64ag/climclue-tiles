Guadalupe, Nuevo León in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in September?
In September, Guadalupe, Nuevo León averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guadalupe, Nuevo León?
September is not one of Guadalupe, Nuevo León’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in September?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (172 mm), the driest February (20 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in September?
