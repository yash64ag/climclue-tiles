How January compares in Guadalupe, Nuevo León

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guadalupe, Nuevo León. Compared with December: 0.5 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Guadalupe, Nuevo León in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in January?
In January, Guadalupe, Nuevo León averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guadalupe, Nuevo León?
January is not one of Guadalupe, Nuevo León’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (187 mm), the driest November (22 mm).
Does it snow in Guadalupe, Nuevo León in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guadalupe, Nuevo León get in January?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guadalupe, Nuevo León in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
