Cuautla at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Cuautla is 51st warmest (22.2 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 2,167 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (1,164 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yautepec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Cuautla is 1.0 °C cooler in April and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.1 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 251 km from the sea.

Why Cuautla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 251 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
