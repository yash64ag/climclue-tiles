Guadalupe Victoria at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Guadalupe Victoria is 449th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 2,620 hours of sunshine a year) and 475th wettest (569 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Victoria de Durango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Guadalupe Victoria is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 110 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.3 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 270 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Guadalupe Victoria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 270 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Guadalupe Victoria itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.447°N, 104.123°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-595-758), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
