Villa del Prado 2da Sección sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Baja California · Mexico

The sea off Villa del Prado 2da Sección averages 20.1 °C (68 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.6 °C (58 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.
