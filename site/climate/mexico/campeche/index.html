Campeche at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Campeche is the 4th warmest (26.4 °C year average). Compared with Mérida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (156 km away), Campeche is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 357 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.8 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, under 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 130% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Campeche has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
