Xico at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Xico is 120th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 107th sunniest (about 2,059 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (832 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chalco de Díaz Covarrubias, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Xico is 1.0 °C warmer in May and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (17.9 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 252 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 6 km from Xico measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (yearly rain and snow: this area has 152% of the station’s). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Xico has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 252 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
