Cabo San Lucas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Baja California Sur · Mexico

The sea off Cabo San Lucas averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 21.0 °C (70 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to December.

Cabo San Lucas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cabo San Lucas?
In September, at about 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cabo San Lucas?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to December. The coolest month is March, at 21.0 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cabo San Lucas in March?
About 21.0 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Cabo San Lucas?
About 24.3 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Cabo San Lucas nearby?
In September: La Cruz (29.9 °C, 329 km), Mazatlán (29.8 °C, 359 km) and Bucerías (29.8 °C, 529 km).
