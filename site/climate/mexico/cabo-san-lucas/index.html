Cabo San Lucas at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Cabo San Lucas is 186th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 78th sunniest (about 2,655 hours of sunshine a year) and 593rd wettest (325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Colonia del Sol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Cabo San Lucas is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.5 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, February (19.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 126% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Cabo San Lucas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cabo San Lucas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.891°N, 109.912°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-611-700), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
