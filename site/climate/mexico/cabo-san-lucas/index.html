Why Cabo San Lucas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cabo San Lucas climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cabo San Lucas?
August, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 28.8 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 26.1 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cabo San Lucas?
February, with an average of 20.1 °C (68 °F), day highs around 22.2 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cabo San Lucas?
September, with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 321 mm (12.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cabo San Lucas?
May, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cabo San Lucas?
May, with about 260 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cabo San Lucas?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cabo San Lucas?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, June, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Cabo San Lucas’s GeoNames location (22.891°N, 109.912°W; ClimaClue area sq-611-700), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 202,694 (GeoNames).
