How September compares in Culiacán

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Culiacán. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. October is 1.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Culiacán in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in September?
In September, Culiacán averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Culiacán?
September is not one of Culiacán’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in September?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (209 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in September?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
