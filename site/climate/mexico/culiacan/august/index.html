August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Culiacán. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Culiacán in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in August?
In August, Culiacán averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Culiacán?
August is not one of Culiacán’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in August?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (209 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in August?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
