How November compares in Culiacán

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Culiacán. Compared with October: 4.0 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Culiacán in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in November?
In November, Culiacán averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Culiacán?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Culiacán’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (205 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in November?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
