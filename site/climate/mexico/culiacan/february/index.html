How February compares in Culiacán

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Culiacán. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 1.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Culiacán in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in February?
In February, Culiacán averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Culiacán?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Culiacán’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (209 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in February?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
