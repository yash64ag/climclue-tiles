How October compares in Culiacán

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Culiacán. Compared with September: 1.5 °C cooler, 86 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Culiacán in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in October?
In October, Culiacán averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Culiacán?
October is not one of Culiacán’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in October?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (205 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in October?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
