How May compares in Culiacán

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Culiacán. Compared with April: 2.5 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 2.4 °C warmer than May on average.

What is the weather like in Culiacán in May?
In May, Culiacán averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Culiacán?
May is not one of Culiacán’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
