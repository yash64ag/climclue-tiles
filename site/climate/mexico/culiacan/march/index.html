How March compares in Culiacán

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Culiacán. Compared with February: 1.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 2.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Culiacán in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in March?
In March, Culiacán averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Culiacán?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Culiacán’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in March?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (209 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in March?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
