Culiacán in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Culiacán in April?
In April, Culiacán averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Culiacán?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Culiacán’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Culiacán in April?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (209 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Culiacán in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Culiacán get in April?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Culiacán in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
