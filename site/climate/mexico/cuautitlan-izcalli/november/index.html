Cuautitlán Izcalli in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuautitlán Izcalli in November?
In November, Cuautitlán Izcalli averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cuautitlán Izcalli?
November is not one of Cuautitlán Izcalli’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (146 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Cuautitlán Izcalli in November?
