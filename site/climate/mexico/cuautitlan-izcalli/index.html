Cuautitlán Izcalli at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Cuautitlán Izcalli is 118th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 2,134 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (739 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuautitlán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Cuautitlán Izcalli is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 89 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.3 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 255 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Cuautitlán Izcalli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 255 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
