Iguala de la Independencia at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Iguala de la Independencia is the 3rd warmest (26.6 °C year average). Compared with Jiutepec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Iguala de la Independencia is 4.5 °C warmer in May and 4.9 °C warmer in January, with 255 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 175 km from the sea.

Why Iguala de la Independencia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 175 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
