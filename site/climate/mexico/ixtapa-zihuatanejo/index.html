Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo is 43rd warmest (26.4 °C year average), 248th sunniest (about 2,389 hours of sunshine a year) and 180th wettest (1,087 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Petatlán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in March, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, March (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in March. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
