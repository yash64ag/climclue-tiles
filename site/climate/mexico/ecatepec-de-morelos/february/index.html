How February compares in Ecatepec de Morelos

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ecatepec de Morelos. Compared with January: 1.5 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 2.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Ecatepec de Morelos in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ecatepec de Morelos in February?
In February, Ecatepec de Morelos averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ecatepec de Morelos?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Ecatepec de Morelos’s mild months (February, March, April, May, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ecatepec de Morelos in February?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ecatepec de Morelos in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ecatepec de Morelos get in February?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ecatepec de Morelos in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
