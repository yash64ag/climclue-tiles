Ecatepec de Morelos in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ecatepec de Morelos in August?
In August, Ecatepec de Morelos averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 126 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ecatepec de Morelos?
August is not one of Ecatepec de Morelos’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ecatepec de Morelos in August?
126 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (137 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ecatepec de Morelos in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ecatepec de Morelos get in August?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ecatepec de Morelos in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
