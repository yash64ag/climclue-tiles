Ecatepec de Morelos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ecatepec de Morelos in April?
In April, Ecatepec de Morelos averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ecatepec de Morelos?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ecatepec de Morelos’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ecatepec de Morelos in April?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (137 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ecatepec de Morelos in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ecatepec de Morelos get in April?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ecatepec de Morelos in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
