Ecatepec de Morelos weather in October

Average October weather in Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Ecatepec de Morelos averages 23.9 °C (75 °F) days and 8.7 °C (48 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 173 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Ecatepec de Morelos

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ecatepec de Morelos. Compared with September: 1.1 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Ecatepec de Morelos in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ecatepec de Morelos in October?
In October, Ecatepec de Morelos averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ecatepec de Morelos?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Ecatepec de Morelos’s mild months (February, March, April, May, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ecatepec de Morelos in October?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (137 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ecatepec de Morelos in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ecatepec de Morelos get in October?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ecatepec de Morelos in October?
