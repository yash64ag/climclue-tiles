How March compares in Ecatepec de Morelos

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ecatepec de Morelos. Compared with February: 2.2 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 1.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Ecatepec de Morelos in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ecatepec de Morelos in March?
In March, Ecatepec de Morelos averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ecatepec de Morelos?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Ecatepec de Morelos’s mild months (February, March, April, May, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ecatepec de Morelos in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ecatepec de Morelos in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ecatepec de Morelos get in March?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ecatepec de Morelos in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
