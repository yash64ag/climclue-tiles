Lázaro Cárdenas at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Lázaro Cárdenas is 427th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 3,275 hours of sunshine a year) and 616th wettest (171 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Felipe, Baja California, the nearest covered town with different numbers (118 km away), Lázaro Cárdenas is 9.3 °C cooler in September and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 91 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (21.8 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Lázaro Cárdenas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
