Minatitlán at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Minatitlán is the 4th wettest (2,461 mm a year). Compared with Coatzacoalcos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Minatitlán is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 83 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 100% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Minatitlán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
