Piedras Negras weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Piedras Negras, Mexico (Coahuila), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Piedras Negras is warmest in August, with 37.7 °C (100 °F) days and 24.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 18.8 °C (66 °F) days and 6.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (79 mm); December is driest (18 mm).

Piedras Negras at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Piedras Negras is 218th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 236th sunniest (about 2,411 hours of sunshine a year) and 494th wettest (530 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nava, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Piedras Negras is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Why Piedras Negras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 359 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Piedras Negras itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.700°N, 100.524°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-552-794), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
