How December compares in Puebla

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Puebla. Compared with November: 1.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Puebla in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puebla in December?
In December, Puebla averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Puebla?
December is not one of Puebla’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puebla in December?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (147 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Puebla in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puebla get in December?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puebla in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
