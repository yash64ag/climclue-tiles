Puebla at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Puebla is 91st warmest (17.3 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 2,426 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (739 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cholula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Puebla is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.4 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 213 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 81% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Puebla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 213 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
