How September compares in Puebla

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Puebla. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. October is 0.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Puebla in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puebla in September?
In September, Puebla averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Puebla?
September is not one of Puebla’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puebla in September?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (147 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Puebla in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puebla get in September?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puebla in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
