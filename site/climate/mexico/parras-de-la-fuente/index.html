Parras de la Fuente at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Parras de la Fuente is 340th warmest (19.8 °C year average), 281st sunniest (about 2,321 hours of sunshine a year) and 583rd wettest (354 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro, Coahuila (25.8°N 103.0°W), the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Parras de la Fuente is 4.1 °C cooler in June and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.1 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 472 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Parras de la Fuente has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 472 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Parras de la Fuente itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.442°N, 102.178°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-585-778), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
