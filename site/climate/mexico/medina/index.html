Medina at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Medina is 377th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 307th sunniest (about 2,282 hours of sunshine a year) and 443rd wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with León de los Aldama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Medina is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.0 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 359 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 93% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Medina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 359 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Medina itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.150°N, 101.622°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-783), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
