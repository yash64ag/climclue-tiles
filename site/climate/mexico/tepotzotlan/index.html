Tepotzotlán at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Tepotzotlán is 504th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 338th sunniest (about 2,216 hours of sunshine a year) and 385th wettest (687 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huilango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Tepotzotlán is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.8 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 250 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tepotzotlán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 250 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tepotzotlán itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.714°N, 99.223°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-642-807), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
