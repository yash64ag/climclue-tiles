Calvillo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Calvillo is 323rd warmest (19.5 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,764 hours of sunshine a year) and 487th wettest (538 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Villa Hidalgo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Calvillo is 2.3 °C warmer in June and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 78 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.3 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 272 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 2 km from Calvillo by up to 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Calvillo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Calvillo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.846°N, 102.719°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-621-772), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
