Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in April?
In April, Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
