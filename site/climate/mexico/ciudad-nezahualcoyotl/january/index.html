Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in January?
In January, Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl?
January is not one of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (143 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl get in January?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
