Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in May?
In May, Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
