Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in September?
In September, Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl?
September is not one of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in September?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (143 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl get in September?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
