Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 101st warmest (16.6 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (690 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Iztapalapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 1.0 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 86 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.3 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 248 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 4 km from Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.9 °C (5.1 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 248 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
