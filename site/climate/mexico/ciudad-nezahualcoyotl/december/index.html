Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in December?
In December, Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl?
December is not one of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in December?
