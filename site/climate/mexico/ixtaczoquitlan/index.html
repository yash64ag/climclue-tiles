Ixtaczoquitlán at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Ixtaczoquitlán is 307th warmest (20.1 °C year average), 617th sunniest (about 1,747 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (1,864 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orizaba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Ixtaczoquitlán is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.3 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, January (17.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 107 km from the sea.

Why Ixtaczoquitlán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 107 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
