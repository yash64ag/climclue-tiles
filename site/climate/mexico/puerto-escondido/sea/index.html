Puerto Escondido sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Oaxaca · Mexico

The sea off Puerto Escondido averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 27.8 °C (82 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Puerto Escondido sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto Escondido?
In August, at about 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto Escondido?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.8 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto Escondido in January?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto Escondido?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
