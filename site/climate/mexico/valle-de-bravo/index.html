Valle de Bravo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Valle de Bravo is 399th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 525th sunniest (about 2,057 hours of sunshine a year) and 156th wettest (1,146 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tejupilco de Hidalgo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Valle de Bravo is 3.8 °C cooler in May and 3.5 °C cooler in January, with 322 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.1 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 231 km from the sea.

Why Valle de Bravo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 231 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C. The 10 km area around Valle de Bravo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.3 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Valle de Bravo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.195°N, 100.132°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-648-798), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
