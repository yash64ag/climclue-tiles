Compared with San Miguel Coatlinchán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Chicoloapan de Juárez is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Why Chicoloapan de Juárez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 239 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
