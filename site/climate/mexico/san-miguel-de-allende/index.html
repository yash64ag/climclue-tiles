Why San Miguel de Allende has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 343 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

San Miguel de Allende climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in San Miguel de Allende?
May, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 29.0 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 14.0 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in San Miguel de Allende?
January, with an average of 14.3 °C (58 °F), day highs around 22.0 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 7.3 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in San Miguel de Allende?

What is the driest month in San Miguel de Allende?

What is the sunniest month in San Miguel de Allende?
March, with about 262 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Allende?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in San Miguel de Allende?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains San Miguel de Allende’s GeoNames location (20.915°N, 100.744°W; ClimaClue area sq-630-792), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 174,615 (GeoNames).
