San Miguel de Allende at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, San Miguel de Allende is 81st warmest (18.3 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,676 hours of sunshine a year) and 117th wettest (489 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Celaya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), San Miguel de Allende is 1.8 °C cooler in May and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.9 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 343 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 12 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 85% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why San Miguel de Allende has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 343 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
