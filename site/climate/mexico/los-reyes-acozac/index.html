Los Reyes Acozac at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Los Reyes Acozac is 500th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 314th sunniest (about 2,270 hours of sunshine a year) and 546th wettest (448 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa María Ajoloapan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Los Reyes Acozac is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.8 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 222 km from the sea.

Why Los Reyes Acozac has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 222 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Los Reyes Acozac itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.774°N, 98.983°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-642-810), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
