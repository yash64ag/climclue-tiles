Naucalpan de Juárez in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in February?
In February, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
February is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
