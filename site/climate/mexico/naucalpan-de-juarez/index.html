Why Naucalpan de Juárez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 266 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
