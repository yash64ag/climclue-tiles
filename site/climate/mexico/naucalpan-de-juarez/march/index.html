Naucalpan de Juárez in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in March?
In March, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
