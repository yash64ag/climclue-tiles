Naucalpan de Juárez in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in January?
In January, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
January is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naucalpan de Juárez in January?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (166 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Naucalpan de Juárez in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naucalpan de Juárez get in January?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
