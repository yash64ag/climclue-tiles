Naucalpan de Juárez in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in December?
In December, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 5.4 °C (42 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
December is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naucalpan de Juárez in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Naucalpan de Juárez in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naucalpan de Juárez get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naucalpan de Juárez in December?
