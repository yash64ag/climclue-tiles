Naucalpan de Juárez in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in October?
In October, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
October is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naucalpan de Juárez in October?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Naucalpan de Juárez in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naucalpan de Juárez get in October?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
