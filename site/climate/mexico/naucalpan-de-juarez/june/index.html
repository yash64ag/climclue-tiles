What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in June?
In June, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
June is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
