Naucalpan de Juárez in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in November?
In November, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
November is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Naucalpan de Juárez in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naucalpan de Juárez get in November?
