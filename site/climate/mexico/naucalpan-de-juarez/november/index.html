Naucalpan de Juárez in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naucalpan de Juárez in November?
In November, Naucalpan de Juárez averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Naucalpan de Juárez?
November is not one of Naucalpan de Juárez’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naucalpan de Juárez in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (166 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Naucalpan de Juárez in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naucalpan de Juárez get in November?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naucalpan de Juárez in November?
