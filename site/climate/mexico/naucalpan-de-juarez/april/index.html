Naucalpan de Juárez weather in April

Average April weather in Naucalpan de Juárez, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Naucalpan de Juárez averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) days and 7.2 °C (45 °F) nights, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 174 hours of sunshine.
