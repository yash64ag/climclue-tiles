Teloloapan at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Teloloapan is 300th warmest (20.5 °C year average), 456th sunniest (about 2,108 hours of sunshine a year) and 188th wettest (1,068 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taxco de Alarcón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Teloloapan is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.6 °C warmer in December, with 116 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (23.8 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, December (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Why Teloloapan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Teloloapan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.369°N, 99.870°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-656-801), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
