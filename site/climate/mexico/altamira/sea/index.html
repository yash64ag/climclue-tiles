Altamira sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tamaulipas · Mexico

The sea off Altamira averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Altamira sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Altamira?
In August, at about 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Altamira?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Altamira in February?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Altamira?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Altamira nearby?
In August: Túxpam de Rodríguez Cano (28.6 °C, 169 km), José Cardel (29.2 °C, 374 km) and Valente Diaz (28.9 °C, 401 km).
