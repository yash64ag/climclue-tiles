Huetamo de Núñez at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Huetamo de Núñez is the 2nd warmest (29.7 °C year average). Compared with Ciudad Altamirano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Huetamo de Núñez is 1.0 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.0 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, January (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Why Huetamo de Núñez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C. The 10 km area around Huetamo de Núñez, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 28.6 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
