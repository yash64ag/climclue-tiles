Huetamo de Núñez at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Huetamo de Núñez is the 2nd warmest (28.5 °C year average). Compared with Ciudad Altamirano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Huetamo de Núñez is 0.3 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.2 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Why Huetamo de Núñez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huetamo de Núñez itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.626°N, 100.900°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-653-791), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
