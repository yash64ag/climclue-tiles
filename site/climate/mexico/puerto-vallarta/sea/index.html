Puerto Vallarta sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Jalisco · Mexico

The sea off Puerto Vallarta averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 23.7 °C (75 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to February.

Puerto Vallarta sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto Vallarta?
In August, at about 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto Vallarta?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to February. The coolest month is March, at 23.7 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto Vallarta in March?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto Vallarta?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
