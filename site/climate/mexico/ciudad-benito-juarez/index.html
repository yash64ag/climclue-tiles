Ciudad Benito Juárez at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Ciudad Benito Juárez is the 4th least sunny (about 1,790 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Guadalupe, Nuevo León, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Ciudad Benito Juárez is 2.4 °C warmer in August and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.1 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 273 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 86% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ciudad Benito Juárez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 273 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
