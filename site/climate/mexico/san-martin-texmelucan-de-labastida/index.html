San Martin Texmelucan de Labastida at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, San Martin Texmelucan de Labastida is 121st warmest (15.5 °C year average), 69th sunniest (about 2,175 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (778 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cholula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), San Martin Texmelucan de Labastida is 1.9 °C cooler in May and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (17.1 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 213 km from the sea.

Why San Martin Texmelucan de Labastida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.
