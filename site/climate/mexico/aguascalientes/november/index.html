How November compares in Aguascalientes

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with October: 2.2 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. December is 2.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Aguascalientes in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in November?
In November, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Aguascalientes’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in November?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in November?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
