How March compares in Aguascalientes

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with February: 2.6 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. April is 2.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Aguascalientes in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in March?
In March, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Aguascalientes’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in March?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in March?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
