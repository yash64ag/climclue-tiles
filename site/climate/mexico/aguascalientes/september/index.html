How September compares in Aguascalientes

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Aguascalientes. Compared with August: 0.6 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. October is 1.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Aguascalientes in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in September?
In September, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Aguascalientes’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in September?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in September?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
