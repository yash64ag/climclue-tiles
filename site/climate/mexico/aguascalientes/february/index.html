How February compares in Aguascalientes

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. March is 2.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Aguascalientes in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in February?
In February, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Aguascalientes’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in February?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in February?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
