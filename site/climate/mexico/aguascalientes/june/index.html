How June compares in Aguascalientes

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with May: 0.3 °C cooler, 63 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. July is 1.9 °C cooler than June on average.

Aguascalientes in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in June?
In June, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Aguascalientes’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in June?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in June?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
