How August compares in Aguascalientes

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with July: about as warm, 31 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. September is 0.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Aguascalientes in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in August?
In August, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
August is not one of Aguascalientes’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in August?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (4 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in August?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
