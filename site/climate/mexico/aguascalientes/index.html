Why Aguascalientes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 323 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Aguascalientes climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Aguascalientes?
May, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 29.5 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 14.6 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Aguascalientes?
January, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 20.9 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 6.7 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Aguascalientes?
July, with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 515 mm (20.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Aguascalientes?
March, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Aguascalientes?
May, with about 263 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Aguascalientes?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, June, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Aguascalientes have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Aguascalientes’s GeoNames location (21.883°N, 102.284°W; ClimaClue area sq-621-777), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 722,250 (GeoNames).
