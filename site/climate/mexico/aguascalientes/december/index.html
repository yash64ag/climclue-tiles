How December compares in Aguascalientes

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with November: 1.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Aguascalientes in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in December?
In December, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
December is not one of Aguascalientes’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (125 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in December?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
