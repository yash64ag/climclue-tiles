How January compares in Aguascalientes

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Aguascalientes. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Aguascalientes in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aguascalientes in January?
In January, Aguascalientes averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aguascalientes?
January is not one of Aguascalientes’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, June, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aguascalientes in January?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (125 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Aguascalientes in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aguascalientes get in January?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aguascalientes in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
