Gabriel Leyva Solano at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Gabriel Leyva Solano is 128th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 2,664 hours of sunshine a year) and 560th wettest (423 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guasave, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Gabriel Leyva Solano is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.6 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Why Gabriel Leyva Solano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
