Paracho de Verduzco at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Paracho de Verduzco is 514th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 259th sunniest (about 2,357 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (1,245 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uruapan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Paracho de Verduzco is 2.0 °C cooler in May and 2.8 °C cooler in January, with 59 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (18.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 194 km from the sea.

Why Paracho de Verduzco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 194 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C. The 10 km area around Paracho de Verduzco, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.6 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
