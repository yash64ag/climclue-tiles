Comitán at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Comitán is 82nd warmest (18.2 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 2,151 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Cristóbal de las Casas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Comitán is 4.1 °C warmer in April and 4.1 °C warmer in January, with 133 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (19.8 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Why Comitán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
