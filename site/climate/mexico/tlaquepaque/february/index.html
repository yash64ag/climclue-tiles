How February compares in Tlaquepaque

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tlaquepaque. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. March is 2.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Tlaquepaque in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlaquepaque in February?
In February, Tlaquepaque averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tlaquepaque?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Tlaquepaque’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlaquepaque in February?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tlaquepaque in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlaquepaque get in February?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlaquepaque in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
