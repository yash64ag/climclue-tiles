How November compares in Tlaquepaque

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tlaquepaque. Compared with October: 1.9 °C cooler, 38 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. December is 1.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Tlaquepaque in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlaquepaque in November?
In November, Tlaquepaque averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tlaquepaque?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Tlaquepaque’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlaquepaque in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tlaquepaque in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlaquepaque get in November?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlaquepaque in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
