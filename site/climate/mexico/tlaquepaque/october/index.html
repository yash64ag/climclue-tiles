How October compares in Tlaquepaque

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tlaquepaque. Compared with September: 0.9 °C cooler, 102 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. November is 1.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Tlaquepaque in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlaquepaque in October?
In October, Tlaquepaque averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tlaquepaque?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Tlaquepaque’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlaquepaque in October?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tlaquepaque in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlaquepaque get in October?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlaquepaque in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
