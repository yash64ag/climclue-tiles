How May compares in Tlaquepaque

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tlaquepaque. Compared with April: 1.6 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. June is 1.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Tlaquepaque in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlaquepaque in May?
In May, Tlaquepaque averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tlaquepaque?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Tlaquepaque’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlaquepaque in May?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tlaquepaque in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlaquepaque get in May?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlaquepaque in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
