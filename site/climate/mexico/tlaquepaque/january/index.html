How January compares in Tlaquepaque

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tlaquepaque. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Tlaquepaque in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tlaquepaque in January?
In January, Tlaquepaque averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tlaquepaque?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Tlaquepaque’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tlaquepaque in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest March (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tlaquepaque in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tlaquepaque get in January?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tlaquepaque in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
