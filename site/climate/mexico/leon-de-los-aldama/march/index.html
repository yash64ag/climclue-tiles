León de los Aldama in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in León de los Aldama in March?
In March, León de los Aldama averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
