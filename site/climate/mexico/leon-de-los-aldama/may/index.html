León de los Aldama in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in León de los Aldama in May?
In May, León de los Aldama averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit León de los Aldama?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of León de los Aldama’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
