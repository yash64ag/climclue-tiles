León de los Aldama weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in León de los Aldama, Mexico (Guanajuato), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

León de los Aldama is warmest in May, with 32.2 °C (90 °F) days and 15.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 24.2 °C (76 °F) days and 6.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (158 mm); March is driest (6 mm).

León de los Aldama at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, León de los Aldama is 346th warmest (19.7 °C year average), 307th sunniest (about 2,282 hours of sunshine a year) and 429th wettest (631 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why León de los Aldama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 359 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for León de los Aldama itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.122°N, 101.683°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-783), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
