León de los Aldama in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in León de los Aldama in October?
In October, León de los Aldama averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit León de los Aldama?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of León de los Aldama’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
