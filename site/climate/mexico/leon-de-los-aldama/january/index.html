León de los Aldama in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in León de los Aldama in January?
In January, León de los Aldama averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit León de los Aldama?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of León de los Aldama’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Does it snow in León de los Aldama in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does León de los Aldama get in January?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
