How January compares in Saltillo

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Saltillo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in January?
In January, Saltillo averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Saltillo?
January is not one of Saltillo’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in January?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in January?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
