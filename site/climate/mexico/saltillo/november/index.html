How November compares in Saltillo

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with October: 2.7 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Saltillo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in November?
In November, Saltillo averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in November?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
