How September compares in Saltillo

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with August: 2.1 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 2.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Saltillo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in September?
In September, Saltillo averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in September?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in September?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
