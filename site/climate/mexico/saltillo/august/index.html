How August compares in Saltillo

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. September is 2.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Saltillo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in August?
In August, Saltillo averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in August?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in August?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
