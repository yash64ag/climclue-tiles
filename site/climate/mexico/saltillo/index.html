Saltillo at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Saltillo is 416th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 427th sunniest (about 2,134 hours of sunshine a year) and 544th wettest (450 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ramos Arizpe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Saltillo is 1.8 °C cooler in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.1 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Why Saltillo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C. The 10 km area around Saltillo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.5 °C a year, 1.0 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saltillo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.426°N, 100.980°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-585-790), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
