Saltillo weather in December

Average December weather in Saltillo, Mexico: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Saltillo averages 20.6 °C (69 °F) days and 5.9 °C (43 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain over the month and about 143 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Saltillo

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Saltillo. Compared with November: 2.4 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Saltillo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in December?
In December, Saltillo averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Saltillo?
December is not one of Saltillo’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in December?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
