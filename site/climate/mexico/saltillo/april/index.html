How April compares in Saltillo

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with March: 3.0 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. May is 2.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Saltillo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in April?
In April, Saltillo averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in April?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in April?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
