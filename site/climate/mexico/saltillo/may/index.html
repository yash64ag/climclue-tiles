How May compares in Saltillo

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with April: 2.3 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. June is 0.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Saltillo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in May?
In May, Saltillo averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in May?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in May?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
