How February compares in Saltillo

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Saltillo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in February?
In February, Saltillo averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Saltillo?
February is not one of Saltillo’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in February?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in February?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
