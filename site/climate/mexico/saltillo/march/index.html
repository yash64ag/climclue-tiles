How March compares in Saltillo

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 3.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Saltillo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in March?
In March, Saltillo averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in March?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
