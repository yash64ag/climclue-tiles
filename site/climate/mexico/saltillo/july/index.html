How July compares in Saltillo

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Saltillo. Compared with June: 0.9 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Saltillo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saltillo in July?
In July, Saltillo averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Saltillo?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Saltillo’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saltillo in July?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Saltillo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saltillo get in July?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saltillo in July?
