Pachuca de Soto at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Pachuca de Soto is the 4th coolest (14.2 °C year average). Compared with Tulancingo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Pachuca de Soto is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 73 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.8 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 184 km from the sea.

Why Pachuca de Soto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 184 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C. The 10 km area around Pachuca de Soto, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 12.9 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
