La Ermita at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, La Ermita is 394th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 345th sunniest (about 2,202 hours of sunshine a year) and 384th wettest (687 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Centro Familiar la Soledad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), La Ermita is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.6 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 352 km from the sea.

Why La Ermita has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 352 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for La Ermita itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.152°N, 101.733°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-782), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
