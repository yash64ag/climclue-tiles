How September compares in Iztapalapa

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Iztapalapa. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 1.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Iztapalapa in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iztapalapa in September?
In September, Iztapalapa averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Iztapalapa?
September is not one of Iztapalapa’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iztapalapa in September?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iztapalapa get in September?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Iztapalapa in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
