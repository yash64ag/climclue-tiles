Why Iztapalapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 254 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Iztapalapa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Iztapalapa?
May, with an average of 18.3 °C (65 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 10.6 °C (51 °F).
What is the coldest month in Iztapalapa?
January, with an average of 13.1 °C (55 °F), day highs around 19.6 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 5.7 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Iztapalapa?

What is the driest month in Iztapalapa?

What is the sunniest month in Iztapalapa?
March, with about 217 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Iztapalapa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Iztapalapa’s GeoNames location (19.355°N, 99.062°W; ClimaClue area sq-646-809), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,835,486 (GeoNames).
