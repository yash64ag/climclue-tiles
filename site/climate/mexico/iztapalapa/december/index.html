How December compares in Iztapalapa

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Iztapalapa. Compared with November: 1.1 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Iztapalapa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iztapalapa in December?
In December, Iztapalapa averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Iztapalapa?
December is not one of Iztapalapa’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iztapalapa in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iztapalapa get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Iztapalapa in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
