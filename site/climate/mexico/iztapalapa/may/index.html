Iztapalapa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iztapalapa in May?
In May, Iztapalapa averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Iztapalapa?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Iztapalapa’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iztapalapa in May?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iztapalapa get in May?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Iztapalapa in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
