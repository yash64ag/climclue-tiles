How April compares in Iztapalapa

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Iztapalapa. Compared with March: 1.7 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. May is 0.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Iztapalapa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iztapalapa in April?
In April, Iztapalapa averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Iztapalapa?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Iztapalapa’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iztapalapa in April?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iztapalapa get in April?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Iztapalapa in April?
