How November compares in Iztapalapa

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Iztapalapa. Compared with October: 1.3 °C cooler, 46 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. December is 1.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Iztapalapa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iztapalapa in November?
In November, Iztapalapa averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Iztapalapa?
November is not one of Iztapalapa’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iztapalapa in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iztapalapa get in November?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Iztapalapa in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
