How February compares in Iztapalapa

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Iztapalapa. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. March is 1.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Iztapalapa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Iztapalapa in February?
In February, Iztapalapa averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Iztapalapa?
February is not one of Iztapalapa’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Iztapalapa in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Iztapalapa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Iztapalapa get in February?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Iztapalapa in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
