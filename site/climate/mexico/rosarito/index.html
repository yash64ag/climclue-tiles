Rosarito at a glance

Among the 141 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mexico, Rosarito is 108th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,076 hours of sunshine a year) and 135th wettest (277 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tijuana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Rosarito is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.1 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, February (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 16 km from Rosarito measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.5 °C (6.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Rosarito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
