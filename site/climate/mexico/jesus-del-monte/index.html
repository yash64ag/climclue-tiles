Jesús del Monte weather by month and climate

Jesús del Monte at a glance

Among the 624 towns ClimaClue covers in Mexico, Jesús del Monte is 598th warmest (13.4 °C year average), 539th sunniest (about 2,043 hours of sunshine a year) and 198th wettest (1,047 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Delegación Cuajimalpa de Morelos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Jesús del Monte is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (15.7 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (10.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 272 km from the sea.

Why Jesús del Monte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jesús del Monte itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.375°N, 99.294°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-646-807), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
