Valletta at a glance

Among the 7 towns ClimaClue covers in Malta, Valletta is the warmest (19.1 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,881 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (535 mm a year). Compared with Sliema, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Valletta is 0.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in February, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, February (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valletta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valletta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
