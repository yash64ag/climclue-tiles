Why Laayoune has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Laayoune climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Laayoune?
August, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 30.6 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 19.8 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Laayoune?
January, with an average of 15.9 °C (61 °F), day highs around 21.4 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 11.2 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Laayoune?
December, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 65 mm (2.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Laayoune?
June, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Laayoune?
May, with about 288 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Laayoune?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Laayoune?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Laayoune’s GeoNames location (27.142°N, 13.188°W; ClimaClue area sq-568-1668), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 196,331 (GeoNames).
