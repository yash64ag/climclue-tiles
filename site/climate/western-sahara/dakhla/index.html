Dakhla at a glance

Compared with Laayoune, the nearest covered town with different numbers (474 km away), Dakhla is 1.6 °C cooler in September and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (22.1 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dakhla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dakhla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
