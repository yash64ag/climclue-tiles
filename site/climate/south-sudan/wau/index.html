Why Wau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,537 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Wau climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Wau?
March, with an average of 30.6 °C (87 °F), day highs around 37.2 °C (99 °F) and night lows around 24.7 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Wau?
August, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.6 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 21.7 °C (71 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Wau?
July, with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,057 mm (41.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Wau?
December, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Wau?
December, with about 292 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Wau?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Wau?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Wau’s GeoNames location (7.701°N, 27.990°E; ClimaClue area sq-762-2079), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 127,384 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
