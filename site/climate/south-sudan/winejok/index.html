Winejok at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Sudan, Winejok is the warmest (28.1 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,939 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (921 mm a year). Compared with Wau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (153 km away), Winejok is 1.7 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 136 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.8 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, August (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,492 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Winejok, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Winejok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,492 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
