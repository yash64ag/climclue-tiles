Lichinga at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Lichinga is the coolest (18.8 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 2,504 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mandimba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (124 km away), Lichinga is 3.5 °C cooler in November and 3.2 °C cooler in July, with 158 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (21.8 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, July (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 538 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lichinga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lichinga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 538 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
