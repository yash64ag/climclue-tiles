Angoche at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Angoche is the 3rd warmest (25.4 °C year average) and the sunniest (about 3,058 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nampula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (142 km away), Angoche is 1.4 °C warmer in December and 2.4 °C warmer in July, with 203 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.4 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, July (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Angoche, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Angoche has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
