Xai-Xai at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Xai-Xai is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,704 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Maputo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Xai-Xai is 1.0 °C warmer in January and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.9 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, July (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xai-Xai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xai-Xai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
