Xai-Xai sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gaza Province · Mozambique

The sea off Xai-Xai averages 27.3 °C (81 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 22.4 °C (72 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May.

Xai-Xai sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xai-Xai?
In February, at about 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xai-Xai?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May. The coolest month is September, at 22.4 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xai-Xai in September?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Xai-Xai?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Xai-Xai nearby?
In February: Massinga (28.2 °C, 260 km) and Vilankulo (28.4 °C, 380 km).
