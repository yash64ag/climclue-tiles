Vilankulo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Inhambane Province · Mozambique

The sea off Vilankulo averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Vilankulo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vilankulo?
In February, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vilankulo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.8 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vilankulo in August?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Vilankulo?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vilankulo nearby?
In February: Beira (29.2 °C, 245 km).
