Tete at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Tete is the warmest (26.7 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (680 mm a year). Compared with Mandimba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (299 km away), Tete is 5.1 °C warmer in November and 4.1 °C warmer in July, with 358 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (30.4 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, July (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 407 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tete, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tete has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 407 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
