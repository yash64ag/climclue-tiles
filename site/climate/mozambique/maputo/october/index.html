How October compares in Maputo

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Maputo. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Maputo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in October?
In October, Maputo averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Maputo?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Maputo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in October?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (145 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in October?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maputo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
