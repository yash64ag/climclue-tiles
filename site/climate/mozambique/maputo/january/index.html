How January compares in Maputo

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Maputo. Compared with December: 0.8 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Maputo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in January?
In January, Maputo averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Maputo?
January is not one of Maputo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in January?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (143 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in January?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maputo in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
