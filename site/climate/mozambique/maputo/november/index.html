How November compares in Maputo

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Maputo. Compared with October: 1.3 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. December is 1.3 °C warmer than November on average.

Maputo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in November?
In November, Maputo averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Maputo?
November is not one of Maputo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in November?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (143 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in November?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maputo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
