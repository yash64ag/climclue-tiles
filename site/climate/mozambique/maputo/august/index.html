How August compares in Maputo

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Maputo. Compared with July: 1.0 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. September is 1.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Maputo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in August?
In August, Maputo averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Maputo?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Maputo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in August?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (145 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in August?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Maputo in August?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (27.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Maputo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
