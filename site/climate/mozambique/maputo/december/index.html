How December compares in Maputo

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Maputo. Compared with November: 1.3 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Maputo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in December?
In December, Maputo averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Maputo?
December is not one of Maputo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in December?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (143 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in December?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maputo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
