How April compares in Maputo

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Maputo. Compared with March: 1.6 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. May is 2.0 °C cooler than April on average.

Maputo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in April?
In April, Maputo averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Maputo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Maputo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in April?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (145 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in April?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Maputo in April?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is February (27.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Maputo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
