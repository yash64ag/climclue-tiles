How July compares in Maputo

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Maputo. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. August is 1.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Maputo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maputo in July?
In July, Maputo averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Maputo?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Maputo’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maputo in July?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (143 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Maputo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maputo get in July?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maputo in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
