Massinga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Inhambane Province · Mozambique

The sea off Massinga averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 23.7 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to July.

Massinga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Massinga?
In February, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Massinga?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.7 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Massinga in August?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Massinga?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Massinga nearby?
In February: Beira (29.2 °C, 392 km) and Chinde (28.9 °C, 540 km).
