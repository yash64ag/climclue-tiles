Pemba at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Mozambique, Pemba is the 5th warmest (25.7 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 3,050 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Macomia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (92 km away), Pemba is 2.0 °C warmer in December and 2.1 °C warmer in July, with 355 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.2 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pemba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pemba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pemba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.974°S, 40.518°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-969-2205), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
