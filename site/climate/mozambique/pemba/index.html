Pemba at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Pemba is the 2nd warmest (25.7 °C year average) and the 3rd sunniest (about 3,050 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nacala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (178 km away), Pemba is 0.5 °C warmer in December and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 113 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.3 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.7 °C (69 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pemba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pemba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
