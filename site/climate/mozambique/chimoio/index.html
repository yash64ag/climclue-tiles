Why Chimoio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 172 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Chimoio climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chimoio?
January, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 27.5 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 19.6 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chimoio?
July, with an average of 16.6 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.9 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 11.6 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chimoio?
January, with 266 mm (10.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,208 mm (47.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Chimoio?
June, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Chimoio?
August, with about 259 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chimoio?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Chimoio?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Chimoio have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Chimoio’s GeoNames location (19.116°S, 33.483°E; ClimaClue area sq-1031-2134), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 422,046 (GeoNames).
