Ressano Garcia at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Ressano Garcia is the driest (615 mm a year). Compared with Matola, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Ressano Garcia is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 144 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.4 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, July (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Why Ressano Garcia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
