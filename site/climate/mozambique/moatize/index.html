Moatize at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Mozambique, Moatize is the 2nd warmest (26.6 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (660 mm a year). Compared with Tete, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Moatize is 0.3 °C cooler in November and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (30.3 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, July (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 395 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Moatize, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Moatize has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 395 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Moatize itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.115°S, 33.729°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1001-2137), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
