Beira at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Beira is the 2nd wettest (1,482 mm a year). Compared with Chimoio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (164 km away), Beira is 3.8 °C warmer in January and 4.4 °C warmer in July, with 275 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (27.1 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beira, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Beira

For mild weather, the best months to visit Beira are May, June, July, August, September and October.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August, September and October.

Why Beira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
