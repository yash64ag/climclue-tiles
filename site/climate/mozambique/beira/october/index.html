How October compares in Beira

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Beira. Compared with September: 1.3 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 1.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Beira in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beira in October?
In October, Beira averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Beira?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Beira’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beira in October?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (268 mm), the driest September (20 mm).
Does it snow in Beira in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beira get in October?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beira in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
