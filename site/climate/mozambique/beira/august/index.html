How August compares in Beira

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Beira. Compared with July: 0.9 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. September is 1.6 °C warmer than August on average.

Beira in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beira in August?
In August, Beira averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Beira?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Beira’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beira in August?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (268 mm), the driest September (20 mm).
Does it snow in Beira in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beira get in August?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beira in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
