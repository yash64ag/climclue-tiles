Beira weather in July

Average July weather in Beira, Mozambique: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Beira averages 26.1 °C (79 °F) days and 15.8 °C (60 °F) nights, with 41 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 241 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Beira

July is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Beira. Compared with June: 0.6 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. August is 0.9 °C warmer than July on average.

Beira in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beira in July?
In July, Beira averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Beira?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Beira’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beira in July?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (266 mm), the driest September (20 mm).
Does it snow in Beira in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beira get in July?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Beira in July?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Beira in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
