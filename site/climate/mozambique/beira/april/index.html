How April compares in Beira

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Beira. Compared with March: 1.4 °C cooler, 131 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. May is 1.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Beira in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beira in April?
In April, Beira averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Beira?
April is not one of Beira’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beira in April?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (268 mm), the driest September (20 mm).
Does it snow in Beira in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beira get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beira in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
