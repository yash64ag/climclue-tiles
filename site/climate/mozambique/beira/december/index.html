Beira weather in December

Average December weather in Beira, Mozambique: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Beira averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 22.9 °C (73 °F) nights, with 234 mm (9.2 in) of rain over the month and about 242 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Beira

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Beira. Compared with November: 0.7 °C warmer, 145 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Beira in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beira in December?
In December, Beira averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Beira?
December is not one of Beira’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beira in December?
234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (266 mm), the driest September (20 mm).
Does it snow in Beira in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beira get in December?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Beira in December?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is February (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Beira in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
