How May compares in Beira

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Beira. Compared with April: 1.8 °C cooler, 65 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Beira in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beira in May?
In May, Beira averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Beira?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Beira’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beira in May?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (268 mm), the driest September (20 mm).
Does it snow in Beira in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beira get in May?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beira in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
