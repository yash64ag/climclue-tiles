Mandimba at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Mandimba is 14th warmest (22.1 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,728 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,038 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lichinga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (124 km away), Mandimba is 3.5 °C warmer in November and 3.2 °C warmer in July, with 158 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (25.3 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, July (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 419 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mandimba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mandimba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 419 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
