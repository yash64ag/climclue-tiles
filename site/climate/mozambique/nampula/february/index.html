How February compares in Nampula

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nampula. Compared with January: 0.3 °C cooler, 58 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Nampula in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nampula in February?
In February, Nampula averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nampula?
February is not one of Nampula’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nampula in February?
256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (314 mm), the driest September (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nampula in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nampula get in February?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nampula in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
