How December compares in Nampula

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nampula. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 142 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Nampula in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nampula in December?
In December, Nampula averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nampula?
December is not one of Nampula’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nampula in December?
202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (314 mm), the driest September (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nampula in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nampula get in December?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nampula in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
