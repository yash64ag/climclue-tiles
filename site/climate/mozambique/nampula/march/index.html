Nampula weather in March

Average March weather in Nampula, Mozambique: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Nampula averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) days and 20.3 °C (69 °F) nights, with 217 mm (8.5 in) of rain over the month and about 221 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Nampula

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nampula. Compared with February: 0.2 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. April is 1.0 °C cooler than March on average.

Nampula in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nampula in March?
In March, Nampula averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nampula?
March is not one of Nampula’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nampula in March?
217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (324 mm), the driest September (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nampula in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nampula get in March?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nampula in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
