Nampula at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Nampula is 9th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,838 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (1,221 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Angoche, the nearest covered town with different numbers (142 km away), Nampula is 0.5 °C cooler in November and 2.4 °C cooler in July, with 203 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.4 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 139 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nampula, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nampula has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 139 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
