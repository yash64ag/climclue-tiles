How April compares in Nampula

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nampula. Compared with March: 1.0 °C cooler, 126 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 1.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Nampula in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nampula in April?
In April, Nampula averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nampula?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Nampula’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nampula in April?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (314 mm), the driest September (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nampula in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nampula get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nampula in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
