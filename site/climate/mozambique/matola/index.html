Matola at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Matola is the 3rd driest (764 mm a year). Compared with Maputo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Matola is 0.2 °C warmer in January and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.1 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, July (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matola, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matola has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
