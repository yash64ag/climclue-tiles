How January compares in Matola

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Matola. Compared with December: 0.8 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Matola in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in January?
In January, Matola averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Matola?
January is not one of Matola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in January?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (139 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in January?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
