How December compares in Matola

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Matola. Compared with November: 1.3 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Matola in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in December?
In December, Matola averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Matola?
December is not one of Matola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in December?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (139 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in December?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
