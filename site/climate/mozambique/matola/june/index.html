How June compares in Matola

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Matola. Compared with May: 2.0 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Matola in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in June?
In June, Matola averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Matola?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Matola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in June?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (138 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in June?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Matola in June?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (27.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Matola in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
