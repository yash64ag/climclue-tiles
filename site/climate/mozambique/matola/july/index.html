How July compares in Matola

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Matola. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. August is 1.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Matola in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in July?
In July, Matola averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Matola?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Matola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in July?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (138 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in July?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Matola in July?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (27.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Matola in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
