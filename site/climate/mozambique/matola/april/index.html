How April compares in Matola

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Matola. Compared with March: 1.8 °C cooler, 49 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. May is 2.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Matola in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in April?
In April, Matola averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Matola?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Matola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in April?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (138 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in April?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
