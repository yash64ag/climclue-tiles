How February compares in Matola

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Matola. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 0.7 °C cooler than February on average.

Matola in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in February?
In February, Matola averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Matola?
February is not one of Matola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in February?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (139 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in February?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
