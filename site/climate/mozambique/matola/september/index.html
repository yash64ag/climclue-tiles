How September compares in Matola

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Matola. Compared with August: 1.6 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Matola in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in September?
In September, Matola averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Matola?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Matola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in September?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (139 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in September?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
