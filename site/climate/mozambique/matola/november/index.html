How November compares in Matola

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Matola. Compared with October: 1.3 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. December is 1.3 °C warmer than November on average.

Matola in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in November?
In November, Matola averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Matola?
November is not one of Matola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in November?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (139 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in November?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
