How October compares in Matola

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Matola. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Matola in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matola in October?
In October, Matola averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Matola?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Matola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matola in October?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (139 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Matola in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matola get in October?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matola in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
