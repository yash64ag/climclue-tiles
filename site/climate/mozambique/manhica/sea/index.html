Manhiça sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maputo Province · Mozambique

The sea off Manhiça averages 27.3 °C (81 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 21.8 °C (71 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May.

Manhiça sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Manhiça?
In February, at about 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Manhiça?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May. The coolest month is August, at 21.8 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in Manhiça in August?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Manhiça?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Manhiça nearby?
In February: Massinga (28.2 °C, 348 km) and Vilankulo (28.4 °C, 456 km).
