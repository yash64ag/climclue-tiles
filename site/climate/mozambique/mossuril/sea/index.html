Mossuril sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nampula · Mozambique

The sea off Mossuril averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Mossuril sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mossuril?
In March, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mossuril?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.2 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mossuril in August?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Mossuril?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mossuril nearby?
In March: Maintirano (29.5 °C, 497 km).
