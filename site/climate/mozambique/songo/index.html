Songo at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Mozambique, Songo is 54th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,886 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (1,043 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tete, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Songo is 5.0 °C cooler in November and 5.3 °C cooler in July, with 388 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (25.6 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.7 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Songo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Songo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 511 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C. The 10 km area around Songo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.0 °C a year, 2.1 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Songo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.596°S, 32.765°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-995-2127), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
