Nacala at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mozambique, Nacala is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,053 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nampula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (164 km away), Nacala is 0.7 °C warmer in December and 2.6 °C warmer in July, with 213 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (26.4 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nacala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nacala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nacala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.563°S, 40.685°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-985-2206), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
