Koutiala at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mali, Koutiala is the 3rd coolest (27.7 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (906 mm a year). Compared with San, the nearest covered town with different numbers (119 km away), Koutiala is 1.1 °C cooler in April and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 230 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.1 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, January (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 810 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Koutiala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Koutiala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 810 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
