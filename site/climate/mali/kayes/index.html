Kayes at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mali, Kayes is the 2nd warmest (30.4 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (589 mm a year). Compared with Kati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (411 km away), Kayes is 4.5 °C warmer in May and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 323 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (36.0 °C), averages 10.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 545 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kayes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kayes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 545 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
