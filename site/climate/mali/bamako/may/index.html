How May compares in Bamako

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bamako. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Bamako in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bamako in May?
In May, Bamako averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bamako?
May is not one of Bamako’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bamako in May?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (267 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bamako in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bamako get in May?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bamako in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
