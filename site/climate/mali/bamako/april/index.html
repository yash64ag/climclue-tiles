How April compares in Bamako

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bamako. Compared with March: 2.1 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. May is 0.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Bamako in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bamako in April?
In April, Bamako averages a day high of 39.1 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bamako?
April is not one of Bamako’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bamako in April?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (267 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bamako in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bamako get in April?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bamako in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
