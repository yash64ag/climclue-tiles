Gao at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mali, Gao is the warmest (30.5 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,086 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (172 mm a year). Compared with Mopti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (486 km away), Gao is 1.2 °C warmer in May and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 309 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (36.0 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,137 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gao, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,137 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
