Kati at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mali, Kati is the coolest (27.1 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,932 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (912 mm a year). Compared with Bamako, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Kati is 0.9 °C cooler in April and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.9 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 709 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kati, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kati has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 709 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
