Abéché at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chad, Abéché is the 2nd warmest (28.1 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,451 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (382 mm a year). Compared with Sarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (585 km away), Abéché is 2.5 °C warmer in May and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 632 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.4 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,674 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Abéché, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Abéché has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,674 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
