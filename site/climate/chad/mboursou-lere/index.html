Mboursou Léré at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Chad, Mboursou Léré is 13th warmest (28.4 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,907 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (844 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Léré, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Mboursou Léré is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (33.3 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 830 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Mboursou Léré has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 830 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mboursou Léré itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.764°N, 14.154°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-742-1941), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
