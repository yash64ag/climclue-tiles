Fada at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Chad, Fada is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,658 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (54 mm a year). Compared with Amdjarass, the nearest covered town with different numbers (183 km away), Fada is 3.7 °C warmer in June and 4.7 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.6 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,486 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Fada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,486 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fada itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.192°N, 21.581°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-668-2015), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
