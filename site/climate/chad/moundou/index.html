Moundou at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chad, Moundou is the coolest (27.0 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,765 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,007 mm a year). Compared with Sarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (261 km away), Moundou is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 897 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Moundou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Moundou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 897 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
