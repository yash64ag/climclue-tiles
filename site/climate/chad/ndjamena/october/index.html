How October compares in N'Djamena

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in N'Djamena. Compared with September: 1.6 °C warmer, 61 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. November is 1.9 °C cooler than October on average.

N'Djamena in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in N'Djamena in October?
In October, N'Djamena averages a day high of 36.3 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit N'Djamena?
October is not one of N'Djamena’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in N'Djamena in October?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (165 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in N'Djamena in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does N'Djamena get in October?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for N'Djamena in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
