How September compares in N'Djamena

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in N'Djamena. Compared with August: 1.1 °C warmer, 88 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. October is 1.7 °C warmer than September on average.

N'Djamena in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in N'Djamena in September?
In September, N'Djamena averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit N'Djamena?
September is not one of N'Djamena’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in N'Djamena in September?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in N'Djamena in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does N'Djamena get in September?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for N'Djamena in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
