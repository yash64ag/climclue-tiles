How August compares in N'Djamena

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in N'Djamena. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C warmer than August on average.

N'Djamena in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in N'Djamena in August?
In August, N'Djamena averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit N'Djamena?
August is not one of N'Djamena’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in N'Djamena in August?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (165 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in N'Djamena in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does N'Djamena get in August?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for N'Djamena in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
