Saco sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maine · United States

The sea off Saco averages 17.1 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.1 °C (38 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Saco sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saco?
In August, at about 17.1 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saco?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 3.1 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saco in March?
About 3.1 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saco?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Saco nearby?
In August: Amesbury (17.8 °C, 82 km), Newburyport (17.8 °C, 84 km) and Gloucester (17.8 °C, 100 km).
