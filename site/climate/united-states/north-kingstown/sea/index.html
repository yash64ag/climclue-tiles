North Kingstown sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rhode Island · United States

The sea off North Kingstown averages 20.0 °C (68 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.2 °C (38 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

North Kingstown sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in North Kingstown?
In August, at about 20.0 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in North Kingstown?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 3.2 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in North Kingstown in February?
About 3.2 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in North Kingstown?
About 11.4 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in North Kingstown nearby?
In August: Westerly (20.6 °C, 36 km), Ledyard (20.9 °C, 47 km) and New London (20.9 °C, 57 km).
