How June compares in Chicago

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with May: 5.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. July is 2.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Chicago in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in June?
In June, Chicago averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chicago?
June is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in June?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in June?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
