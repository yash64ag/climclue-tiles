How September compares in Chicago

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. October is 6.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Chicago in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in September?
In September, Chicago averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chicago?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Chicago’s mild months (September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in September?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (115 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in September?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
