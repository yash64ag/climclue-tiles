How April compares in Chicago

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. May is 6.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Chicago in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in April?
In April, Chicago averages a day high of 12.1 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chicago?
April is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in April?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (115 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.9 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
