Chicago weather in November

Average November weather in Chicago, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Chicago averages 10.1 °C (50 °F) days and 1.3 °C (34 °F) nights, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 110 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Chicago

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. December is 6.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Chicago in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in November?
In November, Chicago averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chicago?
November is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in November?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in November?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
