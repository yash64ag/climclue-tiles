How August compares in Chicago

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Chicago in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in August?
In August, Chicago averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chicago?
August is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in August?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (115 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in August?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
