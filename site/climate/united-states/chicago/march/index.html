How March compares in Chicago

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with February: 4.4 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Chicago in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in March?
In March, Chicago averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chicago?
March is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in March?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (115 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in March?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
