How February compares in Chicago

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. March is 5.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Chicago in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in February?
In February, Chicago averages a day high of 2.4 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -5.6 °C (22 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Chicago?
February is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in February?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in February?
Yes, on average: about 20 cm (7.7 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in February?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
