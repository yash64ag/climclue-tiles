How January compares in Chicago

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Chicago in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in January?
In January, Chicago averages a day high of 0.4 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -7.1 °C (19 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chicago?
January is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in January?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in January?
Yes, on average: about 23 cm (9.0 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in January?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
