How May compares in Chicago

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chicago. Compared with April: 5.8 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. June is 5.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses.

Chicago in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chicago in May?
In May, Chicago averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Chicago?
May is not one of Chicago’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chicago in May?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (57 mm).
Does it snow in Chicago in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chicago get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chicago in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
