Why South Fulton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 393 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

South Fulton climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in South Fulton?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 31.1 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 21.6 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in South Fulton?
January, with an average of 6.0 °C (43 °F), day highs around 11.3 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 2.1 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in South Fulton?
July, with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,330 mm (52.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in South Fulton?
October, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in South Fulton?
May, with about 285 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in South Fulton?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 5.9 cm (2.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in South Fulton?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains South Fulton’s GeoNames location (33.593°N, 84.673°W; ClimaClue area sq-504-953), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 107,436 (GeoNames).
