La Crescenta-Montrose at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, La Crescenta-Montrose is 808th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 385th sunniest (about 3,158 hours of sunshine a year) and 2717th wettest (469 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Cañada Flintridge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), La Crescenta-Montrose is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C cooler in December, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.7 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, December (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in December. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Why La Crescenta-Montrose has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for La Crescenta-Montrose itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.232°N, 118.235°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-497-617), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
