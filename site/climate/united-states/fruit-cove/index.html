Fruit Cove at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Fruit Cove is 382nd warmest (20.5 °C year average), 921st sunniest (about 2,855 hours of sunshine a year) and 352nd wettest (1,376 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fleming Island, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Fruit Cove is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Why Fruit Cove has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
