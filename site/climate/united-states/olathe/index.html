Olathe at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Olathe is 222nd warmest (12.9 °C year average), 182nd sunniest (about 2,720 hours of sunshine a year) and 130th wettest (1,086 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Overland Park, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Olathe is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,028 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Olathe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,028 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
