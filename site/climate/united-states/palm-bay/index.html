Why Palm Bay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Palm Bay climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Palm Bay?
August, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 29.7 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 25.2 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Palm Bay?
January, with an average of 17.4 °C (63 °F), day highs around 21.1 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 15.1 °C (59 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Palm Bay?
August, with 197 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,341 mm (52.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Palm Bay?
January, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Palm Bay?
July, with about 282 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Palm Bay?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Palm Bay?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Palm Bay’s GeoNames location (28.034°N, 80.589°W; ClimaClue area sq-559-994), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 119,760 (GeoNames).
