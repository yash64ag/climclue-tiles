Why Beaumont has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Beaumont climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Beaumont?
July, with an average of 27.8 °C (82 °F), day highs around 32.1 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 24.3 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Beaumont?
January, with an average of 11.2 °C (52 °F), day highs around 16.6 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 7.6 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Beaumont?
June, with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,540 mm (60.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Beaumont?
April, with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Beaumont?
June, with about 283 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Beaumont?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Beaumont?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March and April.
What type of climate does Beaumont have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.6 × 11 km, that contains Beaumont’s GeoNames location (30.086°N, 94.102°W; ClimaClue area sq-539-858), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 115,282 (GeoNames).
