Eugene at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Eugene is 248th warmest (11.7 °C year average), 275th sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (1,288 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Eugene is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, December (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 14 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Eugene has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
