How November compares in Columbus, Ohio

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Columbus, Ohio. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 76 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Columbus, Ohio in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Columbus, Ohio in November?
In November, Columbus, Ohio averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Columbus, Ohio?
November is not one of Columbus, Ohio’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Columbus, Ohio in November?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (116 mm), the driest February (59 mm).
Does it snow in Columbus, Ohio in November?
Yes, on average: about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Columbus, Ohio get in November?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Columbus, Ohio in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
