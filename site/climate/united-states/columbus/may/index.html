Columbus, Ohio in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Columbus, Ohio in May?
In May, Columbus, Ohio averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Columbus, Ohio?
May is not one of Columbus, Ohio’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Columbus, Ohio in May?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (116 mm), the driest February (59 mm).
Does it snow in Columbus, Ohio in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Columbus, Ohio get in May?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Columbus, Ohio in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
