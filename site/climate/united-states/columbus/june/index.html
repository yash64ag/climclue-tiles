Columbus, Ohio in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Columbus, Ohio in June?
In June, Columbus, Ohio averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Columbus, Ohio?
June is not one of Columbus, Ohio’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Columbus, Ohio in June?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (116 mm), the driest February (59 mm).
Does it snow in Columbus, Ohio in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Columbus, Ohio get in June?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Columbus, Ohio in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
