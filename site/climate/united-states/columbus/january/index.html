How January compares in Columbus, Ohio

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Columbus, Ohio. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Columbus, Ohio in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Columbus, Ohio in January?
In January, Columbus, Ohio averages a day high of 2.7 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Columbus, Ohio?
January is not one of Columbus, Ohio’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Columbus, Ohio in January?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (118 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Columbus, Ohio in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Columbus, Ohio get in January?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Columbus, Ohio in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
