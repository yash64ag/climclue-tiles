Columbus, Ohio weather in December

Average December weather in Columbus, Ohio, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Columbus, Ohio averages 4.7 °C (40 °F) days and -3.8 °C (25 °F) nights, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 85 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Columbus, Ohio

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Columbus, Ohio. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Columbus, Ohio in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Columbus, Ohio in December?
In December, Columbus, Ohio averages a day high of 4.7 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Columbus, Ohio?
December is not one of Columbus, Ohio’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Columbus, Ohio in December?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (116 mm), the driest February (59 mm).
Does it snow in Columbus, Ohio in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Columbus, Ohio get in December?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Columbus, Ohio in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
