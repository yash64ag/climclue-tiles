Columbus, Ohio in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Columbus, Ohio in October?
In October, Columbus, Ohio averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Columbus, Ohio?
October is not one of Columbus, Ohio’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Columbus, Ohio in October?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (118 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Columbus, Ohio in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Columbus, Ohio get in October?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Columbus, Ohio in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
