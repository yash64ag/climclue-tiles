Why Sandy Springs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 397 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Sandy Springs climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sandy Springs?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 21.5 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sandy Springs?
January, with an average of 5.5 °C (42 °F), day highs around 10.6 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 1.6 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sandy Springs?
March, with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,367 mm (53.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sandy Springs?
October, with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sandy Springs?
May, with about 279 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sandy Springs?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 8.0 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sandy Springs?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Sandy Springs have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Sandy Springs’s GeoNames location (33.924°N, 84.379°W; ClimaClue area sq-500-956), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 105,330 (GeoNames).
