Jupiter sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Jupiter averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 24.3 °C (76 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Jupiter sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jupiter?
In August, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jupiter?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.3 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jupiter in February?
About 24.3 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Jupiter?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
