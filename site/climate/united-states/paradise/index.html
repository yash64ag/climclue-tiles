Paradise at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Paradise is the 5th driest (147 mm a year). Compared with Las Vegas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Paradise is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.5 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, December (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 377 km from the sea.

Why Paradise has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 377 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
