Why Paradise has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 377 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Paradise climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Paradise?
July, with an average of 33.5 °C (92 °F), day highs around 39.6 °C (103 °F) and night lows around 25.8 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Paradise?
December, with an average of 8.6 °C (47 °F), day highs around 14.2 °C (58 °F) and night lows around 3.9 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Paradise?
February, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 147 mm (5.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Paradise?
June, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Paradise?
June, with about 397 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Paradise?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Paradise?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April and October.
What type of climate does Paradise have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Paradise’s GeoNames location (36.097°N, 115.147°W; ClimaClue area sq-479-648), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 223,167 (GeoNames).
