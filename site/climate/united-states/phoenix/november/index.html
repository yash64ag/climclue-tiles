How November compares in Phoenix

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Phoenix. Compared with October: 7.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Phoenix in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phoenix in November?
In November, Phoenix averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Phoenix?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Phoenix’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phoenix in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (29 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Phoenix in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phoenix get in November?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phoenix in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
