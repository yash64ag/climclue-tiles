How April compares in Phoenix

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Phoenix. Compared with March: 4.6 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Phoenix in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phoenix in April?
In April, Phoenix averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 350 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Phoenix?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Phoenix’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phoenix in April?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (29 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Phoenix in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phoenix get in April?
About 350 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phoenix in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
