How June compares in Phoenix

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Phoenix. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Phoenix in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phoenix in June?
In June, Phoenix averages a day high of 39.8 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 404 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Phoenix?
June is not one of Phoenix’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phoenix in June?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (29 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Phoenix in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phoenix get in June?
About 404 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phoenix in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
