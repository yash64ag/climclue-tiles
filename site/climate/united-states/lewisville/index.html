Lewisville at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Lewisville is 71st warmest (18.7 °C year average), 151st sunniest (about 2,824 hours of sunshine a year) and 165th wettest (973 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Carrollton, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Lewisville is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.4 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 437 km from the sea.

Why Lewisville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 437 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
