Edmonds sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Washington · United States

The sea off Edmonds averages 11.5 °C (53 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.7 °C (46 °F) in April. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Edmonds sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Edmonds?
In August, at about 11.5 °C (53 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Edmonds?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is April, at 7.7 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Edmonds in April?
About 7.7 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Edmonds?
About 9.0 °C (48 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Edmonds nearby?
In August: Anacortes (12.4 °C, 80 km).
