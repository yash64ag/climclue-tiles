Tucson weather in September

Average September weather in Tucson, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Tucson averages 35.0 °C (95 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of rain over the month and about 311 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Tucson

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tucson. Compared with August: 1.8 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Tucson in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tucson in September?
In September, Tucson averages a day high of 35.0 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 311 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tucson?
September is not one of Tucson’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tucson in September?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (59 mm), the driest May (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tucson in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tucson get in September?
About 311 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tucson in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
