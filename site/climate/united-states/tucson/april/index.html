How April compares in Tucson

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tucson. Compared with March: 3.9 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Tucson in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tucson in April?
In April, Tucson averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 350 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tucson?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Tucson’s mild months (March, April, May, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tucson in April?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (59 mm), the driest May (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tucson in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tucson get in April?
About 350 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tucson in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
