Why Tucson has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 241 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tucson climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tucson?
July, with an average of 31.4 °C (88 °F), day highs around 37.3 °C (99 °F) and night lows around 25.3 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tucson?
December, with an average of 11.1 °C (52 °F), day highs around 17.9 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 4.9 °C (41 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tucson?
August, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 311 mm (12.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tucson?
May, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tucson?
May, with about 395 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tucson?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tucson?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, October and November.
What type of climate does Tucson have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Tucson’s GeoNames location (32.222°N, 110.926°W; ClimaClue area sq-517-690), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 542,629 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
