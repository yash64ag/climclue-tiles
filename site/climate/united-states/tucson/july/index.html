How July compares in Tucson

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tucson. Compared with June: 0.5 °C warmer, 51 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Tucson in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tucson in July?
In July, Tucson averages a day high of 37.3 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 341 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tucson?
July is not one of Tucson’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tucson in July?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (58 mm), the driest May (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tucson in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tucson get in July?
About 341 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tucson in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
