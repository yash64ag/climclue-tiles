How January compares in Tucson

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tucson. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Tucson in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tucson in January?
In January, Tucson averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tucson?
January is not one of Tucson’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tucson in January?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (58 mm), the driest May (5 mm).
Does it snow in Tucson in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tucson get in January?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tucson in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
