Richmond, California at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Richmond, California is 197th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 3,100 hours of sunshine a year) and 212th wettest (651 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Berkeley, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Richmond, California is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.1 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Why Richmond, California has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
