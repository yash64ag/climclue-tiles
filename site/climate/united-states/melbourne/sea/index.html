Melbourne sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Melbourne averages 28.0 °C (82 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 20.1 °C (68 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Melbourne sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Melbourne?
In September, at about 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Melbourne?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 20.1 °C (68 °F).
How warm is the sea in Melbourne in February?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Melbourne?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
