Coeur d'Alene weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Coeur d'Alene, United States (Idaho), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Coeur d'Alene is warmest in July, with 30.5 °C (87 °F) days and 12.8 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 2.0 °C (36 °F) days and -4.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around November (89 mm); August is driest (22 mm).

Why Coeur d'Alene has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 427 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Coeur d'Alene itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.678°N, 116.780°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-363-632), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
