Victorville at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Victorville is 790th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 176th sunniest (about 3,328 hours of sunshine a year) and 3091st wettest (185 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Apple Valley, California, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Victorville is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, December (8.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 129 km from the sea.

Why Victorville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Victorville itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.536°N, 117.291°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-494-627), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
