Why Victorville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Victorville climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Victorville?
July, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 35.8 °C (96 °F) and night lows around 19.1 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Victorville?
December, with an average of 7.8 °C (46 °F), day highs around 13.8 °C (57 °F) and night lows around 2.8 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Victorville?
February, with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 189 mm (7.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Victorville?
June, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Victorville?
July, with about 361 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Victorville?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Victorville?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Victorville’s GeoNames location (34.536°N, 117.291°W; ClimaClue area sq-494-627), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 122,225 (GeoNames).
