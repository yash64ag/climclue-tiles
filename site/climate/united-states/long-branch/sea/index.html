Long Branch sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New Jersey · United States

The sea off Long Branch averages 22.6 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.0 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Long Branch sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Long Branch?
In August, at about 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Long Branch?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 4.0 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Long Branch in February?
About 4.0 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Long Branch?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
