Fresno, California weather in June

Average June weather in Fresno, California, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Fresno, California averages 33.8 °C (93 °F) days and 15.7 °C (60 °F) nights, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 403 hours of sunshine.

Is June a good time to visit Fresno, California?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Fresno, California’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
