How March compares in Fresno

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Fresno. Compared with February: 2.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 88 hours more sunshine. April is 2.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Fresno in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fresno in March?
In March, Fresno averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Fresno?
March is not one of Fresno’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fresno in March?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (60 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Fresno in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fresno get in March?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fresno in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
