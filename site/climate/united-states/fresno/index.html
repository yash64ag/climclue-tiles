Fresno at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Fresno is 99th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 3,489 hours of sunshine a year) and 294th wettest (312 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Clovis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Fresno is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, December (8.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 184 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Fresno has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 184 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fresno itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.748°N, 119.772°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-472-602), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
