How December compares in Fresno

December is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fresno. Compared with November: 3.9 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Fresno in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fresno in December?
In December, Fresno averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fresno?
December is not one of Fresno’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fresno in December?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (60 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Fresno in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fresno get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fresno in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
