How April compares in Fresno

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Fresno. Compared with March: 2.8 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. May is 4.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Fresno in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fresno in April?
In April, Fresno averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Fresno?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Fresno’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fresno in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (60 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Fresno in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fresno get in April?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fresno in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
