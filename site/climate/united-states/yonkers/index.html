Yonkers at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Yonkers is 241st warmest (11.8 °C year average), 249th sunniest (about 2,519 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (1,239 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with The Bronx, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Yonkers is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 94% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yonkers has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
