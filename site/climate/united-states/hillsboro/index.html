Hillsboro at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Hillsboro is 243rd warmest (11.8 °C year average), 305th sunniest (about 2,266 hours of sunshine a year) and 108th wettest (1,168 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Portland, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Hillsboro is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in December, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.9 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, December (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Why Hillsboro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
