How December compares in Milwaukee

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Milwaukee. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 2.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Milwaukee in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milwaukee in December?
In December, Milwaukee averages a day high of 1.0 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -4.6 °C (24 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Milwaukee?
December is not one of Milwaukee’s mild months; those are July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milwaukee in December?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest January (39 mm).
Does it snow in Milwaukee in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Milwaukee get in December?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milwaukee in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
