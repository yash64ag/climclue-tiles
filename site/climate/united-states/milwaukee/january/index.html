Milwaukee weather in January

Average January weather in Milwaukee, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Milwaukee averages -0.6 °C (31 °F) days and -9.0 °C (16 °F) nights, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 137 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Milwaukee

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Milwaukee. Compared with December: 3.0 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Milwaukee in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milwaukee in January?
In January, Milwaukee averages a day high of -0.6 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -9.0 °C (16 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Milwaukee?
January is not one of Milwaukee’s mild months; those are July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milwaukee in January?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (104 mm), the driest February (39 mm).
Does it snow in Milwaukee in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Milwaukee get in January?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milwaukee in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
