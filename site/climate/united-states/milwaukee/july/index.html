Milwaukee in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milwaukee in July?
In July, Milwaukee averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 316 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Milwaukee?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Milwaukee’s mild months (July and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milwaukee in July?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (104 mm), the driest February (39 mm).
Does it snow in Milwaukee in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milwaukee get in July?
About 316 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milwaukee in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
