How February compares in Milwaukee

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Milwaukee. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Milwaukee in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milwaukee in February?
In February, Milwaukee averages a day high of -0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -7.1 °C (19 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Milwaukee?
February is not one of Milwaukee’s mild months; those are July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milwaukee in February?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest January (39 mm).
Does it snow in Milwaukee in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Milwaukee get in February?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milwaukee in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
