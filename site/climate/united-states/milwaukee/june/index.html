How June compares in Milwaukee

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Milwaukee. Compared with May: 6.2 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. July is 3.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Milwaukee in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milwaukee in June?
In June, Milwaukee averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 300 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Milwaukee?
June is not one of Milwaukee’s mild months; those are July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milwaukee in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (104 mm), the driest February (39 mm).
Does it snow in Milwaukee in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milwaukee get in June?
About 300 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milwaukee in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
