Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Aurora, Colorado is 2647th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 588th sunniest (about 3,066 hours of sunshine a year) and 2798th wettest (414 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Aurora, Colorado has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aurora itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.729°N, 104.832°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-442-751), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
