Glendale, California at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Glendale, California is 110th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 3,181 hours of sunshine a year) and 251st wettest (456 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Burbank, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Glendale, California is 0.2 °C warmer in August and about as warm in December, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.3 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, December (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in December. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Why Glendale, California has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
