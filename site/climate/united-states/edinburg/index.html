Edinburg at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Edinburg is 131st warmest (23.8 °C year average), 2610th sunniest (about 2,430 hours of sunshine a year) and 2556th wettest (587 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pharr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Edinburg is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Why Edinburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Edinburg itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.302°N, 98.163°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-576-818), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
