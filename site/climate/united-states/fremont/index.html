Fremont at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Fremont is 188th warmest (14.9 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 3,191 hours of sunshine a year) and 236th wettest (510 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hayward, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Fremont is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Why Fremont has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
