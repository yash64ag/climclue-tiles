Queens in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Queens in June?
In June, Queens averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Queens?
June is not one of Queens’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Queens in June?

Does it snow in Queens in June?
