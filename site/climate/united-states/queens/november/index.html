Queens in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Queens in November?
In November, Queens averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Queens?
November is not one of Queens’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Queens in November?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest February (77 mm).
Does it snow in Queens in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Queens get in November?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Queens in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
