Queens in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Queens in April?
In April, Queens averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Queens?
April is not one of Queens’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Queens in April?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (113 mm), the driest February (77 mm).
Does it snow in Queens in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Queens get in April?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Queens in April?
About 7.0 °C (45 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Queens in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
