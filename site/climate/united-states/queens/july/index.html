Queens in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Queens in July?
In July, Queens averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Queens?
July is not one of Queens’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
