Kansas City, Kansas at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Kansas City, Kansas is 223rd warmest (12.9 °C year average), 174th sunniest (about 2,738 hours of sunshine a year) and 149th wettest (1,023 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kansas City, Missouri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Kansas City, Kansas is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 1,060 km from the sea (deep inland).
