How April compares in Los Angeles

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 34 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. May is 1.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Los Angeles in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in April?
In April, Los Angeles averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Los Angeles?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Los Angeles’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in April?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in April?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
