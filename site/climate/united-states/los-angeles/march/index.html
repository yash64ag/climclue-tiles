How March compares in Los Angeles

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with February: 1.2 °C warmer, 38 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Los Angeles in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in March?
In March, Los Angeles averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Los Angeles?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Los Angeles’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in March?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in March?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
