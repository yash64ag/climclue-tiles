How February compares in Los Angeles

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 1.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Los Angeles in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in February?
In February, Los Angeles averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Los Angeles?
February is not one of Los Angeles’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in February?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in February?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
