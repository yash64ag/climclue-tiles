How August compares in Los Angeles

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with July: 0.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Los Angeles in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in August?
In August, Los Angeles averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 340 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Los Angeles?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Los Angeles’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in August?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in August?
About 340 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
