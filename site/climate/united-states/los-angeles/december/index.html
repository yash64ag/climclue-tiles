How December compares in Los Angeles

December is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Los Angeles in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in December?
In December, Los Angeles averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Los Angeles?
December is not one of Los Angeles’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in December?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
