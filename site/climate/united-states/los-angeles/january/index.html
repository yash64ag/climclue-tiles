How January compares in Los Angeles

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Los Angeles in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in January?
In January, Los Angeles averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Los Angeles?
January is not one of Los Angeles’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in January?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in January?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
