How June compares in Los Angeles

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Los Angeles. Compared with May: 2.0 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Los Angeles in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Los Angeles in June?
In June, Los Angeles averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Los Angeles?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Los Angeles’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Los Angeles in June?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (102 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Los Angeles in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Los Angeles get in June?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Los Angeles in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
