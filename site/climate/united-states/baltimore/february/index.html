How February compares in Baltimore

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 4.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Baltimore in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in February?
In February, Baltimore averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -1.3 °C (30 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Baltimore?
February is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in February?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
