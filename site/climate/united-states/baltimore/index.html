Baltimore at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Baltimore is 211th warmest (13.4 °C year average), 235th sunniest (about 2,539 hours of sunshine a year) and 111th wettest (1,159 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Washington, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Baltimore is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 79 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Why Baltimore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
