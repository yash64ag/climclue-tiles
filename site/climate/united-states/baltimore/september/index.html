How September compares in Baltimore

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. October is 6.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Baltimore in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in September?
In September, Baltimore averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Baltimore?
September is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in September?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in September?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
