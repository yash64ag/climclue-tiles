How November compares in Baltimore

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Baltimore in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in November?
In November, Baltimore averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 5.5 °C (42 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Baltimore?
November is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in November?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in November?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
