How October compares in Baltimore

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Baltimore in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in October?
In October, Baltimore averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Baltimore?
October is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in October?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in October?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
