How April compares in Baltimore

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Baltimore in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in April?
In April, Baltimore averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Baltimore?
April is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in April?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
