How January compares in Baltimore

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Baltimore in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in January?
In January, Baltimore averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -2.3 °C (28 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Baltimore?
January is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in January?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in January?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
