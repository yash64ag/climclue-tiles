How March compares in Baltimore

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. April is 5.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Baltimore in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in March?
In March, Baltimore averages a day high of 11.4 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Baltimore?
March is not one of Baltimore’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in March?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (113 mm), the driest January (78 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in March?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baltimore in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
