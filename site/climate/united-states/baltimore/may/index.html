How May compares in Baltimore

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Baltimore. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Baltimore in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baltimore in May?
In May, Baltimore averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Baltimore?
No month in Baltimore is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baltimore in May?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (108 mm), the driest January (76 mm).
Does it snow in Baltimore in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baltimore get in May?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Baltimore in May?
About 14.3 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Baltimore in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
