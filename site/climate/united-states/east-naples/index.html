East Naples weather by month and climate

East Naples at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, East Naples is 141st warmest (23.6 °C year average), 434th sunniest (about 3,130 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (1,430 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naples, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), East Naples is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, January (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.7 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why East Naples has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for East Naples itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.138°N, 81.766°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-578-982), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
