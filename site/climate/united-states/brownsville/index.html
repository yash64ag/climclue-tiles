Brownsville at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Brownsville is 15th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 199th sunniest (about 2,648 hours of sunshine a year) and 208th wettest (701 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with McAllen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Brownsville is 1.3 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 129 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 11.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Brownsville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
