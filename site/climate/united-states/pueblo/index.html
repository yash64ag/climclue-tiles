Why Pueblo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,107 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Pueblo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Pueblo?
July, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 32.8 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 17.8 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Pueblo?
December, with an average of -0.8 °C (30 °F), day highs around 7.6 °C (46 °F) and night lows around -6.2 °C (21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Pueblo?
August, with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 364 mm (14.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Pueblo?
February, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Pueblo?
June, with about 352 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Pueblo?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 109 cm (42.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Pueblo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, August and September.
What type of climate does Pueblo have?
Cold semi-arid (Köppen BSk). Dry grassland climate with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.7 × 11 km, that contains Pueblo’s GeoNames location (38.254°N, 104.609°W; ClimaClue area sq-457-753), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 109,412 (GeoNames).
