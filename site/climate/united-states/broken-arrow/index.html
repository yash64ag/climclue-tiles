Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Broken Arrow is 158th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 170th sunniest (about 2,747 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (1,144 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tulsa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Broken Arrow is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Why Broken Arrow has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 728 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
