Minnetonka weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Minnetonka, United States (Minnesota), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Minnetonka is warmest in July, with 29.2 °C (85 °F) days and 16.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -3.6 °C (26 °F) days and -15.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (120 mm); February is driest (20 mm).

Why Minnetonka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,206 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Minnetonka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.913°N, 93.503°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-390-864), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
