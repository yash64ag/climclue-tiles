Fullerton at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Fullerton is 116th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 3,186 hours of sunshine a year) and 279th wettest (347 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anaheim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Fullerton is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.8 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, December (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 121% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Fullerton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fullerton itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.870°N, 117.925°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-501-620), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
