Why Honolulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Honolulu climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Honolulu?
August, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 23.3 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Honolulu?
February, with an average of 21.1 °C (70 °F), day highs around 22.7 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Honolulu?
December, with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,000 mm (39.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Honolulu?
June, with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Honolulu?
July, with about 294 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Honolulu?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Honolulu?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, April, May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Honolulu have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Honolulu’s GeoNames location (21.307°N, 157.858°W; ClimaClue area sq-626-221), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 350,964 (GeoNames).
