How October compares in Washington

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Washington. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Washington in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington in October?
In October, Washington averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Washington?
October is not one of Washington’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Washington in October?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (111 mm), the driest February (71 mm).
Does it snow in Washington in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Washington get in October?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Washington in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
