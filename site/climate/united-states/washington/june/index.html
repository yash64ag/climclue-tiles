How June compares in Washington

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Washington. Compared with May: 5.2 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Washington in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington in June?
In June, Washington averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Washington?
June is not one of Washington’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Washington in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (111 mm), the driest February (71 mm).
Does it snow in Washington in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Washington get in June?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Washington in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
