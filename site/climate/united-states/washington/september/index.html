How September compares in Washington

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Washington. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. October is 6.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Washington in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington in September?
In September, Washington averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Washington?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Washington’s mild months (August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Washington in September?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (111 mm), the driest February (71 mm).
Does it snow in Washington in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Washington get in September?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Washington in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
