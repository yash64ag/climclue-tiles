How April compares in Washington, District of Columbia

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Washington, District of Columbia. Compared with March: 5.7 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Washington, District of Columbia in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington, District of Columbia in April?
In April, Washington, District of Columbia averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Washington, District of Columbia?
April is not one of Washington, District of Columbia’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Washington, District of Columbia in April?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Washington, District of Columbia in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Washington, District of Columbia get in April?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
