Washington, District of Columbia weather in March

Average March weather in Washington, District of Columbia, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Washington, District of Columbia averages 13.1 °C (56 °F) days and 2.1 °C (36 °F) nights, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 204 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Washington, District of Columbia

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Washington, District of Columbia. Compared with February: 4.4 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. April is 5.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Washington, District of Columbia in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington, District of Columbia in March?
In March, Washington, District of Columbia averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Washington, District of Columbia?
March is not one of Washington, District of Columbia’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Washington, District of Columbia in March?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
Does it snow in Washington, District of Columbia in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Washington, District of Columbia get in March?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Washington, District of Columbia in March?
