How May compares in Washington

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Washington. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 5.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Washington in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington in May?
In May, Washington averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Washington?
May is not one of Washington’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Washington in May?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (111 mm), the driest February (71 mm).
Does it snow in Washington in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Washington get in May?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Washington in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
