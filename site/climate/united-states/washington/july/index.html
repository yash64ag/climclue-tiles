Washington, District of Columbia in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Washington, District of Columbia in July?
In July, Washington, District of Columbia averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Washington, District of Columbia?
July is not one of Washington, District of Columbia’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (109 mm), the driest February (70 mm).
