Long Beach, Mississippi at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Long Beach, Mississippi is 430th warmest (20.0 °C year average), 1484th sunniest (about 2,594 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (1,657 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gulfport, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Long Beach, Mississippi is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Long Beach, Mississippi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
