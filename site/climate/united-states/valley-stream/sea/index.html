Valley Stream sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New York · United States

The sea off Valley Stream averages 22.1 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.7 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Valley Stream sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Valley Stream?
In August, at about 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Valley Stream?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 3.7 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Valley Stream in February?
About 3.7 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Valley Stream?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
