Allentown at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Allentown is 251st warmest (11.5 °C year average), 291st sunniest (about 2,397 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (1,187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Philadelphia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Allentown is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 137 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Allentown has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
