Green Bay at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Green Bay is 322nd warmest (7.9 °C year average), 288th sunniest (about 2,433 hours of sunshine a year) and 202nd wettest (799 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Milwaukee, the nearest covered town with different numbers (165 km away), Green Bay is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 2.4 °C cooler in January, with 95 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 29.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 956 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Green Bay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 956 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
