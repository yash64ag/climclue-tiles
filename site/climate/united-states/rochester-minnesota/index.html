Rochester, Minnesota at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Rochester, Minnesota is the 3rd coolest (7.6 °C year average). Compared with Saint Paul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (114 km away), Rochester, Minnesota is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,220 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Rochester, Minnesota has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
