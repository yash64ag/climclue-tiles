Lompoc sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · California · United States

The sea off Lompoc averages 15.6 °C (60 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 12.1 °C (54 °F) in April. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Lompoc sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lompoc?
In September, at about 15.6 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lompoc?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is April, at 12.1 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lompoc in April?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lompoc?
About 13.9 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Lompoc nearby?
In September: Santa Barbara (17.8 °C, 74 km), Ventura (18.8 °C, 114 km) and Oxnard (19.0 °C, 127 km).
