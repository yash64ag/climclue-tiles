Picnic Point-North Lynnwood sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Washington · United States

The sea off Picnic Point-North Lynnwood averages 11.5 °C (53 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.7 °C (46 °F) in April. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).
