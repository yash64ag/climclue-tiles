Brooklyn in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in May?
In May, Brooklyn averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Brooklyn?
May is not one of Brooklyn’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in May?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (80 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in May?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
