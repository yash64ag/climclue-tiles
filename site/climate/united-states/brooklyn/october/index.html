Brooklyn in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in October?
In October, Brooklyn averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Brooklyn?
October is not one of Brooklyn’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in October?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (80 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in October?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
