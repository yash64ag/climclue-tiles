Brooklyn in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in June?
In June, Brooklyn averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Brooklyn?
June is not one of Brooklyn’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in June?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (80 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in June?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Brooklyn in June?
About 17.1 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
