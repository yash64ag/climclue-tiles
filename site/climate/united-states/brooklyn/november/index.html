How November compares in Brooklyn

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Brooklyn. Compared with October: 5.2 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Brooklyn in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in November?
In November, Brooklyn averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Brooklyn?
November is not one of Brooklyn’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in November?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (116 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in November?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
