How January compares in Brooklyn

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Brooklyn. Compared with December: 3.0 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Brooklyn in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in January?
In January, Brooklyn averages a day high of 3.4 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -3.6 °C (26 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Brooklyn?
January is not one of Brooklyn’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in January?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (80 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in January?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Brooklyn in January?
About 5.4 °C (42 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
