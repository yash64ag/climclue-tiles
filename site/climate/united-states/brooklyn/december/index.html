How December compares in Brooklyn

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Brooklyn. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 3.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Brooklyn in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in December?
In December, Brooklyn averages a day high of 6.3 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Brooklyn?
December is not one of Brooklyn’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in December?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (80 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in December?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Brooklyn in December?
About 8.6 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
