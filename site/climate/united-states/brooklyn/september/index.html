Brooklyn in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brooklyn in September?
In September, Brooklyn averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Brooklyn?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Brooklyn’s mild months (September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brooklyn in September?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (116 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in Brooklyn in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brooklyn get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brooklyn in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
