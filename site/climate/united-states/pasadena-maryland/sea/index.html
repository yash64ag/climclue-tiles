Pasadena, Maryland sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maryland · United States

The sea off Pasadena, Maryland averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.6 °C (37 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Pasadena, Maryland sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pasadena, Maryland?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pasadena, Maryland?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 2.6 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pasadena, Maryland in February?
About 2.6 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pasadena, Maryland?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pasadena, Maryland nearby?
In August: Parole (26.4 °C, 16 km).
