Why Moreno Valley has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Moreno Valley climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Moreno Valley?
August, with an average of 24.8 °C (77 °F), day highs around 32.7 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Moreno Valley?
December, with an average of 10.2 °C (50 °F), day highs around 16.8 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 4.7 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Moreno Valley?
February, with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 445 mm (17.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Moreno Valley?
June, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Moreno Valley?
July, with about 348 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Moreno Valley?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Moreno Valley?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Moreno Valley’s GeoNames location (33.938°N, 117.231°W; ClimaClue area sq-500-627), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 204,198 (GeoNames).
