Tri-Cities at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Tri-Cities is 1783rd warmest (12.2 °C year average), 2674th sunniest (about 2,393 hours of sunshine a year) and 3087th wettest (195 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kennewick, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Tri-Cities is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, December (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 307 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 7 km from Tri-Cities by up to 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Tri-Cities has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tri-Cities itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.245°N, 119.196°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-377-608), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
