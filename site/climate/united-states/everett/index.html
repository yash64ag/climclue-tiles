Everett at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Everett is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,044 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bellevue, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Everett is 1.7 °C cooler in August and 0.8 °C cooler in December, with 75 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (16.3 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, December (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Everett has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
