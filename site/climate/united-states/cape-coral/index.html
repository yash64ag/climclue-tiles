Why Cape Coral has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cape Coral climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cape Coral?
August, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.4 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 25.5 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cape Coral?
January, with an average of 17.8 °C (64 °F), day highs around 21.7 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 14.8 °C (59 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cape Coral?
June, with 230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,304 mm (51.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cape Coral?
December, with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cape Coral?
May, with about 310 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cape Coral?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cape Coral?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Cape Coral have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10.0 × 11 km, that contains Cape Coral’s GeoNames location (26.563°N, 81.950°W; ClimaClue area sq-574-980), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 175,229 (GeoNames).
