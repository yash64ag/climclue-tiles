Worcester at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Worcester is 302nd warmest (9.6 °C year average), 283rd sunniest (about 2,464 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (1,356 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lowell, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Worcester is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Worcester has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
