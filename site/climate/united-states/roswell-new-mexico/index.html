Roswell, New Mexico at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Roswell, New Mexico is 1055th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 199th sunniest (about 3,279 hours of sunshine a year) and 2896th wettest (350 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Carlsbad, New Mexico, the nearest covered town with different numbers (112 km away), Roswell, New Mexico is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 2.1 °C cooler in December, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.0 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, December (4.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 845 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Roswell, New Mexico has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 845 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Roswell itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.394°N, 104.525°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-506-754), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
