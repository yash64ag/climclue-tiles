Milford, Connecticut sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Connecticut · United States

The sea off Milford, Connecticut averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.4 °C (35 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Milford, Connecticut sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Milford, Connecticut?
In August, at about 22.0 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Milford, Connecticut?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 1.4 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Milford, Connecticut in February?
About 1.4 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Milford, Connecticut?
About 11.9 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
