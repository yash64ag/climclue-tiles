Corona at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Corona is 119th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 3,206 hours of sunshine a year) and 282nd wettest (338 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Riverside, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Corona is 1.0 °C cooler in August and about as warm in December, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.3 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, December (11.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 50 km from the sea.

Why Corona has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
