Stamford at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Stamford is 242nd warmest (11.8 °C year average), 252nd sunniest (about 2,515 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (1,258 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with North Stamford, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Stamford is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Stamford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
