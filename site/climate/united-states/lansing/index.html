Lansing at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Lansing is 314th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 297th sunniest (about 2,348 hours of sunshine a year) and 191st wettest (884 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ann Arbor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Lansing is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 26.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 790 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lansing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
