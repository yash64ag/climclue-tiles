Palm City sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Palm City averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 23.2 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Palm City sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Palm City?
In August, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Palm City?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.2 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Palm City in February?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Palm City?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
