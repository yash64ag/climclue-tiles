Fort Pierce sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Fort Pierce averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 22.6 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Fort Pierce sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Fort Pierce?
In August, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Fort Pierce?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 22.6 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Fort Pierce in February?
About 22.6 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Fort Pierce?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Fort Pierce nearby?
In August: Jupiter (29.3 °C, 61 km) and Palm Beach Gardens (29.4 °C, 72 km).
