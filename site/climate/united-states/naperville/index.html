Naperville at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Naperville is 285th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 277th sunniest (about 2,478 hours of sunshine a year) and 153rd wettest (1,009 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aurora, Illinois, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Naperville is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,035 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Naperville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
