Springfield, Missouri at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Springfield, Missouri is 210th warmest (13.5 °C year average), 196th sunniest (about 2,663 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (1,194 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Columbia, Missouri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (211 km away), Springfield, Missouri is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 826 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Springfield, Missouri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 826 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
