Why Cedar Rapids has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,318 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Cedar Rapids climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cedar Rapids?
July, with an average of 23.3 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 18.8 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cedar Rapids?
January, with an average of -6.7 °C (20 °F), day highs around -1.7 °C (29 °F) and night lows around -10.6 °C (13 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cedar Rapids?
June, with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 969 mm (38.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cedar Rapids?
January, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cedar Rapids?
July, with about 315 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cedar Rapids?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 105 cm (41.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Cedar Rapids?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September.
What type of climate does Cedar Rapids have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.3 × 11 km, that contains Cedar Rapids’s GeoNames location (42.008°N, 91.644°W; ClimaClue area sq-419-883), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 130,405 (GeoNames).
