Gautier sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mississippi · United States

The sea off Gautier averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.0 °C (57 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Gautier sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gautier?
In August, at about 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gautier?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 14.0 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gautier in January?
About 14.0 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gautier?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
