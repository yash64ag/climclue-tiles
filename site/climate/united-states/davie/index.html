Davie at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Davie is 7th warmest (24.2 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 3,087 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,562 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miramar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Davie is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.8 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 93% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Davie has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
