Natchitoches at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Natchitoches is 501st warmest (19.0 °C year average), 1178th sunniest (about 2,696 hours of sunshine a year) and 238th wettest (1,440 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alexandria, Louisiana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Natchitoches is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Why Natchitoches has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 234 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Natchitoches itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.761°N, 93.086°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-522-869), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
