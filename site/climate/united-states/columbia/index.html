Columbia, South Carolina at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Columbia, South Carolina is 115th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 166th sunniest (about 2,769 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (1,096 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Charlotte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (137 km away), Columbia, South Carolina is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 191 km from the sea.

Why Columbia, South Carolina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 191 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
