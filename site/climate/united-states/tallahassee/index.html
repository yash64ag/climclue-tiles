Why Tallahassee has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Tallahassee climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tallahassee?
July, with an average of 26.8 °C (80 °F), day highs around 32.0 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 23.0 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tallahassee?
January, with an average of 10.7 °C (51 °F), day highs around 16.6 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 6.7 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tallahassee?
June, with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,561 mm (61.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tallahassee?
May, with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tallahassee?
May, with about 315 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tallahassee?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tallahassee?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, October and November.
What type of climate does Tallahassee have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.6 × 11 km, that contains Tallahassee’s GeoNames location (30.438°N, 84.281°W; ClimaClue area sq-535-957), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 201,731 (GeoNames).
