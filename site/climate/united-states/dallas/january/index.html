Dallas, Texas weather in January

Average January weather in Dallas, Texas, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Dallas, Texas averages 13.5 °C (56 °F) days and 2.1 °C (36 °F) nights, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of rain over the month and about 178 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Dallas, Texas

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dallas, Texas. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Dallas, Texas in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dallas, Texas in January?
In January, Dallas, Texas averages a day high of 13.5 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dallas, Texas?
January is not one of Dallas, Texas’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dallas, Texas in January?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest August (49 mm).
Does it snow in Dallas, Texas in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dallas, Texas get in January?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dallas, Texas in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
