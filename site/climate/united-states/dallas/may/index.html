How May compares in Dallas

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dallas. Compared with April: 4.3 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Dallas in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dallas in May?
In May, Dallas averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Dallas?
May is not one of Dallas’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dallas in May?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (129 mm), the driest August (52 mm).
Does it snow in Dallas in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dallas get in May?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dallas in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
