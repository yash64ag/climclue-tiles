Dallas, Texas in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dallas, Texas in March?

Is March a good time to visit Dallas, Texas?
March is not one of Dallas, Texas’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
