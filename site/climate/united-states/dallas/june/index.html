How June compares in Dallas, Texas

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dallas, Texas. Compared with May: 4.1 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Dallas, Texas in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dallas, Texas in June?
In June, Dallas, Texas averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dallas, Texas?
June is not one of Dallas, Texas’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dallas, Texas in June?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest August (49 mm).
Does it snow in Dallas, Texas in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dallas, Texas get in June?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dallas, Texas in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
