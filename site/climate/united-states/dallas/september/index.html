Dallas, Texas in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dallas, Texas in September?
In September, Dallas, Texas averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dallas, Texas?
September is not one of Dallas, Texas’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dallas, Texas in September?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest August (49 mm).
Does it snow in Dallas, Texas in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dallas, Texas get in September?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dallas, Texas in September?
