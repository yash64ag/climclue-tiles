How December compares in Dallas, Texas

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dallas, Texas. Compared with November: 5.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Dallas, Texas in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dallas, Texas in December?
In December, Dallas, Texas averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dallas, Texas?
December is not one of Dallas, Texas’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dallas, Texas in December?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest August (49 mm).
Does it snow in Dallas, Texas in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dallas, Texas get in December?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dallas, Texas in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
