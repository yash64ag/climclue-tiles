Arlington Heights at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Arlington Heights is 2705th warmest (9.8 °C year average), 2459th sunniest (about 2,463 hours of sunshine a year) and 2094th wettest (959 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rolling Meadows, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Arlington Heights is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Why Arlington Heights has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Arlington Heights itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.088°N, 87.981°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-419-920), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
