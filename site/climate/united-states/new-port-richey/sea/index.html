New Port Richey sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off New Port Richey averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 16.8 °C (62 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

New Port Richey sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in New Port Richey?
In July, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in New Port Richey?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.8 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in New Port Richey in February?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in New Port Richey?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
