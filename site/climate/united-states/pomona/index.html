Pomona at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Pomona is 116th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 45th sunniest (about 3,215 hours of sunshine a year) and 225th wettest (557 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ontario, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Pomona is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.9 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, December (11.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 58 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pomona, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pomona has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 58 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
