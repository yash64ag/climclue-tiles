South Bend at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, South Bend is 286th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 274th sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (1,062 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fort Wayne, the nearest covered town with different numbers (112 km away), South Bend is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 879 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why South Bend has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
