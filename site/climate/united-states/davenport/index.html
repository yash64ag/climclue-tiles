Davenport at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Davenport is 282nd warmest (10.3 °C year average), 266th sunniest (about 2,492 hours of sunshine a year) and 179th wettest (936 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cedar Rapids, the nearest covered town with different numbers (104 km away), Davenport is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 29.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,228 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Davenport has this climate
