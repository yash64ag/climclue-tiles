Why Palmdale has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Palmdale climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Palmdale?
July, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 32.5 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 17.8 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Palmdale?
December, with an average of 7.7 °C (46 °F), day highs around 13.2 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 3.3 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Palmdale?
February, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 310 mm (12.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Palmdale?
June, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Palmdale?
July, with about 352 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Palmdale?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Palmdale?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Palmdale have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Palmdale’s GeoNames location (34.579°N, 118.116°W; ClimaClue area sq-494-618), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 158,351 (GeoNames).
