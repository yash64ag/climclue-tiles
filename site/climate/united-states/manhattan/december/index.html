Manhattan, New York in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manhattan, New York in December?
In December, Manhattan, New York averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Manhattan, New York?
No month in Manhattan, New York is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manhattan, New York in December?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (79 mm).
Does it snow in Manhattan, New York in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Manhattan, New York get in December?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manhattan, New York in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
