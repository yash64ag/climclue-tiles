Manhattan, New York weather in May

Average May weather in Manhattan, New York, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Manhattan, New York averages 21.0 °C (70 °F) days and 11.5 °C (53 °F) nights, with 107 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 257 hours of sunshine.
