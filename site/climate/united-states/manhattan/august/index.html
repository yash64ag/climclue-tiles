Manhattan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manhattan in August?
In August, Manhattan averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Manhattan?
No month in Manhattan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manhattan in August?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (118 mm), the driest February (79 mm).
Does it snow in Manhattan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manhattan get in August?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manhattan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
