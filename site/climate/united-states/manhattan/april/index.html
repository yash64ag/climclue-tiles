How April compares in Manhattan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Manhattan. Compared with March: 5.9 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Manhattan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manhattan in April?
In April, Manhattan averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Manhattan?
No month in Manhattan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manhattan in April?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (116 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in Manhattan in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Manhattan get in April?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manhattan in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
