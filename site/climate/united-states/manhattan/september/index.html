September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Manhattan. Compared with August: 4.0 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. October is 6.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Manhattan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manhattan in September?
In September, Manhattan averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Manhattan?
No month in Manhattan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manhattan in September?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (116 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in Manhattan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manhattan get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manhattan in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
