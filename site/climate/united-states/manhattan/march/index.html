How March compares in Manhattan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Manhattan. Compared with February: 4.0 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. April is 5.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Manhattan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manhattan in March?
In March, Manhattan averages a day high of 8.9 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Manhattan?
No month in Manhattan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manhattan in March?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (116 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in Manhattan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Manhattan get in March?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manhattan in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
