Navarre sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Navarre averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 17.0 °C (63 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Navarre sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Navarre?
In August, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Navarre?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 17.0 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Navarre in February?
About 17.0 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Navarre?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
