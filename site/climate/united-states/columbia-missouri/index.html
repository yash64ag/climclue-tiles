Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Columbia, Missouri is 219th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 218th sunniest (about 2,601 hours of sunshine a year) and 124th wettest (1,109 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Independence, the nearest covered town with different numbers (181 km away), Columbia, Missouri is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year.
