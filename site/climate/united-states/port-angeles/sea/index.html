Port Angeles sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Washington · United States

The sea off Port Angeles averages 11.0 °C (52 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.2 °C (45 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Port Angeles sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Port Angeles?
In August, at about 11.0 °C (52 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Port Angeles?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 7.2 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Port Angeles in February?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Port Angeles?
About 8.9 °C (48 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Port Angeles nearby?
In August: Esquimalt (12.5 °C, 35 km), Victoria (12.5 °C, 36 km) and Saanich (12.5 °C, 48 km).
