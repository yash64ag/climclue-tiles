Spring Valley at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Spring Valley is 54th warmest (20.1 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 3,728 hours of sunshine a year) and 322nd wettest (173 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paradise, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Spring Valley is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.8 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, December (8.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 380 km from the sea.

Why Spring Valley has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 380 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-478-647), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
