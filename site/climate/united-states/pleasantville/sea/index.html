Pleasantville sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New Jersey · United States

The sea off Pleasantville averages 23.5 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.9 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Pleasantville sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pleasantville?
In August, at about 23.5 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pleasantville?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 3.9 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pleasantville in February?
About 3.9 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pleasantville?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
