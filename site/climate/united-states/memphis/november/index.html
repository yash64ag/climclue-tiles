How November compares in Memphis

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Memphis. Compared with October: 5.7 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. December is 5.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Memphis in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in November?
In November, Memphis averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Memphis?
November is not one of Memphis’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in November?
131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in November?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
