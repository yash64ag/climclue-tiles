Memphis at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Memphis is 148th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 149th sunniest (about 2,840 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (1,359 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Little Rock, the nearest covered town with different numbers (209 km away), Memphis is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 545 km from the sea.

Why Memphis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 545 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
