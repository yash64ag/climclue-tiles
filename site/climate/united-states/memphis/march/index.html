How March compares in Memphis

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Memphis. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 5.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Memphis in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in March?
In March, Memphis averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Memphis?
March is not one of Memphis’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in March?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in March?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
