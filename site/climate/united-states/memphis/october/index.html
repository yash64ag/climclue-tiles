How October compares in Memphis

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Memphis. Compared with September: 6.1 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. November is 5.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Memphis in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in October?
In October, Memphis averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Memphis?
October is not one of Memphis’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in October?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in October?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
