Memphis in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in September?
In September, Memphis averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Memphis?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Memphis’s mild months (September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in September?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in September?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
