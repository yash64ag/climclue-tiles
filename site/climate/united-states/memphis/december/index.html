How December compares in Memphis

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Memphis. Compared with November: 5.3 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Memphis in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in December?
In December, Memphis averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Memphis?
December is not one of Memphis’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in December?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in December?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
