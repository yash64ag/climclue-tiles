How April compares in Memphis

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Memphis. Compared with March: 5.1 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. May is 4.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Memphis in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in April?
In April, Memphis averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Memphis?
April is not one of Memphis’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in April?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in April?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
