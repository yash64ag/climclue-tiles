How January compares in Memphis

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Memphis. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Memphis in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Memphis in January?
In January, Memphis averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Memphis?
January is not one of Memphis’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Memphis in January?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (145 mm), the driest August (73 mm).
Does it snow in Memphis in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.0 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Memphis get in January?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Memphis in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
