East Chattanooga at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, East Chattanooga is 168th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 246th sunniest (about 2,520 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (1,312 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chattanooga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), East Chattanooga is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 534 km from the sea.

Why East Chattanooga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
