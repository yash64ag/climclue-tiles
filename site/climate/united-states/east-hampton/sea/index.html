East Hampton sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Virginia · United States

The sea off East Hampton averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.6 °C (42 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

East Hampton sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in East Hampton?
In August, at about 26.6 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in East Hampton?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 5.6 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in East Hampton in February?
About 5.6 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in East Hampton?
About 15.9 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
