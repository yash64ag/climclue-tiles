Peoria, Arizona at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Peoria, Arizona is the sunniest (about 3,819 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Glendale, Arizona, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Peoria, Arizona is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.0 °C), averages 23.3 °C more than its coolest, December (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in December. The area lies about 273 km from the sea.

Why Peoria, Arizona has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 273 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
