Fort Wayne at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Fort Wayne is 277th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 197th sunniest (about 2,653 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (1,002 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with South Bend, the nearest covered town with different numbers (112 km away), Fort Wayne is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 776 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Fort Wayne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 776 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
