Why Anchorage has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Anchorage climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Anchorage?
July, with an average of 14.8 °C (59 °F), day highs around 17.6 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 12.1 °C (54 °F).
What is the coldest month in Anchorage?
January, with an average of -8.8 °C (16 °F), day highs around -6.5 °C (20 °F) and night lows around -10.7 °C (13 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Anchorage?
August, with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 676 mm (26.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Anchorage?
April, with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Anchorage?
May, with about 286 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Anchorage?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 450 cm (177.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 9 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Anchorage?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Anchorage have?
Subarctic, dry summer (Köppen Dsc). Short, cool, dry summers and long, cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 5.4 × 11 km, that contains Anchorage’s GeoNames location (61.218°N, 149.900°W; ClimaClue area sq-227-301), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 289,600 (GeoNames).
