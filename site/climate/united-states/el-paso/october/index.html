El Paso in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in El Paso in October?
In October, El Paso averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit El Paso?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of El Paso’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in El Paso in October?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in El Paso in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does El Paso get in October?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for El Paso in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
