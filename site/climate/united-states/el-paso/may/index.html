How May compares in El Paso

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in El Paso. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. June is 4.3 °C warmer than May on average.

El Paso in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in El Paso in May?
In May, El Paso averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 377 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit El Paso?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of El Paso’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in El Paso in May?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in El Paso in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does El Paso get in May?
About 377 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for El Paso in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
