How August compares in El Paso

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in El Paso. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. September is 2.9 °C cooler than August on average.

El Paso in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in El Paso in August?
In August, El Paso averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 328 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit El Paso?
August is not one of El Paso’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in El Paso in August?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in El Paso in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does El Paso get in August?
About 328 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for El Paso in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
