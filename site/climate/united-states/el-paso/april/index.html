How April compares in El Paso

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in El Paso. Compared with March: 4.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

El Paso in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in El Paso in April?
In April, El Paso averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 340 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit El Paso?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of El Paso’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in El Paso in April?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest March (7 mm).
Does it snow in El Paso in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does El Paso get in April?
About 340 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for El Paso in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
