Lancaster, California at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Lancaster, California is 876th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 282nd sunniest (about 3,193 hours of sunshine a year) and 3094th wettest (176 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Palmdale, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Lancaster, California is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 65 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, December (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 0.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 8 km from Lancaster by up to 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Lancaster, California has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lancaster itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.698°N, 118.137°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-493-618), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
