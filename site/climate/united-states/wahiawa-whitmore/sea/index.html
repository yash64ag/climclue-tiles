Wahiawā-Whitmore sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hawaii · United States

The sea off Wahiawā-Whitmore averages 26.3 °C (79 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 23.5 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to December.

Wahiawā-Whitmore sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Wahiawā-Whitmore?
In September, at about 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Wahiawā-Whitmore?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.5 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Wahiawā-Whitmore in February?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Wahiawā-Whitmore?
About 24.8 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
