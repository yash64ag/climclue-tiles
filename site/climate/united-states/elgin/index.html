Elgin at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Elgin is 299th warmest (9.7 °C year average), 285th sunniest (about 2,462 hours of sunshine a year) and 180th wettest (932 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naperville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Elgin is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 77 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,050 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Elgin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,050 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.
