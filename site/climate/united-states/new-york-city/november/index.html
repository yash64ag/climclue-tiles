How November compares in New York City

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in New York City. Compared with October: 5.2 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

New York City in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New York City in November?
In November, New York City averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit New York City?
November is not one of New York City’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New York City in November?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (115 mm), the driest February (77 mm).
Does it snow in New York City in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New York City get in November?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in New York City in November?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for New York City in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
