How December compares in New York City

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in New York City. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 3.0 °C cooler than December on average.

New York City in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New York City in December?
In December, New York City averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit New York City?
December is not one of New York City’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New York City in December?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest February (76 mm).
Does it snow in New York City in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does New York City get in December?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New York City in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
