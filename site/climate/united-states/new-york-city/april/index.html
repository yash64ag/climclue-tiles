How April compares in New York City

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in New York City. Compared with March: 6.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

New York City in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New York City in April?
In April, New York City averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit New York City?
April is not one of New York City’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New York City in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest February (76 mm).
Does it snow in New York City in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does New York City get in April?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New York City in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
