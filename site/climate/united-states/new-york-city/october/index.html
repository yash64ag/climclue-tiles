How October compares in New York City

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in New York City. Compared with September: 6.3 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

New York City in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New York City in October?
In October, New York City averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit New York City?
October is not one of New York City’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New York City in October?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest February (76 mm).
Does it snow in New York City in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New York City get in October?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New York City in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
