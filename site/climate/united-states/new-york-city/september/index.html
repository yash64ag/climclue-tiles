How September compares in New York City

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in New York City. Compared with August: 4.0 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. October is 6.3 °C cooler than September on average.

New York City in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New York City in September?
In September, New York City averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit New York City?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of New York City’s mild months (June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New York City in September?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest February (76 mm).
Does it snow in New York City in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does New York City get in September?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New York City in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
