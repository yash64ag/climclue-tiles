How March compares in New York City

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in New York City. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. April is 6.1 °C warmer than March on average.

New York City in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in New York City in March?
In March, New York City averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit New York City?
March is not one of New York City’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in New York City in March?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest February (76 mm).
Does it snow in New York City in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does New York City get in March?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for New York City in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
