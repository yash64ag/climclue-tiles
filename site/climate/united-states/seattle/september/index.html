How September compares in Seattle

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Seattle. Compared with August: 2.7 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. October is 4.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Seattle in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seattle in September?
In September, Seattle averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Seattle?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Seattle’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seattle in September?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (174 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seattle in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seattle get in September?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seattle in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
