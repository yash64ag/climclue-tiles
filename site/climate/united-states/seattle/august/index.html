How August compares in Seattle

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Seattle. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. September is 2.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Seattle in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seattle in August?
In August, Seattle averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Seattle?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Seattle’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seattle in August?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (173 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Seattle in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seattle get in August?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seattle in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
