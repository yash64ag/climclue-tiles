How January compares in Seattle

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Seattle. Compared with December: 0.6 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Seattle in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seattle in January?
In January, Seattle averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 67 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Seattle?
January is not one of Seattle’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seattle in January?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (174 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seattle in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seattle get in January?
About 67 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seattle in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
