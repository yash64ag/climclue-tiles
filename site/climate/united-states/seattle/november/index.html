Seattle weather in November

Average November weather in Seattle, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Seattle averages 10.7 °C (51 °F) days and 4.6 °C (40 °F) nights, with 174 mm (6.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 71 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Seattle

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Seattle. Compared with October: 3.7 °C cooler, 67 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. December is 2.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Seattle in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seattle in November?
In November, Seattle averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 71 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Seattle?
November is not one of Seattle’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seattle in November?
174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (174 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seattle in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seattle get in November?
About 71 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Seattle in November?
About 8.7 °C (48 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (11.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Seattle in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
