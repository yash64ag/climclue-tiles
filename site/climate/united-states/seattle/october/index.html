How October compares in Seattle

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Seattle. Compared with September: 4.5 °C cooler, 61 mm more precipitation and 74 hours less sunshine. November is 4.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Seattle in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seattle in October?
In October, Seattle averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Seattle?
October is not one of Seattle’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seattle in October?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (174 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seattle in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seattle get in October?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Seattle in October?
About 9.8 °C (50 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (11.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Seattle in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
