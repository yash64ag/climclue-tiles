Winston-Salem at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Winston-Salem is 192nd warmest (14.7 °C year average), 212th sunniest (about 2,616 hours of sunshine a year) and 73rd wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with High Point, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Winston-Salem is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 304 km from the sea.

Why Winston-Salem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 304 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
