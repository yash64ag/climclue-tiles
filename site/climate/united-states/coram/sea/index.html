Coram sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · New York · United States

The sea off Coram averages 22.1 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.4 °C (40 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Coram sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Coram?
In August, at about 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Coram?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 4.4 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Coram in February?
About 4.4 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Coram?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
