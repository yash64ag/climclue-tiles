Pearland at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Pearland is 41st warmest (20.7 °C year average), 221st sunniest (about 2,594 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (1,404 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pasadena, Texas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Pearland is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Pearland has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
