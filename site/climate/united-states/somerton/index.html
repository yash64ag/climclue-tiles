Somerton at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Somerton is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,868 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (72 mm a year). Compared with San Luis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Somerton is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (33.8 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, December (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in December. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 7 km from Somerton by up to 2.6 °C (4.6 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Somerton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Somerton itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.596°N, 114.710°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-514-652), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
