Why Jackson has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 222 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Jackson climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jackson?
July, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 32.7 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 23.1 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jackson?
January, with an average of 7.8 °C (46 °F), day highs around 13.6 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 4.0 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jackson?
December, with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,373 mm (54.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jackson?
September, with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jackson?
June, with about 293 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jackson?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Jackson?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September and October.
What type of climate does Jackson have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Jackson’s GeoNames location (32.299°N, 90.185°W; ClimaClue area sq-517-898), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 153,701 (GeoNames).
