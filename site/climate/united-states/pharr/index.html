Pharr at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Pharr is 125th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 2640th sunniest (about 2,420 hours of sunshine a year) and 2559th wettest (582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Juan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Pharr is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Why Pharr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pharr itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.195°N, 98.184°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-578-818), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
