Amesbury sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Massachusetts · United States

The sea off Amesbury averages 17.8 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.6 °C (38 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Amesbury sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Amesbury?
In August, at about 17.8 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Amesbury?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 3.6 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Amesbury in March?
About 3.6 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Amesbury?
About 10.0 °C (50 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
