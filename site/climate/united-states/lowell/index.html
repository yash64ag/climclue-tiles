Lowell at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Lowell is 291st warmest (10.0 °C year average), 261st sunniest (about 2,498 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cambridge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Lowell is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Why Lowell has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
