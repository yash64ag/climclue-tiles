How July compares in Indianapolis

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Indianapolis. Compared with June: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Indianapolis in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indianapolis in July?
In July, Indianapolis averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Indianapolis?
July is not one of Indianapolis’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indianapolis in July?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (130 mm), the driest February (61 mm).
Does it snow in Indianapolis in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indianapolis get in July?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indianapolis in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
