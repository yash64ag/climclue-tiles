How May compares in Indianapolis

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Indianapolis. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 5.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Indianapolis in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indianapolis in May?
In May, Indianapolis averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Indianapolis?
May is not one of Indianapolis’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indianapolis in May?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (129 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Indianapolis in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indianapolis get in May?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indianapolis in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
