Indianapolis in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indianapolis in August?
In August, Indianapolis averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Indianapolis?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Indianapolis’s mild months (August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indianapolis in August?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (129 mm), the driest February (60 mm).
Does it snow in Indianapolis in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indianapolis get in August?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indianapolis in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
