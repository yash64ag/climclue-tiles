How November compares in Indianapolis

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Indianapolis. Compared with October: 6.1 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. December is 6.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Indianapolis in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indianapolis in November?
In November, Indianapolis averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Indianapolis?
November is not one of Indianapolis’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indianapolis in November?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (130 mm), the driest February (61 mm).
Does it snow in Indianapolis in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Indianapolis get in November?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indianapolis in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
