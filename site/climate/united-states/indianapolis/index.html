Indianapolis at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Indianapolis is 240th warmest (11.8 °C year average), 289th sunniest (about 2,429 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (1,087 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cincinnati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (158 km away), Indianapolis is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 840 km from the sea (deep inland).

Best time to visit Indianapolis

For mild weather, the best months to visit Indianapolis are August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): August and September.

Why Indianapolis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 840 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
