How December compares in Indianapolis

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Indianapolis. Compared with November: 6.6 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Indianapolis in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indianapolis in December?
In December, Indianapolis averages a day high of 4.2 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -3.4 °C (26 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Indianapolis?
December is not one of Indianapolis’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indianapolis in December?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (130 mm), the driest February (61 mm).
Does it snow in Indianapolis in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Indianapolis get in December?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indianapolis in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
