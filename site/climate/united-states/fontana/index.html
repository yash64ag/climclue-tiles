Fontana at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Fontana is 92nd warmest (18.0 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 3,260 hours of sunshine a year) and 226th wettest (549 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rialto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Fontana is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.8 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, December (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in December. The area lies about 72 km from the sea.

Why Fontana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 72 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
