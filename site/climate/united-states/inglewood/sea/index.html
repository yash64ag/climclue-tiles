Inglewood sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · California · United States

The sea off Inglewood averages 19.7 °C (67 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.3 °C (58 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Inglewood sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Inglewood?
In August, at about 19.7 °C (67 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Inglewood?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 14.3 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Inglewood in February?
About 14.3 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Inglewood?
About 16.7 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Inglewood nearby?
In August: Cerritos (20.4 °C, 29 km) and Westminster, California (20.4 °C, 39 km).
