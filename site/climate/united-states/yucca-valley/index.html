Yucca Valley at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Yucca Valley is 738th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 139th sunniest (about 3,425 hours of sunshine a year) and 3047th wettest (233 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Desert Hot Springs, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Yucca Valley is 3.5 °C cooler in July and 4.3 °C cooler in December, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.2 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, December (8.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 142 km from the sea.

Why Yucca Valley has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 142 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yucca Valley itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.114°N, 116.432°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-498-635), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
