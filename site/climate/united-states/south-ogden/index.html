Why South Ogden has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 966 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
