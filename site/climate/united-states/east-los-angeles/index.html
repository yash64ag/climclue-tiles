East Los Angeles at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, East Los Angeles is 97th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 3,200 hours of sunshine a year) and 246th wettest (470 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Los Angeles, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), East Los Angeles is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, December (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Why East Los Angeles has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
