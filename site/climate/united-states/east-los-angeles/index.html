East Los Angeles at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, East Los Angeles is 671st warmest (18.0 °C year average), 274th sunniest (about 3,200 hours of sunshine a year) and 2766th wettest (435 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maywood, California, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), East Los Angeles is 0.2 °C warmer in August and about as warm in December, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.9 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, December (12.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in December. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Why East Los Angeles has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for East Los Angeles itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.024°N, 118.172°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-499-618), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
