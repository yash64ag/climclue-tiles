Lakewood at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Lakewood is 303rd warmest (9.5 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 3,077 hours of sunshine a year) and 250th wettest (456 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Denver, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Lakewood is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, December (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,204 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lakewood has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,204 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
