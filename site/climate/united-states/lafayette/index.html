Lafayette at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Lafayette is the 5th wettest (1,660 mm a year). Compared with Baton Rouge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Lafayette is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 16.6 °C more than its coolest, January (10.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 108% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Lafayette has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
