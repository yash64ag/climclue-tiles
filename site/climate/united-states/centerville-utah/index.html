Why Centerville, Utah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 966 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C. The 10 km area around Centerville, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 7.1 °C a year, 3.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
