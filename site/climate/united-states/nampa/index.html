Nampa weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Nampa, United States (Idaho), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Nampa is 1739th warmest (12.4 °C year average), 766th sunniest (about 2,926 hours of sunshine a year) and 3009th wettest (285 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kuna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Nampa is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Why Nampa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 616 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nampa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.541°N, 116.563°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-404-634), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
