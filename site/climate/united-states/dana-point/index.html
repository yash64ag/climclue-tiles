Dana Point at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Dana Point is 832nd warmest (17.5 °C year average), 421st sunniest (about 3,137 hours of sunshine a year) and 3007th wettest (286 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Juan Capistrano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Dana Point is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.8 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, February (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 12 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 86% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Dana Point has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dana Point itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.467°N, 117.698°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-505-623), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
