Carlsbad, California at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Carlsbad, California is 872nd warmest (17.2 °C year average), 443rd sunniest (about 3,121 hours of sunshine a year) and 2997th wettest (297 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oceanside, California, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Carlsbad, California is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.4 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, December (14.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in December. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 7 km from Carlsbad by up to 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Carlsbad, California has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Carlsbad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.158°N, 117.351°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-508-626), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
