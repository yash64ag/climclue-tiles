Ruskin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Ruskin averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 17.7 °C (64 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Ruskin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ruskin?
In August, at about 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ruskin?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 17.7 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ruskin in February?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ruskin?
About 24.2 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
