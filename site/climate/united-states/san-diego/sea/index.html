San Diego sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · California · United States

The sea off San Diego averages 20.2 °C (68 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

San Diego sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Diego?
In August, at about 20.2 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Diego?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Diego in February?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in San Diego?
About 17.2 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
