How November compares in San Diego

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in San Diego. Compared with October: 2.8 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. December is 2.6 °C cooler than November on average.

San Diego in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Diego in November?
In November, San Diego averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit San Diego?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of San Diego’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Diego in November?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (56 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Diego in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Diego get in November?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Diego in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
