San Diego in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Diego in August?
In August, San Diego averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit San Diego?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of San Diego’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
