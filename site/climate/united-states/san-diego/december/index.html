How December compares in San Diego

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in San Diego. Compared with November: 2.5 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

San Diego in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Diego in December?
In December, San Diego averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Diego?
December is not one of San Diego’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Diego in December?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (52 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Diego in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Diego get in December?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Diego in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
