How April compares in San Diego

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in San Diego. Compared with March: 1.1 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 1.1 °C warmer than April on average.

San Diego in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Diego in April?
In April, San Diego averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit San Diego?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of San Diego’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Diego in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (56 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Diego in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Diego get in April?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Diego in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
