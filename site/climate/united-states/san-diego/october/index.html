How October compares in San Diego

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in San Diego. Compared with September: 1.6 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 2.6 °C cooler than October on average.

San Diego in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Diego in October?
In October, San Diego averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit San Diego?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of San Diego’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Diego in October?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (52 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Diego in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Diego get in October?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Diego in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
