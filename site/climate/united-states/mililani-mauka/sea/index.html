Mililani Mauka sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hawaii · United States

The sea off Mililani Mauka averages 26.4 °C (80 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 23.6 °C (74 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to January.

Mililani Mauka sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mililani Mauka?
In September, at about 26.4 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mililani Mauka?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to January. The coolest month is February, at 23.6 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mililani Mauka in February?
About 23.6 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Mililani Mauka?
About 24.9 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
