Waterford, Connecticut sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Connecticut · United States

The sea off Waterford, Connecticut averages 21.2 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.1 °C (38 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to August.

Waterford, Connecticut sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Waterford, Connecticut?
In August, at about 21.2 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Waterford, Connecticut?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 3.1 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Waterford, Connecticut in February?
About 3.1 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Waterford, Connecticut?
About 12.0 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Waterford, Connecticut nearby?
In August: Guilford (22.0 °C, 46 km) and Branford (22.0 °C, 57 km).
