Kenner sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Louisiana · United States

The sea off Kenner averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.8 °C (59 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to September.

Kenner sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kenner?
In August, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kenner?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to September. The coolest month is January, at 14.8 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kenner in January?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kenner?
About 22.5 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
