How October compares in Denver

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Denver. Compared with September: 6.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. November is 7.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Denver in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denver in October?
In October, Denver averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Denver?
October is not one of Denver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denver in October?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (61 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Denver in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.4 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Denver get in October?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denver in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
