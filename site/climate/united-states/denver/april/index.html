How April compares in Denver

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Denver. Compared with March: 4.3 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Denver in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denver in April?
In April, Denver averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Denver?
April is not one of Denver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denver in April?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (61 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Denver in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 33 cm (12.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Denver get in April?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denver in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella, waterproof boots and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
