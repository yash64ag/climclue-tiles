How November compares in Denver

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Denver. Compared with October: 6.1 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Denver in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denver in November?
In November, Denver averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Denver?
November is not one of Denver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denver in November?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (61 mm), the driest January (15 mm).
Does it snow in Denver in November?
Yes, on average: about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Denver get in November?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denver in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
