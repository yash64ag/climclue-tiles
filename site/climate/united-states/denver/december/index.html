How December compares in Denver

December is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Denver. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Denver in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denver in December?
In December, Denver averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -7.0 °C (19 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Denver?
December is not one of Denver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denver in December?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (61 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Denver in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Denver get in December?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denver in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
