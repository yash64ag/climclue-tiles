How July compares in Denver

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Denver. Compared with June: 3.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. August is 1.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Denver in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denver in July?
In July, Denver averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 323 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Denver?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Denver’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denver in July?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (61 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Denver in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denver get in July?
About 323 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denver in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
