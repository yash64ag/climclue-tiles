How June compares in Denver

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Denver. Compared with May: 5.2 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Denver in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denver in June?
In June, Denver averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 314 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Denver?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Denver’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denver in June?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (61 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Denver in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denver get in June?
About 314 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denver in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
