Alexandria at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Alexandria is 201st warmest (14.0 °C year average), 257th sunniest (about 2,500 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (1,080 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arlington, Virginia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Alexandria is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 52 km from the sea.

Why Alexandria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 52 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
