Pearl City sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hawaii · United States

The sea off Pearl City averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 24.1 °C (75 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Pearl City sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pearl City?
In September, at about 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pearl City?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.1 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pearl City in February?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Pearl City?
About 25.3 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
