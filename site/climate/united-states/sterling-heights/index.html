Sterling Heights at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Sterling Heights is 298th warmest (9.8 °C year average), 313th sunniest (about 2,185 hours of sunshine a year) and 185th wettest (910 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Warren, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Sterling Heights is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year.
