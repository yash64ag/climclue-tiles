Philadelphia at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Philadelphia is 218th warmest (13.1 °C year average), 284th sunniest (about 2,463 hours of sunshine a year) and 87th wettest (1,215 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Allentown, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Philadelphia is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 80 km from the sea.

Why Philadelphia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
