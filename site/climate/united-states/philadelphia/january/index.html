How January compares in Philadelphia

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Philadelphia. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Philadelphia in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in January?
In January, Philadelphia averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -3.3 °C (26 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in January?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (73 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in January?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
