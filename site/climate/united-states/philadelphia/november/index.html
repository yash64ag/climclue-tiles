How November compares in Philadelphia

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Philadelphia. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Philadelphia in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in November?
In November, Philadelphia averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in November?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (72 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in November?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
