How December compares in Philadelphia

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Philadelphia. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Philadelphia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in December?
In December, Philadelphia averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in December?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (72 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in December?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
