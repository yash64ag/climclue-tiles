Philadelphia in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in April?
In April, Philadelphia averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in April?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (72 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in April?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
