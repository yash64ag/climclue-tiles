How February compares in Philadelphia

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Philadelphia. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Philadelphia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in February?
In February, Philadelphia averages a day high of 6.6 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -2.3 °C (28 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in February?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (72 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in February?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
