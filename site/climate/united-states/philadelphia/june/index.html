How June compares in Philadelphia

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Philadelphia. Compared with May: 5.3 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Philadelphia in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in June?
In June, Philadelphia averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (73 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in June?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
