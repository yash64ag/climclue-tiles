How March compares in Philadelphia

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Philadelphia. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 5.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Philadelphia in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Philadelphia in March?
In March, Philadelphia averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Philadelphia?
No month in Philadelphia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Philadelphia in March?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest February (73 mm).
Does it snow in Philadelphia in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.9 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Philadelphia get in March?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Philadelphia in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
