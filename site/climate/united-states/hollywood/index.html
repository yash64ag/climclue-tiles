Hollywood at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Hollywood is the 3rd warmest (24.3 °C year average). Compared with Pembroke Pines, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Hollywood is about as warm in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.8 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.9 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Hollywood

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hollywood are January, February, March, April, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, April, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (27.8 °C), July (27.6 °C) and September (27.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (47 mm), December (57 mm) and February (75 mm).

Why Hollywood has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
