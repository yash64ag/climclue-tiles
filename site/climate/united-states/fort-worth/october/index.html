How October compares in Fort Worth

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Fort Worth. Compared with September: 5.7 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 6.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Fort Worth in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in October?
In October, Fort Worth averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Fort Worth?
October is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in October?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (118 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in October?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
