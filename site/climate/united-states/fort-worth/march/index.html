Fort Worth in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in March?
In March, Fort Worth averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Fort Worth?
March is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in March?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
