Fort Worth in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in July?
In July, Fort Worth averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 331 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Fort Worth?
July is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in July?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in July?
About 331 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
