How May compares in Fort Worth

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Fort Worth. Compared with April: 4.3 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Fort Worth in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in May?
In May, Fort Worth averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Fort Worth?
May is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in May?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in May?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
