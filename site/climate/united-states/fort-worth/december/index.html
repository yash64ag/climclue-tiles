How December compares in Fort Worth

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fort Worth. Compared with November: 5.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Fort Worth in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in December?
In December, Fort Worth averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fort Worth?
December is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in December?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in December?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
