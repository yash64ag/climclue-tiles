Fort Worth in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in April?
In April, Fort Worth averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Fort Worth?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Fort Worth’s mild months (April), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in April?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in April?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
