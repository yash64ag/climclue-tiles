How February compares in Fort Worth

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Fort Worth. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 4.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Fort Worth in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in February?
In February, Fort Worth averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Fort Worth?
February is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in February?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in February?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
