November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Fort Worth. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. December is 5.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Fort Worth in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in November?
In November, Fort Worth averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Fort Worth?
November is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in November?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
