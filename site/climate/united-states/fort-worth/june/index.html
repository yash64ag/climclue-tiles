Fort Worth in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in June?
In June, Fort Worth averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 299 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Fort Worth?
June is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in June?
About 299 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
