September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Fort Worth. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Fort Worth in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in September?
In September, Fort Worth averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Fort Worth?
September is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in September?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in September?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
