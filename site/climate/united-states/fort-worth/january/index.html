How January compares in Fort Worth

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Fort Worth. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Fort Worth in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fort Worth in January?
In January, Fort Worth averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Fort Worth?
January is not one of Fort Worth’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fort Worth in January?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (115 mm), the driest January (46 mm).
Does it snow in Fort Worth in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fort Worth get in January?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fort Worth in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
