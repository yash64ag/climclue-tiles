Visalia at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Visalia is 89th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 3,445 hours of sunshine a year) and 287th wettest (330 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fresno, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Visalia is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in December, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, December (9.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 189 km from the sea.

Why Visalia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 189 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
