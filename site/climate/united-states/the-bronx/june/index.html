The Bronx in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in The Bronx in June?
In June, The Bronx averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit The Bronx?
No month in The Bronx is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in The Bronx in June?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in The Bronx in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does The Bronx get in June?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for The Bronx in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
