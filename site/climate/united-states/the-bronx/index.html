Why The Bronx has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

The Bronx climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in The Bronx?
July, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 28.2 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 20.8 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in The Bronx?
January, with an average of -0.7 °C (31 °F), day highs around 3.0 °C (37 °F) and night lows around -4.2 °C (24 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in The Bronx?
July, with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,213 mm (47.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in The Bronx?
February, with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in The Bronx?
July, with about 268 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in The Bronx?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 90 cm (35.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in The Bronx?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does The Bronx have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains The Bronx’s GeoNames location (40.850°N, 73.866°W; ClimaClue area sq-431-1061), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,385,108 (GeoNames).
