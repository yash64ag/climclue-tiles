February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in The Bronx. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

The Bronx in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in The Bronx in February?
In February, The Bronx averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -3.3 °C (26 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit The Bronx?
No month in The Bronx is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in The Bronx in February?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in The Bronx in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does The Bronx get in February?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for The Bronx in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
