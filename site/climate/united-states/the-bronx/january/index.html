How January compares in The Bronx

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in The Bronx. Compared with December: 3.1 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

The Bronx in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in The Bronx in January?
In January, The Bronx averages a day high of 3.0 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit The Bronx?
No month in The Bronx is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in The Bronx in January?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in The Bronx in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does The Bronx get in January?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for The Bronx in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
