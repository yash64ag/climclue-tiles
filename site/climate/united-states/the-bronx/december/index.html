How December compares in The Bronx

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in The Bronx. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 3.1 °C cooler than December on average.

The Bronx in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in The Bronx in December?
In December, The Bronx averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.4 °C (31 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit The Bronx?
No month in The Bronx is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in The Bronx in December?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in The Bronx in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does The Bronx get in December?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for The Bronx in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
