How July compares in The Bronx

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the sunniest month in The Bronx. Compared with June: 2.9 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

The Bronx in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in The Bronx in July?
In July, The Bronx averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit The Bronx?
No month in The Bronx is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in The Bronx in July?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (78 mm).
Does it snow in The Bronx in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does The Bronx get in July?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for The Bronx in July?
