About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-420-917), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
