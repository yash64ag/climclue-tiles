How November compares in Atlanta

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Atlanta. Compared with October: 5.1 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Atlanta in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Atlanta in November?
In November, Atlanta averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Atlanta?
November is not one of Atlanta’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Atlanta in November?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (137 mm), the driest April (91 mm).
Does it snow in Atlanta in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Atlanta get in November?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Atlanta in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
