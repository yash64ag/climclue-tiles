How April compares in Atlanta

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Atlanta. Compared with March: 4.2 °C warmer, 44 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Atlanta in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Atlanta in April?
In April, Atlanta averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Atlanta?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Atlanta’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Atlanta in April?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (132 mm), the driest April (88 mm).
Does it snow in Atlanta in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Atlanta get in April?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Atlanta in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
