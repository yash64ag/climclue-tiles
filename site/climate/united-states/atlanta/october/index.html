October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Atlanta. Compared with September: 5.7 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. November is 5.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Atlanta in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Atlanta in October?
In October, Atlanta averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Atlanta?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Atlanta’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Atlanta in October?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (132 mm), the driest April (88 mm).
Does it snow in Atlanta in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Atlanta get in October?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Atlanta in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
