How May compares in Las Vegas

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Las Vegas. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. June is 5.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Las Vegas in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in May?
In May, Las Vegas averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 382 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Las Vegas?
May is not one of Las Vegas’s mild months; those are March, April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in May?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (27 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in May?
About 382 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
