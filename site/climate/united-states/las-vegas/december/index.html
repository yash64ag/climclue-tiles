How December compares in Las Vegas

December is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Las Vegas. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Las Vegas in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in December?
In December, Las Vegas averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Las Vegas?
December is not one of Las Vegas’s mild months; those are March, April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (28 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in December?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
