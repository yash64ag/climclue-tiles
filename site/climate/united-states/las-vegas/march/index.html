How March compares in Las Vegas

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Las Vegas. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 68 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Las Vegas in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in March?
In March, Las Vegas averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Las Vegas?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Las Vegas’s mild months (March, April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in March?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (28 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in March?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
