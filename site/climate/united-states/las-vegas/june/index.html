How June compares in Las Vegas

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Las Vegas. Compared with May: 4.7 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 3.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Las Vegas in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in June?
In June, Las Vegas averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 397 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Las Vegas?
June is not one of Las Vegas’s mild months; those are March, April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in June?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (27 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in June?
About 397 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
