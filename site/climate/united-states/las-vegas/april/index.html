How April compares in Las Vegas

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Las Vegas. Compared with March: 4.2 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Las Vegas in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in April?
In April, Las Vegas averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 339 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Las Vegas?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Las Vegas’s mild months (March, April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (27 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in April?
About 339 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
