How January compares in Las Vegas

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Las Vegas. Compared with December: 0.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 2.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Las Vegas in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in January?
In January, Las Vegas averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Las Vegas?
January is not one of Las Vegas’s mild months; those are March, April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in January?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (27 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in January?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
