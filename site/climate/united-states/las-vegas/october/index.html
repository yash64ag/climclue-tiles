Las Vegas in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in October?
In October, Las Vegas averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Las Vegas?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Las Vegas’s mild months (March, April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in October?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (28 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in October?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
