Las Vegas weather in August

Average August weather in Las Vegas, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Las Vegas averages 40.2 °C (104 °F) days and 25.2 °C (77 °F) nights, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 363 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Las Vegas

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Las Vegas. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Las Vegas in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in August?
In August, Las Vegas averages a day high of 40.2 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 363 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Las Vegas?
August is not one of Las Vegas’s mild months; those are March, April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in August?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (27 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in August?
About 363 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
