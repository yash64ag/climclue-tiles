Las Vegas at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Las Vegas is 336th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 3,742 hours of sunshine a year) and 3104th wettest (136 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with North Las Vegas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Las Vegas is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.9 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, December (8.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 386 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 119% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Las Vegas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 386 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Las Vegas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.175°N, 115.137°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-478-648), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
