How September compares in Las Vegas

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Las Vegas. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Las Vegas in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Las Vegas in September?
In September, Las Vegas averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Las Vegas?
September is not one of Las Vegas’s mild months; those are March, April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Las Vegas in September?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (28 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Las Vegas in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Las Vegas get in September?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Las Vegas in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
