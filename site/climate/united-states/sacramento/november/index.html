How November compares in Sacramento

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Sacramento. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 99 hours less sunshine. December is 3.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Sacramento in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sacramento in November?
In November, Sacramento averages a day high of 18.0 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sacramento?
November is not one of Sacramento’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sacramento in November?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (100 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sacramento in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sacramento get in November?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sacramento in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
