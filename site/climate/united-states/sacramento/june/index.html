Sacramento in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sacramento in June?
In June, Sacramento averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 413 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sacramento?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Sacramento’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sacramento in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (99 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sacramento in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sacramento get in June?
About 413 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sacramento in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
