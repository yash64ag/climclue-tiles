Sacramento in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sacramento in April?
In April, Sacramento averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 327 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sacramento?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Sacramento’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sacramento in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (100 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sacramento in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sacramento get in April?
About 327 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sacramento in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
