How December compares in Sacramento

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Sacramento. Compared with November: 3.5 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Sacramento in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sacramento in December?
In December, Sacramento averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sacramento?
December is not one of Sacramento’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sacramento in December?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (100 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sacramento in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sacramento get in December?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sacramento in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
