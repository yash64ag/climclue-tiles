Sacramento in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sacramento in October?
In October, Sacramento averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sacramento?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Sacramento’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sacramento in October?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (100 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sacramento in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sacramento get in October?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sacramento in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
