Orlando at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Orlando is 35th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 140th sunniest (about 2,911 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (1,375 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lakeland, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Orlando is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 73 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Orlando

For mild weather, the best months to visit Orlando are February, April, May, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, April, May, October, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (27.3 °C), August (27.1 °C) and June (26.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (57 mm), January (63 mm) and April (66 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (275 h), March (274 h) and April (270 h).

Why Orlando has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 73 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
