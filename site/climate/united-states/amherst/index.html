Amherst at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Amherst is 313th warmest (9.3 °C year average), 316th sunniest (about 2,152 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (1,143 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buffalo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Amherst is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 458 km from the sea.

Why Amherst has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 458 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
