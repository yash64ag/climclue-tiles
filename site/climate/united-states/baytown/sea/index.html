Baytown sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Texas · United States

The sea off Baytown averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.3 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Baytown sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Baytown?
In August, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Baytown?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 14.3 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Baytown in February?
About 14.3 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Baytown?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
