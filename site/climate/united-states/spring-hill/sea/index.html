Spring Hill, Florida sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Spring Hill, Florida averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 16.5 °C (62 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Spring Hill, Florida sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Spring Hill, Florida?
In July, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Spring Hill, Florida?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 16.5 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Spring Hill, Florida in January?
About 16.5 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Spring Hill, Florida?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
