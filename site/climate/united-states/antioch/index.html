Antioch at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Antioch is 175th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 3,308 hours of sunshine a year) and 255th wettest (447 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Concord, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Antioch is 1.3 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (9.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Why Antioch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
