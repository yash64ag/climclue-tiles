Thornton at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Thornton is 292nd warmest (9.9 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 3,061 hours of sunshine a year) and 261st wettest (415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Westminster, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Thornton is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, December (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,218 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 115% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Thornton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
