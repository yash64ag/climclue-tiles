How October compares in Austin

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Austin. Compared with September: 5.0 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Austin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Austin in October?
In October, Austin averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Austin?
October is not one of Austin’s mild months; those are March, April, May and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Austin in October?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (111 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Austin in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Austin get in October?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Austin in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
