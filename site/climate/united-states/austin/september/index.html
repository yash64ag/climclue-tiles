How September compares in Austin, Texas

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Austin, Texas. Compared with August: 3.3 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 5.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Austin, Texas in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Austin, Texas in September?
In September, Austin, Texas averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Austin, Texas?
September is not one of Austin, Texas’s mild months; those are March, April, May and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Austin, Texas in September?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest August (46 mm).
Does it snow in Austin, Texas in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Austin, Texas get in September?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Austin, Texas in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
