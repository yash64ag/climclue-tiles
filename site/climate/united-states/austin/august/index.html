How August compares in Austin

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Austin. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. September is 3.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Austin in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Austin in August?
In August, Austin averages a day high of 35.0 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Austin?
August is not one of Austin’s mild months; those are March, April, May and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Austin in August?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (111 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Austin in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Austin get in August?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Austin in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
