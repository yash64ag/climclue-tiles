How December compares in Austin, Texas

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Austin, Texas. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Austin, Texas in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Austin, Texas in December?
In December, Austin, Texas averages a day high of 17.0 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Austin, Texas?
December is not one of Austin, Texas’s mild months; those are March, April, May and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Austin, Texas in December?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest August (46 mm).
Does it snow in Austin, Texas in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Austin, Texas get in December?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Austin, Texas in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
