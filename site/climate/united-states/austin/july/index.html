Austin, Texas weather in July

Average July weather in Austin, Texas, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Austin, Texas averages 35.1 °C (95 °F) days and 23.1 °C (74 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 313 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Austin, Texas

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Austin, Texas. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 52 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Austin, Texas in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Austin, Texas in July?
In July, Austin, Texas averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 313 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Austin, Texas?
July is not one of Austin, Texas’s mild months; those are March, April, May and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Austin, Texas in July?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (106 mm), the driest August (46 mm).
Does it snow in Austin, Texas in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Austin, Texas get in July?
About 313 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Austin, Texas in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
