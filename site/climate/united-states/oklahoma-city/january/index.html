How January compares in Oklahoma City

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Oklahoma City. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Oklahoma City in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oklahoma City in January?
In January, Oklahoma City averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Oklahoma City?
January is not one of Oklahoma City’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oklahoma City in January?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (131 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Oklahoma City get in January?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oklahoma City in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
