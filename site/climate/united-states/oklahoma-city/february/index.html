How February compares in Oklahoma City

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Oklahoma City. Compared with January: 2.6 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 4.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Oklahoma City in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oklahoma City in February?
In February, Oklahoma City averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Oklahoma City?
February is not one of Oklahoma City’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oklahoma City in February?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (131 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Oklahoma City get in February?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oklahoma City in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
