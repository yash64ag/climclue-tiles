Why Oklahoma City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 703 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Oklahoma City climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Oklahoma City?
July, with an average of 28.1 °C (82 °F), day highs around 33.6 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 23.6 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Oklahoma City?
January, with an average of 3.3 °C (38 °F), day highs around 9.8 °C (50 °F) and night lows around -0.4 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Oklahoma City?
June, with 131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 905 mm (35.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Oklahoma City?
January, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Oklahoma City?
July, with about 352 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Oklahoma City?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, September and October.
What type of climate does Oklahoma City have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Oklahoma City’s GeoNames location (35.468°N, 97.516°W; ClimaClue area sq-485-824), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 681,054 (GeoNames).
