How March compares in Oklahoma City

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Oklahoma City. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. April is 5.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Oklahoma City in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oklahoma City in March?
In March, Oklahoma City averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Oklahoma City?
March is not one of Oklahoma City’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oklahoma City in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (131 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Oklahoma City get in March?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oklahoma City in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
