How June compares in Oklahoma City

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Oklahoma City. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. July is 2.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Oklahoma City in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oklahoma City in June?
In June, Oklahoma City averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 321 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Oklahoma City?
June is not one of Oklahoma City’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oklahoma City in June?
131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (131 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oklahoma City get in June?
About 321 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oklahoma City in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
