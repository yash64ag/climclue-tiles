How July compares in Oklahoma City

July is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Oklahoma City. Compared with June: 2.8 °C warmer, 56 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Oklahoma City in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oklahoma City in July?
In July, Oklahoma City averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 352 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Oklahoma City?
July is not one of Oklahoma City’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oklahoma City in July?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (131 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oklahoma City get in July?
About 352 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oklahoma City in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
