How December compares in Oklahoma City

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Oklahoma City. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 0.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Oklahoma City in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oklahoma City in December?
In December, Oklahoma City averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Oklahoma City?
December is not one of Oklahoma City’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oklahoma City in December?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (131 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Oklahoma City in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.4 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Oklahoma City get in December?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oklahoma City in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
