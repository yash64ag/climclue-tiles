Los Lunas weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Los Lunas, United States (New Mexico), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Los Lunas at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Los Lunas is 1218th warmest (15.0 °C year average), 152nd sunniest (about 3,397 hours of sunshine a year) and 3035th wettest (249 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with South Valley, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Los Lunas is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.5 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, December (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 725 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Los Lunas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 725 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Los Lunas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.806°N, 106.733°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-732), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
