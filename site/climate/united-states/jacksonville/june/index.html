Jacksonville, Florida in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jacksonville, Florida in June?
In June, Jacksonville, Florida averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jacksonville, Florida?
June is not one of Jacksonville, Florida’s mild months; those are April, May and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
