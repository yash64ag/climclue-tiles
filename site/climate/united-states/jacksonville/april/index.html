Jacksonville, Florida in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jacksonville, Florida in April?
In April, Jacksonville, Florida averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jacksonville, Florida?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Jacksonville, Florida’s mild months (April, May and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jacksonville, Florida in April?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (215 mm), the driest November (65 mm).
Does it snow in Jacksonville, Florida in April?
