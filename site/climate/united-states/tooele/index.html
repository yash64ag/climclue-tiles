Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Tooele is 2525th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 707th sunniest (about 2,981 hours of sunshine a year) and 2759th wettest (440 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Tooele has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 921 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C. The 10 km area around Tooele, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 8.9 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tooele itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.531°N, 112.298°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-434-677), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
