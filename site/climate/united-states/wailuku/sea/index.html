Wailuku sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hawaii · United States

The sea off Wailuku averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 23.5 °C (74 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to January.

Wailuku sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Wailuku?
In September, at about 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Wailuku?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to January. The coolest month is March, at 23.5 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Wailuku in March?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Wailuku?
About 24.8 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Wailuku nearby?
In September: East Honolulu (26.8 °C, 133 km), Waikīkī (26.8 °C, 145 km) and Honolulu (26.8 °C, 148 km).
