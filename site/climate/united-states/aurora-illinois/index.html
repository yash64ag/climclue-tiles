Aurora, Illinois at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Aurora, Illinois is 288th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 276th sunniest (about 2,480 hours of sunshine a year) and 158th wettest (999 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naperville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Aurora, Illinois is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,051 km from the sea (deep inland).
