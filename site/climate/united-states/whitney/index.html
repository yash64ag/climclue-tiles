Whitney at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Whitney is 292nd warmest (21.8 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 3,755 hours of sunshine a year) and 3115th wettest (115 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Henderson, Nevada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Whitney is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in December, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.8 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, December (9.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 383 km from the sea.

Why Whitney has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 383 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Whitney itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.098°N, 115.036°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-479-649), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
