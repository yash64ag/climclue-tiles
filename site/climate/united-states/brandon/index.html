Brandon at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Brandon is 32nd warmest (22.2 °C year average), 143rd sunniest (about 2,896 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (1,355 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tampa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Brandon is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 11.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 17 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Brandon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
