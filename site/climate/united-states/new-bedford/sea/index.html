New Bedford sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Massachusetts · United States

The sea off New Bedford averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.7 °C (37 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

New Bedford sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in New Bedford?
In August, at about 20.4 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in New Bedford?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 2.7 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in New Bedford in February?
About 2.7 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in New Bedford?
About 11.4 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
