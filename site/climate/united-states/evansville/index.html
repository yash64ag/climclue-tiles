Evansville at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Evansville is 198th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 206th sunniest (about 2,640 hours of sunshine a year) and 100th wettest (1,186 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Louisville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (160 km away), Evansville is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 57 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 835 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Evansville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 835 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
