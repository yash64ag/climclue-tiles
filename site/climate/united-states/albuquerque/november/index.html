How November compares in Albuquerque

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Albuquerque. Compared with October: 7.0 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 5.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Albuquerque in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Albuquerque in November?
In November, Albuquerque averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Albuquerque?
November is not one of Albuquerque’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Albuquerque in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (46 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Albuquerque in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Albuquerque get in November?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Albuquerque in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
