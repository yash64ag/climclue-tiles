How December compares in Albuquerque

December is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Albuquerque. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Albuquerque in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Albuquerque in December?
In December, Albuquerque averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Albuquerque?
December is not one of Albuquerque’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Albuquerque in December?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (45 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Albuquerque in December?
Yes, on average: about 6.6 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Albuquerque get in December?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Albuquerque in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
