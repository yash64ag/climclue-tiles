Albuquerque weather in October

Average October weather in Albuquerque, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Albuquerque averages 22.9 °C (73 °F) days and 6.6 °C (44 °F) nights, with 27 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 276 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Albuquerque

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Albuquerque. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. November is 6.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Albuquerque in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Albuquerque in October?
In October, Albuquerque averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Albuquerque?
October is not one of Albuquerque’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Albuquerque in October?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (45 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Albuquerque in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Albuquerque get in October?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Albuquerque in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
