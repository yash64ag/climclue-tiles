Why El Monte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

El Monte climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in El Monte?
August, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 32.1 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in El Monte?
December, with an average of 12.4 °C (54 °F), day highs around 18.8 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 7.2 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in El Monte?
February, with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 497 mm (19.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in El Monte?
July, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in El Monte?
July, with about 355 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in El Monte?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in El Monte?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains El Monte’s GeoNames location (34.069°N, 118.028°W; ClimaClue area sq-499-619), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 116,732 (GeoNames).
