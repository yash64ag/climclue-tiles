Louisville, Kentucky weather in July

Average July weather in Louisville, Kentucky, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Louisville, Kentucky averages 31.4 °C (89 °F) days and 19.6 °C (67 °F) nights, with 98 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 289 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Louisville, Kentucky

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Louisville, Kentucky. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Louisville, Kentucky in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville, Kentucky in July?
In July, Louisville, Kentucky averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Louisville, Kentucky?
July is not one of Louisville, Kentucky’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
