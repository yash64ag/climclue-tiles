How July compares in Louisville

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Louisville. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Louisville in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in July?
In July, Louisville averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Louisville?
July is not one of Louisville’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in July?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in July?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
