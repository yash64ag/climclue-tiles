Louisville, Kentucky in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville, Kentucky in March?
In March, Louisville, Kentucky averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Louisville, Kentucky?
March is not one of Louisville, Kentucky’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville, Kentucky in March?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (122 mm), the driest October (75 mm).
