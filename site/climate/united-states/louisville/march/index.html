How March compares in Louisville

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Louisville. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 5.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Louisville in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in March?
In March, Louisville averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Louisville?
March is not one of Louisville’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in March?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in March?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
