Louisville, Kentucky in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville, Kentucky in June?
In June, Louisville, Kentucky averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Louisville, Kentucky?
June is not one of Louisville, Kentucky’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville, Kentucky in June?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (122 mm), the driest October (75 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville, Kentucky in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville, Kentucky get in June?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville, Kentucky in June?
