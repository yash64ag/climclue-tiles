How November compares in Louisville

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Louisville. Compared with October: 5.9 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. December is 5.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Louisville in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in November?
In November, Louisville averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Louisville?
November is not one of Louisville’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in November?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in November?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
