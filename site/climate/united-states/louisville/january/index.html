How January compares in Louisville

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Louisville. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Louisville in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in January?
In January, Louisville averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Louisville?
January is not one of Louisville’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in January?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in January?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
