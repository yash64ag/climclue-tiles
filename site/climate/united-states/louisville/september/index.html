How September compares in Louisville

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Louisville. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Louisville in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in September?
In September, Louisville averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Louisville?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Louisville’s mild months (August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in September?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in September?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
