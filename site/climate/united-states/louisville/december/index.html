How December compares in Louisville

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Louisville. Compared with November: 5.9 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Louisville in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in December?
In December, Louisville averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -0.9 °C (30 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Louisville?
December is not one of Louisville’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in December?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in December?
About 115 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
