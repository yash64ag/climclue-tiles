Louisville in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in August?
In August, Louisville averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Louisville?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Louisville’s mild months (August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in August?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in August?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
