April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Louisville. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Louisville in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville in April?
In April, Louisville averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Louisville?
April is not one of Louisville’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville in April?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (123 mm), the driest September (76 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville get in April?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
