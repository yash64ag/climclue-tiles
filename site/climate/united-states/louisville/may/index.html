May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Louisville, Kentucky. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 4.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Louisville, Kentucky in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Louisville, Kentucky in May?
In May, Louisville, Kentucky averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Louisville, Kentucky?
May is not one of Louisville, Kentucky’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Louisville, Kentucky in May?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (122 mm), the driest October (75 mm).
Does it snow in Louisville, Kentucky in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Louisville, Kentucky get in May?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Louisville, Kentucky in May?
