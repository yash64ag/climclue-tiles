Ozark at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Ozark is 1653rd warmest (12.7 °C year average), 1219th sunniest (about 2,678 hours of sunshine a year) and 1348th wettest (1,160 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nixa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Ozark is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 52 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 805 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 17 km from Ozark by up to 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Ozark has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 805 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ozark itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.021°N, 93.206°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-469-867), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
