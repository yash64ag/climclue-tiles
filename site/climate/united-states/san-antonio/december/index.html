How December compares in San Antonio

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in San Antonio. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

San Antonio in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Antonio in December?
In December, San Antonio averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Antonio?
December is not one of San Antonio’s mild months; those are March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Antonio in December?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (111 mm), the driest January (48 mm).
Does it snow in San Antonio in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Antonio get in December?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Antonio in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
