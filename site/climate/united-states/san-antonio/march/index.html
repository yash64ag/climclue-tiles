How March compares in San Antonio

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in San Antonio. Compared with February: 3.6 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 4.0 °C warmer than March on average.

San Antonio in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Antonio in March?
In March, San Antonio averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit San Antonio?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of San Antonio’s mild months (March, April, May, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Antonio in March?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (111 mm), the driest January (48 mm).
Does it snow in San Antonio in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Antonio get in March?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Antonio in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
