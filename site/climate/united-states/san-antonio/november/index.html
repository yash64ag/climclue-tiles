How November compares in San Antonio

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in San Antonio. Compared with October: 5.4 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

San Antonio in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Antonio in November?
In November, San Antonio averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit San Antonio?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of San Antonio’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Antonio in November?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (110 mm), the driest February (47 mm).
Does it snow in San Antonio in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Antonio get in November?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Antonio in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
