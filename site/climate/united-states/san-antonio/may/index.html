How May compares in San Antonio

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in San Antonio. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C warmer than May on average.

San Antonio in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Antonio in May?
In May, San Antonio averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit San Antonio?
May is not one of San Antonio’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Antonio in May?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (110 mm), the driest February (47 mm).
Does it snow in San Antonio in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Antonio get in May?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Antonio in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
