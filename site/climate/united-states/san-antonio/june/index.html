How June compares in San Antonio

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in San Antonio. Compared with May: 3.0 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. July is 0.9 °C warmer than June on average.

San Antonio in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Antonio in June?
In June, San Antonio averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit San Antonio?
June is not one of San Antonio’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Antonio in June?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (110 mm), the driest February (47 mm).
Does it snow in San Antonio in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Antonio get in June?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Antonio in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
