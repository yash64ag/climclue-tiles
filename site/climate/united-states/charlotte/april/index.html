Charlotte in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Charlotte in April?
In April, Charlotte averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Charlotte?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Charlotte’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Charlotte in April?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest November (82 mm).
Does it snow in Charlotte in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Charlotte get in April?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Charlotte in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
