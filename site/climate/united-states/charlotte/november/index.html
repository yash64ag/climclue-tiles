How November compares in Charlotte

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Charlotte. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Charlotte in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Charlotte in November?
In November, Charlotte averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Charlotte?
November is not one of Charlotte’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Charlotte in November?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest November (82 mm).
Does it snow in Charlotte in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Charlotte get in November?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Charlotte in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
