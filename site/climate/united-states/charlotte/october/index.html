Charlotte in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Charlotte in October?
In October, Charlotte averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Charlotte?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Charlotte’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Charlotte in October?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest November (82 mm).
Does it snow in Charlotte in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Charlotte get in October?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Charlotte in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
