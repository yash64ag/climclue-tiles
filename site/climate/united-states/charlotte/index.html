Why Charlotte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 262 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Charlotte climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Charlotte?
July, with an average of 26.4 °C (79 °F), day highs around 31.9 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 21.7 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Charlotte?
January, with an average of 5.0 °C (41 °F), day highs around 9.9 °C (50 °F) and night lows around 0.8 °C (33 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Charlotte?
July, with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,147 mm (45.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Charlotte?
November, with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Charlotte?
May, with about 286 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Charlotte?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Charlotte?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Charlotte have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Charlotte’s GeoNames location (35.227°N, 80.843°W; ClimaClue area sq-487-991), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 911,311 (GeoNames).
