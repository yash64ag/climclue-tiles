How February compares in Charlotte

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Charlotte. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 4.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Charlotte in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Charlotte in February?
In February, Charlotte averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Charlotte?
February is not one of Charlotte’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Charlotte in February?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest November (82 mm).
Does it snow in Charlotte in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Charlotte get in February?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Charlotte in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
