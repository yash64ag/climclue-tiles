How June compares in Charlotte

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Charlotte. Compared with May: 4.2 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 1.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Charlotte in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Charlotte in June?
In June, Charlotte averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Charlotte?
June is not one of Charlotte’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Charlotte in June?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest November (82 mm).
Does it snow in Charlotte in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Charlotte get in June?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Charlotte in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
