How August compares in Charlotte

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Charlotte. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Charlotte in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Charlotte in August?
In August, Charlotte averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Charlotte?
August is not one of Charlotte’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Charlotte in August?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest November (81 mm).
Does it snow in Charlotte in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Charlotte get in August?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Charlotte in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
