Hermiston at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Hermiston is 1894th warmest (11.9 °C year average), 2551st sunniest (about 2,446 hours of sunshine a year) and 3039th wettest (244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kennewick, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Hermiston is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in December, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, December (1.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 330 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 2 km from Hermiston by up to 3.5 °C (6.2 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Hermiston has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 330 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hermiston itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.840°N, 119.289°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-381-607), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
