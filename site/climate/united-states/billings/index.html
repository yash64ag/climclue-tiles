Why Billings has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,082 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Billings climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Billings?
July, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 31.8 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 16.6 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Billings?
December, with an average of -3.0 °C (27 °F), day highs around 2.2 °C (36 °F) and night lows around -7.0 °C (19 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Billings?
May, with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 368 mm (14.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Billings?
January, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Billings?
July, with about 355 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Billings?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 149 cm (58.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Billings?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Billings have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.8 × 11 km, that contains Billings’s GeoNames location (45.783°N, 108.501°W; ClimaClue area sq-382-714), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,116 (GeoNames).
