Minneapolis at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Minneapolis is the 5th coolest (7.6 °C year average). Compared with Saint Paul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Minneapolis is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 32.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,189 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Minneapolis

For mild weather, the best months to visit Minneapolis are May and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (22.8 °C), August (21.4 °C) and June (20.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (20 mm), January (21 mm) and December (29 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (343 h), June (316 h) and August (300 h).

Why Minneapolis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,189 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
