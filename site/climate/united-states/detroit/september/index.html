How September compares in Detroit

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Detroit. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 6.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Detroit in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in September?
In September, Detroit averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Detroit?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Detroit’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in September?
95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (95 mm), the driest January (59 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in September?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
