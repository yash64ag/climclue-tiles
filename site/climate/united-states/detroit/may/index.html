How May compares in Detroit

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Detroit. Compared with April: 6.0 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. June is 5.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Detroit in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in May?
In May, Detroit averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Detroit?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Detroit’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in May?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (95 mm), the driest January (59 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in May?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
