How February compares in Detroit

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Detroit. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 4.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Detroit in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in February?
In February, Detroit averages a day high of 1.4 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -6.4 °C (20 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Detroit?
February is not one of Detroit’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in February?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (96 mm), the driest January (60 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in February?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
