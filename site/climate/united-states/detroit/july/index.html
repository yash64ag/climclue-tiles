How July compares in Detroit

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Detroit. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Detroit in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in July?
In July, Detroit averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Detroit?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Detroit’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in July?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (96 mm), the driest January (60 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in July?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
