How April compares in Detroit

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Detroit. Compared with March: 7.0 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. May is 6.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Detroit in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in April?
In April, Detroit averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Detroit?
April is not one of Detroit’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in April?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (95 mm), the driest January (59 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in April?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
