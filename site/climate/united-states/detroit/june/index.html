How June compares in Detroit

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Detroit. Compared with May: 5.5 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Detroit in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in June?
In June, Detroit averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Detroit?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Detroit’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in June?
95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (96 mm), the driest January (60 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in June?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
