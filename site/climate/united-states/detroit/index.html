Detroit at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Detroit is 293rd warmest (9.9 °C year average), 311th sunniest (about 2,191 hours of sunshine a year) and 170th wettest (967 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Warren, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Detroit is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 661 km from the sea (deep inland).

For mild weather, the best months to visit Detroit are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.

Why Detroit has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 661 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
