How January compares in Detroit

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Detroit. Compared with December: 2.5 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Detroit in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in January?
In January, Detroit averages a day high of -0.4 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -7.5 °C (19 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Detroit?
January is not one of Detroit’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in January?
60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (96 mm), the driest January (60 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 29 cm (11.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in January?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
