How December compares in Detroit

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Detroit. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 2.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Detroit in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Detroit in December?
In December, Detroit averages a day high of 2.0 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -4.0 °C (25 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 80 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Detroit?
December is not one of Detroit’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Detroit in December?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (96 mm), the driest January (60 mm).
Does it snow in Detroit in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Detroit get in December?
About 80 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Detroit in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
