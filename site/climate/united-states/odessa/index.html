Odessa at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Odessa is 102nd warmest (17.8 °C year average), 114th sunniest (about 3,063 hours of sunshine a year) and 278th wettest (354 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Midland, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Odessa is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 683 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Odessa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 683 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
