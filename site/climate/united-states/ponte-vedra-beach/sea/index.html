Ponte Vedra Beach sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Ponte Vedra Beach averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.3 °C (61 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Ponte Vedra Beach sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ponte Vedra Beach?
In August, at about 27.9 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ponte Vedra Beach?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.3 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ponte Vedra Beach in February?
About 16.3 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ponte Vedra Beach?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ponte Vedra Beach nearby?
In August: Wilmington Island (28.6 °C, 200 km).
