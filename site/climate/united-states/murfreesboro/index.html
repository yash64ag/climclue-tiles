Murfreesboro at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Murfreesboro is 176th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 272nd sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (1,358 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nashville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Murfreesboro is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 612 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Murfreesboro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 612 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
