How April compares in Houston

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Houston. Compared with March: 3.7 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 3.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Houston in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in April?
In April, Houston averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Houston?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Houston’s mild months (March and April), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in April?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (173 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in April?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
