How November compares in Houston

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Houston. Compared with October: 5.1 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. December is 4.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Houston in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in November?
In November, Houston averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Houston?
November is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in November?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (173 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in November?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
