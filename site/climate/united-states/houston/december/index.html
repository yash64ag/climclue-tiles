How December compares in Houston

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Houston. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 0.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Houston in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in December?
In December, Houston averages a day high of 18.0 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Houston?
December is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in December?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (174 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in December?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
