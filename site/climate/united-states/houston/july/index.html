How July compares in Houston

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Houston. Compared with June: 1.0 °C warmer, 53 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Houston in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in July?
In July, Houston averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Houston?
July is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in July?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (174 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in July?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
