How March compares in Houston

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Houston. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 3.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Houston in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in March?
In March, Houston averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Houston?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Houston’s mild months (March and April), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in March?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (173 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in March?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
