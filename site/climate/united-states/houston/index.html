Houston at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Houston is 45th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 227th sunniest (about 2,576 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (1,378 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pasadena, Texas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Houston is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Houston

For mild weather, the best months to visit Houston are March and April.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March and April.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.1 °C), August (28.1 °C) and June (27.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (81 mm), April (83 mm) and March (91 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (297 h), June (283 h) and August (272 h).

Why Houston has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
