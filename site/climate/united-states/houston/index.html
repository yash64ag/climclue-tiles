Why Houston has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Houston climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Houston?
July, with an average of 28.1 °C (83 °F), day highs around 32.9 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 24.3 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Houston?
January, with an average of 11.7 °C (53 °F), day highs around 17.4 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 8.0 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Houston?
June, with 174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,378 mm (54.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Houston?
February, with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Houston?
July, with about 297 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Houston?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Houston?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March and April.
What type of climate does Houston have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Houston’s GeoNames location (29.763°N, 95.363°W; ClimaClue area sq-542-846), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,314,157 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
