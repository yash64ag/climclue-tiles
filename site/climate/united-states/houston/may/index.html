How May compares in Houston

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Houston. Compared with April: 3.8 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. June is 2.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Houston in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in May?
In May, Houston averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Houston?
May is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in May?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (174 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in May?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
