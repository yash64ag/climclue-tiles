How August compares in Houston

August is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Houston. Compared with July: 0.2 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. September is 2.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Houston in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in August?
In August, Houston averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Houston?
August is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in August?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (173 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in August?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
