How February compares in Houston

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Houston. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. March is 3.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Houston in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in February?
In February, Houston averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Houston?
February is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in February?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (174 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in February?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
