How September compares in Houston

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Houston. Compared with August: 2.5 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. October is 4.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Houston in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in September?
In September, Houston averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 121 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Houston?
September is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in September?
121 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (173 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in September?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
