How January compares in Houston

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Houston. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Houston in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Houston in January?
In January, Houston averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Houston?
January is not one of Houston’s mild months; those are March and April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Houston in January?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (174 mm), the driest February (81 mm).
Does it snow in Houston in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Houston get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Houston in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
