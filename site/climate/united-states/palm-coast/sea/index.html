Palm Coast sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Palm Coast averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 19.3 °C (67 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Palm Coast sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Palm Coast?
In September, at about 27.9 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Palm Coast?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 19.3 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Palm Coast in February?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Palm Coast?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
