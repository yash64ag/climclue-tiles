Springfield, Massachusetts at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Springfield, Massachusetts is 278th warmest (10.3 °C year average), 248th sunniest (about 2,519 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (1,212 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hartford, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Springfield, Massachusetts is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Why Springfield, Massachusetts has this climate
