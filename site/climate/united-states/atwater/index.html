Atwater at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Atwater is 719th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 129th sunniest (about 3,450 hours of sunshine a year) and 2912th wettest (343 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Merced, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Atwater is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Why Atwater has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 128 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Atwater itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.348°N, 120.609°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-466-593), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
