Placentia weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Placentia, United States (California), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Placentia at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Placentia is 784th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 297th sunniest (about 3,188 hours of sunshine a year) and 2906th wettest (345 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fullerton, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Placentia is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Why Placentia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Placentia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.872°N, 117.870°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-501-621), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
