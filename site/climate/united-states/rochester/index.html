Rochester, New York at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Rochester, New York is 316th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 307th sunniest (about 2,232 hours of sunshine a year) and 167th wettest (971 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amherst, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Rochester, New York is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 173 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 425 km from the sea.

Why Rochester, New York has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 425 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
