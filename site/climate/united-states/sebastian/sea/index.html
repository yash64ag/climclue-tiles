Sebastian sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Florida · United States

The sea off Sebastian averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 21.8 °C (71 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Sebastian sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sebastian?
In August, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sebastian?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 21.8 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sebastian in February?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Sebastian?
About 25.1 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sebastian nearby?
In August: Palm City (28.9 °C, 75 km).
