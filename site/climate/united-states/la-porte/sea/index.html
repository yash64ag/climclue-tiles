La Porte, Texas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Texas · United States

The sea off La Porte, Texas averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

La Porte, Texas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in La Porte, Texas?
In August, at about 29.9 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in La Porte, Texas?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in La Porte, Texas in February?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in La Porte, Texas?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
