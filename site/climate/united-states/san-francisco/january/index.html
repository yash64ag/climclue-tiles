How January compares in San Francisco

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in San Francisco. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

San Francisco in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in January?
In January, San Francisco averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit San Francisco?
January is not one of San Francisco’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in January?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (144 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in January?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in San Francisco in January?
About 11.5 °C (53 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (14.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for San Francisco in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
