How March compares in San Francisco

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in San Francisco. Compared with February: 1.0 °C warmer, 32 mm less precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. April is 0.8 °C warmer than March on average.

San Francisco in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in March?
In March, San Francisco averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit San Francisco?
March is not one of San Francisco’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in March?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (144 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in March?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in San Francisco in March?
About 11.7 °C (53 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (14.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for San Francisco in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
