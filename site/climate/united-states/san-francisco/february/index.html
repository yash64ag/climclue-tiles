How February compares in San Francisco

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in San Francisco. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. March is 1.0 °C warmer than February on average.

San Francisco in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in February?
In February, San Francisco averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit San Francisco?
February is not one of San Francisco’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in February?
131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (144 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in February?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in San Francisco in February?
About 11.6 °C (53 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (14.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for San Francisco in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
