San Francisco weather in May

Average May weather in San Francisco, United States: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, San Francisco averages 18.2 °C (65 °F) days and 10.1 °C (50 °F) nights, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of rain over the month and about 328 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in San Francisco

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in San Francisco. Compared with April: 1.3 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C warmer than May on average.

San Francisco in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in May?
In May, San Francisco averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 328 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit San Francisco?
May is not one of San Francisco’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in May?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (144 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in May?
About 328 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in San Francisco in May?
About 11.2 °C (52 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (14.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for San Francisco in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
