How December compares in San Francisco

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in San Francisco. Compared with November: 2.3 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

San Francisco in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in December?
In December, San Francisco averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Francisco?
December is not one of San Francisco’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in December?
146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (146 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in December?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Francisco in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
