How August compares in San Francisco

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in San Francisco. Compared with July: 0.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. September is about as warm as August on average.

San Francisco in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in August?
In August, San Francisco averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit San Francisco?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of San Francisco’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (146 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in August?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Francisco in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
