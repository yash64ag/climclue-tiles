How October compares in San Francisco

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in San Francisco. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. November is 2.8 °C cooler than October on average.

San Francisco in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Francisco in October?
In October, San Francisco averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit San Francisco?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of San Francisco’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Francisco in October?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (144 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in San Francisco in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Francisco get in October?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in San Francisco in October?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (14.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for San Francisco in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
