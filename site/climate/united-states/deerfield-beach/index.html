Deerfield Beach at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Deerfield Beach is 64th warmest (24.2 °C year average), 610th sunniest (about 3,056 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (1,523 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Boca Raton, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Deerfield Beach is 0.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Why Deerfield Beach has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Deerfield Beach itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.318°N, 80.100°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-576-999), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
