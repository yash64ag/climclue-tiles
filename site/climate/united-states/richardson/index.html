Richardson at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Richardson is 77th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 158th sunniest (about 2,798 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (1,059 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Plano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Richardson is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.9 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 419 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 16 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Richardson has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 419 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
