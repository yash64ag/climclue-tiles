Bridgeport at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Bridgeport is 250th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 237th sunniest (about 2,536 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with New Haven, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Bridgeport is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 137 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bridgeport has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
