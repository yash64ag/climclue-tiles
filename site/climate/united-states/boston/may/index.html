How May compares in Boston

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Boston. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Boston in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Boston in May?
In May, Boston averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Boston?
May is not one of Boston’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Boston in May?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (120 mm), the driest January (93 mm).
Does it snow in Boston in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Boston get in May?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Boston in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
