How September compares in Boston

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Boston. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Boston in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Boston in September?
In September, Boston averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Boston?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Boston’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Boston in September?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (120 mm), the driest September (92 mm).
Does it snow in Boston in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Boston get in September?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Boston in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
