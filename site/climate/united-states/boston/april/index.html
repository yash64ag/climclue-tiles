How April compares in Boston

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Boston. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Boston in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Boston in April?
In April, Boston averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Boston?
April is not one of Boston’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Boston in April?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (120 mm), the driest September (92 mm).
Does it snow in Boston in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Boston get in April?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Boston in April?
About 5.3 °C (42 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (18.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Boston in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
