How July compares in Boston

July is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Boston. Compared with June: 3.0 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Boston in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Boston in July?
In July, Boston averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Boston?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Boston’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Boston in July?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (120 mm), the driest September (92 mm).
Does it snow in Boston in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Boston get in July?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Boston in July?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (18.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Boston in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
