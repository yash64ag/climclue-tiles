How January compares in Boston

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Boston. Compared with December: 3.1 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Boston in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Boston in January?
In January, Boston averages a day high of 2.5 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -6.5 °C (20 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Boston?
January is not one of Boston’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Boston in January?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (120 mm), the driest September (92 mm).
Does it snow in Boston in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 37 cm (14.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Boston get in January?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Boston in January?
About 4.3 °C (40 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (18.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Boston in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
