Tillmans Corner sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Alabama · United States

The sea off Tillmans Corner averages 29.4 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Tillmans Corner sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tillmans Corner?
In August, at about 29.4 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tillmans Corner?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tillmans Corner in January?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tillmans Corner?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
