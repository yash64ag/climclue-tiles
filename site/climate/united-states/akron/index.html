Akron at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Akron is the 4th least sunny (about 2,060 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Cleveland, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Akron is 0.9 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 485 km from the sea.

Why Akron has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 485 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
