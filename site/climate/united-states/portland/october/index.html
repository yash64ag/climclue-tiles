How October compares in Portland

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Portland. Compared with September: 5.1 °C cooler, 53 mm more precipitation and 83 hours less sunshine. November is 4.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Portland in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Portland in October?
In October, Portland averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Portland?
October is not one of Portland’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Portland in October?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (193 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Portland in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Portland get in October?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Portland in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
