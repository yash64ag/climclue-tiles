Portland at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Portland is 239th warmest (11.8 °C year average), 303rd sunniest (about 2,305 hours of sunshine a year) and 94th wettest (1,198 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vancouver, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Portland is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 99 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.9 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, December (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 109 km from the sea.

Why Portland has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 109 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
