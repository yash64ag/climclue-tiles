How May compares in Portland, Oregon

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Portland, Oregon. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. June is 2.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Portland, Oregon in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Portland, Oregon in May?
In May, Portland, Oregon averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Portland, Oregon?
May is not one of Portland, Oregon’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Portland, Oregon in May?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (208 mm), the driest July (17 mm).
Does it snow in Portland, Oregon in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Portland, Oregon get in May?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Portland, Oregon in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
