How January compares in Portland

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Portland. Compared with December: 0.7 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Portland in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Portland in January?
In January, Portland averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Portland?
January is not one of Portland’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Portland in January?
173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (193 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Portland in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Portland get in January?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Portland in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
