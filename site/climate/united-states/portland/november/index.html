How November compares in Portland, Oregon

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Portland, Oregon. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 92 mm more precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. December is 3.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Portland, Oregon in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Portland, Oregon in November?
In November, Portland, Oregon averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Portland, Oregon?
November is not one of Portland, Oregon’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Portland, Oregon in November?
197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (208 mm), the driest July (17 mm).
Does it snow in Portland, Oregon in November?
Yes, on average: about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Portland, Oregon get in November?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Portland, Oregon in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
