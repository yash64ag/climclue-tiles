About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fairview Heights itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.589°N, 89.990°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-454-900), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
