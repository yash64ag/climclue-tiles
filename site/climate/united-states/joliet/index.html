Joliet at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Joliet is 275th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 270th sunniest (about 2,486 hours of sunshine a year) and 139th wettest (1,071 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naperville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Joliet is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 1,022 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Joliet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.
