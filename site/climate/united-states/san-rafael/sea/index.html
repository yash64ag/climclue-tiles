San Rafael sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · California · United States

The sea off San Rafael averages 14.1 °C (57 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 11.2 °C (52 °F) in April. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

San Rafael sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Rafael?
In September, at about 14.1 °C (57 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Rafael?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is April, at 11.2 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Rafael in April?
About 11.2 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in San Rafael?
About 12.3 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
