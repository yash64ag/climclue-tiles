Makakilo-Makaīwa Hills-Kunia at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Makakilo-Makaīwa Hills-Kunia is 125th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 884th sunniest (about 2,887 hours of sunshine a year) and 1938th wettest (1,007 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Makakilo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Makakilo-Makaīwa Hills-Kunia is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 115 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, February (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Makakilo-Makaīwa Hills-Kunia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
