Virginia Beach sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Virginia · United States

The sea off Virginia Beach averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.1 °C (41 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Virginia Beach sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Virginia Beach?
In August, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Virginia Beach?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 5.1 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Virginia Beach in February?
About 5.1 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Virginia Beach?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Virginia Beach nearby?
In August: East Hampton (26.6 °C, 38 km), Elizabeth City (26.4 °C, 67 km) and Havelock (27.1 °C, 235 km).
