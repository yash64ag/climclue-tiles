Why Fort Collins has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,274 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Fort Collins climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Fort Collins?
July, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 30.1 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 15.3 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Fort Collins?
December, with an average of -1.4 °C (29 °F), day highs around 5.2 °C (41 °F) and night lows around -6.3 °C (21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Fort Collins?
May, with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 392 mm (15.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Fort Collins?
January, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Fort Collins?
July, with about 324 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Fort Collins?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 162 cm (63.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Fort Collins?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Fort Collins have?
Cold semi-arid (Köppen BSk). Dry grassland climate with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains Fort Collins’s GeoNames location (40.585°N, 105.084°W; ClimaClue area sq-434-749), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 170,924 (GeoNames).
