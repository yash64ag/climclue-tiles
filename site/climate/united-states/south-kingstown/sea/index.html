South Kingstown sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rhode Island · United States

The sea off South Kingstown averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.9 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

South Kingstown sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in South Kingstown?
In August, at about 20.4 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in South Kingstown?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 3.9 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in South Kingstown in February?
About 3.9 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in South Kingstown?
About 11.8 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in South Kingstown nearby?
In August: Montville Center (21.2 °C, 52 km) and Waterford, Connecticut (21.2 °C, 52 km).
