How November compares in Nashville

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nashville. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. December is 5.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Nashville in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in November?
In November, Nashville averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nashville?
November is not one of Nashville’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in November?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (133 mm), the driest August (82 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in November?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
