How October compares in Nashville

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nashville. Compared with September: 6.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Nashville in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in October?
In October, Nashville averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nashville?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Nashville’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in October?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (133 mm), the driest August (82 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in October?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
