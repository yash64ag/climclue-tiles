How April compares in Nashville

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nashville. Compared with March: 5.2 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Nashville in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in April?
In April, Nashville averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nashville?
April is not one of Nashville’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in April?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (133 mm), the driest August (82 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
