How August compares in Nashville

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nashville. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Nashville in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in August?
In August, Nashville averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nashville?
August is not one of Nashville’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in August?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (133 mm), the driest August (82 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in August?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
