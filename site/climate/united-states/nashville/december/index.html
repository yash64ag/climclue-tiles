How December compares in Nashville

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nashville. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Nashville in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in December?
In December, Nashville averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nashville?
December is not one of Nashville’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in December?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (136 mm), the driest October (83 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
