How January compares in Nashville

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nashville. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Nashville in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in January?
In January, Nashville averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -0.4 °C (31 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nashville?
January is not one of Nashville’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in January?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (136 mm), the driest October (83 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.3 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in January?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
