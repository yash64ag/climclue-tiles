How September compares in Nashville

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nashville. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. October is 6.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Nashville in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashville in September?
In September, Nashville averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nashville?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Nashville’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashville in September?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (136 mm), the driest October (83 mm).
Does it snow in Nashville in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashville get in September?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashville in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
