Elk Grove at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Elk Grove is 149th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 3,529 hours of sunshine a year) and 228th wettest (536 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sacramento, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Elk Grove is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Elk Grove has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
