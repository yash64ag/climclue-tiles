Why Port Saint Lucie has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Port Saint Lucie climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Port Saint Lucie?
July, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Port Saint Lucie?
January, with an average of 18.4 °C (65 °F), day highs around 22.4 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 15.5 °C (60 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Port Saint Lucie?
August, with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,424 mm (56.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Port Saint Lucie?
January, with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Port Saint Lucie?
July, with about 289 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Port Saint Lucie?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Port Saint Lucie?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, April, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Port Saint Lucie’s GeoNames location (27.294°N, 80.350°W; ClimaClue area sq-567-996), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 164,603 (GeoNames).
