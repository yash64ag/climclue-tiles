Elizabeth at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Elizabeth is 229th warmest (12.3 °C year average), 273rd sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (1,215 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Newark, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Elizabeth is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Why Elizabeth has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
