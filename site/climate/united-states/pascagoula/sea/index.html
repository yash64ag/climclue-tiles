Pascagoula sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mississippi · United States

The sea off Pascagoula averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.8 °C (59 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Pascagoula sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pascagoula?
In August, at about 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pascagoula?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 14.8 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pascagoula in January?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pascagoula?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
