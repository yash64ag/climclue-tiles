Bostonia at a glance

Among the 3129 towns ClimaClue covers in the United States, Bostonia is 837th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 408th sunniest (about 3,147 hours of sunshine a year) and 2942nd wettest (327 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Winter Gardens, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Bostonia is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.4 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, December (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in December. The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Why Bostonia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bostonia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.813°N, 116.944°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-511-630), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
