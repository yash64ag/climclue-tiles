Warren at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Warren is 296th warmest (9.8 °C year average), 310th sunniest (about 2,193 hours of sunshine a year) and 174th wettest (961 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sterling Heights, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Warren is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 51 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 666 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Warren has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
