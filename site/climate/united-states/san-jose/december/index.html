How December compares in San Jose

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in San Jose. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

San Jose in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Jose in December?
In December, San Jose averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.4 °C (42 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Jose?
December is not one of San Jose’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Jose in December?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (103 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in San Jose in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Jose get in December?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Jose in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
