San Jose in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Jose in May?
In May, San Jose averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 345 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit San Jose?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of San Jose’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Jose in May?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (109 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in San Jose in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Jose get in May?
About 345 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Jose in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
