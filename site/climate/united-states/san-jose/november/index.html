How November compares in San Jose

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in San Jose. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

San Jose in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Jose in November?
In November, San Jose averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit San Jose?
November is not one of San Jose’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Jose in November?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (103 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in San Jose in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Jose get in November?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Jose in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
