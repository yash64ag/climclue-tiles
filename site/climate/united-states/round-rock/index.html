Round Rock at a glance

Among the 328 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United States, Round Rock is 56th warmest (19.8 °C year average), 205th sunniest (about 2,642 hours of sunshine a year) and 183rd wettest (923 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Austin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Round Rock is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.9 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 255 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Round Rock has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 255 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
