Kamenjane weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kamenjane, North Macedonia (Bogovinje), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kamenjane at a glance

Among the 36 towns ClimaClue covers in North Macedonia, Kamenjane is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,994 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (907 mm a year). Compared with Bogovinje, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Kamenjane is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Why Kamenjane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kamenjane itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.946°N, 20.929°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-420-2009), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
