Kičevo at a glance

Among the 36 towns ClimaClue covers in North Macedonia, Kičevo is the 4th coolest (11.5 °C year average). Compared with Gostivar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Kičevo is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 92 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kičevo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kičevo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C. The 10 km area around Kičevo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.4 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kičevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.513°N, 20.959°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-424-2009), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
