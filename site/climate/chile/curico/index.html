Curicó at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Curicó is 19th warmest (14.7 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,633 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (847 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Talca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Curicó is 0.6 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.4 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, July (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Curicó, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Curicó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
