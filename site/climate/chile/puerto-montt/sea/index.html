Puerto Montt sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Los Lagos Region · Chile

The sea off Puerto Montt averages 19.1 °C (66 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 8.6 °C (47 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Puerto Montt sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto Montt?
In February, at about 19.1 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto Montt?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 8.6 °C (47 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto Montt in August?
About 8.6 °C (47 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto Montt?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
