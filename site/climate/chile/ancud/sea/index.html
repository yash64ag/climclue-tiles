Ancud sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Los Lagos Region · Chile

The sea off Ancud averages 13.8 °C (57 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 10.7 °C (51 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Ancud sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ancud?
In January, at about 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ancud?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 10.7 °C (51 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ancud in August?
About 10.7 °C (51 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ancud?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ancud nearby?
In January: Calbuco (14.8 °C, 58 km), Puerto Montt (18.9 °C, 85 km) and Lebu (15.3 °C, 474 km).
