Coquimbo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Coquimbo Region · Chile

The sea off Coquimbo averages 17.9 °C (64 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Coquimbo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Coquimbo?
In February, at about 17.9 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Coquimbo?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Coquimbo in August?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Coquimbo?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Coquimbo nearby?
In February: Caldera (18.8 °C, 325 km).
