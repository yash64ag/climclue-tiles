Quellón sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Los Lagos Region · Chile

The sea off Quellón averages 12.5 °C (55 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 9.5 °C (49 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Quellón sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Quellón?
In February, at about 12.5 °C (55 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Quellón?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 9.5 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Quellón in August?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Quellón?
About 10.8 °C (52 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Quellón nearby?
In February: Castro (13.6 °C, 73 km), Ancud (13.7 °C, 140 km) and Calbuco (14.8 °C, 155 km).
