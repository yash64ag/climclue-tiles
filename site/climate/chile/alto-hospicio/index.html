Alto Hospicio at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Alto Hospicio is the 3rd warmest (17.8 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (30 mm a year). Compared with Arica, the nearest covered town with different numbers (200 km away), Alto Hospicio is 2.2 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, July (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Alto Hospicio, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Alto Hospicio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
