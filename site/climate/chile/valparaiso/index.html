Valparaíso at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Valparaíso is 25th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,595 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (655 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Viña del Mar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Valparaíso is 0.4 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C warmer in July, with 95 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (16.6 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, July (11.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valparaíso, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valparaíso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
