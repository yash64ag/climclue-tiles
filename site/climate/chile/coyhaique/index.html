Coyhaique at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Coyhaique is the 3rd coolest (8.0 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 2,279 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Puerto Aysén, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Coyhaique is 1.1 °C cooler in February and 1.8 °C cooler in July, with 1,551 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (14.0 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, July (1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in June, July and August. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Why Coyhaique has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C. The 10 km area around Coyhaique, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 6.7 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Coyhaique itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.575°S, 72.066°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1295-1079), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
