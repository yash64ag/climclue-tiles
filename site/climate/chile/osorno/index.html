Osorno at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Osorno is the 3rd coolest (11.3 °C year average) and the 5th least sunny (about 2,506 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Valdivia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Osorno is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.9 °C cooler in July, with 489 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (15.9 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, July (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Osorno, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Osorno has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
