How December compares in Maipú

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Maipú. Compared with November: 2.5 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. January is 1.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Maipú in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maipú in December?
In December, Maipú averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 319 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Maipú?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Maipú’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maipú in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Maipú in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maipú get in December?
About 319 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maipú in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
