How February compares in Maipú

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Maipú. Compared with January: 0.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. March is 1.6 °C cooler than February on average.

Maipú in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maipú in February?
In February, Maipú averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Maipú?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Maipú’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maipú in February?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (114 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Maipú in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maipú get in February?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maipú in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
