Talagante at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Talagante is 31st warmest (15.6 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 2,524 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (422 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Monte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Talagante is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.4 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, July (8.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 74 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Talagante, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Talagante has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 74 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Talagante itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.664°S, 70.927°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1176-1090), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
