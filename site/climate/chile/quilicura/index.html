Quilicura at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Quilicura is the 5th warmest (16.3 °C year average). Compared with Renca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Quilicura is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.8 °C), averages 12.9 °C more than its coolest, July (9.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 93 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quilicura, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quilicura has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 93 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
