Los Vilos sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Coquimbo Region · Chile

The sea off Los Vilos averages 17.4 °C (63 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 13.0 °C (55 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Los Vilos sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Los Vilos?
In February, at about 17.4 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Los Vilos?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 13.0 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Los Vilos in August?
About 13.0 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Los Vilos?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
