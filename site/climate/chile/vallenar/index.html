Vallenar at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Vallenar is the 5th warmest (18.4 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 3,148 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Copiapó, the nearest covered town with different numbers (141 km away), Vallenar is 0.3 °C warmer in January and 1.2 °C cooler in July, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.9 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, July (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vallenar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vallenar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vallenar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.576°S, 70.759°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1125-1092), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
