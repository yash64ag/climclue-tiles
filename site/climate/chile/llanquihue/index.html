Llanquihue weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Llanquihue, Chile (Los Lagos Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Llanquihue is warmest in January, with 20.0 °C (68 °F) days and 10.9 °C nights, and coolest in July, with 10.6 °C (51 °F) days and 4.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around June (271 mm); February is driest (77 mm).

Llanquihue at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Llanquihue is 121st warmest (11.4 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,451 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (1,870 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Puerto Varas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Llanquihue is 0.3 °C warmer in January and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 255 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (15.4 °C), averages 7.8 °C more than its coolest, July (7.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Llanquihue, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Llanquihue

For mild weather, the best months to visit Llanquihue are January and February.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January and February.
  • Warmest (average temperature): January (15.4 °C), February (15.2 °C) and December (14.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (77 mm), January (92 mm) and December (97 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): December (314 h), January (309 h) and November (281 h).

Why Llanquihue has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Llanquihue itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.256°S, 73.006°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1252-1069), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
