How September compares in Puente Alto

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Puente Alto. Compared with August: 2.4 °C warmer, 65 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. October is 3.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Puente Alto in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in September?
In September, Puente Alto averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Puente Alto?
September is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in September?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in September?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
