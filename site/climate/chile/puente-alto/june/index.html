How June compares in Puente Alto

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Puente Alto. Compared with May: 3.1 °C cooler, 44 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. July is 1.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Puente Alto in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in June?
In June, Puente Alto averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Puente Alto?
June is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in June?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in June?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in June (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in June?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
