Puente Alto in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in March?
In March, Puente Alto averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Puente Alto?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Puente Alto’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in March?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in March?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
