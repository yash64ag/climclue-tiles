How December compares in Puente Alto

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Puente Alto. Compared with November: 2.9 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. January is 1.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Puente Alto in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in December?
In December, Puente Alto averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 320 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Puente Alto?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Puente Alto’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in December?
About 320 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
