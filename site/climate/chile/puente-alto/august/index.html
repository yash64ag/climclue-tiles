How August compares in Puente Alto

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Puente Alto. Compared with July: 1.6 °C warmer, 43 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. September is 2.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Puente Alto in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in August?
In August, Puente Alto averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Puente Alto?
August is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in August?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in August?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in August (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in August?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in August?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
