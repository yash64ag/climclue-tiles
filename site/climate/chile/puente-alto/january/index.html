How January compares in Puente Alto

January is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Puente Alto. Compared with December: 1.5 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C cooler than January on average.

Puente Alto in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in January?
In January, Puente Alto averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 327 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Puente Alto?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Puente Alto’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in January?
About 327 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
