Puente Alto weather in July

Average July weather in Puente Alto, Chile: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Puente Alto averages 12.0 °C (54 °F) days and 2.1 °C (36 °F) nights, with 156 mm (6.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 127 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Puente Alto

July is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Puente Alto. Compared with June: 1.1 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. August is 1.6 °C warmer than July on average.

Puente Alto in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in July?
In July, Puente Alto averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Puente Alto?
July is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in July?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in July?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in July (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in July?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
