How July compares in Puente Alto

Puente Alto in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in July?
In July, Puente Alto averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Puente Alto?
July is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in July?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in July?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in July (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in July?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
