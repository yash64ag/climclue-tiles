Puente Alto in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in April?
In April, Puente Alto averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Puente Alto?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Puente Alto’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in April?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
