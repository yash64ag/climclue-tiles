How May compares in Puente Alto

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Puente Alto. Compared with April: 4.5 °C cooler, 80 mm more precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. June is 3.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Puente Alto in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in May?
In May, Puente Alto averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Puente Alto?
May is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in May?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in May?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
