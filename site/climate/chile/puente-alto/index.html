Why Puente Alto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Puente Alto climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Puente Alto?
January, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 15.6 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Puente Alto?
July, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 13.4 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 2.1 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Puente Alto?
July, with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 701 mm (27.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Puente Alto?
January, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Puente Alto?
January, with about 327 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Puente Alto?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in July; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Puente Alto?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.
What type of climate does Puente Alto have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Puente Alto’s GeoNames location (33.612°S, 70.576°W; ClimaClue area sq-1176-1094), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 568,106 (GeoNames).
