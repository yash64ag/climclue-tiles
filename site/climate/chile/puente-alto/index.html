Puente Alto at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Puente Alto is 22nd warmest (14.3 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,566 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (701 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Pintana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Puente Alto is 0.4 °C cooler in January and 1.5 °C cooler in July, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.6 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, July (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puente Alto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puente Alto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
