How October compares in Puente Alto

Puente Alto in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puente Alto in October?
In October, Puente Alto averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Puente Alto?
October is not one of Puente Alto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puente Alto in October?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Puente Alto in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puente Alto get in October?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puente Alto in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
