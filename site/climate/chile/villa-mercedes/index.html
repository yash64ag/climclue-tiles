Villa Mercedes at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Villa Mercedes is the wettest (2,269 mm a year). Compared with Los Ángeles, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Villa Mercedes is 1.3 °C cooler in January and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 1,038 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (17.4 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, July (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Villa Mercedes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Villa Mercedes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
