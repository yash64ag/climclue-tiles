Coronel at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Coronel is the 3rd wettest (1,929 mm a year). Compared with San Pedro de la Paz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Coronel is 0.4 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 270 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (17.3 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, July (9.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Coronel, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Coronel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
