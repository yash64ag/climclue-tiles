Talca at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Talca is 20th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (884 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Curicó, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Talca is 0.6 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.8 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, July (8.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Talca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Talca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
