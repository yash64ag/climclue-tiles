Cabildo at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Cabildo is 10th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,694 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (541 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Ligua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Cabildo is 2.1 °C warmer in January and about as warm in July, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.2 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, July (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cabildo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cabildo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cabildo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.431°S, 71.072°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1164-1089), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
