Talcahuano at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Talcahuano is 27th warmest (13.1 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,649 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,285 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro de la Paz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Talcahuano is 0.7 °C cooler in January and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 374 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (16.9 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, July (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Talcahuano, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Talcahuano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
