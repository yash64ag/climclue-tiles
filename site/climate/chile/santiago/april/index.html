How April compares in Santiago

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Santiago. Compared with March: 3.8 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. May is 4.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Santiago in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago in April?
In April, Santiago averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Santiago?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Santiago’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago in April?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago get in April?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
