How September compares in Santiago

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Santiago. Compared with August: 2.3 °C warmer, 59 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. October is 3.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Santiago in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago in September?
In September, Santiago averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Santiago?
September is not one of Santiago’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago in September?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago get in September?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
