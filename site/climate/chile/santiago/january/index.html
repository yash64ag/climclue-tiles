How January compares in Santiago

January is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Santiago. Compared with December: 1.4 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C cooler than January on average.

Santiago in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago in January?
In January, Santiago averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 325 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Santiago?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Santiago’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago get in January?
About 325 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
