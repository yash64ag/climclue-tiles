Santiago at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Santiago is 11th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,538 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (603 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lo Prado, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Santiago is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.6 °C cooler in July, with 166 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.4 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, July (8.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 102 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santiago, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santiago has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 102 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Santiago climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Santiago?
January, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 29.2 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 16.9 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Santiago?
July, with an average of 8.7 °C (48 °F), day highs around 15.6 °C (60 °F) and night lows around 3.7 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Santiago?
July, with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 603 mm (23.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Santiago?
January, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Santiago?
January, with about 325 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Santiago?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in July; it usually melts within days.
