How June compares in Santiago

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santiago. Compared with May: 2.7 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. July is 0.9 °C cooler than June on average.

Santiago in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago in June?
In June, Santiago averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Santiago?
June is not one of Santiago’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago in June?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago in June?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in June (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago get in June?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
