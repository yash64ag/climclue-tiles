How March compares in Santiago

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Santiago. Compared with February: 1.6 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. April is 3.7 °C cooler than March on average.

Santiago in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago in March?
In March, Santiago averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Santiago?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Santiago’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago get in March?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
