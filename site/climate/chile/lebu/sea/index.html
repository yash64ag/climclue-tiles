Lebu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Biobio · Chile

The sea off Lebu averages 15.3 °C (60 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 12.0 °C (54 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Lebu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lebu?
In January, at about 15.3 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lebu?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 12.0 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lebu in August?
About 12.0 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lebu?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
