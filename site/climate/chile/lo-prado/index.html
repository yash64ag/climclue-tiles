Lo Prado at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Lo Prado is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,505 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Santiago, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Lo Prado is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 166 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.4 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, July (9.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 93 km from the sea.

Why Lo Prado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 93 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
