Hualpén at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Hualpén is 83rd warmest (13.2 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,649 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,426 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro de la Paz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Hualpén is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 160 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (17.4 °C), averages 7.8 °C more than its coolest, July (9.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hualpén, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hualpén has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hualpén itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.789°S, 73.102°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1207-1068), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
