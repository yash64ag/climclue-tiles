San Antonio at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, San Antonio is 71st warmest (13.7 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,565 hours of sunshine a year) and 84th wettest (623 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cartagena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), San Antonio is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (17.1 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, July (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Antonio, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Antonio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Antonio itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.595°S, 71.607°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1175-1083), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
