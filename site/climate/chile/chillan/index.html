Why Chillán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Chillán climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chillán?
January, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 27.0 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 13.1 °C (56 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chillán?
July, with an average of 8.3 °C (47 °F), day highs around 13.1 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 4.4 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chillán?
June, with 303 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,416 mm (55.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Chillán?
January, with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Chillán?
December, with about 341 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chillán?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Chillán?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Chillán’s GeoNames location (36.607°S, 72.103°W; ClimaClue area sq-1206-1078), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 150,396 (GeoNames).
