Concepción sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Biobio · Chile

The sea off Concepción averages 14.5 °C (58 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 11.9 °C (53 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Concepción sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Concepción?
In January, at about 14.5 °C (58 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Concepción?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 11.9 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Concepción in August?
About 11.9 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Concepción?
About 13.1 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Concepción nearby?
In January: Lebu (15.3 °C, 102 km).
