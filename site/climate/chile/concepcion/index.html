Concepción at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Concepción is 29th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,651 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Talcahuano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Concepción is 0.7 °C warmer in January and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 374 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (17.6 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, July (8.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Concepción, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Concepción has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
