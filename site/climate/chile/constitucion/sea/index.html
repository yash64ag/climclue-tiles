Constitución sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maule Region · Chile

The sea off Constitución averages 15.0 °C (59 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 12.3 °C (54 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Constitución sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Constitución?
In February, at about 15.0 °C (59 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Constitución?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 12.3 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Constitución in August?
About 12.3 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Constitución?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Constitución nearby?
In February: San Antonio (16.1 °C, 207 km) and El Quisco (16.2 °C, 225 km).
