Temuco at a glance

Among the 37 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Chile, Temuco is the 4th coolest (11.6 °C year average) and the 4th wettest (1,721 mm a year). Compared with Valdivia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (132 km away), Temuco is 0.3 °C warmer in January and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (16.4 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, July (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Temuco, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Temuco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
