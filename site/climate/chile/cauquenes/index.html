Cauquenes at a glance

Among the 139 towns ClimaClue covers in Chile, Cauquenes is 79th warmest (13.4 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,659 hours of sunshine a year) and 77th wettest (746 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Parral, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Cauquenes is 0.9 °C cooler in January and about as warm in July, with 530 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (19.1 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, July (8.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cauquenes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cauquenes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cauquenes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.967°S, 72.322°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1199-1076), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
