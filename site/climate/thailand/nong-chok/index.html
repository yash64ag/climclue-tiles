Nong Chok at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Nong Chok is the 5th wettest (1,662 mm a year). Compared with Khlong Sam Wa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Nong Chok is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.3 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, December (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nong Chok, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nong Chok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
