Ban Phan Don weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Ban Phan Don, Thailand (Udon Thani), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Ban Phan Don is warmest in April, with 35.5 °C (96 °F) days and 24.0 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 28.6 °C (83 °F) days and 15.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (269 mm); December is driest (4 mm).

Ban Phan Don at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Ban Phan Don is 260th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,615 hours of sunshine a year) and 173rd wettest (1,389 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ban Nong Wua So, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Ban Phan Don is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 237 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.8 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, December (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in December. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ban Phan Don, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ban Phan Don has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ban Phan Don itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.143°N, 102.973°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-668-2829), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 29,211, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
