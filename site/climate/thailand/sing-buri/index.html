Sing Buri at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Sing Buri is the 5th warmest (28.9 °C year average). Compared with Bang Racham, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Sing Buri is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, December (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 156 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sing Buri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sing Buri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sing Buri itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.888°N, 100.405°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-691-2804), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
