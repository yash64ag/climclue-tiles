Phibun Mangsahan at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Phibun Mangsahan is 228th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,623 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (1,803 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Warin Chamrap, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Phibun Mangsahan is about as warm in April and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 147 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.8 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, December (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 285 km from the sea.

Why Phibun Mangsahan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 285 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Phibun Mangsahan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.245°N, 105.229°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-687-2852), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
