Bang Khun Thian at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Bang Khun Thian is 19th warmest (28.4 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,539 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (1,392 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bang Bon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Bang Khun Thian is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.4 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, December (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in December. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bang Khun Thian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bang Khun Thian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
