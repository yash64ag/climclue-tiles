Surat Thani at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Surat Thani is the 4th least sunny (about 2,415 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (1,664 mm a year). Compared with Nakhon Si Thammarat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Surat Thani is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 577 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.9 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Surat Thani, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Surat Thani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
