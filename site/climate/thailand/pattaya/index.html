Pattaya at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Pattaya is the least sunny (about 2,391 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Si Racha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Pattaya is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, December (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pattaya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pattaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
