How April compares in Bangkok

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bangkok. Compared with March: 1.3 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella.

Bangkok in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangkok in April?
In April, Bangkok averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bangkok?
No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangkok in April?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (301 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bangkok in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangkok get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangkok in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
