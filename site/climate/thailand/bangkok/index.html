Bangkok at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Bangkok is 6th warmest (28.5 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,541 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (1,459 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bangkok Noi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Bangkok is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.8 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, December (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in December. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bangkok, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Bangkok

No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are January (26.4 °C, 13 mm), November (27.3 °C, 53 mm) and December (25.9 °C, 7 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): April (30.8 °C), May (30.1 °C) and June (29.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (7 mm), January (13 mm) and February (18 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): January (264 h), March (260 h) and December (255 h).

Why Bangkok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

April, with an average of 30.8 °C (87 °F), day highs around 34.9 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 26.6 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bangkok?
December, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.3 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 20.8 °C (69 °F).

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bangkok itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.754°N, 100.501°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-702-2805), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 5,104,476, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
