How November compares in Bangkok

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bangkok. Compared with October: 0.7 °C cooler, 202 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. December is 1.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Of the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Bangkok ranks 11th warmest (average temperature), 15th sunniest and 24th wettest in November.

Bangkok in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangkok in November?
In November, Bangkok averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bangkok?
No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangkok in November?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (306 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bangkok in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangkok get in November?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangkok in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
