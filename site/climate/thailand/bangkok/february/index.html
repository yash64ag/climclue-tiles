How February compares in Bangkok

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bangkok. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. March is 1.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Bangkok in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangkok in February?
In February, Bangkok averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bangkok?
No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangkok in February?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (306 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bangkok in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangkok get in February?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangkok in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
