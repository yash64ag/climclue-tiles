How January compares in Bangkok

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bangkok. Compared with December: 0.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Bangkok ranks 18th warmest (average temperature), 20th sunniest and 16th wettest in January.

Bangkok in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangkok in January?
In January, Bangkok averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bangkok?
No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangkok in January?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (306 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bangkok in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangkok get in January?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangkok in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
