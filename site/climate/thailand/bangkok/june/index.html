How June compares in Bangkok

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bangkok. Compared with May: 0.4 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

What to pack for Bangkok in June

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Bangkok in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangkok in June?
In June, Bangkok averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bangkok?
No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangkok in June?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (301 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bangkok in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangkok get in June?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangkok in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
