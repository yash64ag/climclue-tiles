How March compares in Bangkok

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bangkok. Compared with February: 1.5 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 1.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Bangkok in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangkok in March?
In March, Bangkok averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bangkok?
No month in Bangkok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangkok in March?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (306 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bangkok in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangkok get in March?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangkok in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
