Si Racha at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Si Racha is the 5th least sunny (about 2,465 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Chon Buri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Si Racha is 0.5 °C cooler in April and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in December. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Si Racha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Si Racha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
