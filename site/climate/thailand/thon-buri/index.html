Why Thon Buri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Thon Buri climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Thon Buri?
April, with an average of 30.8 °C (87 °F), day highs around 35.0 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 26.7 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Thon Buri?
December, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.4 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 21.0 °C (70 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Thon Buri?
September, with 294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,418 mm (55.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Thon Buri?
December, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Thon Buri?
January, with about 262 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Thon Buri?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Thon Buri?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Thon Buri’s GeoNames location (13.725°N, 100.485°E; ClimaClue area sq-702-2804), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 119,708 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
