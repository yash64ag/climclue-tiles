Samut Prakan at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Samut Prakan is 32nd warmest (28.3 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,543 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (1,384 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phra Pradaeng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Samut Prakan is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.1 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Samut Prakan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Samut Prakan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
