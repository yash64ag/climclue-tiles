Dan Khun Thot weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dan Khun Thot, Thailand (Nakhon Ratchasima), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dan Khun Thot is warmest in April, with 36.1 °C (97 °F) days and 23.6 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 29.9 °C (86 °F) days and 17.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (238 mm); December is driest (4 mm).

Dan Khun Thot at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Dan Khun Thot is 191st warmest (27.3 °C year average), 45th sunniest (about 2,661 hours of sunshine a year) and 256th wettest (1,141 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amphoe Sikhiu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Dan Khun Thot is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.8 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, December (23.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 232 km from the sea.

Why Dan Khun Thot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 232 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dan Khun Thot itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.208°N, 101.771°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-687-2817), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
