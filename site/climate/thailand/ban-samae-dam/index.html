Ban Samae Dam at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Ban Samae Dam is 36th warmest (28.2 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,541 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (1,325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bang Bon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Ban Samae Dam is 0.4 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ban Samae Dam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ban Samae Dam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
