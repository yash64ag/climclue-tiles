Sai Mai at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Sai Mai is 33rd warmest (28.3 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,501 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,561 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lak Si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sai Mai is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.6 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, December (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sai Mai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sai Mai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
