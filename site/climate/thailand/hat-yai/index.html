Hat Yai at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Hat Yai is the 2nd wettest (1,893 mm a year). Compared with Nakhon Si Thammarat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (168 km away), Hat Yai is about as warm in April and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 348 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.3 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hat Yai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hat Yai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
