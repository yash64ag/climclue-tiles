Nakhon Ratchasima at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Nakhon Ratchasima is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,674 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (1,118 mm a year). Compared with Kabin Buri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (121 km away), Nakhon Ratchasima is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 1.3 °C cooler in December, with 440 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.1 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in December. The area lies about 226 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nakhon Ratchasima, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Nakhon Ratchasima

For mild weather, the best months to visit Nakhon Ratchasima are January and December.

Why Nakhon Ratchasima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 226 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
