Khanu Woralaksaburi at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Khanu Woralaksaburi is 103rd warmest (28.3 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,660 hours of sunshine a year) and 209th wettest (1,301 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lat Yao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Khanu Woralaksaburi is about as warm in April and about as warm in December, with 106 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, December (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in December. The area lies about 236 km from the sea.

Why Khanu Woralaksaburi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 236 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-679-2798), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
