Why Kabin Buri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kabin Buri climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kabin Buri?
April, with an average of 29.6 °C (85 °F), day highs around 34.6 °C (94 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kabin Buri?
December, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 30.0 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 19.7 °C (67 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kabin Buri?
September, with 294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,558 mm (61.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kabin Buri?
December, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kabin Buri?
December, with about 294 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kabin Buri?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kabin Buri?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kabin Buri’s GeoNames location (13.951°N, 101.717°E; ClimaClue area sq-700-2817), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 140,056 (GeoNames).
