Kabin Buri at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Kabin Buri is 43rd warmest (27.5 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,595 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (1,558 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Si Maha Phot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Kabin Buri is about as warm in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 163 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.6 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, December (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in December. The area lies about 118 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kabin Buri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kabin Buri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
