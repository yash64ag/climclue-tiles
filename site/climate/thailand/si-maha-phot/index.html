Si Maha Phot at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Si Maha Phot is the 3rd wettest (1,721 mm a year). Compared with Kabin Buri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Si Maha Phot is about as warm in April and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 163 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.6 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, December (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 101 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Si Maha Phot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Si Maha Phot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 101 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
