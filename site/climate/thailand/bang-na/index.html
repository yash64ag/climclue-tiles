Bang Na at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Bang Na is 90th warmest (28.4 °C year average), 163rd sunniest (about 2,540 hours of sunshine a year) and 100th wettest (1,510 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phra Khanong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Bang Na is 0.2 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.4 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, December (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.7 °C (69 °F), in December. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bang Na, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bang Na has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bang Na itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.667°N, 100.642°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-703-2806), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
