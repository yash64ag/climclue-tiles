Warin Chamrap at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Thailand, Warin Chamrap is 235th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (1,656 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ubon Ratchathani, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Warin Chamrap is about as warm in April and about as warm in December, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.8 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, December (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 325 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Warin Chamrap has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 325 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Warin Chamrap itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.193°N, 104.863°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-688-2848), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
