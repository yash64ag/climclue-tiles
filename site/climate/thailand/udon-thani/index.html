Udon Thani at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Udon Thani is the 2nd coolest (26.6 °C year average). Compared with Khon Kaen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Udon Thani is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 1.1 °C cooler in December, with 222 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.6 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, December (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in December. The area lies about 358 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Udon Thani, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Udon Thani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 358 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
