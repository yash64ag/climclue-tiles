Nakhon Pathom at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Thailand, Nakhon Pathom is the 5th warmest (28.5 °C year average). Compared with Ban I Chang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Nakhon Pathom is about as warm in April and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.9 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in December. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nakhon Pathom, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nakhon Pathom has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
