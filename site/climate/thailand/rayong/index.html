Why Rayong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Rayong climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Rayong?
April, with an average of 28.6 °C (84 °F), day highs around 31.1 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 26.0 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Rayong?
December, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.8 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 22.5 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Rayong?
September, with 272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,446 mm (56.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Rayong?
December, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Rayong?
December, with about 263 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Rayong?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Rayong?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Rayong’s GeoNames location (12.681°N, 101.258°E; ClimaClue area sq-713-2812), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 106,737 (GeoNames).
