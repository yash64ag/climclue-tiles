Why Nakhon Si Thammarat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Nakhon Si Thammarat climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Nakhon Si Thammarat?
April, with an average of 28.3 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.4 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.0 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Nakhon Si Thammarat?
December, with an average of 25.8 °C (78 °F), day highs around 27.5 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 23.3 °C (74 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Nakhon Si Thammarat?
November, with 524 mm (20.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,241 mm (88.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Nakhon Si Thammarat?
February, with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Nakhon Si Thammarat?
March, with about 252 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Nakhon Si Thammarat?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Nakhon Si Thammarat?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Nakhon Si Thammarat’s GeoNames location (8.433°N, 99.967°E; ClimaClue area sq-755-2799), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 102,152 (GeoNames).
