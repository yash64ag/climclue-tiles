Bertoua at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Bertoua is the 3rd driest (1,440 mm a year). Compared with Yaoundé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (253 km away), Bertoua is 1.5 °C warmer in February and 1.1 °C warmer in July, with 289 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.9 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, July (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 449 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bertoua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bertoua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 449 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
