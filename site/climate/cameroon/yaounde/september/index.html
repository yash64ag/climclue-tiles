How September compares in Yaoundé

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yaoundé. Compared with August: 0.1 °C warmer, 101 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Yaoundé in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaoundé in September?
In September, Yaoundé averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yaoundé?
September is not one of Yaoundé’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaoundé in September?
245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (313 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Yaoundé in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yaoundé get in September?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaoundé in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
