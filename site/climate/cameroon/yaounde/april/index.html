How April compares in Yaoundé

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yaoundé. Compared with March: 0.4 °C cooler, 57 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Yaoundé in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaoundé in April?
In April, Yaoundé averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yaoundé?
April is not one of Yaoundé’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaoundé in April?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (303 mm), the driest January (19 mm).
Does it snow in Yaoundé in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yaoundé get in April?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaoundé in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
