How February compares in Yaoundé

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Yaoundé. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Yaoundé in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaoundé in February?
In February, Yaoundé averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yaoundé?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Yaoundé’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaoundé in February?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (303 mm), the driest January (19 mm).
Does it snow in Yaoundé in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yaoundé get in February?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaoundé in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
