Kumbo at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Kumbo is the coolest (16.7 °C year average). Compared with Foumban, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Kumbo is 5.6 °C cooler in March and 5.2 °C cooler in August, with 906 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (18.8 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, August (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 282 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kumbo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kumbo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 282 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
