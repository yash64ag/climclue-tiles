Buea sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South-West · Cameroon

The sea off Buea averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Buea sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Buea?
In April, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Buea?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.2 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Buea in August?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Buea?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Buea nearby?
In April: Tiko (29.3 °C, 16 km), Bamusso (29.0 °C, 51 km) and Kribi (29.2 °C, 154 km).
