Ébolowa at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Ébolowa is the least sunny (about 1,516 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Yaoundé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (115 km away), Ébolowa is 0.5 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 162 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.9 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, August (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ébolowa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ébolowa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
