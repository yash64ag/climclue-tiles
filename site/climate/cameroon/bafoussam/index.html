Bafoussam at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Bafoussam is the 3rd coolest (20.7 °C year average). Compared with Foumban, the nearest covered town with different numbers (60 km away), Bafoussam is 1.5 °C cooler in February and 1.1 °C cooler in August, with 172 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.1 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, August (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in December. The area lies about 209 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bafoussam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bafoussam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 209 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
