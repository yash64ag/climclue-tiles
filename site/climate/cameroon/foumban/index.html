Foumban at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Foumban is 11th warmest (21.9 °C year average), 4th sunniest (about 2,317 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (2,089 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kumbo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Foumban is 6.0 °C warmer in February and 5.1 °C warmer in July, with 906 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.6 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, July (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 262 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Foumban, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Foumban has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 262 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
