Limbe at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Limbe is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,582 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (4,286 mm a year). Compared with Buea, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Limbe is 3.5 °C warmer in March and 3.5 °C warmer in August, with 517 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.9 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, August (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Limbe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Limbe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
