Ngaoundéré at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Ngaoundéré is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,459 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bertoua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (306 km away), Ngaoundéré is 0.9 °C cooler in March and 3.0 °C cooler in August, with 378 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.0 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, August (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 598 km from the sea.

Why Ngaoundéré has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 598 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
