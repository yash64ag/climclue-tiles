Edéa at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Edéa is 4th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 1,715 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (2,761 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Douala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Edéa is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in August, with 434 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, August (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Edéa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Edéa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
