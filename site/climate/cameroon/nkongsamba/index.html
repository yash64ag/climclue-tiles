Nkongsamba at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Nkongsamba is the 3rd wettest (3,694 mm a year). Compared with Kumba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Nkongsamba is 3.8 °C cooler in February and 3.1 °C cooler in August, with 567 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.6 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, August (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nkongsamba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nkongsamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
