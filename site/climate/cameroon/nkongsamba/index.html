Nkongsamba at a glance

Among the 87 towns ClimaClue covers in Cameroon, Nkongsamba is 70th warmest (21.7 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 1,816 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (3,839 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manjo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Nkongsamba is 1.7 °C cooler in February and 1.5 °C cooler in August, with 605 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.9 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, August (20.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nkongsamba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nkongsamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nkongsamba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.955°N, 9.940°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-790-1899), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
