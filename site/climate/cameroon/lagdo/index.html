Lagdo at a glance

Among the 87 towns ClimaClue covers in Cameroon, Lagdo is 7th warmest (28.4 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,846 hours of sunshine a year) and 73rd wettest (1,123 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Garoua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Lagdo is 0.4 °C cooler in April and about as warm in January, with 126 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.8 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 736 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lagdo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lagdo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 736 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lagdo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.058°N, 13.666°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-749-1936), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
