Penja at a glance

Among the 87 towns ClimaClue covers in Cameroon, Penja is 32nd warmest (25.1 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 1,733 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (2,997 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Njombé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Penja is 0.5 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 350 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, August (23.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.7 °C (69 °F), in September. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Penja, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Penja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Penja itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.639°N, 9.680°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-793-1896), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
