How November compares in Douala

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Douala. Compared with October: 0.6 °C warmer, 226 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. December is 0.4 °C warmer than November on average.

Douala in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in November?
In November, Douala averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in November?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (589 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in November?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
