Douala weather in May

Average May weather in Douala, Cameroon: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Douala averages 30.5 °C (87 °F) days and 22.4 °C (72 °F) nights, with 274 mm (10.8 in) of rain over the month and about 173 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Douala

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Douala. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 44 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Douala in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in May?
In May, Douala averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in May?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (603 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in May?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Douala in May?
About 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Douala in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
