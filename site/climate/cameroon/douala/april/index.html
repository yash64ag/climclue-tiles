How April compares in Douala

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Douala. Compared with March: 0.2 °C cooler, 88 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Douala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in April?
In April, Douala averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 236 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in April?
236 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (589 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in April?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
