How August compares in Douala

August is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Douala. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 59 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Douala in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in August?
In August, Douala averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 589 mm (23.2 in) of precipitation and about 57 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in August?
589 mm (23.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (589 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in August?
About 57 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
