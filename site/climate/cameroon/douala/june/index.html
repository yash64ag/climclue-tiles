How June compares in Douala

June is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Douala. Compared with May: 0.9 °C cooler, 59 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Douala in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in June?
In June, Douala averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 333 mm (13.1 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in June?
333 mm (13.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (603 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in June?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
