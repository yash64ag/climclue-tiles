How October compares in Douala

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Douala. Compared with September: 0.4 °C warmer, 118 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. November is 0.6 °C warmer than October on average.

Douala in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in October?
In October, Douala averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 360 mm (14.2 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in October?
360 mm (14.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (589 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in October?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
