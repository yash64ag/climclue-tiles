Douala at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cameroon, Douala is the 3rd warmest (25.9 °C year average). Compared with Buea, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Douala is 4.7 °C warmer in February and 4.6 °C warmer in August, with 1,608 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.1 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Douala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Douala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
