How December compares in Douala

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Douala. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 106 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Douala in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in December?
In December, Douala averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in December?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (589 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in December?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
