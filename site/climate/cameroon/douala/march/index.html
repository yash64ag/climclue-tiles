How March compares in Douala

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Douala. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 100 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. April is 0.2 °C cooler than March on average.

Douala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Douala in March?
In March, Douala averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Douala?
No month in Douala is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Douala in March?
148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (589 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Douala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Douala get in March?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Douala in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
