Wum at a glance

Among the 87 towns ClimaClue covers in Cameroon, Wum is 76th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,052 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (3,816 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fundong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Wum is 2.7 °C warmer in March and 2.6 °C warmer in August, with 791 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (22.6 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, August (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 250 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wum, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wum has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 250 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Wum itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.383°N, 10.067°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-776-1900), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
