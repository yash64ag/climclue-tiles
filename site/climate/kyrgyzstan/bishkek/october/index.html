How October compares in Bishkek

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bishkek. Compared with September: 7.4 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 69 hours less sunshine. November is 6.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Bishkek in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in October?
In October, Bishkek averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bishkek?
October is not one of Bishkek’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in October?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (81 mm), the driest August (23 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in October?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
