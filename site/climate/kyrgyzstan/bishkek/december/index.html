How December compares in Bishkek

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bishkek. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Bishkek in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in December?
In December, Bishkek averages a day high of 3.6 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bishkek?
December is not one of Bishkek’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in December?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (85 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in December?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
