How March compares in Bishkek

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bishkek. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 7.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Bishkek in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in March?
In March, Bishkek averages a day high of 8.8 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bishkek?
March is not one of Bishkek’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in March?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (85 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 50 cm (19.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 60 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in March?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
