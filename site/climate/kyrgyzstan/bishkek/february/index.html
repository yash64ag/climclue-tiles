How February compares in Bishkek

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bishkek. Compared with January: 1.7 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Bishkek in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in February?
In February, Bishkek averages a day high of 3.6 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bishkek?
February is not one of Bishkek’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in February?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (85 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 51 cm (19.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 62 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in February?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
