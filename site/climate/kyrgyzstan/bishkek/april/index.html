How April compares in Bishkek

Bishkek in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in April?
In April, Bishkek averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bishkek?
April is not one of Bishkek’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in April?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (85 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.7 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 14 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in April?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
