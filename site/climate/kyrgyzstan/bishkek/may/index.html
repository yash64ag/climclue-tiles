Bishkek weather in May

May · Kyrgyzstan

Average May weather in Bishkek: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How May compares in Bishkek

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bishkek. Compared with April: 4.9 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. June is 5.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Bishkek in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in May?
In May, Bishkek averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bishkek?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Bishkek’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in May?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (81 mm), the driest August (23 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in May?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
