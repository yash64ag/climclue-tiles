Bishkek in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in May?
In May, Bishkek averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bishkek?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Bishkek’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in May?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (85 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in May?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella, waterproof boots and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
