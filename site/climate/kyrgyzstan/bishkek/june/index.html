Bishkek in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bishkek in June?
In June, Bishkek averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 310 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bishkek?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Bishkek’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bishkek in June?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (85 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Bishkek in June?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in June (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bishkek get in June?
About 310 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bishkek in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
