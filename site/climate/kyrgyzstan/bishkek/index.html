Bishkek at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kyrgyzstan, Bishkek is the 3rd warmest (10.8 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,556 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (560 mm a year). Compared with Manas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (252 km away), Bishkek is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 144 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,657 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 129% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bishkek has this climate
