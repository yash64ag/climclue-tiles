Kochkor-Ata at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Kyrgyzstan, Kochkor-Ata is the warmest (14.8 °C year average). Compared with Massy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Kochkor-Ata is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 116 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.5 °C), averages 28.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,491 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kochkor-Ata, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kochkor-Ata

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kochkor-Ata are April, May and September.

Why Kochkor-Ata has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,491 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kochkor-Ata itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.037°N, 72.483°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-429-2524), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
