Lebedinovka weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Lebedinovka, Kyrgyzstan (Chuy Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Lebedinovka is warmest in July, with 31.5 °C (89 °F) days and 17.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.2 °C (38 °F) days and -8.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around May (77 mm); August is driest (22 mm).

Lebedinovka at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Kyrgyzstan, Lebedinovka is 13th warmest (11.3 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,554 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (532 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bishkek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Lebedinovka is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,665 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lebedinovka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.885°N, 74.678°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-411-2546), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
