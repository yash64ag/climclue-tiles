Osh at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kyrgyzstan, Osh is the 2nd warmest (12.4 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,537 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (420 mm a year). Compared with Manas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Osh is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 283 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,523 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Osh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Osh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,523 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
