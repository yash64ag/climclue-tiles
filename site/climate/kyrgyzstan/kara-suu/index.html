Kara Suu at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Kyrgyzstan, Kara Suu is 6th warmest (13.2 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,540 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (399 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Osh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Kara Suu is 1.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,528 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kara Suu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,528 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kara Suu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.705°N, 72.867°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-432-2528), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
