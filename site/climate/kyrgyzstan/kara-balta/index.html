Kara-Balta at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Kyrgyzstan, Kara-Balta is 23rd warmest (10.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,585 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (536 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Belovodskoye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Kara-Balta is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 27.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,599 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kara-Balta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kara-Balta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.814°N, 73.848°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-411-2538), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
