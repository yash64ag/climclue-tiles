Balykchy at a glance

Among the 32 towns ClimaClue covers in Kyrgyzstan, Balykchy is the 5th coolest (7.9 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,536 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (250 mm a year). Compared with Tokmok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Balykchy is 4.4 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 282 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,788 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Balykchy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,788 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Balykchy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.460°N, 76.187°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-415-2561), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
