Campbell River sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · British Columbia · Canada

The sea off Campbell River averages 16.1 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.8 °C (44 °F) in January. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Campbell River sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Campbell River?
In August, at about 16.1 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Campbell River?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is January, at 6.8 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Campbell River in January?
About 6.8 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Campbell River?
About 10.8 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
