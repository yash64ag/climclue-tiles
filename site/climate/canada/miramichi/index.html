Miramichi weather by month and climate

Miramichi at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Miramichi is 238th warmest (5.3 °C year average), 170th sunniest (about 1,939 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (1,072 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lutes Mountain, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Miramichi is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 336 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Why Miramichi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.5 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Miramichi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.029°N, 65.502°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-369-1144), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
