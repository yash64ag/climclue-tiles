How December compares in Toronto

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Toronto. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 3.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Toronto in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toronto in December?
In December, Toronto averages a day high of 2.8 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -2.1 °C (28 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Toronto?
December is not one of Toronto’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toronto in December?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest March (56 mm).
Does it snow in Toronto in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 32 cm (12.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Toronto get in December?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toronto in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
