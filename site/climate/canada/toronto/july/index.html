How July compares in Toronto

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Toronto. Compared with June: 4.4 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Toronto in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toronto in July?
In July, Toronto averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Toronto?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Toronto’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toronto in July?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest March (56 mm).
Does it snow in Toronto in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Toronto get in July?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toronto in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella, breathable fabrics (humid air) and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
