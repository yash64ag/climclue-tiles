Trois-Rivières at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Trois-Rivières is 60th warmest (6.0 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 1,763 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (1,084 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Terrebonne, the nearest covered town with different numbers (111 km away), Trois-Rivières is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.8 °C), averages 31.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Why Trois-Rivières has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
