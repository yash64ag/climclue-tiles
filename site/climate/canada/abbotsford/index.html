Abbotsford at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Abbotsford is the 5th least sunny (about 1,706 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Chilliwack, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Abbotsford is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in December, with 62 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.4 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, December (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Why Abbotsford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
