Why Abbotsford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Abbotsford climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Abbotsford?
August, with an average of 18.4 °C (65 °F), day highs around 24.6 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 13.7 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Abbotsford?
December, with an average of 2.5 °C (36 °F), day highs around 4.7 °C (40 °F) and night lows around -0.5 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Abbotsford?
November, with 266 mm (10.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,787 mm (70.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Abbotsford?
July, with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Abbotsford?
July, with about 265 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Abbotsford?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 265 cm (104.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Abbotsford?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Abbotsford have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.3 × 11 km, that contains Abbotsford’s GeoNames location (49.058°N, 122.253°W; ClimaClue area sq-349-577), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 141,397 (GeoNames).
