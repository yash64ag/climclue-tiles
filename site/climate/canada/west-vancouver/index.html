West Vancouver at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, West Vancouver is the 4th warmest (10.9 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (2,452 mm a year). Compared with North Vancouver, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), West Vancouver is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C warmer in December, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.4 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, December (4.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Best time to visit West Vancouver

For mild weather, the best months to visit West Vancouver are July and August.

Why West Vancouver has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C. The 10 km area around West Vancouver, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 8.4 °C a year, 2.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

West Vancouver climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in West Vancouver?
August, with an average of 18.4 °C (65 °F), day highs around 22.9 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 13.9 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in West Vancouver?
December, with an average of 4.1 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.7 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 1.5 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in West Vancouver?
November, with 384 mm (15.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,452 mm (96.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in West Vancouver?
July, with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in West Vancouver?
July, with about 255 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in West Vancouver?
Yes. About 19 cm (7.3 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for West Vancouver itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.329°N, 123.160°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-346-568), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
