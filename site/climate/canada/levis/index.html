Lévis at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Lévis is 62nd warmest (5.1 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 1,808 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,253 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Québec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Lévis is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 31.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 108% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Lévis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
