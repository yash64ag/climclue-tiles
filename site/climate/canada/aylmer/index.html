Aylmer at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Aylmer is 201st warmest (6.5 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 1,993 hours of sunshine a year) and 190th wettest (966 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Le Plateau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Aylmer is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.8 °C), averages 30.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 438 km from the sea.

Why Aylmer has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 438 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.8 °C.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-385-1041), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
