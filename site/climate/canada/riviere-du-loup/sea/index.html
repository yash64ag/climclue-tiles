Rivière-du-Loup sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Quebec · Canada

The sea off Rivière-du-Loup averages 10.8 °C (51 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.6 °C (29 °F) in January. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Rivière-du-Loup sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rivière-du-Loup?
In August, at about 10.8 °C (51 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rivière-du-Loup?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is January, at -1.6 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rivière-du-Loup in January?
About -1.6 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rivière-du-Loup?
About 3.2 °C (38 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rivière-du-Loup nearby?
In August: Baie-Comeau (11.4 °C, 186 km), Sept-Îles (13.1 °C, 350 km) and Saint John (13.1 °C, 390 km).
