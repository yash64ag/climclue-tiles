Waterloo at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Waterloo is 35th warmest (7.4 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 1,901 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (966 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cambridge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Waterloo is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 584 km from the sea.

Why Waterloo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 584 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
