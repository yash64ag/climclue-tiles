How August compares in Winnipeg

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Winnipeg. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. September is 5.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Winnipeg in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in August?
In August, Winnipeg averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Winnipeg?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Winnipeg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in August?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in August?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
