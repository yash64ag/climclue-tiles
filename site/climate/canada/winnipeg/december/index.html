How December compares in Winnipeg

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Winnipeg. Compared with November: 8.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 3.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Winnipeg among towns in Canada in December

Of the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Winnipeg ranks 72nd warmest (average temperature), 2nd sunniest and 67th wettest in December.

Winnipeg in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in December?
In December, Winnipeg averages a day high of -8.6 °C (17 °F) and a night low of -16.2 °C (3 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 92 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Winnipeg?
December is not one of Winnipeg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in December?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (8.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in December?
About 92 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
