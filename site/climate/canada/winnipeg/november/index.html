How November compares in Winnipeg

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Winnipeg. Compared with October: 9.7 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. December is 8.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Winnipeg in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in November?
In November, Winnipeg averages a day high of 0.0 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -6.9 °C (20 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Winnipeg?
November is not one of Winnipeg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in November?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
