How June compares in Winnipeg

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Winnipeg. Compared with May: 5.3 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Winnipeg in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in June?
In June, Winnipeg averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Winnipeg?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Winnipeg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in June?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in June?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
