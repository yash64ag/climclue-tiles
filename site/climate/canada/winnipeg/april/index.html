How April compares in Winnipeg

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Winnipeg. Compared with March: 10.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. May is 7.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Winnipeg in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in April?
In April, Winnipeg averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Winnipeg?
April is not one of Winnipeg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in April?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in April?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
