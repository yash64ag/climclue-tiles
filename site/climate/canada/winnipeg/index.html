Winnipeg at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Winnipeg is the 3rd coolest (3.3 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 2,280 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Regina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (536 km away), Winnipeg is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 2.7 °C cooler in January, with 159 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 36.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 871 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Winnipeg, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Winnipeg

For mild weather, the best months to visit Winnipeg are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (19.8 °C), August (18.9 °C) and June (17.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (17 mm), January (21 mm) and December (24 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (313 h), June (283 h) and August (276 h).

Why Winnipeg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 871 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 36.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

January, with an average of -16.3 °C (3 °F), day highs around -11.1 °C (12 °F) and night lows around -20.1 °C (-4 °F).
