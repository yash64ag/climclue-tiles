How February compares in Winnipeg

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Winnipeg. Compared with January: 3.0 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. March is 7.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Winnipeg in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in February?
In February, Winnipeg averages a day high of -7.6 °C (18 °F) and a night low of -17.0 °C (1 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Winnipeg?
February is not one of Winnipeg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in February?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.6 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 17 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in February?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
