How October compares in Winnipeg

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Winnipeg. Compared with September: 7.9 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. November is 9.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Winnipeg in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Winnipeg in October?
In October, Winnipeg averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Winnipeg?
October is not one of Winnipeg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Winnipeg in October?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (95 mm), the driest February (17 mm).
Does it snow in Winnipeg in October?
Yes, on average: about 6.8 cm (2.7 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Winnipeg get in October?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Winnipeg in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
