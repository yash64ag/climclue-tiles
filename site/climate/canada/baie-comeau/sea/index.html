Baie-Comeau sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Quebec · Canada

The sea off Baie-Comeau averages 11.8 °C (53 °F) in July, its warmest month, and -1.6 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Baie-Comeau sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Baie-Comeau?
In July, at about 11.8 °C (53 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Baie-Comeau?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.6 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Baie-Comeau in February?
About -1.6 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Baie-Comeau?
About 3.9 °C (39 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Baie-Comeau nearby?
In July: Sept-Îles (12.7 °C, 168 km), Charlottetown (16.1 °C, 501 km) and New Glasgow (16.2 °C, 578 km).
