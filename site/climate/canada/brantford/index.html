Brantford at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Brantford is 21st warmest (8.5 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 1,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (891 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cambridge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Brantford is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 543 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Brantford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 543 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
