Montréal weather in July

Average July weather in Montréal, Canada: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Montréal averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) days and 15.8 °C (60 °F) nights, with 113 mm (4.4 in) of rain over the month and about 264 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Montréal

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Montréal. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. August is 1.3 °C cooler than July on average.

What to pack for Montréal in July

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses.

Rules used: day high under 10 °C (50 °F) → warm coat; 10–18 °C → jacket or sweater; 18–25 °C → light layers; 25 °C (77 °F) or more → light, breathable clothes; night low under 0 °C → hat and gloves; night low 10 °C or more below the day high → an extra layer for evenings; 100 mm or more of precipitation → rain jacket or umbrella; 50–100 mm → compact umbrella; 5 cm or more of fresh snow, or snow on the ground → waterproof boots; dew point 16 °C (61 °F) or more → breathable fabrics for humid air; 250 hours of sunshine or more → sunglasses. Montréal in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in July?
In July, Montréal averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Montréal?
No month in Montréal is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in July?
113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (74 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in July?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
