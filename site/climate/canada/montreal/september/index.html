How September compares in Montréal

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Montréal. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. October is 6.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Montréal in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in September?
In September, Montréal averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Montréal?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Montréal’s mild months (September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in September?
98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (106 mm), the driest February (68 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in September?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
