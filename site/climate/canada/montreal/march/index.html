How March compares in Montréal

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Montréal. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 8.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Montréal in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in March?
In March, Montréal averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -6.4 °C (20 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Montréal?
March is not one of Montréal’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in March?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (106 mm), the driest February (68 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 36 cm (14.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in March?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
