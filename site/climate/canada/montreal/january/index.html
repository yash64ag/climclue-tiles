How January compares in Montréal

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Montréal. Compared with December: 4.3 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Montréal in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in January?
In January, Montréal averages a day high of -4.9 °C (23 °F) and a night low of -14.2 °C (6 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Montréal?
January is not one of Montréal’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in January?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (106 mm), the driest February (68 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 46 cm (18.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 28 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in January?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
