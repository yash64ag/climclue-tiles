Montréal weather in April

April · Quebec · Canada

How April compares in Montréal

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Montréal. Compared with March: 8.3 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. May is 7.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Montréal in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in April?
In April, Montréal averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Montréal?
No month in Montréal is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in April?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (74 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in April?
Yes, on average: about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in April?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
