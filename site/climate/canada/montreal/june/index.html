How June compares in Montréal

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Montréal. Compared with May: 5.3 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Montréal in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in June?
In June, Montréal averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Montréal?
June is not one of Montréal’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (106 mm), the driest February (68 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in June?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
