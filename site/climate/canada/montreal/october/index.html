How October compares in Montréal

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Montréal. Compared with September: 7.4 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. November is 6.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Montréal in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Montréal in October?
In October, Montréal averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Montréal?
No month in Montréal is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Montréal in October?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (116 mm), the driest February (74 mm).
Does it snow in Montréal in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Montréal get in October?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Montréal in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
