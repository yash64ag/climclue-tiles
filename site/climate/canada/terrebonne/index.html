Why Terrebonne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Terrebonne climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Terrebonne?
July, with an average of 21.1 °C (70 °F), day highs around 25.6 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 16.6 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Terrebonne?
January, with an average of -10.3 °C (13 °F), day highs around -5.3 °C (22 °F) and night lows around -14.9 °C (5 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Terrebonne?
June, with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,076 mm (42.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Terrebonne?
February, with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Terrebonne?
July, with about 264 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Terrebonne?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 235 cm (92.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Terrebonne?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.8 × 11 km, that contains Terrebonne’s GeoNames location (45.700°N, 73.647°W; ClimaClue area sq-382-1063), averaged over the area’s land.
