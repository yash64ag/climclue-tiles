Gatineau at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Gatineau is 55th warmest (6.5 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,001 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (983 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ottawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Gatineau is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 431 km from the sea.

Why Gatineau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 431 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
