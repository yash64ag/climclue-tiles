Langley at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Langley is the 5th warmest (10.4 °C year average), the 4th least sunny (about 1,682 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,998 mm a year). Compared with Surrey, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Langley is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 172 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.4 °C), averages 15.4 °C more than its coolest, December (3.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 0.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Why Langley has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
