Riley Park at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Riley Park is 23rd warmest (10.5 °C year average), 245th sunniest (about 1,807 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (2,068 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vancouver, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Riley Park is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.1 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, December (3.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Riley Park has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Riley Park itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.243°N, 123.093°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-347-569), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
