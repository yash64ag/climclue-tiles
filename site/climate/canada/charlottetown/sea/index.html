Charlottetown sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Prince Edward Island · Canada

The sea off Charlottetown averages 17.6 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.6 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Charlottetown sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Charlottetown?
In August, at about 17.6 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Charlottetown?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.6 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Charlottetown in February?
About -1.6 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Charlottetown?
About 6.3 °C (43 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Charlottetown nearby?
In August: New Glasgow (18.2 °C, 81 km), Sydney (18.4 °C, 227 km) and Glace Bay (18.4 °C, 244 km).
