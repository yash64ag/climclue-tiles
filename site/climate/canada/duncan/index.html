Duncan at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Duncan is the 2nd warmest (10.9 °C year average). Compared with North Cowichan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Duncan is 0.4 °C warmer in August and about as warm in December, with 90 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.2 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, December (3.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 93% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Duncan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Duncan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.783°N, 123.703°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-352-562), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
