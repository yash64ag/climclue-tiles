Vancouver at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Vancouver is the 3rd warmest (10.6 °C year average) and the 4th wettest (1,993 mm a year). Compared with Richmond, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Vancouver is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in December, with 459 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (17.9 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, December (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 129% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Vancouver has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Vancouver climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Vancouver?
August, with an average of 17.9 °C (64 °F), day highs around 21.8 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 15.1 °C (59 °F).
What is the coldest month in Vancouver?
December, with an average of 4.3 °C (40 °F), day highs around 5.6 °C (42 °F) and night lows around 2.4 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Vancouver?
November, with 313 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,993 mm (78.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Vancouver?
July, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Vancouver?
July, with about 279 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Vancouver?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 34 cm (13.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
