Vancouver at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Vancouver is 14th warmest (10.6 °C year average), 245th sunniest (about 1,807 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (2,071 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Riley Park, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Vancouver is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.0 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, December (3.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 5 km from Vancouver measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (yearly rain and snow: these numbers have 134% of the station’s). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care. Best time to visit Vancouver

For mild weather, the best months to visit Vancouver are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (18.0 °C), July (17.8 °C) and June (15.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (55 mm), August (62 mm) and June (90 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (279 h), August (251 h) and June (227 h).

Why Vancouver has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Vancouver climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Vancouver?
August, with an average of 18.0 °C (64 °F), day highs around 22.5 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 13.5 °C (56 °F).
What is the coldest month in Vancouver?
December, with an average of 3.8 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.4 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 1.3 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Vancouver?
November, with 325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,071 mm (81.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Vancouver?
July, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Vancouver?
July, with about 279 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Vancouver?
Yes. About 29 cm (11.4 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time to visit Vancouver?
For mild weather (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September. Climate only, from 1981–2010 averages: crowds and prices are not considered.
How warm is the sea in Vancouver?
The nearby open sea averages 16.1 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.5 °C (44 °F) in January. It stays below 20 °C (68 °F) all year. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vancouver itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.250°N, 123.119°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-347-568), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
