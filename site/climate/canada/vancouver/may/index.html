How May compares in Vancouver

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Vancouver. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 43 mm less precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Vancouver in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vancouver in May?
In May, Vancouver averages a day high of 15.8 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Vancouver?
May is not one of Vancouver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vancouver in May?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (313 mm), the driest July (53 mm).
Does it snow in Vancouver in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vancouver get in May?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vancouver in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
