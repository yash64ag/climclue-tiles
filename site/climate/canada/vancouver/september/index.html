How September compares in Vancouver

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Vancouver. Compared with August: 2.7 °C cooler, 33 mm more precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 4.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Vancouver in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vancouver in September?
In September, Vancouver averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Vancouver?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Vancouver’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vancouver in September?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (325 mm), the driest July (55 mm).
Does it snow in Vancouver in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vancouver get in September?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Vancouver in September?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Vancouver in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
