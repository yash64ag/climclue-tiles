How February compares in Vancouver

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Vancouver. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 91 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. March is 1.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Vancouver in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vancouver in February?
In February, Vancouver averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Vancouver?
February is not one of Vancouver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vancouver in February?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (313 mm), the driest July (53 mm).
Does it snow in Vancouver in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vancouver get in February?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vancouver in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
