How March compares in Vancouver

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Vancouver. Compared with February: 1.7 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 2.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Vancouver in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vancouver in March?
In March, Vancouver averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Vancouver?
March is not one of Vancouver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vancouver in March?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (313 mm), the driest July (53 mm).
Does it snow in Vancouver in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vancouver get in March?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vancouver in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
