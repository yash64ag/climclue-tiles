How August compares in Vancouver

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Vancouver. Compared with July: 0.2 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. September is 2.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Vancouver in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vancouver in August?
In August, Vancouver averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Vancouver?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Vancouver’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vancouver in August?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (313 mm), the driest July (53 mm).
Does it snow in Vancouver in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vancouver get in August?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vancouver in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
