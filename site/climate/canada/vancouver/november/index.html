How November compares in Vancouver

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Vancouver. Compared with October: 4.3 °C cooler, 93 mm more precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. December is 2.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Vancouver in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vancouver in November?
In November, Vancouver averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation and about 62 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Vancouver?
November is not one of Vancouver’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vancouver in November?
325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (325 mm), the driest July (55 mm).
Does it snow in Vancouver in November?
Yes, on average: about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Vancouver get in November?
About 62 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Vancouver in November?
About 8.5 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Vancouver in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
