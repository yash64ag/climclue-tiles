Kingsville at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Kingsville is 44th warmest (9.9 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 2,061 hours of sunshine a year) and 171st wettest (996 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Leamington, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Kingsville is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 37 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 27.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 628 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kingsville has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kingsville itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.100°N, 82.733°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-418-972), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
