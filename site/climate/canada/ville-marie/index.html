Ville-Marie at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Ville-Marie is 38th warmest (7.0 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 1,966 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (1,081 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Montréal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Ville-Marie is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 30.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 285 km from the sea.

Why Ville-Marie has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 285 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.9 °C.
