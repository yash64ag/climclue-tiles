How January compares in Québec

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Québec. Compared with December: 4.3 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Québec in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Québec in January?
In January, Québec averages a day high of -7.1 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -16.3 °C (3 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Québec?
No month in Québec is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Québec in January?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (77 mm).
Does it snow in Québec in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 65 cm (25.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 60 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Québec get in January?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Québec in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
