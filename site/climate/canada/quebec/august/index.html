How August compares in Québec

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Québec. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. September is 4.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Québec in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Québec in August?
In August, Québec averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Québec?
No month in Québec is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Québec in August?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest March (73 mm).
Does it snow in Québec in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Québec get in August?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Québec in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
