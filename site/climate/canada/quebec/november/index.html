How November compares in Québec

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Québec. Compared with October: 6.7 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. December is 7.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Québec in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Québec in November?
In November, Québec averages a day high of 3.7 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -3.1 °C (26 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Québec?
No month in Québec is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Québec in November?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest March (77 mm).
Does it snow in Québec in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 41 cm (16.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Québec get in November?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Québec in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
