How September compares in Québec

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Québec. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. October is 6.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Québec in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Québec in September?
In September, Québec averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 116 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Québec?
No month in Québec is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Québec in September?
116 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (120 mm), the driest March (73 mm).
Does it snow in Québec in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Québec get in September?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Québec in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
