How March compares in Hamilton

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hamilton. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. April is 6.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Hamilton in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in March?
In March, Hamilton averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.6 °C (26 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hamilton?
March is not one of Hamilton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in March?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (98 mm), the driest February (67 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in March?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
