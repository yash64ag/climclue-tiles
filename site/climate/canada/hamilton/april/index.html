How April compares in Hamilton

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Hamilton. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. May is 6.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Hamilton in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in April?
In April, Hamilton averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hamilton?
April is not one of Hamilton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in April?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in April?
Yes, on average: about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in April?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
