Hamilton weather in October

Average October weather in Hamilton, Canada: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Hamilton averages 15.2 °C (59 °F) days and 6.3 °C (43 °F) nights, with 77 mm (3.0 in) of rain over the month and about 141 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Hamilton

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hamilton. Compared with September: 6.6 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Hamilton in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in October?
In October, Hamilton averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hamilton?
October is not one of Hamilton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in October?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in October?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
