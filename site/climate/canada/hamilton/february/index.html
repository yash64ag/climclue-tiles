How February compares in Hamilton

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hamilton. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. March is 4.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Hamilton in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in February?
In February, Hamilton averages a day high of 0.6 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -7.6 °C (18 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hamilton?
February is not one of Hamilton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in February?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (98 mm), the driest February (67 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 31 cm (12.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in February?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
