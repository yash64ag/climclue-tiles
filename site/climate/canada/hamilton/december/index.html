How December compares in Hamilton

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hamilton. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 3.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Hamilton in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in December?
In December, Hamilton averages a day high of 2.4 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -4.3 °C (24 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 67 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hamilton?
December is not one of Hamilton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in December?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 29 cm (11.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in December?
About 67 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
