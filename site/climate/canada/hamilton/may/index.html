How May compares in Hamilton

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hamilton. Compared with April: 6.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. June is 5.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Hamilton in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in May?
In May, Hamilton averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hamilton?
May is not one of Hamilton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in May?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (66 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in May?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
