How September compares in Hamilton

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Hamilton. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 75 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Hamilton in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamilton in September?
In September, Hamilton averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hamilton?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Hamilton’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamilton in September?
93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (98 mm), the driest February (67 mm).
Does it snow in Hamilton in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamilton get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamilton in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
