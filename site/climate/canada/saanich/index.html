Saanich at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Saanich is 9th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 1,936 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (1,060 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Victoria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Saanich is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C cooler in December, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (16.1 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, December (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 3.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 7 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Saanich has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
