Why Saanich has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Saanich?
December, with an average of 5.3 °C (41 °F), day highs around 6.4 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 3.6 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Saanich?

What is the driest month in Saanich?

What is the sunniest month in Saanich?
July, with about 301 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Saanich?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 37 cm (14.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Saanich?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.4 × 11 km, that contains Saanich’s GeoNames location (48.550°N, 123.369°W; ClimaClue area sq-354-566), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,735 (GeoNames).
