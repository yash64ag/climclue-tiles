Brampton at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Brampton is 26th warmest (8.2 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,007 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (903 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mississauga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Brampton is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.3 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 565 km from the sea.

Why Brampton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 565 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
