How August compares in Brampton

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Brampton. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Brampton in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brampton in August?
In August, Brampton averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Brampton?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Brampton’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brampton in August?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (89 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in Brampton in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brampton get in August?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brampton in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
