How March compares in Brampton

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Brampton. Compared with February: 4.4 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. April is 6.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Brampton in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brampton in March?
In March, Brampton averages a day high of 5.0 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Brampton?
March is not one of Brampton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brampton in March?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (91 mm), the driest March (60 mm).
Does it snow in Brampton in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Brampton get in March?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brampton in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
