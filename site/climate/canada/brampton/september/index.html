How September compares in Brampton

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Brampton. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 6.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Brampton in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brampton in September?
In September, Brampton averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Brampton?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Brampton’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brampton in September?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (89 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in Brampton in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brampton get in September?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brampton in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
