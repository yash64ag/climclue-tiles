How May compares in Brampton

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Brampton. Compared with April: 6.2 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. June is 5.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Brampton in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brampton in May?
In May, Brampton averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Brampton?
May is not one of Brampton’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brampton in May?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (89 mm), the driest March (58 mm).
Does it snow in Brampton in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brampton get in May?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brampton in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
