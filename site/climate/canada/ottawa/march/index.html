How March compares in Ottawa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ottawa. Compared with February: 5.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 8.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Ottawa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ottawa in March?
In March, Ottawa averages a day high of 3.4 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -6.9 °C (20 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ottawa?
March is not one of Ottawa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ottawa in March?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (95 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Ottawa in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 37 cm (14.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 32 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ottawa get in March?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ottawa in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
