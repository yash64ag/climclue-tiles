How November compares in Ottawa

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ottawa. Compared with October: 6.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. December is 7.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Ottawa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ottawa in November?
In November, Ottawa averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ottawa?
November is not one of Ottawa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ottawa in November?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (95 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Ottawa in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ottawa get in November?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ottawa in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
