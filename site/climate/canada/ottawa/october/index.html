How October compares in Ottawa

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ottawa. Compared with September: 7.0 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Ottawa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ottawa in October?
In October, Ottawa averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ottawa?
October is not one of Ottawa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ottawa in October?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (94 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Ottawa in October?
Yes, on average: about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Ottawa get in October?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ottawa in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
