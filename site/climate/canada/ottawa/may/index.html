How May compares in Ottawa

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ottawa. Compared with April: 7.0 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. June is 4.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Ottawa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ottawa in May?
In May, Ottawa averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ottawa?
May is not one of Ottawa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ottawa in May?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (95 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Ottawa in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ottawa get in May?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ottawa in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
