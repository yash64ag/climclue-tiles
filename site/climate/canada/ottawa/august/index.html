Ottawa weather in August

Average August weather in Ottawa, Canada: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Ottawa averages 24.9 °C (77 °F) days and 14.2 °C (58 °F) nights, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 234 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Ottawa

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ottawa. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Ottawa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ottawa in August?
In August, Ottawa averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ottawa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Ottawa’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ottawa in August?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (94 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Ottawa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ottawa get in August?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ottawa in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
