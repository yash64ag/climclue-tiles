Ajax at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Ajax is 25th warmest (8.3 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 1,969 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (921 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Whitby, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ajax is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.3 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 559 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 13 km from Ajax measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Ajax has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 559 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
