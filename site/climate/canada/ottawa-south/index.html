Ottawa South at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Ottawa South is 51st warmest (6.6 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,003 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (966 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ottawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Ottawa South is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 30.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 429 km from the sea.

Why Ottawa South has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-386-1043), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
