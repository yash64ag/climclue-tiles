Gloucester at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Gloucester is 52nd warmest (6.6 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,003 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (1,021 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nepean, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Gloucester is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.8 °C), averages 30.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 429 km from the sea.

Why Gloucester has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
