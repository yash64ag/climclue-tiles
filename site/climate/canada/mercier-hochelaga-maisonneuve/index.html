Mercier–Hochelaga-Maisonneuve at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Mercier–Hochelaga-Maisonneuve is 141st warmest (6.8 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 1,969 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (1,088 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Villeray–Saint-Michel–Parc-Extension, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Mercier–Hochelaga-Maisonneuve is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 278 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mercier–Hochelaga-Maisonneuve itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.572°N, 73.546°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-384-1064), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
