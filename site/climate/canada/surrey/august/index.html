How August compares in Surrey

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 2.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Surrey in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in August?
In August, Surrey averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Surrey?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Surrey’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in August?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (247 mm), the driest July (51 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in August?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Surrey in August?
About 16.1 °C (61 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Surrey in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
