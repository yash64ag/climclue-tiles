How January compares in Surrey

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with December: 0.6 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Surrey in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in January?
In January, Surrey averages a day high of 6.0 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 246 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation and about 51 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Surrey?
January is not one of Surrey’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in January?
246 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (283 mm), the driest July (57 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in January?
About 51 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surrey in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
