How May compares in Surrey

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with April: 3.3 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Surrey in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in May?
In May, Surrey averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Surrey?
May is not one of Surrey’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in May?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (247 mm), the driest July (51 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in May?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surrey in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
