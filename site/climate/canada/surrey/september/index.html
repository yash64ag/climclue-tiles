How September compares in Surrey

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with August: 3.0 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. October is 4.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Surrey in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in September?
In September, Surrey averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Surrey?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Surrey’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in September?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (247 mm), the driest July (51 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in September?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Surrey in September?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Surrey in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
