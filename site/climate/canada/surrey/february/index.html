How February compares in Surrey

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 73 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. March is 2.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Surrey in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in February?
In February, Surrey averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 78 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Surrey?
February is not one of Surrey’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in February?
141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (247 mm), the driest July (51 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in February?
About 78 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Surrey in February?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Surrey in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
