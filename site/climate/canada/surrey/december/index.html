How December compares in Surrey

December is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Surrey. Compared with November: 2.9 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Surrey in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in December?
In December, Surrey averages a day high of 5.2 °C (41 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 51 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Surrey?
December is not one of Surrey’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in December?
241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (283 mm), the driest July (57 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 48 cm (18.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in December?
About 51 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surrey in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
