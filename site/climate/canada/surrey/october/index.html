How October compares in Surrey

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with September: 4.7 °C cooler, 103 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. November is 4.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Surrey in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in October?
In October, Surrey averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Surrey?
October is not one of Surrey’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in October?
188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (283 mm), the driest July (57 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in October?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surrey in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
