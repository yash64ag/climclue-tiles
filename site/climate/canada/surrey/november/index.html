How November compares in Surrey

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Surrey. Compared with October: 4.2 °C cooler, 84 mm more precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. December is 2.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Surrey in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surrey in November?
In November, Surrey averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 247 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation and about 60 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Surrey?
November is not one of Surrey’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surrey in November?
247 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (247 mm), the driest July (51 mm).
Does it snow in Surrey in November?
Yes, on average: about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Surrey get in November?
About 60 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Surrey in November?
About 8.5 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Surrey in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
