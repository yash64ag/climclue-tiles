Thetford-Mines at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Thetford-Mines is 257th warmest (4.4 °C year average), 284th sunniest (about 1,657 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (1,357 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saint-Georges, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Thetford-Mines is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 247 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 30.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Why Thetford-Mines has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thetford-Mines itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.094°N, 71.305°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-379-1086), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 25,704, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
