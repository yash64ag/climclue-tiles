Guelph at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Guelph is 34th warmest (7.4 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 1,951 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (904 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cambridge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Guelph is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.3 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 578 km from the sea.

Why Guelph has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 578 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
