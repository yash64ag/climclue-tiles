How May compares in Mississauga

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mississauga. Compared with April: 5.9 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 5.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Mississauga in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mississauga in May?
In May, Mississauga averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mississauga?
May is not one of Mississauga’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mississauga in May?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (93 mm), the driest March (62 mm).
Does it snow in Mississauga in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mississauga get in May?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mississauga in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
