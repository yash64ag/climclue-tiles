How February compares in Mississauga

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mississauga. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 4.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Mississauga in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mississauga in February?
In February, Mississauga averages a day high of 0.5 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -7.9 °C (18 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mississauga?
February is not one of Mississauga’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mississauga in February?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (93 mm), the driest March (62 mm).
Does it snow in Mississauga in February?
Yes, on average: about 28 cm (10.9 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Mississauga get in February?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mississauga in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
