Mississauga weather in August

August · Ontario · Canada

Average August weather in Mississauga: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How August compares in Mississauga

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mississauga. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Mississauga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mississauga in August?
In August, Mississauga averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mississauga?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mississauga’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mississauga in August?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (93 mm), the driest March (62 mm).
Does it snow in Mississauga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mississauga get in August?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mississauga in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
