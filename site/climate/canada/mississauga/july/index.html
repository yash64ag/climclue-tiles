How July compares in Mississauga

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mississauga. Compared with June: 3.5 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Mississauga in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mississauga in July?
In July, Mississauga averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mississauga?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Mississauga’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mississauga in July?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (92 mm), the driest March (64 mm).
Does it snow in Mississauga in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mississauga get in July?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mississauga in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
