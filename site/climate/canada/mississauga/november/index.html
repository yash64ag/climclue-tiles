How November compares in Mississauga

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mississauga. Compared with October: 5.9 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Mississauga in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mississauga in November?
In November, Mississauga averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 80 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mississauga?
November is not one of Mississauga’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mississauga in November?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (93 mm), the driest March (62 mm).
Does it snow in Mississauga in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.2 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mississauga get in November?
About 80 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mississauga in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
