How December compares in Mississauga

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mississauga. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 3.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Mississauga in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mississauga in December?
In December, Mississauga averages a day high of 2.3 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -3.0 °C (27 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mississauga?
December is not one of Mississauga’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mississauga in December?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (92 mm), the driest March (64 mm).
Does it snow in Mississauga in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 33 cm (12.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Mississauga get in December?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mississauga in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
