Salmon Arm at a glance

Among the 297 towns ClimaClue covers in Canada, Salmon Arm is 45th warmest (9.6 °C year average), 276th sunniest (about 1,698 hours of sunshine a year) and 264th wettest (486 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vernon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Salmon Arm is about as warm in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Why Salmon Arm has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 332 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.
