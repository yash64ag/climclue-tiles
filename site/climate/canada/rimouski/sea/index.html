Rimouski sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Quebec · Canada

The sea off Rimouski averages 10.7 °C (51 °F) in July, its warmest month, and -1.6 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Rimouski sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rimouski?
In July, at about 10.7 °C (51 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rimouski?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.6 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rimouski in February?
About -1.6 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rimouski?
About 3.6 °C (39 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rimouski nearby?
In July: Baie-Comeau (11.8 °C, 90 km), Sept-Îles (12.7 °C, 249 km) and Saint John (11.7 °C, 400 km).
