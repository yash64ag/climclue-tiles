Thunder Bay at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Thunder Bay is the 4th coolest (3.4 °C year average). Compared with Winnipeg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (598 km away), Thunder Bay is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 164 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.6 °C), averages 32.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 708 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Thunder Bay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 708 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
