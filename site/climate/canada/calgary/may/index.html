How May compares in Calgary

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Calgary. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Calgary in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in May?
In May, Calgary averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Calgary?
May is not one of Calgary’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in May?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in May?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
