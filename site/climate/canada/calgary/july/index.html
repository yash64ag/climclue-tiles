Calgary weather in July

Average July weather in Calgary, Canada: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Calgary averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) days and 9.7 °C (49 °F) nights, with 66 mm (2.6 in) of rain over the month and about 311 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Calgary

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Calgary. Compared with June: 2.7 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Calgary in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in July?
In July, Calgary averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 311 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Calgary?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Calgary’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in July?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in July?
About 311 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
