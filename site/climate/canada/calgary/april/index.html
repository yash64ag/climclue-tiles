How April compares in Calgary

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Calgary. Compared with March: 6.0 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Calgary in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in April?
In April, Calgary averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of -1.3 °C (30 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Calgary?
April is not one of Calgary’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in April?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.3 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in April?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
