How June compares in Calgary

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Calgary. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Calgary in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in June?
In June, Calgary averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Calgary?
June is not one of Calgary’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in June?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in June?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
