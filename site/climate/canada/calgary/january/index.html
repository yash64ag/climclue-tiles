How January compares in Calgary

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Calgary. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Calgary in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in January?
In January, Calgary averages a day high of -0.8 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -12.9 °C (9 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Calgary?
January is not one of Calgary’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in January?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in January?
Yes, on average: about 14 cm (5.5 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in January?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
