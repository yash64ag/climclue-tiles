How November compares in Calgary

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Calgary. Compared with October: 7.3 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Calgary in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in November?
In November, Calgary averages a day high of 3.1 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -8.3 °C (17 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Calgary?
November is not one of Calgary’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in November?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in November?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
