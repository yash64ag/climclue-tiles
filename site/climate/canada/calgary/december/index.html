How December compares in Calgary

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Calgary. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Calgary in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calgary in December?
In December, Calgary averages a day high of -1.4 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -12.9 °C (9 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Calgary?
December is not one of Calgary’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calgary in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (93 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Calgary in December?
Yes, on average: about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Calgary get in December?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calgary in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
