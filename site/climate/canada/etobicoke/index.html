Etobicoke at a glance

Among the 73 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Canada, Etobicoke is 20th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,014 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (876 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mississauga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Etobicoke is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 70 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 557 km from the sea.

Why Etobicoke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 557 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
