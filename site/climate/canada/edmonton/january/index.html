How January compares in Edmonton

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Edmonton. Compared with December: 1.1 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. February is 2.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Edmonton in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edmonton in January?
In January, Edmonton averages a day high of -7.0 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -15.5 °C (4 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Edmonton?
January is not one of Edmonton’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edmonton in January?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Edmonton in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 24 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Edmonton get in January?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edmonton in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
