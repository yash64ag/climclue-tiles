How April compares in Edmonton

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Edmonton. Compared with March: 8.5 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 68 hours more sunshine. May is 6.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Edmonton in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edmonton in April?
In April, Edmonton averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Edmonton?
April is not one of Edmonton’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edmonton in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Edmonton in April?
Yes, on average: about 10 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Edmonton get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edmonton in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
