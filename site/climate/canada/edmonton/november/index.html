How November compares in Edmonton

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Edmonton. Compared with October: 9.0 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. December is 5.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Edmonton in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edmonton in November?
In November, Edmonton averages a day high of 0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -8.8 °C (16 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Edmonton?
November is not one of Edmonton’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edmonton in November?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Edmonton in November?
Yes, on average: about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Edmonton get in November?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edmonton in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
