How March compares in Edmonton

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Edmonton. Compared with February: 5.2 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. April is 8.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Edmonton in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edmonton in March?
In March, Edmonton averages a day high of 1.9 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -8.4 °C (17 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Edmonton?
March is not one of Edmonton’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edmonton in March?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Edmonton in March?
Yes, on average: about 16 cm (6.4 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Edmonton get in March?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edmonton in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
