How June compares in Edmonton

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Edmonton. Compared with May: 4.0 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Edmonton in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edmonton in June?
In June, Edmonton averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Edmonton?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Edmonton’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edmonton in June?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Edmonton in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Edmonton get in June?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edmonton in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
