How September compares in Edmonton

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Edmonton. Compared with August: 5.2 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 96 hours less sunshine. October is 6.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Edmonton in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edmonton in September?
In September, Edmonton averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Edmonton?
September is not one of Edmonton’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edmonton in September?
39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (96 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Edmonton in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Edmonton get in September?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edmonton in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
