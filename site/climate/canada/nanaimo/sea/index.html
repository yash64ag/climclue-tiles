Nanaimo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · British Columbia · Canada

The sea off Nanaimo averages 16.6 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.3 °C (43 °F) in January. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Nanaimo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nanaimo?
In August, at about 16.6 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nanaimo?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is January, at 6.3 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nanaimo in January?
About 6.3 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nanaimo?
About 10.8 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
