Dolisie at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Republic of the Congo, Dolisie is the coolest (23.9 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,654 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,385 mm a year). Compared with Nkayi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Dolisie is 1.1 °C cooler in March and 1.6 °C cooler in July, with 257 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.4 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dolisie, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dolisie has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
