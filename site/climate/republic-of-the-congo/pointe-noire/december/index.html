Pointe-Noire in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pointe-Noire in December?
In December, Pointe-Noire averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pointe-Noire?
December is not one of Pointe-Noire’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pointe-Noire in December?
180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (231 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Pointe-Noire in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pointe-Noire get in December?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pointe-Noire in December?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pointe-Noire in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
