Pointe-Noire in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pointe-Noire in July?
In July, Pointe-Noire averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Pointe-Noire?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Pointe-Noire’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pointe-Noire in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (231 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Pointe-Noire in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pointe-Noire get in July?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pointe-Noire in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
