Pointe-Noire in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pointe-Noire in October?
In October, Pointe-Noire averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Pointe-Noire?
October is not one of Pointe-Noire’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pointe-Noire in October?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (231 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Pointe-Noire in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pointe-Noire get in October?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pointe-Noire in October?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pointe-Noire in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
