Pointe-Noire sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Republic of the Congo

The sea off Pointe-Noire averages 28.0 °C (82 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 21.2 °C (70 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to May.

Pointe-Noire sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pointe-Noire?
In March, at about 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pointe-Noire?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to May. The coolest month is July, at 21.2 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pointe-Noire in July?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Pointe-Noire?
About 25.0 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
