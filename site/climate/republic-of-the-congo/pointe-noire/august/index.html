Pointe-Noire in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pointe-Noire in August?
In August, Pointe-Noire averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Pointe-Noire?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Pointe-Noire’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pointe-Noire in August?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (250 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Pointe-Noire in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pointe-Noire get in August?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pointe-Noire in August?
