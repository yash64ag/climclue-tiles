Pointe-Noire weather in April

Average April weather in Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Pointe-Noire averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) days and 23.3 °C (74 °F) nights, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of rain over the month and about 181 hours of sunshine.

Pointe-Noire in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pointe-Noire in April?
In April, Pointe-Noire averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pointe-Noire?
April is not one of Pointe-Noire’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pointe-Noire in April?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (231 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Pointe-Noire in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pointe-Noire get in April?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Pointe-Noire in April?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Pointe-Noire in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
