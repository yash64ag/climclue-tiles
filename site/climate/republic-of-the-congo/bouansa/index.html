Bouansa at a glance

Among the 23 towns ClimaClue covers in the Republic of the Congo, Bouansa is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,558 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (1,170 mm a year). Compared with Loutété, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Bouansa is 0.1 °C warmer in March and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.4 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, July (23.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 218 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bouansa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Bouansa

For mild weather, the best months to visit Bouansa are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

Why Bouansa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 218 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bouansa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.219°S, 13.762°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-882-1937), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
