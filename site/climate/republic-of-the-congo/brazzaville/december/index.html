Brazzaville weather in December

Average December weather in Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Brazzaville averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 201 mm (7.9 in) of rain over the month and about 139 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Brazzaville

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Brazzaville. Compared with November: about as warm, 78 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Brazzaville in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brazzaville in December?
In December, Brazzaville averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Brazzaville?
December is not one of Brazzaville’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brazzaville in December?
201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (278 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Brazzaville in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brazzaville get in December?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brazzaville in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
