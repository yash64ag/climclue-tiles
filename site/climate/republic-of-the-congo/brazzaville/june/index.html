How June compares in Brazzaville

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Brazzaville. Compared with May: 1.5 °C cooler, 120 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Brazzaville in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brazzaville in June?
In June, Brazzaville averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Brazzaville?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Brazzaville’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brazzaville in June?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (278 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Brazzaville in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brazzaville get in June?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brazzaville in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
