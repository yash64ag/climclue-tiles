How May compares in Brazzaville

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Brazzaville. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 88 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Brazzaville in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Brazzaville in May?
In May, Brazzaville averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Brazzaville?
May is not one of Brazzaville’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Brazzaville in May?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (278 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Brazzaville in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Brazzaville get in May?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Brazzaville in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
