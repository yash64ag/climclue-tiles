Ballincollig sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Munster · Ireland

The sea off Ballincollig averages 16.2 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.8 °C (48 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Ballincollig sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ballincollig?
In August, at about 16.2 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ballincollig?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 8.8 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ballincollig in March?
About 8.8 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ballincollig?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
