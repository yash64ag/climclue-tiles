Waterford sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Munster · Ireland

The sea off Waterford averages 15.2 °C (59 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.2 °C (47 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Waterford sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Waterford?
In August, at about 15.2 °C (59 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Waterford?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 8.2 °C (47 °F).
How warm is the sea in Waterford in March?
About 8.2 °C (47 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Waterford?
About 11.6 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Waterford nearby?
In August: Carrigaline (16.2 °C, 101 km), Cork (16.2 °C, 101 km) and Ballincollig (16.2 °C, 109 km).
