How September compares in Dublin

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dublin. Compared with August: 1.9 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. October is 2.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Dublin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dublin in September?
In September, Dublin averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dublin?
September is not one of Dublin’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dublin in September?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (100 mm), the driest July (66 mm).
Does it snow in Dublin in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dublin get in September?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dublin in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
