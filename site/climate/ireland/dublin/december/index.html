How December compares in Dublin

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dublin. Compared with November: 1.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Dublin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dublin in December?
In December, Dublin averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 49 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dublin?
December is not one of Dublin’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dublin in December?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (100 mm), the driest July (66 mm).
Does it snow in Dublin in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dublin get in December?
About 49 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dublin in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
