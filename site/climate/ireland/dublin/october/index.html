How October compares in Dublin

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dublin. Compared with September: 2.8 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. November is 2.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Dublin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dublin in October?
In October, Dublin averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dublin?
October is not one of Dublin’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dublin in October?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (100 mm), the driest July (66 mm).
Does it snow in Dublin in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dublin get in October?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dublin in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
