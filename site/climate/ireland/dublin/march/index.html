How March compares in Dublin

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dublin. Compared with February: 1.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Dublin in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dublin in March?
In March, Dublin averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dublin?
March is not one of Dublin’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dublin in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (100 mm), the driest July (66 mm).
Does it snow in Dublin in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dublin get in March?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dublin in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
