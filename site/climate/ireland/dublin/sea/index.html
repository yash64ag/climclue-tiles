Dublin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Leinster · Ireland

The sea off Dublin averages 14.5 °C (58 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.6 °C (46 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Dublin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dublin?
In August, at about 14.5 °C (58 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dublin?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 7.6 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dublin in March?
About 7.6 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dublin?
About 11.1 °C (52 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dublin nearby?
In August: Wexford (15.1 °C, 112 km) and Waterford (15.2 °C, 133 km).
