Dublin at a glance

Among the 39 towns ClimaClue covers in Ireland, Dublin is 19th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 1,392 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (941 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rathfarnham, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Dublin is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.7 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 122% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Best time to visit Dublin

For mild weather, the best months to visit Dublin are July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (15.7 °C), August (15.6 °C) and June (13.8 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (65 mm), April (66 mm) and February (67 mm).

Why Dublin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Dublin climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dublin?
July, with an average of 15.7 °C (60 °F), day highs around 19.9 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 11.6 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dublin?
January, with an average of 5.3 °C (42 °F), day highs around 8.2 °C (47 °F) and night lows around 2.4 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dublin?
October, with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 941 mm (37.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dublin?
July, with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Dublin?
May, with about 186 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dublin?
Yes. About 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time to visit Dublin?
For mild weather (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July and August. Climate only, from 1981–2010 averages: crowds and prices are not considered.
How warm is the sea in Dublin?
The nearby open sea averages 14.5 °C (58 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.6 °C (46 °F) in March. It stays below 20 °C (68 °F) all year. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dublin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.333°N, 6.249°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-306-1737), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
