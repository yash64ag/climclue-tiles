How April compares in Dublin

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dublin. Compared with March: 1.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Dublin in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dublin in April?
In April, Dublin averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dublin?
April is not one of Dublin’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dublin in April?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (100 mm), the driest July (65 mm).
Does it snow in Dublin in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dublin get in April?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dublin in April?
About 8.2 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (14.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dublin in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
