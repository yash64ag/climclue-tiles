How February compares in Dublin

February is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Dublin. Compared with January: about as warm, 18 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. March is 1.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Dublin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dublin in February?
In February, Dublin averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dublin?
February is not one of Dublin’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dublin in February?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (100 mm), the driest July (66 mm).
Does it snow in Dublin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.1 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dublin get in February?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dublin in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
