Chāndpur at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Chāndpur is the 5th warmest (25.8 °C year average). Compared with Narayanganj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Chāndpur is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 115 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 80 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chāndpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chāndpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
