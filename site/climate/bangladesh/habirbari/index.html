Habirbāri at a glance

Among the 145 towns ClimaClue covers in Bangladesh, Habirbāri is 111th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,129 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (2,312 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jāmirdia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Habirbāri is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 204 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Habirbāri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Habirbāri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 204 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Habirbāri itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.317°N, 90.400°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-596-2704), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
