How August compares in Rangpur

August is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Rangpur. Compared with July: 0.2 °C warmer, 122 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 0.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Rangpur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rangpur in August?
In August, Rangpur averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 378 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Rangpur?
August is not one of Rangpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rangpur in August?
378 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (500 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Rangpur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rangpur get in August?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rangpur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
