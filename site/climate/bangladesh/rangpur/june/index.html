How June compares in Rangpur

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Rangpur. Compared with May: 0.8 °C warmer, 165 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Rangpur in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rangpur in June?
In June, Rangpur averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 477 mm (18.8 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rangpur?
June is not one of Rangpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rangpur in June?
477 mm (18.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (500 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Rangpur in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rangpur get in June?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rangpur in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
