Rangpur at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Rangpur is the 5th coolest (24.8 °C year average). Compared with Saidpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Rangpur is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 128 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 391 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rangpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Rangpur

For mild weather, the best months to visit Rangpur are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (28.6 °C), July (28.4 °C) and June (28.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (9 mm), November (9 mm) and January (12 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): March (226 h), April (224 h) and January (224 h).

Why Rangpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 391 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
