Narayanganj at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Narayanganj is 12th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 2,102 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (2,179 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sonārgaon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Narayanganj is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.6 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Narayanganj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Narayanganj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
