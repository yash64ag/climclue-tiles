Cox’s Bāzār at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Cox’s Bāzār is the sunniest (about 2,230 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (3,520 mm a year). Compared with Chattogram, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Cox’s Bāzār is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 374 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 7.9 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cox’s Bāzār, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cox’s Bāzār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
