How May compares in Khulna

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Khulna. Compared with April: 0.4 °C warmer, 103 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. June is 0.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Khulna in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khulna in May?
In May, Khulna averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Khulna?
May is not one of Khulna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khulna in May?
194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (360 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Khulna in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khulna get in May?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khulna in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
