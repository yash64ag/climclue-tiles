Khulna at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Khulna is the 2nd warmest (25.9 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 2,090 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bagerhat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Khulna is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 138 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.2 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khulna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Khulna

For mild weather, the best months to visit Khulna are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (29.4 °C), June (29.1 °C) and April (28.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (10 mm), January (15 mm) and February (31 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): January (239 h), December (235 h) and March (228 h).

Why Khulna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
