How January compares in Khulna

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Khulna. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 3.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Khulna in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khulna in January?
In January, Khulna averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Khulna?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Khulna’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khulna in January?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (360 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Khulna in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khulna get in January?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khulna in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
