How September compares in Khulna

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Khulna. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Khulna in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khulna in September?
In September, Khulna averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Khulna?
September is not one of Khulna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khulna in September?
294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (360 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Khulna in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khulna get in September?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khulna in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
