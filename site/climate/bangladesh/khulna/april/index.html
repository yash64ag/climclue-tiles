How April compares in Khulna

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Khulna. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Khulna in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khulna in April?
In April, Khulna averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Khulna?
April is not one of Khulna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khulna in April?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (361 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Khulna in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khulna get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khulna in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
