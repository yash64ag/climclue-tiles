How March compares in Khulna

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Khulna. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. April is 2.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Khulna in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khulna in March?
In March, Khulna averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Khulna?
March is not one of Khulna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khulna in March?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (360 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Khulna in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khulna get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khulna in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
