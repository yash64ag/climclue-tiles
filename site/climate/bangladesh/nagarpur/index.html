Nāgarpur at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Nāgarpur is 20th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,105 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (1,953 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tangail, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Nāgarpur is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 196 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nāgarpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nāgarpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 196 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
