Maijdi at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Maijdi is the 3rd wettest (3,174 mm a year). Compared with Chāndpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Maijdi is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 879 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.4 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maijdi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maijdi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
