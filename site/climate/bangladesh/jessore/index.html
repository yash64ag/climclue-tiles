Jessore at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Jessore is the least sunny (about 2,083 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sātkhira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Jessore is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 161 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jessore, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jessore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 161 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
