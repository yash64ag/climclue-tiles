Nagar Naluākot at a glance

Among the 145 towns ClimaClue covers in Bangladesh, Nagar Naluākot is 100th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 2,129 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (2,330 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bājitpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Nagar Naluākot is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 92 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nagar Naluākot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nagar Naluākot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nagar Naluākot itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.156°N, 90.773°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-598-2707), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
