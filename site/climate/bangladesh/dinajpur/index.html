Why Dinajpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 411 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Dinajpur climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dinajpur?
August, with an average of 28.6 °C (84 °F), day highs around 31.3 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 25.4 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dinajpur?
January, with an average of 16.9 °C (62 °F), day highs around 23.1 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 11.1 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dinajpur?
July, with 420 mm (16.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,048 mm (80.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dinajpur?
December, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Dinajpur?
April, with about 230 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dinajpur?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Dinajpur?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Dinajpur have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Dinajpur’s GeoNames location (25.627°N, 88.638°E; ClimaClue area sq-583-2686), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 206,234 (GeoNames).
