Dinajpur at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Dinajpur is 47th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,175 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (2,048 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saidpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Dinajpur is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 253 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 411 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dinajpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Dinajpur

For mild weather, the best months to visit Dinajpur are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (28.6 °C), July (28.6 °C) and June (28.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (8 mm), November (9 mm) and January (12 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (230 h), March (227 h) and January (219 h).

Why Dinajpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 411 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
