How June compares in Comilla

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Comilla. Compared with May: 0.1 °C cooler, 107 mm more precipitation and 76 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Comilla in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in June?
In June, Comilla averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 433 mm (17.0 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Comilla?
June is not one of Comilla’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in June?
433 mm (17.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in June?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
