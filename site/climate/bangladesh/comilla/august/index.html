How August compares in Comilla

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Comilla. Compared with July: 0.2 °C warmer, 101 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Comilla in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in August?
In August, Comilla averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 336 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Comilla?
August is not one of Comilla’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in August?
336 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (437 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in August?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
