Comilla weather in April

Average April weather in Comilla, Bangladesh: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Comilla averages 32.4 °C (90 °F) days and 22.9 °C (73 °F) nights, with 163 mm (6.4 in) of rain over the month and about 203 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Comilla

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Comilla. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 93 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Comilla in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in April?
In April, Comilla averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Comilla?
April is not one of Comilla’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in April?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (437 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in April?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
