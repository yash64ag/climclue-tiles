How July compares in Comilla

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Comilla. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Comilla in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in July?
In July, Comilla averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 443 mm (17.4 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Comilla?
July is not one of Comilla’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in July?
443 mm (17.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in July?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
