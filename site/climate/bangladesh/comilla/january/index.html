How January compares in Comilla

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Comilla. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 3.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Comilla in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in January?
In January, Comilla averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Comilla?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Comilla’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in January?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in January?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
