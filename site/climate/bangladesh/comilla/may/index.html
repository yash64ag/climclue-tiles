How May compares in Comilla

May is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Comilla. Compared with April: 0.5 °C warmer, 162 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. June is 0.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Comilla in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in May?
In May, Comilla averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 326 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Comilla?
May is not one of Comilla’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in May?
326 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in May?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
