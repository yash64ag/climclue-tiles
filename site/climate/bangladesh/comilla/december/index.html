How December compares in Comilla

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Comilla. Compared with November: 3.9 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Comilla in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in December?
In December, Comilla averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Comilla?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Comilla’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in December?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
