How March compares in Comilla

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Comilla. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. April is 1.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Comilla in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Comilla in March?
In March, Comilla averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Comilla?
March is not one of Comilla’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Comilla in March?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (437 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Comilla in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Comilla get in March?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Comilla in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
