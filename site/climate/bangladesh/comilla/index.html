Comilla at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Comilla is 40th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,134 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (2,296 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Brāhmanbāria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Comilla is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 120 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.3 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Comilla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Comilla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
