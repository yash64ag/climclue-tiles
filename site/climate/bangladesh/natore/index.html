Why Natore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 287 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Natore climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Natore?
June, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 32.4 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 25.8 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Natore?
January, with an average of 17.4 °C (63 °F), day highs around 23.8 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 11.8 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Natore?
July, with 346 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,640 mm (64.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Natore?
December, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Natore?
January, with about 226 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Natore?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Natore?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Natore have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Natore’s GeoNames location (24.411°N, 88.987°E; ClimaClue area sq-595-2689), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 369,138 (GeoNames).
