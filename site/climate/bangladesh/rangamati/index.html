Rangamati at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Rangamati is the 4th sunniest (about 2,196 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Karnaphuli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Rangamati is 0.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 350 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.9 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, January (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rangamati, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rangamati has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
