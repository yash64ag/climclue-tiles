Panchagarh at a glance

Among the 145 towns ClimaClue covers in Bangladesh, Panchagarh is the 5th coolest (24.5 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,215 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Thākurgaon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Panchagarh is 0.3 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 644 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.3 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 482 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Panchagarh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Panchagarh

For mild weather, the best months to visit Panchagarh are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.

Why Panchagarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 482 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Panchagarh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.333°N, 88.558°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-576-2685), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
