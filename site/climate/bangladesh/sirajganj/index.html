Sirajganj at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Sirajganj is 34th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,120 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (1,928 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tangail, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Sirajganj is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 92 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 239 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sirajganj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sirajganj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 239 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
