How January compares in Gazipur

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Gazipur. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Gazipur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gazipur in January?
In January, Gazipur averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Gazipur?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Gazipur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gazipur in January?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (406 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Gazipur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gazipur get in January?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gazipur in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
