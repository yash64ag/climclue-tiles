How November compares in Gazipur

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Gazipur. Compared with October: 3.5 °C cooler, 145 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. December is 4.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Gazipur in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gazipur in November?
In November, Gazipur averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Gazipur?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Gazipur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gazipur in November?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (406 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Gazipur in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gazipur get in November?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gazipur in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
