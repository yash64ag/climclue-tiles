How July compares in Gazipur

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Gazipur. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Gazipur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gazipur in July?
In July, Gazipur averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 406 mm (16.0 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Gazipur?
July is not one of Gazipur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gazipur in July?
406 mm (16.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (406 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Gazipur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gazipur get in July?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gazipur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
