Puthia at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Puthia is the 3rd driest (1,582 mm a year). Compared with Natore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Puthia is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.3 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 286 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puthia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puthia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 286 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
