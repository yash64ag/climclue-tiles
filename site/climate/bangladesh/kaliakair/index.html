Kaliakair at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Kaliakair is 41st warmest (25.2 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (2,121 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gazipur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Kaliakair is about as warm in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 63 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.6 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kaliakair, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kaliakair has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
