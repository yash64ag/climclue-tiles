Mymensingh at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Mymensingh is the 3rd coolest (24.8 °C year average). Compared with Sherpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Mymensingh is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 383 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 248 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mymensingh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mymensingh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 248 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
