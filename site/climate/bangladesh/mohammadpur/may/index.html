How May compares in Mohammadpur

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mohammadpur. Compared with April: 0.5 °C warmer, 116 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. June is 0.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Mohammadpur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mohammadpur in May?
In May, Mohammadpur averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mohammadpur?
May is not one of Mohammadpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mohammadpur in May?
174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Mohammadpur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mohammadpur get in May?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mohammadpur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
