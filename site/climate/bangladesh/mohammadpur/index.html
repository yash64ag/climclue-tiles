Mohammadpur at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Mohammadpur is 39th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,137 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (1,704 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nawābganj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Mohammadpur is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 347 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mohammadpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mohammadpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 347 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
