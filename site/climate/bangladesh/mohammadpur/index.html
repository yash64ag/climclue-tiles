Mohammadpur at a glance

Among the 145 towns ClimaClue covers in Bangladesh, Mohammadpur is 108th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 2,137 hours of sunshine a year) and 134th wettest (1,738 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nawābganj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Mohammadpur is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 168 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 347 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mohammadpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mohammadpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 347 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mohammadpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.898°N, 88.528°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-591-2685), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
