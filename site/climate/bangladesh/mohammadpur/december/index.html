How December compares in Mohammadpur

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mohammadpur. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Mohammadpur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mohammadpur in December?
In December, Mohammadpur averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mohammadpur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Mohammadpur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mohammadpur in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Mohammadpur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mohammadpur get in December?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mohammadpur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
