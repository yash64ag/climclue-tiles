How April compares in Mohammadpur

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mohammadpur. Compared with March: 3.4 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. May is 0.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Mohammadpur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mohammadpur in April?
In April, Mohammadpur averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mohammadpur?
April is not one of Mohammadpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mohammadpur in April?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Mohammadpur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mohammadpur get in April?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mohammadpur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
