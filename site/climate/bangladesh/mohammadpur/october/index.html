How October compares in Mohammadpur

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mohammadpur. Compared with September: 1.7 °C cooler, 182 mm less precipitation and 77 hours more sunshine. November is 3.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Mohammadpur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mohammadpur in October?
In October, Mohammadpur averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mohammadpur?
October is not one of Mohammadpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mohammadpur in October?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Mohammadpur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mohammadpur get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mohammadpur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
