Why Sylhet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 248 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sylhet climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sylhet?
August, with an average of 28.1 °C (83 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 25.2 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sylhet?
January, with an average of 17.4 °C (63 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 11.9 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sylhet?
July, with 1,005 mm (39.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 4,904 mm (193.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sylhet?
January, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sylhet?
December, with about 246 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sylhet?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sylhet?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Sylhet have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Sylhet’s GeoNames location (24.899°N, 91.872°E; ClimaClue area sq-591-2718), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 237,000 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
