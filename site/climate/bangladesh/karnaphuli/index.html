Why Karnaphuli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Karnaphuli climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Karnaphuli?
May, with an average of 28.1 °C (82 °F), day highs around 32.4 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 24.4 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Karnaphuli?
January, with an average of 18.9 °C (66 °F), day highs around 25.3 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 13.4 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Karnaphuli?
July, with 553 mm (21.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,601 mm (102.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Karnaphuli?
January, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Karnaphuli?
January, with about 264 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Karnaphuli?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Karnaphuli?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Karnaphuli have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Karnaphuli’s GeoNames location (22.691°N, 92.235°E; ClimaClue area sq-613-2722), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 203,697 (GeoNames).
