Karnaphuli at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Karnaphuli is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,202 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Rangamati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Karnaphuli is 0.2 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 350 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.1 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, January (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Karnaphuli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Karnaphuli

For mild weather, the best months to visit Karnaphuli are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.

Why Karnaphuli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
