Bhairab Bāzār at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Bhairab Bāzār is 35th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,133 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (2,272 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ashuganj City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Bhairab Bāzār is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 161 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhairab Bāzār, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhairab Bāzār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 161 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
