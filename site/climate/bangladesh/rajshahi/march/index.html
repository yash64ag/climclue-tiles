How March compares in Rajshahi

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Rajshahi. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 3.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Rajshahi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rajshahi in March?
In March, Rajshahi averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Rajshahi?
March is not one of Rajshahi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rajshahi in March?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (329 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Rajshahi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rajshahi get in March?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rajshahi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
