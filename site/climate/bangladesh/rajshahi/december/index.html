How December compares in Rajshahi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Rajshahi. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Rajshahi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rajshahi in December?
In December, Rajshahi averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Rajshahi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Rajshahi’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rajshahi in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (328 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Rajshahi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rajshahi get in December?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rajshahi in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
