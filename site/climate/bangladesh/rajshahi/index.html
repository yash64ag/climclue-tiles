Rajshahi at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Rajshahi is the driest (1,541 mm a year). Compared with Puthia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Rajshahi is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 11.9 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 298 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rajshahi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Rajshahi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Rajshahi are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.

Why Rajshahi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 298 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
