How October compares in Rajshahi

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Rajshahi. Compared with September: 1.5 °C cooler, 167 mm less precipitation and 78 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Rajshahi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rajshahi in October?
In October, Rajshahi averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Rajshahi?
October is not one of Rajshahi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rajshahi in October?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (328 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Rajshahi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rajshahi get in October?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rajshahi in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
