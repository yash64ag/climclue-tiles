How November compares in Rajshahi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Rajshahi. Compared with October: 3.8 °C cooler, 104 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. December is 4.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Rajshahi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rajshahi in November?
In November, Rajshahi averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rajshahi?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Rajshahi’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rajshahi in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (328 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Rajshahi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rajshahi get in November?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rajshahi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
