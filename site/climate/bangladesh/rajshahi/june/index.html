Rajshahi weather in June

Average June weather in Rajshahi, Bangladesh: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Rajshahi averages 33.5 °C (92 °F) days and 25.3 °C (78 °F) nights, with 252 mm (9.9 in) of rain over the month and about 122 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Rajshahi

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Rajshahi. Compared with May: about as warm, 98 mm more precipitation and 76 hours less sunshine. July is 0.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Rajshahi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rajshahi in June?
In June, Rajshahi averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rajshahi?
June is not one of Rajshahi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rajshahi in June?
252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (329 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Rajshahi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rajshahi get in June?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rajshahi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
