How March compares in Chattogram

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chattogram. Compared with February: 3.5 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. April is 1.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Chattogram in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chattogram in March?
In March, Chattogram averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chattogram?
March is not one of Chattogram’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chattogram in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (777 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Chattogram in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chattogram get in March?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chattogram in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
