How October compares in Chattogram

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Chattogram. Compared with September: 0.3 °C cooler, 90 mm less precipitation and 82 hours more sunshine. November is 2.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Chattogram in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chattogram in October?
In October, Chattogram averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chattogram?
October is not one of Chattogram’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chattogram in October?
228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (777 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Chattogram in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chattogram get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chattogram in October?
About 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Chattogram in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
