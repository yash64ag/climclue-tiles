Chattogram in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chattogram in April?
In April, Chattogram averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chattogram?
April is not one of Chattogram’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chattogram in April?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (777 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Chattogram in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chattogram get in April?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chattogram in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
