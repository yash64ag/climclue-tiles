How November compares in Chattogram

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chattogram. Compared with October: 2.5 °C cooler, 170 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Chattogram in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chattogram in November?
In November, Chattogram averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chattogram?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Chattogram’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chattogram in November?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (766 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Chattogram in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chattogram get in November?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chattogram in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
