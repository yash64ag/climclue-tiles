How March compares in Dhaka

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dhaka. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. April is 2.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Dhaka among towns in Bangladesh in March

Of the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Dhaka ranks 10th warmest (average temperature), 26th sunniest and 13th wettest in March.

Dhaka in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhaka in March?
In March, Dhaka averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dhaka?
March is not one of Dhaka’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhaka in March?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (411 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dhaka in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhaka get in March?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhaka in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
