How April compares in Dhaka

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dhaka. Compared with March: 2.2 °C warmer, 71 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Dhaka in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhaka in April?
In April, Dhaka averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dhaka?
April is not one of Dhaka’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhaka in April?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (407 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dhaka in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhaka get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhaka in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
