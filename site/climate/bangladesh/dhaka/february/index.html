How February compares in Dhaka

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dhaka. Compared with January: 3.2 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. March is 4.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Dhaka among towns in Bangladesh in February

Of the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Dhaka ranks 18th warmest (average temperature), 17th sunniest and 23rd wettest in February.

Dhaka in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhaka in February?
In February, Dhaka averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dhaka?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Dhaka’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhaka in February?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (411 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dhaka in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhaka get in February?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhaka in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
