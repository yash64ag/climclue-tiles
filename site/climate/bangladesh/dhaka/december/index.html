Dhaka weather in December

Average December weather in Dhaka, Bangladesh: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Dhaka averages 26.1 °C (79 °F) days and 13.1 °C (56 °F) nights, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 258 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Dhaka

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Dhaka. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses.

Dhaka in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhaka in December?
In December, Dhaka averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dhaka?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Dhaka’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhaka in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (407 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dhaka in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhaka get in December?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhaka in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
