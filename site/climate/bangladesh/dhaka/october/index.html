How October compares in Dhaka

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dhaka. Compared with September: 1.1 °C cooler, 150 mm less precipitation and 96 hours more sunshine. November is 3.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Dhaka among towns in Bangladesh in October

Of the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Dhaka ranks 5th warmest (average temperature), 20th sunniest and 27th wettest in October.

Dhaka in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhaka in October?
In October, Dhaka averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dhaka?
October is not one of Dhaka’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhaka in October?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (411 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dhaka in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhaka get in October?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhaka in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
