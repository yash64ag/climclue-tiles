Dhaka at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Dhaka is 15th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,099 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (2,161 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kafrul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Dhaka is 0.1 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.6 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 140 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dhaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Dhaka

For mild weather, the best months to visit Dhaka are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (28.6 °C), May (28.6 °C) and August (28.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (9 mm), December (11 mm) and February (23 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): January (264 h), December (258 h) and November (243 h).

Why Dhaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 140 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
