Bagerhat at a glance

Among the 54 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bangladesh, Bagerhat is the 3rd warmest (25.9 °C year average). Compared with Khulna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Bagerhat is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 138 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bagerhat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bagerhat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
