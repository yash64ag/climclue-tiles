Arroyo Naranjo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Arroyo Naranjo, Cuba (Havana), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Arroyo Naranjo is warmest in August, with 32.0 °C (90 °F) days and 23.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 26.0 °C (79 °F) days and 17.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around June (257 mm); December is driest (50 mm).

Arroyo Naranjo at a glance

Among the 125 towns ClimaClue covers in Cuba, Arroyo Naranjo is 80th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,949 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (1,477 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fontanar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Arroyo Naranjo is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Arroyo Naranjo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-609-976), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
