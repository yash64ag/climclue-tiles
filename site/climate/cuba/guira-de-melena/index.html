Güira de Melena at a glance

Among the 125 towns ClimaClue covers in Cuba, Güira de Melena is 53rd warmest (25.3 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,867 hours of sunshine a year) and 93rd wettest (1,281 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alquízar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Güira de Melena is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.0 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Güira de Melena, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Güira de Melena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
