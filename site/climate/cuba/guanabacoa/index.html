Guanabacoa at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cuba, Guanabacoa is the sunniest (about 2,950 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with San Miguel del Padrón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Guanabacoa is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.5 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Guanabacoa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
