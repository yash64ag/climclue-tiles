Jesús Menéndez at a glance

Among the 125 towns ClimaClue covers in Cuba, Jesús Menéndez is 22nd warmest (26.0 °C year average), 103rd sunniest (about 2,534 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (1,117 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Puerto Padre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Jesús Menéndez is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.9 °C (66 °F), in February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jesús Menéndez, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jesús Menéndez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-1035), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
