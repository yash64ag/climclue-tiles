Venezuela at a glance

Among the 125 towns ClimaClue covers in Cuba, Venezuela is 34th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 2,682 hours of sunshine a year) and 94th wettest (1,280 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ciego de Ávila, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Venezuela is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 124 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Venezuela, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Venezuela

For mild weather, the best months to visit Venezuela are January, February, March, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.1 °C), August (28.1 °C) and June (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (21 mm), December (24 mm) and February (35 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (253 h), March (251 h) and July (248 h).

Why Venezuela has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

July, with an average of 28.1 °C (83 °F), day highs around 33.5 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 22.7 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Venezuela?
January, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.8 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 17.1 °C (63 °F).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Venezuela itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.738°N, 78.793°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-622-1012), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 16,601, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
