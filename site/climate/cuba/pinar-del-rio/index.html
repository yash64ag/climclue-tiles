Pinar del Río at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cuba, Pinar del Río is the 2nd coolest (24.7 °C year average). Compared with Fontanar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (148 km away), Pinar del Río is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 63 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pinar del Río, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pinar del Río has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
