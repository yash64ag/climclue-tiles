Cárdenas at a glance

Among the 125 towns ClimaClue covers in Cuba, Cárdenas is 71st warmest (25.1 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,906 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (1,309 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Varadero, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Cárdenas is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 117 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, January (22.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cárdenas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cárdenas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cárdenas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.036°N, 81.206°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-609-987), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
