Santa Cruz del Norte sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mayabeque · Cuba

The sea off Santa Cruz del Norte averages 29.4 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 25.2 °C (77 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Santa Cruz del Norte sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santa Cruz del Norte?
In August, at about 29.4 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santa Cruz del Norte?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.2 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santa Cruz del Norte in February?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Santa Cruz del Norte?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Santa Cruz del Norte nearby?
In August: Bauta (30.3 °C, 67 km), Güira de Melena (30.3 °C, 72 km) and Guanajay (30.3 °C, 82 km).
