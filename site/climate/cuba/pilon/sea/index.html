Pilón sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Granma Province · Cuba

The sea off Pilón averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 26.4 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Pilón sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pilón?
In August, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pilón?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.4 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pilón in February?
About 26.4 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Pilón?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pilón nearby?
In August: Media Luna (30.2 °C, 29 km), Campechuela (30.2 °C, 37 km) and Manzanillo (30.2 °C, 53 km).
