Ciego de Ávila at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cuba, Ciego de Ávila is 10th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,677 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (1,415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sancti Spíritus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Ciego de Ávila is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 112 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ciego de Ávila, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ciego de Ávila has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
