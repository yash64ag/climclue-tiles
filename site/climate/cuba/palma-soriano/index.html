Palma Soriano at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cuba, Palma Soriano is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,400 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Santiago de Cuba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Palma Soriano is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 89 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Palma Soriano, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Palma Soriano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
