Why Palma Soriano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Palma Soriano?
January, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.6 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 19.1 °C (66 °F).

What is the driest month in Palma Soriano?

What is the sunniest month in Palma Soriano?
July, with about 228 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Palma Soriano?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Palma Soriano?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.
