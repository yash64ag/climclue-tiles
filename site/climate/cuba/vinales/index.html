Viñales at a glance

Among the 125 towns ClimaClue covers in Cuba, Viñales is the 5th coolest (24.1 °C year average). Compared with Pinar del Río, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Viñales is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 197 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.5 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, January (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Viñales has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Viñales itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.619°N, 83.707°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-613-962), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
