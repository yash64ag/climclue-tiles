Viñales sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pinar del Río · Cuba

The sea off Viñales averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 25.6 °C (78 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Viñales sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Viñales?
In August, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Viñales?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.6 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Viñales in February?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Viñales?
About 27.5 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Viñales nearby?
In August: Mariel (30.3 °C, 106 km), Guanajay (30.3 °C, 110 km) and Nueva Gerona (30.4 °C, 123 km).
