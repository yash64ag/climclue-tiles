Guanajay sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Artemisa · Cuba

The sea off Guanajay averages 30.3 °C (87 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 23.9 °C (75 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to December.

Guanajay sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Guanajay?
In August, at about 30.3 °C (87 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Guanajay?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to December. The coolest month is January, at 23.9 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Guanajay in January?
About 23.9 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Guanajay?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
