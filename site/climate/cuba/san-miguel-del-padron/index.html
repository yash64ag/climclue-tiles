San Miguel del Padrón at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cuba, San Miguel del Padrón is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,949 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,487 mm a year). Compared with Guanabacoa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), San Miguel del Padrón is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Miguel del Padrón, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Miguel del Padrón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
