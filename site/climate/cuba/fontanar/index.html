Fontanar at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cuba, Fontanar is 13th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 2,947 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,394 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arroyo Naranjo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Fontanar is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 93 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.6 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fontanar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fontanar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
