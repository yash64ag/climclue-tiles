How June compares in Santiago de Cuba

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Santiago de Cuba. Compared with May: 1.0 °C warmer, 40 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. July is 0.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Santiago de Cuba in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Cuba in June?
In June, Santiago de Cuba averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Santiago de Cuba?
June is not one of Santiago de Cuba’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Cuba in June?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (183 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Cuba in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Cuba get in June?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Cuba in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
