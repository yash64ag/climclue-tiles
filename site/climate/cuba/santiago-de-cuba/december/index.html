How December compares in Santiago de Cuba

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Santiago de Cuba. Compared with November: 1.0 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Santiago de Cuba in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Cuba in December?
In December, Santiago de Cuba averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Santiago de Cuba?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Santiago de Cuba’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Cuba in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (183 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Cuba in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Cuba get in December?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Cuba in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
