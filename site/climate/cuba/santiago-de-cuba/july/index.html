Santiago de Cuba weather in July

Average July weather in Santiago de Cuba, Cuba: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Santiago de Cuba averages 33.2 °C (92 °F) days and 23.2 °C (74 °F) nights, with 99 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 224 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Santiago de Cuba

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Santiago de Cuba. Compared with June: 0.6 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Santiago de Cuba in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Cuba in July?
In July, Santiago de Cuba averages a day high of 33.2 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Santiago de Cuba?
July is not one of Santiago de Cuba’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Cuba in July?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (168 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Cuba in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Cuba get in July?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Santiago de Cuba in July?
About 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Santiago de Cuba in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
