How January compares in Santiago de Cuba

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Santiago de Cuba. Compared with December: 0.5 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Santiago de Cuba in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Cuba in January?
In January, Santiago de Cuba averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Santiago de Cuba?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Santiago de Cuba’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Cuba in January?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (183 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Cuba in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Cuba get in January?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Cuba in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
