How November compares in Santiago de Cuba

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santiago de Cuba. Compared with October: 0.9 °C cooler, 101 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. December is 1.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Santiago de Cuba in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santiago de Cuba in November?
In November, Santiago de Cuba averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Santiago de Cuba?
November is not one of Santiago de Cuba’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santiago de Cuba in November?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (183 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Santiago de Cuba in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santiago de Cuba get in November?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santiago de Cuba in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
