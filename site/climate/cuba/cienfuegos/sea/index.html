Cienfuegos sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Cienfuegos Province · Cuba

The sea off Cienfuegos averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 25.9 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Cienfuegos sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cienfuegos?
In September, at about 29.9 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cienfuegos?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.9 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cienfuegos in February?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Cienfuegos?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
