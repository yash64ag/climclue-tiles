Puerto Padre sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Las Tunas Province · Cuba

The sea off Puerto Padre averages 29.2 °C (85 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 25.5 °C (78 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Puerto Padre sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto Padre?
In September, at about 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto Padre?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.5 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto Padre in February?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto Padre?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Puerto Padre nearby?
In September: Manzanillo (30.2 °C, 109 km), Campechuela (30.2 °C, 128 km) and Media Luna (30.2 °C, 146 km).
