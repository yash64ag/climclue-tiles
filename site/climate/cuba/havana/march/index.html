How March compares in Havana

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Havana. Compared with February: 0.9 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. April is 1.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Havana in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Havana in March?
In March, Havana averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Havana?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Havana’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Havana in March?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (230 mm), the driest March (49 mm).
Does it snow in Havana in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Havana get in March?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Havana in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
