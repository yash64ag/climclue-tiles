How May compares in Havana

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Havana. Compared with April: 1.5 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. June is 1.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Havana in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Havana in May?
In May, Havana averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Havana?
May is not one of Havana’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Havana in May?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest March (49 mm).
Does it snow in Havana in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Havana get in May?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Havana in May?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (29.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Havana in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
