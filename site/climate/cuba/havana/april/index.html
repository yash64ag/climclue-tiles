How April compares in Havana

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Havana. Compared with March: 1.3 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 1.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Havana in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Havana in April?
In April, Havana averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Havana?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Havana’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Havana in April?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (230 mm), the driest March (49 mm).
Does it snow in Havana in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Havana get in April?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Havana in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
