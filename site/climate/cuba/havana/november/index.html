How November compares in Havana

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Havana. Compared with October: 1.7 °C cooler, 65 mm less precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. December is 1.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Havana in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Havana in November?
In November, Havana averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Havana?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Havana’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Havana in November?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (225 mm), the driest March (49 mm).
Does it snow in Havana in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Havana get in November?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Havana in November?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (29.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Havana in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
