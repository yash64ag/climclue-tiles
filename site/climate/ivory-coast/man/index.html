Man at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ivory Coast, Man is the coolest (24.5 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,205 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (1,758 mm a year). Compared with Duekoué, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Man is 1.4 °C cooler in February and 1.7 °C cooler in August, with 228 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.3 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, August (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.3 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 321 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Man, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Man

For mild weather, the best months to visit Man are January, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): February (26.3 °C), March (26.1 °C) and April (25.6 °C).

Why Man has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 321 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
