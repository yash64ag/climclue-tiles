Why Marcory has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Marcory climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Marcory?
March, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 29.3 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 25.0 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Marcory?
August, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 25.6 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 22.6 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Marcory?
June, with 447 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,876 mm (73.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Marcory?
January, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Marcory?
November, with about 216 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Marcory?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Marcory?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Marcory’s GeoNames location (5.312°N, 3.994°W; ClimaClue area sq-786-1760), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 214,061 (GeoNames).
