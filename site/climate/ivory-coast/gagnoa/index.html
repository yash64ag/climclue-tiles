Gagnoa at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ivory Coast, Gagnoa is the 2nd coolest (25.4 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 1,977 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sinfra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Gagnoa is 0.5 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 84 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, July (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gagnoa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gagnoa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
