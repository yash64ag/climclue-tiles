Diawala at a glance

Among the 182 towns ClimaClue covers in Ivory Coast, Diawala is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,689 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nambingué, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Diawala is 0.2 °C cooler in March and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 57 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.8 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 567 km from the sea.

Why Diawala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 567 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Diawala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.107°N, 5.473°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-738-1745), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
