Abidjan weather in April

Average April weather in Abidjan, Ivory Coast: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Abidjan averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 23.4 °C (74 °F) nights, with 170 mm (6.7 in) of rain over the month and about 212 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Abidjan

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Abidjan. Compared with March: about as warm, 50 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Abidjan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in April?
In April, Abidjan averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Abidjan?
April is not one of Abidjan’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in April?
170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (449 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in April?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Abidjan in April?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Abidjan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
