How December compares in Abidjan

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Abidjan. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 78 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Abidjan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in December?
In December, Abidjan averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Abidjan?
December is not one of Abidjan’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in December?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (449 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in December?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Abidjan in December?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Abidjan in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
