How July compares in Abidjan

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Abidjan. Compared with June: 0.9 °C cooler, 298 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Abidjan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in July?
In July, Abidjan averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Abidjan?
July is not one of Abidjan’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in July?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (449 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in July?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abidjan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
