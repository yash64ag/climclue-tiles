How October compares in Abidjan

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Abidjan. Compared with September: 0.9 °C warmer, 122 mm more precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. November is 0.8 °C warmer than October on average.

Abidjan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in October?
In October, Abidjan averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Abidjan?
October is not one of Abidjan’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in October?
214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (449 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Abidjan in October?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Abidjan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
