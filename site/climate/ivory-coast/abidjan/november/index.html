How November compares in Abidjan

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Abidjan. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 49 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. December is 0.1 °C warmer than November on average.

Abidjan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in November?
In November, Abidjan averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Abidjan?
November is not one of Abidjan’s mild months; those are August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in November?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (454 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in November?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abidjan in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
