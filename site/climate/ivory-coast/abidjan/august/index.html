How August compares in Abidjan

August is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Abidjan. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 99 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Abidjan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in August?
In August, Abidjan averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Abidjan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Abidjan’s mild months (August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in August?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (454 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in August?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abidjan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
