How September compares in Abidjan

September is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Abidjan. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. October is 0.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Abidjan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abidjan in September?
In September, Abidjan averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Abidjan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Abidjan’s mild months (August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abidjan in September?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (454 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Abidjan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abidjan get in September?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abidjan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
