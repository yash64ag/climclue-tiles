Abobo weather in September

Average September weather in Abobo, Ivory Coast: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Abobo averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) days and 21.3 °C (70 °F) nights, with 107 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 128 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Abobo

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Abobo. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Abobo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abobo in September?
In September, Abobo averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Abobo?
September is not one of Abobo’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abobo in September?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (479 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Abobo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abobo get in September?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Abobo in September?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Abobo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
