Abobo weather in December

Average December weather in Abobo, Ivory Coast: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Abobo averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 22.0 °C (72 °F) nights, with 100 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 198 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Abobo

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Abobo. Compared with November: about as warm, 90 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Abobo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abobo in December?
In December, Abobo averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Abobo?
December is not one of Abobo’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abobo in December?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (479 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Abobo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abobo get in December?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Abobo in December?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Abobo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
