Duekoué at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ivory Coast, Duekoué is 10th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,095 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,530 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Man, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Duekoué is 1.4 °C warmer in February and 1.7 °C warmer in August, with 228 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.6 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in December. The area lies about 246 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Duekoué, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Duekoué has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 246 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
