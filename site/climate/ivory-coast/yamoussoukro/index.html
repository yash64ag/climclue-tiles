Yamoussoukro at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ivory Coast, Yamoussoukro is the 4th warmest (26.3 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,004 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bouaflé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Yamoussoukro is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 142 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (28.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 200 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yamoussoukro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yamoussoukro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 200 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
