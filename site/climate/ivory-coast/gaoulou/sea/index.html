Gaoulou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bas-Sassandra District · Ivory Coast

The sea off Gaoulou averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 23.9 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Gaoulou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gaoulou?
In May, at about 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gaoulou?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.9 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gaoulou in August?
About 23.9 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Gaoulou?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
