Daloa at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ivory Coast, Daloa is 14th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,073 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,274 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bonon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Daloa is 0.6 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in August, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 221 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Daloa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Daloa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 221 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
