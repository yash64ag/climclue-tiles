How August compares in Bouaké

August is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bouaké. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Bouaké in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bouaké in August?
In August, Bouaké averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bouaké?
No month in Bouaké is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bouaké in August?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bouaké in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bouaké get in August?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bouaké in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
