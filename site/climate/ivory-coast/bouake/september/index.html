How September compares in Bouaké

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bouaké. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Bouaké in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bouaké in September?
In September, Bouaké averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bouaké?
No month in Bouaké is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bouaké in September?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bouaké in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bouaké get in September?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bouaké in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
