Bouaké weather in March

Average March weather in Bouaké, Ivory Coast: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Bouaké averages 33.7 °C (93 °F) days and 22.0 °C (72 °F) nights, with 101 mm (4.0 in) of rain over the month and about 201 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Bouaké

March is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bouaké. Compared with February: 0.1 °C warmer, 57 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. April is 0.6 °C cooler than March on average.

Bouaké in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bouaké in March?
In March, Bouaké averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bouaké?
No month in Bouaké is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bouaké in March?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (175 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bouaké in March?

How many hours of sunshine does Bouaké get in March?
