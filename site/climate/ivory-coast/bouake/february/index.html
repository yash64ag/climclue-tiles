How February compares in Bouaké

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bouaké. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Bouaké in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bouaké in February?
In February, Bouaké averages a day high of 34.1 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bouaké?
No month in Bouaké is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bouaké in February?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bouaké in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bouaké get in February?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bouaké in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
