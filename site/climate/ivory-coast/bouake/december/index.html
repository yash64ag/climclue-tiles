How December compares in Bouaké

December is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bouaké. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Bouaké in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bouaké in December?
In December, Bouaké averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bouaké?
No month in Bouaké is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bouaké in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bouaké in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bouaké get in December?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bouaké in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
