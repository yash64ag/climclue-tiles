Bouaké in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bouaké in April?
In April, Bouaké averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bouaké?
No month in Bouaké is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bouaké in April?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (171 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bouaké in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bouaké get in April?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bouaké in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
