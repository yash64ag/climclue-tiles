Komotiní at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Komotiní is 103rd warmest (14.9 °C year average), 85th sunniest (about 2,707 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (499 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xánthi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Komotiní is 1.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 247 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.0 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Komotiní, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Komotiní has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Komotiní itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.118°N, 25.406°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-428-2054), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
