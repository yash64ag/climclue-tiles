How October compares in Athens

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Athens. Compared with September: 4.7 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. November is 4.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Athens in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in October?
In October, Athens averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Athens?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Athens’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (68 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in October?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Athens in October?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Athens in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
