How May compares in Athens

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Athens. Compared with April: 4.8 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 62 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Athens in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in May?
In May, Athens averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Athens?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Athens’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in May?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (72 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in May?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Athens in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
