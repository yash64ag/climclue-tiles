How February compares in Athens

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Athens. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 2.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Athens in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in February?
In February, Athens averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Athens?
February is not one of Athens’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in February?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (68 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in February?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Athens in February?
About 14.0 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Athens in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
