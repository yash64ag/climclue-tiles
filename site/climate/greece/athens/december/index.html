How December compares in Athens

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Athens. Compared with November: 3.5 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Athens in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in December?
In December, Athens averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Athens?
December is not one of Athens’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in December?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (72 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in December?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Athens in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
