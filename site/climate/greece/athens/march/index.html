How March compares in Athens

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Athens. Compared with February: 2.2 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. April is 3.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Athens in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in March?
In March, Athens averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Athens?
March is not one of Athens’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in March?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (72 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in March?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Athens in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
