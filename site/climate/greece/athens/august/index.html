How August compares in Athens

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Athens. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. September is 4.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Athens in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in August?
In August, Athens averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 355 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Athens?
August is not one of Athens’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in August?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (68 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in August?
About 355 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Athens in August?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Athens in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
