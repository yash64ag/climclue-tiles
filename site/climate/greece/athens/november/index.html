How November compares in Athens

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Athens. Compared with October: 4.6 °C cooler, 29 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 3.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Athens in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in November?
In November, Athens averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Athens?
November is not one of Athens’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in November?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (72 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in November?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Athens in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
