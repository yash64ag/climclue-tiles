How April compares in Athens

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Athens. Compared with March: 3.6 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Athens in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Athens in April?
In April, Athens averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Athens?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Athens’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Athens in April?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (68 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Athens in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Athens get in April?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Athens in April?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Athens in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
