Chaniá at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Chaniá is the 2nd warmest (19.1 °C year average). Compared with Rethymno, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Chaniá is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.7 °C), averages 14.7 °C more than its coolest, February (12.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Chaniá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chaniá itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.511°N, 24.029°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-484-2040), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
