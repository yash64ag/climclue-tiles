Pátra at a glance

Among the 8 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Greece, Pátra is the 3rd coolest (17.2 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,925 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (881 mm a year). Compared with Lárisa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (165 km away), Pátra is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 424 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pátra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pátra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
