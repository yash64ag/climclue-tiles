Why Pátra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Pátra climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Pátra?
August, with an average of 26.4 °C (80 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 22.1 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Pátra?
January, with an average of 9.3 °C (49 °F), day highs around 11.4 °C (53 °F) and night lows around 5.7 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Pátra?
November, with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 881 mm (34.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Pátra?
July, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Pátra?
July, with about 399 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Pátra?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 6.0 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Pátra?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.7 × 11 km, that contains Pátra’s GeoNames location (38.246°N, 21.735°E; ClimaClue area sq-457-2017), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 168,034 (GeoNames).
