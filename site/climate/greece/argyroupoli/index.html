Argyroúpoli at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Argyroúpoli is 40th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,843 hours of sunshine a year) and 73rd wettest (409 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ellinikó, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Argyroúpoli is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (9.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Argyroúpoli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
