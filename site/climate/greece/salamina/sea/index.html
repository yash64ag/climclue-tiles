Salamína sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Attica · Greece

The sea off Salamína averages 25.3 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.7 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Salamína sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Salamína?
In August, at about 25.3 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Salamína?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.7 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Salamína in February?
About 13.7 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Salamína?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
