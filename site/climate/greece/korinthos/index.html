Kórinthos at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Kórinthos is the 3rd driest (355 mm a year). Compared with Mégara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Kórinthos is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kórinthos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-460-2029), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
