Chalkída sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Greece · Greece

The sea off Chalkída averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.3 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Chalkída sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chalkída?
In August, at about 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chalkída?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 13.3 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chalkída in February?
About 13.3 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Chalkída?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Chalkída nearby?
In August: Acharnés (25.7 °C, 44 km), Peristéri (25.7 °C, 51 km) and Athens (25.7 °C, 54 km).
