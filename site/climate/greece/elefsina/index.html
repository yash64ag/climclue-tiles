Elefsína weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Elefsína, Greece (Attica), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Elefsína is warmest in July, with 34.6 °C (94 °F) days and 22.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 13.3 °C (56 °F) days and 6.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around December (59 mm); August is driest (6 mm).

Elefsína at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Elefsína is the 2nd driest (347 mm a year). Compared with Asprópyrgos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Elefsína is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 24 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 97% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Elefsína has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Elefsína itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.041°N, 23.543°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-459-2035), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
