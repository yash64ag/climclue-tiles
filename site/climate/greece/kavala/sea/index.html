Kavála sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · East Macedonia and Thrace · Greece

The sea off Kavála averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.0 °C (52 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Kavála sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kavála?
In August, at about 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kavála?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 11.0 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kavála in February?
About 11.0 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kavála?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kavála nearby?
In August: Kalamariá (25.9 °C, 129 km), Peraía (25.9 °C, 134 km) and Kateríni (25.9 °C, 177 km).
