Agrínio at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Agrínio is the 4th wettest (1,013 mm a year). Compared with Pátra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Agrínio is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 164 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Why Agrínio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C. The 10 km area around Agrínio, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.0 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
