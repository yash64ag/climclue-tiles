Thessaloníki at a glance

Among the 8 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Greece, Thessaloníki is the coolest (15.6 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (549 mm a year). Compared with Lárisa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (121 km away), Thessaloníki is 1.7 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thessaloníki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thessaloníki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
