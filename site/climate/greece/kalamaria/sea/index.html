Kalamariá sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Macedonia · Greece

The sea off Kalamariá averages 25.9 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.5 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Kalamariá sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kalamariá?
In August, at about 25.9 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kalamariá?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 12.5 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kalamariá in February?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kalamariá?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
