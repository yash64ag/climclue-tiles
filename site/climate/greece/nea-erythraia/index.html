Néa Erythraía at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Néa Erythraía is 74th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,865 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (417 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kifisiá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Néa Erythraía is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Why Néa Erythraía has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
