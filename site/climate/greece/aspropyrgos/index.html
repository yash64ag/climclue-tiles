Asprópyrgos at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Asprópyrgos is the driest (341 mm a year). Compared with Elefsína, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Asprópyrgos is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 19.0 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Asprópyrgos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
