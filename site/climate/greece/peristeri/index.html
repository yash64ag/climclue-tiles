Peristéri at a glance

Among the 8 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Greece, Peristéri is the 3rd warmest (17.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,854 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (392 mm a year). Compared with Athens, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Peristéri is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Peristéri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
