Néa Ionía, Thessaly at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Néa Ionía, Thessaly is 84th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 2,687 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Volos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Néa Ionía, Thessaly is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.2 °C), averages 17.3 °C more than its coolest, January (7.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Néa Ionía, Thessaly has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
