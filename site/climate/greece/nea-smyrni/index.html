Néa Smýrni at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Néa Smýrni is 22nd warmest (18.3 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,843 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (406 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kallithéa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Néa Smýrni is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.0 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Néa Smýrni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
