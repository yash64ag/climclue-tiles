Vári at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Greece, Vári is 15th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,844 hours of sunshine a year) and 108th wettest (371 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Voúla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Vári is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Vári has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-461-2038), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
