Gargždai at a glance

Among the 20 towns ClimaClue covers in Lithuania, Gargždai is the 4th warmest (7.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,801 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (881 mm a year). Compared with Klaipėda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Gargždai is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in February, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.8 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 21 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 116% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Gargždai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
