Kaunas at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Lithuania, Kaunas is the 2nd warmest (7.3 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,653 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (672 mm a year). Compared with Vilnius, the nearest covered town with different numbers (91 km away), Kaunas is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 179 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kaunas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 179 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
