Marijampolė at a glance

Among the 20 towns ClimaClue covers in Lithuania, Marijampolė is the 5th warmest (7.5 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 1,633 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (646 mm a year). Compared with Alytus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Marijampolė is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.2 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 152 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Marijampolė, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Marijampolė has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 152 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Marijampolė itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.558°N, 23.350°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-294-2033), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
