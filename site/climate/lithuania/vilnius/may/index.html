How May compares in Vilnius

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Vilnius. Compared with April: 5.9 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 78 hours more sunshine. June is 2.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Vilnius in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vilnius in May?
In May, Vilnius averages a day high of 17.0 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Vilnius?
May is not one of Vilnius’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vilnius in May?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Vilnius in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vilnius get in May?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vilnius in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
