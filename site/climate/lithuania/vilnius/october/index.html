How October compares in Vilnius

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Vilnius. Compared with September: 5.1 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Vilnius in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vilnius in October?
In October, Vilnius averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Vilnius?
October is not one of Vilnius’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vilnius in October?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Vilnius in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Vilnius get in October?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vilnius in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
