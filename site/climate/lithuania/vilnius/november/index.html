How November compares in Vilnius

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Vilnius. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. December is 4.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Vilnius in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vilnius in November?
In November, Vilnius averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 39 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Vilnius?
November is not one of Vilnius’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vilnius in November?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Vilnius in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vilnius get in November?
About 39 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vilnius in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
