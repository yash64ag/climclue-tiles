How September compares in Vilnius

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Vilnius. Compared with August: 5.0 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 86 hours less sunshine. October is 5.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Vilnius in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vilnius in September?
In September, Vilnius averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Vilnius?
September is not one of Vilnius’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vilnius in September?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Vilnius in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vilnius get in September?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vilnius in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
