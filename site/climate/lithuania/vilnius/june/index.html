How June compares in Vilnius

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Vilnius. Compared with May: 2.8 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Vilnius in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vilnius in June?
In June, Vilnius averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Vilnius?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Vilnius’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vilnius in June?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Vilnius in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vilnius get in June?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vilnius in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
