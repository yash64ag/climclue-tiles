How July compares in Vilnius

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Vilnius. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Vilnius in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vilnius in July?
In July, Vilnius averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Vilnius?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Vilnius’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vilnius in July?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (40 mm).
Does it snow in Vilnius in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vilnius get in July?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vilnius in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
