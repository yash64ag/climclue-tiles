How June compares in Cotonou

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cotonou. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 115 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. July is 1.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Cotonou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cotonou in June?
In June, Cotonou averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cotonou?
June is not one of Cotonou’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cotonou in June?
304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (304 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Cotonou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cotonou get in June?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cotonou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
