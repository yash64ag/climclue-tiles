How December compares in Cotonou

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cotonou. Compared with November: about as warm, 23 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Cotonou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cotonou in December?
In December, Cotonou averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cotonou?
December is not one of Cotonou’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cotonou in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (304 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Cotonou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cotonou get in December?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cotonou in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
