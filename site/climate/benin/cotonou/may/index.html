How May compares in Cotonou

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cotonou. Compared with April: 0.5 °C cooler, 77 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Cotonou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cotonou in May?
In May, Cotonou averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cotonou?
May is not one of Cotonou’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cotonou in May?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (307 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Cotonou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cotonou get in May?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cotonou in May?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cotonou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
