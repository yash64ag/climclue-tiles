How January compares in Cotonou

January is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cotonou. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Cotonou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cotonou in January?
In January, Cotonou averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cotonou?
January is not one of Cotonou’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cotonou in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (304 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Cotonou in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cotonou get in January?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cotonou in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
