How July compares in Cotonou

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cotonou. Compared with June: 1.1 °C cooler, 173 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Cotonou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cotonou in July?
In July, Cotonou averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cotonou?
July is not one of Cotonou’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cotonou in July?
131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (304 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Cotonou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cotonou get in July?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cotonou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
