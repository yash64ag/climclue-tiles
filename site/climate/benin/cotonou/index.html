Cotonou at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Benin, Cotonou is the 3rd coolest (26.6 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,234 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,227 mm a year). Compared with Godomè, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Cotonou is about as warm in March and about as warm in August, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.9 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cotonou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cotonou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
