Ségbana at a glance

Among the 66 towns ClimaClue covers in Benin, Ségbana is the 3rd warmest (28.5 °C year average), the 4th sunniest (about 2,826 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (1,000 mm a year). Compared with Kalalé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Ségbana is 2.2 °C warmer in April and 0.9 °C warmer in August, with 166 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (33.1 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, August (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in December. The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ségbana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ségbana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 511 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ségbana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.928°N, 3.694°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-730-1836), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
