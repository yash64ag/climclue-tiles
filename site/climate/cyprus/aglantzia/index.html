Aglantziá weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Aglantziá, Cyprus (Nicosia), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Aglantziá is warmest in July, with 36.9 °C (98 °F) days and 23.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 15.6 °C (60 °F) days and 6.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around January (75 mm); August is driest (2 mm).

Aglantziá at a glance

Among the 16 towns ClimaClue covers in Cyprus, Aglantziá is 9th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 3,138 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (385 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nicosia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Aglantziá is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.4 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aglantziá, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aglantziá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aglantziá itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.154°N, 33.396°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-488-2133), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
