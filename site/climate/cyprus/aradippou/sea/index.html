Aradíppou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Larnaka · Cyprus

The sea off Aradíppou averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.3 °C (61 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Aradíppou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Aradíppou?
In August, at about 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Aradíppou?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.3 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Aradíppou in February?
About 16.3 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Aradíppou?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Aradíppou nearby?
In August: Égkomi (27.5 °C, 36 km), Famagusta (27.5 °C, 38 km) and Kyrenia (27.5 °C, 50 km).
