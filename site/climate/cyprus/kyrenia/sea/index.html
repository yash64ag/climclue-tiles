Kyrenia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Keryneia · Cyprus

The sea off Kyrenia averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.0 °C (61 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Kyrenia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kyrenia?
In August, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kyrenia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.0 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kyrenia in February?
About 16.0 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kyrenia?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
