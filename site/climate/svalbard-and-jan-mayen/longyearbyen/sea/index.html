Longyearbyen sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Svalbard · Svalbard and Jan Mayen

The sea off Longyearbyen averages 2.3 °C (36 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.7 °C (29 °F) in April. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Longyearbyen sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Longyearbyen?
In August, at about 2.3 °C (36 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Longyearbyen?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is April, at -1.7 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Longyearbyen in April?
About -1.7 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Longyearbyen?
About -0.5 °C (31 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
