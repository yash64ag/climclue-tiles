Bắc Quang at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Bắc Quang is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,623 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (3,847 mm a year). Compared with Tuyên Quang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Bắc Quang is 0.7 °C cooler in June and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 1,992 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.6 °C), averages 12.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 288 km from the sea.

Why Bắc Quang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 288 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
