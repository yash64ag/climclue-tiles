Xóm Cái Nước sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · An Giang · Vietnam

The sea off Xóm Cái Nước averages 30.7 °C (87 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 27.0 °C (81 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Xóm Cái Nước sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xóm Cái Nước?
In May, at about 30.7 °C (87 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xóm Cái Nước?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.0 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xóm Cái Nước in January?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Xóm Cái Nước?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
