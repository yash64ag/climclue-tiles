Cam Ranh sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Khanh Hoa · Vietnam

The sea off Cam Ranh averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 24.5 °C (76 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Cam Ranh sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cam Ranh?
In May, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cam Ranh?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 24.5 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cam Ranh in January?
About 24.5 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Cam Ranh?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Cam Ranh nearby?
In May: Phú Quý (29.3 °C, 158 km) and La Gi (29.4 °C, 206 km).
