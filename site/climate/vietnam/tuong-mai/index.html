Tương Mai at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Tương Mai is 102nd warmest (24.1 °C year average), 111th sunniest (about 1,667 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (1,896 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hai Bà Trưng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Tương Mai is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tương Mai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tương Mai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
