How January compares in Cần Thơ

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cần Thơ. Compared with December: 0.1 °C cooler, 38 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Cần Thơ in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cần Thơ in January?
In January, Cần Thơ averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cần Thơ?
No month in Cần Thơ is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cần Thơ in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (299 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cần Thơ in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cần Thơ get in January?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cần Thơ in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
