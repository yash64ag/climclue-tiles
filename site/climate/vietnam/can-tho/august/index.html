Cần Thơ in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cần Thơ in August?
In August, Cần Thơ averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cần Thơ?
No month in Cần Thơ is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cần Thơ in August?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (299 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cần Thơ in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cần Thơ get in August?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cần Thơ in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
