Cần Thơ in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cần Thơ in June?
In June, Cần Thơ averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cần Thơ?
No month in Cần Thơ is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (299 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cần Thơ in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cần Thơ get in June?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
