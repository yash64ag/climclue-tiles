Cần Thơ in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cần Thơ in November?
In November, Cần Thơ averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cần Thơ?
No month in Cần Thơ is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cần Thơ in November?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (299 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cần Thơ in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cần Thơ get in November?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cần Thơ in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
