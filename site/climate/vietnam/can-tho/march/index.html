Cần Thơ in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cần Thơ in March?
In March, Cần Thơ averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cần Thơ?
No month in Cần Thơ is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cần Thơ in March?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (299 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cần Thơ in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cần Thơ get in March?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
