Cần Thơ at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Cần Thơ is 41st warmest (27.1 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (1,720 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ô Môn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Cần Thơ is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.9 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 88 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cần Thơ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cần Thơ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 88 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
