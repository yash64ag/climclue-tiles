How March compares in Vinh

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Vinh. Compared with February: 2.6 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 3.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Vinh in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vinh in March?
In March, Vinh averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Vinh?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Vinh’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vinh in March?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (596 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Vinh in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vinh get in March?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vinh in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
