How December compares in Vinh

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Vinh. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 103 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Vinh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vinh in December?
In December, Vinh averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Vinh?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Vinh’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vinh in December?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (596 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Vinh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vinh get in December?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vinh in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
