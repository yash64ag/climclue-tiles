How February compares in Vinh

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Vinh. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. March is 2.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Vinh in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vinh in February?
In February, Vinh averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Vinh?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Vinh’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vinh in February?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (596 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Vinh in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vinh get in February?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vinh in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
