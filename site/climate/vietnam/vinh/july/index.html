How July compares in Vinh

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Vinh. Compared with June: 0.2 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Vinh in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vinh in July?
In July, Vinh averages a day high of 34.1 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 26.8 °C (80 °F), with 146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Vinh?
July is not one of Vinh’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vinh in July?
146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (596 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Vinh in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vinh get in July?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vinh in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
