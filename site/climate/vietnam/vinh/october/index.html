How October compares in Vinh

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Vinh. Compared with September: 2.5 °C cooler, 140 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. November is 3.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Vinh in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vinh in October?
In October, Vinh averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 599 mm (23.6 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Vinh?
October is not one of Vinh’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vinh in October?
599 mm (23.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (599 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Vinh in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vinh get in October?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vinh in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
