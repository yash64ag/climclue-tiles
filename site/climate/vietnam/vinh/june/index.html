How June compares in Vinh

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Vinh. Compared with May: 1.9 °C warmer, 66 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. July is 0.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Vinh in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vinh in June?
In June, Vinh averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Vinh?
June is not one of Vinh’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vinh in June?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (596 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Vinh in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vinh get in June?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vinh in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
