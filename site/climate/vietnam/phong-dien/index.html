Phong Điền at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Phong Điền is the 5th sunniest (about 2,264 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (3,094 mm a year). Compared with Huế, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Phong Điền is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 145 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phong Điền, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phong Điền has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Phong Điền itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.583°N, 107.364°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-674-2873), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
