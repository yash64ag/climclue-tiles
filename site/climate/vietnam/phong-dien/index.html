Phong Điền at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Phong Điền is the 5th sunniest (about 2,264 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (3,144 mm a year). Compared with Huế, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Phong Điền is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phong Điền, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phong Điền has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
