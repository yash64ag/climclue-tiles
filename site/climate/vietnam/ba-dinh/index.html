Ba Dinh at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ba Dinh is 94th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 122nd sunniest (about 1,645 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (1,874 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thanh Xuân, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Ba Dinh is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 107 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ba Dinh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Same area as Hanoi: Ba Dinh lies in the same ~10 km ClimaClue area as Hanoi, so its numbers are the same as there.

Why Ba Dinh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 107 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
