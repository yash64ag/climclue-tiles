Đông Phú weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Đông Phú, Vietnam (Da Nang City), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Đông Phú is warmest in June, with 33.9 °C (93 °F) days and 25.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 24.8 °C (77 °F) days and 18.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around October (810 mm); February is driest (37 mm).

Đông Phú at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Đông Phú is the 4th wettest (3,178 mm a year). Compared with Điện Bàn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Đông Phú is about as warm in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 521 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.9 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, January (21.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Đông Phú, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Đông Phú are February and March.

Why Đông Phú has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Đông Phú itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.674°N, 108.220°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-683-2882), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
