Đồng Xoài at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Đồng Xoài is 43rd warmest (27.1 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 1,920 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (2,346 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chơn Thành, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Đồng Xoài is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 105 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.9 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in December. The area lies about 144 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Đồng Xoài, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Đồng Xoài has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
