Tân An at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Tân An is 26th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 1,812 hours of sunshine a year) and 117th wettest (1,716 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mỹ Tho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Tân An is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.1 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tân An, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tân An has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
