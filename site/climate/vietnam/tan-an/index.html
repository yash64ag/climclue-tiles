Tân An at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Tân An is 28th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 1,812 hours of sunshine a year) and 112th wettest (1,709 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mỹ Tho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Tân An is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.1 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tân An, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tân An has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tân An itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.536°N, 106.414°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-734-2864), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
