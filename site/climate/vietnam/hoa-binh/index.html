Hòa Bình at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hòa Bình is 125th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 106th sunniest (about 1,693 hours of sunshine a year) and 75th wettest (1,865 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ba Vì, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Hòa Bình is 0.4 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 73 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.7 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hòa Bình, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hòa Bình has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
