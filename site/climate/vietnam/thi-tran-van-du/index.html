Thị Trấn Vân Du at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Vân Du is 264th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 154th sunniest (about 1,814 hours of sunshine a year) and 119th wettest (1,923 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bỉm Sơn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Thị Trấn Vân Du is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 154 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.3 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (17.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Why Thị Trấn Vân Du has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thị Trấn Vân Du itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.152°N, 105.731°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-638-2857), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
