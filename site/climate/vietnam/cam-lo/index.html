Cam Lộ at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Cam Lộ is 148th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,265 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (2,344 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ðông Hà, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Cam Lộ is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 150 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.1 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cam Lộ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cam Lộ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cam Lộ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.808°N, 106.994°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-671-2869), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
