Ninh Hòa at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Ninh Hòa is 75th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,020 hours of sunshine a year) and 271st wettest (1,644 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nha Trang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Ninh Hòa is 0.9 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 65 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.8 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in December. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ninh Hòa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Ninh Hòa

For mild weather, the best months to visit Ninh Hòa are January and February.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January and February.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (29.8 °C), July (29.6 °C) and June (29.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (12 mm), March (29 mm) and July (43 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): March (203 h), April (197 h) and January (182 h).

Why Ninh Hòa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ninh Hòa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.492°N, 109.125°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-715-2891), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
