Ninh Hòa at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ninh Hòa is 53rd warmest (26.8 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 2,020 hours of sunshine a year) and 119th wettest (1,714 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nha Trang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Ninh Hòa is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 83 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.4 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.5 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ninh Hòa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ninh Hòa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
