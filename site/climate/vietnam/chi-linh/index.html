Chí Linh at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Chí Linh is 114th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 1,671 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,829 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hải Dương, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Chí Linh is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chí Linh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chí Linh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chí Linh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.067°N, 106.317°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-629-2863), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
