Dĩ An at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Dĩ An is 19th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 90th sunniest (about 1,735 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (2,038 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thủ Đức, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Dĩ An is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 153 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dĩ An, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dĩ An has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
