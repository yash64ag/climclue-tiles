Dien Bien Phu weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dien Bien Phu, Vietnam (Điện Biên Province), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dien Bien Phu at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Dien Bien Phu is 280th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 150th sunniest (about 1,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 304th wettest (1,325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mường Lay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Dien Bien Phu is 0.9 °C warmer in June and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 723 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.0 °C), averages 9.0 °C more than its coolest, January (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dien Bien Phu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dien Bien Phu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.0 °C. The 10 km area around Dien Bien Phu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.0 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dien Bien Phu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.386°N, 103.023°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-626-2830), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
