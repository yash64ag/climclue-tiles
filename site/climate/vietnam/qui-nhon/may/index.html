How May compares in Qui Nhon

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Qui Nhon. Compared with April: 1.6 °C warmer, 87 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. June is 0.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Qui Nhon in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qui Nhon in May?
In May, Qui Nhon averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 128 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Qui Nhon?
May is not one of Qui Nhon’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qui Nhon in May?
128 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (728 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Qui Nhon in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qui Nhon get in May?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qui Nhon in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
