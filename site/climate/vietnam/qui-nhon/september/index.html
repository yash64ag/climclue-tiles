How September compares in Qui Nhon

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Qui Nhon. Compared with August: 1.1 °C cooler, 185 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. October is 1.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Qui Nhon in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qui Nhon in September?
In September, Qui Nhon averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Qui Nhon?
September is not one of Qui Nhon’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qui Nhon in September?
284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (728 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Qui Nhon in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qui Nhon get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qui Nhon in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
