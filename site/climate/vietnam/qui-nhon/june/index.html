How June compares in Qui Nhon

June is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Qui Nhon. Compared with May: 0.8 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Qui Nhon in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qui Nhon in June?
In June, Qui Nhon averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 26.7 °C (80 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Qui Nhon?
June is not one of Qui Nhon’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qui Nhon in June?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (702 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Qui Nhon in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qui Nhon get in June?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qui Nhon in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
