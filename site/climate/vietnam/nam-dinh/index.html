Nam Định at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Nam Định is 88th warmest (24.2 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 1,764 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (1,891 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hưng Yên, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Nam Định is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.9 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nam Định, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nam Định has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
