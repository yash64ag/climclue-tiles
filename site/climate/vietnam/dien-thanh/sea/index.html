Diễn Thành sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nghệ An Province · Vietnam

The sea off Diễn Thành averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 20.8 °C (69 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Diễn Thành sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Diễn Thành?
In August, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Diễn Thành?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 20.8 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in Diễn Thành in February?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Diễn Thành?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
