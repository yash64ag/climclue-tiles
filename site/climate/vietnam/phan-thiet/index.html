Phan Thiết at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Phan Thiết is the 2nd driest (1,177 mm a year). Compared with La Gi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Phan Thiết is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 620 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.3 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phan Thiết, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phan Thiết has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
