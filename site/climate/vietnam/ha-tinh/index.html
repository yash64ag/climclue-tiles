Hà Tĩnh at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hà Tĩnh is 81st warmest (24.9 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,137 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (2,714 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vinh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Hà Tĩnh is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 485 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hà Tĩnh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Hà Tĩnh

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hà Tĩnh are January, February and March.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and March.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (29.9 °C), June (29.8 °C) and August (29.3 °C).

Why Hà Tĩnh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
