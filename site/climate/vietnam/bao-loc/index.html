Bảo Lộc at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Bảo Lộc is 135th warmest (22.3 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 1,835 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (2,671 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huyện Lâm Hà, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Bảo Lộc is 0.2 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 827 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (23.6 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, December (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 84 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bảo Lộc, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bảo Lộc has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 84 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-724-2878), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
