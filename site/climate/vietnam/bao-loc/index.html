Bảo Lộc at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Bảo Lộc is 134th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 1,835 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (2,514 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huyện Lâm Hà, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Bảo Lộc is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in December, with 585 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (23.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, December (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.4 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 84 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bảo Lộc, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bảo Lộc has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 84 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
