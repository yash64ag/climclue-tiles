Thốt Nốt at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thốt Nốt is 29th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,073 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (1,651 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Long Xuyên, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Thốt Nốt is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.1 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thốt Nốt, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thốt Nốt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
