Phú Quý at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Phú Quý is 120th warmest (26.4 °C year average) and 289th wettest (1,509 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mui Ne, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Phú Quý is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 504 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.9 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phú Quý, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Phú Quý

For mild weather, the best months to visit Phú Quý are January, February and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (27.9 °C), June (27.7 °C) and July (27.4 °C).

Why Phú Quý has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Phú Quý itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.517°N, 108.933°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-734-2889), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
