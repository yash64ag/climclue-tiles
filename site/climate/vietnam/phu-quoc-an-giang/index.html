Phú Quốc at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Phú Quốc is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,315 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Phu Quoc, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Phú Quốc is 0.5 °C cooler in April and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 197 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.4 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.4 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phú Quốc, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phú Quốc has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
