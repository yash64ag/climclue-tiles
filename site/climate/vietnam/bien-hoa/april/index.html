How April compares in Biên Hòa

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Biên Hòa. Compared with March: 0.9 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Biên Hòa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Biên Hòa in April?
In April, Biên Hòa averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Biên Hòa?
No month in Biên Hòa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Biên Hòa in April?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (320 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Biên Hòa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Biên Hòa get in April?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Biên Hòa in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
