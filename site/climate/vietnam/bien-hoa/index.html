Biên Hòa at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Biên Hòa is 24th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 1,741 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,976 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dĩ An, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Biên Hòa is about as warm in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 62 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Biên Hòa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Biên Hòa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
