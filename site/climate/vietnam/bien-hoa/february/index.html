How February compares in Biên Hòa

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Biên Hòa. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. March is 1.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Biên Hòa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Biên Hòa in February?
In February, Biên Hòa averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Biên Hòa?
No month in Biên Hòa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Biên Hòa in February?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (320 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Biên Hòa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Biên Hòa get in February?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Biên Hòa in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
