Biên Hòa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Biên Hòa in October?
In October, Biên Hòa averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Biên Hòa?
No month in Biên Hòa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Biên Hòa in October?
276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (320 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Biên Hòa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Biên Hòa get in October?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Biên Hòa in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
