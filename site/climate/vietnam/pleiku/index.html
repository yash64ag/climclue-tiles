Pleiku at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Pleiku is 128th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,110 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (2,525 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kon Tum, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Pleiku is 0.6 °C cooler in April and 0.4 °C cooler in December, with 811 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.1 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, December (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pleiku, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pleiku has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
