Long Xuyên at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Long Xuyên is 23rd warmest (27.3 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,131 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,640 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thốt Nốt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Long Xuyên is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Long Xuyên, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Long Xuyên has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
