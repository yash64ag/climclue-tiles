Nghĩa Lộ at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Nghĩa Lộ is 272nd warmest (23.8 °C year average), 261st sunniest (about 1,648 hours of sunshine a year) and 300th wettest (1,448 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yên Bái, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Nghĩa Lộ is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 310 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 254 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nghĩa Lộ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nghĩa Lộ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 254 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C. The 10 km area around Nghĩa Lộ, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.6 °C a year, 2.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nghĩa Lộ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.600°N, 104.517°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-623-2845), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
