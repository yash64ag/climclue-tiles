How May compares in Hanoi

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hanoi. Compared with April: 3.0 °C warmer, 121 mm more precipitation and 75 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Hanoi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanoi in May?
In May, Hanoi averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 240 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hanoi?
May is not one of Hanoi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanoi in May?
240 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (328 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Hanoi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanoi get in May?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanoi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
