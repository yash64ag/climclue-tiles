How April compares in Hanoi

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hanoi. Compared with March: 3.9 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. May is 3.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Hanoi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanoi in April?
In April, Hanoi averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hanoi?
April is not one of Hanoi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanoi in April?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (327 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Hanoi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanoi get in April?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanoi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
