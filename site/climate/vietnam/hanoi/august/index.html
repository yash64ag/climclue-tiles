How August compares in Hanoi

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hanoi. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Hanoi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanoi in August?
In August, Hanoi averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hanoi?
August is not one of Hanoi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanoi in August?
322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (327 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Hanoi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanoi get in August?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanoi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
