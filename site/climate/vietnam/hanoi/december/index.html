Hanoi weather in December

Average December weather in Hanoi, Vietnam: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Hanoi averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) days and 14.8 °C (59 °F) nights, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 128 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Hanoi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hanoi. Compared with November: 3.6 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Hanoi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanoi in December?
In December, Hanoi averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hanoi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Hanoi’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanoi in December?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (327 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Hanoi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanoi get in December?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanoi in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
