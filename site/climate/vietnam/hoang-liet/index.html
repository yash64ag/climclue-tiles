Hoàng Liệt weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Hoàng Liệt, Vietnam (Hanoi), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Hoàng Liệt is warmest in June, with 33.0 °C (91 °F) days and 26.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 19.7 °C (67 °F) days and 14.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (322 mm); December is driest (26 mm).

Hoàng Liệt at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Hoàng Liệt is 217th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 230th sunniest (about 1,667 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (1,901 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bạch Mai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Hoàng Liệt is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hoàng Liệt, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Hoàng Liệt

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hoàng Liệt are January, February, March, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (29.6 °C), July (29.6 °C) and August (28.8 °C).

Why Hoàng Liệt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hoàng Liệt itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.964°N, 105.841°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-630-2858), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
