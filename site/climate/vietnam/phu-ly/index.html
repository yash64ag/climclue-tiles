Phủ Lý at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Phủ Lý is 105th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 1,729 hours of sunshine a year) and 90th wettest (1,824 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hưng Yên, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Phủ Lý is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phủ Lý, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phủ Lý has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
