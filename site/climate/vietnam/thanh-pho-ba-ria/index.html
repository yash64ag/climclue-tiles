Thành Phố Bà Rịa at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thành Phố Bà Rịa is 37th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 1,755 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (1,804 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phú Mỹ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Thành Phố Bà Rịa is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 401 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.6 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thành Phố Bà Rịa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thành Phố Bà Rịa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
