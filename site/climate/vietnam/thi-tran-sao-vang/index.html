Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Sao Vàng is 256th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 136th sunniest (about 1,882 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (1,886 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Lam Sơn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Thị Trấn Sao Vàng is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Why Thị Trấn Sao Vàng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
