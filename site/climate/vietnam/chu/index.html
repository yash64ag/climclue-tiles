Chũ at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Chũ is 122nd warmest (23.5 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 1,659 hours of sunshine a year) and 132nd wettest (1,603 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Đông Triều, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Chũ is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 225 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 12.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chũ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chũ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
