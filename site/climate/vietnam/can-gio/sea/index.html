Cần Giờ sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC) · Vietnam

The sea off Cần Giờ averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 25.8 °C (78 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Cần Giờ sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cần Giờ?
In May, at about 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cần Giờ?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 25.8 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cần Giờ in January?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Cần Giờ?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
