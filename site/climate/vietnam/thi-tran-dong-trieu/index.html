Thị Trấn Đông Triều at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Đông Triều is 115th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 107th sunniest (about 1,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (1,828 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chí Linh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Thị Trấn Đông Triều is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.2 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thị Trấn Đông Triều, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thị Trấn Đông Triều has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
