Cẩm Phả at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Cẩm Phả is 130th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 1,728 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (2,464 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cẩm Phả Mines, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Cẩm Phả is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 301 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 11.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cẩm Phả, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cẩm Phả has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
