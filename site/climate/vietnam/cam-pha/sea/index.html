Cẩm Phả sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Quảng Ninh · Vietnam

The sea off Cẩm Phả averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.6 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Cẩm Phả sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cẩm Phả?
In August, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cẩm Phả?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.6 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cẩm Phả in February?
About 18.6 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Cẩm Phả?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
