Thị Trấn Trùng Khánh at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Trùng Khánh is the 3rd coolest (20.4 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 1,552 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Cao Bằng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Thị Trấn Trùng Khánh is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 373 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 211 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thị Trấn Trùng Khánh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thị Trấn Trùng Khánh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 211 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
