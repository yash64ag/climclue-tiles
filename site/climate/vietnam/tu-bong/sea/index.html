Tu Bông sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Khanh Hoa · Vietnam

The sea off Tu Bông averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 23.5 °C (74 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Tu Bông sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tu Bông?
In September, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tu Bông?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is January, at 23.5 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tu Bông in January?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Tu Bông?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Tu Bông nearby?
In September: Tuy Hòa (28.8 °C, 37 km), Sông Cầu (29.1 °C, 77 km) and Qui Nhon (29.0 °C, 112 km).
