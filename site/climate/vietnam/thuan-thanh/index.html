Thuận Thanh at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thuận Thanh is 101st warmest (24.1 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 1,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (1,865 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mỹ Hào, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Thuận Thanh is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thuận Thanh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thuận Thanh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
