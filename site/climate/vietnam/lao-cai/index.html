Lào Cai at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Lào Cai is 132nd warmest (22.9 °C year average), 132nd sunniest (about 1,634 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (1,835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bắc Quang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (92 km away), Lào Cai is 0.5 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 2,012 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.1 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 357 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lào Cai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lào Cai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 357 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
