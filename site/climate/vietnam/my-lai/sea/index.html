Mỹ Lại sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Quang Ngai · Vietnam

The sea off Mỹ Lại averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 23.8 °C (75 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Mỹ Lại sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mỹ Lại?
In August, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mỹ Lại?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.8 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mỹ Lại in February?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Mỹ Lại?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
