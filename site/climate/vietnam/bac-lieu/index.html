Bạc Liêu at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Bạc Liêu is 51st warmest (27.0 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,012 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (2,010 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vĩnh Châu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Bạc Liêu is about as warm in April and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 117 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.4 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bạc Liêu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bạc Liêu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
