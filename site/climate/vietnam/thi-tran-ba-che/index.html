Thị Trấn Ba Chẽ at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Ba Chẽ is 289th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 208th sunniest (about 1,715 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (2,069 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Tiên Yên, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Thị Trấn Ba Chẽ is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 107 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.2 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Why Thị Trấn Ba Chẽ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
