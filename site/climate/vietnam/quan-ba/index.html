Why Quận Ba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Quận Ba climate: questions and answers

April, with an average of 29.4 °C (85 °F), day highs around 34.0 °C (93 °F) and night lows around 25.2 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Quận Ba?
December, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 21.8 °C (71 °F).

What is the driest month in Quận Ba?

What is the sunniest month in Quận Ba?

Does it snow in Quận Ba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Quận Ba?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Quận Ba’s GeoNames location (10.775°N, 106.686°E; ClimaClue area sq-732-2866), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 220,375 (GeoNames).
