Cà Mau at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Cà Mau is 36th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (2,427 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Giá Rai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Cà Mau is 0.1 °C warmer in April and about as warm in December, with 237 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.6 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, December (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cà Mau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cà Mau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
