Thủ Dầu Một at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thủ Dầu Một is 19th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 171st sunniest (about 1,756 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (1,933 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thuận An, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Thủ Dầu Một is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.5 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 81 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thủ Dầu Một, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thủ Dầu Một has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 81 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thủ Dầu Một itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.980°N, 106.652°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-730-2866), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
