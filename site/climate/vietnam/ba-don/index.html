Ba Đồn at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ba Đồn is 77th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,202 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (2,539 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kỳ Anh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Ba Đồn is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ba Đồn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ba Đồn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
