Thanh Khê at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thanh Khê is 70th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,231 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (2,617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Da Nang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Thanh Khê is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 169 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 7.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.6 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thanh Khê, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thanh Khê has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
