Móng Cái at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Móng Cái is 284th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 178th sunniest (about 1,749 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (2,993 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Đầm Hà, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Móng Cái is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 221 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Móng Cái, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Móng Cái has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Móng Cái itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.525°N, 107.966°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-624-2879), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
