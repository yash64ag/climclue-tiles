How January compares in Thuận An

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Thuận An. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Thuận An in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thuận An in January?
In January, Thuận An averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Thuận An?
No month in Thuận An is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thuận An in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (331 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Thuận An in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thuận An get in January?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thuận An in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
