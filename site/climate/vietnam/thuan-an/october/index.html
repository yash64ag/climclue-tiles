Thuận An in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thuận An in October?
In October, Thuận An averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Thuận An?
No month in Thuận An is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thuận An in October?
289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (331 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Thuận An in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thuận An get in October?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thuận An in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
