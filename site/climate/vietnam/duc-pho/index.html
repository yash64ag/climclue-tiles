Đức Phổ at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Đức Phổ is 65th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,200 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (2,784 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quảng Ngãi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Đức Phổ is 0.6 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Đức Phổ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Đức Phổ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
