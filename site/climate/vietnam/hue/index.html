Huế at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Huế is the 3rd wettest (3,151 mm a year). Compared with Phong Điền, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Huế is about as warm in June and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, January (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huế, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Huế

For mild weather, the best months to visit Huế are February and March.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February and March.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (29.4 °C), July (29.4 °C) and August (29.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): March (50 mm), February (59 mm) and April (66 mm).

Why Huế has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
