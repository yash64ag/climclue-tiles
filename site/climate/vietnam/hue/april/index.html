How April compares in Huế

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Huế. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. May is 1.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Huế in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huế in April?
In April, Huế averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Huế?
April is not one of Huế’s mild months; those are February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huế in April?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (871 mm), the driest March (50 mm).
Does it snow in Huế in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huế get in April?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huế in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
