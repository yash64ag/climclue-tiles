How May compares in Huế

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Huế. Compared with April: 1.5 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Huế in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huế in May?
In May, Huế averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Huế?
May is not one of Huế’s mild months; those are February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huế in May?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (838 mm), the driest March (45 mm).
Does it snow in Huế in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huế get in May?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Huế in May?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (29.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Huế in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
