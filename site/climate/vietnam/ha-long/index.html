Hạ Long at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hạ Long is 121st warmest (23.7 °C year average), 85th sunniest (about 1,743 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (1,964 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cẩm Phả, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Hạ Long is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 500 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hạ Long, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hạ Long has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
