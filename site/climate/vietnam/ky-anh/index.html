Kỳ Anh at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Kỳ Anh is 82nd warmest (24.8 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,158 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (2,639 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ba Đồn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Kỳ Anh is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kỳ Anh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kỳ Anh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
