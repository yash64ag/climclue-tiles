Hòa Thành at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Hòa Thành is the 2nd warmest (27.8 °C year average). Compared with Phú Khương, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Hòa Thành is about as warm in April and about as warm in December, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.9 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hòa Thành, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Hòa Thành

No month in Hòa Thành is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are January (27.0 °C, 12 mm), November (26.9 °C, 122 mm) and December (26.6 °C, 35 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): April (29.9 °C), March (29.1 °C) and May (29.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (8 mm), January (12 mm) and March (32 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): March (203 h), April (200 h) and February (193 h).

Why Hòa Thành has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hòa Thành itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.287°N, 106.129°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-727-2861), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
