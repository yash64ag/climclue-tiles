Đố Sơn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hai Phong · Vietnam

The sea off Đố Sơn averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 19.2 °C (67 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Đố Sơn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Đố Sơn?
In August, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Đố Sơn?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 19.2 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Đố Sơn in February?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Đố Sơn?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
