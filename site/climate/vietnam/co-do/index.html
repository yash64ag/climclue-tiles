Why Cờ Đỏ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cờ Đỏ climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cờ Đỏ?
April, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 33.8 °C (93 °F) and night lows around 24.7 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cờ Đỏ?
January, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 21.4 °C (71 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cờ Đỏ?
October, with 301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,806 mm (71.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cờ Đỏ?
February, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cờ Đỏ?
March, with about 210 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cờ Đỏ?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cờ Đỏ?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Cờ Đỏ’s GeoNames location (10.094°N, 105.429°E; ClimaClue area sq-739-2854), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 116,576 (GeoNames).
