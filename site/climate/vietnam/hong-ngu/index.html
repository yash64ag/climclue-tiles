Hồng Ngự at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hồng Ngự is the 3rd warmest (27.8 °C year average). Compared with Tân Châu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Hồng Ngự is 0.1 °C warmer in April and about as warm in December, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.9 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, December (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hồng Ngự, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hồng Ngự has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
