Từ Sơn at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Từ Sơn is 214th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 290th sunniest (about 1,640 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (1,886 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phúc Lợi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Từ Sơn is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 107 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Từ Sơn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Từ Sơn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 107 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Từ Sơn itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.100°N, 105.967°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-2859), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
