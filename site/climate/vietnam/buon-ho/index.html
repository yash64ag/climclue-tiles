Buôn Hồ at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Buôn Hồ is 126th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (2,430 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buôn Ma Thuột, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Buôn Hồ is 2.4 °C cooler in April and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 507 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.4 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 127 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Buôn Hồ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Buôn Hồ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 127 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
