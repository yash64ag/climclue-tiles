Hoa Lư at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hoa Lư is 104th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 1,794 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (1,808 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nam Định, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Hoa Lư is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 83 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hoa Lư, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hoa Lư has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
