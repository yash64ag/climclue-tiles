Why Da Nang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Da Nang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Da Nang?
June, with an average of 29.6 °C (85 °F), day highs around 32.0 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 27.4 °C (81 °F).
What is the coldest month in Da Nang?
January, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 23.3 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 20.2 °C (68 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Da Nang?
October, with 692 mm (27.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,448 mm (96.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Da Nang?
February, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Da Nang?
March, with about 211 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Da Nang?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Da Nang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February and March.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Da Nang’s GeoNames location (16.068°N, 108.221°E; ClimaClue area sq-679-2882), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,276,000 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
