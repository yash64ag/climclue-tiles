How August compares in Da Nang

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Da Nang. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 51 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 1.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Da Nang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in August?
In August, Da Nang averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Da Nang?
August is not one of Da Nang’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in August?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in August?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
