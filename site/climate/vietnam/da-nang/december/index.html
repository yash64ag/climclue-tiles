How December compares in Da Nang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Da Nang. Compared with November: 2.0 °C cooler, 238 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Da Nang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in December?
In December, Da Nang averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Da Nang?
December is not one of Da Nang’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in December?
265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in December?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
