Da Nang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Da Nang City · Vietnam

The sea off Da Nang averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 22.8 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Da Nang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Da Nang?
In August, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Da Nang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 22.8 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Da Nang in February?
About 22.8 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Da Nang?
About 26.4 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
