How October compares in Da Nang

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Da Nang. Compared with September: 1.6 °C cooler, 383 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. November is 1.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Da Nang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in October?
In October, Da Nang averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 692 mm (27.3 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Da Nang?
October is not one of Da Nang’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in October?
692 mm (27.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in October?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
