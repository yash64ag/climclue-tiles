How January compares in Da Nang

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Da Nang. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 176 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Da Nang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in January?
In January, Da Nang averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Da Nang?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Da Nang’s mild months (January, February and March), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in January?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in January?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
