How February compares in Da Nang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Da Nang. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 61 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. March is 1.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Da Nang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in February?
In February, Da Nang averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Da Nang?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Da Nang’s mild months (January, February and March), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in February?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in February?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
