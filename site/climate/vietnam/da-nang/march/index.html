How March compares in Da Nang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Da Nang. Compared with February: 1.5 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. April is 2.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Da Nang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in March?
In March, Da Nang averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Da Nang?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Da Nang’s mild months (January, February and March), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in March?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in March?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
