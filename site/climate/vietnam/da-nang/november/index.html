How November compares in Da Nang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Da Nang. Compared with October: 1.7 °C cooler, 189 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. December is 2.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Da Nang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Da Nang in November?
In November, Da Nang averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 504 mm (19.8 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Da Nang?
November is not one of Da Nang’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Da Nang in November?
504 mm (19.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (692 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Da Nang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Da Nang get in November?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Da Nang in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
