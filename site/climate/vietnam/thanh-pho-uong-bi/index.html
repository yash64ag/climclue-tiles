Thành Phố Uông Bí at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thành Phố Uông Bí is 275th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 216th sunniest (about 1,688 hours of sunshine a year) and 197th wettest (1,852 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Mạo Khê, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Thành Phố Uông Bí is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Why Thành Phố Uông Bí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
