Phổ Yên at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Phổ Yên is 268th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 297th sunniest (about 1,630 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,902 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thành phố Sông Công, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Phổ Yên is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 112 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 137 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phổ Yên, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phổ Yên has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Phổ Yên itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.420°N, 105.890°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-625-2858), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
