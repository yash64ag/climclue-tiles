Thị Trấn Phước Bửu at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Phước Bửu is 102nd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 163rd sunniest (about 1,766 hours of sunshine a year) and 121st wettest (1,922 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Đất Đỏ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Thị Trấn Phước Bửu is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 65 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.3 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thị Trấn Phước Bửu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thị Trấn Phước Bửu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-734-2874), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
