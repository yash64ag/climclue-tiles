Thị Trấn Phước Bửu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC) · Vietnam

The sea off Thị Trấn Phước Bửu averages 29.4 °C (85 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 25.1 °C (77 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Thị Trấn Phước Bửu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Thị Trấn Phước Bửu?
In May, at about 29.4 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Thị Trấn Phước Bửu?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 25.1 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Thị Trấn Phước Bửu in January?
About 25.1 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Thị Trấn Phước Bửu?
About 27.5 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
