Giá Rai at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Giá Rai is 39th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,069 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (2,190 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bạc Liêu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Giá Rai is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 213 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.6 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Giá Rai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Giá Rai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
