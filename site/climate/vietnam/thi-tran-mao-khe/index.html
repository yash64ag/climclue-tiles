Thị Trấn Mạo Khê at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Mạo Khê is 270th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 217th sunniest (about 1,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 227th wettest (1,788 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Đông Triều, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Thị Trấn Mạo Khê is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.2 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Why Thị Trấn Mạo Khê has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thị Trấn Mạo Khê itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.059°N, 106.593°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-629-2865), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
