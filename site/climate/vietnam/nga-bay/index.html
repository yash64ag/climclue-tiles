Ngã Bảy at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ngã Bảy is 49th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 1,985 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,824 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cần Thơ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Ngã Bảy is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.9 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.0 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ngã Bảy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ngã Bảy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
