Hà Tiên at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hà Tiên is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,327 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Phú Quốc, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Hà Tiên is 1.3 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 1,051 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.8 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hà Tiên, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hà Tiên has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
