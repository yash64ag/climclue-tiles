Sóc Sơn at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Sóc Sơn is 249th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 292nd sunniest (about 1,638 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (1,934 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quang Minh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Sóc Sơn is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 122 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sóc Sơn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sóc Sơn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 122 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sóc Sơn itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.258°N, 105.849°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-627-2858), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
