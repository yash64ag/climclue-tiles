Lạng Sơn at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Lạng Sơn is the 3rd coolest (21.6 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 1,618 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Chũ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Lạng Sơn is 1.6 °C cooler in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 95 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lạng Sơn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lạng Sơn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
