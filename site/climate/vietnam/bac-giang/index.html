Bắc Giang at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Bắc Giang is 113th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 1,646 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (1,846 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bắc Ninh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Bắc Giang is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bắc Giang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bắc Giang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
