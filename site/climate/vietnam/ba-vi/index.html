Ba Vì at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ba Vì is 127th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 1,675 hours of sunshine a year) and 105th wettest (1,791 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sơn Tây, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Ba Vì is 1.1 °C cooler in June and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 93 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.4 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 151 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ba Vì, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ba Vì has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 151 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
