Haiphong at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Haiphong is 109th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 1,723 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (1,852 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Đông Triều, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Haiphong is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Haiphong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Haiphong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
