How July compares in Haiphong

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Haiphong. Compared with June: 0.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Haiphong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Haiphong in July?
In July, Haiphong averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 285 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Haiphong?
July is not one of Haiphong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Haiphong in July?
285 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (369 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Haiphong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Haiphong get in July?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Haiphong in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
