How December compares in Haiphong

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Haiphong. Compared with November: 3.5 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Haiphong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Haiphong in December?
In December, Haiphong averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Haiphong?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Haiphong’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Haiphong in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (369 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Haiphong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Haiphong get in December?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Haiphong in December?
About 21.9 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Haiphong in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
