How October compares in Haiphong

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Haiphong. Compared with September: 2.3 °C cooler, 117 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. November is 3.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Haiphong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Haiphong in October?
In October, Haiphong averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Haiphong?
October is not one of Haiphong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Haiphong in October?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (363 mm), the driest December (24 mm).
Does it snow in Haiphong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Haiphong get in October?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Haiphong in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
