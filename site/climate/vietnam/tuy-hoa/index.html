Tuy Hòa at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Tuy Hòa is 119th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 2,102 hours of sunshine a year) and 75th wettest (2,206 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tu Bông, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Tuy Hòa is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 206 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.1 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Tuy Hòa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tuy Hòa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.095°N, 109.321°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-709-2893), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
