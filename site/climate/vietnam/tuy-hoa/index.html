Tuy Hòa at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Tuy Hòa is 61st warmest (26.4 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 2,102 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (2,216 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ninh Hòa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Tuy Hòa is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 502 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tuy Hòa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tuy Hòa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
