Đưc Trọng at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Đưc Trọng is 135th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 1,854 hours of sunshine a year) and 112th wettest (1,743 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huyện Lâm Hà, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Đưc Trọng is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 186 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (23.3 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, December (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Đưc Trọng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Đưc Trọng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
