How June compares in Ho Chi Minh City

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ho Chi Minh City. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 62 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Ho Chi Minh City in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City in June?
In June, Ho Chi Minh City averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ho Chi Minh City?
No month in Ho Chi Minh City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ho Chi Minh City in June?
258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (300 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ho Chi Minh City in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ho Chi Minh City get in June?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ho Chi Minh City in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
