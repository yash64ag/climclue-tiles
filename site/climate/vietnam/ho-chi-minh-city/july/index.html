How July compares in Ho Chi Minh City

July is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ho Chi Minh City. Compared with June: 0.5 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Ho Chi Minh City in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City in July?
In July, Ho Chi Minh City averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ho Chi Minh City?
No month in Ho Chi Minh City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ho Chi Minh City in July?
276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (300 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ho Chi Minh City in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ho Chi Minh City get in July?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ho Chi Minh City in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
