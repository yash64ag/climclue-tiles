How May compares in Ho Chi Minh City

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ho Chi Minh City. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 137 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Ho Chi Minh City in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City in May?
In May, Ho Chi Minh City averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ho Chi Minh City?
No month in Ho Chi Minh City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ho Chi Minh City in May?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (301 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ho Chi Minh City in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ho Chi Minh City get in May?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ho Chi Minh City in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
