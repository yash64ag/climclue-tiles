How January compares in Ho Chi Minh City

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ho Chi Minh City. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ho Chi Minh City ranks 8th warmest (average temperature), 75th sunniest and 104th wettest in January.

Ho Chi Minh City in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City in January?
In January, Ho Chi Minh City averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ho Chi Minh City?
No month in Ho Chi Minh City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ho Chi Minh City in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (301 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ho Chi Minh City in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ho Chi Minh City get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ho Chi Minh City in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
