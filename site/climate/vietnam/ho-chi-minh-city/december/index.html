How December compares in Ho Chi Minh City

December is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ho Chi Minh City. Compared with November: 0.5 °C cooler, 99 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Of the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ho Chi Minh City ranks 9th warmest (average temperature), 97th sunniest and 77th wettest in December.

Ho Chi Minh City in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City in December?
In December, Ho Chi Minh City averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ho Chi Minh City?
No month in Ho Chi Minh City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ho Chi Minh City in December?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (301 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ho Chi Minh City in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ho Chi Minh City get in December?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ho Chi Minh City in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
