How February compares in Ho Chi Minh City

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ho Chi Minh City. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. March is 1.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Ho Chi Minh City in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ho Chi Minh City in February?
In February, Ho Chi Minh City averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ho Chi Minh City?
No month in Ho Chi Minh City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ho Chi Minh City in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (301 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ho Chi Minh City in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ho Chi Minh City get in February?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ho Chi Minh City in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
