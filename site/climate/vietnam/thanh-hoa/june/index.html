How June compares in Thanh Hóa

June is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Thanh Hóa. Compared with May: 1.8 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. July is about as warm as June on average.

Thanh Hóa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thanh Hóa in June?
In June, Thanh Hóa averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Thanh Hóa?
June is not one of Thanh Hóa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thanh Hóa in June?
187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (347 mm), the driest February (21 mm).
Does it snow in Thanh Hóa in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thanh Hóa get in June?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thanh Hóa in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
