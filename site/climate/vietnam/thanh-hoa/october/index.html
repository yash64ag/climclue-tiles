How October compares in Thanh Hóa

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Thanh Hóa. Compared with September: 2.5 °C cooler, 77 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 3.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Thanh Hóa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thanh Hóa in October?
In October, Thanh Hóa averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Thanh Hóa?
October is not one of Thanh Hóa’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thanh Hóa in October?
270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (347 mm), the driest February (21 mm).
Does it snow in Thanh Hóa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thanh Hóa get in October?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thanh Hóa in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
