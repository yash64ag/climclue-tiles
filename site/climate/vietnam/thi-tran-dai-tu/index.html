Thị Trấn Đại Từ at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Đại Từ is the least sunny (about 1,613 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Thái Nguyên, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Thị Trấn Đại Từ is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 63 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.6 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 167 km from the sea.

Why Thị Trấn Đại Từ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 167 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
