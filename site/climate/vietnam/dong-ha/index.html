Ðông Hà at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ðông Hà is 73rd warmest (25.8 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,262 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (2,502 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phong Điền, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Ðông Hà is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 643 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.1 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ðông Hà, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ðông Hà has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
