Vĩnh Yên at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Vĩnh Yên is 181st warmest (24.2 °C year average), 294th sunniest (about 1,634 hours of sunshine a year) and 174th wettest (1,872 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phúc Yên, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Vĩnh Yên is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.4 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 144 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vĩnh Yên, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vĩnh Yên has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vĩnh Yên itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.309°N, 105.605°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-626-2856), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
