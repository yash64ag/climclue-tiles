How September compares in Nha Trang

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nha Trang. Compared with August: 0.9 °C cooler, 140 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Nha Trang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in September?
In September, Nha Trang averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nha Trang?
September is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in September?
217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (419 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in September?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
