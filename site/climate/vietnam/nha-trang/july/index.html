How July compares in Nha Trang

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nha Trang. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Nha Trang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in July?
In July, Nha Trang averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nha Trang?
July is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in July?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (419 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in July?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
