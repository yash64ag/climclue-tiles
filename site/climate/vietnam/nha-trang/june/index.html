How June compares in Nha Trang

June is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nha Trang. Compared with May: 0.1 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Nha Trang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in June?
In June, Nha Trang averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nha Trang?
June is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in June?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (419 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in June?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
