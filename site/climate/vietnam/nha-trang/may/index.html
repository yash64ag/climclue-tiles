How May compares in Nha Trang

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nha Trang. Compared with April: 1.2 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. June is 0.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Nha Trang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in May?
In May, Nha Trang averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nha Trang?
May is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in May?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (428 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in May?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nha Trang in May?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
