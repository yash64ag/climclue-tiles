How March compares in Nha Trang

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Nha Trang. Compared with February: 1.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. April is 1.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Nha Trang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in March?
In March, Nha Trang averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nha Trang?
March is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in March?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (419 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in March?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
