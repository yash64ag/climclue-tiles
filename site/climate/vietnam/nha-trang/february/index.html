How February compares in Nha Trang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nha Trang. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 35 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. March is 1.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Nha Trang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in February?
In February, Nha Trang averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nha Trang?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Nha Trang’s mild months (January and February), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in February?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (428 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in February?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nha Trang in February?
About 24.9 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is September (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
