How October compares in Nha Trang

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nha Trang. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 179 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 1.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Nha Trang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in October?
In October, Nha Trang averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 395 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nha Trang?
October is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in October?
395 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (419 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in October?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
