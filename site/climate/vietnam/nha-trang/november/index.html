How November compares in Nha Trang

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nha Trang. Compared with October: 1.2 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. December is 1.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Nha Trang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nha Trang in November?
In November, Nha Trang averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 419 mm (16.5 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nha Trang?
November is not one of Nha Trang’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nha Trang in November?
419 mm (16.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (419 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Nha Trang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nha Trang get in November?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nha Trang in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
