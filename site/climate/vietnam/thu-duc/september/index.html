Thủ Đức weather in September

Average September weather in Thủ Đức, Vietnam: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Thủ Đức averages 30.7 °C (87 °F) days and 23.1 °C (74 °F) nights, with 307 mm (12.1 in) of rain over the month and about 155 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Thủ Đức

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Thủ Đức. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. October is 0.2 °C cooler than September on average.
