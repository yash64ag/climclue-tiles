How September compares in Thủ Đức

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Thủ Đức. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. October is 0.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Thủ Đức in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thủ Đức in September?
In September, Thủ Đức averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Thủ Đức?
No month in Thủ Đức is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thủ Đức in September?
305 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (305 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Thủ Đức in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thủ Đức get in September?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thủ Đức in September?
