Thủ Đức in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thủ Đức in July?
In July, Thủ Đức averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Thủ Đức?
No month in Thủ Đức is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thủ Đức in July?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (305 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Thủ Đức in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thủ Đức get in July?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thủ Đức in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
