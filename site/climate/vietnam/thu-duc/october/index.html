How October compares in Thủ Đức

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Thủ Đức. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. November is 0.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Thủ Đức in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thủ Đức in October?
In October, Thủ Đức averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Thủ Đức?
No month in Thủ Đức is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thủ Đức in October?
272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (305 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Thủ Đức in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thủ Đức get in October?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thủ Đức in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
