Thủ Đức weather in January

January · Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC) · Vietnam

Average January weather in Thủ Đức: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How January compares in Thủ Đức

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Thủ Đức. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Thủ Đức in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thủ Đức in January?
In January, Thủ Đức averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Thủ Đức?
No month in Thủ Đức is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thủ Đức in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (307 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Thủ Đức in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thủ Đức get in January?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thủ Đức in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
