Thủ Đức weather in June

Average June weather in Thủ Đức, Vietnam: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Thủ Đức averages 31.4 °C (89 °F) days and 23.7 °C (75 °F) nights, with 263 mm (10.4 in) of rain over the month and about 121 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Thủ Đức

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Thủ Đức. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 56 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.
