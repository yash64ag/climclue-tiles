How June compares in Thủ Đức

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Thủ Đức. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 57 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Thủ Đức in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thủ Đức in June?
In June, Thủ Đức averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Thủ Đức?
No month in Thủ Đức is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thủ Đức in June?
263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (305 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Thủ Đức in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thủ Đức get in June?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thủ Đức in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
