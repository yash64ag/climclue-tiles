Thị Trấn Tiên Yên at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Tiên Yên is 288th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 211th sunniest (about 1,712 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (2,176 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thị Trấn Ba Chẽ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Thị Trấn Tiên Yên is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 107 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.2 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Thị Trấn Tiên Yên has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thị Trấn Tiên Yên itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.331°N, 107.405°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-626-2874), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
