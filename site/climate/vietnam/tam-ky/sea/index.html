Tam Kỳ sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Da Nang City · Vietnam

The sea off Tam Kỳ averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December.

Tam Kỳ sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tam Kỳ?
In August, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tam Kỳ?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to December. The coolest month is February, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tam Kỳ in February?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Tam Kỳ?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
