Tam Kỳ at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Tam Kỳ is the 2nd wettest (3,241 mm a year). Compared with Điện Bàn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Tam Kỳ is 0.8 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 512 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tam Kỳ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tam Kỳ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
