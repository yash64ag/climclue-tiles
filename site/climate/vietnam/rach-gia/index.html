Rạch Giá at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Rạch Giá is 28th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,178 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (2,092 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cờ Đỏ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Rạch Giá is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 286 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.9 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rạch Giá, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rạch Giá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
