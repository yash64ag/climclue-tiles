Cẩm Phả Mines at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Cẩm Phả Mines is 129th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 87th sunniest (about 1,740 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (2,766 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cẩm Phả, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Cẩm Phả Mines is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 301 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cẩm Phả Mines, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cẩm Phả Mines has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
