Cẩm Phả Mines at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Cẩm Phả Mines is 291st warmest (22.7 °C year average), 186th sunniest (about 1,740 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (2,765 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cẩm Phả, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Cẩm Phả Mines is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 269 mm more precipitation a year. Why Cẩm Phả Mines has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cẩm Phả Mines itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.017°N, 107.300°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-629-2873), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
