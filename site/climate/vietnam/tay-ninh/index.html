Tây Ninh at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Tây Ninh is the warmest (27.9 °C year average). Compared with Hòa Thành, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Tây Ninh is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, December (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 150 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tây Ninh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tây Ninh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 150 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
