Vinhomes Smart City at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Vinhomes Smart City is 212th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 251st sunniest (about 1,649 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (1,852 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tây Mỗ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Vinhomes Smart City is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vinhomes Smart City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vinhomes Smart City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vinhomes Smart City itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.004°N, 105.740°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-629-2857), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
