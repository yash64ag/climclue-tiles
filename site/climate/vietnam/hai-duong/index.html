Hải Dương at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Hải Dương is 110th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 105th sunniest (about 1,696 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (1,803 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chí Linh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Hải Dương is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 61 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hải Dương, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hải Dương has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 61 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hải Dương itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.941°N, 106.333°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-630-2863), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
