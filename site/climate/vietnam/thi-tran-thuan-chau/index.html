Thị Trấn Thuận Châu at a glance

Among the 310 towns ClimaClue covers in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Thuận Châu is 303rd warmest (21.9 °C year average), 187th sunniest (about 1,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 278th wettest (1,613 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chiềng Cơi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Thị Trấn Thuận Châu is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 280 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.8 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 307 km from the sea.

Why Thị Trấn Thuận Châu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C. The 10 km area around Thị Trấn Thuận Châu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 20.5 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
