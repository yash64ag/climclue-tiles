Thị Trấn Thuận Châu at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Thị Trấn Thuận Châu is the 2nd coolest (20.5 °C year average). Compared with Sơn La, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Thị Trấn Thuận Châu is 1.3 °C cooler in June and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 445 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.4 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in December. The area lies about 307 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thị Trấn Thuận Châu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thị Trấn Thuận Châu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
