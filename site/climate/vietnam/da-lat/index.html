Ðà Lạt at a glance

Among the 140 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Vietnam, Ðà Lạt is the coolest (18.6 °C year average). Compared with Đưc Trọng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Ðà Lạt is 3.1 °C cooler in May and 4.0 °C cooler in January, with 471 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.9 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ðà Lạt, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ðà Lạt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
