Kiryū at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kiryū is 245th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 1,896 hours of sunshine a year) and 180th wettest (1,453 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ōta, Gunma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Kiryū is 1.4 °C cooler in August and 1.9 °C cooler in January, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 23.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kiryū, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kiryū has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
