Usuki sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Oita · Japan

The sea off Usuki averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.7 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Usuki sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Usuki?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Usuki?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.7 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Usuki in February?
About 12.7 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Usuki?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Usuki nearby?
In August: Bungo-Takada-shi (27.0 °C, 59 km), Nobeoka (27.7 °C, 61 km) and Yokkaichi, Oita (27.2 °C, 64 km).
