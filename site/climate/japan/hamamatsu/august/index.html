How August compares in Hamamatsu

August is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hamamatsu. Compared with July: 1.2 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. September is 3.0 °C cooler than August on average.
