How March compares in Hamamatsu

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hamamatsu. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 76 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 5.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Hamamatsu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamamatsu in March?
In March, Hamamatsu averages a day high of 13.5 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hamamatsu?
No month in Hamamatsu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamamatsu in March?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (252 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Hamamatsu in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamamatsu get in March?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamamatsu in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
