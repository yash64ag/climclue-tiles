Hamamatsu weather in March

Average March weather in Hamamatsu, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Hamamatsu averages 14.5 °C (58 °F) days and 5.6 °C (42 °F) nights, with 156 mm (6.1 in) of rain over the month and about 186 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Hamamatsu

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hamamatsu. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 74 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 5.0 °C warmer than March on average.
