How May compares in Kobe

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kobe. Compared with April: 4.7 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Kobe in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kobe in May?
In May, Kobe averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kobe?
No month in Kobe is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kobe in May?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Kobe in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kobe get in May?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kobe in May?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kobe in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
