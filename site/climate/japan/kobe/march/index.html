How March compares in Kobe

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kobe. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Kobe in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kobe in March?
In March, Kobe averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kobe?
No month in Kobe is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kobe in March?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kobe in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kobe get in March?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kobe in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
