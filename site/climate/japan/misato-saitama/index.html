Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Misato, Saitama is 148th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 162nd sunniest (about 1,834 hours of sunshine a year) and 225th wettest (1,385 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nagareyama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Misato, Saitama is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Why Misato, Saitama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
