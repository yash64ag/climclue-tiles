Agano at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Agano is 976th warmest (13.7 °C year average), 1122nd sunniest (about 1,633 hours of sunshine a year) and 152nd wettest (2,347 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Suibara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Agano is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Agano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Agano itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.836°N, 139.223°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-461-3192), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
