Yokosuka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Yokosuka is 92nd warmest (16.1 °C year average), 213th sunniest (about 1,813 hours of sunshine a year) and 77th wettest (1,799 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kamakura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Yokosuka is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C warmer in February, with 163 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, February (7.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, under 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 2.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 123% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yokosuka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
