Tondabayashichō at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Tondabayashichō is 89th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 124th sunniest (about 1,855 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (1,482 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Habikino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Tondabayashichō is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tondabayashichō, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tondabayashichō has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
