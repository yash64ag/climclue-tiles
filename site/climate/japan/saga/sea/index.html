Saga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Japan

The sea off Saga averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Saga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saga?
In August, at about 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saga?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saga in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saga?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Saga nearby?
In August: Kasuga (26.9 °C, 36 km) and Maebaru-chūō (26.9 °C, 37 km).
