Shinagawa at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Shinagawa is 250th warmest (12.7 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 1,858 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (2,190 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Matsuyama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Shinagawa is 3.6 °C cooler in August and 5.7 °C cooler in January, with 808 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shinagawa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shinagawa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
