Maebashi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Maebashi is 237th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 1,910 hours of sunshine a year) and 262nd wettest (1,304 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Takasaki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Maebashi is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 118 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maebashi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maebashi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 118 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
