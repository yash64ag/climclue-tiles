Shibata sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Niigata · Japan

The sea off Shibata averages 26.4 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.6 °C (49 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Shibata sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Shibata?
In August, at about 26.4 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Shibata?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 9.6 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Shibata in March?
About 9.6 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Shibata?
About 17.2 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
