Koshigaya at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Koshigaya is 155th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 139th sunniest (about 1,844 hours of sunshine a year) and 231st wettest (1,370 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sōka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Koshigaya is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Koshigaya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Koshigaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
