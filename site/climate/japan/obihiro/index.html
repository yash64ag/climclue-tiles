Obihiro at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Obihiro is the 4th coolest (7.0 °C year average) and the driest (953 mm a year). Compared with Kushiro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (96 km away), Obihiro is 2.4 °C warmer in August and 3.6 °C cooler in January, with 97 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.4 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Why Obihiro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
