Nikaho sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Akita · Japan

The sea off Nikaho averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.4 °C (45 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Nikaho sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nikaho?
In August, at about 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nikaho?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 7.4 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nikaho in March?
About 7.4 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nikaho?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
