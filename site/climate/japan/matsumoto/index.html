Matsumoto at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Matsumoto is the 4th driest (1,058 mm a year). Compared with Ueda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Matsumoto is 1.5 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Why Matsumoto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
