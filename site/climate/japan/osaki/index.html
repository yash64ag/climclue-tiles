Ōsaki at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ōsaki is 252nd warmest (12.4 °C year average), 214th sunniest (about 1,810 hours of sunshine a year) and 152nd wettest (1,507 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ishinomaki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Ōsaki is 1.5 °C warmer in August and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 287 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ōsaki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ōsaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
