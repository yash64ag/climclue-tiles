Yachiyo at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Yachiyo is 140th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 203rd sunniest (about 1,818 hours of sunshine a year) and 179th wettest (1,455 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Narashino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Yachiyo is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yachiyo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yachiyo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
