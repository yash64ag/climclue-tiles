Why Kushiro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kushiro climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kushiro?
August, with an average of 17.9 °C (64 °F), day highs around 19.8 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 16.4 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kushiro?
January, with an average of -4.3 °C (24 °F), day highs around -1.4 °C (29 °F) and night lows around -7.5 °C (19 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kushiro?
September, with 156 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,050 mm (41.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kushiro?
February, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kushiro?
March, with about 193 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kushiro?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 173 cm (68.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kushiro?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Kushiro have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Kushiro’s GeoNames location (42.975°N, 144.375°E; ClimaClue area sq-410-3243), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 167,875 (GeoNames).
