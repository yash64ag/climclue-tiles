Kushiro at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kushiro is the 3rd coolest (6.7 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (1,050 mm a year). Compared with Obihiro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (96 km away), Kushiro is 2.4 °C cooler in August and 3.6 °C warmer in January, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (17.9 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kushiro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kushiro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
