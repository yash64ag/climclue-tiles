How July compares in Sakai

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sakai. Compared with June: 3.9 °C warmer, 34 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. August is 1.6 °C warmer than July on average.

Sakai in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sakai in July?
In July, Sakai averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sakai?
No month in Sakai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sakai in July?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (197 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Sakai in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sakai get in July?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sakai in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
