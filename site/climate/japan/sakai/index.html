Sakai at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Sakai is 32nd warmest (16.8 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 1,886 hours of sunshine a year) and 241st wettest (1,351 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Matsubara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sakai is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.1 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sakai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sakai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
