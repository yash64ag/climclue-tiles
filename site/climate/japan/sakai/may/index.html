Sakai, Osaka in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sakai, Osaka in May?
In May, Sakai, Osaka averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sakai, Osaka?
No month in Sakai, Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sakai, Osaka in May?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (199 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Sakai, Osaka in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sakai, Osaka get in May?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
