How March compares in Sakai

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Sakai. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. April is 5.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Sakai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sakai in March?
In March, Sakai averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sakai?
No month in Sakai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sakai in March?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (197 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Sakai in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Sakai get in March?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sakai in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
