How December compares in Sakai

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sakai. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Sakai in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sakai in December?
In December, Sakai averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sakai?
No month in Sakai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sakai in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (199 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Sakai in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sakai get in December?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sakai in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
