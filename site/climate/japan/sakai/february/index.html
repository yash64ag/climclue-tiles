How February compares in Sakai

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sakai. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. March is 3.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Sakai in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sakai in February?
In February, Sakai averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Sakai?
No month in Sakai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sakai in February?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (197 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Sakai in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.6 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Sakai get in February?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sakai in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
