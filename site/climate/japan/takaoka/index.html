Takaoka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Takaoka is the 5th least sunny (about 1,604 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Toyama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Takaoka is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Takaoka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Takaoka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
