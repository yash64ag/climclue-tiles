Towada sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Aomori · Japan

The sea off Towada averages 21.0 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.5 °C (38 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Towada sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Towada?
In August, at about 21.0 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Towada?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 3.5 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Towada in March?
About 3.5 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Towada?
About 11.1 °C (52 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Towada nearby?
In August: Aomori (21.6 °C, 46 km), Goshogawara (23.9 °C, 68 km) and Shimokizukuri (23.9 °C, 74 km).
