Itogawa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Wakayama · Japan

The sea off Itogawa averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.2 °C (59 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Itogawa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Itogawa?
In August, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Itogawa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.2 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Itogawa in February?
About 15.2 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Itogawa?
About 20.3 °C (69 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
