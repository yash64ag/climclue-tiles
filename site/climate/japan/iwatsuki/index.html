Iwatsuki at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Iwatsuki is 181st warmest (15.2 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 1,863 hours of sunshine a year) and 245th wettest (1,322 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kasukabe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Iwatsuki is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iwatsuki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iwatsuki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers
