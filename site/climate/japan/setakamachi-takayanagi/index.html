Setakamachi-takayanagi at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Setakamachi-takayanagi is 182nd warmest (17.0 °C year average), 718th sunniest (about 1,823 hours of sunshine a year) and 240th wettest (2,113 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miyama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Setakamachi-takayanagi is 0.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 188 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Setakamachi-takayanagi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
