How March compares in Kagoshima

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kagoshima. Compared with February: 2.8 °C warmer, 69 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Kagoshima in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kagoshima in March?
In March, Kagoshima averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 169 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kagoshima?
March is not one of Kagoshima’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kagoshima in March?
169 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (400 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kagoshima in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kagoshima get in March?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kagoshima in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
