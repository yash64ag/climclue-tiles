How November compares in Kagoshima

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kagoshima. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Kagoshima in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kagoshima in November?
In November, Kagoshima averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kagoshima?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Kagoshima’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kagoshima in November?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (400 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kagoshima in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kagoshima get in November?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kagoshima in November?
About 21.9 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kagoshima in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
