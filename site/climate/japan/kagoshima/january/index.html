How January compares in Kagoshima

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kagoshima. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Kagoshima in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kagoshima in January?
In January, Kagoshima averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kagoshima?
January is not one of Kagoshima’s mild months; those are November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kagoshima in January?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (427 mm), the driest December (73 mm).
Does it snow in Kagoshima in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kagoshima get in January?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kagoshima in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
