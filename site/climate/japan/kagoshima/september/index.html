How September compares in Kagoshima

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kagoshima. Compared with August: 2.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Kagoshima in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kagoshima in September?
In September, Kagoshima averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kagoshima?
September is not one of Kagoshima’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kagoshima in September?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (400 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kagoshima in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kagoshima get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kagoshima in September?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kagoshima in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
