How August compares in Kagoshima

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kagoshima. Compared with July: 0.6 °C warmer, 82 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. September is 2.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Kagoshima in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kagoshima in August?
In August, Kagoshima averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kagoshima?
August is not one of Kagoshima’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kagoshima in August?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (400 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kagoshima in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kagoshima get in August?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kagoshima in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
