How February compares in Kagoshima

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kagoshima. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 2.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Kagoshima in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kagoshima in February?
In February, Kagoshima averages a day high of 12.1 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kagoshima?
February is not one of Kagoshima’s mild months; those are November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kagoshima in February?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (427 mm), the driest December (73 mm).
Does it snow in Kagoshima in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kagoshima get in February?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kagoshima in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
