Kirishima at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kirishima is 12th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 82nd sunniest (about 1,879 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (2,591 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kagoshima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Kirishima is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 342 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kirishima, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kirishima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
