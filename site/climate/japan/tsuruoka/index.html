Tsuruoka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Tsuruoka is 247th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 270th sunniest (about 1,607 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (2,523 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sakata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Tsuruoka is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 81 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.1 °C), averages 22.8 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tsuruoka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tsuruoka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
