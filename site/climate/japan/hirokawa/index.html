Hirokawa at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Hirokawa is 330th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 879th sunniest (about 1,786 hours of sunshine a year) and 270th wettest (2,032 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chikugo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Hirokawa is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (5.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hirokawa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hirokawa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hirokawa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.243°N, 130.552°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-507-3105), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
