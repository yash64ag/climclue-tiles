How June compares in Yokohama

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Yokohama. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Yokohama in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yokohama in June?
In June, Yokohama averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Yokohama?
No month in Yokohama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yokohama in June?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (204 mm), the driest January (52 mm).
Does it snow in Yokohama in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yokohama get in June?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yokohama in June?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yokohama in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
