Yokohama at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Yokohama is 100th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 177th sunniest (about 1,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 136th wettest (1,537 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kawasaki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Yokohama is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 17 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yokohama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
