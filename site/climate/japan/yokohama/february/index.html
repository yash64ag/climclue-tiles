Yokohama in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yokohama in February?
In February, Yokohama averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yokohama?
No month in Yokohama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yokohama in February?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (204 mm), the driest January (52 mm).
Does it snow in Yokohama in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yokohama get in February?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yokohama in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
