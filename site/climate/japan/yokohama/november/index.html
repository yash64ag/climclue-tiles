Yokohama in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yokohama in November?
In November, Yokohama averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Yokohama?
No month in Yokohama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yokohama in November?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (204 mm), the driest January (52 mm).
Does it snow in Yokohama in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yokohama get in November?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yokohama in November?
About 20.0 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yokohama in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
