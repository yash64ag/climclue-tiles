Yokohama in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yokohama in October?
In October, Yokohama averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yokohama?
No month in Yokohama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yokohama in October?
194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (204 mm), the driest January (52 mm).
Does it snow in Yokohama in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yokohama get in October?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yokohama in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
