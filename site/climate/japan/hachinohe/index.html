Hachinohe at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Hachinohe is 263rd warmest (10.9 °C year average), 212th sunniest (about 1,815 hours of sunshine a year) and 265th wettest (1,251 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aomori, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Hachinohe is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 345 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Hachinohe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
