Hachinohe sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Aomori · Japan

The sea off Hachinohe averages 20.7 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.1 °C (41 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Hachinohe sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hachinohe?
In August, at about 20.7 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hachinohe?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 5.1 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hachinohe in March?
About 5.1 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hachinohe?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hachinohe nearby?
In August: Aomori (21.6 °C, 74 km), Mutsu (21.3 °C, 91 km) and Goshogawara (23.9 °C, 96 km).
