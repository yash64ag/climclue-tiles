Fukuoka at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Fukuoka is 129th warmest (17.3 °C year average), 868th sunniest (about 1,789 hours of sunshine a year) and 484th wettest (1,726 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sue, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Fukuoka is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fukuoka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fukuoka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Fukuoka climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Fukuoka?
August, with an average of 28.4 °C (83 °F), day highs around 32.2 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 24.7 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Fukuoka?
January, with an average of 6.9 °C (44 °F), day highs around 10.4 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 3.3 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Fukuoka?
July, with 306 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,726 mm (68.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Fukuoka?
December, with 62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Fukuoka?
August, with about 202 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Fukuoka?
Yes. About 7.5 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time to visit Fukuoka?
For mild weather (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): October. Climate only, from 1981–2010 averages: crowds and prices are not considered.
How warm is the sea in Fukuoka?
The nearby open sea averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.3 °C (58 °F) in March. It is 20 °C (68 °F) or warmer in June, July, August, September, October and November. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fukuoka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.600°N, 130.417°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-503-3104), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
