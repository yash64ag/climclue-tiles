How December compares in Fukuoka

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Fukuoka. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Fukuoka in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fukuoka in December?
In December, Fukuoka averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fukuoka?
December is not one of Fukuoka’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fukuoka in December?
62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (306 mm), the driest December (62 mm).
Does it snow in Fukuoka in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fukuoka get in December?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Fukuoka in December?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Fukuoka in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
