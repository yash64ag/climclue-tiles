How September compares in Fukuoka

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Fukuoka. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Fukuoka in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fukuoka in September?
In September, Fukuoka averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Fukuoka?
September is not one of Fukuoka’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fukuoka in September?
189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (306 mm), the driest December (62 mm).
Does it snow in Fukuoka in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fukuoka get in September?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Fukuoka in September?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Fukuoka in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
