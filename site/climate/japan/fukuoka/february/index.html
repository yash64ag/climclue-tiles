How February compares in Fukuoka

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Fukuoka. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. March is 2.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Fukuoka in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fukuoka in February?
In February, Fukuoka averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Fukuoka?
February is not one of Fukuoka’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fukuoka in February?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (326 mm), the driest December (68 mm).
Does it snow in Fukuoka in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fukuoka get in February?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fukuoka in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
