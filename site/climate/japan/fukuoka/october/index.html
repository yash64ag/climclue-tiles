How October compares in Fukuoka

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Fukuoka. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, 109 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. November is 5.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Fukuoka in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fukuoka in October?
In October, Fukuoka averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Fukuoka?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Fukuoka’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fukuoka in October?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (306 mm), the driest December (62 mm).
Does it snow in Fukuoka in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fukuoka get in October?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Fukuoka in October?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Fukuoka in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
