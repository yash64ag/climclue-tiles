Naha at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Naha is the warmest (23.0 °C year average). Compared with Urasoe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Naha is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 187 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, February (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Naha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
