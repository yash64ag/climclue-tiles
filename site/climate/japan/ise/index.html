Ise at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Ise is 141st warmest (16.7 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 1,966 hours of sunshine a year) and 371st wettest (1,868 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tamaru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Ise is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 75 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.5 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (6.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ise, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Ise has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ise climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ise?
August, with an average of 27.5 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.1 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ise?
January, with an average of 6.7 °C (44 °F), day highs around 10.7 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 2.8 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ise?
September, with 313 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,868 mm (73.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ise?
December, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ise?
August, with about 195 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ise?
Yes. About 5.2 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time to visit Ise?

How warm is the sea in Ise?
The nearby open sea averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.1 °C (57 °F) in February. It is 20 °C (68 °F) or warmer in June, July, August, September and October. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ise itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.483°N, 136.700°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-495-3167), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 123,533, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). How ClimaClue makes these numbers.
