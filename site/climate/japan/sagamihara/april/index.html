Sagamihara in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sagamihara in April?
In April, Sagamihara averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sagamihara?
No month in Sagamihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
