Sagamihara in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sagamihara in May?
In May, Sagamihara averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sagamihara?
No month in Sagamihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sagamihara in May?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (227 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Sagamihara in May?

How many hours of sunshine does Sagamihara get in May?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
