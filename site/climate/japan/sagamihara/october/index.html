Sagamihara in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sagamihara in October?
In October, Sagamihara averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sagamihara?
No month in Sagamihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sagamihara in October?
179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (227 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Sagamihara in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sagamihara get in October?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sagamihara in October?
