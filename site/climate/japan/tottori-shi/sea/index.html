Tottori-shi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tottori · Japan

The sea off Tottori-shi averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.7 °C (53 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Tottori-shi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tottori-shi?
In August, at about 26.6 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tottori-shi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 11.7 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tottori-shi in March?
About 11.7 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tottori-shi?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
