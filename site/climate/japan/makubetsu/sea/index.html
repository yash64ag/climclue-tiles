Makubetsu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Makubetsu averages 17.5 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.2 °C (30 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Makubetsu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Makubetsu?
In August, at about 17.5 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Makubetsu?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.2 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Makubetsu in February?
About -1.2 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Makubetsu?
About 7.0 °C (45 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Makubetsu nearby?
In August: Wakkanai (19.1 °C, 12 km), Rumoi (21.3 °C, 160 km) and Ishikari (21.2 °C, 240 km).
