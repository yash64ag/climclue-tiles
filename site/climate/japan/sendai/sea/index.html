Sendai, Miyagi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Miyagi · Japan

The sea off Sendai, Miyagi averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.0 °C (46 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Sendai, Miyagi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sendai, Miyagi?
In August, at about 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sendai, Miyagi?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 8.0 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sendai, Miyagi in March?
About 8.0 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sendai, Miyagi?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
