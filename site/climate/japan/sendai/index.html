Why Sendai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Sendai climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sendai?
August, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.3 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 21.2 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sendai?
January, with an average of 1.6 °C (35 °F), day highs around 3.8 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -3.0 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sendai?
September, with 208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,518 mm (59.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sendai?
February, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sendai?
May, with about 203 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sendai?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 120 cm (47.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sendai?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Sendai have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.7 × 11 km, that contains Sendai’s GeoNames location (38.267°N, 140.867°E; ClimaClue area sq-457-3208), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,096,704 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
