Kainan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Wakayama · Japan

The sea off Kainan averages 26.3 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Kainan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kainan?
In August, at about 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kainan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kainan in February?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kainan?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kainan nearby?
In August: Gobō (26.9 °C, 30 km).
