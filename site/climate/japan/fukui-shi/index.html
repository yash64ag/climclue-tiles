Fukui-shi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Fukui-shi is the 4th least sunny (about 1,595 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Komatsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Fukui-shi is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 211 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Why Fukui-shi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
