Isesaki at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Isesaki is 221st warmest (14.7 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 1,908 hours of sunshine a year) and 246th wettest (1,345 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maebashi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Isesaki is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Isesaki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Isesaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
