How October compares in Shizuoka

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shizuoka. Compared with September: 4.7 °C cooler, 74 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. November is 4.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Shizuoka in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuoka in October?
In October, Shizuoka averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Shizuoka?
No month in Shizuoka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuoka in October?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (269 mm), the driest December (57 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuoka in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuoka get in October?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuoka in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
