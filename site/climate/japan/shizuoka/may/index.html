How May compares in Shizuoka

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shizuoka. Compared with April: 3.8 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Shizuoka in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuoka in May?
In May, Shizuoka averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Shizuoka?
No month in Shizuoka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuoka in May?
192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (269 mm), the driest December (57 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuoka in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuoka get in May?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuoka in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
