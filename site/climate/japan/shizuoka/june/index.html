How June compares in Shizuoka

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shizuoka. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 76 mm more precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. July is 3.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Shizuoka in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuoka in June?
In June, Shizuoka averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 288 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shizuoka?
No month in Shizuoka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuoka in June?
288 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (291 mm), the driest December (63 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuoka in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuoka get in June?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuoka in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
