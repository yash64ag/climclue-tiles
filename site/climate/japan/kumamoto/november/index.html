How November compares in Kumamoto

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kumamoto. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Kumamoto in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in November?
In November, Kumamoto averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in November?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (558 mm), the driest December (77 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in November?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kumamoto in November?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
