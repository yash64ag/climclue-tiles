How October compares in Kumamoto

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kumamoto. Compared with September: 5.4 °C cooler, 134 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Kumamoto in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in October?
In October, Kumamoto averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in October?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (572 mm), the driest December (78 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in October?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
