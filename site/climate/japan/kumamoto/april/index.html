How April compares in Kumamoto

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kumamoto. Compared with March: 4.7 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Kumamoto in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in April?
In April, Kumamoto averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in April?
225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (572 mm), the driest December (78 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in April?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
