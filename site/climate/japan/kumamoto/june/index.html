How June compares in Kumamoto

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kumamoto. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 288 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. July is 3.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Kumamoto in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in June?
In June, Kumamoto averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 572 mm (22.5 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in June?
572 mm (22.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (572 mm), the driest December (78 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in June?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
