Kumamoto sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Japan

The sea off Kumamoto averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Kumamoto sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kumamoto?
In August, at about 26.7 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kumamoto?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kumamoto in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kumamoto?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
