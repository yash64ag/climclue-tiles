How December compares in Kumamoto

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kumamoto. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kumamoto ranks 72nd warmest (average temperature), 208th sunniest and 40th wettest in December.

Kumamoto in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in December?
In December, Kumamoto averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in December?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (572 mm), the driest December (78 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in December?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
