How August compares in Kumamoto

August is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kumamoto. Compared with July: 1.1 °C warmer, 307 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. September is 3.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Kumamoto in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in August?
In August, Kumamoto averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 248 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in August?
248 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (558 mm), the driest December (77 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in August?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
