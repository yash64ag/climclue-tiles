How January compares in Kumamoto

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kumamoto. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Kumamoto among towns in Japan in January

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kumamoto ranks 65th warmest (average temperature), 215th sunniest and 36th wettest in January.

Kumamoto in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumamoto in January?
In January, Kumamoto averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kumamoto?
No month in Kumamoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumamoto in January?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (572 mm), the driest December (78 mm).
Does it snow in Kumamoto in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.6 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumamoto get in January?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumamoto in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
