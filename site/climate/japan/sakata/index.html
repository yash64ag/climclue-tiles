Sakata at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Sakata is 246th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 271st sunniest (about 1,607 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (2,442 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tsuruoka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Sakata is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 81 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.3 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sakata, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sakata has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
