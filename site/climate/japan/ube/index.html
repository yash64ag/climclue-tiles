Ube at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ube is 44th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 196th sunniest (about 1,820 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (1,989 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shimonoseki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Ube is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ube, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ube has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
