Takarazuka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Takarazuka is 65th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 182nd sunniest (about 1,825 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (1,528 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itami, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Takarazuka is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Takarazuka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Takarazuka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
