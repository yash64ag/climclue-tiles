How February compares in Osaka

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Osaka. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 3.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Osaka in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in February?
In February, Osaka averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in February?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in February?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osaka in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
