How April compares in Osaka

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with March: 5.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 4.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Osaka in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in April?
In April, Osaka averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in April?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in April?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osaka in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
