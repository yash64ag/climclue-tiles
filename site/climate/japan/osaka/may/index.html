How May compares in Osaka

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with April: 4.7 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Osaka in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in May?
In May, Osaka averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in May?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in May?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Osaka in May?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Osaka in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
