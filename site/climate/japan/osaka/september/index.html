How September compares in Osaka

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 67 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. October is 6.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Osaka in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in September?
In September, Osaka averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in September?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in September?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Osaka in September?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Osaka in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
