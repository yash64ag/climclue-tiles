How June compares in Osaka

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 42 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. July is 3.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Osaka in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in June?
In June, Osaka averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in June?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in June?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Osaka in June?
About 20.7 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Osaka in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
