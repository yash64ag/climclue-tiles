How March compares in Osaka

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with February: 3.2 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 5.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Osaka in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in March?
In March, Osaka averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in March?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in March?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osaka in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
