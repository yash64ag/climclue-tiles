How January compares in Osaka

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Osaka in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in January?
In January, Osaka averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in January?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in January?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osaka in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
