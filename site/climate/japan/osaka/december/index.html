How December compares in Osaka

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Osaka. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Osaka in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osaka in December?
In December, Osaka averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Osaka?
No month in Osaka is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osaka in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Osaka in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Osaka get in December?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osaka in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
