Ichihara at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ichihara is 111th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 208th sunniest (about 1,816 hours of sunshine a year) and 125th wettest (1,556 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chiba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Ichihara is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ichihara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ichihara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
