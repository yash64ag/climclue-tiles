Why Ichihara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Ichihara climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ichihara?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.7 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 24.1 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ichihara?
January, with an average of 6.3 °C (43 °F), day highs around 8.7 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 3.2 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ichihara?
September, with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,556 mm (61.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ichihara?
December, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ichihara?
August, with about 186 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ichihara?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ichihara?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Ichihara have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Ichihara’s GeoNames location (35.517°N, 140.083°E; ClimaClue area sq-484-3200), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 283,531 (GeoNames).
