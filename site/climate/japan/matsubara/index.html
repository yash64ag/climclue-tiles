Matsubara at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Matsubara is 62nd warmest (16.6 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 1,864 hours of sunshine a year) and 219th wettest (1,390 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Matsubara is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 164 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 23.3 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matsubara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matsubara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
