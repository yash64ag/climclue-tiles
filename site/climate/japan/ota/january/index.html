How January compares in Ōta, Tokyo

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ōta, Tokyo. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Ōta, Tokyo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ōta, Tokyo in January?
In January, Ōta, Tokyo averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ōta, Tokyo?
No month in Ōta, Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ōta, Tokyo in January?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (52 mm).
Does it snow in Ōta, Tokyo in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ōta, Tokyo get in January?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
