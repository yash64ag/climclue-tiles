Ōta, Tokyo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ōta, Tokyo in February?
In February, Ōta, Tokyo averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ōta, Tokyo?
No month in Ōta, Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ōta, Tokyo in February?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (204 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Ōta, Tokyo in February?
Yes, on average: about 6.4 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Ōta, Tokyo get in February?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Ōta, Tokyo in February?
About 12.9 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
