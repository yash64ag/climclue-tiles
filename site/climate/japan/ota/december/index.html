How December compares in Ōta, Tokyo

Ōta, Tokyo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ōta, Tokyo in December?
In December, Ōta, Tokyo averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ōta, Tokyo?
