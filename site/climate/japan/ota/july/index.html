Ōta, Tokyo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ōta, Tokyo in July?
In July, Ōta, Tokyo averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ōta, Tokyo?
No month in Ōta, Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ōta, Tokyo in July?
147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (204 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Ōta, Tokyo in July?

How many hours of sunshine does Ōta, Tokyo get in July?
