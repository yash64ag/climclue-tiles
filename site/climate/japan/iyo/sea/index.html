Iyo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ehime · Japan

The sea off Iyo averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.4 °C (54 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Iyo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Iyo?
In August, at about 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Iyo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.4 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Iyo in February?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Iyo?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Iyo nearby?
In August: Matsuyama (26.1 °C, 11 km), Hōjō (26.1 °C, 26 km) and Imabari (26.6 °C, 45 km).
