Akishima at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Akishima is 230th warmest (14.5 °C year average), 90th sunniest (about 1,871 hours of sunshine a year) and 217th wettest (1,392 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tachikawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Akishima is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 50 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Akishima, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Akishima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
