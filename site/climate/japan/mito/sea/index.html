Mito sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ibaraki · Japan

The sea off Mito averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.2 °C (54 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October.

Mito sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mito?
In August, at about 23.3 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mito?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 12.2 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mito in March?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mito?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
