Kashima-shi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ibaraki · Japan

The sea off Kashima-shi averages 23.5 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.6 °C (55 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October.

Kashima-shi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kashima-shi?
In August, at about 23.5 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kashima-shi?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 12.6 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kashima-shi in March?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kashima-shi?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kashima-shi nearby?
In August: Asahi (24.7 °C, 28 km), Sōsa (24.4 °C, 29 km) and Choshi (24.3 °C, 31 km).
