Beppu at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Beppu is 211th warmest (14.9 °C year average), 247th sunniest (about 1,755 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (2,350 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ōita, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Beppu is 1.6 °C cooler in August and 3.5 °C cooler in January, with 546 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beppu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beppu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
