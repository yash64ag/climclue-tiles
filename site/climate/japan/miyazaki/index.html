Miyazaki at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Miyazaki is 6th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,023 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (2,393 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miyakonojō, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Miyazaki is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 2.8 °C warmer in January, with 202 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Miyazaki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Miyazaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
