Ōta, Gunma at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ōta, Gunma is 228th warmest (14.6 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 1,903 hours of sunshine a year) and 240th wettest (1,352 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ashikaga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Ōta, Gunma is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 22.8 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Why Ōta, Gunma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
