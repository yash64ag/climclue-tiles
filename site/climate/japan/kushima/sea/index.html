Kushima sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Miyazaki · Japan

The sea off Kushima averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.1 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Kushima sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kushima?
In August, at about 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kushima?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 18.1 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kushima in February?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kushima?
About 22.6 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
