Kuwana at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kuwana is 90th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 1,901 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (1,711 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yokkaichi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kuwana is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 45 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.8 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kuwana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kuwana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
