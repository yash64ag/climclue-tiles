Echizen sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fukui · Japan

The sea off Echizen averages 26.1 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.5 °C (51 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Echizen sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Echizen?
In August, at about 26.1 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Echizen?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 10.5 °C (51 °F).
How warm is the sea in Echizen in March?
About 10.5 °C (51 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Echizen?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Echizen nearby?
In August: Sakai, Fukui (26.7 °C, 29 km), Maruoka (26.7 °C, 31 km) and Awara (26.7 °C, 38 km).
