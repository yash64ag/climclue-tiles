Hōjō sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ehime · Japan

The sea off Hōjō averages 26.1 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.9 °C (57 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Hōjō sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hōjō?
In August, at about 26.1 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hōjō?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 13.9 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hōjō in March?
About 13.9 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hōjō?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hōjō nearby?
In August: Saijō (27.0 °C, 38 km), Niihama (27.0 °C, 49 km) and Innoshima (26.8 °C, 50 km).
