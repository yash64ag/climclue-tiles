Hirosaki at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Hirosaki is 1142nd warmest (10.7 °C year average), 1144th sunniest (about 1,614 hours of sunshine a year) and 532nd wettest (1,673 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hirakawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Hirosaki is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.7 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Why Hirosaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hirosaki itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.593°N, 140.472°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-434-3204), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
