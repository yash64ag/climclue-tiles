Chigasaki at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Chigasaki is 108th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 158th sunniest (about 1,834 hours of sunshine a year) and 122nd wettest (1,562 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ebina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Chigasaki is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.8 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 25 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Chigasaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
