Why Chigasaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Chigasaki climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chigasaki?
August, with an average of 25.8 °C (78 °F), day highs around 27.3 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 23.9 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chigasaki?
January, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 9.0 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 3.5 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chigasaki?
September, with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,562 mm (61.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Chigasaki?
January, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Chigasaki?
August, with about 184 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chigasaki?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Chigasaki?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Chigasaki’s GeoNames location (35.336°N, 139.404°E; ClimaClue area sq-486-3194), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 242,798 (GeoNames).
