Kannabechō-yahiro sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hiroshima · Japan

The sea off Kannabechō-yahiro averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.0 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Kannabechō-yahiro sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kannabechō-yahiro?
In August, at about 27.2 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kannabechō-yahiro?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.0 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kannabechō-yahiro in February?
About 14.0 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kannabechō-yahiro?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
