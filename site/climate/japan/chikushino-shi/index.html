Chikushino-shi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Chikushino-shi is 64th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 232nd sunniest (about 1,784 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (1,915 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ōnojō, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Chikushino-shi is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 199 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chikushino-shi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chikushino-shi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
