Kashihara-shi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kashihara-shi is 77th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 154th sunniest (about 1,835 hours of sunshine a year) and 158th wettest (1,488 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kashiwara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kashihara-shi is 0.7 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 237 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kashihara-shi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kashihara-shi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
