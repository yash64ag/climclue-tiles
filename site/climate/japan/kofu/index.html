Kofu at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kofu is 219th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 1,934 hours of sunshine a year) and 271st wettest (1,141 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fujinomiya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Kofu is 1.9 °C warmer in August and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 1,056 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kofu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kofu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
