Mikuriya sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tottori · Japan

The sea off Mikuriya averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.0 °C (54 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Mikuriya sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mikuriya?
In August, at about 26.6 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mikuriya?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 12.0 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mikuriya in March?
About 12.0 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mikuriya?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
