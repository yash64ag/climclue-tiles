Memuro-minami at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Memuro-minami is 1198th warmest (7.0 °C year average), 328th sunniest (about 1,880 hours of sunshine a year) and 1189th wettest (1,078 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Obihiro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Memuro-minami is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 124 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.5 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. Why Memuro-minami has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Memuro-minami itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.907°N, 143.036°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-410-3230), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
