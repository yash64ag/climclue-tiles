Ōzu, Ehime sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ehime · Japan

The sea off Ōzu, Ehime averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.4 °C (54 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Ōzu, Ehime sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ōzu, Ehime?
In August, at about 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ōzu, Ehime?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.4 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ōzu, Ehime in February?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ōzu, Ehime?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ōzu, Ehime nearby?
In August: Matsuyama (26.1 °C, 43 km), Hōjō (26.1 °C, 57 km) and Mishōhirajō (25.9 °C, 60 km).
