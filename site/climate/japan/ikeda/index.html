Why Ikeda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Ikeda climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ikeda?
August, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.4 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 23.0 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ikeda?
January, with an average of 4.5 °C (40 °F), day highs around 7.1 °C (45 °F) and night lows around -0.3 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ikeda?
June, with 217 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,533 mm (60.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ikeda?
December, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ikeda?
May, with about 191 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ikeda?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ikeda?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Ikeda have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Ikeda’s GeoNames location (34.822°N, 135.430°E; ClimaClue area sq-491-3154), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 104,993 (GeoNames).
