Nishinoomote sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kagoshima · Japan

The sea off Nishinoomote averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.9 °C (66 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Nishinoomote sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nishinoomote?
In August, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nishinoomote?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 18.9 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nishinoomote in February?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nishinoomote?
About 23.3 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
