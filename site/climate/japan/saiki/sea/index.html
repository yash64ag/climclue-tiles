Saiki sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Oita · Japan

The sea off Saiki averages 26.1 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.9 °C (59 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Saiki sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saiki?
In August, at about 26.1 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saiki?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 14.9 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saiki in March?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saiki?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Saiki nearby?
In August: Nobeoka (27.7 °C, 46 km) and Hyūga (27.7 °C, 63 km).
