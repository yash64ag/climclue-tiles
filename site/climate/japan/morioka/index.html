Why Morioka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 77 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Morioka climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Morioka?
August, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 26.5 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 19.5 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Morioka?
January, with an average of -2.5 °C (28 °F), day highs around -0.1 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -9.0 °C (16 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Morioka?
August, with 214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,504 mm (59.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Morioka?
February, with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Morioka?
May, with about 204 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Morioka?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 322 cm (126.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Morioka?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Morioka have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.6 × 11 km, that contains Morioka’s GeoNames location (39.700°N, 141.150°E; ClimaClue area sq-442-3211), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 290,700 (GeoNames).
