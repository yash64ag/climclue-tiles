Morioka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Morioka is 266th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 230th sunniest (about 1,785 hours of sunshine a year) and 153rd wettest (1,504 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ōshū, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Morioka is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 1.9 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 77 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Morioka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Morioka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 77 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
