Maebaru-chūō at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Maebaru-chūō is 92nd warmest (17.5 °C year average), 863rd sunniest (about 1,791 hours of sunshine a year) and 383rd wettest (1,851 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Itoshima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Maebaru-chūō is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Maebaru-chūō has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
