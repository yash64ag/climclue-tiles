Miyakonojō at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Miyakonojō is the 5th wettest (2,595 mm a year). Compared with Kirishima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Miyakonojō is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Miyakonojō, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Miyakonojō has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
