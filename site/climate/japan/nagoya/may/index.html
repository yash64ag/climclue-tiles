How May compares in Nagoya

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Nagoya. Compared with April: 4.6 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Nagoya in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagoya in May?
In May, Nagoya averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nagoya?
No month in Nagoya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagoya in May?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (234 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Nagoya in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagoya get in May?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagoya in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
