How January compares in Nagoya

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nagoya. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Nagoya among towns in Japan in January

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Nagoya ranks 142nd warmest (average temperature), 126th sunniest and 115th wettest in January.

Nagoya in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagoya in January?
In January, Nagoya averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nagoya?
No month in Nagoya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagoya in January?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (234 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Nagoya in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagoya get in January?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagoya in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
