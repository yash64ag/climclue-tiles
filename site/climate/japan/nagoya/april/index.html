How April compares in Nagoya

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nagoya. Compared with March: 5.6 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Nagoya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagoya in April?
In April, Nagoya averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nagoya?
No month in Nagoya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagoya in April?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (239 mm), the driest December (53 mm).
Does it snow in Nagoya in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagoya get in April?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Nagoya in April?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Nagoya in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
