How February compares in Nagoya

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nagoya. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Nagoya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagoya in February?
In February, Nagoya averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nagoya?
No month in Nagoya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagoya in February?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (239 mm), the driest December (53 mm).
Does it snow in Nagoya in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagoya get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagoya in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
