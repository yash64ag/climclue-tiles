Higashiosaka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Higashiosaka is 34th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 1,852 hours of sunshine a year) and 202nd wettest (1,421 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Higashiosaka is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 133 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.9 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Higashiosaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Higashiosaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
