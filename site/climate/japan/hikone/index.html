Hikone at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Hikone is 186th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 249th sunniest (about 1,748 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (1,880 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nagahama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Hikone is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 126 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hikone, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hikone has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
