How November compares in Tokyo

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tokyo. Compared with October: 5.4 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Tokyo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tokyo in November?
In November, Tokyo averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tokyo?
No month in Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tokyo in November?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (50 mm).
Does it snow in Tokyo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tokyo get in November?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tokyo in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
