How August compares in Tokyo

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tokyo. Compared with July: 1.6 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. September is 3.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Tokyo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tokyo in August?
In August, Tokyo averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tokyo?
No month in Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tokyo in August?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (50 mm).
Does it snow in Tokyo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tokyo get in August?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tokyo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
