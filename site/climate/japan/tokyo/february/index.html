February · Japan

How February compares in Tokyo

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tokyo. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Tokyo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tokyo in February?
In February, Tokyo averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tokyo?
No month in Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tokyo in February?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (215 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Tokyo in February?
Yes, on average: about 9.0 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tokyo get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tokyo in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
