How March compares in Tokyo

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tokyo. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 55 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. April is 5.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Tokyo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tokyo in March?
In March, Tokyo averages a day high of 11.8 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tokyo?
No month in Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tokyo in March?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (50 mm).
Does it snow in Tokyo in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tokyo get in March?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tokyo in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
