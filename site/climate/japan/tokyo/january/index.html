How January compares in Tokyo

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tokyo. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Tokyo among towns in Japan in January

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Tokyo ranks 142nd warmest (average temperature), 74th sunniest and 180th wettest in January.

Tokyo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tokyo in January?
In January, Tokyo averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.0 °C (32 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tokyo?
No month in Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tokyo in January?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (50 mm).
Does it snow in Tokyo in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tokyo get in January?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tokyo in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
