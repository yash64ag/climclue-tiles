How September compares in Tokyo

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Tokyo. Compared with August: 3.5 °C cooler, 46 mm more precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. October is 5.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Tokyo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tokyo in September?
In September, Tokyo averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tokyo?
No month in Tokyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tokyo in September?
215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (215 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Tokyo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tokyo get in September?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
