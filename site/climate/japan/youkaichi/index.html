Youkaichi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Youkaichi is 160th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 250th sunniest (about 1,737 hours of sunshine a year) and 94th wettest (1,692 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hikone, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Youkaichi is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 188 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Youkaichi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Youkaichi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
