Youkaichi at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Youkaichi is 737th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 1006th sunniest (about 1,737 hours of sunshine a year) and 519th wettest (1,692 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Echigawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Youkaichi is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 109 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Why Youkaichi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Youkaichi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.116°N, 136.198°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-488-3161), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
