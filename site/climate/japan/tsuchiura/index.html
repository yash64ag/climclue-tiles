Tsuchiura at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Tsuchiura is 165th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 185th sunniest (about 1,824 hours of sunshine a year) and 238th wettest (1,354 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tsukuba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Tsuchiura is about as warm in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 22.3 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tsuchiura, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tsuchiura has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
