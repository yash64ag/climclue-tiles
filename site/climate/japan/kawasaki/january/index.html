How January compares in Kawasaki

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kawasaki. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kawasaki ranks 75th warmest (average temperature), 91st sunniest and 146th wettest in January.

Kawasaki in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawasaki in January?
In January, Kawasaki averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kawasaki?
No month in Kawasaki is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawasaki in January?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (52 mm).
Does it snow in Kawasaki in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawasaki get in January?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawasaki in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
