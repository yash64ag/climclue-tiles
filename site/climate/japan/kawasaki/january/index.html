How January compares in Kawasaki, Kanagawa

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kawasaki, Kanagawa. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Kawasaki, Kanagawa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in January?
In January, Kawasaki, Kanagawa averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kawasaki, Kanagawa?
No month in Kawasaki, Kanagawa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in January?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (202 mm), the driest January (52 mm).
Does it snow in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in January?
Yes, on average: about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawasaki, Kanagawa get in January?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
