How August compares in Kawasaki, Kanagawa

August is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kawasaki, Kanagawa. Compared with July: 1.7 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. September is 3.5 °C cooler than August on average.
