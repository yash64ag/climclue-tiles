How August compares in Kawasaki

August is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kawasaki. Compared with July: 1.6 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. September is 3.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Kawasaki in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawasaki in August?
In August, Kawasaki averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kawasaki?
No month in Kawasaki is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawasaki in August?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (52 mm).
Does it snow in Kawasaki in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawasaki get in August?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawasaki in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
