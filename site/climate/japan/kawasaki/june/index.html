Kawasaki, Kanagawa weather in June

Average June weather in Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Kawasaki, Kanagawa averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) days and 18.1 °C (65 °F) nights, with 164 mm (6.5 in) of rain over the month and about 123 hours of sunshine.
