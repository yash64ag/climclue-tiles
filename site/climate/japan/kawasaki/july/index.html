Kawasaki, Kanagawa weather in July

Average July weather in Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Kawasaki, Kanagawa averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 146 mm (5.8 in) of rain over the month and about 142 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Kawasaki, Kanagawa
