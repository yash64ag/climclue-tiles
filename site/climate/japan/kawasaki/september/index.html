How September compares in Kawasaki

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Kawasaki. Compared with August: 3.0 °C cooler, 52 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Kawasaki in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawasaki in September?
In September, Kawasaki averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kawasaki?
No month in Kawasaki is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawasaki in September?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (52 mm).
Does it snow in Kawasaki in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawasaki get in September?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawasaki in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
