How October compares in Kawasaki, Kanagawa

Kawasaki, Kanagawa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in October?
In October, Kawasaki, Kanagawa averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kawasaki, Kanagawa?
No month in Kawasaki, Kanagawa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in October?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (202 mm), the driest January (52 mm).
Does it snow in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawasaki, Kanagawa get in October?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kawasaki, Kanagawa in October?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kawasaki, Kanagawa in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
