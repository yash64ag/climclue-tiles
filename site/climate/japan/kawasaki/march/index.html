How March compares in Kawasaki

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kawasaki. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 60 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. April is 4.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Kawasaki in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawasaki in March?
In March, Kawasaki averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kawasaki?
No month in Kawasaki is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawasaki in March?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (52 mm).
Does it snow in Kawasaki in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawasaki get in March?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawasaki in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
