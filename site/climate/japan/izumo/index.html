Izumo at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Izumo is 99th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 233rd sunniest (about 1,781 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (1,797 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Matsue, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Izumo is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 93 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Izumo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Izumo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
