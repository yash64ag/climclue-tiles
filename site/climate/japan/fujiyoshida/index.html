Fujiyoshida weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Fujiyoshida, Japan (Yamanashi), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Fujiyoshida is warmest in August, with 25.2 °C (77 °F) days and 17.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 2.9 °C (37 °F) days and -7.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (351 mm); December is driest (62 mm).

Why Fujiyoshida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fujiyoshida itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.440°N, 138.796°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-485-3187), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
