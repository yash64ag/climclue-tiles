Why Kurume has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kurume climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kurume?
August, with an average of 27.9 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 23.6 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kurume?
January, with an average of 6.0 °C (43 °F), day highs around 8.7 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 1.2 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kurume?
July, with 350 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,893 mm (74.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kurume?
December, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kurume?
August, with about 200 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kurume?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kurume?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.
What type of climate does Kurume have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Kurume’s GeoNames location (33.317°N, 130.517°E; ClimaClue area sq-506-3105), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 303,579 (GeoNames).
