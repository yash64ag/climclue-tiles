Ashikaga at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ashikaga is 225th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 1,900 hours of sunshine a year) and 216th wettest (1,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ōta, Gunma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Ashikaga is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 22.8 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ashikaga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ashikaga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ashikaga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.333°N, 139.450°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-476-3194), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
