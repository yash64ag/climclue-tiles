Sunagawa weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sunagawa, Japan (Hokkaido), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sunagawa at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Sunagawa is 1183rd warmest (8.0 °C year average), 1089th sunniest (about 1,665 hours of sunshine a year) and 896th wettest (1,412 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Takikawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Sunagawa is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.6 °C), averages 27.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 50 km from the sea.

Why Sunagawa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sunagawa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.486°N, 141.906°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-405-3219), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
