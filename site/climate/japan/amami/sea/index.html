Amami sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kagoshima · Japan

The sea off Amami averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 20.8 °C (69 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to November.

Amami sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Amami?
In August, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Amami?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to November. The coolest month is February, at 20.8 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in Amami in February?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Amami?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
