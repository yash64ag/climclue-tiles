Yamada sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Iwate · Japan

The sea off Yamada averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.8 °C (41 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Yamada sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yamada?
In August, at about 20.4 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yamada?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 4.8 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yamada in March?
About 4.8 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yamada?
About 12.5 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Yamada nearby?
In August: Ōfunato (21.2 °C, 48 km), Rikuzen-Takata (21.2 °C, 56 km) and Kesennuma (21.2 °C, 71 km).
