Yokkaichi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Yokkaichi is 76th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 1,911 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (1,666 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Suzuka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Yokkaichi is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 71 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yokkaichi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yokkaichi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
