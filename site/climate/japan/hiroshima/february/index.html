How February compares in Hiroshima

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hiroshima. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. March is 3.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Hiroshima in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hiroshima in February?
In February, Hiroshima averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hiroshima?
February is not one of Hiroshima’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hiroshima in February?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (254 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hiroshima in February?
Yes, on average: about 5.6 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hiroshima get in February?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hiroshima in February?
About 12.7 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hiroshima in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
