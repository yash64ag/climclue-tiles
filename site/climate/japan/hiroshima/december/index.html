How December compares in Hiroshima

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hiroshima. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Hiroshima in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hiroshima in December?
In December, Hiroshima averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hiroshima?
December is not one of Hiroshima’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hiroshima in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (254 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hiroshima in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hiroshima get in December?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hiroshima in December?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hiroshima in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
