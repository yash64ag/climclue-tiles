How March compares in Hiroshima

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Hiroshima. Compared with February: 3.1 °C warmer, 55 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 5.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Hiroshima in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hiroshima in March?
In March, Hiroshima averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hiroshima?
March is not one of Hiroshima’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hiroshima in March?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (254 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hiroshima in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hiroshima get in March?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hiroshima in March?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hiroshima in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
