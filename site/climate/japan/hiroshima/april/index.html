How April compares in Hiroshima

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hiroshima. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 4.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Hiroshima in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hiroshima in April?
In April, Hiroshima averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hiroshima?
April is not one of Hiroshima’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hiroshima in April?
146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (264 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Hiroshima in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hiroshima get in April?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hiroshima in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
