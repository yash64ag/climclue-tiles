How September compares in Hiroshima

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hiroshima. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 56 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Hiroshima in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hiroshima in September?
In September, Hiroshima averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hiroshima?
September is not one of Hiroshima’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hiroshima in September?
173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (264 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Hiroshima in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hiroshima get in September?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hiroshima in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
