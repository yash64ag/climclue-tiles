Tsu at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Tsu is 83rd warmest (16.2 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 1,936 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,711 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Matsusaka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Tsu is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 176 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tsu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tsu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
