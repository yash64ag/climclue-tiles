How May compares in Chiba

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Chiba. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 3.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Chiba in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiba in May?
In May, Chiba averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Chiba?
No month in Chiba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiba in May?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (53 mm).
Does it snow in Chiba in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiba get in May?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chiba in May?
About 17.2 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Chiba in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
