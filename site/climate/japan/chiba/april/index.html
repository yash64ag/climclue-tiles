How April compares in Chiba

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chiba. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Chiba in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiba in April?
In April, Chiba averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chiba?
No month in Chiba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiba in April?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (210 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Chiba in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiba get in April?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chiba in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
