How December compares in Chiba

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chiba. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. January is 2.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Chiba in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiba in December?
In December, Chiba averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chiba?
No month in Chiba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiba in December?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest December (53 mm).
Does it snow in Chiba in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiba get in December?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chiba in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
