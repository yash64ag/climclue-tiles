Chiba at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Chiba is 125th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 204th sunniest (about 1,818 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (1,500 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ichihara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Chiba is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chiba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chiba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
