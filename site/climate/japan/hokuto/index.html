Hokuto, Yamanashi weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Hokuto, Yamanashi, Japan, from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Why Hokuto, Yamanashi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 93 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hokuto itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.835°N, 138.396°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-481-3183), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
