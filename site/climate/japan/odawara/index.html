Odawara at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Odawara is 120th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 148th sunniest (about 1,837 hours of sunshine a year) and 77th wettest (1,726 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hadano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Odawara is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 1.2 °C warmer in February, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, February (6.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Odawara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Odawara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
