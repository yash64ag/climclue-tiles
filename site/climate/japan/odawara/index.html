Odawara at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Odawara is 182nd warmest (15.2 °C year average), 148th sunniest (about 1,837 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (1,891 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hadano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Odawara is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 159 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Odawara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Odawara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
