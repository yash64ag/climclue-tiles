Why Koga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Koga climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Koga?
August, with an average of 26.8 °C (80 °F), day highs around 30.0 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 22.8 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Koga?
January, with an average of 3.6 °C (39 °F), day highs around 7.6 °C (46 °F) and night lows around -2.0 °C (28 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Koga?
September, with 210 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,385 mm (54.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Koga?
January, with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Koga?
January, with about 188 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Koga?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (12.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Koga?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Koga have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Koga’s GeoNames location (36.183°N, 139.717°E; ClimaClue area sq-478-3197), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 139,344 (GeoNames).
