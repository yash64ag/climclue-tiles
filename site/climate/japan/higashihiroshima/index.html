Higashihiroshima at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Higashihiroshima is 206th warmest (15.0 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 1,893 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (1,552 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kure, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Higashihiroshima is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Why Higashihiroshima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
