Hamada sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shimane · Japan

The sea off Hamada averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Hamada sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hamada?
In August, at about 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hamada?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hamada in March?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hamada?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
