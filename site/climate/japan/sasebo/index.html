Sasebo at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Sasebo is 33rd warmest (16.8 °C year average), 220th sunniest (about 1,796 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (2,317 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karatsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Sasebo is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 74 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sasebo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sasebo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
