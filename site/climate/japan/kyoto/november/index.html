How November compares in Kyoto

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kyoto. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 5.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Kyoto in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyoto in November?
In November, Kyoto averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kyoto?
No month in Kyoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyoto in November?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (218 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kyoto in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyoto get in November?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyoto in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
