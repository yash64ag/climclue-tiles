How March compares in Kyoto

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kyoto. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 5.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Kyoto in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyoto in March?
In March, Kyoto averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kyoto?
No month in Kyoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyoto in March?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (218 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kyoto in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyoto get in March?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyoto in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
