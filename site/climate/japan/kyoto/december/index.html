How December compares in Kyoto

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kyoto. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Kyoto among towns in Japan in December

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kyoto ranks 208th warmest (average temperature), 230th sunniest and 59th wettest in December.

Kyoto in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyoto in December?
In December, Kyoto averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kyoto?
No month in Kyoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyoto in December?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (218 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kyoto in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyoto get in December?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyoto in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
