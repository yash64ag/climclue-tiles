How May compares in Kyoto

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kyoto. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Kyoto in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyoto in May?
In May, Kyoto averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kyoto?
No month in Kyoto is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyoto in May?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (218 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kyoto in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyoto get in May?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyoto in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
