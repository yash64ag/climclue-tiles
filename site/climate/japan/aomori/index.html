Why Aomori has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Aomori climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Aomori?
August, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.7 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 20.1 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Aomori?
January, with an average of -1.4 °C (29 °F), day highs around -0.2 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -6.4 °C (20 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Aomori?
September, with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,597 mm (62.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Aomori?
April, with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Aomori?
May, with about 206 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Aomori?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 256 cm (100.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Aomori?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Aomori’s GeoNames location (40.817°N, 140.733°E; ClimaClue area sq-431-3207), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 298,394 (GeoNames).
