Aomori at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Aomori is 265th warmest (10.7 °C year average), 263rd sunniest (about 1,662 hours of sunshine a year) and 111th wettest (1,597 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hirosaki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Aomori is 0.7 °C warmer in August and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 335 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Aomori has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
