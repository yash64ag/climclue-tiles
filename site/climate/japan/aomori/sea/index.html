Aomori sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Japan

The sea off Aomori averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.4 °C (35 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Aomori sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Aomori?
In August, at about 21.6 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Aomori?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 1.4 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Aomori in March?
About 1.4 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Aomori?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Aomori nearby?
In August: Goshogawara (23.9 °C, 25 km), Shimokizukuri (23.9 °C, 30 km) and Noshiro (24.8 °C, 90 km).
