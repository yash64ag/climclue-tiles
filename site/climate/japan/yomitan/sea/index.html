Yomitan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Okinawa · Japan

The sea off Yomitan averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 21.2 °C (70 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Yomitan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yomitan?
In August, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yomitan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 21.2 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yomitan in February?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Yomitan?
About 24.8 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
