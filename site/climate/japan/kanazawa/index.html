Kanazawa at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kanazawa is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,580 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Matsutō, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Kanazawa is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 181 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kanazawa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
