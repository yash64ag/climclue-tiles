Mishima at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Mishima is 217th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 1,859 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (1,958 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Numazu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Mishima is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 135 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Mishima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
