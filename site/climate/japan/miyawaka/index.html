Miyawaka weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Miyawaka, Japan (Fukuoka), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Miyawaka is warmest in August, with 31.9 °C (89 °F) days and 24.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 9.7 °C (49 °F) days and 2.5 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (325 mm); December is driest (67 mm).

Miyawaka at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Miyawaka is 217th warmest (16.9 °C year average), 942nd sunniest (about 1,770 hours of sunshine a year) and 368th wettest (1,868 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miyata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Miyawaka is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.3 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Miyawaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Miyawaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Miyawaka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.717°N, 130.641°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-502-3106), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
