Aihara in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aihara in October?
In October, Aihara averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aihara?
No month in Aihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aihara in October?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest January (45 mm).
Does it snow in Aihara in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aihara get in October?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aihara in October?
