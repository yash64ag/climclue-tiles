Aihara weather in January

Average January weather in Aihara, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Aihara averages 9.1 °C (48 °F) days and -2.0 °C (28 °F) nights, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 183 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Aihara

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Aihara. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Aihara in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aihara in January?
In January, Aihara averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aihara?
No month in Aihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aihara in January?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest January (45 mm).
Does it snow in Aihara in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aihara get in January?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
