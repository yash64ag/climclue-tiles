Aihara weather in February

Average February weather in Aihara, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Aihara averages 9.4 °C (49 °F) days and -1.2 °C (30 °F) nights, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 155 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Aihara

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aihara. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.
