How February compares in Aihara

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aihara. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Aihara in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aihara in February?
In February, Aihara averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -1.4 °C (29 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aihara?
No month in Aihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aihara in February?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (203 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Aihara in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aihara get in February?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aihara in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
