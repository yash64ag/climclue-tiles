How August compares in Aihara

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Aihara. Compared with July: 1.2 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. September is 3.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Aihara in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aihara in August?
In August, Aihara averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Aihara?
No month in Aihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aihara in August?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (206 mm), the driest January (45 mm).
Does it snow in Aihara in August?

How many hours of sunshine does Aihara get in August?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
