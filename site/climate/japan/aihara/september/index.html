How September compares in Aihara

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Aihara. Compared with August: 3.7 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Aihara in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aihara in September?
In September, Aihara averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aihara?
