How March compares in Aihara

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Aihara. Compared with February: 3.5 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. April is 5.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Aihara in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aihara in March?
In March, Aihara averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Aihara?
No month in Aihara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aihara in March?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (203 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Aihara in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aihara get in March?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aihara in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
