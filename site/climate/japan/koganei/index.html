Koganei at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Koganei is 191st warmest (15.1 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 1,851 hours of sunshine a year) and 187th wettest (1,432 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kokubunji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Koganei is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Why Koganei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
