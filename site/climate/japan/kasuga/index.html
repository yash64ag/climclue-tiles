Kasuga at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kasuga is 22nd warmest (17.0 °C year average), 227th sunniest (about 1,786 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (1,716 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chikushino-shi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Kasuga is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 199 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.3 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kasuga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kasuga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
