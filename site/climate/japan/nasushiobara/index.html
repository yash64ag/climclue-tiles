Nasushiobara at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Nasushiobara is 249th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 169th sunniest (about 1,831 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,552 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Utsunomiya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Nasushiobara is 1.2 °C cooler in August and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 75 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nasushiobara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nasushiobara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 75 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
