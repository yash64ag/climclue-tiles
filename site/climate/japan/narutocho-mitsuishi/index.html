Narutochō-mitsuishi at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Narutochō-mitsuishi is 183rd warmest (17.0 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 1,984 hours of sunshine a year) and 739th wettest (1,490 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Takabō, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Narutochō-mitsuishi is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Why Narutochō-mitsuishi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
