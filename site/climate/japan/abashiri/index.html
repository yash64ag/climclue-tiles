Abashiri at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Abashiri is the 2nd coolest (6.1 °C year average). Compared with Motomachi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Abashiri is 1.7 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in February, with 96 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.6 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April and December. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Abashiri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-399-3242), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
