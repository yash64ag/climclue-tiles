Asahikawa at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Asahikawa is the 5th coolest (7.2 °C year average). Compared with Ebetsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Asahikawa is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 190 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.3 °C), averages 28.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Why Asahikawa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
