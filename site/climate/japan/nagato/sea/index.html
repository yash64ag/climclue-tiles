Nagato sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Yamaguchi · Japan

The sea off Nagato averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.1 °C (56 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Nagato sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nagato?
In August, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nagato?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 13.1 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nagato in March?
About 13.1 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nagato?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Nagato nearby?
In August: Sanyōonoda (27.2 °C, 39 km), Ube (27.2 °C, 49 km) and Yukuhashi (27.2 °C, 75 km).
