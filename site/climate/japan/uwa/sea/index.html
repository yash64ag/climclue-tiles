Uwa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ehime · Japan

The sea off Uwa averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Uwa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Uwa?
In August, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Uwa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Uwa in February?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Uwa?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Uwa nearby?
In August: Nakamura (27.0 °C, 58 km).
