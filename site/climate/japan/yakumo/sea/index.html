Yakumo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Yakumo averages 20.8 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.3 °C (31 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Yakumo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yakumo?
In August, at about 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yakumo?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at -0.3 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yakumo in March?
About -0.3 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yakumo?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Yakumo nearby?
In August: Yoichi (21.4 °C, 113 km).
