How January compares in Kawaguchi

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kawaguchi. Compared with December: 2.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Kawaguchi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawaguchi in January?
In January, Kawaguchi averages a day high of 8.9 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kawaguchi?
No month in Kawaguchi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawaguchi in January?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (199 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kawaguchi in January?
Yes, on average: about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawaguchi get in January?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
