How June compares in Kawaguchi

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kawaguchi. Compared with May: 3.3 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Kawaguchi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawaguchi in June?
In June, Kawaguchi averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kawaguchi?
No month in Kawaguchi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawaguchi in June?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kawaguchi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawaguchi get in June?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawaguchi in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
