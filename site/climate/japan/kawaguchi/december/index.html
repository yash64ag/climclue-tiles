How December compares in Kawaguchi

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kawaguchi. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. January is 2.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Kawaguchi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawaguchi in December?
In December, Kawaguchi averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kawaguchi?
No month in Kawaguchi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawaguchi in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kawaguchi in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawaguchi get in December?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawaguchi in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
