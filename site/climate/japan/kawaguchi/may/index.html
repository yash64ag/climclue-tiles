How May compares in Kawaguchi

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kawaguchi. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Kawaguchi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawaguchi in May?
In May, Kawaguchi averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 123 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kawaguchi?
No month in Kawaguchi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawaguchi in May?
123 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kawaguchi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawaguchi get in May?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawaguchi in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
