How February compares in Kawaguchi

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kawaguchi. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Kawaguchi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawaguchi in February?
In February, Kawaguchi averages a day high of 8.7 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kawaguchi?
No month in Kawaguchi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawaguchi in February?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kawaguchi in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.9 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawaguchi get in February?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawaguchi in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
