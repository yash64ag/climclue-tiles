How April compares in Kawaguchi

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kawaguchi. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. May is 4.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Kawaguchi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kawaguchi in April?
In April, Kawaguchi averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kawaguchi?
No month in Kawaguchi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kawaguchi in April?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Kawaguchi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kawaguchi get in April?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kawaguchi in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
