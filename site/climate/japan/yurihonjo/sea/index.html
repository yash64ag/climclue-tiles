Yurihonjō sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Akita · Japan

The sea off Yurihonjō averages 24.9 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.3 °C (43 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Yurihonjō sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yurihonjō?
In August, at about 24.9 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yurihonjō?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 6.3 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yurihonjō in March?
About 6.3 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yurihonjō?
About 14.5 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
