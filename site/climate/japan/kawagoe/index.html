Kawagoe at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kawagoe is 182nd warmest (15.2 °C year average), 69th sunniest (about 1,888 hours of sunshine a year) and 242nd wettest (1,325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fujimino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Kawagoe is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.0 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kawagoe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kawagoe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kawagoe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.909°N, 139.485°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-480-3194), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
