Ōdachō-ōda sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shimane · Japan

The sea off Ōdachō-ōda averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.0 °C (55 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Ōdachō-ōda sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ōdachō-ōda?
In August, at about 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ōdachō-ōda?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 13.0 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ōdachō-ōda in March?
About 13.0 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ōdachō-ōda?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
