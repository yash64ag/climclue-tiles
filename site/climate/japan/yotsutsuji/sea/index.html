Yotsutsuji sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kyoto · Japan

The sea off Yotsutsuji averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.2 °C (52 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Yotsutsuji sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yotsutsuji?
In August, at about 26.7 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yotsutsuji?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 11.2 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yotsutsuji in March?
About 11.2 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yotsutsuji?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
