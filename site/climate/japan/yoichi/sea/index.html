Yoichi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Yoichi averages 21.4 °C (71 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.5 °C (35 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Yoichi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yoichi?
In August, at about 21.4 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yoichi?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 1.5 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yoichi in March?
About 1.5 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yoichi?
About 9.7 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
