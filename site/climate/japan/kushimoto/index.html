Kushimoto weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kushimoto, Japan (Wakayama), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kushimoto is warmest in August, with 30.2 °C (86 °F) days and 24.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 12.8 °C (55 °F) days and 5.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around June (356 mm); December is driest (80 mm).

Kushimoto at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Kushimoto is 47th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,002 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (2,595 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shingū, Wakayama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Kushimoto is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 593 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kushimoto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kushimoto itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.468°N, 135.779°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-505-3157), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
