Minakami at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Minakami is 1045th warmest (12.4 °C year average), 570th sunniest (about 1,839 hours of sunshine a year) and 990th wettest (1,370 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Numata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Minakami is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Minakami, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Minakami has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C. The 10 km area around Minakami, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 11.1 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Minakami itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.688°N, 138.986°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-473-3189), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
