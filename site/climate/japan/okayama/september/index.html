How September compares in Okayama

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Okayama. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 55 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 6.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Okayama in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okayama in September?
In September, Okayama averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Okayama?
September is not one of Okayama’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okayama in September?
146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (180 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Okayama in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okayama get in September?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Okayama in September?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Okayama in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
