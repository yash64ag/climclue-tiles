How February compares in Okayama

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Okayama. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Okayama in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okayama in February?
In February, Okayama averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Okayama?
February is not one of Okayama’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okayama in February?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (180 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Okayama in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Okayama get in February?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Okayama in February?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Okayama in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
