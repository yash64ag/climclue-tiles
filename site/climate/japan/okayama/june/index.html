How June compares in Okayama

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Okayama. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. July is 3.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Okayama in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okayama in June?
In June, Okayama averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Okayama?
June is not one of Okayama’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okayama in June?
179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (179 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Okayama in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okayama get in June?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okayama in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
