Why Okayama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Okayama climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Okayama?
August, with an average of 28.6 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.8 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Okayama?
January, with an average of 6.3 °C (43 °F), day highs around 9.0 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 2.2 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Okayama?
June, with 179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,189 mm (46.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Okayama?
December, with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Okayama?
August, with about 213 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Okayama?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Okayama?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Okayama have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Okayama’s GeoNames location (34.650°N, 133.933°E; ClimaClue area sq-493-3139), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 724,691 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
