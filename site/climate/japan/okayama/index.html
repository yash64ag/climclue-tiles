Okayama at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Okayama is 28th warmest (16.9 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 1,953 hours of sunshine a year) and 270th wettest (1,189 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kurashiki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Okayama is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Okayama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Okayama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
