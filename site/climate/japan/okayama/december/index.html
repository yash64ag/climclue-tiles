How December compares in Okayama

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Okayama. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Okayama in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okayama in December?
In December, Okayama averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Okayama?
December is not one of Okayama’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okayama in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (180 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Okayama in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okayama get in December?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okayama in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
