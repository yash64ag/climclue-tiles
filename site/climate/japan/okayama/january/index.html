How January compares in Okayama

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Okayama. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Okayama in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okayama in January?
In January, Okayama averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Okayama?
January is not one of Okayama’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okayama in January?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (180 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Okayama in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Okayama get in January?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okayama in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
