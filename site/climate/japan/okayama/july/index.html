How July compares in Okayama

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Okayama. Compared with June: 3.9 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. August is 1.5 °C warmer than July on average.

Okayama in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Okayama in July?
In July, Okayama averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Okayama?
July is not one of Okayama’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Okayama in July?
170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (179 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Okayama in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Okayama get in July?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Okayama in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
