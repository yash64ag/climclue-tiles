Kurayoshi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tottori · Japan

The sea off Kurayoshi averages 26.3 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.6 °C (53 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Kurayoshi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kurayoshi?
In August, at about 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kurayoshi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 11.6 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kurayoshi in March?
About 11.6 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kurayoshi?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
