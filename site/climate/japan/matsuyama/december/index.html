How December compares in Matsuyama

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Matsuyama. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Matsuyama in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matsuyama in December?
In December, Matsuyama averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Matsuyama?
December is not one of Matsuyama’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matsuyama in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (235 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Matsuyama in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matsuyama get in December?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matsuyama in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
