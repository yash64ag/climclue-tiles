How August compares in Matsuyama

August is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Matsuyama. Compared with July: 1.0 °C warmer, 107 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. September is 3.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Matsuyama in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matsuyama in August?
In August, Matsuyama averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Matsuyama?
August is not one of Matsuyama’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matsuyama in August?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (235 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Matsuyama in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matsuyama get in August?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Matsuyama in August?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Matsuyama in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
