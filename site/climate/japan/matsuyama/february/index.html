How February compares in Matsuyama

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Matsuyama. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. March is 2.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Matsuyama in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matsuyama in February?
In February, Matsuyama averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Matsuyama?
February is not one of Matsuyama’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matsuyama in February?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (235 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Matsuyama in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.9 cm (2.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Matsuyama get in February?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matsuyama in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
