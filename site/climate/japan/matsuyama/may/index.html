How May compares in Matsuyama

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Matsuyama. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Matsuyama in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matsuyama in May?
In May, Matsuyama averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Matsuyama?
May is not one of Matsuyama’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matsuyama in May?
146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (235 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Matsuyama in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Matsuyama get in May?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matsuyama in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
