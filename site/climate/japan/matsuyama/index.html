Matsuyama at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Matsuyama is 24th warmest (17.0 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 1,936 hours of sunshine a year) and 227th wettest (1,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shinagawa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Matsuyama is 3.6 °C warmer in August and 5.7 °C warmer in January, with 808 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matsuyama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matsuyama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
