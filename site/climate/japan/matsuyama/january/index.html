How January compares in Matsuyama

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Matsuyama. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Matsuyama in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Matsuyama in January?
In January, Matsuyama averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Matsuyama?
January is not one of Matsuyama’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Matsuyama in January?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (235 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Matsuyama in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Matsuyama get in January?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Matsuyama in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
