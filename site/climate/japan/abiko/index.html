Abiko at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Abiko is 151st warmest (15.4 °C year average), 198th sunniest (about 1,819 hours of sunshine a year) and 208th wettest (1,414 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kashiwa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Abiko is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Abiko, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Abiko has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
