Izumi, Kagoshima at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Izumi, Kagoshima is 72nd warmest (17.8 °C year average), 793rd sunniest (about 1,812 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (2,243 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Minamata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Izumi, Kagoshima is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.7 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Why Izumi, Kagoshima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-519-3103), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
