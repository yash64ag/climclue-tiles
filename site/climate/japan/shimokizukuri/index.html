Shimokizukuri at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Shimokizukuri is 1146th warmest (10.6 °C year average), 1160th sunniest (about 1,601 hours of sunshine a year) and 574th wettest (1,630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tsugaru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Shimokizukuri is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Shimokizukuri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-431-3203), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
