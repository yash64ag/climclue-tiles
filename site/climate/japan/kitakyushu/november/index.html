Kitakyushu weather in November

Average November weather in Kitakyushu, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Kitakyushu averages 17.5 °C (64 °F) days and 10.0 °C (50 °F) nights, with 94 mm (3.7 in) of rain over the month and about 128 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Kitakyushu

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kitakyushu. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Kitakyushu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitakyushu in November?
In November, Kitakyushu averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kitakyushu?
November is not one of Kitakyushu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitakyushu in November?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (349 mm), the driest December (70 mm).
Does it snow in Kitakyushu in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitakyushu get in November?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kitakyushu in November?
About 19.7 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kitakyushu in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
