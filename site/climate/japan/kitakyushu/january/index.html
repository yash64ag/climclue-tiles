How January compares in Kitakyushu

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kitakyushu. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Kitakyushu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitakyushu in January?
In January, Kitakyushu averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kitakyushu?
January is not one of Kitakyushu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitakyushu in January?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (349 mm), the driest December (70 mm).
Does it snow in Kitakyushu in January?
Yes, on average: about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitakyushu get in January?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kitakyushu in January?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kitakyushu in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
