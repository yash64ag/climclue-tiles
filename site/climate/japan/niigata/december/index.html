How December compares in Niigata

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Niigata in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in December?
In December, Niigata averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 64 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in December?
234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (234 mm), the driest April (99 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in December?
About 64 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niigata in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
