How November compares in Niigata

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Niigata in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in November?
In November, Niigata averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in November?
234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (234 mm), the driest April (99 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in November?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niigata in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
