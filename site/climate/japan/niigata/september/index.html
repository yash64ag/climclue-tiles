Niigata weather in September

Average September weather in Niigata, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Niigata averages 26.3 °C (79 °F) days and 18.7 °C (66 °F) nights, with 167 mm (6.6 in) of rain over the month and about 137 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Niigata

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with August: 3.7 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Niigata in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in September?
In September, Niigata averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in September?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (231 mm), the driest April (97 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in September?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Niigata in September?
About 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Niigata in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
