How February compares in Niigata

February is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with January: about as warm, 70 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. March is 2.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Niigata in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in February?
In February, Niigata averages a day high of 4.7 °C (40 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 81 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in February?
131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (234 mm), the driest April (99 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 47 cm (18.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 21 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in February?
About 81 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niigata in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
