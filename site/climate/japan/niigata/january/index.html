How January compares in Niigata

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Niigata. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Niigata among towns in Japan in January

Of the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Niigata ranks 223rd warmest (average temperature), 269th sunniest and 11th wettest in January.

Niigata in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in January?
In January, Niigata averages a day high of 4.5 °C (40 °F) and a night low of 0.5 °C (33 °F), with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 60 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in January?
201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (234 mm), the driest April (99 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 72 cm (28.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in January?
About 60 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niigata in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
