How June compares in Niigata

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. July is 3.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Niigata in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in June?
In June, Niigata averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in June?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (234 mm), the driest April (99 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in June?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niigata in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
