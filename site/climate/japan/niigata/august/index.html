How August compares in Niigata

August is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with July: 2.0 °C warmer, 49 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Niigata in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in August?
In August, Niigata averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in August?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (231 mm), the driest April (97 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in August?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Niigata in August?
About 26.4 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Niigata in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
