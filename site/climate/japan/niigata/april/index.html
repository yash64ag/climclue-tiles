How April compares in Niigata

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Niigata. Compared with March: 5.0 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Niigata in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niigata in April?
In April, Niigata averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Niigata?
No month in Niigata is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niigata in April?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (231 mm), the driest April (97 mm).
Does it snow in Niigata in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niigata get in April?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Niigata in April?
About 10.9 °C (52 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Niigata in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
