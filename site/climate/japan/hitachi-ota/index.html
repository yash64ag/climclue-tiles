Hitachi-ota weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Hitachi-ota, Japan (Ibaraki), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Hitachi-ota is warmest in August, with 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 21.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 10.4 °C (51 °F) days and -0.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (165 mm); December is driest (40 mm).

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Hitachi-ota is 872nd warmest (14.7 °C year average), 365th sunniest (about 1,870 hours of sunshine a year) and 1143rd wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Hitachi-ota has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hitachi-ota itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.551°N, 140.528°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-474-3205), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
