Nanao sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ishikawa · Japan

The sea off Nanao averages 26.3 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.0 °C (50 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Nanao sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nanao?
In August, at about 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nanao?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 10.0 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nanao in March?
About 10.0 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nanao?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Nanao nearby?
In August: Takaoka (26.9 °C, 33 km), Imizu (26.9 °C, 34 km) and Toyama (26.9 °C, 45 km).
