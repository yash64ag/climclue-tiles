Ueda at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ueda is the 5th driest (1,060 mm a year). Compared with Nagano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Ueda is 0.8 °C cooler in August and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 331 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Why Ueda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
