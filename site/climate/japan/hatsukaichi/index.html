Hatsukaichi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Hatsukaichi is 103rd warmest (15.9 °C year average), 131st sunniest (about 1,850 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (1,795 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hiroshima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Hatsukaichi is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 191 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 22.3 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 24 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hatsukaichi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
