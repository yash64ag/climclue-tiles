Unzen sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nagasaki · Japan

The sea off Unzen averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.8 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Unzen sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Unzen?
In August, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Unzen?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.8 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Unzen in February?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Unzen?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
