Kurihashi at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Kurihashi is 783rd warmest (15.0 °C year average), 369th sunniest (about 1,870 hours of sunshine a year) and 972nd wettest (1,380 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Washinomiya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Kurihashi is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Why Kurihashi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kurihashi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.133°N, 139.700°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-478-3197), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
