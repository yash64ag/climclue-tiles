Aizu-Wakamatsu at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Aizu-Wakamatsu is 262nd warmest (11.1 °C year average), 252nd sunniest (about 1,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 176th wettest (1,461 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kōriyama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Aizu-Wakamatsu is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 232 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.8 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 97 km from the sea.
