Why Aizu-Wakamatsu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 97 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Aizu-Wakamatsu’s GeoNames location (37.491°N, 139.945°E; ClimaClue area sq-465-3199), averaged over the area’s land. The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,159 (GeoNames).
