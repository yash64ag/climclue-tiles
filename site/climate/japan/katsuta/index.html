Why Katsuta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Katsuta climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Katsuta?
August, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.9 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 23.0 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Katsuta?
January, with an average of 5.8 °C (42 °F), day highs around 7.9 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 2.6 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Katsuta?
September, with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,431 mm (56.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Katsuta?
December, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Katsuta?
May, with about 186 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Katsuta?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Katsuta?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Katsuta have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Katsuta’s GeoNames location (36.383°N, 140.533°E; ClimaClue area sq-476-3205), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 155,968 (GeoNames).
