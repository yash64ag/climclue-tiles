Toyama at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Toyama is 185th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 265th sunniest (about 1,627 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (2,443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Takaoka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Toyama is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Toyama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Toyama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
