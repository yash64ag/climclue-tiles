Toyama sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Japan

The sea off Toyama averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.2 °C (50 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Toyama sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Toyama?
In August, at about 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Toyama?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 10.2 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Toyama in March?
About 10.2 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Toyama?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
