Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E) sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E) averages 15.9 °C (61 °F) in September, its warmest month, and -0.9 °C (30 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E) sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E)?
In September, at about 15.9 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E)?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -0.9 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E) in February?
About -0.9 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E)?
About 6.9 °C (44 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Shibetsu, Hokkaido (43.7°N 145.1°E) nearby?
In September: Shizunai-furukawachō (19.4 °C, 269 km), Rumoi (20.4 °C, 282 km) and Tōbetsu (20.2 °C, 296 km).
