Otaru at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Otaru is the coolest (6.0 °C year average). Compared with Sapporo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Otaru is 1.5 °C cooler in August and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 437 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.9 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Otaru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Otaru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
