Why Otaru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Otaru climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Otaru?
August, with an average of 19.9 °C (68 °F), day highs around 22.3 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 17.2 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Otaru?
January, with an average of -7.0 °C (19 °F), day highs around -5.6 °C (22 °F) and night lows around -10.4 °C (13 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Otaru?
September, with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,057 mm (81.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Otaru?
May, with 118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Otaru?
May, with about 194 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Otaru?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 379 cm (149.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 7 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Otaru?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Otaru’s GeoNames location (43.189°N, 141.002°E; ClimaClue area sq-408-3210), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 115,333 (GeoNames).
