Namie sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fukushima · Japan

The sea off Namie averages 22.9 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.1 °C (48 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Namie sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Namie?
In August, at about 22.9 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Namie?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 9.1 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Namie in March?
About 9.1 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Namie?
About 15.7 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
