How February compares in Saitama

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Saitama. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Saitama in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in February?
In February, Saitama averages a day high of 8.7 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -1.1 °C (30 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Saitama?
No month in Saitama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saitama in February?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (199 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Saitama in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.8 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Saitama get in February?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saitama in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
