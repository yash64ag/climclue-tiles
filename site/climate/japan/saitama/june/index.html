How June compares in Saitama

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Saitama. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Saitama in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in June?
In June, Saitama averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Saitama?
No month in Saitama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saitama in June?
147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (199 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Saitama in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saitama get in June?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
