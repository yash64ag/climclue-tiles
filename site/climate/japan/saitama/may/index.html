How May compares in Saitama

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Saitama. Compared with April: 4.6 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 3.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Saitama in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in May?
In May, Saitama averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Saitama?
No month in Saitama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saitama in May?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (199 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Saitama in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saitama get in May?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saitama in May?
