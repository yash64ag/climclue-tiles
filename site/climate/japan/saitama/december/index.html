How December compares in Saitama

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Saitama. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. January is 2.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Saitama in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in December?
In December, Saitama averages a day high of 11.8 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Saitama?
No month in Saitama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saitama in December?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Saitama in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Saitama get in December?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
