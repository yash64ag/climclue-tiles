How October compares in Saitama

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Saitama. Compared with September: 5.9 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Saitama in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in October?
In October, Saitama averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Saitama?
No month in Saitama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saitama in October?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (199 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Saitama in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saitama get in October?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saitama in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
