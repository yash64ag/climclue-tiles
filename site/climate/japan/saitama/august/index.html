How August compares in Saitama

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Saitama. Compared with July: 1.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. September is 3.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Saitama in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in August?
In August, Saitama averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Saitama?
