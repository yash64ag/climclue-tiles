Saitama weather in January

Average January weather in Saitama, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Saitama averages 9.2 °C (49 °F) days and -1.3 °C (30 °F) nights, with 42 mm (1.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 186 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Saitama

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Saitama. Compared with December: 2.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Saitama in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saitama in January?
In January, Saitama averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -1.3 °C (30 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Saitama?
No month in Saitama is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saitama in January?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (198 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Saitama in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.3 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Saitama get in January?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
