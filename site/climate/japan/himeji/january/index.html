How January compares in Himeji

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Himeji. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Himeji in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Himeji in January?
In January, Himeji averages a day high of 10.6 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Himeji?
No month in Himeji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Himeji in January?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (178 mm), the driest January (45 mm).
Does it snow in Himeji in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.1 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Himeji get in January?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Himeji in January?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Himeji in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
