How May compares in Himeji

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Himeji. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Himeji in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Himeji in May?
In May, Himeji averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Himeji?
No month in Himeji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Himeji in May?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (194 mm), the driest January (51 mm).
Does it snow in Himeji in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Himeji get in May?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Himeji in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
