How April compares in Himeji

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Himeji. Compared with March: 5.1 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Himeji in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Himeji in April?
In April, Himeji averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Himeji?
No month in Himeji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Himeji in April?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (194 mm), the driest January (51 mm).
Does it snow in Himeji in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Himeji get in April?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Himeji in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
