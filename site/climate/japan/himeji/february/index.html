How February compares in Himeji

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Himeji. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Himeji in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Himeji in February?
In February, Himeji averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Himeji?
No month in Himeji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Himeji in February?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (178 mm), the driest January (45 mm).
Does it snow in Himeji in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Himeji get in February?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Himeji in February?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Himeji in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
