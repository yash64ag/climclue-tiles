How December compares in Himeji

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Himeji. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Himeji in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Himeji in December?
In December, Himeji averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Himeji?
No month in Himeji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Himeji in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (178 mm), the driest January (45 mm).
Does it snow in Himeji in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Himeji get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Himeji in December?
About 16.5 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Himeji in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
