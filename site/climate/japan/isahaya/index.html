Isahaya at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Isahaya is 15th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 234th sunniest (about 1,775 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (2,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nagasaki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Isahaya is 0.9 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 89 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Isahaya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Isahaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
