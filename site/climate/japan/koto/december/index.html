How December compares in Kotō

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Kotō in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in December?
In December, Kotō averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in December?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (482 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in December?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kotō in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
