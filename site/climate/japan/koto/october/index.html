How October compares in Kotō

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with September: 5.4 °C cooler, 111 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. November is 5.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Kotō in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in October?
In October, Kotō averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in October?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (482 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in October?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kotō in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
