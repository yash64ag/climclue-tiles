April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Kotō in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in April?
In April, Kotō averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in April?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (482 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in April?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kotō in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
