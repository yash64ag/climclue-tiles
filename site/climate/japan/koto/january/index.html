How January compares in Kotō

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Kotō in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in January?
In January, Kotō averages a day high of 11.8 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in January?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (478 mm), the driest December (66 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in January?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kotō in January?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kotō in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
