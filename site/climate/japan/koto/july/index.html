How July compares in Kotō

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with June: 3.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 1.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Kotō in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in July?
In July, Kotō averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 479 mm (18.9 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in July?
479 mm (18.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (482 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in July?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kotō in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
