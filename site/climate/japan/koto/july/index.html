Kotō weather in July

Average July weather in Kotō, Japan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Kotō averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 23.1 °C (74 °F) nights, with 475 mm (18.7 in) of rain over the month and about 179 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Kotō

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with June: 3.6 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 1.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Kotō in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in July?

Is July a good time to visit Kotō?
