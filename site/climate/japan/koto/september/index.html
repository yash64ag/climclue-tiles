Kotō in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in September?
In September, Kotō averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in September?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (478 mm), the driest December (66 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in September?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kotō in September?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kotō in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
