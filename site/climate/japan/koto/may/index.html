How May compares in Kotō

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kotō. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Kotō in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kotō in May?
In May, Kotō averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kotō?
No month in Kotō is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kotō in May?
239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (482 mm), the driest December (67 mm).
Does it snow in Kotō in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kotō get in May?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kotō in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
