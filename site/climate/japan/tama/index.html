Why Tama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Tama climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tama?
August, with an average of 26.4 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 22.8 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tama?
January, with an average of 4.0 °C (39 °F), day highs around 7.8 °C (46 °F) and night lows around -1.2 °C (30 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tama?
September, with 204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,431 mm (56.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tama?
January, with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tama?
May, with about 183 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tama?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 39 cm (15.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Tama?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Tama have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Tama’s GeoNames location (35.644°N, 139.468°E; ClimaClue area sq-483-3194), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 148,285 (GeoNames).
