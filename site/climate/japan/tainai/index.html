Tainai at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Tainai is 987th warmest (13.5 °C year average), 1143rd sunniest (about 1,617 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (2,505 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shibata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Tainai is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Tainai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tainai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.065°N, 139.406°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-459-3194), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
