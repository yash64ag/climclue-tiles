Tomakomai sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Tomakomai averages 19.8 °C (68 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.7 °C (33 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Tomakomai sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tomakomai?
In August, at about 19.8 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tomakomai?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 0.7 °C (33 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tomakomai in March?
About 0.7 °C (33 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tomakomai?
About 8.7 °C (48 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Tomakomai nearby?
In August: Sapporo (21.2 °C, 52 km), Date, Hokkaido (20.7 °C, 63 km) and Tōbetsu (21.1 °C, 65 km).
