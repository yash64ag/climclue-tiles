Tomakomai at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Tomakomai is 271st warmest (7.7 °C year average), 240th sunniest (about 1,769 hours of sunshine a year) and 178th wettest (1,456 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sapporo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Tomakomai is 1.1 °C cooler in August and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 165 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.4 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tomakomai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tomakomai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
