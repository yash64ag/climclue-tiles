Shizukuishi at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Shizukuishi is 1153rd warmest (10.2 °C year average), 988th sunniest (about 1,749 hours of sunshine a year) and 496th wettest (1,717 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Morioka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Shizukuishi is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 321 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 25.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Why Shizukuishi has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shizukuishi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.694°N, 140.984°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-443-3209), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
