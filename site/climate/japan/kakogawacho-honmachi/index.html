Kakogawachō-honmachi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Kakogawachō-honmachi is 51st warmest (16.7 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 1,872 hours of sunshine a year) and 259th wettest (1,320 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Himeji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Kakogawachō-honmachi is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 115 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (6.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).
