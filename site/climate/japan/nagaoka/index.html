Nagaoka at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Nagaoka is 242nd warmest (13.8 °C year average), 257th sunniest (about 1,681 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (2,165 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Niigata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Nagaoka is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 179 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 24.8 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nagaoka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nagaoka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
