Ishikari sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Ishikari averages 21.2 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.6 °C (33 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Ishikari sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ishikari?
In August, at about 21.2 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ishikari?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 0.6 °C (33 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ishikari in March?
About 0.6 °C (33 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ishikari?
About 9.1 °C (48 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
