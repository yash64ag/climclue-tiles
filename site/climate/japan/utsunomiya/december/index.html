Utsunomiya in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Utsunomiya in December?
In December, Utsunomiya averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Utsunomiya?
No month in Utsunomiya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Utsunomiya in December?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Utsunomiya in December?

How many hours of sunshine does Utsunomiya get in December?
