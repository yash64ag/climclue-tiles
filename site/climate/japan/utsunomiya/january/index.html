How January compares in Utsunomiya

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Utsunomiya. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Utsunomiya in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Utsunomiya in January?
In January, Utsunomiya averages a day high of 6.7 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -2.4 °C (28 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Utsunomiya?
No month in Utsunomiya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Utsunomiya in January?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (238 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Utsunomiya in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Utsunomiya get in January?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Utsunomiya in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
