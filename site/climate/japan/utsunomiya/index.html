Why Utsunomiya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Utsunomiya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Utsunomiya?
August, with an average of 25.8 °C (78 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 21.7 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Utsunomiya?
January, with an average of 3.0 °C (37 °F), day highs around 6.7 °C (44 °F) and night lows around -2.4 °C (28 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Utsunomiya?
September, with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,582 mm (62.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Utsunomiya?
January, with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Utsunomiya?
May, with about 188 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Utsunomiya?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 49 cm (19.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Utsunomiya?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Utsunomiya have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Utsunomiya’s GeoNames location (36.567°N, 139.883°E; ClimaClue area sq-474-3198), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 518,757 (GeoNames).
