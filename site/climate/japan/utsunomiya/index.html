Utsunomiya at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Utsunomiya is 236th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 85th sunniest (about 1,876 hours of sunshine a year) and 120th wettest (1,582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tochigi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Utsunomiya is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.8 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Utsunomiya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Utsunomiya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
