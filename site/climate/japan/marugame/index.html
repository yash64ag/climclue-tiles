Marugame at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Marugame is 27th warmest (17.0 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,011 hours of sunshine a year) and 213th wettest (1,401 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Takamatsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Marugame is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 140 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Marugame, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Marugame has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
