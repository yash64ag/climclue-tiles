Uruma at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Uruma is the 2nd warmest (22.8 °C year average). Compared with Ginowan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Uruma is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 16 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 118% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Uruma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
