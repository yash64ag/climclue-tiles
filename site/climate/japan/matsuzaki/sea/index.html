Matsuzaki sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tottori · Japan

The sea off Matsuzaki averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.4 °C (53 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Matsuzaki sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Matsuzaki?
In August, at about 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Matsuzaki?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 11.4 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Matsuzaki in March?
About 11.4 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Matsuzaki?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Matsuzaki nearby?
In August: Yonago (26.8 °C, 51 km), Yasugichō (26.8 °C, 60 km) and Sakaiminato (26.8 °C, 61 km).
