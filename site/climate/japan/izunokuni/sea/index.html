Izunokuni sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shizuoka · Japan

The sea off Izunokuni averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Izunokuni sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Izunokuni?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Izunokuni?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Izunokuni in February?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Izunokuni?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
