Sano at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Sano is 232nd warmest (14.4 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 1,891 hours of sunshine a year) and 175th wettest (1,463 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ashikaga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Sano is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 93 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sano, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 93 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
