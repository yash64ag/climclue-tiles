Ōgaki at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ōgaki is 127th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 114th sunniest (about 1,858 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (1,759 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gifu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Ōgaki is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 153 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ōgaki, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ōgaki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
