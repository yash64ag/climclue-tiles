Why Yamazakichō-nakabirose has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C. The 10 km area around Yamazakichō-nakabirose, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.3 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
