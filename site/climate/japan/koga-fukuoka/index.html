Koga, Fukuoka (33.7°N 130.5°E) at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Koga, Fukuoka (33.7°N 130.5°E) is 135th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 851st sunniest (about 1,793 hours of sunshine a year) and 374th wettest (1,863 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nishifukuma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Koga, Fukuoka (33.7°N 130.5°E) is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Koga, Fukuoka (33.7°N 130.5°E) has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
