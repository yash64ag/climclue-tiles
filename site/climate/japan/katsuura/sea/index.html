Katsuura sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chiba · Japan

The sea off Katsuura averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.6 °C (60 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Katsuura sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Katsuura?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Katsuura?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.6 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Katsuura in February?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Katsuura?
About 20.2 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
