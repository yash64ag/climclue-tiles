Mutsu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Aomori · Japan

The sea off Mutsu averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.6 °C (35 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Mutsu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mutsu?
In August, at about 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mutsu?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 1.6 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mutsu in March?
About 1.6 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mutsu?
About 9.6 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mutsu nearby?
In August: Goshogawara (23.9 °C, 85 km) and Shimokizukuri (23.9 °C, 88 km).
