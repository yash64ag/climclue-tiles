Ōfunato at a glance

Among the 1213 towns ClimaClue covers in Japan, Ōfunato is 1104th warmest (11.4 °C year average), 296th sunniest (about 1,887 hours of sunshine a year) and 603rd wettest (1,598 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rikuzen-Takata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Ōfunato is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 188 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, February (1.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and March. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ōfunato, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Ōfunato has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ōfunato itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.072°N, 141.717°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-449-3217), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
