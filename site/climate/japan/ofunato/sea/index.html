Ōfunato sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Iwate · Japan

The sea off Ōfunato averages 21.2 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.5 °C (42 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Ōfunato sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ōfunato?
In August, at about 21.2 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ōfunato?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 5.5 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ōfunato in March?
About 5.5 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ōfunato?
About 13.3 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ōfunato nearby?
In August: Ishinomaki (22.7 °C, 81 km), Yamoto (22.7 °C, 84 km) and Higashimatsushima (22.7 °C, 87 km).
