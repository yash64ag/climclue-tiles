Hakodate at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Hakodate is 269th warmest (8.2 °C year average), 259th sunniest (about 1,676 hours of sunshine a year) and 267th wettest (1,234 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aomori, the nearest covered town with different numbers (107 km away), Hakodate is 2.4 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 363 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.3 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Hakodate has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
