Ōshū at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Ōshū is 259th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 215th sunniest (about 1,810 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (1,555 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ichinoseki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Ōshū is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 111 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 62 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ōshū, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ōshū has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 62 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
