Ryōtsu-minato sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Niigata · Japan

The sea off Ryōtsu-minato averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.2 °C (49 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Ryōtsu-minato sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ryōtsu-minato?
In August, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ryōtsu-minato?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 9.2 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ryōtsu-minato in March?
About 9.2 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ryōtsu-minato?
About 16.5 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ryōtsu-minato nearby?
In August: Maki (26.2 °C, 53 km), Niigata (26.4 °C, 55 km) and Yoshida-kasugachō (26.2 °C, 59 km).
