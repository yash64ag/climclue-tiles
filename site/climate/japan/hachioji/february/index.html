Hachiōji in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hachiōji in February?
In February, Hachiōji averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -1.5 °C (29 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hachiōji?
No month in Hachiōji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hachiōji in February?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (203 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Hachiōji in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.9 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hachiōji get in February?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hachiōji in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
