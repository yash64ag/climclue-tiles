How January compares in Hachiōji

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Hachiōji. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Hachiōji in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hachiōji in January?
In January, Hachiōji averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hachiōji?
No month in Hachiōji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hachiōji in January?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (203 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Hachiōji in January?
Yes, on average: about 10 cm (4.0 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hachiōji get in January?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hachiōji in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
