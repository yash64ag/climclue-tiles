Hachiōji in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hachiōji in July?
In July, Hachiōji averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hachiōji?
No month in Hachiōji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

Does it snow in Hachiōji in July?

How many hours of sunshine does Hachiōji get in July?
