Hachiōji in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hachiōji in October?
In October, Hachiōji averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hachiōji?
No month in Hachiōji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
