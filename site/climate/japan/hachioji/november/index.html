Hachiōji in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hachiōji in November?
In November, Hachiōji averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hachiōji?
No month in Hachiōji is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hachiōji in November?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (203 mm), the driest January (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hachiōji in November?

How many hours of sunshine does Hachiōji get in November?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
