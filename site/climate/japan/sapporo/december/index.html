How December compares in Sapporo

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sapporo. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 3.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Sapporo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sapporo in December?
In December, Sapporo averages a day high of 2.0 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sapporo?
No month in Sapporo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sapporo in December?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (192 mm), the driest April (91 mm).
Does it snow in Sapporo in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 102 cm (40.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 14 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sapporo get in December?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sapporo in December?
About 2.0 °C (36 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (21.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Sapporo in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
