How October compares in Sapporo

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Sapporo. Compared with September: 6.2 °C cooler, 51 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. November is 6.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Sapporo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sapporo in October?
In October, Sapporo averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sapporo?
No month in Sapporo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sapporo in October?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (192 mm), the driest April (91 mm).
Does it snow in Sapporo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sapporo get in October?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sapporo in October?
About 15.2 °C (59 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (21.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Sapporo in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
