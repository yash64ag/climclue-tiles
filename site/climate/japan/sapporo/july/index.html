How July compares in Sapporo

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Sapporo. Compared with June: 3.9 °C warmer, 57 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. August is 1.8 °C warmer than July on average.

Sapporo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sapporo in July?
In July, Sapporo averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sapporo?
No month in Sapporo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sapporo in July?
170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (202 mm), the driest April (95 mm).
Does it snow in Sapporo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sapporo get in July?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sapporo in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
