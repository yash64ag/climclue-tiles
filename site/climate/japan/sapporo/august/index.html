How August compares in Sapporo

August is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Sapporo. Compared with July: 1.8 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Sapporo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sapporo in August?
In August, Sapporo averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sapporo?
