Sapporo at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Sapporo is 270th warmest (8.1 °C year average), 258th sunniest (about 1,677 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (1,620 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ebetsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Sapporo is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 83 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.4 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sapporo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sapporo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
