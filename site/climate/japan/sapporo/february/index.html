How February compares in Sapporo

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sapporo. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 24 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 4.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Sapporo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sapporo in February?
In February, Sapporo averages a day high of -2.3 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -9.3 °C (15 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Sapporo?
No month in Sapporo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sapporo in February?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (202 mm), the driest April (95 mm).
Does it snow in Sapporo in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 97 cm (38.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 45 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sapporo get in February?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sapporo in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
