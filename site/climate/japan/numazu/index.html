Why Numazu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Numazu climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Numazu?
August, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 28.0 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 22.3 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Numazu?
January, with an average of 5.0 °C (41 °F), day highs around 7.9 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 0.5 °C (33 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Numazu?
September, with 271 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,093 mm (82.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Numazu?
December, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Numazu?
May, with about 182 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Numazu?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 51 cm (20.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Numazu?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Numazu have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Numazu’s GeoNames location (35.100°N, 138.867°E; ClimaClue area sq-488-3188), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 189,486 (GeoNames).
