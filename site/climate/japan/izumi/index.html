Izumi at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Izumi is 70th warmest (16.4 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 1,885 hours of sunshine a year) and 212th wettest (1,403 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kishiwada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Izumi is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 78 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Izumi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Izumi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
