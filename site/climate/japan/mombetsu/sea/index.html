Mombetsu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hokkaido · Japan

The sea off Mombetsu averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.5 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Mombetsu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mombetsu?
In August, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mombetsu?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.5 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mombetsu in February?
About -1.5 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mombetsu?
About 6.4 °C (44 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mombetsu nearby?
In August: Rumoi (21.3 °C, 144 km), Makubetsu (17.5 °C, 166 km) and Wakkanai (19.1 °C, 177 km).
