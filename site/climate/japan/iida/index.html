Iida at a glance

Among the 276 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Japan, Iida is 254th warmest (12.0 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 1,862 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (1,681 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tajimi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Iida is 2.3 °C cooler in August and 4.7 °C cooler in January, with 269 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iida, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
