Hamilton sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hamilton city · Bermuda

The sea off Hamilton averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 19.4 °C (67 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Hamilton sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hamilton?
In August, at about 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hamilton?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is March, at 19.4 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hamilton in March?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hamilton?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
