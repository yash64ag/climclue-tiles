How January compares in Vientiane

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Vientiane. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Vientiane in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vientiane in January?
In January, Vientiane averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Vientiane?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Vientiane’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vientiane in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (365 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Vientiane in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vientiane get in January?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vientiane in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
