How May compares in Vientiane

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Vientiane. Compared with April: 0.7 °C cooler, 158 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. June is 0.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Vientiane in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vientiane in May?
In May, Vientiane averages a day high of 33.2 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Vientiane?
May is not one of Vientiane’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vientiane in May?
252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (365 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Vientiane in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vientiane get in May?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vientiane in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
