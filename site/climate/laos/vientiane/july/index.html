How July compares in Vientiane

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Vientiane. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 45 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Vientiane in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vientiane in July?
In July, Vientiane averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 340 mm (13.4 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Vientiane?
July is not one of Vientiane’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vientiane in July?
340 mm (13.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (365 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Vientiane in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vientiane get in July?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vientiane in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
