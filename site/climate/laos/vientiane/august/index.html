How August compares in Vientiane

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Vientiane. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. September is 0.1 °C cooler than August on average.

What to pack for Vientiane in August

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Vientiane in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vientiane in August?
In August, Vientiane averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 363 mm (14.3 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Vientiane?
August is not one of Vientiane’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vientiane in August?
363 mm (14.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (363 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Vientiane in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vientiane get in August?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vientiane in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
