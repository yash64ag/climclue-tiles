How September compares in Vientiane

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Vientiane. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 80 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. October is 0.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Vientiane in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vientiane in September?
In September, Vientiane averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Vientiane?
September is not one of Vientiane’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vientiane in September?
284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (363 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Vientiane in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vientiane get in September?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vientiane in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
