Vientiane weather in April

Average April weather in Vientiane, Laos: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Vientiane averages 35.2 °C (95 °F) days and 23.1 °C (74 °F) nights, with 94 mm (3.7 in) of rain over the month and about 230 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Vientiane

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Vientiane. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 51 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Vientiane in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vientiane in April?
In April, Vientiane averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Vientiane?
April is not one of Vientiane’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vientiane in April?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (365 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Vientiane in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vientiane get in April?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vientiane in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
