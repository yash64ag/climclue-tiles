Xam Nua at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Laos, Xam Nua is the coolest (20.1 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 1,805 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Muang Phônsavan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (140 km away), Xam Nua is 0.9 °C warmer in June and 1.1 °C cooler in December, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.2 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, December (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in December. The area lies about 217 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xam Nua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xam Nua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xam Nua itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.416°N, 104.045°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-635-2840), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
