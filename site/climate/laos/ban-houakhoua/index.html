Ban Houakhoua weather by month and climate

Ban Houakhoua at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Laos, Ban Houakhoua is 7th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,379 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (1,579 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ban Khoan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Ban Houakhoua is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C warmer in December, with 330 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.7 °C), averages 7.9 °C more than its coolest, December (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 497 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ban Houakhoua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ban Houakhoua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 497 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ban Houakhoua itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.247°N, 100.454°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-637-2804), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
