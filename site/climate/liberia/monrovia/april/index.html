How April compares in Monrovia

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Monrovia. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 101 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

What to pack for Monrovia in April

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Monrovia in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monrovia in April?
In April, Monrovia averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Monrovia?
No month in Monrovia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monrovia in April?
203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (526 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Monrovia in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monrovia get in April?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Monrovia in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
