How August compares in Monrovia

August is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Monrovia. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 78 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Monrovia in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monrovia in August?
In August, Monrovia averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 526 mm (20.7 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Monrovia?
No month in Monrovia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monrovia in August?
526 mm (20.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (526 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Monrovia in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monrovia get in August?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Monrovia in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
