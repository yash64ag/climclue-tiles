Monrovia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Montserrado County · Liberia

The sea off Monrovia averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 26.8 °C (80 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Monrovia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Monrovia?
In May, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Monrovia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.8 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Monrovia in August?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Monrovia?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
