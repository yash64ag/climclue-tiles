How December compares in Monrovia

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Monrovia. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 87 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Monrovia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monrovia in December?
In December, Monrovia averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Monrovia?
No month in Monrovia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monrovia in December?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (525 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Monrovia in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monrovia get in December?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Monrovia in December?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Monrovia in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
