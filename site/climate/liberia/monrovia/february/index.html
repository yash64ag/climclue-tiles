How February compares in Monrovia

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Monrovia. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella.

Monrovia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Monrovia in February?
In February, Monrovia averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Monrovia?
No month in Monrovia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Monrovia in February?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (525 mm), the driest January (32 mm).
Does it snow in Monrovia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Monrovia get in February?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Monrovia in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
