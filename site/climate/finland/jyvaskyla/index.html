Jyväskylä at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Jyväskylä is the 3rd coolest (3.8 °C year average). Compared with Kuopio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (124 km away), Jyväskylä is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C warmer in February, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, February (-8.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 214 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Jyväskylä has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 214 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
