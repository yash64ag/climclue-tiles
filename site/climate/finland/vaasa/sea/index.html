Vaasa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ostrobothnia · Finland

The sea off Vaasa averages 15.8 °C (60 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.1 °C (30 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Vaasa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vaasa?
In August, at about 15.8 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vaasa?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -1.1 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vaasa in March?
About -1.1 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vaasa?
About 5.8 °C (43 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
