Oulu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Ostrobothnia · Finland

The sea off Oulu averages 14.8 °C (59 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.7 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Oulu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Oulu?
In August, at about 14.8 °C (59 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Oulu?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.7 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Oulu in February?
About -1.7 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Oulu?
About 4.1 °C (39 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Oulu nearby?
In August: Korsholm (15.6 °C, 280 km), Umeå (15.5 °C, 282 km) and Vaasa (15.8 °C, 284 km).
