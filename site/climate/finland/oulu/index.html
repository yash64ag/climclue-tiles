Oulu at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Oulu is the coolest (2.7 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,579 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (695 mm a year). Compared with Kuopio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (259 km away), Oulu is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.3 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 131% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Oulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
