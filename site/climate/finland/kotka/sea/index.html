Kotka sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kymenlaakso · Finland

The sea off Kotka averages 16.5 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.2 °C (30 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Kotka sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kotka?
In August, at about 16.5 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kotka?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.2 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kotka in February?
About -1.2 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kotka?
About 5.9 °C (43 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
