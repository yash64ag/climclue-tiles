Jakobstad sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ostrobothnia · Finland

The sea off Jakobstad averages 15.3 °C (60 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.5 °C (29 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Jakobstad sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jakobstad?
In August, at about 15.3 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jakobstad?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -1.5 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jakobstad in March?
About -1.5 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jakobstad?
About 5.1 °C (41 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
