Korsholm sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ostrobothnia · Finland

The sea off Korsholm averages 15.6 °C (60 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.4 °C (29 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Korsholm sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Korsholm?
In August, at about 15.6 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Korsholm?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -1.4 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Korsholm in March?
About -1.4 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Korsholm?
About 5.6 °C (42 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Korsholm nearby?
In August: Pori (16.3 °C, 182 km) and Rauma (16.3 °C, 221 km).
