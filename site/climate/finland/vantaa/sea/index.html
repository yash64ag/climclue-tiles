Vantaa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Uusimaa · Finland

The sea off Vantaa averages 16.9 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.2 °C (30 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Vantaa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vantaa?
In August, at about 16.9 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vantaa?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.2 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vantaa in February?
About -1.2 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vantaa?
About 6.3 °C (43 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vantaa nearby?
In August: Tallinn (17.5 °C, 97 km).
