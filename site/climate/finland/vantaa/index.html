Vantaa at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Vantaa is the 2nd warmest (5.5 °C year average). Compared with Helsinki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Vantaa is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.8 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Vantaa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
