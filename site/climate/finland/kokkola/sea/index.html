Kokkola sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Ostrobothnia · Finland

The sea off Kokkola averages 15.0 °C (59 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.6 °C (29 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Kokkola sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kokkola?
In August, at about 15.0 °C (59 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kokkola?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -1.6 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kokkola in March?
About -1.6 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kokkola?
About 5.0 °C (41 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kokkola nearby?
In August: Korsholm (15.6 °C, 108 km), Vaasa (15.8 °C, 112 km) and Örnsköldsvik (15.6 °C, 227 km).
