Kuopio at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Kuopio is the 2nd coolest (3.4 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 1,363 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Jyväskylä, the nearest covered town with different numbers (124 km away), Kuopio is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 249 km from the sea.

Why Kuopio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
