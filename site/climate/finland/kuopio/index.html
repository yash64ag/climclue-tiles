Kuopio at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Kuopio is the 2nd coolest (3.3 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,363 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (730 mm a year). Compared with Jyväskylä, the nearest covered town with different numbers (124 km away), Kuopio is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 249 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, under 1 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kuopio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kuopio itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (62.892°N, 27.677°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-211-2076), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
