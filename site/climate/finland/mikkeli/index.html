Mikkeli weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Mikkeli, Finland (South Savo), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Mikkeli is warmest in July, with 22.3 °C (72 °F) days and 12.4 °C nights, and coolest in February, with -4.4 °C (24 °F) days and -11.3 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 35 mm in April to 85 mm in July.

Mikkeli at a glance

Among the 78 towns ClimaClue covers in Finland, Mikkeli is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,313 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pieksämäki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Mikkeli is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in February, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Why Mikkeli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mikkeli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (61.689°N, 27.272°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-223-2072), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
