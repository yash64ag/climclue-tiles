Ylivieska at a glance

Among the 78 towns ClimaClue covers in Finland, Ylivieska is 67th warmest (3.3 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 1,472 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Raahe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Ylivieska is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.6 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Why Ylivieska has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 4.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-199-2045), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
