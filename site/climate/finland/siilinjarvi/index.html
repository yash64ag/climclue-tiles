Siilinjärvi weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Siilinjärvi, Finland (North Savo), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Siilinjärvi is warmest in July, with 21.4 °C (71 °F) days and 12.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -5.7 °C (22 °F) days and -12.9 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 35 mm in April to 91 mm in July.

Siilinjärvi at a glance

Among the 78 towns ClimaClue covers in Finland, Siilinjärvi is 68th warmest (3.3 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 1,377 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (736 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kuopio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Siilinjärvi is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 26.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 236 km from the sea.

Why Siilinjärvi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Siilinjärvi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (63.083°N, 27.667°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-209-2076), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
