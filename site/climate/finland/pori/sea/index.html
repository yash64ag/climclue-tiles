Pori sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Satakunta · Finland

The sea off Pori averages 16.3 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.1 °C (32 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Pori sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pori?
In August, at about 16.3 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pori?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 0.1 °C (32 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pori in March?
About 0.1 °C (32 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pori?
About 6.8 °C (44 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pori nearby?
In August: Raisio (17.2 °C, 113 km), Naantali (17.2 °C, 113 km) and Turku (17.2 °C, 117 km).
