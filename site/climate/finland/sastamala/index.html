Sastamala weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sastamala, Finland (Pirkanmaa), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sastamala at a glance

Among the 78 towns ClimaClue covers in Finland, Sastamala is 31st warmest (4.8 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 1,474 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (713 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vammala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Sastamala is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 80 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Sastamala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sastamala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (61.349°N, 22.909°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-226-2029), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
