Tampere at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Tampere is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,433 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (715 mm a year). Compared with Lahti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (116 km away), Tampere is 0.5 °C cooler in July and about as warm in February, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tampere, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tampere has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
