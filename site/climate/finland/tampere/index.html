Why Tampere has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Tampere climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tampere?
July, with an average of 16.9 °C (62 °F), day highs around 20.2 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 13.8 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tampere?
February, with an average of -6.8 °C (20 °F), day highs around -4.4 °C (24 °F) and night lows around -9.6 °C (15 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tampere?
July, with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 715 mm (28.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tampere?
April, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tampere?
June, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tampere?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 186 cm (73.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Tampere?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.
What type of climate does Tampere have?
Subarctic (Köppen Dfc). Short, cool summers and long, very cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 5.3 × 11 km, that contains Tampere’s GeoNames location (61.499°N, 23.787°E; ClimaClue area sq-225-2037), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 260,646 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
