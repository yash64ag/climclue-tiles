Espoo at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Espoo is the wettest (770 mm a year). Compared with Helsinki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Espoo is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 94 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 17 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 118% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Espoo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
