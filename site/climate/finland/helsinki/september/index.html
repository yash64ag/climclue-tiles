How September compares in Helsinki

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Helsinki. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 79 hours less sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Helsinki in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Helsinki in September?
In September, Helsinki averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Helsinki?
September is not one of Helsinki’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Helsinki in September?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (82 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Helsinki in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Helsinki get in September?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Helsinki in September?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Helsinki in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
