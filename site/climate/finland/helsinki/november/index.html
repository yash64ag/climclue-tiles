How November compares in Helsinki

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Helsinki. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. December is 3.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Helsinki in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Helsinki in November?
In November, Helsinki averages a day high of 3.8 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -0.9 °C (30 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 30 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Helsinki?
November is not one of Helsinki’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Helsinki in November?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (82 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Helsinki in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Helsinki get in November?
About 30 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Helsinki in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
