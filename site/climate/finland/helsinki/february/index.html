How February compares in Helsinki

February is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Helsinki. Compared with January: 1.0 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Helsinki in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Helsinki in February?
In February, Helsinki averages a day high of -3.5 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -8.3 °C (17 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 54 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Helsinki?
February is not one of Helsinki’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Helsinki in February?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (82 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Helsinki in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 33 cm (13.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 22 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Helsinki get in February?
About 54 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Helsinki in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
