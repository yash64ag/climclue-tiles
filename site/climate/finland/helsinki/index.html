Helsinki at a glance

Among the 78 towns ClimaClue covers in Finland, Helsinki is the 4th warmest (5.6 °C year average). Compared with Myyrmäki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Helsinki is about as warm in July and 0.6 °C warmer in February, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Helsinki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Helsinki climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Helsinki?
July, with an average of 17.3 °C (63 °F), day highs around 20.8 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 13.8 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Helsinki?
February, with an average of -5.1 °C (23 °F), day highs around -2.3 °C (28 °F) and night lows around -7.8 °C (18 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Helsinki?
August, with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 674 mm (26.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Helsinki?
February, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Helsinki?
June, with about 277 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Helsinki?
Yes. About 158 cm (62.4 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time to visit Helsinki?

How warm is the sea in Helsinki?
The nearby open sea averages 16.9 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.2 °C (30 °F) in February. It stays below 20 °C (68 °F) all year. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Helsinki itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (60.170°N, 24.935°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-238-2049), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 658,864, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). How ClimaClue makes these numbers.
