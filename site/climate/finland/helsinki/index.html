Helsinki at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Finland, Helsinki is the 3rd warmest (5.3 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,594 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (676 mm a year). Compared with Vantaa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Helsinki is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.4 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Best time to visit Helsinki

For mild weather, the best months to visit Helsinki are July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July and August.

Why Helsinki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
