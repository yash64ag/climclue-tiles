Helsinki weather in April

Average April weather in Helsinki, Finland: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Helsinki averages 6.8 °C (44 °F) days and -0.8 °C (31 °F) nights, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 164 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Helsinki

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Helsinki. Compared with March: 5.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. May is 5.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Helsinki in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Helsinki in April?
In April, Helsinki averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Helsinki?
April is not one of Helsinki’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Helsinki in April?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (82 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Helsinki in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Helsinki get in April?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Helsinki in April?
About 0.1 °C (32 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Helsinki in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
