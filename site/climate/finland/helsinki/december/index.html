How December compares in Helsinki

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Helsinki. Compared with November: 3.7 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Helsinki in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Helsinki in December?
In December, Helsinki averages a day high of 0.6 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -5.1 °C (23 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 20 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Helsinki?
December is not one of Helsinki’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Helsinki in December?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (82 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Helsinki in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 37 cm (14.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Helsinki get in December?
About 20 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Helsinki in December?
About 2.8 °C (37 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Helsinki in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
