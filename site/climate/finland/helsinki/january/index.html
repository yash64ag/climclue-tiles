How January compares in Helsinki

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Helsinki. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C cooler than January on average.

Helsinki in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Helsinki in January?
In January, Helsinki averages a day high of -1.6 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -6.6 °C (20 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 27 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Helsinki?
January is not one of Helsinki’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Helsinki in January?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (82 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Helsinki in January?
Yes, on average: about 36 cm (14.0 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Helsinki get in January?
About 27 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Helsinki in January?
About -0.3 °C (31 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Helsinki in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
