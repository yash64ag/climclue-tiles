Grytviken at a glance

Its warmest month, February (2.3 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (-3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in every month. The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Grytviken has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
