Trbovlje weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Trbovlje, Slovenia (Municipality of Trbovlje), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Trbovlje is warmest in July, with 25.9 °C (79 °F) days and 15.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.2 °C (38 °F) days and -3.3 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 51 mm in January to 139 mm in June.

Trbovlje at a glance

Among the 9 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovenia, Trbovlje is the 3rd coolest (10.6 °C year average). Compared with Celje, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Trbovlje is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 160 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.7 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Why Trbovlje has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Trbovlje itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.154°N, 15.052°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-378-1950), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
