How February compares in Bangui

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bangui. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Bangui in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bangui in February?
In February, Bangui averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bangui?
No month in Bangui is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bangui in February?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (189 mm), the driest January (15 mm).
Does it snow in Bangui in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bangui get in February?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bangui in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
