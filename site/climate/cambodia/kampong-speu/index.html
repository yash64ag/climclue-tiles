Kampong Speu at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Cambodia, Kampong Speu is the driest (1,248 mm a year). Compared with Phumĭ Véal Tônsay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Kampong Speu is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 151 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.0 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, December (26.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kampong Speu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kampong Speu

No month in Kampong Speu is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are January (26.6 °C, 10 mm), November (26.5 °C, 99 mm) and December (26.0 °C, 17 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): April (30.0 °C), March (29.4 °C) and May (29.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (6 mm), January (10 mm) and December (17 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): January (263 h), December (253 h) and March (252 h).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Kampong Speu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kampong Speu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.453°N, 104.521°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-725-2845), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 33,231, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
