Phumĭ Véal Tônsay at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Cambodia, Phumĭ Véal Tônsay is 18th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,532 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (1,399 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kampong Speu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Phumĭ Véal Tônsay is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.3 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 128 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phumĭ Véal Tônsay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phumĭ Véal Tônsay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 128 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-723-2845), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
