Pailin at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Cambodia, Pailin is the coolest (25.8 °C year average). Compared with Battambang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Pailin is 3.0 °C cooler in April and 1.7 °C cooler in December, with 688 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.2 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in December. The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pailin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pailin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pailin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.849°N, 102.609°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-711-2826), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
