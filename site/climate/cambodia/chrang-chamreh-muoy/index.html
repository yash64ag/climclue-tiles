Chrang Chamreh Muoy at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Cambodia, Chrang Chamreh Muoy is the 3rd warmest (28.2 °C year average). Compared with Boeng Kak Pir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Chrang Chamreh Muoy is 0.1 °C warmer in April and about as warm in December, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.4 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, December (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 149 km from the sea.

Why Chrang Chamreh Muoy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 149 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chrang Chamreh Muoy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.630°N, 104.885°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-723-2848), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
