Why Battambang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Battambang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Battambang?
April, with an average of 30.1 °C (86 °F), day highs around 35.4 °C (96 °F) and night lows around 25.2 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Battambang?
December, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 30.1 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 20.5 °C (69 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Battambang?
September, with 256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,360 mm (53.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Battambang?
January, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Battambang?
January, with about 270 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Battambang?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Battambang?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Battambang have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Battambang’s GeoNames location (13.103°N, 103.198°E; ClimaClue area sq-708-2831), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 119,251 (GeoNames).
