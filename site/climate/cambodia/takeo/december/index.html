How December compares in Takeo

December is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Takeo. Compared with November: 0.6 °C cooler, 98 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Takeo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Takeo in December?
In December, Takeo averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Takeo?
No month in Takeo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Takeo in December?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (263 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Takeo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Takeo get in December?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Takeo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
