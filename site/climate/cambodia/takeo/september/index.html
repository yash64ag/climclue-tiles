How September compares in Takeo

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Takeo. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. October is 0.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Takeo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Takeo in September?
In September, Takeo averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 207 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Takeo?
No month in Takeo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Takeo in September?
207 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (262 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Takeo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Takeo get in September?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Takeo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
