How February compares in Takeo

February is the 6th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Takeo. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 1.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Takeo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Takeo in February?
In February, Takeo averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Takeo?
No month in Takeo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Takeo in February?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (263 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Takeo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Takeo get in February?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Takeo in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
