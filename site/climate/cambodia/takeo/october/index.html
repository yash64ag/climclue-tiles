Takeo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Takeo in October?
In October, Takeo averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Takeo?
No month in Takeo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Takeo in October?
263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (263 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Takeo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Takeo get in October?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
