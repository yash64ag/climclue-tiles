How April compares in Takeo

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Takeo. Compared with March: 0.6 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Takeo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Takeo in April?
In April, Takeo averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Takeo?
No month in Takeo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Takeo in April?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (263 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Takeo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Takeo get in April?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Takeo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
