Phnom Penh weather in June

Average June weather in Phnom Penh, Cambodia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Phnom Penh averages 33.0 °C (91 °F) days and 24.6 °C (76 °F) nights, with 149 mm (5.9 in) of rain over the month and about 176 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Phnom Penh

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with May: 0.8 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Phnom Penh in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in June?
In June, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in June?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (255 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in June?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
