Phnom Penh at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Cambodia, Phnom Penh is the warmest (28.2 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,549 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,391 mm a year). Compared with Takeo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Phnom Penh is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.4 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, December (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 144 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phnom Penh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phnom Penh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
