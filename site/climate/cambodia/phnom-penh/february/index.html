How February compares in Phnom Penh

February is the 6th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. March is 1.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Phnom Penh in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in February?
In February, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in February?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (255 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in February?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
