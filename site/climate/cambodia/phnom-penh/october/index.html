How October compares in Phnom Penh

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with September: 0.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. November is 0.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Phnom Penh in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in October?
In October, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in October?
252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (253 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
