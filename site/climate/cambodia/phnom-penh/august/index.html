How August compares in Phnom Penh

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Phnom Penh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in August?
In August, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in August?
197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (253 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in August?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
