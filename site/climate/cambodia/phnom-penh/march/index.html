How March compares in Phnom Penh

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with February: 1.4 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Phnom Penh in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in March?
In March, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in March?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (253 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in March?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
