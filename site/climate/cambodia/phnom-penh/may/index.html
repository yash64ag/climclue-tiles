How May compares in Phnom Penh

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with April: 0.8 °C cooler, 46 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. June is 0.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Phnom Penh in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in May?
In May, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in May?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (253 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in May?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
