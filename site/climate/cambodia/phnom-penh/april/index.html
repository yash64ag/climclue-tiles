Phnom Penh weather in April

Average April weather in Phnom Penh, Cambodia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Phnom Penh averages 35.6 °C (96 °F) days and 25.1 °C (77 °F) nights, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of rain over the month and about 233 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Phnom Penh

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with March: 0.7 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Of the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Cambodia, Phnom Penh ranks 10th warmest (average temperature), 18th sunniest and 38th wettest in April.

What to pack for Phnom Penh in April

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella.

Phnom Penh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in April?
In April, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in April?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (255 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in April?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
