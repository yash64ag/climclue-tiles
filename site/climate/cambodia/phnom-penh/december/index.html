How December compares in Phnom Penh

December is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 92 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Phnom Penh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in December?
In December, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (253 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in December?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
