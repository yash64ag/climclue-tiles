How November compares in Phnom Penh

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Phnom Penh. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 139 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. December is 0.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Phnom Penh in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Phnom Penh in November?
In November, Phnom Penh averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Phnom Penh?
No month in Phnom Penh is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Phnom Penh in November?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (253 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Phnom Penh in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Phnom Penh get in November?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Phnom Penh in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
