Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky is 806th warmest (1.8 °C year average), 734th sunniest (about 1,733 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,467 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vilyuchinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky is 0.9 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 59 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (12.9 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
