Artëm at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Artëm is 60th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,188 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (715 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vladivostok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Artëm is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 2.5 °C cooler in January, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.3 °C), averages 34.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Artëm, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Artëm has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
