Ryazan’ at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Ryazan’ is 44th warmest (5.8 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 1,803 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kolomna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Ryazan’ is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 27.3 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 823 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ryazan’ has this climate
