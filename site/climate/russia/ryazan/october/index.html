Ryazan’ in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ryazan’ in October?
In October, Ryazan’ averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ryazan’?
October is not one of Ryazan’’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ryazan’ in October?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest March (26 mm).
Does it snow in Ryazan’ in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.3 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ryazan’ get in October?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ryazan’ in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
