Oktyabrsky at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Oktyabrsky is 108th warmest (3.9 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 2,011 hours of sunshine a year) and 147th wettest (461 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al’met’yevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Oktyabrsky is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 59 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 31.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 837 km from the sea (deep inland).
