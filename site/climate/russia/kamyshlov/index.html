Kamyshlov at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kamyshlov is 702nd warmest (2.8 °C year average), 303rd sunniest (about 2,013 hours of sunshine a year) and 845th wettest (470 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bogdanovich, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Kamyshlov is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 33.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,133 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kamyshlov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kamyshlov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.847°N, 62.712°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-271-2427), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
