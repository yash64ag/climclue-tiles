Novomoskovsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novomoskovsk is 65th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 114th sunniest (about 1,788 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Novomoskovsk is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 769 km from the sea (deep inland).
