Fryazevo weather by month and climate

Fryazevo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Fryazevo is 368th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 755th sunniest (about 1,723 hours of sunshine a year) and 265th wettest (688 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Elektrostal’, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Fryazevo is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 677 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Fryazevo has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fryazevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.733°N, 38.465°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-282-2184), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
