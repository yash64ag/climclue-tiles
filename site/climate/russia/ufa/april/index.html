How April compares in Ufa

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ufa. Compared with March: 10.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. May is 7.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Ufa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in April?
In April, Ufa averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ufa?
April is not one of Ufa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in April?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (72 mm), the driest March (37 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in April?
Yes, on average: about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in April?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
