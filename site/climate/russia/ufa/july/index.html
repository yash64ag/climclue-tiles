How July compares in Ufa

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ufa. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. August is 2.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Ufa in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in July?
In July, Ufa averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ufa?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Ufa’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in July?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (72 mm), the driest March (37 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in July?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
