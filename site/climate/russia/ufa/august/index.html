Ufa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in August?
In August, Ufa averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ufa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Ufa’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in August?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (72 mm), the driest March (37 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in August?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
