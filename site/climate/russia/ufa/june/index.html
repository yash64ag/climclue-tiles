How June compares in Ufa

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ufa. Compared with May: 5.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. July is 1.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Ufa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in June?
In June, Ufa averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ufa?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Ufa’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in June?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (69 mm), the driest March (35 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in June?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
