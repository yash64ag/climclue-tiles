How March compares in Ufa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ufa. Compared with February: 6.7 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. April is 9.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Ufa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in March?
In March, Ufa averages a day high of -0.4 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -8.3 °C (17 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ufa?
March is not one of Ufa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in March?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (69 mm), the driest March (35 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 31 cm (12.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 62 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in March?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
