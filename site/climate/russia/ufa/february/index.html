How February compares in Ufa

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ufa. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. March is 6.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Ufa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in February?
In February, Ufa averages a day high of -6.7 °C (20 °F) and a night low of -17.1 °C (1 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ufa?
February is not one of Ufa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in February?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (72 mm), the driest March (37 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in February?
Yes, on average: about 41 cm (16.2 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 36 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in February?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
