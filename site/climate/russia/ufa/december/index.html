How December compares in Ufa

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ufa. Compared with November: 6.4 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Ufa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ufa in December?
In December, Ufa averages a day high of -6.7 °C (20 °F) and a night low of -14.9 °C (5 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 39 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ufa?
December is not one of Ufa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ufa in December?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (72 mm), the driest March (37 mm).
Does it snow in Ufa in December?
Yes, on average: about 54 cm (21.1 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 17 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ufa get in December?
About 39 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ufa in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
