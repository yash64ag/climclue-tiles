Ufa at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Ufa is 103rd warmest (4.3 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 1,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 65th wettest (651 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sterlitamak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (123 km away), Ufa is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 32.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 903 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ufa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 903 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
