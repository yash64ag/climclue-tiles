Why Nalchik has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 205 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Nalchik climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Nalchik?
July, with an average of 20.6 °C (69 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 16.0 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Nalchik?
January, with an average of -3.0 °C (27 °F), day highs around 0.9 °C (34 °F) and night lows around -7.6 °C (18 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Nalchik?
June, with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,226 mm (48.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Nalchik?
January, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Nalchik?
July, with about 246 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Nalchik?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 247 cm (97.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Nalchik?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Nalchik have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Nalchik’s GeoNames location (43.498°N, 43.619°E; ClimaClue area sq-405-2236), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 239,300 (GeoNames).
