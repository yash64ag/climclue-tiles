Nalchik at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nalchik is the 3rd wettest (1,226 mm a year). Compared with Pyatigorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Nalchik is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 590 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 205 km from the sea.

Why Nalchik has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 205 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
