Why Murmansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Murmansk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Murmansk?
July, with an average of 13.3 °C (56 °F), day highs around 16.4 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 9.0 °C (48 °F).
What is the coldest month in Murmansk?
January, with an average of -11.2 °C (12 °F), day highs around -8.5 °C (17 °F) and night lows around -13.9 °C (7 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Murmansk?
July, with 93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 711 mm (28.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Murmansk?
February, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Murmansk?
June, with about 230 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Murmansk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 250 cm (98.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 8 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Murmansk?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Murmansk have?
Subarctic (Köppen Dfc). Short, cool summers and long, very cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 4.0 × 11 km, that contains Murmansk’s GeoNames location (68.968°N, 33.099°E; ClimaClue area sq-150-2130), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 295,374 (GeoNames).
