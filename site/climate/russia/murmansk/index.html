Murmansk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Murmansk is the least sunny (about 1,279 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Severodvinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (572 km away), Murmansk is 2.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (13.3 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Why Murmansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
