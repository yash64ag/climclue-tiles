Tolyatti in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tolyatti in August?
In August, Tolyatti averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tolyatti?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Tolyatti’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tolyatti in August?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest March (30 mm).
Does it snow in Tolyatti in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tolyatti get in August?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tolyatti in August?
