Tolyatti in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tolyatti in October?
In October, Tolyatti averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 98 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tolyatti?
October is not one of Tolyatti’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tolyatti in October?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest March (30 mm).
Does it snow in Tolyatti in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tolyatti get in October?
About 98 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tolyatti in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
