Tolyatti at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Tolyatti is 66th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 1,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (545 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novokuybyshevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Tolyatti is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.3 °C), averages 31.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 735 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tolyatti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
