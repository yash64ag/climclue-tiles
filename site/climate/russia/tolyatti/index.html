Tolyatti at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Tolyatti is 71st warmest (5.3 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 1,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novokuybyshevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Tolyatti is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 735 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tolyatti has this climate
