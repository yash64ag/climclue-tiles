Trudovoye at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Trudovoye is 328th warmest (5.5 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,193 hours of sunshine a year) and 204th wettest (709 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Artëm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Trudovoye is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Trudovoye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Trudovoye itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.303°N, 132.079°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-406-3120), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
