How March compares in Samara

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Samara. Compared with February: 6.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 10.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Samara in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in March?
In March, Samara averages a day high of -0.5 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -7.5 °C (19 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Samara?
March is not one of Samara’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in March?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 22 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in March?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
