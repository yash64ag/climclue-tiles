Samara in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in August?
In August, Samara averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Samara?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Samara’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in August?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in August?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
