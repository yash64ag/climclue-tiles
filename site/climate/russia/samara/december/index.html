How December compares in Samara

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Samara. Compared with November: 5.9 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Samara in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in December?
In December, Samara averages a day high of -5.9 °C (21 °F) and a night low of -11.3 °C (12 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 45 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Samara?
December is not one of Samara’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 41 cm (16.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 20 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in December?
About 45 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
