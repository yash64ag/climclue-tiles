How April compares in Samara

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Samara. Compared with March: 10.5 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. May is 7.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Samara in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in April?
In April, Samara averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Samara?
April is not one of Samara’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in April?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in April?
Yes, on average: about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in April?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
