Samara weather in June

Average June weather in Samara, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Samara averages 24.8 °C (77 °F) days and 13.9 °C (57 °F) nights, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 297 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Samara

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Samara. Compared with May: 4.8 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. July is 1.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Samara in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in June?
In June, Samara averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Samara?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Samara’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in June?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in June?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
