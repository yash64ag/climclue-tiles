How May compares in Samara

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Samara. Compared with April: 7.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 87 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Samara in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in May?
In May, Samara averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Samara?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Samara’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in May?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in May?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
