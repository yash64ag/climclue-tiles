How September compares in Samara

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Samara. Compared with August: 5.9 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 86 hours less sunshine. October is 7.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Samara in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in September?
In September, Samara averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Samara?
September is not one of Samara’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in September?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in September?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
