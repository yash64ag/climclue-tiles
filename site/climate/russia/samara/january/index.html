How January compares in Samara

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Samara. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Samara in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samara in January?
In January, Samara averages a day high of -7.3 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -13.1 °C (8 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 62 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Samara?
January is not one of Samara’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samara in January?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (54 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Samara in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 19 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Samara get in January?
About 62 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samara in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
