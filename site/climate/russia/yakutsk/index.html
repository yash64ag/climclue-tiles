Why Yakutsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 744 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 58.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Yakutsk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Yakutsk?
July, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 24.4 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 14.2 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Yakutsk?
January, with an average of -38.9 °C (-38 °F), day highs around -34.6 °C (-30 °F) and night lows around -40.7 °C (-41 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Yakutsk?
August, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 277 mm (10.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Yakutsk?
March, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Yakutsk?
July, with about 327 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Yakutsk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 87 cm (34.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in October; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 8 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Yakutsk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Yakutsk have?
Very cold subarctic (Köppen Dfd). Short summers and extremely cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 5.2 × 11 km, that contains Yakutsk’s GeoNames location (62.031°N, 129.723°E; ClimaClue area sq-219-3097), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 235,600 (GeoNames).
