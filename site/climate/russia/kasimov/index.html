Kasimov weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kasimov, Russia (Ryazan Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kasimov is warmest in July, with 25.0 °C (77 °F) days and 14.3 °C nights, and coolest in February, with -4.0 °C (25 °F) days and -11.8 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 32 mm in March to 73 mm in June.

Kasimov at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kasimov is 326th warmest (5.5 °C year average), 560th sunniest (about 1,831 hours of sunshine a year) and 451st wettest (630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Melenki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Kasimov is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 873 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kasimov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kasimov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.944°N, 41.403°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-290-2214), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
