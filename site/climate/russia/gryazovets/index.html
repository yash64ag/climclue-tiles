Gryazovets at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Gryazovets is 648th warmest (3.4 °C year average), 888th sunniest (about 1,671 hours of sunshine a year) and 405th wettest (646 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vologda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Gryazovets is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.7 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 576 km from the sea.

Why Gryazovets has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gryazovets itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (58.880°N, 40.252°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-251-2202), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
