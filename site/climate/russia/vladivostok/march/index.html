How March compares in Vladivostok

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Vladivostok. Compared with February: 6.6 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 6.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Vladivostok in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vladivostok in March?
In March, Vladivostok averages a day high of 2.8 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -4.6 °C (24 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Vladivostok?
No month in Vladivostok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vladivostok in March?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Vladivostok in March?
Yes, on average: about 18 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vladivostok get in March?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Vladivostok in March?
About -0.3 °C (31 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (20.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Vladivostok in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
