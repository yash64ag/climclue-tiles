How August compares in Vladivostok

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Vladivostok. Compared with July: 2.4 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 3.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Vladivostok in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vladivostok in August?
In August, Vladivostok averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Vladivostok?
No month in Vladivostok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vladivostok in August?
136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Vladivostok in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vladivostok get in August?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vladivostok in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
