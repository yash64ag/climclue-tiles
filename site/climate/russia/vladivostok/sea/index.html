Vladivostok sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Primorye · Russia

The sea off Vladivostok averages 20.3 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.7 °C (31 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

Vladivostok sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vladivostok?
In August, at about 20.3 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vladivostok?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at -0.7 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vladivostok in February?
About -0.7 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vladivostok?
About 7.9 °C (46 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vladivostok nearby?
In August: Unggi (20.9 °C, 147 km), Sŏnbong (20.9 °C, 148 km) and Rajin (21.3 °C, 161 km).
