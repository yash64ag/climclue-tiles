How May compares in Vladivostok

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Vladivostok. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. June is 5.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Vladivostok in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vladivostok in May?
In May, Vladivostok averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Vladivostok?
No month in Vladivostok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vladivostok in May?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (150 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Vladivostok in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Vladivostok get in May?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vladivostok in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
