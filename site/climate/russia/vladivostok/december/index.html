How December compares in Vladivostok

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Vladivostok. Compared with November: 8.3 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 3.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Vladivostok in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vladivostok in December?
In December, Vladivostok averages a day high of -4.0 °C (25 °F) and a night low of -11.0 °C (12 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Vladivostok?
No month in Vladivostok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vladivostok in December?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Vladivostok in December?
Yes, on average: about 16 cm (6.2 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vladivostok get in December?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Vladivostok in December?
About 0.8 °C (33 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (20.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Vladivostok in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
