Why Vladivostok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Vladivostok climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Vladivostok?
August, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 22.3 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 19.4 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Vladivostok?
January, with an average of -10.7 °C (13 °F), day highs around -7.5 °C (19 °F) and night lows around -14.4 °C (6 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Vladivostok?
July, with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 781 mm (30.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Vladivostok?
January, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Vladivostok?
May, with about 215 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Vladivostok?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 103 cm (40.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Vladivostok?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Vladivostok have?
Continental, warm summer, dry winter (Köppen Dwb). Warm, wet summers and cold, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Vladivostok’s GeoNames location (43.106°N, 131.874°E; ClimaClue area sq-408-3118), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 604,901 (GeoNames).
