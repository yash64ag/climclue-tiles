How January compares in Vladivostok

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Vladivostok. Compared with December: 3.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Vladivostok ranks 97th warmest (average temperature), 3rd sunniest and 161st wettest in January.

Vladivostok in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vladivostok in January?
In January, Vladivostok averages a day high of -7.4 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -14.2 °C (6 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Vladivostok?
No month in Vladivostok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vladivostok in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Vladivostok in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Vladivostok get in January?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vladivostok in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
