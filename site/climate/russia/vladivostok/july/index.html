How July compares in Vladivostok

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Vladivostok. Compared with June: 4.5 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. August is 2.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Vladivostok in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vladivostok in July?
In July, Vladivostok averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Vladivostok?
No month in Vladivostok is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vladivostok in July?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (150 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Vladivostok in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vladivostok get in July?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vladivostok in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
