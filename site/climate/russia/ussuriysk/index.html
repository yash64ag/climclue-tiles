Ussuriysk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Ussuriysk is 81st warmest (5.1 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,258 hours of sunshine a year) and 90th wettest (606 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Artëm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Ussuriysk is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 2.4 °C cooler in January, with 109 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.4 °C), averages 37.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Why Ussuriysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
