Polevskoy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Polevskoy is 779th warmest (2.1 °C year average), 450th sunniest (about 1,905 hours of sunshine a year) and 607th wettest (568 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Degtyarsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Polevskoy is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.7 °C), averages 31.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,176 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Polevskoy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.442°N, 60.188°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-275-2401), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
