Ivanovo at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Ivanovo is 99th warmest (4.4 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 1,730 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kovrov, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Ivanovo is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 704 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ivanovo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ivanovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 704 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C.
