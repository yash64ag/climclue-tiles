Syktyvkar weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Syktyvkar, Russia (Komi), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Syktyvkar is warmest in July, with 22.7 °C (73 °F) days and 12.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -10.3 °C (13 °F) days and -18.3 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 35 mm in February to 90 mm in August.

Syktyvkar at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Syktyvkar is 843rd warmest (1.2 °C year average), 946th sunniest (about 1,569 hours of sunshine a year) and 149th wettest (727 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ezhva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Syktyvkar is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 31.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 612 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Syktyvkar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (61.664°N, 50.816°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-223-2308), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
