Fedorovskiy weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Fedorovskiy, Russia (Khanty-Mansia), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Fedorovskiy is warmest in July, with 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 12.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -15.8 °C (4 °F) days and -24.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (97 mm); February is driest (27 mm).

Fedorovskiy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Fedorovskiy is 947th warmest (-2.2 °C year average), 692nd sunniest (about 1,753 hours of sunshine a year) and 363rd wettest (659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surgut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Fedorovskiy is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 37.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-20.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 528 km from the sea.

Why Fedorovskiy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fedorovskiy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (61.606°N, 73.714°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-223-2537), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
