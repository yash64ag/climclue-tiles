Borovskiy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Borovskiy is 741st warmest (2.4 °C year average), 278th sunniest (about 2,045 hours of sunshine a year) and 841st wettest (472 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tyumen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Borovskiy is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.2 °C), averages 34.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,069 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Borovskiy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (57.041°N, 65.720°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-269-2457), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
