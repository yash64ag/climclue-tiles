Kurgan at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kurgan is 120th warmest (3.0 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,147 hours of sunshine a year) and 157th wettest (393 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tyumen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (190 km away), Kurgan is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 34.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,248 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kurgan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,248 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
