Belaya Kalitva weather by month and climate

Belaya Kalitva at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Belaya Kalitva is 148th warmest (9.3 °C year average), 230th sunniest (about 2,083 hours of sunshine a year) and 822nd wettest (481 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kamensk-Shakhtinsky, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Belaya Kalitva is 0.4 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 163 km from the sea.

Why Belaya Kalitva has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Belaya Kalitva itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.179°N, 40.779°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-358-2207), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
