Dzerzhinsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Dzerzhinsk is 85th warmest (5.0 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 1,800 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (670 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nizhniy Novgorod, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Dzerzhinsk is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 28.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 881 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dzerzhinsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dzerzhinsk has this climate
