Yoshkar-Ola weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Yoshkar-Ola, Russia (Mariy-El Republic), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Yoshkar-Ola is warmest in July, with 24.4 °C (76 °F) days and 14.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -7.2 °C (19 °F) days and -14.0 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 28 mm in February to 67 mm in June.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yoshkar-Ola itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.639°N, 47.891°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-273-2278), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
