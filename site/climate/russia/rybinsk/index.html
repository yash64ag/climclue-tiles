Rybinsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Rybinsk is 97th warmest (4.5 °C year average), 157th sunniest (about 1,683 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (643 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yaroslavl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Rybinsk is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 541 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Rybinsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 541 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
