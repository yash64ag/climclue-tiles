Baltiysk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kaliningrad Oblast · Russia

The sea off Baltiysk averages 18.5 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.7 °C (35 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Baltiysk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Baltiysk?
In August, at about 18.5 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Baltiysk?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 1.7 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Baltiysk in February?
About 1.7 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Baltiysk?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
