Baltiysk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Baltiysk is 173rd warmest (8.4 °C year average), 923rd sunniest (about 1,631 hours of sunshine a year) and 133rd wettest (735 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Baltiysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Baltiysk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.655°N, 19.909°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-293-1999), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
