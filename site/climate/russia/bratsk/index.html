Bratsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Bratsk is 927th warmest (-1.1 °C year average), 353rd sunniest (about 1,975 hours of sunshine a year) and 885th wettest (437 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vikhorevka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Bratsk is 2.0 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.6 °C), averages 37.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-20.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,768 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 19 km from Bratsk measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Bratsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,768 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bratsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.132°N, 101.614°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-278-2816), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
