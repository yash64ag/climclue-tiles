Shakhty at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Shakhty is 155th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 229th sunniest (about 2,084 hours of sunshine a year) and 618th wettest (564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novoshakhtinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Shakhty is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Why Shakhty has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shakhty itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.719°N, 40.216°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-362-2202), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
