Shakhty at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Shakhty is 20th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,084 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (584 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novocherkassk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Shakhty is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shakhty, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shakhty has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.
