Shakhty at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Shakhty is 22nd warmest (9.2 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,084 hours of sunshine a year) and 101st wettest (564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novocherkassk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Shakhty is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-362-2202), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
