Polyarnyy sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Murmansk · Russia

The sea off Polyarnyy averages 9.5 °C (49 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.1 °C (36 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Polyarnyy sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Polyarnyy?
In August, at about 9.5 °C (49 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Polyarnyy?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 2.1 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Polyarnyy in March?
About 2.1 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Polyarnyy?
About 5.1 °C (41 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Polyarnyy nearby?
In August: Kemi (14.9 °C, 539 km), Tornio (14.9 °C, 542 km) and Haukipudas (14.8 °C, 566 km).
