How October compares in Yekaterinburg

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yekaterinburg. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. November is 9.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Yekaterinburg in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yekaterinburg in October?
In October, Yekaterinburg averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yekaterinburg?
October is not one of Yekaterinburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yekaterinburg in October?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest February (19 mm).
Does it snow in Yekaterinburg in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Yekaterinburg get in October?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yekaterinburg in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
