How May compares in Yekaterinburg

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Yekaterinburg. Compared with April: 7.0 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. June is 5.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Yekaterinburg in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yekaterinburg in May?
In May, Yekaterinburg averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yekaterinburg?
May is not one of Yekaterinburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yekaterinburg in May?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest February (19 mm).
Does it snow in Yekaterinburg in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yekaterinburg get in May?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yekaterinburg in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
