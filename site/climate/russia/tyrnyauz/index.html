Tyrnyauz at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Tyrnyauz is 360th warmest (5.5 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 2,244 hours of sunshine a year) and 110th wettest (748 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chegem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Tyrnyauz is 5.1 °C cooler in July and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 134 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 151 km from the sea.

Why Tyrnyauz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 151 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C. The 10 km area around Tyrnyauz, which the globe shows when you click there, averages -0.8 °C a year, 6.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tyrnyauz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.383°N, 42.918°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-406-2229), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
