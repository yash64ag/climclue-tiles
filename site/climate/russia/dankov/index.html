Dankov at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Dankov is 271st warmest (6.0 °C year average), 542nd sunniest (about 1,843 hours of sunshine a year) and 611th wettest (567 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lebedyan’, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Dankov is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 678 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dankov has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dankov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.250°N, 39.144°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-307-2191), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
