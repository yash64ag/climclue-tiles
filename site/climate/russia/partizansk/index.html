Partizansk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Partizansk is 510th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 184th sunniest (about 2,116 hours of sunshine a year) and 65th wettest (809 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nakhodka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Partizansk is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 3.4 °C cooler in January, with 45 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.8 °C), averages 35.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Why Partizansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Partizansk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.120°N, 133.123°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-408-3131), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
