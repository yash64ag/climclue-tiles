Vereshchagino weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Vereshchagino, Russia (Perm Krai), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Vereshchagino is warmest in July, with 23.6 °C (74 °F) days and 12.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -9.4 °C (15 °F) days and -16.9 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 27 mm in February to 71 mm in July.

Vereshchagino at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Vereshchagino is 751st warmest (2.3 °C year average), 667th sunniest (about 1,770 hours of sunshine a year) and 534th wettest (600 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ochër, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Vereshchagino is 0.6 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vereshchagino itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (58.078°N, 54.656°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-259-2346), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
