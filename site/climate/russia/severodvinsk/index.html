Severodvinsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Severodvinsk is 139th warmest (1.7 °C year average), 166th sunniest (about 1,613 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (680 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arkhangel’sk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Severodvinsk is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.7 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Severodvinsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Severodvinsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
