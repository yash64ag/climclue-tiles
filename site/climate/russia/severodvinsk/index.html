Severodvinsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Severodvinsk, Russia (Arkhangelskaya), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Severodvinsk is warmest in July, with 20.6 °C (69 °F) days and 10.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -7.5 °C (19 °F) days and -15.4 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 35 mm in February to 80 mm in August.

Severodvinsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Severodvinsk is 811th warmest (1.6 °C year average), 937th sunniest (about 1,613 hours of sunshine a year) and 276th wettest (685 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yagry, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Severodvinsk is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Severodvinsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Severodvinsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (64.558°N, 39.830°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 4.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-194-2198), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
