How June compares in Krasnodar

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. July is 3.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Krasnodar in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in June?
In June, Krasnodar averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Krasnodar?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Krasnodar’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in June?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in June?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
