How November compares in Krasnodar

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with October: 6.3 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. December is 4.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Krasnodar in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in November?
In November, Krasnodar averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 87 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Krasnodar?
November is not one of Krasnodar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in November?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.2 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in November?
About 87 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
