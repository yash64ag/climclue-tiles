Krasnodar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in May?
In May, Krasnodar averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Krasnodar?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Krasnodar’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in May?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in May?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
