How September compares in Krasnodar

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with August: 5.1 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. October is 6.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Krasnodar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in September?
In September, Krasnodar averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Krasnodar?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Krasnodar’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in September?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in September?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
