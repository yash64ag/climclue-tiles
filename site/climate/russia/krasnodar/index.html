Krasnodar at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Krasnodar is the 3rd warmest (12.2 °C year average). Compared with Maykop, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Krasnodar is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 92 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Krasnodar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 92 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
