How January compares in Krasnodar

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Krasnodar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in January?
In January, Krasnodar averages a day high of 4.1 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -2.6 °C (27 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Krasnodar?
January is not one of Krasnodar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in January?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in January?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
