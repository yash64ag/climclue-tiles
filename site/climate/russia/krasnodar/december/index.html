How December compares in Krasnodar

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Krasnodar. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Krasnodar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in December?
In December, Krasnodar averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -1.0 °C (30 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 56 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Krasnodar?
December is not one of Krasnodar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in December?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in December?
About 56 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
