How March compares in Krasnodar

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Krasnodar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in March?
In March, Krasnodar averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Krasnodar?
March is not one of Krasnodar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in March?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.6 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in March?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
