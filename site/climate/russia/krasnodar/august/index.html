How August compares in Krasnodar

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 5.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Krasnodar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in August?
In August, Krasnodar averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Krasnodar?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Krasnodar’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in August?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in August?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
