How February compares in Krasnodar

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Krasnodar. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. March is 4.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Krasnodar in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnodar in February?
In February, Krasnodar averages a day high of 5.2 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Krasnodar?
February is not one of Krasnodar’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnodar in February?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnodar in February?
Yes, on average: about 17 cm (6.5 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnodar get in February?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnodar in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
