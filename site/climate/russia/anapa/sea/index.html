Anapa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Krasnodar Krai · Russia

The sea off Anapa averages 24.5 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.6 °C (46 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Anapa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Anapa?
In August, at about 24.5 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Anapa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 7.6 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Anapa in February?
About 7.6 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Anapa?
About 15.1 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Anapa nearby?
In August: Tuapse (25.2 °C, 166 km) and Lazarevskoye (25.4 °C, 194 km).
