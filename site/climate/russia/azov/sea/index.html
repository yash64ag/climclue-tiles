Azov sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rostov · Russia

The sea off Azov averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 3.9 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August.

Azov sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Azov?
In July, at about 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Azov?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August. The coolest month is February, at 3.9 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Azov in February?
About 3.9 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Azov?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Azov nearby?
In July: Yeysk (24.1 °C, 98 km), Primorsko-Akhtarsk (24.6 °C, 151 km) and Berdyansk (24.4 °C, 203 km).
