Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kiselëvsk is 773rd warmest (2.1 °C year average), 204th sunniest (about 2,101 hours of sunshine a year) and 581st wettest (577 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kisilevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Kiselëvsk is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Why Kiselëvsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kiselëvsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.990°N, 86.662°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-300-2666), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
