Leninsk-Kuznetsky at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Leninsk-Kuznetsky is 132nd warmest (2.1 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,057 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (473 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kemerovo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Leninsk-Kuznetsky is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 139 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 35.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,500 km from the sea (deep inland).
