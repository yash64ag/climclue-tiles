Why Leninsk-Kuznetsky has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,500 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Leninsk-Kuznetsky climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
July, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 24.0 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 14.2 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
January, with an average of -16.1 °C (3 °F), day highs around -12.6 °C (9 °F) and night lows around -19.7 °C (-3 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
July, with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 473 mm (18.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
February, with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
July, with about 306 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 219 cm (86.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 7 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Leninsk-Kuznetsky?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Leninsk-Kuznetsky have?
