Sarapul weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sarapul, Russia (Udmurtiya Republic), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sarapul is warmest in July, with 25.1 °C (77 °F) days and 14.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.4 °C (17 °F) days and -16.0 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 26 mm in March to 71 mm in August.

Sarapul at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Sarapul is 641st warmest (3.5 °C year average), 498th sunniest (about 1,870 hours of sunshine a year) and 603rd wettest (570 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chaykovskiy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Sarapul is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 31.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,059 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Sarapul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sarapul itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.476°N, 53.798°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-275-2337), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
