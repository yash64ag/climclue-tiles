Moscow at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Moscow is 63rd warmest (5.4 °C year average), 151st sunniest (about 1,701 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (691 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cherëmushki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Moscow is 0.4 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 59 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 643 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Moscow has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 643 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Moscow climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Moscow?
July, with an average of 19.1 °C (66 °F), day highs around 23.0 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 14.7 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Moscow?
February, with an average of -7.7 °C (18 °F), day highs around -5.1 °C (23 °F) and night lows around -10.8 °C (13 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Moscow?
July, with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 691 mm (27.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Moscow?
March, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Moscow?
June, with about 274 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Moscow?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 185 cm (73.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
