How November compares in Moscow

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Moscow. Compared with October: 7.1 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. December is 4.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Moscow in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Moscow in November?
In November, Moscow averages a day high of 0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -3.9 °C (25 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 31 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Moscow?
November is not one of Moscow’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Moscow in November?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Moscow in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Moscow get in November?
About 31 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Moscow in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
