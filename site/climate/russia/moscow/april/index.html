How April compares in Moscow

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Moscow. Compared with March: 7.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. May is 6.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Moscow in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Moscow in April?
In April, Moscow averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Moscow?
April is not one of Moscow’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Moscow in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Moscow in April?
Yes, on average: about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Moscow get in April?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Moscow in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
