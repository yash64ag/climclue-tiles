How December compares in Moscow

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Moscow. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Moscow in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Moscow in December?
In December, Moscow averages a day high of -3.7 °C (25 °F) and a night low of -8.5 °C (17 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 19 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Moscow?
December is not one of Moscow’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Moscow in December?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Moscow in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 17 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Moscow get in December?
About 19 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Moscow in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
