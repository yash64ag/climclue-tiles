How February compares in Moscow

February is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Moscow. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Moscow in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Moscow in February?
In February, Moscow averages a day high of -3.8 °C (25 °F) and a night low of -10.2 °C (14 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Moscow?
February is not one of Moscow’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Moscow in February?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Moscow in February?
Yes, on average: about 35 cm (13.6 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 24 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Moscow get in February?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Moscow in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
