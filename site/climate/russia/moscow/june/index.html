How June compares in Moscow

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Moscow. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Moscow in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Moscow in June?
In June, Moscow averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Moscow?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Moscow’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Moscow in June?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Moscow in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Moscow get in June?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Moscow in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
