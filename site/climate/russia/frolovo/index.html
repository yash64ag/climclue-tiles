Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Frolovo is 180th warmest (8.2 °C year average), 238th sunniest (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 881st wettest (443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mikhaylovka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Frolovo is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Why Frolovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 440 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.9 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Frolovo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.769°N, 43.654°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-342-2236), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
