How March compares in Kirov

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kirov. Compared with February: 6.8 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 66 hours more sunshine. April is 8.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Kirov in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kirov in March?
In March, Kirov averages a day high of -1.4 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -7.9 °C (18 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kirov?
March is not one of Kirov’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kirov in March?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kirov in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 34 cm (13.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 35 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kirov get in March?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kirov in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
