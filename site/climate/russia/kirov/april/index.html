Kirov, Kirov Oblast in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kirov, Kirov Oblast in April?
In April, Kirov, Kirov Oblast averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kirov, Kirov Oblast?
April is not one of Kirov, Kirov Oblast’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
