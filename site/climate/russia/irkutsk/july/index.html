How July compares in Irkutsk

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Irkutsk. Compared with June: 2.9 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. August is 2.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Irkutsk in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irkutsk in July?
In July, Irkutsk averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Irkutsk?
July is not one of Irkutsk’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irkutsk in July?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Irkutsk in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Irkutsk get in July?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irkutsk in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
