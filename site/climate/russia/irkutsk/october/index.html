How October compares in Irkutsk

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Irkutsk. Compared with September: 7.6 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. November is 9.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Irkutsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irkutsk in October?
In October, Irkutsk averages a day high of 6.7 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Irkutsk?
October is not one of Irkutsk’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irkutsk in October?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Irkutsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.5 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Irkutsk get in October?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irkutsk in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
