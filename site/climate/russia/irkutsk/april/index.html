How April compares in Irkutsk

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Irkutsk. Compared with March: 9.4 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 7.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Irkutsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irkutsk in April?
In April, Irkutsk averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -3.4 °C (26 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Irkutsk?
April is not one of Irkutsk’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irkutsk in April?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (112 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Irkutsk in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Irkutsk get in April?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irkutsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
