Irkutsk weather in May

Average May weather in Irkutsk, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Irkutsk averages 17.6 °C (64 °F) days and 3.0 °C (37 °F) nights, with 34 mm (1.4 in) of rain over the month and about 268 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Irkutsk

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Irkutsk. Compared with April: 7.7 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. June is 5.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Irkutsk in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Irkutsk in May?
In May, Irkutsk averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Irkutsk?
May is not one of Irkutsk’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Irkutsk in May?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Irkutsk in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Irkutsk get in May?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Irkutsk in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
