Nyandoma weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Nyandoma, Russia (Arkhangelskaya), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Nyandoma is warmest in July, with 21.8 °C (71 °F) days and 11.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.2 °C (17 °F) days and -16.0 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 49 mm in April to 101 mm in August.

Nyandoma at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Nyandoma is 793rd warmest (1.8 °C year average), 919th sunniest (about 1,632 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (878 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vel’sk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (121 km away), Nyandoma is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 241 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.4 °C), averages 28.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 283 km from the sea.

Why Nyandoma has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nyandoma itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (61.672°N, 40.212°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-223-2202), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
