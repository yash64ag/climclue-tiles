How August compares in Perm

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Perm. Compared with July: 3.2 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. September is 5.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Perm in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in August?
In August, Perm averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Perm?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Perm’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in August?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in August?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
