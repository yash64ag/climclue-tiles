How September compares in Perm

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Perm. Compared with August: 5.8 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 93 hours less sunshine. October is 6.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Perm in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in September?
In September, Perm averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Perm?
September is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in September?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in September?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
