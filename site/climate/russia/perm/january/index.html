How January compares in Perm

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Perm. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Perm in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in January?
In January, Perm averages a day high of -9.3 °C (15 °F) and a night low of -16.8 °C (2 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 38 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Perm?
January is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in January?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 46 cm (18.3 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 31 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in January?
About 38 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
