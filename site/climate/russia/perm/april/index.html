How April compares in Perm

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Perm. Compared with March: 7.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. May is 7.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Perm in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in April?
In April, Perm averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -1.6 °C (29 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Perm?
April is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in April?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 19 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in April?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
