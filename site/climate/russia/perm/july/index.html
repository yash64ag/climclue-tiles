How July compares in Perm

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Perm. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. August is 3.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Perm in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in July?
In July, Perm averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Perm?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Perm’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in July?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in July?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
