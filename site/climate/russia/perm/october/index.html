Perm weather in October

Average October weather in Perm, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Perm averages 5.7 °C (42 °F) days and 0.2 °C (32 °F) nights, with 63 mm (2.5 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 61 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Perm

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Perm. Compared with September: 6.7 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. November is 9.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Perm in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in October?
In October, Perm averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 0.2 °C (32 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Perm?
October is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in October?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.5 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in October?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
