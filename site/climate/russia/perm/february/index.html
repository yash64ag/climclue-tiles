How February compares in Perm

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Perm. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. March is 7.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Perm in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in February?
In February, Perm averages a day high of -9.1 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -14.8 °C (5 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Perm?
February is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in February?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 71 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in February?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
