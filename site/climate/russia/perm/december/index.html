How December compares in Perm

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Perm. Compared with November: 5.4 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Perm in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in December?
In December, Perm averages a day high of -8.7 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -14.0 °C (7 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 22 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Perm?
December is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in December?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 51 cm (20.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 39 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in December?
About 22 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
