How March compares in Perm

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Perm. Compared with February: 7.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 72 hours more sunshine. April is 7.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Perm in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Perm in March?
In March, Perm averages a day high of -1.3 °C (30 °F) and a night low of -8.2 °C (17 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Perm?
March is not one of Perm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Perm in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Perm in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 36 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Perm get in March?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Perm in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
