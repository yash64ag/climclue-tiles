Krasnogorsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Krasnogorsk is 404th warmest (5.3 °C year average), 845th sunniest (about 1,696 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (692 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 623 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Krasnogorsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Krasnogorsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.819°N, 37.330°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-281-2173), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
