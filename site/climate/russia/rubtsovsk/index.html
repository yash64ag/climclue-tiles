Why Rubtsovsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,721 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Rubtsovsk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Rubtsovsk?
July, with an average of 21.1 °C (70 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 15.5 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Rubtsovsk?
January, with an average of -14.8 °C (5 °F), day highs around -10.7 °C (13 °F) and night lows around -18.3 °C (-1 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Rubtsovsk?
July, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 362 mm (14.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Rubtsovsk?
February, with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Rubtsovsk?
July, with about 328 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Rubtsovsk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 161 cm (63.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Rubtsovsk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
