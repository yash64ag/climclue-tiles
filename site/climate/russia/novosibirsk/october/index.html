How October compares in Novosibirsk

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Novosibirsk. Compared with September: 7.1 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. November is 10.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Novosibirsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novosibirsk in October?
In October, Novosibirsk averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Novosibirsk?
October is not one of Novosibirsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novosibirsk in October?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest February (19 mm).
Does it snow in Novosibirsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (8.0 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Novosibirsk get in October?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novosibirsk in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
