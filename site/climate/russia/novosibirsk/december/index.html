How December compares in Novosibirsk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Novosibirsk. Compared with November: 7.1 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Novosibirsk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novosibirsk in December?
In December, Novosibirsk averages a day high of -10.0 °C (14 °F) and a night low of -18.5 °C (-1 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 54 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Novosibirsk?
December is not one of Novosibirsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novosibirsk in December?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (66 mm), the driest February (18 mm).
Does it snow in Novosibirsk in December?
Yes, on average: about 35 cm (13.8 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 18 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Novosibirsk get in December?
About 54 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novosibirsk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
