How June compares in Novosibirsk

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Novosibirsk. Compared with May: 5.4 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Novosibirsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novosibirsk in June?
In June, Novosibirsk averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Novosibirsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Novosibirsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novosibirsk in June?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest February (19 mm).
Does it snow in Novosibirsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Novosibirsk get in June?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novosibirsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
