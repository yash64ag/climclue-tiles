Novosibirsk weather in April

Average April weather in Novosibirsk, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Novosibirsk averages 7.7 °C (46 °F) days and -3.2 °C (26 °F) nights, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 221 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Novosibirsk

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Novosibirsk. Compared with March: 9.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. May is 9.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Novosibirsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novosibirsk in April?
In April, Novosibirsk averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -3.2 °C (26 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Novosibirsk?
April is not one of Novosibirsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novosibirsk in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (66 mm), the driest February (18 mm).
Does it snow in Novosibirsk in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Novosibirsk get in April?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novosibirsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
