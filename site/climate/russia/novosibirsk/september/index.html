How September compares in Novosibirsk

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Novosibirsk. Compared with August: 6.3 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 75 hours less sunshine. October is 7.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Novosibirsk in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novosibirsk in September?
In September, Novosibirsk averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Novosibirsk?
September is not one of Novosibirsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novosibirsk in September?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (66 mm), the driest February (18 mm).
Does it snow in Novosibirsk in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Novosibirsk get in September?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novosibirsk in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
