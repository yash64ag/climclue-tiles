Novosibirsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novosibirsk is 141st warmest (1.7 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,107 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (463 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barnaul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (192 km away), Novosibirsk is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 35.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,380 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 14 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Novosibirsk

For mild weather, the best months to visit Novosibirsk are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

Why Novosibirsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,380 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.9 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Novosibirsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.023°N, 82.932°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-289-2629), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
