Naberezhnyye Chelny in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naberezhnyye Chelny in April?
In April, Naberezhnyye Chelny averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Naberezhnyye Chelny?
April is not one of Naberezhnyye Chelny’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naberezhnyye Chelny in April?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Naberezhnyye Chelny in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 17 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Naberezhnyye Chelny get in April?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naberezhnyye Chelny in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
