Naberezhnyye Chelny in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naberezhnyye Chelny in October?
In October, Naberezhnyye Chelny averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Naberezhnyye Chelny?
October is not one of Naberezhnyye Chelny’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naberezhnyye Chelny in October?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Naberezhnyye Chelny in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.7 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Naberezhnyye Chelny get in October?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naberezhnyye Chelny in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
