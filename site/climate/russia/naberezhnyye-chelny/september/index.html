Naberezhnyye Chelny in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naberezhnyye Chelny in September?
In September, Naberezhnyye Chelny averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Naberezhnyye Chelny?
September is not one of Naberezhnyye Chelny’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naberezhnyye Chelny in September?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Naberezhnyye Chelny in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naberezhnyye Chelny get in September?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
