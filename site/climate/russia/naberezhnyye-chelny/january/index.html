Naberezhnyye Chelny in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naberezhnyye Chelny in January?
In January, Naberezhnyye Chelny averages a day high of -8.7 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -14.2 °C (6 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 50 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Naberezhnyye Chelny?
January is not one of Naberezhnyye Chelny’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naberezhnyye Chelny in January?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest March (24 mm).
Does it snow in Naberezhnyye Chelny in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 43 cm (16.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 44 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Naberezhnyye Chelny get in January?
About 50 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naberezhnyye Chelny in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
