Naberezhnyye Chelny at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Naberezhnyye Chelny is 107th warmest (4.0 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 1,920 hours of sunshine a year) and 117th wettest (543 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nizhnekamsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Naberezhnyye Chelny is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 31.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 971 km from the sea (deep inland).
