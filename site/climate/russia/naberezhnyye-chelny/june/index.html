What is the weather like in Naberezhnyye Chelny in June?
In June, Naberezhnyye Chelny averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Naberezhnyye Chelny?
