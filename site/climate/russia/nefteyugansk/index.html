Nefteyugansk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nefteyugansk is 166th warmest (-1.6 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 1,785 hours of sunshine a year) and 75th wettest (630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surgut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Nefteyugansk is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 37.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-19.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 579 km from the sea.
