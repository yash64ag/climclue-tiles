Nefteyugansk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nefteyugansk is 165th warmest (-1.6 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 1,785 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (640 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surgut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Nefteyugansk is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 37.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-19.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 579 km from the sea.

Why Nefteyugansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
