Why Balakovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 610 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Balakovo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Balakovo?
July, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.8 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Balakovo?
February, with an average of -9.3 °C (15 °F), day highs around -6.2 °C (21 °F) and night lows around -12.1 °C (10 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Balakovo?
July, with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 608 mm (24.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Balakovo?
May, with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Balakovo?
July, with about 293 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Balakovo?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 170 cm (66.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Balakovo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and August.
What type of climate does Balakovo have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.8 × 11 km, that contains Balakovo’s GeoNames location (52.026°N, 47.797°E; ClimaClue area sq-319-2277), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 199,572 (GeoNames).
