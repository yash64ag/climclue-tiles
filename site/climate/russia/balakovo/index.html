Balakovo at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Balakovo is 36th warmest (6.3 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 1,947 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (608 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Engels, the nearest covered town with different numbers (131 km away), Balakovo is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in February, with 137 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 31.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 610 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Balakovo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Balakovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 610 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.
