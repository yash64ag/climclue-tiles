How January compares in Astrakhan

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Astrakhan. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Astrakhan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astrakhan in January?
In January, Astrakhan averages a day high of -0.1 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -5.9 °C (21 °F), with 17 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 84 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Astrakhan?
January is not one of Astrakhan’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astrakhan in January?
17 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (29 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Astrakhan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Astrakhan get in January?
About 84 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astrakhan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
