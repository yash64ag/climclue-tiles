How August compares in Astrakhan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Astrakhan. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. September is 6.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Astrakhan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astrakhan in August?
In August, Astrakhan averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 302 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Astrakhan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Astrakhan’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astrakhan in August?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (29 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Astrakhan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Astrakhan get in August?
About 302 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astrakhan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
