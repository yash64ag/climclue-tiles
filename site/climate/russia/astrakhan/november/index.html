How November compares in Astrakhan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Astrakhan. Compared with October: 7.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 93 hours less sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Astrakhan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astrakhan in November?
In November, Astrakhan averages a day high of 6.4 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 81 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Astrakhan?
November is not one of Astrakhan’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astrakhan in November?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (29 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Astrakhan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Astrakhan get in November?
About 81 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astrakhan in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
