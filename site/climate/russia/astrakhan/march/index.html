How March compares in Astrakhan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Astrakhan. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. April is 8.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Astrakhan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astrakhan in March?
In March, Astrakhan averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Astrakhan?
March is not one of Astrakhan’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astrakhan in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (29 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Astrakhan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.3 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Astrakhan get in March?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astrakhan in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
