Novokuybyshevsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Novokuybyshevsk, Russia (Samara Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Novokuybyshevsk is warmest in July, with 26.4 °C (80 °F) days and 15.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -6.7 °C (20 °F) days and -13.6 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 32 mm in March to 58 mm in July.

Novokuybyshevsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Novokuybyshevsk is 373rd warmest (5.4 °C year average), 274th sunniest (about 2,047 hours of sunshine a year) and 671st wettest (547 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Samara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Novokuybyshevsk is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 31.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 674 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Novokuybyshevsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.097°N, 49.940°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-309-2299), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
