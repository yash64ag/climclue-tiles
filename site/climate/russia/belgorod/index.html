Belgorod at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Belgorod is 30th warmest (7.3 °C year average), 113th sunniest (about 1,789 hours of sunshine a year) and 85th wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Staryy Oskol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (118 km away), Belgorod is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 418 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Belgorod, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Belgorod has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 418 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
