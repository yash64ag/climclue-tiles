Rostov Veliky weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Rostov Veliky, Russia (Yaroslavl Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Rostov Veliky is warmest in July, with 23.8 °C (75 °F) days and 13.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -5.0 °C (23 °F) days and -11.8 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 26 mm in March to 77 mm in June.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rostov Veliky itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (57.191°N, 39.413°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-268-2194), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
