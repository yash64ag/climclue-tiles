Saratov in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in August?
In August, Saratov averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Saratov?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Saratov’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in August?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (58 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in August?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
