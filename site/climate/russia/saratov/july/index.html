How July compares in Saratov

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Saratov. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. August is 1.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Saratov in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in July?
In July, Saratov averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 312 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Saratov?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Saratov’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in July?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (57 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in July?
About 312 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
