How October compares in Saratov

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Saratov. Compared with September: 7.1 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. November is 7.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Saratov in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in October?
In October, Saratov averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 3.5 °C (38 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Saratov?
October is not one of Saratov’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in October?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (58 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in October?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
