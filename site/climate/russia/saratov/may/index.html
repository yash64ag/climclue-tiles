How May compares in Saratov

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Saratov. Compared with April: 7.8 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. June is 5.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Saratov in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in May?
In May, Saratov averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Saratov?
May is not one of Saratov’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in May?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (58 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in May?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
