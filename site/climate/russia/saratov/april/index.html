How April compares in Saratov

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Saratov. Compared with March: 10.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 74 hours more sunshine. May is 7.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Saratov in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in April?
In April, Saratov averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Saratov?
April is not one of Saratov’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in April?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (58 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in April?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
