How September compares in Saratov

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Saratov. Compared with August: 6.1 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 109 hours less sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Saratov in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in September?
In September, Saratov averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Saratov?
September is not one of Saratov’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in September?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (58 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in September?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
