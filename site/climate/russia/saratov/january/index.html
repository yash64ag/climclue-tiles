How January compares in Saratov

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Saratov. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Saratov in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saratov in January?
In January, Saratov averages a day high of -4.2 °C (24 °F) and a night low of -10.3 °C (13 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 59 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Saratov?
January is not one of Saratov’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saratov in January?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (57 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Saratov in January?
Yes, on average: about 38 cm (14.8 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Saratov get in January?
About 59 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saratov in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
