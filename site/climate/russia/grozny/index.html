Grozny at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Grozny is 7th warmest (11.6 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 1,781 hours of sunshine a year) and 134th wettest (504 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khasavyurt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Grozny is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 154 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Grozny has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 154 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
