How February compares in Voronezh

February is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Voronezh. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. March is 5.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Voronezh in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Voronezh in February?
In February, Voronezh averages a day high of -3.8 °C (25 °F) and a night low of -9.6 °C (15 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 84 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Voronezh?
February is not one of Voronezh’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Voronezh in February?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Voronezh in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 32 cm (12.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Voronezh get in February?
About 84 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Voronezh in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
