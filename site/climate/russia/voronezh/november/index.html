Voronezh weather in November

Average November weather in Voronezh, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Voronezh averages 2.2 °C (36 °F) days and -2.9 °C (27 °F) nights, with 52 mm (2.0 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 44 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Voronezh

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Voronezh. Compared with October: 7.0 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Voronezh in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Voronezh in November?
In November, Voronezh averages a day high of 2.2 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Voronezh?
November is not one of Voronezh’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Voronezh in November?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest March (36 mm).
Does it snow in Voronezh in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Voronezh get in November?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Voronezh in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
