How August compares in Voronezh

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Voronezh. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. September is 6.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Voronezh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Voronezh in August?
In August, Voronezh averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Voronezh?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Voronezh’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Voronezh in August?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Voronezh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Voronezh get in August?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Voronezh in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
