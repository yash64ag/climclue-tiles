How March compares in Voronezh

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Voronezh. Compared with February: 5.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 9.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Voronezh in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Voronezh in March?
In March, Voronezh averages a day high of 2.2 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -5.1 °C (23 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Voronezh?
March is not one of Voronezh’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Voronezh in March?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Voronezh in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 20 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Voronezh get in March?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Voronezh in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
