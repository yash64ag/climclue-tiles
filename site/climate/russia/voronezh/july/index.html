How July compares in Voronezh

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Voronezh. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. August is 1.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Voronezh in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Voronezh in July?
In July, Voronezh averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Voronezh?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Voronezh’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Voronezh in July?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (74 mm), the driest March (34 mm).
Does it snow in Voronezh in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Voronezh get in July?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Voronezh in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
