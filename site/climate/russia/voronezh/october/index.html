How October compares in Voronezh

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Voronezh. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. November is 7.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Voronezh in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Voronezh in October?
In October, Voronezh averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Voronezh?
October is not one of Voronezh’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Voronezh in October?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest March (36 mm).
Does it snow in Voronezh in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Voronezh get in October?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Voronezh in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
