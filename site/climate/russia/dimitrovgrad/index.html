Dimitrovgrad at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Dimitrovgrad is 90th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 1,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (601 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tolyatti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Dimitrovgrad is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 56 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 30.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 808 km from the sea (deep inland).
