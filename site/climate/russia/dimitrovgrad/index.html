Dimitrovgrad at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Dimitrovgrad is 472nd warmest (5.0 °C year average), 347th sunniest (about 1,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 620th wettest (564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tolyatti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Dimitrovgrad is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.7 °C), averages 31.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 808 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dimitrovgrad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.214°N, 49.618°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-297-2296), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
