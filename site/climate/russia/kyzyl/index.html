Kyzyl at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kyzyl is the 4th driest (330 mm a year). Compared with Abakan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (301 km away), Kyzyl is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 4.1 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 42.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-21.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,986 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kyzyl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,986 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 42.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
