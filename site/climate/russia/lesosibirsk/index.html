Lesosibirsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Lesosibirsk, Russia (Krasnoyarsk Krai), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Lesosibirsk is warmest in July, with 25.5 °C (78 °F) days and 12.9 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -15.0 °C (5 °F) days and -24.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (62 mm); March is driest (21 mm).

Lesosibirsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Lesosibirsk is 903rd warmest (-0.2 °C year average), 621st sunniest (about 1,792 hours of sunshine a year) and 771st wettest (510 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yeniseysk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Lesosibirsk is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 39.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-19.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,276 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lesosibirsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lesosibirsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (58.235°N, 92.484°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-257-2724), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
