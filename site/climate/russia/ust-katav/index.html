Ust’-Katav weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Ust’-Katav, Russia (Chelyabinsk), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Ust’-Katav is warmest in July, with 24.4 °C (76 °F) days and 12.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.3 °C (17 °F) days and -17.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (88 mm); February is driest (28 mm).

Why Ust’-Katav has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ust’-Katav itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.937°N, 58.176°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-290-2381), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
