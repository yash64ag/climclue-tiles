How January compares in Kemerovo

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kemerovo. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Kemerovo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kemerovo in January?
In January, Kemerovo averages a day high of -12.2 °C (10 °F) and a night low of -21.3 °C (-6 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 62 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kemerovo?
January is not one of Kemerovo’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kemerovo in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (76 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Kemerovo in January?
Yes, on average: about 35 cm (13.6 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 32 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kemerovo get in January?
About 62 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kemerovo in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
