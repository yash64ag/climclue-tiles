How June compares in Kemerovo

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kemerovo. Compared with May: 5.1 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. July is 2.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Kemerovo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kemerovo in June?
In June, Kemerovo averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kemerovo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Kemerovo’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kemerovo in June?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (76 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Kemerovo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kemerovo get in June?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kemerovo in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
