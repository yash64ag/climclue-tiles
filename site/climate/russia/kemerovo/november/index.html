How November compares in Kemerovo

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kemerovo. Compared with October: 10.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. December is 7.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Kemerovo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kemerovo in November?
In November, Kemerovo averages a day high of -3.5 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -11.1 °C (12 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 56 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kemerovo?
November is not one of Kemerovo’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kemerovo in November?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (76 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Kemerovo in November?
Yes, on average: about 45 cm (17.7 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kemerovo get in November?
About 56 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kemerovo in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
