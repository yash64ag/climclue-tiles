How September compares in Kemerovo

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kemerovo. Compared with August: 6.7 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 78 hours less sunshine. October is 7.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Kemerovo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kemerovo in September?
In September, Kemerovo averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kemerovo?
September is not one of Kemerovo’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kemerovo in September?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (85 mm), the driest February (25 mm).
Does it snow in Kemerovo in September?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in September (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kemerovo get in September?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kemerovo in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
