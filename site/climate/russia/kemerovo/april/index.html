How April compares in Kemerovo

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kemerovo. Compared with March: 8.3 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. May is 9.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Kemerovo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kemerovo in April?
In April, Kemerovo averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.4 °C (24 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kemerovo?
April is not one of Kemerovo’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kemerovo in April?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (85 mm), the driest February (25 mm).
Does it snow in Kemerovo in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 44 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kemerovo get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kemerovo in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
