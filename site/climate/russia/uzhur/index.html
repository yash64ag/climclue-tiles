Uzhur at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Uzhur is 854th warmest (1.2 °C year average), 470th sunniest (about 1,889 hours of sunshine a year) and 927th wettest (393 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sharypovo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Uzhur is about as warm in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 34.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,505 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Uzhur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,505 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Uzhur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.318°N, 89.831°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-286-2698), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
