Glazov at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Glazov is 126th warmest (2.5 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 1,768 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (680 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Izhevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Glazov is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 160 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 31.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 987 km from the sea (deep inland).
