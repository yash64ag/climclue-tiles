Makhachkala at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Makhachkala is the 2nd warmest (12.7 °C year average). Compared with Khasavyurt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Makhachkala is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Why Makhachkala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
