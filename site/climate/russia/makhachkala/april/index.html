How April compares in Makhachkala

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Makhachkala. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 65 hours more sunshine. May is 6.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Makhachkala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makhachkala in April?
In April, Makhachkala averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Makhachkala?
April is not one of Makhachkala’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makhachkala in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (52 mm), the driest April (21 mm).
Does it snow in Makhachkala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makhachkala get in April?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makhachkala in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
