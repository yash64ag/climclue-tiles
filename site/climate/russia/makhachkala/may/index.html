How May compares in Makhachkala

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Makhachkala. Compared with April: 5.9 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 72 hours more sunshine. June is 5.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Makhachkala in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makhachkala in May?
In May, Makhachkala averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Makhachkala?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Makhachkala’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makhachkala in May?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (53 mm), the driest April (21 mm).
Does it snow in Makhachkala in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makhachkala get in May?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Makhachkala in May?
About 14.3 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Makhachkala in May?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
