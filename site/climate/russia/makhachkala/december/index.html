How December compares in Makhachkala

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Makhachkala. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Makhachkala in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makhachkala in December?
In December, Makhachkala averages a day high of 6.3 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 66 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Makhachkala?
December is not one of Makhachkala’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makhachkala in December?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (53 mm), the driest April (21 mm).
Does it snow in Makhachkala in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.9 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Makhachkala get in December?
About 66 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Makhachkala in December?
About 7.7 °C (46 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Makhachkala in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
