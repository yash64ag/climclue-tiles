Makhachkala in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makhachkala in August?
In August, Makhachkala averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Makhachkala?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Makhachkala’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makhachkala in August?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (52 mm), the driest April (21 mm).
Does it snow in Makhachkala in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makhachkala get in August?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makhachkala in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
