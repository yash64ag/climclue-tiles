How September compares in Novokuznetsk

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Novokuznetsk. Compared with August: 6.6 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Novokuznetsk in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novokuznetsk in September?
In September, Novokuznetsk averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Novokuznetsk?
September is not one of Novokuznetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novokuznetsk in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (72 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Novokuznetsk in September?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in September (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Novokuznetsk get in September?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novokuznetsk in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
