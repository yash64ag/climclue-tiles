How April compares in Novokuznetsk

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Novokuznetsk. Compared with March: 9.2 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. May is 8.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Novokuznetsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novokuznetsk in April?
In April, Novokuznetsk averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Novokuznetsk?
April is not one of Novokuznetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novokuznetsk in April?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Novokuznetsk in April?
Yes, on average: about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Novokuznetsk get in April?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novokuznetsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
