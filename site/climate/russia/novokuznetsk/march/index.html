How March compares in Novokuznetsk

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Novokuznetsk. Compared with February: 7.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. April is 9.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Novokuznetsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novokuznetsk in March?
In March, Novokuznetsk averages a day high of -1.3 °C (30 °F) and a night low of -11.2 °C (12 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Novokuznetsk?
March is not one of Novokuznetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novokuznetsk in March?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (72 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Novokuznetsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 29 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Novokuznetsk get in March?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novokuznetsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
