How January compares in Novokuznetsk

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Novokuznetsk. Compared with December: 2.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. February is 2.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Novokuznetsk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novokuznetsk in January?
In January, Novokuznetsk averages a day high of -11.9 °C (11 °F) and a night low of -19.4 °C (-3 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Novokuznetsk?
January is not one of Novokuznetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novokuznetsk in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (72 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Novokuznetsk in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 42 cm (16.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 68 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Novokuznetsk get in January?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novokuznetsk in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
