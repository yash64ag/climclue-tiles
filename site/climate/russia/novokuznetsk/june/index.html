How June compares in Novokuznetsk

Novokuznetsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Novokuznetsk in June?
In June, Novokuznetsk averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Novokuznetsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Novokuznetsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Novokuznetsk in June?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (72 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Novokuznetsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Novokuznetsk get in June?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Novokuznetsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
