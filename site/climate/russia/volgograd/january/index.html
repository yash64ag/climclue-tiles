How January compares in Volgograd

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Volgograd. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Volgograd in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in January?
In January, Volgograd averages a day high of -2.9 °C (27 °F) and a night low of -8.3 °C (17 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 67 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Volgograd?
January is not one of Volgograd’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in January?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest September (27 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 29 cm (11.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in January?
About 67 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
