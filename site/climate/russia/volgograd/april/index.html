How April compares in Volgograd

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Volgograd. Compared with March: 9.8 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. May is 6.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Volgograd in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in April?
In April, Volgograd averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Volgograd?
April is not one of Volgograd’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (40 mm), the driest September (26 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
