Volgograd in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in June?
In June, Volgograd averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Volgograd?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Volgograd’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in June?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest September (27 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in June?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
