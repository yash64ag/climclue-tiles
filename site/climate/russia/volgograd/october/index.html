How October compares in Volgograd

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Volgograd. Compared with September: 7.6 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. November is 8.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Volgograd in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in October?
In October, Volgograd averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Volgograd?
October is not one of Volgograd’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (40 mm), the driest September (26 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in October?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
