How November compares in Volgograd

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Volgograd. Compared with October: 7.9 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 82 hours less sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Volgograd in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in November?
In November, Volgograd averages a day high of 3.8 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -1.5 °C (29 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 59 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Volgograd?
November is not one of Volgograd’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in November?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (40 mm), the driest September (26 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in November?
About 59 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
