How May compares in Volgograd

Volgograd in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in May?
In May, Volgograd averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Volgograd?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Volgograd’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in May?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest September (27 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in May?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
