How December compares in Volgograd

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Volgograd. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Volgograd in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in December?
In December, Volgograd averages a day high of -1.5 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -6.5 °C (20 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 41 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Volgograd?
December is not one of Volgograd’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in December?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest September (27 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in December?
About 41 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
