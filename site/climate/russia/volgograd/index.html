Volgograd at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Volgograd is 26th warmest (8.5 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,116 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (411 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Volzhsky, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Volgograd is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 29.9 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 433 km from the sea.

Why Volgograd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 433 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
