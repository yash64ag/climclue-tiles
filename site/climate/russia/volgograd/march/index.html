How March compares in Volgograd

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Volgograd. Compared with February: 6.1 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 9.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Volgograd in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in March?
In March, Volgograd averages a day high of 3.9 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Volgograd?
March is not one of Volgograd’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in March?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (43 mm), the driest September (27 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in March?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
