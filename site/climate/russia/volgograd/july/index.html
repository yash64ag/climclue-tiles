How July compares in Volgograd

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Volgograd. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. August is 1.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Volgograd in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Volgograd in July?
In July, Volgograd averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 316 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Volgograd?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Volgograd’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Volgograd in July?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (40 mm), the driest September (26 mm).
Does it snow in Volgograd in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Volgograd get in July?
About 316 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Volgograd in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
