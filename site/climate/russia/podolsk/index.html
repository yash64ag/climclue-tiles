Podolsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Podolsk is 58th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 138th sunniest (about 1,714 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (678 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cherëmushki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Podolsk is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 72 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 665 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Podolsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Podolsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 665 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.
