Lyubertsy at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Lyubertsy is 55th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 141st sunniest (about 1,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (694 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zheleznodorozhnyy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Lyubertsy is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 660 km from the sea (deep inland).
