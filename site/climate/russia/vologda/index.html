Vologda at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Vologda is 113th warmest (3.6 °C year average), 160th sunniest (about 1,665 hours of sunshine a year) and 93rd wettest (599 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cherepovets, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Vologda is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 104 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 28.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 527 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Vologda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 527 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
