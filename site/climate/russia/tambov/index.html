Tambov at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Tambov is 39th warmest (6.2 °C year average), 81st sunniest (about 1,911 hours of sunshine a year) and 123rd wettest (534 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lipetsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Tambov is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C cooler in February, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 644 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tambov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 644 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.
