Norilsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Norilsk is the coolest (-9.3 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 1,485 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Noyabrsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (888 km away), Norilsk is 2.6 °C cooler in July and 4.9 °C cooler in January, with 228 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (13.9 °C), averages 41.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-27.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 217 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Norilsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Norilsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 41.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
