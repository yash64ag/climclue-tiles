How October compares in Yaroslavl

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yaroslavl. Compared with September: 6.1 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. November is 7.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Yaroslavl in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaroslavl in October?
In October, Yaroslavl averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yaroslavl?
October is not one of Yaroslavl’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaroslavl in October?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Yaroslavl in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.1 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Yaroslavl get in October?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaroslavl in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
