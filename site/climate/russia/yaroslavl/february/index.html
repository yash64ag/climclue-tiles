How February compares in Yaroslavl

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Yaroslavl. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. March is 5.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Yaroslavl in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaroslavl in February?
In February, Yaroslavl averages a day high of -4.8 °C (23 °F) and a night low of -12.4 °C (10 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 68 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yaroslavl?
February is not one of Yaroslavl’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaroslavl in February?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (78 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Yaroslavl in February?
Yes, on average: about 28 cm (11.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 24 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Yaroslavl get in February?
About 68 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaroslavl in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
