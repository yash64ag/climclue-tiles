Yaroslavl at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Yaroslavl is 98th warmest (4.5 °C year average), 147th sunniest (about 1,702 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kostroma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Yaroslavl is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 27.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 614 km from the sea (deep inland).
