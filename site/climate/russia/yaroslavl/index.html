Yaroslavl weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Yaroslavl, Russia (Yaroslavl Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Yaroslavl at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Yaroslavl is 551st warmest (4.5 °C year average), 826th sunniest (about 1,702 hours of sunshine a year) and 493rd wettest (618 mm a year), near the middle on each. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yaroslavl itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (57.630°N, 39.874°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-263-2198), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
