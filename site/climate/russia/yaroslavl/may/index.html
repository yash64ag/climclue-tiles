How May compares in Yaroslavl

Yaroslavl in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaroslavl in May?
In May, Yaroslavl averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yaroslavl?
May is not one of Yaroslavl’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaroslavl in May?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Yaroslavl in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yaroslavl get in May?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaroslavl in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
