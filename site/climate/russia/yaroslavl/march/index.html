How March compares in Yaroslavl

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yaroslavl. Compared with February: 5.9 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 63 hours more sunshine. April is 8.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Yaroslavl in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yaroslavl in March?
In March, Yaroslavl averages a day high of 1.2 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -6.6 °C (20 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Yaroslavl?
March is not one of Yaroslavl’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yaroslavl in March?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (78 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Yaroslavl in March?
Yes, on average: about 21 cm (8.1 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 22 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Yaroslavl get in March?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yaroslavl in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
