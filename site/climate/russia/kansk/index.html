Kansk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kansk is 158th warmest (0.6 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 1,874 hours of sunshine a year) and 150th wettest (460 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Krasnoyarsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (174 km away), Kansk is about as warm in July and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 38.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-19.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,566 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kansk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,566 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
