Chishmy weather by month and climate

Chishmy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Chishmy is 569th warmest (4.3 °C year average), 367th sunniest (about 1,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 728th wettest (529 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ufa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Chishmy is 0.5 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 154 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.5 °C), averages 32.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 873 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Chishmy has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chishmy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.590°N, 55.382°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-294-2353), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
