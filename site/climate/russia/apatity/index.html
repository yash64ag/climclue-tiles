Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Apatity is 916th warmest (-0.5 °C year average), 970th sunniest (about 1,448 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (828 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Apatity has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Apatity itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (67.583°N, 33.413°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 4.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-164-2134), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
