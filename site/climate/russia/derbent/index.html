Derbent at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Derbent is the 4th warmest (12.0 °C year average). Compared with Makhachkala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (120 km away), Derbent is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Derbent has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
