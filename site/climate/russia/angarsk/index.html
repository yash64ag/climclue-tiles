Angarsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Angarsk is 157th warmest (0.6 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,162 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (414 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Irkutsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Angarsk is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 40.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-20.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,836 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Angarsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,836 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
