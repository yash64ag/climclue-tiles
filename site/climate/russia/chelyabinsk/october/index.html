How October compares in Chelyabinsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chelyabinsk. Compared with September: 6.9 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. November is 10.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Chelyabinsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chelyabinsk in October?
In October, Chelyabinsk averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 99 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chelyabinsk?
October is not one of Chelyabinsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chelyabinsk in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (17 mm).
Does it snow in Chelyabinsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.5 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chelyabinsk get in October?
About 99 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chelyabinsk in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
