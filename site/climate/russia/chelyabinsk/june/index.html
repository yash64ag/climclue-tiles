How June compares in Chelyabinsk

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Chelyabinsk. Compared with May: 5.7 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. July is 1.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Chelyabinsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chelyabinsk in June?
In June, Chelyabinsk averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 298 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chelyabinsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Chelyabinsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chelyabinsk in June?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (17 mm).
Does it snow in Chelyabinsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chelyabinsk get in June?
About 298 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chelyabinsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
