Chelyabinsk in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chelyabinsk in August?
In August, Chelyabinsk averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chelyabinsk?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Chelyabinsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chelyabinsk in August?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (17 mm).
Does it snow in Chelyabinsk in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chelyabinsk get in August?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chelyabinsk in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
