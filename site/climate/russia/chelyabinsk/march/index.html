How March compares in Chelyabinsk

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chelyabinsk. Compared with February: 7.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 65 hours more sunshine. April is 10.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Chelyabinsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chelyabinsk in March?
In March, Chelyabinsk averages a day high of -1.6 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -9.5 °C (15 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chelyabinsk?
March is not one of Chelyabinsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chelyabinsk in March?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest March (17 mm).
Does it snow in Chelyabinsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 43 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chelyabinsk get in March?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chelyabinsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
