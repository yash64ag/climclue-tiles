Velikiy Novgorod at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Velikiy Novgorod is 49th warmest (5.6 °C year average), 159th sunniest (about 1,682 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (651 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kolpino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (142 km away), Velikiy Novgorod is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 52 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.8 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 168 km from the sea.

Why Velikiy Novgorod has this climate
