How July compares in Rostov-on-Don

July is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Rostov-on-Don. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Rostov-on-Don in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rostov-on-Don in July?
In July, Rostov-on-Don averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Rostov-on-Don?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Rostov-on-Don’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rostov-on-Don in July?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (69 mm), the driest October (38 mm).
Does it snow in Rostov-on-Don in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rostov-on-Don get in July?
