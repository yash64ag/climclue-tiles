How January compares in Rostov-on-Don

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Rostov-on-Don. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Rostov-on-Don in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rostov-on-Don in January?
In January, Rostov-on-Don averages a day high of 0.0 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -5.6 °C (22 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 64 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Rostov-on-Don?
January is not one of Rostov-on-Don’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rostov-on-Don in January?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (69 mm), the driest October (38 mm).
Does it snow in Rostov-on-Don in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Rostov-on-Don get in January?
About 64 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rostov-on-Don in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
