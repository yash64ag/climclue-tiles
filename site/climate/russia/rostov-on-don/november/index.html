How November compares in Rostov-on-Don

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Rostov-on-Don. Compared with October: 7.1 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 91 hours less sunshine. December is 4.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Rostov-on-Don in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rostov-on-Don in November?
In November, Rostov-on-Don averages a day high of 6.4 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 63 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rostov-on-Don?
November is not one of Rostov-on-Don’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rostov-on-Don in November?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (69 mm), the driest October (38 mm).
Does it snow in Rostov-on-Don in November?
Yes, on average: about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Rostov-on-Don get in November?
About 63 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rostov-on-Don in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
