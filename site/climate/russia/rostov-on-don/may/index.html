Rostov-on-Don in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rostov-on-Don in May?
In May, Rostov-on-Don averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Rostov-on-Don?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Rostov-on-Don’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rostov-on-Don in May?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (69 mm), the driest October (38 mm).
Does it snow in Rostov-on-Don in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rostov-on-Don get in May?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rostov-on-Don in May?
