Rostov-on-Don in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rostov-on-Don in June?
In June, Rostov-on-Don averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rostov-on-Don?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Rostov-on-Don’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
