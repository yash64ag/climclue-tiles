Rostov-on-Don at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Rostov-on-Don is 15th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,086 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (620 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bataysk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Rostov-on-Don is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 26.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Why Rostov-on-Don has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
