Medvedevo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Medvedevo is 588th warmest (4.2 °C year average), 580th sunniest (about 1,819 hours of sunshine a year) and 598th wettest (572 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yoshkar-Ola, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Medvedevo is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 29.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 982 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-273-2278), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
