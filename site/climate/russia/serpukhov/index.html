Serpukhov at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Serpukhov is 320th warmest (5.6 °C year average), 726th sunniest (about 1,735 hours of sunshine a year) and 483rd wettest (621 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Protvino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Serpukhov is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in February, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.5 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 706 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Serpukhov has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Serpukhov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.920°N, 37.416°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-290-2174), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
