Serpukhov at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Serpukhov is 53rd warmest (5.5 °C year average), 129th sunniest (about 1,735 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (650 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Obninsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Serpukhov is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 706 km from the sea (deep inland).
