Muravlenko at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Muravlenko is 962nd warmest (-4.6 °C year average), 925th sunniest (about 1,629 hours of sunshine a year) and 174th wettest (717 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Noyabrsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Muravlenko is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 73 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.2 °C), averages 38.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 309 km from the sea.

Why Muravlenko has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Muravlenko itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (63.790°N, 74.523°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 4.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-202-2545), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
