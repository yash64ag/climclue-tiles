Kulebaki at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kulebaki is 354th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 567th sunniest (about 1,829 hours of sunshine a year) and 506th wettest (614 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vyksa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Kulebaki is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 888 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kulebaki itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.413°N, 42.532°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-285-2225), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
