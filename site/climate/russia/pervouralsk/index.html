Pervouralsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Pervouralsk is 137th warmest (2.0 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 1,862 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yekaterinburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Pervouralsk is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 31.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,180 km from the sea (deep inland).
