How February compares in Ulyanovsk

February is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ulyanovsk. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. March is 5.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Ulyanovsk in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in February?
In February, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of -7.1 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -12.5 °C (10 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
February is not one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in February?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest March (25 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 33 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in February?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
