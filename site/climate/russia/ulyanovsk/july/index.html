How July compares in Ulyanovsk

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ulyanovsk. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. August is 2.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Ulyanovsk in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in July?
In July, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in July?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (70 mm), the driest March (26 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in July?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
