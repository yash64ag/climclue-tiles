Ulyanovsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Ulyanovsk is 84th warmest (5.0 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 1,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 130th wettest (520 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dimitrovgrad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Ulyanovsk is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in February, with 81 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 30.3 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 837 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ulyanovsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ulyanovsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 837 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
