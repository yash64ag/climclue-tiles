Why Ulyanovsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 837 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Ulyanovsk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ulyanovsk?
July, with an average of 20.4 °C (69 °F), day highs around 24.1 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 16.5 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ulyanovsk?
February, with an average of -9.8 °C (14 °F), day highs around -7.1 °C (19 °F) and night lows around -12.5 °C (10 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ulyanovsk?
June, with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 520 mm (20.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ulyanovsk?
March, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ulyanovsk?
July, with about 291 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 169 cm (66.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ulyanovsk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Ulyanovsk have?
Humid continental, warm summer (Köppen Dfb). Warm summers and cold, snowy winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.5 × 11 km, that contains Ulyanovsk’s GeoNames location (54.328°N, 48.387°E; ClimaClue area sq-296-2283), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 626,540 (GeoNames).
