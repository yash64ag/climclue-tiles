How April compares in Ulyanovsk

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ulyanovsk. Compared with March: 9.6 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. May is 8.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Ulyanovsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in April?
In April, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.2 °C (32 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
April is not one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in April?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest March (25 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.5 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in April?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
