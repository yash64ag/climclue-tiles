How November compares in Ulyanovsk

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ulyanovsk. Compared with October: 8.0 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 5.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Ulyanovsk in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in November?
In November, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of -0.7 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 43 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
November is not one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in November?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest March (25 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in November?
About 43 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
