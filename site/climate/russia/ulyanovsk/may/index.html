How May compares in Ulyanovsk

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ulyanovsk. Compared with April: 8.1 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 75 hours more sunshine. June is 4.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Ulyanovsk in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in May?
In May, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
May is not one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in May?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest March (25 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in May?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
