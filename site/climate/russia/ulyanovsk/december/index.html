How December compares in Ulyanovsk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ulyanovsk. Compared with November: 5.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Ulyanovsk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in December?
In December, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of -5.2 °C (23 °F) and a night low of -11.1 °C (12 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 38 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
December is not one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in December?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (70 mm), the driest March (26 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in December?
About 38 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
