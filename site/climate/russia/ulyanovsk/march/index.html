How March compares in Ulyanovsk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ulyanovsk. Compared with February: 5.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. April is 9.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Ulyanovsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulyanovsk in March?
In March, Ulyanovsk averages a day high of -0.8 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -7.8 °C (18 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ulyanovsk?
March is not one of Ulyanovsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulyanovsk in March?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (70 mm), the driest March (26 mm).
Does it snow in Ulyanovsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 15 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulyanovsk get in March?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulyanovsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
