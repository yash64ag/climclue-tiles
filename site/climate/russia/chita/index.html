Why Chita has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,379 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 44.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Chita climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chita?
July, with an average of 19.6 °C (67 °F), day highs around 24.4 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 14.1 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chita?
January, with an average of -25.4 °C (-14 °F), day highs around -17.8 °C (0 °F) and night lows around -31.0 °C (-24 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chita?
July, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 351 mm (13.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Chita?
February, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Chita?
May, with about 277 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chita?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 70 cm (27.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in April; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 7 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Chita?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Chita have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.8 × 11 km, that contains Chita’s GeoNames location (52.043°N, 113.492°E; ClimaClue area sq-319-2934), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 349,005 (GeoNames).
