Khasavyurt at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Khasavyurt is the 5th warmest (12.0 °C year average). Compared with Grozny, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Khasavyurt is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Why Khasavyurt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
