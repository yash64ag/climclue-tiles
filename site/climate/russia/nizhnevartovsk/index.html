Nizhnevartovsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nizhnevartovsk is the 5th coolest (-1.8 °C year average). Compared with Surgut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (172 km away), Nizhnevartovsk is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 38.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-20.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 636 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nizhnevartovsk has this climate
