Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-272-2400), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
