Saransk weather by month and climate

Saransk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Saransk is 487th warmest (4.9 °C year average), 436th sunniest (about 1,915 hours of sunshine a year) and 617th wettest (564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ruzayevka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Saransk is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 29.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 885 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Saransk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saransk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.185°N, 45.172°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-298-2251), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
