Saransk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Saransk is 87th warmest (4.9 °C year average), 80th sunniest (about 1,915 hours of sunshine a year) and 108th wettest (561 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Penza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Saransk is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 29.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 885 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saransk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.
