Balashikha weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Balashikha, Russia (Moscow Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Balashikha is warmest in July, with 23.9 °C (75 °F) days and 14.1 °C nights, and coolest in February, with -4.1 °C (25 °F) days and -11.2 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 36 mm in March to 88 mm in July.

Balashikha at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Balashikha is 394th warmest (5.3 °C year average), 803rd sunniest (about 1,708 hours of sunshine a year) and 153rd wettest (725 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Reutov, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Balashikha is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 656 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Balashikha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Balashikha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.795°N, 37.948°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-282-2179), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
