Balashikha at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Balashikha is 67th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 142nd sunniest (about 1,708 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (712 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zheleznodorozhnyy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Balashikha is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 656 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 21 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Balashikha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
