How October compares in Saint Petersburg

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Saint Petersburg. Compared with September: 5.2 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Saint Petersburg in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in October?
In October, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 68 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
October is not one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in October?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest April (32 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.7 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in October?
About 68 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Saint Petersburg in October?
About 3.7 °C (39 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (9.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
