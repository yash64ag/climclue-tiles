How September compares in Saint Petersburg

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Saint Petersburg. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Saint Petersburg in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in September?
In September, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of 13.8 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
September is not one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in September?
67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest April (32 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in September?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Saint Petersburg in September?
About 7.3 °C (45 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (9.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
