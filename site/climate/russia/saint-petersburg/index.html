Saint Petersburg at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Saint Petersburg is 513th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 929th sunniest (about 1,621 hours of sunshine a year) and 257th wettest (690 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kudrovo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Saint Petersburg is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Saint Petersburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

July, with an average of 17.1 °C (63 °F), day highs around 21.3 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 13.0 °C (55 °F).
What is the coldest month in Saint Petersburg?
February, with an average of -6.7 °C (20 °F), day highs around -3.7 °C (25 °F) and night lows around -9.6 °C (15 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Saint Petersburg?

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saint Petersburg itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (59.939°N, 30.314°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-240-2103), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
