How November compares in Saint Petersburg

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Saint Petersburg. Compared with October: 6.1 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. December is 3.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Saint Petersburg in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in November?
In November, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of 1.9 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 26 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
November is not one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in November?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest April (32 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in November?
Yes, on average: about 29 cm (11.3 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in November?
About 26 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Saint Petersburg in November?
About 4.5 °C (40 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (9.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
