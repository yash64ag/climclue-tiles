How June compares in Saint Petersburg

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Saint Petersburg. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 3.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Saint Petersburg in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in June?
In June, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in June?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest April (32 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in June?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Saint Petersburg in June?
About 3.8 °C (39 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (9.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
