How December compares in Saint Petersburg

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Saint Petersburg. Compared with November: 3.9 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Saint Petersburg in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in December?
In December, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of -1.6 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -6.3 °C (21 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 14 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
December is not one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in December?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest April (32 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in December?
Yes, on average: about 44 cm (17.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in December?
About 14 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Saint Petersburg in December?
About 0.3 °C (33 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (9.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
