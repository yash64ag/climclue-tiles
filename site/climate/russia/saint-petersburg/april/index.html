How April compares in Saint Petersburg

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Saint Petersburg. Compared with March: 6.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. May is 5.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Saint Petersburg in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in April?
In April, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.2 °C (32 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
April is not one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in April?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest April (33 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.9 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in April?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
