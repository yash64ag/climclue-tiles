How March compares in Saint Petersburg

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Saint Petersburg. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 68 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Saint Petersburg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Saint Petersburg in March?
In March, Saint Petersburg averages a day high of 1.1 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Saint Petersburg?
March is not one of Saint Petersburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Saint Petersburg in March?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest April (32 mm).
Does it snow in Saint Petersburg in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 14 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Saint Petersburg get in March?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Saint Petersburg in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
