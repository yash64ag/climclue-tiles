Saint Petersburg sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · St. Petersburg · Russia

The sea off Saint Petersburg averages 9.0 °C (48 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.5 °C (29 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Saint Petersburg sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint Petersburg?
In August, at about 9.0 °C (48 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint Petersburg?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -1.5 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint Petersburg in March?
About -1.5 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint Petersburg?
About 3.2 °C (38 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Saint Petersburg nearby?
In August: Petrodvorets (9.8 °C, 29 km), Kronstadt (9.8 °C, 31 km) and Sosnovyy Bor (15.3 °C, 69 km).
