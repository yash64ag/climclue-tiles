Fokino sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Primorye · Russia

The sea off Fokino averages 19.7 °C (67 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.1 °C (32 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Fokino sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Fokino?
In August, at about 19.7 °C (67 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Fokino?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 0.1 °C (32 °F).
How warm is the sea in Fokino in February?
About 0.1 °C (32 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Fokino?
About 8.2 °C (47 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Fokino nearby?
In August: Bol'šoj Kamen' (20.3 °C, 16 km), Trudovoye (20.3 °C, 46 km) and Vladivostok (20.3 °C, 46 km).
