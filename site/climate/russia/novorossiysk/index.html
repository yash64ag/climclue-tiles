Novorossiysk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novorossiysk is the 4th warmest (12.6 °C year average). Compared with Krasnodar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Novorossiysk is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.0 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Novorossiysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
