Novorossiysk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novorossiysk is 6th warmest (11.8 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,220 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (761 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Krasnodar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Novorossiysk is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Novorossiysk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Novorossiysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
