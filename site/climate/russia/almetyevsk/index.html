Al’met’yevsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Al’met’yevsk is 112th warmest (3.8 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 1,979 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (520 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nizhnekamsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Al’met’yevsk is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 882 km from the sea (deep inland).
