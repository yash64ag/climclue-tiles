Vanino sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Khabarovsk · Russia

The sea off Vanino averages 15.4 °C (60 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.5 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Vanino sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vanino?
In August, at about 15.4 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vanino?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.5 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vanino in February?
About -1.5 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vanino?
About 5.5 °C (42 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vanino nearby?
In August: Poronaysk (16.1 °C, 207 km), Kholmsk (16.6 °C, 264 km) and Nevel’sk (17.3 °C, 294 km).
