Kinel’ weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kinel’, Russia (Samara Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kinel’ at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kinel’ is 330th warmest (5.5 °C year average), 231st sunniest (about 2,082 hours of sunshine a year) and 804th wettest (489 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Samara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Kinel’ is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.5 °C), averages 32.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 691 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kinel’ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kinel’ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.227°N, 50.626°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-307-2306), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
