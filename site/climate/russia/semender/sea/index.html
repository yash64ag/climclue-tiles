Semender sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Dagestan · Russia

The sea off Semender averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.2 °C (32 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Semender sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Semender?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Semender?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 0.2 °C (32 °F).
How warm is the sea in Semender in March?
About 0.2 °C (32 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Semender?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
