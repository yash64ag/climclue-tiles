Buynaksk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Buynaksk is 76th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 362nd sunniest (about 1,966 hours of sunshine a year) and 707th wettest (535 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Semender, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Buynaksk is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.0 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Why Buynaksk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Buynaksk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.818°N, 47.127°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-411-2271), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
