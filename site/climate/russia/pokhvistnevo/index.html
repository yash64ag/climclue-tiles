Pokhvistnevo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Pokhvistnevo, Russia (Samara Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Pokhvistnevo is warmest in July, with 26.5 °C (80 °F) days and 15.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -7.6 °C (18 °F) days and -15.1 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 27 mm in March to 53 mm in June.

Pokhvistnevo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Pokhvistnevo is 515th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 236th sunniest (about 2,081 hours of sunshine a year) and 839th wettest (473 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buguruslan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Pokhvistnevo is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.8 °C), averages 32.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 737 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pokhvistnevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.652°N, 52.127°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-303-2321), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
