Izobil’nyy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Izobil’nyy is 86th warmest (10.9 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,154 hours of sunshine a year) and 516th wettest (609 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Donskoye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Izobil’nyy is 0.7 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.7 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 259 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Izobil’nyy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.367°N, 41.709°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-386-2217), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
