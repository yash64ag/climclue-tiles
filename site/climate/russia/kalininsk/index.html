Kalininsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kalininsk, Russia (Saratov Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kalininsk is warmest in July, with 27.2 °C (81 °F) days and 15.2 °C nights, and coolest in February, with -5.1 °C (23 °F) days and -12.6 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 29 mm in February to 54 mm in June.

Kalininsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kalininsk is 256th warmest (6.2 °C year average), 287th sunniest (about 2,034 hours of sunshine a year) and 828th wettest (479 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Atkarsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Kalininsk is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.2 °C), averages 30.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-8.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 613 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kalininsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kalininsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.498°N, 44.477°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-325-2244), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
