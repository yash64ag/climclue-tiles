Rtishchevo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Rtishchevo, Russia (Saratov Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Rtishchevo is warmest in July, with 26.0 °C (79 °F) days and 14.5 °C nights, and coolest in February, with -5.1 °C (23 °F) days and -12.5 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 34 mm in April to 67 mm in June.

Rtishchevo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Rtishchevo is 303rd warmest (5.6 °C year average), 335th sunniest (about 1,987 hours of sunshine a year) and 605th wettest (569 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 656 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rtishchevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.260°N, 43.787°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-317-2237), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
