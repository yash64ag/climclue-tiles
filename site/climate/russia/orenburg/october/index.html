How October compares in Orenburg

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Orenburg. Compared with September: 8.1 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 85 hours less sunshine. November is 9.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Orenburg in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orenburg in October?
In October, Orenburg averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Orenburg?
October is not one of Orenburg’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Orenburg in October?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (39 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Orenburg in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Orenburg get in October?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Orenburg in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
