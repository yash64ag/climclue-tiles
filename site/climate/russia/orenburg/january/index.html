How January compares in Orenburg

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Orenburg. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Orenburg in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orenburg in January?
In January, Orenburg averages a day high of -8.4 °C (17 °F) and a night low of -14.9 °C (5 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 75 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Orenburg?
January is not one of Orenburg’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Orenburg in January?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (39 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Orenburg in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 44 cm (17.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 38 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Orenburg get in January?
About 75 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Orenburg in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
