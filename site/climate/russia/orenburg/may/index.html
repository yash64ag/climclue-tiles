Orenburg in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Orenburg in May?
In May, Orenburg averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Orenburg?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Orenburg’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Orenburg in May?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (39 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Orenburg in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Orenburg get in May?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Orenburg in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
