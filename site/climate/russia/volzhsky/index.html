Why Volzhsky has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 422 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Volzhsky climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Volzhsky?
July, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 29.5 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 19.3 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Volzhsky?
February, with an average of -6.0 °C (21 °F), day highs around -2.6 °C (27 °F) and night lows around -9.0 °C (16 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Volzhsky?
May, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 378 mm (14.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Volzhsky?
September, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Volzhsky?
July, with about 315 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Volzhsky?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 103 cm (40.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Volzhsky?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Volzhsky have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.3 × 11 km, that contains Volzhsky’s GeoNames location (48.786°N, 44.780°E; ClimaClue area sq-352-2247), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 323,293 (GeoNames).
