Volzhsky at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Volzhsky is 22nd warmest (9.0 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 2,112 hours of sunshine a year) and 161st wettest (378 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Volgograd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Volzhsky is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 30.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 422 km from the sea.

Why Volzhsky has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 422 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
