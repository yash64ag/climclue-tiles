How October compares in Tyumen

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tyumen. Compared with September: 6.7 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. November is 10.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Tyumen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tyumen in October?
In October, Tyumen averages a day high of 7.2 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tyumen?
October is not one of Tyumen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tyumen in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Tyumen in October?
Yes, on average: about 13 cm (5.1 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tyumen get in October?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tyumen in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
