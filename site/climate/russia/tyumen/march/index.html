How March compares in Tyumen

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tyumen. Compared with February: 8.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. April is 9.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Tyumen in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tyumen in March?
In March, Tyumen averages a day high of -2.5 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -10.1 °C (14 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tyumen?
March is not one of Tyumen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tyumen in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Tyumen in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 21 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tyumen get in March?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tyumen in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
