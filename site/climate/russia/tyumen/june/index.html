How June compares in Tyumen

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tyumen. Compared with May: 5.8 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. July is 1.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Tyumen in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tyumen in June?
In June, Tyumen averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tyumen?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tyumen’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tyumen in June?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Tyumen in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tyumen get in June?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tyumen in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
