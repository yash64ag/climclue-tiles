How January compares in Tyumen

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tyumen. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Tyumen in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tyumen in January?
In January, Tyumen averages a day high of -11.8 °C (11 °F) and a night low of -18.5 °C (-1 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 64 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tyumen?
January is not one of Tyumen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tyumen in January?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Tyumen in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 41 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tyumen get in January?
About 64 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tyumen in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
