How December compares in Tyumen

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tyumen. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Tyumen in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tyumen in December?
In December, Tyumen averages a day high of -9.1 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -17.0 °C (1 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 47 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tyumen?
December is not one of Tyumen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tyumen in December?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Tyumen in December?
Yes, on average: about 27 cm (10.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tyumen get in December?
About 47 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tyumen in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
