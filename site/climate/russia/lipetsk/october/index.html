Lipetsk weather in October

Average October weather in Lipetsk, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Lipetsk averages 10.1 °C (50 °F) days and 2.5 °C (37 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 105 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Lipetsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lipetsk. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. November is 7.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Lipetsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lipetsk in October?
In October, Lipetsk averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lipetsk?
October is not one of Lipetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lipetsk in October?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (71 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Lipetsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lipetsk get in October?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lipetsk in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
