How March compares in Lipetsk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Lipetsk. Compared with February: 5.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 9.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Lipetsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lipetsk in March?
In March, Lipetsk averages a day high of 1.3 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Lipetsk?
March is not one of Lipetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lipetsk in March?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (71 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Lipetsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Lipetsk get in March?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lipetsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
