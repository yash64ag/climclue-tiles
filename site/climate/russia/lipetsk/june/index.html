How June compares in Lipetsk

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Lipetsk. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Lipetsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lipetsk in June?
In June, Lipetsk averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Lipetsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Lipetsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lipetsk in June?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (70 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Lipetsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lipetsk get in June?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lipetsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
