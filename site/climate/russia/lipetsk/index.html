Lipetsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Lipetsk is 35th warmest (6.4 °C year average), 90th sunniest (about 1,874 hours of sunshine a year) and 107th wettest (565 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yelets, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Lipetsk is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 601 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lipetsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lipetsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
