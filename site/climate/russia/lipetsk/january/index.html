How January compares in Lipetsk

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lipetsk. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Lipetsk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lipetsk in January?
In January, Lipetsk averages a day high of -4.5 °C (24 °F) and a night low of -9.7 °C (15 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 60 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lipetsk?
January is not one of Lipetsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lipetsk in January?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (70 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Lipetsk in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 43 cm (17.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 23 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Lipetsk get in January?
About 60 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lipetsk in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
