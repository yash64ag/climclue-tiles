Biysk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Biysk is 114th warmest (3.4 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,221 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (575 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barnaul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (135 km away), Biysk is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 102 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 36.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,691 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Biysk has this climate
