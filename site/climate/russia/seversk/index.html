Seversk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Seversk is 153rd warmest (0.9 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 1,983 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (677 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tomsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Seversk is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 36.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,264 km from the sea (deep inland).
