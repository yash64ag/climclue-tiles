Kronstadt sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · St. Petersburg · Russia

The sea off Kronstadt averages 9.8 °C (50 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.4 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Kronstadt sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kronstadt?
In August, at about 9.8 °C (50 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kronstadt?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.4 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kronstadt in February?
About -1.4 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kronstadt?
About 3.4 °C (38 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kronstadt nearby?
In August: Sosnovyy Bor (15.3 °C, 40 km), Kohtla-Järve (16.4 °C, 155 km) and Hamina (16.5 °C, 156 km).
