Achinsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Achinsk is 154th warmest (0.9 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 1,835 hours of sunshine a year) and 137th wettest (491 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Krasnoyarsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (153 km away), Achinsk is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 35.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,427 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Achinsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,427 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
