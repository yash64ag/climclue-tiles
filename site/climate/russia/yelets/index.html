Yelets at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Yelets is 38th warmest (6.2 °C year average), 96th sunniest (about 1,848 hours of sunshine a year) and 94th wettest (593 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lipetsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Yelets is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 613 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Yelets has this climate
