Sharypovo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sharypovo, Russia (Krasnoyarsk Krai), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sharypovo is warmest in July, with 24.6 °C (76 °F) days and 12.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -10.0 °C (14 °F) days and -20.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (90 mm); February is driest (13 mm).

Sharypovo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Sharypovo is 812th warmest (1.6 °C year average), 457th sunniest (about 1,899 hours of sunshine a year) and 652nd wettest (552 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uzhur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Sharypovo is about as warm in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 159 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 33.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,471 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Sharypovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sharypovo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.540°N, 89.201°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-284-2692), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
