Poronaysk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sakhalin Oblast · Russia

The sea off Poronaysk averages 16.1 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.7 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Poronaysk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Poronaysk?
In August, at about 16.1 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Poronaysk?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.7 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Poronaysk in February?
About -1.7 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Poronaysk?
About 5.3 °C (42 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Poronaysk nearby?
In August: Korsakov (16.8 °C, 288 km), Nevel’sk (17.3 °C, 297 km) and Wakkanai (19.1 °C, 437 km).
