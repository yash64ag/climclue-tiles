Belaya Glina at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Belaya Glina is 77th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 2,161 hours of sunshine a year) and 538th wettest (599 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novopokrovskaya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Belaya Glina is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.2 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Belaya Glina itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.081°N, 40.874°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-379-2208), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
