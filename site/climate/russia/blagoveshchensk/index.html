Why Blagoveshchensk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 732 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 44.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Blagoveshchensk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Blagoveshchensk?
July, with an average of 21.8 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.0 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Blagoveshchensk?
January, with an average of -22.8 °C (-9 °F), day highs around -16.4 °C (2 °F) and night lows around -27.2 °C (-17 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Blagoveshchensk?
July, with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 537 mm (21.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Blagoveshchensk?
February, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Blagoveshchensk?
June, with about 260 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Blagoveshchensk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 83 cm (32.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in November; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 7 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Blagoveshchensk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June.
