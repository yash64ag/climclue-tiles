Donskoye at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Donskoye is 69th warmest (11.1 °C year average), 148th sunniest (about 2,138 hours of sunshine a year) and 643rd wettest (555 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Izobil’nyy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Donskoye is 0.7 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 54 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 278 km from the sea.

Why Donskoye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Donskoye itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.453°N, 41.972°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-385-2219), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
