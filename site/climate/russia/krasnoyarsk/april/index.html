How April compares in Krasnoyarsk

Krasnoyarsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnoyarsk in April?
In April, Krasnoyarsk averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -2.3 °C (28 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Krasnoyarsk?
April is not one of Krasnoyarsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnoyarsk in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnoyarsk in April?
Yes, on average: about 9.0 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnoyarsk get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnoyarsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
