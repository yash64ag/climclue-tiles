How October compares in Krasnoyarsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Krasnoyarsk. Compared with September: 7.3 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. November is 9.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Krasnoyarsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnoyarsk in October?
In October, Krasnoyarsk averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -1.7 °C (29 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Krasnoyarsk?
October is not one of Krasnoyarsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnoyarsk in October?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (72 mm), the driest February (13 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnoyarsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.7 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnoyarsk get in October?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnoyarsk in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
