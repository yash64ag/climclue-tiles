Krasnoyarsk weather in December

Average December weather in Krasnoyarsk, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Krasnoyarsk averages -10.4 °C (13 °F) days and -18.8 °C (-2 °F) nights, with 27 mm (1.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 30 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Krasnoyarsk

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Krasnoyarsk. Compared with November: 7.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 2.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Krasnoyarsk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnoyarsk in December?
In December, Krasnoyarsk averages a day high of -10.4 °C (13 °F) and a night low of -18.8 °C (-2 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 30 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Krasnoyarsk?
December is not one of Krasnoyarsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnoyarsk in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnoyarsk in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (10.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 14 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnoyarsk get in December?
About 30 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnoyarsk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
