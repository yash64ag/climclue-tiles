How March compares in Krasnoyarsk

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Krasnoyarsk. Compared with February: 8.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. April is 9.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Krasnoyarsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnoyarsk in March?
In March, Krasnoyarsk averages a day high of -1.9 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -12.9 °C (9 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Krasnoyarsk?
March is not one of Krasnoyarsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnoyarsk in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (72 mm), the driest February (13 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnoyarsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (8.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 54 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnoyarsk get in March?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnoyarsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
