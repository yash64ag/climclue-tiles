Krasnoyarsk in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Krasnoyarsk in May?
In May, Krasnoyarsk averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Krasnoyarsk?
May is not one of Krasnoyarsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Krasnoyarsk in May?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Krasnoyarsk in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Krasnoyarsk get in May?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Krasnoyarsk in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
