Zimovniki at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Zimovniki is 115th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 2,147 hours of sunshine a year) and 849th wettest (467 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orlovskiy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Zimovniki is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.3 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 250 km from the sea.

Why Zimovniki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zimovniki itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.146°N, 42.470°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-368-2224), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
