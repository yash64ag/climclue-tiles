Syzran at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Syzran is 47th warmest (5.7 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 1,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (472 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tolyatti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Syzran is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 73 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 31.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 707 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Syzran, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Syzran has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
