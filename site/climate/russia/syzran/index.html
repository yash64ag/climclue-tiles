Syzran at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Syzran is 47th warmest (5.7 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 1,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (463 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tolyatti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Syzran is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 31.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 707 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Syzran, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Syzran has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Syzran itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.158°N, 48.468°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-308-2284), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
