Nevinnomyssk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nevinnomyssk is 16th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,103 hours of sunshine a year) and 113th wettest (552 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Stavropol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Nevinnomyssk is 1.6 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 178 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 209 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nevinnomyssk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nevinnomyssk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 209 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
