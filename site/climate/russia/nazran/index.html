Nazran at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nazran is 18th warmest (9.4 °C year average), 92nd sunniest (about 1,863 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (854 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vladikavkaz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Nazran is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 270 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 215 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 22 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 91% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nazran has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 215 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
