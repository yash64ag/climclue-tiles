Nakhodka at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nakhodka is 45th warmest (5.8 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,105 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (787 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Artëm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Nakhodka is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.9 °C), averages 32.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nakhodka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nakhodka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
