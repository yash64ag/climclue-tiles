Kislovodsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kislovodsk is 29th warmest (7.6 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (739 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pyatigorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Kislovodsk is 2.3 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 103 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 184 km from the sea.

Why Kislovodsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 184 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
