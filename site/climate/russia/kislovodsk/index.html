Why Kislovodsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 184 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kislovodsk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kislovodsk?
July, with an average of 18.6 °C (66 °F), day highs around 22.3 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 14.0 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kislovodsk?
January, with an average of -3.0 °C (27 °F), day highs around 1.5 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -7.2 °C (19 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kislovodsk?
June, with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 739 mm (29.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kislovodsk?
January, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kislovodsk?
July, with about 259 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kislovodsk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 205 cm (80.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kislovodsk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: August.
What type of climate does Kislovodsk have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.0 × 11 km, that contains Kislovodsk’s GeoNames location (43.913°N, 42.721°E; ClimaClue area sq-400-2227), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 132,771 (GeoNames).
