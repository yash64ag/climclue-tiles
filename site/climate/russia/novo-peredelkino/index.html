Novo-Peredelkino at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novo-Peredelkino is 71st warmest (5.3 °C year average), 145th sunniest (about 1,703 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (708 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Odintsovo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Novo-Peredelkino is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.8 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 640 km from the sea (deep inland).
