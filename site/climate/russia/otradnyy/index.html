Otradnyy weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Otradnyy, Russia (Samara Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Otradnyy is warmest in July, with 26.7 °C (80 °F) days and 15.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -7.2 °C (19 °F) days and -14.5 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 28 mm in March to 57 mm in June.

Otradnyy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Otradnyy is 442nd warmest (5.2 °C year average), 218th sunniest (about 2,093 hours of sunshine a year) and 775th wettest (509 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kinel’-Cherkassy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Otradnyy is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. The area lies about 701 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Otradnyy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.376°N, 51.345°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-306-2313), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
