Kurchaloy weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kurchaloy, Russia (Chechnya), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kurchaloy is warmest in July, with 29.9 °C (86 °F) days and 18.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 2.3 °C (36 °F) days and -4.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around June (90 mm); February is driest (27 mm).

Kurchaloy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kurchaloy is 55th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 563rd sunniest (about 1,829 hours of sunshine a year) and 532nd wettest (601 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tsotsin-Yurt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kurchaloy is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.3 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 122 km from the sea.

Why Kurchaloy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 122 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kurchaloy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.205°N, 46.088°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-407-2260), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
