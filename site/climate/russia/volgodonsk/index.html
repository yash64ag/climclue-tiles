Volgodonsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Volgodonsk is 13th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,144 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (475 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shakhty, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Volgodonsk is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 109 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 230 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 14 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.
