Akhtubinsk weather by month and climate

Akhtubinsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Akhtubinsk is 146th warmest (9.4 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,122 hours of sunshine a year) and 972nd wettest (310 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Znamensk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Akhtubinsk is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 319 km from the sea.

Why Akhtubinsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 319 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Akhtubinsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.283°N, 46.165°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-357-2261), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
