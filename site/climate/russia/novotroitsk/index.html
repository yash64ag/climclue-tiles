Novotroitsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novotroitsk is 95th warmest (4.6 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,340 hours of sunshine a year) and 164th wettest (358 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Novotroitsk is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 34.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 628 km from the sea (deep inland).
