Novotroitsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Novotroitsk is 514th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,340 hours of sunshine a year) and 962nd wettest (337 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Novotroitsk is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 35.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 628 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Novotroitsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.201°N, 58.313°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-327-2383), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
