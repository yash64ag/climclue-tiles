Pridonskoy weather by month and climate

Pridonskoy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Pridonskoy is 212th warmest (7.1 °C year average), 466th sunniest (about 1,890 hours of sunshine a year) and 569th wettest (582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Semiluki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Pridonskoy is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Why Pridonskoy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pridonskoy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.683°N, 39.068°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-323-2190), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
