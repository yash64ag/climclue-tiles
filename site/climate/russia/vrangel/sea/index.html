Vrangel’ sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Primorye · Russia

The sea off Vrangel’ averages 19.5 °C (67 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.1 °C (34 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Vrangel’ sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vrangel’?
In August, at about 19.5 °C (67 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vrangel’?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 1.1 °C (34 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vrangel’ in February?
About 1.1 °C (34 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vrangel’?
About 8.5 °C (47 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vrangel’ nearby?
In August: Bol'šoj Kamen' (20.3 °C, 73 km), Artëm (20.3 °C, 101 km) and Trudovoye (20.3 °C, 104 km).
