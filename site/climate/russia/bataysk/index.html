Bataysk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Bataysk is 12th warmest (10.3 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,090 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (591 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rostov-on-Don, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Bataysk is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bataysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
