Gornyak weather by month and climate

Gornyak at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Gornyak is 608th warmest (4.0 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,432 hours of sunshine a year) and 901st wettest (421 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rubtsovsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Gornyak is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 35.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,789 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gornyak itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.995°N, 81.467°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-330-2614), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
