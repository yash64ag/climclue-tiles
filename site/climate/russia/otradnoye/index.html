Otradnoye at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Otradnoye is 443rd warmest (5.3 °C year average), 911th sunniest (about 1,646 hours of sunshine a year) and 151st wettest (726 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nikol’skoye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Otradnoye is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Why Otradnoye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Otradnoye itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (59.778°N, 30.818°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-242-2108), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
