Temryuk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Krasnodar Krai · Russia

The sea off Temryuk averages 24.6 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.3 °C (40 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Temryuk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Temryuk?
In August, at about 24.6 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Temryuk?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 4.3 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Temryuk in February?
About 4.3 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Temryuk?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Temryuk nearby?
In August: Tuapse (25.2 °C, 186 km).
