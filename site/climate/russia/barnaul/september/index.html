How September compares in Barnaul

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Barnaul. Compared with August: 6.4 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Barnaul in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barnaul in September?
In September, Barnaul averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Barnaul?
September is not one of Barnaul’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barnaul in September?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (74 mm), the driest March (19 mm).
Does it snow in Barnaul in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barnaul get in September?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barnaul in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
