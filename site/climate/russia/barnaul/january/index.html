How January compares in Barnaul

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Barnaul. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Barnaul in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barnaul in January?
In January, Barnaul averages a day high of -10.6 °C (13 °F) and a night low of -20.5 °C (-5 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Barnaul?
January is not one of Barnaul’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barnaul in January?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (74 mm), the driest March (19 mm).
Does it snow in Barnaul in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 23 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Barnaul get in January?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barnaul in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
