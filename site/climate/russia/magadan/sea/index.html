Magadan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Magadan Oblast · Russia

The sea off Magadan averages 11.4 °C (53 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.7 °C (29 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Magadan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Magadan?
In August, at about 11.4 °C (53 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Magadan?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.7 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Magadan in February?
About -1.7 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Magadan?
About 2.8 °C (37 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Magadan nearby?
In August: Okha (12.7 °C, 818 km).
