Magadan at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Magadan is 957th warmest (-3.7 °C year average), 582nd sunniest (about 1,818 hours of sunshine a year) and 541st wettest (598 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Okha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (818 km away), Magadan is 1.9 °C cooler in August and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 147 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (12.1 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-19.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Magadan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Magadan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (59.563°N, 150.802°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-244-3308), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
