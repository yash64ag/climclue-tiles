Noyabrsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Noyabrsk is the 3rd coolest (-4.2 °C year average). Compared with Surgut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (239 km away), Noyabrsk is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.6 °C), averages 38.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, May, October, November and December. The area lies about 387 km from the sea.

Why Noyabrsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 387 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
