Elektrogorsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Elektrogorsk is 376th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 739th sunniest (about 1,729 hours of sunshine a year) and 298th wettest (679 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pavlovskiy Posad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Elektrogorsk is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 682 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Elektrogorsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.884°N, 38.786°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-281-2187), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
