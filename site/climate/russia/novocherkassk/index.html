Novocherkassk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novocherkassk is 17th warmest (9.9 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,089 hours of sunshine a year) and 103rd wettest (573 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shakhty, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Novocherkassk is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 76 km from the sea.
