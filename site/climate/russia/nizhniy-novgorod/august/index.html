Nizhniy Novgorod in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in August?
In August, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in August?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest April (37 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in August?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
