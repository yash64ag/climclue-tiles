How July compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Nizhniy Novgorod. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. August is 2.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Nizhniy Novgorod in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in July?
In July, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in July?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (38 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in July?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
