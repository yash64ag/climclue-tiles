Nizhniy Novgorod weather in January

Average January weather in Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Nizhniy Novgorod averages -5.9 °C (21 °F) days and -12.3 °C (10 °F) nights, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 42 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nizhniy Novgorod. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Nizhniy Novgorod in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in January?
In January, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of -5.9 °C (21 °F) and a night low of -12.3 °C (10 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 42 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
January is not one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in January?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (38 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 45 cm (17.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 21 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in January?
About 42 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
