How June compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Nizhniy Novgorod. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.
