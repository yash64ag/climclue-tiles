Nizhniy Novgorod in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in June?
In June, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
