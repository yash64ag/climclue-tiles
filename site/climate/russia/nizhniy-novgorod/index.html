Nizhniy Novgorod at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Nizhniy Novgorod is 88th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 1,802 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (678 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dzerzhinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Nizhniy Novgorod is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 906 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.
