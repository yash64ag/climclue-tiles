How May compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

Nizhniy Novgorod in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in May?
In May, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
May is not one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in May?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest April (37 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in May?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
