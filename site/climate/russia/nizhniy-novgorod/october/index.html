How October compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nizhniy Novgorod. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 69 hours less sunshine. November is 7.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Nizhniy Novgorod in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in October?
In October, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 7.4 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
October is not one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in October?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest April (37 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in October?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
