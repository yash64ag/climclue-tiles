How February compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nizhniy Novgorod. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. March is 6.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Nizhniy Novgorod in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in February?
In February, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of -6.1 °C (21 °F) and a night low of -11.8 °C (11 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
February is not one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in February?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest April (37 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 36 cm (14.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 49 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in February?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
