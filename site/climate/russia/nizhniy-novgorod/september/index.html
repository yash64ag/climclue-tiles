Nizhniy Novgorod in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in September?
In September, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
September is not one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in September?
67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (38 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in September?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
