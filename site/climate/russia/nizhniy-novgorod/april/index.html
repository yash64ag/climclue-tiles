How April compares in Nizhniy Novgorod

Nizhniy Novgorod in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nizhniy Novgorod in April?
In April, Nizhniy Novgorod averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nizhniy Novgorod?
April is not one of Nizhniy Novgorod’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nizhniy Novgorod in April?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest April (37 mm).
Does it snow in Nizhniy Novgorod in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.7 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nizhniy Novgorod get in April?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nizhniy Novgorod in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
