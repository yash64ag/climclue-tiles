Balashov weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Balashov, Russia (Saratov Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Balashov is warmest in July, with 26.9 °C (80 °F) days and 15.4 °C nights, and coolest in February, with -4.4 °C (24 °F) days and -11.7 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 32 mm in April to 63 mm in June.

Balashov at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Balashov is 238th warmest (6.6 °C year average), 316th sunniest (about 2,000 hours of sunshine a year) and 711th wettest (533 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Povorino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (75 km away), Balashov is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in February, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 29.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-8.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 568 km from the sea.

Why Balashov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 568 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.2 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Balashov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.551°N, 43.171°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-324-2231), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
