How February compares in Penza

February is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Penza. Compared with January: 0.3 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. March is 5.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Penza in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Penza in February?
In February, Penza averages a day high of -5.5 °C (22 °F) and a night low of -12.8 °C (9 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Penza?
February is not one of Penza’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Penza in February?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Penza in February?
Yes, on average: about 28 cm (11.0 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 20 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Penza get in February?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Penza in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
