Penza at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Penza is 62nd warmest (5.4 °C year average), 69th sunniest (about 1,965 hours of sunshine a year) and 118th wettest (541 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saransk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Penza is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.3 °C), averages 29.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-9.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 788 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Penza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 788 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
