How November compares in Penza

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Penza. Compared with October: 8.0 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. December is 5.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Penza in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Penza in November?
In November, Penza averages a day high of 0.4 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -4.7 °C (24 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 46 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Penza?
November is not one of Penza’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Penza in November?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Penza in November?
Yes, on average: about 28 cm (11.2 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Penza get in November?
About 46 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Penza in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
