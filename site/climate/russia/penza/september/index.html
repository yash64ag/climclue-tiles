How September compares in Penza

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Penza. Compared with August: 5.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 92 hours less sunshine. October is 6.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Penza in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Penza in September?
In September, Penza averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Penza?
September is not one of Penza’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Penza in September?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Penza in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Penza get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Penza in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
