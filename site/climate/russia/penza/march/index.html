How March compares in Penza

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Penza. Compared with February: 5.7 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. April is 10.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Penza in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Penza in March?
In March, Penza averages a day high of 0.0 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -6.9 °C (20 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Penza?
March is not one of Penza’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Penza in March?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Penza in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 18 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Penza get in March?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Penza in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
