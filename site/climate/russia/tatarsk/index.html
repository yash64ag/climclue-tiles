Tatarsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Tatarsk is 802nd warmest (1.7 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 2,174 hours of sunshine a year) and 920th wettest (398 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kalachinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (91 km away), Tatarsk is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 36.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,250 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tatarsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tatarsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.221°N, 75.982°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-287-2559), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
