Leninogorsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Leninogorsk, Russia (Tatarstan Republic), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Leninogorsk is warmest in July, with 24.7 °C (76 °F) days and 13.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.1 °C (17 °F) days and -15.6 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 25 mm in March to 73 mm in June.

Leninogorsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Leninogorsk is 640th warmest (3.5 °C year average), 312th sunniest (about 2,003 hours of sunshine a year) and 588th wettest (574 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bugulma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Leninogorsk is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 30.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 838 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Leninogorsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.597°N, 52.451°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-294-2324), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
