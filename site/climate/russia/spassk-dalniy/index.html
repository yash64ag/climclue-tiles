Spassk-Dal’niy weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Spassk-Dal’niy, Russia (Primorye), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Spassk-Dal’niy is warmest in July, with 26.1 °C (79 °F) days and 17.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -12.9 °C (9 °F) days and -22.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (105 mm); January is driest (14 mm).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Spassk-Dal’niy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.601°N, 132.820°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-393-3128), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
