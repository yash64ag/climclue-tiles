Sol’-Iletsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Sol’-Iletsk is 314th warmest (5.7 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,289 hours of sunshine a year) and 961st wettest (340 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orenburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Sol’-Iletsk is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 34.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 500 km from the sea.

Why Sol’-Iletsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sol’-Iletsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.162°N, 54.992°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-328-2349), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
