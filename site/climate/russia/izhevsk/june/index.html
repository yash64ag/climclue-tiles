Izhevsk weather in June

Average June weather in Izhevsk, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Izhevsk averages 23.1 °C (74 °F) days and 11.6 °C (53 °F) nights, with 61 mm (2.4 in) of rain over the month and about 295 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Izhevsk

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Izhevsk. Compared with May: 5.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 1.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Izhevsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in June?
In June, Izhevsk averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Izhevsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Izhevsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in June?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (65 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in June?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
