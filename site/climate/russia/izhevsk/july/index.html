How July compares in Izhevsk

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. August is 2.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Izhevsk in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in July?
In July, Izhevsk averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Izhevsk?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Izhevsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in July?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (65 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in July?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
