How March compares in Izhevsk

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with February: 6.8 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 63 hours more sunshine. April is 8.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Izhevsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in March?
In March, Izhevsk averages a day high of -0.5 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -9.0 °C (16 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Izhevsk?
March is not one of Izhevsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in March?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (65 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in March?
Yes, on average: about 20 cm (7.9 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 24 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in March?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
