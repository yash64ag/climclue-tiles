How November compares in Izhevsk

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with October: 8.6 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Izhevsk in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in November?
In November, Izhevsk averages a day high of -2.5 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -7.7 °C (18 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 38 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Izhevsk?
November is not one of Izhevsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in November?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (65 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in November?
Yes, on average: about 31 cm (12.2 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in November?
About 38 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
