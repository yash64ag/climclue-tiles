How September compares in Izhevsk

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with August: 5.9 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 102 hours less sunshine. October is 6.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Izhevsk in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in September?
In September, Izhevsk averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Izhevsk?
September is not one of Izhevsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in September?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in September?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
