How October compares in Izhevsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with September: 6.9 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. November is 8.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Izhevsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in October?
In October, Izhevsk averages a day high of 6.2 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Izhevsk?
October is not one of Izhevsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in October?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.8 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in October?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
