How August compares in Izhevsk

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with July: 3.0 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. September is 5.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Izhevsk in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in August?
In August, Izhevsk averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Izhevsk?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Izhevsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in August?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in August?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
