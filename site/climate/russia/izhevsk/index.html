Izhevsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Izhevsk is 115th warmest (3.3 °C year average), 95th sunniest (about 1,848 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (521 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Neftekamsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (107 km away), Izhevsk is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 31.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,097 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Izhevsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,097 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
