How January compares in Izhevsk

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Izhevsk. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Izhevsk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Izhevsk in January?
In January, Izhevsk averages a day high of -9.2 °C (15 °F) and a night low of -15.1 °C (5 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 43 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Izhevsk?
January is not one of Izhevsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Izhevsk in January?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest February (22 mm).
Does it snow in Izhevsk in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 51 cm (20.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 54 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Izhevsk get in January?
About 43 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Izhevsk in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
