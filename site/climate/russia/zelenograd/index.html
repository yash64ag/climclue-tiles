Zelenograd at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Zelenograd is 83rd warmest (5.0 °C year average), 155th sunniest (about 1,695 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (725 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khimki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Zelenograd is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 606 km from the sea (deep inland).
