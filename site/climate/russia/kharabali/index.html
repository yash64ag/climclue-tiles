Kharabali at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kharabali is the 4th driest (253 mm a year). Compared with Akhtubinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (127 km away), Kharabali is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in February, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 30.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 201 km from the sea.

Why Kharabali has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 201 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kharabali itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.408°N, 47.254°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-365-2272), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
