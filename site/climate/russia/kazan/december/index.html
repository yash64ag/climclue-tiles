How December compares in Kazan

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kazan. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Kazan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in December?
In December, Kazan averages a day high of -6.3 °C (21 °F) and a night low of -11.4 °C (11 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 32 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kazan?
December is not one of Kazan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in December?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (65 mm), the driest April (30 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 47 cm (18.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 22 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in December?
About 32 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
