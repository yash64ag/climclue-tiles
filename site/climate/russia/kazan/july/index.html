How July compares in Kazan

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. August is 2.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Kazan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in July?
In July, Kazan averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kazan?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Kazan’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in July?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (64 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in July?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
