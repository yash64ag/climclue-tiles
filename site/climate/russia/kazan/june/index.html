Kazan weather in June

Average June weather in Kazan, Russia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Kazan averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) days and 12.9 °C (55 °F) nights, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of rain over the month and about 289 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Kazan

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kazan. Compared with May: 4.9 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Kazan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in June?
In June, Kazan averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kazan?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Kazan’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in June?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (64 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in June?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
