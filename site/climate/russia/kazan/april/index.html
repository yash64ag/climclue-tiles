How April compares in Kazan

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with March: 9.4 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. May is 8.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Kazan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in April?
In April, Kazan averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kazan?
April is not one of Kazan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in April?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (64 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.5 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in April?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
