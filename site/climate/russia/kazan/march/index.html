How March compares in Kazan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with February: 6.2 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. April is 9.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Kazan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in March?
In March, Kazan averages a day high of -1.8 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -7.6 °C (18 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kazan?
March is not one of Kazan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in March?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (65 mm), the driest April (30 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 31 cm (12.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 50 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in March?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
