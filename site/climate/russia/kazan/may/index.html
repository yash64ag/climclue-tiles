How May compares in Kazan

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with April: 8.3 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 75 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Kazan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in May?
In May, Kazan averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kazan?
May is not one of Kazan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in May?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (65 mm), the driest April (30 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
