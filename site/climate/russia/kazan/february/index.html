How February compares in Kazan

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. March is 6.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Kazan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in February?
In February, Kazan averages a day high of -6.6 °C (20 °F) and a night low of -13.3 °C (8 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kazan?
February is not one of Kazan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in February?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (64 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in February?
Yes, on average: about 30 cm (11.8 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 23 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in February?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
