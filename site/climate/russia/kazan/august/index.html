How August compares in Kazan

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with July: 2.6 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. September is 5.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Kazan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in August?
In August, Kazan averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kazan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kazan’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in August?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (64 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in August?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
