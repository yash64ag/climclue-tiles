Kazan at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kazan is 101st warmest (4.4 °C year average), 85th sunniest (about 1,885 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (544 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novocheboksarsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Kazan is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 30.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 978 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 97% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kazan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 978 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
