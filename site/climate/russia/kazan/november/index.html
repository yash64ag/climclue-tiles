How November compares in Kazan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kazan. Compared with October: 8.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Kazan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kazan in November?
In November, Kazan averages a day high of -1.7 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -6.0 °C (21 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 39 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kazan?
November is not one of Kazan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kazan in November?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (65 mm), the driest April (30 mm).
Does it snow in Kazan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kazan get in November?
About 39 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kazan in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
