Kavalerovo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kavalerovo is 656th warmest (3.3 °C year average), 159th sunniest (about 2,127 hours of sunshine a year) and 741st wettest (525 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dalnegorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Kavalerovo is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 157 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.7 °C), averages 37.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Why Kavalerovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kavalerovo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.272°N, 135.051°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-397-3150), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
