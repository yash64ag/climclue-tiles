How May compares in Omsk

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Omsk. Compared with April: 8.8 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Omsk in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in May?
In May, Omsk averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Omsk?
May is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in May?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest March (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in May?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
