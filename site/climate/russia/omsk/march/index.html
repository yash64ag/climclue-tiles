How March compares in Omsk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Omsk. Compared with February: 7.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. April is 11.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Omsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in March?
In March, Omsk averages a day high of -3.1 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -11.5 °C (11 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Omsk?
March is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 40 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in March?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
