How October compares in Omsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Omsk. Compared with September: 7.0 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. November is 11.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Omsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in October?
In October, Omsk averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Omsk?
October is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in October?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest March (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in October?
Yes, on average: about 9.5 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in October?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
