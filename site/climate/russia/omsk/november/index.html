How November compares in Omsk

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Omsk. Compared with October: 11.0 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. December is 6.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Omsk in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in November?
In November, Omsk averages a day high of -3.7 °C (25 °F) and a night low of -10.7 °C (13 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Omsk?
November is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in November?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest March (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in November?
Yes, on average: about 25 cm (9.7 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in November?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
