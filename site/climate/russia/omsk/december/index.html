How December compares in Omsk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Omsk. Compared with November: 6.5 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Omsk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in December?
In December, Omsk averages a day high of -9.8 °C (14 °F) and a night low of -18.3 °C (-1 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Omsk?
December is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in December?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest March (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in December?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
