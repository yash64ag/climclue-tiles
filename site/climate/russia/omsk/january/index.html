How January compares in Omsk

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Omsk. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Omsk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in January?
In January, Omsk averages a day high of -12.0 °C (10 °F) and a night low of -20.6 °C (-5 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Omsk?
January is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest March (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in January?
Yes, on average: about 21 cm (8.2 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 17 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in January?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
