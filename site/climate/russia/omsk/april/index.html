How April compares in Omsk

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Omsk. Compared with March: 11.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. May is 8.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Omsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omsk in April?
In April, Omsk averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -1.0 °C (30 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Omsk?
April is not one of Omsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omsk in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (61 mm), the driest March (16 mm).
Does it snow in Omsk in April?
Yes, on average: about 7.8 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Omsk get in April?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
