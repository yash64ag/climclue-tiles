Bryansk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Bryansk is 37th warmest (6.2 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 1,738 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (708 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orël, the nearest covered town with different numbers (122 km away), Bryansk is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C warmer in February, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 737 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bryansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 737 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.
