Khot'kovo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Khot'kovo is 503rd warmest (4.8 °C year average), 847th sunniest (about 1,695 hours of sunshine a year) and 232nd wettest (698 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sergiyev Posad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Khot'kovo is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.5 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-8.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 615 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khot'kovo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.257°N, 37.995°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-277-2179), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
