Millerovo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Millerovo is 172nd warmest (8.4 °C year average), 283rd sunniest (about 2,038 hours of sunshine a year) and 722nd wettest (530 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kamensk-Shakhtinsky, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Millerovo is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in February, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 27.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 222 km from the sea.

Why Millerovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 222 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Millerovo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.925°N, 40.400°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-350-2204), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
