Ekazhevo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Ekazhevo is 134th warmest (9.8 °C year average), 522nd sunniest (about 1,857 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (823 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nazran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Ekazhevo is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.5 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 209 km from the sea.

Why Ekazhevo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 209 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ekazhevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.212°N, 44.823°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-407-2248), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
