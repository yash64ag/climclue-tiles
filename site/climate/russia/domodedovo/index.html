Domodedovo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Domodedovo is 345th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 770th sunniest (about 1,718 hours of sunshine a year) and 355th wettest (661 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vostryakovo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Domodedovo is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 673 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Domodedovo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-285-2177), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
