Tomsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tomsk in April?
In April, Tomsk averages a day high of 4.7 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tomsk?
April is not one of Tomsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tomsk in April?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (28 mm).
Does it snow in Tomsk in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (8.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 40 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tomsk get in April?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tomsk in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
