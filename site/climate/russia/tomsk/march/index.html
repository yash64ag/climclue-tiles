How March compares in Tomsk

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tomsk. Compared with February: 7.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 66 hours more sunshine. April is 7.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Tomsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tomsk in March?
In March, Tomsk averages a day high of -3.4 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -11.4 °C (11 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tomsk?
March is not one of Tomsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tomsk in March?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (92 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Tomsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 41 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tomsk get in March?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tomsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
