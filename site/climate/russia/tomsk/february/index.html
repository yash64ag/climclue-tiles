How February compares in Tomsk

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tomsk. Compared with January: 2.6 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. March is 7.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Tomsk in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tomsk in February?
In February, Tomsk averages a day high of -11.5 °C (11 °F) and a night low of -18.2 °C (-1 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tomsk?
February is not one of Tomsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tomsk in February?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (28 mm).
Does it snow in Tomsk in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 29 cm (11.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 73 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tomsk get in February?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tomsk in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
