Tomsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Tomsk is 152nd warmest (1.0 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 1,984 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (683 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Seversk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Tomsk is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 36.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,276 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 120% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tomsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
