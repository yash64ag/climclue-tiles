Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Rayevskiy is 558th warmest (4.4 °C year average), 299th sunniest (about 2,015 hours of sunshine a year) and 874th wettest (449 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 813 km from the sea (deep inland).

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
