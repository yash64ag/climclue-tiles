Zelenokumsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Zelenokumsk is 63rd warmest (11.4 °C year average), 438th sunniest (about 1,915 hours of sunshine a year) and 837th wettest (474 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Georgiyevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Zelenokumsk is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 79 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 238 km from the sea.

Why Zelenokumsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zelenokumsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.410°N, 43.880°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-395-2238), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
