Kirsanov at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kirsanov is 279th warmest (5.9 °C year average), 384th sunniest (about 1,949 hours of sunshine a year) and 647th wettest (553 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rasskazovo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Kirsanov is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 28.9 °C more than its coolest, February (-8.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 663 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kirsanov has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kirsanov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.651°N, 42.735°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-313-2227), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
