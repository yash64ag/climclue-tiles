Berezniki at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Berezniki is 142nd warmest (1.6 °C year average), 126th sunniest (about 1,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (859 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Solikamsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Berezniki is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 32.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 960 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Berezniki has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
