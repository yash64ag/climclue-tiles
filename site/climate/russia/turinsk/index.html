Turinsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Turinsk, Russia (Sverdlovsk Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Turinsk is warmest in July, with 24.0 °C (75 °F) days and 13.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -10.7 °C (13 °F) days and -19.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (76 mm); February is driest (23 mm).

Turinsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Turinsk is 774th warmest (2.0 °C year average), 363rd sunniest (about 1,965 hours of sunshine a year) and 662nd wettest (550 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Irbit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Turinsk is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.7 °C), averages 33.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 990 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Turinsk has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Turinsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (58.046°N, 63.696°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-259-2436), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
