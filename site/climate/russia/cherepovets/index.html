Cherepovets at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Cherepovets is 109th warmest (3.9 °C year average), 161st sunniest (about 1,665 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (703 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vologda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Cherepovets is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 448 km from the sea.

Why Cherepovets has this climate
