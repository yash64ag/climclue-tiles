Neftekamsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Neftekamsk, Russia (Bashkortostan Republic), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Neftekamsk is warmest in July, with 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 14.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.1 °C (17 °F) days and -16.0 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 23 mm in March to 64 mm in August.

Neftekamsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Neftekamsk is 619th warmest (3.8 °C year average), 481st sunniest (about 1,883 hours of sunshine a year) and 712th wettest (533 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Agidel’, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Neftekamsk is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.0 °C), averages 32.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,022 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Neftekamsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.089°N, 54.264°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-279-2342), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
