Noginsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Noginsk is 70th warmest (5.3 °C year average), 136th sunniest (about 1,719 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (702 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Elektrostal’, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Noginsk is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 669 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Noginsk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Noginsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
