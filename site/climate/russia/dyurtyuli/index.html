Dyurtyuli weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dyurtyuli, Russia (Bashkortostan Republic), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dyurtyuli is warmest in July, with 25.9 °C (79 °F) days and 14.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -7.6 °C (18 °F) days and -16.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around June (61 mm); March is driest (20 mm).

Dyurtyuli at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Dyurtyuli is 592nd warmest (4.1 °C year average), 440th sunniest (about 1,913 hours of sunshine a year) and 795th wettest (498 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Birsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Dyurtyuli is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 128 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 32.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 965 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dyurtyuli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dyurtyuli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.487°N, 54.862°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-285-2348), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
