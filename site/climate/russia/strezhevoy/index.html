Strezhevoy weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Strezhevoy, Russia (Tomsk Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Strezhevoy is warmest in July, with 23.6 °C (74 °F) days and 12.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -15.2 °C (5 °F) days and -24.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (90 mm); February is driest (23 mm).

Strezhevoy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Strezhevoy is 932nd warmest (-1.5 °C year average), 590th sunniest (about 1,811 hours of sunshine a year) and 542nd wettest (598 mm a year), near the middle on each. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Strezhevoy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (60.733°N, 77.589°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-232-2575), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
