Klintsy at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Klintsy is 231st warmest (6.7 °C year average), 691st sunniest (about 1,754 hours of sunshine a year) and 321st wettest (671 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Unecha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Klintsy is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 692 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Klintsy has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Klintsy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.760°N, 32.239°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-312-2122), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
