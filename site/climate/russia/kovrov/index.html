Kovrov at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kovrov is 89th warmest (4.8 °C year average), 121st sunniest (about 1,766 hours of sunshine a year) and 73rd wettest (639 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vladimir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Kovrov is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 768 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kovrov, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kovrov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
