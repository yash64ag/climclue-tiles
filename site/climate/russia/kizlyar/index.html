Kizlyar at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kizlyar is 11th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 392nd sunniest (about 1,941 hours of sunshine a year) and 965th wettest (331 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khasavyurt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Kizlyar is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 153 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.5 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 58 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kizlyar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kizlyar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 58 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kizlyar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.847°N, 46.710°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-401-2267), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
