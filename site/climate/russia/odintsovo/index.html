Odintsovo at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Odintsovo is 72nd warmest (5.2 °C year average), 146th sunniest (about 1,703 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (714 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novo-Peredelkino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Odintsovo is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in February, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 636 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Odintsovo has this climate
