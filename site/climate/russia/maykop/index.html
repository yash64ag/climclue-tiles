Maykop at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Maykop is 8th warmest (11.2 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 2,178 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (843 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Armavir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (91 km away), Maykop is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 194 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 114 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maykop, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maykop has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 114 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
