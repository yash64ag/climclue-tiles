Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Yushkovo is 409th warmest (5.3 °C year average), 812th sunniest (about 1,705 hours of sunshine a year) and 246th wettest (693 mm a year), near the middle on each. Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
