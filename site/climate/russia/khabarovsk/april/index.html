How April compares in Khabarovsk

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Khabarovsk. Compared with March: 11.6 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. May is 7.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Khabarovsk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khabarovsk in April?
In April, Khabarovsk averages a day high of 10.2 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Khabarovsk?
April is not one of Khabarovsk’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khabarovsk in April?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (166 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Khabarovsk in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.2 cm (3.6 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Khabarovsk get in April?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khabarovsk in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
