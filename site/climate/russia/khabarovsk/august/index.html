How August compares in Khabarovsk

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Khabarovsk. Compared with July: 1.6 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. September is 6.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Khabarovsk in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khabarovsk in August?
In August, Khabarovsk averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Khabarovsk?
August is not one of Khabarovsk’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khabarovsk in August?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (162 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Khabarovsk in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khabarovsk get in August?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khabarovsk in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
