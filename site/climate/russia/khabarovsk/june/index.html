How June compares in Khabarovsk

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Khabarovsk. Compared with May: 6.0 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 3.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Khabarovsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khabarovsk in June?
In June, Khabarovsk averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Khabarovsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Khabarovsk’s mild months (June), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khabarovsk in June?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (162 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Khabarovsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khabarovsk get in June?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khabarovsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
