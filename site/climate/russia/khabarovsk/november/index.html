How November compares in Khabarovsk

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Khabarovsk. Compared with October: 12.5 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 10.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Khabarovsk in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khabarovsk in November?
In November, Khabarovsk averages a day high of -4.3 °C (24 °F) and a night low of -12.1 °C (10 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Khabarovsk?
November is not one of Khabarovsk’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khabarovsk in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (162 mm), the driest February (11 mm).
Does it snow in Khabarovsk in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Khabarovsk get in November?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khabarovsk in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
