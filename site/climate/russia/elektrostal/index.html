Why Elektrostal’ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 677 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

What is the coldest month in Elektrostal’?

What is the sunniest month in Elektrostal’?

Does it snow in Elektrostal’?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 196 cm (77.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Elektrostal’?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.3 × 11 km, that contains Elektrostal’’s GeoNames location (55.786°N, 38.457°E; ClimaClue area sq-282-2184), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 144,387 (GeoNames).
