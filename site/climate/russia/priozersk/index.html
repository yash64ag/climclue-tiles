Priozërsk at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Priozërsk is 550th warmest (4.5 °C year average), 931st sunniest (about 1,621 hours of sunshine a year) and 240th wettest (695 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Svetogorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Priozërsk is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in February, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-7.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Why Priozërsk has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Priozërsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (61.040°N, 30.139°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-229-2101), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
