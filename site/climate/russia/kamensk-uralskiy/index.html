Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Kamensk-Ural’skiy is 123rd warmest (2.9 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 1,996 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (519 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 1,190 km from the sea (deep inland).
