Tobolsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Tobolsk is 148th warmest (1.3 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 1,973 hours of sunshine a year) and 139th wettest (482 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tyumen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (200 km away), Tobolsk is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 35.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 927 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tobolsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 927 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.0 °C.
