Petrozavodsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Petrozavodsk is 117th warmest (3.1 °C year average), 163rd sunniest (about 1,648 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (757 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saint Petersburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (300 km away), Petrozavodsk is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 3.4 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 275 km from the sea.

Why Petrozavodsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 275 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
