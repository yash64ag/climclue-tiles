Kolpashevo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kolpashevo, Russia (Tomsk Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kolpashevo is warmest in July, with 24.7 °C (76 °F) days and 12.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -14.5 °C (6 °F) days and -23.5 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (82 mm); March is driest (19 mm).

Kolpashevo at a glance

Among the 981 towns ClimaClue covers in Russia, Kolpashevo is 905th warmest (-0.2 °C year average), 393rd sunniest (about 1,941 hours of sunshine a year) and 701st wettest (537 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Seversk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (225 km away), Kolpashevo is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 142 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 37.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-19.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,044 km from the sea (deep inland).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kolpashevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (58.320°N, 82.903°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-256-2629), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
