Novocheboksarsk at a glance

Among the 171 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Russia, Novocheboksarsk is 94th warmest (4.6 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 1,840 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (533 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cheboksary, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Novocheboksarsk is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 30.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,018 km from the sea (deep inland).
