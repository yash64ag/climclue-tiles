Al Khums at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Al Khums is 16th warmest (20.1 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,218 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (305 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zliten, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Al Khums is 1.1 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 101 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Khums, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Khums has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
