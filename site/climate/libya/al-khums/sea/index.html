Al Khums sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Al Marqab · Libya

The sea off Al Khums averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.1 °C (61 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Al Khums sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Al Khums?
In August, at about 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Al Khums?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 16.1 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Al Khums in March?
About 16.1 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Al Khums?
About 21.1 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
