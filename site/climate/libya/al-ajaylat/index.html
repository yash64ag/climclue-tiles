Al Ajaylat at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Al Ajaylat is 4th warmest (21.0 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 3,245 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (237 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Jumayl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Al Ajaylat is 0.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Ajaylat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Ajaylat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
