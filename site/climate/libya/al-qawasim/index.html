Al Qawāsim at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Libya, Al Qawāsim is 49th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 3,246 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (298 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gharyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Al Qawāsim is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Qawāsim, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Qawāsim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Al Qawāsim itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.184°N, 13.060°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-518-1930), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
