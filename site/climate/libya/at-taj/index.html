At Tāj at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Libya, At Tāj is the 3rd warmest (23.3 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,722 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (2 mm a year). Compared with Al Jawf, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), At Tāj is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.5 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 785 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why At Tāj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 785 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-597-2032), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
