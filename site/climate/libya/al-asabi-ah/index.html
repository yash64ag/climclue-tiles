Al Aşābi‘ah at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Libya, Al Aşābi‘ah is the 4th coolest (17.6 °C year average). Compared with Gharyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Al Aşābi‘ah is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.5 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Aşābi‘ah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Aşābi‘ah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Al Aşābi‘ah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.033°N, 12.867°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-519-1928), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
