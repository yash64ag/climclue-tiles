Tripoli weather in October

Average October weather in Tripoli, Libya: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Tripoli averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) days and 18.2 °C (65 °F) nights, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 245 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Tripoli

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tripoli. Compared with September: 2.8 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. November is 4.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Tripoli in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tripoli in October?
In October, Tripoli averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tripoli?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Tripoli’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tripoli in October?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (72 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tripoli in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tripoli get in October?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tripoli in October?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tripoli in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
