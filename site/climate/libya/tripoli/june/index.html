How June compares in Tripoli

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tripoli. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. July is 1.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Is June a good time to visit Tripoli?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tripoli’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
