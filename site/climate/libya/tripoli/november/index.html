How November compares in Tripoli

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tripoli. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. December is 3.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Tripoli in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tripoli in November?
In November, Tripoli averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tripoli?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Tripoli’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tripoli in November?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (72 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tripoli in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tripoli get in November?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tripoli in November?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tripoli in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
