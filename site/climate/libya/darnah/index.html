Darnah at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Darnah is the 2nd coolest (19.4 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 3,089 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Al Bayḑā’, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Darnah is 3.4 °C warmer in August and 3.3 °C warmer in January, with 326 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Darnah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Darnah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
