Zliten at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Zliten is 9th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,235 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (203 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Khums, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Zliten is 1.1 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zliten, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zliten has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
