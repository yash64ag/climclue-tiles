Misratah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Libya

The sea off Misratah averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.2 °C (61 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Misratah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Misratah?
In August, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Misratah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 16.2 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Misratah in March?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Misratah?
About 21.3 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
