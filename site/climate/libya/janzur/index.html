Janzūr at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Janzūr is 11th warmest (20.5 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,227 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (293 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tripoli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Janzūr is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Janzūr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Janzūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
