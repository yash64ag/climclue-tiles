Al Bayḑā’ at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Al Bayḑā’ is the coolest (16.3 °C year average), the least sunny (about 3,086 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (636 mm a year). Compared with Darnah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Al Bayḑā’ is 3.4 °C cooler in August and 3.3 °C cooler in January, with 326 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.8 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in February. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Bayḑā’, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Bayḑā’ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
