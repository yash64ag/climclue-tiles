Sīdī Bin Zīnah at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Libya, Sīdī Bin Zīnah is 15th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 3,246 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (188 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Jumayl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sīdī Bin Zīnah is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sīdī Bin Zīnah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sīdī Bin Zīnah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
