Az Zuwaytīnah at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Libya, Az Zuwaytīnah is 12th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 3,314 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (223 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ajdabiya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Az Zuwaytīnah is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Az Zuwaytīnah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
