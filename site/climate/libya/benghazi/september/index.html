How September compares in Benghazi

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Benghazi. Compared with August: 1.2 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 69 hours less sunshine. October is 2.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Benghazi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in September?
In September, Benghazi averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Benghazi?
September is not one of Benghazi’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (90 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Benghazi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benghazi get in September?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benghazi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
