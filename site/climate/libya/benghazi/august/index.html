How August compares in Benghazi

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Benghazi. Compared with July: 0.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. September is 1.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Benghazi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in August?
In August, Benghazi averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 359 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Benghazi?
August is not one of Benghazi’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in August?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (90 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Benghazi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benghazi get in August?
About 359 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benghazi in August?
