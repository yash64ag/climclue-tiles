How May compares in Benghazi

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Benghazi. Compared with April: 3.0 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. June is 2.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Benghazi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in May?
In May, Benghazi averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 315 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Benghazi?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Benghazi’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in May?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Benghazi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benghazi get in May?
About 315 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benghazi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
