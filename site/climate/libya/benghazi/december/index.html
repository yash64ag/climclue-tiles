How December compares in Benghazi

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Benghazi. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Benghazi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in December?
In December, Benghazi averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Benghazi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Benghazi’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in December?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (90 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Benghazi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benghazi get in December?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benghazi in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
