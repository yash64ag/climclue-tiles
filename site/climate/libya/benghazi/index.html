Benghazi at a glance

Among the 19 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Libya, Benghazi is the 2nd wettest (341 mm a year). Compared with Ajdabiya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (152 km away), Benghazi is 1.6 °C cooler in August and about as warm in February, with 193 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, February (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Benghazi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Benghazi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
