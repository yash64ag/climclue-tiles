How November compares in Benghazi

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Benghazi. Compared with October: 3.9 °C cooler, 33 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Benghazi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in November?
In November, Benghazi averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Benghazi?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Benghazi’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in November?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Benghazi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benghazi get in November?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benghazi in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
