How January compares in Benghazi

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Benghazi. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Benghazi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in January?
In January, Benghazi averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Benghazi?
January is not one of Benghazi’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in January?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (90 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Benghazi in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benghazi get in January?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benghazi in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
