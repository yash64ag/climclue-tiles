Benghazi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benghazi in June?
In June, Benghazi averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 346 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Benghazi?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Benghazi’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benghazi in June?

Does it snow in Benghazi in June?
