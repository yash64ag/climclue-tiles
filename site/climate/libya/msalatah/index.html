Msalātah at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Libya, Msalātah is 50th warmest (19.1 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 3,212 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (299 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al-'Alūaṣ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Msalātah is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.5 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Msalātah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Msalātah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Msalātah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.583°N, 14.036°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-514-1940), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
