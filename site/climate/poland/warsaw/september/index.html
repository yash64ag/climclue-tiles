How September compares in Warsaw

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Warsaw. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 74 hours less sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Warsaw in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warsaw in September?
In September, Warsaw averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Warsaw?
September is not one of Warsaw’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warsaw in September?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (76 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Warsaw in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warsaw get in September?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warsaw in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
