How April compares in Warsaw

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Warsaw. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Warsaw in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warsaw in April?
In April, Warsaw averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Warsaw?
April is not one of Warsaw’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warsaw in April?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (76 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Warsaw in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Warsaw get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warsaw in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
