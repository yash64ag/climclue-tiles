Warsaw weather in May

Average May weather in Warsaw, Poland: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Warsaw averages 19.6 °C (67 °F) days and 8.7 °C (48 °F) nights, with 57 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 219 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Warsaw

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Warsaw. Compared with April: 5.6 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 63 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Warsaw in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warsaw in May?
In May, Warsaw averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Warsaw?
May is not one of Warsaw’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warsaw in May?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (79 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Warsaw in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warsaw get in May?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warsaw in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
