How October compares in Warsaw

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Warsaw. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Warsaw in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warsaw in October?
In October, Warsaw averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Warsaw?
October is not one of Warsaw’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warsaw in October?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (76 mm), the driest February (29 mm).
Does it snow in Warsaw in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Warsaw get in October?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warsaw in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
