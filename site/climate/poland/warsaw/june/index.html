How June compares in Warsaw

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Warsaw. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Warsaw in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warsaw in June?
In June, Warsaw averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Warsaw?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Warsaw’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warsaw in June?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (79 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Warsaw in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warsaw get in June?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warsaw in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
