Kalisz at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Kalisz is the 4th least sunny (about 1,495 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (544 mm a year). Compared with Łódź, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Kalisz is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 304 km from the sea.

Why Kalisz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 304 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
