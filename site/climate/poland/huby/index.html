Huby at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Huby is 24th warmest (9.4 °C year average), 319th sunniest (about 1,479 hours of sunshine a year) and 233rd wettest (578 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gaj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Huby is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Why Huby has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 348 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huby itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.089°N, 17.041°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-329-1970), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
