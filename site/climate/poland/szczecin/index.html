Szczecin at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Szczecin is 15th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 1,578 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (591 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gorzów Wielkopolski, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Szczecin is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Szczecin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
