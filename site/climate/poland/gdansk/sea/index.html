Gdańsk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pomerania · Poland

The sea off Gdańsk averages 19.0 °C (66 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.8 °C (35 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Gdańsk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gdańsk?
In August, at about 19.0 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gdańsk?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 1.8 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gdańsk in February?
About 1.8 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gdańsk?
About 9.6 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
