Mińsk Mazowiecki at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Mińsk Mazowiecki is 283rd warmest (8.2 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 1,595 hours of sunshine a year) and 162nd wettest (613 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sulejówek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Mińsk Mazowiecki is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.7 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 291 km from the sea.

Why Mińsk Mazowiecki has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mińsk Mazowiecki itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.179°N, 21.573°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-318-2015), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
