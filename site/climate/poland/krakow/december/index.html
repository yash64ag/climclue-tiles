How December compares in Kraków

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kraków. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Kraków in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in December?
In December, Kraków averages a day high of 2.4 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -3.4 °C (26 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 35 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kraków?
December is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in December?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in December?
Yes, on average: about 21 cm (8.2 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in December?
About 35 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
