How September compares in Kraków

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. October is 4.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Kraków in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in September?
In September, Kraków averages a day high of 18.3 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kraków?
September is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in September?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in September?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
