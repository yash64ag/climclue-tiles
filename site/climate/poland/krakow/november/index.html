How November compares in Kraków

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 4.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Kraków in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in November?
In November, Kraków averages a day high of 6.6 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 50 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kraków?
November is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in November?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in November?
About 50 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
