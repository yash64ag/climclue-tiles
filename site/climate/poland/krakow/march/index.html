How March compares in Kraków

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 5.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Kraków in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in March?
In March, Kraków averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kraków?
March is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in March?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
