How August compares in Kraków

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 4.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Kraków in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in August?
In August, Kraków averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kraków?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kraków’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in August?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in August?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
