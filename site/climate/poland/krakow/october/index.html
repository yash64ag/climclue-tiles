How October compares in Kraków

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with September: 4.8 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Kraków in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in October?
In October, Kraków averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.4 °C (42 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kraków?
October is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in October?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in October?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
