How April compares in Kraków

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with March: 5.4 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Kraków in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in April?
In April, Kraków averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kraków?
April is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in April?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in April?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
