How July compares in Kraków

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kraków. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Kraków in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in July?
In July, Kraków averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kraków?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Kraków’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in July?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in July?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
