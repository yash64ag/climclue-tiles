How January compares in Kraków

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Kraków ranks 30th warmest (average temperature), 23rd sunniest and 20th wettest in January.

Kraków in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in January?
In January, Kraków averages a day high of 0.8 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -5.4 °C (22 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kraków?
January is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in January?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 10 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in January?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
