How February compares in Kraków

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kraków. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. March is 4.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Kraków in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kraków in February?
In February, Kraków averages a day high of 3.0 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -4.1 °C (25 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kraków?
February is not one of Kraków’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kraków in February?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Kraków in February?
Yes, on average: about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kraków get in February?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kraków in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
