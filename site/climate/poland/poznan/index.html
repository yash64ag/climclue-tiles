Poznań at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Poznań is 10th warmest (9.0 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 1,506 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (545 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kalisz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (107 km away), Poznań is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 218 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Poznań, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Poznań

For mild weather, the best months to visit Poznań are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

Why Poznań has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 218 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
