How April compares in Poznań

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Poznań. Compared with March: 5.0 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Poznań in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Poznań in April?
In April, Poznań averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Poznań?
April is not one of Poznań’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Poznań in April?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest February (28 mm).
Does it snow in Poznań in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Poznań get in April?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Poznań in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
