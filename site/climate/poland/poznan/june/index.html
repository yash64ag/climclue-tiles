How June compares in Poznań

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Poznań. Compared with May: 2.8 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Poznań in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Poznań in June?
In June, Poznań averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Poznań?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Poznań’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Poznań in June?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (81 mm), the driest February (28 mm).
Does it snow in Poznań in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Poznań get in June?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Poznań in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
