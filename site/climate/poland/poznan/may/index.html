Poznań weather in May

Average May weather in Poznań, Poland: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Poznań averages 19.6 °C (67 °F) days and 8.4 °C (47 °F) nights, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of rain over the month and about 216 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Poznań

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Poznań. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 66 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Poznań in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Poznań in May?
In May, Poznań averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Poznań?
May is not one of Poznań’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Poznań in May?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (80 mm), the driest February (28 mm).
Does it snow in Poznań in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Poznań get in May?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Poznań in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
