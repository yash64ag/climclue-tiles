How March compares in Poznań

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Poznań. Compared with February: 3.6 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 5.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Poznań in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Poznań in March?
In March, Poznań averages a day high of 7.4 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 0.0 °C (32 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Poznań?
March is not one of Poznań’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Poznań in March?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (81 mm), the driest February (28 mm).
Does it snow in Poznań in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.7 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Poznań get in March?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Poznań in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
