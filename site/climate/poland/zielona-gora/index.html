Zielona Góra at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Zielona Góra is the 4th warmest (9.2 °C year average). Compared with Gorzów Wielkopolski, the nearest covered town with different numbers (91 km away), Zielona Góra is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 56 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 213 km from the sea.

Why Zielona Góra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
