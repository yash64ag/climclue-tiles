Sokółka at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Sokółka is 338th warmest (7.3 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 1,640 hours of sunshine a year) and 170th wettest (611 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Białystok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Sokółka is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 237 km from the sea.

Why Sokółka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sokółka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.407°N, 23.502°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-305-2035), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
