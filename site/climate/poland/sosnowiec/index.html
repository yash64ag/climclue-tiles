Sosnowiec at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Sosnowiec is 17th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 1,535 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (747 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Katowice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Sosnowiec is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 467 km from the sea.
