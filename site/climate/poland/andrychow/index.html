Andrychów at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Andrychów is 254th warmest (8.4 °C year average), 301st sunniest (about 1,491 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (895 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kęty, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Andrychów is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Why Andrychów has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 511 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Andrychów itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.855°N, 19.338°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-341-1993), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
