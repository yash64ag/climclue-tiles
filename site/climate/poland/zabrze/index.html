Zabrze at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Zabrze is 14th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 1,540 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (740 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gliwice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Zabrze is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 456 km from the sea.

Why Zabrze has this climate
