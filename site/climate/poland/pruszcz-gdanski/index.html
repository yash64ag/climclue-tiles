Pruszcz Gdański at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Pruszcz Gdański is 256th warmest (8.3 °C year average), 158th sunniest (about 1,550 hours of sunshine a year) and 292nd wettest (557 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ujeścisko-Łostowice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Pruszcz Gdański is 0.3 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.2 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Pruszcz Gdański has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pruszcz Gdański itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.262°N, 18.636°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-297-1986), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
