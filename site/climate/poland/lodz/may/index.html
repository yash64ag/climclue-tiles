How May compares in Łódź

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Łódź in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in May?
In May, Łódź averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Łódź?
May is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in May?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (86 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in May?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
