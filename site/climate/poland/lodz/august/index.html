How August compares in Łódź

Łódź in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in August?
In August, Łódź averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Łódź?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Łódź’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in August?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in August?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
