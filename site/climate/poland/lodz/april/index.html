How April compares in Łódź

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Łódź in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in April?
In April, Łódź averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Łódź?
April is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (86 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in April?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
