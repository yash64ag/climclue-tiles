How July compares in Łódź

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Łódź. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Łódź in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in July?
In July, Łódź averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Łódź?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Łódź’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in July?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (86 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in July?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
