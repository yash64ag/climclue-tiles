How September compares in Łódź

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. October is 4.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Łódź in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in September?
In September, Łódź averages a day high of 18.0 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Łódź?
September is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in September?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in September?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
