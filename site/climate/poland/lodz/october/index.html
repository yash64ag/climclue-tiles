How October compares in Łódź

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with September: 4.7 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. November is 5.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Łódź in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in October?
In October, Łódź averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Łódź?
October is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in October?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
