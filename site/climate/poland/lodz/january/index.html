Łódź weather in January

Average January weather in Łódź, Poland: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Łódź averages 0.9 °C (34 °F) days and -4.6 °C (24 °F) nights, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 44 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Łódź

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Łódź in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in January?
In January, Łódź averages a day high of 0.9 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -4.6 °C (24 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Łódź?
January is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in January?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in January?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
