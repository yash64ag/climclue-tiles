How March compares in Łódź

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with February: 3.7 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 5.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Łódź in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in March?
In March, Łódź averages a day high of 6.6 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -1.1 °C (30 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Łódź?
March is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in March?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (86 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in March?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
