Łódź weather in November

Average November weather in Łódź, Poland: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Łódź averages 5.9 °C (43 °F) days and 0.6 °C (33 °F) nights, with 47 mm (1.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 46 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Łódź

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. December is 3.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Łódź in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in November?
In November, Łódź averages a day high of 5.9 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 46 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Łódź?
November is not one of Łódź’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in November?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.8 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in November?
About 46 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
