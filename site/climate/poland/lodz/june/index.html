How June compares in Łódź

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Łódź. Compared with May: 2.6 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Łódź in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Łódź in June?
In June, Łódź averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Łódź?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Łódź’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Łódź in June?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest February (33 mm).
Does it snow in Łódź in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Łódź get in June?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Łódź in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
