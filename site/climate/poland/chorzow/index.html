Chorzów at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Chorzów is 20th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 1,543 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (772 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Katowice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Chorzów is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 456 km from the sea.

Why Chorzów has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 456 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
