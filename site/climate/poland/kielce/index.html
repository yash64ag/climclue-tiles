Kielce at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Kielce is the 5th coolest (8.2 °C year average). Compared with Radom, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Kielce is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 408 km from the sea.

Why Kielce has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 408 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
