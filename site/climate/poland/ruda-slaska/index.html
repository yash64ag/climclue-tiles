Ruda Śląska at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Ruda Śląska is 13th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 1,550 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (758 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zabrze, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ruda Śląska is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 467 km from the sea.
