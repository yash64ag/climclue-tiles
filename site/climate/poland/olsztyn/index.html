Olsztyn at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Olsztyn is the 3rd coolest (7.7 °C year average). Compared with Elbląg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Olsztyn is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 69 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 102 km from the sea.

Best time to visit Olsztyn

For mild weather, the best months to visit Olsztyn are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (17.9 °C), August (17.4 °C) and June (15.6 °C).

Why Olsztyn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 102 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
