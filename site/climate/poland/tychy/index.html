Tychy at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Tychy is the 2nd wettest (818 mm a year). Compared with Katowice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Tychy is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.8 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 478 km from the sea.

Why Tychy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 478 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
