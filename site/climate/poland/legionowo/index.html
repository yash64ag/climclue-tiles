Legionowo at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Legionowo is 197th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 1,575 hours of sunshine a year) and 314th wettest (546 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Łomianki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Legionowo is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Legionowo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Legionowo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.401°N, 20.927°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-315-2009), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
