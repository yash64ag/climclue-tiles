Gorzów Wielkopolski at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Gorzów Wielkopolski is 6th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 1,564 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (589 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Szczecin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Gorzów Wielkopolski is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.
