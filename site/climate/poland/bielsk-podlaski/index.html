Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Bielsk Podlaski is 316th warmest (7.7 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 1,624 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (616 mm a year), near the middle on each. Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-312-2031), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
