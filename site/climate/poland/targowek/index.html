Targówek at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Targówek is 23rd warmest (8.6 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 1,587 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (560 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Białołeka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Targówek is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 267 km from the sea.

Why Targówek has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
