Busko-Zdrój at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Busko-Zdrój is 158th warmest (8.7 °C year average), 81st sunniest (about 1,590 hours of sunshine a year) and 153rd wettest (617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Staszów, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Busko-Zdrój is 0.4 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 453 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Busko-Zdrój itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.471°N, 20.719°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-335-2007), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
