Bielsko-Biala at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Bielsko-Biala is the wettest (924 mm a year). Compared with Tychy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Bielsko-Biala is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 106 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Why Bielsko-Biala has this climate
