Rzeszów at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Rzeszów is the sunniest (about 1,693 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Tarnów, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Rzeszów is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 518 km from the sea.

Why Rzeszów has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 518 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
