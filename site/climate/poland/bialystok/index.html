Białystok at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Białystok is the 2nd coolest (7.6 °C year average). Compared with Targówek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (170 km away), Białystok is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 246 km from the sea.

Why Białystok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 246 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
