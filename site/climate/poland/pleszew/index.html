Pleszew at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Pleszew is 91st warmest (8.9 °C year average), 302nd sunniest (about 1,490 hours of sunshine a year) and 316th wettest (546 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jarocin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Pleszew is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 297 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pleszew, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pleszew has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pleszew itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.896°N, 17.785°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-321-1977), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
