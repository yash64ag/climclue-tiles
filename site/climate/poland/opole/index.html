Opole at a glance

Among the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Opole is the 2nd warmest (9.4 °C year average). Compared with Gliwice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Opole is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 97 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 419 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Opole, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Opole has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 419 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
