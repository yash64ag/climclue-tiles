Augustów at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Augustów is 337th warmest (7.4 °C year average), 119th sunniest (about 1,574 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (573 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Suwałki, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Augustów is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 177 km from the sea.

Why Augustów has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Augustów itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.843°N, 22.980°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-301-2029), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
