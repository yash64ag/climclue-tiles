How January compares in Wrocław

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Wrocław. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Wrocław in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wrocław in January?
In January, Wrocław averages a day high of 2.7 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -3.6 °C (26 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 47 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Wrocław?
January is not one of Wrocław’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wrocław in January?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (27 mm).
Does it snow in Wrocław in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Wrocław get in January?
About 47 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wrocław in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
