How May compares in Wrocław

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Wrocław. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Wrocław in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wrocław in May?
In May, Wrocław averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Wrocław?
May is not one of Wrocław’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wrocław in May?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (27 mm).
Does it snow in Wrocław in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wrocław get in May?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wrocław in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
