How August compares in Wrocław

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Wrocław. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 4.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Wrocław in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wrocław in August?
In August, Wrocław averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Wrocław?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Wrocław’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wrocław in August?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest February (27 mm).
Does it snow in Wrocław in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wrocław get in August?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wrocław in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
