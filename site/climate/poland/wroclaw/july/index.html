Wrocław weather in July

Average July weather in Wrocław, Poland: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Wrocław averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) days and 13.6 °C (56 °F) nights, with 90 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 204 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Wrocław

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Wrocław. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Wrocław in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wrocław in July?
In July, Wrocław averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Wrocław?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Wrocław’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wrocław in July?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (90 mm), the driest February (27 mm).
Does it snow in Wrocław in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wrocław get in July?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wrocław in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
