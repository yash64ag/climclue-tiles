How December compares in Wrocław

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wrocław. Compared with November: 3.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Of the 47 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Poland, Wrocław ranks 6th warmest (average temperature), 18th sunniest and 47th wettest in December.

Wrocław in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wrocław in December?
In December, Wrocław averages a day high of 2.9 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 38 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wrocław?
December is not one of Wrocław’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wrocław in December?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest February (27 mm).
Does it snow in Wrocław in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Wrocław get in December?
About 38 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wrocław in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
