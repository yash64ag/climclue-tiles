Koszalin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · West Pomerania · Poland

The sea off Koszalin averages 18.4 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.7 °C (37 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Koszalin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Koszalin?
In August, at about 18.4 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Koszalin?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.7 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Koszalin in February?
About 2.7 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Koszalin?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Koszalin nearby?
In August: Sopot (19.0 °C, 157 km) and Pruszcz Gdański (19.0 °C, 160 km).
