Wągrowiec at a glance

Among the 345 towns ClimaClue covers in Poland, Wągrowiec is 143rd warmest (8.7 °C year average), 263rd sunniest (about 1,508 hours of sunshine a year) and 297th wettest (555 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chodzież, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Wągrowiec is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 182 km from the sea.

Why Wągrowiec has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Wągrowiec itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.808°N, 17.200°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-311-1972), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
