Molepolole at a glance

Among the 25 towns ClimaClue covers in Botswana, Molepolole is 19th warmest (20.1 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,193 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (465 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thamaga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Molepolole is 0.4 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.9 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, July (12.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 753 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Molepolole, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Molepolole has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 753 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Molepolole itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.407°S, 25.495°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1084-2054), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
