Palapye at a glance

Among the 25 towns ClimaClue covers in Botswana, Palapye is the 3rd least sunny (about 3,149 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (392 mm a year). Compared with Serowe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Palapye is 1.7 °C warmer in January and 0.9 °C warmer in July, with 90 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.2 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, July (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 679 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Palapye, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Palapye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 679 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Palapye itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.546°S, 27.125°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1065-2071), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
