Devínska Nová Ves at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Devínska Nová Ves is 18th warmest (10.5 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 1,778 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (585 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dúbravka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Devínska Nová Ves is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 382 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Devínska Nová Ves itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.207°N, 16.979°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-357-1969), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
