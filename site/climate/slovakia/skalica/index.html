Skalica weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Skalica, Slovakia (Trnava Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Skalica is warmest in July, with 26.6 °C (80 °F) days and 14.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 1.9 °C (35 °F) days and -3.7 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 31 mm in February to 72 mm in June.

Skalica at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Skalica is the 4th driest (568 mm a year). Compared with Senica, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Skalica is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.7 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 448 km from the sea.

Why Skalica has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 448 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Skalica itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.845°N, 17.226°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-351-1972), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
