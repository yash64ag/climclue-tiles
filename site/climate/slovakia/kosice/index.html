Košice weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Košice, Slovakia (Košice Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Košice is warmest in July, with 26.1 °C (79 °F) days and 14.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 0.5 °C (33 °F) days and -5.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (91 mm); January is driest (27 mm).

Košice at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Košice is the sunniest (about 2,192 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Prešov, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Košice is 0.5 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.2 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 646 km from the sea (deep inland).

Best time to visit Košice

For mild weather, the best months to visit Košice are May, June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July and August.

  • Driest (least precipitation): January (27 mm), February (30 mm) and March (33 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (280 h), June (267 h) and May (262 h).

Why Košice has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 646 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Košice itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.714°N, 21.258°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-352-2012), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 225,044, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
