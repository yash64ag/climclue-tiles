Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Liptovský Mikuláš is the 3rd coolest (7.2 °C year average), the 4th least sunny (about 1,539 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (876 mm a year). Why Liptovský Mikuláš has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 577 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.
