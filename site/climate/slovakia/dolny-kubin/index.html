Dolný Kubín at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Dolný Kubín is the 5th coolest (7.3 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,509 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (948 mm a year). Compared with Ružomberok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Dolný Kubín is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 120 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 578 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 20 km from Dolný Kubín by up to 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Dolný Kubín has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 578 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dolný Kubín itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.210°N, 19.303°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-347-1993), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
