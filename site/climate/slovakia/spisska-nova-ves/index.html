Spišská Nová Ves at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Spišská Nová Ves is 51st warmest (7.9 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 1,780 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (632 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Poprad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Spišská Nová Ves is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 199 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 616 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Spišská Nová Ves, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Spišská Nová Ves itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.945°N, 20.562°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-350-2005), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
