Stará Ľubovňa at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Stará Ľubovňa is the 4th coolest (7.2 °C year average). Compared with Kežmarok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Stará Ľubovňa is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 126 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 584 km from the sea.

Why Stará Ľubovňa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 584 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C. The 10 km area around Stará Ľubovňa, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 6.0 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Stará Ľubovňa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.299°N, 20.686°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-347-2006), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
