Bratislava at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Slovakia, Bratislava is the warmest (10.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,774 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (617 mm a year). Compared with Košice, the nearest covered town with different numbers (313 km away), Bratislava is 1.8 °C warmer in July and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 91 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 383 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bratislava, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bratislava has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 383 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
