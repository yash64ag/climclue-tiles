Bardejov at a glance

Among the 59 towns ClimaClue covers in Slovakia, Bardejov is the 5th sunniest (about 1,895 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Prešov, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Bardejov is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 76 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 591 km from the sea.

Why Bardejov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 591 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bardejov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.292°N, 21.273°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-347-2012), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
