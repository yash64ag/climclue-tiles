Capesterre-Belle-Eau at a glance

Among the 9 towns ClimaClue covers in Guadeloupe, Capesterre-Belle-Eau is the 3rd coolest (25.9 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,643 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (2,104 mm a year). Compared with Petit-Bourg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Capesterre-Belle-Eau is 0.1 °C cooler in September and about as warm in February, with 306 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (27.0 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, February (24.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.6 °C (69 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Capesterre-Belle-Eau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Capesterre-Belle-Eau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
