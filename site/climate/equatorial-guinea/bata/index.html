Bata at a glance

Compared with Malabo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (237 km away), Bata is 0.1 °C warmer in March and about as warm in August, with 51 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.8 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bata, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bata has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
