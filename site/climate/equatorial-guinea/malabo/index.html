Malabo at a glance

Compared with Bata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (237 km away), Malabo is 0.1 °C cooler in March and about as warm in August, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.6 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Malabo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Malabo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
