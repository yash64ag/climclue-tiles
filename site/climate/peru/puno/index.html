Why Puno has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. About 231 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Puno climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Puno?
November, with an average of 9.4 °C (49 °F), day highs around 14.7 °C (58 °F) and night lows around 3.1 °C (38 °F).
What is the coldest month in Puno?
June, with an average of 5.8 °C (42 °F), day highs around 12.9 °C (55 °F) and night lows around -2.1 °C (28 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Puno?
January, with 331 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,407 mm (55.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Puno?
July, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Puno?
July, with about 290 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Puno?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in June; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Puno?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Puno have?
Tundra (Köppen ET). Too cold for trees: even the warmest month stays cool. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Puno’s GeoNames location (15.840°S, 70.022°W; ClimaClue area sq-998-1099), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 128,637 (GeoNames).
