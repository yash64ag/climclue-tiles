Pisco sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ica · Peru

The sea off Pisco averages 20.7 °C (69 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 15.6 °C (60 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March.

Pisco sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pisco?
In February, at about 20.7 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pisco?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March. The coolest month is September, at 15.6 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pisco in September?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pisco?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pisco nearby?
In February: Mala (21.9 °C, 126 km) and Minas de Marcona (21.4 °C, 204 km).
