Talara sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Piura · Peru

The sea off Talara averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 18.8 °C (66 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February.

Talara sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Talara?
In February, at about 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Talara?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February. The coolest month is September, at 18.8 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Talara in September?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Talara?
About 20.9 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Talara nearby?
In February: Tumbes (26.0 °C, 146 km), Huaquillas (26.6 °C, 168 km) and Machala (26.8 °C, 207 km).
