Mala sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Lima region · Peru

The sea off Mala averages 21.9 °C (71 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.0 °C (63 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April.

Mala sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mala?
In February, at about 21.9 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mala?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is September, at 17.0 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mala in September?
About 17.0 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mala?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
