Sechura sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Piura · Peru

The sea off Sechura averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.5 °C (64 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April.

Sechura sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sechura?
In February, at about 23.0 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sechura?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is September, at 17.5 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sechura in September?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sechura?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sechura nearby?
In February: Talara (24.0 °C, 120 km), Tumbes (26.0 °C, 226 km) and Huaquillas (26.6 °C, 241 km).
