Santa Anita - Los Ficus at a glance

Among the 24 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Peru, Santa Anita - Los Ficus is the 4th least sunny (about 1,713 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Lima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Santa Anita - Los Ficus is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.8 °C warmer in August, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Santa Anita - Los Ficus has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
