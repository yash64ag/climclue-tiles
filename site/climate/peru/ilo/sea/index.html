Ilo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Moquegua Department · Peru

The sea off Ilo averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 16.4 °C (62 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April.

Ilo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ilo?
In February, at about 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ilo?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 16.4 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ilo in August?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ilo?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
