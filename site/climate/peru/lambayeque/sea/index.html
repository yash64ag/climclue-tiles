Lambayeque sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Peru

The sea off Lambayeque averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 16.8 °C (62 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April.

Lambayeque sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lambayeque?
In February, at about 22.2 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lambayeque?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is September, at 16.8 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lambayeque in September?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lambayeque?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Lambayeque nearby?
In February: Sechura (23.0 °C, 163 km).
