How September compares in Piura

September is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Piura. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. October is 0.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Piura in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Piura in September?
In September, Piura averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Piura?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Piura’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
