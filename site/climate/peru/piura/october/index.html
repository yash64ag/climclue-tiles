Piura weather in October

Average October weather in Piura, Peru: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Piura averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) days and 15.7 °C (60 °F) nights, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 229 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Piura

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Piura. Compared with September: 0.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. November is 0.8 °C warmer than October on average.

Piura in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Piura in October?
In October, Piura averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Piura?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Piura’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Piura in October?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (52 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Piura in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Piura get in October?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Piura in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
