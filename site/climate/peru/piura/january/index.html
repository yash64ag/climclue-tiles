Piura weather in January

Average January weather in Piura, Peru: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Piura averages 32.5 °C (91 °F) days and 20.0 °C (68 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.2 in) of rain over the month and about 205 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Piura

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Piura. Compared with December: 1.8 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Piura in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Piura in January?
In January, Piura averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Piura?
January is not one of Piura’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Piura in January?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (52 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Piura in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Piura get in January?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Piura in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
