How April compares in Lima

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lima. Compared with March: 1.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. May is 1.9 °C cooler than April on average.

Lima in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lima in April?
In April, Lima averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lima?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Lima’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lima in April?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (5 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lima in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lima get in April?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lima in April?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (21.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lima in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
