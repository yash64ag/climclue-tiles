How August compares in Lima

August is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lima. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Lima in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lima in August?
In August, Lima averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 39 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lima?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Lima’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lima in August?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (4 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lima in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lima get in August?
About 39 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lima in August?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
