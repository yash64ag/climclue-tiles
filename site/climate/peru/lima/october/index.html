How October compares in Lima

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lima. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 1.2 °C warmer than October on average.

Lima in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lima in October?
In October, Lima averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lima?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Lima’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lima in October?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (5 mm), the driest May (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lima in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lima get in October?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lima in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
