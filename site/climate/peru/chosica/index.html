Chosica at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Peru, Chosica is 49th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 1,932 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (279 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Anita - Los Ficus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Chosica is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 4.2 °C warmer in July, with 229 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.9 °C), averages 0.7 °C more than its coolest, July (21.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.4 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chosica, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chosica has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.7 °C. The 10 km area around Chosica, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.7 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chosica itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.943°S, 76.709°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-959-1032), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
