Yanacancha, Pasco at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Peru, Yanacancha, Pasco is the 2nd coolest (4.2 °C year average). Compared with Chaupimarca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Yanacancha, Pasco is 4.1 °C cooler in December and 4.3 °C cooler in July, with 236 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (5.2 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, July (2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in every month. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Why Yanacancha, Pasco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-946-1037), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
