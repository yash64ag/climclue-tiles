How March compares in Arequipa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Arequipa. Compared with February: about as warm, 18 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 0.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Arequipa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arequipa in March?
In March, Arequipa averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Arequipa?
March is not one of Arequipa’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arequipa in March?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (62 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Arequipa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arequipa get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arequipa in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
