How February compares in Arequipa

February is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Arequipa. Compared with January: about as warm, 6 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. March is about as warm as February on average.

Arequipa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arequipa in February?
In February, Arequipa averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Arequipa?
February is not one of Arequipa’s mild months; those are April, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arequipa in February?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (51 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Arequipa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arequipa get in February?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arequipa in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
