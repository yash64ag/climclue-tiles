How June compares in Arequipa

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Arequipa. Compared with May: 0.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Arequipa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arequipa in June?
In June, Arequipa averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Arequipa?
June is not one of Arequipa’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arequipa in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (62 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Arequipa in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arequipa get in June?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arequipa in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
