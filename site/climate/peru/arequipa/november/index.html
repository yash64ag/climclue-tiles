How November compares in Arequipa

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Arequipa. Compared with October: 0.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. December is 0.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Arequipa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arequipa in November?
In November, Arequipa averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Arequipa?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Arequipa’s mild months (April, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arequipa in November?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (51 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Arequipa in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arequipa get in November?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arequipa in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
