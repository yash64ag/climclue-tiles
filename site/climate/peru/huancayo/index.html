Huancayo at a glance

Among the 24 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Peru, Huancayo is the 5th coolest (10.2 °C year average). Compared with Ayacucho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (162 km away), Huancayo is 2.1 °C cooler in January and 1.4 °C cooler in July, with 274 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (11.1 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, July (8.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 176 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huancayo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Huancayo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 176 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
