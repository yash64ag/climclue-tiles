How January compares in Chiclayo

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chiclayo. Compared with December: 1.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Chiclayo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiclayo in January?
In January, Chiclayo averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chiclayo?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Chiclayo’s mild months (January, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiclayo in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (22 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Chiclayo in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiclayo get in January?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chiclayo in January?
About 20.5 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Chiclayo in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
