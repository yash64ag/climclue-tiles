How July compares in Chiclayo

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Chiclayo. Compared with June: 0.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Chiclayo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiclayo in July?
In July, Chiclayo averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Chiclayo?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Chiclayo’s mild months (January, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiclayo in July?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (22 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Chiclayo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiclayo get in July?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chiclayo in July?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
