How April compares in Chiclayo

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Chiclayo. Compared with March: 1.8 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. May is 1.9 °C cooler than April on average.

Chiclayo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiclayo in April?
In April, Chiclayo averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chiclayo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Chiclayo’s mild months (January, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiclayo in April?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (25 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Chiclayo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiclayo get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chiclayo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
