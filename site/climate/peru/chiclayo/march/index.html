How March compares in Chiclayo

March is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chiclayo. Compared with February: about as warm, 10 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. April is 1.8 °C cooler than March on average.

Chiclayo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chiclayo in March?
In March, Chiclayo averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chiclayo?
March is not one of Chiclayo’s mild months; those are January, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chiclayo in March?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (25 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Chiclayo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chiclayo get in March?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chiclayo in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
