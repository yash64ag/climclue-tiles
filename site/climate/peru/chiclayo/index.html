Chiclayo at a glance

Among the 24 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Peru, Chiclayo is 7th warmest (21.9 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,422 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (75 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cajamarca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (156 km away), Chiclayo is 13.1 °C warmer in February and 6.5 °C warmer in August, with 1,161 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.6 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, August (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chiclayo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chiclayo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
