Why Chiclayo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Chiclayo?
August, with an average of 19.1 °C (66 °F), day highs around 23.8 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 16.6 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chiclayo?

What is the driest month in Chiclayo?

What is the sunniest month in Chiclayo?
December, with about 218 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chiclayo?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Chiclayo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Chiclayo’s GeoNames location (6.770°S, 79.855°W; ClimaClue area sq-907-1001), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 609,400 (GeoNames).
