Pucallpa at a glance

Among the 24 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Peru, Pucallpa is the warmest (26.2 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 1,644 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,611 mm a year). Compared with Huánuco, the nearest covered town with different numbers (253 km away), Pucallpa is 11.3 °C warmer in October and 11.1 °C warmer in July, with 974 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, July (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 445 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pucallpa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pucallpa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 445 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
