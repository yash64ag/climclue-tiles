Why Huaraz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. About 90 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Huaraz climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Huaraz?
January, with an average of 8.7 °C (48 °F), day highs around 13.6 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 4.8 °C (41 °F).
What is the coldest month in Huaraz?
July, with an average of 7.8 °C (46 °F), day highs around 13.2 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 2.3 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Huaraz?
March, with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 939 mm (37.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Huaraz?
July, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Huaraz?
July, with about 265 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Huaraz?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Huaraz?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Huaraz’s GeoNames location (9.526°S, 77.529°W; ClimaClue area sq-935-1024), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,836 (GeoNames).
