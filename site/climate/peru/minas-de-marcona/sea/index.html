Minas de Marcona sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ica · Peru

The sea off Minas de Marcona averages 21.4 °C (71 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 15.5 °C (60 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March.

Minas de Marcona sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Minas de Marcona?
In February, at about 21.4 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Minas de Marcona?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March. The coolest month is August, at 15.5 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Minas de Marcona in August?
About 15.5 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Minas de Marcona?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Minas de Marcona nearby?
In February: Mollendo (22.3 °C, 387 km).
