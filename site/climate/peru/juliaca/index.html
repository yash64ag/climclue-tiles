Juliaca at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Peru, Juliaca is 123rd warmest (8.9 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,818 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (1,060 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Miguel, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Juliaca is 0.1 °C cooler in November and about as warm in June, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (10.7 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, June (6.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in May, June, July and August. The area lies about 259 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Juliaca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Juliaca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 259 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Juliaca itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.500°S, 70.133°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-994-1098), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
