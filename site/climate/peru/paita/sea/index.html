Paita sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Piura · Peru

The sea off Paita averages 23.1 °C (74 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.5 °C (64 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April.

Paita sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Paita?
In February, at about 23.1 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Paita?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to April. The coolest month is September, at 17.5 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Paita in September?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Paita?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Paita nearby?
In February: Talara (24.0 °C, 60 km), Tumbes (26.0 °C, 186 km) and Huaquillas (26.6 °C, 204 km).
