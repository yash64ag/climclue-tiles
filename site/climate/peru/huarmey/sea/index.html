Huarmey sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ancash · Peru

The sea off Huarmey averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 16.2 °C (61 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March.

Huarmey sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Huarmey?
In February, at about 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Huarmey?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March. The coolest month is September, at 16.2 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Huarmey in September?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Huarmey?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Huarmey nearby?
In February: Huacho (22.0 °C, 131 km) and Virú (22.3 °C, 195 km).
