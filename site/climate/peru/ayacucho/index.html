Ayacucho at a glance

Among the 24 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Peru, Ayacucho is 19th warmest (13.1 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,408 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (719 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huancayo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (162 km away), Ayacucho is 3.1 °C warmer in December and 2.2 °C warmer in July, with 297 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (14.2 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, July (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 219 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ayacucho, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ayacucho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 219 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ayacucho itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.164°S, 74.223°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-971-1057), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
