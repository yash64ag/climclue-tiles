How June compares in Trujillo

June is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Trujillo. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Trujillo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Trujillo in June?
In June, Trujillo averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Trujillo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Trujillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Trujillo in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Trujillo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Trujillo get in June?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Trujillo in June?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (21.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Trujillo in June?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
