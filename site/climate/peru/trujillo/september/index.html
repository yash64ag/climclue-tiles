How September compares in Trujillo

September is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Trujillo. Compared with August: about as warm, about the same precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. October is 0.4 °C warmer than September on average.

Trujillo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Trujillo in September?
In September, Trujillo averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Trujillo?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Trujillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Trujillo in September?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (16 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Trujillo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Trujillo get in September?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Trujillo in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
