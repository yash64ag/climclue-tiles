How July compares in Trujillo

July is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Trujillo. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Trujillo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Trujillo in July?
In July, Trujillo averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Trujillo?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Trujillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Trujillo in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Trujillo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Trujillo get in July?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Trujillo in July?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
