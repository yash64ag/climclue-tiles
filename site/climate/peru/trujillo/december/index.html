How December compares in Trujillo

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Trujillo. Compared with November: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 1.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Trujillo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Trujillo in December?
In December, Trujillo averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Trujillo?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Trujillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Trujillo in December?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Trujillo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Trujillo get in December?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Trujillo in December?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (21.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Trujillo in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
