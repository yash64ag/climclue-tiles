How February compares in Trujillo

February is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Trujillo. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Trujillo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Trujillo in February?
In February, Trujillo averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Trujillo?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Trujillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Trujillo in February?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (18 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Trujillo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Trujillo get in February?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Trujillo in February?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (21.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Trujillo in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
