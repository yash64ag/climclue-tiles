How March compares in Trujillo

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Trujillo. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. April is 1.8 °C cooler than March on average.

Trujillo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Trujillo in March?
In March, Trujillo averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Trujillo?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Trujillo’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Trujillo in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (16 mm), the driest August (2 mm).
Does it snow in Trujillo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Trujillo get in March?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Trujillo in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
