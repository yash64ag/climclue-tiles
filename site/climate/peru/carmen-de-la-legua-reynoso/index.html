Carmen De La Legua Reynoso at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Peru, Carmen De La Legua Reynoso is the least sunny (about 1,505 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Callao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Carmen De La Legua Reynoso is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.4 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, August (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Carmen De La Legua Reynoso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Carmen De La Legua Reynoso itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.054°S, 77.098°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
