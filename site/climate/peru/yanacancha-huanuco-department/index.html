Yanacancha, Huánuco Department at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Peru, Yanacancha, Huánuco Department is the 5th coolest (6.0 °C year average). Compared with Chaupimarca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Yanacancha, Huánuco Department is 2.5 °C cooler in January and 2.3 °C cooler in July, with 163 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (6.8 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, July (4.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Why Yanacancha, Huánuco Department has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
