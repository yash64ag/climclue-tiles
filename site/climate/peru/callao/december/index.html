Callao weather in December

Average December weather in Callao, Peru: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Callao averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) days and 15.2 °C (59 °F) nights, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 167 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Callao

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Callao. Compared with November: 1.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. January is 1.9 °C warmer than December on average.

Callao in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Callao in December?
In December, Callao averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Callao?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Callao’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Callao in December?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (3 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Callao in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Callao get in December?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Callao in December?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (21.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Callao in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
