How October compares in Callao

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Callao. Compared with September: 0.6 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 1.2 °C warmer than October on average.

Callao in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Callao in October?
In October, Callao averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Callao?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Callao’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Callao in October?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (3 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Callao in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Callao get in October?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Callao in October?
About 16.5 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (21.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Callao in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
