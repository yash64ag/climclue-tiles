How April compares in Callao

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Callao. Compared with March: 1.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. May is 2.0 °C cooler than April on average.

Callao in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Callao in April?
In April, Callao averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Callao?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Callao’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Callao in April?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (3 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Callao in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Callao get in April?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Callao in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
