How January compares in Callao

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Callao. Compared with December: 1.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Callao in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Callao in January?
In January, Callao averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Callao?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Callao’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Callao in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (3 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Callao in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Callao get in January?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Callao in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
