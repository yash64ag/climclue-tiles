How July compares in Callao

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Callao. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Callao in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Callao in July?
In July, Callao averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 40 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Callao?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Callao’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Callao in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (3 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Callao in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Callao get in July?
About 40 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Callao in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
