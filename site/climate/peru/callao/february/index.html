How February compares in Callao

February is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Callao. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 0.3 °C cooler than February on average.

Callao in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Callao in February?
In February, Callao averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Callao?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Callao’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Callao in February?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (3 mm), the driest May (1 mm).
Does it snow in Callao in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Callao get in February?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Callao in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
