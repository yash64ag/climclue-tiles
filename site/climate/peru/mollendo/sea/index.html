Mollendo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Arequipa · Peru

The sea off Mollendo averages 22.3 °C (72 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 15.8 °C (60 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April.

Mollendo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mollendo?
In February, at about 22.3 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mollendo?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 15.8 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mollendo in August?
About 15.8 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mollendo?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mollendo nearby?
In February: Arica (22.9 °C, 243 km).
