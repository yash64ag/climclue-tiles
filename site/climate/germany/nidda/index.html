Nidda at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Nidda is 321st warmest (9.8 °C year average), 554th sunniest (about 1,524 hours of sunshine a year) and 433rd wettest (843 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Büdingen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Nidda is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.0 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 361 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 12 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 86% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nidda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 361 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nidda itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.413°N, 9.006°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-335-1890), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
