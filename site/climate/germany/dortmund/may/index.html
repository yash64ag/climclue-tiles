How May compares in Dortmund

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Dortmund. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Dortmund in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dortmund in May?
In May, Dortmund averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Dortmund?
May is not one of Dortmund’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dortmund in May?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (54 mm).
Does it snow in Dortmund in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dortmund get in May?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dortmund in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
