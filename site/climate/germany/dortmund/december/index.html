How December compares in Dortmund

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Dortmund. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Dortmund in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dortmund in December?
In December, Dortmund averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 35 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dortmund?
December is not one of Dortmund’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dortmund in December?
88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (54 mm).
Does it snow in Dortmund in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dortmund get in December?
About 35 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dortmund in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
