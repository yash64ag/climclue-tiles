Langen, Lower Saxony sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Lower Saxony · Germany

The sea off Langen, Lower Saxony averages 18.9 °C (66 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.9 °C (37 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Langen, Lower Saxony sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Langen, Lower Saxony?
In August, at about 18.9 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Langen, Lower Saxony?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.9 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Langen, Lower Saxony in February?
About 2.9 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Langen, Lower Saxony?
About 10.4 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
