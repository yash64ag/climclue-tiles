How June compares in Duisburg

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Duisburg. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Duisburg in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in June?
In June, Duisburg averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Duisburg?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Duisburg’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in June?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duisburg in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
