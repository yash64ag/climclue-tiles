How March compares in Duisburg

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Duisburg. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. April is 3.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Duisburg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in March?
In March, Duisburg averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Duisburg?
March is not one of Duisburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in March?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in March?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duisburg in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
