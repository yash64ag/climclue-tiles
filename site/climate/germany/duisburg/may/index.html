Duisburg weather in May

Average May weather in Duisburg, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Duisburg averages 18.6 °C (65 °F) days and 9.2 °C (49 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain over the month and about 191 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Duisburg

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Duisburg. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Duisburg in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in May?
In May, Duisburg averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Duisburg?
May is not one of Duisburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in May?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in May?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
