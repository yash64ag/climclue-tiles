How January compares in Duisburg

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Duisburg. Compared with December: 0.8 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Duisburg in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in January?
In January, Duisburg averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 41 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Duisburg?
January is not one of Duisburg’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in January?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in January?
Yes, on average: about 6.4 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in January?
About 41 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duisburg in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
