Duisburg in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in August?
In August, Duisburg averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Duisburg?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Duisburg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in August?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in August?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duisburg in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
