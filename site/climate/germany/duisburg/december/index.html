How December compares in Duisburg

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Duisburg. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Duisburg in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in December?
In December, Duisburg averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 36 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Duisburg?
December is not one of Duisburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in December?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.1 cm (3.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in December?
About 36 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duisburg in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
