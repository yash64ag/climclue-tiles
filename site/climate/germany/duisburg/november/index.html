How November compares in Duisburg

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Duisburg. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Duisburg in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Duisburg in November?
In November, Duisburg averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Duisburg?
November is not one of Duisburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Duisburg in November?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (78 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Duisburg in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Duisburg get in November?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Duisburg in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
