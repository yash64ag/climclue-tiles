How May compares in Essen

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Essen. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Essen in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Essen in May?
In May, Essen averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Essen?
May is not one of Essen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Essen in May?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in Essen in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Essen get in May?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Essen in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
