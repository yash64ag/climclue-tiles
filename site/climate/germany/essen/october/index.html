Essen weather in October

Average October weather in Essen, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Essen averages 13.9 °C (57 °F) days and 7.3 °C (45 °F) nights, with 79 mm (3.1 in) of rain over the month and about 105 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Essen

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Essen. Compared with September: 4.0 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. November is 4.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Essen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Essen in October?
In October, Essen averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Essen?
October is not one of Essen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Essen in October?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in Essen in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Essen get in October?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Essen in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
