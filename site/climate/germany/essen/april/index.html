How April compares in Essen

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Essen. Compared with March: 3.3 °C warmer, 24 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Essen in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Essen in April?
In April, Essen averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Essen?
April is not one of Essen’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Essen in April?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in Essen in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Essen get in April?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Essen in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
