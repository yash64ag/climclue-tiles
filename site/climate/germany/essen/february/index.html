How February compares in Essen

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Essen. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. March is 3.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Essen in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Essen in February?
In February, Essen averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.2 °C (32 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Essen?
February is not one of Essen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Essen in February?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in Essen in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Essen get in February?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Essen in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
