How March compares in Essen

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Essen. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 3.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Essen in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Essen in March?
In March, Essen averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Essen?
March is not one of Essen’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Essen in March?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (88 mm), the driest April (55 mm).
Does it snow in Essen in March?
Yes, on average: about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Essen get in March?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Essen in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
