Eislingen weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Eislingen, Germany (Baden-Wurttemberg), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Eislingen is warmest in July, with 24.4 °C (76 °F) days and 13.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 2.6 °C (37 °F) days and -2.9 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 62 mm in February to 96 mm in July.

Eislingen at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Eislingen is 585th warmest (9.4 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 1,646 hours of sunshine a year) and 181st wettest (942 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Göppingen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Eislingen is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 19.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 417 km from the sea.

Why Eislingen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 417 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.0 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Eislingen itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.695°N, 9.707°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-353-1897), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
