Erfurt at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Erfurt is the 4th coolest (8.7 °C year average). Compared with Jena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Erfurt is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 115% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Erfurt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
