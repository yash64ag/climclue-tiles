How April compares in Hannover

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Hannover. Compared with March: 3.9 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. May is 4.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Hannover in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hannover in April?
In April, Hannover averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hannover?
April is not one of Hannover’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hannover in April?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Hannover in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hannover get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hannover in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
