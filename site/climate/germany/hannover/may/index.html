How May compares in Hannover

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hannover. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 62 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Hannover in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hannover in May?
In May, Hannover averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hannover?
May is not one of Hannover’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hannover in May?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Hannover in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hannover get in May?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hannover in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
