Hannover in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hannover in July?
In July, Hannover averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hannover?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Hannover’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hannover in July?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Hannover in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hannover get in July?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hannover in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
