How December compares in Hannover

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hannover. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Hannover in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hannover in December?
In December, Hannover averages a day high of 4.3 °C (40 °F) and a night low of 0.0 °C (32 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 33 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hannover?
December is not one of Hannover’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hannover in December?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Hannover in December?
Yes, on average: about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hannover get in December?
About 33 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hannover in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
