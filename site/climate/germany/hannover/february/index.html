Hannover weather in February

Average February weather in Hannover, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Hannover averages 4.9 °C (41 °F) days and -1.1 °C (30 °F) nights, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 72 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Hannover

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hannover. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. March is 3.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Hannover in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hannover in February?
In February, Hannover averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -1.1 °C (30 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hannover?
February is not one of Hannover’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hannover in February?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Hannover in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hannover get in February?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hannover in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
