How January compares in Hannover

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Hannover. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Hannover in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hannover in January?
In January, Hannover averages a day high of 3.8 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hannover?
January is not one of Hannover’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hannover in January?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (73 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Hannover in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hannover get in January?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hannover in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
