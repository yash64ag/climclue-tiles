Siegen at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Siegen is the 2nd coolest (8.3 °C year average). Compared with Bergisch Gladbach, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Siegen is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 17.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 299 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 90% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Siegen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 299 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
