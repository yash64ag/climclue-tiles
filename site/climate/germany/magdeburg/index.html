Magdeburg at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Magdeburg is the 2nd driest (542 mm a year). Compared with Wolfsburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Magdeburg is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 144 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 212 km from the sea.

Why Magdeburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 212 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
