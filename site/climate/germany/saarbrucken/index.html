Saarbrücken at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Saarbrücken is 32nd warmest (9.9 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 1,583 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (883 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Trier, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Saarbrücken is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Saarbrücken has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
