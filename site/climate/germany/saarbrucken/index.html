Why Saarbrücken has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Saarbrücken climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Saarbrücken?
July, with an average of 19.1 °C (66 °F), day highs around 23.3 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 14.1 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Saarbrücken?
January, with an average of 1.4 °C (34 °F), day highs around 3.9 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -1.2 °C (30 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Saarbrücken?
December, with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 883 mm (34.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Saarbrücken?

What is the sunniest month in Saarbrücken?
July, with about 229 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Saarbrücken?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 60 cm (23.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Saarbrücken?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.3 × 11 km, that contains Saarbrücken’s GeoNames location (49.233°N, 7.010°E; ClimaClue area sq-347-1870), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 182,971 (GeoNames).
