Frankfurt am Main in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in March?
In March, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
March is not one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in March?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.9 cm (2.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in March?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
