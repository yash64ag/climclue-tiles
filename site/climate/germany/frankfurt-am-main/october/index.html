Frankfurt am Main in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in October?
In October, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
October is not one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in October?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in October?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
