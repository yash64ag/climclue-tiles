How December compares in Frankfurt am Main

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Frankfurt am Main. Compared with November: 3.0 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 0.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Frankfurt am Main in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in December?
In December, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 39 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
December is not one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in December?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in December?
Yes, on average: about 12 cm (4.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in December?
About 39 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
