Frankfurt am Main in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in April?
In April, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
April is not one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in April?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in April?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
