Frankfurt am Main in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in August?
In August, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in August?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in August?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in August?
