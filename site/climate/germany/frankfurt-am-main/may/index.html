Frankfurt am Main in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in May?
In May, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
May is not one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in May?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in May?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
