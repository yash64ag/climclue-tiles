Frankfurt am Main in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Frankfurt am Main in June?
In June, Frankfurt am Main averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Frankfurt am Main?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Frankfurt am Main’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Frankfurt am Main in June?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (70 mm), the driest April (47 mm).
Does it snow in Frankfurt am Main in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Frankfurt am Main get in June?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Frankfurt am Main in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
