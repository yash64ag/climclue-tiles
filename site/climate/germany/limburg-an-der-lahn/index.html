Limburg an der Lahn at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Limburg an der Lahn is 290th warmest (9.9 °C year average), 564th sunniest (about 1,521 hours of sunshine a year) and 712th wettest (744 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Idstein, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Limburg an der Lahn is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 337 km from the sea.

Why Limburg an der Lahn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 337 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Limburg an der Lahn itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.384°N, 8.050°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-336-1880), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
