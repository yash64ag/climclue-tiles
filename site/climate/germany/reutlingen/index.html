Reutlingen at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Reutlingen is the coolest (8.2 °C year average). Compared with Stuttgart, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Reutlingen is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 59 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.4 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 417 km from the sea.

Why Reutlingen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 417 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
