How July compares in Hamburg

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Hamburg in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in July?
In July, Hamburg averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hamburg?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Hamburg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in July?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in July?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
