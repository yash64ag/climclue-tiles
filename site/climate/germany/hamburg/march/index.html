Hamburg weather in March

Average March weather in Hamburg, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Hamburg averages 7.8 °C (46 °F) days and 1.1 °C (34 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 103 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Hamburg

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with February: 2.8 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 4.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Hamburg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in March?
In March, Hamburg averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hamburg?
March is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in March?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in March?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
