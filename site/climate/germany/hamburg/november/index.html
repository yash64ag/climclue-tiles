Hamburg weather in November

Average November weather in Hamburg, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Hamburg averages 7.7 °C (46 °F) days and 2.6 °C (37 °F) nights, with 71 mm (2.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 51 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Hamburg

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 3.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Hamburg in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in November?
In November, Hamburg averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 51 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hamburg?
November is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in November?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in November?
About 51 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
