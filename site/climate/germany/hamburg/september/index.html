How September compares in Hamburg

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with August: 3.6 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. October is 4.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Hamburg in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in September?
In September, Hamburg averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hamburg?
September is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in September?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in September?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
