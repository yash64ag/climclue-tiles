How April compares in Hamburg

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with March: 4.0 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. May is 4.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Hamburg in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in April?
In April, Hamburg averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hamburg?
April is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in April?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in April?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
