How June compares in Hamburg

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hamburg. Compared with May: 2.7 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Hamburg in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in June?
In June, Hamburg averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Hamburg?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Hamburg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in June?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
