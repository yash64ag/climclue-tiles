Hamburg at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Hamburg is 64th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 1,535 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (812 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lübeck, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Hamburg is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 16.6 °C more than its coolest, January (1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 81 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Hamburg

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hamburg are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (17.9 °C), August (17.4 °C) and June (15.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): April (44 mm), February (52 mm) and May (60 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): June (218 h), May (216 h) and July (205 h).

Why Hamburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 81 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
