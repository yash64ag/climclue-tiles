How January compares in Hamburg

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Hamburg in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in January?
In January, Hamburg averages a day high of 3.5 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -0.9 °C (30 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 42 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hamburg?
January is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in January?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in January?
About 42 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
