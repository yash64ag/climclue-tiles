How December compares in Hamburg

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hamburg. Compared with November: 3.0 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Hamburg in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in December?
In December, Hamburg averages a day high of 4.4 °C (40 °F) and a night low of 0.0 °C (32 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 35 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hamburg?
December is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in December?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in December?
Yes, on average: about 9.5 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in December?
About 35 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
