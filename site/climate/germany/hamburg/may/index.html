How May compares in Hamburg

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hamburg. Compared with April: 4.4 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Hamburg in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamburg in May?
In May, Hamburg averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hamburg?
May is not one of Hamburg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamburg in May?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (83 mm), the driest April (44 mm).
Does it snow in Hamburg in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamburg get in May?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hamburg in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
