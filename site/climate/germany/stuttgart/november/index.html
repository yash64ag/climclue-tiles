How November compares in Stuttgart

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Stuttgart. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. December is 3.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Stuttgart in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stuttgart in November?
In November, Stuttgart averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 64 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Stuttgart?
November is not one of Stuttgart’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stuttgart in November?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (89 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Stuttgart in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.2 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Stuttgart get in November?
About 64 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stuttgart in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
