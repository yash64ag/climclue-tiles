How October compares in Stuttgart

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Stuttgart. Compared with September: 4.5 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. November is 5.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Stuttgart in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stuttgart in October?
In October, Stuttgart averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Stuttgart?
October is not one of Stuttgart’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stuttgart in October?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (93 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Stuttgart in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Stuttgart get in October?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stuttgart in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
