How April compares in Stuttgart

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Stuttgart. Compared with March: 3.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Stuttgart in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stuttgart in April?
In April, Stuttgart averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Stuttgart?
April is not one of Stuttgart’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stuttgart in April?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (89 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Stuttgart in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Stuttgart get in April?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stuttgart in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
