How March compares in Stuttgart

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Stuttgart. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Stuttgart in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stuttgart in March?
In March, Stuttgart averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Stuttgart?
March is not one of Stuttgart’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stuttgart in March?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (93 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Stuttgart in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Stuttgart get in March?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stuttgart in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
