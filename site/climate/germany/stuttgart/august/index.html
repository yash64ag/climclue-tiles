How August compares in Stuttgart

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Stuttgart. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Stuttgart in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stuttgart in August?
In August, Stuttgart averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Stuttgart?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Stuttgart’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stuttgart in August?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (89 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Stuttgart in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Stuttgart get in August?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stuttgart in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
