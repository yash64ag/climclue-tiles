Stuttgart weather in January

Average January weather in Stuttgart, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Stuttgart averages 3.7 °C (39 °F) days and -2.1 °C (28 °F) nights, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 54 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Stuttgart

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Stuttgart. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Stuttgart in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stuttgart in January?
In January, Stuttgart averages a day high of 3.7 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -2.1 °C (28 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 54 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Stuttgart?
January is not one of Stuttgart’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stuttgart in January?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (89 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Stuttgart in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Stuttgart get in January?
About 54 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stuttgart in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
