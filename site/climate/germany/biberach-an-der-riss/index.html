Biberach an der Riß at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Biberach an der Riß is 866th warmest (8.7 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 1,652 hours of sunshine a year) and 319th wettest (877 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Laupheim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Biberach an der Riß is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 360 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Biberach an der Riß itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.093°N, 9.791°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-1897), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
