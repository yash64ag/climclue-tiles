Why Bonn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 253 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Bonn climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bonn?
July, with an average of 18.9 °C (66 °F), day highs around 23.0 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 14.3 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bonn?
January, with an average of 2.5 °C (37 °F), day highs around 4.9 °C (41 °F) and night lows around 0.0 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bonn?
June, with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 758 mm (29.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bonn?
February, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bonn?
July, with about 200 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bonn?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 48 cm (19.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Bonn?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Bonn have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.0 × 11 km, that contains Bonn’s GeoNames location (50.734°N, 7.095°E; ClimaClue area sq-332-1870), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 330,579 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
