Schwerin at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Schwerin is 825th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 231st sunniest (about 1,620 hours of sunshine a year) and 837th wettest (674 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wismar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Schwerin is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.7 °C), averages 17.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Why Schwerin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Schwerin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (53.629°N, 11.413°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-303-1914), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
