Husum sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Schleswig-Holstein · Germany

The sea off Husum averages 18.3 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.8 °C (37 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Husum sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Husum?
In August, at about 18.3 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Husum?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.8 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Husum in February?
About 2.8 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Husum?
About 10.1 °C (50 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Husum nearby?
In August: Cuxhaven (18.9 °C, 72 km) and Langen, Lower Saxony (18.9 °C, 102 km).
