How November compares in Dresden

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dresden. Compared with October: 5.2 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. December is 3.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Dresden in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dresden in November?
In November, Dresden averages a day high of 7.1 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 50 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Dresden?
November is not one of Dresden’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dresden in November?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest February (43 mm).
Does it snow in Dresden in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dresden get in November?
About 50 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dresden in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
