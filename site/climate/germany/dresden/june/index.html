Dresden in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dresden in June?
In June, Dresden averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dresden?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Dresden’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dresden in June?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest February (43 mm).
Does it snow in Dresden in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dresden get in June?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dresden in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
