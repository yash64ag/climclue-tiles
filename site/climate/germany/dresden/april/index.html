How April compares in Dresden

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dresden. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Dresden in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dresden in April?
In April, Dresden averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dresden?
April is not one of Dresden’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dresden in April?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest February (43 mm).
Does it snow in Dresden in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.5 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dresden get in April?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dresden in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
