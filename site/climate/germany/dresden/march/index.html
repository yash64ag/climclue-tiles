How March compares in Dresden

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dresden. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. April is 4.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Dresden in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dresden in March?
In March, Dresden averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dresden?
March is not one of Dresden’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dresden in March?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (90 mm), the driest February (43 mm).
Does it snow in Dresden in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dresden get in March?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dresden in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
