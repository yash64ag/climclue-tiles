Leverkusen at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Leverkusen is the 5th warmest (10.5 °C year average). Compared with Köln, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Leverkusen is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 57 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 229 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Leverkusen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 229 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
