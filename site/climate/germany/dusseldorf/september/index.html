How September compares in Düsseldorf

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Düsseldorf. Compared with August: 3.5 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 4.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Düsseldorf in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in September?
In September, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Düsseldorf’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in September?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in September?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
