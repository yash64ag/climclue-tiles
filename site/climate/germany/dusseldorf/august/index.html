Düsseldorf weather in August

Average August weather in Düsseldorf, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Düsseldorf averages 23.1 °C (74 °F) days and 13.6 °C (56 °F) nights, with 77 mm (3.0 in) of rain over the month and about 180 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Düsseldorf

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Düsseldorf. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. September is 3.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Düsseldorf in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in August?
In August, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Düsseldorf’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in August?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in August?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
