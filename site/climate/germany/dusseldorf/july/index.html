How July compares in Düsseldorf

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Düsseldorf. Compared with June: 2.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Düsseldorf in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in July?
In July, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Düsseldorf’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in July?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in July?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
