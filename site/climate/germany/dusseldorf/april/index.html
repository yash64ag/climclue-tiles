How April compares in Düsseldorf

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Düsseldorf. Compared with March: 3.3 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Düsseldorf in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in April?
In April, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
April is not one of Düsseldorf’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in April?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in April?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
