How November compares in Düsseldorf

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Düsseldorf. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Düsseldorf in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in November?
In November, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
November is not one of Düsseldorf’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in November?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (81 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in November?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
