Düsseldorf weather in May

Average May weather in Düsseldorf, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Düsseldorf averages 18.7 °C (66 °F) days and 9.2 °C (49 °F) nights, with 72 mm (2.8 in) of rain over the month and about 192 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Düsseldorf

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Düsseldorf. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Düsseldorf in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in May?
In May, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
May is not one of Düsseldorf’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in May?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in May?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
