How March compares in Düsseldorf

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Düsseldorf. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 3.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Düsseldorf in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Düsseldorf in March?
In March, Düsseldorf averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Düsseldorf?
March is not one of Düsseldorf’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Düsseldorf in March?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (82 mm), the driest April (53 mm).
Does it snow in Düsseldorf in March?
Yes, on average: about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Düsseldorf get in March?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Düsseldorf in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
