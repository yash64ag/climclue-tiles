Hamm at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Hamm is 28th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 1,436 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (889 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dortmund, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Hamm is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 16.3 °C more than its coolest, January (2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 209 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hamm, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hamm has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 209 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
