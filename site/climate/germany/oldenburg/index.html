Oldenburg at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Oldenburg is 48th warmest (9.5 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 1,454 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (849 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bremen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Oldenburg is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Oldenburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
