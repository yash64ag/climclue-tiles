How February compares in Berlin

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Berlin. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. March is 3.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Berlin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Berlin in February?
In February, Berlin averages a day high of 4.0 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -1.6 °C (29 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Berlin?
February is not one of Berlin’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Berlin in February?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (62 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Berlin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Berlin get in February?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Berlin in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
