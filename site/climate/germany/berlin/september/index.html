How September compares in Berlin

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Berlin. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. October is 4.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Berlin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Berlin in September?
In September, Berlin averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Berlin?
September is not one of Berlin’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Berlin in September?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (61 mm), the driest April (33 mm).
Does it snow in Berlin in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Berlin get in September?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Berlin in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
