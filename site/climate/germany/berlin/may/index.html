How May compares in Berlin

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Berlin. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Berlin in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Berlin in May?
In May, Berlin averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Berlin?
May is not one of Berlin’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Berlin in May?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (61 mm), the driest April (33 mm).
Does it snow in Berlin in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Berlin get in May?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Berlin in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
