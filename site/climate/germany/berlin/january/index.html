How January compares in Berlin

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Berlin. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Berlin in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Berlin in January?
In January, Berlin averages a day high of 2.6 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Berlin?
January is not one of Berlin’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Berlin in January?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (62 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Berlin in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Berlin get in January?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Berlin in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
