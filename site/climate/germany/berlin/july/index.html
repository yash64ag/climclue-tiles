Berlin weather in July

Average July weather in Berlin, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Berlin averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) days and 14.0 °C (57 °F) nights, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of rain over the month and about 220 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Berlin

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Berlin. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Berlin in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Berlin in July?
In July, Berlin averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Berlin?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Berlin’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Berlin in July?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (61 mm), the driest April (33 mm).
Does it snow in Berlin in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Berlin get in July?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Berlin in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
