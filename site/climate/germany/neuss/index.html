Neuss at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Neuss is 7th warmest (10.5 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 1,423 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (829 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Düsseldorf, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Neuss is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.8 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 205 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Neuss has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 205 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
