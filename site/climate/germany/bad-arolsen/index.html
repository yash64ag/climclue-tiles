Bad Arolsen at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Bad Arolsen is 876th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 755th sunniest (about 1,446 hours of sunshine a year) and 674th wettest (758 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Marsberg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Bad Arolsen is 0.3 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 122 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 263 km from the sea.

Why Bad Arolsen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 263 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-326-1890), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
