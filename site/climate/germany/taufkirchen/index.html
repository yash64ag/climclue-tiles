Taufkirchen at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Taufkirchen is 886th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 1,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (1,099 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Taufkirchen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taufkirchen itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.049°N, 11.617°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-1916), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
