Gilching at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Gilching is 790th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 1,686 hours of sunshine a year) and 121st wettest (1,034 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Puchheim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Gilching is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 65 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.3 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 317 km from the sea.

Why Gilching has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gilching itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.108°N, 11.294°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-358-1912), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
