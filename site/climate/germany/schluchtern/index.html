Schlüchtern weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Schlüchtern, Germany (Hesse), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Schlüchtern is warmest in July, with 23.6 °C (74 °F) days and 13.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 2.2 °C (36 °F) days and -2.5 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 57 mm in April to 97 mm in December.

Schlüchtern at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Schlüchtern is 681st warmest (9.2 °C year average), 523rd sunniest (about 1,534 hours of sunshine a year) and 205th wettest (930 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Schlüchtern has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Schlüchtern itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.349°N, 9.525°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-336-1895), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
