Potsdam at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Potsdam is the 3rd driest (580 mm a year). Compared with Berlin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Potsdam is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 182 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Potsdam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 182 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
