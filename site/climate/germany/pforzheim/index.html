Pforzheim at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Pforzheim is 69th warmest (9.0 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 1,631 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (994 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karlsruhe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Pforzheim is 2.4 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 144 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 470 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 123% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Pforzheim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 470 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
