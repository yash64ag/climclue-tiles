Gelsenkirchen at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Gelsenkirchen is 19th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 1,416 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (901 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Essen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Gelsenkirchen is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 204 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 94% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Gelsenkirchen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 204 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
