Fürth at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Fürth is the 2nd sunniest (about 1,657 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nuremberg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Fürth is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 456 km from the sea.

Why Fürth has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 456 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
