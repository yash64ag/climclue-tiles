Bergisch Gladbach at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Bergisch Gladbach is 27th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 1,461 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,038 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Leverkusen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Bergisch Gladbach is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 162 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 247 km from the sea.

Why Bergisch Gladbach has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 247 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
