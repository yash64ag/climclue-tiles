Köln weather in September

Average September weather in Köln, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Köln averages 19.4 °C (67 °F) days and 10.5 °C (51 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain over the month and about 136 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Köln

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Köln. Compared with August: 3.6 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 4.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Köln in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Köln in September?
In September, Köln averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Köln?
September is not one of Köln’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Köln in September?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Köln in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Köln get in September?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Köln in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
