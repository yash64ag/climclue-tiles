Köln in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Köln in June?
In June, Köln averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Köln?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Köln’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Köln in June?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Köln in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Köln get in June?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Köln in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
