How October compares in Köln

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Köln. Compared with September: 3.9 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. November is 4.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Köln in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Köln in October?
In October, Köln averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Köln?
October is not one of Köln’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Köln in October?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Köln in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Köln get in October?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Köln in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
