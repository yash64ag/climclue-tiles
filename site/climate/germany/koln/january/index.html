How January compares in Köln

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Köln. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Köln in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Köln in January?
In January, Köln averages a day high of 5.0 °C (41 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 41 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Köln?
January is not one of Köln’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Köln in January?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (80 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Köln in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Köln get in January?
About 41 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Köln in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
