How November compares in Köln

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Köln. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Köln in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Köln in November?
In November, Köln averages a day high of 8.9 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 51 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Köln?
November is not one of Köln’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Köln in November?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (79 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Köln in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Köln get in November?
About 51 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Köln in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
