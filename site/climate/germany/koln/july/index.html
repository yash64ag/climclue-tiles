How July compares in Köln

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Köln. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Köln in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Köln in July?
In July, Köln averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Köln?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Köln’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Köln in July?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (80 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Köln in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Köln get in July?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Köln in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
