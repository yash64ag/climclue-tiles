Leipzig weather in March

Average March weather in Leipzig, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Leipzig averages 9.0 °C (48 °F) days and 1.0 °C (34 °F) nights, with 46 mm (1.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 113 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Leipzig

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Leipzig. Compared with February: 3.5 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 4.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Leipzig in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leipzig in March?
In March, Leipzig averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Leipzig?
March is not one of Leipzig’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leipzig in March?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (75 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Leipzig in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Leipzig get in March?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leipzig in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
