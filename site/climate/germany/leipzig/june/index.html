How June compares in Leipzig

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Leipzig. Compared with May: 2.7 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Leipzig in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leipzig in June?
In June, Leipzig averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Leipzig?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Leipzig’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leipzig in June?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (75 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Leipzig in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Leipzig get in June?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leipzig in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
