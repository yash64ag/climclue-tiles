How December compares in Leipzig

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Leipzig. Compared with November: 3.4 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Leipzig in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leipzig in December?
In December, Leipzig averages a day high of 3.8 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 35 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Leipzig?
December is not one of Leipzig’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leipzig in December?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (75 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Leipzig in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Leipzig get in December?
About 35 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leipzig in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
