How February compares in Leipzig

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Leipzig. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Leipzig in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leipzig in February?
In February, Leipzig averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -1.5 °C (29 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Leipzig?
February is not one of Leipzig’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leipzig in February?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (75 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Leipzig in February?
Yes, on average: about 11 cm (4.4 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Leipzig get in February?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leipzig in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
