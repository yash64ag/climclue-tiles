How April compares in Leipzig

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Leipzig. Compared with March: 4.1 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Leipzig in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leipzig in April?
In April, Leipzig averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Leipzig?
April is not one of Leipzig’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leipzig in April?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (75 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Leipzig in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Leipzig get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leipzig in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
