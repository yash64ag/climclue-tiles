How October compares in Leipzig

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Leipzig. Compared with September: 4.6 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. November is 5.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Leipzig in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leipzig in October?
In October, Leipzig averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Leipzig?
October is not one of Leipzig’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leipzig in October?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (75 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Leipzig in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Leipzig get in October?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leipzig in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
