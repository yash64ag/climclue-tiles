Garmisch-Partenkirchen at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Garmisch-Partenkirchen is the sunniest (about 1,789 hours of sunshine a year). Why Garmisch-Partenkirchen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 254 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Garmisch-Partenkirchen itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.492°N, 11.096°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
