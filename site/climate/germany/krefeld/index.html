Krefeld at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Krefeld is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,405 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Moers, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Krefeld is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 187 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Krefeld has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 187 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
