Bremerhaven at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Bremerhaven is 53rd warmest (9.4 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 1,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (851 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oldenburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Bremerhaven is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.4 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bremerhaven has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
