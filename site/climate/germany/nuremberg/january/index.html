Nuremberg weather in January

Average January weather in Nuremberg, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Nuremberg averages 2.6 °C (37 °F) days and -3.0 °C (27 °F) nights, with 55 mm (2.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 46 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Nuremberg

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nuremberg. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Nuremberg in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nuremberg in January?
In January, Nuremberg averages a day high of 2.6 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -3.0 °C (27 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 46 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nuremberg?
January is not one of Nuremberg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nuremberg in January?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (85 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Nuremberg in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nuremberg get in January?
About 46 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nuremberg in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
