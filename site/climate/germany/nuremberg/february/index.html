Nuremberg in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nuremberg in February?
In February, Nuremberg averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -2.5 °C (28 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nuremberg?
February is not one of Nuremberg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nuremberg in February?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (85 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Nuremberg in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nuremberg get in February?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nuremberg in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
