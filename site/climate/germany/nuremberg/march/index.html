Nuremberg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nuremberg in March?
In March, Nuremberg averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nuremberg?
March is not one of Nuremberg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nuremberg in March?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Nuremberg in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nuremberg get in March?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nuremberg in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
