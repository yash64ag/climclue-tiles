Nuremberg weather in March

Average March weather in Nuremberg, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Nuremberg averages 9.4 °C (49 °F) days and 0.5 °C (33 °F) nights, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 122 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Nuremberg

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nuremberg. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Nuremberg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nuremberg in March?
In March, Nuremberg averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.5 °C (33 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nuremberg?
March is not one of Nuremberg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nuremberg in March?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (85 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Nuremberg in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nuremberg get in March?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nuremberg in March?
