Nuremberg at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Nuremberg is the 3rd sunniest (about 1,656 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Fürth, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Nuremberg is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 454 km from the sea.

Why Nuremberg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 454 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
