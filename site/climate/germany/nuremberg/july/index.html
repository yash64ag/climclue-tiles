Nuremberg in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nuremberg in July?
In July, Nuremberg averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nuremberg?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Nuremberg’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
