How December compares in Nuremberg

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nuremberg. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Nuremberg in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nuremberg in December?
In December, Nuremberg averages a day high of 3.5 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -1.8 °C (29 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 41 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nuremberg?
December is not one of Nuremberg’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nuremberg in December?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (85 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Nuremberg in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Nuremberg get in December?
About 41 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nuremberg in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
