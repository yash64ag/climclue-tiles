How January compares in Munich

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Munich. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Munich in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Munich in January?
In January, Munich averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 53 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Munich?
No month in Munich is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Munich in January?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest February (49 mm).
Does it snow in Munich in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (10.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Munich get in January?
About 53 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Munich in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
