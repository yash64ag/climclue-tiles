Munich at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Munich is the 4th sunniest (about 1,655 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Augsburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Munich is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 86 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 307 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Munich has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
