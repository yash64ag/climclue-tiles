Munich weather in October

Average October weather in Munich, Germany: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Munich averages 13.8 °C (57 °F) days and 4.7 °C (40 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain over the month and about 119 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Munich

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Munich. Compared with September: 4.6 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.
