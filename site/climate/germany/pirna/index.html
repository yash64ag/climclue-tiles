Pirna at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Pirna is 637th warmest (9.3 °C year average), 113th sunniest (about 1,646 hours of sunshine a year) and 773rd wettest (719 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Heidenau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Pirna is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 313 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 108% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Pirna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 313 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pirna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.958°N, 13.937°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-330-1939), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
