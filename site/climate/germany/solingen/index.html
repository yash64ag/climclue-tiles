Why Solingen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 228 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Solingen climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Solingen?
July, with an average of 18.1 °C (65 °F), day highs around 22.1 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 13.8 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Solingen?
January, with an average of 1.9 °C (36 °F), day highs around 4.2 °C (40 °F) and night lows around -0.5 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Solingen?
December, with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,169 mm (46.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Solingen?
April, with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Solingen?
May, with about 193 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Solingen?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 59 cm (23.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Solingen?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: August.
What type of climate does Solingen have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.0 × 11 km, that contains Solingen’s GeoNames location (51.173°N, 7.084°E; ClimaClue area sq-328-1870), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 164,359 (GeoNames).
