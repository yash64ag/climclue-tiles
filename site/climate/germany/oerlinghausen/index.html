Oerlinghausen weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Oerlinghausen, Germany (North Rhine-Westphalia), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Oerlinghausen is warmest in July, with 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 12.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.4 °C (38 °F) days and -1.2 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 61 mm in April to 99 mm in December.

Oerlinghausen at a glance

Among the 962 towns ClimaClue covers in Germany, Oerlinghausen is 791st warmest (8.9 °C year average), 726th sunniest (about 1,452 hours of sunshine a year) and 123rd wettest (1,033 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Leopoldshöhe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Oerlinghausen is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 153 mm more precipitation a year. Why Oerlinghausen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.2 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Oerlinghausen itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.955°N, 8.662°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-320-1886), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
