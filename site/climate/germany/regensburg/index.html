Regensburg at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Regensburg is 66th warmest (9.3 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 1,594 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (729 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ingolstadt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Regensburg is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.2 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 387 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Regensburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 387 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Regensburg itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.015°N, 12.102°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-349-1921), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
