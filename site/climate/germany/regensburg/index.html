Regensburg at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Regensburg is 65th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 1,594 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (738 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ingolstadt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Regensburg is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 387 km from the sea.

Why Regensburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 387 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
