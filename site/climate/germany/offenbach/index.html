Offenbach at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Offenbach is 15th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 1,558 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (714 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Frankfurt am Main, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Offenbach is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 18.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 387 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Offenbach has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 387 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Offenbach itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.101°N, 8.766°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-338-1887), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
