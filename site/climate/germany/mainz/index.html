Mainz at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Mainz is 22nd warmest (10.1 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 1,570 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (645 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wiesbaden, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Mainz is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 18.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 374 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Mainz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 374 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
