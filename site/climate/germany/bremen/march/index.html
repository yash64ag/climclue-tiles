How March compares in Bremen

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bremen. Compared with February: 2.9 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 3.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Bremen in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in March?
In March, Bremen averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bremen?
March is not one of Bremen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in March?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in March?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
