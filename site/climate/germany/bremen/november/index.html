How November compares in Bremen

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bremen. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Bremen in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in November?
In November, Bremen averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 51 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bremen?
November is not one of Bremen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in November?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in November?
Yes, on average: about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in November?
About 51 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
