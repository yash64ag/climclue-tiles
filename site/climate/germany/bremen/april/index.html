How April compares in Bremen

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bremen. Compared with March: 3.7 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Bremen in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in April?
In April, Bremen averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bremen?
April is not one of Bremen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in April?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in April?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
