Bremen in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in June?
In June, Bremen averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bremen?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Bremen’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in June?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in June?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
