How February compares in Bremen

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bremen. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Bremen in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in February?
In February, Bremen averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -1.0 °C (30 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bremen?
February is not one of Bremen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in February?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in February?
Yes, on average: about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in February?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
