Bremen in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in August?
In August, Bremen averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bremen?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Bremen’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in August?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in August?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
