How December compares in Bremen

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bremen. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Bremen in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in December?
In December, Bremen averages a day high of 4.4 °C (40 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 34 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bremen?
December is not one of Bremen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in December?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.9 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in December?
About 34 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
