How July compares in Bremen

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bremen. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Bremen in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in July?
In July, Bremen averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bremen?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Bremen’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in July?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in July?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
