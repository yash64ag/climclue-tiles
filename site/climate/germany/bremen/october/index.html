How October compares in Bremen

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bremen. Compared with September: 4.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. November is 4.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Bremen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bremen in October?
In October, Bremen averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bremen?
October is not one of Bremen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bremen in October?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (78 mm), the driest April (43 mm).
Does it snow in Bremen in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bremen get in October?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bremen in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
