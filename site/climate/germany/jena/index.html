Jena at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Jena is the 5th coolest (8.8 °C year average). Compared with Gera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Jena is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Why Jena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
