Karlsruhe at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Karlsruhe is the warmest (11.0 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 1,653 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pforzheim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Karlsruhe is 2.4 °C warmer in July and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 144 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 444 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 108% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Karlsruhe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 444 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
