Ingolstadt at a glance

Among the 80 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Germany, Ingolstadt is 41st warmest (9.6 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 1,643 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (759 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Regensburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Ingolstadt is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 371 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ingolstadt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 371 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
