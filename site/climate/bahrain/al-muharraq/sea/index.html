Al Muharraq sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Muharraq · Bahrain

The sea off Al Muharraq averages 33.5 °C (92 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.3 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Al Muharraq sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Al Muharraq?
In August, at about 33.5 °C (92 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Al Muharraq?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.3 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Al Muharraq in February?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Al Muharraq?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
