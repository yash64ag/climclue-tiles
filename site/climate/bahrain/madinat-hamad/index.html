Madīnat Ḩamad at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bahrain, Madīnat Ḩamad is the 3rd warmest (25.8 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,320 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (82 mm a year). Compared with Ar Rifā‘, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Madīnat Ḩamad is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (33.5 °C), averages 16.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Madīnat Ḩamad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Madīnat Ḩamad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
