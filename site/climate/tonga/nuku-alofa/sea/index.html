Nuku‘alofa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tongatapu · Tonga

The sea off Nuku‘alofa averages 27.6 °C (82 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 23.6 °C (74 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to July.

Nuku‘alofa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nuku‘alofa?
In February, at about 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nuku‘alofa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.6 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nuku‘alofa in August?
About 23.6 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Nuku‘alofa?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Nuku‘alofa nearby?
In February: Alofi (28.3 °C, 598 km), Nasinu (28.5 °C, 741 km) and Suva (28.5 °C, 746 km).
