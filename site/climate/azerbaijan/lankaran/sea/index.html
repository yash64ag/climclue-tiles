Lankaran sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Lənkəran · Azerbaijan

The sea off Lankaran averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.2 °C (49 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Lankaran sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lankaran?
In August, at about 26.5 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lankaran?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 9.2 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lankaran in March?
About 9.2 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lankaran?
About 17.5 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Lankaran nearby?
In August: Āstārā (27.1 °C, 36 km), Neftçala (27.1 °C, 77 km) and Hashtpar (27.2 °C, 107 km).
