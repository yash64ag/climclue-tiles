Bakıxanov sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Baki · Azerbaijan

The sea off Bakıxanov averages 25.8 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.4 °C (44 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Bakıxanov sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bakıxanov?
In August, at about 25.8 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bakıxanov?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 6.4 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bakıxanov in February?
About 6.4 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bakıxanov?
About 15.1 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
