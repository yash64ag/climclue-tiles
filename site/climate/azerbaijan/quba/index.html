Quba at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Azerbaijan, Quba is the 4th coolest (10.1 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 1,887 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Qusar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Quba is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Quba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.361°N, 48.513°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-426-2285), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
