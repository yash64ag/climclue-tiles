Yeni Suraxanı at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Azerbaijan, Yeni Suraxanı is 26th warmest (14.9 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,144 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (230 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amirdzhan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Yeni Suraxanı is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, February (5.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Yeni Suraxanı has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-435-2300), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
