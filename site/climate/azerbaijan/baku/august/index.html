Baku weather in August

Average August weather in Baku, Azerbaijan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Baku averages 30.2 °C (86 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 270 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Baku

August is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Baku. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. September is 3.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Baku in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in August?
In August, Baku averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Baku?
August is not one of Baku’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in August?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (34 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in August?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Baku in August?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Baku in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
