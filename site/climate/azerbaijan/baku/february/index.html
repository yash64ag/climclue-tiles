How February compares in Baku

February is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Baku. Compared with January: 0.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. March is 1.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Baku in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in February?
In February, Baku averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Baku?
February is not one of Baku’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in February?
22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (37 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in February?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baku in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
