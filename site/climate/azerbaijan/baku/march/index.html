How March compares in Baku

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Baku. Compared with February: 1.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 4.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Baku in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in March?
In March, Baku averages a day high of 8.8 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Baku?
March is not one of Baku’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in March?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (37 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in March?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baku in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
