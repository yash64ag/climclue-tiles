How May compares in Baku

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Baku. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 76 hours more sunshine. June is 5.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Baku in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in May?
In May, Baku averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Baku?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Baku’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in May?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (37 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in May?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baku in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
