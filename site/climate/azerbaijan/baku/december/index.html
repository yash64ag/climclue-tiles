How December compares in Baku

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Baku. Compared with November: 4.0 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Baku in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in December?
In December, Baku averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Baku?
December is not one of Baku’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (37 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in December?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baku in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
