Baku weather in April

Average April weather in Baku, Azerbaijan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Baku averages 15.7 °C (60 °F) days and 6.8 °C (44 °F) nights, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain over the month and about 187 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Baku

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Baku. Compared with March: 4.0 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Baku in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in April?
In April, Baku averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Baku?
April is not one of Baku’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in April?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (34 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in April?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Baku in April?
About 10.0 °C (50 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Baku in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
