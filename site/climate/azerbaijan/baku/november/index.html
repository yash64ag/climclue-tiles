How November compares in Baku

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Baku. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 4.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Baku in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baku in November?
In November, Baku averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 92 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Baku?
November is not one of Baku’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baku in November?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (34 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Baku in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baku get in November?
About 92 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Baku in November?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Baku in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
