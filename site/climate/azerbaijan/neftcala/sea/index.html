Neftçala sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Azerbaijan

The sea off Neftçala averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.8 °C (48 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Neftçala sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Neftçala?
In August, at about 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Neftçala?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 8.8 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Neftçala in February?
About 8.8 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Neftçala?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
