Siyazan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Siyǝzǝn · Azerbaijan

The sea off Siyazan averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.9 °C (44 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Siyazan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Siyazan?
In August, at about 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Siyazan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 6.9 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Siyazan in February?
About 6.9 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Siyazan?
About 14.7 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Siyazan nearby?
In August: Maştağa (25.8 °C, 97 km) and Baku (26.1 °C, 102 km).
