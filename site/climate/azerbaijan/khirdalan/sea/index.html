Khirdalan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Abşeron · Azerbaijan

The sea off Khirdalan averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.3 °C (43 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Khirdalan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Khirdalan?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Khirdalan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 6.3 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Khirdalan in February?
About 6.3 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Khirdalan?
About 15.0 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
