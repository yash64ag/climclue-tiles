Shusha at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Azerbaijan, Shusha is the coolest (8.2 °C year average) and the 3rd sunniest (about 2,394 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Aghdam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Shusha is 5.8 °C cooler in July and 4.7 °C cooler in January, with 285 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 214 km from the sea.

Why Shusha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 214 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shusha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.760°N, 46.750°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-442-2267), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
