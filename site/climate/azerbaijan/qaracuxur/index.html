Qaraçuxur at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Azerbaijan, Qaraçuxur is 38th warmest (14.7 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,123 hours of sunshine a year) and 52nd wettest (235 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bakıxanov, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Qaraçuxur is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, February (5.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Qaraçuxur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Qaraçuxur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.397°N, 49.974°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-436-2299), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
