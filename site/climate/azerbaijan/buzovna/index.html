Buzovna at a glance

Among the 65 towns ClimaClue covers in Azerbaijan, Buzovna is the 5th driest (220 mm a year). Compared with Mardakan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Buzovna is about as warm in August and about as warm in February, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.7 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, February (5.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Buzovna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Buzovna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Buzovna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.519°N, 50.114°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-434-2301), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
