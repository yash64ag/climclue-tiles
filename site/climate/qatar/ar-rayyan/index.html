Ar Rayyān at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Qatar, Ar Rayyān is the warmest (27.3 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,397 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (93 mm a year). Compared with Doha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Ar Rayyān is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.4 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.2 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ar Rayyān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ar Rayyān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
