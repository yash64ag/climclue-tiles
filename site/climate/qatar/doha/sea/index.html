Doha sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Baladiyat ad Dawhah · Qatar

The sea off Doha averages 33.5 °C (92 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 19.0 °C (66 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Doha sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Doha?
In August, at about 33.5 °C (92 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Doha?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 19.0 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Doha in February?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Doha?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
