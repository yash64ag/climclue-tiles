Al Wakrah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Qatar

The sea off Al Wakrah averages 33.7 °C (93 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 19.2 °C (67 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Al Wakrah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Al Wakrah?
In August, at about 33.7 °C (93 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Al Wakrah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 19.2 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Al Wakrah in February?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Al Wakrah?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
