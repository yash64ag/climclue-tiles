Reino de Antula at a glance

Among the 9 towns ClimaClue covers in Guinea-Bissau, Reino de Antula is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,675 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (1,553 mm a year). Compared with Plaque, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Reino de Antula is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.2 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Why Reino de Antula has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Reino de Antula itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.900°N, 15.583°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-720-1644), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
