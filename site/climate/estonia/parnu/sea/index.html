Pärnu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pärnumaa · Estonia

The sea off Pärnu averages 17.8 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.3 °C (31 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Pärnu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pärnu?
In August, at about 17.8 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pärnu?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -0.3 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pärnu in February?
About -0.3 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pärnu?
About 7.6 °C (46 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pärnu nearby?
In August: Riga (18.9 °C, 162 km), Jūrmala (18.8 °C, 163 km) and Mārupe (18.9 °C, 167 km).
