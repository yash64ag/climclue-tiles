Maardu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Harjumaa · Estonia

The sea off Maardu averages 17.3 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -1.2 °C (30 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Maardu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Maardu?
In August, at about 17.3 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Maardu?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at -1.2 °C (30 °F).
How warm is the sea in Maardu in February?
About -1.2 °C (30 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Maardu?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
