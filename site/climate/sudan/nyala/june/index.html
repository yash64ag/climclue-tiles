How June compares in Nyala

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with May: 1.8 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. July is 2.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Nyala in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in June?
In June, Nyala averages a day high of 35.5 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nyala?
June is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in June?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (118 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in June?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
