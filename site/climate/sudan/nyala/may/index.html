How May compares in Nyala

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with April: 0.5 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. June is 1.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Nyala in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in May?
In May, Nyala averages a day high of 37.3 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nyala?
May is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in May?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (123 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in May?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
