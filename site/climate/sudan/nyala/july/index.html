How July compares in Nyala

July is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with June: 2.8 °C cooler, 69 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Nyala in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in July?
In July, Nyala averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nyala?
July is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in July?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (118 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in July?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
