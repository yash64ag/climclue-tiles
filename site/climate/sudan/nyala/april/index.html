How April compares in Nyala

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with March: 2.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Nyala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in April?
In April, Nyala averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nyala?
April is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in April?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (123 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in April?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
