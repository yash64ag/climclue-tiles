How March compares in Nyala

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 2.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Nyala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in March?
In March, Nyala averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nyala?
March is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in March?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (118 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in March?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
