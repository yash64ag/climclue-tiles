How October compares in Nyala

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with September: 1.6 °C warmer, 45 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. November is 1.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Nyala in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in October?
In October, Nyala averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nyala?
October is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in October?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (118 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in October?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
