How September compares in Nyala

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nyala. Compared with August: 0.8 °C warmer, 57 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. October is 1.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Nyala in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nyala in September?
In September, Nyala averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nyala?
September is not one of Nyala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nyala in September?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (123 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nyala in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nyala get in September?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nyala in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
