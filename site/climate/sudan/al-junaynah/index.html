Al-Junaynah at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Sudan, Al-Junaynah is 68th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 3,317 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (459 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Geneina Fort, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Al-Junaynah is about as warm in May and about as warm in December, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.3 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, December (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,717 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al-Junaynah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Al-Junaynah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,717 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Al-Junaynah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.453°N, 22.447°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-705-2024), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
