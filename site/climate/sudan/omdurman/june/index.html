How June compares in Omdurman

June is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Omdurman. Compared with May: 0.1 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. July is 2.0 °C cooler than June on average.

Omdurman in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Omdurman in June?
In June, Omdurman averages a day high of 40.1 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 26.5 °C (80 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Omdurman?
June is not one of Omdurman’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Omdurman in June?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest February (0 mm).
Does it snow in Omdurman in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Omdurman get in June?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Omdurman in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
