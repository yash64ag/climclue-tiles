Atbara at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, Atbara is the 3rd warmest (29.9 °C year average), the 4th sunniest (about 3,473 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (37 mm a year). Compared with Ad-Damir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Atbara is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (35.4 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 397 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Atbara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Atbara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 397 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
