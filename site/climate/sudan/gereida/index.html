Gereida at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, Gereida is the 3rd least sunny (about 3,040 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (458 mm a year). Compared with Nyala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (91 km away), Gereida is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,576 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gereida, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gereida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,576 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
