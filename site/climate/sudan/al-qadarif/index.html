Al Qadarif at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, Al Qadarif is the 2nd wettest (589 mm a year). Compared with Singa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (185 km away), Al Qadarif is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (33.1 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 479 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Qadarif, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Qadarif has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 479 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
