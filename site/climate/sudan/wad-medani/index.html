Wad Medani at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, Wad Medani is the warmest (30.0 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 3,453 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Al Manāqil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (60 km away), Wad Medani is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.3 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 644 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wad Medani, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wad Medani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 644 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
