Singa at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, Singa is the 3rd wettest (497 mm a year). Compared with Sennar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Singa is about as warm in April and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 120 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (33.5 °C), averages 7.3 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.5 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 659 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Singa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Singa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 659 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
