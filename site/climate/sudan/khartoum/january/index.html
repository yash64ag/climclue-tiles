How January compares in Khartoum

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Khartoum. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.
