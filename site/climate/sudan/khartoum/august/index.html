How August compares in Khartoum

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Khartoum. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.8 °C warmer than August on average.

Khartoum in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khartoum in August?
In August, Khartoum averages a day high of 37.2 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Khartoum?
August is not one of Khartoum’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khartoum in August?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest February (0 mm).
Does it snow in Khartoum in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khartoum get in August?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khartoum in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
