How May compares in Khartoum

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Khartoum. Compared with April: 2.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. June is about as warm as May on average.

Khartoum in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khartoum in May?
In May, Khartoum averages a day high of 40.5 °C (105 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Khartoum?
May is not one of Khartoum’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khartoum in May?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (47 mm), the driest February (0 mm).
Does it snow in Khartoum in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khartoum get in May?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khartoum in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
