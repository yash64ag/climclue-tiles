Kosti at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Sudan, Kosti is 36th warmest (29.2 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 3,114 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (311 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rabak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Kosti is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.0 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 785 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kosti, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kosti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 785 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kosti itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.163°N, 32.663°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-708-2126), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
