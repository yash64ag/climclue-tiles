El Geneina Fort at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, El Geneina Fort is the coolest (25.6 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (451 mm a year). Compared with Nyala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (307 km away), El Geneina Fort is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 64 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,717 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Geneina Fort, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.
