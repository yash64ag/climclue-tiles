Rufā‘a weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Rufā‘a, Sudan (Al Jazirah), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Rufā‘a at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Sudan, Rufā‘a is the 4th warmest (30.0 °C year average). Compared with Al Hasaheisa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Rufā‘a is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.4 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, January (24.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 638 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Rufā‘a has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 638 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rufā‘a itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.760°N, 33.368°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-692-2133), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
