Kuraymah at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Sudan, Kuraymah is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,683 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (12 mm a year). Compared with Dongola, the nearest covered town with different numbers (161 km away), Kuraymah is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (35.2 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (19.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 587 km from the sea.

Why Kuraymah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 587 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-654-2118), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
