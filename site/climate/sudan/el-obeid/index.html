El Obeid at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sudan, El Obeid is 18th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,313 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (358 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with An Nuhūd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (201 km away), El Obeid is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 10.2 °C more than its coolest, January (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,005 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Obeid, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Obeid has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,005 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
