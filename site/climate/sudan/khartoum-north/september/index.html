Khartoum North in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khartoum North in September?
In September, Khartoum North averages a day high of 39.1 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Khartoum North?
September is not one of Khartoum North’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khartoum North in September?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest February (0 mm).
Does it snow in Khartoum North in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).

About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
