Khartoum North in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Khartoum North in October?
In October, Khartoum North averages a day high of 39.4 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 298 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Khartoum North?
October is not one of Khartoum North’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Khartoum North in October?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest February (0 mm).
Does it snow in Khartoum North in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Khartoum North get in October?
About 298 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Khartoum North in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
