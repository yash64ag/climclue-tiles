Isidro Casanova at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Isidro Casanova is 32nd warmest (16.6 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,560 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (1,128 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Justo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Isidro Casanova is 0.1 °C warmer in January and about as warm in July, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.8 °C), averages 14.1 °C more than its coolest, July (9.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Isidro Casanova, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Isidro Casanova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
