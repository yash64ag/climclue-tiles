Libertador General San Martín at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Libertador General San Martín is 50th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 276th sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 189th wettest (1,006 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro de Jujuy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Libertador General San Martín is 0.2 °C warmer in January and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 325 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.3 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, July (13.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 587 km from the sea.

Why Libertador General San Martín has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 587 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Libertador General San Martín itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.813°S, 64.794°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1078-1152), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
