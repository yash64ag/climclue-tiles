Lanús sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Buenos Aires · Argentina

The sea off Lanús averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 12.2 °C (54 °F) in July. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to March.

Lanús sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lanús?
In January, at about 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lanús?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to March. The coolest month is July, at 12.2 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lanús in July?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lanús?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Lanús nearby?
In January: Berisso (24.4 °C, 50 km).
