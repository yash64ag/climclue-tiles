Cutral-Có at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Cutral-Có is 302nd warmest (13.1 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,889 hours of sunshine a year) and 301st wettest (232 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zapala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Cutral-Có is 3.4 °C warmer in January and 2.2 °C warmer in July, with 146 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.9 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, July (4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in June, July and August. The area lies about 355 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cutral-Có, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cutral-Có has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 355 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cutral-Có itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.934°S, 69.231°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1229-1107), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
