Puerto Madryn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chubut · Argentina

The sea off Puerto Madryn averages 17.0 °C (63 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 9.9 °C (50 °F) in September. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Puerto Madryn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto Madryn?
In February, at about 17.0 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto Madryn?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is September, at 9.9 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto Madryn in September?
About 9.9 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto Madryn?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Puerto Madryn nearby?
In February: San Antonio Oeste (18.5 °C, 227 km), Punta Alta (21.3 °C, 499 km) and Bahía Blanca (21.3 °C, 507 km).
