Ezeiza at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Ezeiza is 37th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,584 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (1,110 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Laferrere, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Ezeiza is 0.2 °C cooler in January and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.6 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, July (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ezeiza, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ezeiza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ezeiza itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.854°S, 58.525°W). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1188-1214), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
