Resistencia at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Resistencia is the 4th wettest (1,494 mm a year). Compared with Corrientes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Resistencia is about as warm in January and about as warm in July, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.9 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 760 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Resistencia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Resistencia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 760 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
