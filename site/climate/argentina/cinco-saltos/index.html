Cinco Saltos at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Cinco Saltos is 267th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 2,716 hours of sunshine a year) and 298th wettest (239 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Centenario, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Cinco Saltos is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.1 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, July (6.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 0.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 346 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cinco Saltos, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Cinco Saltos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 346 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cinco Saltos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.822°S, 68.063°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1228-1119), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
