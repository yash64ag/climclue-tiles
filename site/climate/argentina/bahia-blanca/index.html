Bahía Blanca at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Bahía Blanca is the 4th coolest (15.5 °C year average). Compared with Santa Rosa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (294 km away), Bahía Blanca is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 15.3 °C more than its coolest, July (8.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bahía Blanca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bahía Blanca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
