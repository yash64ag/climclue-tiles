San Salvador de Jujuy at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, San Salvador de Jujuy is the 5th least sunny (about 2,345 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Salta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), San Salvador de Jujuy is 1.6 °C cooler in January and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 522 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.1 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, July (10.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 540 km from the sea.

Why San Salvador de Jujuy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 540 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
