How March compares in Salta

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Salta. Compared with February: 0.8 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. April is 2.6 °C cooler than March on average.

Salta in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salta in March?
In March, Salta averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Salta?
March is not one of Salta’s mild months; those are April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salta in March?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Salta in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salta get in March?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salta in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
