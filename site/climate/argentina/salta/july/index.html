How July compares in Salta

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Salta. Compared with June: 0.6 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. August is 2.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Salta in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salta in July?
In July, Salta averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Salta?
July is not one of Salta’s mild months; those are April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salta in July?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Salta in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salta get in July?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salta in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
