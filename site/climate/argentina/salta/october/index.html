Salta weather in October

Average October weather in Salta, Argentina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Salta averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) days and 11.3 °C (52 °F) nights, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 181 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Salta

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Salta. Compared with September: 3.4 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 1.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Salta in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salta in October?
In October, Salta averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Salta?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Salta’s mild months (April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salta in October?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (194 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Salta in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salta get in October?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salta in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
