How December compares in Salta

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Salta. Compared with November: 1.1 °C warmer, 79 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Salta in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salta in December?
In December, Salta averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Salta?
December is not one of Salta’s mild months; those are April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salta in December?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (194 mm), the driest June (3 mm).
Does it snow in Salta in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salta get in December?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salta in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
