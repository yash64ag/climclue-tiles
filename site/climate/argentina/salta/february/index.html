How February compares in Salta

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Salta. Compared with January: 0.8 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. March is 0.8 °C cooler than February on average.

Salta in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salta in February?
In February, Salta averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Salta?
February is not one of Salta’s mild months; those are April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salta in February?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Salta in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salta get in February?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salta in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
