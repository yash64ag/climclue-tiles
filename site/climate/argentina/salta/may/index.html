How May compares in Salta

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Salta. Compared with April: 3.1 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. June is 2.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Salta in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salta in May?
In May, Salta averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Salta?
May is not one of Salta’s mild months; those are April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salta in May?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (204 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Salta in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salta get in May?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salta in May?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
