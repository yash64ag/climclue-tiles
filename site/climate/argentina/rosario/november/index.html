How November compares in Rosario

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Rosario. Compared with October: 2.8 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. December is 2.5 °C warmer than November on average.

Rosario in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rosario in November?
In November, Rosario averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rosario?
No month in Rosario is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rosario in November?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (147 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Rosario in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rosario get in November?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rosario in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
