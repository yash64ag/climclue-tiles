How May compares in Rosario

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Rosario. Compared with April: 3.5 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. June is 3.0 °C cooler than May on average.
