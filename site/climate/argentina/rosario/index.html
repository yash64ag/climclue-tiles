Why Rosario has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 264 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Rosario climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Rosario?
January, with an average of 24.8 °C (77 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Rosario?
July, with an average of 10.6 °C (51 °F), day highs around 15.3 °C (60 °F) and night lows around 7.2 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Rosario?
March, with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,067 mm (42.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Rosario?
July, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Rosario?
January, with about 292 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Rosario?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Rosario?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Rosario have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Rosario’s GeoNames location (32.947°S, 60.639°W; ClimaClue area sq-1169-1193), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 948,312 (GeoNames).
