How April compares in Rosario

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Rosario. Compared with March: 4.0 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. May is 3.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Rosario in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rosario in April?
In April, Rosario averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Rosario?
No month in Rosario is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rosario in April?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (147 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Rosario in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rosario get in April?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rosario in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
