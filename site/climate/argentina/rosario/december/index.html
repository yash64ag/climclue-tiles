How December compares in Rosario

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rosario. Compared with November: 2.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Rosario in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rosario in December?
In December, Rosario averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Rosario?
No month in Rosario is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rosario in December?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (147 mm), the driest July (30 mm).
Does it snow in Rosario in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rosario get in December?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rosario in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
