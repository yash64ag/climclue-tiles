Morteros at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Morteros is 84th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 2,669 hours of sunshine a year) and 158th wettest (1,061 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sunchales, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Morteros is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.3 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, July (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 530 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Morteros, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Morteros has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 530 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Morteros itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.711°S, 62.007°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1147-1179), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
