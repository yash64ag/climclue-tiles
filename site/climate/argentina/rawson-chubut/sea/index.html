Rawson, Chubut sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chubut · Argentina

The sea off Rawson, Chubut averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 8.6 °C (47 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Rawson, Chubut sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rawson, Chubut?
In February, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rawson, Chubut?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 8.6 °C (47 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rawson, Chubut in August?

What is the average sea temperature in Rawson, Chubut?
