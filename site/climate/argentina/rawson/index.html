Rawson at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Rawson is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,160 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (97 mm a year). Compared with Mendoza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (148 km away), Rawson is 1.6 °C warmer in January and 1.0 °C warmer in July, with 149 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.6 °C), averages 17.0 °C more than its coolest, July (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 294 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rawson, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rawson has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 294 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
