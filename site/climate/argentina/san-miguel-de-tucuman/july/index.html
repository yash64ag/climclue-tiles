How July compares in San Miguel de Tucumán

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in San Miguel de Tucumán. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. August is 2.8 °C warmer than July on average.

San Miguel de Tucumán in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in July?
In July, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
July is not one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months; those are April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in July?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (250 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in July?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
