How September compares in San Miguel de Tucumán

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in San Miguel de Tucumán. Compared with August: 2.7 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. October is 3.8 °C warmer than September on average.

San Miguel de Tucumán in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in September?
In September, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months (April, May, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in September?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (243 mm), the driest August (12 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in September?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
