How January compares in San Miguel de Tucumán

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in San Miguel de Tucumán. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 71 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 1.1 °C cooler than January on average.

San Miguel de Tucumán in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in January?
In January, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 250 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
January is not one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months; those are April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in January?
250 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (250 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in January?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
