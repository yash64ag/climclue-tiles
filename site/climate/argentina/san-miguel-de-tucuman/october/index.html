San Miguel de Tucumán in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in October?
In October, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months (April, May, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in October?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (250 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in October?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
