San Miguel de Tucumán in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in April?
In April, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months (April, May, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in April?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (243 mm), the driest August (12 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in April?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
