How June compares in San Miguel de Tucumán

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in San Miguel de Tucumán. Compared with May: 3.0 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

San Miguel de Tucumán in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in June?
In June, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
June is not one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months; those are April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in June?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (243 mm), the driest August (12 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in June?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
