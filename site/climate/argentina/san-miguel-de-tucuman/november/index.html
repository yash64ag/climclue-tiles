San Miguel de Tucumán in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in November?
In November, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
November is not one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months; those are April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in November?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (250 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in November?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
