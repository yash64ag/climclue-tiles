How December compares in San Miguel de Tucumán

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in San Miguel de Tucumán. Compared with November: 1.4 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

San Miguel de Tucumán in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Miguel de Tucumán in December?
In December, San Miguel de Tucumán averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Miguel de Tucumán?
December is not one of San Miguel de Tucumán’s mild months; those are April, May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Miguel de Tucumán in December?
179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (250 mm), the driest August (13 mm).
Does it snow in San Miguel de Tucumán in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Miguel de Tucumán get in December?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Miguel de Tucumán in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
