San Luis at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, San Luis is the 4th sunniest (about 3,021 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Río Cuarto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (184 km away), San Luis is 1.2 °C warmer in January and 1.1 °C warmer in July, with 251 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.4 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, July (9.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 490 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Luis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Luis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 490 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
