Why San Luis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 490 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

San Luis climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in San Luis?
January, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 29.8 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 19.3 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in San Luis?
July, with an average of 9.9 °C (50 °F), day highs around 16.1 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 5.3 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in San Luis?
January, with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,133 mm (44.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in San Luis?
June, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in San Luis?
January, with about 314 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in San Luis?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in San Luis?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does San Luis have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains San Luis’s GeoNames location (33.291°S, 66.325°W; ClimaClue area sq-1172-1136), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 169,947 (GeoNames).
