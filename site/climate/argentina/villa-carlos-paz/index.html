Villa Carlos Paz at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Villa Carlos Paz is 221st warmest (16.6 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 2,642 hours of sunshine a year) and 199th wettest (988 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Calera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Villa Carlos Paz is 0.8 °C cooler in January and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.6 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, July (9.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 654 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Villa Carlos Paz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 654 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Villa Carlos Paz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.418°S, 64.490°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1154-1155), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
