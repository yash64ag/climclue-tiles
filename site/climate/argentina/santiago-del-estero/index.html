Why Santiago del Estero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 657 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Santiago del Estero climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Santiago del Estero?
January, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 32.3 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 22.5 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Santiago del Estero?
July, with an average of 14.2 °C (57 °F), day highs around 20.7 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 9.3 °C (49 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Santiago del Estero?
January, with 131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 619 mm (24.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Santiago del Estero?
August, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Santiago del Estero?
December, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Santiago del Estero?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Santiago del Estero?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Santiago del Estero’s GeoNames location (27.800°S, 64.263°W; ClimaClue area sq-1117-1157), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 252,192 (GeoNames).
