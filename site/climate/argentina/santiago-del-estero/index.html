Santiago del Estero at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Santiago del Estero is the 3rd warmest (21.6 °C year average). Compared with San Miguel de Tucumán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (144 km away), Santiago del Estero is 2.5 °C warmer in January and 1.6 °C warmer in July, with 601 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (27.4 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, July (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 657 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santiago del Estero, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Santiago del Estero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 657 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
