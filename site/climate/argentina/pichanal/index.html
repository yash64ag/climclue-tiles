Pichanal at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Pichanal is the 3rd warmest (22.7 °C year average). Compared with Embarcación, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Pichanal is 0.1 °C cooler in December and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 98 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.2 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, July (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 629 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pichanal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pichanal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 629 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pichanal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.316°S, 64.218°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1073-1157), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
