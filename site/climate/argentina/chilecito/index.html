Chilecito at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Chilecito is the 5th sunniest (about 3,182 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with La Rioja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Chilecito is 4.6 °C cooler in January and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 134 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.8 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, July (10.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 381 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chilecito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chilecito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 381 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chilecito itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.162°S, 67.499°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1131-1125), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
