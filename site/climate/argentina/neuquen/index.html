Neuquén at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Neuquén is the 3rd driest (230 mm a year). Compared with Santa Rosa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (421 km away), Neuquén is 1.2 °C warmer in January and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 512 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.6 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, July (6.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 339 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Neuquén, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Neuquén has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 339 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
