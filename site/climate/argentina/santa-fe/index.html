Santa Fe at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Santa Fe is 11th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,765 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (1,116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paraná, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Santa Fe is 0.2 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.6 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, July (11.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in July. The area lies about 374 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santa Fe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Santa Fe

For mild weather, the best months to visit Santa Fe are May and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May and September.

Why Santa Fe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 374 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
