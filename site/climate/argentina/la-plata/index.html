La Plata at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, La Plata is the 4th least sunny (about 2,319 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Berazategui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), La Plata is about as warm in January and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, July (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of La Plata, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why La Plata has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
