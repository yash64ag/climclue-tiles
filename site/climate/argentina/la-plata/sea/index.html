La Plata sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Buenos Aires · Argentina

The sea off La Plata averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 12.0 °C (54 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to February.

La Plata sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in La Plata?
In January, at about 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in La Plata?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from January to February. The coolest month is July, at 12.0 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in La Plata in July?
About 12.0 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in La Plata?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
