Comodoro Rivadavia at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Comodoro Rivadavia is the coolest (12.2 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,092 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (297 mm a year). Compared with Neuquén, the nearest covered town with different numbers (770 km away), Comodoro Rivadavia is 6.7 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (18.0 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, July (6.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Comodoro Rivadavia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Comodoro Rivadavia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
