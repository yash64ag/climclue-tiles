Corrientes at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Corrientes is the 5th warmest (21.3 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (1,504 mm a year). Compared with Resistencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Corrientes is about as warm in January and about as warm in July, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.9 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 759 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Corrientes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Corrientes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 759 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
