Catriel at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Catriel is 239th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,857 hours of sunshine a year) and 287th wettest (271 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cinco Saltos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Catriel is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.1 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, July (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 415 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Catriel, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Catriel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 415 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Catriel itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.876°S, 67.795°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1218-1122), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
