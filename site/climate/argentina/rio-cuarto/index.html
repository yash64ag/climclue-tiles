Río Cuarto at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Río Cuarto is 35th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,722 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (882 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Luis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (184 km away), Río Cuarto is 1.2 °C cooler in January and 1.1 °C cooler in July, with 251 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.3 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, July (8.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in July. The area lies about 570 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Río Cuarto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Río Cuarto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 570 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
