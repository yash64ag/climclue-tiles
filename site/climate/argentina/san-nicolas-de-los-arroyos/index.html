San Nicolás de los Arroyos at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, San Nicolás de los Arroyos is 123rd warmest (17.6 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,683 hours of sunshine a year) and 134th wettest (1,096 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Villa Constitución, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), San Nicolás de los Arroyos is about as warm in January and about as warm in July, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.7 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, July (10.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in July. The area lies about 208 km from the sea.

Why San Nicolás de los Arroyos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 208 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Nicolás de los Arroyos itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.334°S, 60.211°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1173-1197), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
