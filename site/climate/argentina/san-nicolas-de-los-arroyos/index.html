San Nicolás de los Arroyos at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, San Nicolás de los Arroyos is 16th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,683 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (1,089 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rosario, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), San Nicolás de los Arroyos is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.6 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, July (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 208 km from the sea.

Why San Nicolás de los Arroyos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 208 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
