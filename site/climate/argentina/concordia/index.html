Why Concordia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 323 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Concordia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Concordia?
January, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 20.9 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Concordia?
July, with an average of 12.1 °C (54 °F), day highs around 16.7 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 8.5 °C (47 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Concordia?
April, with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,329 mm (52.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Concordia?
July, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Concordia?
December, with about 287 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Concordia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Concordia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Concordia’s GeoNames location (31.392°S, 58.017°W; ClimaClue area sq-1153-1219), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 145,210 (GeoNames).
