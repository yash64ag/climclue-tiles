Río Grande sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tierra del Fuego · Argentina

The sea off Río Grande averages 10.4 °C (51 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 3.5 °C (38 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Río Grande sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Río Grande?
In February, at about 10.4 °C (51 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Río Grande?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 3.5 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Río Grande in August?
About 3.5 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Río Grande?
About 6.9 °C (44 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Río Grande nearby?
In February: Puerto Deseado (13.3 °C, 683 km), Caleta Olivia (15.2 °C, 816 km) and Comodoro Rivadavia (14.7 °C, 881 km).
