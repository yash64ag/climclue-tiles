How April compares in Mar del Plata

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mar del Plata. Compared with March: 3.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. May is 3.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Mar del Plata in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in April?
In April, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mar del Plata’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in April?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (97 mm), the driest August (55 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in April?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
