How July compares in Mar del Plata

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mar del Plata. Compared with June: 1.0 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. August is 1.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Mar del Plata in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in July?
In July, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
July is not one of Mar del Plata’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in July?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (97 mm), the driest August (55 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in July?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
