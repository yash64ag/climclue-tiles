How January compares in Mar del Plata

January is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mar del Plata. Compared with December: 1.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Mar del Plata in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in January?
In January, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Mar del Plata’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in January?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (97 mm), the driest August (55 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in January?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
