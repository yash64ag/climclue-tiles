How October compares in Mar del Plata

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mar del Plata. Compared with September: 2.6 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. November is 2.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Mar del Plata in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in October?
In October, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
October is not one of Mar del Plata’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in October?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (98 mm), the driest August (56 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in October?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mar del Plata in October?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (20.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
