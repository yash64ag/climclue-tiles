How December compares in Mar del Plata

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mar del Plata. Compared with November: 2.5 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 2.0 °C warmer than December on average.

Mar del Plata in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in December?
In December, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Mar del Plata’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in December?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (98 mm), the driest August (56 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in December?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mar del Plata in December?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (20.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
