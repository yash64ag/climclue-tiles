Mar del Plata at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Mar del Plata is the 3rd coolest (14.3 °C year average). Compared with Tandil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (158 km away), Mar del Plata is 0.2 °C cooler in January and 1.8 °C warmer in July, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.4 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, July (8.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mar del Plata, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Mar del Plata

For mild weather, the best months to visit Mar del Plata are January, February, March, April, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, April, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): January (20.4 °C), February (19.9 °C) and March (18.6 °C).

Why Mar del Plata has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
