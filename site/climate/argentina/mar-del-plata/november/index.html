How November compares in Mar del Plata

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Mar del Plata. Compared with October: 2.6 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. December is 2.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Mar del Plata in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in November?
In November, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Mar del Plata’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in November?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (97 mm), the driest August (55 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in November?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
