How June compares in Mar del Plata

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mar del Plata. Compared with May: 2.8 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. July is 1.0 °C cooler than June on average.

Mar del Plata in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in June?
In June, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
June is not one of Mar del Plata’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in June?
60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (97 mm), the driest August (55 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in June?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
