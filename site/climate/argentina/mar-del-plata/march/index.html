How March compares in Mar del Plata

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mar del Plata. Compared with February: 1.3 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. April is 3.2 °C cooler than March on average.

Mar del Plata in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mar del Plata in March?
In March, Mar del Plata averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mar del Plata?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Mar del Plata’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mar del Plata in March?
98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (98 mm), the driest August (56 mm).
Does it snow in Mar del Plata in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mar del Plata get in March?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mar del Plata in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
