How June compares in Buenos Aires

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Buenos Aires. Compared with May: 2.9 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. July is 0.9 °C cooler than June on average.

Buenos Aires in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in June?
In June, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in June?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in June?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Buenos Aires in June?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is January (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
