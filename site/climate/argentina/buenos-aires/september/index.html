How September compares in Buenos Aires

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with August: 1.5 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. October is 3.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Buenos Aires in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in September?
In September, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in September?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in September?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
