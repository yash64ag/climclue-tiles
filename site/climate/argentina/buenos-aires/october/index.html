Buenos Aires weather in October

Average October weather in Buenos Aires, Argentina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Buenos Aires averages 20.9 °C (70 °F) days and 12.0 °C (54 °F) nights, with 124 mm (4.9 in) of rain over the month and about 222 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Buenos Aires

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with September: 3.1 °C warmer, 55 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. November is 2.7 °C warmer than October on average.

Buenos Aires in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in October?
In October, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in October?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in October?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Buenos Aires in October?
About 16.9 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is January (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
