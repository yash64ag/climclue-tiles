How March compares in Buenos Aires

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with February: 1.4 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. April is 3.7 °C cooler than March on average.

Buenos Aires in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in March?
In March, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in March?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in March?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
