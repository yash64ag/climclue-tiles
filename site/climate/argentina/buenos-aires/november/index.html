Buenos Aires weather in November

Average November weather in Buenos Aires, Argentina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Buenos Aires averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) days and 14.4 °C (58 °F) nights, with 118 mm (4.7 in) of rain over the month and about 245 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Buenos Aires

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with October: 2.7 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. December is 2.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Buenos Aires in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in November?
In November, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in November?
118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in November?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Buenos Aires in November?
About 19.7 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is January (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
