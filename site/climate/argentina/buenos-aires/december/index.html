Buenos Aires weather in December

Average December weather in Buenos Aires, Argentina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Buenos Aires averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) days and 16.9 °C (62 °F) nights, with 116 mm (4.6 in) of rain over the month and about 259 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Buenos Aires

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with November: 2.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. January is 1.7 °C warmer than December on average.

Buenos Aires in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in December?
In December, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in December?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in December?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Buenos Aires in December?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is January (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
