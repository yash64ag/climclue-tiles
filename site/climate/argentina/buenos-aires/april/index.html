How April compares in Buenos Aires

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with March: 3.8 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. May is 3.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Buenos Aires in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in April?
In April, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in April?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest June (52 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in April?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
