How August compares in Buenos Aires

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with July: 1.6 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. September is 1.6 °C warmer than August on average.

Buenos Aires in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in August?
In August, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in August?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest June (52 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in August?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in August?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
