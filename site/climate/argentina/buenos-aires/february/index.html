How February compares in Buenos Aires

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Buenos Aires. Compared with January: 1.1 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. March is 1.5 °C cooler than February on average.

Buenos Aires in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buenos Aires in February?
In February, Buenos Aires averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 123 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Buenos Aires?
No month in Buenos Aires is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buenos Aires in February?
123 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (55 mm).
Does it snow in Buenos Aires in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buenos Aires get in February?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Buenos Aires in February?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is January (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Buenos Aires in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
