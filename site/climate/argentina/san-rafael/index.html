Why San Rafael has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 332 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

San Rafael climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in San Rafael?
January, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 30.6 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 18.0 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in San Rafael?
July, with an average of 7.2 °C (45 °F), day highs around 14.0 °C (57 °F) and night lows around 1.8 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in San Rafael?
January, with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 404 mm (15.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in San Rafael?
June, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in San Rafael?
January, with about 328 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in San Rafael?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in July; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in San Rafael?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains San Rafael’s GeoNames location (34.615°S, 68.332°W; ClimaClue area sq-1186-1116), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,009 (GeoNames).
