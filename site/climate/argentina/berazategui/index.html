Berazategui at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Berazategui is 22nd warmest (16.7 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 2,411 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (1,113 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lomas de Zamora, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Berazategui is 0.2 °C cooler in January and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, July (10.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Berazategui, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Berazategui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
