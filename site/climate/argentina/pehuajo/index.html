Pehuajó at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Pehuajó is 255th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 160th sunniest (about 2,627 hours of sunshine a year) and 204th wettest (972 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Carlos Casares, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Pehuajó is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.1 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, July (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 335 km from the sea.

Why Pehuajó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 335 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1198-1181), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
