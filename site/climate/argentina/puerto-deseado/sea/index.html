Puerto Deseado sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Santa Cruz · Argentina

The sea off Puerto Deseado averages 13.3 °C (56 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 5.7 °C (42 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Puerto Deseado sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto Deseado?
In February, at about 13.3 °C (56 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto Deseado?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 5.7 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto Deseado in August?
About 5.7 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto Deseado?
About 9.4 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Puerto Deseado nearby?
In February: Caleta Olivia (15.2 °C, 190 km), Comodoro Rivadavia (14.7 °C, 243 km) and Rawson, Chubut (16.8 °C, 499 km).
