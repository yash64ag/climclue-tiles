Miramar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Buenos Aires · Argentina

The sea off Miramar averages 19.8 °C (68 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 10.2 °C (50 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Miramar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Miramar?
In February, at about 19.8 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Miramar?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 10.2 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Miramar in August?
About 10.2 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Miramar?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Miramar nearby?
In February: Villa Gesell (20.7 °C, 135 km), Pinamar (20.7 °C, 155 km) and Mar de Ajó (21.6 °C, 200 km).
