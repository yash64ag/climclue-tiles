Rosario de Lerma at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Rosario de Lerma is 178th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 305th sunniest (about 2,117 hours of sunshine a year) and 255th wettest (717 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Rosario de Lerma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 504 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
