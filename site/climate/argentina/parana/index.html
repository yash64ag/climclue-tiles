Paraná at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Paraná is 12th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,724 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,122 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Fe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Paraná is 0.2 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.4 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, July (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in July. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Paraná, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Paraná

For mild weather, the best months to visit Paraná are May and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): January (25.4 °C), February (23.9 °C) and December (23.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (31 mm), August (34 mm) and June (35 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): January (289 h), December (282 h) and November (276 h).

Why Paraná has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 354 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
