Río Segundo at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Río Segundo is 131st warmest (17.5 °C year average), 142nd sunniest (about 2,639 hours of sunshine a year) and 241st wettest (831 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pilar, Cordoba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Río Segundo is about as warm in January and about as warm in July, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.1 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, July (9.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 602 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Río Segundo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 602 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Río Segundo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.650°S, 63.912°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1156-1160), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
