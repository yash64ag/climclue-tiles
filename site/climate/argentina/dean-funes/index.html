Deán Funes at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Deán Funes is 146th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,719 hours of sunshine a year) and 251st wettest (754 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cruz del Eje, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Deán Funes is 2.6 °C cooler in January and 1.5 °C cooler in July, with 158 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, July (10.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 683 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Deán Funes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 683 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Deán Funes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.425°S, 64.353°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1144-1156), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
