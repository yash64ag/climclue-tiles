San Justo at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, San Justo is 25th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,560 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (1,144 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Castelar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), San Justo is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.6 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, July (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Justo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Justo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
