Ushuaia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tierra del Fuego · Argentina

The sea off Ushuaia averages 8.9 °C (48 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 4.8 °C (41 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Ushuaia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ushuaia?
In February, at about 8.9 °C (48 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ushuaia?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 4.8 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ushuaia in August?
About 4.8 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ushuaia?
About 6.8 °C (44 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ushuaia nearby?
In February: Río Grande (10.4 °C, 120 km), Puerto Natales (9.8 °C, 441 km) and Stanley (9.9 °C, 776 km).
