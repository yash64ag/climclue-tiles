Laferrere at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Laferrere is 33rd warmest (16.6 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,581 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (1,106 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Isidro Casanova, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Laferrere is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 37 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.8 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, July (9.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Laferrere, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Laferrere has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
