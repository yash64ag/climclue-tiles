Ituzaingó at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Ituzaingó is the 2nd warmest (21.8 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (1,746 mm a year). Compared with Posadas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Ituzaingó is 0.7 °C warmer in January and about as warm in July, with 114 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (27.4 °C), averages 11.7 °C more than its coolest, July (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 624 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ituzaingó, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ituzaingó has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 624 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
