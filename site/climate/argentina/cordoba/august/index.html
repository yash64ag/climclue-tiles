How August compares in Córdoba

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with July: 2.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. September is 2.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Córdoba in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in August?
In August, Córdoba averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Córdoba?
August is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in August?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in August?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
