How March compares in Córdoba

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with February: 1.5 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. April is 3.6 °C cooler than March on average.

Córdoba in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in March?
In March, Córdoba averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Córdoba?
March is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in March?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in March?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
