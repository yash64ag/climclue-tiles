How April compares in Córdoba

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with March: 3.6 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. May is 3.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Córdoba in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in April?
In April, Córdoba averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Córdoba?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Córdoba’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in April?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in April?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
