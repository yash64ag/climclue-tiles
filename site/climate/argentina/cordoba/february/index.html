How February compares in Córdoba

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with January: 1.4 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. March is 1.5 °C cooler than February on average.

Córdoba in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in February?
In February, Córdoba averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Córdoba?
February is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in February?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in February?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
