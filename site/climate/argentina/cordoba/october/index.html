How October compares in Córdoba

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with September: 4.0 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. November is 2.7 °C warmer than October on average.

Córdoba in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in October?
In October, Córdoba averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Córdoba?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Córdoba’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in October?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in October?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
