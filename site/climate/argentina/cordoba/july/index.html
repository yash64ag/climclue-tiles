How July compares in Córdoba

July is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with June: 0.7 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. August is 2.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Córdoba in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in July?
In July, Córdoba averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Córdoba?
July is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in July?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in July?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
