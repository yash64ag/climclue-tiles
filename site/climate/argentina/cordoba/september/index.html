Córdoba weather in September

Average September weather in Córdoba, Argentina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Córdoba averages 21.8 °C (71 °F) days and 7.4 °C (45 °F) nights, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 215 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Córdoba

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with August: 2.6 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. October is 3.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Córdoba in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in September?
In September, Córdoba averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Córdoba?
September is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in September?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (154 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
