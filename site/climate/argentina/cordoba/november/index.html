How November compares in Córdoba

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with October: 2.7 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. December is 2.0 °C warmer than November on average.

Córdoba in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in November?
In November, Córdoba averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Córdoba?
November is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in November?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in November?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
