Why Córdoba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 630 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Córdoba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Córdoba?
January, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 18.5 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Córdoba?
July, with an average of 9.7 °C (49 °F), day highs around 16.0 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 5.2 °C (41 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Córdoba?
December, with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 877 mm (34.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Córdoba?
June, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Córdoba?
January, with about 267 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Córdoba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Córdoba?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Córdoba have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Córdoba’s GeoNames location (31.406°S, 64.189°W; ClimaClue area sq-1154-1158), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,106,734 (GeoNames).
