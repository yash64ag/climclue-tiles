Córdoba at a glance

Among the 45 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Argentina, Córdoba is 19th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,592 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (877 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Río Cuarto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (192 km away), Córdoba is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.6 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, July (9.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in July. The area lies about 630 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Córdoba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Córdoba

For mild weather, the best months to visit Córdoba are April and October.

Why Córdoba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 630 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
