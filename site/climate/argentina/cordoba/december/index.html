How December compares in Córdoba

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Córdoba. Compared with November: 2.0 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Córdoba in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in December?
In December, Córdoba averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Córdoba?
December is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in December?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in December?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
