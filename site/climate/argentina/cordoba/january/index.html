Córdoba weather in January

Average January weather in Córdoba, Argentina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Córdoba averages 30.2 °C (86 °F) days and 17.2 °C (63 °F) nights, with 144 mm (5.7 in) of rain over the month and about 267 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Córdoba

January is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Córdoba. Compared with December: 0.5 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Córdoba in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in January?
In January, Córdoba averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Córdoba?
January is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in January?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (154 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in January?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
