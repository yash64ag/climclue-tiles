How June compares in Córdoba

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Córdoba. Compared with May: 3.2 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Córdoba in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Córdoba in June?
In June, Córdoba averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Córdoba?
June is not one of Córdoba’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Córdoba in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (153 mm), the driest June (5 mm).
Does it snow in Córdoba in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Córdoba get in June?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Córdoba in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
