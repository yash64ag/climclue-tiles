Embarcación at a glance

Among the 318 towns ClimaClue covers in Argentina, Embarcación is the warmest (22.8 °C year average). Compared with Pichanal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Embarcación is 0.1 °C warmer in December and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (27.4 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, July (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 648 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Embarcación has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 648 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Embarcación itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.210°S, 64.100°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1072-1159), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
