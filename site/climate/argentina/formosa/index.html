Why Formosa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 835 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Formosa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Formosa?
January, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 32.4 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 23.4 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Formosa?
July, with an average of 16.4 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.1 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 12.8 °C (55 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Formosa?
November, with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,472 mm (57.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Formosa?
August, with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Formosa?
December, with about 283 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Formosa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Formosa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July and August.
What type of climate does Formosa have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10.0 × 11 km, that contains Formosa’s GeoNames location (26.185°S, 58.173°W; ClimaClue area sq-1101-1218), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 222,226 (GeoNames).
