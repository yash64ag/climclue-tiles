Isfara at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tajikistan, Isfara is the 2nd coolest (13.5 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,634 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (242 mm a year). Compared with Konibodom, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Isfara is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,353 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Isfara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Isfara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,353 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
