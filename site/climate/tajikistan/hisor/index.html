Hisor at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Tajikistan, Hisor is the 5th wettest (667 mm a year). Compared with Dushanbe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Hisor is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 79 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.0 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 1,217 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 22 km from Hisor by up to 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Hisor has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,217 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hisor itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.525°N, 68.551°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-454-2485), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
