Bokhtar at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tajikistan, Bokhtar is the warmest (17.7 °C year average) and the 3rd sunniest (about 2,704 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Dushanbe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Bokhtar is 4.3 °C warmer in July and 3.3 °C warmer in January, with 413 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.9 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,258 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 108% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bokhtar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,258 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
