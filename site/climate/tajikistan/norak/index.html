Norak at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Tajikistan, Norak is the 3rd wettest (726 mm a year). Compared with Yovon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Norak is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.7 °C), averages 25.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,290 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Norak, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Norak has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,290 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.4 °C. The 10 km area around Norak, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.3 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Norak itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.389°N, 69.323°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-456-2493), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
