Yovon at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Tajikistan, Yovon is the 4th wettest (675 mm a year). Compared with Norak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Yovon is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.7 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,265 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yovon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Yovon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,265 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C. The 10 km area around Yovon, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.2 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yovon itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.314°N, 69.038°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-456-2490), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
