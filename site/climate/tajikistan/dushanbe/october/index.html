How October compares in Dushanbe

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with September: 6.8 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Dushanbe in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in October?
In October, Dushanbe averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dushanbe?
October is not one of Dushanbe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in October?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (135 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in October?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
