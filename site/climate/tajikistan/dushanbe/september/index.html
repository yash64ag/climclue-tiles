How September compares in Dushanbe

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. October is 6.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Dushanbe in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in September?
In September, Dushanbe averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dushanbe?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Dushanbe’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in September?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (135 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in September?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
