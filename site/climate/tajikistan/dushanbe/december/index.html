How December compares in Dushanbe

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dushanbe. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Dushanbe in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in December?
In December, Dushanbe averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -2.6 °C (27 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dushanbe?
December is not one of Dushanbe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in December?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (135 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 44 cm (17.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in December?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
