Dushanbe weather in July

Average July weather in Dushanbe, Tajikistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Dushanbe averages 34.9 °C (95 °F) days and 18.7 °C (66 °F) nights, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 355 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Dushanbe

July is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Dushanbe. Compared with June: 2.7 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Dushanbe in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in July?
In July, Dushanbe averages a day high of 34.9 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 355 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Dushanbe?
July is not one of Dushanbe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in July?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (140 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in July?
About 355 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
