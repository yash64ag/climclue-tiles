How June compares in Dushanbe

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with May: 4.2 °C warmer, 53 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Dushanbe in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in June?
In June, Dushanbe averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 341 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dushanbe?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Dushanbe’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in June?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (135 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in June?
About 341 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
