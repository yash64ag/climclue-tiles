How April compares in Dushanbe

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with March: 6.2 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. May is 4.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Dushanbe in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in April?
In April, Dushanbe averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dushanbe?
April is not one of Dushanbe’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in April?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (140 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in April?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
