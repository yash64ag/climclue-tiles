How November compares in Dushanbe

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Dushanbe in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in November?
In November, Dushanbe averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Dushanbe?
November is not one of Dushanbe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in November?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (135 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in November?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
