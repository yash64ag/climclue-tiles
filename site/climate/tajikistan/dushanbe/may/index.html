How May compares in Dushanbe

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with April: 4.5 °C warmer, 41 mm less precipitation and 82 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Dushanbe in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in May?
In May, Dushanbe averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Dushanbe?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Dushanbe’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in May?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (135 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in May?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
