Dushanbe weather in January

January · Tajikistan

Average January weather in Dushanbe: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How January compares in Dushanbe

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dushanbe. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Dushanbe in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dushanbe in January?
In January, Dushanbe averages a day high of 5.8 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.2 °C (26 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dushanbe?
January is not one of Dushanbe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dushanbe in January?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (140 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Dushanbe in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 32 cm (12.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dushanbe get in January?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dushanbe in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
