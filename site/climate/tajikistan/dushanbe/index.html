Dushanbe at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Tajikistan, Dushanbe is the 2nd wettest (746 mm a year). Compared with Boshkengash, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Dushanbe is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 88 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.7 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 1,233 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 2 km from Dushanbe by up to 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations at a similar height, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Dushanbe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,233 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C. The 10 km area around Dushanbe, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.4 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dushanbe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.536°N, 68.779°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-454-2487), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
