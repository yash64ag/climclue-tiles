Konibodom at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tajikistan, Konibodom is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,668 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (216 mm a year). Compared with Isfara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Konibodom is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,334 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Konibodom has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,334 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
