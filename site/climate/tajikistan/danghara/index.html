Danghara weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Danghara, Tajikistan (Khatlon Province), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Danghara at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Tajikistan, Danghara is 14th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,630 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (516 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Norak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Danghara is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 210 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.5 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 1,300 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Danghara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Danghara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,300 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Danghara itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.096°N, 69.340°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-459-2493), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
