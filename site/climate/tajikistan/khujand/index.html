Khujand at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tajikistan, Khujand is the 3rd warmest (15.9 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,740 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (249 mm a year). Compared with Istaravshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Khujand is 5.6 °C warmer in July and 3.2 °C warmer in January, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,267 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Khujand has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,267 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
