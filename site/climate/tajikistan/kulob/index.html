Kŭlob at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tajikistan, Kŭlob is the 2nd warmest (16.1 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,601 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (623 mm a year). Compared with Bokhtar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Kŭlob is 2.3 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 311 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,337 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kŭlob has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,337 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
