Boshkengash at a glance

Among the 31 towns ClimaClue covers in Tajikistan, Boshkengash is 13th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,676 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dushanbe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Boshkengash is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 88 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 1,245 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Boshkengash has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,245 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C. The 10 km area around Boshkengash, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 13.9 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-455-2488), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
