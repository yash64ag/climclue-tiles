How August compares in Port Said

August is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Port Said. Compared with July: 0.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 0.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Port Said in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Said in August?
In August, Port Said averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 354 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Port Said?
August is not one of Port Said’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Said in August?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (20 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Port Said in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Said get in August?
About 354 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Port Said in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
