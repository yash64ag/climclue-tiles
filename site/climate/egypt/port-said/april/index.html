How April compares in Port Said

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Port Said. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Port Said in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Said in April?
In April, Port Said averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Port Said?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Port Said’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Said in April?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (19 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Port Said in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Said get in April?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Port Said in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
