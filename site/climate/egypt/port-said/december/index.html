How December compares in Port Said

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Port Said. Compared with November: 3.6 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Port Said in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Said in December?
In December, Port Said averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Port Said?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Port Said’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Said in December?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (20 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Port Said in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Said get in December?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Port Said in December?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Port Said in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
