How November compares in Port Said

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Port Said. Compared with October: 3.4 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Port Said in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Said in November?
In November, Port Said averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Port Said?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Port Said’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Said in November?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (20 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Port Said in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Said get in November?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Port Said in November?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Port Said in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
