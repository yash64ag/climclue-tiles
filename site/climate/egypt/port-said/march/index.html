How March compares in Port Said

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Port Said. Compared with February: 1.5 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 2.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Port Said in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Said in March?
In March, Port Said averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Port Said?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Port Said’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Said in March?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (19 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Port Said in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Said get in March?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Port Said in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
