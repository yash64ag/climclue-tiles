Al Başalīyah Baḩrī at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Al Başalīyah Baḩrī is 7th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,831 hours of sunshine a year) and 226th wettest (2 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with As Sibā‘īyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Al Başalīyah Baḩrī is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.4 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 195 km from the sea.

Why Al Başalīyah Baḩrī has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 195 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Al Başalīyah Baḩrī itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.150°N, 32.717°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-588-2127), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
