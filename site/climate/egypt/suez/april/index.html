How April compares in Suez

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Suez. Compared with March: 4.1 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 3.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Suez in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suez in April?
In April, Suez averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Suez?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Suez’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suez in April?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (4 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Suez in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suez get in April?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Suez in April?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Suez in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
