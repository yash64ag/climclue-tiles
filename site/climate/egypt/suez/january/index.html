How January compares in Suez

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Suez. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Suez in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suez in January?
In January, Suez averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Suez?
January is not one of Suez’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suez in January?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (4 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Suez in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suez get in January?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Suez in January?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Suez in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
