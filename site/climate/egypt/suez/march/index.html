How March compares in Suez

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Suez. Compared with February: 2.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 4.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Suez in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suez in March?
In March, Suez averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Suez?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Suez’s mild months (March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suez in March?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (4 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Suez in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suez get in March?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suez in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
