How December compares in Suez

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Suez. Compared with November: 4.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Suez in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suez in December?
In December, Suez averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Suez?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Suez’s mild months (March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suez in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (4 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Suez in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suez get in December?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suez in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
