El-Tor sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Sinai · Egypt

The sea off El-Tor averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 20.5 °C (69 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to November.

El-Tor sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in El-Tor?
In August, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in El-Tor?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to November. The coolest month is February, at 20.5 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in El-Tor in February?
About 20.5 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in El-Tor?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in El-Tor nearby?
In August: Sharm el-Sheikh (28.2 °C, 78 km), Hurghada (28.3 °C, 111 km) and Safaga (28.6 °C, 169 km).
