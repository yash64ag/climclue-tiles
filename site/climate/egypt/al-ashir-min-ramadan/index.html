Al ‘Āshir min Ramaḑān at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Al ‘Āshir min Ramaḑān is 52nd warmest (21.2 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 3,361 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (23 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bilbeis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Al ‘Āshir min Ramaḑān is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al ‘Āshir min Ramaḑān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al ‘Āshir min Ramaḑān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
