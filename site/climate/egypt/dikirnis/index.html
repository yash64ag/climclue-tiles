Dikirnis at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Dikirnis is 55th warmest (21.2 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 3,365 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (50 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ţalkhā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Dikirnis is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dikirnis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dikirnis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
