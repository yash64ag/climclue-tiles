How April compares in Alexandria

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Alexandria. Compared with March: 2.3 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Alexandria in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in April?
In April, Alexandria averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Alexandria?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Alexandria’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in April?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in April?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alexandria in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
