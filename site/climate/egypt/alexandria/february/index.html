Alexandria weather in February

Average February weather in Alexandria, Egypt: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Alexandria averages 19.7 °C (67 °F) days and 9.6 °C (49 °F) nights, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 204 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Alexandria

February is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Alexandria. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 1.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Alexandria in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in February?
In February, Alexandria averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Alexandria?
February is not one of Alexandria’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in February?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in February?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Alexandria in February?
About 17.1 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (27.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Alexandria in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
