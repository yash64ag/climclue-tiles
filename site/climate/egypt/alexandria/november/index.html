How November compares in Alexandria

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Alexandria. Compared with October: 3.4 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. December is 3.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Alexandria in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in November?
In November, Alexandria averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Alexandria?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Alexandria’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in November?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alexandria in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
