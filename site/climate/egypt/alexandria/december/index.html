How December compares in Alexandria

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Alexandria. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Alexandria in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in December?
In December, Alexandria averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Alexandria?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Alexandria’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in December?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alexandria in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
