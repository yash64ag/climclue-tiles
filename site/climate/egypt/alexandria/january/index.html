How January compares in Alexandria

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Alexandria. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Alexandria in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in January?
In January, Alexandria averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Alexandria?
January is not one of Alexandria’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in January?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in January?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alexandria in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
