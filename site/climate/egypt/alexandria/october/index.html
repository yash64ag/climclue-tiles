How October compares in Alexandria

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Alexandria. Compared with September: 2.2 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 3.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Alexandria in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in October?
In October, Alexandria averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Alexandria?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Alexandria’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in October?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in October?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alexandria in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
