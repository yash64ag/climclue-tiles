Why Alexandria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Alexandria climate: questions and answers

August, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.2 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 24.3 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Alexandria?
February, with an average of 14.6 °C (58 °F), day highs around 16.8 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 12.1 °C (54 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Alexandria?

What is the driest month in Alexandria?

What is the sunniest month in Alexandria?
July, with about 359 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Alexandria?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Alexandria?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, June, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Alexandria’s GeoNames location (31.202°N, 29.916°E; ClimaClue area sq-527-2099), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 5,263,542 (GeoNames).
