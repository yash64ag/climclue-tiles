How March compares in Alexandria

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Alexandria. Compared with February: 1.3 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 2.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Alexandria in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alexandria in March?
In March, Alexandria averages a day high of 18.3 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Alexandria?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Alexandria’s mild months (March, April, May, June, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alexandria in March?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (57 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Alexandria in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alexandria get in March?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alexandria in March?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
