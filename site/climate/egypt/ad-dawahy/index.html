Aḍ-Ḍawāḥy at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Aḍ-Ḍawāḥy is 206th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 3,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (81 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Az-Zuhūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Aḍ-Ḍawāḥy is 0.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in February, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, February (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Aḍ-Ḍawāḥy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
