Az-Zuhūr at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Az-Zuhūr is 64th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 3,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (83 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Port Said, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Az-Zuhūr is 0.2 °C cooler in August and about as warm in February, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, February (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Az-Zuhūr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Az-Zuhūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
