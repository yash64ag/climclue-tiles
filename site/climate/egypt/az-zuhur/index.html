Az-Zuhūr at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Az-Zuhūr is 208th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 3,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (82 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aḍ-Ḍawāḥy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Az-Zuhūr is 0.1 °C cooler in August and about as warm in February, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, February (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Az-Zuhūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-527-2122), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
