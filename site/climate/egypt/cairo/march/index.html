How March compares in Cairo

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cairo. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. April is 4.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Cairo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cairo in March?
In March, Cairo averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cairo?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Cairo’s mild months (March, April, May, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cairo in March?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (4 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Cairo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cairo get in March?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cairo in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
