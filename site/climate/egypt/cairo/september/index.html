How September compares in Cairo

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cairo. Compared with August: 1.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. October is 3.2 °C cooler than September on average.
