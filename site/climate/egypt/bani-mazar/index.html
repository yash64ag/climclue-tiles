Banī Mazār at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Banī Mazār is 20th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 3,595 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (6 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maghāghah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Banī Mazār is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.8 °C), averages 17.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Banī Mazār, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Banī Mazār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
