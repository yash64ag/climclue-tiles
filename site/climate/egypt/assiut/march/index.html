How March compares in Assiut

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Assiut. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 5.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Assiut in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Assiut in March?
In March, Assiut averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Assiut?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Assiut’s mild months (February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Assiut in March?

Does it snow in Assiut in March?

How many hours of sunshine does Assiut get in March?
