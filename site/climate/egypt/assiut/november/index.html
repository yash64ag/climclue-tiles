How November compares in Assiut

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Assiut. Compared with October: 6.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Assiut in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Assiut in November?
In November, Assiut averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Assiut?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Assiut’s mild months (February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Assiut in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (2 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Assiut in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Assiut get in November?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Assiut in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
