Al Ghanāyim at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Al Ghanāyim is 47th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 3,744 hours of sunshine a year) and 194th wettest (4 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ţimā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Al Ghanāyim is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.3 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Al Ghanāyim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-571-2113), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
