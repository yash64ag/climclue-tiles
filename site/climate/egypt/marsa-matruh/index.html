Marsá Maţrūḩ at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Marsá Maţrūḩ is the 3rd coolest (20.4 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (178 mm a year). Compared with Alexandria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (255 km away), Marsá Maţrūḩ is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Marsá Maţrūḩ, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Marsá Maţrūḩ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
