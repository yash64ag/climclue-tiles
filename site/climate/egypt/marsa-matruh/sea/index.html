Marsá Maţrūḩ sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Matruh · Egypt

The sea off Marsá Maţrūḩ averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.8 °C (62 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Marsá Maţrūḩ sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Marsá Maţrūḩ?
In August, at about 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Marsá Maţrūḩ?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 16.8 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Marsá Maţrūḩ in March?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Marsá Maţrūḩ?
About 21.6 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
