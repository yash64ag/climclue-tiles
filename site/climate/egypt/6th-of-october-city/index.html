6th of October City at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, 6th of October City is 37th warmest (21.7 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 3,338 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (16 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Ḩawāmidīyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), 6th of October City is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 137 km from the sea.

Why 6th of October City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
