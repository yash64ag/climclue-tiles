Arīsh sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Sinai · Egypt

The sea off Arīsh averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 17.1 °C (63 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Arīsh sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Arīsh?
In August, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Arīsh?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 17.1 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Arīsh in February?
About 17.1 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Arīsh?
About 22.6 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
