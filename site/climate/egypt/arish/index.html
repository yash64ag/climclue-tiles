Arīsh at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Arīsh is the coolest (20.3 °C year average). Compared with Port Said, the nearest covered town with different numbers (143 km away), Arīsh is about as warm in August and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Arīsh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Arīsh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
