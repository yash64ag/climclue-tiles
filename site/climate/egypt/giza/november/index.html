How November compares in Giza

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Giza. Compared with October: 4.6 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 4.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Giza in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Giza in November?
In November, Giza averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Giza?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Giza’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Giza in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (3 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Giza in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Giza get in November?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Giza in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
