What is the weather like in Al Fayyum in April?
In April, Al Fayyum averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Al Fayyum?
