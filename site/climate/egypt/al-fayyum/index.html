Al Fayyum at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Al Fayyum is 26th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 3,433 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (11 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sinnūris, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Al Fayyum is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.1 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Fayyum, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Fayyum has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
