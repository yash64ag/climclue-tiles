Maghāghah at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Maghāghah is 64th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 3,557 hours of sunshine a year) and 171st wettest (7 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al ‘Idwah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Maghāghah is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.8 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 188 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maghāghah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maghāghah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 188 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Maghāghah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.648°N, 30.841°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-553-2108), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
