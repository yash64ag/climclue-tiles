Damietta at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Damietta is 66th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 3,404 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (112 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Manzalah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Damietta is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, February (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Damietta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Damietta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
