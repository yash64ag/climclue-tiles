Bi’r al ‘Abd sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Sinai · Egypt

The sea off Bi’r al ‘Abd averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.7 °C (62 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Bi’r al ‘Abd sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bi’r al ‘Abd?
In August, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bi’r al ‘Abd?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.7 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bi’r al ‘Abd in February?
About 16.7 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bi’r al ‘Abd?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
