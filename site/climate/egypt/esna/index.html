Esna at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Esna is the 3rd warmest (26.0 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 3,828 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (2 mm a year). Compared with Luxor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Esna is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.0 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 206 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Esna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Esna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 206 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
