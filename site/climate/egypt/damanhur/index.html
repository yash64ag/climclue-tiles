Why Damanhur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Damanhur’s GeoNames location (31.034°N, 30.468°E; ClimaClue area sq-529-2104), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 318,207 (GeoNames).
