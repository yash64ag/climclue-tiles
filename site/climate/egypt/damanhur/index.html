Damanhur at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Damanhur is the 5th least sunny (about 3,276 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Disūq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Damanhur is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 13.5 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Why Damanhur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
