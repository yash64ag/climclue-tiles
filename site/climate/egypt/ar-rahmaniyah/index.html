Ar Raḩmānīyah at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Ar Raḩmānīyah is 193rd warmest (20.9 °C year average), 223rd sunniest (about 3,277 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (85 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Disūq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Ar Raḩmānīyah is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ar Raḩmānīyah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ar Raḩmānīyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
