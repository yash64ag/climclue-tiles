Zefta at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Zefta is 44th warmest (21.5 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 3,312 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (40 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tanta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Zefta is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zefta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zefta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
