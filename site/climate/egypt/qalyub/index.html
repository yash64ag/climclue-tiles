Qalyub at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Qalyub is 37th warmest (21.7 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 3,329 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (21 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shubrā al Khaymah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Qalyub is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.5 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 128 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qalyub, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qalyub has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 128 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
