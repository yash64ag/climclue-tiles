Juhaynah at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Juhaynah is 39th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 3,751 hours of sunshine a year) and 214th wettest (3 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ţahţā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Juhaynah is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.6 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Juhaynah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
