Abnūb at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Abnūb is 12th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,720 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (4 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Assiut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Abnūb is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.1 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 235 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Abnūb, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Abnūb has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 235 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
