El Gouna sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Red Sea · Egypt

The sea off El Gouna averages 27.8 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 21.8 °C (71 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to December.

El Gouna sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in El Gouna?
In August, at about 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in El Gouna?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to December. The coolest month is February, at 21.8 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in El Gouna in February?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in El Gouna?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in El Gouna nearby?
In August: Safaga (28.6 °C, 76 km) and Duba (29.6 °C, 199 km).
