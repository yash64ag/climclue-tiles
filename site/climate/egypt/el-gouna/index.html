El Gouna at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, El Gouna is 42nd warmest (24.3 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 3,750 hours of sunshine a year) and 174th wettest (6 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hurghada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), El Gouna is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (30.7 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Gouna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Gouna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for El Gouna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.394°N, 33.678°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-566-2136), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
