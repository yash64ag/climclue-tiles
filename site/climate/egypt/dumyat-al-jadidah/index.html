Dumyāţ al Jadīdah at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Dumyāţ al Jadīdah is 214th warmest (20.7 °C year average), 122nd sunniest (about 3,384 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (111 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kafr al Baţţīkh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Dumyāţ al Jadīdah is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.7 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, February (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Dumyāţ al Jadīdah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
