Al Maţarīyah at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Al Maţarīyah is 203rd warmest (20.8 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 3,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (64 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Manzalah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Al Maţarīyah is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 12.3 °C more than its coolest, January (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Al Maţarīyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
