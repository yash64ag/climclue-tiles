Aswān at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Aswān is the 2nd warmest (26.8 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,849 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (2 mm a year). Compared with Kom Ombo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Aswān is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.5 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 240 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aswān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aswān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 240 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
