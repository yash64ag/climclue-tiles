Mīt Abū Ghālib weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Mīt Abū Ghālib, Egypt (Damietta), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Mīt Abū Ghālib at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Mīt Abū Ghālib is 195th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 124th sunniest (about 3,379 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (78 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with As Sarw, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Mīt Abū Ghālib is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Why Mīt Abū Ghālib has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
