As Sinbillāwayn at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, As Sinbillāwayn is 47th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 3,334 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (41 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Mansurah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), As Sinbillāwayn is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 73 km from the sea.

Why As Sinbillāwayn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 73 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
