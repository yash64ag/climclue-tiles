Hala'ib sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Red Sea · Egypt

The sea off Hala'ib averages 31.1 °C (88 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 24.1 °C (75 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Hala'ib sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hala'ib?
In August, at about 31.1 °C (88 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hala'ib?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.1 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hala'ib in February?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Hala'ib?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
