Al Mansurah at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Al Mansurah is 53rd warmest (21.2 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 3,333 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (54 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with As Sinbillāwayn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Al Mansurah is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Mansurah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Mansurah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
