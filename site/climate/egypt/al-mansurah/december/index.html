How December compares in Al Mansurah

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Al Mansurah. Compared with November: 4.0 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Al Mansurah in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Al Mansurah in December?
In December, Al Mansurah averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Al Mansurah?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Al Mansurah’s mild months (March, April, May, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Al Mansurah in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (14 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Al Mansurah in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Al Mansurah get in December?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Al Mansurah in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
