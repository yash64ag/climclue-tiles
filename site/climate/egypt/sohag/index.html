Sohag at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Sohag is the 5th driest (3 mm a year). Compared with Akhmīm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Sohag is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.6 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 231 km from the sea.

Why Sohag has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 231 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
