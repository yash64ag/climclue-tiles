Qina at a glance

Among the 72 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Egypt, Qina is the 4th warmest (25.5 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 3,798 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Luxor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Qina is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.8 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 146 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qina, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 146 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
