Shubrā al Khaymah at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Shubrā al Khaymah is 107th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 170th sunniest (about 3,329 hours of sunshine a year) and 133rd wettest (20 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cairo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Shubrā al Khaymah is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 128 km from the sea.

Why Shubrā al Khaymah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 128 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
