Shibīn al Qanāţir at a glance

Among the 237 towns ClimaClue covers in Egypt, Shibīn al Qanāţir is 118th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 163rd sunniest (about 3,331 hours of sunshine a year) and 121st wettest (25 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mashtūl as Sūq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Shibīn al Qanāţir is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Why Shibīn al Qanāţir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
