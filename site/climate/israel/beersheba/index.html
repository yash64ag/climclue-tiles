Beersheba at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Beersheba is the 2nd coolest (19.7 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,418 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (265 mm a year). Compared with Ashkelon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Beersheba is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 244 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Why Beersheba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
