Why Beersheba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Beersheba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Beersheba?
August, with an average of 26.4 °C (79 °F), day highs around 33.0 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 20.7 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Beersheba?
January, with an average of 11.8 °C (53 °F), day highs around 16.0 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 7.6 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Beersheba?
January, with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 265 mm (10.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Beersheba?
June, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Beersheba?
July, with about 384 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Beersheba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Beersheba?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, June, September, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Beersheba’s GeoNames location (31.252°N, 34.791°E; ClimaClue area sq-527-2147), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 186,600 (GeoNames).
