Why Jerusalem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

August, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 32.6 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 19.4 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jerusalem?
January, with an average of 8.9 °C (48 °F), day highs around 12.8 °C (55 °F) and night lows around 4.9 °C (41 °F).

What is the driest month in Jerusalem?

What is the sunniest month in Jerusalem?

Does it snow in Jerusalem?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Jerusalem?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Jerusalem’s GeoNames location (31.769°N, 35.216°E; ClimaClue area sq-522-2152), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 971,800 (GeoNames).
