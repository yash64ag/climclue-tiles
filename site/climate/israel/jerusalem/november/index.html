How November compares in Jerusalem

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jerusalem. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. December is 4.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Jerusalem in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jerusalem in November?
In November, Jerusalem averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jerusalem?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Jerusalem’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jerusalem in November?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (165 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jerusalem in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jerusalem get in November?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jerusalem in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
