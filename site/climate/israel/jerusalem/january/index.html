How January compares in Jerusalem

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jerusalem. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 46 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Jerusalem in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jerusalem in January?
In January, Jerusalem averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jerusalem?
January is not one of Jerusalem’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jerusalem in January?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (149 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jerusalem in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jerusalem get in January?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jerusalem in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
