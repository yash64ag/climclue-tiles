How December compares in Jerusalem

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jerusalem. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Jerusalem in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jerusalem in December?
In December, Jerusalem averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jerusalem?
December is not one of Jerusalem’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jerusalem in December?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (149 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jerusalem in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jerusalem get in December?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jerusalem in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
