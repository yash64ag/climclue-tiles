How September compares in Jerusalem

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jerusalem. Compared with August: 1.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 3.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Jerusalem in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jerusalem in September?
In September, Jerusalem averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 315 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jerusalem?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Jerusalem’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jerusalem in September?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (149 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jerusalem in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jerusalem get in September?
About 315 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jerusalem in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
