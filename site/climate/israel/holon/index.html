H̱olon at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, H̱olon is the 3rd wettest (657 mm a year). Compared with Rishon LeTsiyyon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), H̱olon is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Why H̱olon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
