Or Akiva at a glance

Among the 110 towns ClimaClue covers in Israel, Or Akiva is 6th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 3,187 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (620 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Binyamina-Giv‘at ‘Ada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Or Akiva is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 14.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Or Akiva, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Or Akiva has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-514-2149), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
