Hadera at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Hadera is the 3rd warmest (20.5 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 3,220 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Netanya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Hadera is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hadera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hadera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
