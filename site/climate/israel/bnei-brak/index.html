Bnei Brak at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Bnei Brak is 10th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 3,267 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (626 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tel Aviv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Bnei Brak is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in February. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bnei Brak has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
