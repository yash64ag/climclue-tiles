Haifa at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Haifa is the least sunny (about 3,149 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (731 mm a year). Compared with Hadera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Haifa is 1.2 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 117 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Haifa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Haifa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
