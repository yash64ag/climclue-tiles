Rishon LeTsiyyon at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Rishon LeTsiyyon is the 2nd wettest (660 mm a year). Compared with H̱olon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Rishon LeTsiyyon is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 127% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Rishon LeTsiyyon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
