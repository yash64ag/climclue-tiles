Rishon LeTsiyyon sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central District · Israel

The sea off Rishon LeTsiyyon averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 17.6 °C (64 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Rishon LeTsiyyon sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rishon LeTsiyyon?
In August, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rishon LeTsiyyon?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 17.6 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rishon LeTsiyyon in February?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rishon LeTsiyyon?
About 22.8 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
