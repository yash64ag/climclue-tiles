Eilat sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Southern District · Israel

The sea off Eilat averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 19.2 °C (67 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Eilat sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Eilat?
In August, at about 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Eilat?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 19.2 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Eilat in February?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Eilat?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Eilat nearby?
In August: Sharm el-Sheikh (28.2 °C, 192 km), El-Tor (27.5 °C, 195 km) and Ash Shaykh Zuwayd (28.3 °C, 201 km).
