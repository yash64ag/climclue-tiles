Bet Shemesh at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Bet Shemesh is the 3rd coolest (19.7 °C year average). Compared with Jerusalem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Bet Shemesh is 0.7 °C warmer in August and 3.0 °C warmer in January, with 56 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 22 km from Bet Shemesh measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.1 °C (5.7 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Bet Shemesh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
