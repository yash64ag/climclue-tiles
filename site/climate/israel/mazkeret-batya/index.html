Mazkeret Batya at a glance

Among the 110 towns ClimaClue covers in Israel, Mazkeret Batya is 34th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 3,306 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (602 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Reẖovot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Mazkeret Batya is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 17 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 116% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Mazkeret Batya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mazkeret Batya itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.854°N, 34.846°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-521-2148), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
