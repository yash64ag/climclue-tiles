Karmi’el weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Karmi’el, Israel (Northern District), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Karmi’el at a glance

Among the 110 towns ClimaClue covers in Israel, Karmi’el is the 5th least sunny (about 3,101 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Majd el Kurūm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Karmi’el is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 106 mm less precipitation a year. Why Karmi’el has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-510-2152), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
