Judeida Makr at a glance

Among the 110 towns ClimaClue covers in Israel, Judeida Makr is the 5th wettest (820 mm a year). Compared with Yirkā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Judeida Makr is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 180 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Judeida Makr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-510-2151), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
