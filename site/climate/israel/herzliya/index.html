Herzliya at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Israel, Herzliya is 9th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,261 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ramat Gan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Herzliya is 0.2 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 119% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Herzliya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
