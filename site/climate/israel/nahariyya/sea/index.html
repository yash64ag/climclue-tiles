Nahariyya sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Northern District · Israel

The sea off Nahariyya averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.8 °C (62 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Nahariyya sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Nahariyya?
In August, at about 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Nahariyya?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.8 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Nahariyya in February?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Nahariyya?
About 22.0 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
