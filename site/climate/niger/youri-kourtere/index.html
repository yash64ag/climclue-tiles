Youri Kourtéré weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Youri Kourtéré, Niger (Tillabéri Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Youri Kourtéré is warmest in May, with 40.6 °C (105 °F) days and 28.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 16.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (159 mm); January is driest (0 mm).

Youri Kourtéré at a glance

Among the 42 towns ClimaClue covers in Niger, Youri Kourtéré is 7th warmest (29.4 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,906 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (479 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kollo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Youri Kourtéré is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.4 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (23.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 786 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Youri Kourtéré, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Youri Kourtéré has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 786 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Youri Kourtéré itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.334°N, 2.248°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-706-1822), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
