Maradi at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Niger, Maradi is the 2nd coolest (27.6 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,927 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (420 mm a year). Compared with Zinder, the nearest covered town with different numbers (207 km away), Maradi is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.6 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, January (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 864 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maradi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maradi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 864 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
