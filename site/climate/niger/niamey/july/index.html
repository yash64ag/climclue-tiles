How July compares in Niamey

July is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Niamey. Compared with June: 2.6 °C cooler, 62 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Niamey in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niamey in July?
In July, Niamey averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Niamey?
July is not one of Niamey’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niamey in July?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (155 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Niamey in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niamey get in July?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niamey in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
