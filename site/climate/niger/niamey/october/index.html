How October compares in Niamey

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Niamey. Compared with September: 1.2 °C warmer, 67 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. November is 3.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Niamey in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niamey in October?
In October, Niamey averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Niamey?
October is not one of Niamey’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niamey in October?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (155 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Niamey in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niamey get in October?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niamey in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
