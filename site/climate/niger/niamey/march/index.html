Niamey in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niamey in March?
In March, Niamey averages a day high of 37.7 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Niamey?
March is not one of Niamey’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niamey in March?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (155 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Niamey in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niamey get in March?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niamey in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
