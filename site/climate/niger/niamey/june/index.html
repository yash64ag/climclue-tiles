How June compares in Niamey

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Niamey. Compared with May: 2.0 °C cooler, 44 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. July is 2.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Niamey in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Niamey in June?
In June, Niamey averages a day high of 38.2 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Niamey?
June is not one of Niamey’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Niamey in June?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (149 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Niamey in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Niamey get in June?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Niamey in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
