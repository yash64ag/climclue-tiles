Aguié at a glance

Among the 42 towns ClimaClue covers in Niger, Aguié is the 2nd coolest (27.3 °C year average). Compared with Gazaoua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Aguié is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.4 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 888 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aguié, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aguié has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 888 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aguié itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.506°N, 7.779°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-704-1877), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
