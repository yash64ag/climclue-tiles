Agadez at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Niger, Agadez is the 3rd warmest (29.1 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,188 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (127 mm a year). Compared with Arlit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (206 km away), Agadez is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 75 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.8 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,249 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Agadez, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Agadez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,249 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
