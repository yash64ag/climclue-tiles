Agadez at a glance

Among the 42 towns ClimaClue covers in Niger, Agadez is the 4th sunniest (about 3,188 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (128 mm a year). Compared with Alaghsas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Agadez is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.7 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,249 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Agadez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,249 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-670-1879), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
