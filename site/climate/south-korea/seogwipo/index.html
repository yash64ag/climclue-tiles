Seogwipo at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Seogwipo is the 2nd warmest (15.3 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,121 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (2,113 mm a year). Compared with Jeju City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Seogwipo is about as warm in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 331 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Seogwipo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Seogwipo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
