Yeosu at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Yeosu is 9th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,628 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gwangyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Yeosu is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 22.8 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yeosu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yeosu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
