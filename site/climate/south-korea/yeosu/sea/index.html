Yeosu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Jeollanam-do · South Korea

The sea off Yeosu averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.3 °C (51 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Yeosu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yeosu?
In August, at about 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yeosu?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 10.3 °C (51 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yeosu in February?
About 10.3 °C (51 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yeosu?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Yeosu nearby?
In August: Busan (25.8 °C, 130 km) and Tsushima, Nagasaki (26.9 °C, 162 km).
