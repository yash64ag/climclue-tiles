How January compares in Goyang-si

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Goyang-si. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 3.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Goyang-si in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Goyang-si in January?
In January, Goyang-si averages a day high of 0.6 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -9.1 °C (16 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Goyang-si?
No month in Goyang-si is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Goyang-si in January?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (406 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Goyang-si in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Goyang-si get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Goyang-si in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
