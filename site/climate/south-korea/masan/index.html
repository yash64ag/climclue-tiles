Masan at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Masan is 16th warmest (13.7 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 2,285 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,457 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gwangju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Masan is 0.9 °C warmer in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 209 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Masan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Masan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
