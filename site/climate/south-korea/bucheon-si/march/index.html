Bucheon-si in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucheon-si in March?
In March, Bucheon-si averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 0.5 °C (33 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bucheon-si?
No month in Bucheon-si is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucheon-si in March?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (393 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Bucheon-si in March?
