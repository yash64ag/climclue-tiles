Bucheon-si weather in June

Average June weather in Bucheon-si, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Bucheon-si averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 16.7 °C (62 °F) nights, with 131 mm (5.2 in) of rain over the month and about 222 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Bucheon-si

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bucheon-si. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. July is 3.3 °C warmer than June on average.
