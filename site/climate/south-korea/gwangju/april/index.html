How April compares in Gwangju

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Gwangju. Compared with March: 6.1 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Gwangju in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwangju in April?
In April, Gwangju averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gwangju?
April is not one of Gwangju’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwangju in April?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (341 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Gwangju in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwangju get in April?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwangju in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
