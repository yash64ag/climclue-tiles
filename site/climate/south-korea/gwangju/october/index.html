How October compares in Gwangju

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Gwangju. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 131 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. November is 6.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Gwangju in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwangju in October?
In October, Gwangju averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Gwangju?
No month in Gwangju is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwangju in October?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (368 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Gwangju in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwangju get in October?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwangju in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
