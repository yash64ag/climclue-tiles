How January compares in Gwangju

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gwangju. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Gwangju ranks 24th warmest (average temperature), 55th sunniest and 6th wettest in January.

Gwangju in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwangju in January?
In January, Gwangju averages a day high of 3.5 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Gwangju?
No month in Gwangju is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwangju in January?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (368 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Gwangju in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Gwangju get in January?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwangju in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
