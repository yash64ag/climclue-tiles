How May compares in Gwangju

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Gwangju. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Gwangju in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwangju in May?
In May, Gwangju averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Gwangju?
No month in Gwangju is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwangju in May?
118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (368 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Gwangju in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwangju get in May?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwangju in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
