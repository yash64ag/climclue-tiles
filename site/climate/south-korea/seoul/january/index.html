How January compares in Seoul

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Seoul. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 3.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Seoul in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in January?
In January, Seoul averages a day high of 1.1 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -7.7 °C (18 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Seoul?
No month in Seoul is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (412 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.9 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in January?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
