How June compares in Seoul

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Seoul. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. July is 3.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Seoul in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in June?
In June, Seoul averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Seoul?
June is not one of Seoul’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in June?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (399 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in June?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
