How April compares in Seoul

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Seoul. Compared with March: 6.7 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Seoul in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in April?
In April, Seoul averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.4 °C (42 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Seoul?
No month in Seoul is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in April?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (412 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in April?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
