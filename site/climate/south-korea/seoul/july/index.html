How July compares in Seoul

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Seoul. Compared with June: 3.0 °C warmer, 264 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. August is 0.6 °C warmer than July on average.

Of the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Seoul ranks 26th warmest (average temperature), 59th sunniest and 7th wettest in July.

Seoul in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in July?
In July, Seoul averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 399 mm (15.7 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Seoul?
July is not one of Seoul’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in July?
399 mm (15.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (399 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in July?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
