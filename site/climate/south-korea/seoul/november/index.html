How November compares in Seoul

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Seoul. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. December is 6.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Seoul in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in November?
In November, Seoul averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Seoul?
No month in Seoul is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in November?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (412 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in November?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
