How December compares in Seoul

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Seoul. Compared with November: 6.8 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Seoul among towns in South Korea in December

Of the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Seoul ranks 49th warmest (average temperature), 53rd sunniest and 44th wettest in December.

Warmest: Jeju City (8.6 °C) · coolest: Yangju (-1.1 °C) · sunniest: Changwon (195 h) · wettest: Seogwipo (57 mm) · driest: Daegu (15 mm).

Seoul in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in December?
In December, Seoul averages a day high of 3.4 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Seoul?
December is not one of Seoul’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in December?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (399 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.5 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in December?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
