December · South Korea

How December compares in Seoul

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Seoul. Compared with November: 6.8 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Seoul in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seoul in December?
In December, Seoul averages a day high of 3.7 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Seoul?
No month in Seoul is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seoul in December?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (412 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Seoul in December?
Yes, on average: about 4.9 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Seoul get in December?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seoul in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
