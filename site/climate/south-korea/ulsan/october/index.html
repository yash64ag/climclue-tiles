How October compares in Ulsan

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ulsan. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, 112 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Ulsan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulsan in October?
In October, Ulsan averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ulsan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Ulsan’s mild months (May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulsan in October?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (225 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ulsan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulsan get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Ulsan in October?
About 21.0 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Ulsan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
