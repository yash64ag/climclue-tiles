How January compares in Ulsan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ulsan. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Ulsan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulsan in January?
In January, Ulsan averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -1.8 °C (29 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ulsan?
January is not one of Ulsan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulsan in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (224 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ulsan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulsan get in January?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulsan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
