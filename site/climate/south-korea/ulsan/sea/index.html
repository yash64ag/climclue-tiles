Ulsan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Korea

The sea off Ulsan averages 24.6 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.3 °C (54 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Ulsan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ulsan?
In August, at about 24.6 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ulsan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.3 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ulsan in February?
About 12.3 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ulsan?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ulsan nearby?
In August: Gijang (25.4 °C, 34 km), Busan (25.8 °C, 55 km) and Sinhyeon (25.7 °C, 96 km).
