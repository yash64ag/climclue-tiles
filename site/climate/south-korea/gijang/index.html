Gijang at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Gijang is the 5th warmest (14.5 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,263 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Yangsan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Gijang is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 137 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gijang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gijang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
