Beolgyo at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Beolgyo is 27th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 2,313 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (1,451 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Suncheon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Beolgyo is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 124 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beolgyo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beolgyo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Beolgyo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.849°N, 127.341°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-3073), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
