Anyang-si weather in September

Average September weather in Anyang-si, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Anyang-si averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 15.9 °C (61 °F) nights, with 175 mm (6.9 in) of rain over the month and about 201 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Anyang-si

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Anyang-si. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 159 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.
