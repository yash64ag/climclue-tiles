Anyang-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Anyang-si is 50th warmest (12.0 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,353 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,457 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gunpo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Anyang-si is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.3 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Why Anyang-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
