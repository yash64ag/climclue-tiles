How December compares in Anyang-si

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Anyang-si. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Anyang-si in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anyang-si in December?
In December, Anyang-si averages a day high of 4.2 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Anyang-si?
No month in Anyang-si is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anyang-si in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (405 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Anyang-si in December?
Yes, on average: about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Anyang-si get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
