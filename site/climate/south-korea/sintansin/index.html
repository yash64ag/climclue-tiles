Sintansin weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sintansin, South Korea (Daejeon), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sintansin at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Sintansin is 73rd warmest (13.2 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,415 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (1,233 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Daejeon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Sintansin is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 27.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 92 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sintansin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sintansin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 92 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sintansin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.454°N, 127.431°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-475-3074), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
