Guri-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Guri-si is 46th warmest (12.0 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,347 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (1,372 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hanam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Guri-si is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 28.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Why Guri-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
