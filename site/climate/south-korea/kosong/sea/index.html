Kosong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gangwon-do · South Korea

The sea off Kosong averages 23.7 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.2 °C (45 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Kosong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kosong?
In August, at about 23.7 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kosong?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 7.2 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kosong in February?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kosong?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
