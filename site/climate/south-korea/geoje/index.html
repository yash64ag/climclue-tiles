Geoje at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Geoje is the 3rd warmest (14.9 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (1,840 mm a year). Compared with Busan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Geoje is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 246 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Geoje, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Geoje has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
