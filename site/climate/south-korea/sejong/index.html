Sejong at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Sejong is 30th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,452 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (1,385 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cheongju-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Sejong is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 120 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 77 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sejong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sejong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 77 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
