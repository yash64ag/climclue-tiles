Gumi at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Gumi is the 4th sunniest (about 2,472 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (1,077 mm a year). Compared with Gimcheon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Gumi is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 26.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gumi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gumi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
