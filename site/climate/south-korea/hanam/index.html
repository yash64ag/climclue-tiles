Why Hanam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hanam climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hanam?
August, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 21.0 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hanam?
January, with an average of -3.9 °C (25 °F), day highs around 0.1 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -9.8 °C (14 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hanam?
July, with 424 mm (16.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,471 mm (57.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hanam?
December, with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hanam?
May, with about 252 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hanam?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 44 cm (17.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Hanam?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Hanam have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Hanam’s GeoNames location (37.540°N, 127.206°E; ClimaClue area sq-464-3072), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 254,415 (GeoNames).
