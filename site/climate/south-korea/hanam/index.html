Hanam at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Hanam is the 4th coolest (11.5 °C year average). Compared with Guri-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Hanam is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Why Hanam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
