How April compares in Changwon

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Changwon. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 4.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Changwon in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changwon in April?
In April, Changwon averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Changwon?
April is not one of Changwon’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changwon in April?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (303 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Changwon in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changwon get in April?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Changwon in April?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Changwon in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
