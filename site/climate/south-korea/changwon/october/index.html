How October compares in Changwon

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Changwon. Compared with September: 5.8 °C cooler, 111 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. November is 6.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Changwon in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changwon in October?
In October, Changwon averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Changwon?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Changwon’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changwon in October?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (303 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Changwon in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changwon get in October?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Changwon in October?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Changwon in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
