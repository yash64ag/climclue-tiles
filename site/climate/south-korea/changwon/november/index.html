How November compares in Changwon

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Changwon. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 5.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Changwon in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changwon in November?
In November, Changwon averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Changwon?
November is not one of Changwon’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changwon in November?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (303 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Changwon in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changwon get in November?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Changwon in November?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Changwon in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
