How February compares in Changwon

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Changwon. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Changwon in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changwon in February?
In February, Changwon averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -1.1 °C (30 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Changwon?
February is not one of Changwon’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changwon in February?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (303 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Changwon in February?
Yes, on average: about 4.5 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Changwon get in February?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Changwon in February?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Changwon in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
