Why Changwon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Changwon climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Changwon?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.8 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 22.4 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Changwon?
January, with an average of 1.6 °C (35 °F), day highs around 5.5 °C (42 °F) and night lows around -3.2 °C (26 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Changwon?
July, with 313 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,505 mm (59.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Changwon?
December, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Changwon?
May, with about 242 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Changwon?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Changwon?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.
What type of climate does Changwon have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Changwon’s GeoNames location (35.228°N, 128.681°E; ClimaClue area sq-487-3086), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,025,702 (GeoNames).
