Changwon at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Changwon is 18th warmest (14.5 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,419 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,452 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naesŏ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Changwon is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 63 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Changwon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Changwon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Changwon climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Changwon?
August, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 30.4 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 22.3 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Changwon?
January, with an average of 2.0 °C (36 °F), day highs around 7.3 °C (45 °F) and night lows around -3.2 °C (26 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Changwon?
July, with 303 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,452 mm (57.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Changwon?
December, with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Changwon?
May, with about 242 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Changwon?
Yes. About 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time to visit Changwon?

How warm is the sea in Changwon?
The nearby open sea averages 25.4 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.4 °C (54 °F) in March. It is 20 °C (68 °F) or warmer in July, August, September and October. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Changwon itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.228°N, 128.681°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-487-3086), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 1,025,702, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). How ClimaClue makes these numbers.
