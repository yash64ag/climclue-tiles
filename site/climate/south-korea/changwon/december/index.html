How December compares in Changwon

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Changwon. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Changwon in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changwon in December?
In December, Changwon averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -1.4 °C (29 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Changwon?
December is not one of Changwon’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changwon in December?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (303 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Changwon in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Changwon get in December?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Changwon in December?
About 15.6 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Changwon in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
