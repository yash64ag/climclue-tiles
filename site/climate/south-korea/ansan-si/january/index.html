How January compares in Ansan-si

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ansan-si. Compared with December: 2.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Ansan-si in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ansan-si in January?
In January, Ansan-si averages a day high of 1.1 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ansan-si?
January is not one of Ansan-si’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ansan-si in January?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (367 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Ansan-si in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.5 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ansan-si get in January?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ansan-si in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
