How December compares in Ansan-si

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ansan-si. Compared with November: 6.5 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 2.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Ansan-si in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ansan-si in December?
In December, Ansan-si averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.0 °C (27 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ansan-si?
December is not one of Ansan-si’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ansan-si in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (367 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Ansan-si in December?
Yes, on average: about 4.7 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Ansan-si get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Ansan-si in December?
About 9.4 °C (49 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Ansan-si in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
