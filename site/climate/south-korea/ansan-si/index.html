Ansan-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Ansan-si is 49th warmest (11.9 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,355 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (1,388 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anyang-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Ansan-si is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 27.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Ansan-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
