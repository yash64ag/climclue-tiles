Ansan-si in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ansan-si in July?
In July, Ansan-si averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 367 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ansan-si?
No month in Ansan-si is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ansan-si in July?
367 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (367 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Ansan-si in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ansan-si get in July?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
