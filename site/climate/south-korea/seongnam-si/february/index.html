Seongnam-si weather in February

Average February weather in Seongnam-si, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Seongnam-si averages 4.8 °C (41 °F) days and -4.9 °C (23 °F) nights, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 176 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Seongnam-si

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Seongnam-si. Compared with January: 3.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. March is 5.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Seongnam-si in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in February?
In February, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 4.8 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
February is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in February?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (376 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.7 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in February?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seongnam-si in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
