How December compares in Seongnam-si

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Seongnam-si. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 2.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Seongnam-si in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in December?
In December, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 3.4 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
December is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in December?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (376 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in December?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seongnam-si in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
