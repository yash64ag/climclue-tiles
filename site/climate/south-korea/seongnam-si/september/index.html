Seongnam-si weather in September

Average September weather in Seongnam-si, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Seongnam-si averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) days and 15.4 °C (60 °F) nights, with 168 mm (6.6 in) of rain over the month and about 200 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Seongnam-si

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Seongnam-si. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, 138 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. October is 6.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Seongnam-si in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in September?
In September, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
September is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in September?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (376 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in September?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
