How July compares in Seongnam-si

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Seongnam-si. Compared with June: 3.0 °C warmer, 246 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C warmer than July on average.

Seongnam-si in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in July?
In July, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 376 mm (14.8 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
July is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in July?
376 mm (14.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (376 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in July?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seongnam-si in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
