How March compares in Seongnam-si

Seongnam-si in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in March?
In March, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
March is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in March?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (376 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.1 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in March?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seongnam-si in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
