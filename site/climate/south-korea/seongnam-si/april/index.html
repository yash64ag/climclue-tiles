Seongnam-si in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in April?
In April, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
April is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in April?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (396 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in April?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seongnam-si in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
