How November compares in Seongnam-si

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Seongnam-si. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. December is 6.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Seongnam-si in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Seongnam-si in November?
In November, Seongnam-si averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Seongnam-si?
November is not one of Seongnam-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Seongnam-si in November?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (376 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Seongnam-si in November?
Yes, on average: about 1.6 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Seongnam-si get in November?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Seongnam-si in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
