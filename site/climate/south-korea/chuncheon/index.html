Chuncheon at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Chuncheon is the coolest (11.4 °C year average). Compared with Hwado, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Chuncheon is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 191 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 28.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 90 km from the sea.

Why Chuncheon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 90 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
