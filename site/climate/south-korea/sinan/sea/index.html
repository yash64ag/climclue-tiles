Sinan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Jeollanam-do · South Korea

The sea off Sinan averages 24.4 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.9 °C (46 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Sinan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sinan?
In August, at about 24.4 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sinan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is March, at 7.9 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sinan in March?
About 7.9 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sinan?
About 15.1 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sinan nearby?
In August: Buan (25.3 °C, 115 km), Gunsan (25.3 °C, 139 km) and Yeosu (25.2 °C, 142 km).
