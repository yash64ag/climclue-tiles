Pohang at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Pohang is 10th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (1,163 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gyeongju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Pohang is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 77 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pohang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pohang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
