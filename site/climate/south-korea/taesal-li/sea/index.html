Taesal-li sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chungcheongnam-do · South Korea

The sea off Taesal-li averages 23.7 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.1 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Taesal-li sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Taesal-li?
In August, at about 23.7 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Taesal-li?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 4.1 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Taesal-li in February?
About 4.1 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Taesal-li?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Taesal-li nearby?
In August: Boryeong (24.4 °C, 70 km), Yŏnan-ŭp (24.6 °C, 107 km) and Gunsan (25.3 °C, 113 km).
