Why Hwado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Hwado?
January, with an average of -4.0 °C (25 °F), day highs around 0.0 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -10.1 °C (14 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hwado?
July, with 431 mm (17.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,483 mm (58.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hwado?
December, with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hwado?
May, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hwado?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 51 cm (20.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Hwado?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Hwado’s GeoNames location (37.652°N, 127.308°E; ClimaClue area sq-463-3073), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 106,358 (GeoNames).
