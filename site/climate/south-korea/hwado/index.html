Hwado at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Hwado is the 5th coolest (11.5 °C year average). Compared with Hanam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Hwado is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 28.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hwado, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hwado has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
