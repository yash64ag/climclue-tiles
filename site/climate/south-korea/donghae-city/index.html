Why Donghae City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Donghae City climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Donghae City?
August, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.6 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 22.7 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Donghae City?
January, with an average of 0.6 °C (33 °F), day highs around 3.1 °C (38 °F) and night lows around -3.6 °C (26 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Donghae City?
August, with 272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,353 mm (53.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Donghae City?
December, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Donghae City?
May, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Donghae City?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 112 cm (44.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Donghae City?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and October.
What type of climate does Donghae City have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Donghae City’s GeoNames location (37.544°N, 129.107°E; ClimaClue area sq-464-3091), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 101,128 (GeoNames).
