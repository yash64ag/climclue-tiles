Donghae City at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Donghae City is 26th warmest (13.3 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,368 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (1,353 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gangneung, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Donghae City is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 282 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Donghae City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Donghae City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
