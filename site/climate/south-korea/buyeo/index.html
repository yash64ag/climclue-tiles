Buyeo at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Buyeo is the sunniest (about 2,565 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nonsan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Buyeo is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.2 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Why Buyeo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Buyeo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.275°N, 126.909°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-477-3069), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
