How October compares in Suwon

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 114 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. November is 7.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Suwon in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in October?
In October, Suwon averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Suwon?
October is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in October?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (357 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in October?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
