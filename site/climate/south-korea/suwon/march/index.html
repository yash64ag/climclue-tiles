How March compares in Suwon

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with February: 5.0 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 6.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Suwon in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in March?
In March, Suwon averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Suwon?
March is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in March?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (352 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.7 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in March?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
