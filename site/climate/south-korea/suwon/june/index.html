How June compares in Suwon

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. July is 3.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Suwon in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in June?
In June, Suwon averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Suwon?
June is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in June?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (357 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in June?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
