How April compares in Suwon

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Suwon in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in April?
In April, Suwon averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Suwon?
April is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in April?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (352 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
