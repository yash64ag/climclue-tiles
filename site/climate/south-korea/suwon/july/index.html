How July compares in Suwon

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with June: 3.1 °C warmer, 227 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. August is 0.6 °C warmer than July on average.

Suwon in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in July?
In July, Suwon averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 352 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Suwon?
July is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in July?
352 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (352 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in July?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
