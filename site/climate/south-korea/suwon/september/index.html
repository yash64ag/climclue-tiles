How September compares in Suwon

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 133 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Suwon in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in September?
In September, Suwon averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Suwon?
September is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in September?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (357 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in September?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
