How December compares in Suwon

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with November: 6.5 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 2.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Suwon in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in December?
In December, Suwon averages a day high of 3.7 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Suwon?
December is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in December?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (357 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.1 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in December?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
