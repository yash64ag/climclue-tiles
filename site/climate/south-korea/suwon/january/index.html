How January compares in Suwon

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Suwon. Compared with December: 2.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 2.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Suwon in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suwon in January?
In January, Suwon averages a day high of 2.6 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -7.5 °C (19 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Suwon?
January is not one of Suwon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suwon in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (357 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suwon in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.7 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Suwon get in January?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suwon in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
