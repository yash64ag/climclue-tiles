Iksan at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Iksan is the 5th sunniest (about 2,471 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Jeonju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Iksan is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iksan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iksan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
