Sangju at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Sangju is 29th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,448 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (1,247 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gimcheon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Sangju is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 184 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sangju, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sangju has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
