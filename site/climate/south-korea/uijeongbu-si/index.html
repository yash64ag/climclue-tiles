Uijeongbu-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Uijeongbu-si is the 3rd coolest (11.4 °C year average). Compared with Yangju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Uijeongbu-si is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 63 km from the sea.

Why Uijeongbu-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
