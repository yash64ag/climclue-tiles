Ungsang at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Ungsang is 33rd warmest (14.0 °C year average), 122nd sunniest (about 2,308 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (1,614 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yangsan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Ungsang is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 254 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Why Ungsang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ungsang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.406°N, 129.169°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-485-3091), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
