Why Gunsan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Gunsan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Gunsan?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 23.6 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Gunsan?
January, with an average of 0.7 °C (33 °F), day highs around 3.0 °C (37 °F) and night lows around -2.6 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Gunsan?
July, with 295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,307 mm (51.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Gunsan?
January, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Gunsan?
May, with about 257 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Gunsan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 33 cm (13.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Gunsan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Gunsan’s GeoNames location (35.979°N, 126.711°E; ClimaClue area sq-480-3067), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 264,656 (GeoNames).
