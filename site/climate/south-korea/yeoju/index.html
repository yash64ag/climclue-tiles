Yeoju at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Yeoju is 37th warmest (12.3 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,469 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (1,322 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Icheon-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Yeoju is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yeoju, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yeoju has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
