Cheonan weather in July

Average July weather in Cheonan, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Cheonan averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 21.3 °C (70 °F) nights, with 306 mm (12.0 in) of rain over the month and about 193 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Cheonan

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cheonan. Compared with June: 2.8 °C warmer, 177 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Cheonan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheonan in July?
In July, Cheonan averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cheonan?
July is not one of Cheonan’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheonan in July?
306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (306 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Cheonan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheonan get in July?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheonan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
