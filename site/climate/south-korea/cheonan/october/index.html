How October compares in Cheonan

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cheonan. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 109 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 7.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Cheonan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheonan in October?
In October, Cheonan averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Cheonan?
October is not one of Cheonan’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheonan in October?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (306 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Cheonan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheonan get in October?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheonan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
