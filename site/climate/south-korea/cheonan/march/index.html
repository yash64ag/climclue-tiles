How March compares in Cheonan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cheonan. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 6.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Cheonan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheonan in March?
In March, Cheonan averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -0.1 °C (32 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cheonan?
March is not one of Cheonan’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheonan in March?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (328 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cheonan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.6 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheonan get in March?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheonan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
