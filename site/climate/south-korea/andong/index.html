Andong at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Andong is the sunniest (about 2,513 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (1,069 mm a year). Compared with Sangju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Andong is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 178 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.3 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 71 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Andong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Andong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 71 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
