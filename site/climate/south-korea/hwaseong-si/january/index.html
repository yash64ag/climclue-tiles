How January compares in Hwaseong-si

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hwaseong-si. Compared with December: 3.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Hwaseong-si in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hwaseong-si in January?
In January, Hwaseong-si averages a day high of 2.9 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -5.6 °C (22 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hwaseong-si?
January is not one of Hwaseong-si’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hwaseong-si in January?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (360 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Hwaseong-si in January?
Yes, on average: about 9.5 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hwaseong-si get in January?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hwaseong-si in January?
About 5.9 °C (43 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hwaseong-si in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
