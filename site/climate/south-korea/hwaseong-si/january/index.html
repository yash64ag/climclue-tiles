How January compares in Hwaseong-si

Hwaseong-si in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hwaseong-si in January?
In January, Hwaseong-si averages a day high of 1.1 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hwaseong-si?
No month in Hwaseong-si is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hwaseong-si in January?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (361 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Hwaseong-si in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.5 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hwaseong-si get in January?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hwaseong-si in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
