Hwaseong-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Hwaseong-si is 42nd warmest (12.1 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (1,357 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ansan-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Hwaseong-si is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Why Hwaseong-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
