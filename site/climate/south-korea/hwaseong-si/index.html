Hwaseong-si weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Hwaseong-si, South Korea (Gyeonggi-do), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Hwaseong-si is warmest in August, with 28.6 °C (83 °F) days and 21.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.2 °C (38 °F) days and -5.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (360 mm); January is driest (24 mm).

Hwaseong-si at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Hwaseong-si is 121st warmest (12.1 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (1,370 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ansan-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Hwaseong-si is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.8 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Why Hwaseong-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hwaseong-si itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.207°N, 126.817°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-467-3068), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
