Gimpo-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Gimpo-si is 40th warmest (12.1 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,349 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (1,410 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Goyang-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Gimpo-si is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Gimpo-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
