Busan at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Busan is 6th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 2,289 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (1,594 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kimhae, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Busan is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 154 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Busan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Busan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
