Busan weather in October

Average October weather in Busan, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Busan averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 13.4 °C (56 °F) nights, with 61 mm (2.4 in) of rain over the month and about 195 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Busan

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Busan. Compared with September: 4.8 °C cooler, 115 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Busan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Busan in October?
In October, Busan averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Busan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Busan’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Busan in October?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (341 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Busan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Busan get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Busan in October?
About 21.4 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Busan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
