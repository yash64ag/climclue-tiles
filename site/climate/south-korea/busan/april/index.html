How April compares in Busan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Busan. Compared with March: 4.6 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. May is 3.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Busan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Busan in April?
In April, Busan averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Busan?
April is not one of Busan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Busan in April?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (331 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Busan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Busan get in April?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Busan in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
