How December compares in Busan

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Busan. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Busan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Busan in December?
In December, Busan averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Busan?
December is not one of Busan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Busan in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (341 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Busan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Busan get in December?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Busan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
