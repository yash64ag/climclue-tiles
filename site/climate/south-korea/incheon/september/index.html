How September compares in Incheon

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Incheon. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 152 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. October is 6.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Incheon in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Incheon in September?
In September, Incheon averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Incheon?
No month in Incheon is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Incheon in September?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (391 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Incheon in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Incheon get in September?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Incheon in September?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Incheon in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
