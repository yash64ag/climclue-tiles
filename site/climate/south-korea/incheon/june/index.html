How June compares in Incheon

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Incheon. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. July is 3.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Incheon in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Incheon in June?
In June, Incheon averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Incheon?
No month in Incheon is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Incheon in June?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (391 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Incheon in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Incheon get in June?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Incheon in June?
About 16.9 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Incheon in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
