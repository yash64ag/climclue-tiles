How December compares in Incheon

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Incheon. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 3.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Incheon in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Incheon in December?
In December, Incheon averages a day high of 5.2 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -3.3 °C (26 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Incheon?
No month in Incheon is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Incheon in December?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (391 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Incheon in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Incheon get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Incheon in December?
About 9.4 °C (49 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Incheon in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
