How July compares in Incheon

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Incheon. Compared with June: 3.5 °C warmer, 261 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. August is 1.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Incheon in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Incheon in July?
In July, Incheon averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 391 mm (15.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Incheon?
No month in Incheon is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Incheon in July?
391 mm (15.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (391 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Incheon in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Incheon get in July?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Incheon in July?
About 21.0 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Incheon in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
