Jangheung at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Jangheung is 28th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 140th sunniest (about 2,251 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (1,443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Boseong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Jangheung is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 79 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 24.8 °C more than its coolest, January (1.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jangheung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jangheung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jangheung itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.682°N, 126.907°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-493-3069), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
