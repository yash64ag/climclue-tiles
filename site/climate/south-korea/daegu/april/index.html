How April compares in Daegu

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Daegu. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Daegu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daegu in April?
In April, Daegu averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Daegu?
April is not one of Daegu’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daegu in April?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (214 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Daegu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daegu get in April?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daegu in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
