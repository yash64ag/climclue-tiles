How December compares in Daegu

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Daegu. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Daegu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daegu in December?
In December, Daegu averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -3.5 °C (26 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Daegu?
December is not one of Daegu’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daegu in December?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (214 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Daegu in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Daegu get in December?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daegu in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
