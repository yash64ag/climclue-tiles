How September compares in Daegu

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Daegu. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, 84 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. October is 6.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Daegu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daegu in September?
In September, Daegu averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Daegu?
September is not one of Daegu’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daegu in September?
131 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (214 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Daegu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daegu get in September?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daegu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
