Daegu at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Daegu is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,483 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,062 mm a year). Compared with Gyeongsan-si, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Daegu is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Daegu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Daegu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
