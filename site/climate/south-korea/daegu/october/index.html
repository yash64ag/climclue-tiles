How October compares in Daegu

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Daegu. Compared with September: 6.1 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. November is 7.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Daegu in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daegu in October?
In October, Daegu averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Daegu?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Daegu’s mild months (May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daegu in October?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (214 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Daegu in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daegu get in October?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daegu in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
