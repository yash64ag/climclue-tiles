Suncheon at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Suncheon is 25th warmest (13.3 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,312 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gwangyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Suncheon is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Suncheon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Suncheon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
