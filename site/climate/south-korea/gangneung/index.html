Gangneung at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Gangneung is 38th warmest (12.2 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,327 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,635 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Donghae City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Gangneung is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 282 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gangneung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gangneung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
