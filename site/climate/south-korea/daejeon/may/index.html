Daejeon weather in May

May · South Korea

Average May weather in Daejeon: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How May compares in Daejeon

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Daejeon. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. June is 4.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Daejeon in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daejeon in May?
In May, Daejeon averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Daejeon?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Daejeon’s mild months (May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daejeon in May?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (288 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Daejeon in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daejeon get in May?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daejeon in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
