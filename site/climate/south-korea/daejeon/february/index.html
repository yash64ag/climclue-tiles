How February compares in Daejeon

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Daejeon. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. March is 5.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Daejeon in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daejeon in February?
In February, Daejeon averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Daejeon?
February is not one of Daejeon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daejeon in February?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Daejeon in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Daejeon get in February?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daejeon in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
