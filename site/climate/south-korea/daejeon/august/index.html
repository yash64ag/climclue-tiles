How August compares in Daejeon

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Daejeon. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Daejeon in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daejeon in August?
In August, Daejeon averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Daejeon?
August is not one of Daejeon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daejeon in August?
294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Daejeon in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daejeon get in August?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daejeon in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
