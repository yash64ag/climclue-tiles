Daejeon weather in June

Average June weather in Daejeon, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Daejeon averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) days and 17.6 °C (64 °F) nights, with 151 mm (6.0 in) of rain over the month and about 230 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Daejeon

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Daejeon. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 66 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Daejeon in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daejeon in June?
In June, Daejeon averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Daejeon?
June is not one of Daejeon’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daejeon in June?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (288 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Daejeon in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daejeon get in June?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daejeon in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
