Jinan-gun at a glance

Among the 147 towns ClimaClue covers in South Korea, Jinan-gun is 122nd warmest (12.1 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (1,586 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Jinan-gun is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 199 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.8 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Why Jinan-gun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jinan-gun itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.792°N, 127.425°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-482-3074), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
