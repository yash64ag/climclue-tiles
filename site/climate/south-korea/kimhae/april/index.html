How April compares in Kimhae

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kimhae. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 4.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Kimhae in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kimhae in April?
In April, Kimhae averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kimhae?
April is not one of Kimhae’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kimhae in April?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (301 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Kimhae in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kimhae get in April?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kimhae in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
