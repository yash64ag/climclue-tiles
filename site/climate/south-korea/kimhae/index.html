Kimhae at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Kimhae is 7th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (1,440 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changwon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Kimhae is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 65 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kimhae, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kimhae has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
