How February compares in Kimhae

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kimhae. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 4.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Kimhae in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kimhae in February?
In February, Kimhae averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kimhae?
February is not one of Kimhae’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kimhae in February?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (290 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Kimhae in February?
Yes, on average: about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Kimhae get in February?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kimhae in February?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kimhae in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
