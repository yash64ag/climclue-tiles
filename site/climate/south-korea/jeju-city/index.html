Jeju City at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Jeju City is the 2nd warmest (15.7 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,105 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,761 mm a year). Compared with Seogwipo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Jeju City is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 164 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jeju City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jeju City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jeju City itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.510°N, 126.522°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-504-3065), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
