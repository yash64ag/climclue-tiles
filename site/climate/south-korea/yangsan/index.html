Yangsan at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Yangsan is 14th warmest (13.8 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,313 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (1,461 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kimhae, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Yangsan is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yangsan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yangsan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
