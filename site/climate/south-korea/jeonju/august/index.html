How August compares in Jeonju

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jeonju. Compared with July: 0.5 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. September is 4.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Jeonju in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jeonju in August?
In August, Jeonju averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jeonju?
August is not one of Jeonju’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jeonju in August?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (307 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Jeonju in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jeonju get in August?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jeonju in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
