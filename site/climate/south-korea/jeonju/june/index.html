Jeonju weather in June

Average June weather in Jeonju, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Jeonju averages 27.6 °C (82 °F) days and 17.3 °C (63 °F) nights, with 171 mm (6.7 in) of rain over the month and about 222 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Jeonju

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Jeonju. Compared with May: 4.2 °C warmer, 78 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. July is 3.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Jeonju in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jeonju in June?
In June, Jeonju averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jeonju?
June is not one of Jeonju’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jeonju in June?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (307 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Jeonju in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jeonju get in June?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jeonju in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
