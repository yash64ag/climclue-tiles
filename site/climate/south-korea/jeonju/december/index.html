How December compares in Jeonju

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Jeonju. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Jeonju in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jeonju in December?
In December, Jeonju averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jeonju?
December is not one of Jeonju’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jeonju in December?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (307 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Jeonju in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.9 cm (2.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jeonju get in December?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jeonju in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
