Why Jeonju has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jeonju climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jeonju?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.2 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 22.1 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jeonju?
January, with an average of -0.5 °C (31 °F), day highs around 3.1 °C (38 °F) and night lows around -5.2 °C (23 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jeonju?
July, with 310 mm (12.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,364 mm (53.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jeonju?
December, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jeonju?
May, with about 255 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jeonju?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 43 cm (17.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Jeonju?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and October.
What type of climate does Jeonju have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Jeonju’s GeoNames location (35.825°N, 127.148°E; ClimaClue area sq-481-3071), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 638,421 (GeoNames).
