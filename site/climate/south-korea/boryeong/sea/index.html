Boryeong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chungcheongnam-do · South Korea

The sea off Boryeong averages 24.4 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.9 °C (41 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Boryeong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Boryeong?
In August, at about 24.4 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Boryeong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 4.9 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Boryeong in February?
About 4.9 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Boryeong?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Boryeong nearby?
In August: Gunsan (25.3 °C, 42 km), Buan (25.3 °C, 70 km) and Yeosu (25.2 °C, 201 km).
