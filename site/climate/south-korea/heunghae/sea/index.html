Heunghae sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gyeongsangbuk-do · South Korea

The sea off Heunghae averages 24.4 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.6 °C (53 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Heunghae sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Heunghae?
In August, at about 24.4 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Heunghae?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 11.6 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Heunghae in February?
About 11.6 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Heunghae?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Heunghae nearby?
In August: Gijang (25.4 °C, 97 km), Busan (25.8 °C, 116 km) and Sinhyeon (25.7 °C, 151 km).
