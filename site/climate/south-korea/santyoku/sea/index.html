Santyoku sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gangwon-do · South Korea

The sea off Santyoku averages 23.7 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.6 °C (49 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Santyoku sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santyoku?
In August, at about 23.7 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santyoku?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 9.6 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santyoku in February?
About 9.6 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Santyoku?
About 15.9 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Santyoku nearby?
In August: Heunghae (24.4 °C, 149 km), Pohang (24.6 °C, 158 km) and Ulsan (24.6 °C, 212 km).
