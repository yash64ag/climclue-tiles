Icheon-si at a glance

Among the 59 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Korea, Icheon-si is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,478 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Yeoju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Icheon-si is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 74 km from the sea.

Why Icheon-si has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 74 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
