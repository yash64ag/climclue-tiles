How August compares in Cheongju-si

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cheongju-si. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. September is 4.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Cheongju-si in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheongju-si in August?
In August, Cheongju-si averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 279 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cheongju-si?
August is not one of Cheongju-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheongju-si in August?
279 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (299 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cheongju-si in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheongju-si get in August?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheongju-si in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
