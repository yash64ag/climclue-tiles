Cheongju-si weather in March

Average March weather in Cheongju-si, South Korea: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Cheongju-si averages 12.3 °C (54 °F) days and 0.3 °C (33 °F) nights, with 46 mm (1.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 218 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Cheongju-si

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cheongju-si. Compared with February: 5.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 6.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Cheongju-si in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheongju-si in March?
In March, Cheongju-si averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cheongju-si?
March is not one of Cheongju-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheongju-si in March?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (299 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cheongju-si in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheongju-si get in March?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheongju-si in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
