How November compares in Cheongju-si

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cheongju-si. Compared with October: 7.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. December is 6.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Cheongju-si in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheongju-si in November?
In November, Cheongju-si averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cheongju-si?
November is not one of Cheongju-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheongju-si in November?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (304 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cheongju-si in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheongju-si get in November?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheongju-si in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
