How April compares in Cheongju-si

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cheongju-si. Compared with March: 6.9 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Cheongju-si in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cheongju-si in April?
In April, Cheongju-si averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cheongju-si?
April is not one of Cheongju-si’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cheongju-si in April?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (299 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cheongju-si in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cheongju-si get in April?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cheongju-si in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
