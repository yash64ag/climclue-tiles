Sakiet ed Daier at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tunisia, Sakiet ed Daier is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,119 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sakiet ez Zit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Sakiet ed Daier is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.9 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sakiet ed Daier has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
