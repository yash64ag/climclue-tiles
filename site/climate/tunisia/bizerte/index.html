Bizerte at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tunisia, Bizerte is the 2nd coolest (18.3 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,689 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (731 mm a year). Compared with Aryanah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Bizerte is 1.1 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 199 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, February (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bizerte, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bizerte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
