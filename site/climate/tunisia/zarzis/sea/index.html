Zarzis sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Medenine Governorate · Tunisia

The sea off Zarzis averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.8 °C (59 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Zarzis sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Zarzis?
In August, at about 27.9 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Zarzis?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 14.8 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Zarzis in February?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Zarzis?
About 21.1 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Zarzis nearby?
In August: Mareth (28.6 °C, 78 km), Teboulbou (28.6 °C, 100 km) and Gabès (28.6 °C, 103 km).
