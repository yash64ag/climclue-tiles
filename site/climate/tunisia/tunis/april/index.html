How April compares in Tunis

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tunis. Compared with March: 2.3 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 3.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Tunis in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tunis in April?
In April, Tunis averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 10.7 °C (51 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tunis?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Tunis’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tunis in April?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tunis in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tunis get in April?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tunis in April?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tunis in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
