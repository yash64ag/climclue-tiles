How November compares in Tunis

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tunis. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Tunis in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tunis in November?
In November, Tunis averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tunis?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Tunis’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tunis in November?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tunis in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tunis get in November?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tunis in November?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tunis in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
