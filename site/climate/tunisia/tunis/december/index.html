How December compares in Tunis

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tunis. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Tunis in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tunis in December?
In December, Tunis averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tunis?
December is not one of Tunis’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tunis in December?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (80 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tunis in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tunis get in December?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tunis in December?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tunis in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
