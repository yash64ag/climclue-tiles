Dar el Haj Taïeb at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Dar el Haj Taïeb is 25th warmest (20.0 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 3,119 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (246 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gremda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Dar el Haj Taïeb is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Dar el Haj Taïeb has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dar el Haj Taïeb itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.784°N, 10.744°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-492-1907), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
