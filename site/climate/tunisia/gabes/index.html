Gabès at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tunisia, Gabès is the warmest (20.5 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,239 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (187 mm a year). Compared with Sfax, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Gabès is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 54 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.9 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gabès, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gabès has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
