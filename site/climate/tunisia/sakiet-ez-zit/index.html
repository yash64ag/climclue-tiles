Sakiet ez Zit at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Sakiet ez Zit is 28th warmest (20.0 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 3,096 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (251 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Ain, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Sakiet ez Zit is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sakiet ez Zit has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-1907), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
