Sīdī Ḥusayn at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Sīdī Ḥusayn is 62nd warmest (19.2 °C year average), 83rd sunniest (about 2,767 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (470 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Le Bardo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Sīdī Ḥusayn is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.6 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in February. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Why Sīdī Ḥusayn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sīdī Ḥusayn itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.756°N, 10.118°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-472-1901), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
