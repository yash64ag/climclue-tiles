El Mourouj at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tunisia, El Mourouj is 8th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,769 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (480 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sīdī Ḥusayn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), El Mourouj is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Mourouj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Mourouj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
