Grombalia weather by month and climate

Grombalia at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Grombalia is 91st warmest (18.8 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 2,770 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (444 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beni Khalled, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Grombalia is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (12.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in February. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Grombalia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Grombalia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.599°N, 10.500°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-474-1905), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
