Mennzel Bou Zelfa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nabeul Governorate · Tunisia

The sea off Mennzel Bou Zelfa averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.2 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Mennzel Bou Zelfa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mennzel Bou Zelfa?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mennzel Bou Zelfa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.2 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mennzel Bou Zelfa in February?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mennzel Bou Zelfa?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
