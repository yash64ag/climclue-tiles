Teboulbou weather by month and climate

Teboulbou at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Teboulbou is 19th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 3,240 hours of sunshine a year) and 110th wettest (190 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gabès, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Teboulbou is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.7 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Teboulbou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Teboulbou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.846°N, 10.114°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-501-1901), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
