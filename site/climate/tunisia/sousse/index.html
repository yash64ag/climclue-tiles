Why Sousse has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sousse climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sousse?
August, with an average of 28.1 °C (83 °F), day highs around 33.4 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 22.6 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sousse?
January, with an average of 12.3 °C (54 °F), day highs around 15.9 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 7.9 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sousse?
October, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 358 mm (14.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sousse?
July, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sousse?
July, with about 356 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sousse?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sousse?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, October and November.
What type of climate does Sousse have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Sousse’s GeoNames location (35.825°N, 10.637°E; ClimaClue area sq-481-1906), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 221,715 (GeoNames).
