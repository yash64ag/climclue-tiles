Rhennouch sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gabès Governorate · Tunisia

The sea off Rhennouch averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.7 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October.

Rhennouch sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rhennouch?
In August, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rhennouch?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 13.7 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rhennouch in February?
About 13.7 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rhennouch?
About 20.8 °C (70 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
