Ksour Essaf at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Ksour Essaf is 26th warmest (20.0 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,984 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (327 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mahdia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Ksour Essaf is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.2 °C), averages 15.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Ksour Essaf has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
