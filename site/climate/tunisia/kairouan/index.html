Kairouan at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tunisia, Kairouan is 4th warmest (19.9 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 3,015 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (317 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sousse, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Kairouan is 1.1 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (29.3 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kairouan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kairouan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
