Aryanah at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tunisia, Aryanah is the 3rd coolest (18.7 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,748 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (532 mm a year). Compared with Sukrah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Aryanah is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aryanah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aryanah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
