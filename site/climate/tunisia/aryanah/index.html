Aryanah at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Aryanah is 84th warmest (18.8 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,748 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (532 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rawad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Aryanah is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.7 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in February. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aryanah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aryanah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aryanah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.860°N, 10.193°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-471-1901), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
