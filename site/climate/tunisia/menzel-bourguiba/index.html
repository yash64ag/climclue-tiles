Menzel Bourguiba at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Menzel Bourguiba is 86th warmest (18.8 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 2,702 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tinja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Menzel Bourguiba is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in February. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Why Menzel Bourguiba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-468-1897), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
