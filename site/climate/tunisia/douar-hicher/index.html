Douar Hicher at a glance

Among the 126 towns ClimaClue covers in Tunisia, Douar Hicher is 74th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,748 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (506 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ettadhamen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Douar Hicher is 0.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in February. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Why Douar Hicher has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-471-1900), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
