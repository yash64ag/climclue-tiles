Sarandë sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Vlorë County · Albania

The sea off Sarandë averages 25.0 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.6 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Sarandë sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sarandë?
In August, at about 25.0 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sarandë?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 14.6 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sarandë in February?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sarandë?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sarandë nearby?
In August: Casarano (25.7 °C, 158 km) and Galatina (25.7 °C, 160 km).
