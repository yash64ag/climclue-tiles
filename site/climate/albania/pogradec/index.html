Pogradec at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Albania, Pogradec is the 4th coolest (12.2 °C year average), the 4th least sunny (about 2,244 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (765 mm a year). Compared with Korçë, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Pogradec is 1.1 °C warmer in August and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 149 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.4 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pogradec, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pogradec has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pogradec itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.902°N, 20.652°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-430-2006), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
