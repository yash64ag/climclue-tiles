Librazhd weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Librazhd, Albania (Elbasan County), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Librazhd is warmest in July, with 31.1 °C (88 °F) days and 18.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 7.8 °C (46 °F) days and -0.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around November (157 mm); August is driest (46 mm).

Librazhd at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Albania, Librazhd is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,187 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Elbasan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Librazhd is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.5 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 84 km from the sea.

Why Librazhd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 84 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. The 10 km area around Librazhd, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 11.6 °C a year, 2.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Librazhd itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.179°N, 20.315°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-428-2003), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
