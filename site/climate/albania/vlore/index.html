Vlorë at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Albania, Vlorë is the warmest (17.2 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,437 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (989 mm a year). Compared with Elbasan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Vlorë is 2.4 °C warmer in August and 4.8 °C warmer in January, with 303 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vlorë, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vlorë has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
