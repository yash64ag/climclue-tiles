Durrës at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Albania, Durrës is the 2nd warmest (16.7 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,449 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (956 mm a year). Compared with Tirana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Durrës is about as warm in August and 4.2 °C warmer in January, with 471 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.3 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (9.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Durrës, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Durrës has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
