Elbasan weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Elbasan, Albania (Elbasan County), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Elbasan is warmest in July, with 32.4 °C (90 °F) days and 18.7 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 9.9 °C (50 °F) days and 1.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around November (158 mm); July is driest (45 mm).

Elbasan at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Albania, Elbasan is the 5th least sunny (about 2,295 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Librazhd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Elbasan is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.5 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (5.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Elbasan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Elbasan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C. The 10 km area around Elbasan, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.1 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Elbasan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.111°N, 20.082°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-428-2000), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
