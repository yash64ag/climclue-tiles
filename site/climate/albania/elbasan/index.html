Elbasan at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Albania, Elbasan is the coolest (14.1 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,295 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,292 mm a year). Compared with Tirana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Elbasan is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 135 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Elbasan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Elbasan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
