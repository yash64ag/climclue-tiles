Krujë at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Albania, Krujë is the 3rd coolest (12.1 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (1,583 mm a year). Compared with Fushë-Krujë, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Krujë is 3.8 °C cooler in July and 4.1 °C cooler in January, with 251 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.2 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Krujë, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Krujë

For mild weather, the best months to visit Krujë are June, July and August.

Why Krujë has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C. The 10 km area around Krujë, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.3 °C a year, 2.3 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Krujë itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.509°N, 19.793°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-424-1997), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
