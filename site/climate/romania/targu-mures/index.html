Târgu Mureş at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Târgu Mureş is 85th warmest (9.8 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 1,876 hours of sunshine a year) and 75th wettest (596 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Târnăveni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Târgu Mureş is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 406 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Târgu Mureş itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.542°N, 24.557°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-374-2045), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
