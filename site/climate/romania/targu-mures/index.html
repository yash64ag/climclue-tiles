Târgu Mureş at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Târgu Mureş is the 5th coolest (9.6 °C year average). Compared with Cluj-Napoca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Târgu Mureş is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 406 km from the sea.

Why Târgu Mureş has this climate
