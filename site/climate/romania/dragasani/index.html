Drăgăşani at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Drăgăşani is 24th warmest (11.7 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,055 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Slatina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Drăgăşani is 0.4 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.2 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 347 km from the sea.

Why Drăgăşani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 347 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Drăgăşani itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.650°N, 24.267°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-393-2042), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
