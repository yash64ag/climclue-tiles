Piatra Neamţ at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Piatra Neamţ is the coolest (8.0 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 1,813 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bacău, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Piatra Neamţ is 2.7 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 103 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 292 km from the sea.

Why Piatra Neamţ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
