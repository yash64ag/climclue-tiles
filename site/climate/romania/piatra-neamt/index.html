Why Piatra Neamţ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Piatra Neamţ climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Piatra Neamţ?

What is the coldest month in Piatra Neamţ?

Which month gets the most rain in Piatra Neamţ?

What is the driest month in Piatra Neamţ?

What is the sunniest month in Piatra Neamţ?

Does it snow in Piatra Neamţ?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 163 cm (64.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Piatra Neamţ?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June and August.

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.6 × 11 km, that contains Piatra Neamţ’s GeoNames location (46.917°N, 26.333°E; ClimaClue area sq-370-2063), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest).
