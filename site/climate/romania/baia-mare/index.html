Baia Mare at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Baia Mare is the 4th coolest (9.5 °C year average) and the wettest (1,048 mm a year). Compared with Satu Mare, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Baia Mare is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 358 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 516 km from the sea.

Why Baia Mare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 516 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
