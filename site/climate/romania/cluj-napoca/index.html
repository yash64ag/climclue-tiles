Cluj-Napoca at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Cluj-Napoca is the 3rd coolest (8.7 °C year average). Compared with Târgu Mureş, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Cluj-Napoca is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 22.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 478 km from the sea.

Why Cluj-Napoca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 478 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
