Satu Mare at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Satu Mare is the 5th wettest (690 mm a year). Compared with Baia Mare, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Satu Mare is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 358 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 568 km from the sea.

Why Satu Mare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
