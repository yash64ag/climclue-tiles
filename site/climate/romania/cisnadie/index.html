Cisnădie at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Cisnădie is 82nd warmest (9.8 °C year average), 103rd sunniest (about 1,819 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (825 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sibiu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Cisnădie is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 398 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cisnădie itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.717°N, 24.150°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-382-2041), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
