Craiova at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Craiova is the 2nd warmest (12.1 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 2,104 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Piteşti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (103 km away), Craiova is 2.6 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 363 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 100% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Craiova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 363 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
