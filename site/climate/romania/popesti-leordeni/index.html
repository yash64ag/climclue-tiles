Popeşti-Leordeni at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Popeşti-Leordeni is 26th warmest (11.7 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bucharest, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Popeşti-Leordeni is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.5 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 202 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Popeşti-Leordeni itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.383°N, 26.167°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-396-2061), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
