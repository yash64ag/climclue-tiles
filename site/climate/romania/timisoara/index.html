Why Timişoara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 433 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Timişoara climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Timişoara?
July, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 27.4 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 17.1 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Timişoara?
January, with an average of 0.1 °C (32 °F), day highs around 3.8 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -2.9 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Timişoara?
June, with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 607 mm (23.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Timişoara?
February, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Timişoara?
July, with about 295 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Timişoara?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 54 cm (21.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Timişoara?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Timişoara have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.8 × 11 km, that contains Timişoara’s GeoNames location (45.754°N, 21.226°E; ClimaClue area sq-382-2012), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 250,849 (GeoNames).
