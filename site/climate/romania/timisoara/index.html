Timişoara at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Timişoara is the 4th warmest (11.7 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Arad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Timişoara is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 433 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Timişoara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Timişoara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 433 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
