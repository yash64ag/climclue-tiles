Bacău at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Bacău is the 5th least sunny (about 1,876 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (529 mm a year). Compared with Piatra Neamţ, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Bacău is 2.7 °C warmer in July and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 231 km from the sea.

Why Bacău has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 231 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
