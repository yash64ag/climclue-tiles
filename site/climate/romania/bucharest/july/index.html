How July compares in Bucharest

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bucharest. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Bucharest in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in July?
In July, Bucharest averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bucharest?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Bucharest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in July?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (35 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in July?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
