How March compares in Bucharest

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with February: 5.2 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. April is 6.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Bucharest in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in March?
In March, Bucharest averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bucharest?
March is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in March?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in March?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
