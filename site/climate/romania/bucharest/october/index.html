How October compares in Bucharest

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with September: 6.1 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. November is 6.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Bucharest in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in October?
In October, Bucharest averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bucharest?
October is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in October?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in October?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
