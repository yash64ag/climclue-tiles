How December compares in Bucharest

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bucharest. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Of the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Bucharest ranks 8th warmest (average temperature), 3rd sunniest and 8th wettest in December.

Bucharest in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in December?
In December, Bucharest averages a day high of 4.0 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bucharest?
December is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in December?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
