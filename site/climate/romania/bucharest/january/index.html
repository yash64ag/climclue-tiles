How January compares in Bucharest

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Bucharest in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in January?
In January, Bucharest averages a day high of 2.9 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -4.5 °C (24 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bucharest?
January is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in January?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in January?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
