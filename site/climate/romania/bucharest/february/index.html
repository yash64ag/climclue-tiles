How February compares in Bucharest

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. March is 5.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Bucharest in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in February?
In February, Bucharest averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.5 °C (26 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bucharest?
February is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in February?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in February?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
