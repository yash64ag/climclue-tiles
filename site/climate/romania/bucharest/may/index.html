How May compares in Bucharest

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Bucharest in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in May?
In May, Bucharest averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bucharest?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Bucharest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in May?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in May?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
