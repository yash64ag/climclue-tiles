How November compares in Bucharest

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 85 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Bucharest in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in November?
In November, Bucharest averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 86 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bucharest?
November is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in November?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in November?
About 86 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
