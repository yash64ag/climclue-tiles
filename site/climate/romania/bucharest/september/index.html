How September compares in Bucharest

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. October is 6.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Bucharest in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in September?
In September, Bucharest averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bucharest?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Bucharest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in September?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in September?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
