How April compares in Bucharest

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bucharest. Compared with March: 5.7 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. May is 5.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Bucharest in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bucharest in April?
In April, Bucharest averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bucharest?
April is not one of Bucharest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bucharest in April?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (35 mm).
Does it snow in Bucharest in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bucharest get in April?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bucharest in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
