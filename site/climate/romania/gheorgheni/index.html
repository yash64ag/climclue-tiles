Gheorgheni at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Gheorgheni is the 2nd coolest (6.5 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 1,713 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Miercurea-Ciuc, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Gheorgheni is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 81 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 331 km from the sea.

Why Gheorgheni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 331 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C. The 10 km area around Gheorgheni, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 4.7 °C a year, 1.8 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gheorgheni itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.723°N, 25.601°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-372-2056), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
