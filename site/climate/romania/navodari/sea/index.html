Năvodari sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Constanța County · Romania

The sea off Năvodari averages 24.3 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.8 °C (41 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Năvodari sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Năvodari?
In August, at about 24.3 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Năvodari?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 4.8 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Năvodari in February?
About 4.8 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Năvodari?
About 14.0 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
