Râmnicu Sărat at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Râmnicu Sărat is 40th warmest (11.3 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 100th wettest (538 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buzău, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Râmnicu Sărat is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 174 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Râmnicu Sărat itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.383°N, 27.050°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-386-2070), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
