Buzău at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Buzău is the 4th driest (520 mm a year). Compared with Ploieşti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Buzău is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Buzău has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
