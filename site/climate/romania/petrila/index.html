Petrila at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Petrila is the 5th wettest (959 mm a year). Compared with Petroşani, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Petrila is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.2 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 439 km from the sea.

Why Petrila has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 439 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C. The 10 km area around Petrila, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 7.1 °C a year, 1.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Petrila itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.450°N, 23.417°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-385-2034), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
