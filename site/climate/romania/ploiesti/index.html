Ploieşti at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Ploieşti is 10th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,001 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (586 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bucharest, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Ploieşti is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 227 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ploieşti, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ploieşti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 227 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
