Iaşi at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Iaşi is 13th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 1,945 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (556 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bacău, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Iaşi is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 227 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Iaşi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 227 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
