Galaţi at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Galaţi is the 5th warmest (11.7 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,123 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (506 mm a year). Compared with Brăila, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Galaţi is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 7 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Galaţi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
