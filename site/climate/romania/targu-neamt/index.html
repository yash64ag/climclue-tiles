Târgu Neamţ at a glance

Among the 127 towns ClimaClue covers in Romania, Târgu Neamţ is 106th warmest (8.5 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 1,825 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paşcani, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Târgu Neamţ is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 76 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 309 km from the sea.

Why Târgu Neamţ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Târgu Neamţ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.200°N, 26.367°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-367-2063), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
