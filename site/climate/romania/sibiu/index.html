Sibiu at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Sibiu is the 4th least sunny (about 1,826 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (717 mm a year). Compared with Târgu Mureş, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Sibiu is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 101 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 403 km from the sea.

Why Sibiu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 403 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
