Braşov at a glance

Among the 20 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Romania, Braşov is the 2nd coolest (8.1 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 1,739 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (763 mm a year). Compared with Ploieşti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Braşov is 3.7 °C cooler in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 177 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 286 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Braşov, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Braşov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 286 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
