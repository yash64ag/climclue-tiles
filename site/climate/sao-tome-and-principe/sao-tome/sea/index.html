São Tomé sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · São Tomé Island · Sao Tome and Principe

The sea off São Tomé averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 25.3 °C (78 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

São Tomé sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in São Tomé?
In March, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in São Tomé?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.3 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in São Tomé in August?
About 25.3 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in São Tomé?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
