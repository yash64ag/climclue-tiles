Praia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Cabo Verde

The sea off Praia averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 22.5 °C (73 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to December.

Praia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Praia?
In September, at about 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Praia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to December. The coolest month is February, at 22.5 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Praia in February?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Praia?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Praia nearby?
In September: Dakar (28.4 °C, 653 km), Guédiawaye (28.4 °C, 657 km) and Pikine (28.4 °C, 658 km).
