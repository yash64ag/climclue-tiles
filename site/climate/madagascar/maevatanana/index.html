Maevatanana at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Maevatanana is the 3rd warmest (27.2 °C year average). Compared with Ambato Boeny, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Maevatanana is 0.1 °C warmer in November and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 108 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (29.3 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, July (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maevatanana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maevatanana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Maevatanana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.950°S, 46.833°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1009-2268), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
