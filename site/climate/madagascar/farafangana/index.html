Farafangana at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Farafangana is 46th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,572 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (2,421 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ifatsy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Farafangana is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 147 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.3 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, July (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Farafangana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Farafangana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1068-2278), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
