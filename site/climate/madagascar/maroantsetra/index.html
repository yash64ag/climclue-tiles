Maroantsetra at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Maroantsetra is 47th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 2,544 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (3,402 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mananara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Maroantsetra is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.7 °C cooler in August, with 81 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.4 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, August (20.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Maroantsetra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
