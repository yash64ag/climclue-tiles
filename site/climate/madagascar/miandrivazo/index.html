Miandrivazo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Miandrivazo, Madagascar (Menabe), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Miandrivazo at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Miandrivazo is 10th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,178 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (1,570 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Belo sur Tsiribihina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Miandrivazo is 0.5 °C warmer in November and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 673 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.2 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Miandrivazo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Miandrivazo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Miandrivazo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.529°S, 45.456°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1035-2254), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
