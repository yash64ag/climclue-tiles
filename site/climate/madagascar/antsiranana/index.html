Antsiranana at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Madagascar, Antsiranana is the 2nd warmest (25.3 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,955 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,176 mm a year). Compared with Mahajanga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (496 km away), Antsiranana is 1.1 °C cooler in December and 2.0 °C cooler in August, with 321 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (26.6 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, August (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in August. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Antsiranana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Antsiranana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
