Ambatondrazaka at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Ambatondrazaka is 72nd warmest (20.8 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 2,505 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (1,231 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Didy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Ambatondrazaka is 1.9 °C warmer in January and 2.3 °C warmer in July, with 582 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ambatondrazaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ambatondrazaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ambatondrazaka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.833°S, 48.417°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1018-2284), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
