Ambato Boeny at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Ambato Boeny is the warmest (27.3 °C year average). Compared with Marovoay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Ambato Boeny is 0.2 °C warmer in November and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (29.2 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, July (24.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Why Ambato Boeny has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ambato Boeny itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.467°S, 46.717°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1004-2267), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
