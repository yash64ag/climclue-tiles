Toamasina at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Madagascar, Toamasina is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,393 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (3,608 mm a year). Compared with Antananarivo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (214 km away), Toamasina is 5.5 °C warmer in February and 7.0 °C warmer in July, with 2,154 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.4 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, July (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Toamasina, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Toamasina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
