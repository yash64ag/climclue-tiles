Itampolo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Atsimo-Andrefana · Madagascar

The sea off Itampolo averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 23.1 °C (74 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to June.

Itampolo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Itampolo?
In February, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Itampolo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to June. The coolest month is August, at 23.1 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Itampolo in August?
About 23.1 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Itampolo?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Itampolo nearby?
In February: Toliara (29.1 °C, 151 km), Morombe (29.5 °C, 332 km) and Morondava (29.7 °C, 490 km).
