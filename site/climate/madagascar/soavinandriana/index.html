Soavinandriana at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Soavinandriana is 95th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,648 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (2,052 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miandrarivo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Soavinandriana is 0.6 °C cooler in January and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 355 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (19.4 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 237 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Soavinandriana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Soavinandriana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 237 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1031-2267), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
