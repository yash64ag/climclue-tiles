Ambovombe sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Androy · Madagascar

The sea off Ambovombe averages 26.3 °C (79 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 22.0 °C (72 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May.

Ambovombe sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ambovombe?
In January, at about 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ambovombe?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May. The coolest month is August, at 22.0 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ambovombe in August?
About 22.0 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Ambovombe?
About 24.2 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ambovombe nearby?
In January: Itampolo (28.0 °C, 222 km), Vohipaho (27.4 °C, 231 km) and Vangaindrano (27.4 °C, 255 km).
