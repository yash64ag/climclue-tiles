Ankazoabo at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Ankazoabo is 34th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 3,219 hours of sunshine a year) and 103rd wettest (598 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sakaraha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Ankazoabo is 1.2 °C warmer in November and 1.1 °C warmer in July, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.6 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, July (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in July. The area lies about 141 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ankazoabo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ankazoabo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 141 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ankazoabo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.283°S, 44.517°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1062-2245), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
