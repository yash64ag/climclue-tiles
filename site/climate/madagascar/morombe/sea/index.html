Morombe sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Atsimo-Andrefana · Madagascar

The sea off Morombe averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 24.3 °C (76 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Morombe sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Morombe?
In February, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Morombe?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 24.3 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Morombe in August?
About 24.3 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Morombe?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
