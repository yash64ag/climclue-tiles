Toliara at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Madagascar, Toliara is the 3rd warmest (25.1 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,513 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (428 mm a year). Compared with Fianarantsoa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (410 km away), Toliara is 7.5 °C warmer in February and 8.1 °C warmer in July, with 884 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.9 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, July (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Toliara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Toliara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
