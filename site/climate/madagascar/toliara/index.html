Why Toliara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Toliara climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Toliara?
February, with an average of 27.9 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 25.3 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Toliara?
July, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Toliara?
January, with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 428 mm (16.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Toliara?
August, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Toliara?
November, with about 310 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Toliara?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Toliara?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Toliara have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Toliara’s GeoNames location (23.350°S, 43.667°E; ClimaClue area sq-1073-2236), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 178,725 (GeoNames).
