Ambanja sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Diana · Madagascar

The sea off Ambanja averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Ambanja sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ambanja?
In April, at about 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ambanja?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ambanja in August?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Ambanja?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
