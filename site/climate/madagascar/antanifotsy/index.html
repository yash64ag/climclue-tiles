Antanifotsy at a glance

Among the 108 towns ClimaClue covers in Madagascar, Antanifotsy is the 4th coolest (16.4 °C year average). Compared with Soanindrariny, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Antanifotsy is 1.2 °C warmer in January and 1.1 °C warmer in July, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (19.3 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, July (12.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 161 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Antanifotsy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Antanifotsy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 161 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Antanifotsy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.650°S, 47.317°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1036-2273), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
