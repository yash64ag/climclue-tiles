How September compares in Antananarivo

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Antananarivo. Compared with August: 1.9 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. October is 2.0 °C warmer than September on average.

Antananarivo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in September?
In September, Antananarivo averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Antananarivo?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Antananarivo’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in September?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (357 mm), the driest June (8 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in September?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
