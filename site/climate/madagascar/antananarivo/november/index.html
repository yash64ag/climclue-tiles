How November compares in Antananarivo

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Antananarivo. Compared with October: 1.5 °C warmer, 75 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. December is 0.7 °C warmer than November on average.

Antananarivo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in November?
In November, Antananarivo averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Antananarivo?
November is not one of Antananarivo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in November?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (359 mm), the driest June (9 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in November?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
