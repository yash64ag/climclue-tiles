How July compares in Antananarivo

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Antananarivo. Compared with June: 0.9 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C warmer than July on average.

Antananarivo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in July?
In July, Antananarivo averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Antananarivo?
July is not one of Antananarivo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in July?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (359 mm), the driest June (9 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in July?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in July?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
