How December compares in Antananarivo

December is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Antananarivo. Compared with November: 0.7 °C warmer, 136 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Antananarivo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in December?
In December, Antananarivo averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 275 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Antananarivo?
December is not one of Antananarivo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in December?
275 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (359 mm), the driest June (9 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in December?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
