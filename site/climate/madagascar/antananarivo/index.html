Antananarivo at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Madagascar, Antananarivo is the 3rd coolest (18.3 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (1,454 mm a year). Compared with Antsirabe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (118 km away), Antananarivo is 1.2 °C warmer in January and 1.4 °C warmer in July, with 120 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.9 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, July (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Antananarivo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Antananarivo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
