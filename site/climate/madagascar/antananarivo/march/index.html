How March compares in Antananarivo

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Antananarivo. Compared with February: 0.3 °C cooler, 73 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. April is 1.2 °C cooler than March on average.

Antananarivo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in March?
In March, Antananarivo averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Antananarivo?
March is not one of Antananarivo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in March?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (357 mm), the driest June (8 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in March?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
