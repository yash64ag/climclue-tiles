How January compares in Antananarivo

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Antananarivo. Compared with December: about as warm, 84 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Antananarivo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in January?
In January, Antananarivo averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 359 mm (14.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Antananarivo?
January is not one of Antananarivo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in January?
359 mm (14.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (359 mm), the driest June (9 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in January?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
