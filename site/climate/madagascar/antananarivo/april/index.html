How April compares in Antananarivo

Antananarivo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Antananarivo in April?
In April, Antananarivo averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Antananarivo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Antananarivo’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Antananarivo in April?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (359 mm), the driest June (9 mm).
Does it snow in Antananarivo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Antananarivo get in April?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Antananarivo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
