Soanierana Ivongo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Analanjirofo · Madagascar

The sea off Soanierana Ivongo averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 24.0 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Soanierana Ivongo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Soanierana Ivongo?
In February, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Soanierana Ivongo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 24.0 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Soanierana Ivongo in August?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Soanierana Ivongo?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
