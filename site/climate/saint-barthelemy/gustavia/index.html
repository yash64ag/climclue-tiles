Gustavia at a glance

Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, February (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gustavia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Gustavia

For mild weather, the best months to visit Gustavia are January, February, March and April.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March and April.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (26.9 °C), September (26.9 °C) and July (26.8 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): March (50 mm), February (51 mm) and January (64 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): March (264 h), August (260 h) and April (259 h).

Why Gustavia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
