Philipsburg at a glance

Its warmest month, August (26.9 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, February (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Philipsburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
