Mansa at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Mansa is the least sunny (about 2,619 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mufulira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (166 km away), Mansa is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 197 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.9 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, July (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,157 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mansa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mansa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,157 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
