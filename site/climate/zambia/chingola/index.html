Chingola at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Chingola is 13th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,763 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,305 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chililabombwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Chingola is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.4 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, July (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,111 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chingola, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chingola has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,111 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
