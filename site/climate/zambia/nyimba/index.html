Nyimba at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Zambia, Nyimba is the 5th warmest (24.1 °C year average). Compared with Petauke, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Nyimba is 2.6 °C warmer in October and 1.9 °C warmer in July, with 335 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.3 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, July (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 730 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nyimba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nyimba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 730 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nyimba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.557°S, 30.815°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-985-2108), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
