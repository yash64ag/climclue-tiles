Chisasa at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Zambia, Chisasa is 53rd warmest (20.6 °C year average), 69th sunniest (about 2,634 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,356 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manyama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Chisasa is 0.2 °C cooler in September and about as warm in July, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.4 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,277 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chisasa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chisasa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,277 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chisasa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.109°S, 25.496°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-961-2054), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
