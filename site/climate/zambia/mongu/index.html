Mongu at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Mongu is the warmest (22.9 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 3,032 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Livingstone, the nearest covered town with different numbers (409 km away), Mongu is about as warm in October and 1.7 °C warmer in July, with 323 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.9 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, July (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.5 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,086 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mongu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mongu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,086 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
