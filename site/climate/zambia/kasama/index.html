Kasama at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Zambia, Kasama is the 3rd wettest (1,409 mm a year). Compared with Chinsali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Kasama is 0.7 °C cooler in October and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 432 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.7 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 922 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kasama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kasama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 922 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kasama itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.213°S, 31.181°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-942-2111), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
