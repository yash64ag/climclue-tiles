How May compares in Ndola

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ndola. Compared with April: 1.2 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Ndola in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ndola in May?
In May, Ndola averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ndola?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Ndola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ndola in May?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (354 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ndola in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ndola get in May?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ndola in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
