How March compares in Ndola

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ndola. Compared with February: 0.2 °C warmer, 57 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. April is 0.3 °C cooler than March on average.

Ndola in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ndola in March?
In March, Ndola averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ndola?
March is not one of Ndola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ndola in March?
204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (354 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ndola in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ndola get in March?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ndola in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
