How November compares in Ndola

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ndola. Compared with October: 1.3 °C cooler, 109 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. December is 2.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Ndola in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ndola in November?
In November, Ndola averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ndola?
November is not one of Ndola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ndola in November?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (360 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ndola in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ndola get in November?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ndola in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
