How February compares in Ndola

February is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ndola. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 93 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Ndola in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ndola in February?
In February, Ndola averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ndola?
February is not one of Ndola’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ndola in February?
261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (354 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ndola in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ndola get in February?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ndola in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
