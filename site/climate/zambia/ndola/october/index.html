How October compares in Ndola

October is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ndola. Compared with September: 1.5 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. November is 1.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Ndola in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ndola in October?
In October, Ndola averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ndola?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Ndola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ndola in October?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (360 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ndola in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ndola get in October?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ndola in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
