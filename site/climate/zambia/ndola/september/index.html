How September compares in Ndola

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ndola. Compared with August: 3.3 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Ndola in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ndola in September?
In September, Ndola averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ndola?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Ndola’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ndola in September?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (360 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ndola in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ndola get in September?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ndola in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
