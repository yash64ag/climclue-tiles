Why Kabwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 914 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kabwe climate: questions and answers

What is the coldest month in Kabwe?
July, with an average of 16.3 °C (61 °F), day highs around 22.1 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 10.8 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kabwe?

What is the driest month in Kabwe?
August, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kabwe?
August, with about 312 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kabwe?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kabwe?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kabwe’s GeoNames location (14.447°S, 28.446°E; ClimaClue area sq-984-2084), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 288,598 (GeoNames).
