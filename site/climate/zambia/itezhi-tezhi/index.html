Itezhi-Tezhi at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Zambia, Itezhi-Tezhi is 22nd warmest (22.2 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (754 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Namwala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Itezhi-Tezhi is 0.4 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.4 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, July (17.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,042 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itezhi-Tezhi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itezhi-Tezhi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,042 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Itezhi-Tezhi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.737°S, 26.042°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-997-2060), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
