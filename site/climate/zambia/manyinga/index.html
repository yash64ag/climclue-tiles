Manyinga at a glance

Among the 77 towns ClimaClue covers in Zambia, Manyinga is 21st warmest (22.2 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,672 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (1,104 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kabompo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Manyinga is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.5 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (19.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,171 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Manyinga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Manyinga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,171 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Manyinga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.416°S, 24.335°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-974-2043), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
