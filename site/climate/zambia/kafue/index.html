Kafue at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Kafue is the 2nd driest (690 mm a year). Compared with Lusaka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Kafue is 1.6 °C warmer in October and 1.5 °C warmer in July, with 191 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.6 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 849 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kafue, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kafue has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 849 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
