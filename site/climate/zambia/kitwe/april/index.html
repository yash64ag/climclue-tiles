How April compares in Kitwe

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kitwe. Compared with March: 0.4 °C cooler, 169 mm less precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. May is 1.2 °C cooler than April on average.

Kitwe in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitwe in April?
In April, Kitwe averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kitwe?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Kitwe’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitwe in April?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (350 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kitwe in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitwe get in April?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kitwe in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
