How March compares in Kitwe

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kitwe. Compared with February: 0.1 °C warmer, 51 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C cooler than March on average.

Kitwe in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitwe in March?
In March, Kitwe averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kitwe?
March is not one of Kitwe’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitwe in March?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (337 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kitwe in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitwe get in March?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kitwe in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
