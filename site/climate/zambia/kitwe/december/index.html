Kitwe weather in December

Average December weather in Kitwe, Zambia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Kitwe averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) days and 16.5 °C (62 °F) nights, with 306 mm (12.0 in) of rain over the month and about 156 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Kitwe

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kitwe. Compared with November: 1.9 °C cooler, 166 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Kitwe in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitwe in December?
In December, Kitwe averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kitwe?
December is not one of Kitwe’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitwe in December?
306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (350 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kitwe in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitwe get in December?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kitwe in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
