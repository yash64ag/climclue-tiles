Kitwe at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Kitwe is 5th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,795 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,288 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kalulushi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Kitwe is 0.4 °C warmer in October and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.1 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, July (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,057 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kitwe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kitwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,057 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
