Kitwe weather in May

Average May weather in Kitwe, Zambia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Kitwe averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) days and 10.9 °C (52 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 276 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Kitwe

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kitwe. Compared with April: 1.2 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. June is 2.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Kitwe in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitwe in May?
In May, Kitwe averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kitwe?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kitwe’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitwe in May?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (350 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kitwe in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitwe get in May?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kitwe in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
