How September compares in Kitwe

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kitwe. Compared with August: 3.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. October is 1.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Kitwe in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kitwe in September?
In September, Kitwe averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kitwe?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Kitwe’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kitwe in September?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (350 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kitwe in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kitwe get in September?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kitwe in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
