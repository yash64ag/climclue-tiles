Luanshya at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Luanshya is 9th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,800 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,198 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ndola, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Luanshya is 0.4 °C warmer in October and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 172 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.0 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,018 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Luanshya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Luanshya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,018 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Luanshya itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.137°S, 28.417°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-971-2084), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
