How November compares in Lusaka

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lusaka. Compared with October: 0.5 °C cooler, 77 mm more precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. December is 2.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Lusaka in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in November?
In November, Lusaka averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lusaka?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Lusaka’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lusaka in November?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (244 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lusaka in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lusaka get in November?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lusaka in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
