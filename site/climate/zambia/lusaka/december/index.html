How December compares in Lusaka

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lusaka. Compared with November: 2.0 °C cooler, 101 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Lusaka in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in December?
In December, Lusaka averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lusaka?
December is not one of Lusaka’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lusaka in December?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (244 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lusaka in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lusaka get in December?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lusaka in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
