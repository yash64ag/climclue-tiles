How September compares in Lusaka

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lusaka. Compared with August: 3.8 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. October is 2.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Lusaka in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in September?
In September, Lusaka averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lusaka?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Lusaka’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
