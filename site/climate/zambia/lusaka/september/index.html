Lusaka weather in September

Average September weather in Lusaka, Zambia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Lusaka averages 29.4 °C (85 °F) days and 14.5 °C (58 °F) nights, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of rain over the month and about 283 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Lusaka

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lusaka. Compared with August: 3.8 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. October is 2.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Lusaka in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in September?
In September, Lusaka averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lusaka?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Lusaka’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lusaka in September?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (247 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lusaka in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lusaka get in September?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lusaka in September?
