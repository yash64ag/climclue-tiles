Lusaka at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Lusaka is the coolest (20.1 °C year average). Compared with Chunga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Lusaka is 0.4 °C cooler in October and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.1 °C), averages 8.5 °C more than its coolest, July (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 860 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lusaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lusaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 860 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
