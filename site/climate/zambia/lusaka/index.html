Why Lusaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 860 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Lusaka climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lusaka?
October, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 18.4 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lusaka?
July, with an average of 15.6 °C (60 °F), day highs around 21.5 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 10.3 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lusaka?
January, with 244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 881 mm (34.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lusaka?
August, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lusaka?
August, with about 298 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lusaka?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Lusaka?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Lusaka have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Lusaka’s GeoNames location (15.407°S, 28.287°E; ClimaClue area sq-994-2082), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,212,301 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
