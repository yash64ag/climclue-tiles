How August compares in Lusaka

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Lusaka. Compared with July: 2.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. September is 3.8 °C warmer than August on average.

Lusaka in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in August?
In August, Lusaka averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 298 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lusaka?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Lusaka’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
