How October compares in Lusaka

October is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Lusaka. Compared with September: 2.1 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 0.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Lusaka in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in October?
In October, Lusaka averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lusaka?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Lusaka’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lusaka in October?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (244 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lusaka in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lusaka get in October?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lusaka in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
