How February compares in Lusaka

February is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lusaka. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Lusaka in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lusaka in February?
In February, Lusaka averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lusaka?
February is not one of Lusaka’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lusaka in February?
200 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (244 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lusaka in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lusaka get in February?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lusaka in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
