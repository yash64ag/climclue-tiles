Mufulira at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Zambia, Mufulira is the wettest (1,359 mm a year). Compared with Kitwe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Mufulira is 0.2 °C cooler in October and about as warm in July, with 71 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.9 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, July (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,083 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mufulira, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mufulira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,083 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
