Franceville at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Gabon, Franceville is the 3rd warmest (24.8 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,583 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,872 mm a year). Compared with Libreville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (511 km away), Franceville is 1.1 °C cooler in March and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 1,265 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.6 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, July (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 377 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Franceville, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Franceville has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 377 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
