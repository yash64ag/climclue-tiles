Port-Gentil at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Gabon, Port-Gentil is the 2nd warmest (25.5 °C year average), the sunniest (about 1,659 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,860 mm a year). Compared with Libreville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (144 km away), Port-Gentil is 0.3 °C warmer in March and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 1,276 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.1 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Port-Gentil, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Port-Gentil has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
