Libreville in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Libreville in May?
In May, Libreville averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 288 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Libreville?
May is not one of Libreville’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Libreville in May?
288 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (524 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Libreville in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Libreville get in May?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Libreville in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
