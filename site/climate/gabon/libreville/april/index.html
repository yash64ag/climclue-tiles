How April compares in Libreville

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Libreville. Compared with March: about as warm, 9 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Libreville in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Libreville in April?
In April, Libreville averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Libreville?
April is not one of Libreville’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Libreville in April?
330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (516 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Libreville in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Libreville get in April?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Libreville in April?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Libreville in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
