How December compares in Libreville

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Libreville. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 165 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Libreville in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Libreville in December?
In December, Libreville averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 332 mm (13.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Libreville?
December is not one of Libreville’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Libreville in December?
332 mm (13.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (516 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Libreville in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Libreville get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Libreville in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
