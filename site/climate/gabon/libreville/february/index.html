How February compares in Libreville

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Libreville. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. March is 0.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Libreville in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Libreville in February?
In February, Libreville averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Libreville?
February is not one of Libreville’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Libreville in February?
256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (516 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Libreville in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Libreville get in February?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Libreville in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
