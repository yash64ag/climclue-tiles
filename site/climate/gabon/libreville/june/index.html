How June compares in Libreville

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Libreville. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 210 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Libreville in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Libreville in June?
In June, Libreville averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Libreville?
June is not one of Libreville’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Libreville in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (524 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Libreville in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Libreville get in June?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Libreville in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
