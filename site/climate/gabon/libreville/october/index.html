October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Libreville. Compared with September: 0.3 °C warmer, 362 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. November is 0.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Libreville in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Libreville in October?
In October, Libreville averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 516 mm (20.3 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Libreville?
October is not one of Libreville’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Libreville in October?
516 mm (20.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (516 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Libreville in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Libreville get in October?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Libreville in October?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Libreville in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
