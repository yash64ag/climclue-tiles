Beledweyne at a glance

Among the 42 towns ClimaClue covers in Somalia, Beledweyne is 8th warmest (28.6 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 3,110 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (265 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buulobarde, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Beledweyne is 0.8 °C warmer in March and 1.3 °C warmer in July, with 81 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (30.4 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, July (27.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 251 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beledweyne, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beledweyne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 251 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Beledweyne itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.736°N, 45.204°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-792-2252), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
