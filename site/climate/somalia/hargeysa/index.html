Hargeysa at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Somalia, Hargeysa is the 2nd coolest (21.5 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 3,048 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Ruqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Hargeysa is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 170 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (23.9 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 109 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hargeysa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hargeysa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 109 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
