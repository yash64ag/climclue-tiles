Marka at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Somalia, Marka is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,108 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (588 mm a year). Compared with Mogadishu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Marka is about as warm in April and about as warm in August, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.9 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Marka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Marka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
