How February compares in Borama

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Borama. Compared with January: 1.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. March is 2.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Borama in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borama in February?
In February, Borama averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Borama?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Borama’s mild months (January, February, March, May, June, July, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borama in February?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Borama in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borama get in February?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Borama in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
