How April compares in Borama

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Borama. Compared with March: 0.8 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. May is 1.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Borama in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borama in April?
In April, Borama averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Borama?
April is not one of Borama’s mild months; those are January, February, March, May, June, July, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borama in April?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Borama in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borama get in April?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Borama in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
