How January compares in Borama

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Borama. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Borama in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borama in January?
In January, Borama averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Borama?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Borama’s mild months (January, February, March, May, June, July, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borama in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Borama in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borama get in January?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Borama in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
