How March compares in Borama

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Borama. Compared with February: 2.2 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 0.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Borama in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borama in March?
In March, Borama averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Borama?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Borama’s mild months (January, February, March, May, June, July, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borama in March?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Borama in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borama get in March?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Borama in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
