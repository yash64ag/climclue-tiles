How September compares in Borama

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Borama. Compared with August: 0.2 °C warmer, 40 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. October is 2.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Borama in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borama in September?
In September, Borama averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Borama?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Borama’s mild months (January, February, March, May, June, July, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borama in September?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (102 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Borama in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borama get in September?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Borama in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
