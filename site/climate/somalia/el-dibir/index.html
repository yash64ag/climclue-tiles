El Dibir at a glance

Among the 42 towns ClimaClue covers in Somalia, El Dibir is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,958 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (181 mm a year). Compared with Qandala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (150 km away), El Dibir is 2.9 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.3 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Dibir, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Dibir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for El Dibir itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.767°N, 51.217°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-722-2312), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
