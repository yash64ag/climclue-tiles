Mogadishu weather in December

Average December weather in Mogadishu, Somalia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Mogadishu averages 31.6 °C (89 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 284 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Mogadishu

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mogadishu. Compared with November: about as warm, 41 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Mogadishu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mogadishu in December?
In December, Mogadishu averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mogadishu?
December is not one of Mogadishu’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mogadishu in December?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (96 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mogadishu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mogadishu get in December?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mogadishu in December?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mogadishu in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
