How May compares in Mogadishu

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mogadishu. Compared with April: 0.9 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Mogadishu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mogadishu in May?
In May, Mogadishu averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mogadishu?
May is not one of Mogadishu’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mogadishu in May?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (96 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mogadishu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mogadishu get in May?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mogadishu in May?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mogadishu in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
