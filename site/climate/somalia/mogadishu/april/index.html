How April compares in Mogadishu

April is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mogadishu. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 75 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. May is 0.9 °C cooler than April on average.

Mogadishu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mogadishu in April?
In April, Mogadishu averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mogadishu?
April is not one of Mogadishu’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mogadishu in April?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (96 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Mogadishu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mogadishu get in April?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mogadishu in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
