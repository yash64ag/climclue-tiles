How October compares in Mogadishu

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mogadishu. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. November is 0.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Mogadishu in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mogadishu in October?
In October, Mogadishu averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mogadishu?
October is not one of Mogadishu’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mogadishu in October?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (96 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Mogadishu in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mogadishu get in October?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mogadishu in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
