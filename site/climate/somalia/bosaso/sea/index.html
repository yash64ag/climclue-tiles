Bosaso sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bari · Somalia

The sea off Bosaso averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 25.6 °C (78 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Bosaso sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bosaso?
In June, at about 31.0 °C (88 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bosaso?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 25.6 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bosaso in January?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Bosaso?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
