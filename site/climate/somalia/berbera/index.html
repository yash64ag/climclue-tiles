Berbera at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Somalia, Berbera is the warmest (28.9 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,149 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (200 mm a year). Compared with Hargeysa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (143 km away), Berbera is 9.7 °C warmer in June and 6.4 °C warmer in January, with 153 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.5 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.0 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Berbera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Berbera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
