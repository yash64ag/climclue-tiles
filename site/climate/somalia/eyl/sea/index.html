Eyl sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nugaal · Somalia

The sea off Eyl averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to June.

Eyl sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Eyl?
In April, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Eyl?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to June. The coolest month is August, at 23.8 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Eyl in August?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Eyl?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
