Kismayo at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Somalia, Kismayo is the 3rd warmest (27.0 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,135 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (626 mm a year). Compared with Marka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (338 km away), Kismayo is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.6 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, July (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kismayo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kismayo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
