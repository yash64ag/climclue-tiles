Mohale's Hoek at a glance

Among the 11 towns ClimaClue covers in Lesotho, Mohale's Hoek is the 3rd coolest (13.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,148 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (911 mm a year). Compared with Quthing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Mohale's Hoek is 0.8 °C cooler in January and 1.3 °C cooler in July, with 297 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (19.8 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, July (5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in June and July. The area lies about 261 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mohale's Hoek, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mohale's Hoek has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 261 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1141-2074), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
