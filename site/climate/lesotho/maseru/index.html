Why Maseru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 328 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Maseru climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Maseru?
January, with an average of 20.8 °C (69 °F), day highs around 26.8 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 14.4 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Maseru?
June, with an average of 6.7 °C (44 °F), day highs around 14.3 °C (58 °F) and night lows around 0.1 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Maseru?
January, with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 662 mm (26.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Maseru?
July, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Maseru?
December, with about 295 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Maseru?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in July; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Maseru?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Maseru have?
Subtropical highland, dry winter (Köppen Cwb). Mild highland climate with wet summers and dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Maseru’s GeoNames location (29.317°S, 27.483°E; ClimaClue area sq-1133-2074), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 359,753 (GeoNames).
