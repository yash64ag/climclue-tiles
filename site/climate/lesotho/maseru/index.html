Maseru at a glance

Among the 11 towns ClimaClue covers in Lesotho, Maseru is the 3rd warmest (14.6 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 3,144 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (658 mm a year). Compared with Mabote, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Maseru is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.8 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, July (6.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in June and July. The area lies about 328 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maseru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maseru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 328 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Maseru itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.317°S, 27.483°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1133-2074), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
