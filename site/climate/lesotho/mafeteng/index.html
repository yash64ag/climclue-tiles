Mafeteng at a glance

Among the 11 towns ClimaClue covers in Lesotho, Mafeteng is the sunniest (about 3,169 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mohale's Hoek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Mafeteng is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 229 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.2 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, July (5.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in June and July. The area lies about 298 km from the sea.

Why Mafeteng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 298 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mafeteng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.823°S, 27.237°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1138-2072), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
