Monaco at a glance

Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 20 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Monaco

For mild weather, the best months to visit Monaco are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (23.4 °C), July (23.2 °C) and September (20.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (20 mm), August (28 mm) and June (41 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (309 h), August (279 h) and June (265 h).

Why Monaco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C. The 10 km area around Monaco, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 13.1 °C a year, 2.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Monaco climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Monaco?
August, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 27.2 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 19.7 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Monaco?
January, with an average of 8.8 °C (48 °F), day highs around 12.1 °C (54 °F) and night lows around 5.6 °C (42 °F).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Monaco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.737°N, 7.421°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-402-1874), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
