How January compares in Macau

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Macau. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Macau in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macau in January?
In January, Macau averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Macau?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Macau’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macau in January?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (366 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Macau in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macau get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macau in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
