How September compares in Macau

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Macau. Compared with August: 1.1 °C cooler, 107 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. October is 2.3 °C cooler than September on average.

What to pack for Macau in September

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Macau in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macau in September?
In September, Macau averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Macau?
September is not one of Macau’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macau in September?
244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (366 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Macau in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macau get in September?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macau in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
