How February compares in Macau

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Macau. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. March is 2.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Macau in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macau in February?
In February, Macau averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Macau?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Macau’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macau in February?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (377 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Macau in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macau get in February?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macau in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
