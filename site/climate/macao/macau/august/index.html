How August compares in Macau

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Macau. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 55 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C cooler than August on average.

What to pack for Macau in August

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Macau in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Macau in August?
In August, Macau averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 351 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Macau?
August is not one of Macau’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Macau in August?
351 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (366 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Macau in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Macau get in August?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Macau in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
