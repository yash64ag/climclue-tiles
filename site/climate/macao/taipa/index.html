Taipa at a glance

Compared with Macau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Taipa is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 205 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taipa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taipa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
