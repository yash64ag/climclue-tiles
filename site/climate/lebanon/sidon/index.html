Sidon at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Lebanon, Sidon is the 2nd warmest (19.2 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,970 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,086 mm a year). Compared with Nabatîyé et Tahta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Sidon is 1.2 °C warmer in August and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sidon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sidon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
