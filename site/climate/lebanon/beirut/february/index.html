How February compares in Beirut

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Beirut. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 56 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. March is 2.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Beirut in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beirut in February?
In February, Beirut averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Beirut?
February is not one of Beirut’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beirut in February?
196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (252 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Beirut in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beirut get in February?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beirut in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
