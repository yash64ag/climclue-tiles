How September compares in Beirut

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Beirut. Compared with August: 1.4 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 2.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Beirut in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beirut in September?
In September, Beirut averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Beirut?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Beirut’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beirut in September?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (252 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Beirut in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beirut get in September?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beirut in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
