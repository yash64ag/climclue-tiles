How April compares in Beirut

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Beirut. Compared with March: 3.6 °C warmer, 86 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. May is 3.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Beirut in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beirut in April?
In April, Beirut averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Beirut?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Beirut’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beirut in April?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (252 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Beirut in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beirut get in April?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beirut in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
