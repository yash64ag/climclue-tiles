How December compares in Beirut

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Beirut. Compared with November: 4.0 °C cooler, 33 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Beirut in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beirut in December?
In December, Beirut averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Beirut?
December is not one of Beirut’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beirut in December?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (252 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Beirut in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beirut get in December?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beirut in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
