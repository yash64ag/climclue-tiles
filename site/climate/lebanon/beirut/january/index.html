How January compares in Beirut

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Beirut. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, 59 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Beirut in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beirut in January?
In January, Beirut averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 236 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Beirut?
January is not one of Beirut’s mild months; those are April, May, June and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beirut in January?
236 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (236 mm), the driest August (1 mm).
Does it snow in Beirut in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beirut get in January?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Beirut in January?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (27.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Beirut in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
