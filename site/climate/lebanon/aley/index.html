Aley at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Lebanon, Aley is the coolest (15.8 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,952 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,130 mm a year). Compared with Beirut, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Aley is 2.3 °C cooler in August and 4.0 °C cooler in January, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.9 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (7.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aley, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aley has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
