How April compares in Zagreb

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with March: 4.6 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Zagreb in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in April?
In April, Zagreb averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zagreb?
April is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in April?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (106 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in April?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
