How December compares in Zagreb

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zagreb. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Zagreb in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in December?
In December, Zagreb averages a day high of 4.1 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -2.1 °C (28 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 60 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zagreb?
December is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in December?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (123 mm), the driest January (54 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in December?
About 60 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
