How January compares in Zagreb

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with December: 1.1 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Zagreb in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in January?
In January, Zagreb averages a day high of 3.6 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -3.7 °C (25 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zagreb?
January is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in January?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (123 mm), the driest January (54 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in January?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
