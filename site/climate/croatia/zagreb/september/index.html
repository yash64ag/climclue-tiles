How September compares in Zagreb

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. October is 4.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Zagreb in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in September?
In September, Zagreb averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zagreb?
September is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in September?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (123 mm), the driest January (54 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in September?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
