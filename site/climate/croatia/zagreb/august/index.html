Zagreb weather in August

Average August weather in Zagreb, Croatia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Zagreb averages 27.3 °C (81 °F) days and 16.4 °C (62 °F) nights, with 97 mm (3.8 in) of rain over the month and about 250 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Zagreb

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Zagreb in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in August?
In August, Zagreb averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zagreb?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Zagreb’s mild months (May, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in August?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (106 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in August?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
