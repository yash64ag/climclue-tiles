Zagreb at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Croatia, Zagreb is the 3rd warmest (10.6 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,920 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,080 mm a year). Compared with Rijeka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (131 km away), Zagreb is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 666 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Best time to visit Zagreb

For mild weather, the best months to visit Zagreb are May and July.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May and July.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (20.9 °C), August (20.4 °C) and June (18.6 °C).

Why Zagreb has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
