How March compares in Zagreb

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Zagreb in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in March?
In March, Zagreb averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zagreb?
March is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in March?
62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (106 mm), the driest January (47 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in March?
Yes, on average: about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in March?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
