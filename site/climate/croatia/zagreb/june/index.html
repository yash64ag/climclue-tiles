How June compares in Zagreb

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with May: 3.2 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Zagreb in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in June?
In June, Zagreb averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zagreb?
June is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in June?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (123 mm), the driest January (54 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in June?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
