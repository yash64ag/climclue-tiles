How October compares in Zagreb

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zagreb. Compared with September: 4.8 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Zagreb in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zagreb in October?
In October, Zagreb averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zagreb?
October is not one of Zagreb’s mild months; those are May and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zagreb in October?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (123 mm), the driest January (54 mm).
Does it snow in Zagreb in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zagreb get in October?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zagreb in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
