Rijeka at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Croatia, Rijeka is the 2nd warmest (12.0 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,104 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,746 mm a year). Compared with Zagreb, the nearest covered town with different numbers (131 km away), Rijeka is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 666 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Rijeka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
