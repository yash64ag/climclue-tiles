Šibenik sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Šibenik-Knin · Croatia

The sea off Šibenik averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Šibenik sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Šibenik?
In August, at about 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Šibenik?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Šibenik in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Šibenik?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Šibenik nearby?
In August: Porto Sant'Elpidio (25.5 °C, 180 km), Civitanova Marche (25.5 °C, 182 km) and San Benedetto del Tronto (25.5 °C, 185 km).
