Čakovec at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Croatia, Čakovec is the 2nd coolest (11.2 °C year average). Compared with Varaždin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Čakovec is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 197 km from the sea.

Why Čakovec has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 197 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Čakovec itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.384°N, 16.434°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-376-1964), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
