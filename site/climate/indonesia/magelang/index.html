Magelang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Magelang is 149th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 2,253 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (3,044 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salatiga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Magelang is 2.2 °C warmer in April and 1.5 °C warmer in August, with 98 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.6 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, August (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in August. The area lies about 65 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Magelang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Magelang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 65 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
