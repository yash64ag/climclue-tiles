Palembang weather in May

Average May weather in Palembang, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Palembang averages 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 22.7 °C (73 °F) nights, with 215 mm (8.4 in) of rain over the month and about 228 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Palembang

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Palembang. Compared with April: 0.2 °C warmer, 133 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. June is 0.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Palembang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palembang in May?
In May, Palembang averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 215 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Palembang?
No month in Palembang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palembang in May?
215 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (420 mm), the driest August (89 mm).
Does it snow in Palembang in May?

How many hours of sunshine does Palembang get in May?
