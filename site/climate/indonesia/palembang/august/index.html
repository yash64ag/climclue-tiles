How August compares in Palembang

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Palembang. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 46 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Palembang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palembang in August?
In August, Palembang averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Palembang?
No month in Palembang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palembang in August?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (415 mm), the driest August (87 mm).
Does it snow in Palembang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palembang get in August?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Palembang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
