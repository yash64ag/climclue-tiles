How October compares in Palembang

October is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Palembang. Compared with September: about as warm, 109 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 0.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Palembang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palembang in October?
In October, Palembang averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Palembang?
No month in Palembang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palembang in October?
239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (420 mm), the driest August (89 mm).
Does it snow in Palembang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palembang get in October?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Palembang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
