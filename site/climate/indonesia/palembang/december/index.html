How December compares in Palembang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Palembang. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Palembang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palembang in December?
In December, Palembang averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 364 mm (14.3 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Palembang?
No month in Palembang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palembang in December?
364 mm (14.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (415 mm), the driest August (87 mm).
Does it snow in Palembang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palembang get in December?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Palembang in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
