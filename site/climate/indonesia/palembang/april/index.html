How April compares in Palembang

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Palembang. Compared with March: 0.3 °C warmer, 72 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Palembang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palembang in April?
In April, Palembang averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 348 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Palembang?
No month in Palembang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palembang in April?
348 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (420 mm), the driest August (89 mm).
Does it snow in Palembang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palembang get in April?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Palembang in April?
