How August compares in Medan

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Medan. Compared with July: about as warm, 46 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Medan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medan in August?
In August, Medan averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Medan?
No month in Medan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medan in August?
243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (377 mm), the driest February (123 mm).
Does it snow in Medan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medan get in August?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
