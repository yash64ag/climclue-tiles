Medan weather in January

Average January weather in Medan, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Medan averages 30.7 °C (87 °F) days and 21.1 °C (70 °F) nights, with 179 mm (7.0 in) of rain over the month and about 165 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Medan

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Medan. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 101 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Medan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Medan in January?
In January, Medan averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Medan?
No month in Medan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Medan in January?
179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (376 mm), the driest February (121 mm).
Does it snow in Medan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Medan get in January?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Medan in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
