Tabanan at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Tabanan is 354th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 3,299 hours of sunshine a year) and 209th wettest (2,560 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sukawati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Tabanan is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 83 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.3 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.6 °C (69 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tabanan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tabanan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tabanan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.541°S, 115.125°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-925-2951), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
