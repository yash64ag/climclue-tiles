Tabanan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bali · Indonesia

The sea off Tabanan averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 25.4 °C (78 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Tabanan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tabanan?
In March, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tabanan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.4 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tabanan in August?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Tabanan?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Tabanan nearby?
In March: Dalung (29.1 °C, 11 km), Ubud (29.1 °C, 16 km) and Denpasar (29.1 °C, 16 km).
