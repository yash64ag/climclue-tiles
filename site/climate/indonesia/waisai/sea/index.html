Waisai sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Southwest Papua · Indonesia

The sea off Waisai averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 28.3 °C (83 °F) in July. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Waisai sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Waisai?
In April, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Waisai?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is July, at 28.3 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Waisai in July?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Waisai?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Waisai nearby?
In April: Masohi (29.7 °C, 381 km), Amahai (29.7 °C, 387 km) and Bintuni (30.1 °C, 395 km).
