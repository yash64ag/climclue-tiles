How October compares in Semarang

October is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Semarang. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 77 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. November is 0.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Semarang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Semarang in October?
In October, Semarang averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Semarang?
No month in Semarang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Semarang in October?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (392 mm), the driest August (50 mm).
Does it snow in Semarang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Semarang get in October?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Semarang in October?
About 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is November (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Semarang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
