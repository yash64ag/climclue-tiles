How July compares in Semarang

July is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Semarang. Compared with June: about as warm, 39 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Semarang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Semarang in July?
In July, Semarang averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Semarang?
No month in Semarang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Semarang in July?
60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (392 mm), the driest August (47 mm).
Does it snow in Semarang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Semarang get in July?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Semarang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
