How December compares in Semarang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Semarang. Compared with November: 0.8 °C cooler, 73 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Semarang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Semarang in December?
In December, Semarang averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 320 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Semarang?
No month in Semarang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Semarang in December?
320 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (392 mm), the driest August (47 mm).
Does it snow in Semarang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Semarang get in December?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Semarang in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
