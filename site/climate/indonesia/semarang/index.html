Semarang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Semarang is 6th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,835 hours of sunshine a year) and 115th wettest (2,415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ungaran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Semarang is 2.2 °C warmer in September and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 356 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (28.4 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Semarang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Semarang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
