Semarang weather in May

Average May weather in Semarang, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Semarang averages 32.1 °C (90 °F) days and 22.8 °C (73 °F) nights, with 165 mm (6.5 in) of rain over the month and about 256 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Semarang

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Semarang. Compared with April: 0.3 °C warmer, 72 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 0.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Semarang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Semarang in May?
In May, Semarang averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Semarang?
No month in Semarang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Semarang in May?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (392 mm), the driest August (50 mm).
Does it snow in Semarang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Semarang get in May?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Semarang in May?
About 29.9 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is November (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Semarang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
