How September compares in Semarang

September is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Semarang. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Semarang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Semarang in September?
In September, Semarang averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Semarang?
No month in Semarang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Semarang in September?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (392 mm), the driest August (47 mm).
Does it snow in Semarang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Semarang get in September?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Semarang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
