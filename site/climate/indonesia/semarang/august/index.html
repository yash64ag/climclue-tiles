How August compares in Semarang

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Semarang. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Semarang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Semarang in August?
In August, Semarang averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 304 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Semarang?
No month in Semarang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Semarang in August?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (392 mm), the driest August (47 mm).
Does it snow in Semarang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Semarang get in August?
About 304 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Semarang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
