Kendari at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Kendari is 108th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 81st sunniest (about 2,561 hours of sunshine a year) and 156th wettest (1,901 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baubau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (165 km away), Kendari is 0.2 °C warmer in November and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 144 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.9 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kendari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kendari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
