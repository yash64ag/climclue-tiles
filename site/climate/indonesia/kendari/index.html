Kendari at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Kendari is 74th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 81st sunniest (about 2,561 hours of sunshine a year) and 162nd wettest (1,764 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baubau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (165 km away), Kendari is 0.1 °C cooler in November and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 143 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.6 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, July (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kendari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kendari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kendari itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.978°S, 122.515°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-879-3025), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
