Serang weather in June

Average June weather in Serang, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Serang averages 32.2 °C (90 °F) days and 22.0 °C (72 °F) nights, with 112 mm (4.4 in) of rain over the month and about 225 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Serang

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Serang. Compared with May: 0.3 °C cooler, 75 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Serang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Serang in June?
In June, Serang averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Serang?
No month in Serang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Serang in June?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (333 mm), the driest August (93 mm).
Does it snow in Serang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Serang get in June?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Serang in June?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Serang in June?
