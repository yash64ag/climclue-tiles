Depok, West Java in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Depok, West Java in December?
In December, Depok, West Java averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 313 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Depok, West Java?
No month in Depok, West Java is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
