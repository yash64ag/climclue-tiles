Depok, West Java in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Depok, West Java in October?
In October, Depok, West Java averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 267 mm (10.5 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Depok, West Java?
No month in Depok, West Java is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Depok, West Java in October?
267 mm (10.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (408 mm), the driest August (157 mm).
Does it snow in Depok, West Java in October?
