Depok, West Java at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Depok, West Java is 80th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,669 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (3,160 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sawangan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Depok, West Java is 0.3 °C warmer in September and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 217 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.9 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Depok, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Depok, West Java has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
