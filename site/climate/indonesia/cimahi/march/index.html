Cimahi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cimahi in March?
In March, Cimahi averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 382 mm (15.0 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cimahi?
March is not one of Cimahi’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cimahi in March?
382 mm (15.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (382 mm), the driest August (88 mm).
Does it snow in Cimahi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cimahi get in March?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
