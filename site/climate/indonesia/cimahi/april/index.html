Cimahi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cimahi in April?
In April, Cimahi averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 297 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cimahi?
April is not one of Cimahi’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cimahi in April?
297 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (382 mm), the driest August (88 mm).
Does it snow in Cimahi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cimahi get in April?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cimahi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
