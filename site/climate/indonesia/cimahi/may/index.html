How May compares in Cimahi

May is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cimahi. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 86 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Cimahi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cimahi in May?
In May, Cimahi averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 197 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cimahi?
May is not one of Cimahi’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cimahi in May?
197 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (362 mm), the driest August (84 mm).
Does it snow in Cimahi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cimahi get in May?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cimahi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
