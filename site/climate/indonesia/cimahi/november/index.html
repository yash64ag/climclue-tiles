Cimahi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cimahi in November?
In November, Cimahi averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cimahi?
November is not one of Cimahi’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cimahi in November?
322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (362 mm), the driest August (84 mm).
Does it snow in Cimahi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cimahi get in November?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cimahi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
