Cimahi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cimahi in December?
In December, Cimahi averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 340 mm (13.4 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cimahi?
December is not one of Cimahi’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cimahi in December?
340 mm (13.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (382 mm), the driest August (88 mm).
Does it snow in Cimahi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cimahi get in December?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cimahi in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
