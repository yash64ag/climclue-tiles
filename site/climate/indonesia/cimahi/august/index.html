How August compares in Cimahi

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Cimahi. Compared with July: about as warm, 7 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Cimahi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cimahi in August?
In August, Cimahi averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cimahi?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Cimahi’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cimahi in August?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (382 mm), the driest August (88 mm).
Does it snow in Cimahi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cimahi get in August?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cimahi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
