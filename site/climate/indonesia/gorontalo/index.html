Gorontalo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Gorontalo is 100th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 137th sunniest (about 2,237 hours of sunshine a year) and 157th wettest (1,898 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kotamobagu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (141 km away), Gorontalo is 3.5 °C warmer in October and 3.5 °C warmer in July, with 1,371 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.6 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, July (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gorontalo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gorontalo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
