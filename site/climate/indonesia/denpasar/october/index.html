How October compares in Denpasar

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Denpasar. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 50 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. November is 0.6 °C warmer than October on average.

Denpasar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denpasar in October?
In October, Denpasar averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 136 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 313 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Denpasar?
October is not one of Denpasar’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denpasar in October?
136 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (423 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Denpasar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denpasar get in October?
About 313 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denpasar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
