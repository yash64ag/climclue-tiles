Denpasar weather in April

Average April weather in Denpasar, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Denpasar averages 30.6 °C (87 °F) days and 23.3 °C (74 °F) nights, with 149 mm (5.9 in) of rain over the month and about 286 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Denpasar

April is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Denpasar. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 93 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Denpasar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denpasar in April?
In April, Denpasar averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Denpasar?
April is not one of Denpasar’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denpasar in April?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (423 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Denpasar in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denpasar get in April?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Denpasar in April?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Denpasar in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
