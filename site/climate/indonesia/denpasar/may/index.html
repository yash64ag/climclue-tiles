How May compares in Denpasar

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Denpasar. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 42 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. June is 0.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Denpasar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denpasar in May?
In May, Denpasar averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 293 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Denpasar?
May is not one of Denpasar’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denpasar in May?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (415 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Denpasar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denpasar get in May?
About 293 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denpasar in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
