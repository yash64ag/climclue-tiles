How March compares in Denpasar

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Denpasar. Compared with February: 0.1 °C warmer, 89 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 0.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Denpasar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denpasar in March?
In March, Denpasar averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Denpasar?
March is not one of Denpasar’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denpasar in March?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (415 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Denpasar in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denpasar get in March?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denpasar in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
