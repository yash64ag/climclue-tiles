Why Denpasar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Denpasar climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Denpasar?
April, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Denpasar?
August, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 23.8 °C (75 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Denpasar?
January, with 415 mm (16.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,258 mm (88.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Denpasar?
August, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Denpasar?
October, with about 313 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Denpasar?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Denpasar?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Denpasar’s GeoNames location (8.650°S, 115.217°E; ClimaClue area sq-926-2952), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 670,210 (GeoNames).
