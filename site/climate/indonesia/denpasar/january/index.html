How January compares in Denpasar

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Denpasar. Compared with December: about as warm, 89 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Denpasar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denpasar in January?
In January, Denpasar averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 415 mm (16.3 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Denpasar?
January is not one of Denpasar’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denpasar in January?
415 mm (16.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (415 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Denpasar in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denpasar get in January?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denpasar in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
