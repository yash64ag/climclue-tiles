How December compares in Denpasar

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Denpasar. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 106 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Denpasar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Denpasar in December?
In December, Denpasar averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 326 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Denpasar?
December is not one of Denpasar’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Denpasar in December?
326 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (415 mm), the driest August (54 mm).
Does it snow in Denpasar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Denpasar get in December?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Denpasar in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
