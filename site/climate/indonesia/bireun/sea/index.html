Bireun sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Aceh · Indonesia

The sea off Bireun averages 30.2 °C (86 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 28.4 °C (83 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Bireun sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bireun?
In May, at about 30.2 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bireun?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 28.4 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bireun in January?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Bireun?
About 29.4 °C (85 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
