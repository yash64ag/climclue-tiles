How February compares in Bekasi

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bekasi. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 41 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. March is 0.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Bekasi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bekasi in February?
In February, Bekasi averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 365 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bekasi?
No month in Bekasi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bekasi in February?
365 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (406 mm), the driest August (98 mm).
Does it snow in Bekasi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bekasi get in February?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bekasi in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
