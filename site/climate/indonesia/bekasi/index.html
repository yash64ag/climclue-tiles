Bekasi at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Bekasi is 48th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,651 hours of sunshine a year) and 92nd wettest (2,659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jakarta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Bekasi is about as warm in October and about as warm in January, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bekasi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bekasi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
