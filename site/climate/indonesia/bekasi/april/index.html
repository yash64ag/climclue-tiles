How April compares in Bekasi

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bekasi. Compared with March: 0.4 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 0.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Bekasi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bekasi in April?
In April, Bekasi averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 248 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bekasi?
No month in Bekasi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bekasi in April?
248 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (406 mm), the driest August (98 mm).
Does it snow in Bekasi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bekasi get in April?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bekasi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
