How March compares in Bekasi

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bekasi. Compared with February: 0.3 °C warmer, 86 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Bekasi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bekasi in March?
In March, Bekasi averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 278 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bekasi?
No month in Bekasi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bekasi in March?
278 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (406 mm), the driest August (98 mm).
Does it snow in Bekasi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bekasi get in March?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bekasi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
