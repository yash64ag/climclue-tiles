Kediri at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Kediri is 125th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 3,113 hours of sunshine a year) and 110th wettest (2,448 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pare, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Kediri is about as warm in November and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 186 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.6 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in August. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kediri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kediri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
