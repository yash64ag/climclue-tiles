Pelabuhanratu at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Pelabuhanratu is 113th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 84th sunniest (about 2,547 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (3,607 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sukabumi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Pelabuhanratu is 3.2 °C warmer in May and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 146 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.1 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pelabuhanratu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pelabuhanratu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C. The 10 km area around Pelabuhanratu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.8 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
