Tanjungbalai at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Tanjungbalai is 26th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,477 hours of sunshine a year) and 60th wettest (2,876 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kisaran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Tanjungbalai is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 82 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, December (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tanjungbalai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tanjungbalai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
