Banjarmasin weather in January

Average January weather in Banjarmasin, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Banjarmasin averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) days and 23.0 °C (73 °F) nights, with 414 mm (16.3 in) of rain over the month and about 138 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Banjarmasin

January is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Banjarmasin. Compared with December: about as warm, 23 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.
