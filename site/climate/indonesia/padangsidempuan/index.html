Padangsidempuan at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Padangsidempuan is 148th warmest (24.0 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,450 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (3,716 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rantauprapat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Padangsidempuan is 2.6 °C cooler in May and 2.7 °C cooler in September, with 588 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.6 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, September (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Padangsidempuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Padangsidempuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
