Purwokerto at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Purwokerto is 127th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,668 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (3,526 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cilacap, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Purwokerto is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 180 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.4 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in August. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Purwokerto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Purwokerto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
