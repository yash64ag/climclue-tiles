Surakarta at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Surakarta is 79th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 69th sunniest (about 2,631 hours of sunshine a year) and 141st wettest (2,143 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Grogol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Surakarta is 0.2 °C warmer in October and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.1 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Surakarta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Surakarta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
