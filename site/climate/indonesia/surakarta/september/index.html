How September compares in Surakarta

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Surakarta. Compared with August: 0.6 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. October is 0.4 °C warmer than September on average.

Surakarta in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surakarta in September?
In September, Surakarta averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Surakarta?
No month in Surakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surakarta in September?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (346 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Surakarta in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surakarta get in September?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surakarta in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
