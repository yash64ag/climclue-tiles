How May compares in Surakarta

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Surakarta. Compared with April: 0.2 °C warmer, 83 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Surakarta in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surakarta in May?
In May, Surakarta averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Surakarta?
No month in Surakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surakarta in May?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (351 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Surakarta in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surakarta get in May?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surakarta in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
