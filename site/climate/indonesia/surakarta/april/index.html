How April compares in Surakarta

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Surakarta. Compared with March: 0.3 °C warmer, 53 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Surakarta in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surakarta in April?
In April, Surakarta averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Surakarta?
No month in Surakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surakarta in April?
220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (346 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Surakarta in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surakarta get in April?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surakarta in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
