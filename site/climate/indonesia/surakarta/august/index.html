How August compares in Surakarta

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Surakarta. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.6 °C warmer than August on average.

Surakarta in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surakarta in August?
In August, Surakarta averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Surakarta?
No month in Surakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surakarta in August?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (346 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Surakarta in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surakarta get in August?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surakarta in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
