How January compares in Surakarta

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Surakarta. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 92 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Of the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Surakarta ranks 88th warmest (average temperature), 76th sunniest and 84th wettest in January.

Surakarta in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surakarta in January?
In January, Surakarta averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 351 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Surakarta?
No month in Surakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surakarta in January?
351 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (351 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Surakarta in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surakarta get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surakarta in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
