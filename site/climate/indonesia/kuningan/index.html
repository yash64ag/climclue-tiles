Kuningan at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Kuningan is 163rd warmest (21.8 °C year average), 111th sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (3,202 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Astanajapura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Kuningan is 5.1 °C cooler in April and 5.4 °C cooler in August, with 1,118 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (22.3 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, August (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in August. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kuningan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kuningan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kuningan are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.

Why Kuningan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 12.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
