Kuningan at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Kuningan is 153rd warmest (23.9 °C year average), 111th sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (3,101 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Astanajapura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Kuningan is 3.1 °C cooler in May and 3.4 °C cooler in August, with 1,127 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.4 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, August (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kuningan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kuningan are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.

Why Kuningan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C. The 10 km area around Kuningan, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.8 °C a year, 2.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kuningan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.976°S, 108.483°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-909-2884), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
