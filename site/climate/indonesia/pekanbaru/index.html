Why Pekanbaru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Pekanbaru climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Pekanbaru?
May, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 30.6 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 23.7 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Pekanbaru?
January, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.0 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 22.4 °C (72 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Pekanbaru?
December, with 304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,794 mm (110.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Pekanbaru?
June, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Pekanbaru?
May, with about 215 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Pekanbaru?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Pekanbaru?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Pekanbaru have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Pekanbaru’s GeoNames location (0.517°N, 101.442°E; ClimaClue area sq-834-2814), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,167,599 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
