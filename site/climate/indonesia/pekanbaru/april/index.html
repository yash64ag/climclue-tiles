Pekanbaru in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pekanbaru in April?
In April, Pekanbaru averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 293 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pekanbaru?
No month in Pekanbaru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pekanbaru in April?
293 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (302 mm), the driest June (136 mm).
Does it snow in Pekanbaru in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pekanbaru get in April?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pekanbaru in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
