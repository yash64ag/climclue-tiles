How December compares in Pekanbaru

December is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Pekanbaru. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Pekanbaru in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pekanbaru in December?
In December, Pekanbaru averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pekanbaru?
No month in Pekanbaru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pekanbaru in December?
304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (304 mm), the driest June (135 mm).
Does it snow in Pekanbaru in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pekanbaru get in December?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pekanbaru in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
