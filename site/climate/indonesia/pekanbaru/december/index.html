Pekanbaru weather in December

Average December weather in Pekanbaru, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Pekanbaru averages 30.3 °C (87 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 302 mm (11.9 in) of rain over the month and about 161 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Pekanbaru

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Pekanbaru. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Pekanbaru in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pekanbaru in December?
In December, Pekanbaru averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 302 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pekanbaru?
No month in Pekanbaru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pekanbaru in December?
302 mm (11.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (302 mm), the driest June (136 mm).
Does it snow in Pekanbaru in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pekanbaru get in December?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pekanbaru in December?
