How January compares in Pekanbaru

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Pekanbaru. Compared with December: about as warm, 53 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Pekanbaru in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pekanbaru in January?
In January, Pekanbaru averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Pekanbaru?
No month in Pekanbaru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pekanbaru in January?
251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (304 mm), the driest June (135 mm).
Does it snow in Pekanbaru in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pekanbaru get in January?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pekanbaru in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
