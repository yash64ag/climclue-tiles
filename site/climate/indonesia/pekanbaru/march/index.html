Pekanbaru in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pekanbaru in March?
In March, Pekanbaru averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Pekanbaru?
No month in Pekanbaru is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pekanbaru in March?
268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (304 mm), the driest June (135 mm).
Does it snow in Pekanbaru in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pekanbaru get in March?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pekanbaru in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
