How June compares in Tangerang

June is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tangerang. Compared with May: 0.2 °C cooler, 78 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Tangerang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangerang in June?
In June, Tangerang averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tangerang?
No month in Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangerang in June?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (399 mm), the driest August (79 mm).
Does it snow in Tangerang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangerang get in June?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangerang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
