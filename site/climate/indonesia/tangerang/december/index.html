How December compares in Tangerang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tangerang. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 58 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Tangerang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangerang in December?
In December, Tangerang averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 255 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tangerang?
No month in Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangerang in December?
255 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (400 mm), the driest August (86 mm).
Does it snow in Tangerang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangerang get in December?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangerang in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
