How September compares in Tangerang

September is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tangerang. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

Tangerang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangerang in September?
In September, Tangerang averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tangerang?
No month in Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangerang in September?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (399 mm), the driest August (79 mm).
Does it snow in Tangerang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangerang get in September?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangerang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
