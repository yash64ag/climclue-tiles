How November compares in Tangerang

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tangerang. Compared with October: 0.4 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 0.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Tangerang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangerang in November?
In November, Tangerang averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tangerang?
No month in Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangerang in November?
185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (399 mm), the driest August (79 mm).
Does it snow in Tangerang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangerang get in November?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangerang in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
