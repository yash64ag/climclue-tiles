Tangerang weather in July

Average July weather in Tangerang, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Tangerang averages 32.3 °C (90 °F) days and 21.5 °C (71 °F) nights, with 99 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 270 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Tangerang

July is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tangerang. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Tangerang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangerang in July?
In July, Tangerang averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tangerang?
No month in Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangerang in July?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (400 mm), the driest August (86 mm).
Does it snow in Tangerang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangerang get in July?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tangerang in July?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tangerang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
