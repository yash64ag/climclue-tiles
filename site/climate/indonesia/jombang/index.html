Jombang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Jombang is 67th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,180 hours of sunshine a year) and 132nd wettest (2,274 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mojokerto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Jombang is 0.7 °C cooler in October and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 176 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.6 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, July (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jombang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jombang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
