Jombang at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Jombang is 206th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 3,180 hours of sunshine a year) and 309th wettest (2,223 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Diwek, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Jombang is 0.2 °C warmer in October and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 86 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.5 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, July (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jombang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jombang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jombang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.546°S, 112.233°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-915-2922), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
