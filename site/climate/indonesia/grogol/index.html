Grogol at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Grogol is 99th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,619 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (2,100 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surakarta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Grogol is 0.2 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.9 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Grogol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Grogol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
