Sidoarjo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Sidoarjo is the 2nd warmest (27.7 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 3,317 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Surabaya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Sidoarjo is 0.3 °C warmer in October and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (29.3 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, February (26.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sidoarjo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sidoarjo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
