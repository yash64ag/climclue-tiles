Sidoarjo at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Sidoarjo is the 4th warmest (27.7 °C year average). Compared with Buduran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Sidoarjo is about as warm in October and about as warm in February, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (29.3 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, February (26.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sidoarjo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sidoarjo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.448°S, 112.718°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-914-2927), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
