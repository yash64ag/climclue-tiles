Sukawati at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Sukawati is 144th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 3,026 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (2,873 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Denpasar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Sukawati is 1.7 °C cooler in March and 1.6 °C cooler in August, with 615 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.1 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, August (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sukawati, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sukawati has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
