Cirebon at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Cirebon is 32nd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,867 hours of sunshine a year) and 151st wettest (1,968 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Plumbon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Cirebon is 0.3 °C warmer in October and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 197 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.0 °C (75 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cirebon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cirebon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
