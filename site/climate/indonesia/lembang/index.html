Lembang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Lembang is 164th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 103rd sunniest (about 2,432 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (2,847 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cimahi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Lembang is 0.9 °C cooler in May and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.6 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, January (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lembang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lembang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
