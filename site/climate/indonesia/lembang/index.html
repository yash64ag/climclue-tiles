Why Lembang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Lembang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lembang?
May, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 24.5 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lembang?
January, with an average of 20.6 °C (69 °F), day highs around 23.5 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lembang?
March, with 403 mm (15.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,847 mm (112.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lembang?
August, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lembang?
August, with about 249 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lembang?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Lembang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Lembang have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Lembang’s GeoNames location (6.812°S, 107.618°E; ClimaClue area sq-908-2876), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 183,130 (GeoNames).
