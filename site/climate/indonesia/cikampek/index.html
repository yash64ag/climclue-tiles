Cikampek at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Cikampek is 51st warmest (26.8 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,726 hours of sunshine a year) and 123rd wettest (2,308 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Purwakarta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Cikampek is 0.2 °C warmer in October and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.5 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cikampek, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cikampek has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers
