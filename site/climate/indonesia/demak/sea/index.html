Demak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Java · Indonesia

The sea off Demak averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 28.2 °C (83 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Demak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Demak?
In November, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Demak?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 28.2 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Demak in August?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Demak?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
