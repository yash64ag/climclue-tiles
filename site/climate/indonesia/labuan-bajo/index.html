Labuan Bajo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Labuan Bajo is the 4th driest (1,640 mm a year). Compared with Bima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Labuan Bajo is 0.3 °C cooler in November and 0.5 °C warmer in July, with 94 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.1 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, July (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Labuan Bajo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Labuan Bajo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
