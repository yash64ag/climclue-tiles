Labuan Bajo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · East Nusa Tenggara · Indonesia

The sea off Labuan Bajo averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) in December, its warmest month, and 26.7 °C (80 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Labuan Bajo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Labuan Bajo?
In December, at about 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Labuan Bajo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.7 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Labuan Bajo in August?
About 26.7 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Labuan Bajo?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Labuan Bajo nearby?
In December: Galesong (30.0 °C, 358 km).
