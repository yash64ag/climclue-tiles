How June compares in Batam

June is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Batam. Compared with May: 0.1 °C warmer, 46 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Batam in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Batam in June?
In June, Batam averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 26.7 °C (80 °F), with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Batam?
No month in Batam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Batam in June?
173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (357 mm), the driest February (114 mm).
Does it snow in Batam in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Batam get in June?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Batam in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
