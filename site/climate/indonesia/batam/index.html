Batam at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Batam is 29th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 163rd sunniest (about 2,037 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (2,597 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tanjung Pinang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Batam is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 531 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.8 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 25.4 °C (78 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Batam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Batam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
