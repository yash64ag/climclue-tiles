How March compares in Batam

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Batam. Compared with February: 0.5 °C warmer, 85 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 0.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Batam in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Batam in March?
In March, Batam averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Batam?
No month in Batam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Batam in March?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (350 mm), the driest February (113 mm).
Does it snow in Batam in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Batam get in March?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Batam in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
