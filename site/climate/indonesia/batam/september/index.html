How September compares in Batam

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Batam. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Batam in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Batam in September?
In September, Batam averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Batam?
No month in Batam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Batam in September?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (357 mm), the driest February (114 mm).
Does it snow in Batam in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Batam get in September?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Batam in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
