How October compares in Batam

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Batam. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. November is 0.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Batam in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Batam in October?
In October, Batam averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Batam?
No month in Batam is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Batam in October?
208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (357 mm), the driest February (114 mm).
Does it snow in Batam in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Batam get in October?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Batam in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
