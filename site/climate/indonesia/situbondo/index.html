Situbondo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Situbondo is 25th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,194 hours of sunshine a year) and 140th wettest (2,148 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jember, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Situbondo is 3.0 °C warmer in October and 0.8 °C warmer in February, with 595 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, February (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Situbondo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Situbondo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
