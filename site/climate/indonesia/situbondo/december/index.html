How December compares in Situbondo

December is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Situbondo. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 157 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Situbondo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Situbondo in December?
In December, Situbondo averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 346 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Situbondo?
No month in Situbondo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Situbondo in December?
346 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (346 mm), the driest August (19 mm).
Does it snow in Situbondo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Situbondo get in December?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Situbondo in December?
About 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (30.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Situbondo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
