Situbondo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Situbondo in September?
In September, Situbondo averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 315 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Situbondo?
No month in Situbondo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Situbondo in September?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (359 mm), the driest August (23 mm).
Does it snow in Situbondo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Situbondo get in September?
About 315 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Situbondo in September?
