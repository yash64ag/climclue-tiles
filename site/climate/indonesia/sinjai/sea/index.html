Sinjai sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Sulawesi · Indonesia

The sea off Sinjai averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 27.9 °C (82 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Sinjai sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sinjai?
In April, at about 29.9 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sinjai?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 27.9 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sinjai in August?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Sinjai?
About 29.2 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
