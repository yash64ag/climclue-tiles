Prabumulih at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Prabumulih is 47th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 119th sunniest (about 2,359 hours of sunshine a year) and 64th wettest (2,866 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baturaja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Prabumulih is 0.3 °C warmer in September and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (27.6 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 154 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Prabumulih, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Prabumulih has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 154 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
