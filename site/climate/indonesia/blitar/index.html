Blitar at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Blitar is 137th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 3,108 hours of sunshine a year) and 108th wettest (2,450 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kediri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Blitar is 0.7 °C cooler in April and 1.0 °C cooler in August, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.6 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, August (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.2 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Blitar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Blitar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
