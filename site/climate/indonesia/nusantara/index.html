Nusantara at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Nusantara is 89th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 130th sunniest (about 2,260 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (2,803 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Balikpapan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Nusantara is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.8 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, July (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.2 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nusantara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nusantara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
