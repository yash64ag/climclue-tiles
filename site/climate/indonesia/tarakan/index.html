Tarakan at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Tarakan is 72nd warmest (26.6 °C year average), 161st sunniest (about 2,066 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (3,995 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bontang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (354 km away), Tarakan is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1,955 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.9 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 25.0 °C (77 °F), in February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tarakan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tarakan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
