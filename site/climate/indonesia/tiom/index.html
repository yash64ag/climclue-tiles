Tiom at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Tiom is the 3rd coolest (15.9 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 1,403 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mulia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Tiom is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 2,774 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (16.2 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, July (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in September. The area lies about 176 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tiom, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tiom has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 176 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C. The 10 km area around Tiom, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.6 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tiom itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.924°S, 138.455°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-879-3184), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
