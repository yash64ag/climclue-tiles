Jatibarang at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Jatibarang is 13th warmest (27.6 °C year average), 111th sunniest (about 2,881 hours of sunshine a year) and 416th wettest (1,799 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karangampel, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Jatibarang is 0.8 °C warmer in October and about as warm in January, with 107 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.7 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, January (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jatibarang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jatibarang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jatibarang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.475°S, 108.315°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-904-2883), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
