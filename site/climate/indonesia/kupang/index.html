Kupang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Kupang is the 5th driest (1,676 mm a year). Compared with Labuan Bajo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (449 km away), Kupang is 1.2 °C warmer in November and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (28.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, July (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kupang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kupang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
