Palangkaraya at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Palangkaraya is 73rd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,282 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (3,112 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sampit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Palangkaraya is 0.2 °C warmer in September and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 270 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (27.1 °C), averages 0.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.6 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 120 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Palangkaraya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Palangkaraya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 120 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
