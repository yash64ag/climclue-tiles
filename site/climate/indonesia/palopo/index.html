Palopo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Palopo is the 3rd wettest (4,994 mm a year). Compared with Parepare, the nearest covered town with different numbers (130 km away), Palopo is 0.8 °C cooler in November and 1.8 °C cooler in July, with 2,217 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (25.8 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Palopo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Palopo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
