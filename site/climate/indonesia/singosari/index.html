Singosari at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Singosari is 390th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 96th sunniest (about 2,899 hours of sunshine a year) and 122nd wettest (2,907 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lawang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Singosari is 0.1 °C warmer in November and about as warm in July, with 142 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (25.2 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, July (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in August. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Singosari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Singosari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C. The 10 km area around Singosari, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.9 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Singosari itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.892°S, 112.666°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-918-2926), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
