Singosari at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Singosari is 159th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,899 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (3,178 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Malang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Singosari is 1.4 °C cooler in October and 1.4 °C cooler in July, with 275 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.6 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Singosari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Singosari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
