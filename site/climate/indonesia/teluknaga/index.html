Teluknaga at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Teluknaga is 9th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,613 hours of sunshine a year) and 137th wettest (2,183 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sepatan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Teluknaga is 0.1 °C warmer in October and about as warm in January, with 253 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.8 °C), averages 1.2 °C more than its coolest, January (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.0 °C (75 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Teluknaga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Teluknaga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
