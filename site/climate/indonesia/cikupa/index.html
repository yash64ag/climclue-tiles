Cikupa at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Cikupa is 42nd warmest (26.8 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 2,643 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (2,840 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pasarkemis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Cikupa is 0.3 °C cooler in October and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 404 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cikupa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cikupa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
