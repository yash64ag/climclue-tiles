Sibolga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Sumatra · Indonesia

The sea off Sibolga averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 29.2 °C (85 °F) in October. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Sibolga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sibolga?
In March, at about 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sibolga?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is October, at 29.2 °C (85 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sibolga in October?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Sibolga?
About 29.7 °C (85 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
