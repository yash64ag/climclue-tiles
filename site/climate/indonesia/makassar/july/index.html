How July compares in Makassar

July is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Makassar. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Makassar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makassar in July?
In July, Makassar averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Makassar?
No month in Makassar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makassar in July?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (741 mm), the driest August (24 mm).
Does it snow in Makassar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makassar get in July?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makassar in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
