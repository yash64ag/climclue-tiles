How December compares in Makassar

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Makassar. Compared with November: 0.6 °C cooler, 318 mm more precipitation and 94 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Makassar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makassar in December?
In December, Makassar averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 608 mm (23.9 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Makassar?
No month in Makassar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makassar in December?
608 mm (23.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (736 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Makassar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makassar get in December?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makassar in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
