Makassar weather in October

Average October weather in Makassar, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Makassar averages 32.0 °C (90 °F) days and 22.9 °C (73 °F) nights, with 99 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 295 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Makassar

October is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Makassar. Compared with September: 0.2 °C warmer, 51 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is 0.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Makassar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makassar in October?
In October, Makassar averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Makassar?
No month in Makassar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makassar in October?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (741 mm), the driest August (24 mm).
Does it snow in Makassar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makassar get in October?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Makassar in October?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Makassar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
