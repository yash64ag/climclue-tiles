How September compares in Makassar

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Makassar. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. October is 0.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Makassar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makassar in September?
In September, Makassar averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 299 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Makassar?
No month in Makassar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makassar in September?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (741 mm), the driest August (24 mm).
Does it snow in Makassar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makassar get in September?
About 299 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Makassar in September?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Makassar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
