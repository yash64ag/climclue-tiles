Bima at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Bima is the 2nd driest (1,545 mm a year). Compared with Labuan Bajo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Bima is 0.6 °C warmer in October and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, July (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bima, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
