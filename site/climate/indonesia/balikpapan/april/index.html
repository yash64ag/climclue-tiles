Balikpapan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balikpapan in April?
In April, Balikpapan averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Balikpapan?
No month in Balikpapan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

Does it snow in Balikpapan in April?
