Balikpapan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balikpapan in August?
In August, Balikpapan averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Balikpapan?
No month in Balikpapan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balikpapan in August?
189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (273 mm), the driest October (182 mm).
Does it snow in Balikpapan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balikpapan get in August?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
