Arjawinangun at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Arjawinangun is 11th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,856 hours of sunshine a year) and 160th wettest (1,884 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Plumbon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Arjawinangun is 0.7 °C warmer in October and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 280 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.9 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.3 °C (76 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Arjawinangun, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Arjawinangun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
