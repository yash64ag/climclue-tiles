Candi Prambanan at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Candi Prambanan is 358th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 291st sunniest (about 2,423 hours of sunshine a year) and 274th wettest (2,354 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jogonalan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Candi Prambanan is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 174 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.1 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, August (24.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.8 °C (66 °F), in August. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Why Candi Prambanan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Candi Prambanan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.750°S, 110.494°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-917-2904), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
