How February compares in Surabaya

February is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Surabaya. Compared with January: about as warm, 31 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 0.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Surabaya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surabaya in February?
In February, Surabaya averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Surabaya?
No month in Surabaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surabaya in February?
301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (331 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Surabaya in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surabaya get in February?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surabaya in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
