How March compares in Surabaya

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Surabaya. Compared with February: 0.4 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 0.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Surabaya in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surabaya in March?
In March, Surabaya averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 293 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Surabaya?
No month in Surabaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surabaya in March?
293 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (331 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Surabaya in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surabaya get in March?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surabaya in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
