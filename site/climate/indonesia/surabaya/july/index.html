How July compares in Surabaya

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Surabaya. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Surabaya in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surabaya in July?
In July, Surabaya averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 323 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Surabaya?
No month in Surabaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surabaya in July?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (331 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Surabaya in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surabaya get in July?
About 323 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surabaya in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
