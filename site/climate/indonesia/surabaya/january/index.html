How January compares in Surabaya

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Surabaya. Compared with December: 0.5 °C cooler, 61 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Surabaya in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surabaya in January?
In January, Surabaya averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 331 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Surabaya?
No month in Surabaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surabaya in January?
331 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (331 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Surabaya in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surabaya get in January?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surabaya in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
