How April compares in Surabaya

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Surabaya. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 112 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Surabaya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surabaya in April?
In April, Surabaya averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Surabaya?
No month in Surabaya is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surabaya in April?
182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (331 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Surabaya in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surabaya get in April?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surabaya in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
