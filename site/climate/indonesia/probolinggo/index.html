Probolinggo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Probolinggo is the sunniest (about 3,344 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pasuruan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Probolinggo is 0.9 °C cooler in October and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.8 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, July (26.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.1 °C (75 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Probolinggo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
