Probolinggo at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Probolinggo is the sunniest (about 3,344 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pasuruan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Probolinggo is 1.0 °C cooler in October and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, July (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.9 °C (75 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Probolinggo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
