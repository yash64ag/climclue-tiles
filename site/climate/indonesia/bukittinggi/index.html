Bukittinggi at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Bukittinggi is the 2nd coolest (18.9 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (5,067 mm a year). Compared with Payakumbuh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Bukittinggi is 4.2 °C cooler in May and 4.0 °C cooler in January, with 1,871 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.6 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bukittinggi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bukittinggi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 11.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
