Benoa at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Benoa is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,351 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Tuban, Bali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Benoa is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.3 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, August (25.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Benoa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Benoa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.755°S, 115.218°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-927-2952), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
