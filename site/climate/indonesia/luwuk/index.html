Luwuk at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Luwuk is 219th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 364th sunniest (about 2,203 hours of sunshine a year) and 439th wettest (1,442 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gorontalo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (168 km away), Luwuk is 0.4 °C warmer in November and 1.1 °C cooler in August, with 503 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.2 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, August (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.9 °C (70 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Luwuk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Luwuk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C. The 10 km area around Luwuk, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.5 °C a year, 3.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Luwuk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (0.952°S, 122.788°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-849-3027), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
