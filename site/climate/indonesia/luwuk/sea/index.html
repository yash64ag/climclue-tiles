Luwuk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Sulawesi · Indonesia

The sea off Luwuk averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 26.6 °C (80 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Luwuk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Luwuk?
In February, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Luwuk?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.6 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Luwuk in August?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Luwuk?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Luwuk nearby?
In February: Bungku (29.0 °C, 199 km), Kendari (29.1 °C, 338 km) and Palopo (28.9 °C, 367 km).
