Ransiki sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · West Papua · Indonesia

The sea off Ransiki averages 29.4 °C (85 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 28.6 °C (83 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Ransiki sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ransiki?
In November, at about 29.4 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ransiki?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 28.6 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ransiki in August?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Ransiki?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
