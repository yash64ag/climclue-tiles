How May compares in Jepara

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jepara. Compared with April: 0.2 °C warmer, 62 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 0.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Jepara in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jepara in May?
In May, Jepara averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jepara?
No month in Jepara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jepara in May?
169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (477 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Jepara in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jepara get in May?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jepara in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
