How January compares in Jepara

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jepara. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 152 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Jepara in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jepara in January?
In January, Jepara averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 480 mm (18.9 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jepara?
No month in Jepara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jepara in January?
480 mm (18.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (480 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Jepara in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jepara get in January?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jepara in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
