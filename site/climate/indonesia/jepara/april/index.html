How April compares in Jepara

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jepara. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Jepara in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jepara in April?
In April, Jepara averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jepara?
No month in Jepara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jepara in April?
231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (477 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Jepara in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jepara get in April?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Jepara in April?
About 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is November (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Jepara in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
