How August compares in Jepara

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Jepara. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 26 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Jepara in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jepara in August?
In August, Jepara averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 301 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jepara?
No month in Jepara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jepara in August?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (480 mm), the driest August (45 mm).
Does it snow in Jepara in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jepara get in August?
About 301 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jepara in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
