How February compares in Jepara

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jepara. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 70 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. March is 0.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Jepara in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jepara in February?
In February, Jepara averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 407 mm (16.0 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jepara?
No month in Jepara is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jepara in February?
407 mm (16.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (477 mm), the driest August (44 mm).
Does it snow in Jepara in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jepara get in February?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jepara in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
