Pemalang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Pemalang is 7th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,900 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (2,761 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tegal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Pemalang is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 246 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.9 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, January (26.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pemalang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pemalang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
