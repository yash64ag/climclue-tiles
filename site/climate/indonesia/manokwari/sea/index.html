Manokwari sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · West Papua · Indonesia

The sea off Manokwari averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 29.2 °C (85 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Manokwari sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Manokwari?
In May, at about 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Manokwari?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 29.2 °C (85 °F).
How warm is the sea in Manokwari in February?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Manokwari?
About 29.6 °C (85 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
