Negara at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Negara is 113th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 3,222 hours of sunshine a year) and 144th wettest (2,109 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Banyuwangi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Negara is 0.4 °C warmer in March and 1.0 °C warmer in August, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.6 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.1 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Negara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Negara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
