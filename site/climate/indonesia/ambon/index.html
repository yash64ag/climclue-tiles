Ambon at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Ambon is 132nd warmest (25.3 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,012 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (3,099 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sorong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (464 km away), Ambon is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 1.4 °C cooler in July, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.1 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ambon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ambon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
