Ternate at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Ternate is 139th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 157th sunniest (about 2,082 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (2,938 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bitung, the nearest covered town with different numbers (261 km away), Ternate is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.9 °C cooler in July, with 164 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.1 °C), averages 0.6 °C more than its coolest, July (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in July. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ternate, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ternate has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
