Why Ternate has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Ternate climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ternate?
May, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 24.3 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ternate?
July, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 25.5 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 23.7 °C (75 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ternate?
May, with 322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,938 mm (115.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ternate?
September, with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ternate?
November, with about 189 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ternate?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ternate?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Ternate have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Ternate’s GeoNames location (0.791°N, 127.384°E; ClimaClue area sq-832-3073), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 210,836 (GeoNames).
