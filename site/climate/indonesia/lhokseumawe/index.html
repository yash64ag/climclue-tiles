Lhokseumawe at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Lhokseumawe is the 3rd driest (1,493 mm a year). Compared with Langsa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (120 km away), Lhokseumawe is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 386 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.9 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lhokseumawe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lhokseumawe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
