Banda Aceh at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Banda Aceh is the 5th least sunny (about 1,895 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Lhokseumawe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (205 km away), Banda Aceh is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 372 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.8 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, December (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Banda Aceh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Banda Aceh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
