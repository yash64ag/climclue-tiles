Citeureup at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Citeureup is 103rd warmest (26.2 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,680 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (3,378 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Depok, West Java, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Citeureup is 0.3 °C cooler in September and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 218 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.6 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Citeureup, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Citeureup has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
