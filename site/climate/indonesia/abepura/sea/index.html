Abepura sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Papua · Indonesia

The sea off Abepura averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 28.3 °C (83 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Abepura sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Abepura?
In May, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Abepura?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 28.3 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Abepura in February?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Abepura?
About 28.7 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Abepura nearby?
In May: Madang (29.9 °C, 642 km).
