Batang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Batang is 34th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,823 hours of sunshine a year) and 105th wettest (2,495 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jepara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Batang is 0.1 °C cooler in October and about as warm in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.6 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Batang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Batang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
