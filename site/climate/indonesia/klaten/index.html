Klaten at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Klaten is 117th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 92nd sunniest (about 2,499 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (2,131 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Depok, Yogyakarta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Klaten is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in August, with 208 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.4 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, August (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in August. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Klaten, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Klaten has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
