How November compares in Jambi City

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jambi City. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. December is 0.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Jambi City in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jambi City in November?
In November, Jambi City averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jambi City?
No month in Jambi City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jambi City in November?
268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (273 mm), the driest June (116 mm).
Does it snow in Jambi City in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jambi City get in November?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jambi City in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
