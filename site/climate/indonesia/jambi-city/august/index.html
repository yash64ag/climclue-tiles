Jambi City in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jambi City in August?
In August, Jambi City averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jambi City?
No month in Jambi City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jambi City in August?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (273 mm), the driest June (116 mm).
Does it snow in Jambi City in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jambi City get in August?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
