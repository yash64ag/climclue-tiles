How December compares in Jambi City

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jambi City. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Jambi City in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jambi City in December?
In December, Jambi City averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 277 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jambi City?
No month in Jambi City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jambi City in December?
277 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (278 mm), the driest June (117 mm).
Does it snow in Jambi City in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jambi City get in December?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jambi City in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
