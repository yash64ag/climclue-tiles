Soreang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Soreang is 157th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 2,354 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (2,806 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bandung, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Soreang is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 1.0 °C cooler in August, with 415 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (23.6 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, August (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Soreang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Soreang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
