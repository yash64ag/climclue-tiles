Purwodadi at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Purwodadi is 19th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 2,774 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (1,931 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Purwodadi is 0.4 °C warmer in October and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 200 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Purwodadi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Purwodadi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
