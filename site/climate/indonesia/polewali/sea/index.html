Polewali sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · West Sulawesi · Indonesia

The sea off Polewali averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 27.3 °C (81 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Polewali sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Polewali?
In November, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Polewali?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 27.3 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Polewali in August?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Polewali?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Polewali nearby?
In November: Maros (29.7 °C, 177 km), Makassar (29.7 °C, 191 km) and Galesong (30.0 °C, 210 km).
