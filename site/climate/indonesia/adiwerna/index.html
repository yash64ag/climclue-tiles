Adiwerna at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Adiwerna is 10th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 85th wettest (2,669 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tegal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Adiwerna is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 187 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.8 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (26.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.4 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Adiwerna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Adiwerna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Adiwerna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.938°S, 109.132°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-909-2891), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
