How January compares in Padang

January is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Padang. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 106 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Padang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Padang in January?
In January, Padang averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 426 mm (16.8 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Padang?
No month in Padang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Padang in January?
426 mm (16.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (634 mm), the driest May (333 mm).
Does it snow in Padang in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Padang get in January?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Padang in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
