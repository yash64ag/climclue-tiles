How April compares in Padang

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Padang. Compared with March: about as warm, 39 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Padang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Padang in April?
In April, Padang averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 396 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Padang?
No month in Padang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Padang in April?
396 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (589 mm), the driest May (306 mm).
Does it snow in Padang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Padang get in April?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Padang in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
