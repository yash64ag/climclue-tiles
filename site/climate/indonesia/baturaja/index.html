Baturaja at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Baturaja is 78th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 122nd sunniest (about 2,334 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (2,831 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Prabumulih, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Baturaja is 0.3 °C cooler in September and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (27.4 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 114 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Baturaja, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Baturaja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 114 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
