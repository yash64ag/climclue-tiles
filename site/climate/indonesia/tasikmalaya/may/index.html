How May compares in Tasikmalaya

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tasikmalaya. Compared with April: 0.1 °C cooler, 120 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Tasikmalaya in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tasikmalaya in May?
In May, Tasikmalaya averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tasikmalaya?
May is not one of Tasikmalaya’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tasikmalaya in May?
179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (439 mm), the driest August (61 mm).
Does it snow in Tasikmalaya in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tasikmalaya get in May?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tasikmalaya in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
