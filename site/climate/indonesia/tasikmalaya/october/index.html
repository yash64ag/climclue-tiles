How October compares in Tasikmalaya

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tasikmalaya. Compared with September: 1.0 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. November is 0.6 °C warmer than October on average.

Tasikmalaya in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tasikmalaya in October?
In October, Tasikmalaya averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tasikmalaya?
October is not one of Tasikmalaya’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tasikmalaya in October?
188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (444 mm), the driest August (66 mm).
Does it snow in Tasikmalaya in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tasikmalaya get in October?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tasikmalaya in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
