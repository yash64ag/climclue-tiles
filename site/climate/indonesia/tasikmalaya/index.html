Why Tasikmalaya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tasikmalaya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tasikmalaya?
April, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 21.5 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tasikmalaya?
August, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 25.7 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 19.7 °C (67 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tasikmalaya?
January, with 444 mm (17.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,987 mm (117.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tasikmalaya?
August, with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tasikmalaya?
August, with about 240 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tasikmalaya?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tasikmalaya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Tasikmalaya have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Tasikmalaya’s GeoNames location (7.327°S, 108.221°E; ClimaClue area sq-913-2882), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 770,839 (GeoNames).
