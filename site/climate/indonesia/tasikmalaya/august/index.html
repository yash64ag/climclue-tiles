How August compares in Tasikmalaya

August is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Tasikmalaya. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Tasikmalaya in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tasikmalaya in August?
In August, Tasikmalaya averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tasikmalaya?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Tasikmalaya’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tasikmalaya in August?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (444 mm), the driest August (66 mm).
Does it snow in Tasikmalaya in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tasikmalaya get in August?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tasikmalaya in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
