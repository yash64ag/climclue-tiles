How July compares in Tasikmalaya

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tasikmalaya. Compared with June: 1.1 °C cooler, 38 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Tasikmalaya in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tasikmalaya in July?
In July, Tasikmalaya averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tasikmalaya?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Tasikmalaya’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tasikmalaya in July?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (444 mm), the driest August (66 mm).
Does it snow in Tasikmalaya in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tasikmalaya get in July?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tasikmalaya in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
