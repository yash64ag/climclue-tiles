Sumbawa Besar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · West Nusa Tenggara · Indonesia

The sea off Sumbawa Besar averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 27.2 °C (81 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Sumbawa Besar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sumbawa Besar?
In April, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sumbawa Besar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 27.2 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sumbawa Besar in August?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Sumbawa Besar?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
