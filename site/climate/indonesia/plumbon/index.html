Plumbon at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Plumbon is 70th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,796 hours of sunshine a year) and 139th wettest (2,164 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Weru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Plumbon is 0.3 °C cooler in October and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 197 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.3 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Plumbon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Plumbon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
