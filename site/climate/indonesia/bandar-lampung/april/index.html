Bandar Lampung in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandar Lampung in April?
In April, Bandar Lampung averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bandar Lampung?
No month in Bandar Lampung is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

Does it snow in Bandar Lampung in April?

How many hours of sunshine does Bandar Lampung get in April?
