Bandar Lampung in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandar Lampung in March?
In March, Bandar Lampung averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bandar Lampung?
No month in Bandar Lampung is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandar Lampung in March?
325 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (408 mm), the driest August (107 mm).
Does it snow in Bandar Lampung in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandar Lampung get in March?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandar Lampung in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
