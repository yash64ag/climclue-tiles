How August compares in Bandar Lampung

August is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Bandar Lampung. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Bandar Lampung in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandar Lampung in August?
In August, Bandar Lampung averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bandar Lampung?
No month in Bandar Lampung is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandar Lampung in August?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (408 mm), the driest August (107 mm).
Does it snow in Bandar Lampung in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandar Lampung get in August?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandar Lampung in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
