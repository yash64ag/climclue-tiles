Bandar Lampung in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandar Lampung in June?
In June, Bandar Lampung averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bandar Lampung?
No month in Bandar Lampung is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandar Lampung in June?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (373 mm), the driest August (99 mm).
Does it snow in Bandar Lampung in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandar Lampung get in June?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
