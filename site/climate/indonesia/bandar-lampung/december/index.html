How December compares in Bandar Lampung

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bandar Lampung. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 65 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Bandar Lampung in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandar Lampung in December?
In December, Bandar Lampung averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 280 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bandar Lampung?
No month in Bandar Lampung is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandar Lampung in December?
280 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (408 mm), the driest August (107 mm).
Does it snow in Bandar Lampung in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandar Lampung get in December?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandar Lampung in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
