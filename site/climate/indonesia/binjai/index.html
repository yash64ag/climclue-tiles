Binjai at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Binjai is 66th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 152nd sunniest (about 2,140 hours of sunshine a year) and 94th wettest (2,617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sunggal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Binjai is 0.4 °C warmer in June and about as warm in December, with 293 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (27.4 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, December (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Binjai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Binjai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
