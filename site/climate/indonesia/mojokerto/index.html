Mojokerto at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Mojokerto is 16th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 3,209 hours of sunshine a year) and 109th wettest (2,450 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jombang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Mojokerto is 0.7 °C warmer in October and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 176 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.3 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mojokerto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mojokerto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
