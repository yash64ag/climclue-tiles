Cileunyi at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Cileunyi is 152nd warmest (23.5 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 2,420 hours of sunshine a year) and 118th wettest (2,402 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paseh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Cileunyi is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (23.9 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 80 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cileunyi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cileunyi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
