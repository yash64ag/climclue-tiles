Jakarta weather in July

Average July weather in Jakarta, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Jakarta averages 31.7 °C (89 °F) days and 21.7 °C (71 °F) nights, with 103 mm (4.1 in) of rain over the month and about 273 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Jakarta

July is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jakarta. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

What to pack for Jakarta in July

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses.

Rules used: day high under 10 °C (50 °F) → warm coat; 10–18 °C → jacket or sweater; 18–25 °C → light layers; 25 °C (77 °F) or more → light, breathable clothes; night low under 0 °C → hat and gloves; night low 10 °C or more below the day high → an extra layer for evenings; 100 mm or more of precipitation → rain jacket or umbrella; 50–100 mm → compact umbrella; 5 cm or more of fresh snow, or snow on the ground → waterproof boots; dew point 16 °C (61 °F) or more → breathable fabrics for humid air; 250 hours of sunshine or more → sunglasses. Jakarta in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jakarta in July?
In July, Jakarta averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jakarta?
No month in Jakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jakarta in July?
103 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (408 mm), the driest August (91 mm).
Does it snow in Jakarta in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jakarta get in July?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Jakarta in July?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Jakarta in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
