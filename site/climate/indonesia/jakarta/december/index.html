Jakarta weather in December

Average December weather in Jakarta, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Jakarta averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 22.4 °C (72 °F) nights, with 274 mm (10.8 in) of rain over the month and about 175 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Jakarta

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jakarta. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 67 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

What to pack for Jakarta in December

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Jakarta in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jakarta in December?
In December, Jakarta averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jakarta?
No month in Jakarta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jakarta in December?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (408 mm), the driest August (91 mm).
Does it snow in Jakarta in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jakarta get in December?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Jakarta in December?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Jakarta in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
