How February compares in Bandung

February is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with January: about as warm, 11 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 0.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Bandung in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in February?
In February, Bandung averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bandung?
February is not one of Bandung’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in February?
265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in February?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
