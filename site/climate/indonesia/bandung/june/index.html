How June compares in Bandung

June is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with May: 0.4 °C cooler, 71 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Bandung in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in June?
In June, Bandung averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bandung?
June is not one of Bandung’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in June?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
