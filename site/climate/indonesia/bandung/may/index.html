How May compares in Bandung

May is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 84 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Bandung in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in May?
In May, Bandung averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bandung?
May is not one of Bandung’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in May?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in May?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
