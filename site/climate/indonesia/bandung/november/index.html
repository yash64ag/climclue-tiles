How November compares in Bandung

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with October: 0.1 °C cooler, 99 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. December is 0.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Bandung in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in November?
In November, Bandung averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 282 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bandung?
November is not one of Bandung’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in November?
282 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in November?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
