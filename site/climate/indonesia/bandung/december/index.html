How December compares in Bandung

December is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Bandung in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in December?
In December, Bandung averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 283 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bandung?
December is not one of Bandung’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in December?
283 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in December?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
