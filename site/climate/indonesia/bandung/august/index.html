How August compares in Bandung

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Bandung. Compared with July: about as warm, 12 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Bandung in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in August?
In August, Bandung averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bandung?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Bandung’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in August?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in August?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
