Bandung at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Bandung is 150th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 2,454 hours of sunshine a year) and 120th wettest (2,391 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cimahi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Bandung is 1.7 °C warmer in May and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 483 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.1 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.9 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 74 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bandung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Bandung

For mild weather, the best months to visit Bandung are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (24.1 °C), April (24.1 °C) and October (24.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): August (64 mm), July (76 mm) and September (87 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): August (251 h), July (247 h) and June (227 h).

Why Bandung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 74 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
