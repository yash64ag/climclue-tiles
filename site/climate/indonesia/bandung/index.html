Why Bandung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 74 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bandung climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bandung?
May, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 27.2 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 20.1 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bandung?
January, with an average of 23.3 °C (74 °F), day highs around 26.0 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 20.2 °C (68 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bandung?
March, with 337 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,391 mm (94.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bandung?
August, with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bandung?
August, with about 251 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bandung?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bandung?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Bandung have?
Tropical rainforest (Köppen Af). Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Bandung’s GeoNames location (6.922°S, 107.607°E; ClimaClue area sq-909-2876), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,528,163 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
