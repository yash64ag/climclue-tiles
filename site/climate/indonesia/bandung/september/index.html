How September compares in Bandung

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. October is 0.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Bandung in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in September?
In September, Bandung averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bandung?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Bandung’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in September?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
