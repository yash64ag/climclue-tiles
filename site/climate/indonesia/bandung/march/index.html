How March compares in Bandung

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bandung. Compared with February: 0.4 °C warmer, 72 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Bandung in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bandung in March?
In March, Bandung averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 337 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bandung?
March is not one of Bandung’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bandung in March?
337 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (337 mm), the driest August (64 mm).
Does it snow in Bandung in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bandung get in March?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bandung in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
