Rengasdengklok at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Rengasdengklok is 36th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,688 hours of sunshine a year) and 119th wettest (2,372 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karawang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Rengasdengklok is about as warm in October and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (26.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.4 °C (74 °F), in July. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rengasdengklok, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rengasdengklok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
