Ciamis at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Ciamis is 140th warmest (24.7 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,576 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (2,964 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tasikmalaya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Ciamis is 0.5 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C warmer in August, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.6 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, August (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in August. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ciamis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ciamis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
