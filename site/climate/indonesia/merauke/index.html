Merauke at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Merauke is 88th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 158th sunniest (about 2,074 hours of sunshine a year) and 149th wettest (2,017 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Timika, the nearest covered town with different numbers (597 km away), Merauke is 0.8 °C warmer in November and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 2,737 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.4 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, July (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.1 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Merauke, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Merauke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
