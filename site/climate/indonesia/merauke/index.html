Why Merauke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Merauke climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Merauke?
November, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 30.5 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 24.8 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Merauke?
July, with an average of 25.3 °C (77 °F), day highs around 27.7 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 23.4 °C (74 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Merauke?
February, with 324 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,017 mm (79.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Merauke?
August, with 39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Merauke?
October, with about 220 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Merauke?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Merauke?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Merauke’s GeoNames location (8.500°S, 140.406°E; ClimaClue area sq-924-3204), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 116,864 (GeoNames).
