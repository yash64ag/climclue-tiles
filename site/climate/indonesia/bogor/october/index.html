How October compares in Bogor

October is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bogor. Compared with September: 0.1 °C warmer, 87 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 0.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Bogor in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogor in October?
In October, Bogor averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 319 mm (12.5 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bogor?
No month in Bogor is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogor in October?
319 mm (12.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (386 mm), the driest July (184 mm).
Does it snow in Bogor in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogor get in October?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogor in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
