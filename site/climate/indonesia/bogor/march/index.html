How March compares in Bogor

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bogor. Compared with February: 0.4 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Bogor in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogor in March?
In March, Bogor averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 350 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bogor?
No month in Bogor is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogor in March?
350 mm (13.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (386 mm), the driest July (184 mm).
Does it snow in Bogor in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogor get in March?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogor in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
