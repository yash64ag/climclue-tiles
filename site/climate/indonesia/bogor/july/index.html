Bogor weather in July

Average July weather in Bogor, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Bogor averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 19.9 °C (68 °F) nights, with 189 mm (7.4 in) of rain over the month and about 275 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Bogor

July is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bogor. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

What to pack for Bogor in July

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses.

Rules used: day high under 10 °C (50 °F) → warm coat; 10–18 °C → jacket or sweater; 18–25 °C → light layers; 25 °C (77 °F) or more → light, breathable clothes; night low under 0 °C → hat and gloves; night low 10 °C or more below the day high → an extra layer for evenings; 100 mm or more of precipitation → rain jacket or umbrella; 50–100 mm → compact umbrella; 5 cm or more of fresh snow, or snow on the ground → waterproof boots; dew point 16 °C (61 °F) or more → breathable fabrics for humid air; 250 hours of sunshine or more → sunglasses. Bogor in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogor in July?
In July, Bogor averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bogor?
No month in Bogor is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogor in July?
189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (374 mm), the driest July (189 mm).
Does it snow in Bogor in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogor get in July?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogor in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
