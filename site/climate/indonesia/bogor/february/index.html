Bogor weather in February

Average February weather in Bogor, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Bogor averages 29.2 °C (85 °F) days and 20.6 °C (69 °F) nights, with 361 mm (14.2 in) of rain over the month and about 149 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Bogor

February is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bogor. Compared with January: about as warm, 13 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. March is 0.4 °C warmer than February on average.

What to pack for Bogor in February

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Bogor in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogor in February?
In February, Bogor averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 361 mm (14.2 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bogor?
No month in Bogor is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogor in February?
361 mm (14.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (374 mm), the driest July (189 mm).
Does it snow in Bogor in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogor get in February?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogor in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
