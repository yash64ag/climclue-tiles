How November compares in Bogor

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bogor. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 0.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Bogor in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bogor in November?
In November, Bogor averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bogor?
No month in Bogor is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bogor in November?
344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (386 mm), the driest July (184 mm).
Does it snow in Bogor in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bogor get in November?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bogor in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
