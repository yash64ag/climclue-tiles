Seririt at a glance

Among the 449 towns ClimaClue covers in Indonesia, Seririt is 148th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 3,095 hours of sunshine a year) and 215th wettest (2,543 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Banjar, Bali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Seririt is 0.1 °C cooler in November and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (27.4 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Seririt, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Seririt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Seririt itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.193°S, 114.939°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-921-2949), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
