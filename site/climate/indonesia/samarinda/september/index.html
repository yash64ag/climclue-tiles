Samarinda in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarinda in September?
In September, Samarinda averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Samarinda?
No month in Samarinda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarinda in September?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (266 mm), the driest August (133 mm).
Does it snow in Samarinda in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarinda get in September?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarinda in September?
