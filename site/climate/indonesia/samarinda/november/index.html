How November compares in Samarinda

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Samarinda. Compared with October: 0.1 °C cooler, 52 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Samarinda in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarinda in November?
In November, Samarinda averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Samarinda?
No month in Samarinda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarinda in November?
231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (260 mm), the driest August (132 mm).
Does it snow in Samarinda in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarinda get in November?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
