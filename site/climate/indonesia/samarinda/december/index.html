How December compares in Samarinda

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Samarinda. Compared with November: 0.2 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Samarinda in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarinda in December?
In December, Samarinda averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Samarinda?
No month in Samarinda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarinda in December?
242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (266 mm), the driest August (133 mm).
Does it snow in Samarinda in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarinda get in December?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Samarinda in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
