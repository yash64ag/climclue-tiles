Samarinda in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Samarinda in March?
In March, Samarinda averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Samarinda?
No month in Samarinda is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Samarinda in March?
235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (260 mm), the driest August (132 mm).
Does it snow in Samarinda in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Samarinda get in March?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
