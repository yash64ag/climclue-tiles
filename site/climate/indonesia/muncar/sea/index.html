Muncar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · East Java · Indonesia

The sea off Muncar averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 26.0 °C (79 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Muncar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Muncar?
In January, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Muncar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.0 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Muncar in August?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Muncar?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
