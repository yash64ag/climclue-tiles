Martapura at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Martapura is 82nd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 117th sunniest (about 2,360 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (2,941 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Banjarmasin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Martapura is 0.2 °C cooler in October and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.1 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Martapura, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Martapura has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
