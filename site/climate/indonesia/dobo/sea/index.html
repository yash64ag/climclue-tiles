Dobo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maluku · Indonesia

The sea off Dobo averages 29.2 °C (85 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 25.8 °C (78 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Dobo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dobo?
In April, at about 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dobo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.8 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dobo in August?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Dobo?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dobo nearby?
In April: Bintuni (30.1 °C, 414 km).
