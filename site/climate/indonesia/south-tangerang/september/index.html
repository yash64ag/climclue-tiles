How September compares in South Tangerang

September is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in South Tangerang. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

South Tangerang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in South Tangerang in September?
In September, South Tangerang averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit South Tangerang?
No month in South Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in South Tangerang in September?
126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (411 mm), the driest August (107 mm).
Does it snow in South Tangerang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does South Tangerang get in September?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for South Tangerang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
