How December compares in South Tangerang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in South Tangerang. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 53 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

South Tangerang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in South Tangerang in December?
In December, South Tangerang averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 280 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit South Tangerang?
No month in South Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in South Tangerang in December?
280 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (411 mm), the driest August (107 mm).
Does it snow in South Tangerang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does South Tangerang get in December?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for South Tangerang in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
