South Tangerang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in South Tangerang in March?
In March, South Tangerang averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 296 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit South Tangerang?
No month in South Tangerang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in South Tangerang in March?
296 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (412 mm), the driest August (122 mm).
Does it snow in South Tangerang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does South Tangerang get in March?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in South Tangerang in March?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
