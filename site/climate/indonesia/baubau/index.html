Baubau at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Baubau is 120th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 2,644 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (1,757 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kendari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (165 km away), Baubau is 0.2 °C cooler in November and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 144 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.8 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, July (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Baubau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Baubau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
