Manggar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bangka–Belitung Islands · Indonesia

The sea off Manggar averages 29.8 °C (86 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 28.1 °C (83 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Manggar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Manggar?
In May, at about 29.8 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Manggar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 28.1 °C (83 °F).
How warm is the sea in Manggar in February?
About 28.1 °C (83 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Manggar?
About 28.9 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
