Pare at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Pare is 121st warmest (25.8 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,135 hours of sunshine a year) and 93rd wettest (2,634 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kediri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Pare is about as warm in November and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 186 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (26.6 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in July. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
