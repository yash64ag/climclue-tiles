Teluk Dalam sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Sumatra · Indonesia

The sea off Teluk Dalam averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 28.8 °C (84 °F) in November. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Teluk Dalam sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Teluk Dalam?
In June, at about 29.9 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Teluk Dalam?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is November, at 28.8 °C (84 °F).
How warm is the sea in Teluk Dalam in November?
About 28.8 °C (84 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Teluk Dalam?
About 29.4 °C (85 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
