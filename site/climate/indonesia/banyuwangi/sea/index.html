Banyuwangi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · East Java · Indonesia

The sea off Banyuwangi averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in December, its warmest month, and 26.0 °C (79 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Banyuwangi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Banyuwangi?
In December, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Banyuwangi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.0 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Banyuwangi in August?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Banyuwangi?
About 27.5 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Banyuwangi nearby?
In December: Muncar (29.0 °C, 23 km), Panji (29.8 °C, 63 km) and Situbondo (30.0 °C, 70 km).
