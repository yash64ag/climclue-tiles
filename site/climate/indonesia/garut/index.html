Garut at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Garut is the 4th coolest (20.9 °C year average). Compared with Paseh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Garut is 2.3 °C cooler in April and 3.3 °C cooler in August, with 386 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (21.6 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, August (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in August. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Garut, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Garut has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
