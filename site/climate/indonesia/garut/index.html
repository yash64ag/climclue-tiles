Why Garut has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Garut climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Garut?
April, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 24.7 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Garut?
August, with an average of 19.8 °C (68 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 16.0 °C (61 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Garut?
March, with 397 mm (15.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,734 mm (107.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Garut?
August, with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Garut?
August, with about 233 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Garut?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Garut?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Garut’s GeoNames location (7.245°S, 107.921°E; ClimaClue area sq-912-2879), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 131,809 (GeoNames).
