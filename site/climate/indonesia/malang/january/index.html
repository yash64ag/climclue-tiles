How January compares in Malang

January is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Malang. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 40 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Malang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malang in January?
In January, Malang averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 448 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Malang?
January is not one of Malang’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malang in January?
448 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (448 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Malang in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malang get in January?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malang in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
