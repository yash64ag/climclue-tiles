How February compares in Malang

February is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Malang. Compared with January: about as warm, 26 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Malang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malang in February?
In February, Malang averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 422 mm (16.6 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Malang?
February is not one of Malang’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malang in February?
422 mm (16.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (448 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Malang in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malang get in February?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malang in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
