How December compares in Malang

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Malang. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 100 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Malang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malang in December?
In December, Malang averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 408 mm (16.1 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Malang?
December is not one of Malang’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malang in December?
408 mm (16.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (448 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Malang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malang get in December?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malang in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
