How September compares in Malang

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Malang. Compared with August: 0.8 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Malang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malang in September?
In September, Malang averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Malang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Malang’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malang in September?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (448 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Malang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malang get in September?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
