Malang at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Malang is 145th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,952 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (2,903 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Singosari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Malang is 1.5 °C warmer in November and 1.4 °C warmer in July, with 275 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (25.1 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, July (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Malang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Malang

For mild weather, the best months to visit Malang are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.

Why Malang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
