Why Malang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Malang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Malang?
November, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 21.7 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Malang?
July, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 18.7 °C (66 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Malang?
January, with 448 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,903 mm (114.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Malang?
August, with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Malang?
August, with about 290 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Malang?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Malang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Malang have?
Tropical monsoon (Köppen Am). Hot all year, with a very wet monsoon season and a short drier spell. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Malang’s GeoNames location (7.980°S, 112.630°E; ClimaClue area sq-919-2926), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 889,359 (GeoNames).
