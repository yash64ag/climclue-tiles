Malang weather in May

Average May weather in Malang, Indonesia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Malang averages 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 18.7 °C (66 °F) nights, with 192 mm (7.5 in) of rain over the month and about 247 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Malang

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Malang. Compared with April: 0.2 °C cooler, 88 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. June is 0.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Malang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malang in May?
In May, Malang averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 192 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Malang?
May is not one of Malang’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malang in May?
192 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (439 mm), the driest August (40 mm).
Does it snow in Malang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malang get in May?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
