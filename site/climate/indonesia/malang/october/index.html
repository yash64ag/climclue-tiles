How October compares in Malang

October is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Malang. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 83 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. November is 0.1 °C warmer than October on average.

Malang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Malang in October?
In October, Malang averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 156 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Malang?
October is not one of Malang’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Malang in October?
156 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (448 mm), the driest August (38 mm).
Does it snow in Malang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Malang get in October?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Malang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
