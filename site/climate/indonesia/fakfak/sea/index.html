Fakfak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Papua · Indonesia

The sea off Fakfak averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 25.8 °C (78 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Fakfak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Fakfak?
In April, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Fakfak?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.8 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Fakfak in August?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Fakfak?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Fakfak nearby?
In April: Bintuni (30.1 °C, 164 km), Sorong (29.2 °C, 255 km) and Ransiki (29.2 °C, 261 km).
