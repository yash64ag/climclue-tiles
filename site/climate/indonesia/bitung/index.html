Bitung at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Bitung is 126th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 143rd sunniest (about 2,176 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (2,774 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manado, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Bitung is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 253 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.9 °C), averages 0.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in February. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bitung, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bitung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
