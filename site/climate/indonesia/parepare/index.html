Parepare at a glance

Among the 169 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Indonesia, Parepare is 97th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 2,652 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (2,778 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Watampone, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Parepare is 0.1 °C warmer in October and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 498 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Parepare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Parepare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
