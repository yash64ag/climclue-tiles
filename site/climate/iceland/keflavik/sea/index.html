Keflavík sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Southern Peninsula · Iceland

The sea off Keflavík averages 10.9 °C (52 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.6 °C (40 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Keflavík sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Keflavík?
In August, at about 10.9 °C (52 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Keflavík?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 4.6 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Keflavík in March?
About 4.6 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Keflavík?
About 7.3 °C (45 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
