Refund policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

Extra questions. Members can buy 100 extra questions for $3 (a one-time payment, tax included). They are used after the weekly questions run out, don’t expire while you’re a member, can’t be used without an active membership, and are not refundable.

Membership payments are not refundable. Membership is cheap ($2.50 a month or $12 a year), so we keep it simple: you can cancel any time so you aren’t charged again, and you keep membership until the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

Nothing here takes away any rights you have under the consumer laws of the country you live in. If those laws give you a right to a refund, we’ll honour it: email contact@climaclue.com from the email address of your ClimaClue account.

Who we are

ClimaClue is run by Yash Agarwal, New Jersey, USA. Contact: contact@climaclue.com.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from Manage or cancel on the membership page. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep membership until the end of the period you’ve already paid for.

Your rights where you live

This policy is in addition to your legal rights. If the consumer laws of your country give you more (for example a right to cancel a purchase within a set time), those rights still apply.
