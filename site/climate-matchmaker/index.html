Find your ideal climate

Where should you live, based on climate? ClimaClue’s free Climate Matchmaker lets you describe the climate you like, such as mild winters, summers that are warm but not too hot, plenty of sun and low humidity, and ranks towns around the world by how well they fit.

How the Climate Matchmaker works

Switch on only the things you care about, slide each one to the value you would like, and say how much it matters: a little, some or a lot. Under each slider a bar shows where the world’s towns sit, from the lowest 10% to the highest 10%, so you can see whether a wish is common or rare. You can also ask for a place near the coast.

The measures you can set are:

Temperature

    Rain and snow

    • Rain and snow: total a year
    • Dry months: months with under 30 mm
    • Snowfall: fresh snow a year

    Sun and air

    • Sunshine: hours a year
    • Humidity: average relative humidity
    • Mugginess: muggiest month’s dew point (above 20 °C feels sticky)
    • Wind: average wind speed

    Each town gets a score out of 100% for every measure you switched on: 100% at exactly the value you chose, falling to 0% at 10 °C away for temperatures and dew point, and for the other measures at half the gap between the world’s low and high towns. The match is the weighted average of those scores, with “a lot” counting three times “some”, and “some” three times “a little”, so adding more wishes never drags every town to zero. The list shows at most two towns per country, so you see the whole world rather than one country’s suburbs, and every result says which of your wishes it meets and which it misses. The matches light up on the globe, and one click opens a town’s full climate profile.

    What the matches are based on

    The Matchmaker uses towns of 100,000+ people, each described by the monthly averages for its ~10 km area: CHELSA V2.1 temperature and precipitation (1981–2010), CRU sunshine (about 1961–1990) and ERA5-Land dew point and snow (1981–2010). It matches climate only. Cost of living, jobs, safety and everything else that makes a place a good home are up to you, and averages say nothing about a single summer or winter, so treat the list as a starting point for research.

    To check a result in detail, open its town page, for example London, New York City, Tokyo, Sydney, Dubai, or put two candidates side by side with the comparison tool.

    What members get: members see the full list of 20 matches and can save up to 50 places (free accounts: 5), from $1 a month (membership).

    Questions and answers

    How do I find a city with my ideal climate?
    Open the Climate Matchmaker, switch on the measures you care about (winter and summer temperature, rain, sunshine, humidity, snow, wind, the coast), set the values you like, and it ranks towns worldwide by how well they fit.
    Where should I live based on climate?
    That depends on what you like. The Matchmaker turns your preferences into a ranked list of towns and shows which wishes each one meets, so you can compare the candidates in detail.
    Which towns does the Climate Matchmaker include?
    Towns of 100,000+ people worldwide, each described by modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages for its area of about 10 km.
    Does it consider cost of living or other factors?
    No. It matches climate only: temperature, rain and snow, sunshine, humidity, dew point, wind and closeness to the coast.
    Is the Climate Matchmaker free?
    Yes, it is free to use in a web browser.
