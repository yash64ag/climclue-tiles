Humid continental, warm summer climate (Köppen Dfb)

Warm summers and cold, snowy winters, with rain or snow all year.

  • No dry season
  • Warm summers
  • Long, cold winters with lasting snow

Where the humid continental, warm summer climate occurs

2,523 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 41 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 19.1 °C (66 °F), the median coolest month -3.7 °C (25 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 666 mm (26.2 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted.
Rain and snow
Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
Sunshine and cloud
Cloudiness follows passing storms; long summer days can still bring plenty of sunshine.
Moisture
Cold winter air holds little water vapour; summer dew points are much higher.
Wind
Storm tracks bring windier weather in the cold season.
