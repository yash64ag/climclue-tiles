Too cold for trees: even the warmest month stays cool.

  • The warmest month averages below 10 °C
  • Long, cold winters
  • Low plants, mosses and often frozen ground

Where the tundra climate occurs

21 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 7 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 8.2 °C (47 °F), the median coolest month 4.4 °C (40 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 990 mm (39.0 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees.
Rain and snow
Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light.
Sunshine and cloud
Cloud often lingers near cold coasts; day length dominates sunshine through the year.
Moisture
Very cold air holds little water vapour, so dew points are low.
Wind
Open, treeless land is often windy.
