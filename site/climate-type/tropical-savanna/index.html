Tropical savanna climate (Köppen Aw)

Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season.

  • Warm in every month
  • Rain concentrated in one season
  • Months of dry weather, typical of grassland with scattered trees

Where the tropical savanna climate occurs

984 of ClimaClue’s towns of 100,000+ people have this type, in 59 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 28.4 °C (83 °F), the median coolest month 23.4 °C (74 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 1,387 mm (54.6 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages for its ~10 km area, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings.
Rain and snow
The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away.
Sunshine and cloud
The dry season is sunny; the wet season is much cloudier.
Moisture
Dew points rise in the wet season and fall when drier air takes over.
Wind
Steady trade winds often dominate the dry season.
