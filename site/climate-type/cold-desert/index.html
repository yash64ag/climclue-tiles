Cold desert climate (Köppen BWk)

Very dry, with cold winters.

  • Very little rain or snow
  • Warm or hot summers but cold winters
  • Often far inland, at height, or beside a cold ocean current

Where the cold desert climate occurs

271 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 20 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 26.2 °C (79 °F), the median coolest month -3.8 °C (25 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 130 mm (5.1 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large.
Rain and snow
Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare.
Sunshine and cloud
Sinking dry air suppresses clouds, so sunshine is abundant.
Moisture
Little water is available to evaporate, so dew points are low and humidity drops sharply in the afternoon heat.
Wind
Strong daytime heating can stir gusty winds and dust.
