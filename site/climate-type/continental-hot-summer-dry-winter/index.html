Where the continental, hot summer, dry winter climate occurs

496 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 5 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 25.2 °C (77 °F), the median coolest month -3.0 °C (27 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 681 mm (26.8 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm.
Rain and snow
A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain.
Sunshine and cloud
Winters are clear and sunny under high pressure.
Moisture
Winter air is extremely dry; summer brings moisture with the rains.
Wind
Cold winter outflow winds can be strong and steady.
