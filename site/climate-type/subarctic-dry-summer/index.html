Where the subarctic, dry summer climate occurs

7 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 6 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 14.1 °C (57 °F), the median coolest month -9.9 °C (14 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 625 mm (24.6 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm.
Rain and snow
Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow.
Sunshine and cloud
Dry summers are sunny.
Moisture
Cold winter air holds little moisture.
Wind
Winter storms bring stronger winds.
