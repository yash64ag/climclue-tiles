Tropical monsoon climate (Köppen Am)

Hot all year, with a very wet monsoon season and a short drier spell.

  • Warm in every month
  • Extremely heavy rain for part of the year
  • A brief dry season that the wet season more than makes up for

Where the tropical monsoon climate occurs

336 of ClimaClue’s towns of 100,000+ people have this type, in 44 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 27.6 °C (82 °F), the median coolest month 24.1 °C (75 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 2,299 mm (90.5 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages for its ~10 km area, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains.
Rain and snow
Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse.
Sunshine and cloud
Sunshine drops sharply in the cloudy monsoon months.
Moisture
Moisture peaks when monsoon winds blow in from the ocean.
Wind
Monsoon winds reverse direction between seasons.
