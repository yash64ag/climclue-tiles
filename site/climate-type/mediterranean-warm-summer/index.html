Mediterranean, warm summer climate (Köppen Csb)

Köppen Csb

Mediterranean, warm summer
  • 580towns

Warm, dry summers and mild, wet winters.

  • Summer is the dry season
  • Summers are warm rather than hot, often cooled by the sea
  • Rainy, mild winters

Where the mediterranean, warm summer climate occurs

580 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 24 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 20.0 °C (68 °F), the median coolest month 9.5 °C (49 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 1,061 mm (41.8 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold.
Rain and snow
In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain.
Sunshine and cloud
Summer high pressure brings long sunny spells; winter is cloudier.
Moisture
Summers are often dry inland but can be humid with low cloud near cool coasts.
Wind
Winter storms bring the stronger winds; summer sea breezes are common near coasts.
