Hot semi-arid climate (Köppen BSh)

Köppen BSh

Hot semi-arid
  • 1,965towns
  • 67countries
  • 30.7 °Ctypical warmest month
  • 16.8 °Ctypical coolest month

Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season.

  • Low rainfall that varies a lot from year to year
  • Hot for most of the year
  • Grass and scrub rather than forest

Where the hot semi-arid climate occurs

1,965 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 67 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 30.7 °C (87 °F), the median coolest month 16.8 °C (62 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 581 mm (22.9 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp.
Rain and snow
It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid.
Sunshine and cloud
Mostly sunny, with clouds tied to the short rainy season.
Moisture
Moisture comes and goes with the brief wet season.
Wind
Open, dry land often has steady or gusty winds.
