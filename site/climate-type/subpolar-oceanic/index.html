Subpolar oceanic climate (Köppen Cfc)

Cool and damp all year, with short cool summers.

  • No dry season
  • Only a few months are properly warm
  • Winters stay mild for the latitude because of the sea

Where the subpolar oceanic climate occurs

7 of ClimaClue’s towns have this type, in 4 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 10.9 °C (52 °F), the median coolest month 0.9 °C (34 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 729 mm (28.7 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot.
Rain and snow
Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month.
Sunshine and cloud
Frequent cloud from passing weather systems limits sunshine, especially in winter.
Moisture
Ocean air keeps relative humidity fairly high through the year.
Wind
Storm tracks bring the windiest weather in the cooler months.
