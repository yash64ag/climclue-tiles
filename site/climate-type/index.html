Climate types (Köppen–Geiger)

The Köppen–Geiger system sorts climates by their yearly pattern of temperature and precipitation. These are the types found among ClimaClue’s towns, calculated from each town’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

Tropical (A)

  • Tropical rainforest (Af) · 1419 towns · Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season.
  • Tropical monsoon (Am) · 1882 towns · Hot all year, with a very wet monsoon season and a short drier spell.
  • Tropical savanna (Aw) · 5520 towns · Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season.

Dry (B)

  • Hot desert (BWh) · 1320 towns · Very dry and hot: far more water could evaporate than ever falls.
  • Cold desert (BWk) · 271 towns · Very dry, with cold winters.
  • Hot semi-arid (BSh) · 1965 towns · Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season.
  • Cold semi-arid (BSk) · 936 towns · Dry grassland climate with cold winters.

Temperate (C)

  • Mediterranean, hot summer (Csa) · 2671 towns · Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters.
  • Mediterranean, warm summer (Csb) · 632 towns · Warm, dry summers and mild, wet winters.
  • Humid subtropical, dry winter (Cwa) · 1857 towns · Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters.
  • Subtropical highland, dry winter (Cwb) · 558 towns · Mild highland climate with wet summers and dry winters.
  • Subpolar highland, dry winter (Cwc) · 7 towns · Cool highland climate with wet summers and dry winters: found on high tropical mountains.
  • Humid subtropical (Cfa) · 4233 towns · Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year.
  • Oceanic (Cfb) · 3390 towns · Mild all year, with rain in every season.
  • Subpolar oceanic (Cfc) · 6 towns · Cool and damp all year, with short cool summers.

Continental (D)

  • Continental, hot dry summer (Dsa) · 189 towns · Hot, dry summers and cold, snowy winters.
  • Continental, warm dry summer (Dsb) · 53 towns · Warm, dry summers and cold, snowy winters.
  • Subarctic, dry summer (Dsc) · 7 towns · Short, cool, dry summers and long, cold winters.
  • Continental, hot summer, dry winter (Dwa) · 488 towns · Hot, wet summers and cold, dry winters.
  • Continental, warm summer, dry winter (Dwb) · 182 towns · Warm, wet summers and cold, dry winters.
  • Subarctic, dry winter (Dwc) · 23 towns · Short, cool summers and long, very cold, dry winters.
  • Humid continental, hot summer (Dfa) · 1486 towns · Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year.
  • Humid continental, warm summer (Dfb) · 2523 towns · Warm summers and cold, snowy winters, with rain or snow all year.
  • Subarctic (Dfc) · 174 towns · Short, cool summers and long, very cold winters.
  • Very cold subarctic (Dfd) · 1 town · Short summers and extremely cold winters.

Polar (E)

  • Tundra (ET) · 21 towns · Too cold for trees: even the warmest month stays cool.

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), relative humidity calculated from that dew point and the CHELSA night lows and day highs, CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine and the day–night range), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
