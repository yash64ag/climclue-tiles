Climate types (Köppen–Geiger)

The Köppen–Geiger system sorts climates by their yearly pattern of temperature and precipitation. These are the types found among ClimaClue’s towns of 100,000+ people, calculated from each town’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

Tropical (A)

  • Tropical rainforest (Af) · 351 towns · Hot and wet all year, with no real dry season.
  • Tropical monsoon (Am) · 336 towns · Hot all year, with a very wet monsoon season and a short drier spell.
  • Tropical savanna (Aw) · 984 towns · Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season.

Dry (B)

  • Hot desert (BWh) · 368 towns · Very dry and hot: far more water could evaporate than ever falls.
  • Cold desert (BWk) · 60 towns · Very dry, with cold winters.
  • Hot semi-arid (BSh) · 364 towns · Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season.
  • Cold semi-arid (BSk) · 219 towns · Dry grassland climate with cold winters.

Temperate (C)

  • Mediterranean, hot summer (Csa) · 437 towns · Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters.
  • Mediterranean, warm summer (Csb) · 102 towns · Warm, dry summers and mild, wet winters.
  • Humid subtropical, dry winter (Cwa) · 441 towns · Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters.
  • Subtropical highland, dry winter (Cwb) · 130 towns · Mild highland climate with wet summers and dry winters.
  • Subpolar highland, dry winter (Cwc) · 2 towns · Cool highland climate with wet summers and dry winters: found on high tropical mountains.
  • Humid subtropical (Cfa) · 841 towns · Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year.
  • Oceanic (Cfb) · 389 towns · Mild all year, with rain in every season.

Continental (D)

  • Continental, hot dry summer (Dsa) · 41 towns · Hot, dry summers and cold, snowy winters.
  • Continental, warm dry summer (Dsb) · 5 towns · Warm, dry summers and cold, snowy winters.
  • Subarctic, dry summer (Dsc) · 1 town · Short, cool, dry summers and long, cold winters.
  • Continental, hot summer, dry winter (Dwa) · 201 towns · Hot, wet summers and cold, dry winters.
  • Continental, warm summer, dry winter (Dwb) · 48 towns · Warm, wet summers and cold, dry winters.
  • Subarctic, dry winter (Dwc) · 4 towns · Short, cool summers and long, very cold, dry winters.
  • Humid continental, hot summer (Dfa) · 152 towns · Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year.
  • Humid continental, warm summer (Dfb) · 362 towns · Warm summers and cold, snowy winters, with rain or snow all year.
  • Subarctic (Dfc) · 25 towns · Short, cool summers and long, very cold winters.
  • Very cold subarctic (Dfd) · 1 town · Short summers and extremely cold winters.

Polar (E)

  • Tundra (ET) · 5 towns · Too cold for trees: even the warmest month stays cool.

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
