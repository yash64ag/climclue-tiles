Mediterranean, hot summer climate (Köppen Csa)

Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters.

  • Summer is the dry season
  • Most rain falls in the cooler half of the year
  • Long, sunny, hot summers

Where the mediterranean, hot summer climate occurs

437 of ClimaClue’s towns of 100,000+ people have this type, in 36 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 26.6 °C (80 °F), the median coolest month 9.8 °C (50 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 683 mm (26.9 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages for its ~10 km area, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold.
Rain and snow
In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain.
Sunshine and cloud
Summer high pressure brings long sunny spells; winter is cloudier.
Moisture
Summers are often dry inland but can be humid with low cloud near cool coasts.
Wind
Winter storms bring the stronger winds; summer sea breezes are common near coasts.
