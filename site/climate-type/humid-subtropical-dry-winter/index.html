Humid subtropical, dry winter climate (Köppen Cwa)

Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters.

  • Most rain falls in summer, often from a monsoon
  • Winters are dry and mild
  • Hot, humid summers

Where the humid subtropical, dry winter climate occurs

445 of ClimaClue’s towns of 100,000+ people have this type, in 22 countries. Across them, the median warmest month averages 28.6 °C (83 °F), the median coolest month 14.9 °C (59 °F), and the median yearly precipitation is 1,550 mm (61.0 in).

Types are calculated from each town’s own modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages, with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).

What typically causes this climate

Textbook mechanisms that typically produce this type; any one place can differ.

Temperature
Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights.
Rain and snow
Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land.
Sunshine and cloud
Dry winters are sunny; summer monsoon months are cloudier.
Moisture
Dew points climb when the moist summer monsoon arrives.
Wind
Winds shift with the monsoon seasons.
