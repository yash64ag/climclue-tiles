A real ClimaClue answer
Why does it rain so much more here than in Oslo?

Bergen gets about 2,920 mm of rain a year and Oslo about 1,020 mm, nearly three times less. Bergen faces the North Sea: westerly winds bring in moist air that hits the mountains right behind the city and is forced up, cools, and drops its rain. Oslo lies inland, in the rain shadow of Norway’s mountains. The gap is biggest in winter: 302 mm in January in Bergen, against 75 mm in Oslo.

How do the weekly questions and the refill work?

You get 15 questions of your own each week. The count starts again every Monday at 00:00 UTC; unused questions don’t carry over. If you run out, press Use my monthly refill in the chat for 15 more that week. There is one refill per calendar month (it renews on the 1st, UTC), and refills don’t stack: you never hold more than one. ClimaClue’s suggested questions are never counted.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from Manage or cancel on this page when you’re signed in. Your membership carries on to the end of the period you’ve paid for, and isn’t renewed after that.

Can I get a refund?

Membership is cheap, so payments aren’t refundable. Instead, you can cancel any time: you won’t be charged again, and you keep membership to the end of the period you’ve paid for. See the refund policy.

Who handles payment?

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach ClimaClue; we only learn that you’re a member, which plan you chose and when it renews.

Do I need an account?

Yes: membership belongs to your ClimaClue account, so it works on every device you sign in on. Sign in with Google from the top right of the globe, then come back here.
