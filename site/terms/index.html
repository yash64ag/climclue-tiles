What the climate numbers are, and aren’t

ClimaClue shows modelled long-term averages (climate normals, mostly for 1981–2010) for areas about 10 km across. They are estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not weather forecasts. Real weather in any given year, month or day can be very different. Don’t rely on ClimaClue for safety decisions (for example travel in extreme heat, cold, mountains or storms); check official forecasts and local advice. Where the data comes from is set out on the About page.

AI answers can be wrong

ClimaClue’s chat is written by an AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic). It is told to use ClimaClue’s own numbers, but it can still make mistakes, misread a question or state general facts (about visas, languages, costs or anything else) that are out of date or wrong. Treat answers as a helpful starting point, and check anything important with official sources.
