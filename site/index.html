What the climate map shows

ClimaClue is a free interactive climate map on a 3D globe. The globe is coloured by climate, and you choose what it shows: Köppen–Geiger climate zones, average temperature, day highs or night lows, rain and snow, snowfall, sunshine hours, wind or humidity, for the whole year or for any single month. Click a place and its full climate profile opens beside the map.

Questions

Is the climate map free? Yes. Exploring the globe, the monthly maps, comparing places, the daily games and the weather-by-month pages are free. A small membership adds your own questions to the ClimaClue guide and unlimited game rounds.

Where does the data come from? Temperature and precipitation are CHELSA 1981–2010 climate normals; snow and dew point come from ERA5-Land (Copernicus); sunshine from CRU. Every number on the site names its source and period.

How detailed is it? Each square of the map is about 10 km across, covering all land on Earth, and each one shows both its average and how much it varies inside.
