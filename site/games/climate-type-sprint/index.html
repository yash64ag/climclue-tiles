Climate Type Sprint: a Köppen climate quiz

Free daily game

How fast can you read a climate chart? Climate Type Sprint shows a real town’s monthly temperature and rain and asks for its Köppen climate type. You have 60 seconds.

Play now ›

How to play

Each chart comes with four climate types to choose from; answer with a tap or the keys 1 to 4. The wrong options always come from other climate families, so the chart alone is enough to tell them apart at a glance (the three tropical types count as one family here, since they differ only by fine thresholds of dry-season rain). Score as many as you can before the minute is up. After each answer the town is named, with a one-line description of its type. There is one daily set, the same for everyone on the same date, and it changes at your local midnight. Your result can be shared as a short spoiler-free line.

The Köppen–Geiger climate types

The Köppen–Geiger system sorts the world’s climates by their yearly pattern of temperature and precipitation. The first letter is the main group: A tropical (every month averages 18 °C or more), B dry (more water could evaporate than falls as rain), C temperate, D continental (cold winters, warm summers) and E polar. Further letters say when the dry season falls and how hot the summers are. ClimaClue calculates each town’s type from its own 1981–2010 monthly averages with the rules of Peel et al. (2007).

Tips for reading a chart fast

Look at the coldest month first: above 18 °C means tropical; below 0 °C (or −3 °C in some definitions) with a warm summer means continental. Then look at the rain: almost none means a desert, a long dry summer means Mediterranean, a dry winter and very wet summer means a monsoon or savanna pattern. It works well in a classroom: the daily set is the same for every student on the same date, a round takes a few minutes, and every answer comes with a short explanation to discuss.

Place-based games use towns of 100,000+ people. Each town’s values were compared with the nearest NOAA weather station that has 1981–2010 averages, and towns whose values disagree with that station are left out, so a round never rests on a doubtful number.

Questions and answers

What is a Köppen climate quiz?
A quiz where you identify the Köppen–Geiger climate type (such as Mediterranean, humid subtropical or tundra) from a place’s temperature and precipitation.
How long is a Climate Type Sprint?
60 seconds. Answer as many charts as you can, up to 40.
What do the Köppen letters mean?
The first letter is the main group: A tropical, B dry, C temperate, D continental, E polar. Later letters give the dry season and summer heat.
Is the quiz free?
Yes. The daily sprint is free in a web browser.
