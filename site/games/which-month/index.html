Which Month? Guess the month from the weather

A daily weather quiz about seasons. You see one month’s average temperature and rain in a real town; pick which month it is.

Play now ›

How to play

Each of the 6 rounds names a town and gives one month’s average temperature and its total rain and snow. Choose from four months. Once you answer, every card shows that month’s own average in the town, so you can see how the year unfolds there. The town is marked on the globe.

Only towns with real seasons are used (at least 8 °C between the warmest and coolest month), and the four months on offer always differ clearly in temperature or rain, so there is a fair way to tell them apart. There is one daily set, the same for everyone on the same date, and it changes at your local midnight. Your result can be shared as a short spoiler-free line.

What you learn

To answer you need the town’s hemisphere (in Sydney or Buenos Aires, January is summer), its climate (a monsoon town’s wettest month gives it away) and how seasons lag behind the sun: in much of the mid-latitudes the warmest month is July or August in the north, not June, because land and sea take weeks to warm up. It works well in a classroom: the daily set is the same for every student on the same date, a round takes a few minutes, and every answer comes with a short explanation to discuss.

Place-based games use towns of 100,000+ people. Each town’s values were compared with the nearest NOAA weather station that has 1981–2010 averages, and towns whose values disagree with that station are left out, so a round never rests on a doubtful number. Values are CHELSA V2.1 monthly averages for 1981–2010.

What members get: members get unlimited practice rounds of every game and up to 50 saved places (free accounts: 5), from $1 a month (membership); the daily games stay free for everyone.

Questions and answers

How do you play Which Month?
Read one month’s average temperature and rain and snow for a real town, then pick which of four months it is.
Why is July often warmer than June?
Land and oceans take weeks to warm up, so in many mid-latitude places the warmest month comes a month or more after the longest day.
Is Which Month? free?
Yes. The daily game is free in a web browser.
