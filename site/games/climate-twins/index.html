Climate Twins: find cities with the same climate

Places on opposite sides of the world can share almost the same climate. In Climate Twins you see one town’s climate and pick its twin among four far-away towns.

Play now ›

How to play

Each of the 5 rounds shows a real town’s monthly temperature and rain chart, with its name. Below are four towns, all at least 3,000 km away. One has the climate most like it; the others differ clearly. Pick the twin. The twin’s own chart is then drawn over the first one, so you can compare the shapes, and all five towns are marked on the globe.

Twins in opposite hemispheres have their seasons half a year apart (July in one is like January in the other), so the game lines their seasons up before comparing. There is one daily set, the same for everyone on the same date, and it changes at your local midnight. Your result can be shared as a short spoiler-free line.

How “most alike” is measured

The game compares the twelve months of both towns: the typical difference in monthly temperature (in °C), plus the typical difference in monthly rain and snow on a logarithmic scale, so that 10 mm against 20 mm counts as much as 100 mm against 200 mm. A round is only used when the best twin is genuinely close and the three other options are clearly further off. The same measure picks the “climate twins elsewhere” listed on every town page; for example see Lisbon, Cape Town, Kunming.

What you learn

The same few causes repeat around the globe: cool ocean currents along western coasts, subtropical high pressure that dries out summers, mountains that cool the tropics. Climate Twins makes these patterns visible: Mediterranean climates in California, Chile, South Africa and Australia, or mild highland climates near the equator. It works well in a classroom: the daily set is the same for every student on the same date, a round takes a few minutes, and every answer comes with a short explanation to discuss.

Place-based games use towns of 100,000+ people. Each town’s values were compared with the nearest NOAA weather station that has 1981–2010 averages, and towns whose values disagree with that station are left out, so a round never rests on a doubtful number.

Questions and answers

What is a climate twin?
A town far away whose monthly temperature and rain pattern is very close to another town’s, once the seasons are lined up across hemispheres.
How does ClimaClue decide which climates are most alike?
It compares all twelve months: the typical monthly temperature difference plus the typical difference in rain and snow on a log scale.
How far apart are the twins?
All four options in a round are at least 3,000 km from the target town.
Is Climate Twins free?
Yes. The daily game is free in a web browser.
