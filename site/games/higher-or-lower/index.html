Higher or Lower: which town is hotter or wetter?

A quick daily climate game. Each round shows two real towns and asks one question about their climate. Pick the one with the higher value.

Play now ›

How to play

A game has 8 rounds. Each one names two towns, usually in different countries, and asks one of these questions: which is warmer in a given month, which gets more rain and snow in a given month, which gets more over the whole year, which is warmer over the year, which has the bigger gap between its warmest and coolest month, or whose wettest month is wetter. Tap a town. The real values appear in both cards, and both towns are marked on the globe.

Rounds are only asked when the answer is clear-cut: at least 2 °C apart for temperature, and for rain at least 40% and 15 mm apart. Near-ties never decide a round. There is one daily set, the same for everyone on the same date, and it changes at your local midnight. Your result can be shared as a short spoiler-free line.

What you learn

The questions are built to surprise. A tropical town can be cooler than a desert town much further from the equator; a famously rainy city can get less rain in a year than a sunny holiday resort with short, heavy downpours; and the gap between summer and winter grows quickly as you move inland. Every answer comes with the numbers, so you build a real sense of the world’s climates. It works well in a classroom: the daily set is the same for every student on the same date, a round takes a few minutes, and every answer comes with a short explanation to discuss.

Where the numbers come from

Place-based games use towns of 100,000+ people. Each town’s values were compared with the nearest NOAA weather station that has 1981–2010 averages, and towns whose values disagree with that station are left out, so a round never rests on a doubtful number. Values are CHELSA V2.1 monthly averages for 1981–2010 for each town’s ~10 km area. You can look any town up afterwards on its own page in climate by country.

What members get: members get unlimited practice rounds of every game and up to 50 saved places (free accounts: 5), from $1 a month (membership); the daily games stay free for everyone.

Questions and answers

How do you play Higher or Lower?
Each of the 8 rounds shows two real towns and one climate question, such as which is warmer in March. Pick the one with the higher value.
Can a round be a near-tie?
No. A round is only asked when the towns differ clearly: at least 2 °C for temperature, or at least 40% and 15 mm for rain and snow.
Is Higher or Lower free?
Yes. The daily game is free in a web browser.
