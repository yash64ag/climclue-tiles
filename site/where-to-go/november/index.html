Questions

Where is warm in Europe in November?

The warmest big towns in Europe in November are Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (Spain, 23 °C / 73 °F days), Telde (Spain, 22 °C / 72 °F days), Siracusa (Italy, 21 °C / 70 °F days), from 1981–2010 averages.

Where is beach weather in November?

156 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more, a sea of 22 °C (72 °F) or warmer and little rain in November, including Hurghada (North Africa), Jeddah (Middle East), Cape Coral (United States), Acapulco de Juárez (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
