Where is warm in Europe in August?

Where is beach weather in August?

287 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more with little rain in August, including Barcelona (Europe), Alexandria (North Africa), Istanbul (Middle East), La Paz (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
