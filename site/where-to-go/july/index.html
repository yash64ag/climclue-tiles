Where is beach weather in July?

259 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more, a sea of 22 °C (72 °F) or warmer and little rain in July, including Barcelona (Europe), Alexandria (North Africa), Istanbul (Middle East), La Paz (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean).

Is this a forecast?
