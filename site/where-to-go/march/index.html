Questions

Where is warm in Europe in March?

The warmest big towns in Europe in March are Sevilla (Spain, 22 °C / 71 °F days), Dos Hermanas (Spain, 21 °C / 70 °F days), Córdoba (Spain, 21 °C / 70 °F days), from 1981–2010 averages.

Where is beach weather in March?

202 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more, a sea of 22 °C (72 °F) or warmer and little rain in March, including Jeddah (Middle East), Havana (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean), Maracaibo (South America), Mogadishu (Africa south of the Sahara).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
