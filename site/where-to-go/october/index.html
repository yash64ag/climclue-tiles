Where is beach weather in October?

184 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more, a sea of 22 °C (72 °F) or warmer and little rain in October, including Alexandria (North Africa), Jeddah (Middle East), La Paz (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean), Fortaleza (South America).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
