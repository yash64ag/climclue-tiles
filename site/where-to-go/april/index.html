Where is warm in Europe in April?

The warmest big towns in Europe in April are Sevilla (Spain, 23 °C / 74 °F days), Dos Hermanas (Spain, 23 °C / 73 °F days), Murcia (Spain, 23 °C / 73 °F days), from 1981–2010 averages.

Where is beach weather in April?

165 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more, a sea of 22 °C (72 °F) or warmer and little rain in April, including Hurghada (North Africa), Jeddah (Middle East), Tampa (United States), Cancún (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
