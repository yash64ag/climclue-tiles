Questions

Where is warm in Europe in February?

The warmest big towns in Europe in February are Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (Spain, 20 °C / 68 °F days), Telde (Spain, 19 °C / 67 °F days), Alicante (Spain, 18 °C / 64 °F days), from 1981–2010 averages.

Where is beach weather in February?

232 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more with little rain in February, including Jeddah (Middle East), Port-au-Prince (Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean), Maracaibo (South America), Lagos (Africa south of the Sahara).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
