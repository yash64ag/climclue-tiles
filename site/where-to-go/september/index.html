Where is warm in Europe in September?

The warmest big towns in Europe in September are Córdoba (Spain, 33 °C / 91 °F days), Sevilla (Spain, 32 °C / 90 °F days), Dos Hermanas (Spain, 32 °C / 89 °F days), from 1981–2010 averages.

Where is beach weather in September?

217 big coastal towns average day highs of 27 °C (81 °F) or more with little rain in September, including Athens (Europe), Alexandria (North Africa), Jeddah (Middle East), Oxnard (United States).

Is this a forecast?

No. These are 1981–2010 averages, what a typical year looks like. Any single year can be warmer, cooler, wetter or drier.
