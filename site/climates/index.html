Climate themes

Lists of towns that meet a stated rule, applied to modelled 1981–2010 monthly averages for ClimaClue’s towns of 100,000+ people.

  • Cities where it never snows but winters are cool · under 1 cm of snow a year, coldest month 10 °C or less
  • Cities with no winter · coldest month averages 18 °C or more
  • Cities that are mild all year · every month averages 10–25 °C
  • Cities with four distinct seasons · coldest month 0 °C or below, warmest 20 °C+, 20 mm+ of rain every month
  • Monsoon cities: most of the year’s rain in four months · 60%+ of yearly rain in 4 consecutive months, 750 mm+ a year
  • Desert cities · Köppen desert climate (BWh, BWk)
  • Sunniest big cities · towns of 1M+ ranked by yearly sunshine
  • Muggiest cities · muggiest month dew point 22 °C or more
  • Coldest big cities · towns of 1M+ ranked by yearly average temperature
  • Cities with dry summers · Mediterranean climate (Köppen Cs)
  • Cities with the smallest temperature swing through the year · smallest gap between warmest and coldest month
  • Cities with the biggest day-night temperature swing · largest gap between day high and night low

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
