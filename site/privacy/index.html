Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version

You can use ClimaClue without an account. The only cookies we use without an account are Google Analytics cookies, to count visits (in Europe, the UK and Switzerland only if you accept them; see Analytics). Signing in with Google is optional; if you do, we keep your Google account ID, email, name, profile picture link and your saved places, and set one sign-in cookie. We don't sell or share personal data.

What stays in your browser

When you're not signed in, places you save stay in your browser's local storage. Your current view (camera position, selected place, compared places, units, chosen tab) is saved in your browser's local storage so it's there when you come back. It never leaves your device unless you use Share: that puts your view, including any question you typed, into the link you copy.

Accounts (optional): Sign in with Google

If you choose Sign in, Google's sign-in script is loaded from Google (only when you open Sign in) and Google tells ClimaClue who you are. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google does during sign-in. ClimaClue's server then stores:

  • your Google account ID (a number that identifies your Google account to ClimaClue), email address, name and the link to your Google profile picture;
  • when your account was created, and whether it is a member account;
  • your saved places (the names you gave them and their coordinates). When you first sign in, places saved in this browser move into your account and are removed from the browser;
  • a sign-in session: one cookie (__Host-climaclue_session, HttpOnly, Secure) holding a random code; our server stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of it. It lasts 30 days or until you sign out.

These are kept on ClimaClue's own server (Hetzner, in Germany) and are not shared with anyone. We don't ask Google for your contacts, files or anything else. To limit sign-in abuse, the server counts sign-in attempts per internet address in memory only.

Deleting your account: when signed in, open the menu under your name and choose Delete my account; your account details, saved places and sign-in sessions are removed from the server straight away. You can also email contact@climaclue.com and we'll delete it for you. Signing out ends the session on this device; your account and places stay until you delete them.

Membership payments

Membership payments are handled by Stripe: you pay on Stripe's own checkout page, your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach ClimaClue, and Stripe's privacy policy covers what Stripe keeps. For a member account ClimaClue's server also stores the Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the plan (monthly or yearly), the subscription's status and renewal date, and how many of your own questions you've asked this week and whether you've used this month's refill (a count, not the questions). Deleting your account removes these from our server; Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires. See the terms and refund policy.

What your browser asks our servers for

To draw a profile, your browser downloads climate data for the area you click and, when you search or zoom in, a list of towns. These are the same files for everyone and contain no personal data. Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives standard request information such as your IP address in order to deliver the site.

Chatting with ClimaClue

When you ask ClimaClue a question, your question, the place you're looking at, that place's climate numbers and your last few questions and answers in that chat are sent to our server and on to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI model (Claude) that writes the answer. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models. We don't keep your questions. To limit how many questions one visitor can ask per day, our server counts questions per internet address in memory only; the count is never written down and resets daily. Answers to suggested questions are stored after the first time they're asked and reused for everyone, without any information about who asked. Answers are written by AI and can contain mistakes.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies or local storage, and it doesn't track you across sites.

We also use Google Analytics to see which pages are useful and how visitors find the site (for example, which pages are read, for how long, and from which kind of device and country). Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_…, kept up to 2 years) and Google processes this information under its own privacy policy; we keep the data for 14 months. We don't use it for advertising, we don't send Google your name, email or questions, and we don't sell it.

  • In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland no analytics cookies are set unless you click Accept. Until then (or if you click No thanks), Google receives only basic, cookie-less signals that cannot be tied to you.
  • Elsewhere analytics cookies are on by default; you can turn them off below.

Your choice is kept in this browser's local storage. · You can also block or delete cookies in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on.

Email updates

If you sign up for email updates, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, Buttondown, and used only to send ClimaClue updates. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.

Contact

To ask about your data or have it deleted: contact@climaclue.com
