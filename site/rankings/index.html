Values are modelled averages for each town’s own location (the ~3 km around its centre; sunshine, dew point and snow: the ~10 km area around it), not station measurements; ranking is a plain sort of the stored values (ties: larger town first).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), relative humidity calculated from that dew point and the CHELSA night lows and day highs, CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine and the day–night range), NOAA GHCN-Daily (station normals 1981–2010; temperatures and snowfall adjusted toward nearby stations), GeoNames (town names, populations and heights, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
